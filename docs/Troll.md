**Troll** is an other topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2020-04-23. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* Regular trolls have no requirements, but ice trolls need The Fremennik Isles to access them and to use a Neitiznot faceguard.[^cXlY9a7Ne04-0]

## Methods

* To reach the ice trolls, get to Relleka (lyre teleport, house teleport, or a house tablet to the Relleka portal, or Fremennik sea boots 4 with the elite Fremennik diary), take the boat to Jatizso, run through the gates, and set the cannon on a specific tile.[^cXlY9a7Ne04-94]

## Stats and numbers

* Ice trolls give about 50k Slayer XP an hour, along with 75k combat XP an hour and 50k ranged XP an hour.[^cXlY9a7Ne04-0]
* Ice trolls have decent alchable drops in granite shields and rune warhammers, which can in some cases cover the cost of cannonballs.[^cXlY9a7Ne04-0]
* On that task Gnomonkey received only one rune warhammer and no snapdragon or ranarr seeds, which he calls a fairly below-average run, though he still profited after cannonballs; he says on average you should at least break even.[^cXlY9a7Ne04-123]
* That ice troll task gave 12k Slayer XP, about 46k Slayer XP an hour, with 75k melee XP an hour and 50k ranged XP an hour.[^cXlY9a7Ne04-123]

## Tips

* The Neitiznot faceguard tremendously reduces ice trolls' maximum hit and Gnomonkey says you will most likely die without it.[^cXlY9a7Ne04-0]
* Even with Bandos armour the melee trolls smash through your defences, so he uses protect from melee and piety for the whole task.[^cXlY9a7Ne04-94]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey calls troll tasks a classic staple and a great cannonable task.[^cXlY9a7Ne04-0]

## Records

* A full task of 176 trolls took Gnomonkey about 17 minutes, using 550 cannonballs (about 90k) and giving about 120k gp in loot.[^cXlY9a7Ne04-123]
