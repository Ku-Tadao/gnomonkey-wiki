## Methods

* On a first visit to Verzik set up tile indicators for the middle tiles for phase two and for tiles behind pillars (safe from the phase one magic attack), drop potions between a set of pillars as your marked spot, ensure at least one player with a tentacle whip or serpentine helm on each side to poison the purple Nylocas, and keep at least two inventory spaces open for the staff and two-handed weapons.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4030]
* Verzik phase 1: players take turns speccing with the Dawnbringer and attacking Verzik between her attacks, with regular attacks capped at a max hit of 10 and the Dawnbringer spec doing 75 to 150 damage uncapped; the Dawnbringer cannot splash and Gnomonkey says taking damage in P1 is a huge waste since you need the supplies later.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4093]
* Verzik's phase 1 magic attack has a max hit of 68 through prayer and hits anyone in her line of sight; hiding behind a pillar makes her hit the pillar instead, which collapses after about four to five attacks and deals around 50 damage to anyone next to it. After the first pillar falls the team moves to the other northern pillar.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4126]
* To start Verzik phase 1 stand on the ready tile and pray Protect from Magic, as an unprotected mage hit can hit for up to 137; the first player in orb order has the Dawnbringer spec primed and starts the fight, and nobody should repot or use Piety during P1 since the prayer points are not worth it.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4153]
* Each Dawnbringer spec uses 35% special energy; the first in orb order fires two specs then drops the staff, the second picks it up for two specs and runs behind the pillar, and so on through the orb order as specs regenerate, with the others fitting in two whip hits or two scythe hits (one tick of leeway) per attack by clicking Verzik when she spreads her legs wide.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4181]
* Verzik phase 2: she fires ranged skull bombs every four ticks that explode on contact with the ground, and after every four bombs a lightning ball at a random target that bounces up to four times between teammates; if not bounced into Verzik the last player takes about 50, so where you stand to attack matters.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4293]
* In phase 2 Verzik summons Nylocas bombs that target a player and walk toward them, stopping next to the target's tile and exploding a tick later for up to 64 damage at the centre, 8 at two tiles away and 0 at three tiles.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4325]
* In phase 2 Verzik also fires a large purple orb at a random player when summoning Nylocas, dealing over 50 if directly under it, which becomes a large purple Nylocas that gradually heals Verzik until poisoned (poisoning stops the healing and deals a large poison splat).[^sCmYo21i2I8-4325]
* At 35% health in phase 2 Verzik summons two red Nylocas on each side and puts up a barrier for a short time where any damage dealt to her heals her for the full amount; the red Nylocas heal her their full remaining health after several of her attacks, and she begins using magic attacks that heal her while still occasionally firing skull bombs.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4355]
* At the start of phase 2, each player stands on their tile on their potion side, uses the brief window while she moves to the centre to repot and activate Piety and Protect from Ranged; Gnomonkey recommends a whip over a scythe for inexperienced players since timing is more lenient, going in for one hit right after she attacks and zigzagging out, using her cape moving as the cue.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4387]
* Once Verzik hits 35%, activate Protect from Magic and attack the red Nylocas only as needed because they heal Verzik one to one; scythers should return to Verzik after two hits (only two hits land on the Nylocas) while whippers lower the Nylocas health.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4474]
* Verzik phase 3 is the final challenge and a DPS race where most food is used; she turns into a spider and alternates magic and ranged attacks randomly, with damage calculated when the attack hits like Jad or Nightmare, so switch prayers to match the telegraphed attack; keep health above 60 and recombat pot after brewing.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4503]
* In phase 3 Verzik selects a random tank; if the tank is in melee range when she attacks she can melee the whole team for up to 60, so the tank should stay under her or step away each attack, and if the tank is out of melee range for more than two attacks she swaps tanks. She does four normal attacks before each special in a fixed order: Nylocas bombs, webs, musical chairs (yellow tiles), then a death ball.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4536]
* When Verzik summons bombs in phase 3, spread out first to see who they target and pull them away from the tank if they target you; after four more attacks she goes to the centre and sprays webs, and a player stepping on a web is stunned and takes around 50 when it disappears unless teammates deal at least 8 damage to it.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4568]
* Experienced teams web run in phase 3: wait on the tile for Verzik to reach the centre, spam click, hit and wait a tick, then run and hit on each side of her; others put on mage gear and staff and hit her while running clockwise around her, freeing anyone caught in webs. The tank can hit twice before going under Verzik thanks to a delay after webs.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4595]
* In musical chairs Verzik becomes immune to damage and yellow tiles appear around the arena, one per player; each player must stand alone on a tile, and if two or more share a tile everyone takes the full yellow orb damage of around 70. Gnomonkey says to stay put once on a tile, and in a pinch you have about two ticks to tick eat the orb or can chug bruise.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4627]
* Verzik's death ball hits a random player for 474, so you usually tank it, healing to 115 with bruise during musical chairs; if a teammate is standing on you when it lands it bounces to them for small damage to both, and bouncing it to three unique players dissipates it, which Gnomonkey says is rarely practical.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4655]
* Below 20% Verzik enrages, which Gnomonkey calls the most dangerous and difficult part of the raid: she summons a purple tornado per player, each following only its owner, and being hit takes half your current HP and heals Verzik (a 30 hit heals her 3% of your total health); she also attacks every 5 ticks instead of 7.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4715]
* Verzik P1 improvement: when not holding the staff, open with a claw swipe then three scythe hits and get behind the pillar tick perfectly (four scythe hits do not fit; four whip hits always fit), or switch to a staff behind the pillar for a pre-hit then swap to scythe for two hits; the same pre-hit works with the Dawnbringer after the first four players used two specs.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4869]
* Scythe walking at Verzik P2: she has a four-tick attack cycle and the scythe is five-tick, so you stand next to her on any safe tick and leave only when she attacks; you click when you see her cape move and click back onto the bombs when they explode, but visual cues are unreliable when she skips bombs or teammates run far.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4899]
* In hard mode, Verzik P2 is very different from regular ToB: her lightning only maxes at 14, so the player does not need to constantly tick-eat it.[^43IYFOMfalw-1501]
* In hard mode Verzik P2, her bombs drop poison, so the player has to avoid the crab pop tile.[^43IYFOMfalw-1501]
* At 35%, only one red crab spawns in hard mode Verzik instead of two, so the fight can be done completely normally without tick-eating for 15 minutes.[^43IYFOMfalw-1718]
* Verzik P3 in hard mode is just an extremely long normal P3 fight; the player has to make as few mistakes as possible so supplies hold out, and uses ancient godsword effects where possible for health.[^43IYFOMfalw-2021]
* In his solo ToB, Gnomonkey skipped the Scythe at one room and cast instead, saying that with heart (as transcribed) and full mage robes casting is slightly better because Scythe hit splats are capped at 10 while the spell is uncapped with a decent max hit, and casting does not lose ticks like the Scythe does.[^gO3aMYXs-QA-8738]
* The lightning attacks in the Verzik fight hit 50 every single time, so you have to tick eat them to survive.[^gO3aMYXs-QA-8931]
* Gnomonkey warns in a solo not to swing at Verzik after she drops under 35%, or the raid will end.[^gO3aMYXs-QA-12312]
* In solo ToB, Gnomonkey says if you attack Verzik at any point after the threshold she spawns red crabs which heal her indefinitely, so the only option is to wait for purple crabs to spawn and damage her by poisoning them while you tick eat for 15 minutes.[^gO3aMYXs-QA-12741]
* At Verzik P2 in solo Theatre of Blood after the update, Gnomonkey uses mage and piety, relies on Blood Fury healing to tank the lightning, and brews or uses Redemption when heals are unlucky; he notes you may not need to tick eat at all.[^nJICcC6IrLQ-866]
* Gnomonkey used Blood Barrage on Verzik P1 in the run, calling it tech, and says it always seemed to give three heals.[^nJICcC6IrLQ-889]

## Stats and numbers

* Verzik's max hit is over a player's max health, so a mistake at Verzik P2 means instant death.[^nJICcC6IrLQ-866]

## Tips

* New Verzik players should drop a large amount of potions (all but two brews and two restores), because if you die with them your team cannot use them; as you get comfortable you can drop fewer.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4059]
* After the pillar goes down in phase 1 you can still attack twice then run diagonally to the other pillar; if forced to use the back pillars swap to mage for two to three mage hits. When Verzik's HP hits zero all remaining pillars collapse, so be away from them.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4234]
* In phase 2 zigzag towards the centre of Verzik rather than above or below her, since otherwise lightning can bounce to the other side without hitting her; a bounce can deal up to about 40 damage and usually leads to a bomb hit, so if bounced immediately click back on to get back in cycle.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4417]
* In phase 2, if a Nylocas bomb is coming at you, wait until it is one tile away then run three tiles away, but do not run to the edge of the map, since lightning then will not chain to teammates and hits you for 50 and bomb timing is slower for everyone; poison the purple Nylocas with a free hit if you have a tentacle whip or serpentine helm.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4447]
* Communicate before starting the sub-20% phase of Verzik and hold off attacking if she is near 20% right before a dangerous special like musical chairs; once below 20% keep moving as the phase starts (you can often run through a tornado and avoid damage), and dump both claw specs, which if the whole team does without taking a tornado is usually enough to KO her.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4747]
* In the sub-20% Verzik phase, priorities are one, not taking a tornado, two, not dying, and three, hitting the boss; a tank should keep running from Verzik, getting one hit per her attack to avoid melee, and can run through her if stuck in a corner. Getting caught in a web is a guaranteed tornado.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4777]
* In the sub-20% phase, if only one person web runs, Verzik may shoot in front of them so run a bit further each hit; for musical chairs run farther from the yellow tile to lure the tornado away then run toward it, and if timing is off just tick eat the yellow orb and keep moving; this is why the team waits until after yellow tiles to start sub-20%.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4810]
* A trainer exists for scythe walking Verzik (linked in the description) though it does not feel 100% accurate to him, and suggests practising on bronze or iron dragons near the Catacombs entrance in full tank gear with an anti-dragon shield and a low-damage five-tick weapon, for around 30 minutes before attempting it in the Theatre of Blood.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4929]
* Players with Lunars can cast Pot Share on teammates' super restores during the Verzik fight to save total restores, easiest on off-ticks while attacking Verzik or at any time below 35%.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4963]
* For Verzik P3 below 20%, Gnomonkey says to stop worrying about your own health, pray magic and stop switching prayers, dump both claw specs and hit at every opportunity even while healing; he says players running around taking tornadoes and doing no damage would help the team more by being dead.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4991]
* Vengeance casters comfortable with P3 can pot share a stamina to everyone before webs so teammates bring more brews instead of stamina; to always reach webs, count four attacks after bombs come out then stand in the web position. Tanks can one-tick tank by moving away or under Verzik only the tick before she attacks to sometimes gain an extra scythe swing.[^sCmYo21i2I8-5019]
* Gnomonkey considers leaving your teammates only a single potion after the Verzik kill rude and says to steal as many potions as possible immediately after the kill ends while leaving more for others, comparing it to picking up garbage for friends after a movie.[^sCmYo21i2I8-5047]
* Gnomonkey describes the trio 1-1-2 spec order in Verzik phase 1, where the first two players do one spec each and the next does two before getting behind the pillar, all tick perfect.[^HkVt4enEesI-4417]
* Before Verzik, Gnomonkey and his helpers discuss supplies: five extra doses of prayer, divine potion at the start of P2, three restores for P3, no flicking in P3 and two shield charges for the red phase.[^43IYFOMfalw-1283]
* Verzik P1 in hard mode is the same as in regular solo ToB; Gnomonkey alternates sides because she drops rocks around the pillars, and a mage hit can hit over ninety-nine.[^43IYFOMfalw-1377]
* Gnomonkey describes a green ball redemption trick: it works the same as tick-eating the green ball, but only works with Redemption prayer, not by eating normal food.[^43IYFOMfalw-2133]
* He takes his Blood Fury off while swinging at Verzik P3 to prevent healing, because healing too much can prevent Redemption from going off and kill him.[^43IYFOMfalw-2133]
* Viewers told him procing the red crabs is tech because the raid just ends and you do not wait the 15 minutes of tick eating, and he tried it.[^gO3aMYXs-QA-9164]
* At Verzik P2 Gnomonkey says Verzik's mage attack drains prayer, which he thinks is about five prayer points per hit, so prayer can get tight and he flicks a bit.[^nJICcC6IrLQ-866]
* After the update, Verzik can still shoot lightning in solo, but only after throwing four bombs in a row, and her mage attacks do not count toward that, so the lightning is very rare.[^nJICcC6IrLQ-1002]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey calls Verzik by far the most dangerous boss of the Theatre of Blood, with three phases that are more like separate boss fights: a pillar phase swapping the Dawnbringer, a middle-of-the-room bomb phase, and a spider phase alternating ranged and magic.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4003]
* Gnomonkey treats Verzik Vitur as one boss with three phases and says explaining all the mechanics would take about ten minutes.[^HkVt4enEesI-4320]
* Gnomonkey says phase 2 has the least interesting skill of the fight: a four tick attack cycle, healing crabs after 35%, and some skill in knowing when to ignore the crabs.[^HkVt4enEesI-4445]
* Gnomonkey says Verzik has to be an S tier fight, especially the tornadoes at the end, with a lot of skill in not losing ticks, and that it ends up above Nylocas.[^HkVt4enEesI-4574]
* Gnomonkey asks Jagex to change regular solo ToB so the 35% red crab phase works like the hard mode one.[^43IYFOMfalw-1718]
* Gnomonkey concludes hard mode Verzik is winnable.[^43IYFOMfalw-2514]
* Gnomonkey says Blood Fury makes the solo P2 change night and day, since he no longer has to run away and start tick eating and can simply click Verzik.[^nJICcC6IrLQ-1062]

## Trivia

* Gnomonkey describes phase 1: Verzik attacks about every 15 ticks hitting up to a 60 through Protect from Magic, you hide behind pillars, Scythe and thralls give the most DPS, and you cast Dawnbringer specs in an order across the team.[^HkVt4enEesI-4320]
* Gnomonkey describes phase 3: she picks a tank who must stay out of melee range on a seven tick cycle, spawns tick bombs, web running is easy, yellow tiles are about communication, and the green ball hits up to a 74 so health must be high enough.[^HkVt4enEesI-4511]
