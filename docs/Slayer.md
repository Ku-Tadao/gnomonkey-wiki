Slayer is a skill Gnomonkey covers in two ways: 2020 to 2022 task-by-task guides with inventories and gear, and from 2024 opinion and update coverage in which he calls it the slowest skill in the game, clunky and archaic, while welcoming the 2025 point-cost reductions and block list changes.

[TOC]

## Opinion on the skill

- In July 2024 he found Slayer incredibly tedious and inefficient, while understanding that Ironman accounts need supplies from Slayer monsters[^e0y_auD9JhY-232]. He said it is not the best early money-making despite what people swear by, because effective grinding costs money and by the time it is done properly the player is only breaking even while getting the XP[^e0y_auD9JhY-292]. See [Ironman Mode](Ironman_Mode.md).
- His advice then was to take the fastest tasks for high XP rather than boss tasks, unless sidetracking into pet hunting, and return to bosses later with max gear; his Discord has a spreadsheet of a block list for maximum XP per hour[^e0y_auD9JhY-321].
- In July 2025 he said Slayer is the slowest skill in the game, feels clunky and archaic, and that tasks added over time are not worth doing for Slayer XP, but that the blog's changes remove many pain points[^0hoSb6coaVk-825].
- In September 2025 he said anything cannonable or hittable with a Venator bow is good in his book for Slayer because it is really slow, and that anything added with bad Slayer XP makes it even slower[^sK0iG_wg3QA-400][^sK0iG_wg3QA-722].

## 2025 Slayer update

The big Slayer update arrived in August 2025; because every master has a separate block list, players wanted new updated block lists[^BWnqrytMj8I-0]. See also [Update: Slayer block lists per master](Update_Slayer_block_lists_per_master.md) and [Slayer task blocking](Slayer_task_blocking.md).

| Unlock / change | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Mazchna / Vannaka points per task | (not stated) | 6 / 8[^0hoSb6coaVk-269] |
| Bigger and Badder | 150 points | 50 points[^0hoSb6coaVk-303] |
| Task storage | 1000 points | 500 points[^0hoSb6coaVk-336][^BWnqrytMj8I-113] |
| Ring Bling | 300 points | 150 points[^0hoSb6coaVk-398] |
| Slug Salter, Reptile Freezer | (reptile freezer recalled as around 100) | 10 points each[^0hoSb6coaVk-398][^BWnqrytMj8I-27] |

- Gnomonkey said Mazchna and Vannaka tasks still will not be worth doing, but new players are not hit as hard by the early masters[^0hoSb6coaVk-269]. He said Bigger and Badder can now be unlocked on a new iron with a single 10-point streak and that superior creatures like the giant cave crawler will now be seen early[^0hoSb6coaVk-303]. Task storage was previously mostly a post-99 unlock (for example storing a Lizardman shaman task for Chambers of Xeric speed runs) and is now probably worth unlocking on the way to 99[^0hoSb6coaVk-366]; he called the change massive and worth getting early to save bosses for later or to do [Turael](Turael.md) with the new block list[^BWnqrytMj8I-113]. He called Ring Bling an incredibly good unlock that was too expensive at 300 and fairer at 150[^0hoSb6coaVk-398].
- Reduced unlock costs were refunded[^0hoSb6coaVk-432]. After the update block lists were wiped and refunds arrived in the mail; he logged in with about 700 points on his hardcore, and others could have up to about 1000[^BWnqrytMj8I-84]. The reptile freezer automatically finishes lizards at zero HP, though auto-finish unlocks waste one tick per kill[^BWnqrytMj8I-27].
- He suggested re-toggling an unlocked task toggle might cost about a third of the unlock price, since Jagex worries that free toggling would create an unfun UI-toggling loop[^0hoSb6coaVk-462].
- The Slayer master interface has a new "view list" button that shows exact task odds and lets the player block tasks without first getting the task; it shows quantities based on extends but sorts only alphabetically[^BWnqrytMj8I-911]. He says to block efficiently rather than whatever is disliked, because otherwise the player finishes with leftover points; once all needed unlocks are bought, points are not needed[^BWnqrytMj8I-872].
- He judged the changes great, making Turael tremendously faster and the wanted task much easier to get, with much less friction[^BWnqrytMj8I-969].

### Hydra to 99 (2021)

Gnomonkey calculated that 95 to 99 Slayer purely on Hydra needs 3,230 kills, an average of 21 Hydra tasks[^7ehTfsD--gM-817]. With the highest-weight Konar task blocked and every other task skipped, that takes about 570 skip points on average, so around 1,000 points should near-guarantee skipping every non-Hydra task[^7ehTfsD--gM-838]. A slaughter bracelet switch every kill would need only 16 tasks and 450 skip points on average, which he considers good if the pet is wanted[^7ehTfsD--gM-838]. Task cancelling with Turael is also not terrible if only Hydra is wanted[^7ehTfsD--gM-868]. See [Slayer point boosting](Slayer_point_boosting.md).

### Grotesque Guardians (2022)

Gnomonkey highly recommends the Gargoyle smasher unlock for [Grotesque Guardians](Grotesque_Guardians.md) trips and does not recommend extending the gargoyle task, which is already long; a [Crystal halberd](Crystal_halberd.md) helps tremendously, though other options are decent. He advises against the double treble unlock (smashing Dawn also counts as a task kill), since it only ends the task faster and costs 500 points[^b_kFNIJAcGE-62][^b_kFNIJAcGE-95].

## Thralls on Slayer bosses (2021)

- At the Dagannoth Kings thralls work perfectly and damage whichever king is attacked; with a [Sanguinesti staff](Sanguinesti_staff.md) and Saradomin godsword no Blood Barrage healing is needed[^26wPQVuqmNY-448].
- He names the [Kalphite Queen](Kalphite_Queen.md) the standout Slayer boss for thralls, since they keep attacking under her and can continue through her phase transition, and she has defence rather than real prayers[^26wPQVuqmNY-448]. At [Cerberus](Cerberus.md) thralls give free damage[^26wPQVuqmNY-448].
- At the Grotesque Guardians thralls give free damage and can help hit the heal skip on Dawn more often, though Explorer's ring menu time might cancel the saving; he said it needs more testing[^26wPQVuqmNY-479]. At Sarachnis thralls lag behind then barely waste ticks and help clean up minions; an Explorer's ring gives enough alchemy casts for the trip[^26wPQVuqmNY-479][^26wPQVuqmNY-509].
- At the Kraken no thralls do any damage, not even the mage thrall, which he says seems like a bug[^26wPQVuqmNY-515]. See [Thralls](Thralls.md).

## New Slayer content (2025 to 2026)

- **Varlamore (2025-01):** The Final Dawn adds a new Slayer dungeon with several variants and tiers of monsters with hefty HP, low to mid defence and some elemental weakness, which he compares to crabs; he hoped they would be cannonable with decent XP[^4YGVut4hMtA-1210][^4YGVut4hMtA-1241]. See [Varlamore: The Final Dawn](Varlamore_The_Final_Dawn.md).
- **Sailing (2025-09 and 2025-11):** he was not worried about the gryphon task being forced, noting from the blog that the new creatures are not forced and are not a Slayer nerf[^sK0iG_wg3QA-90]. He thought the blog good for Slayer, considered Aquanites likely a high-XP task, and hoped single-target additions like lava strykewyrms get buffed XP so they do not clog the unlock list[^sK0iG_wg3QA-1036]. In the prep blog he noted the Slayer level is for the new monsters, which drop dragon parts for the ship, that griffins are around 50 Slayer so mid-level players can access them, and that reaching them quickly could be a good money source[^pwZKK8dpq8o-472][^pwZKK8dpq8o-505]. See [Sailing](Sailing.md) and [Update: Sailing slayer monsters](Update_Sailing_slayer_monsters.md).
- **Vampyres (2026-01):** venators (alongside sentinels) are an alternative on vampyre tasks, need 74 Slayer, are mostly single combat and reward-focused, and give a higher rate of blood shards; he says they address crowded sentinels and give irons a decent passive source of blood shards[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-660]. See [Vampyre](Vampyre.md).
- **Frost dragons (2026-05):** Jagex is adding a task extension and a weighting increase for frost dragons, with dragon cheats dropping more frequently from frost dragons and lava strykewyrms; he called the extension much needed since tens of thousands must be killed, and the cheats far too rare[^7WIZw1stPas-210].
- **Summer Sweep-up (2026-06):** new early-game drops are the amulets of air from killerwatts, water from sea snake hatchlings, earth from molanisks and fire from infernal mages, each at 1 in 64, giving +2 max hit to spells of the element and +10 magic accuracy[^6tsf-brc-sE-2560]. He would not have put them on monsters nobody kills, preferring early monsters included in Turael tasks[^6tsf-brc-sE-2594]. A necklace of fangs drops from Turoths at 1 in 128 with +1 ranged strength and +12 ranged accuracy; he thinks it is a bigger deal than the magic amulets, as irons will want it as their main ranged strength until an amulet of anguish[^6tsf-brc-sE-2621][^6tsf-brc-sE-2654]. Abyssal creature drop rates from the harder monsters are increased and their defence reduced, because players were killing only the leeches[^6tsf-brc-sE-2688]. See [Update: Summer Sweep-up](Update_Summer_Sweep-up.md) and [Elemental amulets](Elemental_amulets.md).
- **Mortimer (2026-07):** Jagex says [Mortimer](Mortimer.md) is not designed to replace [Duradel](Duradel.md), Konar or other masters, that Duradel and Mortimer are currently extremely close in XP per hour and that Duradel should pull ahead as more block slots unlock[^nLY-srkZ_N8-507]. Duradel offers tasks Mortimer cannot, notably many more boss tasks[^nLY-srkZ_N8-567]. Gnomonkey says Konar is still best for only a Dragon hunter lance or grinding Hydra, because Turael skipping cannot be used with Mortimer[^nLY-srkZ_N8-567]. He thinks Mortimer suits players below 99 Slayer with several goals at once (the heart, Slayer XP, clue scrolls) and is less beneficial at 99 for the heart alone[^nLY-srkZ_N8-711], and says on a fresh hardcore ironman he might train Sailing to reach Mortimer[^nLY-srkZ_N8-834].

## Suggested changes (2026-05 wish-list)

- Slayer helmet recolours cost around 9,000 points to unlock all and the level 99 helmet needs another 1,000, which he considers absurd; he suggests a one-time 1,000 point unlock or 100 to 200 points each[^NzW7JFgc8qU-299][^NzW7JFgc8qU-325][^NzW7JFgc8qU-358].
- Toggleable task extensions once unlocked, noting revenants are the only task toggleable for free, so the coding exists[^NzW7JFgc8qU-358][^NzW7JFgc8qU-388].
- A "prefer task" option costing about 100 points with three slots giving the preferred task double weight, to reduce Turael skipping, make Slayer less of a slog and help get an [Imbued heart](Imbued_heart.md)[^NzW7JFgc8qU-421][^NzW7JFgc8qU-451].
- A very rare (around 1 in 5,000) imbued heart drop from all Slayer bosses, like a pet[^NzW7JFgc8qU-482], and a toggle for the harder-master prompt shown when swapping masters through NPC Contact[^NzW7JFgc8qU-543].
- Rebalancing some Slayer levels: the Kraken trident drop at 87 should be in the mid-70s, [Cerberus](Cerberus.md) is at an incredibly high level for its loot, and a number of unlocks could drop five to ten levels[^NzW7JFgc8qU-2248][^NzW7JFgc8qU-2281].
