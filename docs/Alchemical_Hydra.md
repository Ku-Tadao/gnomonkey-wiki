The Alchemical Hydra is a Slayer boss that Gnomonkey presents as one of the most profitable soloable bosses in the game and an easy one to learn, "basically Vorkath with valuable rare drops"[^7ehTfsD--gM-0]. His guides (2020 and 2021) cover requirements, gear, the pool-luring mechanic, the lightning and fire skips, and profit rates; later tier-list videos rate the fight itself as dull.

[TOC]

## Requirements
- 95 [Slayer](Slayer.md) and a Hydra task from Konar, since only Konar can assign Hydras[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-30][^7ehTfsD--gM-0].
- At least 90 [Ranged](Ranged.md) and a high [Prayer](Prayer.md) level with Rigor unlocked for much more DPS[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-30][^7ehTfsD--gM-32].
- The hard diary gives the teleport to the mountain (three daily teleports)[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-30][^7ehTfsD--gM-32]. In 2021 he also recommends the elite diary so Devout boots can be worn and the floor's heat damage ignored[^7ehTfsD--gM-32]; in 2020 the same effect came from high Prayer plus Rigor with Pegasian boots[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-30].
- Past the rock before the entrance the fight is instanced, so he advises the lowest-ping world to make the skips easier[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-233][^7ehTfsD--gM-281]. Items dropped on the floor stay for 30 minutes (2021: "over 30 minutes") instead of two, so spare food and potions can be left there[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-233][^7ehTfsD--gM-281].

## Gear
- **Head:** a Slayer helmet, since the player is always on task, which makes Void a poor choice[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-60][^7ehTfsD--gM-65].
- **Weapon, 2021:** the [Twisted bow](Twisted_bow.md) with dragon arrows is the best, about two extra kills an hour over a [blowpipe](Toxic_blowpipe.md) for a total of 27; the blowpipe has slightly less DPS and much higher supply cost[^7ehTfsD--gM-65]. Earlier (2020) he named the twisted bow with amethyst arrows as best, with the blowpipe next[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-60].
- The [Dragon hunter crossbow](Dragon_hunter_crossbow.md) also works, up to 6.5% more DPS than the blowpipe if ruby bolts hit 100 and used as a switch with the pipe; with amethyst darts switch back to the pipe at 22% (240 HP) remaining, with dragon darts at 38% (420 HP). He says not to bother with the crossbow if a twisted bow is owned[^7ehTfsD--gM-94]. In 2020 he already noted the blowpipe out-DPSes the crossbow during parts of the fight because Hydra's defence is low, and did not switch to a blowpipe with ruby bolts at the end[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-60][^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-91].
- Dragon darts gain only one max set against Hydra, so amethyst darts are more profitable, though dragon darts make sense for getting the pet faster[^7ehTfsD--gM-94]. The [Bow of Faerdhinen](Bow_of_Faerdhinen.md) is about 8% worse DPS than the blowpipe even with crystal armour, so he would never bring it[^7ehTfsD--gM-158].
- Because Hydra has very low defence, he prioritises prayer bonus: Necklace of anguish, Ring of the gods (Ring of suffering acceptable) and Crystal armour, which gives two extra prayer bonus and uses few charges[^7ehTfsD--gM-127]. Blessed dragonhide or Armadyl armour are acceptable[^7ehTfsD--gM-127].
- **Boots:** Devout boots are best, blessed dragonhide boots are fine, and Brimstone boots are the next best without the diary[^7ehTfsD--gM-127]. In 2020 he wore Pegasian boots, with Blessed dragonhide boots acceptable if Pegasians are unaffordable[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-30][^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-120].
- **Other slots:** any god bracers for gloves; the [Max cape](Max_cape.md) is best-in-slot (the Assembler gives no max hit), then a ranged cape, with an Assembler acceptable[^7ehTfsD--gM-158].
- Rada's blessing is kept in the inventory for teleporting to Mount Karuulm[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-120][^7ehTfsD--gM-127].

## Inventory and supplies
- Inventory: Antidote++, three divine ranging potions, a rune pouch with alching or elemental runes and a free slot for rune drops (usually blood runes), roughly eleven manta rays (2021: about 11 food healing 22) and twelve prayer potions[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-150][^7ehTfsD--gM-186]. He suggests at least four food if very consistent, since some damage is guaranteed[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-150][^7ehTfsD--gM-217].
- He does not recommend Saradomin brews, which stop use of divine ranging potions' DPS benefit[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-177], and said divine bastion potions, if released, would be better than divine ranging potions[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-177].
- In 2021 he does not recommend thralls with an Explorer's ring 4, since the alchs run out before a task ends[^7ehTfsD--gM-186].
- A bonecrusher necklace can be switched in after the killing blow, because Jagex removed the delay before it could restore prayer. Hydra bones are worth four prayer points and Hydra drops two, so eight prayer points are refunded per kill; every 17 kills equals one super restore at 99 Prayer with a holy wrench[^7ehTfsD--gM-217][^7ehTfsD--gM-248].
- Blowpipe special attacks do 50% more damage; in 2020 he saves them for the third or fourth phase, in 2021 for the fire phase or last phase[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-531][^7ehTfsD--gM-633].
- Tile indicators help a lot, and all of them can be added from outside the boss room. In 2020 he used a layout from Mammal's guide, slightly improved by reducing walking distance; in 2021 he provides an import code in the description[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-652][^7ehTfsD--gM-720].

## Getting there
- Hydra is in the Karuulm Slayer Dungeon. The fastest route is a teleport with Rada's blessing; otherwise fairy ring code CIR and climbing the nearby cliffside[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-206][^7ehTfsD--gM-264].
- With 88 Agility the pipe leads straight to Hydra, which is not faster but avoids the other hydras[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-206][^7ehTfsD--gM-264].
- The door cannot be used to leave while the Hydra is alive, so a teleport is needed if Rada's blessing is not brought[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-652][^7ehTfsD--gM-686].

## Mechanics
- Hydra has two regular attacks, magic and ranged, using one style for three attacks and then swapping; special attacks are ignored in this rotation[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-261][^7ehTfsD--gM-308]. He notes the claim that ranged comes from the left heads and magic from the right, but finds the attack's shape easier to watch[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-261]. By December 2021 Jagex had added an animation swap when the style changes, so counting attacks is no longer strictly necessary, although the third attack after the fire phase still matters[^7ehTfsD--gM-308].
- Walking under Hydra is safe because it has no stomp or melee attack[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-294][^7ehTfsD--gM-370].
- Three special attacks, one per phase type, are used after the first three attacks in a phase and again after nine more; the counter resets when the phase ends, and with high DPS usually only one special is seen per phase[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-294][^7ehTfsD--gM-339].
  - **Green and last phases (poison):** poison fires on one tile or in a shotgun spread; moving a couple of tiles avoids the guaranteed splotch on the player's tile, which is why he brings an Antidote++[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-324][^7ehTfsD--gM-370]. Poison globules stay on the floor, and a single tile can be crossed by starting on the tile before and running to the tile after[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-446][^7ehTfsD--gM-512].
  - **Blue phase (lightning):** four electrical tornadoes chase the player, deal about 10 damage and freeze in place[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-324][^7ehTfsD--gM-399].
  - **Red phase (fire):** the player is walled in and fire is shot behind them; a hit deals 10 to 20 damage plus five hits of 5, which he calls the most punishing special[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-353][^7ehTfsD--gM-399].
- Hydra must be lured onto a coloured pool each phase except the last, or its defence is not lowered (2021: damage reduced by 75%); a roar and chat message confirm a correct lure[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-384][^7ehTfsD--gM-424]. Luring early raises its max hit each time the pool spouts but reduces time in damage-reduction mode; he would avoid it while learning[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-417][^7ehTfsD--gM-455].
- Phases: green, lure to the south-eastern red pool; below 825 HP (75%) it turns blue and goes to the north-eastern green pool; below 550 HP (50%) it turns red and goes to the north-western blue pool; below 275 HP (25%) it turns grey and needs no lure[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-384][^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-417][^7ehTfsD--gM-455].

## Strategy
- Start with Protect from Magic, as magic is slightly more dangerous than ranged[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-417][^7ehTfsD--gM-482].
- After every kill he stands on the south-eastern red-pool tile, which always lures the respawn; on the first kill of a trip the player may need to move around to lure properly[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-446][^7ehTfsD--gM-512][^1bBT0uOCQsM-0]. He snakes around the Hydra in the first phase to keep damaging while pulling it closer[^1bBT0uOCQsM-0].
- **Blue phase:** after the third attack lightning forms; waiting until the nearest lightning is close before running west to the lure tile avoids being hit[^1bBT0uOCQsM-30][^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-477][^7ehTfsD--gM-542]. He added a lightning skip in 2021: with a one-tile gap to the lightning, step diagonally one tile and back to trap the lightning under the player; it does not despawn until the next phase, so run a good distance before leaving the tile[^7ehTfsD--gM-561].
- **Red phase fire skip:** after three attacks the Hydra walks to the middle of the room and the player must already be on the lure tile; when its head moves, running to the lower tile skips the fire phase, giving several ticks[^1bBT0uOCQsM-85][^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-504][^7ehTfsD--gM-604]. Not being on the tile when it starts gets the player bumped or stunned, ruining the skip[^1bBT0uOCQsM-85][^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-504]. If it fails, turn off run and walk until the fire stops following; if stunned off the tile, spam-click to the right tile when the head moves and then to the skip tile[^7ehTfsD--gM-604]. He calls the skip easy and better than fire walking, and says to do it every encounter[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-531].
- **Final phase:** the first prayer is the opposite of the last attack received in the fire phase, then prayers alternate[^1bBT0uOCQsM-85][^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-568][^7ehTfsD--gM-649]. Poison comes after the third attack, dodged by walking two tiles south[^1bBT0uOCQsM-111], and a second poison follows after another nine attacks, rare when potted up[^1bBT0uOCQsM-111][^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-595]. A missed prayer changes max hit from two hits of up to 17 to one hit up to 52[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-595][^7ehTfsD--gM-660]. He does not consider "Jad phase" accurate because no reaction is required[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-568][^7ehTfsD--gM-649].
- **Between kills:** Hydra takes about 20 seconds to respawn; leaving and re-entering from the rock is slightly faster but despawns floor items, so he does not bother[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-626][^7ehTfsD--gM-686]. He tidies the inventory, picks up restores and decants them, then walks to the lure tile[^1bBT0uOCQsM-141]. At the end of a trip he fills the inventory with bones (6.5k each in 2020, under 5k in 2021), uses Rada's blessing and then the minigame teleport to Clan Wars[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-637][^7ehTfsD--gM-686].
- After a death, items are reclaimed from the NPC outside the boss room; without the diary he brings new boots of stone, as a second death loses everything permanently[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-685][^7ehTfsD--gM-747].

## Rates and profit

| Source | Kills/hr | Loot/hr | Supplies/hr | Net/hr |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2020 guide | 24 to 25 (max gear, blowpipe) | over 5m | about 500k | 4 to 4.5m with supply drops[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-711][^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-742] |
| 2021 guide | 24 to 25 (max gear, blowpipe) | over 4m | about 500k | about 3.5m blowpipe, about 4m twisted bow[^7ehTfsD--gM-747][^7ehTfsD--gM-778] |

- An average kill takes about two minutes plus about 20 seconds respawn[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-711][^7ehTfsD--gM-747]. The twisted bow gives slightly more DPS, an extra kill or two an hour and lower supply use[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-711].
- Much of the profit is the hydra claw used to make the Dragon hunter lance, valued at 80 million in 2020 and 50 million in 2021[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-711][^7ehTfsD--gM-747]. Even without rare drops he says it is easily over 3m gp an hour[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-0][^7ehTfsD--gM-0].
- On average 3 to 4 brimstone keys drop per task, which he says the OSRS Wiki calculator leaves out, adding about 50k gp/hr[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-742][^7ehTfsD--gM-805].
- He gets about 30k Slayer XP and 130k Ranged XP an hour, calling it surprisingly one of the best boss tasks for Slayer XP[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-776][^7ehTfsD--gM-805]. The description of the first guide gives an average of 230k per run[^1bBT0uOCQsM-141].
- He had received four 1/3,000 drops by kill count 266[^NjF_kFyA02E-324].

## Gnomonkey's opinion
- 2020: easy to learn even compared with [Vorkath](Vorkath.md), but not as consistent money since much profit is claw and leather drops; he calls it basically the best boss Slayer task[^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-0][^Rpu8p3tqQ8U-776].
- 2021: one of the most profitable soloable bosses, averaging over 4.5m gp/hr[^7ehTfsD--gM-0].
- In March 2022 he rated its pet B tier, noting it is not uncommon since everyone grinds it for gp[^vwCm9NKJhRE-291], and rated the boss C tier as boring and mindless: "if you can count to three attacks you are good at it"[^HkVt4enEesI-1015]. He adds that luring onto the vents early gives extra DPS, the electricity and fire skips are simple, and all mechanics are learnable in 10 or 20 kills[^HkVt4enEesI-1044].
