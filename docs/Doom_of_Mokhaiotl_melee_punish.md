# Doom of Mokhaiotl: melee punish

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2025-07-25 to 2025-08-09). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Melee punish is when the Doom of Mokhaiotl throws up its ranged/mage prayer shield with the charge meter and you hit it with a weapon to block the attack: you then deal bonus damage in an additional hit splat. ([2025-07-25 0:33](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=33))
- For his Doom of Mokhaiotl punish switch Gnomonkey brings either ferocious gloves (14 strength) or a Blood fury; he aims for a total strength bonus ending on a multiple of five (such as 110, 115, 120), and if the gloves leave him short of a threshold (e.g. 114) he brings the Blood fury for the healing. ([2025-07-25 8:37](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=517))
- Gnomonkey pushes the Doom of Mokhaiotl into the shield phase with a big Chally punish, gets spec back from Death Charge off the grubs, and then continues; the result leaves the boss at a similar health to a Zaryte crossbow opening but spending 30% spec instead of 72 to 75%. ([2025-07-31 16:49](https://youtu.be/draz0HZBq00?t=1009))
- In the Doom of Mokhaiotl melee punish, usually before 75% health, the boss raises Protect from Missiles and Magic and charges a beam; you must hit it with a melee weapon to break the laser, and the hit does bonus damage per hitsplat based on your total strength bonus. ([2025-08-09 2:29](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=149))

## Stats and numbers

- The Doom of Mokhaiotl melee punish deals bonus damage per hit splat equal to your total strength bonus divided by five, and Gnomonkey says you can hit well over 100 during this phase. ([2025-07-25 1:04](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=64))
- In Gnomonkey's calcs the Scythe of Vitur does about 28 DPS in the melee punish against about 26 for a Chally; the Chally max is 156 and the Scythe max is 115, but the Chally is seven tick, the Scythe is five tick and has three hit splats. ([2025-07-25 6:12](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=372))
- Gnomonkey says Gearscape does not show the correct damage for claws at the time, though claws are better than a Scythe; he still prefers spending spec on the car phase rather than the melee punish. ([2025-07-25 7:09](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=429))
- The melee punish bonus hit splats do not trigger Blood fury or special effects such as Saradomin godsword or Bandos godsword drains; the bonus damage is only damage to the boss. ([2025-07-25 9:06](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=546))
- A Scythe has better DPS than a Chally spec on paper because it is 5 tick versus 7 tick, but Gnomonkey says lost ticks do not matter on the melee punish because the boss does not attack you and there are no grubs yet. ([2025-07-31 16:23](https://youtu.be/draz0HZBq00?t=983))
- Gnomonkey says the Nally has high strength bonus for a two-hander and averages about 50 on the melee punish, and can hit from out of range like the Chally, but he would not bring it over a Chally. ([2025-08-09 4:07](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=247))
- If the Doom of Mokhaiotl melee punish beam charges fully, it fully heals the boss and sends a death laser starting at hits in the 60s and going up to 99 at a certain delve. ([2025-08-09 12:00](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=720))

## Tips

- Piety, combat potions or a Void melee helm do not affect strength bonus, which is the only thing that affects the melee punish bonus damage, so switching to boost the normal swing is not worth it; the goal is always to maximise strength bonus. ([2025-07-25 5:43](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=343))
- Gnomonkey advises not wasting spec energy on the Doom of Mokhaiotl melee punish and instead using it on the car phase, unless you are opening with a Zaryte crossbow to leave the initial phase quickly. ([2025-07-25 7:38](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=458))
- If you do not have a Scythe, Gnomonkey recommends Scythe-style 'boppers' as vastly better than anything else, with burning claws probably second best for the melee punish. ([2025-07-25 23:29](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=1409))
- For the Chally punish, get the boss around 84% health (a sweet spot) and do not keep shooting once you have done a big hit near 90%; when the prayer shield goes up, click a green tile and then the boss, and you run straight to the boss's corner where there is the best chance of no acid. ([2025-07-31 15:54](https://youtu.be/draz0HZBq00?t=954))
- If you use a halberd for extra range in longer delves, you must also start the punish from a green tile. ([2025-07-31 15:54](https://youtu.be/draz0HZBq00?t=954))
- Gnomonkey says that using a Scythe from there would have poisoned him and hurt a lot, and also do less damage than the Chally. ([2025-07-31 16:49](https://youtu.be/draz0HZBq00?t=1009))
- Gnomonkey says to judge melee punish weapons by average hit, not DPS, because you only get one or two punishes per fight; the Scythe needs no spec and averages about 70 with triple-splat bonus damage, while the Chally averages about 92 for 30% spec. ([2025-08-09 3:02](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=182))
- Gnomonkey says boppers hit the same as a Nally on the melee punish and, because they double hit, can one-shot the two-health grubs, so they can replace a Demon bane weapon and save an inventory slot. ([2025-08-09 4:34](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=274))
- Gnomonkey says that if you are on attack cooldown you can still do the Doom of Mokhaiotl melee punish, like Zebak jugs, so there is never a time you are unable to punish after shooting. ([2025-08-09 12:47](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=767))
- Gnomonkey says that on delves 2 to 8 if you finish the boss with a melee punish while it is at low health, holy water shoots out that clears acid and restores about 15 prayer points and around 50 health, but the developers made it not work on delve 9 or higher. ([2025-08-09 17:13](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=1033))
- Gnomonkey says that on delve 3 the shield phase's bonus hitsplat can break an in-progress melee punish and disrupt boulder prayer timing, which he thought might be hotfixed. ([2025-08-09 19:41](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=1181))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the Chally spec is not worth using on the melee punish because it spends 30% spec energy on a slow double hit splat, when swinging a Scythe uses no spec energy and the energy is better used on Dragon knives during the car phase. ([2025-07-25 6:41](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=401))
- Gnomonkey considers a large multi-switch (up to six-way) melee punish setup not worth the brew slots, even though it could hit around 180, because the damage only pushes you into the shield phase and does not make the car phase safer; he uses a two-way switch. ([2025-07-25 8:07](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=487))
- Gnomonkey changes his opinion on the Chally for the Doom of Mokhaiotl melee punish: it gives the most damage on the punish but costs 30% spec; he used to say always use a Scythe, but from a green tile the Chally is better. ([2025-07-31 15:26](https://youtu.be/draz0HZBq00?t=926))
- Gnomonkey recommends bringing a Chally even if you own a Scythe and no ZCB, because you need to use up specs, and the Chally can also hit the boss from out of range, helpful when there is a lot of acid. ([2025-08-09 3:34](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=214))
- Gnomonkey says Claws are only decent for the melee punish because they have about 50 strength bonus versus about 120 for a Chally, and Burning claws' extra 5% against demons is negligible. ([2025-08-09 5:00](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=300))
- Gnomonkey says Ralos, Warhammer and Elder maul are awful at Doom of Mokhaiotl and worse than simply shooting the boss, because about 70% of the fight you have 100% accuracy. ([2025-08-09 5:00](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=300))
