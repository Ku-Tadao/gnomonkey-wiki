**Maw of Whispers** is an other topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2026-01-02. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* Hedron recommends a Sailing level of 80 to 85 for the Maw of Whispers island, because it is tied to an endgame boss and she wanted something for players who had levelled Sailing that high.[^0sdm6y2SYko-127]

## Methods

* Hedron proposes that loot at the Maw of Whispers be per player, with everyone getting their own loot to pick up, like the Titans.[^0sdm6y2SYko-431]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Hedron made the hard mode variant so that more of the community could do a normal mode boss and still feel rewarded.[^0sdm6y2SYko-251]
* Hedron deliberately did not detail the Maw's mechanics publicly, so that if Jagex used the boss there would be mystery and a day-one scramble to figure it out; she did submit a document fleshing out the phases with the island.[^0sdm6y2SYko-281]
* Hedron suggests Jagex could either attach a coral handle to the existing Ghrazi rapier to keep it relevant, or retire the Ghrazi rapier and make the Maw rapier the new rapier; she hoped it would have a bleed mechanic.[^0sdm6y2SYko-372]
* Hedron says the reward space did not have to be special: ideas include permanent character buffs such as a crystal that increases water elemental damage, or a new magic amulet.[^0sdm6y2SYko-405]
* Hedron hopes the Maw of Whispers is a decent moneymaker, perhaps dropping Sailing items such as dragon plates, without uniques carrying it the way they do on the boss she calls Mocha.[^0sdm6y2SYko-431]
* Hedron notes the Maw of Whispers would be locked behind high Sailing, so she hopes bots stay away from it for a while.[^0sdm6y2SYko-431]

## Events

* Hedron designed a Player Island Competition entry called the Maw of Whispers: a Sailing island containing a new endgame duo boss with a darker theme and ties to Whisperer's Blackstone.[^0sdm6y2SYko-0]

## Trivia

* In the Maw of Whispers lore, the island was created by Doom digging around and causing earthquakes, which floated a piece of the ocean floor up to the surface.[^0sdm6y2SYko-94]
* The Maw of Whispers island is located between Varlamore and Corrend and is reached by sailing.[^0sdm6y2SYko-127]
* The goal of the Maw of Whispers island was to be a new one-off boss, since Jagex is extremely busy with Raids 4.[^0sdm6y2SYko-127]
* The Maw of Whispers boss is a duo encounter designed as an endgame fight and requires two people; soloing it would be like soloing Theatre of Blood, not intended but doable with enough skill.[^0sdm6y2SYko-158]
* The Maw of Whispers boss has no name; in lore it is just a sea creature corrupted by Blackstone. Hedron calls it Fred or simply the Maw.[^0sdm6y2SYko-191]
* Hedron left the boss unnamed and unsentient so that Jagex has room to tie it into lore or not, and because the player island rules say entries should not create new metas or questlines that hinder Jagex's progression.[^0sdm6y2SYko-191]
* The Maw is a three-phase encounter with a hard mode variant; each phase is linear and has a different theme of mechanics, such as bleed and corrosion, plus an escape sequence.[^0sdm6y2SYko-224]
* In the Maw's escape sequence, the party may have to escape the boss or may be able to kill it, depending on how they manage their resources during the fight.[^0sdm6y2SYko-251]
* In the Maw of Whispers hard mode, all three linear phases are active at once and players must handle all of the mechanics together.[^0sdm6y2SYko-251]
* Hedron designed a new rapier for the Maw of Whispers because the boss fight involves a lot of stabbing, and the boss stabs itself.[^0sdm6y2SYko-344]
