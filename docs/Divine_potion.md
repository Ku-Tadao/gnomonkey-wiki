**Divine potion** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2019-07-31 and 2020-01-13. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Making divine super combat potions needs 97 Herblore, which Gnomonkey doubts most viewers have.[^l1y2oE5E9x4-61]
* Drinking a dose of a divine potion hits the player for 10 damage, and they are made with ground-up crystal shards, making them pricier than the standard potions.[^cqAUsddqJok-0]
* A divine super combat dose costs about 4.2k, while a sip of each of the three divine melee potions is about 1.4k, a substantial difference, due to the large difference in level requirements to make them.[^cqAUsddqJok-82]

## Tips

* Divine potions are made by mixing crushed crystal shard dust into most combat potions, and each dust makes one dose.[^l1y2oE5E9x4-61]
* Divine potions are upgraded versions of their regular counterparts that keep stats at their maximum boost for five minutes before draining completely.[^cqAUsddqJok-0]
* If divine potion stats are drained, such as by drinking a brew, the boost is not reapplied; it stays at the level it was reduced to.[^cqAUsddqJok-0]
* Gnomonkey could imagine using the imbued heart and divine magic potion together at Zulrah, and the only exception he can think of is using divine magic potions to boost up to Ice Barrage when bursting, so the level is consistently kept up instead of drained.[^cqAUsddqJok-134]
* In locations where brews are used, only regular potions work because the divine boost is lost upon using a brew, and PKers may find regular potions more useful for the same reason.[^cqAUsddqJok-163]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the DPS boost of constantly maximum-boosted stats cannot be understated: with Ranged and Melee, losing a level or two can reduce max hit, around a 1 to 2% DPS loss, equivalent to swapping Pegasian boots for blessed dragonhide boots.[^cqAUsddqJok-26]
* Gnomonkey says the 10 damage is made up for by being able to carry fewer stat-boosting potions and more food.[^cqAUsddqJok-26]
* Gnomonkey says divine ranging potions are useful basically everywhere while ranging, with no time they should not be used, especially since they are cheap.[^cqAUsddqJok-54]
* Gnomonkey says Vorkath, Alchemical Hydra, brutal black dragons and the Fight Caves all benefit from divine ranging potions.[^cqAUsddqJok-54]
* Gnomonkey thought divine super attack, strength and defence seemed pointless since divine super combat combines all of them and damages the player only once.[^cqAUsddqJok-54]
* Gnomonkey considers the three separate divine melee potions more cost effective where inventory space and health matter less, such as most Slayer tasks like Kurask, gargoyles, wyverns, Suqah and Dagannoth.[^cqAUsddqJok-82]
* Gnomonkey says divine super combats are more worthwhile where food and inventory space matter, like most boss encounters: Bandos, Zamorak, the Kalphite Queen, Corporeal Beast, Chambers of Xeric, and longer trips such as at the Giant Mole.[^cqAUsddqJok-108]
* Gnomonkey calls the divine magic potion the only potion that is nearly worthless in his opinion, since the imbued heart provides a much higher boost.[^cqAUsddqJok-108]
* Gnomonkey says boosting Magic only boosts accuracy and not damage, so divine magic potions do not help as much as the other divine potions.[^cqAUsddqJok-134]
