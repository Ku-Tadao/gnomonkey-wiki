# Trouble Brewing

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2024-11-15 to 2025-01-27). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- The Trouble Brewing loop is to put rat meat into the grub mound (one-tick spam) until a swarm spawns or the mound depletes, run to the tree's blue tile (the swarm's aggro radius) so the swarm stops attacking, chop the tree, then return and repeat. ([2024-11-15 5:58](https://youtu.be/m_QPkyjfRIs?t=358))
- Gnomonkey tick-manipulates the Trouble Brewing tree, turning the usually four-tick chop into a three-tick action, which he describes as roughly 25 percent faster. ([2024-11-15 6:54](https://youtu.be/m_QPkyjfRIs?t=414))
- After filling the inventory in Trouble Brewing, Gnomonkey deposits logs in the fletching hopper, which he one-ticks, and then the grubs and bark into the sweet grub hopper and the bark hopper for Cooking, repeating the whole loop until needed levels are done. ([2024-11-15 7:51](https://youtu.be/m_QPkyjfRIs?t=471))
- Gnomonkey describes Trouble Brewing as a method that does not scale and gives a flat 200k Cooking XP and 100k Fletching XP, with no input cost, which suits an account that rushes there early. ([2025-01-27 2:10](https://youtu.be/3kmWbjwmZqI?t=130))

## Requirements

- Trouble Brewing requires one other person to play: a friend or an alt account, and both sides can play at the same time without competing, each skilling on their own side. ([2024-11-15 1:56](https://youtu.be/m_QPkyjfRIs?t=116))
- You cannot bring any items into Trouble Brewing in your inventory, and you cannot wear a helmet, but you can wear other equipment such as Graceful. ([2024-11-15 2:28](https://youtu.be/m_QPkyjfRIs?t=148))
- Trouble Brewing has decent quest requirements, including many stats in the 40s and 50s and Prayer, which Gnomonkey says must be raised first. ([2025-01-27 2:10](https://youtu.be/3kmWbjwmZqI?t=130))

## Stats and numbers

- After the buff, Trouble Brewing gives about 200k Cooking XP per hour plus roughly 100k XP per hour split between Fletching and Hunter, around 50k each, adjustable depending on the levels needed. ([2024-11-15 0:00](https://youtu.be/m_QPkyjfRIs))
- At one point while depositing in Trouble Brewing, Gnomonkey's XP counter showed about 460k XP per hour, which he says is not an accurate sustained rate. ([2024-11-15 8:25](https://youtu.be/m_QPkyjfRIs?t=505))
- A full Trouble Brewing game in this video took about 20 minutes and gave the expected 200k Cooking and about 100k split between Hunter and Fletching. ([2024-11-15 12:18](https://youtu.be/m_QPkyjfRIs?t=738))
- Before the nerf, Trouble Brewing gave a little over 100k Fletching XP per hour along with about 200k Cooking XP per hour, according to Gnomonkey. ([2025-01-27 0:00](https://youtu.be/3kmWbjwmZqI))

## Tips

- Trouble Brewing XP does not scale with level, so it is best for skipping very low early levels; Gnomonkey says it is less good after roughly level 70s in these stats. ([2024-11-15 0:27](https://youtu.be/m_QPkyjfRIs?t=27))
- There is a three-minute timer between Trouble Brewing games; with an alt that hops worlds, you can wait out the timer by entering the lobby early and then start instantly by joining. ([2024-11-15 2:28](https://youtu.be/m_QPkyjfRIs?t=148))
- Gnomonkey says higher Agility (about 60 or so rather than 52) and Graceful help a lot in Trouble Brewing because you run around constantly. ([2024-11-15 2:56](https://youtu.be/m_QPkyjfRIs?t=176))
- A better axe (a Dragon axe is ideal) and a higher Woodcutting level make Trouble Brewing faster, since the loop involves chopping a tree. ([2024-11-15 2:56](https://youtu.be/m_QPkyjfRIs?t=176))
- In the Trouble Brewing lobby, Gnomonkey grabs a knife, a tinderbox for passive Firemaking (about 20k Firemaking XP per hour at most), and about 10 meat, using custom swaps to make the left click Take-5. ([2024-11-15 4:59](https://youtu.be/m_QPkyjfRIs?t=299))
- Before starting a Trouble Brewing game, Gnomonkey marks the grub mound and the scrapie tree, plus a second set on the other end of the island that he does not typically use. ([2024-11-15 5:30](https://youtu.be/m_QPkyjfRIs?t=330))
- While waiting for the mound or tree to respawn in Trouble Brewing, you can throw a regular log in the boiler for passive Firemaking; he says the boiler gives 320 and is a decent way to get early Firemaking levels. ([2024-11-15 6:54](https://youtu.be/m_QPkyjfRIs?t=414))
- Gnomonkey says that if you skip Cooking XP and only fletch with tick manipulation in Trouble Brewing, you should get around 170k Fletching XP per hour, so XP can be shifted between skills depending on need. ([2024-11-15 12:18](https://youtu.be/m_QPkyjfRIs?t=738))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey considers Trouble Brewing relevant even for mains because it is completely free with no input cost, while being especially good for early ironmen. ([2024-11-15 9:27](https://youtu.be/m_QPkyjfRIs?t=567))
- Gnomonkey says some HCIM players laughed at him for using Trouble Brewing because Cooking karambwans and Fletching broad arrows with zero-time Fletching were so strong that Trouble Brewing was barely worth it. ([2025-01-27 1:40](https://youtu.be/3kmWbjwmZqI?t=100))
- Gnomonkey lists Trouble Brewing's downsides as big quest requirements and needing a second player to access it, though he notes the second-player requirement encouraged communication and group play. ([2025-01-27 6:26](https://youtu.be/3kmWbjwmZqI?t=386))
- Gnomonkey says Trouble Brewing was not as good as the best methods for either Fletching or Cooking even when combined XP is considered, so it was an early hybrid method rather than a meta method. ([2025-01-27 6:26](https://youtu.be/3kmWbjwmZqI?t=386))
- Gnomonkey considers it better to unlock broad arrows and make broads for Fletching, and better to buy cooked karambwans with gp from other methods for Cooking, so Trouble Brewing was worse than those and merely an option for players who do not want to put in input. ([2025-01-27 8:40](https://youtu.be/3kmWbjwmZqI?t=520))
- Gnomonkey says no Ironman Discord communities were reporting records broken with Trouble Brewing, so he argues it was never meta and should not have been nerfed at all. ([2025-01-27 8:40](https://youtu.be/3kmWbjwmZqI?t=520))

## Records

- On a fresh hardcore ironman, Gnomonkey arrived at Trouble Brewing with level 9 Hunter and reached level 28 Hunter in a single game. ([2024-11-15 0:27](https://youtu.be/m_QPkyjfRIs?t=27))

## Events

- Trouble Brewing was buffed hard a couple of months before this video (November 2024), after which Gnomonkey says it has very high XP rates despite its former reputation as dead content. ([2024-11-15 0:00](https://youtu.be/m_QPkyjfRIs))
- Jagex heavily buffed Trouble Brewing roughly six months before this video; Gnomonkey made a guide on it back then. ([2025-01-27 0:00](https://youtu.be/3kmWbjwmZqI))
- Jagex nerfed Trouble Brewing's XP rates to a little under one-third of what they were, which Gnomonkey says makes the method completely dead. ([2025-01-27 0:33](https://youtu.be/3kmWbjwmZqI?t=33))
- The same update patched the exploit where players could get Firemaking XP in the one boiler; that boiler no longer gives XP. ([2025-01-27 0:33](https://youtu.be/3kmWbjwmZqI?t=33))

## Trivia

- Trouble Brewing's only rewards are cosmetics and a teleport back, plus a small amount of brewing stuff that Gnomonkey says nobody uses. ([2025-01-27 7:27](https://youtu.be/3kmWbjwmZqI?t=447))
