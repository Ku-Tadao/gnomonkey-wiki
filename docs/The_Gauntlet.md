**The Gauntlet** is an activity topic that Gnomonkey covers in 5 videos between 2019-07-26 and 2026-07-23. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* Gnomonkey recommends 85+ Magic, Ranged and Defence for consistent completion, and the highest possible Mining, Fishing, Farming and Woodcutting to collect resources faster.[^guGTfLIzYBc-0]

## Methods

* A vial costs ten shards; filling the vial with water at the pump, adding a grimy leaf and then ten shards ground with the pestle and mortar makes a potion.[^guGTfLIzYBc-67]
* Gnomonkey collects nine of each resource for a full tier 2 armor set, thinking tier 3 armor costs too much time and does not help much over tier 2.[^guGTfLIzYBc-305]
* Gnomonkey tries to get at least his bow or staff to tier 3, which increases DPS an insane amount, and finds only two potions are needed for the whole dungeon; he enters the boss with 25 food, a tier 2 and tier 3 weapon and full tier 2 armor.[^guGTfLIzYBc-305]
* Opening steps: find basic enemies fast for two weapon molds, collect resources as encountered, make a bow as soon as possible and upgrade it to tier 2 if there are enough shards.[^guGTfLIzYBc-332]
* Once one weapon is tier 2, run to the outskirts to hunt a dragon or dark beast for the tier 3 upgrade, using a teleport seed since the outskirts are far from the start.[^guGTfLIzYBc-332]
* Make the two potions when running low on energy and start building armor; kill enemies with the tier 3 weapon if shards run low, and have 25 fish before the timer runs out.[^guGTfLIzYBc-360]
* For the regular Gauntlet speed time Gnomonkey makes no armor, because Hunllef's max hit without armor is only 12; he makes one tier three weapon plus one tier two weapon and uses the five to one method.[^JmglE_R2gGs-60]
* For regular Gauntlet speed, the prep goal is about four to eight fish, one potion, no armor, one tier three weapon and an extra weapon; you reset constantly, for example if the first room has no monsters.[^JmglE_R2gGs-90]
* Regular Gauntlet speed route: kill the demi-boss opposite the boss, pick up its item, teleport back, make the tier three weapon and the extra weapon if needed, cook your fish, make your potion and go in.[^JmglE_R2gGs-149]

## Stats and numbers

* The Gauntlet potion gives the same prayer points as a dose of prayer potion and the effects of a stamina potion, restoring 40 run energy.[^guGTfLIzYBc-95]
* Weapons can be crafted after obtaining a weapon mold from a monster: a halberd, bow or staff each costs 20 shards; the bow and staff need no arrows or runes, so Gnomonkey finds them more useful than the halberd.[^guGTfLIzYBc-95]
* Weapons upgrade from tier 1 to tier 2 for 60 additional shards, and from tier 2 to tier 3 using an upgrade piece from one of the demibosses on the dungeon's outskirts.[^guGTfLIzYBc-95]
* Armor needs one piece each of bark, linen and ore plus 40 shards per piece; tier 2 uses 2 of each resource and 60 shards, and tier 3 uses 3 of each and 80 shards.[^guGTfLIzYBc-128]
* Regular Gauntlet boss kills have a lot of RNG, ranging from about 1:30 to 2:30, and with only tier one armor he is so inaccurate it may take several tries; he does not expect the speed time on the first attempt.[^JmglE_R2gGs-599]

## Tips

* The player starts with no armor and a crystal scepter equipped; the scepter is what allows opening new rooms, so dropping it means the player cannot advance.[^guGTfLIzYBc-33]
* The player also starts with a crystal hatchet, pickaxe and harpoon to gather the dungeon's three main resources, a pestle and mortar to grind crystal shards for potions, and a teleport seed that teleports the player to the starting room.[^guGTfLIzYBc-33]
* Crystal shards from foraging and killing monsters let the player forge weapons, armor, teleport seeds and potions at the singing bowl in the first room.[^guGTfLIzYBc-67]
* Gauntlet armor has equal defences against all attack styles and no negative attack bonuses.[^guGTfLIzYBc-128]
* Resources come from trees, ore veins and spools throughout the dungeon, each giving three resources before depleting permanently, and gathering can also yield crystal shards.[^guGTfLIzYBc-157]
* Items left on the ground in the Gauntlet do not despawn, unlike in Dungeoneering.[^guGTfLIzYBc-157]
* Paddlefish can be fished from pools throughout the dungeon, giving four fish before the pool depletes; they heal exactly the same as sharks and are the only healing method.[^guGTfLIzYBc-157]
* Regular Gauntlet monsters are fairly standard beasts such as wolves, spiders and scorpions; higher combat monsters give more crystal shards and are more likely to drop resources.[^guGTfLIzYBc-184]
* On the very outskirts of the dungeon three demibosses can spawn: a bear, a dark beast and a dragon; the bear drops an upgrade component for the halberd, the dragon for the staff and the dark beast for the bow.[^guGTfLIzYBc-184]
* Gnomonkey hunts for a dragon or dark beast as soon as possible because they drastically increase damage output.[^guGTfLIzYBc-184]
* The bear uses melee, the dragon uses magic and the dark beast uses ranged, and they can hit fairly hard so the player should protect against the matching style.[^guGTfLIzYBc-184]
* Before the boss, drop all tools, shards, leftover teleport seeds and the scepter, and decant the potions to about four doses; spare time can go into tier 3 armor or starting the fight early.[^guGTfLIzYBc-360]
* In the regular Gauntlet speed run, Gnomonkey kills one mob in the first room for a weapon frame and at least 50 shards, makes a tier two staff, and if he has 70 shards also makes two vials.[^JmglE_R2gGs-90]
* If the demi-boss at the edge room is a bear and you have no extra weapon frame, Gnomonkey usually resets, because you need a halberd and the extra frame for the five to one method.[^JmglE_R2gGs-119]
* Gnomonkey pre-venges (Vengeance) before entering both Gauntlet speed runs and uses the vengeance on a demi-boss, since the venge damage carries into the Gauntlet for some bonus damage.[^JmglE_R2gGs-178]
* Gnomonkey prefers the closest first room in the speed Gauntlet; killing all three mobs is fine, but leave the room once you have 70+ shards.[^JmglE_R2gGs-178]
* You only need one herb and one vial for the regular Gauntlet speed method (two herbs only if making two potions); a second vial is a bonus for extra Redemptions.[^JmglE_R2gGs-238]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the Gauntlet is extremely similar to Dungeoneering as it was in RuneScape just before the Evolution of Combat: you go in with nothing, gather resources for 10 minutes, then use the scraps to try to kill a boss.[^guGTfLIzYBc-0]
* Gnomonkey considers the Gauntlet boss much harder than any Dungeoneering boss and says it requires careful preparation.[^guGTfLIzYBc-0]
* Gnomonkey compares the weapon upgrades to going from a rune scimitar to a dragon scimitar and then a Ghrazi rapier, so they are very important.[^guGTfLIzYBc-128]
* Gnomonkey says rewards are not known because there is not enough data; after six attempts he received two elite clue scrolls, and there is a new sword drop that may only come from hard mode.[^guGTfLIzYBc-393]
* Gnomonkey treats the Gauntlet (normal and corrupted) as one piece of content, with the normal Gauntlet an easy mode that teaches corrupted safely.[^HkVt4enEesI-1876]
* Gnomonkey says the Gauntlet has the lowest skill floor and the highest skill cap gap in the game: you can start really bad and work up to very good, which he calls one of its best design features.[^HkVt4enEesI-1876]
* Gnomonkey dislikes that as you get faster in the Gauntlet you eventually have to chance it, working down the number of fish and dying if you do not hit hard enough, which he says makes the skill cap arbitrarily lower than it needs to be; a perfect 10 fish kill is where he says it is a guaranteed kill.[^HkVt4enEesI-1907]
* Gnomonkey rates the Gauntlet A tier, great content but not S, and in the final order places it at the top of A tier.[^HkVt4enEesI-1937]
* Gnomonkey says the Gauntlet is not quite raid difficulty for the most part but is a good step up from other boss encounters.[^IuHh4gfs39o-588]
* Gnomonkey rates the Gauntlet 2/5 for inventory management (no switches, but tracking items during prep and supply usage in the fight), 5/5 for movement, and 3/5 for prayer management.[^IuHh4gfs39o-588]
* For Gauntlet movement, Gnomonkey notes prep movement between skilling nodes and pathing matters, but it ramps up in the Hunllef fight where you avoid ground tiles, tornadoes and not walking under Hunllef.[^IuHh4gfs39o-605]
* Gnomonkey says Gauntlet prayer management is mostly counting Hunllef's attacks, switching prayer and turning prayers back on when he disables them, though it can be hard to keep track of.[^IuHh4gfs39o-605]
* Gnomonkey says the Gauntlet (2019, with Song of the Elves) has a lot of mechanical depth and was the hardest content of that year; completions are not very difficult but fast completions and speedruns require a lot of game knowledge.[^iG23QZE9pds-336]
* Gnomonkey says the Gauntlet is not his favourite content because at a high level you basically need to chance death to go faster.[^iG23QZE9pds-363]
* Gnomonkey thinks the new regular Gauntlet speed time is much harder than it used to be and rough, even though the Corrupted Gauntlet time is easier.[^JmglE_R2gGs-0]

## Records

* After the Summer Sweep-up the regular Gauntlet speed time combat achievement is 3:45 instead of 4:00.[^JmglE_R2gGs-0]
* Gnomonkey's example regular Gauntlet run finished in 3:26 against the 3:45 speed time.[^JmglE_R2gGs-595]
