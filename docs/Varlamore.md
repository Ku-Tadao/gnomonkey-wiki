## Stats and numbers

- Varlamore is a new area added with Leagues 5 and has the most dangerous Echo boss, Echo Sol (Sol Heredit), the only boss placed in the Grand Master difficulty tier, with no waves: you jump straight into fighting him. ([2024-11-25 7:02](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=422))
- Echo Sol drops the Sunlight bracers, gloves that double all healing from all sources including Redemption and Blood fury, and the Sunlight spear, a two-handed spear and shield combo with a stacking special attack that unleashes a prayer-bonus-scaling AoE spec after seven auto attacks. ([2024-11-25 7:15](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=435))
- Varlamore in Leagues 5 has the Colosseum (a strong money generator, also dropping Echo boots, the glaive and quiver), Moons of Peril with powerful early-game gear, Vardorvis, the best-stocked gem stall in the game, house thieving, Prayer training and mining, and sulphur nagua for combat XP. ([2024-11-25 8:05](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=485))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey considers the Sunlight spear fairly weak compared to other options and the Sunlight bracers useful but boring in design. ([2024-11-25 7:15](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=435))
- Gnomonkey says Varlamore does not dramatically boost any one build but will be a very fun region to mess with. ([2024-11-25 8:05](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=485))
- Gnomonkey calls Varlamore a big expansion with an overall very solid set of updates, with solid skilling content, popular Vale Totems and a fairly solid Mastering Mixology. ([2026-03-10 0:58](https://youtu.be/1cRMBXNb4rc?t=58))
- Gnomonkey has gripes with some Varlamore content such as Mocha and the Colosseum, calling them not perfect but generally good additions. ([2026-03-10 0:58](https://youtu.be/1cRMBXNb4rc?t=58))
