**Varlamore** is an other topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2024-11-25 and 2026-03-10. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Stats and numbers

* Varlamore is a new area added with Leagues 5 and has the most dangerous Echo boss, Echo Sol (Sol Heredit), the only boss placed in the Grand Master difficulty tier, with no waves: you jump straight into fighting him.[^ZVceO0RWJuY-422]
* Echo Sol drops the Sunlight bracers, gloves that double all healing from all sources including Redemption and Blood fury, and the Sunlight spear, a two-handed spear and shield combo with a stacking special attack that unleashes a prayer-bonus-scaling AoE spec after seven auto attacks.[^ZVceO0RWJuY-435]
* Varlamore in Leagues 5 has the Colosseum (a strong money generator, also dropping Echo boots, the glaive and quiver), Moons of Peril with powerful early-game gear, Vardorvis, the best-stocked gem stall in the game, house thieving, Prayer training and mining, and sulphur nagua for combat XP.[^ZVceO0RWJuY-485]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey considers the Sunlight spear fairly weak compared to other options and the Sunlight bracers useful but boring in design.[^ZVceO0RWJuY-435]
* Gnomonkey says Varlamore does not dramatically boost any one build but will be a very fun region to mess with.[^ZVceO0RWJuY-485]
* Gnomonkey calls Varlamore a big expansion with an overall very solid set of updates, with solid skilling content, popular Vale Totems and a fairly solid Mastering Mixology.[^1cRMBXNb4rc-58]
* Gnomonkey has gripes with some Varlamore content such as Mocha and the Colosseum, calling them not perfect but generally good additions.[^1cRMBXNb4rc-58]
