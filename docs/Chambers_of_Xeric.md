Chambers of Xeric (CoX, "Chambers") is a raid that Gnomonkey describes as easily one of the best pieces of PvM content in the game, with a deep solo and Challenge Mode scene that he speedruns, but also the slowest raid to complete, with very rare purples[^iG23QZE9pds-240][^6tsf-brc-sE-1609]. Across 23 videos he covers rating it, learning it as a solo, Challenge Mode records, plugins, and the January 2025 mega-scale nerf and 2026 loot reweighting.

See also [Chambers of Xeric: Challenge Mode](Chambers_of_Xeric_Challenge_Mode.md), [Great Olm](Great_Olm.md) and [Update: Entry Mode CM CoX](Update_Entry_Mode_CM_CoX.md).

## Overview and ratings

- Gnomonkey says Chambers was janky when it came out, but [Olm](Great_Olm.md) is an incredible boss fight, and that the raid added new prayers and arguably too many items, which is good for a first attempt at a raid[^iG23QZE9pds-240].
- In the March 2022 tier list he says the raid has a lot of thought in its layout and scouting, but scouting is disliked and holds it back; regular Chambers is A tier, not S, because the regular bosses need little skill[^HkVt4enEesI-3253]. Challenge Mode has a ton of skill and teamwork (one person finishing Vanguard prep, tightrope skips, crab communication) but boring downtime[^HkVt4enEesI-3344]. He places Challenge Mode at the bottom of S, below Olm, despite calling the [Muttadile](Muttadile.md) room horrible there[^HkVt4enEesI-3464], and places the entire raid just above [Xarpus](Xarpus.md) in his final order[^HkVt4enEesI-5520].
- In the May 2022 skill-pillar ratings, Chambers gets 5/5 for [Inventory management](Inventory_management.md) (losing the inventory mid-run may as well lose the raid), 4/5 for movement (Olm, especially solo, and nearly all standard rooms involve movement; [Corner trapping](Corner_trapping.md) matters at the Mystics) and 3/5 for prayer management (Vespula with [Redemption](Redemption.md), and Olm's prayer orbs and alternating attacks)[^IuHh4gfs39o-634][^IuHh4gfs39o-667].
- In July 2023 he says Challenge Mode is Chambers "cranked to 11" and needs careful inventory management, banking and route planning[^iG23QZE9pds-305]; it is one of the few pieces of content with mechanical depth along with [Theatre of Blood](Theatre_of_Blood.md) and the [Inferno](Inferno.md)[^iG23QZE9pds-1550].
- In July 2024 he calls Chambers and Theatre of Blood fun and great raids, but their overall purple chance is less than half of [Tombs of Amascut](Tombs_of_Amascut.md)[^e0y_auD9JhY-492]. He says Chambers typically caps out around 10 million GP per hour[^e0y_auD9JhY-525].
- In June 2026 he says Chambers is by far the longest raid to complete; Theatre of Blood is the perfect length, Tombs slightly too fast, and Chambers too long since full ancestral can take longer than a Twisted bow[^6tsf-brc-sE-1654].

## Layout, scouting and rooms

- Chambers is the only raid with scouting, so the bosses are random, and the rooms to avoid depend on the weapons owned[^wxGBi89vE_s-148]. There are only two possible room rotations, and RuneLite scouting sometimes shows unknown because the game needs two combat rooms to reveal the whole raid[^wxGBi89vE_s-238].
- Gnomonkey says people fear the V-rooms (Vanguards, Vespula, Vasa), but [Vanguards](Vanguards.md) is the hardest regular room and one to avoid if possible[^wxGBi89vE_s-148]. Vasa and Vespula are the fastest rooms with a good weapon such as [Twisted bow](Twisted_bow.md) or [Shadow](Tumeken's_shadow.md)[^wxGBi89vE_s-179].
- As of the October 2024 video, the stairs had been updated about six months earlier so the raid can be reloaded to reroll the layout[^wxGBi89vE_s-208]. The layout letters show room types (scavs, farming, combat, puzzle), so scavengers can be done before a puzzle room and seeds grabbed from Mystics, Guardians, Shamans or by raking[^wxGBi89vE_s-1073].
- Certain bosses (Muttadile, [Tekton](Tekton.md) and the V-rooms) drop overloads of the best tier, whereas making the best overload needs 90 Herblore[^wxGBi89vE_s-208]. Vespula and Vasa were changed to drop overloads, which made a VSV-style Bofa speed raid possible, roughly what world record runs are[^wxGBi89vE_s-2453].
- Specialty items per room: a pickaxe for Guardians, a salve amulet for Mystics, a lockpick for thieving and an axe for Muttadile's tree; axe tier does nothing, so the lowest tier is fine, while pickaxe tier and Woodcutting level matter[^wxGBi89vE_s-329][^wxGBi89vE_s-363].
- [Thralls](Thralls.md) give free damage in almost every room, but cannot reach [Vespula](Vespula.md) and the Guardians' portal; they do not damage Vasa's crystals but do hit [Vasa](Vasa_Nistirio.md); the Ice Demon's 66% damage reduction applies to them, so their max hit becomes one and they hit that one out of four attacks[^26wPQVuqmNY-248][^26wPQVuqmNY-305][^26wPQVuqmNY-515]. They shine at Tekton because of his high defence and they keep hitting while he walks to the anvil[^26wPQVuqmNY-278], and they are extremely beneficial for Olm solos since a thrall hit on one hand while attacking the other still keeps Olm from turning to the middle[^26wPQVuqmNY-248].
- Bofa versus Atlatl, February 2025: on shamans on-paper DPS was 8.99 for the Bofa against 8.75 for the [Atlatl](Atlatl.md) (about 3% better); Vasa about 7% better with the Bofa, though the Atlatl loses its burn effect at the crystal; Olm head with max stats about 8% better; Mystics about 20% better[^NxfbgWY3ZQk-852][^NxfbgWY3ZQk-904][^NxfbgWY3ZQk-935][^NxfbgWY3ZQk-1182]. He calls the Vespula portal one of the Bofa's best uses in the game because the Atlatl cannot reach it[^NxfbgWY3ZQk-874]. A hammer special attack is quite good on Vasa since the quality-of-life changes mean it no longer regains defence quickly[^NxfbgWY3ZQk-904].

## Solo learning guide

Gnomonkey's October 2024 guide is an entry-level solo guide based on his ironman's gear[^wxGBi89vE_s-0].

- **Gear**: entry-level setup is a Fury amulet, [Eclipse Moon armour](Eclipse_armour.md), [Barrows gloves](Barrows_gloves.md), dragon boots and a Berserker ring[^wxGBi89vE_s-26]. Eclipse Moon armour doubles as ranged armour (two-way switch) with Blue Moon armour for mage (six-way switch)[^wxGBi89vE_s-117]. A hasta and the abyssal whip are both very good; the hasta is easier to get earlier but maxes lower and can crush, which matters for Tekton[^wxGBi89vE_s-26]. The Dragon hunter lance is probably the biggest possible melee upgrade, and with the [Sanguinesti staff](Sanguinesti_staff.md) (which heals) the two biggest upgrades[^wxGBi89vE_s-3818][^wxGBi89vE_s-3848]. He says the Lightbearer is a common mistake and a camped melee ring such as the Berserker ring is usually better; the Ultor ring is good with the lance and the Bellator ring with a scythe[^wxGBi89vE_s-3791][^wxGBi89vE_s-3818].
- **Weapons**: Osmumten's fang should not be the only weapon, as it is five-tick and Olm needs a four-tick weapon, but it can be brought additionally for rooms like Tekton[^wxGBi89vE_s-89]. The dragon warhammer is the worst thing to grind but is wanted for Tekton and Olm's hands; he estimates raids are about five minutes slower without one, and without a hammer he would bring [Burning claws](Burning_claws.md) and spam specs[^wxGBi89vE_s-56].
- **Prerequisite grinds**: an ironman should do the basic grinds first (Xeric-related, dragon warhammer, [Moons of Peril](Moons_of_Peril.md))[^wxGBi89vE_s-56].
- **Learning Olm**: the regular raid is easy compared to Olm, so learning Chambers is mostly learning Olm[^wxGBi89vE_s-950]. He plants Buchu seeds (two needed, three plenty) and kills scavengers for potion secondaries[^wxGBi89vE_s-978]; about three restores at most are needed for Olm, and he ends with about 12 brews plus an overload, enhance and about three restores[^wxGBi89vE_s-1009]. Potions can be made while running[^wxGBi89vE_s-1040]. A no-prep approach is possible with experience, but beginners take more damage at Olm and should prep[^wxGBi89vE_s-3028]. Dropping a brew and a restore as a pile before Olm is called a "noob tactic"[^wxGBi89vE_s-1105].
- **Entry Mode setup (May 2025)**: a seven-way mage setup with the Book of the Dead for thralls, a Zamorak godsword for Muttadile, a [Bow of Faerdhinen](Bow_of_Faerdhinen.md) setup and a melee setup with lance and [Fire cape](Fire_cape.md)[^jdvQVwg8niM-121]; two brews, a restore, two Phoenix necklaces for the tightrope skip, crystal pickers, a salve amulet and a lance, with a fang optionally swapped for a brew or the restore[^jdvQVwg8niM-149][^jdvQVwg8niM-179]. He likes [Amulet of blood fury](Blood_fury.md) while learning and [torture or rancour](Amulet_of_rancour.md) longer term[^jdvQVwg8niM-206]. Fire, blood and cosmic runes help Olm turn correctly in a solo[^jdvQVwg8niM-206], and an antidote is needed before the Shamans[^jdvQVwg8niM-236].
- **Banking**: the hardest step for learners[^jdvQVwg8niM-1130]. After Thieving he takes only the hammer for Vasa, ZGS for Muttadile, Salve for the Mystics, a pickaxe for Guardians and two Phoenix necklaces[^jdvQVwg8niM-1161]; he leaves four slots free for Vespula potions and three before Vasa[^jdvQVwg8niM-1194], and banks as much as possible before the Vanguards[^jdvQVwg8niM-2059]. Three restores while learning Olm and two later, with three stamina doses (two on a first solo Olm)[^jdvQVwg8niM-2087].
- **Shadow running and the lance method**: the 12:0 and 8:1 [Shadow running](Shadow_running.md) methods and the 4:1 lance method are described as not tough to learn[^K8dFZLPwDBE-0]; the long range attack style should be bound to a tab[^K8dFZLPwDBE-1000]. He remembers his method by labelled tiles and the hits of the cycle[^K8dFZLPwDBE-1055]. He says he thinks he is the first to use a three-tick weapon on Olm's head phase[^N7UM0bqdcU4-2567]. His 4-1 method is credited in a tribute[^SzcDqZLP4R4-110].

## Challenge Mode

Challenge Mode (CM) is covered on its own page, [Chambers of Xeric: Challenge Mode](Chambers_of_Xeric_Challenge_Mode.md); this section gives what Gnomonkey said across videos.

- **Requirements**: June 2023, he says CM has the highest gear requirements of any content in the game; it is doable without an Inquisitor's mace, but he calls Twisted bow, Tumeken's Shadow and the mace all incredibly important[^8JxarzSiiME-0]. October 2024, he advises against learning solo CM without a Tbow and near-max gear, since normal raids quickly give better purples per hour[^wxGBi89vE_s-0].
- **Prep**: potions can be prepped after about the third boss, or CM can be done no-prep; rooms drop many potions so the inventory can be filled with switches[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-177]. The minimum to enter Olm for a no-prep CM is about four brews[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-1541]; prep gives a cushion while learning[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-1515]. Vials come from the Shaman area[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-1495].
- **Opinions**: skill is mostly logistics, and he dislikes that everything has its defence cranked for no reason and that missing hammers is annoying[^HkVt4enEesI-3434]; he calls CM Tekton an example of annoying high-defence design[^iG23QZE9pds-1550]; he complains about loading lag lines of about three ticks, which he calls an engine bug[^HkVt4enEesI-3495]. As an Ironman he considers CM some of the worst content ever, though doable[^8JxarzSiiME-1912].
- **Speedrunning**: spellbook swaps with the [Max cape](Max_cape.md) right after Ice Demon, about six minutes into a roughly 30-minute raid, limit him to five CM runs a day[^iG23QZE9pds-520]. The CM solo-time Combat Achievement requires 38:30 and gets flak for being too easy; he says it was very tough when made, is now realistic, and should not be changed[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-1015][^ZjG3P7PTeIs-1042][^ZjG3P7PTeIs-1074].
- **Naked run (September 2023)**: Gnomonkey did a naked solo CM, died once, finished the early rooms in almost 15 and a half minutes, and beat the challenge time for 5,000 extra points[^f1Y2Pd6dQoA-3437][^f1Y2Pd6dQoA-6712][^f1Y2Pd6dQoA-7250]. Room times: Muttadiles about 2:30, Mystics about 4:00, Vasa about 6:00, Guardians about 3:00, Vespula about 6:00, Thieving 2:39, Vanguards 3:45, Shamans 2:15, Ice Demon 6:39, Tekton 2:00[^f1Y2Pd6dQoA-6712]. Osmumten's fang was 69.42 percent better than the scythe for Tekton in that run[^f1Y2Pd6dQoA-6343]. See also [Naked Man Triathlon](Naked_Man_Triathlon.md).
- **Clue scroll gear (October 2023)**: a solo run using only clue scroll gear in about 27 minutes of raid time[^N7UM0bqdcU4-2840]; see [Clue Scroll Raider](Challenge_Clue_Scroll_Raider.md).

## Plugins

- Most helpers are bundled under the CoX Editions plugin, with Crab Solver and Stun Timer used separately[^Z5ZONYTlS1w-518]. The instance timer setting lets him enter on the tick the raid counter starts, which allows pulling Tekton to the anvil instantly[^Z5ZONYTlS1w-518]. See [RuneLite plugins](RuneLite_plugins.md).
- Crab Solver shows the style needed to paint crabs and a tick timer for how long they remain still[^Z5ZONYTlS1w-538]; the CoX Vanguards plugin shows each vanguard's style and damage before reset[^Z5ZONYTlS1w-538]; Bat Locator finds bats and the poison chest[^Z5ZONYTlS1w-570]; a prayer enhance timer is used at Vespula[^Z5ZONYTlS1w-570]; at Olm CoX Editions shows where Olm pops up, HP over his limbs and the current phase[^Z5ZONYTlS1w-602].
- Raid Reloader adds a button so that running near the entrance and reloading lets RuneLite see more of the map[^wxGBi89vE_s-271]. The Chambers of Zarek plugin whitelists and blacklists rooms, for example blacklisting the V-rooms without a Bofa[^wxGBi89vE_s-301]. He uses [True tile](True_tile.md) markers on crabs and orbs at Tekton[^8JxarzSiiME-15] and Entity Hider with "hide dead monsters"[^8JxarzSiiME-7224].

## Rewards and changes

- **January 2025 mega-scale nerf**: players who leave a raid in progress are still eligible to roll uniques but the reward is discarded, so leaving no longer transfers loot chances to remaining participants[^fLx3y5MTVjw-372]. Gnomonkey calls it a nerf that needed to happen[^fLx3y5MTVjw-402] and thinks players mega scaled because purples are very rare and the raid is much slower than the other two[^fLx3y5MTVjw-433]. He had always said Tombs drops were too common, but this update made him reconsider and he now thinks Chambers should be brought in line, citing Tombs purples at rates like one in nine and about one in four in his solo 500 raids[^fLx3y5MTVjw-433]. He suggests moving some drops to new Slayer monsters or bosses, as with the Dragon harpoon and Dragon throwing knives[^fLx3y5MTVjw-580], and says the nerf is fair while he expects mega scaling to remain the best way to get purples with some gear[^fLx3y5MTVjw-702]. See also [Mega rare](Mega_rare.md).
- **June 2026 (Summer Sweep-Up)**: the [Surge potion](Surge_potion.md) cooldown is no longer reset on death inside Chambers, ending the speedrun meta of taking a two-dose surge potion to Olm and deliberately dying between phases to reset the cooldown[^6tsf-brc-sE-1206]. He thinks this is good but the world record will now be hard to beat[^6tsf-brc-sE-1234]. Night-wave progress is no longer reset when leaving via the door[^6tsf-brc-sE-1234]. See [Update: Summer Sweep-up](Update_Summer_Sweep-up.md).
- **Prayer scroll weighting (June 2026)**: per the blog, the unique tables are weighted heavily towards the arcane and dexterous prayer scrolls, so roughly 58% of uniques seen on average are a prayer scroll, also in CM[^6tsf-brc-sE-1263]. CM completion adds the twisted ancestral kit and metamorphic dust to a tertiary table without changing the ordinary table, whereas Theatre of Blood hard mode reduces the Avernic defender hilt weighting by 7.7%[^6tsf-brc-sE-1263][^6tsf-brc-sE-1289]. He notes the dragon sword, dragon thrown axe and dragon harpoon used to be on the drop table[^6tsf-brc-sE-1683].
- **July 2026 proposed reweighting**: regular raid total weighting drops from 69 to 60 and each ancestral piece rises from 3 to 4, a 33% increase in chance[^EPlArwCehYY-608]. Arcane and Dexterous scroll weighting falls from 20 to 14 in regular raids and to 12 in CM; the old weighting meant about five prayer scrolls per ancestral piece[^EPlArwCehYY-668]. Jagex expects prices to shift but GP per hour not to drop drastically[^EPlArwCehYY-608]. Gnomonkey says ancestral pieces were far too rare, usually the last item ironmen chase over hundreds of raids[^EPlArwCehYY-694]. He thinks CM would be best with a larger completion bonus (currently 5k points, ideally 10k to 20k) and that it may now be better than regular raids for players who have a Twisted bow[^EPlArwCehYY-694]. See also [Opinion: Chambers prayer scroll weighting](Opinion_Chambers_prayer_scroll_weighting.md).
- In a June 2026 video about his Deadman All-Stars experience, prayer scrolls from Chambers saved the team about 10 blood money of their 40 total[^lJexGv6dxu8-2444][^lJexGv6dxu8-2222]. See [Deadman All-Stars](Deadman_All-Stars.md).
- Gnomonkey also says going dry on a Twisted bow is the scariest dry streak in the game, because it is essential[^fLx3y5MTVjw-489].
