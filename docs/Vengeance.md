# Vengeance

## Tips

- Gnomonkey uses Vengeance in the Inferno to get overkill damage on monsters, calling the Vengeance overkill huge. ([2024-12-09 1:29](https://youtu.be/7VKipTJkybM?t=89))
- Gnomonkey says Vengeance reflected 100% of the damage from one hit in the Inferno, which he calls huge. ([2024-12-09 10:57](https://youtu.be/7VKipTJkybM?t=657))
- Gnomonkey advises that if you are ever going to take damage in the Inferno you should make sure to cast Vengeance first. ([2024-12-09 15:27](https://youtu.be/7VKipTJkybM?t=927))
- Gnomonkey says most deep delvers skip Vengeance at Doom of Mokhaiotl because it wastes a slot, but still bring Lunar spells for cure me and spellbook swap for just Death Charge. ([2025-08-22 17:24](https://youtu.be/Glp4chf4yiA?t=1044))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says with thralls useless at Doom of Mokhaiotl you should bring Vengeance, but the only attacks you can venge there are direct melee attacks and missed boulder prayers, not regular auto attacks. ([2025-08-22 16:57](https://youtu.be/Glp4chf4yiA?t=1017))
- Gnomonkey asks why Jagex hates Vengeance, saying many bosses are pointlessly immune to recoil, citing Nex and the Doom of Mokhaiotl giant death laser that hits 99 but gives no recoil damage. ([2025-08-22 17:24](https://youtu.be/Glp4chf4yiA?t=1044))
