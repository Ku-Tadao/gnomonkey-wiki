**Ranged** is a skill topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2019-07-31 and 2020-06-27. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* If training Ranged as cheaply as possible with darts, the armour progression Gnomonkey lists is leather at level 1, studded leather at 20, frog leather at 25, snakeskin at 30, green d'hide at 40, blue d'hide at 50, red d'hide at 60 and black d'hide at 70.[^bIShDvVDueM-252]

## Stats and numbers

* Ranged DPS is calculated differently from melee: the ranged weapon typically provides only ranged accuracy, while max hit comes from the ammunition's ranged strength bonus and your Ranged level.[^bIShDvVDueM-32]
* The main exceptions to ammunition determining ranged strength are the Twisted bow and the Toxic blowpipe, which provide ranged strength themselves.[^bIShDvVDueM-32]
* The only other gear pieces that affect max hit are Ava's assembler and the Necklace of anguish.[^bIShDvVDueM-65]

## Tips

* A merchant in the Tree Gnome Village sells 30 bronze arrows at a time, so Gnomonkey hops worlds and buys all 30 repeatedly, reaching 467 arrows.[^RfZ_xQKG7Xg-475]
* Gnomonkey advises throwing an unusual boss/gear question into the OSRS DPS calculator rather than relying on the guide, since he will miss niche cases where another gear set is better.[^bIShDvVDueM-0]
* Gnomonkey recommends using the best ammunition you can reasonably afford, since most gear upgrades just improve accuracy while the ammo does the heavy lifting.[^bIShDvVDueM-65]
* D'hide shields provide a ranged attack bonus while training, and the early Ranged levels can easily be skipped by training with a cannon.[^bIShDvVDueM-286]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says making every piece of gold go as far as possible helps your account's efficiency tremendously, and this guide focuses on getting the most DPS out of Ranged equipment, though defence or accuracy matter more in cases like bossing.[^bIShDvVDueM-0]
* Gnomonkey calls the Ranged gear set incredibly important because it is used for staple moneymakers such as Vorkath, Zulrah and the Alchemical Hydra.[^bIShDvVDueM-32]

## Events

* Gnomonkey finds a wizard hat from goblins that is a straight upgrade for his head slot.[^RfZ_xQKG7Xg-386]
* Gnomonkey buys a bow from his weapons dealer Gullock and has 140 bronze arrows for Ranged training, finding a safe spot where he can shoot through a small section of gate.[^RfZ_xQKG7Xg-414]
* Gnomonkey reaches level 22 Ranged before the final Warlord kill.[^RfZ_xQKG7Xg-532]
