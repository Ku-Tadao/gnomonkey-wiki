**Shamans (Chambers of Xeric)** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2024-10-27 and 2025-05-01. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* To avoid the Shamans' AoE, Gnomonkey moves three tiles every time they do their attack animation, since they read the tile he was standing on.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-1453]
* Whenever a Shaman starts its animation, move up three tiles to avoid the attack.[^jdvQVwg8niM-679]

## Stats and numbers

* Shamans attack roughly every five or six ticks and launch a projectile at your location; if you are within three tiles of it you take massive damage.[^jdvQVwg8niM-653]

## Tips

* To stay adjacent to a wall when fighting Shamans because if you are next to a wall they cannot jump, letting you keep shooting them.[^wxGBi89vE_s-2733]
* To move three tiles every time the Shamans do their attack animation, and ideally stay out of range of the next one until you are close to killing the first.[^wxGBi89vE_s-2733]
* You can redemption off an overload, so if you are low on prayer and around 50 health it is a way to get some health back.[^wxGBi89vE_s-2733]
* Gnomonkey warns that Shamans poisoning you for around 16 once poison wears off can end the raid, so bring antidote.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-1152]
* At the Shamans in CM, kill the left one first because it wanders closer to the entrance, and stay at the lower area so the other cannot reach you.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-1420]
* Always stay next to a wall at Shamans, because they cannot use the jump attack when you are against a wall.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-1453]
* You can safe spot Shamans by luring them to a spot where they get stuck, as long as you outrange their eight-tile range, then AFK them.[^jdvQVwg8niM-708]
* If you are prepping, grab the Butchu seeds here, and Gnomonkey says to lure at least the last Shaman to a safe spot while learning.[^jdvQVwg8niM-708]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says safe spotting Shamans is trolling because it slows the raid down and it is easy enough to just kill them.[^wxGBi89vE_s-2764]
