# Shamans (Chambers of Xeric)

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2024-10-27 to 2025-05-01). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | boss |
| **Videos** | 3 (first 2024-10-27, latest 2025-05-01) |

## Methods

- To avoid the Shamans' AoE, Gnomonkey moves three tiles every time they do their attack animation, since they read the tile he was standing on. ([2024-11-08 24:13](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=1453))
- Whenever a Shaman starts its animation, move up three tiles to avoid the attack. ([2025-05-01 11:19](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=679))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says Shamans attack roughly every five or six ticks and launch a projectile at your location; if you are within three tiles of it you take massive damage. ([2025-05-01 10:53](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=653))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says to stay adjacent to a wall when fighting Shamans because if you are next to a wall they cannot jump, letting you keep shooting them. ([2024-10-27 45:33](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=2733))
- Gnomonkey says to move three tiles every time the Shamans do their attack animation, and ideally stay out of range of the next one until you are close to killing the first. ([2024-10-27 45:33](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=2733))
- Gnomonkey says you can redemption off an overload, so if you are low on prayer and around 50 health it is a way to get some health back. ([2024-10-27 45:33](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=2733))
- Gnomonkey warns that Shamans poisoning you for around 16 once poison wears off can end the raid, so bring antidote. ([2024-11-08 19:12](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=1152))
- At the Shamans in CM, kill the left one first because it wanders closer to the entrance, and stay at the lower area so the other cannot reach you. ([2024-11-08 23:40](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=1420))
- Always stay next to a wall at Shamans, because they cannot use the jump attack when you are against a wall. ([2024-11-08 24:13](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=1453))
- Gnomonkey says you can safe spot Shamans by luring them to a spot where they get stuck, as long as you outrange their eight-tile range, then AFK them. ([2025-05-01 11:48](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=708))
- If you are prepping, grab the Butchu seeds here, and Gnomonkey says to lure at least the last Shaman to a safe spot while learning. ([2025-05-01 11:48](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=708))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says safe spotting Shamans is trolling because it slows the raid down and it is easy enough to just kill them. ([2024-10-27 46:04](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=2764))
