# Gnome Restaurant

## Methods

- Gnomonkey starts by talking to Gianne Jnr. for a job, after which the customers ask for specific gnome food such as toad crunchies, and he cooks them fresh. ([2019-08-07 5:54](https://youtu.be/xIjOXE9-IS8?t=354))

## Requirements

- About 80 gnome crunchies got Gnomonkey to a high enough Cooking level to start Gnome Restaurant. ([2019-08-07 5:54](https://youtu.be/xIjOXE9-IS8?t=354))

## Tips

- Cocktail training from the restaurant gives a bunch of dough, which Gnomonkey says is worth about 100 GP and is hard to get. ([2019-08-07 7:25](https://youtu.be/xIjOXE9-IS8?t=445))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says in the description that Gnome Restaurant delivery is a fairly decent money maker with low requirements and recommends it for brand new players. ([2019-08-07 0:00](https://youtu.be/xIjOXE9-IS8))
- Gnomonkey notes that a delivery took about five minutes to walk back, making him think it may be better to wait until the Grand Tree quest to do many deliveries. ([2019-08-07 9:26](https://youtu.be/xIjOXE9-IS8?t=566))

## Events

- Gnomonkey needs to grind the restaurant for opals to train Crafting; once Crafting is level 10 he can craft bowstrings and fletch new bows, and hopes for the rarer gnome scarf and gnome goggles. ([2019-08-07 0:00](https://youtu.be/xIjOXE9-IS8))
- Gnomonkey makes his first cocktails (fruit blast, pineapple punch, wizard blizzard and a short green guy), noting they look similar to other gnome foods. ([2019-08-07 7:25](https://youtu.be/xIjOXE9-IS8?t=445))
- His first delivery reward was an uncut jade and his second a red topaz; he cannot cut either but is getting close to the opal he wants. ([2019-08-07 9:26](https://youtu.be/xIjOXE9-IS8?t=566))
