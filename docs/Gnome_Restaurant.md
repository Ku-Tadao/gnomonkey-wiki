**Gnome Restaurant** is an activity topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2019-08-07. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* About 80 gnome crunchies got Gnomonkey to a high enough Cooking level to start Gnome Restaurant.[^xIjOXE9-IS8-354]

## Methods

* Gnomonkey starts by talking to Gianne Jnr. for a job, after which the customers ask for specific gnome food such as toad crunchies, and he cooks them fresh.[^xIjOXE9-IS8-354]

## Tips

* Cocktail training from the restaurant gives a bunch of dough, which Gnomonkey says is worth about 100 GP and is hard to get.[^xIjOXE9-IS8-445]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says in the description that Gnome Restaurant delivery is a fairly decent money maker with low requirements and recommends it for brand new players.[^xIjOXE9-IS8-0]
* Gnomonkey notes that a delivery took about five minutes to walk back, making him think it may be better to wait until the Grand Tree quest to do many deliveries.[^xIjOXE9-IS8-566]

## Events

* Gnomonkey needs to grind the restaurant for opals to train Crafting; once Crafting is level 10 he can craft bowstrings and fletch new bows, and hopes for the rarer gnome scarf and gnome goggles.[^xIjOXE9-IS8-0]
* Gnomonkey makes his first cocktails (fruit blast, pineapple punch, wizard blizzard and a short green guy), noting they look similar to other gnome foods.[^xIjOXE9-IS8-445]
* His first delivery reward was an uncut jade and his second a red topaz; he cannot cut either but is getting close to the opal he wants.[^xIjOXE9-IS8-566]
