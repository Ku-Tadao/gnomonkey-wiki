## Opinions

- Gnomonkey argues the demand for an immediate fix is wrong: fixing Sailing combat correctly needs a very clear plan, and he suggests it should take months or even over a year, not weeks. ([2026-03-20 7:06](https://youtu.be/fZ5RVn7tNhI?t=426))
- Gnomonkey says Zeah was not fixed immediately because Chambers of Xeric was a priority a year later and the dev team was much smaller, and he feels the same about Sailing combat today. ([2026-03-20 8:05](https://youtu.be/fZ5RVn7tNhI?t=485))
- Gnomonkey thinks the community anger partly comes from vitriol over the membership price increase, and that demanding an immediate fix with pitchforks will make the update worse. ([2026-03-20 10:09](https://youtu.be/fZ5RVn7tNhI?t=609))
- Gnomonkey says rushing a fix could be four or five botched updates, and a quick fix may make a proper fix harder later because it feels almost good enough. ([2026-03-20 10:41](https://youtu.be/fZ5RVn7tNhI?t=641))
- Gnomonkey says he cares more about Raids 4, quality-of-life updates to existing content and the sparse roadmap than Sailing combat. ([2026-03-20 11:32](https://youtu.be/fZ5RVn7tNhI?t=692))
- Gnomonkey says he is more worried about the design of the Maggot king (recently delayed further), whether another Yama-like drop of overpowered items will unbalance the game, and the struggle over what the next mega rare should be. ([2026-03-20 11:59](https://youtu.be/fZ5RVn7tNhI?t=719))
- Gnomonkey jokes that he is more concerned about the Arceuus spellbook teleport arrangement, with spells in the wrong place, than Sailing combat. ([2026-03-20 12:27](https://youtu.be/fZ5RVn7tNhI?t=747))
