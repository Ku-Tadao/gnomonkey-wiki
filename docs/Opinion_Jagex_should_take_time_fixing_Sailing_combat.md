**Opinion: Jagex should take time fixing Sailing combat** is an opinion topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2026-03-20. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey argues the demand for an immediate fix is wrong: fixing Sailing combat correctly needs a very clear plan, and he suggests it should take months or even over a year, not weeks.[^fZ5RVn7tNhI-426]
* Gnomonkey says Zeah was not fixed immediately because Chambers of Xeric was a priority a year later and the dev team was much smaller, and he feels the same about Sailing combat today.[^fZ5RVn7tNhI-485]
* Gnomonkey thinks the community anger partly comes from vitriol over the membership price increase, and that demanding an immediate fix with pitchforks will make the update worse.[^fZ5RVn7tNhI-609]
* Gnomonkey says rushing a fix could be four or five botched updates, and a quick fix may make a proper fix harder later because it feels almost good enough.[^fZ5RVn7tNhI-641]
* Gnomonkey says he cares more about Raids 4, quality-of-life updates to existing content and the sparse roadmap than Sailing combat.[^fZ5RVn7tNhI-692]
* Gnomonkey says he is more worried about the design of the Maggot king (recently delayed further), whether another Yama-like drop of overpowered items will unbalance the game, and the struggle over what the next mega rare should be.[^fZ5RVn7tNhI-719]
* Gnomonkey jokes that he is more concerned about the Arceuus spellbook teleport arrangement, with spells in the wrong place, than Sailing combat.[^fZ5RVn7tNhI-747]

## Events

* Gnomonkey compared Zeah at release, which looked like a private-server map with a big square and giant empty buildings, to the current cleaner Zeah, which took years of incremental work including the Hosidius rework.[^fZ5RVn7tNhI-453]
