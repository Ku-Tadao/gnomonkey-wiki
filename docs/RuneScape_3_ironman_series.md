**RuneScape 3 ironman series** is an account topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2023-10-18 and 2023-10-26. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Tips

* Basically any extra XP from lamps goes straight into Herblore on his RS3 ironman and will stay that way for all eternity.[^Cbhq22YeYFk-269]
* On the day's itinerary, Gnomonkey plans about two hours minimum of dailies and then quests, with the guide being mostly quests up to roughly step 240.[^wRHsOi8plqM-146]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says RS3 music is absolutely insane, and he no longer plays his own music on stream when playing RS3.[^Cbhq22YeYFk-0]
* Gnomonkey says the early game of RS3 is worse than the early game of OSRS and that early game is not what you come to RS3 for.[^Cbhq22YeYFk-61]
* Gnomonkey says the early game of RS3 is really similar to early game OSRS: rush all the quests you can for big XP drops and to unlock important items and areas.[^wRHsOi8plqM-0]
* Gnomonkey notes RS3 has no RuneLite, no plugins, no true tile and no radius markers.[^wRHsOi8plqM-62]

## Records

* He has a main that is maxed except for new skills, and has done some basic PvM there like Araxxor and very low enraged Telos.[^wRHsOi8plqM-625]

## Events

* This is day two of Gnomonkey's journey in RuneScape 3 as an ironman; he says questing seems even more relevant and rewarding than in OSRS with additional XP and teleport areas.[^Cbhq22YeYFk-0]
* Per the description, RS3 videos had been heavily requested, and now that SubTember is over and he has an assistant editor, Gnomonkey says he can start making RS3 content.[^Cbhq22YeYFk-0]
* He is playing an ironman in RS3 because RS3 is chock full of microtransactions such as keys and chests that give XP or bonus XP, which make it inauthentic to RuneScape; ironmen cannot use or acquire those keys.[^wRHsOi8plqM-146]
* Step 32 of the guide he follows was to train magic to level 40 in the Burthorpe troll cave, but magic XP is maybe a fifth of what it was, so he got magic to 30 by killing cows for about an hour and moved on.[^wRHsOi8plqM-539]
* He has not been making RS3 content because he hit a wall later on, and that he has been AFKing a lot of smithing and crafting.[^wRHsOi8plqM-575]
* He still plays both RS3 and OSRS roughly equally on Twitch and has quit neither game, and that he has never really done a series before so he asks viewers to comment on it.[^wRHsOi8plqM-716]
