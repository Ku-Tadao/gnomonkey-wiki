## Tips

- Gnomonkey says basically any extra XP from lamps goes straight into Herblore on his RS3 ironman and will stay that way for all eternity. ([2023-10-18 4:29](https://youtu.be/Cbhq22YeYFk?t=269))
- On the day's itinerary, Gnomonkey plans about two hours minimum of dailies and then quests, with the guide being mostly quests up to roughly step 240. ([2023-10-26 2:26](https://youtu.be/wRHsOi8plqM?t=146))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says RS3 music is absolutely insane, and he no longer plays his own music on stream when playing RS3. ([2023-10-18 0:00](https://youtu.be/Cbhq22YeYFk))
- Gnomonkey says the early game of RS3 is worse than the early game of OSRS and that early game is not what you come to RS3 for. ([2023-10-18 1:01](https://youtu.be/Cbhq22YeYFk?t=61))
- Gnomonkey says the early game of RS3 is really similar to early game OSRS: rush all the quests you can for big XP drops and to unlock important items and areas. ([2023-10-26 0:00](https://youtu.be/wRHsOi8plqM))
- Gnomonkey notes RS3 has no RuneLite, no plugins, no true tile and no radius markers. ([2023-10-26 1:02](https://youtu.be/wRHsOi8plqM?t=62))

## Records

- Gnomonkey says he has a main that is maxed except for new skills, and has done some basic PvM there like Araxxor and very low enraged Telos. ([2023-10-26 10:25](https://youtu.be/wRHsOi8plqM?t=625))

## Events

- This is day two of Gnomonkey's journey in RuneScape 3 as an ironman; he says questing seems even more relevant and rewarding than in OSRS with additional XP and teleport areas. ([2023-10-18 0:00](https://youtu.be/Cbhq22YeYFk))
- Per the description, RS3 videos had been heavily requested, and now that SubTember is over and he has an assistant editor, Gnomonkey says he can start making RS3 content. ([2023-10-18 0:00](https://youtu.be/Cbhq22YeYFk))
- Gnomonkey says he is playing an ironman in RS3 because RS3 is chock full of microtransactions such as keys and chests that give XP or bonus XP, which make it inauthentic to RuneScape; ironmen cannot use or acquire those keys. ([2023-10-26 2:26](https://youtu.be/wRHsOi8plqM?t=146))
- Gnomonkey says step 32 of the guide he follows was to train magic to level 40 in the Burthorpe troll cave, but magic XP is maybe a fifth of what it was, so he got magic to 30 by killing cows for about an hour and moved on. ([2023-10-26 8:59](https://youtu.be/wRHsOi8plqM?t=539))
- Gnomonkey says he has not been making RS3 content because he hit a wall later on, and that he has been AFKing a lot of smithing and crafting. ([2023-10-26 9:35](https://youtu.be/wRHsOi8plqM?t=575))
- Gnomonkey says he still plays both RS3 and OSRS roughly equally on Twitch and has quit neither game, and that he has never really done a series before so he asks viewers to comment on it. ([2023-10-26 11:56](https://youtu.be/wRHsOi8plqM?t=716))
