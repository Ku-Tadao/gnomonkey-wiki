The **Toxic blowpipe** is a cheap [Ranged](Ranged.md) weapon that Gnomonkey regards as the clearest example of power creep in the game. Since 2020 he has called it the best main weapon for the Fight Caves and, other than the [Twisted bow](Twisted_bow.md) (or the [Dragon hunter crossbow](Dragon_hunter_crossbow.md) in specific situations), the highest-DPS ranged weapon.[^oG65hOavtJg-86][^oG65hOavtJg-150][^bIShDvVDueM-678] In 2022 he called it the worst power creep in OSRS history by far, and in 2025 he still uses it as a heal and a damage special across the Inferno, Tombs of Amascut and Doom of Mokhaiotl.[^SO4k9z1A8-0-535][^bBnPK55ZqUM-1012][^_q6rmQQkN1I-119][^2xviK0wGI-o-2830]

## Why it is strong

It is one of the few weapons giving both ranged strength and accuracy, and darts do not slow it: it still attacks every two ticks on rapid.[^bIShDvVDueM-343] Gnomonkey says it was intended to be three ticks, but a Jmod forgot that rapid reduces attack speed by a tick.[^SO4k9z1A8-0-535] It was added with [Zulrah](Zulrah.md) near the start of OSRS, when developers were less experienced and had worse tools.[^SO4k9z1A8-0-535][^SO4k9z1A8-0-554] Even after the Twisted bow arrived, it challenged that weapon in many locations, with a mid-level boss dropping arguably the best ranged weapon in the game.[^SO4k9z1A8-0-554] The developers later nerfed it so that weapons like the [Bow of Faerdhinen](Bow_of_Faerdhinen.md) could shine in their tier, but he still thinks it is fairly overpowered and says it has shaped the direction of the game.[^SO4k9z1A8-0-587]

All Fight Caves enemies can be envenomed except Jad, who can only be poisoned, and the DPS plus venom make it the clear choice; on its own it annihilates every enemy in the cave.[^oG65hOavtJg-86][^oG65hOavtJg-150] A Twisted bow is better against Jad and the 360s if the player can afford one.[^oG65hOavtJg-116]

## Cost and darts

In 2020 the blowpipe cost about 400k gp/hr in scale cost alone, before darts, and about 450k gp/hr with adamant darts (more if the darts are left on the ground).[^bIShDvVDueM-312][^7dh6_0u13Nc-96][^7dh6_0u13Nc-96] Because it fires every two ticks, darts are lost faster than on other ranged weapons.[^7dh6_0u13Nc-148]

| Dart | Ranged strength | Added cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adamant | +10 | About 45k gp/hr | Gnomonkey's baseline; use almost always[^7dh6_0u13Nc-96][^7dh6_0u13Nc-96][^7dh6_0u13Nc-148] |
| Rune | +14 | Just under 600k gp/hr |[^7dh6_0u13Nc-96][^7dh6_0u13Nc-96] |
| Dragon | +20 | Just under 1.5m gp/hr, about 1m gp/hr over rune |[^7dh6_0u13Nc-96][^7dh6_0u13Nc-96][^7dh6_0u13Nc-208] |

Each dart tier has a small accuracy bonus and typically gives one extra max hit, roughly 4% DPS.[^7dh6_0u13Nc-96] That upgrade is worth using only when making over 10m gp/hr, and it breaks even on paper at just over 12m gp/hr.[^7dh6_0u13Nc-148] In 2020 he knew of only the [Theatre of Blood](Theatre_of_Blood.md) (up to 11m gp/hr with perfect trios) and the Nightmare, where the blowpipe is not used, earning that much.[^7dh6_0u13Nc-148] Skeletal Mystics and the [Chambers of Xeric](Chambers_of_Xeric.md) are among the few high-defence targets where the pipe is used, and there the upgrade is about 5% DPS.[^7dh6_0u13Nc-148] He says dragon darts, over 20m gp/hr to pay off, are only for pet hunters or boss kill count high scores.[^7dh6_0u13Nc-208]

By 2025 he treats dragon darts as the standard in demanding content: a dragon-dart pipe is basically always better than a Bofa except on triple Jads and Zuk, so it can be main-handed. Amethyst darts cost only one max hit on normal attacks and two on specs.[^_q6rmQQkN1I-488][^2xviK0wGI-o-1103][^2xviK0wGI-o-2804]

## Special attack

The special does double damage and double accuracy, so it is good for killing and not just healing.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-1860][^2xviK0wGI-o-979] It heals the user, which makes it a staple at [Zulrah](Zulrah.md) even when the pipe is not the main weapon.[^bBnPK55ZqUM-1012] He recommends using it even at full health just to start the spec energy recharging.[^bBnPK55ZqUM-1017][^UmAjGV8bBAQ-2480]

* Best used on the tankiest enemy, the melee, whenever the player has spec and full health; a good time to spec is while tanking damage.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-2130][^2xviK0wGI-o-3018]
* Spec bats rather than tanking them, since bats drain stats and run energy, slow the player and hit hard.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-3050]
* A mage under about 30 HP can be finished with the blowpipe, saving about three ticks over a Twisted bow shot that would deal only about five.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-3060]
* If all else fails against a melee, use [Deadeye](Deadeye.md) or Rigour with specs, which heal with the Light bearer.[^2xviK0wGI-o-979]

## Usage notes

| Place | Advice |
|---|---|
| Zulrah | Overtakes the [Atlatl](Atlatl.md) only with at least rune darts, since the atlatl is a lot more accurate.[^bBnPK55ZqUM-976] |
| Grotesque Guardians | Fill with dragon darts, or the heal skip is rarely achieved; Barrows gloves are fine since Zaryte vambraces are a very small upgrade.[^b_kFNIJAcGE-150][^b_kFNIJAcGE-150] During Dawn, move to a set tile between shots while praying ranged and Rigour, which two-tick offs both bosses; in the heal-orb phase stand next to Dawn on melee prayer and Rigour and DPS her instead of chasing orbs.[^b_kFNIJAcGE-332][^b_kFNIJAcGE-427] |
| Doom of Mokhaiotl | Without a ZCB the budget specs are the blowpipe and Dragon knifes; the pipe heals, the knives cost half the spec energy and hit slightly higher but do not heal, so he brings both.[^_q6rmQQkN1I-119][^_q6rmQQkN1I-119] A dragon-dart pipe is about 5% worse than a Twisted bow and about 11% better than the Scorching bow on the car phase, which he calls one of the most ignored important items.[^_q6rmQQkN1I-455][^_q6rmQQkN1I-488] Spamming knife specs, or anything with double hitsplats, spawns a lot of acid on delve 3 and above.[^_q6rmQQkN1I-1033] |
| Inferno | The Inferno cannot be learned without a pipe, since it is vital for Zuk's healers; tagging them all with Bofa would add about 15 shots.[^2xviK0wGI-o-2830] A Bofa is better against tanky targets if only amethyst darts are available, but a viewer noted the pipe is better whenever range potted, which is most of the time on waves that matter.[^2xviK0wGI-o-1133][^2xviK0wGI-o-2804][^2xviK0wGI-o-3107] |
