# Toxic blowpipe

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 9 videos (2020-04-27 to 2025-12-07). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Stats and numbers

- All Fight Caves enemies can be envenomed except Jad, who can only be poisoned, and Gnomonkey says the pipe's DPS plus venom make it a clear choice. ([2020-04-27 1:26](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=86))
- Even with only adamant darts the Toxic blowpipe costs about 450k gp an hour to use, more if you leave the darts on the ground. ([2020-06-27 5:12](https://youtu.be/bIShDvVDueM?t=312))
- The Toxic blowpipe is one of the few weapons giving both ranged strength and accuracy, and it does not reduce attack speed from darts, still two ticks on rapid. ([2020-06-27 5:43](https://youtu.be/bIShDvVDueM?t=343))
- In 2020 a toxic blowpipe costs about 400k GP an hour to use in scale cost alone, before darts. ([2020-10-01 1:36](https://youtu.be/7dh6_0u13Nc?t=96))
- Adamant darts add only about 45k GP an hour and are Gnomonkey's baseline; rune darts cost just under 600k GP an hour and dragon darts just under 1.5m GP an hour to use. ([2020-10-01 1:36](https://youtu.be/7dh6_0u13Nc?t=96))
- Adamant darts have +10 range strength, rune darts +14 and dragon darts +20, each with a small ranged accuracy bonus; each dart upgrade typically gives one extra max hit, roughly 4% DPS. ([2020-10-01 1:36](https://youtu.be/7dh6_0u13Nc?t=96))
- For a roughly 4% DPS dart upgrade to be worth using you need to be making over 10m GP an hour, and you break even on dart cost on paper at just over 12m GP an hour. ([2020-10-01 2:28](https://youtu.be/7dh6_0u13Nc?t=148))
- Skeletal Mystics and the Chambers of Xeric are among the few monsters piped with high defence, but the upgrade there is still only about a 5% DPS increase versus 4%. ([2020-10-01 2:28](https://youtu.be/7dh6_0u13Nc?t=148))
- Gnomonkey says Toxic blowpipe specs do double damage and double accuracy, so they are very good at killing things and not just for healing. ([2025-07-13 31:00](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=1860))
- Dragon knives cost half as much spec energy as the blowpipe at Doom of Mokhaiotl and hit a little higher, but do not heal; Gnomonkey says to bring both if you have access to Dragon knives. ([2025-08-09 1:59](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=119))
- Gnomonkey says a blowpipe with dragon darts is only about 5% worse than a Twisted bow at Doom of Mokhaiotl and does about 11% more DPS than the Scorching bow on car phase. ([2025-08-09 7:35](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=455))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says the blowpipe on its own annihilates all the enemies in the cave and is best in slot on a Fight Caves task. ([2020-04-27 2:30](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=150))
- Because the toxic blowpipe fires every two ticks you lose darts much more quickly than other ranged weapons. ([2020-10-01 2:28](https://youtu.be/7dh6_0u13Nc?t=148))
- At the Grotesque Guardians the blowpipe should be filled with dragon darts; Gnomonkey says any less and you will not get the heal skip often. ([2022-03-16 2:30](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=150))
- Gnomonkey says Zaryte vambraces are only a very small upgrade over Barrows gloves for the blowpipe switch and that Barrows gloves are perfectly acceptable at the Grotesque Guardians. ([2022-03-16 2:30](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=150))
- During the Dawn phase, Gnomonkey moves between blowpipe shots to a set tile while praying ranged and Rigor, which two-tick offs both bosses. ([2022-03-16 5:32](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=332))
- In the heal-orb phase, Gnomonkey stands next to Dawn praying melee and Rigor and DPSes her with the blowpipe rather than chasing the orbs. ([2022-03-16 7:07](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=427))
- Gnomonkey says the blowpipe is very good at Zulrah even if you do not use it as the main weapon, since its special attack heals you. ([2025-04-24 16:52](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=1012))
- Gnomonkey recommends using the blowpipe special attack even at full health just to start the special attack energy recharging. ([2025-04-24 16:57](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=1017))
- Gnomonkey says the blowpipe spec is best used on the melee, which is the tankiest enemy, whenever the player has spec and full health. ([2025-07-13 35:30](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=2130))
- Gnomonkey says to keep the spec charging by using it for damage rather than sitting at full spec. ([2025-07-13 41:20](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=2480))
- Gnomonkey says to spec bats that are on you instead of tanking them, because bats drain stats and run energy, slow you down and do a lot of damage. ([2025-07-13 50:50](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=3050))
- Gnomonkey says a mage below about 30 HP can be finished with the blowpipe, saving about three ticks over a Twisted bow shot that would only do about five damage. ([2025-07-13 51:00](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=3060))
- Gnomonkey says that without a ZCB the budget ranged specs at Doom of Mokhaiotl are the blowpipe and Dragon knives; the blowpipe spec heals you, which is why it is also the choice when low on health. ([2025-08-09 1:59](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=119))
- Gnomonkey says one of the most important items people ignore is the blowpipe on car phase at Doom of Mokhaiotl, and that using Amethyst darts instead of dragon darts is only a one max hit loss for normal attacks and two for specs. ([2025-08-09 8:08](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=488))
- Gnomonkey says spamming Dragon knife specs, or anything that makes double hitsplats, spawns a lot of acid on Doom of Mokhaiotl delve 3 and above, so it is not worth it. ([2025-08-09 17:13](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=1033))
- Gnomonkey says if all else fails against a melee, use Deadeye or Rigor with blowpipe specs, which hit for double damage and double accuracy and heal you with the Light bearer. ([2025-12-07 16:19](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=979))
- Gnomonkey says a blowpipe loaded with dragon darts is basically always better than a Bow of Faerdhinen except on triple Jads and Zuk, so it can be main handed. ([2025-12-07 18:23](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=1103))
- Gnomonkey says a Bow of Faerdhinen is better on tanky targets such as melee NPCs and mages if you only have amethyst darts. ([2025-12-07 18:53](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=1133))
- Gnomonkey says amethyst dart blowpipe is worse than Bofa on mages and melees, with dragon darts being what makes the pipe better. ([2025-12-07 46:44](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=2804))
- Gnomonkey says to use blowpipe specs to kill things rather than as a heal, and a good time to spec is when you are tanking damage. ([2025-12-07 50:18](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=3018))
- A viewer in chat said the pipe is only better than the Bofa when you are range potted, which Gnomonkey says is the case most of the time on waves that matter. ([2025-12-07 51:47](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=3107))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says a blowpipe, requiring 75 Ranged, is the best main weapon for the Fight Caves. ([2020-04-27 1:26](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=86))
- Gnomonkey says a twisted bow is the best option for Jad and the 360s if the player can afford it, but a player with one probably would not be watching a guide like this. ([2020-04-27 1:56](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=116))
- Gnomonkey calls the Toxic blowpipe overpowered for such a cheap ranged weapon. ([2020-06-27 5:43](https://youtu.be/bIShDvVDueM?t=343))
- Gnomonkey says the blowpipe has the highest DPS of any ranged weapon other than the Twisted bow, or the Dragon hunter crossbow in specific situations. ([2020-06-27 11:18](https://youtu.be/bIShDvVDueM?t=678))
- Gnomonkey says the only place he knows of that makes this much is the Theatre of Blood, at up to 11m GP an hour with perfect trios, and the Nightmare, where you do not use the blowpipe anyway. ([2020-10-01 2:28](https://youtu.be/7dh6_0u13Nc?t=148))
- Gnomonkey says you should almost always use adamant darts instead of rune darts in the blowpipe. ([2020-10-01 2:28](https://youtu.be/7dh6_0u13Nc?t=148))
- Gnomonkey says dragon darts, costing an extra 1m GP an hour over rune darts for one more max hit, are only for pet hunters or people going for boss kill count high scores, as you would need over 20m GP an hour to make them pay off, which he knows of no source for. ([2020-10-01 3:28](https://youtu.be/7dh6_0u13Nc?t=208))
- Gnomonkey calls the toxic blowpipe the worst case of power creep in OSRS history by far; it was added with Zulrah near the start of OSRS. ([2022-05-25 8:55](https://youtu.be/SO4k9z1A8-0?t=535))
- Gnomonkey says the devs back then were less experienced with worse tools; even when Twisted bow came out later, the blowpipe was so overpowered it challenged the Twisted bow in many locations, with a mid-level boss dropping arguably the best ranged weapon in the game. ([2022-05-25 9:14](https://youtu.be/SO4k9z1A8-0?t=554))
- Gnomonkey says the blowpipe overtakes the atlatl at Zulrah only with at least rune darts, because the atlatl is a lot more accurate. ([2025-04-24 16:16](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=976))
- Gnomonkey says you cannot learn Inferno without a blowpipe because it is vital for the healers on Zuk, and tagging them all with Bofa would add about 15 extra shots. ([2025-12-07 47:10](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=2830))

## Events

- Years later the devs nerfed the blowpipe, letting other weapons like Bow of Faerdhinen shine in their tier; Gnomonkey thinks the blowpipe is still fairly overpowered and has truly shaped the direction the game has taken. ([2022-05-25 9:47](https://youtu.be/SO4k9z1A8-0?t=587))

## Trivia

- According to Gnomonkey the blowpipe was intended to have an attack speed of three ticks, but when creating it a Jmod forgot about the rapid attack style reducing attack speed by a tick. ([2022-05-25 8:55](https://youtu.be/SO4k9z1A8-0?t=535))
