# Mixed hide armour

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 5 videos (2024-05-18 to 2024-06-27). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | item |
| **Videos** | 5 (first 2024-05-18, latest 2024-06-27) |

## Methods

- Gnomonkey explains that Osmumten's fang rolls accuracy twice, so strength bonus matters less for it while stab bonus matters a lot; he likens the fang to a mini Shadow that rolls stab twice. ([2024-05-30 2:04](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=124))
- Because Mixed hide replaces the ranged armour switch, Gnomonkey brings eight restore potions in a 500, up from four or five in his previous teaching stream, and says this makes the 500 much more relaxed. ([2024-05-30 3:35](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=215))

## Stats and numbers

- Mixed hide armour provides both strength bonus and stab bonus; Gnomonkey says the chestplate has the strength bonus while the legs have only stab bonus, and his full setup showed a stab bonus of 137. ([2024-05-30 1:33](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=93))
- In Gnomonkey's DPS calculator against Ba-Ba at raid level 500, path level 2, with no defence drained, Mixed hide had 1.5% better accuracy than Bandos and killed half a second faster; Torva was still about two seconds faster than Mixed hide. ([2024-05-30 2:32](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=152))
- Mixed hide has a 27 ranged bonus (hybrid ranged armour), so Gnomonkey wears it instead of Masori in Tombs of Amascut, saving two inventory slots for extra potions. ([2024-05-30 3:05](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=185))
- Gnomonkey says Mixed hide has very low ranged defence, so he gets hit hard by ranged attacks in Tombs of Amascut, but that defence matters in very few rooms there. ([2024-05-30 11:45](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=705))
- Gnomonkey says the Mixed hide legs need 57 Hunter and the full set needs 72 Hunter; he first thought it also needed 72 Crafting, then learned from chat that you can pay for the crafting, so it is effectively just 72 Hunter. ([2024-05-30 26:36](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=1596))
- Gnomonkey says his calc shows Mixed hide gives better DPS than Bandos down to 300 invocation level, even if you carry a Masori switch for money. ([2024-05-30 28:04](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=1684))
- Gnomonkey describes Mixed hide armour as Hunter armour that costs about 10k and has strength bonus and stab bonus, both of which he says are very good. ([2024-06-01 26:39](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1599))
- Gnomonkey says Mixed hide loses one max hit behind Bandos, but beats Bandos with a fang against something with very high stab defence. ([2024-06-01 27:39](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1659))
- In the budget run Gnomonkey had seven and a half restores, double what he normally takes. ([2024-06-01 37:54](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=2274))
- Gnomonkey finished the Mixed hide budget run with two restores left over. ([2024-06-01 42:53](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=2573))
- Mixed hide is one max hit lower than Bandos at Nex but its stab bonus gives better DPS than Bandos there. ([2024-06-15 1:27](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=87))

## Tips

- The downside of Mixed hide is awkward switching: with two pieces of armour used on both sides, Gnomonkey says he has to swap back into the ranged setup first to keep his inventory set up properly. ([2024-05-30 6:10](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=370))
- Mixed hide takes more damage from Akkha's hits, but Gnomonkey says this is fine because he butterflies Akkha and takes almost no damage. ([2024-05-30 17:30](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=1050))
- Gnomonkey says he calculated Eclipse moon and it is not a max hit, so he wants Mixed hide for its stab bonus at Nex. ([2024-06-15 5:36](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=336))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey describes mixed hide body as effectively Bandos but with stab bonus and ranged bonus, and says almost every budget setup now includes it. ([2024-05-18 8:35](https://youtu.be/WvmEY9v6AnI?t=515))
- Gnomonkey describes himself as a big proponent of mixed hide. ([2024-05-18 8:35](https://youtu.be/WvmEY9v6AnI?t=515))
- Gnomonkey says that as long as you are using Osmumten's fang, mixed hide is really good, though its strength bonus is not the same as Bandos. ([2024-05-18 9:05](https://youtu.be/WvmEY9v6AnI?t=545))
- Gnomonkey calls Mixed hide armour the new meta setup for a Tombs of Amascut raid level 500 and says it is overpowered; he says it is essentially better Bandos for use with Osmumten's fang and also gives ranged stats. ([2024-05-30 0:00](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U))
- Gnomonkey says that across almost every boss, Mixed hide with the fang beats Bandos with the fang, including at Vorkath, and that anyone using Bandos with a fang should switch to Mixed hide; he notes only rare cases where Bandos is slightly better. ([2024-05-30 3:05](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=185))
- Gnomonkey calls Mixed hide the stronger option over Bandos and over Torva for getting a completion rather than for speed GP, and says it slots in DPS between Bandos and Torva. ([2024-05-30 26:36](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=1596))
- Gnomonkey says Mixed hide is not good without Osmumten's fang because it is stab armour with negative bonuses otherwise, and he would not use it below 300 invocations or with the Bow of Faerdhinen. ([2024-05-30 28:04](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=1684))
- Gnomonkey says you should just wear Mixed hide instead of Bandos when using a fang, calling Bandos effectively dead content for fang users. ([2024-05-30 28:38](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=1718))
- Gnomonkey says Mixed hide is best in slot below Masori for a budget setup and better than Karil's in every way, describing it as black dragonhide with strength bonus and stab bonus. ([2024-06-01 26:39](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1599))
- Gnomonkey says Mixed hide might genuinely be better than Masori for a first 500 because you get so many restores. ([2024-06-01 33:52](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=2032))
- Gnomonkey says he thinks Mixed hide is the new meta for first 500s, better than Masori, and notes it is the only armour set that matches the Quiver. ([2024-06-01 34:26](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=2066))
- Gnomonkey says Mixed hide has stab, strength and ranged bonus and lets you carry far more Sanfew serums; he says Torva helps face-tank Kephri's hatchlings and zero prayer, but Mixed hide lets him take about six more Sanfew. ([2024-06-01 35:00](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=2100))
- After first thinking the lower defence of Mixed hide made Akkha too dangerous, Gnomonkey concluded the Mixed hide setup made the 500 feel like a 150. ([2024-06-01 36:54](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=2214))
- Gnomonkey says Mixed hide is the meta for sub-75 tier gear in Tombs of Amascut. ([2024-06-01 38:21](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=2301))
- Gnomonkey says mixed hide is for Twisted bow setups and Moons gear is for Bofa setups; if you have a Twisted bow, mixed hide is dramatically better than Bandos and better than a Blue Moon hybrid. ([2024-06-27 0:00](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs))
- Gnomonkey says mixed hide is directly better than Bandos, but one is better off hybriding with Blue Moon in some cases because Blue Moon has strength bonus. ([2024-06-27 9:34](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=574))
- Gnomonkey acknowledges you lose a few max hits on Core (Kephri-room) with mixed hide but says it is still worth it. ([2024-06-27 10:12](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=612))

## Events

- In the video description Gnomonkey says Mixed hide is a new item that replaces the Karil's plus Bandos or melee setups from his earlier guides, letting you stack supplies while doing outrageous damage compared with even Bandos. ([2024-06-15 0:00](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU))
