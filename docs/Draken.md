## Requirements

* You will likely have at least 8 brews by the final phase and should be ready to spam them.[^2YTqbYFyliU-400]

## Methods

* At the start of the Draken fight he throws rapid spear jabs and the arrows telegraph the direction to dodge; you dodge in the opposite direction of the one forecast (for example left, right, left).[^2YTqbYFyliU-66]
* When Draken does punches ending in a giant burst of energy into himself, you click on him to move forward through the big ring AoE instead of dodging backwards.[^2YTqbYFyliU-112]
* Draken's special stab animation (rearing back) is an AoE in front of and behind him, so you dodge to the side; he always throws a jab afterwards, and Gnomonkey thinks later in the fight he can do two.[^2YTqbYFyliU-142]
* Draken's second special AoE curls the spear behind him in a sweeping arc around him, with the area directly behind him safe, so you run around behind him to dodge it.[^2YTqbYFyliU-160]
* After the portal phase Draken pops back into the arena with four hit splats on him as he drains his own blood to fire at you; you must immediately put on Protect from Magic, as this is basically the only one-shot mechanic in the fight.[^2YTqbYFyliU-222]
* If you dodge the series of punches after the blood-drain attack perfectly (left, right, left), Draken takes a massive hit splat (155 in the footage) and the portals disappear immediately; if you mess up a punch you do not get the splat and must do another set of waves.[^2YTqbYFyliU-252]
* Later in the fight Draken occasionally throws in a mage attack, visible as blood behind him; you have about two or three ticks to react and switch on Protect from Magic to block it. Gnomonkey believes it heals Draken if it hits you.[^2YTqbYFyliU-283]
* In the lightning phase Draken does a sweep with a red flash then a dash towards you; since he always sweeps in a 90-degree arc out of the way, you can dodge by clicking in the direction you immediately see him on your screen.[^2YTqbYFyliU-311]
* Below 10% health, which Gnomonkey says is basically nothing, you can pretty much face-tank the whole phase and just spam brews while Draken throws very fast rapid jabs.[^2YTqbYFyliU-372]

## Stats and numbers

* Gnomonkey thinks Draken only starts using his two special AoE attacks below about 80% health.[^2YTqbYFyliU-168]
* Below roughly 70% health, Draken dashes away and spawns blood portals that send out waves.[^2YTqbYFyliU-168]

## Tips

* Draken flashes red as the cue to start clicking through the dodges rapidly for his basic punch attack.[^2YTqbYFyliU-84]
* He died the first time to Draken's blood-drain magic attack because he did not pray mage in time.[^2YTqbYFyliU-252]
* In the lightning phase you can get two perfect punish sets (a smaller splat, 80 in the footage) because Draken does the sweep attack twice, so a perfect run finishes the fight faster.[^2YTqbYFyliU-343]
* Draken does not care if blood is in your way and will make you dash right into it; the blood takes your prayer off and does about 20 damage per game tick, so rotate around the boss between attacks to avoid being forced into blood.[^2YTqbYFyliU-433]
* An alternative dodge Gnomonkey credits to MMORPG: if you panic run backwards you are out of the AoE, Draken only steps forward one tile, and you can dodge the next sweep if it goes in the correct direction.[^2YTqbYFyliU-521]
* For the blood wave attack and Draken's swoop dashes, dodge in straight cardinal lines only, with no diagonal steps, because diagonal movement may stop him from dashing.[^2YTqbYFyliU-579]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey considers Loerniel Draken, the final boss of Blood Moon Rises, the hardest quest boss in the entire game, and says many people are struggling with him.[^2YTqbYFyliU-0]

## Events

* After about 800 health has been chunked from Draken, he sweeps away and lightning strikes start spawning blood pools on the ground.[^2YTqbYFyliU-302]
* You cannot refight Draken after stepping out of the arena.[^2YTqbYFyliU-550]
