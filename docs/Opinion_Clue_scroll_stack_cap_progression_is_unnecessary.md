# Opinion: Clue scroll stack cap progression is unnecessary

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 1 video (2025-04-08). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

|---|---|
| **Type** | opinion |
| **Videos** | 1 (first 2025-04-08, latest 2025-04-08) |

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says players need to complete 75 master clues to reach the full five master clue capacity under the proposed table. ([2025-04-08 4:58](https://youtu.be/WQ9Zg4fQRJo?t=298))
- Gnomonkey says he has 59 master clues completed on his main, and that reaching 75 for the second cap takes a very large number of hours. ([2025-04-08 5:54](https://youtu.be/WQ9Zg4fQRJo?t=354))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the stingy base cap of two when players could previously hold around a hundred clues means the change is a massive nerf, and that most players would not get past three clues for a long time. ([2025-04-08 4:30](https://youtu.be/WQ9Zg4fQRJo?t=270))
- Gnomonkey questions the point of the capacity progression, wondering whether it exists to dissuade bots, and says a complicated table behind an RNG mimic kill is ridiculous because players would have to look up the wiki to know how close they are. ([2025-04-08 4:58](https://youtu.be/WQ9Zg4fQRJo?t=298))
- Gnomonkey says he is glad Jagex did not lock the stack cap behind combat achievements as was floated on Reddit, because combat achievements should not reward unrelated things. ([2025-04-08 5:27](https://youtu.be/WQ9Zg4fQRJo?t=327))
- Gnomonkey wants Jagex to scrap the progression entirely, so that completing X Marks the Spot allows holding the full number of scroll boxes. ([2025-04-08 6:24](https://youtu.be/WQ9Zg4fQRJo?t=384))
- Gnomonkey says he does not understand why five is a firm cap and why it could not be 10 or 15, and wishes Jagex would explain the reasoning, possibly botting. ([2025-04-08 8:28](https://youtu.be/WQ9Zg4fQRJo?t=508))
- Gnomonkey says the per-tier chart is obnoxious and difficult to follow because players must track how many clues of each tier they completed to know whether they can hold more. ([2025-04-08 17:10](https://youtu.be/WQ9Zg4fQRJo?t=1030))
