# Corrupted Hunllef

## Methods

- In the Corrupted Hunllef fight Gnomonkey starts by clicking protect from ranged first, because ranged is always the first attack the boss uses. ([2020-04-14 0:00](https://youtu.be/nOgO8mPz0SI))
- Gnomonkey counts the Corrupted Hunllef's attacks out loud throughout the fight so that he knows when its attack style is about to change. ([2020-04-14 0:00](https://youtu.be/nOgO8mPz0SI))
- Gnomonkey counts each attack the Hunllef throws, tracks which prayer it switches to, and swaps weapons accordingly. ([2020-04-14 12:12](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=732))
- When the Corrupted Hunllef is throwing ranged attacks with no tornadoes on the field, Gnomonkey uses that time to drink prayer potions between attacks. ([2020-04-14 12:12](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=732))
- Corrupted Hunllef starts with a ranged attack and switches attack style every four attacks (with ranged first then mage); at the same time every six attacks you do changes his prayer to the style of the weapon you used, so you use the style he is not praying against. ([2024-11-02 11:23](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=683))
- Hunllef does an animation where he jumps up and stomps down, which means he is changing attack styles. ([2024-11-02 11:58](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=718))
- Hunllef spawns tornadoes that follow you and hurt you every tick you stand on them: two at the start, then three, then four as his health gets lower. ([2024-11-02 11:58](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=718))
- Hunllef lights up a chunk of tiles pale red that turns yellow, and standing on yellow tiles hurts you. ([2024-11-02 12:26](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=746))
- When Hunllef mages you there is a chance of a pink attack that turns your protection prayer off, so you must turn it back on; if you stand under him he stomps you for around 800 damage but you can tick eat it. ([2024-11-02 12:50](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=770))

## Stats and numbers

- The player needs to swap weapons roughly every five hits against the Corrupted Hunllef, matching the boss's switch between magic and ranged attacks. ([2020-04-14 0:26](https://youtu.be/nOgO8mPz0SI?t=26))
- The Corrupted Hunllef usually performs its tornado special attack at about 10 attacks. ([2020-04-14 0:26](https://youtu.be/nOgO8mPz0SI?t=26))
- The Corrupted Hunllef sends out three tornadoes once it is under 500 health. ([2020-04-14 1:59](https://youtu.be/nOgO8mPz0SI?t=119))
- The Corrupted Hunllef sends out four tornadoes in the last part of the fight, which Gnomonkey describes as very hectic. ([2020-04-14 2:52](https://youtu.be/nOgO8mPz0SI?t=172))
- Corrupted Hunllef kills often take a bit over four minutes, and sometimes about three minutes with luck. ([2020-04-14 3:53](https://youtu.be/nOgO8mPz0SI?t=233))
- The Corrupted Hunllef uses two main attack styles, ranged and magic, which each hit a maximum of 13 through prayer and which it swaps every four attacks. ([2020-04-14 9:30](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=570))
- If the player prays the wrong style the Corrupted Hunllef's attacks can max at 58. ([2020-04-14 9:30](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=570))
- The Corrupted Hunllef uses protection prayers, which it swaps roughly every five to six times the player attacks with the correct style. ([2020-04-14 9:30](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=570))
- In the Corrupted Hunllef room the floor randomly lights red then a dull yellow; standing on tiles when they turn yellow deals 15 to 25 damage each game tick. ([2020-04-14 9:50](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=590))
- The Corrupted Hunllef's ranged attacks are grey spit blobs and its magic attacks are bright red bursts of energy. ([2020-04-14 10:16](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=616))
- The Corrupted Hunllef's special attacks count towards the four attacks before it switches style. ([2020-04-14 10:16](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=616))
- While using magic the Corrupted Hunllef can throw a bright pink burst of energy that instantly disables the player's prayers, usually once per volley of four magic attacks, which makes ranged attacks much safer to handle. ([2020-04-14 10:16](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=616))
- After about eight to fourteen attacks the Corrupted Hunllef rears up and slams the ground, sending out slowly advancing tornadoes that deal 15 to 25 each tick and can stack. ([2020-04-14 10:43](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=643))
- The Corrupted Hunllef sends two tornadoes from 1000 to 500 health, three from 500 to 250, and four from 250 until death. ([2020-04-14 10:43](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=643))
- The Corrupted Hunllef has a melee attack that only happens if the player stands underneath it for too long, with the same 58 maximum as its other styles. ([2020-04-14 11:13](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=673))
- The Corrupted Hunllef's melee attack is the only attack that does not count towards its style change, so the player should pause their attack count for one attack if hit by it. ([2020-04-14 11:42](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=702))
- Gnomonkey says Corrupted Hunllef has a thousand HP. ([2024-11-02 0:56](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=56))
- Corrupted Hunllef starts by attacking with ranged and swaps to mage after several attacks; turn on protect from range when you enter, since ranged is his first attack. ([2026-07-22 13:02](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=782))
- While Corrupted Hunllef is using mage, he can send a dagger-shaped attack that turns your protection prayer off, so you must turn it back on; this can only happen during his mage attacks. ([2026-07-22 13:32](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=812))
- Floor tiles that light up under Corrupted Hunllef damage you, and tornadoes deal that damage every game tick if they catch you; armor reduces the boss damage, the floor damage and the tornado damage. ([2026-07-22 13:58](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=838))
- Every sixth attack you land on Corrupted Hunllef changes his overhead prayer, which is why you need to swap to a different weapon (two tier three weapons). ([2026-07-22 14:26](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=866))
- At 66% and again at 33% health Corrupted Hunllef adds an extra tornado, so there are four tornadoes at the end of the fight, and floor patterns activate faster as the fight goes on. ([2026-07-22 15:28](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=928))
- If you stand under Corrupted Hunllef when he attacks he will melee you; Gnomonkey says your default reaction on a mistake should be to eat a piece of food before the hit lands. ([2026-07-22 19:27](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=1167))
- Gnomonkey says about three minutes is a pretty average Corrupted Hunllef kill with two tier three weapons. ([2026-07-22 27:59](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=1679))

## Tips

- While the Corrupted Hunllef is on only two tornadoes it is easy to attack it while the tornadoes are out. ([2020-04-14 0:54](https://youtu.be/nOgO8mPz0SI?t=54))
- Gnomonkey drinks prayer doses while the Corrupted Hunllef is using ranged attacks, since that is an easy time to do it. ([2020-04-14 0:54](https://youtu.be/nOgO8mPz0SI?t=54))
- Almost every time the Corrupted Hunllef uses magic it will also use the prayer-disabling attack, so the player should swap to ranged afterwards. ([2020-04-14 0:54](https://youtu.be/nOgO8mPz0SI?t=54))
- The floor in the Corrupted Hunllef room can pinch the player, so Gnomonkey advises keeping distance from the boss so he is not stuck under it. ([2020-04-14 1:28](https://youtu.be/nOgO8mPz0SI?t=88))
- From three tornadoes onwards Gnomonkey focuses mainly on healing, since the tornadoes become much more dangerous. ([2020-04-14 1:59](https://youtu.be/nOgO8mPz0SI?t=119))
- Tornadoes in the Corrupted Hunllef fight are easy to dodge as long as the player keeps running; standing still will get the player killed. ([2020-04-14 1:59](https://youtu.be/nOgO8mPz0SI?t=119))
- Gnomonkey keeps his cursor over the next thing he expects to do, such as the next prayer, or the weapon if he expects to swap weapons first. ([2020-04-14 2:25](https://youtu.be/nOgO8mPz0SI?t=145))
- In the four-tornado phase the Corrupted Hunllef can also melee the player, and Gnomonkey mainly focuses on healing. ([2020-04-14 2:52](https://youtu.be/nOgO8mPz0SI?t=172))
- Gnomonkey eats an extra fish for 18 health instead of 20 to make sure he is at full health in the last part of the fight, which he calls very dangerous. ([2020-04-14 3:21](https://youtu.be/nOgO8mPz0SI?t=201))
- Gnomonkey warns not to assume hits will land at the end of the Corrupted Hunllef fight, since the player's weapons can hit zeros five or even more times in a row. ([2020-04-14 3:53](https://youtu.be/nOgO8mPz0SI?t=233))
- Four tornadoes stacked on the player will instantly kill them, but tornadoes can be run over without damage, which Gnomonkey compares to the traps in the Rogues' Den where the game thinks you are moving two tiles at a time. ([2020-04-14 11:13](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=673))
- Gnomonkey finds quietly counting each Corrupted Hunllef attack out loud easier than counting in his head, and suggests trying it if struggling. ([2020-04-14 12:44](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=764))
- Gnomonkey says Corrupted Hunllef will obliterate you without armour, and that killing him without armour is a combat achievement. ([2024-11-02 2:32](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=152))
- Gnomonkey says to drag Hunllef away from the edge because the yellow tiles near the edge give you little room to work with. ([2024-11-02 12:57](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=777))
- Gnomonkey's rule of thumb for learning: only eat food when tornadoes are active, because your APM is highest then and you cannot attack anyway, so you spam eat without losing game ticks. ([2024-11-02 13:28](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=808))
- Gnomonkey says you can run over yellow tiles and over tornadoes if you time it right. ([2024-11-02 15:52](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=952))
- Gnomonkey says keeping your health low during the fight lets you spam food when the tornadoes come out, and that high health makes it easy to lose ticks; the biggest thing people mess up is running around instead of damaging the boss. ([2024-11-02 22:50](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=1370))
- Gnomonkey says that if you play perfectly around eight fish is all you ever need, including redempting off hits and tick eating attacks. ([2024-11-02 26:53](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=1613))
- Gnomonkey says to swap weapons while you eat fish, and to look for Hunllef's animation to swap prayer. ([2024-11-02 27:26](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=1646))
- Gnomonkey says as you improve you should bring less food each time and track how many fish you end kills with, rather than a full inventory every time. ([2024-11-02 28:27](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=1707))
- Gnomonkey says you can tick eat Hunllef's hits by eating as the attack comes out from far away, since the hit can only hit your remaining health, and you can also redempt off hits since his max hit is 13. ([2024-11-02 32:49](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=1969))
- Gnomonkey's general Corrupted Hunllef strategy is to not eat during the fight except when he spawns tornadoes, then spam food while running from the tornadoes. ([2026-07-22 15:59](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=959))
- Running in straight lines helps dodge the Corrupted Hunllef tornadoes because they tend to move diagonally towards you only on their last step. ([2026-07-22 21:28](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=1288))
- To dodge Hunllef's tornadoes in the Gauntlet, move in straight lines, because they travel straight before moving diagonally towards you on the last step. ([2026-07-23 8:10](https://youtu.be/JmglE_R2gGs?t=490))
- In the Corrupted Gauntlet you can step directly onto tornadoes when they are next to you, similar to Hydra's lightning skip, then click one tile out and run in a straight line away from them. ([2026-07-23 16:55](https://youtu.be/JmglE_R2gGs?t=1015))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey describes Corrupted Hunllef as one of the harder boss fights players meet early on an account and says it was probably the hardest boss he did early in his own RuneScape progress. ([2026-07-22 14:54](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=894))
