**Daeyalt Essence Mine** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2020-06-09. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* To mine Daeyalt essence you must have completed the Sins of the Father quest.[^5sBQOXz4VBE-31]

## Methods

* To reach the mine, teleport to Theatre of Blood with the Drakan's medallion and talk to the Vyrewatch by the bank, who offers to send you to the mines. Talk to a Vyre in the mines while wearing the Vyre noble outfit to be released immediately, then crawl through the northern wall and walk down the stairs just inside Darkmire.[^5sBQOXz4VBE-90]
* There are three Daeyalt essence mining spots in the mine, and one at a time lights up to show it is mineable, similar to the essence blocks in Zeah runecrafting.[^5sBQOXz4VBE-90]
* Daeyalt shards are handed in to an NPC in the corner of the essence mine, who sends all of the shards to your bank as essence.[^5sBQOXz4VBE-151]

## Stats and numbers

* Daeyalt essence used for runecrafting provides 50% more XP than normal essence.[^5sBQOXz4VBE-0]
* ZMI gives 170% more XP per essence, and this stacks multiplicatively with Daeyalt essence's 50% for 255% more XP per essence, which Gnomonkey describes as about an 85% increase from Daeyalt essence at ZMI rather than the regular 50%.[^5sBQOXz4VBE-0]
* More than 4,000 Daeyalt essence an hour can be mined, which in Gnomonkey's testing lasted about an hour and a half of ZMI.[^5sBQOXz4VBE-31]
* A lit Daeyalt essence pillar can typically be mined for about 30 seconds before it jumps to another pillar and you have to move to it.[^5sBQOXz4VBE-118]
* Gnomonkey tested a black pickaxe and found it significantly slower than a dragon pickaxe at mining Daeyalt essence, so a better pickaxe gives better rates; he is unsure whether mining level also matters but thinks it likely does.[^5sBQOXz4VBE-118]
* Each successful mine gives two to three Daeyalt essence, and with a dragon pickaxe Gnomonkey received about 4,000 essence an hour without mining perfectly, so rates could be a bit higher.[^5sBQOXz4VBE-118]
* Compared with pure essence at ZMI, Daeyalt essence gives somewhat less total XP an hour, less total profit from runecrafting, and half the pet chance.[^5sBQOXz4VBE-151]

## Tips

* Daeyalt essence is stackable, so only one free inventory space is needed while mining it.[^5sBQOXz4VBE-60]
* Gnomonkey recommends wearing the Prospector's outfit for the very small mining XP bonus at the Daeyalt essence mine, but says it is by no means necessary.[^5sBQOXz4VBE-60]
* Gems can be received while mining Daeyalt essence, and wearing a charged amulet of glory increases the rate at which they are received, so he also brings a gem bag.[^5sBQOXz4VBE-60]
* This route through Darkmire is also the fastest way to reach the lab for the new Blood Altar spot.[^5sBQOXz4VBE-90]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey considers Daeyalt ZMI an incredibly good option for early Ironman builds, since it can be tough to get essence early on an Ironman.[^5sBQOXz4VBE-31]
* Gnomonkey thinks the downsides of Daeyalt essence are worth accepting to make early runecrafting more tolerable if you are doing ZMI anyway.[^5sBQOXz4VBE-151]

## Events

* Daeyalt essence mining was recently added to the game with the Sins of the Father quest, making ZMI much more similar to Zeah runecrafting.[^5sBQOXz4VBE-0]
