# Daeyalt Essence Mine

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 1 video (2020-06-09). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- To reach the mine, teleport to Theatre of Blood with the Drakan's medallion and talk to the Vyrewatch by the bank, who offers to send you to the mines. Talk to a Vyre in the mines while wearing the Vyre noble outfit to be released immediately, then crawl through the northern wall and walk down the stairs just inside Darkmire. ([2020-06-09 1:30](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=90))
- There are three Daeyalt essence mining spots in the mine, and one at a time lights up to show it is mineable, similar to the essence blocks in Zeah runecrafting. ([2020-06-09 1:30](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=90))
- Daeyalt shards are handed in to an NPC in the corner of the essence mine, who sends all of the shards to your bank as essence. ([2020-06-09 2:31](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=151))

## Requirements

- To mine Daeyalt essence you must have completed the Sins of the Father quest. ([2020-06-09 0:31](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=31))

## Stats and numbers

- Daeyalt essence used for runecrafting provides 50% more XP than normal essence. ([2020-06-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE))
- ZMI gives 170% more XP per essence, and this stacks multiplicatively with Daeyalt essence's 50% for 255% more XP per essence, which Gnomonkey describes as about an 85% increase from Daeyalt essence at ZMI rather than the regular 50%. ([2020-06-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE))
- More than 4,000 Daeyalt essence an hour can be mined, which in Gnomonkey's testing lasted about an hour and a half of ZMI. ([2020-06-09 0:31](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=31))
- A lit Daeyalt essence pillar can typically be mined for about 30 seconds before it jumps to another pillar and you have to move to it. ([2020-06-09 1:58](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=118))
- Gnomonkey tested a black pickaxe and found it significantly slower than a dragon pickaxe at mining Daeyalt essence, so a better pickaxe gives better rates; he is unsure whether mining level also matters but thinks it likely does. ([2020-06-09 1:58](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=118))
- Each successful mine gives two to three Daeyalt essence, and with a dragon pickaxe Gnomonkey received about 4,000 essence an hour without mining perfectly, so rates could be a bit higher. ([2020-06-09 1:58](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=118))
- Compared with pure essence at ZMI, Daeyalt essence gives somewhat less total XP an hour, less total profit from runecrafting, and half the pet chance. ([2020-06-09 2:31](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=151))

## Tips

- Daeyalt essence is stackable, so only one free inventory space is needed while mining it. ([2020-06-09 1:00](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=60))
- Gnomonkey recommends wearing the Prospector's outfit for the very small mining XP bonus at the Daeyalt essence mine, but says it is by no means necessary. ([2020-06-09 1:00](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=60))
- Gems can be received while mining Daeyalt essence, and wearing a charged amulet of glory increases the rate at which they are received, so he also brings a gem bag. ([2020-06-09 1:00](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=60))
- Gnomonkey says this route through Darkmire is also the fastest way to reach the lab for the new Blood Altar spot. ([2020-06-09 1:30](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=90))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey considers Daeyalt ZMI an incredibly good option for early Ironman builds, since it can be tough to get essence early on an Ironman. ([2020-06-09 0:31](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=31))
- Gnomonkey thinks the downsides of Daeyalt essence are worth accepting to make early runecrafting more tolerable if you are doing ZMI anyway. ([2020-06-09 2:31](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=151))

## Events

- Daeyalt essence mining was recently added to the game with the Sins of the Father quest, making ZMI much more similar to Zeah runecrafting. ([2020-06-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE))
