## Stats and numbers

## Tips

- For the sub 65 setup, Gnomonkey uses a Crystal shield and says an Elysian spirit shield (Ely) is better and keeps you a little healthier if owned, but is expensive and its only use is learning Inferno or maybe solo Theatre of Blood. ([2025-07-13 0:59](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=59))
- Gnomonkey wears Virtus top since the run is barrage only, to boost damage slightly, with max range gear for the rest; Pegasian boots or Echo boots are good, and Zaryte vambraces or Barrows gloves are fine downgrades. ([2025-07-13 1:25](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=85))
- A quiver is not required for Inferno speeds (an assembler alone is fine), but Gnomonkey says it is ideal because it lets him wear another cape and put arrows in the quiver slot, which saves prayer equal to about two doses of restore over the run. ([2025-07-13 1:53](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=113))
- Gnomonkey runs a Kodai wand and says a Nightmare staff is the exact same for the run, since any 15% damage staff works with basically no difference. ([2025-07-13 1:53](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=113))
- Gnomonkey's setup is a basic three-way of blowpipe, anguish and Masori with a Twisted bow, and the big difference from a first cape setup is bringing the arsenal: many, many blowpipe bullets. ([2025-07-13 2:21](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=141))
- Gnomonkey brings three Divine bastion potions (about 60 minutes of divine) and pre-pots before entering, for a total of about 65 minutes of divine uptime. ([2025-07-13 2:50](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=170))
- Gnomonkey brings two regular ranging potions so he can use brews and re-pot back up; a regular ranging potion keeps the boost if the divine boost is lost, and one is used on Zuk while spamming brew at Zuk healers. ([2025-07-13 2:50](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=170))
- Gnomonkey pre-pots range potion, prayer regen and stamina; he says he would normally pre-pot divine super combat but that only matters when scything, which he is not doing. ([2025-07-13 26:00](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=1560))
- Gnomonkey says a stamina potion is not needed for the Inferno since the run energy changes: bats do not drain you if you pray properly against them, and you do not need stamina for Zuk. ([2025-07-13 26:30](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=1590))
- Before starting, Gnomonkey sets autocast to blood barrage and sets quick prayers to ranged and Rigour, swapping to mage and Rigour once on the mage waves; one tick flicking helps but is not necessary because prayer regeneration potions are very strong and he brings two. ([2025-07-13 30:23](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=1823))
- At the start of every Inferno run Gnomonkey first drops a pot around the starting area and then casts at a nibbler before killing things; he later picks the restore back up off the ground at the end of a wave. ([2025-07-13 30:51](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=1851))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey calls a fast Inferno speedrun 'a game of chess': the order monsters are killed in, where they are pulled to, and the reaction to each wave all change with your hits, how you get hit and the waves you get. ([2022-06-03 3:04](https://youtu.be/g-y4YBfOfaU?t=184))
- Gnomonkey says solo Theatre of Blood (which he has not done yet and few have) requires near-perfect play: losing ticks in the Nylocas room, wasting tiles at Xarpus or missing a single tick eat over hundreds results in death; it is technically profitable if consistent but worse than duo Theatre of Blood for gp. ([2022-06-03 3:17](https://youtu.be/g-y4YBfOfaU?t=197))
- Gnomonkey considers Lightbearer the best ring choice by far for Inferno speeds because pipe specs save time and give health, so he needs to heal less and goes faster. ([2025-07-13 2:21](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=141))
- Gnomonkey says Bastion potions are important because extra defence levels reduce damage taken, which means less healing and a faster run. ([2025-07-13 3:17](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=197))

## Records
