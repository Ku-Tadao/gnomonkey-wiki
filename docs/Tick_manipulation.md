**Tick manipulation** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2024-07-30 and 2026-07-23. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Tips

* Gnomonkey uses the weapon attack timer metronome so that, as soon as the attack counter ticks from one to zero, he catches a fish; attacking and fishing alternately saves roughly three ticks per fish.[^JmglE_R2gGs-206]
* You can also tick manipulate fishing from range with a staff against a demi-boss, and filling a vial at a fishing spot can be used to three-tick a fish; all his tick manipulation saved around 20 game ticks.[^JmglE_R2gGs-270]
* When you have lost ticks (gone into negative on the attack timer), eating a piece of food lets you reclaim them; Gnomonkey has a short video on how to eat in Old School RuneScape.[^JmglE_R2gGs-515]
* Tick eating: eating as an attack lands when you are very low lowers the boss's max hit to your current health, which saves damage.[^JmglE_R2gGs-573]
* Gnomonkey recaptured three game ticks in a Corrupted Gauntlet run by eating before switching weapon and attacking instantly after the style swap; he says to use fish to recapture lost ticks when you make a mistake.[^JmglE_R2gGs-1052]
* In the Corrupted Gauntlet, the weapon attack timer is very useful for speedrunning, especially for tick manipulating resource gathering.[^JmglE_R2gGs-1470]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey disagrees with the common take that tick manipulation had to be removed to safeguard Granite, and says Jagex has not really been saying that, only trying to flesh out Mining with a new method.[^Fo-0k8WROo4-483]
* Gnomonkey, a former tick manipulation hater, says trying it showed it is engaging and gets you into a flow, and he wishes more people would be open to it.[^Fo-0k8WROo4-639]

## Events

* Saebae's early path in the group hardcore is rushing most of the long-term tick manipulation grinds.[^ETOLP88HtYU-139]
* Chat counts a teammate as having wasted 22 ticks while fighting near the Giant Mole area, with a joke that another player tallies the ticks he loses.[^ETOLP88HtYU-500]
* Gnomonkey tick manipulates shrimp fishing using snow while teammates talk, joking he may be the first person to do so.[^ETOLP88HtYU-885]
