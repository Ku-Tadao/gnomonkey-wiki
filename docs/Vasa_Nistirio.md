**Vasa Nistirio** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 6 videos between 2022-03-19 and 2025-05-01. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* After the Guardian dies he equips mace set to spike for stabbing crystals, equips claws, then his range setup so switches are in the correct order; Vasa requires very fast switches to avoid losing ticks.[^F0qIr7azCwg-597]
* When Vasa teleports him Gnomonkey eats three sweets and drinks a brew if his overload will restore stats, spam clicks for a shot without Rigor, and avoids Vasa's boulders by moving right before his bow can shoot again.[^F0qIr7azCwg-597]
* He does one final shot, switches to claws and double specs the crystal, maces it if not dead and switches back to the Twisted bow; a good Vasa is dead before it reaches the second crystal.[^F0qIr7azCwg-597]
* Vasa teleports you at the start and bombs you, dealing your health at the time of the teleport minus five, so he leaves you with five health regardless; Gnomonkey therefore does not heal before it.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2352]
* Vasa throws 3x3 AoE rocks, so Gnomonkey shoots, then moves four times, then shoots again, and says you must keep moving constantly to be completely safe.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2434]
* When Vasa reaches a crystal, Gnomonkey quickly switches to Void waker on stab, kills the crystal, then switches back to ranged; after the first crystal Vasa never walks back to the crystal just killed, so that is a safe place to spec.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2490]

## Stats and numbers

* With bad bow RNG Vasa can take two minutes or more, ending the run.[^F0qIr7azCwg-629]
* When you enter Vasa's room she teleports you, scans your health and then hits you for that health minus five, so you can heal up after being teleported, and the Phoenix necklace gives health back.[^wxGBi89vE_s-2764]
* Vasa goes to crystals over and over, and after roughly the third crystal she heals off them and goes to the middle to do another bomb, and that repeats.[^wxGBi89vE_s-2855]
* On entering Vasa's room you are teleported and stunned, then she drops a bomb that deals your current health minus five.[^jdvQVwg8niM-1657]
* Vasa usually heals when she reaches the third crystal, so you want to kill her before then.[^jdvQVwg8niM-1750]

## Tips

* The last floor is a killer: every room has a huge amount of RNG that can end the run, and Guardians is the only easy room.[^F0qIr7azCwg-545]
* In Guardians you can summon the skeleton thrall to potentially save a tick by overkilling the second Guardian.[^F0qIr7azCwg-545]
* In his Blood fury setup Gnomonkey tries to take damage if it heals him to 99, since he is not venging Vasa and Vasa takes all your health away anyway, so lower HP lets sweets raise it higher.[^F0qIr7azCwg-565]
* If you have to run through Vasa, pray melee to reduce the trample damage.[^F0qIr7azCwg-629]
* Bofa at Vasa is not the play at all and the rune crossbow is better.[^8JxarzSiiME-889]
* You have one Revite for the Vasa floor and that is all you get; if you did not get a revite at Vanguards you only get one from Muttadiles.[^8JxarzSiiME-969]
* A really good Vasa is anything below a two-crystal; a one-crystal Vasa is great, and killing it before the second crystal makes it about a minute Vasa.[^8JxarzSiiME-1003]
* At Vasa, Gnomonkey says to always be moving when on cooldown; the red tiles he marked are where you get dragged.[^8JxarzSiiME-1003]
* At Vasa, you drag it over and can flick Masori prayer in between for some defence; between casts you can equip Masori to avoid some damage.[^8JxarzSiiME-1086]
* To drink brew whenever your overload is about to refresh, every 15 seconds, so you are full health; brewing on the 15s is really important.[^8JxarzSiiME-1147]
* When Vasa walks towards you she tramples you, and praying melee reduces the damage though you do not have to.[^wxGBi89vE_s-2797]
* To hammer Vasa but not at the end of the fight.[^wxGBi89vE_s-2797]
* Void waker doubles as a defence-ignoring spec weapon and the stab weapon for Vasa's crystals.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2332]
* To have a decent overload timer before entering Vasa, sipping a new one early if needed, since he has to re-overload right after the bomb.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2400]
* Gnomonkey puts Rigor on before the switches, saying getting prayer on matters more than completing all seven switches.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2552]
* After being teleported by Vasa, sip a brew to reduce the bomb damage, then use a hammer, which Gnomonkey says works as a good spec until she heals from the crystals.[^jdvQVwg8niM-1657]
* To put your gear on stab (poke) when attacking Vasa's crystals because they are very tanky to anything that is not stab, and to swap quickly because she heals fully or a lot and bombs again if you are slow.[^jdvQVwg8niM-1716]
* Vasa is on a three-tick cycle, so move whenever you are on weapon cooldown and then attack.[^jdvQVwg8niM-1750]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says Vasa is a DPS check like Vespula but not a bad one, with an eight-way into stab to hit crystals, typically two or three crystals to kill her, and rates it B tier, with less skill than Vanguards but less annoying.[^HkVt4enEesI-3049]
* Gnomonkey notes that not a single regular Chambers of Xeric boss made it into A tier.[^HkVt4enEesI-3109]
* Gnomonkey believes his 'funny walk' in Guardians is superior to the typical two tile running method because it makes flicking prayers easier and needs fewer clicks and no timing, though it does not matter much.[^F0qIr7azCwg-565]
* Gnomonkey says Void Waker is reduced on Vasa crystals, which makes it bad there, though it would be great on Vanguards.[^8JxarzSiiME-1086]
* Gnomonkey calls Vasa the second hardest room in CM and the most switch-heavy room.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2352]

## Trivia

* Vasa instantly health bombs you at the start, so players heal to full and take the hit on purpose to Vengeance the boss, which he calls the only boss where Vengeance is technically better than thralls.[^HkVt4enEesI-3019]
