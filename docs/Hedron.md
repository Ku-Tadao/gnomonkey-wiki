**Hedron** is a person topic that Gnomonkey covers in 4 videos between 2025-03-16 and 2025-10-29. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Hedron does terrible grinds such as crafting grinds off-stream so that Gnomonkey does not have to stream them, which makes the content more interesting.[^0_DI-SbhksU-494]

## Stats and numbers

* In the mistake-filled run, Hedron was at 54 minutes on wave 65, pacing about 60 to 61 minutes.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-1516]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says he will permanently ban anyone in chat who blames his wife for his own mistake on the account, regardless of how many subs they gave.[^0_DI-SbhksU-405]
* Gnomonkey says people claiming his account does not matter because Hedron did some grinds is fine with him, since he does not care about the prestige of doing everything himself.[^0_DI-SbhksU-466]

## Events

* Hedron is Gnomonkey's partner and the one he married in the in-game wedding.[^2S0r-IoEsJ4-0]
* Gnomonkey refers to Hedron as his wife and the two duo the raid together.[^tTn3JmSD4fI-0]
* Hedron gave him all her RNG and viewers suggested sending a raid with his wife was the key to the Twisted bow drop.[^tTn3JmSD4fI-2756]
* Gnomonkey's wife Hedron was going for her sub 65 Inferno and provided footage of beginner mistakes that Gnomonkey uses in the guide.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-229]
* Some people claimed Gnomonkey killed the account for content or that Hedron (his wife) logged in and killed it; Gnomonkey says that is untrue and that Hedron had not touched the account in months, only doing things like glass blowing and mining before then.[^0_DI-SbhksU-372]
* Hedron has been building a backup Hardcore Ironman for Gnomonkey, which is in a good place to start the Corrupted Gauntlet grind.[^0_DI-SbhksU-541]
* Hedron completed the Barrows Gloves requirements off-stream, and got a dragon axe in three kill count.[^0_DI-SbhksU-631]

## Trivia

* The ceremony jokingly describes Hedron and Gnomonkey as having spent years doing Nex together, 'probably the worst boss in the game', and making it a place of culture within the community.[^2S0r-IoEsJ4-386]
* Gnomonkey and Hedron planned to duo Nex after the wedding.[^2S0r-IoEsJ4-574]
