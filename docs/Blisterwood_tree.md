# Blisterwood tree

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 1 video (2020-09-02). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- When chopped the Blisterwood tree never falls down, and it can be chopped a tick faster by spam clicking on it, similar to Ardougne Knights for Thieving. ([2020-09-02 0:00](https://youtu.be/UCnmQbwhjBs))
- To reach the Blisterwood tree, teleport to Darkmeyer with the Drakan's medallion and run to the Arboretum slightly to the northeast; you can enter without the Vyre outfit by tanking the vampyres on the way. ([2020-09-02 1:34](https://youtu.be/UCnmQbwhjBs?t=94))
- Method 1: rebind a key (Gnomonkey used the Windows key) to left click, spam click the tree, and drop logs when the inventory fills; he recommends an infernal axe here since it works on the tree and gives an additional 20k Firemaking XP an hour while needing fewer log drops. ([2020-09-02 1:34](https://youtu.be/UCnmQbwhjBs?t=94))
- Method 2: line your mouse up with part of your inventory and hold the inventory key (F key or a remapped key) so the inventory flickers constantly, turn on Windows sticky keys with shift locked so you can drop logs, and click the tree with the mouse. ([2020-09-02 2:06](https://youtu.be/UCnmQbwhjBs?t=126))
- Pressing another keyboard key while flickering the inventory makes it stop flickering even if the key is held, so you must release and repress it; sticky keys avoid this, and a remapped keyboard key cannot be used to click while flickering, so you must click with a mouse. ([2020-09-02 2:06](https://youtu.be/UCnmQbwhjBs?t=126))

## Stats and numbers

- Spam-click methods on the Blisterwood tree can give up to 80k Woodcutting XP an hour at 99 with a dragon axe. ([2020-09-02 0:28](https://youtu.be/UCnmQbwhjBs?t=28))
- Simply clicking and AFKing the Blisterwood tree gave Gnomonkey around 60k XP an hour, which he says is not worth doing if that is all you want. ([2020-09-02 0:28](https://youtu.be/UCnmQbwhjBs?t=28))

## Tips

- You cannot get bird's nests from the Blisterwood tree, so a rabbit's foot necklace and the Woodcutting cape are completely pointless here; Gnomonkey wears a Drakan's medallion in the neck slot instead. ([2020-09-02 1:34](https://youtu.be/UCnmQbwhjBs?t=94))

## Records

- Gnomonkey reached 78k Woodcutting XP an hour on the Blisterwood tree with the key-rebinding method without chopping perfectly. ([2020-09-02 2:06](https://youtu.be/UCnmQbwhjBs?t=126))
- The inventory-flicker method needed much less user interaction but gave Gnomonkey the same 78k XP an hour, because it misses ticks where you could be clicking the tree to chop faster. ([2020-09-02 3:05](https://youtu.be/UCnmQbwhjBs?t=185))

## Events

- The video description says Gnomonkey put this video back up the minute the Blisterwood nerf was reverted. ([2020-09-02 0:00](https://youtu.be/UCnmQbwhjBs))
- According to a tweet by Mod Ash, the pet can still be obtained from the Blisterwood tree, but Gnomonkey says the rates were not yet known. ([2020-09-02 4:27](https://youtu.be/UCnmQbwhjBs?t=267))

## Trivia

- The Blisterwood tree was added with Sins of the Father in the middle of Darkmeyer; it is used in the quest to craft the blisterwood flail and afterwards has no use other than burning its logs for almost the same XP as willow logs. ([2020-09-02 0:00](https://youtu.be/UCnmQbwhjBs))
- Gnomonkey had heard a USB foot pedal used to click would not stop the inventory flicker but had not tested it himself. ([2020-09-02 2:06](https://youtu.be/UCnmQbwhjBs?t=126))
