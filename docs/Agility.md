# Agility

## Methods

- The main Agility training method is rooftop courses, which have a small chance to spawn a Mark of Grace. ([2020-07-25 0:30](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=30))
- Completing Recruitment Drive, Tourist Trap, The Depths of Despair and The Grand Tree gives 19,700 Agility XP, taking you from level 1 to level 33. ([2020-07-25 1:36](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=96))
- From level 1 to 10 only the Gnome Stronghold Agility course can be used; it takes 14 laps (about 15 minutes) to reach level 10, and it gives no Marks of Grace because it is not a rooftop course. ([2020-07-25 2:32](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=152))
- Gnomonkey would train Agility through Hallowed Sepulchre, which supplies lots of GP and has a high pet chance. ([2022-08-03 4:25](https://youtu.be/6OkBpQdcXRw?t=265))

## Stats and numbers

- The Sepulchre pet chance depends on the floor you exit at, from 1 in 35,000 when leaving floor one to 1 in 2,000 at floor five. ([2020-06-14 45:07](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=2707))
- At 9-minute laps to floor five, the Sepulchre would take almost exactly 300 hours for the giant squirrel pet, compared with an average of almost 500 hours for the Prifddinas agility course, 400 hours for the Ardougne course, and about 150 hours for the Penguin course. ([2020-06-14 45:33](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=2733))
- Every seven Agility levels increases the speed of run energy regeneration by 5 to 10%; you recover energy two times faster at level 50 and three times faster at level 99. ([2020-07-25 0:00](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ))
- The Kandarin Hard Diary raises the Seers' Village rooftop course from about 45k to 55k XP an hour and also gives some Magic experience. ([2020-07-25 4:44](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=284))
- Doing the Seers' Village course from 60 to 75 takes 1,644 laps, or 1,098 laps to level 72. ([2020-07-25 4:44](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=284))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says pretty much every random event is useful on this account; he hopes the captain event gives a gold bar needed later for Monkey Madness, as it is his only way of getting one. ([2019-08-07 0:28](https://youtu.be/xIjOXE9-IS8?t=28))
- Between Agility obstacles you can cast Humidify or High Level Alchemy for Magic XP, or fletch darts or bolts, to gain extra XP. ([2020-07-25 1:57](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=117))
- Gnomonkey advises holding Agility training at level 60 until the Kandarin Hard Diary is done, since it lets you set your Camelot Teleport to land next to the Seers' Village course start. ([2020-07-25 4:44](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=284))
- Gnomonkey suggests giving energy potions a 15 second stamina effect and super energy potions a 30 second stamina effect, since both are currently useless and made in bulk and never touched. ([2026-05-27 4:30](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=270))
- Gnomonkey suggests that a stamina effect on energy potions could be used at Blast Furnace by drinking a dose every lap. ([2026-05-27 4:59](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=299))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey calls the Sepulchre pet chance really good compared to other agility methods, much better than Prifddinas or Ardougne but not as good as the Penguin course. ([2020-06-14 45:33](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=2733))
- Gnomonkey considers Agility one of the few skills that passively benefits you at all times, and the most profitable skill in the game as of 2020. ([2020-07-25 0:00](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ))
- Many people dislike Agility for being click-intensive, but Gnomonkey says the Hallowed Sepulchre changed this for him and made him enjoy the skill. ([2020-07-25 0:00](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ))
- Gnomonkey typically cast High Level Alchemy on rooftop courses because it keeps him focused on Agility instead of watching something else on the side, which would lower his XP rate. ([2020-07-25 1:57](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=117))
- Gnomonkey calls Agility a very useful 99 and easily his favorite skill, so he would likely grind it first. ([2022-08-03 4:25](https://youtu.be/6OkBpQdcXRw?t=265))
- Gnomonkey wants early Agility rooftop XP rates increased slightly, because going from Draynor at 9 to 10k XP per hour to Al Kharid gives only about 1k XP per hour more, which hardly feels worth the trip. ([2026-05-27 4:01](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=241))
- Gnomonkey says the Agility rooftop XP curve has a small increase up to Canifis followed by a massive jump at Falador, and suggests raising the lower courses by around 5k XP per hour. ([2026-05-27 4:01](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=241))
- Gnomonkey says low run energy early on an account feels really bad because you are walking everywhere, and he would like Agility made a little easier in general. ([2026-05-27 4:30](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=270))
- Gnomonkey says that as much as it will pain some players, Agility is here to stay, and the Summer Sweep-Up includes Agility changes he had asked for. ([2026-06-04 4:37](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=277))
- Gnomonkey says the Colossal Worm Agility Course did not seem worth doing in the current game and that the change might make it viable, depending on how AFK it actually is between steps. ([2026-06-04 7:01](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=421))

## Events

- Gnomonkey starts the account with a long period of Agility training, which he jokes every Ironman does. ([2019-07-24 3:46](https://youtu.be/O6INgH6Adw0?t=226))
- Gnomonkey spends about eleven and a half hours training Agility to level 47 at the start of episode 3. ([2019-08-07 0:00](https://youtu.be/xIjOXE9-IS8))
- The Colossal Wyrm (referred to as the worm course) Agility course in Varlamore is made 25 to 30% longer in duration, with XP and termites increased to match, so it gives the same XP and termites per hour with fewer inputs. ([2026-06-04 7:01](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=421))
- A new shortcut was added in Arceuus Library to jump from the top floor down to the bottom floor, which Gnomonkey says could speed up the library Magic or Runecrafting XP method. ([2026-06-04 7:36](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=456))
- New shortcuts were added for clue scroll players: one over to the elite clue spot and one west of Piscatoris, replacing a long run around a mountain. ([2026-06-04 7:36](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=456))
- Several grapple shortcuts become usable without a crossbow or gear if the player is highly overleveled: the River Lum grapple, the Falador wall climb, the Catherby to Taverley rock climb (still 68 Agility), the Catherby water obelisk jump at 72, the Yanille wall climb at 69 (grapple still needed for the diary), and trees south of the Karamja volcano at 78. ([2026-06-04 8:09](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=489))
