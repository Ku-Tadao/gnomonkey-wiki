**Raids 4: The Fractured Archive reward suggestions** is an opinion topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2026-03-05. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey rejects a double ring slot as a permanent unlock because it cannot be resold, and says wearing two rings such as Lightbearer and Ultor is boring and would not change how he approaches the game.[^_XtlpCDD7fI-1647]
* Gnomonkey describes the RuneScape 3 turmoil prayer (used by Nex) as a super Piety that saps enemy stats; he says it would be an extremely strong permanent unlock like Rigor but for melee, which he does not want.[^_XtlpCDD7fI-1677]
* Gnomonkey says he does not want Soul Split because it would let players tick-eat unless made a percent chance, and he points to the problems Blood Fury already added.[^_XtlpCDD7fI-1703]
* Gnomonkey says boots are out because Avernic treads are already mega rare tier for the boot slot, and ammo does not work because it would be a one in 200 drop that rich players use and everyone else sells, like dragon arrows or dragon knives from Hydra.[^_XtlpCDD7fI-1759]
* Gnomonkey says current jewelry all comes from Slayer monsters, so changing that would be odd, but in the wrap-up he says he personally thinks jewelry has a lot of potential as a mega rare space.[^_XtlpCDD7fI-1770]
* Gnomonkey says prayer-bonus or weight-scaling mega rare gear would only turn prayer gear into a stand-in for max strength gear and spike Sunfire gear prices, without changing how content is approached.[^_XtlpCDD7fI-1825]
* Gnomonkey says super-tanky gear does not work because defense is weak in OSRS PvM, since killing faster also stops damage faster, and Old School lacks damage soaking or reflection systems.[^_XtlpCDD7fI-1850]
* Gnomonkey says armor-swap mechanics (swapping every attack, as in Challenge Mode or Chambers of Xeric) are not fun, and that cheesing content with tank gear would not be interesting.[^_XtlpCDD7fI-1939]
* Gnomonkey says offensive shields that reflect damage or have spikes could be interesting, giving less DPS than an Avernic defender but hurting enemies when you take damage.[^_XtlpCDD7fI-1972]
