## Opinions

- Gnomonkey rejects a double ring slot as a permanent unlock because it cannot be resold, and says wearing two rings such as Lightbearer and Ultor is boring and would not change how he approaches the game. ([2026-03-05 27:27](https://youtu.be/_XtlpCDD7fI?t=1647))
- Gnomonkey describes the RuneScape 3 turmoil prayer (used by Nex) as a super Piety that saps enemy stats; he says it would be an extremely strong permanent unlock like Rigor but for melee, which he does not want. ([2026-03-05 27:57](https://youtu.be/_XtlpCDD7fI?t=1677))
- Gnomonkey says he does not want Soul Split because it would let players tick-eat unless made a percent chance, and he points to the problems Blood Fury already added. ([2026-03-05 28:23](https://youtu.be/_XtlpCDD7fI?t=1703))
- Gnomonkey says boots are out because Avernic treads are already mega rare tier for the boot slot, and ammo does not work because it would be a one in 200 drop that rich players use and everyone else sells, like dragon arrows or dragon knives from Hydra. ([2026-03-05 29:19](https://youtu.be/_XtlpCDD7fI?t=1759))
- Gnomonkey says current jewelry all comes from Slayer monsters, so changing that would be odd, but in the wrap-up he says he personally thinks jewelry has a lot of potential as a mega rare space. ([2026-03-05 29:30](https://youtu.be/_XtlpCDD7fI?t=1770))
- Gnomonkey says prayer-bonus or weight-scaling mega rare gear would only turn prayer gear into a stand-in for max strength gear and spike Sunfire gear prices, without changing how content is approached. ([2026-03-05 30:25](https://youtu.be/_XtlpCDD7fI?t=1825))
- Gnomonkey says super-tanky gear does not work because defense is weak in OSRS PvM, since killing faster also stops damage faster, and Old School lacks damage soaking or reflection systems. ([2026-03-05 30:50](https://youtu.be/_XtlpCDD7fI?t=1850))
- Gnomonkey says armor-swap mechanics (swapping every attack, as in Challenge Mode or Chambers of Xeric) are not fun, and that cheesing content with tank gear would not be interesting. ([2026-03-05 32:19](https://youtu.be/_XtlpCDD7fI?t=1939))
- Gnomonkey says offensive shields that reflect damage or have spikes could be interesting, giving less DPS than an Avernic defender but hurting enemies when you take damage. ([2026-03-05 32:52](https://youtu.be/_XtlpCDD7fI?t=1972))
