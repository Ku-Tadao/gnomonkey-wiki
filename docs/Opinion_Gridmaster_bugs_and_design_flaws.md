# Opinion: Gridmaster bugs and design flaws

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 1 video (2025-10-22). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | opinion |
| **Videos** | 1 (first 2025-10-22, latest 2025-10-22) |

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey took about five raids to get a Chambers of Xeric purple in Gridmaster, where there are no guaranteed Challenge Mode purples, and says raids take 15 to 20 minutes even with the Leagues relics, so ten kc would be two and a half hours for one tile. ([2025-10-22 16:51](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=1011))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey thought recall was broken in Gridmaster because it set his special attack energy and prayer to zero, but it was the save memory feature working; he thinks Jagex should make full stats the default and let players save a state instead, and says he wasted about two hours not spamming specials. ([2025-10-22 6:49](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=409))
- Gnomonkey says XP lamps in Gridmaster were inconsistent: only the Dragon Slayer 2 tile gave XP lamps (100k XP), not even Monkey Madness 2, but the combat achievements and achievement diary tiles gave full lamps, which he did not realise and which mattered for the 92 Agility tile. ([2025-10-22 10:20](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=620))
- Gnomonkey says the many teleport relics in Gridmaster created a huge, overwhelming menu of every bank and teleport location, and he suggests letting players teleport by clicking an icon on the world map, such as a bank or fairy ring. ([2025-10-22 11:26](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=686))
- Gnomonkey admits the world-map teleport idea may be impossible due to engine limits but says it would be much better if Jagex could do it before the next Leagues. ([2025-10-22 12:28](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=748))
- Gnomonkey says so many utility relics filled his inventory that he played like an Ultimate Ironman, never banking because every item would be important within 30 seconds, with about 12 slots locked by important relics. ([2025-10-22 13:00](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=780))
- Gnomonkey thinks Jagex should have let players combine relic items, and that things like Sage's Greaves, which give passive Agility XP, should have been a character effect rather than boots. ([2025-10-22 13:27](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=807))
- Gnomonkey says the QA and blogs said players could change their choices later, but the new system linking three skills together for shared XP was permanently locked and had no confirmation prompt, so he chose wrongly. ([2025-10-22 13:27](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=807))
- Gnomonkey says not linking Agility properly cost him about two extra hours on the 92 Agility tile, and he thinks Jagex should add a confirmation and a chance to change links, for example when a row is cleared. ([2025-10-22 14:28](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=868))
- Gnomonkey says some Gridmaster tiles take ten times longer than others, such as the 92 Agility tile and obtaining Blorva, which requires getting orbs and then successfully clearing the boss. ([2025-10-22 14:54](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=894))
- Gnomonkey says the Whisperer in Gridmaster was genuinely hard because the sanity system still kills you, and he died several times. ([2025-10-22 14:54](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=894))
- Gnomonkey argues Jagex should have avoided RNG drop tiles because it encouraged racing with rewards for rank one, two and three, or else fully lean into a bingo style without encouraging competition. ([2025-10-22 15:54](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=954))
- Gnomonkey thinks Jagex should never add rewards for first clear in this kind of event, because it adds frustration from bugs and bad luck. ([2025-10-22 17:22](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=1042))
- Gnomonkey says he did an Inferno for no reason and got no XP, having made an earlier bad choice that stopped him training Slayer quickly, and felt he should have been able to skill his way around the mistake. ([2025-10-22 17:22](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=1042))
- Gnomonkey says leaving out competition would make Gridmaster more fun, since winning the top three, who get to design a relic, mostly came down to routing efficiently and then getting lucky at Huey, Yama and Chambers. ([2025-10-22 17:50](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=1070))
- Gnomonkey says the unique-drop tiles in Gridmaster were inconsistent: an Araxxor head drop did not count but a Vorkath head did, and a Yama dossier, a Radiant contract purifying sigil, and a Desert Treasure 2 kit did not count. ([2025-10-22 18:18](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=1098))
- Gnomonkey says getting a single kit at Tombs of Amascut, such as a gem or thread, instantly counted as a mega rare tile, while the other two raids needed much longer grinds, and he thinks all the raids should have been equally consistent. ([2025-10-22 19:19](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=1159))
- Gnomonkey says the grid design means that eventually you are down to three or four tiles and the rewards are useless, noting he did not see anyone use the Sunlight Spear, which required a Yama tile and an Araxxor tile (92 Slayer and a Yama rare). ([2025-10-22 19:48](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=1188))
- Gnomonkey says the Kopesh, probably the best item in Gridmaster, is a reward for the easy 99 Construction tile when it would make more sense as a hard row reward, and that rewards behind hard tiles lock many items out of ever being used. ([2025-10-22 20:19](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=1219))
- Gnomonkey says once the grid is done you are done, with very little to do and no Echo bosses or hard-mode raid variants (which are 50% harder) to test your power on. ([2025-10-22 20:49](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=1249))
- Gnomonkey finds the Gridmaster rewards unattractive and says there was nothing to look forward to cosmetically, though he concedes Gridmaster is a hard theme to make look cool. ([2025-10-22 21:45](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=1305))

## Records

- Gnomonkey needed about 40 kill count at Yama to get the required drop in Gridmaster, while he says some players needed over 100 kc at Yama and at Huey. ([2025-10-22 15:28](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=928))

## Events

- Gnomonkey reports that with the Slayer Master relic in Gridmaster, which puts you on task everywhere, killing Zuk gave a 20k Slayer XP drop instead of the expected 1.5 million, because the game put him on a hard Slayer task rather than a Zuk task, wasting half an hour. ([2025-10-22 7:46](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=466))
- Gnomonkey reports the gemstone crab boss tile was bugged at first so it could not be unlocked, but it was patched quickly, within about an hour. ([2025-10-22 8:20](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=500))
- Gnomonkey reports that Gridmaster farming timers were not boosted at first, which hurt anyone rushing the fruit tree early. ([2025-10-22 8:48](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=528))
- Gnomonkey reports that building marble fences for the Golden God tile pulled up the house loading screen every time, flashing every three ticks, which he found super frustrating. ([2025-10-22 8:48](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=528))
- In Gridmaster Yama only dropped dossiers, with no other drops for flavour, so Gnomonkey, burnt out at the end of his stream, sat through kill after kill of useless drops until the tile drop arrived. ([2025-10-22 16:23](https://youtu.be/i76LSxy7Hac?t=983))
