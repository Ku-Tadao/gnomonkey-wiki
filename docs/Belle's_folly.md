# Belle's folly

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2025-11-19 to 2026-06-04). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey describes the new rapier, Belle's folly, as a very slow, heavy-hitting stab weapon, basically the Zombie axe but for stab. ([2025-11-19 8:22](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=502))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey suggests Irons planning to do Moons and then Tombs of Amascut can use the rapier to skip getting a Hasta; he says it is almost as good as a Hasta in Tombs of Amascut and very accurate. ([2025-11-19 8:23](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=503))
- On his hardcore account Gnomonkey skipped getting Bell's Folly by grinding Tormented Demons for a Scorching bow and killing K'ril early for a Zamorakian hasta, which he says is way faster because of K'ril's fairly fast drop rate. ([2026-05-11 6:35](https://youtu.be/7WIZw1stPas?t=395))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey considers the rapier an excellent option for Tombs of Amascut and very good for killing Eclipse Moon, a really good mid-level stab weapon. ([2025-11-19 8:23](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=503))
- Gnomonkey says the Black Kirus (as transcribed) exists and is better than the rapier but takes a while to obtain, so get the rapier first and swap to the Black Kirus later, or to a Fang if you get one. ([2025-11-19 8:53](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=533))
- Gnomonkey says Bell's Folly is already fairly powerful and is just annoying to get hold of, and wonders whether a buff would make it stronger than a rapier. ([2026-05-11 4:01](https://youtu.be/7WIZw1stPas?t=241))
- Gnomonkey says the Bell's Folly is now worth considering in ironman progression and is a decent option instead of getting a scobo from Tormented Demons and using a Hosta to kill Zamorak, and is quite a bit better than Hosta against Eclipse because it is a slow weapon. ([2026-06-04 37:39](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2259))

## Events

- Bell's Folly (referred to by the blog as the drop from the Eclipse-style boss) drop rate is increased from 1 in 256 to 1 in 75, tripling the rate. ([2026-06-04 37:39](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2259))
