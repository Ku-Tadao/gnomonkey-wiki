## Requirements

- The Graveyard quest requires completing Olaf's quest, Fremennik Exiles and Pandemonium, plus 65 Sailing, 65 Agility, 56 Woodcutting and 54 Fletching, according to Gnomonkey (the requirements were not listed in the blog). ([2026-09-14 0:32](https://youtu.be/3qB8AKXsvWs?t=32))

## Stats and numbers

- Jagex says the AFK sections of the Graveyard are targeted to take about the same time each, in the region of 20 to 30 seconds. ([2026-09-14 3:10](https://youtu.be/3qB8AKXsvWs?t=190))
- At the top end of the Graveyard, Jagex expects 65,000 to 75,000 Agility XP per hour, around the Ardougne rooftop course and just below Hallowed Sepulchre floor four, and more than 30 marks of grace per hour. ([2026-09-14 4:41](https://youtu.be/3qB8AKXsvWs?t=281))
- Gnomonkey says the best current marks of grace source is the Ardougne rooftop at 23 marks of grace per hour, so the Graveyard's 30 plus per hour at a much lower level without a diary requirement would be the best marks source in the game. ([2026-09-14 4:41](https://youtu.be/3qB8AKXsvWs?t=281))
- On the slower, more AFK Graveyard route, Jagex expects around 55,000 to 60,000 Agility XP per hour and about 14 to 15 marks of grace per hour, which keeps it ahead of Seers' Village while leaving higher level methods relevant. ([2026-09-14 5:15](https://youtu.be/3qB8AKXsvWs?t=315))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey notes the second-best pet option was Hallowed Sepulchre floor five, so the Graveyard would be the best or equal to the Penguin course and the best option for money and marks of grace. ([2026-09-14 5:47](https://youtu.be/3qB8AKXsvWs?t=347))
- Gnomonkey likes the new graceful cosmetic set and thinks it would look great during hunter rumours, but wonders if the chest will slow your rates too much. ([2026-09-14 6:32](https://youtu.be/3qB8AKXsvWs?t=392))
- Gnomonkey believes the Graveyard speeds up Agility training and graceful unlocks so much that it is probably worth doing on a main too, and gives Sailing a worthwhile progression use beyond Wyrmscraig. ([2026-09-14 8:29](https://youtu.be/3qB8AKXsvWs?t=509))

## Events

- Jagex's blog announced a new master-level quest, following Olaf's quest and tied to Sailing, that unlocks a new level 65 to 80 Agility course called the Graveyard, set among worn-down old ghost ships you jump across. ([2026-09-14 0:32](https://youtu.be/3qB8AKXsvWs?t=32))
- Jagex says the Graveyard is a hybrid course aimed at players between level 65 and 80, with traditional rooftop mechanics for about two-thirds of the course and the remaining third offering a choice between an AFK option and a more intense one. ([2026-09-14 1:02](https://youtu.be/3qB8AKXsvWs?t=62))
- Jagex's example for the Graveyard's choice obstacle is a narrow ledge (slower, less XP, lets you do something else) versus Yama-style stepping stones where you click once every tick to pass substantially faster. ([2026-09-14 1:35](https://youtu.be/3qB8AKXsvWs?t=95))
- Jagex says Graveyard stepping stone sections will step up in difficulty, requiring you to identify the right stones while keeping a one-tick rhythm, and take inspiration from Maud Rice's work on Vampyrium, such as an obstacle needing a double or triple click in one tick. ([2026-09-14 2:09](https://youtu.be/3qB8AKXsvWs?t=129))
- Jagex says the Graveyard is intended as a competitive alternative to the Penguin Agility course for hunting the giant squirrel pet, which is currently the most efficient place. ([2026-09-14 5:47](https://youtu.be/3qB8AKXsvWs?t=347))
- The Graveyard adds a new graceful cosmetic set as a rare drop from a picklockable chest just before the final obstacle, which requires 65 Thieving. ([2026-09-14 6:32](https://youtu.be/3qB8AKXsvWs?t=392))
