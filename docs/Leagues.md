**Leagues** is an activity topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2026-01-19 and 2026-03-10. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey definitely plans on giving RS3 Leagues 2 a shot.[^GutGor_Ixx0-1017]
* Gnomonkey says Jagex showed little about how Leagues VI will work gameplay-wise but hinted at slight deviations from past Leagues.[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-59]
* Gnomonkey thinks the Leagues VI rewards might be the strongest he has seen from Leagues.[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-120]
* Gnomonkey says he likes Leagues a lot and has fun with it, but argues that if a temporary game mode is cited as a reason to raise membership then Jagex should not have done it, since it does not affect the long-term health of the game.[^1cRMBXNb4rc-86]
* Gnomonkey says many players were bummed that Leagues was on the roadmap because it is a temporary mode, and that if modes like Leagues or Deadman require more staff and funding that drives price increases, Jagex should hold off on them.[^1cRMBXNb4rc-1074]

## Events

* RuneScape 3 announced a second Leagues, with Jagex saying they know the mistakes made in the first Leagues, which Gnomonkey describes as very slow; they are looking at quest unlocks and similar improvements.[^GutGor_Ixx0-780]
* Leagues VI (Demonic Pacts) is coming in April, with a 666 theme featuring Yama, and players start in Yama's layer.[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-59]

## Trivia

* Leagues VI rewards include an outfit, a banner with flames and Yama, a teleport animation where imps drag you down into hell, Demonic Thrones, Thrall reskins, trident and staff reskins, the Soul Reaper axe (Yama's axe), Oathplate and Slayer helmet skins, a Radiant Slayer helmet skin and a reskinned Demon Butler.[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-86]
