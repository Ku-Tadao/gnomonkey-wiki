## Opinions

- Gnomonkey definitely plans on giving RS3 Leagues 2 a shot. ([2026-01-19 16:57](https://youtu.be/GutGor_Ixx0?t=1017))
- Gnomonkey says Jagex showed little about how Leagues VI will work gameplay-wise but hinted at slight deviations from past Leagues. ([2026-01-25 0:59](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=59))
- Gnomonkey thinks the Leagues VI rewards might be the strongest he has seen from Leagues. ([2026-01-25 2:00](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=120))
- Gnomonkey says he likes Leagues a lot and has fun with it, but argues that if a temporary game mode is cited as a reason to raise membership then Jagex should not have done it, since it does not affect the long-term health of the game. ([2026-03-10 1:26](https://youtu.be/1cRMBXNb4rc?t=86))
- Gnomonkey says many players were bummed that Leagues was on the roadmap because it is a temporary mode, and that if modes like Leagues or Deadman require more staff and funding that drives price increases, Jagex should hold off on them. ([2026-03-10 17:54](https://youtu.be/1cRMBXNb4rc?t=1074))

## Events

- RuneScape 3 announced a second Leagues, with Jagex saying they know the mistakes made in the first Leagues, which Gnomonkey describes as very slow; they are looking at quest unlocks and similar improvements. ([2026-01-19 13:00](https://youtu.be/GutGor_Ixx0?t=780))
- Leagues VI (Demonic Pacts) is coming in April, with a 666 theme featuring Yama, and players start in Yama's layer. ([2026-01-25 0:59](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=59))

## Trivia

- Leagues VI rewards include an outfit, a banner with flames and Yama, a teleport animation where imps drag you down into hell, Demonic Thrones, Thrall reskins, trident and staff reskins, the Soul Reaper axe (Yama's axe), Oathplate and Slayer helmet skins, a Radiant Slayer helmet skin and a reskinned Demon Butler.
