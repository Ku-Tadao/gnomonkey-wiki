[TOC]

## Methods

* Open the Mad Angel fight with protect from melee and Piety, and use your special attacks right away; the dragon mace is okay as a budget spec and a Dragon warhammer would be better for draining her defence.[^dlM9cpU1-Tc-120]
* Mad Angel mage attack: this timed mage attack is guaranteed to be a max hit for you if you click protect from magic as soon as her wings are all the way backwards, and you take zero damage.[^dlM9cpU1-Tc-147]
* Mad Angel sweeping arc attack: run across and attack every time she turns to you; she repeats this about five times.[^dlM9cpU1-Tc-147]
* Mad Angel's last attack: stand on the orb so it bounces off her repeatedly (the third bounce is the last) doing a lot of damage; if you miss it she hits the whole arena with a shockwave that damages you.[^dlM9cpU1-Tc-177]

## Stats and numbers

* The Mad Angel drops granite dust (which upgrades cannonballs), many alchables such as rune full helms and other rune items, and super combats.[^dlM9cpU1-Tc-32]

## Tips

* Base 70 to 75 melee stats are enough to kill the Mad Angel.[^dlM9cpU1-Tc-32]
* Gnomonkey's budget gear for the Mad Angel is a Zombie axe, dragon defender, barrows gloves, berserker ring, mixed hide boots or some melee boots, fire cape, glory and Blood moon; he says armor barely matters and even rune armor would do fine.[^dlM9cpU1-Tc-62]
* A Zombie axe on crush is nice against the Mad Angel because she is quite tanky and crush punches through her defense.[^dlM9cpU1-Tc-62]
* You can one-tick the protect from magic prayer to catch the Mad Angel's mage attack without timing the wing cue, and it also works with an offensive prayer up.[^dlM9cpU1-Tc-177]
* Near the end of the kill the Mad Angel spams the mage attack repeatedly, which Gnomonkey says means the boss dies quickly.[^dlM9cpU1-Tc-206]
* The Mad Angel has a heavy weakness to crush; the Fang is okay because she is not very tanky against stab, but you should ideally use a crush weapon.[^dlM9cpU1-Tc-298]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey rates the Mad Angel as a very low level and easy boss on the tier of the Titans and Scurrius.[^dlM9cpU1-Tc-0]

## Records

* Gnomonkey's example Mad Angel kill with a Zombie axe took 1:43, while a kill with a Granite hammer took 49 seconds.[^dlM9cpU1-Tc-240]

## Events

* The Mad Angel is a new boss on Wyrmscraig, reached in the church there after the new quest Fallen from Grace.[^dlM9cpU1-Tc-0]
