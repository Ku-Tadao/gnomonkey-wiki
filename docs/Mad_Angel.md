## Methods

- Open the Mad Angel fight with protect from melee and Piety, and use your special attacks right away; the dragon mace is okay as a budget spec and a Dragon warhammer would be better for draining her defence. ([2026-07-29 2:00](https://youtu.be/dlM9cpU1-Tc?t=120))
- Mad Angel mage attack: this timed mage attack is guaranteed to be a max hit for you if you click protect from magic as soon as her wings are all the way backwards, and you take zero damage. ([2026-07-29 2:27](https://youtu.be/dlM9cpU1-Tc?t=147))
- Mad Angel sweeping arc attack: run across and attack every time she turns to you; she repeats this about five times. ([2026-07-29 2:27](https://youtu.be/dlM9cpU1-Tc?t=147))
- Mad Angel's last attack: stand on the orb so it bounces off her repeatedly (the third bounce is the last) doing a lot of damage; if you miss it she hits the whole arena with a shockwave that damages you. ([2026-07-29 2:57](https://youtu.be/dlM9cpU1-Tc?t=177))

## Stats and numbers

- The Mad Angel drops granite dust (which upgrades cannonballs), many alchables such as rune full helms and other rune items, and super combats. ([2026-07-29 0:32](https://youtu.be/dlM9cpU1-Tc?t=32))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says base 70 to 75 melee stats are enough to kill the Mad Angel. ([2026-07-29 0:32](https://youtu.be/dlM9cpU1-Tc?t=32))
- Gnomonkey's budget gear for the Mad Angel is a Zombie axe, dragon defender, barrows gloves, berserker ring, mixed hide boots or some melee boots, fire cape, glory and Blood moon; he says armor barely matters and even rune armor would do fine. ([2026-07-29 1:02](https://youtu.be/dlM9cpU1-Tc?t=62))
- A Zombie axe on crush is nice against the Mad Angel because she is quite tanky and crush punches through her defense. ([2026-07-29 1:02](https://youtu.be/dlM9cpU1-Tc?t=62))
- You can one-tick the protect from magic prayer to catch the Mad Angel's mage attack without timing the wing cue, and it also works with an offensive prayer up. ([2026-07-29 2:57](https://youtu.be/dlM9cpU1-Tc?t=177))
- Near the end of the kill the Mad Angel spams the mage attack repeatedly, which Gnomonkey says means the boss dies quickly. ([2026-07-29 3:26](https://youtu.be/dlM9cpU1-Tc?t=206))
- The Mad Angel has a heavy weakness to crush; the Fang is okay because she is not very tanky against stab, but you should ideally use a crush weapon. ([2026-07-29 4:58](https://youtu.be/dlM9cpU1-Tc?t=298))
