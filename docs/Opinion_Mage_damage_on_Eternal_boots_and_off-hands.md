**Opinion: Mage damage on Eternal boots and off-hands** is an opinion topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2024-04-18. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says that on paper adding damage to Eternal boots sounds good, since a dead equipment slot is bad design, but in practice it encourages bad switching problems.[^P5yHDSocecY-57]
* Gnomonkey says once boot switches matter, players are pushed toward nine-way switches, and with damage also added to off-hands like Mage's book, Malediction ward, Ancient wyvern shield and Arcane spirit shield, mid-game players would be encouraged to do up to ten-way switches.[^P5yHDSocecY-57]
* Gnomonkey says nine-way switches are so difficult he put his pegasian boots back in the bank and stopped using them in Coliseum speedruns, even though they were worth it.[^P5yHDSocecY-118]
* Gnomonkey finds it bizarre to encourage nine- and ten-way switches right after adding hybrid gear from the Moons update to get people into PvM.[^P5yHDSocecY-118]
* Gnomonkey says primordial boots being the only useful boots is for a good reason: boot switches become a nightmare, and he rejects the argument that it is just 2% damage and optional.[^P5yHDSocecY-150]
* Gnomonkey says in raids and speedruns every inventory slot has value; in CM speedruns he values each slot down to the second in time saved and drops the item with the least time save as new items come out.[^P5yHDSocecY-191]
* Gnomonkey gives the example that if Eternal boots gain damage, a Tombs of Amascut Voidwaker plus claws switch for Akkha may no longer be worth it, so players would swap in the boots and face nine-way mage switches.[^P5yHDSocecY-191]
* Gnomonkey says Jagex should not put mage damage on Eternal boots or off-hands, because encouraging more switches for mage damage is not the way to make mage usable and large numbers of switches are extremely annoying at a high level.[^P5yHDSocecY-810]
