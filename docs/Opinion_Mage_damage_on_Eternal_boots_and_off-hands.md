# Opinion: Mage damage on Eternal boots and off-hands

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says that on paper adding damage to Eternal boots sounds good, since a dead equipment slot is bad design, but in practice it encourages bad switching problems. ([2024-04-18 0:57](https://youtu.be/P5yHDSocecY?t=57))
- Gnomonkey says once boot switches matter, players are pushed toward nine-way switches, and with damage also added to off-hands like Mage's book, Malediction ward, Ancient wyvern shield and Arcane spirit shield, mid-game players would be encouraged to do up to ten-way switches. ([2024-04-18 0:57](https://youtu.be/P5yHDSocecY?t=57))
- Gnomonkey says nine-way switches are so difficult he put his pegasian boots back in the bank and stopped using them in Coliseum speedruns, even though they were worth it. ([2024-04-18 1:58](https://youtu.be/P5yHDSocecY?t=118))
- Gnomonkey finds it bizarre to encourage nine- and ten-way switches right after adding hybrid gear from the Moons update to get people into PvM. ([2024-04-18 1:58](https://youtu.be/P5yHDSocecY?t=118))
- Gnomonkey says primordial boots being the only useful boots is for a good reason: boot switches become a nightmare, and he rejects the argument that it is just 2% damage and optional. ([2024-04-18 2:30](https://youtu.be/P5yHDSocecY?t=150))
- Gnomonkey says in raids and speedruns every inventory slot has value; in CM speedruns he values each slot down to the second in time saved and drops the item with the least time save as new items come out. ([2024-04-18 3:11](https://youtu.be/P5yHDSocecY?t=191))
- Gnomonkey gives the example that if Eternal boots gain damage, a Tombs of Amascut Voidwaker plus claws switch for Akkha may no longer be worth it, so players would swap in the boots and face nine-way mage switches. ([2024-04-18 3:11](https://youtu.be/P5yHDSocecY?t=191))
- Gnomonkey says Jagex should not put mage damage on Eternal boots or off-hands, because encouraging more switches for mage damage is not the way to make mage usable and large numbers of switches are extremely annoying at a high level. ([2024-04-18 13:30](https://youtu.be/P5yHDSocecY?t=810))
