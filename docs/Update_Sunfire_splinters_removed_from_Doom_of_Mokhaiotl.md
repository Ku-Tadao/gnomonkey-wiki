**Update: Sunfire splinters removed from Doom of Mokhaiotl** is an update topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2025-10-01. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* The Colosseum is now terrible money, roughly 6m per hour, because Sunfire splinter prices have dropped so low.[^ZB3ldcsIivg-56]
* Sunfire splinters were about a 1 in 102 drop from Doom in quantities of 500 to 1500, so players do not get that many from the grind, especially when not claiming loot at each delve.[^ZB3ldcsIivg-147]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey disputes Jagex's claim, pointing to the price chart where splinters were already around 235-242 GP in July 2025 before Doom's release on 23 July 2025, and says the decline began long before Doom.[^ZB3ldcsIivg-27]
* Gnomonkey says he is not Jagex and does not have their data, but the price chart does not look to him like Doom is dropping splinters much faster than they were already falling, so he does not believe Doom is the cause.[^ZB3ldcsIivg-118]
* Gnomonkey finds it mind-boggling that Jagex would remove an item from a drop table that already has many problems when there is an obvious alternative solution, and notes that the OSRS Wiki had already updated for the removal.[^ZB3ldcsIivg-147]
* Gnomonkey hopes Jagex walks back the removal and uses the opportunity to fix Doom's drop table, saying he does not buy that Doom is the cause of splinters dropping in price.[^ZB3ldcsIivg-1023]

## Events

* Jagex's blog on the second round of its UI interface uplift included a line at the end announcing that Sunfire splinters are being removed from Doom of Mokhaiotl's loot table in that day's game update.[^ZB3ldcsIivg-0]
* Jagex's blog said Sunfire splinters fell from about 500 GP each to roughly 140 GP each since Doom's release, and that splinters gained per player at Doom were only about 400 an hour but the sheer number of players meant millions were entering the game passively.[^ZB3ldcsIivg-27]
* Jagex said removing splinters from Doom was meant so that Doom is about chasing its uniques while the Colosseum remains a rewarding and valuable endgame activity.[^ZB3ldcsIivg-27]
