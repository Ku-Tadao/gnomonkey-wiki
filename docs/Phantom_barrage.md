# Phantom barrage

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 6 videos (2022-07-22 to 2026-07-13). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Gnomonkey says the most common phantom is to bring a blob down to a certain pillar position, give it a blood barrage, then manually cast again so it queues up and you cast at the corpse of the monster, healing off it. ([2022-07-22 5:59](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=359))
- Gnomonkey explains phantom: if you are far enough away, your barrage has an invisible travel time, and while it is in the air you can cast at a corpse and get the full heal off it, so with bloblets you heal off three corpses. ([2022-07-22 36:58](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2218))
- A phantom barrage is when you manually cast at a monster eight tiles or further away and cast at its corpse, which gives you the health it would have hit. ([2024-01-17 5:57](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=357))
- Even with the autocast delay removed, Gnomonkey says you still have to manually cast to make a phantom barrage work. ([2024-06-13 10:51](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=651))
- A phantom barrage is manually casting at an NPC after it has died (turned red with the HP tracker); because you cast at the corpse the blood barrage still heals you full health. ([2024-06-13 11:20](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=680))
- Phantom barrage: cast a spell at a far-away target, then manually cast the next spell so it casts at the corpse as it dies, giving full health healing. ([2024-10-03 10:55](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=655))
- A phantom barrage means casting manually at a monster, then casting again at its corpse so the barrage counts as extra hits and heals you for the full damage that cast would have dealt. ([2025-12-07 10:32](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=632))
- Phantom barrage is a game engine quirk: if you cast Blood barrage from eight or more tiles away at a monster as it dies, the barrage still lands on its corpse and heals you, even if your hit is a splash or 1 damage. ([2026-07-13 12:57](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=777))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey demonstrates a phantom taking him from 84 to 98 health, and says phantom is powerful and should be used. ([2022-07-22 40:00](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2400))
- Gnomonkey says phantom requires you to be at least eight tiles away, and that barrage has a 10-tile range, the same as a Twisted bow, so at max range it always works. ([2022-07-22 41:47](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2507))
- Phantoms only work from eight tiles away or further, because the cast travel time has to be long, and you then have to manually cast at the corpse after it dies. ([2024-06-13 11:50](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=710))
- A phantom barrage on a set of bloblets from the good position gives about 40 health back, and a phantom off six nibblers gives about 80. ([2025-12-07 11:00](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=660))
- The minimum range to do a phantom is eight tiles, so stand at the max range of your sceptre. ([2025-12-07 11:56](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=716))
- Gnomonkey says a phantom usually gives 10 to 20 health from the extra cast, though results vary. ([2025-12-07 22:52](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=1372))
- In the demo Gnomonkey healed 8 health from phantoming bloblet corpses and 30 health from a phantom later in the run. ([2026-07-13 13:24](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=804))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey keeps his autocast on blood barrage because most of the time when barraging for health you are AFKing on it, and he manually casts ice barrage for the most part. ([2022-07-22 2:24](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=144))
- Gnomonkey says you have to manually cast to phantom, but it does not matter when you click the spell on the monsters after you cast. ([2022-07-22 40:47](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2447))
- Gnomonkey says you can phantom on any mob, such as bats, rangers or melees, but it is less effective than with bloblets. ([2022-07-22 45:08](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2708))
- Gnomonkey says if you are below 60 health, you should heal on the blobs. ([2022-07-22 50:23](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=3023))
- Gnomonkey also uses phantom barrage on nibblers to confirm he has killed them all, and notes it works on blobs, nibblers and rangers. ([2024-01-17 6:25](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=385))
- If a blob is in a position where you cannot normally phantom it, you can stand at a position and wait a tick so the mage blocks two monsters, letting you run out and phantom off it; he says he almost never sees first-cape players do this. ([2024-01-17 6:53](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=413))
- Gnomonkey says nibblers have the least magic defence of any mob, so phantoming far-away nibblers can give a lot of health too. ([2024-01-17 13:16](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=796))
- Gnomonkey notes rangers have a pretty low magic level, so you can use them to get health back, and that barrage accuracy is checked for each monster, with chinchompas being unique in that it is rolled off the target. ([2024-01-17 42:53](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=2573))
- You can phantom barrage three bloblet targets at once, and Gnomonkey says to treat it as the main way you will heal in Inferno. ([2024-06-13 14:51](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=891))
- A phantom barrage does not work if the mage is not pulled in fully against the pillar, so Gnomonkey says to let it pull in first. ([2024-06-13 35:39](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2139))
- Gnomonkey says you can still phantom barrage on a blob plus ranger stack if the ranger is in the back. ([2024-06-13 52:43](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=3163))
- Gnomonkey says blobs split into three bloblets so you can phantom barrage three corpses at once for a ton of health, making it your primary healing source in the Inferno. ([2024-10-03 10:55](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=655))
- For a phantom barrage you need to be over eight tiles away and the blob must come in close enough to the pillar for the phantom to work. ([2024-10-03 10:55](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=655))
- Gnomonkey says you can phantom nibblers to guarantee you can still cast at the pile, and can also phantom off pets. ([2024-10-03 11:29](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=689))
- Gnomonkey names two phantom spots in the Inferno and says the phantom lets you extend far to get nibblers, tank damage and return behind the pillar to heal back. ([2024-10-03 12:00](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=720))
- Gnomonkey uses the Tizar HP tracker plugin for the health on the pillars and enemies and for tile markers. ([2024-10-03 12:16](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=736))
- Gnomonkey says to do a phantom every single time even if you do not need health, as there is no reason not to. ([2025-12-07 19:27](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=1167))
- Gnomonkey describes a cheeky phantom where, with the blob pulled down on the corner, you step into the pillar and step back down when bloblets appear so only the mage is on you. ([2025-12-07 30:21](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=1821))
- Autocast has no delay but a phantom needs a manual cast at the corpse to go through. ([2025-12-07 52:20](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=3140))
- Gnomonkey takes his shield off for the Phantom barrage so his mage accuracy is higher and recommends getting in phantom position against blobs even when he does not need health. ([2026-07-13 12:57](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=777))
- Gnomonkey says there are two positions you can phantom a blob from, and a third secret spot where you stand on the tile with the blob as far down as it can be, kill it, step back up, and click when the bloblets appear. ([2026-07-13 14:22](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=862))
- Phantom barrage does not work with autocast; you must manually cast Blood barrage at the corpse. ([2026-07-13 22:22](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=1342))
- Gnomonkey says you can also phantom nibblers off spawn when you are safe, and that nobody does this. ([2026-07-13 29:39](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=1779))

## Opinions
