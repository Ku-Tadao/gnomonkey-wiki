**Soulflame horn** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2025-03-01 and 2025-05-14. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* The players must be near each other for the Soulflame horn to work, so Gnomonkey pre-horns right before starting the fight next to the boss.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-210]

## Methods

* Gnomonkey's duo DPS loop with Emberlight is swing, horn, swing, horn, which he calls the best DPS you can do in a spec.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-88]
* The horn lets an Elder maul land on Yama, and one horn basically puts Yama at the defence cap, so a single Elder maul spec from a teammate is all the defence drain needed.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-88]

## Stats and numbers

* The Soulflame horn spec affects allies within two tiles; if the target is an NPC the boosted attack always hits, applying the effect to an ally also applies it to you, and it costs 25% spec per player affected, for up to four allies.[^GcMlWY-2-v4-602]
* As of this May 2025 video, the Soulflame horn costs only around 50 million gp and is a new spec weapon.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-62]

## Tips

* The obvious use of the Soulflame horn is guaranteeing defence-reducing specs such as the Dragon warhammer and Bandos godsword, since landing them is annoying in PvM.[^GcMlWY-2-v4-660]
* Gnomonkey thinks the horn might make a Scythe of vitur on Nex competitive with the Zaryte crossbow, since a guaranteed scythe rolls accuracy on all three hits and Nex has very high defence.[^GcMlWY-2-v4-689]
* Gnomonkey suggests in team Tombs of Amascut everyone could bring a Soul reaper axe, charge it during skulls and then pop the horn so everyone specs the Warden for free damage.[^GcMlWY-2-v4-735]
* Gnomonkey suggests standing in the middle of Verzik at P2 (three by three) with proper timing to pop the horn, and for a team to skip reds by giving everyone spec on the last hit and using their halberd specs together to dump the health bar.[^GcMlWY-2-v4-750]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey compares the horn to a pot-share-style spec effect, sharing your spec energy over a five-by-five area, and says in a five-man Theatre of Blood team the whole spec bar would be used.[^GcMlWY-2-v4-629]
* Gnomonkey says the horn encourages team play, increases the skill cap, and gives team Tombs of Amascut a reason to be explored since it stacks with the red keris (as he says it), and that it is equally good in any group content.[^GcMlWY-2-v4-812]
* Gnomonkey considers the Soulflame horn an amazing addition that will be very strong with unique use cases only in team content, and he is already coming up with ideas for Nex with it.[^W1OX5PVaPxs-976]
* Gnomonkey says he is really excited for the Soulflame horn and plans to make a video when it comes out.[^W1OX5PVaPxs-976]

## Events

* The Soulflame horn is a team-oriented spec weapon from Yama whose special attack boosts the accuracy of allies' next melee attack, including special attacks, within six seconds by 100%.[^GcMlWY-2-v4-574]

## Trivia

* The Soulflame horn costs 25% special attack and buffs a teammate; then both players' next melee hits are guaranteed to hit.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-62]
* The Soulflame horn does not work with any multi-splat hit, such as the scythe, and only works for single-splat hits like the Elder maul or bandos godsword.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-403]
* The Abyssal dagger works with the horn only because it calculates in a weird way.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-425]
