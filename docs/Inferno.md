# Inferno

The Inferno is a 69-wave TzHaar activity ending in three Jads at once and the boss [TzKal-Zuk](TzKal-Zuk.md). Gnomonkey describes it as one of the toughest pieces of content in RuneScape and, across 2022-2025, as the best content in the game. ([2021-05-21 0:00](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=0)) ([2022-03-19 34:18](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2058))

## Structure and mechanics

- The Inferno has 69 waves, more than the Fight Caves because an in-between wave of six nibblers precedes the first blob, first melee, first ranger and first mage, plus two extra waves after Jad (triple Jads and Zuk). ([2021-05-21 10:02](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=602))
- Waves work like the Fight Caves (a monster is added each wave and two combine into the next harder monster), but spawn points are completely random. ([2021-05-21 10:02](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=602)) Anytime two of the same monster have been killed in a wave, the following wave contains six nibblers. ([2025-12-07 7:12](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=432))
- Every Inferno monster is a souped-up version of the Fight Caves variant and can melee a player in melee range; the mages have devastating melee attacks. ([2021-05-21 11:11](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=671)) A mage's melee attack maxes in the 50s (about 56), and it is only fifty-fifty whether one uses it. ([2023-07-12 23:00](https://youtu.be/I1NgO3f61yE?t=1380)) The ranger punch max is nineteen. ([2023-07-12 12:00](https://youtu.be/I1NgO3f61yE?t=720))
- There are three pillars usable as safe spots (north, west, south). Waves start behind or near the north pillar, the south pillar can be rotated to when things get sticky, and the west pillar is rarely used outside early waves. ([2021-05-21 10:50](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=650)) The pillars start at 255 health each. ([2024-10-03 7:51](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=471)) ([2024-01-17 39:27](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=2367))
- Monsters do not drain prayer in the Inferno. ([2024-05-09 7:46](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=466)) Bats drain run energy on every hit. ([2024-06-13 25:42](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1542))
- Rangers have no special mechanics and just hit hard; Gnomonkey says they hit about 5% of the time against a first-run player thanks to beginner's defence. ([2025-02-26 22:22](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=1342)) From wave 50 a mage and ranger spawn together each wave with no attack delay, so the ranger can hit instantly off spawn. ([2024-06-13 42:04](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2524)) ([2025-12-07 60:02](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=3602))
- The final waves are double ranger with the mage, double mage, single Jad, triple Jad, then Zuk. ([2025-01-19 42:54](https://youtu.be/gH0WVB_jMjQ?t=2574)) Gnomonkey says Jad has 480 defence, which is why he hits many zeros against him. ([2025-01-19 48:00](https://youtu.be/gH0WVB_jMjQ?t=2880)) In the Inferno the triple Jads have 3 healers each, versus 5 per Jad in the [Jad Challenge](Jad_Challenge.md). ([2021-03-08 2:28](https://youtu.be/Nu5mkhqaWlI?t=148))
- Monsters treat pillars as obstacles, so a [corner trap](Corner_trapping.md) or safe spot can keep them from reaching the player. ([2023-03-30 12:38](https://youtu.be/1sv6tIkEHjs?t=758)) A monster always moves west or east before north or south, and if it cannot it stays put. ([2023-09-17 25:22](https://youtu.be/PaJs_xLnNwc?t=1522))

## Requirements

Stat advice from the 2021 guide, aimed at first-cape attempts: ([2021-05-21 1:55](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=115)) ([2021-05-21 2:26](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=146)) ([2021-05-21 2:53](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=173))

- Ranged and Hitpoints: would not attempt before 99, since each Ranged level is about 1% DPS.
- Magic: 94 is a hard requirement for Ice Barrage and 99 is better, because bats drain stats and a lower Magic level removes the autocast.
- Prayer: 96 is the last level that benefits prayer gained per divine dose and about the lowest he would send an attempt with; 99 is best.
- Defence: 90 is a good minimum; he was smashed by rangers on his ultimate ironman at 80 Defence.
- Agility in the 80s is good but not required, and other melee stats do not matter.

The Jad Challenge is gated behind the Inferno: a fire cape allows the first two challenges, and the infernal cape is required for 3 to 6 Jads. ([2021-03-08 0:00](https://youtu.be/Nu5mkhqaWlI?t=0)) In 2021 he put the cost of an attempt at a mil or two in supplies, mostly blood runes for [Blood Barrage](Blood_Barrage.md), with a long run using as many as 4,000 blood runes. ([2021-05-21 0:55](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=55))

## Gear and inventory

Gnomonkey's stated goal for any setup is to maximise ranged defence and prayer bonus. ([2022-07-22 0:00](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=0)) ([2022-11-04 0:00](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=0)) See also [Inferno gear](Inferno_gear.md).

### Weapon and setup, by date

- **2021:** recommends a [twisted bow](Twisted_bow.md) for first capes, because an armadyl crossbow makes Zuk incredibly difficult and grueling (off-ticking and repeatedly flicking the ranger and mage spawns). Using a crossbow only because of lack of money usually means going too early, since PvMing for money builds relevant experience. A crossbow cape is recommended only if the Inferno was already done on a main or on an ironman. ([2021-05-21 0:28](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=28)) ([2021-05-21 0:55](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=55))
- **2022-07 (twisted bow stream):** boots (Devout, Guardian, Pegasian), Armadyl versus crystal armour and a [Crystal shield](Crystal_shield.md) are all fine; a Kodai wand saves an inventory spot (no waters) and an [Occult necklace](Occult_necklace.md) raises the chance of one-shotting nibblers. With vambraces he recommends one divine and one regular ranging potion (the twisted bow hits up to 75 instead of 73 at max boost). He wears the max cape with [Barrows gloves](Barrows_gloves.md), otherwise the Ava's assembler. He calls the Ring of the Gods far better than the Ring of Suffering (about three doses of restore, 8 prayer bonus versus 4) and says it should be the default. Eight brews is the normal number. ([2022-07-22 0:28](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=28)) ([2022-07-22 0:57](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=57)) ([2022-07-22 1:26](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=86)) ([2022-07-22 2:57](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=177))
- **2022-08:** would hold off on the Inferno until full crystal armour and a [Bow of Faerdhinen](Bow_of_Faerdhinen.md), which would make it completely AFK. ([2022-08-03 2:58](https://youtu.be/6OkBpQdcXRw?t=178))
- **2024-01 (ironman budget):** Staff of the dead, devout or Pegasian boots, full crystal, Anguish, Bow of Faerdhinen and Lightbearer. ([2024-01-17 0:00](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=0))
- **2024-06 (after Mage Rebalance):** Virtus top, Crystal shield, a 15% damage mage staff and Occult; a budget version can bring Bow of Faerdhinen instead of Masori/Twisted bow as they are within 15%. He does not use Blood or Smoke scepters on first capes, prefers the Masori mask over tank helmets, and calls bringing Justiciar pieces or a Saradomin godsword trolling. Inventory: eight brews, a bunch of restores, one stamina potion for Zuk, plus one divine and one regular ranging potion. From wave 50 he casts in a Masori top for its ranged defence. ([2024-06-13 0:00](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=0)) ([2024-06-13 6:13](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=373)) ([2024-06-13 7:41](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=461)) ([2024-06-13 8:15](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=495)) ([2024-06-13 8:47](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=527)) ([2024-06-13 42:04](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2524))
- **Quiver:** most people doing a first cape will not own a [Dizana's Quiver](Dizana's_Quiver.md), and he recommends doing the Inferno before getting one. ([2024-06-13 0:00](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=0)) ([2025-02-26 26:06](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=1566))
- **2024-10:** the Bofa is plenty fine for first capes, and gear will not save a player from bad skills and poor planning. ([2024-10-03 0:00](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=0))
- **2025-02:** after the [Royal Titans](Royal_Titans.md) update the Inferno is doable with the Bofa and the [Deadeye](Deadeye.md) prayer, and players rushing [Chambers of Xeric](Chambers_of_Xeric.md) with the new prayers can reach it immediately. He advises getting Rigour with the Inferno cape, since Rigour speeds up Chambers. ([2025-02-26 0:00](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=0)) ([2025-02-26 86:14](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=5174))
- **2026-07:** with the new [Necklace of Rupture](Necklace_of_Rupture.md) and [Seeker arrows](Seeker_arrows.md) he claims about 3% more DPS on the Jads and 5% more on Zuk, which matters because Zuk is a DPS race; the wave solves still apply with downgraded gear, and upgrades just shorten the time in the fight. ([2026-07-13 0:00](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=0))

### Supplies and prayer: changes over time

- **Flicking:** in 2021 he says a first cape absolutely needs [prayer flicking](Prayer_flicking.md), though one-tick flicking is not necessary. ([2021-05-21 0:55](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=55)) By 2024-10 an Inferno was completed without hard flicking ([2024-10-03 0:00](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=0)), and by 2025-12 he says players no longer need to flick much and can face-tank damage and spam brews. ([2025-12-07 1:33](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=93))
- **Restores:** per his 2024-10 video description, the new [prayer regeneration potion](Prayer_regeneration_potion.md) is single-handedly the biggest update for ease of completion, freeing three slots of restores for mage bottoms, Bastion potions and brews; the run in that video used five prayer restoring potions (two regeneration, three restores). He also says it makes the Falador shield basically irrelevant (before he had used the potion). ([2024-10-03 0:00](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=0)) ([2024-10-03 0:59](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=59)) In 2025-01 he says earlier Inferno required a mage shortbow or rune crossbow, a lot of flicking and about 19 restores at Zuk, and that prayer regeneration potions are the main thing that made it easier. ([2025-01-19 3:31](https://youtu.be/gH0WVB_jMjQ?t=211)) ([2025-01-19 4:37](https://youtu.be/gH0WVB_jMjQ?t=277))
- **2025-12:** Armadyl brews, [Confliction gauntlets](Confliction_gauntlets.md) and prayer regeneration potions are the "golden trio" that made the Inferno easier than ever after the [Sailing](Sailing.md) release. He recommends lots of brew, lots of restore and a couple of ranged switches, and advises against cheesy items that leave fewer supplies at Zuk. ([2025-12-07 0:00](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=0)) ([2025-12-07 3:38](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=218))
- He regards pillar health as a supply, and says the name of the game is keeping supplies up. ([2022-07-22 3:56](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=236))

## Wave strategy

See also [Inferno waves](Inferno_waves.md) and [Inferno pillars](Inferno_pillars.md).

- **Start tile:** he starts every wave on one specific tile, which blocks the most spawns and gives the best line of sight on nibblers. ([2024-01-17 1:52](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=112)) ([2024-10-03 6:54](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=414)) ([2025-02-26 9:20](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=560)) ([2025-12-07 6:40](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=400)) In his 2023 speedrun stream he starts from the center up to wave 50, then from a different tile in order to two-tick. ([2023-03-30 3:53](https://youtu.be/1sv6tIkEHjs?t=233))
- **Nibblers:** in the early waves the goal is not to let nibblers touch the pillars. ([2022-11-04 2:30](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=150)) The more damage a pillar takes early the harder it is to defend later; damage to the player can be healed but pillars cannot. ([2024-10-03 7:51](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=471)) ([2025-12-07 6:40](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=400)) The north pillar matters most and needs to last through wave 65. ([2024-01-17 16:13](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=973)) ([2024-01-17 39:27](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=2367)) He says to go for nibblers in waves 1-50 when it is safe ([2025-12-07 29:25](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=1765)), not to chase them in the forties ([2024-10-03 27:41](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=1661)) (though another 2024-10 rule of thumb is to chase all nibblers from wave 50 plus ([2024-10-03 32:13](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=1933))), and to solve a bad wave before killing nibblers. ([2022-11-04 36:05](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=2165)) After wave 50 he gives nibblers one cast and solves the wave first. ([2024-06-13 43:34](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2614)) A single nibbler does not matter. ([2022-11-04 45:57](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=2757))
- **Pillars as distraction:** the other pillars take aggro off the main pillar. ([2025-01-19 12:50](https://youtu.be/gH0WVB_jMjQ?t=770)) In the 50s he never goes to the south pillar roughly 98% of the time. ([2025-02-26 55:00](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=3300)) In 2022 he said running to the south pillar can get a cape, but that consistency comes from not running there and not panicking. ([2022-07-22 31:26](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1886))
- **Health:** aim for full (99) health before each wave; starting a wave below 99 is bad planning rather than misplay. ([2022-07-22 9:18](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=558)) ([2024-01-17 18:10](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=1090)) ([2025-12-07 36:01](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=2161)) The wave before the double ranger wave should be at 99 because two rangers could hit about 92 combined off spawn. ([2024-06-13 55:50](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=3350))
- **Prayer:** being able to alternate every one or two ticks solves every wave with blobs, and one-tick alternating solves almost every ranger/mager stack behind the pillar. ([2021-05-21 1:25](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=85)) First-timers are told to practise [one-tick alternating](One-tick_alternating.md) for about ten minutes at the first blob wave. ([2022-11-04 3:55](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=235)) Flick easy attacks when safe, but solve the wave first. ([2022-11-04 38:55](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=2335)) The prayer plugin's bar can be used to practise flicking. ([2025-01-19 40:00](https://youtu.be/gH0WVB_jMjQ?t=2400)) From wave 50 he starts every wave on Augury. ([2024-01-17 34:26](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=2066))
- **Melee, bats and priority:** always have a plan for when a melee digs or respawns. ([2022-11-04 74:15](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=4455)) ([2026-07-13 42:56](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=2576)) Bats are the priority when they are hitting, then melees, then majors. ([2024-06-13 25:42](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1542)) On the double ranger wave, pray the tank prayer first, not ranged. ([2025-12-07 77:42](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=4662)) If there is no perfect solve, do something such as killing an enemy. ([2025-12-07 77:13](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=4633))
- **Using early waves:** experiment with corner traps and safe spots, since the knowledge helps later. ([2022-11-04 9:30](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=570)) The most useful skills are quickly safe spotting monsters and understanding monster pathfinding. ([2021-05-21 1:55](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=115))
- **Summary:** in 2026 he says the Inferno comes down to getting the melee alone away from everything else and one-tick alternating blobs. ([2026-07-13 21:15](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=1275)) In 2025 he calls it a knowledge check more than a skill check. ([2025-02-26 32:56](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=1976))

### Pausing and dropped potions

Clicking logout mid-wave pauses it after everything is killed (run energy does not recover, special attack does); clicking twice logs out and restarts the wave. He recommends pausing before the single Jad, the triple Jads and Zuk. ([2021-05-21 18:55](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1135)) ([2024-01-17 55:19](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=3319)) ([2024-10-03 38:38](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=2318)) ([2025-02-26 69:03](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=4143)) ([2025-12-07 68:41](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=4121)) Dropped potions do not despawn for 30 minutes since the area is instanced, but must be picked up before logging out. ([2021-05-21 19:38](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1178))

## Speedrunning

See also [Inferno speedrunning](Inferno_speedrunning.md).

- Core rule: in 2023, always be divined, always be potted and always be attacking something; in 2025, always be potted, always have Rigour on and always be shooting something. Either gets a sub 65 even with mistakes, and the 2023 version scales to sub 60. ([2023-03-30 0:00](https://youtu.be/1sv6tIkEHjs?t=0)) ([2025-07-13 4:12](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=252)) In 2025 he says a player has about 12 minutes of cushion for mistakes. ([2025-07-13 70:04](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=4204)) The 2023 stream brought enough divine potions for 60 minutes of divine. ([2023-03-30 0:00](https://youtu.be/1sv6tIkEHjs?t=0))
- Time loss: waiting for monsters to pull up to the pillar is the main loss ([2025-07-13 11:01](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=661)); NPC drag (a monster dying mid-step, costing two ticks each time) is the most common beginner mistake and adds up to over a minute over 66 waves. ## Rewards

A successful Inferno gives the infernal cape, and the first completion allows selecting the Inferno as a [Slayer](Slayer.md) task. Zuk gives 100,000 Slayer XP and each monster adds its own, about 125,000 per task, which he calls some of the best Slayer experience in the game if completed quickly.
