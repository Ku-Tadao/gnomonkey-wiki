# Ironman Mode

## Requirements

- The Maggot King budget setup uses Torms, the Mage cape, full switches and a Tome of fire, and Gnomonkey calls it pretty good. ([2026-06-30 1:57](https://youtu.be/0iIPIZTfsBU?t=117))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says ironmen can sell Castle Wars rune arrows and javelins to shops for alchemy value, making the crates a very good early gp source. ([2025-04-03 1:34](https://youtu.be/Jktw_nf9kKs?t=94))
- Gnomonkey says the typical fresh ironman play is to go to Soul Wars at 500 total level for runes, arrows and armour to sell for gold. ([2025-04-03 3:05](https://youtu.be/Jktw_nf9kKs?t=185))
- Gnomonkey says not to cut down your gear switches when using fire spells against the Maggot King, because you need the accuracy; even dropping a cape swap costs about 30 seconds per kill. ([2026-06-30 2:23](https://youtu.be/0iIPIZTfsBU?t=143))
- Gnomonkey brings an antidote (the boss poisons for 8), three restores (he has used two to four), and a divine super combat to the Maggot King. ([2026-06-30 3:22](https://youtu.be/0iIPIZTfsBU?t=202))
- Gnomonkey says to bring full mage switches to the Maggot King and not cut the switches down. ([2026-06-30 9:52](https://youtu.be/0iIPIZTfsBU?t=592))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says early game Ironman is fun because you constantly get progress, jump around the game, and can do micro efficiency like multitasking and planning ahead, so he understands why people like it. ([2024-07-30 19:07](https://youtu.be/ETOLP88HtYU?t=1147))
- Gnomonkey says endgame Ironman is not fun, though he admits he has not gotten there and doubts the group will on these accounts, while early to mid-game Ironman is fine. ([2024-07-30 19:35](https://youtu.be/ETOLP88HtYU?t=1175))
- Gnomonkey says mid-game Ironman has some bad grinds like the Bow of Faerdhinen, but also easy fast levels, big dopamine and a lot of skill to speed things up. ([2024-07-30 19:35](https://youtu.be/ETOLP88HtYU?t=1175))
- At the end of the video Gnomonkey jokingly says he hates Ironman so much, contradicting his earlier statements. ([2024-07-30 21:40](https://youtu.be/ETOLP88HtYU?t=1300))
- Gnomonkey says a new ironman would get a minimum of 120k gp of loot per Castle Wars game with no AFK requirements and it is now the place to be for new ironmen. ([2025-04-03 1:02](https://youtu.be/Jktw_nf9kKs?t=62))
- Gnomonkey's Maggot King setup is fairly budget and fairly Ironman-friendly, aimed at bare minimum to get your first kill counts. ([2026-06-30 0:00](https://youtu.be/0iIPIZTfsBU))
- Gnomonkey goes through the game modes, saying each has a distinct appeal: Ironman (get everything yourself), Hardcore Ironman (lose status on death), Ultimate Ironman (no bank), Group Ironman (shared bank with friends) and Hardcore Group Ironman, while Unranked GIM is harder to defend. ([2026-08-12 8:12](https://youtu.be/FrZfpY0uoko?t=492))
- Gnomonkey says the real reason everybody quits Ironman at some point is getting stuck on a long grind, with the Corrupted Gauntlet grind for the Bow of Faerdhinen being the common 'prison'. ([2026-08-12 9:30](https://youtu.be/FrZfpY0uoko?t=570))
