# Update: Summer Sweep-up

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2025-05-30 to 2026-07-22). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | update |
| **Videos** | 3 (first 2025-05-30, latest 2026-07-22) |

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the Summer Sweep-up is one of the few updates he has literally zero qualms with: every change is good and many massive problems he has with the game are being fixed. ([2025-05-30 0:29](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=29))
- Gnomonkey says this is potentially the best QOL change ever made to the game, and that he wants to clear out about half his teleflaws (tile/mechanic notes) because so many problems are being fixed. ([2025-05-30 16:40](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=1000))
- Gnomonkey says the update covers every pain point he has with the game and feels like Jagex was listening to him. ([2025-05-30 19:33](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=1173))
- Gnomonkey says that for a long time high-level players' complaints felt ignored as a small part of the player base, but lately the team has been hitting those pain points, including the Yama contract changes. ([2025-05-30 19:33](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=1173))
- Gnomonkey says he has been on the verge of burning out a couple of times, but with these changes and the upcoming PvM content he can potentially play his main and be excited about updates again, beyond just hardcore. ([2025-05-30 20:29](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=1229))
- Gnomonkey says Hunter is really bad, especially early Hunter, where players skip it by doing bird houses or use poor hunter rumours with rings of pursuit. ([2026-05-29 6:06](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=366))
- Gnomonkey says lowering the level to access the Hallowed Sepulchre floors would be amazing, and hopes the Colossal Wyrm AFK change lets players do bigger chunks per click so the course is worth doing. ([2026-05-29 7:07](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=427))
- Gnomonkey is nervous about faster Gauntlet prep because he is one of the few players who likes the prep, thinks it takes more skill than credited, and worries a much faster prep will affect the economy by increasing Bow of Faerdhinen supply and Gauntlet clears. ([2026-05-29 7:40](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=460))
- Gnomonkey says Gauntlet prep can get old, and he does not mind it being treated as a pain point so that players can get through it more consistently without feeling short of resources. ([2026-05-29 8:13](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=493))
- Gnomonkey considers the Summer Sweep-up changes to be really good all around, apart from a couple of minor bugs he expects Jagex to fix fairly soon. ([2026-07-22 15:33](https://youtu.be/EPlArwCehYY?t=933))

## Events

- Jagex published the Summer Sweep-up combat and loot blog, a large quality-of-life update scheduled to arrive in a couple of months from late May 2025. ([2025-05-30 0:00](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM))
- Jagex said it will share a detailed blog the following week on everything it is eyeing for the Summer Sweep-up, saying it was inundated with player suggestions and a good number are already on its shortlist. ([2026-05-29 5:05](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=305))
- The Summer Sweep-up will include changes to various skilling methods, including long overdue improvements to core Hunter training, gear rebalancing focused on buffing items not living up to their potential, improvements to common PvM pain points like the Corrupted Gauntlet, and new Agility shortcuts and improvements to existing ones. ([2026-05-29 5:32](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=332))
- In the video, Jagex said there will be big Agility changes in the Summer Sweep-up, including changing Hallowed Sepulchre requirements, improving the AFK of the Colossal Wyrm course, and adding new shortcuts. ([2026-05-29 6:39](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=399))
- Jagex did not mention Corrupted Gauntlet drop rate changes but said it will make massive improvements to Gauntlet prep. ([2026-05-29 7:07](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=427))
- The Summer Sweep-up PvM changes went live; Gnomonkey recorded this reaction video live and cut it down afterwards. ([2026-07-22 0:00](https://youtu.be/EPlArwCehYY))
- Most of the Summer Sweep-up changes had already been discussed in Gnomonkey's previous video, but the live blog contained some slightly different details and mentioned some future changes that were not part of this update. ([2026-07-22 0:00](https://youtu.be/EPlArwCehYY))
- Gnomonkey said he would make a new Corrupted Gauntlet guide very soon covering the changes, and that it would probably include tier two armor. ([2026-07-22 15:33](https://youtu.be/EPlArwCehYY?t=933))
