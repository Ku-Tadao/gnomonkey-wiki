# The Wardens

The Wardens are the final fight of the [Tombs of Amascut](Tombs_of_Amascut.md), a four-phase encounter that Gnomonkey describes as long, involving three bosses and carrying the majority of the raid's points ([2025-10-04 53:48](https://youtu.be/X-PTHMq_10A?t=3228)). By mid-2025 he calls it the hardest part of the raid ([2025-06-25 40:40](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=2440)). His advice centres on tick-eating the phase 1 orbs, counting core hits in phase 2, skull skipping in phase 3 and dodging lightning in phase 4.

## Structure and points

- Phase 3 is worth the most points of the entire raid, with a 2.5 times multiplier ([2022-11-18 16:30](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=990)); in 2025 he says it gives the best points of the whole raid ([2025-06-25 52:47](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=3167)).
- Phase 2 is "extremely good for points": the game keeps giving points through the third down of the core but stops after it, so he always aims for a three-down core ([2025-06-28 30:45](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=1845)) and purposefully does not kill the core on the second down ([2025-06-25 44:48](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=2688) [2026-04-01 53:01](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=3181)).
- Gnomonkey says the faster the Wardens die, the less there is to deal with ([2023-06-30 41:34](https://youtu.be/cmLBaxdoHpQ?t=2494)).

## Phase 1: obelisk

**Mechanics.** The obelisk shoots skulls at statues that charge and drop UFOs ([2026-04-01 47:20](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=2840)). In 2022 he compared it with Verzik P1: energy skulls flow from each side of the pillar so one side is tanked for a few hits, and the UFOs can be dodged by moving a few tiles or ignored by running to the safe spot ([2022-10-26 4:30](https://youtu.be/bxsp-hCAlT8?t=270)). By 2025 he says staying against the left wall avoids the UFOs entirely ([2025-10-04 33:19](https://youtu.be/X-PTHMq_10A?t=1999)); in 2022 he gave a far-side-of-the-arena option that needs a 10 tile range weapon ([2022-11-18 13:42](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=822)). Tanking skulls while killing the obelisk causes an explosion for roughly 30, which he says has killed him repeatedly ([2022-11-18 14:34](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=874)).

**Which Warden appears.** Tanking skulls on the left side decides the Warden fought in later phases ([2024-10-24 31:34](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=1894)). The required count has been stated differently:
- 2023: tank a minimum of four sets of orbs; with three he got the wrong Warden because of a bug ([2023-04-25 33:10](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=1990)).
- 2025-06: four skulls (sets) before the Warden dies ([2025-06-27 32:58](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=1978)). He always blocks the left side; blocking the right makes later-phase Wardens chase, melee harder and give worse ghosts in phase 3 ([2025-06-25 40:40](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=2440)).
- 2026 (latest): tank three skulls on the left; tanking zero defaults to the unwanted Warden ([2026-04-01 47:20](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=2840)).

**Orbs.** Each set of orbs deals 75 damage ([2022-11-18 13:42](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=822) [2023-05-09 36:58](https://youtu.be/K7VALGO6md8?t=2218)); in the 2026 solo guide the two-orb special hits roughly 60s at raid level 300 and 75 at 375, which is the cap, so health should stay over 75 ([2026-04-01 48:35](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=2915)). Tanking down to 76 HP leaves exactly 1 HP ([2023-05-09 36:58](https://youtu.be/K7VALGO6md8?t=2218)); in 2023 he used 78 HP (76 if healed) ([2023-05-09 36:51](https://youtu.be/K7VALGO6md8?t=2211) [2023-06-30 35:26](https://youtu.be/cmLBaxdoHpQ?t=2126)).
- The skinny orb from the right Warden has the timing of Yellow at P3 [Verzik Vitur](Verzik_Vitur.md) (spec when it is about mid-screen); the big orb from the left Warden has the timing of [Sotetseg](Sotetseg.md) (tick eat when it touches the head) ([2022-11-18 14:01](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=841)).
- Not tanking skulls keeps both orbs on the same timing so both can be tick eaten at once; at 1 HP a tick-eaten set deals 2 instead of 75 ([2022-11-18 14:01](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=841)). Tick eating does not negate prayer drain (15 points with Deadly Prayers on) ([2022-11-18 14:34](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=874)).
- The 2024 method: turn on Rapid Heal before the fight, tank to 75, turn it off at 76, and click it when the monster HP percentage ticks from 46 to 48 to auto-tick eat the next orb ([2024-06-27 18:35](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=1115)). He recommends the plugin's Warden HP percentage option for orb timing ([2024-06-27 17:11](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=1031)).
- Orb sets: one set is most common, two if slightly unlucky, three astronomically unlikely with the shadow ([2025-07-06 31:24](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=1884)). Without much damage a second set is fine ([2025-06-28 28:50](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=1730)); two missed Bandos godsword specs give three sets instead of two ([2025-06-27 31:58](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=1918)).

**Opening damage and weapons.** The recommended opener has changed with his setups:
- 2022: [Tumeken's shadow](Tumeken's_shadow.md) is the best DPS; without it, [Bandos godsword](Bandos_godsword.md) on the obelisk for 30 defence drain, then the [blowpipe](Toxic_blowpipe.md) ([2022-11-18 13:42](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=822)). In 2023 he says [Bow of Faerdhinen](Bow_of_Faerdhinen.md) is better than the blowpipe here in a 500 raid ([2023-05-09 37:40](https://youtu.be/K7VALGO6md8?t=2260)).
- 2025: Bandos godsword specs to 40 damage, block four orbs, then Bow of Faerdhinen on the pillar and AFK ([2025-06-25 41:30](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=2490)); no-Keris budget: two Bandos godsword specs with adrenaline and six brews ([2025-06-27 31:58](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=1918)); ironman budget: dump every spec while tanking to roughly 75, with the Atlatl best DPS and the Keris about 10% worse ([2025-06-28 27:48](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=1668) [2025-06-28 28:18](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=1698)).
- 2026: Bandos godsword spec, Drain potion, one Burning claw spec, then Bow of Faerdhinen, slightly better than the [Eye of Ayak](Eye_of_Ayak.md) ([2026-04-01 48:56](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=2936)).

## Phase 2: core

**Damage taken.** Phase 2 attacks max 10 (ranged and magic) and 4 (melee), so standing in melee range and praying melee right after the ranged hitsplat wastes less prayer ([2023-04-25 33:38](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=2018) [2023-05-09 38:49](https://youtu.be/K7VALGO6md8?t=2329) [2025-06-27 34:29](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=2069)). He calls it optional ([2024-05-30 21:17](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=1277) [2024-07-01 20:07](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=1207)). Orbs cannot be caught in melee range because they hit a tick earlier ([2024-06-27 20:33](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=1233)). It is the biggest supply drain ([2024-10-24 34:34](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=2074)).

**Core hits.** The core takes guaranteed max hits ([2025-06-25 43:27](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=2607)). The first down gives five four-tick hits then a sixth ([2022-11-18 15:31](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=931)); later downs give 8 hits below 80% Warden HP and 10 below 60% ([2022-11-18 15:31](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=931) [2023-04-25 35:16](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=2116)), permanently 10 afterwards ([2024-05-30 22:10](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=1330)). In 2024 he said comments claiming 12 or 14 hits at lower percentages are false ([2024-06-27 21:10](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=1270)). Five Dragon dagger specs into a Bandos godsword whack brings the Warden below 60% on the first down ([2022-11-18 15:31](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=931) [2025-06-25 44:00](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=2640)); the last hit should be a slow, heavy weapon ([2025-10-04 36:22](https://youtu.be/X-PTHMq_10A?t=2182)). Counting hits is called the hardest part of phase 2 ([2025-06-27 35:26](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=2126)). Killing on the third down is optimal for points ([2022-11-18 15:31](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=931)).

**Gear and supplies.**
- Magic: [Ancients](Magic.md) (Ice Barrage) is used only for the Wardens ([2023-05-09 8:26](https://youtu.be/K7VALGO6md8?t=506)); [Augury](Prayer.md) is about 6% DPS in the final phase ([2023-04-25 37:02](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=2222) [2023-06-30 39:55](https://youtu.be/cmLBaxdoHpQ?t=2395)), a three-max-hit difference in the 300 test ([2024-10-24 32:40](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=1960)). Accuracy sets the minimum hit, so negative pieces (boots, bird helmet) come off ([2022-11-18 15:04](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=904) [2025-06-28 28:50](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=1730)).
- Use a zombie, skeletal or melee thrall (the ghost thrall cannot hit through magic prayer) ([2022-11-18 15:04](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=904) [2023-04-25 34:42](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=2082)); a ranged thrall stuns on hit ([2025-06-25 46:39](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=2799)).
- [Death Charge](Death_Charge.md) procs when the core is sucked back, giving 15% spec energy each down ([2022-11-18 15:59](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=959) [2024-05-30 19:08](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=1148)).
- Do not BGS when mageing ([2024-07-01 24:51](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=1491)). Below 10 health, use the Keris, or a bit earlier if nervous ([2024-05-30 21:17](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=1277) [2024-06-27 22:10](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=1330)); poking the Warden with it gives 11 HP per poke, but not at max health ([2024-06-27 23:35](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=1415)).

**Specials.** Marking two tiles avoids windmill and radial specials without dragging the Warden ([2022-11-18 15:04](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=904)). Skulls leave two safe tiles around them, so standing next to the shadow or in line two tiles away is safe ([2024-05-30 22:10](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=1330) [2024-10-24 33:43](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=2023) [2025-07-06 33:47](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=2027)). White skulls are ranged, red energy skulls mage ([2025-06-25 43:27](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=2607) [2025-06-28 29:22](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=1762)). The skull bomb (cross pattern, about 50, disables prayer) is the most dangerous special ([2025-06-25 47:59](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=2879) [2025-06-28 29:22](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=1762)). Since the June 2025 change it can no longer land under the active Warden or the obelisk ([2025-06-22 9:50](https://youtu.be/JjIAGBBDmL8?t=590)); Gnomonkey says this makes phase 2 safer, as it was the one way he died there ([2025-06-22 9:50](https://youtu.be/JjIAGBBDmL8?t=590)).

## Phase 3: skulls

- Skulls appear every 20% of the Warden's health, starting at 80% ([2024-07-01 23:15](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=1395) [2024-05-30 23:05](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=1385)). Killing them damages the Warden for 5% of his total HP, not credited as points ([2022-11-18 16:12](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=972)).
- Stand close: far positions make projectiles slower and attacks toward the Warden are nulled when skull phase starts ([2022-11-18 16:12](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=972) [2023-04-25 37:58](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=2278)). He later says to start at the back to see which side, then move up ([2023-05-09 43:37](https://youtu.be/K7VALGO6md8?t=2617)).
- At 60% Zebak's ghost spawns (pray the first attack); at 40% Baba's ghost spawns (dodge one boulder) ([2025-06-25 52:47](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=3167)). A level 4 Zebak makes projectiles travel a tick faster in phases 3 and 4 ([2022-11-18 17:26](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=1046) [2023-05-09 47:40](https://youtu.be/K7VALGO6md8?t=2860)). Boulders only damage the area the Warden is hitting ([2025-06-28 36:22](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=2182)).
- Under 100 health, the Keris on skulls overheals; it drains five prayer per skull even when overhealed ([2024-07-01 23:57](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=1437) [2025-07-06 37:06](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=2226)).

**Skull skipping.** His position has changed over time:
- 2022: skipping all four skulls gives 20% more damage, about 5% more total loot, and is worth it for GP per hour ([2022-11-18 16:30](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=990)).
- 2023: first 500 should not skip; skip all waves only for points ([2023-04-25 41:32](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=2492)). In a no-Keris video he advised against skipping, since a level 4 Zebak brings hits a tick earlier and completion matters more than loot ([2023-06-30 40:40](https://youtu.be/cmLBaxdoHpQ?t=2440)).
- 2024-09: free and safe provided one skull is leapt ([2024-09-14 17:54](https://youtu.be/HeM0yGenyx8?t=1074)).
- 2025-06-27: not to skip in an Overly Draining 500, the only time he says so, since it costs prayer and time ([2025-06-27 40:57](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=2457)).
- 2025-06/07 and 2026 (latest): skip. It gives more points and loot ([2025-06-25 50:00](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=3000)), is easier than the normal mechanic and worth learning before completing the raid ([2025-06-28 35:21](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=2121)), and raises purple chance because the skulls hit the Warden for 5% ([2025-07-06 36:34](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=2194) [2025-07-06 37:06](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=2226)).

Method: kill all but one skull so a mistake is not lethal (about 70 damage if hit), run to a red tile, then click toward the middle when the orange particles explode ([2022-11-18 16:30](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=990) [2026-04-01 55:09](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=3309) [2025-07-06 39:07](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=2347)). Spam-click a far skull; clicking the nearest one does not work and killed him ([2025-06-28 36:55](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=2215)). He uses the trident because the Atlatl has short range ([2025-06-28 33:46](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=2026) [2025-06-28 35:55](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=2155)). If a skip goes wrong, redo it immediately ([2023-04-25 41:32](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=2492)). With Insanity, go to the back afterwards ([2026-04-01 56:41](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=3401)).

## Phase 4: last row

Phase 4 begins at 5% health ([2025-06-27 42:56](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=2576)). Boulders hit for around 50 and are the main thing to avoid ([2022-11-18 16:58](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=1018) [2024-05-30 25:32](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=1532)). Lightning follows a six-tick cycle of three shadows then three strikes; stepping onto the first shadow as it strikes avoids all damage and also dodges the Baba boulder ([2025-06-25 55:50](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=3350) [2025-06-27 44:30](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=2670) [2025-07-06 40:06](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=2406)). He says phase 4 is not RNG and is consistent ([2024-05-30 24:31](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=1471)), but some patterns force a hit ([2025-07-06 41:03](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=2463)). In 2025 he calls it probably the hardest part of the raid, though not a DPS check ([2025-06-27 42:27](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=2547)), with about 95% of patterns dodgeable ([2025-06-25 56:24](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=3384)). Last row can be sustained for 20 minutes ([2025-06-27 45:35](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=2735)).

- Preparation: stay far away at the end of phase 3 for an extra cast or hit ([2023-04-25 40:34](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=2434) [2024-05-30 24:03](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=1443)); pop a scarab and drink an overheal ([2025-06-28 37:23](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=2243)); use the last surge dose here ([2025-07-06 39:07](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=2347)).
- Damage: double Bandos godsword spec aiming for the 60 damage cap ([2025-06-27 42:56](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=2576) [2025-06-25 55:23](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=3323)); Void Waker spam, saving energy for the Keris if not done a 500 ([2025-07-06 39:34](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=2374)).
- Mistakes: do not panic; use the Keris and return to hitting ([2024-06-27 25:39](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=1539)).
