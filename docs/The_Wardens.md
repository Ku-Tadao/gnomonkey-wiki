The Wardens are the final fight of the [Tombs of Amascut](Tombs_of_Amascut.md), a four-phase encounter that Gnomonkey describes as long, involving three bosses and carrying the majority of the raid's points[^X-PTHMq_10A-3228]. By mid-2025 he calls it the hardest part of the raid[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-2440]. His advice centres on tick-eating the phase 1 orbs, counting core hits in phase 2, skull skipping in phase 3 and dodging lightning in phase 4.

## Structure and points

- Phase 3 is worth the most points of the entire raid, with a 2.5 times multiplier[^q3D2jeguGz8-990]; in 2025 he says it gives the best points of the whole raid[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-3167].
- Phase 2 is "extremely good for points": the game keeps giving points through the third down of the core but stops after it, so he always aims for a three-down core[^BVZEAFBsj10-1845] and purposefully does not kill the core on the second down[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-2688][^N2DCZ1nt4p4-3181].
- Gnomonkey says the faster the Wardens die, the less there is to deal with[^cmLBaxdoHpQ-2494].

## Phase 1: obelisk

**Mechanics.** The obelisk shoots skulls at statues that charge and drop UFOs[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-2840]. In 2022 he compared it with Verzik P1: energy skulls flow from each side of the pillar so one side is tanked for a few hits, and the UFOs can be dodged by moving a few tiles or ignored by running to the safe spot[^bxsp-hCAlT8-270]. By 2025 he says staying against the left wall avoids the UFOs entirely[^X-PTHMq_10A-1999]; in 2022 he gave a far-side-of-the-arena option that needs a 10 tile range weapon[^q3D2jeguGz8-822]. Tanking skulls while killing the obelisk causes an explosion for roughly 30, which he says has killed him repeatedly[^q3D2jeguGz8-874].

**Which Warden appears.** Tanking skulls on the left side decides the Warden fought in later phases[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-1894]. The required count has been stated differently:
- 2023: tank a minimum of four sets of orbs; with three he got the wrong Warden because of a bug[^vQO8khf302s-1990].
- 2025-06: four skulls (sets) before the Warden dies[^UhLzyieXrbY-1978]. He always blocks the left side; blocking the right makes later-phase Wardens chase, melee harder and give worse ghosts in phase 3[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-2440].
- 2026 (latest): tank three skulls on the left; tanking zero defaults to the unwanted Warden[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-2840].

**Orbs.** Each set of orbs deals 75 damage[^q3D2jeguGz8-822][^K7VALGO6md8-2218]; in the 2026 solo guide the two-orb special hits roughly 60s at raid level 300 and 75 at 375, which is the cap, so health should stay over 75[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-2915]. Tanking down to 76 HP leaves exactly 1 HP[^K7VALGO6md8-2218]; in 2023 he used 78 HP (76 if healed)[^K7VALGO6md8-2211][^cmLBaxdoHpQ-2126].
- The skinny orb from the right Warden has the timing of Yellow at P3 [Verzik Vitur](Verzik_Vitur.md) (spec when it is about mid-screen); the big orb from the left Warden has the timing of [Sotetseg](Sotetseg.md) (tick eat when it touches the head)[^q3D2jeguGz8-841].
- Not tanking skulls keeps both orbs on the same timing so both can be tick eaten at once; at 1 HP a tick-eaten set deals 2 instead of 75[^q3D2jeguGz8-841]. Tick eating does not negate prayer drain (15 points with Deadly Prayers on)[^q3D2jeguGz8-874].
- The 2024 method: turn on Rapid Heal before the fight, tank to 75, turn it off at 76, and click it when the monster HP percentage ticks from 46 to 48 to auto-tick eat the next orb[^YizVhRWXnxs-1115]. He recommends the plugin's Warden HP percentage option for orb timing[^YizVhRWXnxs-1031].
- Orb sets: one set is most common, two if slightly unlucky, three astronomically unlikely with the shadow[^91vxCCm7Jck-1884]. Without much damage a second set is fine[^BVZEAFBsj10-1730]; two missed Bandos godsword specs give three sets instead of two[^UhLzyieXrbY-1918].

**Opening damage and weapons.** The recommended opener has changed with his setups:
- 2022: [Tumeken's shadow](Tumeken's_shadow.md) is the best DPS; without it, [Bandos godsword](Bandos_godsword.md) on the obelisk for 30 defence drain, then the [blowpipe](Toxic_blowpipe.md)[^q3D2jeguGz8-822]. In 2023 he says [Bow of Faerdhinen](Bow_of_Faerdhinen.md) is better than the blowpipe here in a 500 raid[^K7VALGO6md8-2260].
- 2025: Bandos godsword specs to 40 damage, block four orbs, then Bow of Faerdhinen on the pillar and AFK[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-2490]; no-Keris budget: two Bandos godsword specs with adrenaline and six brews[^UhLzyieXrbY-1918]; ironman budget: dump every spec while tanking to roughly 75, with the Atlatl best DPS and the Keris about 10% worse[^BVZEAFBsj10-1668][^BVZEAFBsj10-1698].
- 2026: Bandos godsword spec, Drain potion, one Burning claw spec, then Bow of Faerdhinen, slightly better than the [Eye of Ayak](Eye_of_Ayak.md)[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-2936].

## Phase 2: core

**Damage taken.** Phase 2 attacks max 10 (ranged and magic) and 4 (melee), so standing in melee range and praying melee right after the ranged hitsplat wastes less prayer[^vQO8khf302s-2018][^K7VALGO6md8-2329][^UhLzyieXrbY-2069]. He calls it optional[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-1277][^LxYODChv40A-1207]. Orbs cannot be caught in melee range because they hit a tick earlier[^YizVhRWXnxs-1233]. It is the biggest supply drain[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-2074].

**Core hits.** The core takes guaranteed max hits[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-2607]. The first down gives five four-tick hits then a sixth[^q3D2jeguGz8-931]; later downs give 8 hits below 80% Warden HP and 10 below 60%[^q3D2jeguGz8-931][^vQO8khf302s-2116], permanently 10 afterwards[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-1330]. In 2024 he said comments claiming 12 or 14 hits at lower percentages are false[^YizVhRWXnxs-1270]. Five Dragon dagger specs into a Bandos godsword whack brings the Warden below 60% on the first down[^q3D2jeguGz8-931][^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-2640]; the last hit should be a slow, heavy weapon[^X-PTHMq_10A-2182]. Counting hits is called the hardest part of phase 2[^UhLzyieXrbY-2126]. Killing on the third down is optimal for points[^q3D2jeguGz8-931].

**Gear and supplies.**
- Magic: [Ancients](Magic.md) (Ice Barrage) is used only for the Wardens[^K7VALGO6md8-506]; [Augury](Prayer.md) is about 6% DPS in the final phase[^vQO8khf302s-2222][^cmLBaxdoHpQ-2395], a three-max-hit difference in the 300 test[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-1960]. Accuracy sets the minimum hit, so negative pieces (boots, bird helmet) come off[^q3D2jeguGz8-904][^BVZEAFBsj10-1730].
- Use a zombie, skeletal or melee thrall (the ghost thrall cannot hit through magic prayer)[^q3D2jeguGz8-904][^vQO8khf302s-2082]; a ranged thrall stuns on hit[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-2799].
- [Death Charge](Death_Charge.md) procs when the core is sucked back, giving 15% spec energy each down[^q3D2jeguGz8-959][^Mg9hcqcFC1U-1148].
- Do not BGS when mageing[^LxYODChv40A-1491]. Below 10 health, use the Keris, or a bit earlier if nervous[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-1277][^YizVhRWXnxs-1330]; poking the Warden with it gives 11 HP per poke, but not at max health[^YizVhRWXnxs-1415].

**Specials.** Marking two tiles avoids windmill and radial specials without dragging the Warden[^q3D2jeguGz8-904]. Skulls leave two safe tiles around them, so standing next to the shadow or in line two tiles away is safe[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-1330][^vEs4Qvs_Rag-2023][^91vxCCm7Jck-2027]. White skulls are ranged, red energy skulls mage[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-2607][^BVZEAFBsj10-1762]. The skull bomb (cross pattern, about 50, disables prayer) is the most dangerous special[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-2879][^BVZEAFBsj10-1762]. Since the June 2025 change it can no longer land under the active Warden or the obelisk[^JjIAGBBDmL8-590]; Gnomonkey says this makes phase 2 safer, as it was the one way he died there[^JjIAGBBDmL8-590].

## Phase 3: skulls

- Skulls appear every 20% of the Warden's health, starting at 80%[^LxYODChv40A-1395][^Mg9hcqcFC1U-1385]. Killing them damages the Warden for 5% of his total HP, not credited as points[^q3D2jeguGz8-972].
- Stand close: far positions make projectiles slower and attacks toward the Warden are nulled when skull phase starts[^q3D2jeguGz8-972][^vQO8khf302s-2278]. He later says to start at the back to see which side, then move up[^K7VALGO6md8-2617].
- At 60% Zebak's ghost spawns (pray the first attack); at 40% Baba's ghost spawns (dodge one boulder)[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-3167]. A level 4 Zebak makes projectiles travel a tick faster in phases 3 and 4[^q3D2jeguGz8-1046][^K7VALGO6md8-2860]. Boulders only damage the area the Warden is hitting[^BVZEAFBsj10-2182].
- Under 100 health, the Keris on skulls overheals; it drains five prayer per skull even when overhealed[^LxYODChv40A-1437][^91vxCCm7Jck-2226].

**Skull skipping.** His position has changed over time:
- 2022: skipping all four skulls gives 20% more damage, about 5% more total loot, and is worth it for GP per hour[^q3D2jeguGz8-990].
- 2023: first 500 should not skip; skip all waves only for points[^vQO8khf302s-2492]. In a no-Keris video he advised against skipping, since a level 4 Zebak brings hits a tick earlier and completion matters more than loot[^cmLBaxdoHpQ-2440].
- 2024-09: free and safe provided one skull is leapt[^HeM0yGenyx8-1074].
- 2025-06-27: not to skip in an Overly Draining 500, the only time he says so, since it costs prayer and time[^UhLzyieXrbY-2457].
- 2025-06/07 and 2026 (latest): skip. It gives more points and loot[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-3000], is easier than the normal mechanic and worth learning before completing the raid[^BVZEAFBsj10-2121], and raises purple chance because the skulls hit the Warden for 5%[^91vxCCm7Jck-2194][^91vxCCm7Jck-2226].

Method: kill all but one skull so a mistake is not lethal (about 70 damage if hit), run to a red tile, then click toward the middle when the orange particles explode[^q3D2jeguGz8-990][^N2DCZ1nt4p4-3309][^91vxCCm7Jck-2347]. Spam-click a far skull; clicking the nearest one does not work and killed him[^BVZEAFBsj10-2215]. He uses the trident because the Atlatl has short range[^BVZEAFBsj10-2026][^BVZEAFBsj10-2155]. If a skip goes wrong, redo it immediately[^vQO8khf302s-2492]. With Insanity, go to the back afterwards[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-3401].

## Phase 4: last row

Phase 4 begins at 5% health[^UhLzyieXrbY-2576]. Boulders hit for around 50 and are the main thing to avoid[^q3D2jeguGz8-1018][^Mg9hcqcFC1U-1532]. Lightning follows a six-tick cycle of three shadows then three strikes; stepping onto the first shadow as it strikes avoids all damage and also dodges the Baba boulder[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-3350][^UhLzyieXrbY-2670][^91vxCCm7Jck-2406]. He says phase 4 is not RNG and is consistent[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-1471], but some patterns force a hit[^91vxCCm7Jck-2463]. In 2025 he calls it probably the hardest part of the raid, though not a DPS check[^UhLzyieXrbY-2547], with about 95% of patterns dodgeable[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-3384]. Last row can be sustained for 20 minutes[^UhLzyieXrbY-2735].

- Preparation: stay far away at the end of phase 3 for an extra cast or hit[^vQO8khf302s-2434][^Mg9hcqcFC1U-1443]; pop a scarab and drink an overheal[^BVZEAFBsj10-2243]; use the last surge dose here[^91vxCCm7Jck-2347].
- Damage: double Bandos godsword spec aiming for the 60 damage cap[^UhLzyieXrbY-2576][^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-3323]; Void Waker spam, saving energy for the Keris if not done a 500[^91vxCCm7Jck-2374].
- Mistakes: do not panic; use the Keris and return to hitting[^YizVhRWXnxs-1539].

## Invocations

- Overclocked changes the phase 3 attack from six ticks to five; Overclocked 2 to four; Insanity to three[^DaUViQQ8TL8-783]. He calls them extremely free and says not to enter without Overclocked 2[^DaUViQQ8TL8-833].
- Penetration makes obelisk specials hit harder and disables prayer; he ranks it B tier after being combo'd for about 70[^a9V3c9mDf9E-2375][^a9V3c9mDf9E-2405], and advises it only from around 300s[^DaUViQQ8TL8-833].
- Ancient Haste causes more orbs, costing roughly 70 to 140 damage at high raid level[^a9V3c9mDf9E-2437]. Acceleration (A tier) is "free and always on" in 2025, but he turned it off in a 300 to avoid flicking[^a9V3c9mDf9E-2461][^DaUViQQ8TL8-833][^HeM0yGenyx8-950].
- [Insanity](Insanity.md) is S tier at 50 raid level[^a9V3c9mDf9E-2591]. Skulls appear at each 20% of HP; practise at 150 or 300 without it[^a9V3c9mDf9E-2623]. One missed skull hits for about 70; two kill[^a9V3c9mDf9E-2685]. In 2025 he suggests turning it on around 300 to 350[^DaUViQQ8TL8-867].

## Supplies

- Preparation: over seventy health for a 500 solo[^h4hf5hPQHms-2400]; six brews, five restores and an [Ambrosia](Ambrosia.md) for a 300[^HeM0yGenyx8-787]; six brews for a no-Keris 500, reserved for the Wardens[^UhLzyieXrbY-1978][^UhLzyieXrbY-2243].
- Keep prayer high[^vQO8khf302s-2149]. A scarab gives roughly 30 seconds of unlimited prayer[^BVZEAFBsj10-2087][^vEs4Qvs_Rag-2074].
- Set the bag shift-click to Resupply to refill doses mid-fight[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-1860][^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-2535][^N2DCZ1nt4p4-2936]. Take life from the final supply ghost for unlimited potions[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-1793].
