**Sulliuscep** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2019-09-25 and 2020-07-31. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* Sulliuscep require a minimum of 65 Woodcutting to chop, and high Prayer and Hitpoints are very useful.[^ldS-QuV3CKY-27]
* Sulliuscepts are not realistic for extremely low-level skillers because a tar monster's max hit is 11, so you should have at least 12 HP.[^_eDJaUupzBc-474]

## Methods

* To start, teleport to Fossil Island (if not unlocked, use a dixite pendant on the strange machine in the mansion). On the first run, run to the swamp in the southeast of Fossil Island and jump on the mushroom; the first landing deals half the player's health, and clicking the magic mushtree unlocks the teleport.[^ldS-QuV3CKY-118]
* Before moving on, place nine bittercap mushrooms in the pit landed in to prevent fall damage in future; the pit needs nine more mushrooms every time the player lands on it.[^ldS-QuV3CKY-151]
* After a run of seven trees Gnomonkey picks up at least nine bittercap mushrooms before jumping on the rubber cap mushrooms to start another run; when the trip is done he teleports out, restores stats, banks fossils, teleports back and uses the magic mushtree to reach the sticky swamp.[^ldS-QuV3CKY-237]
* From level 65 to 90 chop Sulliuscepts (mushroom trees) on Fossil Island; Gnomonkey says they do not get enough love as they are very AFK with very good XP.[^_eDJaUupzBc-474]

## Stats and numbers

* In the footage Gnomonkey got only two fossils from about fifty trees, which he says was incredibly unlucky; typically a seven-tree run gives about two to three fossils, and once he received ten fossils in one run.[^ldS-QuV3CKY-0]
* Occasionally a sulliuscep cap drops; these are worth about 1,500 GP, are the only mushroom Gnomonkey keeps, and are used to make mushroom pies; they can be dropped to extend trips.[^ldS-QuV3CKY-209]
* The method can easily pull 100 to 110k Woodcutting XP an hour if the player pays full attention, while Gnomonkey, who likes AFK, typically gets around 80k an hour.[^ldS-QuV3CKY-237]
* Sulliuscepts give up to 100k XP an hour, and completing the fossil exhibits gives 120k XP in lamps in any skill; starting at level 65 you finish them with fossils left over.[^_eDJaUupzBc-474]
* Going from level 65 to 90 on Sulliuscepts takes 38,559 mushrooms chopped.[^_eDJaUupzBc-474]

## Tips

* Skillers cannot realistically use this method because the spine mushrooms always deal ten damage when they hit.[^ldS-QuV3CKY-27]
* Gnomonkey recommends getting the Lumberjack outfit before training Woodcutting past this level, and says having Preserve unlocked increases chop rates quite a bit.[^ldS-QuV3CKY-27]
* Gnomonkey's goal with gear is to maximize Woodcutting XP and prayer bonus: full Lumberjack outfit, trimmed skillcape for prayer bonus (he wears the crafting cape for bank teleport), an Ardougne cloak 4 provides more prayer bonus if owned, an infernal axe (a dragon axe is effectively the same since mushrooms are not burnt), and the best prayer bonus gear for other slots.[^ldS-QuV3CKY-60]
* Gnomonkey uses a desert amulet 4 to restore stats between trips; without it bring home teleport tabs, a construction cape or a ring of dueling. An Hitpoints skillcape and regen bracelet are smart to bring because the player is damaged often.[^ldS-QuV3CKY-60]
* Inventory: numulite (received in large amounts and simply kept), a rake to remove vines blocking the path, antipoison because spine mushrooms poison for 5 damage, and exactly seven sharks, since by the seventh shark Gnomonkey has typically run out of Prayer and ends the trip.[^ldS-QuV3CKY-86]
* Trips sometimes end early because the player gets too many fossils to carry, and dixite pendants are needed to teleport back to Fossil Island after a trip.[^ldS-QuV3CKY-118]
* Each sulliuscep can be chopped for a random number of mushrooms before it falls; the sulliuscep then moves to a different location in a set pattern, and the route through the swamp between them is always the same.[^ldS-QuV3CKY-151]
* Keep Preserve on at all times while chopping and use the special attack.[^ldS-QuV3CKY-151]
* As the player runs through the swamp, tar bubbles turn into tar monsters (a max of seven); Gnomonkey says to simply run past them, as the method of distracting them with hoop snakes is a huge waste of time.[^ldS-QuV3CKY-181]
* Spine mushrooms randomly attack the player as they run past, always dealing ten damage and poisoning, so the player should drink an antidote when hit.[^ldS-QuV3CKY-181]
* Bittercap mushrooms and mortmyre fungus should be dropped as they are not worth much, while fossils are worth keeping.[^ldS-QuV3CKY-181]
* Gnomonkey hops worlds if he sees another player; other players do not reduce mushrooms because those are client side, but tar monsters are more likely to spawn, and he finds it surprising Jagex added a sulliuscep world when it is competitive like this.[^ldS-QuV3CKY-209]
* Sulliuscepts are the best provider of fossils in the game, which can be turned in to the museum for XP lamps, giving free Runecrafting XP.[^_eDJaUupzBc-474]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey calls sulliuscep one of the most rewarding methods of Woodcutting training; it is more AFK than non-tick-manipulated teak trees, gives over 100k Woodcutting XP an hour and more profit than AFK yew trees, and is the best source of fossils.[^ldS-QuV3CKY-0]
