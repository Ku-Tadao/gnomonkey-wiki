# Sulliuscep

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2019-09-25 to 2020-07-31). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- To start, teleport to Fossil Island (if not unlocked, use a dixite pendant on the strange machine in the mansion). On the first run, run to the swamp in the southeast of Fossil Island and jump on the mushroom; the first landing deals half the player's health, and clicking the magic mushtree unlocks the teleport. ([2019-09-25 1:58](https://youtu.be/ldS-QuV3CKY?t=118))
- Before moving on, place nine bittercap mushrooms in the pit landed in to prevent fall damage in future; the pit needs nine more mushrooms every time the player lands on it. ([2019-09-25 2:31](https://youtu.be/ldS-QuV3CKY?t=151))
- After a run of seven trees Gnomonkey picks up at least nine bittercap mushrooms before jumping on the rubber cap mushrooms to start another run; when the trip is done he teleports out, restores stats, banks fossils, teleports back and uses the magic mushtree to reach the sticky swamp. ([2019-09-25 3:57](https://youtu.be/ldS-QuV3CKY?t=237))
- From level 65 to 90 chop Sulliuscepts (mushroom trees) on Fossil Island; Gnomonkey says they do not get enough love as they are very AFK with very good XP. ([2020-07-31 7:54](https://youtu.be/_eDJaUupzBc?t=474))

## Requirements

- Sulliuscep require a minimum of 65 Woodcutting to chop, and high Prayer and Hitpoints are very useful. ([2019-09-25 0:27](https://youtu.be/ldS-QuV3CKY?t=27))
- Sulliuscepts are not realistic for extremely low-level skillers because a tar monster's max hit is 11, so you should have at least 12 HP. ([2020-07-31 7:54](https://youtu.be/_eDJaUupzBc?t=474))

## Stats and numbers

- In the footage Gnomonkey got only two fossils from about fifty trees, which he says was incredibly unlucky; typically a seven-tree run gives about two to three fossils, and once he received ten fossils in one run. ([2019-09-25 0:00](https://youtu.be/ldS-QuV3CKY))
- Occasionally a sulliuscep cap drops; these are worth about 1,500 GP, are the only mushroom Gnomonkey keeps, and are used to make mushroom pies; they can be dropped to extend trips. ([2019-09-25 3:29](https://youtu.be/ldS-QuV3CKY?t=209))
- The method can easily pull 100 to 110k Woodcutting XP an hour if the player pays full attention, while Gnomonkey, who likes AFK, typically gets around 80k an hour. ([2019-09-25 3:57](https://youtu.be/ldS-QuV3CKY?t=237))
- Sulliuscepts give up to 100k XP an hour, and completing the fossil exhibits gives 120k XP in lamps in any skill; starting at level 65 you finish them with fossils left over. ([2020-07-31 7:54](https://youtu.be/_eDJaUupzBc?t=474))
- Going from level 65 to 90 on Sulliuscepts takes 38,559 mushrooms chopped. ([2020-07-31 7:54](https://youtu.be/_eDJaUupzBc?t=474))

## Tips

- Skillers cannot realistically use this method because the spine mushrooms always deal ten damage when they hit. ([2019-09-25 0:27](https://youtu.be/ldS-QuV3CKY?t=27))
- Gnomonkey recommends getting the Lumberjack outfit before training Woodcutting past this level, and says having Preserve unlocked increases chop rates quite a bit. ([2019-09-25 0:27](https://youtu.be/ldS-QuV3CKY?t=27))
- Gnomonkey's goal with gear is to maximize Woodcutting XP and prayer bonus: full Lumberjack outfit, trimmed skillcape for prayer bonus (he wears the crafting cape for bank teleport), an Ardougne cloak 4 provides more prayer bonus if owned, an infernal axe (a dragon axe is effectively the same since mushrooms are not burnt), and the best prayer bonus gear for other slots. ([2019-09-25 1:00](https://youtu.be/ldS-QuV3CKY?t=60))
- Gnomonkey uses a desert amulet 4 to restore stats between trips; without it bring home teleport tabs, a construction cape or a ring of dueling. An Hitpoints skillcape and regen bracelet are smart to bring because the player is damaged often. ([2019-09-25 1:00](https://youtu.be/ldS-QuV3CKY?t=60))
- Inventory: numulite (received in large amounts and simply kept), a rake to remove vines blocking the path, antipoison because spine mushrooms poison for 5 damage, and exactly seven sharks, since by the seventh shark Gnomonkey has typically run out of Prayer and ends the trip. ([2019-09-25 1:26](https://youtu.be/ldS-QuV3CKY?t=86))
- Trips sometimes end early because the player gets too many fossils to carry, and dixite pendants are needed to teleport back to Fossil Island after a trip. ([2019-09-25 1:58](https://youtu.be/ldS-QuV3CKY?t=118))
- Each sulliuscep can be chopped for a random number of mushrooms before it falls; the sulliuscep then moves to a different location in a set pattern, and the route through the swamp between them is always the same. ([2019-09-25 2:31](https://youtu.be/ldS-QuV3CKY?t=151))
- Keep Preserve on at all times while chopping and use the special attack. ([2019-09-25 2:31](https://youtu.be/ldS-QuV3CKY?t=151))
- As the player runs through the swamp, tar bubbles turn into tar monsters (a max of seven); Gnomonkey says to simply run past them, as the method of distracting them with hoop snakes is a huge waste of time. ([2019-09-25 3:01](https://youtu.be/ldS-QuV3CKY?t=181))
- Spine mushrooms randomly attack the player as they run past, always dealing ten damage and poisoning, so the player should drink an antidote when hit. ([2019-09-25 3:01](https://youtu.be/ldS-QuV3CKY?t=181))
- Bittercap mushrooms and mortmyre fungus should be dropped as they are not worth much, while fossils are worth keeping. ([2019-09-25 3:01](https://youtu.be/ldS-QuV3CKY?t=181))
- Gnomonkey hops worlds if he sees another player; other players do not reduce mushrooms because those are client side, but tar monsters are more likely to spawn, and he finds it surprising Jagex added a sulliuscep world when it is competitive like this. ([2019-09-25 3:29](https://youtu.be/ldS-QuV3CKY?t=209))
- Sulliuscepts are the best provider of fossils in the game, which can be turned in to the museum for XP lamps, giving free Runecrafting XP. ([2020-07-31 7:54](https://youtu.be/_eDJaUupzBc?t=474))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey calls sulliuscep one of the most rewarding methods of Woodcutting training; it is more AFK than non-tick-manipulated teak trees, gives over 100k Woodcutting XP an hour and more profit than AFK yew trees, and is the best source of fossils. ([2019-09-25 0:00](https://youtu.be/ldS-QuV3CKY))
