**Opinion: PvM boss tier list** is an opinion topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2022-03-19. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey's boss tier list ranks bosses purely by how much he enjoys them, based on their skill floor and skill cap; he says it is opinion.[^HkVt4enEesI-0]
* In the final order, Gnomonkey places the Inferno at the very top of S tier, with Theater of Blood content, Fight Caves, Verzik Vitur, Nylocas, and the Great Olm following in S tier, with Challenge Mode Chambers of Xeric near the bottom of S tier.[^HkVt4enEesI-5345]
* Gnomonkey puts The Gauntlet at the top of A tier, with Nex above Phosani's Nightmare and Hallowed Sepulchre right below Nex; Maiden of Sugadinti is the best of the A tier Theater of Blood fights.[^HkVt4enEesI-5432]
* In B tier Gnomonkey places Zalcano, Tempoross, Tekton and Xarpus hard mode toward the bottom, with Dagannoth Kings toward the top, Kril Tsutsaroth a step above Vanguards, and Vanguards above Vorkath.[^HkVt4enEesI-5520]
* In C tier Gnomonkey puts Kree'arra at the top followed by Giant Mole and Barrows right below it, with Zilyana, Vespula, Cerberus, Alchemical Hydra, Abyssal Sire, Chaos Elemental and Sarachnis below, calling Cerberus mechanically more interesting than most of them.[^HkVt4enEesI-5520]
* Gnomonkey says Muttadile is the worst boss in the game, with Wilderness bosses next, then Mimic, Corporeal Beast, Wintertodt, Thermonuclear smoke devil and Kraken, which he says is worse than the smoke devil.[^HkVt4enEesI-5520]
* Gnomonkey concludes the list has lots of F-tier bosses but lots of S and A tier content, and calls it a better balance than his pets video.[^HkVt4enEesI-5831]

## Trivia

* Gnomonkey defines a skill floor as how difficult content is to jump into (a higher floor is worse), and a skill cap as how skilled you can become before the content peaks out (a high cap is good); he says some content has an infinite skill cap.[^HkVt4enEesI-0]
* In the tier list, the greyed-out versions of top bosses are the hard mode variants of Theater of Blood bosses, which he rates separately because they are mechanically different.[^HkVt4enEesI-33]
* Gnomonkey does not rank Challenge Mode variants of Chambers of Xeric bosses separately because they just have higher defence and health and are the same fight.[^HkVt4enEesI-67]
