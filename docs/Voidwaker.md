**Voidwaker** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 8 videos between 2023-07-12 and 2026-06-25. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Gnomonkey uses the Voidwaker as his whack weapon in the Wardens' phase 2, with DDS for spec.[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-270]
* The Voidwaker special counts as magic damage, so speccing with it does not end Ba-Ba's boulder phase; Gnomonkey can spec during boulder throwing for free damage.[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-442]

## Stats and numbers

* The Voidwaker provides 5 mage bonus, which he finds very nice for the melee Inferno setup.[^I1NgO3f61yE-0]
* The Voidwaker has a max hit of only about 80 on its special, compared with 110 for a dragon dagger special.[^znDIsdrEbgk-168]
* The proposed Voidwaker special no longer ignores accuracy but rolls with plus 200 percent increased accuracy against the target's slash or magic defence, whichever is lower.[^Kzc2e6H8i7o-412]
* In the description that 400% accuracy still leaves the Voidwaker less than 60% accurate on current best-in-slot locations, and that it is still useless after a nerf, so players will bring claws if the number stays.[^P5yHDSocecY-0]
* The Voidwaker one-shots all the monkey room monsters and is four-tick, so he uses it there.[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-270]

## Tips

* Gnomonkey used his Voidwaker on a late attempt at the sub-20 solo Nex kill, saying he wanted to go for it really badly.[^znDIsdrEbgk-949]
* Gnomonkey still uses the Voidwaker at Chambers of Xeric Challenge Mode because things have high mage level and slash defence there, and it bypasses that.[^Kzc2e6H8i7o-447]
* To swap the Voidwaker back to its normal style after using it on stab, because it has a lower max hit on stab.[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-442]
* Void Waker is an okay stab weapon with a very good spec and 10% more damage, hitting well over 80.[^lJexGv6dxu8-1799]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says he thinks the Voidwaker would fry a Barbarian-type enemy and would be strong at Nex.[^I1NgO3f61yE-1033]
* In the description, Gnomonkey says the Voidwaker fits in so well among the other spec weapons without stepping on their toes, and asks why it is being nerfed.[^t5mXf8KJztU-0]
* Gnomonkey calls the Voidwaker nerf the most questionable decision in the blog; he assumed it was a PvP nerf, but the blog did not mention PvP at all.[^t5mXf8KJztU-186]
* Gnomonkey says the Voidwaker slots in beautifully in the current PvM meta: the Zaryte crossbow is the best DPS spec if you can land it and the target has 500+ HP, Dragon claws are best if the target has low defence or is weak to slash, and the Voidwaker is best if the target meets neither condition.[^t5mXf8KJztU-216]
* Gnomonkey says various bosses have damage modifiers that prevent Voidwaker use, citing Akkha, Vasa and the Olm melee hand, and that the Zaryte crossbow sees far more use than the other spec options simply because it hits so ridiculously high; the point of a DPS spec is a highly accurate and powerful hit, which is why the Voidwaker is useful.[^t5mXf8KJztU-243]
* Gnomonkey worries the Voidwaker nerf means Jagex will focus on extremely tanky bosses that cannot be drained, similar to Tombs of Amascut.[^t5mXf8KJztU-270]
* Gnomonkey jokes that an Iron Man should not have to grind bosses just to get a Voidwaker to click a blue orb for 23 damage.[^znDIsdrEbgk-168]
* Gnomonkey says the change makes the Voidwaker completely useless at all locations, effectively replacing it with Dragon claws, which are worse in no way; it is good only against very tanky things like Kephri and Nex that have high magic and slash defence.[^Kzc2e6H8i7o-420]
* Gnomonkey argues the Voidwaker deserves to be good since it drops in pieces from the Wilderness bosses at a very rare rate, and if it is useless no one will kill those bosses any more, not even the bots.[^Kzc2e6H8i7o-487]
* Gnomonkey says the Voidwaker change is probably the most questionable on the entire blog and that feedback from Mod Goblin showed they were unsure about it.[^Kzc2e6H8i7o-502]
* Gnomonkey says it feels like one Jagex mod is pushing an obsession with nerfing the Voidwaker, and that nobody wants it nerfed.[^P5yHDSocecY-0]
* Gnomonkey says the Voidwaker is still not useful even with 400% accuracy at Kephri, Nex or Chambers of Xeric, calling the change taking it from completely useless to completely useless.[^P5yHDSocecY-354]
* Gnomonkey says the point of the Voidwaker is to bypass defence on very tanky targets rather than roll against it, and that if you must roll against defence you just use claws or the Zaryte crossbow.[^P5yHDSocecY-412]
* Gnomonkey says claws are better than the Voidwaker when rolling against defence because they max higher and are already very accurate against slash.[^P5yHDSocecY-439]
* Gnomonkey argues Jagex should just leave the Voidwaker alone and let it bypass accuracy, rather than raising the number to plus 600%, 800% or 1000%.[^P5yHDSocecY-439]
* Gnomonkey says Voidwaker was good on the Nightmare, Kephri and Nex, but is not used there anymore, and thinks whoever designed the change misunderstands its purpose in PvM.[^P5yHDSocecY-470]
* Gnomonkey says BGS is not worth using on Ba-Ba if you have the Voidwaker because you would just spec with the Voidwaker; without a Voidwaker, BGS is worth it.[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-552]
* Gnomonkey says claws are probably worth bringing only if they are your only DPS spec, as they are much worse than the Voidwaker.[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-552]
* Gnomonkey's description says the Voidwaker is in the bin for Nex after the defence changes.[^AzCKJvLDRxo-0]
* Gnomonkey says that since Nex's defence change the Voidwaker is no longer worth bringing as the old standard setup; the Zaryte crossbow is a better spec for damage.[^AzCKJvLDRxo-146]

## Events

* According to the blog, the reason for the Voidwaker nerf is that guaranteed hits that ignore accuracy checks in PvM make it difficult to design worthwhile spec DPS weapons in future without creeping upwards significantly.[^t5mXf8KJztU-216]
* Jagex's second Project Rebalance blog raised the Voidwaker's proposed special attack accuracy bonus from plus 200% (triple accuracy) to 400% (five times accuracy), after feedback that it had barely any use compared to the Zaryte crossbow or Dragon claws.[^P5yHDSocecY-324]
* Dubie Dobies went very unlucky on the Void Waker grind but got the team one Void Waker, and Gnomonkey got a second corrupted Void Waker from the Colosseum, which helped because the team was dry on Void Wakers.[^lJexGv6dxu8-1168]
