**Metabolize** is an other topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2023-03-07 and 2023-04-19. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Stats and numbers

* Metabolize reduces eating delay by 0.6 seconds while active; when disabled the action delay would be reset to your weapon's attack speed or left unchanged to prevent flicking.[^trol59VccdE-509]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says Metabolize effectively means eating wastes two ticks instead of three, a cool buff to hard food, but wonders whether it would work for potions and notes combo eating would be awkward with different delays.[^trol59VccdE-541]
* Gnomonkey notes Metabolize could allow two tick barbarian fishing by flicking it on and off, which would need an engine change to prevent or would be a massive skilling buff if allowed.[^trol59VccdE-541]
* Gnomonkey wants Metabolize scrapped because it does not actually allow you to eat any faster and has little use.[^8VyPkG4sEwQ-242]
* Gnomonkey says you typically never want to eat hard food mid-combat anyway, and Metabolize also has a ridiculous prayer drain.[^8VyPkG4sEwQ-242]
