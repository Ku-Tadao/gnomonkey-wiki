## Stats and numbers

- Metabolize reduces eating delay by 0.6 seconds while active; when disabled the action delay would be reset to your weapon's attack speed or left unchanged to prevent flicking. ([2023-03-07 8:29](https://youtu.be/trol59VccdE?t=509))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says Metabolize effectively means eating wastes two ticks instead of three, a cool buff to hard food, but wonders whether it would work for potions and notes combo eating would be awkward with different delays. ([2023-03-07 9:01](https://youtu.be/trol59VccdE?t=541))
- Gnomonkey notes Metabolize could allow two tick barbarian fishing by flicking it on and off, which would need an engine change to prevent or would be a massive skilling buff if allowed. ([2023-03-07 9:01](https://youtu.be/trol59VccdE?t=541))
- Gnomonkey wants Metabolize scrapped because it does not actually allow you to eat any faster and has little use. ([2023-04-19 4:02](https://youtu.be/8VyPkG4sEwQ?t=242))
- Gnomonkey says you typically never want to eat hard food mid-combat anyway, and Metabolize also has a ridiculous prayer drain. ([2023-04-19 4:02](https://youtu.be/8VyPkG4sEwQ?t=242))
