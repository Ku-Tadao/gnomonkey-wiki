# Dark crab

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2020-03-19 to 2020-03-21). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- The escape method uses a lockpick: the room just north of the resource area needs a lockpick to enter and is full of weak ents-type enemies; the player attacks one, brews their stats down, waits about 10 seconds and then X-logs (force closes the client) so the character keeps attacking and then logs out. ([2020-03-21 2:58](https://youtu.be/Fkqxv-KW9fU?t=178))

## Requirements

- To fish dark crabs efficiently the player needs the Elite Wilderness Diary complete and 85 Fishing. ([2020-03-19 1:58](https://youtu.be/0uQwmS-qeB8?t=118))
- Fishing dark crabs requires 85 Fishing, and the Elite Wilderness Diary to fish them efficiently; they benefit from Rada's Blessing, from 2% with the easy diary to 8% with the elite diary. ([2020-03-21 0:00](https://youtu.be/Fkqxv-KW9fU))

## Stats and numbers

- Dark crabs provide 40k Fishing XP an hour and 400k GP an hour, which Gnomonkey calls a straight upgrade over the eels and far more AFK. ([2020-03-19 1:58](https://youtu.be/0uQwmS-qeB8?t=118))
- Dark crabs give about 40k Fishing XP an hour, compared with about 60k for non-tick-manipulated barbarian fishing, and about 400k GP an hour, which Gnomonkey says is almost the highest GP from an AFK method besides ringed dragons. ([2020-03-21 0:30](https://youtu.be/Fkqxv-KW9fU?t=30))
- Gnomonkey says player killers typically leave as soon as the player enters this room, and in about ten escapes none has ever followed him in. ([2020-03-21 2:58](https://youtu.be/Fkqxv-KW9fU?t=178))
- With clan members Gnomonkey typically fishes about a thousand crabs before banking, or 500 if solo; over thirty hours he died only twice, both when fishing on mobile or while away from the computer. ([2020-03-21 4:22](https://youtu.be/Fkqxv-KW9fU?t=262))

## Tips

- The dark crab fishing spot never moves or disappears, making it the most AFK fishing method, period, similar to redwood trees, according to Gnomonkey. ([2020-03-19 1:58](https://youtu.be/0uQwmS-qeB8?t=118))
- Dark crab spots never disappear or move, making them the most AFK thing to fish in the game according to Gnomonkey, as the player only needs to interact once the inventory fills. ([2020-03-21 0:00](https://youtu.be/Fkqxv-KW9fU))
- Gnomonkey brings a Dinh's bulwark, which he calls the ultimate piece of armor for running away from player killers, plus dragonhide armor and a blessing; diary boots, gloves, helmets and capes give a free bonus if available but are not necessary. ([2020-03-21 1:01](https://youtu.be/Fkqxv-KW9fU?t=61))
- Some players bring a Zamorak godsword instead of the bulwark with the special attack primed, so when player killers attack they can auto-retaliate and freeze them for 20 seconds to escape, but this risks being spec'd out by dragon claws or an AGS. ([2020-03-21 1:26](https://youtu.be/Fkqxv-KW9fU?t=86))
- Quick prayers should include Protect from Magic, since the main danger is being frozen and killed by mages because dark crab fishing uses brews that reduce Magic and so defence; Augury gives 25% regular and 25% magic defence, and Piety works if Augury is not unlocked. ([2020-03-21 1:55](https://youtu.be/Fkqxv-KW9fU?t=115))
- Dark crab inventory: a lobster pot and dark fishing bait (about 4 GP each on the Grand Exchange), a knife to cut the web to the resource area, coins to note the crabs, and some extra coins if using Rada's Blessing since extra crabs come at no bait cost. ([2020-03-21 2:01](https://youtu.be/Fkqxv-KW9fU?t=121))
- Gnomonkey brings three brews and a super restore to escape player killers; some bring as many as seven, but he found three is plenty. ([2020-03-21 2:24](https://youtu.be/Fkqxv-KW9fU?t=144))
- Set up RuneLite notifications so the client plays a tone and set the idle/low-health notification threshold to one below max health, so the client warns the player if a player killer attacks; this makes mobile play difficult. ([2020-03-21 3:28](https://youtu.be/Fkqxv-KW9fU?t=208))
- Gnomonkey advises finding safety in numbers: fishing in a large pile of players means a player is unlikely to be singled out, and some clan members have PKing alts to deal with lower-level pests. ([2020-03-21 3:55](https://youtu.be/Fkqxv-KW9fU?t=235))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey thinks people are sleeping on dark crabs, which he calls incredibly good, and says his method for fishing them was covered in the next week's video. ([2020-03-19 1:58](https://youtu.be/0uQwmS-qeB8?t=118))
- Gnomonkey says that if the player has access to dark crabs and can check back occasionally, dark crabs are the clear winner. ([2020-03-19 2:58](https://youtu.be/0uQwmS-qeB8?t=178))
- Gnomonkey calls dark crabs an incredibly good AFK fishing method that trumped all other contenders in his AFK fishing video. ([2020-03-21 0:00](https://youtu.be/Fkqxv-KW9fU))
- Gnomonkey recommends fishing in high-risk worlds, which only disable the Protect Item prayer, because player killers risk more and will most likely not bring expensive spec weapons like dragon claws. ([2020-03-21 1:26](https://youtu.be/Fkqxv-KW9fU?t=86))
