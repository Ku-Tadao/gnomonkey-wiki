## Methods

- To reach Ankou, teleport to Xeric's Heart, enter the Catacombs, run south of the altar and climb through a hole to get to them. ([2020-04-16 1:12](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o?t=72))

## Requirements

- Ankou have no requirements to fight and Gnomonkey calls them incredibly easy. ([2020-04-16 0:00](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o))

## Stats and numbers

- A task of 53 Ankou took Gnomonkey roughly seven minutes, making it one of Duradel's shortest tasks. ([2020-04-16 0:00](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o))
- Ankou have an average weight of five from Duradel, so they come up occasionally. ([2020-04-16 0:00](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o))
- Gnomonkey's Ankou task of about seven minutes gave only 30k in loot and 3k Slayer XP. ([2020-04-16 1:40](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o?t=100))

## Tips

- For Ankou Gnomonkey brings standard melee gear, and says the gear does not matter much so pieces can be downgraded. ([2020-04-16 0:00](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o))
- The Salve amulet works on Ankou but does not stack with the Slayer helmet, and Gnomonkey says the Slayer helmet's bonus is more beneficial. ([2020-04-16 0:33](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o?t=33))
- Ankou do not drop bones, so Gnomonkey says a bone crusher is not needed, and they have no alchable drops so the rune pouch can be skipped. ([2020-04-16 0:33](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o?t=33))
- With decent stats and armour you can tank multiple Ankou at once without needing food, so Gnomonkey uses auto-retaliate and piety. ([2020-04-16 1:12](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o?t=72))
- Ankou drop items worth picking up, such as arrows and runes, but the loot is not great overall. ([2020-04-16 1:40](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o?t=100))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says Ankou have horrible drops and terrible XP rates, but like Zygomites and Dark Beasts the task is worth doing just because it is so fast. ([2020-04-16 0:00](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o))
