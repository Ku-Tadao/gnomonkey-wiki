**Ankou** is an other topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2020-04-16. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* Ankou have no requirements to fight and Gnomonkey calls them incredibly easy.[^9QNIj0pLk5o-0]

## Methods

* To reach Ankou, teleport to Xeric's Heart, enter the Catacombs, run south of the altar and climb through a hole to get to them.[^9QNIj0pLk5o-72]

## Stats and numbers

* A task of 53 Ankou took Gnomonkey roughly seven minutes, making it one of Duradel's shortest tasks.[^9QNIj0pLk5o-0]
* Ankou have an average weight of five from Duradel, so they come up occasionally.[^9QNIj0pLk5o-0]
* Gnomonkey's Ankou task of about seven minutes gave only 30k in loot and 3k Slayer XP.[^9QNIj0pLk5o-100]

## Tips

* For Ankou Gnomonkey brings standard melee gear, and says the gear does not matter much so pieces can be downgraded.[^9QNIj0pLk5o-0]
* The Salve amulet works on Ankou but does not stack with the Slayer helmet, and Gnomonkey says the Slayer helmet's bonus is more beneficial.[^9QNIj0pLk5o-33]
* Ankou do not drop bones, so Gnomonkey says a bone crusher is not needed, and they have no alchable drops so the rune pouch can be skipped.[^9QNIj0pLk5o-33]
* With decent stats and armour you can tank multiple Ankou at once without needing food, so Gnomonkey uses auto-retaliate and piety.[^9QNIj0pLk5o-72]
* Ankou drop items worth picking up, such as arrows and runes, but the loot is not great overall.[^9QNIj0pLk5o-100]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says Ankou have horrible drops and terrible XP rates, but like Zygomites and Dark Beasts the task is worth doing just because it is so fast.[^9QNIj0pLk5o-0]
