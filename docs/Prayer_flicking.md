**Prayer flicking** is toggling a protection prayer on only when an attack is about to land, saving prayer points. Gnomonkey says flicking is required to clear the Inferno and that learning it is really important, with some flicking better than none[^6lRmbYCYtME-950]. A first Inferno cape needs flicking but not one-tick flicking[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-55][^XMTe9TYbDls-1746], and the more a player flicks the more brews they can take, though lazy flicking typically gets through fine[^FfNQI63ZKys-2335].

## Lazy flicking

In lazy flicking the player turns the protection prayer on as soon as the monster's animation snaps back to normal, and turns it off afterwards[^XMTe9TYbDls-1418]. In the Inferno the attack is calculated as soon as the animation starts, so the player switches the prayer on as soon as the monster hunkers down, and flicking even for two ticks still saves prayer[^6lRmbYCYtME-921]. As the animation starts the player has already been hit, with Jad the only exception, where the hit lands once the projectile is on screen[^XMTe9TYbDls-1449].

Monster cycles:

| Monster | Cue | Cycle |
|---|---|---|
| Melee | Attack animation | Four ticks, so toggle every four ticks[^FfNQI63ZKys-630] |
| Ranger | Hunkers down as the attack registers; turn prayer on then off | Four ticks[^FfNQI63ZKys-1019] |
| Mage | A small flicker under it indicates it attacked | Four ticks, so flick every fourth tick[^FfNQI63ZKys-2257][^XMTe9TYbDls-2027] |

Learning lazy flicking timing helps with off-ticking monsters and knowing when damage actually hits, which matters when praying against both a mage and a ranger[^XMTe9TYbDls-1476]. A player can easily get a cape with just lazy flicking; Gnomonkey did not know how to one-tick flick when he got his first cape, and one-tick flicking only speeds things up[^6lRmbYCYtME-2101][^XMTe9TYbDls-1746]. Mages cannot be one-tick flicked if the player is going to mess it up, so lazy flick them if not consistent[^XMTe9TYbDls-2761].

## One-tick flicking

One ticking is clicking twice per game tick, turning prayers on and off, and gaining the effect without draining any prayer[^_cIv_pRhm9U-3438]. Gnomonkey compares it to a Gears of War reload: click twice per tick, double click repeatedly, using the prayer helper and prayer plugin[^6lRmbYCYtME-1680]. Turn the prayers on before double clicking, or the player is just rapidly turning them off[^6lRmbYCYtME-1771]. He wears a compression glove because he hurt his hand from too much one-ticking[^_cIv_pRhm9U-3470].

### Alternating against combinations

* **Ranger and blob:** pray ranged, click the ranger, and alternate as soon as he hunkers down, keeping both prayers lit and clicking the next as soon as one unlights[^6lRmbYCYtME-1247]. When a blob can see the player and a ranger is away from a safe spot, one-tick or two-tick alternating between ranged and mage prayer avoids all damage[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1530]. Pray ranged until the ranger's animation starts, then alternate; it may hit once more if the player starts on an unfortunate tick, but not after that if one-tick alternating properly[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1548].
* **Mage and blob:** pray magic until the light flickers under the mage, then one-tick alternate ranged and mage prayer as the mage is killed[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1574].
* **Ranger and mager two ticks apart:** one-tick alternating does not work; the player needs to two-tick alternate to flick both along with blobs[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1802]. He does not recommend two-ticking on a first cape, because people often get the blob off-tick and still take every blob hit; one-tick is easier because the player just alternates when the prayer turns off[^6lRmbYCYtME-1405].
* If the player needs to move or attack, they should not be flicking: get into a position where moving is unnecessary, then alternate[^6lRmbYCYtME-1432].

### Flinching

Gnomonkey calls flinching a very bad habit and never recommends it. He says to learn to one-tick, which solves just about every wave[^6lRmbYCYtME-1308]. A player who learns to flinch and never one-ticks cannot solve the ranger and blob solves that come up a lot[^6lRmbYCYtME-1555].

### Timing cues

Asked about timing, he goes off sound if not looking at the screen and goes off the prayer unlighting if looking at the prayers, and does not like relying on sound cues in the game[^6lRmbYCYtME-2859].

## Other content

* **Fight Caves:** he one-tick flicks when mages approach and keeps prayer on during dangerous waves[^_cIv_pRhm9U-3496].
* **Tombs of Amascut (500s):** learning to lazy flick the offensive prayer is probably the biggest thing for 500s, because it lets the player bring much less restore[^UhLzyieXrbY-960]. With prayer regeneration Zebak should be a zero supply room. He ended it with about 80 to 90 prayer points and says the player can flick just the offensive prayer, camp the overhead and use about one restore dose[^UhLzyieXrbY-1140]. At Warden about two restores are enough to full camp prayers; he still flicks because it leaves extra prayer, and ended the raid with four restores left[^UhLzyieXrbY-2420]. There is wiggle room in the Sandfew supply so flicking need not be perfect, but it is not enough to never flick through the raid; roughly three Sandfews in total are fine[^UhLzyieXrbY-2820].
