**Prayer flicking** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 6 videos between 2021-05-21 and 2025-06-27. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* When a blob can see you and a ranger is away from a safe spot, you can one-tick or two-tick alternate between ranged and mage prayer to avoid all damage.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1530]
* Pray ranged until you see the ranger's animation start, then start alternating; it may hit once more if you start on an unfortunate tick, but after that it will not hit you if you keep one-tick alternating properly.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1548]
* For a mage plus blob, pray magic until the light flickers under the mage, then one-tick alternate ranged and mage prayer as you kill it.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1574]
* Lazy flicking: for all attacks in the Inferno the attack is calculated as soon as the animation starts, so you switch the prayer on as soon as the monster hunkers down, and flicking even for two ticks still saves prayer.[^6lRmbYCYtME-921]
* To alternate prayers against a ranger and a blob by praying ranged, clicking the ranger, and alternating as soon as you see him hunker down, keeping both prayers lit and clicking the next as soon as one unlights.[^6lRmbYCYtME-1247]
* Gnomonkey describes one-tick flicking like a Gears of War reload: you click twice per tick, double click repeatedly, and he uses the prayer helper and prayer plugin.[^6lRmbYCYtME-1680]
* In Inferno, as the animation starts you have already been hit, with Jad being the only exception where you are hit once the projectile is on screen.[^XMTe9TYbDls-1449]

## Tips

* Flicking is required for a first Inferno cape but one-tick flicking is not.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-55]
* You cannot just one-tick alternate if a ranger and mager are on you two ticks apart; you need to two-tick alternate to flick both along with blobs.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1802]
* If you need to move or attack you should not be flicking; get into a position where you do not need to move, then start alternating.[^6lRmbYCYtME-1432]
* To turn your prayers on before double clicking or you are just rapidly turning your prayers off.[^6lRmbYCYtME-1771]
* Asked about timing, Gnomonkey says he goes off sound if not looking at the screen and goes off the prayer unlighting if looking at the prayers, and that he does not like relying on sound cues in the game.[^6lRmbYCYtME-2859]
* Melee Inferno monsters attack on a four-tick cycle, so flicking them means toggling the melee prayer every four ticks.[^FfNQI63ZKys-630]
* Rangers hunker down as the attack registers and attack on a four-tick cycle, so you flick by turning prayer on and then off.[^FfNQI63ZKys-1019]
* To flick a mage by watching the small flicker under it, which indicates it did the attack, and it is a four-tick cycle like the ranger.[^FfNQI63ZKys-2257]
* Gnomonkey describes lazy flicking for Inferno monsters: click the protection prayer as soon as the monster's animation snaps back to normal, and these attack on a four-tick cycle.[^XMTe9TYbDls-1418]
* Learning lazy flicking timing helps you off-tick monsters and know when damage actually hits you, which matters when you are praying against both a mage and a ranger.[^XMTe9TYbDls-1476]
* A mage's light flickering underneath it shows it has attacked, the same cue as a ranger hunkering down, and mages have a four-tick attack delay so you flick every fourth tick.[^XMTe9TYbDls-2027]
* You cannot one-tick flick a mage if you are going to mess it up, so lazy flick them if you cannot do it consistently.[^XMTe9TYbDls-2761]
* Gnomonkey describes one ticking as clicking twice per game tick, so you turn prayers on and off and get the effect without draining any prayer.[^_cIv_pRhm9U-3438]
* He wears a compression glove because he hurt his hand from too much one-ticking.[^_cIv_pRhm9U-3470]
* He one tick flicks in the Fight Caves when mages approach and keeps prayer on during dangerous waves.[^_cIv_pRhm9U-3496]
* Learning to lazy flick the offensive prayer is probably the biggest thing for 500s because it lets you bring much less restore.[^UhLzyieXrbY-960]
* With prayer regeneration, Zebak should be a zero supply room, and Gnomonkey ended it with about 80 to 90 prayer points; he says you can flick just the offensive prayer, camp the overhead and use about one restore dose.[^UhLzyieXrbY-1140]
* At Warden Gnomonkey says about two restores are enough to full camp prayers, and he still flicks because it leaves extra prayer; he ended the raid with four restores left.[^UhLzyieXrbY-2420]
* There is plenty of wiggle room in the Sandfew supply so you do not have to flick perfectly, but he says it is not enough to never flick through the raid, and that you can use roughly three Sandfews in total and still be fine.[^UhLzyieXrbY-2820]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says you have to flick to get through the Inferno and that learning it is really important, with some flicking better than none.[^6lRmbYCYtME-950]
* Gnomonkey says flinching is a very bad habit and that he never recommends it; he says to learn how to one-tick, which solves just about every wave.[^6lRmbYCYtME-1308]
* Gnomonkey says he does not recommend two-ticking on a first cape, since people often get their blob off-tick and still take every blob hit, and that one-tick is easier because you just alternate when the prayer turns off.[^6lRmbYCYtME-1405]
* Gnomonkey says if you learn to flinch and never learn to one-tick, solves with a ranger and blob that come up a lot are not possible.[^6lRmbYCYtME-1555]
* Gnomonkey says you do not need to one-tick flick to get through Inferno; it just speeds things up, and he did not know how to one-tick flick on his own first cape.[^6lRmbYCYtME-2101][^XMTe9TYbDls-1746]
* Gnomonkey says the more you flick the more brews you can take, but typically lazy flicking gets you through fine.[^FfNQI63ZKys-2335]
