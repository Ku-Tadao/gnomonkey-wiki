**Temple Trekking** is an activity topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2018-12-03. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Methods

* Start at the Temple Trekking board and escort either Dalcian Fang or Fiona Fray, the two easy escorts.[^I-8v5RpD8KY-33]
* Choose Route 1, because Routes 2 and 3 are harder and do not let the player evade the combat events, which wastes a lot of time.[^I-8v5RpD8KY-67]

## Tips

* If the player sees a combat event they cannot evade that is not the bridge event, they should teleport back to Burgh de Rott with the diary legs and start again.[^I-8v5RpD8KY-67]
* The bridge event has a large broken bridge and undead lumberjacks come out of the water to attack the player.[^I-8v5RpD8KY-67]
* A very similar-looking event has no lumberjacks and has the player chopping a cluster of trees; if that tree cluster appears in the bridge event the player should teleport out and start again.[^I-8v5RpD8KY-67]
* In the bridge event a wave of five lumberjacks attacks; if no piece drops, another wave of five spawns after roughly a minute and they spawn indefinitely until the player gets a piece.[^I-8v5RpD8KY-97]
* If a Temple Trekking event features Abador Crank, the player should talk to him quickly and evade the event, as he overheals HP and gives a Defence buff.[^I-8v5RpD8KY-97]
* If the player completes the trek without teleporting out, they can spend the tokens on some XP and teleport back.[^I-8v5RpD8KY-97]
* For Ironmen some Temple Trekking resource rewards may be useful, but Gnomonkey says they are very small so players should not waste time doing events to get them.[^I-8v5RpD8KY-130]
