## Methods

- Start at the Temple Trekking board and escort either Dalcian Fang or Fiona Fray, the two easy escorts. ([2018-12-03 0:33](https://youtu.be/I-8v5RpD8KY?t=33))
- Choose Route 1, because Routes 2 and 3 are harder and do not let the player evade the combat events, which wastes a lot of time. ([2018-12-03 1:07](https://youtu.be/I-8v5RpD8KY?t=67))

## Tips

- If the player sees a combat event they cannot evade that is not the bridge event, they should teleport back to Burgh de Rott with the diary legs and start again. ([2018-12-03 1:07](https://youtu.be/I-8v5RpD8KY?t=67))
- The bridge event has a large broken bridge and undead lumberjacks come out of the water to attack the player. ([2018-12-03 1:07](https://youtu.be/I-8v5RpD8KY?t=67))
- A very similar-looking event has no lumberjacks and has the player chopping a cluster of trees; if that tree cluster appears in the bridge event the player should teleport out and start again. ([2018-12-03 1:07](https://youtu.be/I-8v5RpD8KY?t=67))
- In the bridge event a wave of five lumberjacks attacks; if no piece drops, another wave of five spawns after roughly a minute and they spawn indefinitely until the player gets a piece. ([2018-12-03 1:37](https://youtu.be/I-8v5RpD8KY?t=97))
- If a Temple Trekking event features Abador Crank, the player should talk to him quickly and evade the event, as he overheals HP and gives a Defence buff. ([2018-12-03 1:37](https://youtu.be/I-8v5RpD8KY?t=97))
- If the player completes the trek without teleporting out, they can spend the tokens on some XP and teleport back. ([2018-12-03 1:37](https://youtu.be/I-8v5RpD8KY?t=97))
- For Ironmen some Temple Trekking resource rewards may be useful, but Gnomonkey says they are very small so players should not waste time doing events to get them. ([2018-12-03 2:10](https://youtu.be/I-8v5RpD8KY?t=130))
