# RuneScape 3 Leagues

## Stats and numbers

- RS3 Leagues works like Old School Leagues: boosted XP rates, with tasks that unlock relics at certain tiers which hugely buff the player (fast mining, woodcutting, catching, up to turning into a literal god). ([2025-09-19 1:01](https://youtu.be/bHKYk5VY_oI?t=61))
- This RS3 league does not include combat masteries or Echo bosses as seen in Echo Leagues; Gnomonkey considers it a simpler, more straightforward league that may suit players easing into RS3. ([2025-09-19 1:32](https://youtu.be/bHKYk5VY_oI?t=92))
- There are no microtransactions whatsoever in RS3 Leagues, as everybody is an Ironman and Ironman in the base game already has none. ([2025-09-19 2:31](https://youtu.be/bHKYk5VY_oI?t=151))
- Gnomonkey estimates tier 3 is roughly 10% of the total points needed for tier 7, and that early progress was about 20% of the speed of an Echo League; in Echo Leagues a solo player had all relics and combat masteries unlocked by the end of day one. ([2025-09-19 3:32](https://youtu.be/bHKYk5VY_oI?t=212))
- Some quests auto-complete in RS3 Leagues, mainly the elf questline, but many quest lines such as the vampire questline are not auto-unlocked. ([2025-09-19 4:33](https://youtu.be/bHKYk5VY_oI?t=273))
- The combat relic is tier 5, which Gnomonkey says would take roughly three days of play from the beginning point spread to reach, so players do little combat in the first days and mostly skill and quest. ([2025-09-19 6:06](https://youtu.be/bHKYk5VY_oI?t=366))
- RS3 Leagues has combat unlocks called blessings, similar to the combat point systems in Old School Leagues. ([2026-08-23 5:18](https://youtu.be/59f7Sm5ip_I?t=318))
- RS3 Leagues gives up to four resets of your choices, just like Old School Leagues, so you can try builds and change if they don't work. ([2026-08-23 6:52](https://youtu.be/59f7Sm5ip_I?t=412))
- RS3 Leagues combat point tasks (blessing tasks) range from easy ones, such as those tied to a new giant boss called Ivor similar to Obor, to very hard ones like defeating Telos at 4,000% enrage or killing a camel warrior needing 96 Slayer. ([2026-08-23 7:48](https://youtu.be/59f7Sm5ip_I?t=468))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey's first relic pick was Golden Touch, which acts like Old School's Sage's greaves granting Agility XP while running (he hit 99 Agility passively in hour five) and gives much more GP from pickpocketing, leaving him with 200 million coins. ([2026-08-23 4:22](https://youtu.be/59f7Sm5ip_I?t=262))
- Gnomonkey's RS3 Leagues build was a Drygore mace with a shield and heavy tank armor, where the more defence he had the more damage he dealt via blessings, with empowered defensive abilities such as Bash and an AoE Reflect. ([2026-08-23 5:52](https://youtu.be/59f7Sm5ip_I?t=352))
- For learning RS3 Leagues, Gnomonkey links the guide he used for the first six hours and RSGuide.com by RS Guy, which explains how RS3 systems work. ([2026-08-23 9:47](https://youtu.be/59f7Sm5ip_I?t=587))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey played the RS3 Leagues for quite a while, streamed it, had a lot of fun, and recommends anyone curious to give it a try since the worst that can happen is logging out. ([2025-09-19 0:00](https://youtu.be/bHKYk5VY_oI))
- Gnomonkey says this is RS3's very first league and it shows, with progression extremely slow even after a change cutting the points required per relic tier by about 40%. ([2025-09-19 3:03](https://youtu.be/bHKYk5VY_oI?t=183))
- Gnomonkey says RS3 Leagues has no region locking but did not add extra points to compensate, so players have roughly the same point total as an Old School league across a much bigger game map. ([2025-09-19 4:02](https://youtu.be/bHKYk5VY_oI?t=242))
- Gnomonkey does not expect to return to RS3 Leagues because Jagex have been slow on changes and say they cannot do much to auto-complete quests; he thinks they needed more time to learn from Old School's league mistakes and the hype has died. ([2025-09-19 27:04](https://youtu.be/bHKYk5VY_oI?t=1624))
- Gnomonkey believes the RS3 Leagues is RuneScape 3's last chance to use a bump in player numbers to demand sweeping changes. ([2025-09-19 27:04](https://youtu.be/bHKYk5VY_oI?t=1624))
- Gnomonkey says RuneScape 3's second ever Leagues is a great experience, very comparable to an Old School league, because progression is fairly quick, you feel powerful and he barely had to do any quests, unlike the first RS3 Leagues which he disliked. ([2026-08-23 0:00](https://youtu.be/59f7Sm5ip_I))
- Gnomonkey says RS3 builds are very strong in Leagues so you don't need to be good at combat to try things, and abilities are added gradually as you level, which he says makes the ability bar less overwhelming. ([2026-08-23 1:27](https://youtu.be/59f7Sm5ip_I?t=87))
- Gnomonkey says that this Leagues he hasn't needed a quest helper plugin like in the last one, so the lack of plugins has not detracted from it. ([2026-08-23 4:23](https://youtu.be/59f7Sm5ip_I?t=263))
- Gnomonkey likes the RS3 Leagues passive tiers, which give massive boosts such as Slayer points and Reaper points being five times more common, tons of Dungeoneering tokens, and unlocking all skill cape perks passively for 99 skills. ([2026-08-23 4:49](https://youtu.be/59f7Sm5ip_I?t=289))
- Gnomonkey's major complaint with RS3 Leagues 2 is that unlocking the final region needs about 120 tasks, there are too few easy bulk tasks in each region, and unlocking areas can be a slog. ([2026-08-23 6:40](https://youtu.be/59f7Sm5ip_I?t=400))
- Gnomonkey says he will check out every future RS3 Leagues and hopes RS3 gets a resurgence, but is not sure he will become an RS3 main. ([2026-08-23 26:07](https://youtu.be/59f7Sm5ip_I?t=1567))

## Events

- Gnomonkey, led by RS3 streamer RS Guy along with Foe, Kirby and Sick Nerd, cleared a 4,000% enrage Amascut in RS3 Leagues, which he calls the hardest group content he has done in either game. ([2026-08-23 0:27](https://youtu.be/59f7Sm5ip_I?t=27))
- RS3 does not have plugin support yet; there is Alt1, which scans the screen but is not a hooked plugin, and Jagex is planning to release plugin support in a couple of months. ([2026-08-23 3:35](https://youtu.be/59f7Sm5ip_I?t=215))
- Gnomonkey says the chaotic pop-ups and sounds that blocked the settings at the start of Leagues were a real bug that Jagex fixed, and he made fun of it as a meme. ([2026-08-23 8:36](https://youtu.be/59f7Sm5ip_I?t=516))
