**Gambit** is an other topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2023-03-07 and 2023-04-19. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Gambit increases accuracy with all combat styles by 1% per 3% missing HP, which is 33% increased accuracy at 1 HP.[^trol59VccdE-285]

## Tips

* Streamer and PvMer Hugo suggested Gambit give a flat 10% accuracy boost and 30% special attack accuracy, which would keep it worth flicking and somewhat fix the defence draining meta by letting Bandos godsword and hammers land more consistently.[^trol59VccdE-318]
* Gambit is incredibly powerful at Tekton, and he had fun flicking it at the Ranged Vanguards with step-unders; it also works with necks.[^8VyPkG4sEwQ-574]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey calls Gambit probably the most interesting prayer offered because it is an overhead usable alongside the offensive boost prayers, so it could be flicked to between protection prayers.[^trol59VccdE-285]
* Gnomonkey dislikes the low life mechanic because it basically requires a locator orb everywhere, and in some situations you cannot use a low locator orb; he thinks a flat buff would make more sense.[^trol59VccdE-285]
* Gnomonkey says Gambit is great: flicking it is fun and it is not a big enough buff to feel necessary anywhere.[^8VyPkG4sEwQ-574]
