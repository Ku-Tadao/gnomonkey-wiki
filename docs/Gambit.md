# Gambit

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2023-03-07 to 2023-04-19). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Stats and numbers

- Gambit increases accuracy with all combat styles by 1% per 3% missing HP, which is 33% increased accuracy at 1 HP. ([2023-03-07 4:45](https://youtu.be/trol59VccdE?t=285))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey notes that streamer and PvMer Hugo suggested Gambit give a flat 10% accuracy boost and 30% special attack accuracy, which would keep it worth flicking and somewhat fix the defence draining meta by letting Bandos godsword and hammers land more consistently. ([2023-03-07 5:18](https://youtu.be/trol59VccdE?t=318))
- Gnomonkey says Gambit is incredibly powerful at Tekton, and he had fun flicking it at the Ranged Vanguards with step-unders; it also works with necks. ([2023-04-19 9:34](https://youtu.be/8VyPkG4sEwQ?t=574))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey calls Gambit probably the most interesting prayer offered because it is an overhead usable alongside the offensive boost prayers, so it could be flicked to between protection prayers. ([2023-03-07 4:45](https://youtu.be/trol59VccdE?t=285))
- Gnomonkey dislikes the low life mechanic because it basically requires a locator orb everywhere, and in some situations you cannot use a low locator orb; he thinks a flat buff would make more sense. ([2023-03-07 4:45](https://youtu.be/trol59VccdE?t=285))
- Gnomonkey says Gambit is great: flicking it is fun and it is not a big enough buff to feel necessary anywhere. ([2023-04-19 9:34](https://youtu.be/8VyPkG4sEwQ?t=574))
