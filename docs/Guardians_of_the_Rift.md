# Guardians of the Rift

## Methods

- To reach Guardians of the Rift head to the basement of the Wizards' Tower, using a Necklace of Passage, fairy ring code DIS, or the minigame teleport; enter through the large magic gate, with a bank chest nearby. ([2022-03-30 3:57](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=237))
- Mine the large guardian remains until a portal appears at roughly 250 fragments mined (a bubble shows its location at top left); it teleports you to the west cliff, where huge guardian remains give fully crafted essence, so mine until all pouches are full and leave. ([2022-03-30 10:46](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=646))
- In a mass, enter the highest level available altar; if it is within five seconds of changing and no overcharged cell is offered, wait for something better, then make a guardian if not capped and craft more essence at the bench. ([2022-03-30 10:46](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=646))
- The mass loop is to alternate making an inventory of essence, crafting, taking the portal, and mining an inventory of essence, crafting, until the game ends; make a guardian whenever possible, otherwise upgrade or charge the shields. ([2022-03-30 11:20](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=680))
- On small scale, Gnomonkey maintains the main gate's HP by throwing weak cells into it whenever he runs to an altar; he calls this Gandalf Tech because he only guards the main gate and lets nothing pass. ([2022-03-30 11:51](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=711))
- With at least two outfit pieces and the colossal pouch you can bind combination runes in the main game, which matters far more in small scale; bring a binding necklace, wear a tome of fire, and use earth or water runes on the altar. ([2022-03-30 12:24](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=744))
- At small scale the strategy is the same from one to ten players: the main shield is far more important, powerful guardians are extremely important, and at the start you throw a cell into the main gate and run to the large guardians. ([2022-03-30 13:20](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=800))
- An alt with 27 Runecrafting can help: it wears full graceful (or brings a stamina potion), carries a full inventory of binding necklaces, and repeatedly charges the gates, leaving right before the enemies enter so scale is unaffected. ([2022-03-30 13:50](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=830))
- At small scale, mine at the large guardians until you have 120 to 150 fragments (varies with rewards), and after the first three altar shifts the portal pops up; run to the workbench once you have enough. ([2022-03-30 14:24](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=864))
- In a solo, if you make the first altar you can create a guardian, throw stones into the main guardian, and hop into another overcharged altar without essence to get another cell, guaranteeing two overcharged guardians at the start; you must be in the third altar immediately when it pops to make the portal. ([2022-03-30 16:19](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=979))

## Requirements

- The mandatory requirements for Guardians of the Rift are just 27 Runecrafting and completing the Temple of the Eye quest, which takes about twenty minutes. ([2022-03-30 0:30](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=30))
- 56 Agility is extremely useful in Guardians of the Rift for mining more essence early on; Runecrafting levels 44, 54, 65 and 77 each boost rates at an unlocked altar. ([2022-03-30 0:30](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=30))
- NPC Contact (Lunar Diplomacy) is a soft requirement for Guardians of the Rift because it is needed to repair pouches and guardians. ([2022-03-30 0:58](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=58))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says the minigame is decently intensive but provides a very solid 60 to 75k Runecrafting XP per hour and around 1m gp per hour. ([2022-03-30 0:00](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I))
- The energies at the top left are your rewards for the game, capped at 1,200 combined energy per game. ([2022-03-30 8:46](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=526))
- Energy comes from the stone type used on the main guardian and from using cells; creating a guardian gives the most points (45 for an overcharged cell), upgrading a barrier gives 50% of that in both energy types, and recharging gives 50% of an upgrade in both. ([2022-03-30 9:17](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=557))
- An alt improves Guardians of the Rift game speed by about 30 seconds per game, and one alt can cover as many as five people. ([2022-03-30 13:50](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=830))
- Each altar shift has a 1 in 4 chance of an overcharged cell, or 1 in 2 for at least strong; if all three altar shifts are below strong, Gnomonkey says the game is pretty much lost. ([2022-03-30 15:22](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=922))
- The tarnished locket can be received from any ornate pouch pulled from a reward and can be given to the Lumbridge Guide for the amulet of the eye, which Gnomonkey says does nothing interesting but looks stylish. ([2022-03-30 20:23](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=1223))
- The Lost bag cosmetic fills the cape slot and bounces when you walk or run, though Gnomonkey says the max cape is always the most stylish cape-slot option. ([2022-03-30 20:50](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=1250))
- A guardian orb can be bought from the wizard for 3,000 abyssal pearls (possibly reduced in the future) and transmogs your Rift Guardian to look like the main guardian. ([2022-03-30 20:50](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=1250))
- Under the lantern change, blisterwood increases blood runes crafted by 20%, magic logs increase runes crafted by 10%, and redwood makes essence pouches not degrade and gives 5% more runes. ([2026-06-04 9:10](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=550))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey highly recommends downloading the Guardians of the Rift plugin from the RuneLite plugin hub for timers on altars and portals. ([2022-03-30 9:47](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=587))
- Gnomonkey recommends learning Guardians of the Rift in mass worlds such as World 420 (almost always full) or World 302; masses are much slower and worse than low scale games but easy to jump into. ([2022-03-30 9:47](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=587))
- Before starting, right-click each of the low level Runecrafting totems and toggle them to avoid useless talismans; do not toggle fire, death or blood altars since those give extremely useful talismans. ([2022-03-30 10:15](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=615))
- With more than one player the main gate holds four weak cells before full health, so with more than four players some should throw cells into the side corner gates. ([2022-03-30 13:50](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=830))
- Watch the plugin timer: if using an alt, have it leave before the timer reaches 110, and when it reaches 115 start running regardless of how much essence you have, which is right when the first altars pop up. ([2022-03-30 14:54](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=894))
- Powerful guardians are far more impactful than upgrading the shield; use medium cells to upgrade the main gate since it can only go one tier at a time, and you need at least 50% of the guardian cap in strong or overcharged guardians at the beginning to survive (a single overcharged guardian in a solo). ([2022-03-30 15:52](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=952))
- If the main gate goes down while mining at the beginning, repair it with the essence just mined and throw a weak cell in when you can; from there it is the same as a mass. ([2022-03-30 16:50](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=1010))
- Be careful when the guardian is about to reach 60% total energy: bring a cell if charging past that threshold and immediately upgrade the main gate, since upgrading instantly heals a tremendous amount of health. ([2022-03-30 16:50](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=1010))
- Gnomonkey says with a solid team you typically end the game right after crafting with the essence from the second portal, and that low scale gives similar points to masses but ends in literally half the time, giving more XP and points an hour. ([2022-03-30 17:20](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=1040))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the minigame's rewards dramatically buffed every essence-using Runecrafting method (ZMI, lava and mud runes, regular runes), making them solid gp and XP. ([2022-03-30 0:00](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I))
- Gnomonkey says lower scale Guardians of the Rift games are much faster and more consistent but require thought and planning to not lose; depending on rewards you can hit as many as 10 game completions an hour. ([2022-03-30 11:20](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=680))
- Gnomonkey asks Jagex not to nerf the combination rune stone gain, but warns not to get greedy because you still need catalytic energy for points. ([2022-03-30 12:52](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=772))
- Gnomonkey says making guardians with weak cells at small scale is a great way to lose games, because creatures only stop attacking the gate if attacked and weak guardians have a very low max hit and barely help. ([2022-03-30 14:24](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=864))
- Gnomonkey's main complaint about Guardians of the Rift at the time was crashers in small scale games. ([2022-03-30 21:16](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=1276))
- Gnomonkey calls the Abyssal Protector easily one of the ugliest pets in the game, putting it in his F-tier. ([2022-03-30 21:22](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=1282))
- Gnomonkey says the lantern change is a big deal and good for people manually crafting blood runes with blood essence, for example on an ironman. ([2026-06-04 9:37](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=577))

## Events

- Gnomonkey says the Runecrafting minigame Guardians of the Rift had just recently come out when the video was made and completely shifted the entire Runecrafting skill. ([2022-03-30 0:00](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I))
- The Summer Sweep-Up lets players purchase the abyssal needle with pearls (the Goter reward currency), so players who go very dry can buy it. ([2026-06-04 8:45](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=525))
- The Abyssal Lantern is changed to function outside of Guardians of the Rift when burning logs that give an effect beyond the activity; willow, blisterwood, magic and redwood will work, while normal, oak and maple will not. ([2026-06-04 9:10](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=550))

## Trivia

- Mining level does not affect Guardians of the Rift, but a better pickaxe does; a crystal pickaxe works there and burns through charges quickly. ([2022-03-30 0:30](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=30))
- The goal is to craft runes at the various altars using Guardian essence; crafting runes provides guardian stones, which charge the central guardian's energy, and the game is won and rewards given when energy reaches 100%. ([2022-03-30 3:57](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=237))
- The central guardian's energy doubles as its health bar and drops if it is damaged; abyssal creatures constantly attack it and must be fended off by crafting guardians with energy cells and charging shields with the same cells. ([2022-03-30 4:27](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=267))
- Fragments for crafting guardian essence are mined from loose guardian piles; the large guardians to the east, down the shortcut, give an extra fragment per action; one fragment equals one essence but fragments are stackable. ([2022-03-30 4:27](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=267))
- Roughly every two minutes a portal spawns (any time after 110 on the plugin timer) that takes you to the West Cliff to mine huge guardians for guardian essence directly, which is far faster than fragments; it remains for a bit after the game so you can wait at the cliff for the next game for a head start. ([2022-03-30 4:59](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=299))
- Fragments are taken to the workbench in the southwest to create guardian essence; you spend most of the game there. ([2022-03-30 4:59](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=299))
- There are 12 central runecrafting totems that lead to the respective altars; two of them light up every 20 seconds, always one elemental and one catalytic altar. ([2022-03-30 5:27](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=327))
- Crafting essence at an active altar gives runes plus a base amount of catalytic or elemental stones depending on the altar, and charges an uncharged cell into a powered cell whose tier depends on the altar used. ([2022-03-30 5:27](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=327))
- Cell tiers: elemental altars range from air (weakest) to fire (most powerful); for catalytic altars body and mind are weak, cosmic and chaos medium, nature and law strong, and death and blood overcharged. ([2022-03-30 5:56](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=356))
- A created guardian's max hit and HP scale with the tier of the cell used; overcharged guardians are extremely powerful, and the cap on guardians is two in a solo, increasing by two for each extra player up to ten. ([2022-03-30 6:28](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=388))
- Shields can only be upgraded to the next tier regardless of cell tier, so an overcharged cell turns a weak shield into a medium one; a same-or-lower tier cell just heals the shield; upgrading raises max health and sets current health to half the next tier's max. ([2022-03-30 6:28](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=388))
- A cell does not need essence to be charged; you can enter an altar without essence just to charge a cell. ([2022-03-30 6:55](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=415))
- If a shield is destroyed, you must repair it with 10 guardian essence before adding a cell, so avoid letting it break. ([2022-03-30 7:22](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=442))
- Crafting at an altar sometimes gives a talisman that lets you consume it to enter that altar again even when inactive (not consumed if the altar is active); these are very useful for high level altars. ([2022-03-30 7:22](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=442))
- Abyssal creatures spawn in waves attacking the front shield (the main gate) first, and if there are too many they spread to the side barriers; creatures only retaliate against guardians that attack them and otherwise walk past, so the shield often takes a few hits regardless. ([2022-03-30 7:22](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=442))
- If a guardian is killed you can mine its remains to retrieve some crafted essence, usually about an inventory and a half. ([2022-03-30 8:11](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=491))
- When the main guardian's energy reaches 60%, the rift quakes and heavily damages all shields (it cannot kill them but can leave them at 1 HP) and abyssal spawn rates increase from then on. ([2022-03-30 8:16](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=496))
- The main guardian defends itself but its attacks are very slow and it only attacks if something passes the main gates. ([2022-03-30 8:16](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=496))
- To search the rewards guardian you need one of each reward point, catalytic and elemental; 100 energy in either type converts to one point (a tuning), and points are banked: 700 catalytic and 200 elemental becomes 7 and 2, usable for two searches with 5 catalytic points banked. ([2022-03-30 8:46](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=526))
- Creating a catalytic guardian gives catalytic energy and an elemental guardian gives elemental energy; where the cell came from does not matter. ([2022-03-30 9:17](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=557))
- A game starts with a two minute warmup; for the first 30 seconds you cannot do anything, so collect ten uncharged cells and then go to the east rock cliff and mine the large guardian remains. ([2022-03-30 10:15](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=615))
- At small scale the side gates do nothing and the enemies head directly to the main gate, so only the main gate matters; it is easy to lose a low scale game if you are not careful. ([2022-03-30 12:24](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=744))
- If you bind combination runes in the minigame, the stones you receive equal the runes created, so with multipliers it gives far more stones than any other altar; Gnomonkey says with the full outfit and lantern you can get over a hundred stones per full craft at an elemental altar, ending a solo game in three crafts. ([2022-03-30 12:52](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=772))
- The first altar's uptime is completely random and can be as short as one tick; Gnomonkey hopes Jagex changes this, calling it one of the most annoying parts of the minigame. ([2022-03-30 14:54](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=894))
- At the time of the video the drop rate of the Abyssal Protector pet from the rewards guardian was unknown; Gnomonkey guessed 1/3,000 to 1/5,000. ([2022-03-30 20:50](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=1250))
- The Rift Guardian pet cannot be received from the Guardians of the Rift minigame and must be grinded separately. ([2022-03-30 21:22](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=1282))
