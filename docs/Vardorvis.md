# Vardorvis

## Methods

- Vardorvis always passes his accuracy roll if you are not using a protection prayer; with a protection prayer active he rolls against your defence, which Gnomonkey thinks is unique to this boss. ([2024-05-13 3:58](https://youtu.be/hI6cALUKOzQ?t=238))

## Stats and numbers

- As you damage Vardorvis, his defence level drops over time. ([2024-05-13 2:57](https://youtu.be/hI6cALUKOzQ?t=177))
- Vardorvis heals based on the visible damage splat, so reducing the splat with the Elysian spirit shield proc reduces his healing; Gnomonkey observed healing drop from 19 to 9. ([2024-05-13 6:30](https://youtu.be/hI6cALUKOzQ?t=390))
- Gnomonkey calculates that Justiciar armour lowers Vardorvis's accuracy against you from 91% to 70%, a 21% drop, and thinks Justiciar might be underrated here. ([2024-05-13 11:42](https://youtu.be/hI6cALUKOzQ?t=702))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says the difference between Void and Torva is about 10% Vardorvis accuracy, and that the more Vardorvis hits you the more he heals. ([2024-05-13 3:58](https://youtu.be/hI6cALUKOzQ?t=238))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey prefers using good offensive items on Vardorvis over tank pieces, because hitting him harder is probably good as well as tanking. ([2024-05-13 3:58](https://youtu.be/hI6cALUKOzQ?t=238))

## Records

- Gnomonkey kills Vardorvis in about two minutes using the wiki's worst downgraded gear with an Elysian spirit shield, which he says is better than expected. ([2024-05-13 7:00](https://youtu.be/hI6cALUKOzQ?t=420))
- Gnomonkey's budget Soulreaper axe kill of Vardorvis took 125 seconds, which he says is about average for a Soulreaper axe kill (around 120), and thinks a faster time is feasible. ([2024-05-13 9:34](https://youtu.be/hI6cALUKOzQ?t=574))
