**Vardorvis** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2024-05-13. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Vardorvis always passes his accuracy roll if you are not using a protection prayer; with a protection prayer active he rolls against your defence, which Gnomonkey thinks is unique to this boss.[^hI6cALUKOzQ-238]

## Stats and numbers

* As you damage Vardorvis, his defence level drops over time.[^hI6cALUKOzQ-177]
* Vardorvis heals based on the visible damage splat, so reducing the splat with the Elysian spirit shield proc reduces his healing; Gnomonkey observed healing drop from 19 to 9.[^hI6cALUKOzQ-390]
* Gnomonkey calculates that Justiciar armour lowers Vardorvis's accuracy against you from 91% to 70%, a 21% drop, and thinks Justiciar might be underrated here.[^hI6cALUKOzQ-702]

## Tips

* The difference between Void and Torva is about 10% Vardorvis accuracy, and that the more Vardorvis hits you the more he heals.[^hI6cALUKOzQ-238]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey prefers using good offensive items on Vardorvis over tank pieces, because hitting him harder is probably good as well as tanking.[^hI6cALUKOzQ-238]

## Records

* Gnomonkey kills Vardorvis in about two minutes using the wiki's worst downgraded gear with an Elysian spirit shield, which he says is better than expected.[^hI6cALUKOzQ-420]
* Gnomonkey's budget Soulreaper axe kill of Vardorvis took 125 seconds, which he says is about average for a Soulreaper axe kill (around 120), and thinks a faster time is feasible.[^hI6cALUKOzQ-574]
