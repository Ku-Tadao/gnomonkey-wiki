The Fortis Colosseum is the wave-based Varlamore combat encounter that Gnomonkey describes as the "Blue Inferno"[^88kOTb9qUKc-0]. He held its world record in 2024, wrote a series of guides on it, and regards it as an excellent, fast-paced encounter and a strong money-maker, while repeatedly criticising its rewards and invocation RNG.

[TOC]

## Overview

- Announced as a shorter wave-based encounter than the [Inferno](Inferno.md), paying out both GP and untradable unlocks[^88kOTb9qUKc-0]. The Blue Inferno was sold as a 12-wave encounter, with waves 9 through 12 aimed at players capable of obtaining an Infernal cape[^88kOTb9qUKc-0]. Gnomonkey says the last boss, [Sol Heredit](Sol_Heredit.md), fight is Inferno mixed with [Tombs of Amascut](Tombs_of_Amascut.md)[^IR0MIzsbFeM-84].
- Each wave rolls a modifier that buffs enemies, debuffs the player or modifies terrain, and enemies stack through the whole encounter[^88kOTb9qUKc-0]. See [Colosseum invocations](Colosseum_invocations.md).
- Endless mode lets the player re-run every wave with all accumulated modifiers plus new ones, with the reward being Glory[^88kOTb9qUKc-31][^88kOTb9qUKc-384]. Per the announcement blog Glory is not a currency but a representation of personal best performance, higher for finishing faster with fewer mistakes, and untradeable rewards need a Glory threshold[^88kOTb9qUKc-354][^88kOTb9qUKc-384]. In March 2024 he said he did not know exactly how Glory works and thought it also depends on wave speed[^EVOs96kyHSM-1383].
- The Colosseum came out on March 20th, 2024, the last high-level update before the [Doom of Mokhaiotl](Doom_of_Mokhaiotl.md), roughly ten months before January 2025[^kkmUE6oQxy4-1264].
- Gnomonkey likes that it feels like being dropped in at around Inferno wave 50: fast paced, short and unique, though without the Inferno's chip damage through prayer[^IR0MIzsbFeM-514]. He says it has unique monster design and an excellent boss encounter[^wiJAXY2rvSo-297].

## Waves and enemies

- Spawns arrive roughly every 30 to 40 seconds through the gate[^EVOs96kyHSM-608]. After exactly 40 seconds (40.2 seconds according to the plugin timer) reinforcements spawn from whichever of two gates is closest, so every spawn has reinforcements and there is time pressure[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-319][^EZs7lRnEL0Y-1357][^kIfF1pNro2c-268]. From wave 6 onward shockwave colossi appear and the reinforcements then include a Minotaur[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-1769]. There is a delay at the start of each wave to turn prayer on[^kIfF1pNro2c-458].
- **Javelin colossi / Fremennik**: throw a javelin every five attacks that falls from the air, cannot be prayed against, and hits very hard[^EVOs96kyHSM-520][^EZs7lRnEL0Y-1709]. Shockwave colossi also throw a spear every five attacks, hitting for about what they would off prayer but guaranteed[^kIfF1pNro2c-208].
- **Jaguars**: standard melee with three hit splats of max 47, which Gnomonkey says can kill in one hit[^EVOs96kyHSM-545].
- **Manticores**: attack tick after tick in the order mage, range, melee from the tick they see the player; flicking them is a major skill[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-1470]. They do not always keep the same attack style[^c6oOmOGl5Lg-1138].
- **Shamans / Minotaurs**: shamans stop moving on seeing the player (mage block), Minotaurs keep moving[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-1604]. The shaman has a ten-tile range and the shockwave colossus fifteen[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-2314]. A Colossus always hits harder than a Shaman, though the Shaman is more likely to be tanked[^kIfF1pNro2c-302].
- **Minotaur healing**: it has a five-tick melee and heals any damaged target in its line of sight that was not attacked, by his estimate over about 25% of the target's health[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-1799]; in April 2024 he estimated a heal cap of about 20 percent, so chipping each target avoids healing[^O_eC5OV3w1o-569].
- **Sol Heredit**: 800 ranged and mage defence, weak only to slash, so the [Scythe of Vitur](Scythe_of_Vitur.md) is a must[^IR0MIzsbFeM-0]. Equal defence on everything except stab being worse than slash[^nEIeyY_m_gw-1152]. It teleports the player to the arena centre, so everything should be picked up and special attack should be full beforehand[^kIfF1pNro2c-1330]. Double-hammering saves about 11 seconds if the spec lands[^kIfF1pNro2c-1415].
- **Hardest waves**: wave 6 is where learners die most[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-1604], wave 11 is the hardest with two manticores, a javelin and a shockwave colossus[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-2525]. In March 2025 he said wave 8 is the hardest and wave 11 very dangerous[^c6oOmOGl5Lg-572]; in December 2025 (pillarless) he called wave 8 and wave 11 by far the two hardest[^E51JMiU2gsk-603]. See [Pillarless Colosseum](Pillarless_Colosseum.md).

## Strategy

### Setup and gear

- Recommended first-run setup (March 2024): max melee, with a mace and Ellie[^EVOs96kyHSM-0]; roughly seven brews and as many restores[^EVOs96kyHSM-56]. Used 4-way [Shadow](Tumeken's_shadow.md) to one-shot the melee Fremennik and a Webweaver bow to 2-shot the mage, SGS specs on the ranger (specs count as max hits), a mace for small targets, and thralls with soul runes for [Death Charge](Death_Charge.md) to keep SGS specs up[^IR0MIzsbFeM-0][^IR0MIzsbFeM-29].
- Budget (March 2024): about 180 million gp, which he calls the minimum he would recommend; he suggests grinding GP rather than learning in worse gear[^nEIeyY_m_gw-0][^nEIeyY_m_gw-233]. A Shadow is not needed; the scythe is 30 percent better than the whip[^nEIeyY_m_gw-1152][^nEIeyY_m_gw-1513]. Without the scythe, a tent whip run is about 30% worse[^EVOs96kyHSM-1511].
- Updated (June 2024): after the [Soulreaper axe](Soulreaper_axe.md) spec changes it is within 10% of Scythe; the suggested budget setup swaps the Tent Whip for the axe, adding a Sanguinesti staff, a hammer, an SGS for specs and an Attle weapon that two-shots the Mage[^kIfF1pNro2c-0]. The hammer can be skipped with the staff but is worth bringing; an [Elder maul](Elder_maul.md) is better if owned[^kIfF1pNro2c-56]. Runes: death, fire, soul and cosmic, with Arceuus spells and blood runes pulled from the staff[^kIfF1pNro2c-116]. See also [Fortis Colosseum setup](Fortis_Colosseum_setup.md).

### Pillars and positioning

- March 2024: the only useful tiles are the two B5 tiles[^EVOs96kyHSM-212]; standing between the two key green tiles prevents close spawns, and he preferred the South pillar start because a melee enemy exiting that side gets corner-trapped[^nEIeyY_m_gw-410][^nEIeyY_m_gw-443].
- July 2024 (updated guide): the guide is titled the SW Pillar Master Guide, and he says he sees a lot of misinformation about which pillar is best[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-0]. Spawn points within about five tiles are blocked; the B5 tile is named after a Reddit user and G5 after himself, which opens one far spawn, blocks only two and puts him behind the pillar instantly[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-1051][^EZs7lRnEL0Y-1079][^EZs7lRnEL0Y-1129]. Tile markers were shared via a chat command[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-0]. See [Fortis Colosseum pillars](Fortis_Colosseum_pillars.md).
- Spawn manipulation forces spawns toward the middle[^IR0MIzsbFeM-540]; if patched, he says invocations would need rebalancing[^IR0MIzsbFeM-540]. See [Spawn manipulation](Spawn_manipulation.md).

### Tips

- Start each wave from the same tile, kill the Berserker first, then the lowest-HP target to flick less; do not ignore the Shockwave Colossus (~120 health, hits around 50s)[^EVOs96kyHSM-402][^kIfF1pNro2c-179][^kIfF1pNro2c-540].
- Off-ticking makes waves easier but is rarely used, and on wave 11 he stays at the bottom of the pillar rather than rotating when the off-tick is obvious[^kIfF1pNro2c-1235]. On pillarless wave 11 he went to the bottom corner so the shockwave goes around the manticore[^E51JMiU2gsk-1295].
- Take Blasphemy first among invocations; otherwise take or finish an invocation already offered, which removes it from the pool[^kIfF1pNro2c-147][^kIfF1pNro2c-356]. Without Blasphemy early there is less flicking[^EVOs96kyHSM-1783].
- Divine before the end of a wave to get Blood Fury health back[^nEIeyY_m_gw-2409]. The Saradomin godsword spec meter is not shown, so check it[^kIfF1pNro2c-208].
- Do not panic after a mistake: put gear back and keep going[^EVOs96kyHSM-2452].

## Speedrunning and records

| Date | Record |
|---|---|
| 2024-03-22 | World record claim, best time 26:22 (global best 26:22)[^IR0MIzsbFeM-0] |
| 2024-04-01 | Video titled "16:10 WR"; wave two skip took 39 seconds, since reinforcements spawn at 40 seconds both targets must die before then[^O_eC5OV3w1o-155] |

- Going for the record needs a wave two skip and a wave four skip[^O_eC5OV3w1o-155]. Methods: off-ticking a mage to sync with the Manticore, starting on the dangerous side before reinforcements, killing the target next to the Minotaur spawn first, and keeping the archer alive to finish simultaneously[^O_eC5OV3w1o-181][^O_eC5OV3w1o-247][^O_eC5OV3w1o-447][^O_eC5OV3w1o-598]. See [Fortis Colosseum speedrun](Fortis_Colosseum_speedrun.md).
- The wave 1 clear takes about 15 seconds and guarantees 80 Sunfire splinters, making it common for bots[^ZB3ldcsIivg-85].

## Rewards and profit

### Rewards

- Announced rewards: [Dizana's Quiver](Dizana's_Quiver.md), the [Glaive of Ralos](Glaive_of_Ralos.md), [Sunfire runes](Sunfire_runes.md), Sunfire Fanatic armour and an Echo crystal for boots with recoil in a 3x3 area, all created or charged with Sunfire dust[^88kOTb9qUKc-31][^88kOTb9qUKc-64].
- Pre-release Gnomonkey found them lacking apart from the quiver and felt mid-tier prayer ranged armour was out of place[^88kOTb9qUKc-294]. In April 2024 he said all rewards aside from the quiver and the Colosseum cape are dead content[^dkYhPWxsOGk-461]. [Echo boots](Echo_boots.md) recoil one damage per hit splat taken per four ticks, about one-fifth as effective as a recoil ring[^dkYhPWxsOGk-475]. He said the Glaive is outclassed by the Dragon warhammer and Bandos godsword except at Maiden and mega-scale Chambers, and its defence drain of 20 percent total should be higher[^dkYhPWxsOGk-485]. Sunfire armour was called fashion scape, though good that it was obtainable in early waves[^dkYhPWxsOGk-544].
- January-February 2025: he repeated that the rare drops are poor (the prayer armour is useless once obtained, the quiver amazing but untradeable, the Tonalztics of Ralos niche), Sunfire armour does not fit, and buff suggestions were ignored[^kkmUE6oQxy4-656][^kkmUE6oQxy4-666][^oAc5voBILb8-126][^oAc5voBILb8-936]. He thought Jagex was scared to give upgrades with the Colosseum but is much less scared now[^4YGVut4hMtA-1180].
- He also asked Jagex to fix NPC hitboxes[^dkYhPWxsOGk-544] and criticised invocation RNG, wanting Relentless but being offered otherwise[^Aysv5rNWgFs-1627]. In March 2024 he called the system broken, with more resetting than playing due to Doom, Doom Scorpion and Totemic[^wiJAXY2rvSo-0].

### Profit

| Date | Claim |
|---|---|
| 2024-07-07 | About 12 to 15 million GP per hour from splinter and quiver trade-ins plus occasional 10 million uniques[^e0y_auD9JhY-525] |
| 2025-01-30 | Three kills an hour is the best realistic rate; three quivers convert to 7.6 million in splinters plus about 6,000 splinters worth 3.7 million, so about half the profit is splinters[^kkmUE6oQxy4-719] |
| 2025-10-01 | About 6m per hour, due to the low value of Sunfire splinters[^ZB3ldcsIivg-56] |
| 2026-01-23 | A good run is at least a million, a really good one around two million, excluding quiver splinters worth about 500k; wave 10 loot of 75 onyx bolts is about 650k[^5qsH_-3UNQ8-3046][^5qsH_-3UNQ8-3076][^5qsH_-3UNQ8-3578] |

- Current position: the profit now estimated at about 6m per hour (October 2025), down from 12 to 15m per hour in July 2024. Earlier (January 2025) he said the splinter currency was the only thing that kept the Colosseum profitable[^kkmUE6oQxy4-659].

## Order and who should do it

- Gnomonkey would not recommend the Colosseum before the Inferno, because the Inferno cape is too good and each Colosseum death costs 500k[^XMTe9TYbDls-1685][^EZs7lRnEL0Y-0]. He considers them equal in difficulty on a beta world but the Colosseum harder in a normal world[^XMTe9TYbDls-2203]. It is a higher-skill money-maker and the source of the best ranged cape, not for new players[^e0y_auD9JhY-525].
- He would not suggest grinding unless maxed or very comfortable[^e0y_auD9JhY-525]. Rushing it on his group hardcore ironman skips the Vorkath grind[^ETOLP88HtYU-167]; he completed it there and received the quiver[^_cIv_pRhm9U-331] and later did a Hardcore Ironman clear with 38k glory, rank 19 on the hardcore high scores[^c6oOmOGl5Lg-2076].

## Other appearances

- Group hardcore ironman: plan to rush with moons gear using all five lives, 75 Ranged needed for thralls[^ETOLP88HtYU-405][^iUjSbo1TQFQ-0][^iUjSbo1TQFQ-1348]. A wave 10 retry used a six-pillar rotation and Frailty on wave 11[^Aysv5rNWgFs-285][^Aysv5rNWgFs-1630].
- Leagues 5: he could clear it depending on wave and invocation RNG[^M0Y3Hj3YIGw-60].
- 2025-10-29: he used the Colosseum to get dragon arrows for the Doom of Mokhaiotl, and described a wave 11 plan[^0_DI-SbhksU-0][^0_DI-SbhksU-94][^0_DI-SbhksU-123].
- DMM Allstars (2026): he chose it for GP because it is basically safe[^lJexGv6dxu8-802].
