# Opinion: Araxxor Slayer boss design

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2024-06-19 to 2024-08-28). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | opinion |
| **Videos** | 2 (first 2024-06-19, latest 2024-08-28) |

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey estimates Araxxor gives about 70k Slayer XP an hour in max gear, and about 65k with bank trips, which he says is better than any other Slayer boss; he notes Hydra is only about 30k XP an hour. ([2024-08-28 12:46](https://youtu.be/2hGhcuPU-8o?t=766))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says he was so excited for Araxxor but was pretty disappointed after hearing it would not be the same Araxxor as RS3 and would be a toned-down Slayer boss. ([2024-06-19 0:00](https://youtu.be/X0MGEJoT7gM))
- Gnomonkey says that because Araxxor is a Slayer boss he hopes the fight keeps its interesting pieces rather than being watered down for mid-level players. ([2024-06-19 1:55](https://youtu.be/X0MGEJoT7gM?t=115))
- In the video description Gnomonkey calls Araxxor a very easy Slayer boss, easier than the recently released Tormented Demons, and calls it a let down: basically a copy-pasted low level spider boss with a 92 Slayer requirement, Spindel. ([2024-08-28 0:00](https://youtu.be/2hGhcuPU-8o))
- Gnomonkey considers Araxxor somewhere in the middle of existing Slayer bosses: not complex and not interesting, and says Cerberus is honestly a better boss fight and the growth tests are more interesting. ([2024-08-28 11:17](https://youtu.be/2hGhcuPU-8o?t=677))
- Gnomonkey says the ranged spiders currently have huge negatives for killing them, so there is no reason to do so, and suggests they leave a trail of acid or something that makes you want to deal with them. ([2024-08-28 11:46](https://youtu.be/2hGhcuPU-8o?t=706))
- Gnomonkey says the intended risk-reward of killing or not killing eggs does not really exist, since you always kill it the same way at any gear or skill level. ([2024-08-28 12:16](https://youtu.be/2hGhcuPU-8o?t=736))
- Gnomonkey says that if it were a 75 Slayer boss that would be fine, and that the 92 Slayer requirement is what makes it not great. ([2024-08-28 12:46](https://youtu.be/2hGhcuPU-8o?t=766))
- Gnomonkey says Araxxor is honestly probably worth killing just for the Slayer XP. ([2024-08-28 12:46](https://youtu.be/2hGhcuPU-8o?t=766))
