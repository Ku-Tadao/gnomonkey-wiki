**Opinion: Araxxor Slayer boss design** is an opinion topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2024-06-19 and 2024-08-28. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Gnomonkey estimates Araxxor gives about 70k Slayer XP an hour in max gear, and about 65k with bank trips, which he says is better than any other Slayer boss; he notes Hydra is only about 30k XP an hour.[^2hGhcuPU-8o-766]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says he was so excited for Araxxor but was pretty disappointed after hearing it would not be the same Araxxor as RS3 and would be a toned-down Slayer boss.[^X0MGEJoT7gM-0]
* Gnomonkey says that because Araxxor is a Slayer boss he hopes the fight keeps its interesting pieces rather than being watered down for mid-level players.[^X0MGEJoT7gM-115]
* In the video description Gnomonkey calls Araxxor a very easy Slayer boss, easier than the recently released Tormented Demons, and calls it a let down: basically a copy-pasted low level spider boss with a 92 Slayer requirement, Spindel.[^2hGhcuPU-8o-0]
* Gnomonkey considers Araxxor somewhere in the middle of existing Slayer bosses: not complex and not interesting, and says Cerberus is honestly a better boss fight and the growth tests are more interesting.[^2hGhcuPU-8o-677]
* Gnomonkey says the ranged spiders currently have huge negatives for killing them, so there is no reason to do so, and suggests they leave a trail of acid or something that makes you want to deal with them.[^2hGhcuPU-8o-706]
* Gnomonkey says the intended risk-reward of killing or not killing eggs does not really exist, since you always kill it the same way at any gear or skill level.[^2hGhcuPU-8o-736]
* Gnomonkey says that if it were a 75 Slayer boss that would be fine, and that the 92 Slayer requirement is what makes it not great.[^2hGhcuPU-8o-766]
* Gnomonkey says Araxxor is honestly probably worth killing just for the Slayer XP.[^2hGhcuPU-8o-766]

## Trivia

* Gnomonkey credits the Araxxor footage to EvilLucario, whom he calls the Woox of RS3, who has been playing OSRS in a GIM team.[^X0MGEJoT7gM-0]
