**Westham Weasels** is an other topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2026-06-06 and 2026-06-10. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* The Odablock Warriors expect the Rhinos to have won the first breach and to be camping the rope after the team is flushed out.[^FwjOiTIPQXE-180]

## Events

* A Westham Weasels player teleports in on Gnomonkey and freezes him as he tries to escape during the first Deadman Season 3 breach.[^FwjOiTIPQXE-0]
* The Odablock Warriors treat the Westham Weasels and the Rhinos as two clans pursuing them while the Dino Nuggets stall.[^FwjOiTIPQXE-94]
* Gnomonkey reports that the Westham Weasels were hitting a monster, which pulled its aggro onto other players.[^qyG_lTC4pDU-300]
* While the Odablock Warriors lured Dagannoth Rex, another team moved in to attack the Westham Weasels, which Gnomonkey said was great because the other teams were distracted.[^qyG_lTC4pDU-898]
* As the Odablock Warriors finished killing monsters, the Westham Weasels moved back in toward their spot.[^qyG_lTC4pDU-1431]
* He did not want to leave his teammates because the Westham Weasels would then have wiped the whole team.[^qyG_lTC4pDU-2062]
