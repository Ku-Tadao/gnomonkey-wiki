## Tips

- Gnomonkey expects ecumenical keys to work for Nex, preferable to getting kill count, and recommends collecting some before release because getting them will be awful afterwards. ([2021-11-16 1:03](https://youtu.be/fQySZrGmyOY?t=63))
- Gnomonkey warns that if you die at Nex and run back you should take ancient protection, and predicts another 'Hydra day of release scandal' where people lose their gear to stupidity. ([2021-11-16 2:08](https://youtu.be/fQySZrGmyOY?t=128))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey predicts Nex will be behind the frozen door in the God Wars Dungeon; in RS2 it required collecting frozen key fragments from killing monsters of each faction, which shattered after five uses. ([2021-11-16 0:31](https://youtu.be/fQySZrGmyOY?t=31))
- Because the news post said access would be unlocked from a mini quest, Gnomonkey assumes it will be a permanent key gained after the quest, likely still involving killing monsters in the God Wars Dungeon. ([2021-11-16 1:03](https://youtu.be/fQySZrGmyOY?t=63))
- Gnomonkey says Nex will require kill count like any other boss and will almost certainly be the most difficult kill count in the dungeon, as the news post said it consists of spiritual creatures and blood reavers, neither of which are easy. ([2021-11-16 1:03](https://youtu.be/fQySZrGmyOY?t=63))
- Gnomonkey tells PKers to sharpen their pitchforks, since a lot of wealthy PvMers will be in the Wilderness God Wars Dungeon. ([2021-11-16 1:36](https://youtu.be/fQySZrGmyOY?t=96))
- Gnomonkey says Jagex will add the ancient ceremonial robes set, which in RS2 let you access Nex without kill count, but in Old School it will not bypass kill count and is practically just cosmetic monk robes. ([2021-11-16 1:36](https://youtu.be/fQySZrGmyOY?t=96))
- Gnomonkey notes the news post mentioned the NPC guarding the door but no bank, so he expects no banker there and the ghost to be a death reclaim NPC like Torfinn for Vorkath. ([2021-11-16 2:08](https://youtu.be/fQySZrGmyOY?t=128))

## Events

- As of November 2021, Jagex announced it would bring Nex to Old School RuneScape. ([2021-11-16 0:31](https://youtu.be/fQySZrGmyOY?t=31))
