# Shellbane Gryphon

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2025-09-18 to 2025-11-26). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- To reach the Shellbane Gryphon, use fairy ring CJQ, which takes you very close to the cave entrance just north of it; a Dramen staff is needed unless the diary is done. ([2025-11-19 3:02](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=182))

## Requirements

- Gryphons are met through the Tortugon questline and require the quest to unlock; they need 51 Slayer, 45 Sailing and 60 combat. ([2025-09-18 0:28](https://youtu.be/sK0iG_wg3QA?t=28))
- The Shellbane Gryphon requires 51 Slayer and 45 Sailing, and you must also complete the quest Troubled Tortugans, which unlocks at 45 Sailing. ([2025-11-19 3:02](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=182))
- To fight the Shellbane Gryphon you must unlock it as a Slayer task (Gnomonkey thinks it costs 80 Slayer points) and get it as a task; it can only be killed on task. ([2025-11-19 3:02](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=182))

## Stats and numbers

- Gryphons attack with melee and are weak to magic; Gnomonkey notes air spells make sense because anything flying has an air weakness, and you can also melee them. ([2025-09-18 0:28](https://youtu.be/sK0iG_wg3QA?t=28))
- Gryphons can be assigned by Vannaka, Konar, Nieve and Duradel. ([2025-09-18 1:30](https://youtu.be/sK0iG_wg3QA?t=90))
- Gryphons drop gryphon feathers, tradable items used to charge the Horn of Plenty. ([2025-09-18 1:55](https://youtu.be/sK0iG_wg3QA?t=115))
- The Shellbane gryphon is a new Slayer boss requiring 51 Slayer; Gnomonkey says it is the lowest-level Slayer boss in the game, as most are above the 70s. ([2025-09-18 3:01](https://youtu.be/sK0iG_wg3QA?t=181))
- Like normal gryphons, the Shellbane gryphon is weak to magic but can also be defeated with melee; it primarily attacks with melee but has a ranged attack too. ([2025-09-18 3:01](https://youtu.be/sK0iG_wg3QA?t=181))
- The Shellbane Gryphon is weak to stab, so Gnomonkey brings an Arkan blade. ([2025-11-19 0:00](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts))
- The Tortugan shield, a quest item from the quest that unlocks the Shellbane Gryphon, blocks almost all of the damage the Gryphon does; Gnomonkey says without it melee hits are basically guaranteed and with it they do not hit at all. ([2025-11-19 1:32](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=92))
- The Shellbane Gryphon has a mechanic where, if you have more than 45 kilograms of weight equipped, you block its knockback attack. ([2025-11-19 1:59](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=119))
- If you are under 45 kg equipped, the Shellbane Gryphon's knockback attack hits for roughly 10 to 30 damage and throws you backwards. ([2025-11-19 1:59](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=119))
- If you have over 45 kg equipped, the Shellbane Gryphon is stunned instead, you block the damage and you get a bonus hit (two swings) on the boss. ([2025-11-19 2:25](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=145))
- The Shellbane Gryphon alternates between an occasional ranged attack and a melee attack, and performs a special attack every seven attacks. ([2025-11-19 3:56](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=236))
- As one of its specials, the Shellbane Gryphon places tornadoes on the ground near your feet roughly every five ticks (Gnomonkey is unsure of the exact timing); they grow into larger tornadoes, hurt you every tick if you stand on them, and explode if you are next to them. ([2025-11-19 3:56](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=236))
- The Shellbane Gryphon's acid attack is easy to dodge by stepping out of the way; if it hits you it applies a status effect called corrosion that hits you for 3s with a new hit splat. ([2025-11-19 4:26](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=266))
- Only equipped weight counts for the Shellbane Gryphon mechanic, not the weight of your inventory; Gnomonkey demonstrated taking off his body armour and being knocked back, taking a 28. ([2025-11-19 5:37](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=337))
- If you ignore the Shellbane Gryphon's tornadoes and five stack on top of you, they explode for about 70 damage (Gnomonkey saw hits of 60 and 70); this is the only thing that can really hurt or kill you in the fight. ([2025-11-19 5:40](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=340))
- Gnomonkey says the Shellbane Gryphon's new rapier drops at around 1 in 440, and many players skip it as not worth the time. ([2025-11-26 1:05:08](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=3908))
- The Shellbane Gryphon must be killed on task; it is an 80 point Slayer unlock and takes about three Slayer tasks on average to get the new rapier, and its weight from Neve (as transcribed) is six, the highest you can get. ([2025-11-26 1:08:39](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=4119))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey approaches the Shellbane Gryphon from the perspective of an Iron Man starting bossing, using an Arkan blade, Barrows gloves, mixed hide boots, a steel ring, and tank armour from Barrows (or Rune platebody and legs are fine). ([2025-11-19 0:00](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts))
- Gnomonkey notes a steel ring (a drop from the Deranged archaeologist on Fossil Island) is really heavy and useful for reaching the weight threshold, and mixed hide boots are a little heavier than climbing boots. ([2025-11-19 1:03](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=63))
- A Berserker ring is really good for the Shellbane Gryphon, but running no ring is also fine. ([2025-11-19 1:03](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=63))
- Gnomonkey says to disassemble your Slayer helmet with the right-click disassemble option and wear a black mask instead for the Shellbane Gryphon, because the black mask is heavier. ([2025-11-19 1:59](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=119))
- For supplies Gnomonkey brings supers, some prayer potions, and food, saying lower tier food is fine, plus a teleport away. ([2025-11-19 2:08](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=128))
- If you cannot get over 45 kg equipped you can still easily kill the Shellbane Gryphon; you just take a little extra damage. ([2025-11-19 2:25](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=145))
- Wearing the Tortugan shield means the Shellbane Gryphon's regular attacks do almost no damage, so trips can be tremendously longer. ([2025-11-19 3:56](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=236))
- To stop the corrosion damage from the Shellbane Gryphon's acid, take off all armour that gives defensive bonuses (including the ring); Gnomonkey notes this is awkward because you may have to drop items to make inventory space. ([2025-11-19 4:26](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=266))
- Gnomonkey says the stacked tornadoes only hurt you if you fall asleep; moving a little bit avoids them entirely. ([2025-11-19 6:40](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=400))
- Gnomonkey says as a main the Shellbane Gryphon drops are probably not worth it, but as an Iron Man there are some really good drops. ([2025-11-19 7:36](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=456))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey notes that magic gear is lightweight, so a mager might need extra weight, but that endgame armours such as Masori are very heavy so high-level players should not be affected. ([2025-09-18 0:55](https://youtu.be/sK0iG_wg3QA?t=55))
- Gnomonkey expects the Shellbane gryphon to be mageable, assumes players will need extra weight as with regular gryphons, and doubts it will be very hard. ([2025-09-18 3:29](https://youtu.be/sK0iG_wg3QA?t=209))
- Gnomonkey says the Shellbane gryphon is at the low end of mid-level content at 50 Slayer, so it is probably not going to be very hard. ([2025-09-18 18:44](https://youtu.be/sK0iG_wg3QA?t=1124))
- Gnomonkey considers the Shellbane Gryphon, the new mid-level Slayer boss added with Sailing, maybe the easiest boss ever added to the game, easier than Scurrius. ([2025-11-19 0:00](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts))
- Gnomonkey considers the Arkan blade, a spec weapon from the quest that unlocks Mocha, a decent stab weapon and decent spec that works okay at the Shellbane Gryphon. ([2025-11-19 0:00](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts))
- Gnomonkey says there is very little going on with the Shellbane Gryphon: you basically just move occasionally and dodge everything while you attack. ([2025-11-19 3:56](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts?t=236))
- Gnomonkey says the Shellbane Gryphon is much easier than Scurrius and teaches new players nothing, with very simple mechanics, no prayer switching, and nothing preparing them for other content. ([2025-11-26 1:04:36](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=3876))
- Gnomonkey cites the Shellbane Gryphon pet jar, which says it cannot be stored in the display case at this time, as evidence that Sailing was rushed. ([2025-11-26 1:11:10](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=4270))
- Gnomonkey contrasts Moons (48 Slayer, teaching movement and combat) and Scurrius (teaching prayer switching and dealing with small mobs) with the Gryphon, calling it a nothing burger as if Kraken was added again. ([2025-11-26 1:11:40](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=4300))
