**Shellbane Gryphon** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2025-09-18 and 2025-11-26. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* Gryphons are met through the Tortugon questline and require the quest to unlock; they need 51 Slayer, 45 Sailing and 60 combat.[^sK0iG_wg3QA-28]
* The Shellbane Gryphon requires 51 Slayer and 45 Sailing, and you must also complete the quest Troubled Tortugans, which unlocks at 45 Sailing.[^JwkGh1TXLts-182]
* To fight the Shellbane Gryphon you must unlock it as a Slayer task (Gnomonkey thinks it costs 80 Slayer points) and get it as a task; it can only be killed on task.[^JwkGh1TXLts-182]

## Methods

* To reach the Shellbane Gryphon, use fairy ring CJQ, which takes you very close to the cave entrance just north of it; a Dramen staff is needed unless the diary is done.[^JwkGh1TXLts-182]

## Stats and numbers

* Gryphons attack with melee and are weak to magic; Gnomonkey notes air spells make sense because anything flying has an air weakness, and you can also melee them.[^sK0iG_wg3QA-28]
* Gryphons can be assigned by Vannaka, Konar, Nieve and Duradel.[^sK0iG_wg3QA-90]
* Gryphons drop gryphon feathers, tradable items used to charge the Horn of Plenty.[^sK0iG_wg3QA-115]
* The Shellbane gryphon is a new Slayer boss requiring 51 Slayer; Gnomonkey says it is the lowest-level Slayer boss in the game, as most are above the 70s.[^sK0iG_wg3QA-181]
* Like normal gryphons, the Shellbane gryphon is weak to magic but can also be defeated with melee; it primarily attacks with melee but has a ranged attack too.[^sK0iG_wg3QA-181]
* The Shellbane Gryphon is weak to stab, so Gnomonkey brings an Arkan blade.[^JwkGh1TXLts-0]
* The Tortugan shield, a quest item from the quest that unlocks the Shellbane Gryphon, blocks almost all of the damage the Gryphon does; Gnomonkey says without it melee hits are basically guaranteed and with it they do not hit at all.[^JwkGh1TXLts-92]
* The Shellbane Gryphon has a mechanic where, if you have more than 45 kilograms of weight equipped, you block its knockback attack.[^JwkGh1TXLts-119]
* If you are under 45 kg equipped, the Shellbane Gryphon's knockback attack hits for roughly 10 to 30 damage and throws you backwards.[^JwkGh1TXLts-119]
* If you have over 45 kg equipped, the Shellbane Gryphon is stunned instead, you block the damage and you get a bonus hit (two swings) on the boss.[^JwkGh1TXLts-145]
* The Shellbane Gryphon alternates between an occasional ranged attack and a melee attack, and performs a special attack every seven attacks.[^JwkGh1TXLts-236]
* As one of its specials, the Shellbane Gryphon places tornadoes on the ground near your feet roughly every five ticks (Gnomonkey is unsure of the exact timing); they grow into larger tornadoes, hurt you every tick if you stand on them, and explode if you are next to them.[^JwkGh1TXLts-236]
* The Shellbane Gryphon's acid attack is easy to dodge by stepping out of the way; if it hits you it applies a status effect called corrosion that hits you for 3s with a new hit splat.[^JwkGh1TXLts-266]
* Only equipped weight counts for the Shellbane Gryphon mechanic, not the weight of your inventory; Gnomonkey demonstrated taking off his body armour and being knocked back, taking a 28.[^JwkGh1TXLts-337]
* If you ignore the Shellbane Gryphon's tornadoes and five stack on top of you, they explode for about 70 damage (Gnomonkey saw hits of 60 and 70); this is the only thing that can really hurt or kill you in the fight.[^JwkGh1TXLts-340]
* The Shellbane Gryphon's new rapier drops at around 1 in 440, and many players skip it as not worth the time.[^KRl59CBUR8U-3908]
* The Shellbane Gryphon must be killed on task; it is an 80 point Slayer unlock and takes about three Slayer tasks on average to get the new rapier, and its weight from Neve (as transcribed) is six, the highest you can get.[^KRl59CBUR8U-4119]

## Tips

* Gnomonkey approaches the Shellbane Gryphon from the perspective of an Iron Man starting bossing, using an Arkan blade, Barrows gloves, mixed hide boots, a steel ring, and tank armour from Barrows (or Rune platebody and legs are fine).[^JwkGh1TXLts-0]
* A steel ring (a drop from the Deranged archaeologist on Fossil Island) is really heavy and useful for reaching the weight threshold, and mixed hide boots are a little heavier than climbing boots.[^JwkGh1TXLts-63]
* A Berserker ring is really good for the Shellbane Gryphon, but running no ring is also fine.[^JwkGh1TXLts-63]
* To disassemble your Slayer helmet with the right-click disassemble option and wear a black mask instead for the Shellbane Gryphon, because the black mask is heavier.[^JwkGh1TXLts-119]
* For supplies Gnomonkey brings supers, some prayer potions, and food, saying lower tier food is fine, plus a teleport away.[^JwkGh1TXLts-128]
* If you cannot get over 45 kg equipped you can still easily kill the Shellbane Gryphon; you just take a little extra damage.[^JwkGh1TXLts-145]
* Wearing the Tortugan shield means the Shellbane Gryphon's regular attacks do almost no damage, so trips can be tremendously longer.[^JwkGh1TXLts-236]
* To stop the corrosion damage from the Shellbane Gryphon's acid, take off all armour that gives defensive bonuses (including the ring); Gnomonkey notes this is awkward because you may have to drop items to make inventory space.[^JwkGh1TXLts-266]
* The stacked tornadoes only hurt you if you fall asleep; moving a little bit avoids them entirely.[^JwkGh1TXLts-400]
* As a main the Shellbane Gryphon drops are probably not worth it, but as an Iron Man there are some really good drops.[^JwkGh1TXLts-456]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey notes that magic gear is lightweight, so a mager might need extra weight, but that endgame armours such as Masori are very heavy so high-level players should not be affected.[^sK0iG_wg3QA-55]
* Gnomonkey expects the Shellbane gryphon to be mageable, assumes players will need extra weight as with regular gryphons, and doubts it will be very hard.[^sK0iG_wg3QA-209]
* Gnomonkey says the Shellbane gryphon is at the low end of mid-level content at 50 Slayer, so it is probably not going to be very hard.[^sK0iG_wg3QA-1124]
* Gnomonkey considers the Shellbane Gryphon, the new mid-level Slayer boss added with Sailing, maybe the easiest boss ever added to the game, easier than Scurrius.[^JwkGh1TXLts-0]
* Gnomonkey considers the Arkan blade, a spec weapon from the quest that unlocks Mocha, a decent stab weapon and decent spec that works okay at the Shellbane Gryphon.[^JwkGh1TXLts-0]
* Gnomonkey says there is very little going on with the Shellbane Gryphon: you basically just move occasionally and dodge everything while you attack.[^JwkGh1TXLts-236]
* Gnomonkey says the Shellbane Gryphon is much easier than Scurrius and teaches new players nothing, with very simple mechanics, no prayer switching, and nothing preparing them for other content.[^KRl59CBUR8U-3876]
* Gnomonkey cites the Shellbane Gryphon pet jar, which says it cannot be stored in the display case at this time, as evidence that Sailing was rushed.[^KRl59CBUR8U-4270]
* Gnomonkey contrasts Moons (48 Slayer, teaching movement and combat) and Scurrius (teaching prayer switching and dealing with small mobs) with the Gryphon, calling it a nothing burger as if Kraken was added again.[^KRl59CBUR8U-4300]

## Events

* The Shellbane gryphon's unique drop is the gleaming rapier.[^sK0iG_wg3QA-209]
* A gryphon pet is being added; Gnomonkey says no picture was shown and he hopes it can fly, since he likes flying pets such as Kree'arra.[^sK0iG_wg3QA-331]

## Trivia

* Gryphons have a special mechanic where if a player's total weight is too low, the gryphon knocks them back, dealing unavoidable damage.[^sK0iG_wg3QA-28]
