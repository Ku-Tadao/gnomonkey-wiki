**Thermonuclear smoke devil** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2019-10-17 and 2022-03-19. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Skips at the smoke devil such as walking under it with a Scythe or harm staff, or freezing it out of range and then going to max range with a sanguinesti staff, but says none requires skill.[^HkVt4enEesI-341]

## Stats and numbers

* A nuclear smoke devil gives only 2,400 XP on kill, which Gnomonkey thinks is low for a superior with this Slayer requirement.[^xubsqAKD9tk-213]
* It has the highest chance of any superior of accessing the ultra drop table, at 1 in 25 according to Gnomonkey.[^xubsqAKD9tk-243]

## Tips

* If a nuclear smoke devil superior spawns, simply lure it to the stack like any other devil; it only attacks using ranged and is not dangerous.[^xubsqAKD9tk-213]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey rates the Thermonuclear smoke devil F tier: it is left-click and it dies, with typeless damage like Kraken, though it dies fast.[^HkVt4enEesI-341]
* Gnomonkey says the most skilled thing at the smoke devil is running an alt from a house and spec transferring over and over, which he dislikes and does not think sets a good precedent.[^HkVt4enEesI-374]
