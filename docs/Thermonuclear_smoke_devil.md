# Thermonuclear smoke devil

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2019-10-17 to 2022-03-19). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | boss |
| **Videos** | 2 (first 2019-10-17, latest 2022-03-19) |

## Methods

- Gnomonkey notes skips at the smoke devil such as walking under it with a Scythe or harm staff, or freezing it out of range and then going to max range with a sanguinesti staff, but says none requires skill. ([2022-03-19 5:41](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=341))

## Stats and numbers

- A nuclear smoke devil gives only 2,400 XP on kill, which Gnomonkey thinks is low for a superior with this Slayer requirement. ([2019-10-17 3:33](https://youtu.be/xubsqAKD9tk?t=213))
- It has the highest chance of any superior of accessing the ultra drop table, at 1 in 25 according to Gnomonkey. ([2019-10-17 4:03](https://youtu.be/xubsqAKD9tk?t=243))

## Tips

- If a nuclear smoke devil superior spawns, simply lure it to the stack like any other devil; it only attacks using ranged and is not dangerous. ([2019-10-17 3:33](https://youtu.be/xubsqAKD9tk?t=213))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey rates the Thermonuclear smoke devil F tier: it is left-click and it dies, with typeless damage like Kraken, though it dies fast. ([2022-03-19 5:41](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=341))
- Gnomonkey says the most skilled thing at the smoke devil is running an alt from a house and spec transferring over and over, which he dislikes and does not think sets a good precedent. ([2022-03-19 6:14](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=374))
