**Bees invocation** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2024-03-24 and 2024-04-12. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Ender Bees fly towards the player very slowly, and if they fly underneath the player they deal 10 poison hit splats every game tick.[^wiJAXY2rvSo-40]

## Stats and numbers

* The update increased the Bees invocation's time between movement from 7 ticks to 12 ticks and its respawn delay from 18 ticks (about 11 seconds) to 50 ticks (30 seconds).[^dkYhPWxsOGk-39]

## Tips

* Ender Bees is an okay pick if the goal is only to reach higher waves rather than the boss: they are a guaranteed max hit and you can heal off them with various specs.[^wiJAXY2rvSo-40]
* The Venator bow bounces to Ender Bees when they are inside enemies.[^wiJAXY2rvSo-40]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says Ender Bees is basically impossible to deal with in the melee setup, and the bees also spawn at Sol Heredit, making that fight practically impossible even at tier one.[^wiJAXY2rvSo-40]
* Gnomonkey says Bees is still not viable because it makes you waste ticks, is annoying on the boss, and can really only be used for pure wave farming as it makes the boss nearly impossible.[^dkYhPWxsOGk-39]
* Gnomonkey suggests making the bees like Zebak jugs with no attack delay and requiring only one tick to attack, enabling time saves such as free bonus splits from a one-tick scythe, one-tick Venator ricochets and one-tick chinchompas.[^dkYhPWxsOGk-60]
