# Inquisitor's armour

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2023-12-22 to 2025-09-03). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | item |

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says Inquisitor's armour typically gives a 6% increase to hammer accuracy, has the defensive stats of Adamant armour, and has negative accuracy bonuses outside of crush. ([2023-12-22 4:30](https://youtu.be/t5mXf8KJztU?t=270))
- Gnomonkey says Inquisitor's armour gives only about 0.05 better DPS than being naked in the Soulreaper axe slash setup for Muspah, because its strength bonus raises the max hit but it reduces accuracy. ([2025-09-03 2:33](https://youtu.be/vuti6ln_q10?t=153))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey notes that Inquisitor's armour still has a strength bonus, so it increases the max hit, which keeps it slightly better than being naked even though it lowers accuracy. ([2025-09-03 2:33](https://youtu.be/vuti6ln_q10?t=153))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says Inquisitor's armour very rightly needs a buff, noting its only uses are Challenge Mode Chambers and Nightmare, and even at Chambers many runners are starting to use Torva because the gap is so small after the changes. ([2023-12-22 4:30](https://youtu.be/t5mXf8KJztU?t=270))
- Gnomonkey suggests removing the negative bonuses and slightly buffing accuracy and strength bonuses to put Inquisitor's armour in a much better spot. ([2023-12-22 5:02](https://youtu.be/t5mXf8KJztU?t=302))
