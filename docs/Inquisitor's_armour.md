**Inquisitor's armour** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2023-12-22 and 2025-09-03. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Inquisitor's armour typically gives a 6% increase to hammer accuracy, has the defensive stats of Adamant armour, and has negative accuracy bonuses outside of crush.[^t5mXf8KJztU-270]
* Inquisitor's armour gives only about 0.05 better DPS than being naked in the Soulreaper axe slash setup for Muspah, because its strength bonus raises the max hit but it reduces accuracy.[^vuti6ln_q10-153]

## Tips

* Inquisitor's armour still has a strength bonus, so it increases the max hit, which keeps it slightly better than being naked even though it lowers accuracy.[^vuti6ln_q10-153]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says Inquisitor's armour very rightly needs a buff, noting its only uses are Challenge Mode Chambers and Nightmare, and even at Chambers many runners are starting to use Torva because the gap is so small after the changes.[^t5mXf8KJztU-270]
* Gnomonkey suggests removing the negative bonuses and slightly buffing accuracy and strength bonuses to put Inquisitor's armour in a much better spot.[^t5mXf8KJztU-302]
