# Zaryte crossbow

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 4 videos (2024-06-23 to 2025-08-09). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

|---|---|
| **Type** | item |

## Methods

- Gnomonkey says the Zaryte crossbow makes ruby bolts hit for 110 and is typically the most powerful spec weapon for DPS, assuming the target has 500 HP or more. ([2024-07-07 17:25](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1045))

## Stats and numbers

- After Nex's defence change, Gnomonkey says the Zaryte crossbow is about 9% better than the Twisted bow when ranging the boss in this setup. ([2024-06-23 0:00](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo))
- The Zaryte crossbow's special attack is now better than the Voidwaker's against Nex since her defence was changed, so Gnomonkey says the Voidwaker is more of a budget option and not mandatory. ([2024-06-23 2:26](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=146))
- At Doom of Mokhaiotl the Zaryte crossbow spec is typically used right before or after shield phase, so it hits around 90 to 100 rather than its full hit; it is a guaranteed hit on car phase and otherwise about 85% accurate. ([2025-08-09 1:02](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=62))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey calculates the crossbow spec by multiplying its 110 max by its accuracy (about 0.7 before the defence change) to get average damage; now the average is high enough to beat the Voidwaker, though he thinks they are very close and the Voidwaker is just barely worse. ([2024-06-23 4:01](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=241))
- Gnomonkey says the Zaryte crossbow is worth using at all team scales, including trios. ([2024-06-23 4:01](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=241))
- Gnomonkey swaps in the Zaryte crossbow for one shot on Fumus, since there is a chance of a bolt proc and it is very close to a Twisted bow shot on the first hit. ([2024-06-23 5:27](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=327))
- Gnomonkey says the Zaryte crossbow is useful as a main-hand weapon on extremely tanky targets with lots of HP, like Nex, and has DPS similar to the Bow of Faerdhinen when paired with Masori. ([2024-07-07 17:25](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1045))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says that whenever you range Nex you should use the Zaryte crossbow, that you need it 100%, and that the Twisted bow is for the minions; before the update the Twisted bow was slightly better in max gear. ([2024-06-23 2:26](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=146))
- Gnomonkey says many ranged builds in Leagues 5 use the Zaryte crossbow 'bug' where you brew to 0 stats and spam Zaryte crossbow specs without losing spec energy. ([2024-12-20 0:00](https://youtu.be/m8m-6TBJqhQ))
- Gnomonkey chose not to use the Zaryte crossbow spec bug because it sounds boring, though he acknowledges it is another way to annihilate ToA at high invocations. ([2024-12-20 0:00](https://youtu.be/m8m-6TBJqhQ))
- Gnomonkey thinks that with a Twisted bow and better gear than he had, a 1000 invocation ToA could easily be done in time. ([2024-12-20 0:00](https://youtu.be/m8m-6TBJqhQ))
- Gnomonkey considers the Zaryte crossbow the best ranged spec weapon for Doom of Mokhaiotl; it does about 36 DPS at full power and he says it wipes the floor with the other weapons. ([2025-08-09 1:02](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=62))
