**Zaryte crossbow** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 4 videos between 2024-06-23 and 2025-08-09. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* The Zaryte crossbow makes ruby bolts hit for 110 and is typically the most powerful spec weapon for DPS, assuming the target has 500 HP or more.[^e0y_auD9JhY-1045]

## Stats and numbers

* After Nex's defence change, Gnomonkey says the Zaryte crossbow is about 9% better than the Twisted bow when ranging the boss in this setup.[^AzCKJvLDRxo-0]
* The Zaryte crossbow's special attack is now better than the Voidwaker's against Nex since her defence was changed, so Gnomonkey says the Voidwaker is more of a budget option and not mandatory.[^AzCKJvLDRxo-146]
* At Doom of Mokhaiotl the Zaryte crossbow spec is typically used right before or after shield phase, so it hits around 90 to 100 rather than its full hit; it is a guaranteed hit on car phase and otherwise about 85% accurate.[^_q6rmQQkN1I-62]

## Tips

* Gnomonkey calculates the crossbow spec by multiplying its 110 max by its accuracy (about 0.7 before the defence change) to get average damage; now the average is high enough to beat the Voidwaker, though he thinks they are very close and the Voidwaker is just barely worse.[^AzCKJvLDRxo-241]
* The Zaryte crossbow is worth using at all team scales, including trios.[^AzCKJvLDRxo-241]
* Gnomonkey swaps in the Zaryte crossbow for one shot on Fumus, since there is a chance of a bolt proc and it is very close to a Twisted bow shot on the first hit.[^AzCKJvLDRxo-327]
* The Zaryte crossbow is useful as a main-hand weapon on extremely tanky targets with lots of HP, like Nex, and has DPS similar to the Bow of Faerdhinen when paired with Masori.[^e0y_auD9JhY-1045]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says that whenever you range Nex you should use the Zaryte crossbow, that you need it 100%, and that the Twisted bow is for the minions; before the update the Twisted bow was slightly better in max gear.[^AzCKJvLDRxo-146]
* Gnomonkey says many ranged builds in Leagues 5 use the Zaryte crossbow 'bug' where you brew to 0 stats and spam Zaryte crossbow specs without losing spec energy.[^m8m-6TBJqhQ-0]
* Gnomonkey chose not to use the Zaryte crossbow spec bug because it sounds boring, though he acknowledges it is another way to annihilate ToA at high invocations.[^m8m-6TBJqhQ-0]
* Gnomonkey thinks that with a Twisted bow and better gear than he had, a 1000 invocation ToA could easily be done in time.[^m8m-6TBJqhQ-0]
* Gnomonkey considers the Zaryte crossbow the best ranged spec weapon for Doom of Mokhaiotl; it does about 36 DPS at full power and he says it wipes the floor with the other weapons.[^_q6rmQQkN1I-62]
