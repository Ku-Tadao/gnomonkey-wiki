# Burning claws

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 8 videos (2024-07-15 to 2026-07-22). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Gnomonkey says Burning claws are unique among spec weapons in that they can spec on either stab or slash, which makes them good in some unusual situations. ([2024-07-15 10:33](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=633))
- For Warden, Gnomonkey swaps burning claws to the slash style because Warden has no weakness to any style and the claws have more slash bonus (95) than stab (83), making them more accurate and hit harder. ([2025-06-28 27:48](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=1668))

## Requirements

- Gnomonkey says Ironmen need to get two Burning claws to combine them into the full set. ([2024-07-15 10:21](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=621))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says Burning claws apply a burn and are similar to Dragon claws but with only a 30% special attack cost, and that he tested them extensively on stream with Mo Goat Kirby to find where they are good. ([2024-07-15 10:21](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=621))
- Gnomonkey says Burning claws get a slight 5% damage buff against demon-type monsters, which usually equates to about two max hits. ([2024-07-15 10:33](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=633))
- Gnomonkey says Burning claws take longer to spec with than normal claws because of the low spec cost, so you swing your main-hand weapon a bit less, and Dragon claws equal out when your main hand is around 14 DPS. ([2024-07-15 14:42](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=882))
- Gnomonkey says Burning claws give damage over time with a 45% chance to proc a burn on spec and only a 30% spec cost. ([2024-07-26 20:14](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1214))
- Gnomonkey says he has not calced whether the Scythe beats Burning claws, and guesses the claws max 42 against the Scythe's 50 but are close. ([2024-07-26 22:37](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1357))
- Gnomonkey notes Burning claw specs hit a consistent 40 on Kephri, and the red hitsplats are burn damage dealing an additional 10 damage over time. ([2024-10-24 16:07](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=967))
- Gnomonkey notes burning claws were better than the Voidwaker up to invocation level 375 in Tombs of Amascut, which felt strange. ([2026-06-04 40:38](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2438))
- The Summer Sweep-up increased the burning claws' special attack energy cost to 35%, up from 30%. ([2026-07-22 8:35](https://youtu.be/EPlArwCehYY?t=515))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says Burning claws will be good in Tombs of Amascut anything under a 400 invocation with defence drain, on Ba-Ba and Kephri, and on Zebak if you do not have a Zaryte crossbow though not great there. ([2024-07-15 10:33](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=633))
- Gnomonkey says Burning claws are good on Akkha's shadows but not on Akkha's final phase, because the burn actually makes him jump. ([2024-07-15 11:04](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=664))
- Gnomonkey says Burning claws are not great on Tombs of Amascut's Warden because of his extremely high defence breakpoints, and are significantly worse on the core than a Dragon dagger special because of a lower max hit and the burn not applying to the core. ([2024-07-15 11:04](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=664))
- Gnomonkey says in Chambers of Xeric the Zaryte crossbow and Voidwaker are still king because of the high defences in that raid, nothing in Chambers is really weak to slash or stab, and Burning claws were awful on Vasa Cristallus. ([2024-07-15 11:04](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=664))
- Gnomonkey says Burning claws are very good on Sotetseg at all drains and will beat Dragon claws, since you usually have leftover spec at the end and Chally is god-awful at finishing Sotetseg. ([2024-07-15 11:28](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=688))
- Gnomonkey says Burning claws are bugged on Xarpus because the burn triggers his turn and he kills you after Screech, so you should not use them there, and they only match regular Dragon claws' DPS if the team fully drains him. ([2024-07-15 12:08](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=728))
- Gnomonkey says on Verzik, Burning claws should not be used in P1 even though the burn hits her, in P2 she heals the burn damage when dealing with red crabs so spec timing matters, and in P3 they are really good, especially post-tornadoes. ([2024-07-15 12:08](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=728))
- Gnomonkey says Burning claws are not as good in the Inferno because nothing lives long enough for the burn to apply, and the point of claws is to finish targets quickly. ([2024-07-15 13:16](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=796))
- Gnomonkey says Burning claws are best in slot in the Colosseum by about 5% over Dragon claws, especially on Soul where you have leftover spec at the end, and all Colosseum mobs have gigantic health pools allowing the burn to tick. ([2024-07-15 13:16](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=796))
- Gnomonkey says Nex's defence is too high and she heals too much for Burning claws to be good, ranking them below the Voidwaker and far below the Zaryte crossbow as a spec, and that Bloodveld-style testing at Nex dragged on far longer than it should have. ([2024-07-15 13:48](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=828))
- Gnomonkey says at Corporeal Beast you should still use a Voidwaker spec as it is the only thing not reduced due to his weird mechanics, and that Sarachnis is completely immune to burn despite being a demon, which he calls an oversight. ([2024-07-15 13:48](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=828))
- Gnomonkey says Burning claws are best in slot at Vardorvis, with the spec best used around the beginning and middle of the kill to maximise burn DPS. ([2024-07-15 14:15](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=855))
- Gnomonkey says at Vorkath you can triple spec and stack the burn, though the burn damage is halved during the acid phase and nullified during the freeze, and Burning claws are still the best option. ([2024-07-15 14:15](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=855))
- Gnomonkey says Burning claws are a good spec if you do not have the Voidwaker yet or are an Ironman who does not want to grind Wilderness content for Voidwaker pieces, but anywhere you currently use a Zaryte crossbow they will not beat it. ([2024-07-15 14:42](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=882))
- Gnomonkey says Burning claws must be put on the stab style in ToA, as most things there are weak to stab over slash, and if they are not on stab they will be very bad. ([2024-10-24 2:24](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=144))
- Gnomonkey prefers Burning claws over dragon claws in ToA because dragon claws do not spec using slash defence while Burning claws can, so Burning claws are really strong in the raid. ([2024-10-24 11:35](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=695))
- Gnomonkey says Burning claws are one of the few special attacks whose attack style can be changed, so everything in ToA being weak to stab means they can be set to stab for a super accurate special attack on every boss. ([2025-06-25 4:28](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=268))
- At the start of Kephri's room, Gnomonkey drops a brew, instantly specs with burning claws, then summons a thrall; he notes the claws hit well even on Kephri's fairly high defence. ([2025-06-28 12:48](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=768))
- Burning claws are an amazing spec against Akkha, but as soon as you spec with them Akkha jumps, so Gnomonkey recommends two Keris pokes first and then burning claws, which makes him jump around the room less. ([2025-06-28 25:47](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=1547))
- Burning claws combined with adrenaline potions let you use many special attacks on Warden; Gnomonkey calls it incredibly strong. ([2025-06-28 28:18](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=1698))
- Gnomonkey described Burning claws as basically a max claw spec on Vorkath. ([2025-09-06 14:54](https://youtu.be/qCsJAdpSeTU?t=894))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says overall Burning claws are 2-6% better than the places Dragon claws would be used, unless the target has low HP or extremely low defence. ([2024-07-15 10:33](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=633))
- Gnomonkey says that currently Burning claws are worth bringing in addition to Dragon claws in Theatre of Blood but not replacing them, and that they absolutely do not replace Chally because of its huge last-hit value. ([2024-07-15 12:28](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=748))
- Gnomonkey says Burning claws had controversy after release, with some calling them bad and some good, and that any item at the beginning of an update that has not been tested is volatile. ([2024-07-15 15:11](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=911))
- Gnomonkey says Burning claws are very good on Sol Heredit because they give a bonus spec at the end of the kill you would not normally get, and for now are directly better than Dragon claws, though he expects they may be nerfed. ([2024-07-26 20:14](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1214))
- Gnomonkey calls Burning claws an extremely high-value special attack weapon at around 20 to 23 mil, and says they are the best spec weapon in ToA until you hit raid level 375. ([2024-10-24 1:55](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=115))
- Gnomonkey says at a 300 raid level Burning claws cost about 10 million and will beat a Voidwaker, and he recommends bringing them. ([2025-06-25 4:28](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=268))
- Gnomonkey calls burning claws an amazing special attack weapon against Baba, Kephri and Akkha, and your best possible spec DPS until you get above 375 raid level, where you avoid Akkha. ([2025-06-28 4:24](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=264))
- Gnomonkey calls the Burning claws an amazing spec weapon for Vorkath in the 50M budget setup. ([2025-09-06 10:52](https://youtu.be/qCsJAdpSeTU?t=652))
- Gnomonkey says he previously ranted about how good burning claws are, and agrees they were overtuned, being very good in Tombs of Amascut and the Colosseum and a sidegrade to dragon claws when they were supposed to be a worse stepping stone. ([2026-06-04 39:46](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2386))
- Gnomonkey thinks the burning claws nerf is justified but sad for the people who like using them everywhere, especially early on an ironman. ([2026-06-04 40:38](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2438))
- Gnomonkey had said burning claws were a little overpowered on release since they beat regular claws in a lot of places, and considers the nerf fair; he says regular claws, and probably Voidwaker, will now be better nearly all the time. ([2026-07-22 8:35](https://youtu.be/EPlArwCehYY?t=515))
- Gnomonkey enjoyed using burning claws everywhere as a strong spec weapon, for example super good for early Tombs of Amascut, and says they are now an early spec weapon that gets outclassed by other spec weapons. ([2026-07-22 8:35](https://youtu.be/EPlArwCehYY?t=515))

## Records

- Gnomonkey says he walked Vorkath on stream with Burning claws and on his very first attempt got a Grandmaster time with a simple melee camp, a 53.4 second personal best, where the typical time was a 60. ([2024-07-15 14:15](https://youtu.be/eIAkPiQivJk?t=855))

- Gnomonkey says he spent every day, eight plus hours each day, testing Burning claws in every location he could, outside of calcs because calcs were not yet updated, lacked proper formulas or were bugged, and with Mo Goat Kirby concluded Burning claws are good. ## Trivia

- Gnomonkey notes a quirk of burning claws: they can go on stab, and their special attack cannot have its style affected because it only uses one style; the Tormented Demon grind also gets you the burning claws. ([2025-06-28 3:26](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=206))
