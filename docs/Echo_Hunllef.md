**Echo Hunllef** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2024-12-01. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Gnomonkey got the Echo Corrupted Gauntlet orb at around 7 to 8 KC and the Crystal Blessing around 18 Echo kills.[^lk-ZaW-SqIc-0]

## Tips

* As soon as the prayer drops in the Echo Corrupted Gauntlet fight, you want to use the wrong weapon to avoid being hit.[^lk-ZaW-SqIc-60]
* Echo Hunllef hits his normal maximum but as two splats, and that Redemption procs on the first splat even if it would kill you, so with 10 health and two 9s you would live.[^lk-ZaW-SqIc-344]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says he did melee-only attempts with and without armor but they were not fun, though still possible for someone who hates themselves enough.[^lk-ZaW-SqIc-0]
* Gnomonkey considers Echo Corrupted Gauntlet and Echo Sol among the best upgrades to existing content and wishes they could be added as additional levels in the main game.[^lk-ZaW-SqIc-0]

## Records

* Gnomonkey killed Echo Hunllef with no armor in two tries.[^lk-ZaW-SqIc-97]
* Gnomonkey killed Echo Hunllef with no food and no armor, saying he got super lucky and did not have to Redemption proc even once, which he found a little dissatisfying.[^lk-ZaW-SqIc-463]

## Events

* Gnomonkey beat Echo Corrupted Gauntlet in Leagues 5 with no inventory, and also did no armor, no food, and no food and no armor runs back to back.[^lk-ZaW-SqIc-0]
