## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey got the Echo Corrupted Gauntlet orb at around 7 to 8 KC and the Crystal Blessing around 18 Echo kills. ([2024-12-01 0:00](https://youtu.be/lk-ZaW-SqIc))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says that as soon as the prayer drops in the Echo Corrupted Gauntlet fight, you want to use the wrong weapon to avoid being hit. ([2024-12-01 1:00](https://youtu.be/lk-ZaW-SqIc?t=60))
- Gnomonkey explains that Echo Hunllef hits his normal maximum but as two splats, and that Redemption procs on the first splat even if it would kill you, so with 10 health and two 9s you would live. ([2024-12-01 5:44](https://youtu.be/lk-ZaW-SqIc?t=344))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says he did melee-only attempts with and without armor but they were not fun, though still possible for someone who hates themselves enough. ([2024-12-01 0:00](https://youtu.be/lk-ZaW-SqIc))
- Gnomonkey considers Echo Corrupted Gauntlet and Echo Sol among the best upgrades to existing content and wishes they could be added as additional levels in the main game. ([2024-12-01 0:00](https://youtu.be/lk-ZaW-SqIc))

## Records

- Gnomonkey killed Echo Hunllef with no armor in two tries. ([2024-12-01 1:37](https://youtu.be/lk-ZaW-SqIc?t=97))

## Events
