## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the RS3 prayer book is pretty much exactly what he does not want in Old School: everything is a direct upgrade, especially Turmoil, so there is no interesting choice. ([2023-01-07 1:35](https://youtu.be/bJsOQWJY45Q?t=95))
- Gnomonkey says Soul Split broke the game wide open in RS3 and made every PvM encounter use Soul Split every attack for free HP; he likes the idea of a useful non-protection overhead but says it provides ridiculous sustain. ([2023-01-07 2:02](https://youtu.be/bJsOQWJY45Q?t=122))

## Trivia

- RuneScape 3's ancient prayer book is called Curses and is a direct upgrade over the standard prayer book except in rare cases. ([2023-01-07 0:32](https://youtu.be/bJsOQWJY45Q?t=32))
- In RS3 Curses, protection prayers are replaced by deflection prayers that protect the same amount but also deflect 6.7% of damage on average. ([2023-01-07 0:32](https://youtu.be/bJsOQWJY45Q?t=32))
- RS3's Curses book includes Wrath, described as a better Retribution, and still has Protect Item. ([2023-01-07 0:32](https://youtu.be/bJsOQWJY45Q?t=32))
- In Curses the passive boosting prayers are replaced by leech curses, which give the same boost but also drain the stats of the target and give them to you over time. ([2023-01-07 0:32](https://youtu.be/bJsOQWJY45Q?t=32))
- Turmoil is the capstone passive boost curse in RS3: it starts a bit stronger than Piety when activated and, as it leeches the target's stats, becomes significantly more powerful than Piety. ([2023-01-07 1:05](https://youtu.be/bJsOQWJY45Q?t=65))
- Soul Split is an additional RS3 overhead that gives back a percentage of damage dealt as HP; it was initially 25% and later nerfed to 10%, and it can still be flicked between deflection prayers by good RS3 PvMers. ([2023-01-07 1:05](https://youtu.be/bJsOQWJY45Q?t=65))
