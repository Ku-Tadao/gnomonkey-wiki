# Eldritch veil

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2025-01-09 to 2025-02-04). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | item |
| **Videos** | 3 (first 2025-01-09, latest 2025-02-04) |

## Stats and numbers

- The Eldritch veil is a new best-in-slot cape for magic offence, made by using a demonic mark (a tradable consumable) on any imbued god cape, while the finished cape is untradeable. ([2025-01-09 6:38](https://youtu.be/4YGVut4hMtA?t=398))
- The Eldritch veil gives five extra magic attack and 1% magic damage with a trade-off of negative two prayer bonus. ([2025-01-09 6:54](https://youtu.be/4YGVut4hMtA?t=414))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey thinks 1% magic damage with negative Prayer is odd and says it feels like the Tumeken's shadow is holding back magic as a whole, so he believes it should be 4% magic damage, noting the magic rebalance already hindered magic. ([2025-01-09 6:54](https://youtu.be/4YGVut4hMtA?t=414))
- Gnomonkey is happy that nearly all Doom of Mokhaiotl rewards are tradable, and says the encounter is not suited to a quiver-style untradeable unlock. ([2025-01-09 7:49](https://youtu.be/4YGVut4hMtA?t=469))
- Gnomonkey says raising the Eldritch Veil's prayer stat from negative two to plus one makes sense, and he asks why its defence stats cannot simply match the Mage Arena 2 cape. ([2025-01-14 3:38](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=218))
- Gnomonkey likes that the Eldritch Veil no longer has to be attached to an imbued god cape, because it lets players skip the Mage Arena 2 capes, but notes it reduces the prestige some players valued from needing the Wilderness-obtained capes. ([2025-01-14 4:04](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=244))
- Gnomonkey says the minimum hit of two makes the Eldritch Veil significantly better for fast-hitting weapons like the Trice or the Sangstaff (as he names them), and he considers it effectively plus one max hit that matters more on faster weapons. ([2025-01-14 4:34](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=274))
- Gnomonkey says he looked into it and the minimum hit change is not very good for the Tumeken's shadow, which gets max hits in the Chambers of Xeric and outside raids but not in the Tombs of Amascut. ([2025-01-14 4:34](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=274))
- Gnomonkey says he really does not like the veil being untradeable and does not understand it, since Jagex gave no reasoning for the change after giving several reasons to keep it tradable. ([2025-01-14 5:01](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=301))
- Gnomonkey says he does not see an issue with this tradability tweak, saying it is the same as the Amulet of rancor, and he doesn't really care. ([2025-02-04 1:01](https://youtu.be/oAc5voBILb8?t=61))
- Gnomonkey finds it baffling that a minimum hit of two plus 2% magic damage is considered power creep from Inferno-tier content, saying it works out to about two max hits for a Sang in good situations. ([2025-02-04 4:34](https://youtu.be/oAc5voBILb8?t=274))

## Events

- Following feedback, Jagex decided to make the Eldritch Veil untradeable. ([2025-01-14 1:06](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=66))
- Jagex described the Eldritch Veil as intended to be a minor upgrade, a stopgap between the imbued god capes and an eventual magic cape. ([2025-01-14 1:36](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=96))
- Jagex said it did not intend the Eldritch Veil to replace the Inferno cape. ([2025-01-14 3:35](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=215))
- Changes announced to the Eldritch Veil: it becomes an untradeable item, its prayer bonus is raised to plus one, and Jagex is re-examining its defence stats. ([2025-01-14 3:35](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=215))
- The Eldritch Veil becomes a guaranteed drop at delve level 8 and a standalone item, so it no longer needs to be attached to an imbued god cape and the demonic mark is removed. ([2025-01-14 4:02](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=242))
- The Eldritch Veil gains a passive effect of a flat minimum hit of two: any successful accuracy roll rolls between two and the max hit, and this does not interact with the two-roll mechanics (shadow) or multiplier effects. ([2025-01-14 4:34](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=274))
- The Eldritch necklace (the reward then under poll) was changed so the necklace itself would be tradeable while the demonic mark component would be untradeable. ([2025-02-04 1:01](https://youtu.be/oAc5voBILb8?t=61))
- Gnomonkey says the third blog stated that the necklace would not have the minimum hit of two, which was in the previous blog. ([2025-02-04 4:07](https://youtu.be/oAc5voBILb8?t=247))
- Jagex said about the two minimum hit that it mainly wants to avoid additional power creep, and that combined with the existing plus 2% magic damage it would end up too strong. ([2025-02-04 4:34](https://youtu.be/oAc5voBILb8?t=274))
