# Challenge: Budget Tombs of Amascut 500 with Mixed hide

## Methods

- Gnomonkey builds his own budget setup of roughly the same value as the 39 mil downgrade setup (he says about 35 mil), which he expects to be about 50% more effective, and gives it a single attempt. ([2024-06-01 25:09](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1509))
- The budget setup uses Blood bark, Book of the Dead as the offhand, a Trident of the Swamp (three max hit higher than the regular Trident), an Occult necklace, a very cheap cape and Mixed hide armour. ([2024-06-01 26:10](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1570))
- The budget setup also uses a Lightbearer, dragon boots, gloves, a Blood fury and a Quiver, which Gnomonkey says are free or cheap. ([2024-06-01 26:39](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1599))
- He uses a bone dagger as a very good cheap defence-lowering spec and a DDS for the core, plus Osmumten's fang as his main melee weapon. ([2024-06-01 27:39](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1659))
- Gnomonkey says thralls path towards the southwest tile only, so a thrall he summoned while attacking Kephri failed to find the boss. ([2024-06-01 32:21](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1941))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey uses a Hunter's sunlight crossbow (about 3k, a good MSB with better range) instead of a blowpipe, and says his range switch is a two-way, with 170 ranged accuracy compared with 184 for the Dragon crossbow. ([2024-06-01 27:10](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1630))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says he should have brought chinchompas for Kephri's room and regrets it. ([2024-06-01 31:50](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1910))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says killing things fast is better than blocking damage, because if they die they stop hitting you. ([2024-06-01 30:48](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1848))
- Gnomonkey says the budget run avoided the mental tax of flicking an extra half hour with fewer Sanfew and that every room was free, calling a clear under 50 minutes for under 30 mil unbelievable. ([2024-06-01 43:54](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=2634))

## Records

- Gnomonkey completed the 500 in the budget setup in 47 minutes 30 seconds, compared with 1 hour 12 minutes in the downgrade setup, and says the budget setup cost about 32 mil. ([2024-06-01 43:22](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=2602))
- The budget run made about 1.1 mil in loot, more than the downgrade run, though Gnomonkey got no purple again. ([2024-06-01 43:22](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=2602))

## Trivia

- Gnomonkey found it is bugged to wear a Quiver in the Tombs of Amascut Tombs setup, which costs him a slot, but he says it does not matter because he has infinite restore. ([2024-06-01 28:11](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1691))
