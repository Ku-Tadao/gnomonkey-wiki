**Challenge: Budget Tombs of Amascut 500 with Mixed hide** is a challenge topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2024-06-01. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Gnomonkey builds his own budget setup of roughly the same value as the 39 mil downgrade setup (he says about 35 mil), which he expects to be about 50% more effective, and gives it a single attempt.[^17ynF_0ltPc-1509]
* The budget setup uses Blood bark, Book of the Dead as the offhand, a Trident of the Swamp (three max hit higher than the regular Trident), an Occult necklace, a very cheap cape and Mixed hide armour.[^17ynF_0ltPc-1570]
* The budget setup also uses a Lightbearer, dragon boots, gloves, a Blood fury and a Quiver, which Gnomonkey says are free or cheap.[^17ynF_0ltPc-1599]
* He uses a bone dagger as a very good cheap defence-lowering spec and a DDS for the core, plus Osmumten's fang as his main melee weapon.[^17ynF_0ltPc-1659]
* Thralls path towards the southwest tile only, so a thrall he summoned while attacking Kephri failed to find the boss.[^17ynF_0ltPc-1941]

## Stats and numbers

* Gnomonkey uses a Hunter's sunlight crossbow (about 3k, a good MSB with better range) instead of a blowpipe, and says his range switch is a two-way, with 170 ranged accuracy compared with 184 for the Dragon crossbow.[^17ynF_0ltPc-1630]

## Tips

* He should have brought chinchompas for Kephri's room and regrets it.[^17ynF_0ltPc-1910]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says killing things fast is better than blocking damage, because if they die they stop hitting you.[^17ynF_0ltPc-1848]
* Gnomonkey says the budget run avoided the mental tax of flicking an extra half hour with fewer Sanfew and that every room was free, calling a clear under 50 minutes for under 30 mil unbelievable.[^17ynF_0ltPc-2634]

## Records

* Gnomonkey completed the 500 in the budget setup in 47 minutes 30 seconds, compared with 1 hour 12 minutes in the downgrade setup, and says the budget setup cost about 32 mil.[^17ynF_0ltPc-2602]
* The budget run made about 1.1 mil in loot, more than the downgrade run, though Gnomonkey got no purple again.[^17ynF_0ltPc-2602]

## Trivia

* Gnomonkey found it is bugged to wear a Quiver in the Tombs of Amascut Tombs setup, which costs him a slot, but he says it does not matter because he has infinite restore.[^17ynF_0ltPc-1691]
