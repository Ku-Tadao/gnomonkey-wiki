# Vorkath

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 7 videos (2019-04-08 to 2025-09-11). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Requirements

- To fight Vorkath at all one needs Dragon Slayer II (per the video description); the guide's baseline requirements are at least 80 Ranged and 74 Prayer for Rigor. ([2019-04-08 0:59](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=59))
- Minimum Ranged is 80; going from 80 to 99 Ranged is about a 20% DPS difference. At least 74 Prayer is needed for Rigor, which is a 6-10% DPS increase, though kills are possible with Eagle Eye. ([2019-04-08 0:59](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=59))
- Gnomonkey says 80+ Magic, Defence and Hitpoints are effectively necessary; Magic makes up 66% of the player's magic defence and Defence makes up 33%. ([2019-04-08 1:33](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=93))
- Gnomonkey says the setup needs full elite void, an imbued salve amulet and a rune pouch; kills are possible with regular void but are significantly slower and use more prayer. ([2019-04-08 1:33](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=93))
- The old wiki Vorkath setup Gnomonkey used had downgrades from a salve amulet to an amulet of power, Infernal cape to a skill cape (he wore a thieving cape), Bandos chestplate to Ink hauberk, Tassets to a Fremennik kilt, a Leaf-bladed sword and Toktz-ket-xil shield, a ring of life, and an Armadyl godsword spec. ([2025-09-06 0:26](https://youtu.be/qCsJAdpSeTU?t=26))

## Stats and numbers

- A 3-dose super antifire gives 18 minutes of protection, up to 23 minutes if the next trip begins immediately, and a 6-kill trip should not take more than 14 minutes, so Gnomonkey does not bring a fourth dose. ([2019-04-08 2:59](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=179))
- Vorkath's ranged hairball attack is his most common attack, with a max hit of 35, which is why Gnomonkey plays ranged rather than magic. ([2019-04-08 6:29](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=389))
- Vorkath's magic attack is used about a third as often as his ranged attack, has a max hit of 30 and is the player's main source of incoming damage. ([2019-04-08 6:29](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=389))
- Vorkath's fireball is his most powerful regular attack and can hit up to 121 damage if the player is directly under it; it does half damage one tile away and can be avoided completely by being two or more tiles away. ([2019-04-08 7:40](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=460))
- During the ice phase Vorkath launches a zombified spawn that crawls towards the player and explodes with a max hit of 60 if it reaches the player before it is killed. ([2019-04-08 8:11](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=491))
- In the acid phase Vorkath fires fireballs that land where the player stood each tick, so standing still means fireballs can hit for well over 30 each. ([2019-04-08 9:40](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=580))
- Walking into acid hits the player for 7 to 10 damage each tick, and those damage splats heal Vorkath by an equal amount. ([2019-04-08 9:40](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=580))
- Vorkath's instance is special in that all items on the ground in it do not disappear for 30 minutes, so items can be left on the ground for the whole trip. ([2019-04-08 15:52](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=952))
- In the downgraded Vorkath setup Gnomonkey's max hit is 37 with Piety and 44 with extra boosts, while he calls the setup's magic bonus very negative so he has to take off pieces for the spawn. ([2024-05-18 3:01](https://youtu.be/WvmEY9v6AnI?t=181))
- Gnomonkey notes the Abyssal dagger special attack reduces your max hit, and says it is probably still better than Arclight. ([2024-05-18 4:01](https://youtu.be/WvmEY9v6AnI?t=241))
- Gnomonkey says the Arclight special drains Vorkath's defence by 10 levels after first saying 12. ([2024-05-18 4:30](https://youtu.be/WvmEY9v6AnI?t=270))
- With the Toktz-ket-xil shield, the old wiki setup gives about 125 ranged defence even with empty slots (about 60 without it), compared with 105 in the Fremennik-way max gear, according to Gearscape. ([2025-09-06 1:23](https://youtu.be/qCsJAdpSeTU?t=83))
- Gnomonkey's kill of Vorkath with the old wiki setup (empty helm/glove/boot slots, Leaf-bladed sword, AGS) took 5 minutes 40 seconds; he called it not that bad. ([2025-09-06 8:30](https://youtu.be/qCsJAdpSeTU?t=510))
- Gnomonkey's later Fang and Burning claws kill of Vorkath took roughly two minutes (the transcript renders the time as '2016'), which he said was a bit better than the 5:40 old wiki setup kill. ([2025-09-06 14:54](https://youtu.be/qCsJAdpSeTU?t=894))
- Gnomonkey's 1k setup kill of Vorkath took about four minutes, which he said was slow versus the expectation, and used 40 knives worth about 8k GP in total. ([2025-09-06 21:04](https://youtu.be/qCsJAdpSeTU?t=1264))

## Tips

- Equipment in the guide: full elite void including gloves, an imbued salve amulet, Ava's assembler (or accumulator), enchanted ruby dragon bolts, dragon hunter crossbow, dragonfire ward, Pegasian boots and an imbued archers ring. ([2019-04-08 2:12](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=132))
- If using an odium ward or anti-dragon shield instead of a dragonfire ward, the player must pray Protect from Magic instead of Ranged or Vorkath's dragon breath will do heavy damage. ([2019-04-08 2:29](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=149))
- Blessed dragonhide boots are acceptable until Pegasian boots are affordable, as Pegasian boots are only about a 1% DPS increase. ([2019-04-08 2:29](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=149))
- Inventory in the guide: three 4-dose prayer potions (one can be swapped for food if very experienced with prayer flicking and 96+ Prayer), diamond dragon bolts, a 4-dose ranging potion, anti-venom+, a 3-dose super antifire, 19 manta rays, a rune pouch with law, dust and chaos runes, and Fremennik sea boots 4. ([2019-04-08 2:48](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=168))
- Gnomonkey advises bringing manta rays or the cheapest 22+ healing food and not lower-healing food, which is inefficient at Vorkath. ([2019-04-08 3:30](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=210))
- The law, dust and chaos runes in the rune pouch allow casting Crumble Undead and teleporting to house. ([2019-04-08 3:30](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=210))
- Without the Elite Diary a Lunar Isle teleport portal in the player's house plus Bird Eye Jack's bank booth can prep the next trip; then talking to another banker kicks the player off the island in front of the dock to Vorkath. ([2019-04-08 3:48](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=228))
- Vorkath uses six regular attacks before each of his two special attacks, with the regular attacks chosen completely randomly; the same attack can be used up to six times in a row. ([2019-04-08 6:29](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=389))
- The orange dragonfire breath does zero damage if the player has full fire protection. ([2019-04-08 6:29](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=389))
- The green breath is a dragonfire attack that does zero damage with full protection but inflicts venom if the player has not drunk anti-venom; drinking a dose of anti-venom cures it. ([2019-04-08 7:07](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=427))
- The pink breath is a dragonfire attack that turns the player's prayers off; clicking the quick prayers icon turns them back on, and doing so does not break the player's attack. ([2019-04-08 7:07](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=427))
- If a player approaches Vorkath's melee range he can swing at them for high damage, but this should not occur when using ranged. ([2019-04-08 7:40](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=460))
- After six regular attacks Vorkath begins the ice phase or the acid phase. The first special is chosen randomly, after which he alternates between them. ([2019-04-08 8:07](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=487))
- Vorkath's ice breath does no damage but instantly freezes the player in place and prevents Vorkath from taking any damage until the phase ends. ([2019-04-08 8:11](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=491))
- Crumble Undead always instantly kills the spawn. ([2019-04-08 8:42](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=522))
- Gnomonkey says the ice phase is the best time to eat during the fight because the player cannot damage Vorkath anyway. ([2019-04-08 8:42](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=522))
- The player should count six regular attacks after the acid phase to know when Vorkath will freeze them, and be ready to click a tile because the character continues attacking when the phase starts. ([2019-04-08 8:42](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=522))
- If the player accidentally attacks Vorkath twice at the start of the ice phase they will not have time to kill the spawn, let alone eat; if they do not attack at all they can eat as much as three pieces of food. ([2019-04-08 9:11](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=551))
- All damage dealt during the acid phase is halved, including ruby bolt specials. ([2019-04-08 9:11](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=551))
- In the acid phase Vorkath spews acid on random tiles; if the player is far enough from the entrance no acid spawns near it, which is why Gnomonkey stands on his marked tiles. ([2019-04-08 9:40](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=580))
- A blob of acid always lands where the character stood when the phase started, so the player must move as soon as the phase starts; the acid is visible in the air right before it hits, giving one tick to react. ([2019-04-08 10:10](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=610))
- Gnomonkey recommends beginners who struggle simply walk around the arena without attacking Vorkath during the acid phase until they have a handle on the other attacks. ([2019-04-08 10:10](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=610))
- Trips typically last six kills because that is the point where the inventory is usually completely full of loot. ([2019-04-08 15:52](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=952))
- A player who never dies could leave all loot on the ground until the end of the trip to save time; Gnomonkey does not do this because he still occasionally dies and all drops on the ground are lost on death. ([2019-04-08 15:52](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=952))
- Each Ranged level is about a 1% DPS increase, so Gnomonkey keeps his Ranged boost high; he lets the boost decay to +9 and then immediately re-pots. ([2019-04-08 16:47](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=1007))
- Gnomonkey ranks the most efficient times to eat at Vorkath: between kills first, then during the ice phase, then during the acid phase because damage is halved. ([2019-04-08 17:14](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=1034))
- Eating one piece of food right as the breath leaves Vorkath's mouth wastes zero ticks; two pieces are possible if the player has not accidentally attacked him, and a third piece after casting Crumble Undead on the spawn. ([2019-04-08 17:14](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=1034))
- Eating more food during the ice phase slows killing the spawn, which may cause the player to be hit by it. ([2019-04-08 17:14](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=1034))
- Vorkath is one of the easiest monsters to tick eat because his attacks take five ticks to reach the player; when health is below 30 the player can wait to eat until Vorkath fires a magic attack. ([2019-04-08 17:47](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=1067))
- Gnomonkey says that with the budget setup he has enough mage attack bonus that he does not need to worry about the spawn. ([2024-05-18 7:01](https://youtu.be/WvmEY9v6AnI?t=421))
- Gnomonkey tries to brew only on Vorkath's specials in this setup because it wastes less DPS. ([2025-09-06 4:25](https://youtu.be/qCsJAdpSeTU?t=265))
- Gnomonkey notes that your damage is halved while standing in Vorkath's acid, so that is a good time to brew. ([2025-09-06 4:53](https://youtu.be/qCsJAdpSeTU?t=293))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey made the Vorkath guide because he felt most guides did not comprehensively show how to effectively deal with the acid phase. ([2019-04-08 0:00](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40))
- Gnomonkey describes the dragon hunter crossbow as, at the time of the video (2019), the most effective weapon for killing Vorkath. ([2019-04-08 0:00](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40))
- Gnomonkey advises against a melee setup because a dragon hunter lance can give a negative magic attack bonus, which can make Crumble Undead splash on the spawn. ([2019-04-08 8:42](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=522))
- Gnomonkey calls the acid phase by far the most mechanically complicated and difficult attack to deal with. ([2019-04-08 9:11](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=551))
- Gnomonkey says a Wrath talisman is basically Vorkath's worst drop and leaves it on the ground. ([2019-04-08 15:52](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=952))
- Gnomonkey describes Vorkath as consistent money, unlike the Corrupted Gauntlet where most of the money comes from the Blade of Saeldor. ([2020-04-14 0:27](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=27))
- Gnomonkey suggests that players who struggle to become consistent at the Corrupted Gauntlet may be better off doing something less difficult like Vorkath for consistent cash. ([2020-04-14 1:32](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=92))
- Gnomonkey says Vorkath is a standard money boss everyone does and he is not a huge fan of its pet, a little blue dragon, placing it in D tier just above Hellpuppy. ([2022-03-12 28:02](https://youtu.be/vwCm9NKJhRE?t=1682))
- Gnomonkey says Vorkath is interesting mostly because of Woox walking, and likes it more with the Dragon hunter crossbow; Torva with the Dragon hunter lance is another method, and a ZCB may now be faster. ([2022-03-19 37:16](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2236))
- Gnomonkey says there is not a lot going on at Vorkath beyond avoiding fireballs, Woox walking the whole kill with the lance, and clicking the spawn when frozen. ([2022-03-19 37:45](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2265))
- Gnomonkey places Vorkath in B tier, lower than Zulrah, saying it is not bad but not great. ([2022-03-19 38:13](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2293))
- Gnomonkey says the blowpipe option for Vorkath is standard and boring, and that the crossbow with rune bolts is probably worse than his setup. ([2024-05-18 2:30](https://youtu.be/WvmEY9v6AnI?t=150))
- Gnomonkey notes the Fremennik kilt downgrade is a pure item and that a pure cannot complete Dragon Slayer 2, so he says the downgrade never has a use case for Vorkath. ([2025-09-06 0:53](https://youtu.be/qCsJAdpSeTU?t=53))
- Gnomonkey says the old wiki setup is surprisingly slightly easier than the Fremennik way of killing Vorkath because the shield also gives a strength bonus and is decently tanky. ([2025-09-06 1:55](https://youtu.be/qCsJAdpSeTU?t=115))
- Gnomonkey questions the wiki listing a Ring of the gods for Vorkath, since the trip is always the same length and you barely need prayer potions. ([2025-09-06 2:27](https://youtu.be/qCsJAdpSeTU?t=147))
- Gnomonkey says the specs listed for the old setup were not the worst except the Armadyl godsword, which he called absurd. ([2025-09-06 2:27](https://youtu.be/qCsJAdpSeTU?t=147))
- Gnomonkey says Fang is so good on Vorkath. ([2025-09-06 14:23](https://youtu.be/qCsJAdpSeTU?t=863))
- Gnomonkey considers the Vorkath CA of hitting Vorkath 12 times during the acid special without being hit by rapid fire or the acid pools a good CA because it teaches Woox walking, a more advanced tactic that gives faster kills. ([2025-09-11 10:26](https://youtu.be/ZjG3P7PTeIs?t=626))
- Gnomonkey says the Vorkath Woox walk CA is the kind he wishes there were more of, one that teaches an emergent piece of gameplay and rewards you for learning it. ([2025-09-11 10:55](https://youtu.be/ZjG3P7PTeIs?t=655))
