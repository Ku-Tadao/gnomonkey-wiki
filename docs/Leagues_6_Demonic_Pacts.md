# Leagues 6: Demonic Pacts

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2026-04-23 to 2026-05-05). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Gnomonkey's build uses a blind bag, which gives a chance to pull a heavy weapon from the inventory and swing it with one-tick attack speed on every attack. ([2026-04-23 0:27](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=27))
- In his build, melee attacks have a chance to trigger ranged damage echo splats, which cascade into more ranged splats because he specced into the ranged tree, producing procs on procs. ([2026-04-23 0:59](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=59))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says there are about 10 different minor builds in the pact tree, including a light weapon spec melee build and a thorns melee build that makes enemies damage themselves. ([2026-04-23 0:59](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=59))
- In the pact tree, earth magic drains defence and adds damage based on the defence drained, fire ricochets to other enemies, water heals a lot (up to about 180 total hitpoints), and air full maxes every hit with enough prayer bonus. ([2026-04-23 1:25](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=85))
- In the pact tree ranged section, crossbows are guaranteed to max hit but much slower, while double chin builds throw faster. ([2026-04-23 1:25](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=85))
- Pact tree points in Leagues 6 are unlocked by doing specific tasks in each region, which range from easy ones, like killing a troll in Asgarnia, to extremely hard ones. ([2026-04-23 4:19](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=259))
- Some final-tier region tasks were extremely long, for example full Oathplate in Kourend, or ranger boots in Asgarnia (about 200 medium clues), while the Kandarin final task was easier. ([2026-04-23 4:19](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=259))
- Some tier tasks require 25 Chambers of Xeric and 25 Theatre of Blood completions to get a single point on the tree. ([2026-04-23 4:50](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=290))
- Gnomonkey did not go into any mage for this run because he lacks the regions that would be best for this challenge; he says ideally a water mage tank build would kill the Jads faster. ([2026-05-05 1:38](https://youtu.be/AuUbRTt0YnI?t=98))
- In Leagues 6 the tier 8 relic Minion is the only relic Gnomonkey considers relevant to the Infinite Jad run, as the minion can automatically tag most of the healers. ([2026-05-05 2:36](https://youtu.be/AuUbRTt0YnI?t=156))
- In the Leagues 6 Infinite Jad run, 39,470 total Leagues points (toggle reward) were rewarded after completing it. ([2026-05-05 6:46](https://youtu.be/AuUbRTt0YnI?t=406))
- At the rate of this run, Gnomonkey says he would only need to kill 26 hours of Jads to get his onyx. ([2026-05-05 6:46](https://youtu.be/AuUbRTt0YnI?t=406))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey notes that a fire mage's ricochet damage is typeless and one fire mage can clear the entire Nylocas room in Theatre of Blood. ([2026-04-23 2:22](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=142))
- Gnomonkey says the Flask relic has tech where stalling with NPC Contact extends the invincibility frames, allowing maze skips and hitting Bloat repeatedly while invincible. ([2026-04-23 15:48](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=948))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey's Leagues 6 build uses a seven-tile halberd, the same range as a ring crossbow, which gives a damage and accuracy bonus at full max range. ([2026-04-23 0:27](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=27))
- Gnomonkey considers the huge pact skill tree one of the best things about Leagues 6, a very cool system for combat, and recommends it to people on the fence about playing. ([2026-04-23 0:31](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=31))
- Gnomonkey likes that builds in the pact tree will all be viable for most end-game content, with some tuned for specific content, such as a clip of a thorns build AFKing the six Jad challenge. ([2026-04-23 1:53](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=113))
- Gnomonkey finds the pact tree much deeper than past Leagues, where the approach was to shove points into one style, and says it feels like playing old Diablo. ([2026-04-23 1:53](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=113))
- Gnomonkey says building teammates into different builds for different roles is a cool part of raiding in Leagues 6. ([2026-04-23 2:22](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=142))
- Gnomonkey thinks Jagex should always stick with a skill-tree system for future Leagues, since it adds complexity, strategy, engagement and player choice. ([2026-04-23 7:51](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=471))
- Gnomonkey has minor nitpicks about the balance of some trees, finding some weaker than the others, and says that Jagex clearly had an idea how players would spec. ([2026-04-23 7:51](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=471))
- Gnomonkey thinks Leagues is a free opportunity to experiment with systems such as a thorns piece of equipment or ricocheting magic, which could become future raid rewards. ([2026-04-23 8:23](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=503))
- Gnomonkey finds Leagues 6 much slower than the previous Leagues, taking him three days to finish his halberd and begin using the melee build, and says the slower pace grows on him because decisions matter more and feel more rewarding. ([2026-04-23 9:23](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=563))
- Gnomonkey thinks the Leagues 6 relics were solid compared to the last Leagues, with no obviously wrong choice like Fairy's flight in Raging Echoes. ([2026-04-23 14:55](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=895))
- Butler's Bell versus Transmute was a big discussion point because both were very good, and the clue relic remains very good for getting points. ([2026-04-23 15:20](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=920))
- Gnomonkey's only relic regret was his tier eight pick of the Minion relic, which picks up loot, has damage boosted by giving it Zamorak items and can do AoE. ([2026-04-23 15:20](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=920))
- Gnomonkey says Leagues 6 was probably the buggiest Leagues, which is saying a lot, with really annoying bugs. ([2026-04-23 16:16](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=976))
- Gnomonkey says getting the Ivandis flail was much harder without Perdu and that Perdu should always be in the starting area of a Leagues. ([2026-04-23 17:14](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=1034))
- Gnomonkey credits Jagex for fixing basically all of the listed bugs, though he says it sucks to take a week to fix them, and says the slower pace helped him actually benefit from the fixes unlike in the last Leagues. ([2026-04-23 19:52](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=1192))
- Gnomonkey considers the Leagues 6 questing very tedious and hopes the next Leagues lets players bypass quests, like the buyable quest unlocks in Shattered Relics. ([2026-04-23 20:21](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=1221))
- Gnomonkey was annoyed that the quest for Echo Moxie, the first Echo boss, was required, and that Sins of the Father was required for several pact points. ([2026-04-23 20:50](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=1250))
- Gnomonkey says Leagues 6 is not his favourite Leagues; he prefers Raging Echoes for its endgame potential with Echo Sol and more impactful Echo weapons. ([2026-04-23 21:45](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=1305))
- Gnomonkey predicts the next Leagues will be the best ever because Jagex spent so much effort making the pact tree that it dropped other focuses like Echo bosses and an uber endgame boss like Yama. ([2026-04-23 22:14](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=1334))
- Gnomonkey believes Leagues 6 was worth it and not a waste of time, because the tree system gives Jagex many avenues to test items and ideas that provide value to the main game, even though some people are bummed about two seasonal modes in one year (Deadman and Leagues). ([2026-04-23 22:41](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=1361))

## Events

- One week after Leagues 6 launched, Jagex released a set of changes to the league, which Gnomonkey discusses in this video. ([2026-04-23 0:00](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y))
- The update added four bonus points, so there are now 44 obtainable points for a cap of 40 points, letting players skip some tasks. ([2026-04-23 4:50](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=290))
- The update added easier point tasks such as equipping a piece of Moons armour or an obby cape. ([2026-04-23 5:23](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=323))
- The update buffed many neutral nodes with more accuracy across the board and more prayer penetration, and percent damage nodes now also give accuracy. ([2026-04-23 5:23](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=323))
- The Flask relic was bugged against bosses in walls such as Ulm and Verzik, which was fixed in the update so it now works on everything, which was why Gnomonkey took the Minion. ([2026-04-23 15:48](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=948))
- Players told Gnomonkey that Doom of Mokhaiotl deep delves had very low drop rates; he could not confirm this and did only delves one through eight for drops. ([2026-04-23 16:45](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=1005))
- Perdu is not in every region; without him there was no way to get the ectophial in Morytania, and one task to get Morytania legs 2 required using an ectophial, which has since been auto-finished. ([2026-04-23 16:45](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=1005))
- Many tasks were bugged; Gnomonkey chopped about a thousand redwood logs for the chop 250 redwood logs into shafts task, a free 200 point task, but it never completed. ([2026-04-23 17:14](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=1034))
- For several days the total level achievements did not unlock, apparently because Sailing was stuck at level one, which was fixed fairly early. ([2026-04-23 17:47](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=1067))
- The Transmute relic, supposed to auto-transform resources every five ticks, stopped working whenever the player was doing anything else such as chopping, fishing or mining. ([2026-04-23 18:18](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=1098))
- The quiver extra slot would not regenerate ammo, which broke the ranged tree and crystal blessing crossbow builds; this was fixed on the day of the video. ([2026-04-23 18:50](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=1130))
- Many regions did not unlock the Slayer tasks they should, and Gnomonkey skipped around 2,000 points of tasks trying to get Hydras, which appeared only after telling the Slayer master he would do hard tasks, at combat level 121. ([2026-04-23 19:20](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=1160))
- Gnomonkey planned a stream to kill Zuk through his healers with his melee build and a seven-tile halberd. ([2026-04-23 23:12](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=1392))
- In Leagues 6: Demonic Pacts there are self-imposed challenges players can do, and Gnomonkey chooses killing 1,000 Jads in a row. ([2026-05-05 0:00](https://youtu.be/AuUbRTt0YnI))
- Gnomonkey says he originally planned to go Thorns build to mess around with things after he finished having fun with blind bag in Leagues. ([2026-05-05 1:38](https://youtu.be/AuUbRTt0YnI?t=98))
