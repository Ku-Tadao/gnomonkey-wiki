## Methods

- Once Prime is dealt with, switch to melee and attack Supreme; if you are at 100% special, always drop a Saradomin godsword spec on Supreme regardless of his HP for the prayer restoration. ([2022-04-19 7:42](https://youtu.be/QNv-UATHGPs?t=462))
- The Odablock Warriors killed Dagannoth Supreme first once the rival teams left, which gave them all the monsters for themselves. ([2026-06-10 22:49](https://youtu.be/qyG_lTC4pDU?t=1369))

## Stats and numbers

- Dagannoth Supreme is ranged and Dagannoth Rex is melee; the kings follow the combat triangle but are not invincible against the wrong style, just very tanky. ([2022-04-19 6:28](https://youtu.be/QNv-UATHGPs?t=388))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says Dagannoth Supreme is really strong toward range, so he cannot range it and asks teammates to help him mage it, including with Arcane swiftness. ([2026-06-10 23:51](https://youtu.be/qyG_lTC4pDU?t=1431))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says Dagannoth Supreme's range is insane and that it is a monster that should only be attacked if it can be pulled. ([2026-06-10 22:18](https://youtu.be/qyG_lTC4pDU?t=1338))
