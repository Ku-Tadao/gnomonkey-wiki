**Dagannoth Supreme** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2022-04-19 and 2026-06-10. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Once Prime is dealt with, switch to melee and attack Supreme; if you are at 100% special, always drop a Saradomin godsword spec on Supreme regardless of his HP for the prayer restoration.[^QNv-UATHGPs-462]
* The Odablock Warriors killed Dagannoth Supreme first once the rival teams left, which gave them all the monsters for themselves.[^qyG_lTC4pDU-1369]

## Stats and numbers

* Dagannoth Supreme is ranged and Dagannoth Rex is melee; the kings follow the combat triangle but are not invincible against the wrong style, just very tanky.[^QNv-UATHGPs-388]

## Tips

* Dagannoth Supreme is really strong toward range, so he cannot range it and asks teammates to help him mage it, including with Arcane swiftness.[^qyG_lTC4pDU-1431]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says Dagannoth Supreme's range is insane and that it is a monster that should only be attacked if it can be pulled.[^qyG_lTC4pDU-1338]
