**RuneScape 3** is an other topic that Gnomonkey covers in 4 videos between 2023-07-30 and 2026-08-23. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Stats and numbers

* Doing all RS3 dailies would take about four to six hours, with weeklies, monthlies and tokens to repeat them on top.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-828]
* Each Wilderness Flash Event gives an XP chunk and a sack of wild rewards worth an average of 170k.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-972]
* The Infernal Star and Evil Tree events have three stages and give a sack of very wild rewards worth an average of 300k.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-1003]

## Tips

* Alt1 is a tool that scans your screen and can solve clue scrolls and similar, but is nowhere close to RuneLite.[^KIticRFI1Rc-548]
* Gnomonkey shows the Daily Scape website, a checklist for RS3 players with reset timers for all dailies.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-784]
* Your Old School membership lets you log into your RuneScape 3 account and play the Leagues on your account, so there is no risk in trying it.[^59f7Sm5ip_I-1507]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says RS3 receives update after update for its endgame PvM while the best OSRS players are left kicking sand.[^iG23QZE9pds-698]
* Gnomonkey says RS3 has loads of non-raid endgame PvM, including three Elite Dungeons mini-raids tied into Dungeoneering, which are Diablo-style dungeons ending in a huge final boss.[^iG23QZE9pds-711]
* Gnomonkey says RS3 has multiple difficult endgame raid bosses (he names Kalphite King, Vorago, Barrows: Rise of the Six, Solak and Zamorak) and difficult solo bosses including Araxxor, Zuk, Arch-Glacor and Telos, while OSRS only has Nex and Nightmare as similar content.[^iG23QZE9pds-731]
* Gnomonkey says Telos in RS3 has a risk mechanic where you can leave loot in the chest and do another kill, increasing your loot modifier but risking the stored loot, with difficulty rising each kill up to 4,000%; Arch-Glacor has a similar Rage Meter.[^iG23QZE9pds-762]
* Gnomonkey says RS3 has horrible problems such as microtransactions and dailies, but with that and the lack of polls the developers have total freedom to make cool endgame content, and OSRS should take ideas like Telos streaking and raid bosses and implement them with the old school touch.[^iG23QZE9pds-793]
* Gnomonkey says the Wilderness is effectively removed in RS3 (PvP no longer exists), with skilling Wilderness flash events starting at the top of every hour via the Edgeville Wilderness lodestone, and activities listed on the wiki.[^KIticRFI1Rc-482]
* Gnomonkey says RS3 and OSRS are at extreme opposite ends for run energy: in RS3 run energy is almost irrelevant, you can recover it with a Musician, and there are no stamina potions, so you will never need them.[^KIticRFI1Rc-616]
* Gnomonkey likes that in RS3 you can look into the distance and spot landmarks like the Dominion Tower, saying that is not a thing in Old School.[^KIticRFI1Rc-882]
* Gnomonkey notes that in RS3 one to three sealed clue scrolls can be obtained about every 15 minutes from skilling, and that he understands 200 million Woodcutting XP yields around 5k clue scrolls.[^KIticRFI1Rc-1305]
* Gnomonkey says RS3's Necromancy release was a chance to make the early game more accessible for new players, but Jagex nerfed combat XP and made levelling harder for early players, which he calls an interesting decision.[^KIticRFI1Rc-1307]
* Gnomonkey says Evolution of Combat has improved since launch and RS3's high-tier PvMers are impressive, but the time-gated dailies ruined RS3 for him.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-1127]
* Gnomonkey says time-gated content in RS3 ruins it more than microtransactions, because an RS3 ironman can avoid microtransactions but cannot avoid dailies; he says Jagex will never remove dailies.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-1161]
* Gnomonkey says RuneScape 3 is a really enjoyable game and not total garbage, and he has now finally experienced some of its endgame.[^59f7Sm5ip_I-588]
* Gnomonkey says he would want tile markers and a menu entry swapper in RS3 and that plugin support makes him want to try playing RS3, possibly a streamer group ironman.[^59f7Sm5ip_I-1393]
* Gnomonkey says RuneScape 3 at its core is a really good game with big flaws that Jagex has been polishing for the last year, and hopes players come back and give feedback.[^59f7Sm5ip_I-1432]

## Events

* Gnomonkey notices mining has changed since he last played RS3, and that the tutorial is much the same as before, with a special hyper rock added.[^KIticRFI1Rc-33]
* RuneScape 3 reworked and cleaned up early areas such as Lumbridge, removing eyesores like a giant crater and trash, which makes it easier to get into.[^59f7Sm5ip_I-561]
* RuneScape 3 has cut a lot of dailies and almost completely cut microtransactions; you can still buy bonus XP directly but there is no gambling aspect like prismatic traps.[^59f7Sm5ip_I-681]
* RuneScape 3 is releasing a plugin API in a couple of months that Jagex said will be open source, like RuneLite, and group Ironman was added not long ago.[^59f7Sm5ip_I-1387]

## Trivia

* RS3's interface is completely scalable and can be arranged however you want.[^KIticRFI1Rc-0]
* RS3 lets you change the whole skybox, with options like an eclipse and shadow-world skyboxes that he thinks look really cool.[^KIticRFI1Rc-548]
* In RS3 players are mostly at the Max Guild because everybody is maxed, the Lumbridge-Al Kharid bridge area is connected, and the Al Kharid gate has been removed.[^KIticRFI1Rc-583]
* Tutorial-era Gielinor features like Entrana-style islands have changed, and he believes an island he asks about (Taverley-area 'Toriel Island' as transcribed) was deleted.[^KIticRFI1Rc-659]
* RS3 has roughly 100 times the dailies of OSRS, including daily Treasure Hunter spins, a traveling merchant with a daily random stock, daily challenges, Jack of Trades, Soul Reaper, player-owned ports and farm, and more.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-796]
* All shops in RS3 are on a daily reset timer and that buying out the rune shops is the main way an RS3 ironman gets runes.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-828]
* RS3 removed the Wilderness, replacing it with Wilderness Flash Events that happen every hour on the hour and have almost no requirements (90% can be done at combat level 3).[^uDhGFEjrpyc-941]
* Brawling gloves are a rare reward that give very powerful tier 87 weapons, which he compares to ripping a twisted bow out of a sack, along with a very rare Dark Onyx core.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-1035]
* RS3 ironmen must repeat Wilderness Flash Events constantly to get three of a rare item that has two rolls at 2 in 830 odds.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-1064]
* Gethixian caches in RS3 let you do Divination twice a day for about 30k XP in five to ten minutes, in a skill whose base rate is about 10k XP an hour at level 10 and peaks around 80k XP an hour, so people train it just by doing the caches.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-1064]
* He mained RuneScape 3 long ago and had a max cape, then moved to Old School in about 2017 because of a lack of updates and wanting plugin support.[^59f7Sm5ip_I-1392]
