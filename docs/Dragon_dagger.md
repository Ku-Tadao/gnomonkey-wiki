## Methods

- Gnomonkey wants weapon poison specifically for his dragon dagger. ([2024-08-08 31:39](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=1899))
- In the Fight Caves, Gnomonkey stabs with the poisoned dragon dagger until poison lands, then switches back to his dragon scimitar. ([2024-08-08 1:01:49](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=3709))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says a dragon dagger costs around 30k and that dragon dagger poking is better, but he needs the Attack level to wield it. ([2024-08-08 8:04](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=484))
- Gnomonkey says a Dragon dagger is needed for Warden phase 2 because the core takes guaranteed max hits on weapons, and that no other spec weapon can be substituted there. ([2025-06-25 4:55](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=295))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says an Abyssal dagger does not work as a substitute for the Dragon dagger at the Warden core because it maxes lower. ([2025-06-25 5:22](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=322))
