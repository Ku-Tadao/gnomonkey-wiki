**Dragon dagger** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2024-08-08 and 2025-06-25. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Gnomonkey wants weapon poison specifically for his dragon dagger.[^_cIv_pRhm9U-1899]
* In the Fight Caves, Gnomonkey stabs with the poisoned dragon dagger until poison lands, then switches back to his dragon scimitar.[^_cIv_pRhm9U-3709]

## Tips

* A dragon dagger costs around 30k and that dragon dagger poking is better, but he needs the Attack level to wield it.[^_cIv_pRhm9U-484]
* A Dragon dagger is needed for Warden phase 2 because the core takes guaranteed max hits on weapons, and that no other spec weapon can be substituted there.[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-295]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says an Abyssal dagger does not work as a substitute for the Dragon dagger at the Warden core because it maxes lower.[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-322]
