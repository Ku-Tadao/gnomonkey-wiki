# Tombs of Amascut gear setup

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 7 videos (2022-11-18 to 2026-04-01). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Gnomonkey's Tombs of Amascut gear setup is seven ways for each combat style, and notes each ranged switch is a max hit salted. ([2022-11-18 2:28](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=148))
- He camps Lightbearer and Blood fury with max melee. ([2022-11-18 2:28](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=148))
- His inventory includes Yellow Kiris, a Toxic blowpipe and 5 restores; to flick heavily he suggests bringing a Zaryte crossbow instead of claws with 4 restores. ([2022-11-18 2:28](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=148))
- The rest of his inventory is all runes and the book, a small stack of chinchompas, a divine super combat potion and a rapier for P2 of the Warden. ([2022-11-18 3:01](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=181))
- The setup melees, crystal armour as the melee set, with a one-way Bow of Faerdhinen: you shadow Zebak and Akkha, melee Ba-Ba and Kephri, shadow everything on the Wardens, and range only phase 2. ([2024-07-01 0:54](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=54))
- For this 300 ToA, Gnomonkey wears Blue Moon armour instead of melee armour because it has a strength bonus, so it works as melee armour, and it also has noticeable crush defence for tanking Baba's punches. ([2024-10-24 0:26](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=26))
- Gnomonkey wears a Serpentine helm in ToA because it is fairly tanky and protects against venom, and says buying a Neitiznot faceguard later when you have money is great but the Serpentine helm works for now. ([2024-10-24 0:59](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=59))
- Gnomonkey wears an Amulet of fury as a one-way setup and uses Barrows gloves as his ranging gloves too, so he needs few gear switches; he also wears a Fire cape and dragon boots. ([2024-10-24 0:59](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=59))
- Gnomonkey's range switch for ToA is a blowpipe with Barrows gloves and an assembler, plus a crossbow; he also brings a dragon dagger (DDS) for the Warden core. ([2024-10-24 1:55](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=115))
- Gnomonkey's setup uses a Toxic blowpipe for utility, swarms and the monkey room, a Bow of Faerdhinen as the main ranged weapon for Zebak and the Wardens, and the Eye of Ayak as the main magic weapon. ([2026-04-01 0:26](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=26))
- He wears Virtus armor (Blue Moon armor is also good) and brings five Restore potions (one a Sanfew serum), three Brews, a Divine super combat, Burning claws as the special weapon, and a Dragon dagger needed to spec out the core. ([2026-04-01 0:53](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=53))
- He brings Aether runes to use Thralls and Death Charge, and says Thralls speed up a Tombs of Amascut run by one to two minutes because everything is tanky; double Death Charge from Yama is very good to have. ([2026-04-01 1:24](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=84))
- Bandos godsword is used on Ba-Ba, Kephri and Zebak; a Bone dagger is slightly worse because it cannot be re-specced but, as an opening spec, it is guaranteed to hit and still drains levels. ([2026-04-01 1:40](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=100))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says the Mixed hide and blowpipe setup is about 30% worse on the Wardens' phase 2 than the Bow of Faerdhinen setup, but is close enough to still succeed with. ([2024-07-01 0:31](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=31))
- Gnomonkey says he brings four Sanfew serums for a money run, three if feeling spicy, and thinks ten is enough here. ([2024-07-01 38:43](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=2323))
- Gnomonkey says the whole Shadow setup costs about two billion flat, mostly the Shadow, plus an Avernic defender. ([2024-07-01 42:43](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=2563))
- Gnomonkey says the whole 300 ToA setup costs about 65 mil (he first reads it as about 70 mil), and the video later describes it as roughly 70 mil gear. ([2024-10-24 5:55](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=355))
- Gnomonkey says thralls usually make up around 5 to 10 percent of your DPS in ToA, and matter even more with weak or budget gear, so they noticeably speed up the raid. ([2024-10-24 6:49](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=409))
- Gnomonkey says the Bow of faerdhinen setup in this guide costs about 200 million gp for the ranged setup, and later says the Atlatl setup costs about 25 million compared to the Bofa's 180 million. ([2025-06-25 2:07](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=127))
- Gnomonkey says thralls add 0.625 DPS, meaning about two thirds DPS on every boss, which speeds up the raid by several minutes especially with weaker gear because the number is static. ([2025-06-25 3:01](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=181))

## Tips

- Zebak has a 1 in 6 chance of being level 4 by the third room with Pathseeker, and Gnomonkey wants to keep him below level 4 if possible. ([2022-11-18 3:01](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=181))
- The boss order puts melee-heavy bosses first because melee benefits less from Salt than Tumeken's shadow or the Twisted bow do, since melee gets a much smaller max hit increase. ([2022-11-18 3:30](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=210))
- A Voidwaker would be massive in this setup and is a great weapon for Core, saving a lot of time; without it, Gnomonkey says to use a dragon dagger special (DDS) on the Core, which guarantees three down. ([2024-07-01 1:30](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=90))
- Gnomonkey says the Bandos godsword is only used on Ba-Ba and could be dropped; the Bandos godsword does not work well on Kephri but can still land, with about 30% accuracy. ([2024-07-01 2:28](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=148))
- Gnomonkey says if you have a Voidwaker you should bring it, and Chinchompas are also nice for catching Kephri's swarms and clearing the monkey room faster, though two fewer Sanfew serums means eleven. ([2024-07-01 38:32](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=2312))
- If you have no Bow of Faerdhinen and use a Mixed hide plus blowpipe instead, you must pot a regular ranging potion to kill the Ba-Ba boulders, which barely gets the needed max hit with Rigor, and you may need to put the pipe on accurate. ([2024-07-01 42:43](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=2563))
- Gnomonkey reminds viewers to charge their Trident of the swamp, because it does nothing without charges; his mage switch also uses an imbued mage cape, Book of the Dead offhand and an occult necklace. ([2024-10-24 1:25](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=85))
- Gnomonkey says anyone who has a Phosani's-style upgrade (sand few) can bring it as anti-poison; he wears anti-poison at all times with his Serpentine helm. ([2024-10-24 6:16](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=376))
- Gnomonkey wears Blue moon armour in ToA because it acts as both strength armour and mage armour (equal to Ancestral for magic) while also giving a strength bonus, though it is thin armour. ([2025-06-25 0:37](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=37))
- Gnomonkey wears Barrows gloves (which double as ranged gloves), Dragon boots and a Lightbearer, saying Lightbearer is cheap and lets you spam specs without hurting melee DPS much. ([2025-06-25 1:25](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=85))
- Gnomonkey strongly recommends bringing a Blood fury while learning ToA because it completely negates Akkha's chip damage and recovers from mistakes, though it is semi-expensive; he also lists Torture and Rancour as good alternatives. ([2025-06-25 1:25](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=85))
- Gnomonkey brings a blowpipe mainly for utility (guaranteed max hits in the Monkey room and parts of Baba, tagging swarms at Kephri and shooting Akkha's shadows) rather than for raw DPS. ([2025-06-25 2:28](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=148))
- Gnomonkey's magic switch is a simple trident of the swamp with occult necklace, imbued god cape and tormented bracelet, with a Book of the Dead as the offhand switch for thralls. ([2025-06-25 3:01](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=181))
- Gnomonkey's supplies for the early rooms are brews, restores, a divine ranging potion and a divine super combat potion, both used at Kephri. ([2025-06-25 3:26](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=206))
- Gnomonkey says players without the money for a Bow of faerdhinen can use an Atlatl, which he calls a super cheap and good ranged option, and he plans a separate guide for it. ([2025-06-25 59:20](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=3560))
- For 150 raid level presets, Gnomonkey's preferred setup has both Overclocked invocations on, preparing you for Insanity, and he does not think you should do 150s without both of them, though many early invocations are preference-based. ([2025-06-26 15:29](https://youtu.be/DaUViQQ8TL8?t=929))
- In his 150 setup the trickiest invocations are Aerial Assault and On a Diet, since you cannot eat the crunchy bugs on death, but with Moon (blue moon) hybrid gear you have plenty of space for supplies. ([2025-06-26 16:00](https://youtu.be/DaUViQQ8TL8?t=960))
- For 200s Gnomonkey adds more Warden invocations because they are fairly simple and speed up the fight, and suggests learning Mind the Gap early since it is required at higher invocation levels and only requires staying near the room edges. ([2025-06-26 16:34](https://youtu.be/DaUViQQ8TL8?t=994))
- By 300s Gnomonkey says you should be comfortable with a faster Warden and that Insanity is a must, being 50 points to avoid higher boss invocations or fewer supplies; he highly recommends his first Insanity 300 preset. ([2025-06-26 17:09](https://youtu.be/DaUViQQ8TL8?t=1029))
- As an alternative to Insanity at 300, Gnomonkey says you can keep Walk the Path on, but the bosses will be stronger and you will have to turn on many Warden invocations and Baba and Aerial Assault. ([2025-06-26 17:30](https://youtu.be/DaUViQQ8TL8?t=1050))
- For 350s, Gnomonkey's standard setup has Insanity and Walk the Path on, a timer on, Softcore on and all Warden invocations on, adding more boss invocations. ([2025-06-26 17:37](https://youtu.be/DaUViQQ8TL8?t=1057))
- For 450s Gnomonkey adds Pathfinder (so some level 4 bosses appear), turns on both Baba invocations and all Warden invocations, and in a second example uses Pathseeker and adds Keep Back. ([2025-06-26 18:59](https://youtu.be/DaUViQQ8TL8?t=1139))
- At 450 with a yellow Keris, Gnomonkey says you could use Dehydration (worth 30 points) and take off annoying boss invocations like Boulder Dash. ([2025-06-26 19:29](https://youtu.be/DaUViQQ8TL8?t=1169))
- His typical 500 setup has Dehydration on as a must, the timer off, and Double Trouble on; a second setup without Double Trouble uses Overly Draining instead, which makes raids much longer and loses a Keris at phase 4 Warden. ([2025-06-26 19:38](https://youtu.be/DaUViQQ8TL8?t=1178))
- For a 500 without a yellow Keris, Gnomonkey says you can do it without Dehydration, but you must have everything else on including Overly Draining and Keep Back. ([2025-06-26 19:58](https://youtu.be/DaUViQQ8TL8?t=1198))
- Gnomonkey wears Blue moon hybrid armour (strength and mage armour) in this 500 run, saving about three slots, because melee DPS matters little in the two rooms he melees (Baba, which no longer hurts through prayer, and Kephri). ([2025-06-27 0:26](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=26))
- Gnomonkey strongly recommends a Blood fury without a Keris because it prevents using brews in every room before Warden; he says the six brews are all needed for Warden, and a Rancour would also work. ([2025-06-27 0:58](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=58))
- His ranged setup is a Bow of faerdhinen with Assembler (max cape), because Warden phase 4 is the hardest part of the raid and he wants as much ranged DPS as possible, and the Assembler also gives max hits. ([2025-06-27 1:27](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=87))
- Gnomonkey brings a whip to hit the core in Warden phase 2 because Overly Draining is on, noting it is a four down, and uses a basic mage swap that is only used in phase 2. ([2025-06-27 1:55](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=115))
- His 500 supplies include six brews (needed to tank orbs in phase 1 and all of phase 2), seven Sandfew potions, a prayer regeneration potion (equivalent to about two Sandfews, used throughout the raid), a Bandos godsword and a divine super combat for Kephri. ([2025-06-27 1:55](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=115))
- Gnomonkey brings a Bandos godsword instead of a Bone dagger even with Overly Draining on, because the godsword is better when forced to use ranged damage on Warden; he specs it on phases 1, 3 and 4 and uses it to last-hit the core in phase 2. ([2025-06-27 2:28](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=148))
- Gnomonkey says players with Aether runes (from Yama) get thralls and Death charge for free because Aether runes count as both cosmic and soul runes, though he says Death charge does not do much in this setup. ([2025-06-27 2:28](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=148))
- His 500 pre-pot is prayer regeneration, extended anti-venom, two divines and stamina. ([2025-06-27 4:01](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=241))
- Gnomonkey brings Ava's assembler to save darts and because it gives the Bow of Faerdhinen a max hit, and says to check that Virtus mask actually gives a max hit in your setup, otherwise don't bring it. ([2026-04-01 3:01](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=181))
- Pre-potting stamina, Divine super combat, Divine ranging (or regular ranging) and a prayer regeneration potion is good, because the monkey room uses a lot of prayer. ([2026-04-01 3:57](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=237))
- Extended anti-venom lasts long enough to carry from Ba-Ba into Kephri, so you can skip the Sanfew serum if you use it. ([2026-04-01 3:59](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=239))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey considers the Bandos godsword hugely important for solos: it can re-spec bosses if it misses and can hit the core on final hits, making it better than the Bone dagger despite how much he liked the dagger. ([2022-11-18 2:28](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=148))
- Gnomonkey calls Eternal boots and Pegasian boots an absolute waste of space in the Tombs of Amascut, as both are sub 1% DPS increases in the rooms where you use them. ([2022-11-18 3:01](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=181))
- Gnomonkey calculated that with a Shadow but no Twisted bow, a Bow of Faerdhinen plus top, bottom, gloves and Blood fury is much better on the Wardens' phase 2 than a Mixed hide plus blowpipe setup. ([2024-07-01 0:00](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A))
- Gnomonkey says the Torva helm, switches, Ferocious gloves, Zaryte vambraces, Assembler, Crystal helm and Anguish are all max hits, but he thinks even a two-way with vambraces and Pharaoh's gloves is better than other range switches, and he camps eternal boots. ([2024-07-01 3:00](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=180))
- Gnomonkey says Virtus is really bad for the shadow: replacing Ancestral loses five max hits, and the shadow multiplies max hits tremendously, so you always need the full Ancestral set with it. ([2024-07-01 35:20](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=2120))
- Gnomonkey calls the Osmumten's fang (about 13 mil at the time) the most powerful weapon in his inventory for this raid. ([2024-10-24 1:25](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=85))
- Gnomonkey tells beginners not to bring Barrage to ToA and to bring thralls instead, saying the monkey room is easy these days and does not require ancient magicks. ([2024-10-24 6:16](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=376))
- Gnomonkey says an Osmumten's fang is desperately needed for Tombs of Amascut and he would not recommend doing it as a main without one; at the time of the video it cost roughly 20 to 30 million gp. ([2025-06-25 0:55](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=55))
- Gnomonkey says the Bow of faerdhinen is super good in ToA, the best DPS on Akkha in this setup, and is used on Zebak and makes the Monkey room easier. ([2025-06-25 2:07](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=127))
- Gnomonkey says you do not need Blood barrage in this raid and recommends double Death charge, noting it is a free slot because Either runes count as both soul and cosmic runes and the runes fit in the rune pouch. ([2025-06-25 3:26](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=206))
- Gnomonkey says the Eye of Ayak is close to the Tumeken's shadow but not quite as good in Tombs of Amascut because the Shadow's bonus is multiplied by four, yet it is a little better than the Bow of Faerdhinen on Akkha and the Wardens. ([2026-04-01 0:26](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=26))
- Gnomonkey says Burning claws give better DPS than the Voidwaker up to about raid level 375, while the Voidwaker is better at higher levels and can hit the core; at lower raid levels stick with Burning claws. ([2026-04-01 0:53](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=53))
- Gnomonkey says a Surge potion adds only a little DPS with weak spec weapons like Burning claws or Voidwaker and no Zaryte crossbow, so it is optional. ([2026-04-01 2:28](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=148))
- Gnomonkey says Oathplate is an option that is not too expensive, has the same strength as the shown armor, slightly worse defense that mostly does not matter, lands the Bandos godsword more often, and lets you spam Burning claws more accurately at the Wardens. ([2026-04-01 3:29](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=209))
