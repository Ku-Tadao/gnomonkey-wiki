## Requirements

- Master clues require various skills and several unlocked items: the Lumberjack outfit, prospector's kit, angler's outfit, full graceful, a fire cape, a fighter torso, infernal axe and Barrows gloves. ([2019-08-14 0:00](https://youtu.be/4b71E7ucBUo))
- Master clues require 100% Arceuus favour, 75% Hosidius favour, 100% Piscarilius favour, 100% Shayzien favour, and a huge variety of quests up to and including Song of the Elves. ([2019-08-14 0:26](https://youtu.be/4b71E7ucBUo?t=26))

## Stats and numbers

- Master clue scrolls are six to eight step clues that drop the bloodhound pet at a 1 in 5000 rate, which Gnomonkey says makes it one of, if not the rarest, pets in the game. ([2019-08-14 0:26](https://youtu.be/4b71E7ucBUo?t=26))
- After completing a master clue there is a reasonable 1 in 15 chance to fight the mimic, which is required for the music cape. ([2019-08-14 0:57](https://youtu.be/4b71E7ucBUo?t=57))
- Master clues give around 200k to 500k per clue in regular drops alone; all six of the 3rd age items in the set are worth over 300 million, with the 3rd age pickaxe at 1.1 billion at the time. ([2019-08-14 0:57](https://youtu.be/4b71E7ucBUo?t=57))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey's master clue gear tab has basic void ranged gear, desert amulet 4 for regen and banking, quest point cape for fairy rings, Kandarin headgear 4 for teleports to Sherlock, and a music cape for teleporting to Falador (which he cannot use until he finds a mimic). ([2019-08-14 1:30](https://youtu.be/4b71E7ucBUo?t=90))
- He also brings a full set of basic runes for teleports, five sharks, and a ranging potion; a crafting cape is very smart to bring if available. ([2019-08-14 1:30](https://youtu.be/4b71E7ucBUo?t=90))
- Gnomonkey used RuneLite to create a tab for items Watson may want and does not keep an Armadyl helmet in the bank since it would only be used for clues; tabs can be imported from codes in the pinned comments via the import tag button. ([2019-08-14 1:59](https://youtu.be/4b71E7ucBUo?t=119))
- Gnomonkey does not explain how to solve the clues because RuneLite does that for the most part. ([2019-08-14 2:15](https://youtu.be/4b71E7ucBUo?t=135))
- When a master clue requires buying items like an Armadyl helmet or dragon chainbody, Gnomonkey advises taking the chance for a profitable flip if planning to sell right after, since there is no point rushing if the loss on selling matches the clue's gain. ([2019-08-14 4:00](https://youtu.be/4b71E7ucBUo?t=240))
