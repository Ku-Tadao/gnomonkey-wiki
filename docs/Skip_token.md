## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says RuneScape 3 has skip tickets for specific step types, such as the puzzle box ticket, which he cites as around 1.3 million RuneScape GP, and which he says would equate to about 100K to 200K in Old School RuneScape. ([2025-04-08 10:43](https://youtu.be/WQ9Zg4fQRJo?t=643))
- Gnomonkey says the RuneScape 3 puzzle box skip ticket recently dropped from a price as high as 4 million, which he estimates as roughly 300K to 500K in Old School RuneScape. ([2025-04-08 11:24](https://youtu.be/WQ9Zg4fQRJo?t=684))
- Gnomonkey notes that the blog does not mention a limit on how many skip tokens can be used per clue, so players could rip through all steps except the final one. ([2025-04-08 11:53](https://youtu.be/WQ9Zg4fQRJo?t=713))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey expects skip tokens to be used mainly by collection log players chasing max rank, and not by regular players, because they would spend large amounts of GP spamming through clues. ([2025-04-08 10:21](https://youtu.be/WQ9Zg4fQRJo?t=621))
- Gnomonkey says tradable skip tokens would make selling the tokens to cloggers the main profit from clues, so clue loot like a third age piece becomes secondary to getting a ticket roll. ([2025-04-08 10:48](https://youtu.be/WQ9Zg4fQRJo?t=648))
- Gnomonkey says he does not like skip tokens even if they were untradeable, calling them a band-aid for people who liked stacking clues. ([2025-04-08 12:15](https://youtu.be/WQ9Zg4fQRJo?t=735))
- Gnomonkey notes that other players have suggested reroll tokens or making skip tokens untradeable, but argues that anything that skips or re-rolls steps is a buff that reduces clue item value. ([2025-04-08 16:12](https://youtu.be/WQ9Zg4fQRJo?t=972))
- Gnomonkey argues that tickets and tokens in any form should be removed, because a drop from a clue that boosts clue scrolls further only buffs clues for the sake of buffing them. ([2025-04-08 20:58](https://youtu.be/WQ9Zg4fQRJo?t=1258))

## Events

- The Poll 84 proposal introduces a skip token for each clue tier, obtained from clue scroll reward chests, which is tradable and stackable. ([2025-04-08 9:15](https://youtu.be/WQ9Zg4fQRJo?t=555))
- A skip token skips a single clue step within a scroll and adds a step of progress without doing it, but cannot be used on the final step of a clue. ([2025-04-08 9:53](https://youtu.be/WQ9Zg4fQRJo?t=593))
