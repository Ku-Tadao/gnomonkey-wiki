**Mokha boots** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2025-03-11 and 2025-03-24. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* The base boots start with 1% damage, 2 ranged strength and 4 melee strength, rising to 6 melee strength, 3 ranged strength and 2% mage damage when fully upgraded; each Cerberus boot adds roughly +2 melee, +1 ranged strength and +1% mage damage.[^-98-rsX1soM-54]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey likes the change that lets players add Cerberus boots slowly over time, because being required to obtain all the crystals immediately was really annoying; he says they definitely needed to do that.[^-98-rsX1soM-27]
* Gnomonkey thinks the stat buff to the boots was not necessary but is not necessarily bad, and says the boots were always fine and Jagex should stop touching them.[^-98-rsX1soM-29]
* Gnomonkey notes that 4 melee strength by default is worse than primordial boots and the same as dragon boots, but the boots are very good for ranging and are best in slot for ranged strength as soon as they drop.[^-98-rsX1soM-54]
* Gnomonkey would have liked getting kits for the boots but accepts it, and says the boots look sick.[^-98-rsX1soM-114]
* Gnomonkey says the boots were totally fine, that some people had an issue with them for some reason, and he is glad they were not touched.[^-E1yidIYQ4Y-84]

## Events

* In blog #47 the Mokha boots now drop in a form that can be worn immediately and are upgraded by using Cerberus boots one at a time, rather than requiring Pegasian boots (pegs) up front.[^-98-rsX1soM-27]
* Jagex said the new design removes the outright requirement to own Pegasian boots, which should relieve economic pressure on ranger boots.[^-98-rsX1soM-83]
* The base boots are tradable but become untradable once upgrading starts, and they can be reverted to their component parts.[^-98-rsX1soM-83]
* Upgrading the boots takes a number of demon tiers per step, and three sets of Cerberus boots are needed for fully maxed boots.[^-98-rsX1soM-83]
* Jagex said the three planned aesthetic options (kits) for the boots are dropped because the new design complicates that work, so only one look will exist.[^-98-rsX1soM-114]
* Jagex made no changes to the Avernic treads in the second blog, and Gnomonkey says they were perfect and never needed changing.[^-E1yidIYQ4Y-25]
* Jagex corrected its earlier blog: the base treads do not drop with the original stats, and melee strength is kept at four so that upgrading with primordial, Pegasian or eternal boots feels impactful.[^-E1yidIYQ4Y-84]
* Jagex said the treads will pair nicely with the Oathplate armor coming with Yama, if it passes the poll.[^-E1yidIYQ4Y-84]
