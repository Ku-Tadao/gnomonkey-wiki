## Tips

- Gnomonkey says the Accursed sceptre's magic-draining special attack works for one spec from one person and is typically useless in PvM, including Tombs of Amascut. ([2024-07-07 18:45](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1125))
- Gnomonkey says the Bandos godsword's accurate defence-draining slash special drains exactly the levels it hits, great for bosses around 100 defence or when a small drain is needed like in Tombs of Amascut, and fairly useless on targets with tons of defence. ([2024-07-07 18:54](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1134))
- Gnomonkey says the Elder maul drains 35% of the target's current defence, is directly better than the Dragon warhammer except for its max hit, is amazing against defence over 100 and useless under 100. ([2024-07-07 19:15](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1155))
- Gnomonkey says the weapon he calls the toenails of Ralos drains a flat amount of defence based on the target's magic level, is useless nearly everywhere compared to other options, is a solid option for Maiden where it is the best option for draining her, and that its high price is baffling. ([2024-07-07 19:15](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1155))
- Gnomonkey says Dragon claws are an extremely accurate slash special, great if the target is weak to slash or low defence, and do lower damage the higher the opponent's defence. ([2024-07-07 19:28](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1168))
- Gnomonkey says the Voidwaker has a guaranteed accurate special with a lower max hit than claws, is amazing on extremely tanky targets like Kephri, and worse than Zaryte crossbow or claws on low defence. ([2024-07-07 19:28](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1168))
- Gnomonkey says the weapon he calls 'Chali' has an extremely slow but powerful special attack, great just before a boss has downtime such as Bloat or Grotesque Guardians, terrible as a generic DPS spec where even the Dragon dagger special is superior. ([2024-07-07 20:01](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1201))
- At The Hueycoatl Death charge works on the body segments at the start, so you can drop a special attack and grab a death charge off them. ([2024-09-25 2:02](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=122))
- Gnomonkey says the Voidwaker is decent for The Hueycoatl if you have it, but a Zaryte crossbow is no good because the boss has over 300 ranged defence. ([2024-09-25 2:29](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=149))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey believes Dragon claws are the best special attack weapon for The Hueycoatl, with Burning claws a cheap alternative that has issues because the burn is removed when the boss reaches 50% and does not work on the body segments. ([2024-09-25 2:02](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=122))
