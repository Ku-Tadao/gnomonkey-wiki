**Special attack weapons** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2024-07-07 and 2024-09-25. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Tips

* The Accursed sceptre's magic-draining special attack works for one spec from one person and is typically useless in PvM, including Tombs of Amascut.[^e0y_auD9JhY-1125]
* The Bandos godsword's accurate defence-draining slash special drains exactly the levels it hits, great for bosses around 100 defence or when a small drain is needed like in Tombs of Amascut, and fairly useless on targets with tons of defence.[^e0y_auD9JhY-1134]
* The Elder maul drains 35% of the target's current defence, is directly better than the Dragon warhammer except for its max hit, is amazing against defence over 100 and useless under 100.[^e0y_auD9JhY-1155]
* The weapon he calls the toenails of Ralos drains a flat amount of defence based on the target's magic level, is useless nearly everywhere compared to other options, is a solid option for Maiden where it is the best option for draining her, and that its high price is baffling.[^e0y_auD9JhY-1155]
* Dragon claws are an extremely accurate slash special, great if the target is weak to slash or low defence, and do lower damage the higher the opponent's defence.[^e0y_auD9JhY-1168]
* The Voidwaker has a guaranteed accurate special with a lower max hit than claws, is amazing on extremely tanky targets like Kephri, and worse than Zaryte crossbow or claws on low defence.[^e0y_auD9JhY-1168]
* The weapon he calls 'Chali' has an extremely slow but powerful special attack, great just before a boss has downtime such as Bloat or Grotesque Guardians, terrible as a generic DPS spec where even the Dragon dagger special is superior.[^e0y_auD9JhY-1201]
* At The Hueycoatl Death charge works on the body segments at the start, so you can drop a special attack and grab a death charge off them.[^e0g1Ff667G0-122]
* The Voidwaker is decent for The Hueycoatl if you have it, but a Zaryte crossbow is no good because the boss has over 300 ranged defence.[^e0g1Ff667G0-149]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey believes Dragon claws are the best special attack weapon for The Hueycoatl, with Burning claws a cheap alternative that has issues because the burn is removed when the boss reaches 50% and does not work on the body segments.[^e0g1Ff667G0-122]
