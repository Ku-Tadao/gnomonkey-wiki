# Update: Tombs of Amascut changes

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2025-06-22 to 2025-06-25). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | update |
| **Videos** | 3 (first 2025-06-22, latest 2025-06-25) |

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says that for probably over 90% of players the upcoming Toa changes (from the Summer Sweep-up) are a dramatic improvement, speeding up raids and giving even more loot. ([2025-06-22 0:00](https://youtu.be/JjIAGBBDmL8))
- Gnomonkey says the changes remove about half of his roughly 200 tile markers for Toa flaws outside the raid, and address almost all his problems with the raid. ([2025-06-22 10:00](https://youtu.be/JjIAGBBDmL8?t=600))
- Gnomonkey says players should wait for the update rather than push Toa now, noting the update arrives in a few days and that he is waiting on his hardcore account since the changes lower risk. ([2025-06-22 19:24](https://youtu.be/JjIAGBBDmL8?t=1164))
- Gnomonkey says the Toa changes update that arrived in June 2025 made the raid dramatically easier with multiple rooms much easier, and calls the Akkha change the biggest. ([2025-06-25 0:00](https://youtu.be/6Qmuhnpa0gc))
- Gnomonkey says that so far the update is very solid and Toa does not feel like a slog, especially in lower tier gear, and that he will make a lot of updated guides. ([2025-06-25 0:03](https://youtu.be/6Qmuhnpa0gc?t=3))
- In the description Gnomonkey says shadow is still best in slot at Akkha but Bofa, ZCB, Atlatl and other crossbows now work, never a twisted bow, and that a 500 Toa is now very achievable with Bofa or budget gear. ([2025-06-25 0:03](https://youtu.be/7ZyZC0OcvsU?t=3))

## Events

- Gnomonkey says he will make a suite of refresher guides after the update, including 300s with different gear setups, new 500 guides, and guides for other Summer Sweep-up changes such as Phantom Muspah elemental reworks and possibly Cerberus. ([2025-06-22 20:20](https://youtu.be/JjIAGBBDmL8?t=1220))
- The June 2025 Tombs of Amascut changes made Ba-Ba no longer hit through prayer and many rooms much easier, with Akkha the biggest change for raids according to Gnomonkey. ([2025-06-25 0:00](https://youtu.be/7ZyZC0OcvsU))
