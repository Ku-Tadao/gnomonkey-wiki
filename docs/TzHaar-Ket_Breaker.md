## Methods

- Gnomonkey explains the Breaker's damage rolls using Nex: Nex has some defences tankier than its crush defence (like stab), giving six damage rolls, and the Breaker picks the largest of them; a boss weak to crush would get an extra seventh roll. ([2026-06-16 26:04](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1564))
- Like the Scythe of Vitur, the TzHaar-Ket Breaker needs to be charged using a resource, in this case ascension shards obtained from the raid. ([2026-06-16 27:35](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1655))
- The retuned Breaker rolls damage once by default, then an additional two times for every melee defense value higher than crush, so an NPC weakest to crush gets five rolls; previously it also compared magic and range defense, which were nearly always higher, giving two free rolls. ([2026-07-07 3:59](https://youtu.be/lNaYz-3A15Y?t=239))
- The Breaker's range is not three tiles: it hits three tiles in front of where you swing, but you attack from adjacent and are dragged like a normal Scythe. ([2026-07-07 5:25](https://youtu.be/lNaYz-3A15Y?t=325))

## Stats and numbers

- The TzHaar-Ket Breaker's thrust style has a five-tick attack speed, hits up to three targets in a line like a Scythe, and damage scales with NPC size, up to 50% bonus damage on larger targets. ([2026-06-16 24:01](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1441))
- The TzHaar-Ket Breaker's smash style is a three-tick crush attack that loses the piercing and damage-roll effects of thrust. ([2026-06-16 24:28](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1468))
- The TzHaar-Ket Breaker has a very low strength bonus and an insanely high crush bonus, and rolls damage up to seven times depending on how many of the target's defences are higher than its crush defence, taking the highest hit. ([2026-06-16 25:01](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1501))
- Jagex reduced the Breaker's damage bonus scaling with NPC size from 25-50% to 20-40%. ([2026-07-07 4:25](https://youtu.be/lNaYz-3A15Y?t=265))
- Because Nex is weakest to stab, then crush, then slash, the retuned Breaker gets three rolls on her. ([2026-07-07 4:25](https://youtu.be/lNaYz-3A15Y?t=265))
- In Gnomonkey's calcs the retuned Breaker is not best in slot over the Osmumten's fang, which is 6% better on Nex. ([2026-07-07 4:57](https://youtu.be/lNaYz-3A15Y?t=297))
- In Gnomonkey's calcs the retuned Breaker is no longer best in slot over the Scythe of Vitur in any Theatre of Blood room. ([2026-07-07 4:57](https://youtu.be/lNaYz-3A15Y?t=297))
- Kephri is half weak to crush so the Breaker rolls three times, and the Osmumten's fang is quite a bit better there. ([2026-07-07 5:25](https://youtu.be/lNaYz-3A15Y?t=325))
- The Tzhaar-ket-breaker now rolls damage two times by default, plus an extra roll for every melee defence that is higher than crush, so it rolls four times against an enemy weakest to crush. ([2026-07-30 0:29](https://youtu.be/U6bZhE88Tp0?t=29))
- The Tzhaar-ket-breaker must be charged using a rune (not yet decided; Gnomonkey guesses nature runes) and Ascension shards obtained from the raid, like the Scythe of Vitur needs charging. ([2026-07-30 5:37](https://youtu.be/U6bZhE88Tp0?t=337))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says the smash style will support niche uses against small targets, especially Nylocas. ([2026-06-16 25:33](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1533))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the Breaker could beat the Scythe on many bosses in the Theatre of Blood because many of its defences are neutral, though not on Verzik, and he sees that as a problem Jagex may need to fix. ([2026-06-16 28:05](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1685))
- Gnomonkey expects the Breaker to be best in slot on Nex and on Doom, because Doom of Mokhaiotl is weak to crush and would give seven rolls, which would make melee the meta on both bosses. ([2026-06-16 28:05](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1685))
- Gnomonkey says the Breaker's thrust and smash design is much easier to understand than the original fractured chain concept with five, four and three tick styles, and says mega rares need to be strong but simple. ([2026-06-16 29:07](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1747))
- Gnomonkey says the Breaker is about as good as a new mega rare could be and he does not hate it, adding that Jagex 'smashed it' even though he previously suggested Jagex should stay away from mega rares. ([2026-06-16 30:10](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1810))
- Gnomonkey likes that the Breaker changes how Nex and Doom are approached instead of being just a better Scythe everywhere, and says he will need DPS calcs because the Breaker is very hard to calc. ([2026-06-16 31:41](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1901))
- Gnomonkey says the Breaker mega rare was disgustingly overpowered in the first proposal and has now been retuned to be reasonable. ([2026-07-07 3:51](https://youtu.be/lNaYz-3A15Y?t=231))
- Gnomonkey thinks the Breaker may now be too niche, a mega rare only good against crush-weak monsters, though he does not think it is terrible and it is far less useful than before. ([2026-07-07 5:25](https://youtu.be/lNaYz-3A15Y?t=325))
- Jagex says the Tzhaar-ket-breaker still beats the Scythe of Vitur against enemies weakest to crush but is more competitive against neutral defence NPCs, though the Scythe should win out a little on neutral NPCs. ([2026-07-30 0:29](https://youtu.be/U6bZhE88Tp0?t=29))
- Gnomonkey likes that the Tzhaar-ket-breaker does not completely invalidate the Scythe of Vitur but becomes usable in more situations than only crush-weak targets. ([2026-07-30 0:56](https://youtu.be/U6bZhE88Tp0?t=56))
- Gnomonkey's favourite breaker redesign is the green spiky ball, which he thinks matches the raid a little better, though he would be fine with any of them. ([2026-07-30 5:20](https://youtu.be/U6bZhE88Tp0?t=320))
- Gnomonkey says that even before the buff the breaker was only slightly better than the Fang at Nex, but with the lowered crush defence it should be a heavy buff, worth wearing paper (Inquisitor's) armor to kill Nex faster. ([2026-07-30 6:20](https://youtu.be/U6bZhE88Tp0?t=380))

## Events

- The TzHaar-Ket Breaker is the mega rare in the Raids 4 blog, a crush weapon with a chain attack based on a fractured chain concept suggested by community artist Hunt OSRS. ([2026-06-16 21:56](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1316))
- In the second Raids 4 reward blog the Tzhaar-ket-breaker was felt to be too weak after its earlier nerf (it had been too strong in the first blog), and Jagex chose a middle ground. ([2026-07-30 0:22](https://youtu.be/U6bZhE88Tp0?t=22))
- Jagex offered a ranked choice survey question pitting three distinct Tzhaar-ket-breaker concepts against each other; the redesigns Gnomonkey describes include a blue one with a holy aura and a green one with thorny gothic spikes and a green aura, compared with the original design. ([2026-07-30 4:00](https://youtu.be/U6bZhE88Tp0?t=240))
- Jagex will lower the Nex's crush defence so that the Tzhaar-ket-breaker becomes the new best in slot weapon at Nex and benefits from all of its damage rolls. ([2026-07-30 5:46](https://youtu.be/U6bZhE88Tp0?t=346))
