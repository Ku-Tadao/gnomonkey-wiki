# Barracuda Trials

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2025-11-04 to 2025-12-03). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | activity |
| **Videos** | 3 (first 2025-11-04, latest 2025-12-03) |

## Methods

- Barracuda Trials are described as like Hallowed Sepulchre for boats: you race a route picking up cargo as fast as possible and are given a chunk of XP at the end. ([2025-11-04 11:19](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=679))
- In Barracuda Trials you race a course with your boat grabbing crates, with a main objective such as moving rum to another boat, going through the teleporters on the elf city course, or moving jubblies, plus the side objective of grabbing all the crates. ([2025-11-26 1:22:24](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=4944))

## Requirements

- Barracuda Trials unlock at 30 Sailing. ([2025-11-04 11:19](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=679))

## Stats and numbers

- Players call Jubbly Jive 'Laggy Lagoon' because it is hard to get over 40 FPS there; Gnomonkey says the frame rates made him sick and he quit Sailing on stream at that point. ([2025-11-26 1:23:23](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=5003))
- Gnomonkey says Hallowed Sepulchre floors (except the last) have variants, giving about 12 total variants across five floors, plus random teleporter pads and crossbow bolts, which keeps racing for a personal best engaging up to 99. ([2025-11-26 1:23:55](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=5035))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey likes that Barracuda Trials are an active Sepulchre-style activity where you can chase personal bests, get a bonus for beating a certain time, and optimise routing, boat turning and speed-boost timing. ([2025-11-26 18:45](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=1125))
- Gnomonkey says the Barracuda Trial crystal extractor, which gives a speed boost every 60 seconds, sits right next to the helm, so mis-clicking the helm stops your ship mid-race; he suggests the extractor be a button on the Sailing interface like the speed boost. ([2025-11-26 40:47](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=2447))
- Gnomonkey personally dislikes Barracuda Trials, calling them super sweaty and like Hallowed Sepulchre for boats, though he understands why others enjoy them. ([2025-11-26 1:21:55](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=4915))
- Gnomonkey says you cannot tell the exact hitbox range of your boat for sucking in crates, so missing one means slowly turning all the way back; he hopes a plugin will show it. ([2025-11-26 1:22:56](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=4976))
- Gnomonkey says Barracuda Trials lack variation compared with Hallowed Sepulchre: crates and boost pads are always in the same spots, there are no race variants, and players always spam the hardest of the three races for the best XP. ([2025-11-26 1:25:29](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=5129))
- Gnomonkey suggests level-locking some of the Barracuda Trial races and giving dramatically more XP as they unlock, instead of unlocking all three races early and spamming the hardest for 30 levels. ([2025-11-26 1:25:57](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=5157))
- Gnomonkey wishes there were more than three Barracuda Trials, because they get stale quickly and visually need more variance between race types. ([2025-11-26 1:26:31](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=5191))
- Gnomonkey says Gwenith Glide has always been the best-XP method, giving over double what Salvaging gives regardless of method. ([2025-12-03 17:05](https://youtu.be/6BvUKVInPTs?t=1025))
- Gnomonkey says Barracuda Trials were already in a good place, giving more than double the XP per hour of Salvaging. ([2025-12-03 17:05](https://youtu.be/6BvUKVInPTs?t=1025))

## Events

- Jagex said they were not planning XP changes to Barracuda Trials in the week 2 update, citing the hit from the extractor changes and that players keep finding better routes and options like natural trimming. ([2025-12-03 1:27](https://youtu.be/6BvUKVInPTs?t=87))
- Jagex said they plan to give Gwenith Glide some extra XP to compensate for the extractor nerf, the only place the extractor XP nerf was partly offset. ([2025-12-03 12:43](https://youtu.be/6BvUKVInPTs?t=763))
