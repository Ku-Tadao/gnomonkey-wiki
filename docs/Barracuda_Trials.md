**Barracuda Trials** is an activity topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2025-11-04 and 2025-12-03. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* Barracuda Trials unlock at 30 Sailing.[^pwZKK8dpq8o-679]

## Methods

* Barracuda Trials are described as like Hallowed Sepulchre for boats: you race a route picking up cargo as fast as possible and are given a chunk of XP at the end.[^pwZKK8dpq8o-679]
* In Barracuda Trials you race a course with your boat grabbing crates, with a main objective such as moving rum to another boat, going through the teleporters on the elf city course, or moving jubblies, plus the side objective of grabbing all the crates.[^KRl59CBUR8U-4944]

## Stats and numbers

* Players call Jubbly Jive 'Laggy Lagoon' because it is hard to get over 40 FPS there; Gnomonkey says the frame rates made him sick and he quit Sailing on stream at that point.[^KRl59CBUR8U-5003]
* Hallowed Sepulchre floors (except the last) have variants, giving about 12 total variants across five floors, plus random teleporter pads and crossbow bolts, which keeps racing for a personal best engaging up to 99.[^KRl59CBUR8U-5035]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey likes that Barracuda Trials are an active Sepulchre-style activity where you can chase personal bests, get a bonus for beating a certain time, and optimise routing, boat turning and speed-boost timing.[^KRl59CBUR8U-1125]
* Gnomonkey says the Barracuda Trial crystal extractor, which gives a speed boost every 60 seconds, sits right next to the helm, so mis-clicking the helm stops your ship mid-race; he suggests the extractor be a button on the Sailing interface like the speed boost.[^KRl59CBUR8U-2447]
* Gnomonkey personally dislikes Barracuda Trials, calling them super sweaty and like Hallowed Sepulchre for boats, though he understands why others enjoy them.[^KRl59CBUR8U-4915]
* Gnomonkey says you cannot tell the exact hitbox range of your boat for sucking in crates, so missing one means slowly turning all the way back; he hopes a plugin will show it.[^KRl59CBUR8U-4976]
* Gnomonkey says Barracuda Trials lack variation compared with Hallowed Sepulchre: crates and boost pads are always in the same spots, there are no race variants, and players always spam the hardest of the three races for the best XP.[^KRl59CBUR8U-5129]
* Gnomonkey suggests level-locking some of the Barracuda Trial races and giving dramatically more XP as they unlock, instead of unlocking all three races early and spamming the hardest for 30 levels.[^KRl59CBUR8U-5157]
* Gnomonkey wishes there were more than three Barracuda Trials, because they get stale quickly and visually need more variance between race types.[^KRl59CBUR8U-5191]
* Gnomonkey says Gwenith Glide has always been the best-XP method, giving over double what Salvaging gives regardless of method.[^6BvUKVInPTs-1025]
* Gnomonkey says Barracuda Trials were already in a good place, giving more than double the XP per hour of Salvaging.[^6BvUKVInPTs-1025]

## Events

* Jagex said they were not planning XP changes to Barracuda Trials in the week 2 update, citing the hit from the extractor changes and that players keep finding better routes and options like natural trimming.[^6BvUKVInPTs-87]
* Jagex said they plan to give Gwenith Glide some extra XP to compensate for the extractor nerf, the only place the extractor XP nerf was partly offset.[^6BvUKVInPTs-763]
