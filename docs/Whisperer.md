**Whisperer** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2024-05-10. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* The Whisperer requires the grandmaster quest Desert Treasure 2, which Gnomonkey says should make many low-tier gear downgrades irrelevant.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-0]

## Methods

* Gnomonkey uses a powered staff on the Whisperer so he can use thralls, and drinks forgotten brews for a 10 magic level boost.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-83]
* Gnomonkey uses the Rune thrownaxe special attack to kill the Whisperer's Lost Souls.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-83]
* Gnomonkey used the Gearscape budget tool with a 9 million gp budget; its setup uses void mage and Infinity boots and reaches a max hit of 38 after boosting.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-314]

## Stats and numbers

* Gnomonkey's downgraded setup has a max hit of 26 on the Whisperer with an Amulet of power, and the amulet's effect decays instantly.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-29]
* In Gnomonkey's setup the powered staff has nine tile range, the same as a Sanguinesti staff or Toxic trident, so he did not need freezes.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-83]
* The Whisperer's attack that bounces back only hits two targets rather than all three.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-194]
* In the budget setup Gnomonkey's expected time to kill was 140 seconds, which the calculator says should be about two and a half minutes without downtime.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-414]
* The wiki's worst downgrade setup had a mage attack bonus of 143 and a 38 max hit; Gnomonkey says the budget setup had about 50% more magic accuracy and 50% higher max hit.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-570]

## Tips

* The Gearscape budget tool lets you export the generated setup straight to a bank tab.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-457]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says awakening sanity loss is more concerning than the Whisperer's damage, and the end of the fight is the only scary part.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-204]
* Gnomonkey was surprised the budget tool recommends Infinity boots (about 400k) and a Sears ring, but says accuracy matters so the choice is plausible.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-329]
* Gnomonkey says Moon (Lunar-themed) gear is turning out more okay than he expected, and that it is very cheap and great for budgets.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-338]
* Gnomonkey says the Whisperer is a boring boss in general and that it feels slow without Shadow, since the boss is balanced around having Shadow.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-450]
* Gnomonkey ranks the Whisperer roughly third among the Desert Treasure 2 bosses, saying Duke Sucellus is definitely worse.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-511]
* Gnomonkey finds the Whisperer's reduced damage taken while it does its special attack to be what makes the fight annoying.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-585]
* Gnomonkey says that despite the budget setup having 13 extra max hits, the improvement was smaller than he expected, and speculates bad gear is just bad at the Whisperer.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-598]
* Gnomonkey suggests mage gear helps little against the Whisperer because there is so much downtime on the boss, so mage just does not do well here until you have Shadow.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-630]

## Records

* The budget setup killed the Whisperer in about 3 minutes 50 seconds, roughly a minute faster and about 20% faster than the worst-gear kill, whereas Gnomonkey expected about two minutes saved.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-586]
