# Whisperer

## Methods

- Gnomonkey uses a powered staff on the Whisperer so he can use thralls, and drinks forgotten brews for a 10 magic level boost. ([2024-05-10 1:23](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=83))
- Gnomonkey uses the Rune thrownaxe special attack to kill the Whisperer's Lost Souls. ([2024-05-10 1:23](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=83))
- Gnomonkey used the Gearscape budget tool with a 9 million gp budget; its setup uses void mage and Infinity boots and reaches a max hit of 38 after boosting. ([2024-05-10 5:14](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=314))

## Requirements

- The Whisperer requires the grandmaster quest Desert Treasure 2, which Gnomonkey says should make many low-tier gear downgrades irrelevant. ([2024-05-10 0:00](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey's downgraded setup has a max hit of 26 on the Whisperer with an Amulet of power, and the amulet's effect decays instantly. ([2024-05-10 0:29](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=29))
- In Gnomonkey's setup the powered staff has nine tile range, the same as a Sanguinesti staff or Toxic trident, so he did not need freezes. ([2024-05-10 1:23](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=83))
- Gnomonkey says the Whisperer's attack that bounces back only hits two targets rather than all three. ([2024-05-10 3:14](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=194))
- In the budget setup Gnomonkey's expected time to kill was 140 seconds, which the calculator says should be about two and a half minutes without downtime. ([2024-05-10 6:54](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=414))
- The wiki's worst downgrade setup had a mage attack bonus of 143 and a 38 max hit; Gnomonkey says the budget setup had about 50% more magic accuracy and 50% higher max hit. ([2024-05-10 9:30](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=570))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey notes the Gearscape budget tool lets you export the generated setup straight to a bank tab. ([2024-05-10 7:37](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=457))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says awakening sanity loss is more concerning than the Whisperer's damage, and the end of the fight is the only scary part. ([2024-05-10 3:24](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=204))
- Gnomonkey was surprised the budget tool recommends Infinity boots (about 400k) and a Sears ring, but says accuracy matters so the choice is plausible. ([2024-05-10 5:29](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=329))
- Gnomonkey says Moon (Lunar-themed) gear is turning out more okay than he expected, and that it is very cheap and great for budgets. ([2024-05-10 5:38](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=338))
- Gnomonkey says the Whisperer is a boring boss in general and that it feels slow without Shadow, since the boss is balanced around having Shadow. ([2024-05-10 7:30](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=450))
- Gnomonkey ranks the Whisperer roughly third among the Desert Treasure 2 bosses, saying Duke Sucellus is definitely worse. ([2024-05-10 8:31](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=511))
- Gnomonkey finds the Whisperer's reduced damage taken while it does its special attack to be what makes the fight annoying. ([2024-05-10 9:45](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=585))
- Gnomonkey says that despite the budget setup having 13 extra max hits, the improvement was smaller than he expected, and speculates bad gear is just bad at the Whisperer. ([2024-05-10 9:58](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=598))
- Gnomonkey suggests mage gear helps little against the Whisperer because there is so much downtime on the boss, so mage just does not do well here until you have Shadow. ([2024-05-10 10:30](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=630))

## Records

- The budget setup killed the Whisperer in about 3 minutes 50 seconds, roughly a minute faster and about 20% faster than the worst-gear kill, whereas Gnomonkey expected about two minutes saved. ([2024-05-10 9:46](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=586))
