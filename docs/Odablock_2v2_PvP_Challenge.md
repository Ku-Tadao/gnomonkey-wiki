## Methods

- Odablock's usual challenge format is a two-hour segment from scratch followed by a fight at the end; this time it is a 2v2 with Odablock and Gnomonkey against SickNerd and Skillspecs. ([2026-01-23 1:05](https://youtu.be/5qsH_-3UNQ8?t=65))
- Odablock's plan for himself and Gnomonkey was to start with one Corrupted Gauntlet run each, then PvP for an hour to stack cash, and use the rest for PvM such as Colosseum. ([2026-01-23 9:22](https://youtu.be/5qsH_-3UNQ8?t=562))
- Agreed team plan: Gnomonkey and Odablock do Corrupted Gauntlet first for starting cash, then PvP for the hour, and Gnomonkey then attempts Colosseum with the money, while Odablock PvPs for the rest of the time. ([2026-01-23 10:27](https://youtu.be/5qsH_-3UNQ8?t=627))
- Odablock tells Gnomonkey to buy cheap PvP gear including a rune crossbow, black dragonhide top and bottom, rune gloves, climbing boots, a glory and a water staff, plus Ranging potions. ([2026-01-23 18:18](https://youtu.be/5qsH_-3UNQ8?t=1098))

## Requirements

- Rule for the challenge: each player gets one hour of optional PvM and one hour of PvP, and in PvM each monster may only be killed once, so each boss gets one kill count. ([2026-01-23 1:59](https://youtu.be/5qsH_-3UNQ8?t=119))
- Rule for the challenge: all four players stay in the Discord call the whole time, because Odablock thinks it is more entertaining and gives more trash talk. ([2026-01-23 2:53](https://youtu.be/5qsH_-3UNQ8?t=173))
- Rule for the challenge: no Chaos Altar, because on the 2350 total level worlds some people risk one to three million and just run around naked donating it. ([2026-01-23 3:23](https://youtu.be/5qsH_-3UNQ8?t=203))
- Rule for the challenge: the Construction cape and Magic cape are free to use as much as you like; players without 99 Construction should bring house tabs but not sell them. ([2026-01-23 3:53](https://youtu.be/5qsH_-3UNQ8?t=233))
- Rule for the challenge: unlocking untradeables such as the Avernic defender, Infernal cape, quiver and Divine wound pouch costs one million per item and cannot be refunded when gearing for the final fight. ([2026-01-23 3:53](https://youtu.be/5qsH_-3UNQ8?t=233))
- Rule for the challenge: players should try to track potions and supplies as cleanly as possible (Odablock says do not be a rat), and can buy things like 100 blighted anglerfish for about 15k in one chunk. ([2026-01-23 4:22](https://youtu.be/5qsH_-3UNQ8?t=262))
- Rule for the challenge: players could take a weapon costing 250k out of the bank after paying 250k for it (heard as the Arcan blade), described as a very cheap but good weapon. ([2026-01-23 5:21](https://youtu.be/5qsH_-3UNQ8?t=321))
- Teleport crystals are not allowed; players must teleport with the Construction cape to Prifddinas instead of going to the Grand Exchange. ([2026-01-23 8:09](https://youtu.be/5qsH_-3UNQ8?t=489))
