# Vespula

## Methods

- Gnomonkey says his team cheeses Vespula instead of doing it the intended way (shooting her down and hitting the portal until she gets back up), praying Rigor and Redemption and following a tile pattern to Twisted bow the portal. ([2022-03-19 47:24](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2844))
- Gnomonkey brings plenty of food to Vespula because she takes all his health away, and he wants to heal up and pick up her potions afterward. ([2024-10-27 42:26](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=2546))
- Gnomonkey shoots Vespula's portal with the Bofa from a green tile at the left corner of the square every time. ([2024-10-27 42:26](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=2546))
- At Vespula he takes a shot from the corner, then cycles by waiting one tick on the tile and going back in; Cox Editions can show a tick timer on prayer enhance that helps use it. ([2024-11-08 36:29](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=2189))
- Gnomonkey runs in as soon as he gets the XP drop and deactivates Rigor so he is never smited while running. ([2024-11-08 36:56](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=2216))
- Gnomonkey does Vespula by flinching in under her from a marked green tile, shooting the portal and running back, while she tramples you; the Prayer enhance gives prayer back to proc Redemption repeatedly. ([2025-05-01 20:13](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1213))

## Requirements

- Gnomonkey says you should have 82 Prayer for this method because below that level the Prayer enhance restores prayer points a tick slower (a seven-tick timer instead of six). ([2025-05-01 20:53](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1253))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says the Thieving room needs 30 grubs total to finish Vespula, and a counter shows how many you still need. ([2023-06-14 11:20](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=680))
- Gnomonkey says a Thieving cape is maybe a 20-30 second time save and can boost Thieving to 110-120 if you are lucky, or give no help at all. ([2023-06-14 33:54](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=2034))
- Vespula's trample has a max hit of eight, and Redemption procs under 10 health, so Gnomonkey says the method is guaranteed safe with unlimited health. ([2025-05-01 20:27](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1227))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says you can use prayer enhance or extra prayer potions made in a CM so you waste no prayer potions at Vespula. ([2022-03-19 47:53](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2873))
- Before Vespula Portal Gnomonkey removes his boots, sets his staff to long range, sorts his inventory and drinks Kodai for the mage boost. ([2023-02-02 6:59](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=419))
- He watches the Enhanced Timer the whole kill, since Enhance gives a prayer point every six ticks and knowing when it arrives lets him waste the fewest ticks. ([2023-02-02 6:59](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=419))
- Gnomonkey sometimes runs into Vespula without prayer and uses Redemption on the way back if it saves ticks. ([2023-02-02 7:25](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=445))
- In the Thieving room, Gnomonkey runs Light bearer for consistency but says you can swap it for a Thieving cape, which at 100 gives a chance at four grubs because it boosts your Thieving level. ([2023-06-14 10:19](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=619))
- In the Thieving room, you need to get the grubs as quickly as possible and keep feeding the Vespula; whenever she is not eating grubs she heals slowly over time. ([2023-06-14 10:50](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=650))
- Gnomonkey stresses being fast banking in the Thieving room, because bad thieving luck can lose a lot of time. ([2023-06-14 11:54](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=714))
- Gnomonkey says with the Bofa you can shoot again the instant you reach that tile, which is difficult, and gets awkward once you have to throw in resort doses. ([2024-10-27 42:59](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=2579))
- Gnomonkey says to bank most brew, revitalisation and mage switches after Thieving and keep Sanguinesti staff, salve, pickaxe and a fresh stamina for Vespula. ([2024-11-08 36:01](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=2161))
- Gnomonkey tells Hedron during the Vespula fight that you have to stall two ticks. ([2025-03-20 14:33](https://youtu.be/tTn3JmSD4fI?t=873))
- Gnomonkey does a boots takeoff if possible, swaps quick-prayers to Rigor and Redemption, and sips stamina as he enters the room. ([2025-05-01 21:24](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1284))
- Gnomonkey says to detach the Prayer enhance timer from the buff meter using Cox Editions so you can see which tick you get a prayer point back. ([2025-05-01 21:54](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1314))
- Gnomonkey turns off Rigor as he does the shot because after Redemption drains prayer, the other prayer stays on and drains your prayer point. ([2025-05-01 22:25](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1345))
- Gnomonkey says to wait for your overload to wear off before Vespula, because you do not want it to run out mid-room. ([2025-05-01 23:25](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1405))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey rates Vespula high C tier, a DPS check against a portal with stupid defence that cannot be drained because it is outside melee distance, and says it is not skilled. ([2022-03-19 47:53](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2873))
- Gnomonkey says the Bofa is better DPS than the Twisted bow at Vespula, but it is tick-perfect and difficult to use without losing ticks. ([2025-05-01 23:25](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1405))

## Events

- Gnomonkey does the whole Vespula fight with a crystal bow and needs the three prayer potions he brought, with Hedron noting that it is not many. ([2025-03-20 15:02](https://youtu.be/tTn3JmSD4fI?t=902))
- Vespula drops an enhance potion in the raid, so the enhance is available immediately after the kill. ([2025-03-20 16:34](https://youtu.be/tTn3JmSD4fI?t=994))
