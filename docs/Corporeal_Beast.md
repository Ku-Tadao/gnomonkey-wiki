# Corporeal Beast

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2022-03-12 to 2024-05-16). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | boss |
| **Videos** | 3 (first 2022-03-12, latest 2024-05-16) |

## Methods

- The wiki's low-drain Corp setup says to use three warhammer specs and four to six Arclight specs before equipping it, aiming to bring Corp's defence to 1. ([2024-05-16 0:27](https://youtu.be/BShbhmfpnZE?t=27))
- Gnomonkey says Arclight drains Corp's strength, attack and defence, while a Bandos godsword drains defence, so he alternates the two and goes back to BGS to drain Corp's mage. ([2024-05-16 8:56](https://youtu.be/BShbhmfpnZE?t=536))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says the Corporeal Beast pet is 1 in 5,000, kills are super slow, and you pretty much have to boost it, making it an expensive gp sink. ([2022-03-12 11:19](https://youtu.be/vwCm9NKJhRE?t=679))
- In the low-drain setup Gnomonkey did about 427 damage on one inventory of food, which he estimates is about 10% of Corp's health, and says one inventory of food is about 15% of his health. ([2024-05-16 2:35](https://youtu.be/BShbhmfpnZE?t=155))
- Gnomonkey reads Corp's stomp as happening on seven tick intervals, so there is no guarantee you can step under him in time. ([2024-05-16 2:50](https://youtu.be/BShbhmfpnZE?t=170))
- Even after death piling 180 food Gnomonkey says he did not have enough, and he estimates the low-drain Corp kill needs about 300 food with nobody ragging. ([2024-05-16 7:03](https://youtu.be/BShbhmfpnZE?t=423))
- The wiki's alternative Corp setup Gnomonkey tries costs about 35 million gp, which he reduces to about 33 million with a Blood fury, then further to around 16 million. ([2024-05-16 8:28](https://youtu.be/BShbhmfpnZE?t=508))
- Corp at zero defence is almost never missed, and Corp at zero melee stats means the next goal is to BGS repeatedly to drain mage. ([2024-05-16 10:46](https://youtu.be/BShbhmfpnZE?t=646))

## Tips

- The wiki's Corp setup lists spec downgrades from Arclight to Bandos godsword to Osmumten's fang, which Gnomonkey mocks as irrelevant. ([2024-05-16 0:27](https://youtu.be/BShbhmfpnZE?t=27))
- Corp heals when you leave the room, so Gnomonkey says repeatedly leaving and re-entering to flinch him would not work. ([2024-05-16 3:49](https://youtu.be/BShbhmfpnZE?t=229))
- Gnomonkey says Corp randomly moves, so a death pile can end up out of position and the boss gets farther from the pile over time. ([2024-05-16 3:53](https://youtu.be/BShbhmfpnZE?t=233))
- Gnomonkey notes a hammer would make the kill much faster even in the crappy setup, so an early attempt for a shield is not the worst idea. ([2024-05-16 15:34](https://youtu.be/BShbhmfpnZE?t=934))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey rates the Corporeal Beast pet lower A (a big spiky dog-looking thing), better than the Penance Queen pet. ([2022-03-12 11:19](https://youtu.be/vwCm9NKJhRE?t=679))
- Gnomonkey calls Corporeal Beast so bad it cannot go anywhere but F: drain all its defence and hit it with a spear, with groups slightly more interesting because you drain it less before fighting. ([2022-03-19 18:47](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1127))
- Gnomonkey says the most interesting Corporeal Beast mechanic is walking back and forth on the dark core, but everyone just freezes it in place; he rates it a higher F tier. ([2022-03-19 19:17](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1157))
- Gnomonkey says the wiki's low stat drain Corporeal Beast setup is genuinely not possible and is the funniest setup in the series, though he tries it anyway. ([2024-05-16 0:00](https://youtu.be/BShbhmfpnZE))
- Gnomonkey says Corp is such a bad boss, and notes the mods have talked about adding a Phosani's-style Corp at some point. ([2024-05-16 7:50](https://youtu.be/BShbhmfpnZE?t=470))
- Gnomonkey says you should not do Corp solo with a 35 million gp setup, but that if you have to get a kill with that little money you should fully drain Corp. ([2024-05-16 9:28](https://youtu.be/BShbhmfpnZE?t=568))
- Gnomonkey says the low drain setup has become meta for accounts with maximum gear who death pile, or use Narda-style teleports as he understands it. ([2024-05-16 9:28](https://youtu.be/BShbhmfpnZE?t=568))
- Gnomonkey says the boss drops nothing useful aside from the arcane sigil, and there is no reason to kill Corp early on an account. ([2024-05-16 15:05](https://youtu.be/BShbhmfpnZE?t=905))
- Gnomonkey says that if you have only 35 million gp and want to solo Corp, don't, and a low-level Ironman consulting the wiki for Corp should go do anything else. ([2024-05-16 15:30](https://youtu.be/BShbhmfpnZE?t=930))
- Gnomonkey considers killing Corp on a budget feasible only for ironmen and says any main would get crashed on a public Corp world. ([2024-05-16 15:34](https://youtu.be/BShbhmfpnZE?t=934))

## Records

- Gnomonkey's 35 million gp Corp kill took about 25 minutes, including about 20 minutes of draining at the start, and used about 300 Anglerfish worth of supplies. ([2024-05-16 13:30](https://youtu.be/BShbhmfpnZE?t=810))
