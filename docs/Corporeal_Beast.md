**Corporeal Beast** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2022-03-12 and 2024-05-16. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* The wiki's low-drain Corp setup says to use three warhammer specs and four to six Arclight specs before equipping it, aiming to bring Corp's defence to 1.[^BShbhmfpnZE-27]
* Arclight drains Corp's strength, attack and defence, while a Bandos godsword drains defence, so he alternates the two and goes back to BGS to drain Corp's mage.[^BShbhmfpnZE-536]

## Stats and numbers

* The Corporeal Beast pet is 1 in 5,000, kills are super slow, and you pretty much have to boost it, making it an expensive gp sink.[^vwCm9NKJhRE-679]
* In the low-drain setup Gnomonkey did about 427 damage on one inventory of food, which he estimates is about 10% of Corp's health, and says one inventory of food is about 15% of his health.[^BShbhmfpnZE-155]
* Gnomonkey reads Corp's stomp as happening on seven tick intervals, so there is no guarantee you can step under him in time.[^BShbhmfpnZE-170]
* Even after death piling 180 food Gnomonkey says he did not have enough, and he estimates the low-drain Corp kill needs about 300 food with nobody ragging.[^BShbhmfpnZE-423]
* The wiki's alternative Corp setup Gnomonkey tries costs about 35 million gp, which he reduces to about 33 million with a Blood fury, then further to around 16 million.[^BShbhmfpnZE-508]
* Corp at zero defence is almost never missed, and Corp at zero melee stats means the next goal is to BGS repeatedly to drain mage.[^BShbhmfpnZE-646]

## Tips

* The wiki's Corp setup lists spec downgrades from Arclight to Bandos godsword to Osmumten's fang, which Gnomonkey mocks as irrelevant.[^BShbhmfpnZE-27]
* Corp heals when you leave the room, so Gnomonkey says repeatedly leaving and re-entering to flinch him would not work.[^BShbhmfpnZE-229]
* Corp randomly moves, so a death pile can end up out of position and the boss gets farther from the pile over time.[^BShbhmfpnZE-233]
* A hammer would make the kill much faster even in the crappy setup, so an early attempt for a shield is not the worst idea.[^BShbhmfpnZE-934]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey rates the Corporeal Beast pet lower A (a big spiky dog-looking thing), better than the Penance Queen pet.[^vwCm9NKJhRE-679]
* Gnomonkey calls Corporeal Beast so bad it cannot go anywhere but F: drain all its defence and hit it with a spear, with groups slightly more interesting because you drain it less before fighting.[^HkVt4enEesI-1127]
* Gnomonkey says the most interesting Corporeal Beast mechanic is walking back and forth on the dark core, but everyone just freezes it in place; he rates it a higher F tier.[^HkVt4enEesI-1157]
* Gnomonkey says the wiki's low stat drain Corporeal Beast setup is genuinely not possible and is the funniest setup in the series, though he tries it anyway.[^BShbhmfpnZE-0]
* Gnomonkey says Corp is such a bad boss, and notes the mods have talked about adding a Phosani's-style Corp at some point.[^BShbhmfpnZE-470]
* Gnomonkey says you should not do Corp solo with a 35 million gp setup, but that if you have to get a kill with that little money you should fully drain Corp.[^BShbhmfpnZE-568]
* Gnomonkey says the low drain setup has become meta for accounts with maximum gear who death pile, or use Narda-style teleports as he understands it.[^BShbhmfpnZE-568]
* Gnomonkey says the boss drops nothing useful aside from the arcane sigil, and there is no reason to kill Corp early on an account.[^BShbhmfpnZE-905]
* Gnomonkey says that if you have only 35 million gp and want to solo Corp, don't, and a low-level Ironman consulting the wiki for Corp should go do anything else.[^BShbhmfpnZE-930]
* Gnomonkey considers killing Corp on a budget feasible only for ironmen and says any main would get crashed on a public Corp world.[^BShbhmfpnZE-934]

## Records

* Gnomonkey's 35 million gp Corp kill took about 25 minutes, including about 20 minutes of draining at the start, and used about 300 Anglerfish worth of supplies.[^BShbhmfpnZE-810]
