**Blood fury** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 6 videos between 2020-10-09 and 2026-05-05. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Blood Fury makes any mistakes you make get erased over time; after taking about 40 damage in mistakes he was back at 94 health.[^EVOs96kyHSM-1179]
* With the Blood fury, Gnomonkey heals based on the Thorns damage reflected during the Infinite Jad run.[^AuUbRTt0YnI-98]

## Stats and numbers

* Gnomonkey says, though he did not test it personally, that the Blood fury heals on average about 360 health over a Nightmare kill, equivalent to five Saradomin brews.[^gDZKQ3FTuFk-123]
* Even camping Blood Fury at Grotesque Guardians, Gnomonkey loses roughly 10 HP per kill because of divine damage and the bosses' auto attacks.[^b_kFNIJAcGE-614]
* Blood fury drains about five charges per second from all the Thorns damage in the Infinite Jad run, so Gnomonkey brought three of them.[^AuUbRTt0YnI-241]
* In total 26 blood shards were used for the Infinite Jad effort, with six full shards used during the actual run.[^AuUbRTt0YnI-417]

## Tips

* Gnomonkey recommends having at least two of the three healing items (Sanguinesti staff, Blood fury, SGS) for a Nightmare solo; he says SGS is less helpful than the other two but any two are enough to secure kills.[^gDZKQ3FTuFk-484]
* Gnomonkey camps Blood Fury while scything unless he has a steady stack of about 8 or more mushroom potato drops on the ground.[^b_kFNIJAcGE-614]
* To hang on to your spec until the start of the next wave if possible, because you might need to quickly SGS spec the ranger to heal after taking damage off spawn.[^EVOs96kyHSM-1206]
* Akkha is the room where Blood fury is most useful, according to Gnomonkey, since it heals chip damage back to full.[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-2127]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the Blood fury is incredibly good at The Nightmare.[^gDZKQ3FTuFk-94]
* Gnomonkey says Blood Fury is incredibly important at Grotesque Guardians.[^b_kFNIJAcGE-123]
* Gnomonkey says melee is best in slot on everything in the Colosseum and that you wear a Blood Fury, which he thinks is always the best choice.[^EVOs96kyHSM-976]
* Gnomonkey agrees Blood fury is too strong and needs a nerf, a view that came from discussion on his livestream about how overpowered it is.[^X0MGEJoT7gM-307]
* Gnomonkey worries that a strong nerf to Blood fury would hurt melee as a style because melee is already not the most prevalent style at most content, so he prefers upgrading the torture amulet instead.[^X0MGEJoT7gM-339]
* Gnomonkey says a sufficiently strong rancor would turn Blood fury into an expensive tool for learning content, with the rancor as the competent player's best option.[^X0MGEJoT7gM-368]
* Gnomonkey says a strong rancor would remove Blood fury's stranglehold on the melee style and require more intelligent play not backed by huge healing whenever you attack.[^X0MGEJoT7gM-496]
* Gnomonkey highly recommends a Blood fury when learning ToA because it negates Akkha's chip damage and mistakes, though it is semi-expensive.[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-85]
