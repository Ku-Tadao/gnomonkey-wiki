# Update: Slayer block lists per master

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 1 video (2025-07-18). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | update |
| **Videos** | 1 (first 2025-07-18, latest 2025-07-18) |

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey estimates that Turael point boosting with the new Turael-specific block list will be easily 25% faster, because the tasks left are much faster than the ones blocked. ([2025-07-18 2:25](https://youtu.be/0hoSb6coaVk?t=145))
- Gnomonkey says a Wilderness Slayer block list makes Wilderness Slayer the fastest way to get Slayer points, roughly 50% faster than point boosting through Turael. ([2025-07-18 2:59](https://youtu.be/0hoSb6coaVk?t=179))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey made a Turael block list for when the update goes live: banshees, bears, dogs, dwarves, lizards, skeletons and wolves, and he intends to leave cow fights unblocked on Turael because the Desert Amulet 4 takes him right to the cave. ([2025-07-18 1:53](https://youtu.be/0hoSb6coaVk?t=113))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey considers the per-master block list an amazing change because players can do Wilderness Slayer at will without rebuilding their block list, and says it drastically improves Turael. ([2025-07-18 2:59](https://youtu.be/0hoSb6coaVk?t=179))
- Gnomonkey says the per-master block lists, including a Konar-specific list, will change how people approach Slayer and make many things faster. ([2025-07-18 13:30](https://youtu.be/0hoSb6coaVk?t=810))

## Events

- The Slayer blog announced that every Slayer Master gets their own block list, so players no longer need to unset and reset blocks when switching between goals such as Wilderness Slayer and regular Slayer. ([2025-07-18 0:59](https://youtu.be/0hoSb6coaVk?t=59))
- As of the update, different Slayer Masters have different prices for blocks: Gnomonkey cites 40 points to unlock the Turael block list, 80 points for Konar, 90 for another master (heard as Steve), and 100 for Krystilia. ([2025-07-18 1:26](https://youtu.be/0hoSb6coaVk?t=86))
- The update wipes everyone's block list and refunds all the points, letting players immediately set up block lists for every master; Gnomonkey expects a large lump sum of points on his main. ([2025-07-18 7:00](https://youtu.be/0hoSb6coaVk?t=420))
