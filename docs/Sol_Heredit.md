Sol Heredit is the final boss of the [Fortis Colosseum](Fortis_Colosseum.md). Gnomonkey describes the fight as intense, built around a spear and shield attack cycle, a triple melee-prayer parry, a gear-piece grapple and lethal lasers, and has taught it in several guides after his world record kill in March 2024.

[TOC]

## Stats and gear

- Sol has 800 ranged and magic defence and his only weakness is slash[^IR0MIzsbFeM-0][^EZs7lRnEL0Y-889]. Gnomonkey says he got the world's first Monster Examine on him[^IR0MIzsbFeM-0].
- The [Scythe of Vitur](Scythe_of_Vitur.md) is the best option and has better DPS on Sol than both [Tumeken's shadow](Tumeken's_shadow.md) and the [Twisted bow](Twisted_bow.md)[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-889][^EZs7lRnEL0Y-906].
- With a whip, DPS is 30 percent lower than with a scythe, and ticks are lost flinching away every five ticks, since the boss is five-tick and the whip four-tick[^nEIeyY_m_gw-2352]. See also the [Soulreaper axe](Soulreaper_axe.md) guide[^kIfF1pNro2c-1456].
- Death Charge helps because it can be charged off the first minion[^nEIeyY_m_gw-2381]. Blood Fury heals while swinging[^EVOs96kyHSM-2542].
- The mage Fremennik is handled equally by the Webweaver bow (two-shots on a six-tick weapon) or the four-tick [Venator bow](Venator_bow.md) (one-shots but needs the ranger kept up)[^EVOs96kyHSM-3122].

## Attack pattern

- The fight is melee; keep attacking at all times, even when brewing, restoring or drinking super combat, since Sol does not stop attacking until he dies[^EVOs96kyHSM-2510][^EVOs96kyHSM-2542][^nEIeyY_m_gw-2381].
- **Spear and shield.** Sol's two main attacks are a spear lunge and a shield slam with different areas of effect[^kIfF1pNro2c-1456]. What he last did modifies the next: a second spear goes from two-pronged to three-pronged, and a second shield slam from one tile out to two[^EVOs96kyHSM-2542][^kIfF1pNro2c-1537]. The cycle resets whenever he switches attack[^EVOs96kyHSM-2604]. After a special (grapple or parry) the counter resets, so the next is spear one or shield one[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-3071][^kIfF1pNro2c-1828].
- **Dodging.** After a spear, step back from the middle top or diagonally; after a shield dodge one tile back, and two after a second shield[^nEIeyY_m_gw-2439][^nEIeyY_m_gw-2469]. Gnomonkey moves to the tile that avoids both possible next attacks (after a spear diagonally back, after a shield two tiles back)[^nEIeyY_m_gw-2500][^EVOs96kyHSM-2573], comparing it to biking while staring forward[^kIfF1pNro2c-1569]. In July 2024 he described dodging from a corner for spear one, diagonally for spear two, the other corner after a phase transition, then the middle, before dragging Sol[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-2996].
- **Phases.** At 90%, 75%, 50%, 25% and 10% health Sol shoots lightning and is guaranteed to follow with a spear[^kIfF1pNro2c-1507]; transitions drop puddles that stack Doom and hit for 20[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-2969]. In the last 10% he shoots lots of lightning, but he dies in two or three swings[^kIfF1pNro2c-1769].
- **Parry (triple protect melee).** He charges with an orange glow, then attacks three times; protect from melee must be on the tick each hits (three ticks, three ticks, four ticks; below 50% health three, three, then five)[^IR0MIzsbFeM-1829][^EVOs96kyHSM-2690][^nEIeyY_m_gw-2618]. He says it is easier by feel than by tick counts[^kIfF1pNro2c-1635], and the EZs7lRnEL0Y guide says to turn prayer off and back on for the longer final gap[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-2823].
- **Grapple.** He shouts a body part (cape, boots, gloves, body or legs), shown in white text and the chat box, and the matching gear piece must be clicked[^IR0MIzsbFeM-1829][^EVOs96kyHSM-2723]. Blocking guarantees a max hit on the next attack, about 100 damage to the boss, and saves about 10 to 15 seconds[^EVOs96kyHSM-2757][^kIfF1pNro2c-1698][^EZs7lRnEL0Y-2909]. Clicking as his hand starts moving is the last tick; once he closes his fist it is too late[^kIfF1pNro2c-1698]. He later says there are around five ticks, so he hovers the piece and clicks rather than clicking out of panic[^kIfF1pNro2c-1769]. A Saradomin godsword spec can follow the perfect parry[^nEIeyY_m_gw-2562]. He suggests zooming out far more for the fight, as missing clicks was his issue[^EVOs96kyHSM-3097].
- **Lasers.** The lasers hit for over 70 (70 to 80) and are the highest damage in the fight; take any other hit instead[^IR0MIzsbFeM-1829][^EVOs96kyHSM-2785][^nEIeyY_m_gw-2651]. They can one-shot him at 60 health[^O_eC5OV3w1o-1100].
- Solar Flare orbs circle in a square, pull prayer off and add Doom stacks[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-2640]. Totemic heals the boss, Ender Bees spawn, and the totem respawns if not killed fast[^EVOs96kyHSM-2818]. With Quartet an extra Fremennik spawns and should be killed immediately; his plan was to claw spec on entry then melee, killing the extra Fremennik first[^EVOs96kyHSM-2818][^IR0MIzsbFeM-1799]. See [Colosseum invocations](Colosseum_invocations.md).

## Approach and advice

- Call out every attack aloud and fight proactively, moving to a tile that dodges both possible next attacks[^O_eC5OV3w1o-1100][^kIfF1pNro2c-1920][^EZs7lRnEL0Y-2761]. Dodge instead of healing, since brew spam after a mistake gets you hit again[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-3071].
- Control the boss and avoid being pinned to a wall by rotating or moving when puddles appear; have 100% special before Soul so the fight is not 30 seconds longer[^kIfF1pNro2c-1849][^EZs7lRnEL0Y-3355].
- He recommends the Soul Simulator, a 3D version of Sol with adjustable ping, UI size, keybinds and attacks, and says a tile set can be imported with !soultiles[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-2680][^EZs7lRnEL0Y-2640]. A plugin shows a volatility icon on Sol[^E51JMiU2gsk-1628].
- He chose Frailty 2 for the boss, reasoning one-shots are avoided at full health; a viewer argued a laser would one-shot, and he noted lasers do 80[^EVOs96kyHSM-2847].

He argues wave 8 is on average the hardest wave, wave 9 a gimme, leaving waves 10 and 11[^M0Y3Hj3YIGw-300]. In the no-brew fist-only fight, the one attack he doubted was protect melee[^IfJVUHF6fyc-1050].
- Sol's fight is similar to his Zuk and Inferno teaching: keep attacking at all times[^EVOs96kyHSM-2510].
