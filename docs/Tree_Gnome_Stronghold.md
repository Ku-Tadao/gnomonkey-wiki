**Tree Gnome Stronghold** is an other topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2019-07-24 and 2024-07-30. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* The Tree Gnome Stronghold step needs 25 Agility.[^ETOLP88HtYU-712]

## Stats and numbers

* The stronghold's axe seller charges 200 coins for a steel axe; Gnomonkey buys the iron axe instead, which has better stats than a bronze pickaxe, as his new melee weapon.[^O6INgH6Adw0-492]

## Tips

* Gnomonkey wears the Ironman armor to safely get through the Stronghold of Security and earn 5k GP to buy a house.[^O6INgH6Adw0-81]
* To reach the Tree Gnome Stronghold from the shop area Gnomonkey must run past White Wolf Mountain, and warns that dropping armor too early made the wolves dangerous.[^O6INgH6Adw0-164]
* A quest the group completes in the Tree Gnome area gets them tree teleports, which he calls massive, and that they might do the Tree Gnome Stronghold agility course while there.[^ETOLP88HtYU-712]

## Trivia

* Gnomonkey discovers that a seller in the area also sells saws, which cost him 13 coins.[^O6INgH6Adw0-138]
