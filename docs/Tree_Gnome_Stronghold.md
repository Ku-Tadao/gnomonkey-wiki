# Tree Gnome Stronghold

## Requirements

- Gnomonkey notes the Tree Gnome Stronghold step needs 25 Agility. ([2024-07-30 11:52](https://youtu.be/ETOLP88HtYU?t=712))

## Stats and numbers

- The stronghold's axe seller charges 200 coins for a steel axe; Gnomonkey buys the iron axe instead, which has better stats than a bronze pickaxe, as his new melee weapon. ([2019-07-24 8:12](https://youtu.be/O6INgH6Adw0?t=492))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey wears the Ironman armor to safely get through the Stronghold of Security and earn 5k GP to buy a house. ([2019-07-24 1:21](https://youtu.be/O6INgH6Adw0?t=81))
- To reach the Tree Gnome Stronghold from the shop area Gnomonkey must run past White Wolf Mountain, and warns that dropping armor too early made the wolves dangerous. ([2019-07-24 2:44](https://youtu.be/O6INgH6Adw0?t=164))
- Gnomonkey says a quest the group completes in the Tree Gnome area gets them tree teleports, which he calls massive, and that they might do the Tree Gnome Stronghold agility course while there. ([2024-07-30 11:52](https://youtu.be/ETOLP88HtYU?t=712))

## Trivia

- Gnomonkey discovers that a seller in the area also sells saws, which cost him 13 coins. ([2019-07-24 2:18](https://youtu.be/O6INgH6Adw0?t=138))
