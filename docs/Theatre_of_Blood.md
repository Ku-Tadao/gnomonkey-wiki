The Theatre of Blood is a six-room raid (Maiden through Verzik) that Gnomonkey describes as one of the hardest pieces of PvM to learn and, later, as the best raid in the game, with a very high skill ceiling and mechanical depth in every room. His 2021 money-making guide covers requirements, roles, setups and loot; later videos cover plugins, solo runs, hard mode and the effect of Project Rebalance on Verzik.

[TOC]

## Difficulty and Gnomonkey's opinion
- In January 2021 he calls it one of, if not the, hardest PvM encounters to learn, harder to complete consistently with low-KC randoms than the [Inferno](Inferno.md), since one mistake can wipe the team, and much harder to learn than [Chambers of Xeric](Chambers_of_Xeric.md) because a wipe means starting over, which makes learning [Verzik](Verzik_Vitur.md) at the end a nightmare[^sCmYo21i2I8-0].
- By March 2022 he calls it amazing S tier content, above CMs, where every room requires skill and allows harder, chancier strategies that lower health but make the raid faster, which he says Jagex should focus on[^HkVt4enEesI-5230]. A through line is that mistakes punish the team[^HkVt4enEesI-4077]; the main thing he dislikes is that almost every room requires reducing defence[^HkVt4enEesI-5263]. See also the [PvM boss tier list](Opinion_PvM_boss_tier_list.md).
- In May 2022 he rates it 4/5 for inventory management, 5/5 for movement (exact placement in most rooms) and 3/5 for prayer management, since prayers are restored every room[^IuHh4gfs39o-699][^IuHh4gfs39o-729].
- In October 2022 he says it is very hard to jump into at base skill level but has an ocean of depth, with tech hidden in every room, and that with over 800 KC he is nowhere near mastering it[^bxsp-hCAlT8-198][^bxsp-hCAlT8-241].
- In July 2023 he calls it the best raid in the game, with Nylocas the best room, and says Maiden, Bloat, Sotetseg, Xarpus and Verzik all have incredible depth for improving times[^iG23QZE9pds-274][^iG23QZE9pds-305]; hitting its ceiling is almost impossible[^iG23QZE9pds-1020]. He quotes several high-level players who call it their favourite or the only fun thing in the game[^iG23QZE9pds-1590].
- In January 2021 he also calls it the best money maker in the game besides alting, with Nightmare close[^sCmYo21i2I8-0]; by July 2024 he says it and Chambers typically cap out around 10 million GP per hour[^e0y_auD9JhY-525]. He says complex switches practised at [Nex](Nex.md) prepare a player for it[^e0y_auD9JhY-406].

## Requirements
- Requirements are significantly lower than Chambers. The stat requirements he lists come from the We Do Raids Discord and are bare minimums; lacking something listed will often stop teams taking you[^sCmYo21i2I8-60].
- Melee stats matter most as melee is used in every room; 90 combat stats are the bare minimum, but he strongly recommends maxing combat first[^sCmYo21i2I8-60]. 99 Strength versus 90 is a 10% DPS difference, which he calls 50% of the upgrade from a tentacle whip to a scythe; he recommends training in the [Nightmare Zone](Nightmare_Zone.md)[^sCmYo21i2I8-146].
- 94 Magic is needed for the freezer role at Maiden (Ice Barrage); non-freezers want it for [Vengeance](Vengeance.md)[^sCmYo21i2I8-93]. High Prayer reduces super restores used, and Agility matters, especially at Bloat[^sCmYo21i2I8-93].
- A Taste of Hope unlocks the Drakan's medallion teleport; Haunted Mine is needed for the Salve amulet and the Western Provinces hard diary for Elite Void and the [Crystal halberd](Crystal_halberd.md)[^sCmYo21i2I8-120].
- Required unlocks: full Elite Void with each helmet, Crystal halberd, Salve amulets (stockpiled, since one is dropped each raid; he collected 500 in under an hour), Assembler, Dragon defender, rune pouch, Rigor and Augury[^sCmYo21i2I8-209].

## Teams and roles
- Most teams are threes or fours. For learners he recommends fours; trios earn much more per hour but one death often seals a trio's fate. Five-man raids are much harder, and the raid does not scale below trios[^sCmYo21i2I8-209].
- Four-man roles are South Mage, North Mage, Melee DPS and Ranged DPS, deciding who freezes which side at Maiden and who does what at Nylocas; in a trio the melee DPS role is cut[^sCmYo21i2I8-239].
- DPS roles bring a rune pouch with lunar runes (mud, astral, death) for Pot Share and Vengeance, and cast Vengeance whenever it is off cooldown[^sCmYo21i2I8-265].
- The South Mage takes a mage offhand (Book of Darkness, droppable after Sotetseg) for 100% accuracy on the Nylocas at Maiden, soul runes to tank at Maiden and heal back with [Blood Barrage](Blood_Barrage.md), and an [imbued heart](Imbued_heart.md)[^sCmYo21i2I8-265]. The North Mage and melee DPS take a three-tick melee weapon unless using a whip; the melee DPS brings a [Bandos godsword](Bandos_godsword.md); the ranged DPS takes a ranging potion and about 20 chinchompas[^sCmYo21i2I8-298].
- In advanced teams the South Mage can bring all the freezes and freeze Maiden Nylocas 1 to 4 on the south side tick-perfectly while the other two machine-gun N1 and N2 with blowpipes, giving an extra Vengeance caster[^sCmYo21i2I8-298].
- At the board the South Mage should form the party, since Maiden prioritises players in party order from top to bottom[^sCmYo21i2I8-730]. The order players leave becomes the next orb order[^sCmYo21i2I8-5175].
- Teams are found through the We Do Raids Discord (strict scammer ban list, own ToB guides), world 416 for free-for-all randoms, or a PvM clan with voice comms[^sCmYo21i2I8-579].
- In July 2021 he notes the fourth and fifth members can take [thralls](Thralls.md) for free, since only two mages are needed, possibly creating a "melee thraller" role; he says the discussed meta of everyone bringing spellbook-swap runes wastes a giant section of inventory[^26wPQVuqmNY-333].

## Gear and supplies
- He says stats matter much more than gear, and AFK'd combat at Ammonite Crabs between raids until maxed[^sCmYo21i2I8-120].
- Learners should stay in Full Void until comfortable with supplies; using no supplies in Verzik phase 2 is a sign to move on[^sCmYo21i2I8-360]. The basic setup's Swift blade is a melee-role item for Nylocas (needed only with a scythe) and can be swapped for about 20 black chinchompas or about 20 soul runes; Crystal halberd, Dragon claws and Dragon warhammer are all used, so he takes all three plus the BGS if in the role[^sCmYo21i2I8-360].
- If a learner owns a scythe he still recommends a whip for Verzik phase 2, since two-stepping with a whip is easier than scythe walking; he usually has learners try scythe walking after about ten KC. The blowpipe should hold at least rune darts[^sCmYo21i2I8-393].
- The intermediate setup uses fighter torso and obsidian legs until Bandos is affordable and drops void melee for Bandos; a BGS user brings a face guard[^sCmYo21i2I8-485]. It drops Bruise Brew for divine potions, with one divine super combat covering the raid before Verzik and a final super combat for phase 3[^sCmYo21i2I8-518]. He keeps void mage helm and magic cape in inventory slots that work in both directions at Nylocas boss switches, and non-freezers can bank them for more food[^sCmYo21i2I8-545].
- Before entering he pre-pots a divine ranging potion and divine super combat, eats a shark then an anglerfish, and uses an imbued heart (important for the freezers)[^sCmYo21i2I8-730]. He stays fully potted almost always, especially when hammer speccing; with regular potions or a heart at Nylocas, use Preserve throughout[^sCmYo21i2I8-790].
- The goal is no food in a room, but learners should eat rather than die; more than one brew at Maiden, or a death, is a sign to reset[^sCmYo21i2I8-913]. For each room the whole team spam clicks the door and the party creator starts it so everyone enters on the same tick[^sCmYo21i2I8-943].

## Rules
- Finishing a room instantly restores prayer, health and run energy, not drained stats[^sCmYo21i2I8-179].
- A dead player is caged rather than ending the raid; it ends only if the whole team dies, and reclaiming costs 100k, taken from the desk coffer before the bank[^sCmYo21i2I8-179]. A Hardcore Ironman loses status on dying in any room[^sCmYo21i2I8-179].

## Rooms
- **Maiden:** progression described in 2022 is blowpipe around freezing most crabs, clumping crabs for a ranger to chinchompa, scything while moving each attack to avoid blood, equipping a ranged weapon as she attacks to avoid stat drain, and freezing the final crabs to kill her before they unfreeze; stacking the first and second crab spawns for area damage is another technique[^bxsp-hCAlT8-198][^bxsp-hCAlT8-213]. She calculates damage on her animation, on the same tick as stat drain[^gO3aMYXs-QA-10164]. A [Surge potion](Surge_potion.md) claw plus ZCB use is valuable for stacking crabs in team speed runs, and the large crab pile makes a double [Death Charge](Death_Charge.md) easy; duo raids have exactly two crabs[^W1OX5PVaPxs-578][^W1OX5PVaPxs-759].
- **Bloat, Nylocas, Sotetseg, Xarpus:** thralls at Bloat do half damage while he is walking[^26wPQVuqmNY-360]. At Nylocas thralls only damage Nylocas of their style, without reflecting damage or locking out any Nylocas, and at Nylocas Vasilias only damage the boss on his correct style[^26wPQVuqmNY-360]. In 2023 he says Nylocas has depth from semi-random spawns, with speedrunners carefully keeping the room empty[^iG23QZE9pds-274]. Solo, he calls it easily the hardest room[^nJICcC6IrLQ-184].
- **Verzik:** thralls shine at all phases because her defence cannot be drained, especially phase one (damage capped at 10) and phase two (through her shield)[^26wPQVuqmNY-386]. Phase 1 involves spreading special attacks, running behind a pillar with careful timing, pre-hits and tactical tanking[^bxsp-hCAlT8-241]. Surge potions are good at phase 2 for skipping the last red crabs[^W1OX5PVaPxs-578]. For phase 3 one-tick tanking, set Visual Metronome to 7 ticks and click under her every 7th tick[^Z5ZONYTlS1w-786].
- Speedruns use a spellbook swap with the Max cape at Sotetseg, giving with CM Chambers a pool of 5 runs per day[^iG23QZE9pds-540].

## Loot
- Regular loot from a deathless raid typically pays for supplies with a scythe, averaging about 400k, dropping slightly per death[^sCmYo21i2I8-5079].
- A deathless raid has a 1 in 9.1 chance of a purple chest regardless of team size, so trios are more profitable; the purple most likely goes to the MVP[^sCmYo21i2I8-5110]. In 2024 he gives the chance as 9% split among the entire team, with the average purple worth far less than at [Tombs of Amascut](Tombs_of_Amascut.md)[^e0y_auD9JhY-492].
- MVP points go to the top damage dealer in a room: two for each boss and Verzik phase, one for Nylocas boss and Sotetseg; each death costs four[^sCmYo21i2I8-5110].
- Purples: Avernic defender hilt 8 in 19, every other purple except the scythe 2 in 19 (including each Justiciar piece), [Scythe of Vitur](Scythe_of_Vitur.md) 1 in 19[^sCmYo21i2I8-5143].
- Average chest value when splitting (see table below).

## Solo and hard mode
- In March 2024 he attempted a solo ToB aiming at sub 55 minutes after not doing regular solo in years, finding solo Nylocas completely different from hard mode[^gO3aMYXs-QA-6171][^gO3aMYXs-QA-6199]. He thinks solo was always possible, with lack of Blood Fury the early obstacle at Maiden, and agrees solo is hard to learn while team raids are not too bad[^gO3aMYXs-QA-6526][^gO3aMYXs-QA-8992]. The attempt ended at Verzik when he swung at 35.2% health[^gO3aMYXs-QA-9085].
- On Verzik phase 2 red crabs, in July 2023 he says solo requires tick-eating for 15 minutes in Xarpus's phase 2 and suggests one red crab spawn like hard mode[^iG23QZE9pds-765]. In April 2024 he says [Project Rebalance](Update_Project_Rebalance.md) now spawns only one red crab if one player is left, a great change for solos, but lightning damage was untouched at 50, so a lone player dies instantly; he wants it scaled down as in hard mode[^Kzc2e6H8i7o-1019][^P5yHDSocecY-881].
- A May 2024 update changed the solo red crabs to a single crab and capped lightning damage; he says tick eating with purple sweets for 15 minutes is no longer needed, and criticises that normal mode had not been fixed sooner[^nJICcC6IrLQ-0]. He would not make a Wiki Guides video for solo ToB, saying no one should enter with budget setups[^nJICcC6IrLQ-0].
- His solo normal-mode run finished in about 49 minutes, a personal best beating his old barely sub-55 by over five minutes[^nJICcC6IrLQ-1129].
- On hard mode, in 2023 he calls it a complete step down in every way except Verzik, which gets more health after the tornadoes[^iG23QZE9pds-390][^iG23QZE9pds-420].
- In September 2023 he did the Entry Mode ToB for the [Naked Man Triathlon](Naked_Man_Triathlon.md), saying it is unbelievably hard without armour and that Nylocas is the worst room; he joked he would never do it again[^f1Y2Pd6dQoA-6712][^f1Y2Pd6dQoA-7080][^f1Y2Pd6dQoA-7790]. He notes Verzik P1 appeared to AFK because she could not see him[^f1Y2Pd6dQoA-7388].

## Plugins
- In 2021 he recommends the external ToB Damage Counter plugin (percent of damage per room; alerts chat if someone forgot a salve at Bloat or healed Verzik P3 by being hit by a tornado) and the ToB HealthBars plugin (player names in place of the orbs, helping phase 1 Verzik spec order)[^sCmYo21i2I8-669]. He lists the TobQOL plugin and others in 2024[^Z5ZONYTlS1w-634][^Z5ZONYTlS1w-667][^Z5ZONYTlS1w-698][^Z5ZONYTlS1w-731][^Z5ZONYTlS1w-759]. Details are on [RuneLite plugins](RuneLite_plugins.md).

## Suggestions
- He wants party order static, with the creator staying leader[^NzW7JFgc8qU-877][^NzW7JFgc8qU-905]. He suggests an arena CA such as Verzik phase 3 solo without tanking a melee hit, and notes the Perfect Theatre CA is usually done in a trio because of Xarpus and encourages arbitrarily harder play[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-320][^ZjG3P7PTeIs-2207].
