# RuneScape 3 quests

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2023-10-18 to 2023-10-26). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | quest |
| **Videos** | 2 (first 2023-10-18, latest 2023-10-26) |

## Stats and numbers

- In RS3, every 25 quest points you can talk to Mei outside Varrock for a big chunk of coins, and at 150 quest points she gives Vanquish, which Gnomonkey calls a really powerful tier 75 weapon for all styles. ([2023-10-26 0:30](https://youtu.be/wRHsOi8plqM?t=30))
- Gnomonkey lists early quests he is working through: Digsite, Witch's House, Death Plateau, Lost Tribe and more, then elf city quests like Murder Mystery, Plague City and Biohazard, and Tree Gnome Village and Grand Tree to unlock Gnome area. ([2023-10-26 0:30](https://youtu.be/wRHsOi8plqM?t=30))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says he is rushing quest points because it gives a really powerful weapon once he has enough, and quests also give a bunch of XP like in old school. ([2023-10-26 2:26](https://youtu.be/wRHsOi8plqM?t=146))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey likes that in RS3 you can see buildings and monuments in the distance. ([2023-10-18 1:36](https://youtu.be/Cbhq22YeYFk?t=96))
- Gnomonkey says old quests in RS3 are more outdated than in OSRS because they never get quality-of-life updates. ([2023-10-26 1:02](https://youtu.be/wRHsOi8plqM?t=62))
- Gnomonkey says he expects to be able to kill Queen Black Dragon once he has the weapon from quests and around base 70 stats. ([2023-10-26 6:31](https://youtu.be/wRHsOi8plqM?t=391))

## Events

- Gnomonkey chose to side with the dog-like god (his words: 'we're picking the dog') in the RS3 god-choice quest, joking that anything he picked would make him a furry. ([2023-10-18 3:02](https://youtu.be/Cbhq22YeYFk?t=182))

## Trivia

- Gnomonkey enjoys the voice acting in RS3 quests, though he notes that not every character is voice acted. ([2023-10-18 1:36](https://youtu.be/Cbhq22YeYFk?t=96))
- The quest Missing, Presumed Death lets the player choose between gods or go godless, and Gnomonkey says Guthix looks the worst of all of them in his opinion. ([2023-10-18 2:34](https://youtu.be/Cbhq22YeYFk?t=154))
