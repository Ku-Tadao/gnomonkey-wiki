**RuneScape 3 quests** is a quest topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2023-10-18 and 2023-10-26. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* In RS3, every 25 quest points you can talk to Mei outside Varrock for a big chunk of coins, and at 150 quest points she gives Vanquish, which Gnomonkey calls a really powerful tier 75 weapon for all styles.[^wRHsOi8plqM-30]
* Gnomonkey lists early quests he is working through: Digsite, Witch's House, Death Plateau, Lost Tribe and more, then elf city quests like Murder Mystery, Plague City and Biohazard, and Tree Gnome Village and Grand Tree to unlock Gnome area.[^wRHsOi8plqM-30]

## Tips

* He is rushing quest points because it gives a really powerful weapon once he has enough, and quests also give a bunch of XP like in old school.[^wRHsOi8plqM-146]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey likes that in RS3 you can see buildings and monuments in the distance.[^Cbhq22YeYFk-96]
* Gnomonkey says old quests in RS3 are more outdated than in OSRS because they never get quality-of-life updates.[^wRHsOi8plqM-62]
* Gnomonkey says he expects to be able to kill Queen Black Dragon once he has the weapon from quests and around base 70 stats.[^wRHsOi8plqM-391]

## Events

* Gnomonkey chose to side with the dog-like god (his words: 'we're picking the dog') in the RS3 god-choice quest, joking that anything he picked would make him a furry.[^Cbhq22YeYFk-182]

## Trivia

* Gnomonkey enjoys the voice acting in RS3 quests, though he notes that not every character is voice acted.[^Cbhq22YeYFk-96]
* The quest Missing, Presumed Death lets the player choose between gods or go godless, and Gnomonkey says Guthix looks the worst of all of them in his opinion.[^Cbhq22YeYFk-154]
