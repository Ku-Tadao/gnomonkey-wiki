Akkha is the Het (fire) path boss of the [Tombs of Amascut](Tombs_of_Amascut.md), which Gnomonkey repeatedly calls the hardest room of the raid, and the main subject of his [Butterfly Tech](Butterfly_Tech.md) guides. His position changed sharply after the June 2025 Tombs of Amascut changes, which let players butterfly him for the whole kill with ranged weapons.

[TOC]

## Overview and difficulty

Gnomonkey calls Akkha the hardest room of the raid, with loads of little intricacies ([^q3D2jeguGz8-656]), and the most dangerous room ([^Mg9hcqcFC1U-894]). He rates Akkha as one of the hardest rooms and arguably harder than the [Wardens](The_Wardens.md) ([^vQO8khf302s-1485]), though in 2022 he called him the most supply-draining boss besides the Wardens, with [Ba-Ba](Ba-Ba.md) a close second ([^pSBxLCy7-44-55]). In a low-gear 300 raid he named Akkha the room he was most worried about ([^HeM0yGenyx8-410]), and for a no-Keris 500 he calls Akkha probably the hardest room besides the Wardens because mistakes and brews cannot be afforded ([^UhLzyieXrbY-1621]). In 2026 he describes Akkha as the hardest room apart from the last boss and the most tile-heavy room ([^N2DCZ1nt4p4-2136]). In 2022 he said he places Akkha first in the path order because it is the most RNG room ([^h4hf5hPQHms-0]), and that he just hates Akkha ([^h4hf5hPQHms-486]).

After the 2025 update his view reversed in practice: with perfect butterfly Akkha is a zero-damage room that is "ten times easier" for mid-level players and irons without a shadow ([^6Qmuhnpa0gc-840]), and he calls it a very low-supply room if no melee hit is taken ([^X-PTHMq_10A-1664]). See [Changes in the June 2025 update](#changes-in-the-june-2025-update).

## Mechanics

- **Styles and targeting.** Akkha does not change attack style or use special attacks if he does not hit the player, and he has unique smart pathing ([^pSBxLCy7-44-0]). The player who enters the fight first is the one he targets ([^pSBxLCy7-44-146]). Most NPCs hit on the same tick they approach, but his smart pathing prevents this ([^pSBxLCy7-44-356]).
- **Style switching.** He has a chance to switch style each time he does a special attack (every 60 seconds) and each time he melees the player ([^q3D2jeguGz8-692]). In 2022 he said that if Akkha melees more than six times he switches style and butterfly stops working ([^pSBxLCy7-44-416]). He starts red, so the player must mage him, and takes heavy melee damage at the start ([^vEs4Qvs_Rag-1196]). Each special has a chance of swapping him to ranged ([^LxYODChv40A-848]), and he can be switched to ranged at any point with any special ([^cmLBaxdoHpQ-1921]). In 2026 he states Akkha swaps between melee, ranged and mage, that all his attacks hit through prayer, melee hardest ([^N2DCZ1nt4p4-2181]).
- **Attack speed.** His attacks are six-tick, and Keep Back melee comes out the instant the melee animation starts ([^vQO8khf302s-1668]). He can melee for seventies, so health should stay above seventy ([^vQO8khf302s-1718]); in 2025 he gave Akkha's max as 70 ([^6Qmuhnpa0gc-690]).
- **Defence and freezing.** Akkha can be drained 10 defence levels but recovers about a level every 15 seconds, so draining is not worth it ([^q3D2jeguGz8-656]); a 2022 video also notes each phase reduces defence and suggests a Bandos godsword on the last hit of a phase ([^cmLBaxdoHpQ-2062]). He cannot be frozen and may be immune to Ice Barrage ([^m8m-6TBJqhQ-293]). His mage defence of 10 makes little difference ([^N2DCZ1nt4p4-2293]). His shadows proc [Death Charge](Death_Charge.md) ([^q3D2jeguGz8-656]).
- **Shadows.** Every 20 percent of damage dealt spawns shadows in four quadrants with hourglass timers; one must be killed before its timer fills or a wave of damage hits. The death (skull) and burn quadrants hurt most, lightning turns prayer off and white slows attacks by a tick ([^N2DCZ1nt4p4-2237]). The timer pauses during Simon Says ([^6Qmuhnpa0gc-405]), and Akkha must stay in the quadrant until another 20 percent is dealt before the other shadows become vulnerable ([^vEs4Qvs_Rag-1320]). An earlier video places the first shadows at 80 percent, with the area attack never a problem at 300 or below or in teams ([^pSBxLCy7-44-261]).
- **Simon Says.** The special lights four tiles at room level 0 to 1, five at 2 to 3 and six at 4 or higher ([^6Qmuhnpa0gc-376][^N2DCZ1nt4p4-2355]); the memory tiles go off every two ticks ([^vEs4Qvs_Rag-1229]). A 2025-06-28 video states five to remember above level two and four at level one or two ([^BVZEAFBsj10-1372]). He calls it probably the most common cause of death in the room ([^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-2127]). Hitting during Simon Says is only an optimisation ([^BVZEAFBsj10-1431]).
- **Black-character orbs.** When the character turns black, standing still drops orbs and moving spawns orbs behind the player ([^vEs4Qvs_Rag-1291][^BVZEAFBsj10-1404]). The attack hits as hard as the death wave if the player stops ([^N2DCZ1nt4p4-2355]). A chat tip confirmed by Gnomonkey is that the middle special can be avoided by walking one tile each time ([^h4hf5hPQHms-87]).

## Puzzle room

- In 2022 the puzzle needed Mining 85 for a one-down, and boosting Mining over 100 raised the max hit by 2 ([^q3D2jeguGz8-624]). In 2024 he said 85 Mining with a dragon pickaxe guarantees a one-down; otherwise it takes two tries ([^vEs4Qvs_Rag-1140]).
- The Tombs of Amascut plugin shows where to place the mirror and an indicator for clicking when the statue turns green, standing at least a tile away, for the one-down ([^q3D2jeguGz8-624][^vEs4Qvs_Rag-1140]). Shadow orbs in the puzzle room can overlap and hit for over 60 ([^q3D2jeguGz8-624]).
- A lower Mining level or worse pickaxe can need two or three attempts ([^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-1915]). Mining progress catches up if the player moves between mining ticks ([^K7VALGO6md8-1517]).
- After the 2025 update the green tile is no longer needed: with 99 Mining only standing next to the pillar is needed ([^UhLzyieXrbY-1410]), and a rune pickaxe with 85 Mining is enough ([^BVZEAFBsj10-1286][^91vxCCm7Jck-1521]).

## Butterfly

[Butterfly Tech](Butterfly_Tech.md) means dodging out of Akkha's radius after every attack so he never lands a melee hit, so he never switches style or uses specials against the player ([^pSBxLCy7-44-55][^N2DCZ1nt4p4-2266]). In 2022 this allowed using any number of Akkha invocations without interacting with them ([^pSBxLCy7-44-55]).

- **Setup.** Enter the room quickly so Akkha does not stand in a bad quadrant; Gnomonkey recommends salting in the previous room and entering instantly ([^LxYODChv40A-692][^91vxCCm7Jck-1552]). Begin when his true tile is one tick from the player ([^Mg9hcqcFC1U-906][^LxYODChv40A-686]). He butterflies in one direction only because swapping quadrants is easier ([^Mg9hcqcFC1U-906]), and in 2025 starts from the bottom-left quadrant ([^91vxCCm7Jck-1552]).
- **Cycle.** Stand on the attack tile and shoot; when his true tile is one tile away, move immediately, click through the intermediate indicator tiles, and attack on the next attack tile. The direction of the run differs between quadrants (counter-clockwise on one, clockwise on another) ([^6Qmuhnpa0gc-331]). Three-tick butterfly is the same cycle with more attacks ([^7ZyZC0OcvsU-233]).
- **Transitions.** He marks magic/red tiles that move the player tick-perfectly to the next set of butterfly tiles, also diagonally ([^Mg9hcqcFC1U-969][^91vxCCm7Jck-1735][^q3D2jeguGz8-747]). Three ticks separate damage instances, so moving twice in a quadrant is safe on rare bad layouts ([^Mg9hcqcFC1U-1032]). Radius markers show the style colour (Twitch command `!akkhacolor` / `!akkacolor` as transcribed) ([^LxYODChv40A-773][^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-2127]).
- **Shadow DPS skip.** In 2022 the cue was Akkha slamming the pole into the ground, then moving to the outer diagonals ([^vQO8khf302s-1627]). In 2025 he describes standing two tiles from the attack tile and clicking to it as the shadow stabs, jumping the wave; at raid level 500 with Bofa it is almost never needed and at 300 never ([^6Qmuhnpa0gc-585][^7ZyZC0OcvsU-677]).
- **Stalled Akkha.** If he stalls in a bad spot, stand next to him and step away just before he attacks ([^91vxCCm7Jck-1674]). Body blocking after Simon Says can gain an extra attack ([^q3D2jeguGz8-747]).
- **Not required at 300.** With plenty of restores butterfly can be skipped by healing through the damage ([^YizVhRWXnxs-654]), but the fancy methods are needed at raid levels like 400 to 500 ([^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-1943]).

- Without a Keris the room must be done near-perfectly, since tanking 70 melees or Simon Says hits leaves too little health ([^cmLBaxdoHpQ-1865]). With Keep Back on and a Keris, new players are advised to keep the Keris up ([^LxYODChv40A-848]).

- Thralls: no thrall should be active on Akkha's orbs, so a forgotten thrall is swapped for a zombie thrall ([^q3D2jeguGz8-811][^vQO8khf302s-1911]); a thrall that will stay up about 20 seconds or longer is swapped to zombie rather than resummoned ([^K7VALGO6md8-2093]).

## Prayer and supplies

- Augury is a 3% DPS increase against Akkha and Mystic Lore 1.5% at a fifth of the drain ([^q3D2jeguGz8-692][^vQO8khf302s-1595]); he suggests camping Mystic Lore when prayer is tight ([^vQO8khf302s-1510][^cmLBaxdoHpQ-1724]). In 2024 he said Augury need not be flicked since it is a max hit ([^LxYODChv40A-773]), and in 2025 that he camps Rigor because flicking during butterfly is difficult ([^UhLzyieXrbY-1555]).
- In May 2023 he advised flicking offensive prayers because camping them means bringing 14 restores, and said Akkha uses many restores ([^K7VALGO6md8-1008][^K7VALGO6md8-1527]). In 2024 he finished the room with seven restores left ([^YizVhRWXnxs-1050]).
- Zebak uses basically no supplies, so brews are for Ba-Ba and Akkha; brew while running to Akkha ([^vEs4Qvs_Rag-1412]). Salt gives unlimited run energy, replacing stamina ([^N2DCZ1nt4p4-2562]), and two doses restore stats near full boost ([^BVZEAFBsj10-1404]).

## Room order and levels

With Pathfinder, if Zebak would be level four, do Akkha first to keep Zebak lower ([^YizVhRWXnxs-645]). In a duo he did Zebak first to keep Akkha at level one, with four memory steps ([^X-PTHMq_10A-1241]). A level three Akkha is only slightly more annoying ([^cmLBaxdoHpQ-630]). At 1000 invocations he faced a level 6 Akkha ([^m8m-6TBJqhQ-349]).

## Group attempts

In a high-risk group raid the team reached Akkha at invocation level 3, which Gnomonkey called the most insane thing he had done ([^QkJq2KHiKW8-1903]). The group struggled with the colour orb mechanics and burning ([^QkJq2KHiKW8-2017]), and used tears next to each other to restore prayer ([^QkJq2KHiKW8-2437]).

## Final phase

In 2022 to 2023 he called the final phase the hardest part: true tile on orbs, camp Piety, prayer flicking optional ([^vQO8khf302s-1853][^q3D2jeguGz8-811]). Mage prayer does nothing beyond 375 raid level here ([^q3D2jeguGz8-780]). In 2024 he prayed mage with claws and spammed damage, using two brews ([^vEs4Qvs_Rag-1439]). After the update the final phase is dramatically easier ([^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-2235]), with about 30% fewer orbs by his estimate ([^UhLzyieXrbY-1856]); orbs hit about 25 at 300 ([^BVZEAFBsj10-1575]). Akkha jumps after three non-zero hitsplats ([^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-2252]) and, at the end of the fight, heals and teleports to a corner after three successful melee hits ([^N2DCZ1nt4p4-2412]). The closing flood hits roughly 25 ([^N2DCZ1nt4p4-2471]). He spams fang specs here, saving spec for the Keris partisan if under 50 health ([^91vxCCm7Jck-1818]). Use Death Charge shadow kills for extra claws specs and spend all specs before the last phase ([^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-2204]).

## Akkha's Arcane

Gnomonkey says Akkha's Arcane teleports instantly if hit for more than 40 in one hitsplat; otherwise three hitsplats between 3 and 40 make it jump, and each extra teammate adds one required hitsplat ([^vQO8khf302s-1011][^vQO8khf302s-1044]). Melee heals it ([^vQO8khf302s-1044]).

## Changes in the June 2025 update

- Protection prayers mitigate more damage, so tanking chips less ([^JjIAGBBDmL8-176]); Keep Back chip damage is about 40% lower ([^7ZyZC0OcvsU-232]).
- Stay Vigilant was reworked: Akkha swaps style whenever he uses a special and only if he has hit the player with a regular attack first, so perfect butterfly means he never changes style ([^JjIAGBBDmL8-176][^JjIAGBBDmL8-205]). One video says specials come every seven attacks rather than six ([^JjIAGBBDmL8-357]); another says every six instead of seven ([^6Qmuhnpa0gc-61]).
- Akkha prays only against the style he is attacking with, starting melee, then ranged, then mage, so ranged butterfly (Bofa, Atlatl) works and mage is no longer needed ([^JjIAGBBDmL8-232][^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-1976]). Gnomonkey says Trident is 20% worse than Bofa, 25% in a 500 ([^6Qmuhnpa0gc-1]).
- Stat drain affects only combat skills and the come-phase orb count is reduced ([^JjIAGBBDmL8-357]).
- He calls it by far the biggest time save, about four minutes faster on average without a shadow ([^JjIAGBBDmL8-386][^6Qmuhnpa0gc-88]); before, butterfly success was random and could add six minutes ([^JjIAGBBDmL8-1100]).
