# JalTok-Jad

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 6 videos (2022-11-04 to 2025-12-07). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | boss |
| **Videos** | 6 (first 2022-11-04, latest 2025-12-07) |

## Methods

- For the Inferno Jad, Gnomonkey says to stand on a certain tile where, once Jad sends healers, you can step in to block them almost always, and that he thinks first-cape players should do this too. ([2024-01-17 54:55](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=3295))
- To lure Jad's healers when they spawn badly, pray melee and run back and forth catching its prayer, wait for the next attack and run through; keep praying melee because the healers can kill a player who stops. ([2024-01-17 55:23](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=3323))
- To avoid wasting brew on Jad healers, stand one tile northwest of Jad, one tile away from his southwest corner, so healers walking toward his southwest brain tend to walk behind you and sit there without hitting. ([2025-12-07 1:21:46](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=4906))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says six hours counts as 36,000 game ticks and he mentions the six-hour log out timer, which he says is typically reset by logging out at Jad. ([2022-11-04 1:40:00](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=6000))
- There are five healers with a single Jad and three with triple Jads, and healers only heal if they are right next to Jad. ([2024-06-13 56:50](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=3410))
- Gnomonkey says Jads are as far as he knows the highest defence mob in the entire game, with about 480 defence. ([2024-06-13 57:22](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=3442))
- Triple Jads have a three tick delay between each Jad, which Gnomonkey says is an eternity so they are actually very easy; keep Rigor on and stay potted. ([2024-06-13 57:52](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=3472))
- Inferno Jads are nine-tick, meaning three ticks between each attack, giving you 1.8 seconds per prayer on the triple Jad. ([2025-02-26 1:11:51](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=4311))
- Jad healers max hit 18, and Gnomonkey says they hurt more than expected, especially in Crystal armour which has good ranged defence but poor melee defence. ([2025-02-26 1:12:43](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=4363))
- Gnomonkey says Jad has a very high mage defence (he states 480 mage and 510 as numbers). ([2025-02-26 1:16:24](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=4584))
- Gnomonkey says healers proc below 180 Jad health. ([2025-07-13 1:16:16](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=4576))
- Gnomonkey says doing a Jad skip (also a combat achievement) saves about a minute, and he suggests doing the Jad skip and Zuk fight at the same time. ([2025-07-13 1:20:20](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=4820))
- Jad's healers spawn when Jad reaches 175 health. ([2025-12-07 1:24:25](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=5065))
- On triple Jads each Jad attack is three ticks apart in a one-two-three rhythm instead of a nine-tick rhythm. ([2025-12-07 1:26:59](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=5219))

## Tips

- The first Jad spawns five healers once he gets to half health, so stand in line with his southwest tile and right next to him so that healers are stuck behind you. ([2022-11-04 1:39:59](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=5999))
- Gnomonkey says healers only heal if they can reach Jad in melee range, so if they are stuck behind you they are not healing and you do not have to tag them. ([2022-11-04 1:40:25](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=6025))
- Gnomonkey says to lure healers by running through them, and that stacking them up like this is really nice for keeping them together so you will not fail the healer lure. ([2022-11-04 1:41:20](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=6080))
- Gnomonkey says to leave the melee prayer on while luring the healers, which many people forget, and to log out before triples. ([2022-11-04 1:44:45](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=6285))
- Gnomonkey says to full brew, restore and re-divine after killing Jad, because now is the best time to heal before the healers. ([2024-01-17 55:19](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=3319))
- If the healers are not in melee range they do not heal Jad, so Gnomonkey can stand where they cannot reach him. ([2024-01-17 56:50](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=3410))
- Gnomonkey always starts on the tile next to Jad's south-west side because healers walk toward Jad's south-west tile, so it has the highest chance of blocking them. ([2024-06-13 56:50](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=3410))
- If you stand far away when sending the shot that will kill, it delays the shot and lets you run in to melee distance. ([2024-06-13 57:22](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=3442))
- Gnomonkey says standing in the bottom left spot makes Jads and healers corner trap each other so only one can hit you, and you should back up if multiple healers hit you. ([2024-06-13 59:18](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=3558))
- Gnomonkey says you can stack Jads and run through to heal but does not need to; in the video he heals off healers and pipe specs for health. ([2024-06-13 59:47](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=3587))
- On the wave 67 double mage, Gnomonkey recommends getting one mage to low health (under 39 so it can be killed) before hitting the other, and camping the mage prayer. ([2025-02-26 1:08:38](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=4118))
- On the Jad wave, stand on a particular tile so healers get behind you and cannot heal Jad because they need to be right next to him. ([2025-02-26 1:09:20](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=4160))
- Gnomonkey shows a run-through: pray against Jad, stack the healers by running back and forth, then run through while praying melee; he says not praying melee as you run through is the most common death. ([2025-02-26 1:10:22](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=4222))
- For triple Jads, start on a specific important tile; Gnomonkey says starting there saves about 30 health on the first Jad compared with starting anywhere in the middle. ([2025-02-26 1:11:24](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=4284))
- Casting at Jad hits everything around Jad's south-west tile, which usually hits all the healers on that side. ([2025-02-26 1:14:14](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=4454))
- Standing in melee range of a lone Jad corner-traps the healer, so nothing can hit you. ([2025-02-26 1:16:11](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=4571))
- For Jad healers in speeds, Gnomonkey stands one tile away from Jad so his bow shot lands in two ticks instead of one, which gives more time to react when healers proc, and steps into melee range on the shot expected to proc healers so the healer corner traps. ([2025-07-13 1:16:16](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=4576))
- Gnomonkey says after a Jad heal, step back one tile so healers corner trap on each other and the player is not tanking two healers, which can hit for about 30 damage. ([2025-07-13 1:18:50](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=4730))
- Gnomonkey says to brew before Zuk if the player is at 30 to 40 health after the triple Jads. ([2025-07-13 1:20:20](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=4820))
- On Jad, Gnomonkey says to react to the sound of the mage attack, since only mage has a sound: default pray ranged, pray mage when you hear a sound, and keep game sounds on. ([2025-07-13 1:22:01](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=4921))
- Healers only heal when in melee range of Jad, and a healer behind you creates a safe spot because it blocks the other healers from hitting you. ([2025-12-07 1:22:50](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=4970))
- Standing north of Jad lets a barrage aimed at Jad tag everything around his southwest corner where healers gather. ([2025-12-07 1:23:52](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=5032))
- If you take a Jad hit, Gnomonkey says to keep clicking: catch the next prayer, click brew, repeat, because you have very high odds of surviving, and dying while panicking throws away the cape. ([2025-12-07 1:25:57](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=5157))
- Equipping a staff and clicking Jad tags healers that are further back. ([2025-12-07 1:26:30](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=5190))
- If two healers are on you, step back one more tile to auto-corner trap them rather than tanking two healers. ([2025-12-07 1:28:59](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=5339))
- After triples you can lure healers together to heal off them, and healing off the middle one lets you heal off Jad too. ([2025-12-07 1:29:57](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=5397))
- You can tick eat Jad (eat as he hits) but you cannot tick eat Zuk. ([2025-12-07 1:44:43](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=6283))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey would not waste a ranging potion dose on the first Jad and would keep it for later fights, and says not to use Rigour if below four restores. ([2022-11-04 1:40:52](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=6052))
- Gnomonkey says players die more often on the second Jad than on the first or third. ([2025-12-07 1:28:25](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=5305))
- Gnomonkey says triples are not the hard part of the Inferno, just keeping your nerves in check, as they use the same rhythm as a single Jad but faster. ([2025-12-07 1:30:29](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=5429))
