**JalTok-Jad** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 6 videos between 2022-11-04 and 2025-12-07. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* For the Inferno Jad, Gnomonkey says to stand on a certain tile where, once Jad sends healers, you can step in to block them almost always, and that he thinks first-cape players should do this too.[^h6LxupiygPA-3295]
* To lure Jad's healers when they spawn badly, pray melee and run back and forth catching its prayer, wait for the next attack and run through; keep praying melee because the healers can kill a player who stops.[^h6LxupiygPA-3323]
* To avoid wasting brew on Jad healers, stand one tile northwest of Jad, one tile away from his southwest corner, so healers walking toward his southwest brain tend to walk behind you and sit there without hitting.[^2xviK0wGI-o-4906]

## Stats and numbers

* Six hours counts as 36,000 game ticks and he mentions the six-hour log out timer, which he says is typically reset by logging out at Jad.[^FfNQI63ZKys-6000]
* There are five healers with a single Jad and three with triple Jads, and healers only heal if they are right next to Jad.[^XMTe9TYbDls-3410]
* Jads are as far as he knows the highest defence mob in the entire game, with about 480 defence.[^XMTe9TYbDls-3442]
* Triple Jads have a three tick delay between each Jad, which Gnomonkey says is an eternity so they are actually very easy; keep Rigor on and stay potted.[^XMTe9TYbDls-3472]
* Inferno Jads are nine-tick, meaning three ticks between each attack, giving you 1.8 seconds per prayer on the triple Jad.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-4311]
* Jad healers max hit 18, and Gnomonkey says they hurt more than expected, especially in Crystal armour which has good ranged defence but poor melee defence.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-4363]
* Jad has a very high mage defence (he states 480 mage and 510 as numbers).[^H-Iup_IFUVc-4584]
* Healers proc below 180 Jad health.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-4576]
* Doing a Jad skip (also a combat achievement) saves about a minute, and he suggests doing the Jad skip and Zuk fight at the same time.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-4820]
* Jad's healers spawn when Jad reaches 175 health.[^2xviK0wGI-o-5065]
* On triple Jads each Jad attack is three ticks apart in a one-two-three rhythm instead of a nine-tick rhythm.[^2xviK0wGI-o-5219]

## Tips

* The first Jad spawns five healers once he gets to half health, so stand in line with his southwest tile and right next to him so that healers are stuck behind you.[^FfNQI63ZKys-5999]
* If the healers are not in melee range they do not heal Jad, so Gnomonkey can stand where they cannot reach him.[^FfNQI63ZKys-6025][^h6LxupiygPA-3410]
* To lure healers by running through them, and that stacking them up like this is really nice for keeping them together so you will not fail the healer lure.[^FfNQI63ZKys-6080]
* To leave the melee prayer on while luring the healers, which many people forget, and to log out before triples.[^FfNQI63ZKys-6285]
* To full brew, restore and re-divine after killing Jad, because now is the best time to heal before the healers.[^h6LxupiygPA-3319]
* Gnomonkey always starts on the tile next to Jad's south-west side because healers walk toward Jad's south-west tile, so it has the highest chance of blocking them.[^XMTe9TYbDls-3410]
* If you stand far away when sending the shot that will kill, it delays the shot and lets you run in to melee distance.[^XMTe9TYbDls-3442]
* Standing in the bottom left spot makes Jads and healers corner trap each other so only one can hit you, and you should back up if multiple healers hit you.[^XMTe9TYbDls-3558]
* You can stack Jads and run through to heal but does not need to; in the video he heals off healers and pipe specs for health.[^XMTe9TYbDls-3587]
* On the wave 67 double mage, Gnomonkey recommends getting one mage to low health (under 39 so it can be killed) before hitting the other, and camping the mage prayer.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-4118]
* On the Jad wave, stand on a particular tile so healers get behind you and cannot heal Jad because they need to be right next to him.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-4160]
* Gnomonkey shows a run-through: pray against Jad, stack the healers by running back and forth, then run through while praying melee; he says not praying melee as you run through is the most common death.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-4222]
* For triple Jads, start on a specific important tile; Gnomonkey says starting there saves about 30 health on the first Jad compared with starting anywhere in the middle.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-4284]
* Casting at Jad hits everything around Jad's south-west tile, which usually hits all the healers on that side.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-4454]
* Standing in melee range of a lone Jad corner-traps the healer, so nothing can hit you.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-4571]
* For Jad healers in speeds, Gnomonkey stands one tile away from Jad so his bow shot lands in two ticks instead of one, which gives more time to react when healers proc, and steps into melee range on the shot expected to proc healers so the healer corner traps.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-4576]
* After a Jad heal, step back one tile so healers corner trap on each other and the player is not tanking two healers, which can hit for about 30 damage.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-4730]
* To brew before Zuk if the player is at 30 to 40 health after the triple Jads.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-4820]
* On Jad, Gnomonkey says to react to the sound of the mage attack, since only mage has a sound: default pray ranged, pray mage when you hear a sound, and keep game sounds on.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-4921]
* Healers only heal when in melee range of Jad, and a healer behind you creates a safe spot because it blocks the other healers from hitting you.[^2xviK0wGI-o-4970]
* Standing north of Jad lets a barrage aimed at Jad tag everything around his southwest corner where healers gather.[^2xviK0wGI-o-5032]
* If you take a Jad hit, Gnomonkey says to keep clicking: catch the next prayer, click brew, repeat, because you have very high odds of surviving, and dying while panicking throws away the cape.[^2xviK0wGI-o-5157]
* Equipping a staff and clicking Jad tags healers that are further back.[^2xviK0wGI-o-5190]
* If two healers are on you, step back one more tile to auto-corner trap them rather than tanking two healers.[^2xviK0wGI-o-5339]
* After triples you can lure healers together to heal off them, and healing off the middle one lets you heal off Jad too.[^2xviK0wGI-o-5397]
* You can tick eat Jad (eat as he hits) but you cannot tick eat Zuk.[^2xviK0wGI-o-6283]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey would not waste a ranging potion dose on the first Jad and would keep it for later fights, and says not to use Rigour if below four restores.[^FfNQI63ZKys-6052]
* Gnomonkey says players die more often on the second Jad than on the first or third.[^2xviK0wGI-o-5305]
* Gnomonkey says triples are not the hard part of the Inferno, just keeping your nerves in check, as they use the same rhythm as a single Jad but faster.[^2xviK0wGI-o-5429]

## Trivia

* Jad is the highest defence monster in the entire game as far as he knows.[^h6LxupiygPA-3504]
