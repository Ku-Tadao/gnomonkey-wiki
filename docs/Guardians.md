**Guardians** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2024-11-08 and 2025-05-01. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* The one difference at Guardians from regular to CM is that they max quite high.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2310]

## Tips

* Guardians in CM is basically a zero-damage room and your Blood Fury heals you, so brewing beforehand is a complete waste.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2310]
* At the Guardians, Gnomonkey hammers the first one; the hammer always hits zero on the first but still reduces defence if it would have hit.[^jdvQVwg8niM-1597]
* In normal Chambers you do not need to pray melee at the Guardians, but in a real Challenge Mode they hit hard and you should.[^jdvQVwg8niM-1625]
* Make sure the pickaxe is set to crush, because the Guardians are weaker to crush.[^jdvQVwg8niM-1625]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says using a pickaxe special or an Elder maul on Guardians is never worth it, since specs are saved for Vasa.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2340]
