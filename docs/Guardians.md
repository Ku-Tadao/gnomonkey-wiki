## Stats and numbers

- The one difference at Guardians from regular to CM is that they max quite high. ([2024-11-08 38:30](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=2310))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says Guardians in CM is basically a zero-damage room and your Blood Fury heals you, so brewing beforehand is a complete waste. ([2024-11-08 38:30](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=2310))
- At the Guardians, Gnomonkey hammers the first one; the hammer always hits zero on the first but still reduces defence if it would have hit. ([2025-05-01 26:37](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1597))
- In normal Chambers you do not need to pray melee at the Guardians, but in a real Challenge Mode they hit hard and you should. ([2025-05-01 27:05](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1625))
- Make sure the pickaxe is set to crush, because the Guardians are weaker to crush. ([2025-05-01 27:05](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1625))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says using a pickaxe special or an Elder maul on Guardians is never worth it, since specs are saved for Vasa. ([2024-11-08 39:00](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=2340))
