# Bow of Faerdhinen

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 12 videos (2021-09-03 to 2026-02-07). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Gnomonkey says the Bow of Faerdhinen is a powerful mid-game ranged set that is useless without crystal armour, which boosts accuracy and max hit per piece. ([2024-07-07 17:25](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1045))
- In his second raid Gnomonkey runs a Bofa setup using Blue Moon armour as his melee and mage armour, bringing a Phoenix necklace for Vasa. ([2024-10-27 40:53](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=2453))

## Requirements

- Crystal armour is needed to use the Bow of Faerdhinen, so Gnomonkey wears it, and notes that Crystal has very good ranged defence. ([2025-02-26 2:15](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=135))

## Stats and numbers

- Imbuing the Bow of Faerdhinen takes 2,000 crystal shards. ([2021-09-03 1:53](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=113))
- Gnomonkey estimates the Bow of Faerdhinen costs roughly 106 GP per shot to fire, low compared with a tentacle whip at about 200 GP a swing, and about 150k GP an hour to fire continuously at Bandos. ([2021-09-03 9:07](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=547))
- Gnomonkey says Bow of Faerdhinen and twisted bow both have a range of 10 tiles in the Inferno. ([2022-11-04 22:50](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=1370))
- Gnomonkey says twisted bow scales with the target's magic level while Bow of Faerdhinen does not, and that Bow of Faerdhinen is much more accurate, making it better against monsters with very high defence. ([2022-11-04 1:11:01](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=4261))
- Gnomonkey says doing Warden with a Dragon crossbow setup means about 25% less damage, which he says is not the play. ([2024-06-27 9:00](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=540))
- Gnomonkey says Shamans poison you so he brings an antidote, which lasts 12 minutes (he believes), and says you can also bring an anti-poison or a sand few as anti-poison. ([2024-10-27 41:54](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=2514))
- According to Gnomonkey, the Bow of Faerdhinen costs about 160 million on its own, and a full Bofa set with crystal armour costs about 240 million versus about 11 million for the Atlatl set. ([2025-02-22 5:16](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=316))
- Gnomonkey says the Bofa's helmet alone costs about eight and a half million. ([2025-02-22 5:44](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=344))
- Gnomonkey says getting a Bofa takes an average of about 400 kills at the Gauntlet (Corrupted Gauntlet), which he describes as everybody hating the Red Prison on an iron. ([2025-02-22 7:40](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=460))
- Gnomonkey estimates that using a Rune crossbow in the Inferno would give roughly 30-40% worse DPS than the Bow of Faerdhinen, which he calls extremely bad. ([2025-02-26 53:00](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=3180))
- Gnomonkey says the Bofa is about 5 to 8% better than the atlatl on Zulrah depending on stats, and it is convenient because you skip the Bellator ring switch. ([2025-04-24 16:52](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=1012))

## Tips

- He wears the Assembler even with a max cape because it always provides a max hit. ([2021-09-03 0:29](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=29))
- With Bow of Faerdhinen you can pick off a ranger at distance without alternating prayers, something he says you cannot do in a twisted bow run. ([2022-11-04 22:40](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=1360))
- Gnomonkey says Bow of Faerdhinen is only a little harder than twisted bow overall in Inferno, mostly making Zuk harder because you use a four-tick weapon instead of a five-tick weapon, making it harder not to lose ticks. ([2022-11-04 1:43:00](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=6180))
- Gnomonkey says the Bow of Faerdhinen punches through defence quite well and has very long range, which can be more useful than a blowpipe in some situations. ([2024-07-07 17:25](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1045))
- Gnomonkey says the Bofa is four-tick, which makes it harder to move with at Zuk than a Twisted bow, and that most people stick it on accurate to make it five tick. ([2024-10-03 44:04](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=2644))
- Gnomonkey says the Bofa requires crystal armour to be any good, since the armour gives a huge amount of damage and accuracy. ([2025-02-22 5:16](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=316))
- While fighting Zuk, Gnomonkey notes that a six-, seven- or eight-tick Bofa because of hesitation would be equivalent to using a Rune crossbow, so he tells players not to lose ticks. ([2025-02-26 1:06:30](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=3990))
- With the Bofa setup, Gnomonkey says to put the recoil back on when it breaks and that you simply play brain-off. ([2025-04-24 16:52](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=1012))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the release of the Bow of Faerdhinen, combined with crystal armour, makes solo Bandos kills per hour similar to twisted bow kills at other God Wars bosses. ([2021-09-03 0:00](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg))
- Gnomonkey thinks Bow of Faerdhinen is better on melees, and says it is best on monsters with high defence such as mages and melees, while on rangers pipe is better. ([2022-11-04 12:30](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=750))
- Gnomonkey says the waves are about the same difficulty with Bow of Faerdhinen as with twisted bow. ([2022-11-04 1:11:00](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=4260))
- Gnomonkey says to run Bofa as the main weapon on this budget setup, with crystal armour required to make it work, and downgrading to a crossbow with mixed hide is not worth it. ([2024-06-27 0:02](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=2))
- Gnomonkey says the Bow of Faerdhinen is the first thing an Ironman could possibly grind for that you can go horribly dry on. ([2024-07-30 19:35](https://youtu.be/ETOLP88HtYU?t=1175))
- Gnomonkey says a streamer was reportedly stuck for about a year on this bow grind in chat discussion, and that this kind of grind sucks. ([2024-07-30 19:35](https://youtu.be/ETOLP88HtYU?t=1175))
- Gnomonkey says the Bow of Faerdhinen feels like the gateway to late-game PvM and ends up feeling like a bottleneck because it is the first really low drop-rate item, though there are ways around it. ([2024-07-30 20:08](https://youtu.be/ETOLP88HtYU?t=1208))
- Gnomonkey says the Bofa is a bit better DPS than the blowpipe on Inferno melees. ([2024-10-03 15:49](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=949))
- Gnomonkey says the Bofa's DPS is very close to the Twisted bow in the Inferno. ([2024-10-03 26:18](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=1578))
- Gnomonkey says Vespula is very tanky and the Bofa cuts through it, giving it better DPS than a Tbow there, and that the Tbow is better on Vasa but the Bofa works well enough. ([2024-10-27 41:26](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=2486))
- Gnomonkey says Bofa is better for far targets, since it has 10-tile range versus six tiles for the Atlatl and attacks more slowly, and it can safespot. ([2025-02-22 14:13](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=853))
- Gnomonkey says the Bofa remains necessary for the Vespula portal in Chambers and for Mystics, and calls Vespula portal one of the Bofa's best uses. ([2025-02-22 19:06](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=1146))
- Gnomonkey says the Bofa's most important use case is the Inferno, where trying to get a first cape with anything else is almost impossible. ([2025-02-22 19:12](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=1152))
- Gnomonkey says the Bofa is better at God Wars but that the Atlatl cannot be used there, and that the Scorching bow annihilates other weapons at Zamorak, so he would not grind the Bofa early. ([2025-02-22 20:04](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=1204))
- Gnomonkey says Leviathan's last part favours the Bofa over a crossbow, but a crossbow is plenty good thanks to Leviathan's crossbow weakness. ([2025-02-22 20:29](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=1229))
- Gnomonkey describes this as an Inferno 'with Bofa budget', arguing the Bow of Faerdhinen is the standard weapon for a Bofa-and-Deadeye Inferno attempt. ([2025-02-26 0:00](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc))
- Gnomonkey says the Bow of Faerdhinen is fairly similar to the Twisted bow for the Inferno, and that doing it pre-Bofa is basically impossible for most people. ([2025-02-26 55:00](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=3300))
- Gnomonkey argues against Bofa camping on Zulrah, saying it is 12% worse on the green phase and 61% worse on the melee phase than the Twinflame setup, and that you cannot pillar stall with it. ([2025-04-24 23:45](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=1425))
- Gnomonkey says Bofa camping leads to taking more damage and a roughly equal trip length, so it is not a net positive; he says it is fine to use if you want to chill, but he always recommends bringing a mage switch. ([2025-04-24 24:12](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=1452))
- Gnomonkey says the Bofa is a lot better than the atlatl on the blue phase if both strength and ranged are maxed, but the atlatl still works fine. ([2025-04-24 25:55](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=1555))
- Gnomonkey says Bofa is the main butterfly weapon at Akkha after the change, far better than the Trident of the swamp, with the gap growing at higher raid levels. ([2025-06-25 0:00](https://youtu.be/6Qmuhnpa0gc))
- The description says shadow is still best in slot at Akkha but you can now use Bofa, ZCB, Atlatl and other crossbows, and that 500 Toa is now very achievable with Bofa or budget gear. ([2025-06-25 0:03](https://youtu.be/6Qmuhnpa0gc?t=3))
- Gnomonkey says Bofa rather than Zaryte crossbow butterfly is good: ZCB butterfly works but its DPS is deceptive because each hit cannot cross the 80% quadrant threshold (a hit at 82% pulls him to exactly 80%), so it is lower DPS than calcs suggest. ([2025-06-25 15:59](https://youtu.be/6Qmuhnpa0gc?t=959))
- Gnomonkey says he has always been a proponent of avoiding the Bow of Faerdhinen grind immediately, but it is worth getting on a hardcore at some point because it saves so much time on many grinds. ([2026-02-07 12:57](https://youtu.be/R6F2mWXa_fM?t=777))
- Gnomonkey recommends getting a Nightmare-style Faerdhinen later (in his words 'a NALI') and not also going for Sailedor, but if you enjoy Corrupted Gauntlet you can go for a second enhanced crystal weapon. ([2026-02-07 12:57](https://youtu.be/R6F2mWXa_fM?t=777))
