## Methods

- Gnomonkey plans to try to get the backup account to Song of the Elves, and to send the Corrupted Gauntlet in parts of streams, switching to other tasks such as the Moons grind, quests like Unlocking While Gothic Sleeps, or Tormented Demons when he is not feeling it. ([2025-10-29 10:31](https://youtu.be/0_DI-SbhksU?t=631))

## Stats and numbers

- The backup Hardcore Ironman is fairly close to Song of the Elves; the furthest requirement is 70 Construction, and Gnomonkey is working on 70 Hunter. ([2025-10-29 9:18](https://youtu.be/0_DI-SbhksU?t=558))
- The backup account has a black mask, some Moons of Peril armour pieces, lots of tank legs but no tank body, a completed Warhammer, a dexterity prayer scroll for Rigor, and claws. ([2025-10-29 9:38](https://youtu.be/0_DI-SbhksU?t=578))
- The backup account has 74 Prayer, enough for Rigor; it does not have Augury yet, which is the only thing Gnomonkey would want as a drop from the Corrupted Gauntlet grind. ([2025-10-29 10:05](https://youtu.be/0_DI-SbhksU?t=605))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says he feels you burn out very quickly doing the Corrupted Gauntlet all day, so having other grinds on the side means he does not have to do it straight. ([2025-10-29 11:03](https://youtu.be/0_DI-SbhksU?t=663))
- Gnomonkey says he has never done the Corrupted Gauntlet on a Hardcore Ironman and is kind of excited to learn something new there, even though he always says he hates it. ([2025-10-29 11:03](https://youtu.be/0_DI-SbhksU?t=663))
- Gnomonkey says restarting on the backup is a perfect position to be in, because he has other grinds to work on and does not need a Bow of Faerdhinen to send Tormented Demons. ([2025-10-29 11:03](https://youtu.be/0_DI-SbhksU?t=663))

## Events

- Gnomonkey plans to de-iron the backup account's settings after the 6thBrother death; he will keep a spare alt similar to Raddy with the same stats, and the GP on it becomes his. ([2025-10-29 9:01](https://youtu.be/0_DI-SbhksU?t=541))
