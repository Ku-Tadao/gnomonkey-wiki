# Barrows

## Methods

- For Prayer experience, Gnomonkey does a Barrows task requiring him to kill all six brothers and recover an icon from the chest in their crypt. ([2024-08-08 11:02](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=662))
- Gnomonkey kills only one brother per trip and takes the melee brothers without praying, saving his prayer for Karil, whom he must fight early so he does not run out of prayer. ([2024-08-08 12:34](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=754))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says he wants D-plate legs for Moons, quotes a one in seven chance from wave five and six, and says if he sees them on wave six he will send it. ([2024-08-17 10:33](https://youtu.be/iUjSbo1TQFQ?t=633))
- Bolt racks replace rune rolls in the Barrows chest and require a very high reward potential to receive. ([2026-05-27 20:07](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=1207))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey brings Jangerberries to Barrows because they restore one prayer point each. ([2024-08-08 11:02](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=662))
- Gnomonkey tried to spec Ahrim with his dragon dagger and missed every special attack, and notes Ahrim drains strength and that he has ten less slash defence than stab defence, then decides to mage all of the brothers. ([2024-08-08 11:35](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=695))
- Gnomonkey proposes a chance for the Barrows chest to give a partially used, untradeable strange old lockpick of around 10 to 25 charges instead of bolt racks. ([2026-05-27 19:03](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=1143))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey considers Barrows good mid-level content and what Jagex should strive for if they add more, though he thinks they should not add many mid-level bosses. ([2022-03-19 39:39](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2379))
- Gnomonkey says all mid-level content should not have a pet, because it keeps mid-levels grinding it instead of high levels; he notes Barrows has no pet. ([2022-03-19 40:10](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2410))
- Gnomonkey rates Barrows C tier, below Giant Mole, as solid mid-level content for mid-levels. ([2022-03-19 40:43](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2443))
- Gnomonkey notes players typically do not kill Barrows before the Hallowed Sepulchre because the Sepulchre needs Sins of the Father, so the strange old lockpick is mainly for clogging outfits like the Dharok set. ([2026-05-27 19:34](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=1174))
- Gnomonkey's lockpick idea would give a trade-off where killing more tunnel creatures for reward potential gives the lockpick to skip doors, allowing faster kills per hour, and keeps the Hallowed Sepulchre lockpick valuable. ([2026-05-27 20:07](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=1207))
