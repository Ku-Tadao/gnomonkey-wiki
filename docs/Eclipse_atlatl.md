# Eclipse atlatl

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 4 videos (2025-04-24 to 2025-06-25). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Gnomonkey says you can also butterfly Akkha with the Eclipse atlatl, which is three-tick but uses the same tiles as the four-tick method; he will make a separate guide. ([2025-06-25 14:17](https://youtu.be/6Qmuhnpa0gc?t=857))
- With the Akkha change, three-tick butterfly is possible with the Eclipse atlatl; Gnomonkey notes you never had a three-tick weapon before outside Leagues. ([2025-06-25 0:55](https://youtu.be/7ZyZC0OcvsU?t=55))
- The Eclipse atlatl scales with strength bonus for max hit and ranged bonus for accuracy, so you want hybrid gear with both strength and ranged bonus such as Barrows gloves, and a Fury is the best amulet. ([2025-06-25 1:56](https://youtu.be/7ZyZC0OcvsU?t=116))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says the atlatl scales with strength level and strength bonus while its accuracy uses ranged level and ranged bonus, so it is fairly accurate and attacks quickly. ([2025-04-24 1:23](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=83))
- Gnomonkey says the atlatl does not get its full set burn damage on Zulrah, because she purges the burn when going underwater, so it deals only two or three burn splats per phase at most. ([2025-04-24 1:23](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=83))
- Gnomonkey says the Bofa is roughly 5 to 8% better than the atlatl on Zulrah, depending on levels, and is also easier to use. ([2025-04-24 2:04](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=124))
- Gnomonkey shows at max stats a 14% difference on the blue phase between the necklaces for the atlatl, but says the gap is closer if your strength level is higher than your ranged level. ([2025-04-24 25:17](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=1517))
- Gnomonkey says the Eclipse atlatl scales off strength bonus and he brings it with an Ava's assembler as a two-way. ([2025-05-07 1:33](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=93))
- Gnomonkey says the Atlatl setup costs a sixth of the Bofa setup (about 20 mil) and has the same DPS on the shadows as Bofa, being only a little worse on Akkha himself, with the same effectively zero chance of missing a DPS skip. ([2025-06-25 1:26](https://youtu.be/7ZyZC0OcvsU?t=86))

## Tips

- The atlatl does not require wasting ranging potions, so Gnomonkey drinks super strength rather than ranging potions on Ironmen. ([2025-04-24 1:23](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=83))
- The atlatl is a three-tick weapon, so Gnomonkey says to use a Bellator ring switch while ranging, because it takes two ticks to put on and those ticks are very important. ([2025-04-24 2:21](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=141))
- Gnomonkey warns against wearing Necklace of anguish with the atlatl and says Amulet of fury is much better, since atlatl scales on strength. ([2025-04-24 24:38](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=1478))
- Gnomonkey says the atlatl is needed in the melee setup for cases where Zulrah is behind poison clouds and cannot be meleed, and in some cases it lets you fit an extra hit. ([2025-05-07 1:33](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=93))
- At the start of kills, throwing one atlatl dart gives a free hit, and it lets you finish the phase with a Crystal halberd spec. ([2025-05-07 3:38](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=218))
- Gnomonkey starts each phase with an atlatl hit before the Crystal halberd, because you otherwise lose a full swing of four ticks at the end. ([2025-05-07 7:01](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=421))
- Gnomonkey says you probably want to fit an atlatl hit in some phases, as some rotations are awkward for melee. ([2025-05-07 12:02](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=722))
- Gnomonkey says to wear the full Eclipse armour set as melee gear because it lets the Atlatl burn, adding about 0.7 DPS (burn stacks up to five), and the set gives both ranged and strength bonus; the only upgrades are Primordial boots and Ultor ring. ([2025-06-25 2:23](https://youtu.be/7ZyZC0OcvsU?t=143))
- Gnomonkey says Pharaoh gloves are worse for this setup and Necklace of anguish is terrible, never to be used; Fury beats torture or rancour because it has accuracy for range. ([2025-06-25 2:55](https://youtu.be/7ZyZC0OcvsU?t=175))
- The Eclipse atlatl benefits from both Divine ranging and Divine super combat potions, and salts boost both; Gnomonkey also summons a thrall. ([2025-06-25 5:55](https://youtu.be/7ZyZC0OcvsU?t=355))
- Gnomonkey recommends a Berserker ring (about 5% DPS) or Ultor ring as a switch for Akkha and Ba-Ba, using Lightbearer for the rest of the raid. ([2025-06-25 19:50](https://youtu.be/7ZyZC0OcvsU?t=1190))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says Zulrah is not the atlatl's best boss, but you do not need a Bofa to kill Zulrah and can get kills with it. ([2025-04-24 25:55](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=1555))
- Gnomonkey says the atlatl got most of his Zulrah KC before the Twisted bow, as described in the video description. ([2025-04-24 26:18](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=1578))
- Gnomonkey says the Atlatl is about 10 to 15% worse than Bofa but extremely cheap, and calls Atlatl, Bofa or shadow the best options at Akkha now, any of which can do a 500. ([2025-06-25 14:17](https://youtu.be/6Qmuhnpa0gc?t=857))
- Gnomonkey says to just camp the Eclipse set as melee armour for the whole raid since Ba-Ba no longer has chip damage and Akkha's chip damage is reduced, accepting a slight DPS decrease on the Fang. ([2025-06-25 13:25](https://youtu.be/7ZyZC0OcvsU?t=805))
- Gnomonkey says the setup costs nothing relative to Bofa and that Toa has never been easier, more accessible or enjoyable. ([2025-06-25 20:30](https://youtu.be/7ZyZC0OcvsU?t=1230))
