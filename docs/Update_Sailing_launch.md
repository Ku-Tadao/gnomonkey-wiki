# Update: Sailing launch

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2025-11-04 to 2025-11-19). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | update |
| **Videos** | 2 (first 2025-11-04, latest 2025-11-19) |

## Methods

- For the Sailing launch, Jagex pulls the worlds down and puts them back up without Sailing, as was done for the Group Ironman (Gridmaster) release, then adds the update in a buffer window and Sailing goes live. ([2025-11-04 0:32](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=32))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey advises paying attention to the Sailing introduction quest, which explains the skill and its methods; in the beta he remembers it taking about 10 to 15 minutes. ([2025-11-04 1:32](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=92))
- Gnomonkey describes this as a Day 1 guide in which some things may change and a better meta may appear in a few weeks. ([2025-11-19 0:00](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey considers the pull-down-and-buffer launch approach much better than the usual one-to-two hour delay on major releases; it worked well for the Gridmaster update, which was ready exactly when Jagex said. ([2025-11-04 1:01](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=61))

## Events

- Jagex released a blog and a video starring Gnomonkey two weeks before the Sailing skill launched, explaining how to prepare for Sailing. ([2025-11-04 0:00](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o))
- Sailing is set to launch at 7:15 AM UTC on November 19th. ([2025-11-04 0:32](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=32))
- Gnomonkey planned to go live on stream at 3 AM on November 19th the instant Sailing appeared and train it immediately. ([2025-11-04 16:00](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=960))
- Sailing launched with a new mid-level Slayer boss, the Shellbane Gryphon, and this guide was made on release day. ([2025-11-19 0:00](https://youtu.be/JwkGh1TXLts))
