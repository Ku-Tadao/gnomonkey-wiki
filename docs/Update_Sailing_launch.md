**Update: Sailing launch** is an update topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2025-11-04 and 2025-11-19. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* For the Sailing launch, Jagex pulls the worlds down and puts them back up without Sailing, as was done for the Group Ironman (Gridmaster) release, then adds the update in a buffer window and Sailing goes live.[^pwZKK8dpq8o-32]

## Tips

* Gnomonkey advises paying attention to the Sailing introduction quest, which explains the skill and its methods; in the beta he remembers it taking about 10 to 15 minutes.[^pwZKK8dpq8o-92]
* Gnomonkey describes this as a Day 1 guide in which some things may change and a better meta may appear in a few weeks.[^JwkGh1TXLts-0]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey considers the pull-down-and-buffer launch approach much better than the usual one-to-two hour delay on major releases; it worked well for the Gridmaster update, which was ready exactly when Jagex said.[^pwZKK8dpq8o-61]

## Events

* Jagex released a blog and a video starring Gnomonkey two weeks before the Sailing skill launched, explaining how to prepare for Sailing.[^pwZKK8dpq8o-0]
* Sailing is set to launch at 7:15 AM UTC on November 19th.[^pwZKK8dpq8o-32]
* Gnomonkey planned to go live on stream at 3 AM on November 19th the instant Sailing appeared and train it immediately.[^pwZKK8dpq8o-960]
* Sailing launched with a new mid-level Slayer boss, the Shellbane Gryphon, and this guide was made on release day.[^JwkGh1TXLts-0]
