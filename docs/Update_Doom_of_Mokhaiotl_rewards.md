## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says Jagex is replacing a profit-keeping thrall upgrade currency with teleport scrolls and something that lets players skip to cave three so they can immediately get the necklace, and he reacts with disbelief. ([2025-01-30 4:34](https://youtu.be/kkmUE6oQxy4?t=274))
- Gnomonkey says the third blog is very short and made things worse than the previous blog, which he calls impressive because the second blog had many issues he wanted answered. ([2025-01-30 5:05](https://youtu.be/kkmUE6oQxy4?t=305))
- Gnomonkey tells viewers not to accept the third blog update as good enough just because the blog count is wearing everyone out, and tells Jagex to delay the update if it needs to. ([2025-01-30 25:07](https://youtu.be/kkmUE6oQxy4?t=1507))
- Gnomonkey's overall verdict on blog #47 is that the boots and shovel are good, the staff has major issues that need changes, and the gauntlets are utterly broken. ([2025-03-11 29:07](https://youtu.be/-98-rsX1soM?t=1747))
- Gnomonkey says the items for Yama were much better than these after blog after blog of this process. ([2025-03-11 29:07](https://youtu.be/-98-rsX1soM?t=1747))
- Gnomonkey compares the swing from the earlier 1% mage damage and negative prayer on the cape to the gauntlets as sliding from one end of the boat to the other, and wants a happy medium. ([2025-03-11 30:27](https://youtu.be/-98-rsX1soM?t=1827))
- Gnomonkey worries players are exhausted by this many rewards blogs and will not read them any more, so if the poll goes ahead the gauntlets will pass and break the game in half. ([2025-03-11 33:27](https://youtu.be/-98-rsX1soM?t=2007))
- Gnomonkey hopes Jagex will pull the gauntlets as it did with the Nightmare's super high mage damage tome, which was dropped even though it passed its vote. ([2025-03-11 34:12](https://youtu.be/-98-rsX1soM?t=2052))
- Gnomonkey thinks the revised Mokha delve boss rewards are finally a good set, saying Jagex has gone through a lot of changes but nailed it this time, and that the Yama rewards were perfect as they were. ([2025-03-24 0:00](https://youtu.be/-E1yidIYQ4Y))
- Gnomonkey's main complaints about the previous blog were that the gauntlets were insanely broken for the Tumeken's shadow and that the new weapon's spec was basically useless; both were addressed. ([2025-03-24 0:00](https://youtu.be/-E1yidIYQ4Y))
- Gnomonkey says Jagex conquered every complaint he had about the rewards and that he is glad he complained, despite people being mad at him for complaining about things being too powerful. ([2025-03-24 11:52](https://youtu.be/-E1yidIYQ4Y?t=712))
- Gnomonkey says he is no longer voting no on everything and will vote yes to everything in the poll, being very happy with the update. ([2025-03-24 12:20](https://youtu.be/-E1yidIYQ4Y?t=740))
- Gnomonkey was happy with the update before the blog and expected the new blog to make things worse, guessing they would change the spade. ([2025-03-28 0:00](https://youtu.be/u-RJBe_5Nmo))
- Gnomonkey finds Jagex's closing thanks to the community for the rewards roller coaster very based and says Reddit did not win this time. ([2025-03-28 9:28](https://youtu.be/u-RJBe_5Nmo?t=568))
- Gnomonkey says he was expecting the worst from the blog, that Reddit would win, and is surprised it turned out pretty good. ([2025-03-28 9:58](https://youtu.be/u-RJBe_5Nmo?t=598))
- Gnomonkey says he loves the commitment to looking at Tumeken's shadow and Jagex's general design philosophy going forward. ([2025-03-28 10:07](https://youtu.be/u-RJBe_5Nmo?t=607))

## Events

- Jagex put together a whole new blog collecting the latest information on the Mokha reward proposals, which Gnomonkey says was in response to the proposal updates being complicated to follow. ([2025-01-30 0:25](https://youtu.be/kkmUE6oQxy4?t=25))
- The third blog replaced the previously proposed cape with an untradeable Occult necklace upgrade after back and forth about a tradable, untradeable or no cape. ([2025-01-30 1:36](https://youtu.be/kkmUE6oQxy4?t=96))
- The third blog said Jagex plans to replace the thrall upgrade with a teleport and possibly a second tradable drop that affects the encounter itself, such as an item that skips straight to Delve level 3 when consumed; Jagex said it would not poll these additions. ([2025-01-30 4:06](https://youtu.be/kkmUE6oQxy4?t=246))
- Mokha blog number 47 was released while Gnomonkey was on his honeymoon, and he reacted to it in this video (video title: Mohka Rewards #4). ([2025-03-11 0:00](https://youtu.be/-98-rsX1soM))
- Jagex said the earlier rewards were not strong or interesting enough, so the new designs were built with that in mind; content-specific rewards such as a teleport item and a pet are still planned, but this blog focuses on the rewards players will vote on. ([2025-03-11 0:00](https://youtu.be/-98-rsX1soM))
- Gnomonkey describes the blog's four rewards as revised boots, a new mage staff (Eye of Ayak), Confliction gauntlets (a tormented bracelet upgrade) and a shovel that instantly harvests herbs. ([2025-03-11 34:16](https://youtu.be/-98-rsX1soM?t=2056))
- The blog says the rewards were tweaked after player feedback with a handful of small changes, and includes all the upcoming poll questions. ([2025-03-24 0:25](https://youtu.be/-E1yidIYQ4Y?t=25))
- Jagex's final Delve boss (Doom of Mokhaiotl) reward blog begins with Jagex reflecting on how the rewards have been handled, calling nailing them a challenge and the understatement of the year. ([2025-03-28 0:32](https://youtu.be/u-RJBe_5Nmo?t=32))
- Jagex announced polls for the Doom of Mokhaiotl rewards would open the following Monday, March 31st. ([2025-03-28 3:30](https://youtu.be/u-RJBe_5Nmo?t=210))
- Jagex said it believes the Eye of Ayak, Avernic Treads (gauntlets and consumable tiers) make a tight and impactful set of rewards that should stay in high demand and give players an incentive to risk it all at deeper delve levels. ([2025-03-28 7:43](https://youtu.be/u-RJBe_5Nmo?t=463))
