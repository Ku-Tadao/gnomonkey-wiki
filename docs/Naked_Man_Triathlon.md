# Naked Man Triathlon

## Stats and numbers

- In the naked ToA run Gnomonkey notes room times of about 7:30 for Akkha, 5:18 for Baba and 4:12 for the Monkey (Zebak/Kephri-area) room, and says all the times are basically the same as doing the raid solo. ([2023-09-12 47:30](https://youtu.be/f1Y2Pd6dQoA?t=2850))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says that if you want the experience of a solo Tombs of Amascut raid but do not have an alt, you can just go in naked, probably with an Osmumten's fang. ([2023-09-12 48:02](https://youtu.be/f1Y2Pd6dQoA?t=2882))

## Opinions

- After the naked Challenge Mode Chambers run, Gnomonkey says he could easily do a naked Entry Mode Theatre of Blood but that a naked no-armour solo Theatre of Blood (normal mode) is something he cannot do. ([2023-09-12 1:51:52](https://youtu.be/f1Y2Pd6dQoA?t=6712))

## Records

- Gnomonkey says the naked 500 invocation Tombs of Amascut run took 46:51 total raid time with no armour whatsoever, and that it was much easier than he thought it would be. ([2023-09-12 47:30](https://youtu.be/f1Y2Pd6dQoA?t=2850))
- Gnomonkey finished the Naked Man Challenge Mode Chambers of Xeric run in 1 hour 1 minute 12 seconds, beating the challenge target time for an extra 5,000 points. ([2023-09-12 1:51:52](https://youtu.be/f1Y2Pd6dQoA?t=6712))
- Gnomonkey says his naked Theatre of Blood Entry Mode run took about 17 minutes overall, which he estimates is about a three-minute time loss from wearing the chicken outfit. ([2023-09-12 2:04:10](https://youtu.be/f1Y2Pd6dQoA?t=7450))

## Events

- The Naked Man Mode Triathlon is a challenge featuring all three of the major OSRS raids: Tombs of Amascut at 500 invocation level, Challenge Mode Chambers of Xeric, and Theatre of Blood Entry Mode (which Gnomonkey says is not possible in normal mode). ([2023-09-12 0:00](https://youtu.be/f1Y2Pd6dQoA))
- Gnomonkey did the runs with no armour but allowed himself jewellery, and brought about every weapon in his bank. ([2023-09-12 0:00](https://youtu.be/f1Y2Pd6dQoA))
- Gnomonkey describes this challenge as part of Subtember, a series featured on his Twitch for the month of September for hitting 200 subs, with more challenges to follow as chat picks them. ([2023-09-12 0:00](https://youtu.be/f1Y2Pd6dQoA))
