**Naked Man Triathlon** is a challenge topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2023-09-12. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* In the naked ToA run Gnomonkey notes room times of about 7:30 for Akkha, 5:18 for Baba and 4:12 for the Monkey (Zebak/Kephri-area) room, and says all the times are basically the same as doing the raid solo.[^f1Y2Pd6dQoA-2850]

## Tips

* If you want the experience of a solo Tombs of Amascut raid but do not have an alt, you can just go in naked, probably with an Osmumten's fang.[^f1Y2Pd6dQoA-2882]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* After the naked Challenge Mode Chambers run, Gnomonkey says he could easily do a naked Entry Mode Theatre of Blood but that a naked no-armour solo Theatre of Blood (normal mode) is something he cannot do.[^f1Y2Pd6dQoA-6712]

## Records

* The naked 500 invocation Tombs of Amascut run took 46:51 total raid time with no armour whatsoever, and that it was much easier than he thought it would be.[^f1Y2Pd6dQoA-2850]
* Gnomonkey finished the Naked Man Challenge Mode Chambers of Xeric run in 1 hour 1 minute 12 seconds, beating the challenge target time for an extra 5,000 points.[^f1Y2Pd6dQoA-6712]
* His naked Theatre of Blood Entry Mode run took about 17 minutes overall, which he estimates is about a three-minute time loss from wearing the chicken outfit.[^f1Y2Pd6dQoA-7450]

## Events

* The Naked Man Mode Triathlon is a challenge featuring all three of the major OSRS raids: Tombs of Amascut at 500 invocation level, Challenge Mode Chambers of Xeric, and Theatre of Blood Entry Mode (which Gnomonkey says is not possible in normal mode).[^f1Y2Pd6dQoA-0]
* Gnomonkey did the runs with no armour but allowed himself jewellery, and brought about every weapon in his bank.[^f1Y2Pd6dQoA-0]
* Gnomonkey describes this challenge as part of Subtember, a series featured on his Twitch for the month of September for hitting 200 subs, with more challenges to follow as chat picks them.[^f1Y2Pd6dQoA-0]
