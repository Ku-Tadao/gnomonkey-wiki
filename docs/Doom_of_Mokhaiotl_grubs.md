# Doom of Mokhaiotl: grubs

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2025-07-23 to 2025-08-09). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Doom of Mokhaiotl grubs die in one hit to any demonbane weapon they are not praying against; they have two health and non-demonbane weapons do one damage, so you want demonbane to tag them quickly because there are many grubs later. ([2025-07-23 8:53](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=533))
- Grubs spawn in the corners of the Doom of Mokhaiotl room and must be killed before they reach the boss; if they reach its centre they hurt you a little and heal the boss, compounding over the run. ([2025-08-09 10:45](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=645))

## Stats and numbers

- Doom of Mokhaiotl's grubs move to the centre of the boss and, on arrival, heal it for around 10 health and damage you for 1 to 15; Gnomonkey says this punishment is very minor and never changes in later caves. ([2025-07-23 8:53](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=533))
- When a Doom of Mokhaiotl grub dies it does a 3x3 AoE of about 15 damage; in melee range you have one tick after killing it to step out of the way. ([2025-07-23 9:25](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=565))
- Attacking Doom of Mokhaiotl grubs is zero tick: you can attack them while on cooldown from attacking the boss without losing any ticks. ([2025-07-23 9:51](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=591))
- Spells (manually cast or staff-cast demonbane) against Doom of Mokhaiotl grubs are not zero tick: you must be off cooldown for them to land, which Gnomonkey says feels like a bug that may be changed. ([2025-07-23 10:22](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=622))
- On Doom of Mokhaiotl cave 8, grubs spawn in triples; Gnomonkey prefers to simply get rid of them quickly since too much else is happening to take advantage of the bonus damage. ([2025-07-23 17:47](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=1067))
- You have until a grub reaches the boss's centre, plus one extra tick, to hit it; once you attack a grub it cannot move, and killing it causes a shockwave hitting up to about 20 if you are next to it, but a shockwave touching the boss cannot hurt you. ([2025-08-09 11:17](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=677))
- Grubs pray protection styles: one at the start, and later two styles at once, coloured, so you need the exact Demon bane weapon; Gnomonkey recommends the Demonic larva plugin from the plugin hub to highlight and colour them. ([2025-08-09 11:47](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=707))
- After the most recent patch Doom of Mokhaiotl has big grubs that take only two shots, instead of the old triple grub spawn, with the same shockwave damage as a small grub. ([2025-08-09 36:18](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=2178))

## Tips

- Killing a grub next to or under the Doom of Mokhaiotl makes its explosion damage the boss (about 8 damage in his example) and not you; the grubs always go to a corner, so stand near that corner. ([2025-07-23 9:51](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=591))
- Gnomonkey says to ignore a Doom of Mokhaiotl grub if reaching it forces you into venom or a melee hit, since it only heals the boss about 10 and hurts you very little. ([2025-07-23 25:11](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=1511))
- Gnomonkey notes that in the Doom of Mokhaiotl car phase a grub on the field usually despawns before it reaches the car, so you usually do not have to tag grubs during the car phase. ([2025-07-23 29:05](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=1745))
- Gnomonkey says not to greed grubs at the Doom of Mokhaiotl: they only deal around 3 to 4 damage, and he kept dying from going out of his way to tag them. ([2025-07-23 30:34](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=1834))
- Gnomonkey says melee grubs are the main struggle on delve 4 because they are awkward to catch depending on acid placement. ([2025-08-09 21:15](https://youtu.be/_q6rmQQkN1I?t=1275))
