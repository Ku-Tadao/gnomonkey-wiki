**Doom of Mokhaiotl: grubs** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2025-07-23 and 2025-08-09. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Doom of Mokhaiotl grubs die in one hit to any demonbane weapon they are not praying against; they have two health and non-demonbane weapons do one damage, so you want demonbane to tag them quickly because there are many grubs later.[^DUL0hiFwEfk-533]
* Grubs spawn in the corners of the Doom of Mokhaiotl room and must be killed before they reach the boss; if they reach its centre they hurt you a little and heal the boss, compounding over the run.[^_q6rmQQkN1I-645]

## Stats and numbers

* Doom of Mokhaiotl's grubs move to the centre of the boss and, on arrival, heal it for around 10 health and damage you for 1 to 15; Gnomonkey says this punishment is very minor and never changes in later caves.[^DUL0hiFwEfk-533]
* When a Doom of Mokhaiotl grub dies it does a 3x3 AoE of about 15 damage; in melee range you have one tick after killing it to step out of the way.[^DUL0hiFwEfk-565]
* Attacking Doom of Mokhaiotl grubs is zero tick: you can attack them while on cooldown from attacking the boss without losing any ticks.[^DUL0hiFwEfk-591]
* Spells (manually cast or staff-cast demonbane) against Doom of Mokhaiotl grubs are not zero tick: you must be off cooldown for them to land, which Gnomonkey says feels like a bug that may be changed.[^DUL0hiFwEfk-622]
* On Doom of Mokhaiotl cave 8, grubs spawn in triples; Gnomonkey prefers to simply get rid of them quickly since too much else is happening to take advantage of the bonus damage.[^DUL0hiFwEfk-1067]
* You have until a grub reaches the boss's centre, plus one extra tick, to hit it; once you attack a grub it cannot move, and killing it causes a shockwave hitting up to about 20 if you are next to it, but a shockwave touching the boss cannot hurt you.[^_q6rmQQkN1I-677]
* Grubs pray protection styles: one at the start, and later two styles at once, coloured, so you need the exact Demon bane weapon; Gnomonkey recommends the Demonic larva plugin from the plugin hub to highlight and colour them.[^_q6rmQQkN1I-707]
* After the most recent patch Doom of Mokhaiotl has big grubs that take only two shots, instead of the old triple grub spawn, with the same shockwave damage as a small grub.[^_q6rmQQkN1I-2178]

## Tips

* Killing a grub next to or under the Doom of Mokhaiotl makes its explosion damage the boss (about 8 damage in his example) and not you; the grubs always go to a corner, so stand near that corner.[^DUL0hiFwEfk-591]
* To ignore a Doom of Mokhaiotl grub if reaching it forces you into venom or a melee hit, since it only heals the boss about 10 and hurts you very little.[^DUL0hiFwEfk-1511]
* In the Doom of Mokhaiotl car phase a grub on the field usually despawns before it reaches the car, so you usually do not have to tag grubs during the car phase.[^DUL0hiFwEfk-1745]
* Not to greed grubs at the Doom of Mokhaiotl: they only deal around 3 to 4 damage, and he kept dying from going out of his way to tag them.[^DUL0hiFwEfk-1834]
* Melee grubs are the main struggle on delve 4 because they are awkward to catch depending on acid placement.[^_q6rmQQkN1I-1275]
