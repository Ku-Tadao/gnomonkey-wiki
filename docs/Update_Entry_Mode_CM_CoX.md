**Update: Entry Mode CM CoX** is an update topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2025-05-01. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Completing an Entry Mode CM CoX gives roughly 45k points and a Combat Achievement.[^jdvQVwg8niM-64][^jdvQVwg8niM-3596]

## Tips

* You can also do Entry Mode CM CoX in teams, where it is even easier because teammates can teach a basic version.[^jdvQVwg8niM-3712]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says Entry Mode CM CoX is a good opportunity to learn Challenge Mode and Chambers of Xeric in general because you do not have to scout and you get to practise every possible room in a row.[^jdvQVwg8niM-32]
* Gnomonkey says that if you have a Twisted bow, doing regular full-scale Challenge Mode is much better for points, but for players without mega rares or with only a Shadow, Entry Mode is really solid for points.[^jdvQVwg8niM-64]
* Gnomonkey says Entry Mode gives so many brews and potions that it is a great way to learn no-prep Chambers, in place of scouting for Vanguard raids.[^jdvQVwg8niM-93]
* Gnomonkey says the Entry Mode raid gives effectively unlimited brews, so it is great for learning no-prep, solos and Chambers in general, and he considers the update a nice learning tool that also lets you escape scouting.[^jdvQVwg8niM-3650]

## Events

* Jagex released Entry Mode challenge mode Chambers of Xeric, which has every room of the raid at once as in Challenge Mode but at a normal scale rather than the hugely scaled-up Challenge Mode stats.[^jdvQVwg8niM-0]
