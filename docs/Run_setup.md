## Methods

- Gnomonkey uses Ice Barrage as the main mage switch because enough magic bonus guarantees landing a freeze on all the war band, giving him time to think and deal with spawns. ([2025-12-12 3:32](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=212))
- Gnomonkey brought an Ellie (Elidinis' Ward or similar) to tank at the start and a Venator bow to pick off the Frem mage after freezing. ([2025-12-12 3:32](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=212))
- Gnomonkey heals mainly with Blood Fury or Blood Barrage, and also brings a Saradomin godsword spec to restore health. ([2025-12-12 7:04](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=424))

## Tips

- Anything outside the arena cannot be dragged into melee range so it must be dealt with using mage. ([2025-12-12 2:59](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=179))
- Missing a flick is extremely dangerous because the player is in robes much of the time with basically no ranged defence; a Jaguar hit can one-shot, and a double javelin hit can do roughly 90 damage. ([2025-12-12 8:28](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=508))
- Gnomonkey tries to get 99 health every wave because a lot of hits are taken off spawn. ([2025-12-12 14:58](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=898))
- Gnomonkey notes brews will not save you if you are tanking hits of about 50 every tick. ([2025-12-12 17:04](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=1024))
- Gnomonkey deals first with things he cannot heal off, mageing NPCs outside the arena and then killing the ones he can drag in, similar to Inferno. ([2025-12-12 19:11](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=1151))
- Starting a wave without full health is very bad, and Gnomonkey avoids brewing because supplies are limited, saving super combats more than brews. ([2025-12-12 19:37](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=1177))
- Gnomonkey likes picking javelins off early because their javelin toss is annoying, and it is nice to stand still and just flick prayers. ([2025-12-12 20:03](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=1203))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says supplies are not the issue in the run; the issue is not getting stacked out by 50 hits at a time. ([2025-12-12 7:04](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=424))
