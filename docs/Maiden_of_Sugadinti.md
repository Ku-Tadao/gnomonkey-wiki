# Maiden of Sugadinti

## Methods

- The Maiden of Sugadinti is the first boss of the Theatre of Blood and one of the harder rooms; many of her attacks heal her, and the more she is healed the higher her max hit rises for all her attacks, so a bad room becomes a downward spiral and wipes are not uncommon if freezers are off. ([2021-01-29 15:41](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=941))
- If more than five Nylocas are let through to the Maiden she can start hitting over 30 through prayer on every attack. She stays on the west side of the room and throws basic magic attacks at the nearest player, with a chance to drain the stat of the combat style the target is using. ([2021-01-29 16:09](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=969))
- As soon as the Maiden begins her attack animation, who she targets and the damage are already decided; if two players share a tile next to her she attacks the one higher in the orb order. ([2021-01-29 16:09](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=969))
- The Maiden throws blood splats under everyone and on random tiles around one player; they damage players standing in them each tick and heal her for the same amount, hitting up to one tick after the animation when next to her, and she will not throw blood again until at least three attacks have passed. At high healing these can hit in the thirties each tick. ([2021-01-29 16:38](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=998))
- The Maiden randomly spawns blood spawns (up to eight in the room), with double spawn chance if someone steps in her thrown blood; they roam leaving a blood trail that damages players and heals the Maiden just like thrown blood. ([2021-01-29 17:06](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=1026))
- At 70%, 50% and 30% health the Maiden spawns two Nylocas per player (up to ten) that walk to her and heal her for their full remaining health if they arrive; each has 200 health and the community labels them north 1 to 4 and south 1 to 4. The goal is to freeze them, in stacks where possible, then kill the final clump with ice barrages or chinchompas while others hit loose ones. ([2021-01-29 17:32](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=1052))
- Gnomonkey starts the Maiden fight in full melee gear with the Dragon warhammer spec ready, Piety and Protect from Magic on; BGS users attack with their main weapon until both hammer specs are dumped on her, then both BGS specs. It is efficient to BGS rather than hammer if two hammer hits landed. ([2021-01-29 17:58](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=1078))
- If new to the Maiden, Gnomonkey says to switch to the blowpipe for the rest of the fight (removing prims) to give more time to react to blood; the two non-mages should constantly cast Vengeance on whoever is tanking, and must be ready to move whenever she throws blood. ([2021-01-29 18:30](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=1110))
- Non-mages stand relatively close to the Maiden to tank hits while the South Mage, first in the party, takes the hits more than 50% of the time; once she is at 75 to 80% the mages switch into mage gear, activate Augury and prepare Ice Barrage. ([2021-01-29 18:58](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=1138))
- In a typical team the North Mage freezes North 1, 2 and 3: North 1 and 2 are frozen outside the clump and North 3 falls within it. Casting barrage ahead of time on each next Nylocas works if you clicked North 1 immediately; the window is three ticks, so if timing felt slow skip 2s and make sure to freeze 3s in the clump. ([2021-01-29 20:26](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=1226))
- The South Mage typically freezes South 1, 2 and 4: freeze South 1 immediately, wait until South 2 is closer to the Maiden to freeze it (both 2s and 4s can be clumped on the south side), and freeze the 4s when they are one step from the clump. Missing the four Nylocas clumped in the 4s heals the Maiden 800 and dramatically raises her max hit, and missing 4s twice almost certainly wipes. ([2021-01-29 21:53](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=1313))
- The ranged DPS throws chinchompas at the clump as the South Mage freezes, with chins set to medium fuse and standing four to six tiles away for maximum accuracy, communicating with the mage about whether they need more Blood Barrage healing; the melee DPS stays on the boss and helps finish loose Nylocas. ([2021-01-29 22:50](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=1370))
- To skip the 30% Nylocas at the Maiden with a scythe-heavy team, each player uses only one spec at the start to land two hammer specs and two decent BGS specs (the BGSer can throw an extra spec if hitting below about 40); in a trio the BGSer dumps both specs after two hammers land. Specs are kept so claws can be used later. ([2021-01-29 24:19](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=1459))
- When the 30% Nylocas spawn in a skip attempt, the mages freeze them all as fast as possible instead of clumping and everyone switches to melee, dumping claw specs; at about 8 to 10% Maiden health all four players dump Crystal halberd specs so she dies before any Nylocas arrive, and if any Nylocas get through you should usually abort the skip. ([2021-01-29 25:17](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=1517))
- In trios Gnomonkey often uses the machine gun method: the South Mage freezes every south Nylocas while the other two shoot N1 and N2 twice each with blowpipes, typically nearly or fully killing both; placing tile indicators where South 1 spawns helps freeze it instantly, but it is very hard for a South Mage with bad ping. ([2021-01-29 26:18](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=1578))

## Requirements

- To avoid splashing Nylocas at the Maiden you need at least 55 magic attack bonus with Augury on and an avoid setup, reached by taking boots off or using an offhand magic weapon like a Book of Darkness; boots on without an offhand can still reach 100% accuracy with an imbued heart boost but only if you do not tank hits in mage gear. ([2021-01-29 19:52](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=1192))

## Stats and numbers

- According to Gnomonkey's DPS chart, the Scythe of Vitur beats all weapons at the Maiden regardless of her defense, with the blowpipe and Twisted bow fairly close; a blowpipe with dragon darts overtakes the Twisted bow at 46 defense. ([2021-01-29 23:52](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=1432))

## Tips

- Freeze timing at the Maiden is very tight and missing freezes can wipe the team; a common learner mistake is forgetting to turn on Augury or not equipping void gloves. ([2021-01-29 19:25](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=1165))
- Gnomonkey freezes a Nylocas twice if the 1s or 2s are missing and the first freeze was well timed, since it is free damage and helps get the 3s in the clump; after freezing, switch back to range gear and kill loose Nylocas outside the clump. ([2021-01-29 21:26](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=1286))
- At the Maiden, freeze earlier than you think rather than later; once everything is frozen the South Mage runs in front of her to tank and casts Blood Barrage on the clump to heal, or Ice Barrage if at full health since it does more damage. ([2021-01-29 22:20](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=1340))
- If there are more than four blood spawns at the Maiden, Gnomonkey starts piping blood spawns the mages have frozen; once the 30% Nylocas are dealt with and spec has regenerated, switch to melee gear and throw a Crystal halberd or claw spec, then pick up food and move on to Bloat equipping the salve. ([2021-01-29 23:20](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=1400))
- An easy improvement at the Maiden is to enter in full mage gear or in range gear with a twisted bow and fire one free Ice Barrage or Twisted bow shot, then switch into melee gear and hammer while running to the boss. ([2021-01-29 23:52](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=1432))
- Using a scythe as a DPSer through the whole Maiden fight is another improvement, but you must move every time she attacks (every 10 ticks) to avoid blood splats, which hit a tick after the animation begins. ([2021-01-29 24:19](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=1459))
- A South Mage who freezes the first Nylocas quickly can tell the North Mage to freeze only North 1 and 2 and return to the boss while South freezes the rest, giving two extra scythe swings and a better chance of the skip. ([2021-01-29 25:47](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=1547))
- If freezing all at the Maiden and a Nylocas takes more than one step after being frozen, you were a tick off and must skip 2s to make up for it; if missing 1s or 2s, time the freeze on 3s so they freeze side by side instead of in a pile, since catching 3s and 4s still needs to be tick perfect. ([2021-01-29 26:50](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=1610))
- Gnomonkey describes MG in a trio, where the team kills the ticks on one side while the mage freezes the other side, and stacking in four or five man, where two mages tick-perfect freeze while DPS drop claw specs and barrage the clump. ([2022-03-19 59:15](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=3555))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says he really likes Maiden of Sugadinti and thinks she may be the best boss of the Theater of Blood; he rates her high A tier, not quite S. ([2022-03-19 58:44](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=3524))
- Gnomonkey says Maiden's blood mechanic is annoying because the whole team is punished when one person messes up, but he considers it simple to dodge and says mages have the hardest role in the fight. ([2022-03-19 1:00:20](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=3620))
- Gnomonkey says solo hard mode Maiden of Sugadinti depends heavily on RNG, giving something like a two in three chance of even getting through the room. ([2023-10-29 41:24](https://youtu.be/43IYFOMfalw?t=2484))

## Events

- During the Maiden attempts, Gnomonkey comments that the Dragon Warhammer RNG was bad, a factor he cites in the description alongside misplays. ([2023-10-29 2:03](https://youtu.be/43IYFOMfalw?t=123))
- The Maiden room took around seven minutes in the successful run, which Gnomonkey says is unusually long and attributes to unlucky swings. ([2023-10-29 5:04](https://youtu.be/43IYFOMfalw?t=304))

## Trivia

- Gnomonkey says everyone scythes Maiden as best DPS, and near 70% health one or two mages quickly switch to magic and cast freezes on the blood tick spawns. ([2022-03-19 58:44](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=3524))
