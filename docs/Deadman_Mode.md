## Methods

- Breaches in Deadman: Annihilation spawn many random bosses roughly every seven hours (as Gnomonkey recalls); killing them gives PvP-helping drops such as emblems and supply drops, plus points. ([2026-02-04 0:58](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=58))
- In Deadman: Annihilation, once you reach a certain combat level you are booted from the world, cannot gain XP, and can access the bank only a few times before moving to a new world for a different combat bracket. ([2026-02-04 1:42](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=102))
- Each new bracket entered gives one hour of immunity in which no one can attack you, which Gnomonkey enjoyed min-maxing; he spent his on Barrows, got nothing, then started killing people. ([2026-02-04 1:53](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=113))
- Deadman points work like Leagues points and are spent on sigils, such as an instant crop-harvesting sigil or a sigil giving one tick faster mage attack speed, and on unlocking things like Ancient Magicks and Barrows gloves. ([2026-02-04 4:25](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=265))
- Other Deadman: Annihilation point sources include combat achievements, regular diaries, levelling stats and clues (a sigil makes clues a single step), so the best method is skilling, raids (ignoring purples for combat achievements) and clues. ([2026-02-04 6:23](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=383))
- Deadman chests are announced in chat; you open one by sitting at it doing a thieving-style opening animation for 60 seconds, after which you get strong loot. ([2026-02-04 7:26](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=446))

## Requirements

- Breach points were meant to be one point per point of damage dealt to a boss, but only the top 16 damage dealers receive any points, which Jagex did not mention when marketing it. ([2026-02-04 4:51](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=291))

## Stats and numbers

- In Deadman: Annihilation a level-up gives five points, while one point of damage on a breach boss gives one point. ([2026-02-04 5:12](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=312))
- With over a thousand players at breaches, getting into the top 16 damage dealers is very hard, so points from breaches were barely obtainable. ([2026-02-04 5:20](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=320))
- Killing a player in Deadman: Annihilation gives five points per kill up to 100 kills, equal to roughly one level-up, which Gnomonkey says is poor when XP rates are ten times normal. ([2026-02-04 6:23](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=383))
- The Deadman chests in Deadman: Annihilation can give the Dragon sword and the Khopesh, which Gnomonkey calls absolutely wild. ([2026-02-04 7:56](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=476))
- Deadman Mode involved 120 hours of streaming over a week, so Gnomonkey wants to give full context for the story behind everything, since many viewers jumped between streams and clips. ([2026-06-16 33:07](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1987))
- Deadman Allstars had a 120 hour play cap over eight days, which Gnomonkey says meant 18-hour play days for his team versus about 14 hours for other teams, plus 20 to 30 hours logged out discussing plans, so he estimates about 150 hours total. ([2026-06-25 20:32](https://youtu.be/lJexGv6dxu8?t=1232))
- Gnomonkey streamed 18 hours a day on usually two to four hours of sleep, lost 13 pounds from stress, and fell asleep face down on the carpet for three hours after the last day. ([2026-06-25 21:00](https://youtu.be/lJexGv6dxu8?t=1260))

- In the breach, monsters can aggro unexpectedly, so the team stands on tiles with line of sight so they can shoot from safe positions. ([2026-06-10 12:27](https://youtu.be/qyG_lTC4pDU?t=747))
- When luring a monster in the breach, Gnomonkey says not to mage it too early because the team wants to keep it in their position; if a teammate does mage it, he says to use blood spells. ([2026-06-10 16:26](https://youtu.be/qyG_lTC4pDU?t=986))

- Gnomonkey says an area far east of the fairy ring is out of range of the Hunter's Guild where the Dino Nuggets bank, so monsters pulled there can be farmed with less risk. ([2026-06-10 29:25](https://youtu.be/qyG_lTC4pDU?t=1765))

- After killing Zuk a teammate tells Gnomonkey not to celebrate on the spot and to go to Draynor, then to grab a ring of returning and go to the Grand Exchange, because enemy teams are coming. ([2026-06-14 12:56](https://youtu.be/H6ScEEQ3AZ4?t=776))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey calls Yama's meatballs maybe the best addition to any Deadman mode ever and loves the chaos of players being wiped out in droves. ([2026-02-04 1:25](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=85))
- Gnomonkey felt the combat brackets in Deadman: Annihilation were pretty well balanced even though many players complained. ([2026-02-04 1:25](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=85))
- Gnomonkey says brackets enabled pure and other builds and had fun in the level 60 to 80 bracket, though he heard the 1 to 60 bracket became horrible after a few days from pker camping. ([2026-02-04 1:53](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=113))
- Gnomonkey says grinding Deadman points through skilling, raids and clues is boring and not dangerous, like Leagues but in a PvP game mode. ([2026-02-04 6:50](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=410))
- Gnomonkey says points are essential: without grinding them you will not keep up when moving up brackets, as others will attack faster, have much higher accuracy and unlimited prayer points. ([2026-02-04 7:26](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=446))
- Gnomonkey says basically only players with ultimate-level setups or Giga Max risks bother with the chests, since sitting defenceless for 60 seconds is dangerous. ([2026-02-04 7:56](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=476))
- Gnomonkey considers the Deadman chests a lame addition and says most players ignored them because the loot was not worth the trouble. ([2026-02-04 8:58](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=538))
- Gnomonkey calls the Deadman sigil shop menu horrendous: it is not alphabetical, the sigils appear in random order, the filters are haphazard, and it has no search function. ([2026-02-04 13:49](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=829))

- Gnomonkey says that in hindsight the Corrupted weapon reward was not really worth the risk of the Inferno mission, but the moment itself made it worth it. ([2026-06-14 0:00](https://youtu.be/H6ScEEQ3AZ4))
- Gnomonkey says he hoped the mission was not both the Fire cape and the kill count, because the team does not share the cape; he was glad the kill count was enough. ([2026-06-14 19:01](https://youtu.be/H6ScEEQ3AZ4?t=1141))
- Gnomonkey hopes this Inferno run redeems the team's last breach in Deadman Mode. ([2026-06-14 19:35](https://youtu.be/H6ScEEQ3AZ4?t=1175))
