**Zombie axe** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2024-08-17 and 2026-06-30. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* The slower the weapon, the more bonus damage Gnomonkey gets from the Maggot King's clone hits, and the Zombie axe is a super accurate, slow, heavy-hitting crush weapon.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-143]
* The Zombie axe is a five-tick weapon, which Gnomonkey describes as pretty easy and straightforward to use against the Maggot King.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-534]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the zombie axe is good because it is fairly accurate compared to a dragon scimitar, so it is very strong against high defence targets.[^iUjSbo1TQFQ-394]
* Gnomonkey says the Zombie axe is really good at the Maggot King because in the melee phases you get bonus damage for free, effectively clones of whatever you are using.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-143]
* Gnomonkey says the Maggot King is weak to crush, and that Scythe, Ink mace and Soul Reaper axe are generally going to be better than a Zombie axe.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-548]

## Events

* Gnomonkey received a zombie axe on his first Barrows kill count after the quest and says it is a one in 800 drop.[^iUjSbo1TQFQ-353]
* He will high alch the zombie axe as an RNG sacrifice for the rest of the group.[^iUjSbo1TQFQ-486]
