# Zombie axe

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2024-08-17 to 2026-06-30). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Stats and numbers

- The slower the weapon, the more bonus damage Gnomonkey gets from the Maggot King's clone hits, and the Zombie axe is a super accurate, slow, heavy-hitting crush weapon. ([2026-06-30 2:23](https://youtu.be/0iIPIZTfsBU?t=143))
- The Zombie axe is a five-tick weapon, which Gnomonkey describes as pretty easy and straightforward to use against the Maggot King. ([2026-06-30 8:54](https://youtu.be/0iIPIZTfsBU?t=534))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the zombie axe is good because it is fairly accurate compared to a dragon scimitar, so it is very strong against high defence targets. ([2024-08-17 6:34](https://youtu.be/iUjSbo1TQFQ?t=394))
- Gnomonkey says the Zombie axe is really good at the Maggot King because in the melee phases you get bonus damage for free, effectively clones of whatever you are using. ([2026-06-30 2:23](https://youtu.be/0iIPIZTfsBU?t=143))
- Gnomonkey says the Maggot King is weak to crush, and that Scythe, Ink mace and Soul Reaper axe are generally going to be better than a Zombie axe. ([2026-06-30 9:08](https://youtu.be/0iIPIZTfsBU?t=548))

## Events

- Gnomonkey received a zombie axe on his first Barrows kill count after the quest and says it is a one in 800 drop. ([2024-08-17 5:53](https://youtu.be/iUjSbo1TQFQ?t=353))
- Gnomonkey says he will high alch the zombie axe as an RNG sacrifice for the rest of the group. ([2024-08-17 8:06](https://youtu.be/iUjSbo1TQFQ?t=486))
