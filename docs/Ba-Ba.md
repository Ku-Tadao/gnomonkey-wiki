Ba-Ba (also written Baba) is the baboon boss room of [Tombs of Amascut](Tombs_of_Amascut.md). Gnomonkey has attacked the fight with the "red X" stall method since 2022, and describes it after the June 2025 changes as a zero-damage, zero-brew room that needs no special tech[^h4hf5hPQHms-533][^q3D2jeguGz8-276][^JjIAGBBDmL8-630].

[TOC]

## Overview

- Gnomonkey does Ba-Ba first in the raid so his pre-pot helps in the monkey room, which wants ranging and strength boosts[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-428][^BVZEAFBsj10-1220]. In the 2022 solo 500 he instead did Ba-Ba second because salt is not needed for it[^h4hf5hPQHms-533].
- Early opinion (2022): he hoped Jagex would fix a bug or issue with Ba-Ba and stop her hitting so hard through prayer[^h4hf5hPQHms-1049].
- Current position (June 2025 onward): Ba-Ba no longer hits through prayer, so praying melee means no damage from her[^JjIAGBBDmL8-30][^UhLzyieXrbY-410][^N2DCZ1nt4p4-702]. He calls it a zero-brew room with no Red X and no five-to-one, saying even in a 500 it is totally free and just takes a while, especially when the [Bandos godsword](Bandos_godsword.md) misses[^JjIAGBBDmL8-630][^UhLzyieXrbY-645].
- In a duo, he called the monkey room the biggest supply drain of the whole raid, more than any boss, and said Ba-Ba has a lot of drain too (2025)[^X-PTHMq_10A-697][^X-PTHMq_10A-911]. He recommends targeting the main monkey to reduce drain[^X-PTHMq_10A-697].

## Mechanics

- **Shockwave / slam**: Ba-Ba slams the back of the room every 33 percent of her health, killing the player instantly in front of the pit; Gnomonkey marks safe tiles and uses a Monster HP Percentage plugin[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-789]. Boulder phases occur at 66% and 33%[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-748]. Chip damage: she drops one rock on the head and two elsewhere for about 50 damage each; a thrown big rock is avoided by standing next to one of the rocks or a sarcophagus, though a sarcophagus hit twice opens and releases balls of death[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-727]. Not standing next to a rock when the thrown rock lands does ten times more damage; standing next to a sarcophagus halves damage[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-597][^vEs4Qvs_Rag-629].
- **Area attack**: if the cycle is messed up in the red X setup, the area attack hits for about 30[^vQO8khf302s-503]. The gap does not kill from the top row[^cmLBaxdoHpQ-571][^YizVhRWXnxs-342].
- **Monkeys**: the spawned monkeys are one-tick, ignore attack delay, and are good for gaining double [Death Charge](Death_Charge.md) spec (2026)[^BVZEAFBsj10-613][^N2DCZ1nt4p4-755]. After the June 2025 update any damage guarantees a one-shot, and they can be skipped at low health because they despawn[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-715][^BVZEAFBsj10-736]; with Circle swap or a blowpipe they are one-ticked[^91vxCCm7Jck-744][^UhLzyieXrbY-443]. Earlier (2024) he killed the small baboons with a crossbow because they do a lot of damage[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-629].
- **Coffins**: still usable to block one hit each without breaking, and one can stand a tile off one and take no damage (2025)[^91vxCCm7Jck-804].
- **Boulders**: one of five boulders is cracked and must be one-shot with the [Bow of Faerdhinen](Bow_of_Faerdhinen.md); he waits until the next boulder touches his character on a marked tile row, then runs up and hits Ba-Ba with the fang[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-820][^91vxCCm7Jck-862]. Standing on the corner of the boulders gives room to run to the other corner when she knocks back or places the shadow AoE[^UhLzyieXrbY-523][^BVZEAFBsj10-643]. In 2025 he said not to range her while boulders roll, since melee DPS is much better[^BVZEAFBsj10-736]. The Voidwaker did not one-shot the cracked boulder after the May 2024 update without a potion, so he suggested an accurate blowpipe with a full switch and a pre-potted ranging potion[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-462]; the Bow of Faerdhinen avoids boulder issues[^LxYODChv40A-297].
- **Shadow step marker**: run across her to the other side; running under her and clicking melee drags the player into the boulder[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-915].

## Red X method (2022-2024)

Before the June 2025 update Gnomonkey used the red X method to negate all of her damage: a red click keeps Ba-Ba in place and monsters cannot path under the player, forcing her to proc Shadow Stomp repeatedly and not attack[^h4hf5hPQHms-1067][^q3D2jeguGz8-276][^YizVhRWXnxs-450].

- Ba-Ba attacks at six-tick speed, so red X slows melee to that speed[^q3D2jeguGz8-309]. Setup (2022): wait right of the gap, hit once, walk right one tile, right click the obelisk, walk three under, click Ba-Ba to walk three back, then cycle two under and two back[^q3D2jeguGz8-309]. Restart after boulders: drop a potion left of Ba-Ba, run to the top right, hit once, step back one tile, step three under, attack, then cycle[^q3D2jeguGz8-337]. Boulders are avoided by stepping diagonally[^q3D2jeguGz8-309].
- Entry shortcuts: a four-tick hit (Keris) then any hit and two under[^YizVhRWXnxs-342]; Voidwaker on stab then four-tick hit into a two-step under[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-442]; drop a Sanfew serum, poke with the [Keris](Keris_partisan_of_breaching.md), swap to the [Osmumten's fang](Osmumten's_fang.md) and go two under two back on the swing[^LxYODChv40A-338]. Without Keris: a four-tick hit into a Fang hit into two steps under[^cmLBaxdoHpQ-484].
- Cue to continue the cycle is her paw going up, then the obelisk click; the window is two ticks[^vQO8khf302s-471]. Switches between attacks are possible, such as a Zaryte crossbow spec and back to melee[^q3D2jeguGz8-364]. He said the red X is the easy way: 500s are possible without it but harder than needed[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-442], and fighting conventionally is always fastest but she becomes much less consistent to face tank with Dehydration[^q3D2jeguGz8-276]. See [Tombs of Amascut gear setup](Tombs_of_Amascut_gear_setup.md).

After the update, the red X is no longer required: Jagex's wording that Red X "still has its place" was confusing to Gnomonkey; he reported it was confirmed on Reddit that the Ba-Ba changes in the initial blog were kept[^JjIAGBBDmL8-112].

## Specs, gear and supplies

- Bandos godsword: drain at least 20 defence levels (the 20 damage cap) before melee, usable while red Xing since it is six-tick[^q3D2jeguGz8-364][^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-689][^N2DCZ1nt4p4-879]. A 2024 note puts a threshold at about 1000 damage[^YizVhRWXnxs-450]. He then uses [Burning claws](Burning_claws.md) on stab (bosses have around 500 slash defence), or Voidwaker[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-879][^LxYODChv40A-392]. Voidwaker spec again at the 90% boulder costs fifty spec (2023)[^cmLBaxdoHpQ-392]. Double Death Charge can recover 100% spec around 50% if the godsword missed[^UhLzyieXrbY-469]. Bone dagger special drains her on entry (2024 300 raid)[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-571][^HeM0yGenyx8-284]. See [Bone dagger](Bone_dagger.md).
- Nothing in the room is weak to crush, so no Dragon warhammer or Arclight[^cmLBaxdoHpQ-421]. He considers bringing Barrage trolling because thralls are too valuable ([Thralls](Thralls.md)); he does not thrall constantly in this room to avoid a dose[^K7VALGO6md8-293][^cmLBaxdoHpQ-451].
- Prayer and supplies: Ba-Ba needs no prayer and no resources if done properly, aiming for zero doses (2023)[^vQO8khf302s-386][^vQO8khf302s-2616]. He did the room on two prayer points and walked out with the same[^K7VALGO6md8-264], used one restore dose in May 2024 saying he could have used zero[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-515], and one brew in October 2024, expecting one to two on average[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-722]. Under the June 2025 changes: no brews needed, use them freely after chip damage (practice used two doses)[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-661][^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-840]; April 2026: no brews, one restore dose with some Piety flicking, two or three without flicking[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-1031]. With a Blue moon setup no tanky armour is needed and he one-tick flicks Piety with protect from melee[^UhLzyieXrbY-410].
- Freezing Ba-Ba (and Kephri) keeps them still[^K7VALGO6md8-592]. Re-pot a super combat after each full brew[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-629].

## Monkey room

- All monkeys except thralls (brawler, thrower, mage, shaman, volatile) count toward a cap preventing the next wave. First two waves need zero alive, the second to last has a cap of two, and others one. Kill low-HP monkeys and blow up volatiles quickly; chinchompas roll off defence so can kill throwers and brawlers from thralls; summoned thralls do not attack cursed baboons[^q3D2jeguGz8-243][^q3D2jeguGz8-276]. See [Monkey Room](Monkey_Room.md).
- Supply drain is highest here in duo runs[^X-PTHMq_10A-697]; a "use" tech on the green vial shift-click cures a teammate instantly[^X-PTHMq_10A-731].
