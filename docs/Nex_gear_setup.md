# Nex gear setup

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 5 videos (2022-04-11 to 2024-06-23). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Gnomonkey's revised Nex setup adds a ZCB (Zaryte crossbow) switch and a shield switch, which also requires bolts. ([2022-04-11 2:03](https://youtu.be/Y2KvOrUXUeA?t=123))
- Whenever Nex targets him, Gnomonkey camps his shield to prevent the damage and then switches back to a Vernic; he also flicks the shield whenever possible to lengthen trips, and supplies are shared with teammates depending on who was targeted most. ([2022-04-11 2:28](https://youtu.be/Y2KvOrUXUeA?t=148))
- Gnomonkey's budget duo Nex setup camps Karil's armour because it has good mage defence and no negative melee stats, with the best melee gear otherwise, a Fang, a four-way range switch and an ACB, with a pipe for minions and Reavers. ([2023-07-04 0:00](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4))
- Gnomonkey says to bring some kind of Zaros item (such as ancient bracers) to hit the Ultar altar: without one it only restores prayer, with one it gives full health, prayer, spec and energy; a ZCB counts as a Zaros item. ([2023-07-04 0:56](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=56))
- In this setup Gnomonkey uses nine-way ranged gear for the Zaryte crossbow (switching quickly isn't needed except maybe for Blood Sacrifice) and an eight-way setup for the Twisted bow when switching quickly to a minion. ([2024-06-23 1:54](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=114))

## Requirements

- The budget setup uses Mixed hide armour, which Gnomonkey says is lower in max hit than Bandos but has a stab bonus, giving better DPS than Bandos at Nex, doubles as ranged armour, and is extremely cheap. ([2024-06-15 1:27](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=87))

## Stats and numbers

- ZCB is around 15% better DPS on Nex than Twisted bow in his setup, without counting its special attack, but Twisted bow is still incredibly good against the minions. ([2022-04-11 2:03](https://youtu.be/Y2KvOrUXUeA?t=123))
- From Gnomonkey's experience, Elysian saves roughly two doses of brew per kill depending on how you use it; Spectral saves a bit less but also saves prayer. ([2022-04-11 2:28](https://youtu.be/Y2KvOrUXUeA?t=148))
- The altar at Nex gives full health, prayer, run energy and spec restore, but only if you have an ancient item equipped. ([2024-06-15 3:31](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=211))

## Tips

- At the start of the fight Gnomonkey hits Nex a tick before she is vulnerable on purpose to make shield flicking very easy. ([2022-04-11 2:28](https://youtu.be/Y2KvOrUXUeA?t=148))
- With a Spectral shield you can help your team by taking the virus and camping the Spectral, which reduces the prayer drain of the virus. ([2022-04-11 3:01](https://youtu.be/Y2KvOrUXUeA?t=181))
- Gnomonkey brings full Torva for duo Nex because it reduces a lot of damage; you use about the same supplies, dropping two brews but taking much less damage, which allows meleeing on phase 2 and speeds it up dramatically. ([2023-05-28 0:54](https://youtu.be/vFVvnNJnyZQ?t=54))
- Gnomonkey brings four Prayer restores because his Grandmaster-level setup saves about a dose; he suggests five or six restores if you are learning. ([2023-05-28 0:54](https://youtu.be/vFVvnNJnyZQ?t=54))
- Gnomonkey brings seven brews; if learning, drop the Buckler and bring about eight brews. ([2023-05-28 1:20](https://youtu.be/vFVvnNJnyZQ?t=80))
- Gnomonkey typically uses Voidwaker on phase 2 because it works out spec-energy wise, and previously one Voidwaker spec would leave you at 100% spec energy and sitting on it. ([2023-05-28 1:20](https://youtu.be/vFVvnNJnyZQ?t=80))
- In the budget setup Gnomonkey does not wear the Light bearer because there is no good special attack weapon besides Fang specs; he skips death charge as the specs are bad anyway. ([2023-07-04 0:27](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=27))
- Gnomonkey says the first upgrade is Masori top and bottom, the most important thing, since it has better mage defence, a good ranged bonus and no negative melee, so it is way better than Karil's. ([2023-07-04 0:27](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=27))
- Gnomonkey says to drink Menaphite remedy first before other potions, because it restores your stats every 15 seconds like an overload if you are brewed down, and it cancels divine potions if drunk after them. ([2023-07-04 1:30](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=90))
- Gnomonkey says to upgrade in this order: Masori top and bottom first, then Voidwaker, then sell the Voidwaker to afford a ZCB, then sell the ZCB to buy a Twisted bow; he says not to buy Dragon claws (about 90m) because claws are bad. ([2023-07-04 15:35](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=935))
- Gnomonkey says there is no good special weapon before ZCB or Voidwaker, so use Fang specs until then. ([2023-07-04 15:35](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=935))
- At peak, Gnomonkey says Torva is really good because you can melee on phase 2, and it makes you take less damage so you use about the same or slightly less brew over the kill even though you carry two extra swaps. ([2023-07-04 16:57](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=1017))
- Gnomonkey says the Light bearer is better once you have a good spec weapon like ZCB or Voidwaker, because Fang max hits do not matter too much since it is so accurate. ([2023-07-04 18:58](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=1138))
- Gnomonkey says the cape swap and Masori helmet switch are not max hits, so in a struggling setup you can drop the switch, bring an extra potion, and dump your bolts on the ground. ([2024-06-15 1:59](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=119))
- The budget setup uses Ferocious gloves and Primordial boots, a regular dragon defender, and Gnomonkey names the Avernic defender (about 50 million) as the next melee upgrade. ([2024-06-15 2:28](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=148))
- Gnomonkey names the Voidwaker (about 80 million at the time) as the next major purchase, and says it is way better than fang specing; if you own a Voidwaker and later can afford a Zaryte crossbow, sell the Voidwaker, though it is a solid backup budget spec. ([2024-06-15 2:28](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=148))
- Zaryte vambraces (about 100 million, previously about 180) are recommended because they count as an ancient item, which is required to receive the full restore from the altar in the middle of the fight. ([2024-06-15 2:58](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=178))
- Gnomonkey says Pharaoh's gloves give a huge strength bonus and a switch to bracers is the best option, and that the Twisted bow is for the minions while the crossbow is for the boss. ([2024-06-15 4:04](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=244))
- Bring runes for death charge and thralls; Gnomonkey says thralls are a huge part of your DPS because they hit through Nex's very high defence. ([2024-06-15 4:36](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=276))
- Gnomonkey says an adamant crossbow is preferred over a rune crossbow because its higher range lets you flinch out of Nex's close-range damage more easily, even though both fire rubies. ([2024-06-15 5:08](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=308))
- Gnomonkey says that if you leave without an ancient item equipped you should make sure you have the Zaryte item on, otherwise things will hit you and kill you. ([2024-06-15 26:59](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=1619))
- Gnomonkey says not to bring a rune defender because the 5 stab attack bonus loss matters more than the 1 strength difference. ([2024-06-15 35:58](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=2158))
- If you do not have a Quiver (no ammo slot in this setup), Gnomonkey says to ditch the pegs, noting pegs are about 1% better than not having them, a fairly big DPS increase. ([2024-06-23 0:00](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo))
- Without a Quiver, you swap out the boots; Gnomonkey says a boot switch is not necessary but very nice. ([2024-06-23 1:54](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=114))
- The second range potion is optional; one is fine, and if you are struggling with kills you can ditch a range potion for an extra brew or for a resource (restore) potion if you run out of prayer. ([2024-06-23 3:29](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=209))
- Gnomonkey says four resource potions should be plenty for a normal kill, and as a baseline he brings five resource and eight brew. ([2024-06-23 3:29](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=209))
- Gnomonkey brings fire runes in his pouch for thrall and death charge; death charge is nice because it works on the minions and the Reavers, and the charge carries into the next kill when you kill her. ([2024-06-23 3:29](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=209))
- Gnomonkey says a Super Combat... minify potion... (transcribed as Minify pot) is nice as a pre-pot because brewing lets you get stats back, and it restores stats when you get virus. ([2024-06-23 4:28](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=268))

## Opinions

- For the shield switch Gnomonkey ranks Elysian greater than Spectral; with neither, he suggests running no shield, and says Buckler is not worth the inventory slot over a brew. ([2022-04-11 2:03](https://youtu.be/Y2KvOrUXUeA?t=123))
- Gnomonkey is torn between bringing a six-way (BGS/Fang setup) with a ZCB or just a Twisted bow with Pegasian boots and Assembler; he says the T-Bow is only 2% worse than ZCB when just autoing Nex at full ranged potion. ([2023-05-28 0:00](https://youtu.be/vFVvnNJnyZQ))
- Gnomonkey says the T-Bow is really good for minions and Reavers, while bringing the ZCB is really good for potting down, and he thinks either choice is very close. ([2023-05-28 0:26](https://youtu.be/vFVvnNJnyZQ?t=26))
- Gnomonkey says Voidwaker is a better special attack than the Claws/ZCB option at Nex. ([2023-05-28 0:26](https://youtu.be/vFVvnNJnyZQ?t=26))
- Gnomonkey says Voidwaker is a better spec than ZCB on Nex, roughly 15% more efficient per spec. ([2023-07-04 16:26](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=986))
- Gnomonkey says an eight-way Twisted bow setup with Assembler is within 2% of just a ZCB on Nex, so you do not even need to buy a ZCB. ([2023-07-04 16:57](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=1017))
- Gnomonkey says you cannot do duo Nex with Karil's alone at the high end; without Masori and better you will get shredded. ([2023-07-04 18:58](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=1138))
- Gnomonkey says the Osmumten's fang is about 35% better than a crossbow at Nex and is also your spec weapon if you cannot afford one; Voidwaker or the Zaryte crossbow are better spec weapons, with the Zaryte crossbow best-in-slot for spec. ([2024-06-15 0:29](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=29))
- Gnomonkey says if you do not own Masori, run Mixed hide; if you do own Masori he thinks camping it is slightly faster overall than Mixed hide, and Mixed hide plus a Masori switch is faster still as you get better. ([2024-06-15 1:27](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=87))
- Gnomonkey says a Twisted bow plus Zaryte crossbow is the meta now, with the Twisted bow replacing the blowpipe, and that the Torva helmet alone is a solid upgrade because of its defence and max hit. ([2024-06-15 4:03](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=243))
- Gnomonkey says that because Nex is weak to crossbows, an adamant crossbow does more damage than a Twisted bow on Nex, so you always bring a crossbow in addition to a Twisted bow and never camp the Twisted bow. ([2024-06-15 4:04](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=244))
- Gnomonkey says the toxic blowpipe is the second-best option for minions but not very good because blowpipes do poorly against high defence targets. ([2024-06-15 4:36](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=276))
- Gnomonkey says the Bow of Faerdhinen is a terrible choice here and tells people never to bring it, since it is worse than an adamant crossbow on Nex and worse than the blowpipe on minions. ([2024-06-15 4:48](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=288))
- Gnomonkey says to always wear the Lightbearer at Nex because it is better DPS than any other ring, including an Ultor ring or Venator ring, even when using fang specs, and he would not recommend Nex until you own one. ([2024-06-15 5:22](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=322))
- Gnomonkey says it is difficult for a learner with low gear to find a Nex duo, because people with poor gear are also bad at Nex, though kills in bad gear are absolutely doable. ([2024-06-15 35:13](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=2113))
- Gnomonkey says bringing extra potions is totally fine for learning, even with infinite money, rather than not bringing the full Torva setup. ([2024-06-23 0:26](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=26))
- Gnomonkey says the Buckler is extremely good, giving about 5% DPS when ranging, so it is very good to have. ([2024-06-23 3:29](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=209))
