The **Nex gear setup** is Gnomonkey's evolving advice on what to bring to [Nex](Nex.md), from the 2022 Zaryte crossbow setup to the budget duo setups of 2023 and 2024. Core themes: a crossbow for the boss, a Twisted bow or pipe for the minions, an ancient item for the altar, and thralls and Death Charge for DPS.[^rnLS-oZr3wU-244][^rnLS-oZr3wU-244][^rnLS-oZr3wU-276]

## Weapons

His April 2022 setup added a ZCB (Zaryte crossbow) switch and a shield switch, which also requires bolts.[^Y2KvOrUXUeA-123] The ZCB is around 15% better DPS on Nex than the Twisted bow without counting its special attack, but the Twisted bow is still incredibly good against the minions.[^Y2KvOrUXUeA-123] In May 2023 he is torn between a six-way (BGS/Fang) with a ZCB and a Twisted bow with Pegasian boots and Assembler, noting the T-bow is only 2% worse than the ZCB when just autoing at full ranged potion.[^vFVvnNJnyZQ-0] The T-bow is really good for minions and Reavers; the ZCB is really good for potting down; he sees the choice as very close.[^vFVvnNJnyZQ-26] In July 2023 an eight-way Twisted bow setup with Assembler is within 2% of a ZCB, so a ZCB need not be bought.[^I1_FHkP4IV4-1017]

By June 2024 he states a Twisted bow plus Zaryte crossbow is the meta, with the Twisted bow replacing the blowpipe.[^rnLS-oZr3wU-243] Because Nex is weak to crossbows, an adamant crossbow does more than a Twisted bow on Nex, so a crossbow is always brought and the Twisted bow never camped; the Twisted bow is for minions and the crossbow for the boss.[^rnLS-oZr3wU-244][^rnLS-oZr3wU-244] An adamant crossbow beats a rune crossbow since its higher range lets players flinch out of Nex's close-range damage, though both fire rubies.[^rnLS-oZr3wU-308] The toxic blowpipe is second best for minions but poor against high defence.[^rnLS-oZr3wU-276] He tells people never to bring the Bow of Faerdhinen: it is worse than an adamant crossbow on Nex and worse than a blowpipe on minions.[^rnLS-oZr3wU-288]

### Special attacks and melee

* Voidwaker is a better special than the Claws/ZCB option[^vFVvnNJnyZQ-26], roughly 15% more efficient per spec than the ZCB (July 2023).[^I1_FHkP4IV4-986] By June 2024 he lists Voidwaker or ZCB as better than Fang, with the ZCB best in slot for spec.[^rnLS-oZr3wU-29] He typically uses Voidwaker on phase 2: one spec used to leave 100% energy and sit on it.[^vFVvnNJnyZQ-80]
* No good special weapon exists before ZCB or Voidwaker, so use Fang specs until then.[^I1_FHkP4IV4-935] The [Osmumten's fang](Osmumten's_fang.md) is about 35% better than a crossbow at Nex and also the fallback spec weapon.[^rnLS-oZr3wU-29] The Lightbearer is better with a good spec weapon, since Fang max hits matter little when it is so accurate.[^I1_FHkP4IV4-1138]
* Upgrade path (July 2023): Masori top and bottom first, then Voidwaker, then sell it for a ZCB, then sell the ZCB for a Twisted bow. Do not buy Dragon claws (about 90m) because claws are bad.[^I1_FHkP4IV4-935] In June 2024: Voidwaker (about 80m then) is the next major purchase and way better than Fang specs; sell it if a ZCB later becomes affordable, though it is a solid backup.[^rnLS-oZr3wU-148]
* Pharaoh's gloves give a huge strength bonus, so a bracers switch is best.[^rnLS-oZr3wU-244] Budget melee uses Ferocious gloves, Primordial boots and a regular dragon defender; the Avernic defender (about 50m) is the next upgrade.[^rnLS-oZr3wU-148] Do not bring a rune defender: the 5 stab attack bonus lost matters more than 1 strength.[^rnLS-oZr3wU-2158]

## Armour

| Setup | Notes |
|---|---|
| Karil's (budget duo, 2023) | Good mage defence and no negative melee stats, with the best melee gear otherwise, a Fang, a four-way range switch, an ACB and a pipe for minions and Reavers.[^I1_FHkP4IV4-0] Cannot work at the high end; without Masori or better you get shredded.[^I1_FHkP4IV4-1138] |
| Masori | First upgrade, the most important: better mage defence, a good ranged bonus and no negative melee, way better than Karil's.[^I1_FHkP4IV4-27] |
| Mixed hide (budget, 2024) | Lower max hit than Bandos but with a stab bonus, so better DPS at Nex; doubles as ranged armour and is extremely cheap.[^rnLS-oZr3wU-87] Without Masori, run Mixed hide; with it, camping Masori is slightly faster, and Mixed hide with a Masori switch is faster still as you improve.[^rnLS-oZr3wU-87] |
| Torva | Full Torva for duo Nex reduces a lot of damage; about the same supplies (two brews dropped), allowing melee on phase 2 and speeding it up dramatically.[^vFVvnNJnyZQ-54][^I1_FHkP4IV4-1017] The Torva helmet alone is a solid upgrade for defence and max hit.[^rnLS-oZr3wU-243] Extra potions are fine for learning rather than skipping full Torva.[^AzCKJvLDRxo-26] |

The cape swap and Masori helmet switch are not max hits, so a struggling setup can drop the switch, bring an extra potion and dump the bolts.[^rnLS-oZr3wU-119] Without a Quiver (no ammo slot), ditch the pegs (about 1% better than none, a fairly big DPS increase)[^AzCKJvLDRxo-0] and swap the boots; a boot switch is not necessary but very nice.[^AzCKJvLDRxo-114] In the budget setup he skips the Light bearer because only Fang specs are available, and skips Death Charge as the specs are bad anyway.[^I1_FHkP4IV4-27]

Always wear the Lightbearer at Nex: it is better DPS than any other ring, including an Ultor or Venator ring, even with Fang specs, and he would not recommend Nex until you own one.[^rnLS-oZr3wU-322] The Buckler gives about 5% DPS when ranging and is extremely good[^AzCKJvLDRxo-209], though in 2022 he saw it as not worth the inventory slot over a brew.[^Y2KvOrUXUeA-123]

## Shield

In 2022 he ranks Elysian over Spectral; with neither, run no shield.[^Y2KvOrUXUeA-123] The Elysian saves roughly two brew doses per kill; the Spectral saves a bit less but also prayer.[^Y2KvOrUXUeA-148] Whenever Nex targets him he camps the shield to prevent the damage, then returns to a Vernic; he flicks it whenever possible to lengthen trips, and supplies are shared by who was targeted most.[^Y2KvOrUXUeA-148] He hits Nex a tick early at the start so shield flicking is easy.[^Y2KvOrUXUeA-148] With a Spectral you can take the virus and camp it, reducing the prayer drain.[^Y2KvOrUXUeA-181]

## Altar and supplies

The altar gives full health, prayer, run energy and spec restore, but only with an ancient item equipped.[^rnLS-oZr3wU-211] Bring some Zaros item (such as ancient bracers) to use the Ultar altar: without one it restores only prayer. A ZCB counts.[^I1_FHkP4IV4-56] Zaryte vambraces (about 100m, previously about 180m) are recommended for this.[^rnLS-oZr3wU-178] If you leave without an ancient item equipped, wear the Zaryte item or you will be hit and killed.[^rnLS-oZr3wU-1619]

* Drink Menaphite remedy first: it restores stats every 15 seconds like an overload when brewed down, and cancels divine potions if drunk after them.[^I1_FHkP4IV4-90] A Super Combat minify potion is a nice pre-pot since it restores stats after brewing and on virus.[^AzCKJvLDRxo-268]
* Peak 2023 baseline: four Prayer restores (his setup saves about a dose; five or six when learning)[^vFVvnNJnyZQ-54], and seven brews (about eight when learning, dropping the Buckler).[^vFVvnNJnyZQ-80] In 2024 he brings five resource and eight brews, and says four resource potions are plenty for a normal kill.[^AzCKJvLDRxo-209] The second range potion is optional; ditch it for an extra brew or restore if struggling.[^AzCKJvLDRxo-209]
* Bring runes for Death Charge and thralls; thralls are a huge part of DPS because they hit through Nex's very high defence ([Thralls](Thralls.md)).[^rnLS-oZr3wU-276] Fire runes go in the pouch; Death Charge works on minions and Reavers and carries into the next kill.[^AzCKJvLDRxo-209]
* It is hard for a learner in low gear to find a Nex duo, since poor gear and poor Nex skill go together, though kills in bad gear are absolutely doable.[^rnLS-oZr3wU-2113]
* He ranks the ZCB with a nine-way (fast switching only mattering for Blood Sacrifice) and the Twisted bow with an eight-way for quick minion switches.[^AzCKJvLDRxo-114]
