# General Graardor

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2021-09-03 to 2022-03-19). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|

## Methods

- Gnomonkey's gear: full crystal armour, Bow of Faerdhinen, Anguish, Assembler (even with a max cape, as it always provides a max hit), Unholy blessing for Zamorak protection and Bandos bracers for Bandos protection, plus Pegasian boots. ([2021-09-03 1:27](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=87))
- Inventory: a Kodai wand and ancestral robe top to Blood Barrage HP back between kills, a blowpipe loaded with dragon darts for minions and HP specs, a cannon base, three anglerfish, seven Divine bastion potions, one stamina and seven restores. ([2021-09-03 2:26](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=146))
- To enter, drop a restore potion inside the door, pick up the hammer and go through, drop another restore on the other side, go back for the first, go through one last time dropping the hammer and taking your full supplies. ([2021-09-03 3:35](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=215))
- He also brings a rune pouch with soul, death and blood runes for Blood Barrage; bones to peaches tablets are not really needed and another restore can be taken instead for longer trips. ([2021-09-03 3:48](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=228))
- The orange marker is where the cannon base is placed to perform red clicks, and each tile is a checkpoint 'like Mario Kart'; the cycle starts at the green marker between kills with two shots at Graardor. ([2021-09-03 4:54](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=294))
- Cycle: fire twice at Graardor from the green marker, run to checkpoint one and fire again, then checkpoints two to five firing once each, and at checkpoint six fire and then click the cannon base. ([2021-09-03 4:54](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=294))
- As soon as your true tile is over checkpoint seven, fire again, then move to eight and nine firing each time, then loop back to checkpoint one until Graardor is dead. ([2021-09-03 5:20](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=320))
- Before entering the room, sip a Divine bastion, hit the imbued heart, turn on Preserve, and use Protect from Melee and Rigor; the private instance pulls the coins directly from your bank if you have them unlocked. ([2021-09-03 6:21](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=381))
- On entering, fire at Graardor and let him walk closer, run through him and immediately set the cannon base on the orange tile; Gnomonkey says you will likely take at least two hits while setting up. ([2021-09-03 6:21](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=381))
- Once the cannon base is down, run to checkpoint nine, fire once, wait for Graardor to get closer, then run to checkpoint one and start the cycle; the move from nine to one is lenient so you are almost always fine. ([2021-09-03 6:48](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=408))
- After Graardor dies, turn run off, equip the max cape if you have it, and immediately switch to the blowpipe and kill the mage minion with specs and Rigor. ([2021-09-03 6:48](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=408))
- Stand near the ranged minion and Blood Barrage the melee and ranged minions, flicking both; if you hit 99 HP or there is excess food drops, pipe these two down to speed it up and save blood runes. ([2021-09-03 7:17](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=437))
- Gnomonkey's tank gear is absolute max tank gear: bulwark, full Justiciar, Infernal or Fire cape, Guardian boots, Barrows gloves, Ring of suffering, and Unholy blessing. ([2021-11-25 2:04](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=124))
- The tank inventory has Guthan's armour (absolutely necessary) and a Bandos godsword for extra DPS each kill, which Gnomonkey considers better than a Dragon warhammer. ([2021-11-25 2:32](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=152))
- The tank brings about 10 cheap food healing 22 and around 8 super restores, and uses the Arceuus spellbook for extra DPS withdrawals and Death Charge for additional Bandos godsword specs. ([2021-11-25 3:04](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=184))
- The rune pouch holds blood, cosmic and fire runes, with loose death and soul runes, plus Bones to Peaches tabs, and one of the two players must bring an Explorer's ring 4; on the standard spellbook, inventory space is freed for extra restores or food, and an imbued heart adds magic defence. ([2021-11-25 3:31](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=211))
- Gnomonkey's attacker uses Bow of Faerdhinen with Crystal armour, an Amulet of anguish, and the Max cape (Ava's assembler does not give a max hit with the Bow of Faerdhinen), though a Prayer or Hitpoints cape also works. ([2021-11-25 3:39](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=219))
- The attacker inventory has Guthan's (absolutely necessary), six Divine bastion potions and six super combats to pot share to both players, a blowpipe filled with dragon darts (for killing minions and special-attack healing), two emergency anglerfish and five super restores. ([2021-11-25 4:29](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=269))
- When Bandos dies, both players attack the mage minion; both keep praying Piety until it dies, and whoever the mage attacks prays Protect from Magic until it goes down. ([2021-11-25 7:30](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=450))

## Requirements

- Gnomonkey puts 90 Ranged as the bare minimum for solo Bandos with the Bofa and 99 as ideal because it provides a lot of max hits. ([2021-09-03 0:57](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=57))
- He puts 80 Prayer as the minimum, since higher Prayer lengthens trips, and 80 Agility as the minimum, because high Agility means less run restoration (close to no stamina potions at 99 Agility). ([2021-09-03 0:57](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=57))
- Gnomonkey highly recommends at least the hard combat diary for private instances, as Bandos is extremely packed and teams can crash you on a public world. ([2021-09-03 1:27](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=87))
- Song of the Elves must be completed to make crystal equipment. ([2021-09-03 1:27](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=87))
- The Bandos tank wants 90+ combat stats, focusing on Defence and Strength as high as possible, and over 80 Prayer to lengthen trips. ([2021-11-25 1:25](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=85))
- For the attacker, Gnomonkey wants 90+ Ranged and 80+ Defence for the ranged setup (or 90+ combat stats for melee), 80+ Prayer, at least 84 Magic for Pot Share, and 93 Magic for Vengeance Other; Explorer's ring 4 is helpful but only one player needs it. ([2021-11-25 1:30](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=90))

## Stats and numbers

- Graardor does not have the 200 Magic of the other bosses, so a twisted bow does not work for soloing him, according to Gnomonkey. ([2021-09-03 0:29](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=29))
- Because you red-clicked to path to the cannon base, Graardor is stuck in place while you run through him, so you always safely run straight through him. ([2021-09-03 5:20](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=320))
- The cannon must be picked up and put back down before 20 minutes or it will despawn; if it despawns or you are forced to teleport out, you can get a replacement from Nulodion. ([2021-09-03 7:43](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=463))
- Average Bandos trips last from 25 to as many as 75 kills, and trips last as long as you get restore drops for. ([2021-09-03 8:15](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=495))
- Per the wiki, a Bandos kill is worth about 145k GP on average including the minions, which at 25 kills per hour gives about 3,625,000 GP per hour; Gnomonkey rounds this down to 3.5m for gearing and kill count. ([2021-09-03 8:15](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=495))
- Trips are limited to two and a half hours at most by divine potion doses, but you usually run out of restores at the one to two hour mark. ([2021-09-03 8:15](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=495))
- Without uniques Bandos is only worth about 24k a kill, so you really need the Bandos chestplate and tassets drops to make money here. ([2021-09-03 8:41](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=521))
- In the setup shown, Bandos trips can last up to two hours depending on food and restore drops. ([2021-11-25 0:28](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=28))
- At the time of the video (November 2021), a Bandos kill was worth about 180k gp on average, up from 150k when Gnomonkey made his solo Bandos guide a month or so earlier; later in the video he quotes 184,982 gp per kill. ([2021-11-25 0:28](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=28))
- With max gear and the maximum of 30 kills per hour, Gnomonkey says about 5.4m gp/hr total is possible (later quoted as just over 5.5m), or just shy of 3m gp/hr (about 2.75m) each when splitting with a partner. ([2021-11-25 0:58](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=58))
- Graardor has a unique mechanic where if his attack lands it is guaranteed to hit at least 15 and up to 35, but it can still be blocked for a zero, so the tank setup helps dramatically. ([2021-11-25 6:46](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=406))
- Bandos' melee attack maxes at 60 and shreds even tank armour, so the tank needs Protect from Melee; as a rule of thumb the tank eats if their health drops below 40. ([2021-11-25 7:15](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=435))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says this is also one of the best methods to unlock the solo Bandos combat achievements at the higher tiers. ([2021-09-03 0:29](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=29))
- An Explorer's ring 4 allows high alchemy and extra run energy restoration, so it is ideal. ([2021-09-03 0:57](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=57))
- You can optionally wear Devout boots instead for the prayer bonus, and the Ring of suffering is a good option to increase defence and prayer bonus to lengthen trips. ([2021-09-03 1:53](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=113))
- A Ring of endurance is a decent option if you do not have high Agility but is unnecessary at 99. ([2021-09-03 1:53](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=113))
- The ancestral top is 4% better DPS than an occult necklace on the minions because you need some accuracy; without the top bring an occult necklace instead, and the Kodai can be swapped for a nightmare staff or master wand. ([2021-09-03 2:26](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=146))
- The Explorer's ring is for the many rune items Graardor drops and for restoring run energy; the imbued heart is purely for mage defence against the mage minion and is not necessary. ([2021-09-03 2:54](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=174))
- Gnomonkey brings a hilt that is the hard combat achievements reward (transcribed as 'Gomel's Hilt') to teleport to God Wars, swappable for a Trollheim tab, and the max cape for HP and run restoration between kills, the holy wrench effect and teleporting out. ([2021-09-03 3:21](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=201))
- Graardor is the fastest kill count of the whole God Wars Dungeon, and faster still with the Bofa setup: hop to a free world, run around the main area tagging every goblin, then kill goblins near the door until a hammer drops. ([2021-09-03 3:35](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=215))
- Gnomonkey recommends enabling 'highlight true tile' in the Tile Indicators plugin, turning on Boss Timers and marking General Graardor in NPC Indicators, and importing his tile markers into RuneLite via the world map import option. ([2021-09-03 4:22](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=262))
- If you lose a tick, move to the next checkpoint and pray melee until Graardor falls back into the cycle, which usually takes a couple of checkpoints and always fixes itself. ([2021-09-03 5:20](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=320))
- If you mess up, Gnomonkey often runs immediately to checkpoint six and through him again with the cannon, or runs up to checkpoint nine and waits for him to approach before going to checkpoint one. ([2021-09-03 5:52](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=352))
- To eat mid-fight, eat, run to the next checkpoint, click Graardor as if firing the bow, then move to the next checkpoint; the grey tiles are optional clicks that save some run energy. ([2021-09-03 5:52](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=352))
- Switch to Protect from Range when it is safe, as the ranged minion hits the hardest. ([2021-09-03 6:48](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=408))
- Try to flick the minions' attacks, which reduces the blood runes needed to restore HP and guarantees you return to 99 HP; damage is calculated as soon as the animation starts on the minion. ([2021-09-03 7:17](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=437))
- Gnomonkey says flicking is a useful skill to learn for the Inferno and recommends practising in a safe environment like God Wars. ([2021-09-03 7:17](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=437))
- Between kills, juggle extra restore drops if you cannot fit them, keep run energy off when possible so it stays high, pick up drops, use the altar for prayer if not on cooldown, and walk to the green start tile with run on for the next kill. ([2021-09-03 7:43](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=463))
- Gnomonkey says if you watch this in the future, take the wiki's value per kill and multiply it by 25 to get GP per hour. ([2021-09-03 8:15](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=495))
- If you run the Bandos duo (dolo) with your own alt, the gp/hr is insane because you keep the full amount, and Gnomonkey says it is the time to "grant out" a pet drop if you have an alt and want it. ([2021-11-25 0:58](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=58))
- The tank is ideally on the Arceuus spellbook with 80 Magic for thralls and Death Charge, though they can bring alchs instead; higher Magic helps tank the magic minion, which is where about half of the damage taken comes from. ([2021-11-25 2:04](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=124))
- The tank can optionally swap the Ring of suffering for a Berserker ring, which gives a higher max hit but only when the tank is max potted, so Gnomonkey recommends the Ring of suffering for its defence and prayer bonus. ([2021-11-25 2:32](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=152))
- If neither player has an Explorer's ring 4 or the Arceuus spellbook, bring the standard spellbook with alchs, optionally with lava runes for Bones to Peaches, otherwise bring tablets; the Arceuus spellbook requires bringing the Book of the Dead. ([2021-11-25 3:04](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=184))
- Other attacker gear mentioned: Boots such as Pegasian boots (as heard in the auto-transcript) or blessed dragonhide-style boots are fine, a Ring of suffering or Ring of the gods works, and Zamorak bracers and War blessing or vice versa. ([2021-11-25 3:59](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=239))
- If you cannot afford the Bow of Faerdhinen, Gnomonkey recommends max melee with a rapier or Abyssal tentacle, which is far better DPS than a blowpipe; replace ranged potions with more food and restores, and bring your own Bandos godsword in place of the blowpipe. ([2021-11-25 4:29](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=269))
- The attacker must be on the Lunar spellbook with a rune pouch of mud, death and astral runes to Pot Share and cast Vengeance Other on the tank; without the Magic level for Vengeance, bring earth, astral and water runes instead. Pick up the blowpipe darts after minions unless you have a Max cape. ([2021-11-25 4:56](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=296))
- Before entering the boss room, both players pray Preserve; the attacker pot shares a super combat, and the tank enters the room first, immediately specs Bandos with the Bandos godsword and starts attacking with the bulwark. ([2021-11-25 6:27](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=387))
- Once the tank has attacked Bandos, the attacker swaps in a Divine bastion potion, enters, prays Rigor and Protect from Ranged and attacks immediately; with a melee setup the attacker prays Piety and Protect from Ranged. ([2021-11-25 6:46](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=406))
- Gnomonkey says the duo method is doable on an ultimate ironman, since the tank and main take low enough damage to be manageable. ([2021-11-25 7:15](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=435))
- If one player is at full health and the other is low, stop attacking so the other can heal with Guthan's; if both are at max HP, switch to DPS gear and kill the remaining minions, with the attacker able to dump blowpipe specs on them if using the Bow of Faerdhinen setup. ([2021-11-25 7:41](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=461))
- If you cannot heal to full with Guthan's, pick up bones and use Bones to Peaches on them; check the melee minion's kebab drops too, as they heal more than expected. The player with the Explorer's ring alchs drops and collects the coins, and both cram food and restore drops when possible. ([2021-11-25 7:41](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=461))
- If either player needs prayer the attacker pot shares restores; if the tank's Strength drops to 116 or lower the attacker pot shares another super combat, and re-pots Divine bastion when it runs out. ([2021-11-25 8:13](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=493))
- Sit next to Graardor's respawn point with the attacker a decent way away, and make sure the tank attacks him first when he respawns, otherwise he gets stuck on the attacker; if that happens, run around the room praying Melee while the tank hits him until he switches aggro. ([2021-11-25 8:13](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=493))
- To calculate future gp/hr, check the OSRS Wiki's Bandos kill value and multiply it by your kills per hour, which is 30 at most. ([2021-11-25 8:42](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=522))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says Bandos (General Graardor) has always been one of the harder God Wars Dungeon bosses to solo: maging him is really bad DPS and 6:0 with an armadyl crossbow is probably even worse. ([2021-09-03 0:00](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg))
- Gnomonkey says not to imbue the Bow of Faerdhinen under most circumstances because it takes 2,000 crystal shards, equivalent to 200,000 shots to break even and 20m GP in shards, and you are stuck with a bow that becomes relatively useless once you get a twisted bow. ([2021-09-03 1:53](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=113))
- Gnomonkey says General Graardor (Bandos) hits like a truck, so it is fairly common to duo him with a tank pulling his aggro. ([2021-11-25 0:28](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=28))
- Gnomonkey says the Amulet of blood fury is very important for this setup because it keeps health high during the kill, and that Bandos armour could probably be used instead of Justiciar but is inferior in max hit and dramatically worse in defence; he highly recommends full Justiciar. ([2021-11-25 2:32](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=152))
- Gnomonkey says the Bow of Faerdhinen is by far the best DPS at Bandos because of his high defence; a toxic blowpipe is still over 30% worse DPS and causes the attacker to be targeted by minions more often due to weird minion mechanics. ([2021-11-25 3:59](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=239))
- Gnomonkey calls the solo melee method at General Graardor, with shield flicking and going under the boss to set up minion patterns so you can flick everything, crazy skilled and very cool to see on an Ironman. ([2022-03-19 22:25](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1345))
- Gnomonkey also likes the Bova (9-0 / 7-0) method that involves clicking around the room constantly, and says duo or DOLO is just hitting the boss, which is boring. ([2022-03-19 22:54](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1374))
- Gnomonkey places General Graardor in lower A tier because the shield method makes the boss transcend B tier, saying methods change how interesting a boss is. ([2022-03-19 23:21](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1401))

## Records

- With the Bow of Faerdhinen and crystal armour, Gnomonkey says you can get kills per hour similar to the other God Wars bosses with a twisted bow, easily hitting 25 kills per hour at 99 Ranged in max gear. ([2021-09-03 0:29](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=29))

## Trivia

- It is a nine-attack cycle taking zero hits from Graardor, which is why the method is called 9:0. ([2021-09-03 5:20](https://youtu.be/YBKT-_USIQg?t=320))
