**Rejuvenation** is an other topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2023-03-07 and 2023-04-19. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Rejuvenation regenerates 1 HP every 20 seconds while active, with a hefty drain rate attached according to Jagex so it is not worth leaving on forever.[^trol59VccdE-56]
* Rejuvenation is the same as Rapid Heal with some odd differences; Gnomonkey says it is still not worth using for health because of an insane drain rate even with the faster regeneration.[^8VyPkG4sEwQ-105]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the new prayer book needs Rapid Heal to reset your regen cooldown, but a Rapid Heal with a high drain rate is as useless as the old one; he does not mind it being not particularly useful or interesting since it is a necessary function.[^trol59VccdE-56]
* Gnomonkey would prefer Rejuvenation have the same drain rate as Rapid Heal, since the book already has loads of trade-offs.[^trol59VccdE-82]
* Gnomonkey asks Jagex to make Rejuvenation the same as Rapid Heal.[^8VyPkG4sEwQ-105]

## Events

* The only thing Rejuvenation changed was Tyrope-skip setups, and he does not know why it differs from Rapid Heal at all.[^8VyPkG4sEwQ-105]
