# Rejuvenation

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2023-03-07 to 2023-04-19). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | other |

## Stats and numbers

- Rejuvenation regenerates 1 HP every 20 seconds while active, with a hefty drain rate attached according to Jagex so it is not worth leaving on forever. ([2023-03-07 0:56](https://youtu.be/trol59VccdE?t=56))
- Rejuvenation is the same as Rapid Heal with some odd differences; Gnomonkey says it is still not worth using for health because of an insane drain rate even with the faster regeneration. ([2023-04-19 1:45](https://youtu.be/8VyPkG4sEwQ?t=105))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the new prayer book needs Rapid Heal to reset your regen cooldown, but a Rapid Heal with a high drain rate is as useless as the old one; he does not mind it being not particularly useful or interesting since it is a necessary function. ([2023-03-07 0:56](https://youtu.be/trol59VccdE?t=56))
- Gnomonkey would prefer Rejuvenation have the same drain rate as Rapid Heal, since the book already has loads of trade-offs. ([2023-03-07 1:22](https://youtu.be/trol59VccdE?t=82))
- Gnomonkey asks Jagex to make Rejuvenation the same as Rapid Heal. ([2023-04-19 1:45](https://youtu.be/8VyPkG4sEwQ?t=105))

## Events

- Gnomonkey says the only thing Rejuvenation changed was Tyrope-skip setups, and he does not know why it differs from Rapid Heal at all. ([2023-04-19 1:45](https://youtu.be/8VyPkG4sEwQ?t=105))
