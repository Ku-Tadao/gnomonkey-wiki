**Monofly** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2025-05-18. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* As with Donofly, you must reach Yama on tick three after the Judge, so if you spawn close to Yama you delay one tick on a tile first.[^AyPfaq5Kvnk-0]

## Methods

* In Monofly, as soon as your true tile reaches a tile you click the boss; the cycle goes tile one, two, three, four, five, each followed by a hit, so it is a five-hit cycle.[^AyPfaq5Kvnk-58]

## Tips

* Because the hit on tile two is a five-tick hit anyway, Gnomonkey throws in a scythe swing there for slightly more damage than a five-tick Emberlight.[^AyPfaq5Kvnk-87]
* In Monofly you can throw in a burning claw spec any time you would swing Emberlight for bonus DPS.[^AyPfaq5Kvnk-263]

## Events

* Monofly is a superior version of the Donofly Yama melee tile method, named after Mono who found it.[^AyPfaq5Kvnk-0]
* Per the description, Mono also came up with a slightly different walk method and a tile-set pastebin for Monofly was shared.[^AyPfaq5Kvnk-0]

## Trivia

* In Monofly you never have to delay on any tile and go purely off your true tile, which Gnomonkey says makes it easier than Donofly in every way.[^AyPfaq5Kvnk-0]
* Per the description, Monofly works in both duo and solo; in duo you simply Monofly because you outDPS the set of orbs and do not need to deal with them.[^AyPfaq5Kvnk-0]
* In the Monofly cycle you lose one tick from tile two to three because you must use a five-tick hit there or get meleed.[^AyPfaq5Kvnk-87]
* After running through the waves and reaching tile five the next cycle, the orbs spawn and you exit the cycle to kill them and mage the boss.[^AyPfaq5Kvnk-87]
