## Methods

- In Monofly, as soon as your true tile reaches a tile you click the boss; the cycle goes tile one, two, three, four, five, each followed by a hit, so it is a five-hit cycle. ([2025-05-18 0:58](https://youtu.be/AyPfaq5Kvnk?t=58))

## Requirements

- As with Donofly, you must reach Yama on tick three after the Judge, so if you spawn close to Yama you delay one tick on a tile first. ([2025-05-18 0:00](https://youtu.be/AyPfaq5Kvnk))

## Tips

- Because the hit on tile two is a five-tick hit anyway, Gnomonkey throws in a scythe swing there for slightly more damage than a five-tick Emberlight. ([2025-05-18 1:27](https://youtu.be/AyPfaq5Kvnk?t=87))
- In Monofly you can throw in a burning claw spec any time you would swing Emberlight for bonus DPS. ([2025-05-18 4:23](https://youtu.be/AyPfaq5Kvnk?t=263))

## Events

- Monofly is a superior version of the Donofly Yama melee tile method, named after Mono who found it. ([2025-05-18 0:00](https://youtu.be/AyPfaq5Kvnk))
- Per the description, Mono also came up with a slightly different walk method and a tile-set pastebin for Monofly was shared. ([2025-05-18 0:00](https://youtu.be/AyPfaq5Kvnk))

## Trivia

- In Monofly you never have to delay on any tile and go purely off your true tile, which Gnomonkey says makes it easier than Donofly in every way. ([2025-05-18 0:00](https://youtu.be/AyPfaq5Kvnk))
- Per the description, Monofly works in both duo and solo; in duo you simply Monofly because you outDPS the set of orbs and do not need to deal with them. ([2025-05-18 0:00](https://youtu.be/AyPfaq5Kvnk))
- In the Monofly cycle you lose one tick from tile two to three because you must use a five-tick hit there or get meleed. ([2025-05-18 1:27](https://youtu.be/AyPfaq5Kvnk?t=87))
- After running through the waves and reaching tile five the next cycle, the orbs spawn and you exit the cycle to kill them and mage the boss. ([2025-05-18 1:27](https://youtu.be/AyPfaq5Kvnk?t=87))
