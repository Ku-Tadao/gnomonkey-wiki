## Requirements

- In the Clue Scroll Raider challenge, all gear must come from clues as an item; items from implings or the Grand Exchange are not allowed, though implings can be opened to obtain clues. ([2023-10-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/N7UM0bqdcU4))
- The challenge has a three-hour timer to complete clues; after that no more clues may be completed. Supplies are completely allowed. ([2023-10-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/N7UM0bqdcU4))
- Ammo is allowed if it drops from clues, but Gnomonkey allowed amethyst arrows because no arrows drop from any clue. ([2023-10-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/N7UM0bqdcU4))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey wanted the following from elite clues: DDS, rune crossbow, dragon mace/longsword, rune armour again and dragonstone jewellery. ([2023-10-09 0:32](https://youtu.be/N7UM0bqdcU4?t=32))
- Gnomonkey notes there are no magic robes or weapons from any tier of clue besides the Third Age wand, none of which is useful for him, and he cannot use supplies from clues to make things, he needs the item itself. ([2023-10-09 0:59](https://youtu.be/N7UM0bqdcU4?t=59))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says he did not do 'dragon' clues because he is poor, saying that is 20 mil a clue. ([2023-10-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/N7UM0bqdcU4))
- Gnomonkey determined that elite clues are the most useful tier to complete because of all the weapons, while hard clues offer bows, rune armour, black dragonhide and a rune pickaxe, and master clues offer dragon weaponry. ([2023-10-09 0:32](https://youtu.be/N7UM0bqdcU4?t=32))

## Records

- Gnomonkey's final clue gear: a dragon mace as main weapon, a dragon longsword as a separate spec option, a rune kiteshield, a Bandos plate skirt (one prayer bonus), a rune pickaxe for Guardians, a dragonstone bracelet he allowed to be turned into a combat bracelet because he also got charge-jewellery scrolls, blessed dragonhide boots, a magic shortbow and a full set of black dragonhide. ([2023-10-09 2:25](https://youtu.be/N7UM0bqdcU4?t=145))

## Events

- For the SubTember challenge, Gnomonkey was challenged to complete raids using only Clue Scroll gear, which had to be obtained within 3 hours from his own clue rewards. ([2023-10-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/N7UM0bqdcU4))
- Gnomonkey says the farmer's hat step for Elite clues was impossible because he does not own the hat, and he did not end up getting that step. ([2023-10-09 0:59](https://youtu.be/N7UM0bqdcU4?t=59))
- Per the video description, the Master casket dropping dragon longswords and later picking up a dragon mace helped, as did the magic shortbow and the full set of dragonhide pieces he got. ([2023-10-09 2:25](https://youtu.be/N7UM0bqdcU4?t=145))

## Trivia

- Gnomonkey's chat pointed out that crystal dust cannot be obtained from clues, so the Chambers run technically breaks the rules, and he agreed. ([2023-10-09 11:53](https://youtu.be/N7UM0bqdcU4?t=713))
