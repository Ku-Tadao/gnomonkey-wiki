# Tombs of Amascut

Tombs of Amascut (Raids 3, "ToA") is the third raid, built around scalable difficulty through [invocations](Invocations_(Tombs_of_Amascut).md). Gnomonkey covers it from its 2022 release onward: first as a hopeful critic of its rewards, then as a speed-runner and teacher of 300, 400 and 500 raid-level setups, and later as a critic of its depth and loot. His position shifted after the May 2025 [Summer Sweep-up](Update_Summer_Sweep-up.md), which he says made the raid much faster and easier.

## Concept and release

Raids 3 adds a difficulty system of invocations: negative modifiers chosen before the raid that make it harder but more rewarding ([2022-06-03 1:00](https://youtu.be/g-y4YBfOfaU?t=60)). Jagex said the base raid with no invocations would likely be easier than [Chambers of Xeric](Chambers_of_Xeric.md) or [Theatre of Blood](Theatre_of_Blood.md) ([2022-06-03 1:34](https://youtu.be/g-y4YBfOfaU?t=94)). Gnomonkey was happy with about 80% of the rewards but disappointed by a few, mainly the [Heka of Tumeken](Heka_of_Tumeken.md) ([2022-02-26 0:00](https://youtu.be/uxwiO2KeF34?t=0)). In June 2022 he argued that rewards should scale exponentially with each invocation ([2022-06-03 11:20](https://youtu.be/g-y4YBfOfaU?t=680)), and feared a reward cap that would make a single set meta optimal ([2022-06-03 10:46](https://youtu.be/g-y4YBfOfaU?t=646)).

## Rooms and bosses

**Room order.** In the September 2022 solo 500 he went Akkha, Ba-Ba, Zebak, Kephri, calling Akkha the most RNG and Kephri a free room ([2022-09-13 0:00](https://youtu.be/h4hf5hPQHms?t=0)). In November 2022 he gave the speed-optimal order as Ba-Ba, Kephri, usually Zebak, then Akkha; doing Ba-Ba first lets him pre-pot every useful potion for the [Monkey Room](Monkey_Room.md), so no ranged potion or stamina is needed ([2022-11-18 2:13](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=133) [2022-11-18 3:15](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=195)). In May 2024 he explained that the melee rooms (Ba-Ba and Kephri) go first with a pre-pot because salts benefit melee least ([2024-05-30 8:35](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=515)). In June 2025 he says the order should always be Ba-Ba (after the Monkey Room), Kephri, Zebak, Akkha, with basically no exception except at very high raid levels ([2025-06-25 6:17](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=377)). In 2026 the last two rooms are described as flexible depending on room levels, and starting with Zebak is done only around raid level 600 with Pathmaster to avoid a level 4 Zebak; he does not recommend it otherwise ([2026-04-01 4:24](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=264) [2026-04-01 4:55](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=295)). A level 4 Zebak is a one-in-six chance that he calls bad luck rather than a reset, and Akkha can be done first if it occurs ([2024-07-01 9:00](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=540) [2024-07-01 10:00](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=600)).

**[Akkha](Akkha.md).** Butterfly Tech cut his average Akkha time from 3 minutes to 2.5 minutes over 150 solos, mainly by avoiding the Simon Says phase (see [Butterfly Tech](Butterfly_Tech.md)) ([2022-08-30 1:28](https://youtu.be/pSBxLCy7-44?t=88)). He notes that Akkha can combo for 270 damage on a mistake ([2023-07-30 16:40](https://youtu.be/iG23QZE9pds?t=1000)). He recommends the invocation that reduces the memory game to one tick between steps, calling it very free ([2025-10-04 6:35](https://youtu.be/X-PTHMq_10A?t=395)). A Tumeken's shadow makes Akkha about ten times easier ([2023-05-09 0:58](https://youtu.be/K7VALGO6md8?t=58)).

**[Ba-Ba](Ba-Ba.md).** After a rock-based special, Ba-Ba's next melee attack makes her swap target: to the lowest-HP player in a full team, or between players in a duo ([2024-03-02 56:03](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=3363)). Her phase thresholds are 66% and 33% of her HP ([2024-02-25 14:02](https://youtu.be/Z5ZONYTlS1w?t=842)).

**[Kephri](Kephri.md).** His 2022 efficiency route chins agile scarabs and skips skulls ([2022-11-18 17:59](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=1079)).

**[Zebak](Zebak.md).** He calls Zebak the hardest room, requiring adrenaline and salt ([2022-09-13 0:00](https://youtu.be/h4hf5hPQHms?t=0)). The thralls drain prayer, so only one should be tanked at a time ([2023-05-09 3:25](https://youtu.be/K7VALGO6md8?t=205)).

**Monkey Room.** In 2023 he said the Monkey Room is universally regarded as terrible ([2023-07-30 26:30](https://youtu.be/iG23QZE9pds?t=1590)); he calls it by far the longest puzzle room ([2024-04-17 17:48](https://youtu.be/Kzc2e6H8i7o?t=1068)). [Project Rebalance](Update_Project_Rebalance.md) (April 2024) raised the health of style-based enemies there so they always take max damage from the opposing style ([2024-04-17 17:48](https://youtu.be/Kzc2e6H8i7o?t=1068)); Jagex then clarified that baboon shamans, volatile baboons and cursed baboons would not be changed in that way ([2024-04-18 13:23](https://youtu.be/P5yHDSocecY?t=803)). By June 2024 he says monkeys have 10 health and shamans 16, so with hybrid gear he runs up and one-shots everything ([2024-06-27 3:42](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=222)). He acknowledges the argument that the room is now zero skill but says Jagex lacked the resources to make it a good room ([2024-06-27 22:55](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=1375)).

**[The Wardens](The_Wardens.md).** The Warden is by far the best point source ([2022-11-18 0:00](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=0)) and the majority of the raid's points, so dying there is the worst death; he and Sardaco settled on about 20% of points lost (he first said about a third) ([2025-10-04 53:48](https://youtu.be/X-PTHMq_10A?t=3228)). If three skulls are not tanked the wrong Warden appears, and for solos he suggests tanking about four ([2024-03-02 89:02](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=5342)). Most restores go to the Wardens because of the number of specs ([2024-03-02 97:11](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=5831)). In phase 1 he tick-eats the smaller orb because it does more damage ([2023-06-30 45:50](https://youtu.be/cmLBaxdoHpQ?t=2750)). He strongly recommends hiding thralls because they hide ground indicators ([2024-02-25 15:37](https://youtu.be/Z5ZONYTlS1w?t=937)).

## Invocations and raid level

His September 2022 tier list ranks invocations by raid level given, usefulness for speed and whether they slow the raid ([2022-09-25 17:00](https://youtu.be/a9V3c9mDf9E?t=1020)). Feeling Special is the best, Insanity second; Medic is the worst, Quiet Prayers second worst and Overly Draining third worst ([2022-09-25 46:30](https://youtu.be/a9V3c9mDf9E?t=2790) [2022-09-25 58:19](https://youtu.be/a9V3c9mDf9E?t=3499)).

For a 500 in April 2023 he treats Hardcore, No Help Needed, Walk the Path and Pathfinder as givens, runs Dehydration, and calls Overly Draining trolling ([2023-04-25 0:00](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=0) [2023-04-25 0:26](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=26) [2023-04-25 44:32](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=2672)). He keeps every boss invocation including Ancient Haste ([2023-04-25 1:26](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=86)), and calls Double Trouble the most valuable room invocation at 20 points ([2023-04-25 45:38](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=2738)). His no-Keris 500 (June 2023) has Dehydration off and excludes Pathmaster and Medic ([2023-06-30 0:30](https://youtu.be/cmLBaxdoHpQ?t=30) [2023-06-30 45:21](https://youtu.be/cmLBaxdoHpQ?t=2721)). In May 2024 he likes Pathfinder, Overly Draining off and Overlords as an option, though he personally prefers Overlords off ([2024-05-30 4:04](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=244)); in June 2025 with a Keris he would turn Dehydration on and Overly Draining off ([2025-06-27 47:08](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=2828)). See [On a Diet / Dehydration / Overly Draining](On_a_Diet_Dehydration_Overly_Draining.md).

Raid level dramatically increases GP/hr ([2022-11-18 0:29](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=29)). In October 2024 he says raising raid level is always worth it while the raid is still completable, which stops being true above roughly 500 ([2024-10-24 42:43](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=2563)), and advises adding 25 at a time ([2024-10-24 42:43](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=2563)). Loot does not increase beyond raid level 600 ([2026-05-07 15:10](https://youtu.be/76_3oQhlW8I?t=910)). In 2023 he complained that raising raid level only buffs enemies, and that for a while it was not worth going above 150 for loot ([2023-07-30 16:13](https://youtu.be/iG23QZE9pds?t=973)). A bug once allowed raid levels up to 770 ([Bug abuse](Bug_abuse.md)) ([2026-05-07 14:49](https://youtu.be/76_3oQhlW8I?t=889)).

## Rewards and purple chance

Gnomonkey's stated figures vary by video:

- Leagues, 1000 invocations (2023): 24.5% unique chance ([2023-11-27 21:34](https://youtu.be/kfmhAeg0nEM?t=1294)). May 2024, 500 raid: 14% purple chance ([2024-05-30 26:30](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=1590)).
- July 2024: 4.2% purple chance in a 300 expert raid, 0.17% of those a [Tumeken's shadow](Tumeken's_shadow.md), about one shadow per 565 deathless 300 raids ([2024-07-07 7:44](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=464)).
- October 2024: roughly 1 in 20 at 300 and 1 in 9 to 1 in 11 at 400, not to be quoted exactly ([2024-10-24 43:39](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=2619)). October 2025: about 1 in 21 at 300, with [Osmumten's fang](Osmumten's_fang.md) and [Lightbearer](Lightbearer.md) the most common drops ([2025-10-04 52:18](https://youtu.be/X-PTHMq_10A?t=3138)).

He says purple values dropped about 90% except the shadow ([2024-07-07 7:44](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=464)), and that the only valuable drop is the shadow ([2025-05-30 7:22](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=442)). He argued for a nerf because the fang price (around 9 million) was ridiculously low ([2025-05-29 13:36](https://youtu.be/9ugDmmDILoI?t=816) [2025-05-29 13:48](https://youtu.be/9ugDmmDILoI?t=828) [2025-05-30 6:53](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=413)) (see [Opinion: Toa loot nerf](Opinion_Toa_loot_nerf.md)). After the Summer Sweep-up, purple chances were nerfed only above raid level 300 ([2025-06-25 0:00](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=0)).

On GP/hr: in November 2022 an average 510 raid gave just under 16M, at 1.5 raids an hour or about 24M GP/hr ([2022-11-18 17:59](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=1079)); in July 2024 a 500 makes approximately 20 to 25M GP/hr and ToA is the best solo raid for money ([2024-07-07 8:12](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=492)). In 2023 he said [Nex](Nex.md) was the best GP/hr with ToA second ([2023-07-30 17:30](https://youtu.be/iG23QZE9pds?t=1050)), and in July 2024 that Nex gives better money and a faster return on investment ([2024-07-01 7:42](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=462)). He recommends ToA as the next best option after Nex ([2024-07-07 7:44](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=464)).

## Gear and supplies

**Budget 500.** The setup cost 424M in May 2023 (47 minutes, third attempt) ([2023-05-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/K7VALGO6md8?t=0) [2023-05-09 48:23](https://youtu.be/K7VALGO6md8?t=2903) [2023-05-09 50:06](https://youtu.be/K7VALGO6md8?t=3006)). In May 2024, after the Monkey Room update, he called [Mixed hide](Mixed_hide_armour.md) the meta for a 500 ([2024-05-30 0:00](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=0)); in June 2024 he said that after running calcs it is not good for a budget 500 unless one has a Twisted bow, since with a dragon crossbow it was about 25% worse than a [Bow of Faerdhinen](Bow_of_Faerdhinen.md) ([2024-06-27 0:00](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=0)). That budget setup cost 287M ([2024-06-27 1:06](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=66)). After the Summer Sweep-up his no-Keris 500 setup costs about 280M without the rune pouch ([2025-06-27 0:00](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=0)). See [Tombs of Amascut gear setup](Tombs_of_Amascut_gear_setup.md).

**Atlatl.** In February 2025 his Gearscape calcs gave [Atlatl](Atlatl.md) DPS of 5.69 against 6.81 for the Bofa (16% less without burn, about 6% with it) ([2025-02-22 8:41](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=521)). He concludes ToA can be done with an Atlatl rather than saving about 250M for a Bofa, with a raid setup around 50M ([2025-02-22 21:58](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=1318)).

**Shadow setup.** His shadow rebuild melees Ba-Ba and Kephri, uses the shadow on Zebak, Akkha and the Wardens, and brings a Bofa one-way for Wardens phase 2 ([2024-07-01 0:00](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=0)); he brought 13 Sanfew serums and used three ([2024-07-01 0:00](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=0)). In July 2025 he says a shadow setup needs no flicking ([2025-07-06 41:34](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=2494)). See [Shadow rebuild setup](Shadow_rebuild_setup.md).

**300 raids.** In October 2024 he completed a 300 in about 40 minutes in about 70M of gear ([2024-10-24 42:22](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=2542)). After the Summer Sweep-up he estimates a clean 300 is 10 to 15 minutes faster than before ([2025-06-25 58:27](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=3507)) and says 300s are very easy compared to before ([2025-06-25 58:27](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=3507)). His ironman guide recommends staying at around 300 until an Osmumten's fang drops ([2025-06-28 40:25](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=2425)), and he always picks Power over Chaos at the path screens ([2026-04-01 24:26](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=1466) [2026-04-01 46:01](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=2761)).

**Supplies and consumables.** He pre-pots with a Chugging barrel ([2025-06-25 6:53](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=413)). Divine potions keep damage high ([2025-10-04 9:04](https://youtu.be/X-PTHMq_10A?t=544)), and salts give unlimited run energy ([2025-10-04 29:08](https://youtu.be/X-PTHMq_10A?t=1748)). Drain defence first with a [Bone dagger](Bone_dagger.md) or [Bandos godsword](Bandos_godsword.md); a warhammer is poor on Baba and Zebak ([2025-02-22 12:53](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=773) [2025-06-28 42:58](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=2578)). A shadow's accuracy uses magic level, so he drains with a Seercull ([2025-07-06 43:11](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=2591)). See [Tombs of Amascut supplies](Tombs_of_Amascut_supplies.md).

**Plugins.** The ToA [plugin](RuneLite_plugins.md) solves puzzles before Kephri and shows baboon wave information ([2024-02-25 13:18](https://youtu.be/Z5ZONYTlS1w?t=798) [2024-02-25 13:40](https://youtu.be/Z5ZONYTlS1w?t=820)).

## Updates and changes

- April 2024: Project Rebalance changed the Monkey Room and cut Baba's damage through protect from melee to 25% from 33%, which he called basically a non-change at high raid levels ([2024-04-17 17:48](https://youtu.be/Kzc2e6H8i7o?t=1068) [2024-04-17 18:30](https://youtu.be/Kzc2e6H8i7o?t=1110)).
- January 2025: the water jugs were fixed so they can be hit with magic ([2025-01-08 13:20](https://youtu.be/fLx3y5MTVjw?t=800)).
- May 2025: the Summer Sweep-up removed Ba-Ba's chip damage, reduced Akkha's chip damage and reworked Stay Vigilant, made Kephri's scarabs always move, made Zebak's waves not push but deal 30% more damage, stopped skull bombs landing under the Warden, and shortened the Monkey Room further ([2025-05-30 4:59](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=299) [2025-05-30 5:26](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=326) [2025-05-30 5:54](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=354) [2025-05-30 6:24](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=384) [2025-05-30 6:53](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=413)). Gnomonkey said it removes only unfair mechanics and one-shots ([2025-05-30 16:57](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=1017)). Afterwards he says 500s are much easier ([2025-06-27 0:00](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=0)).
- June 2026: the lifesteal restriction against the Wardens was removed ([2026-06-04 19:06](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1146)).

## Gnomonkey's opinion

In July 2023 he said he had been incredibly hopeful for ToA, hoping invocations would give veterans a challenge and others an accessible raid, but instead considered it the mid-level raid ([2023-07-30 15:44](https://youtu.be/iG23QZE9pds?t=944)); he had cataloged over 50 bugs and flaws ([2023-07-30 16:40](https://youtu.be/iG23QZE9pds?t=1000)) (see [Opinion: Tombs of Amascut lacks depth](Opinion_Tombs_of_Amascut_lacks_depth.md)). In March 2024 he said it is way harder than the [Inferno](Inferno.md), comparable to about 400 invocations ([2024-03-22 1:24](https://youtu.be/IR0MIzsbFeM?t=84)). In May 2025 he called it currently the worst of the three raids, possibly on par with Chambers of Xeric after the update ([2025-05-30 17:25](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=1045)).
