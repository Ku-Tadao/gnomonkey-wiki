Tombs of Amascut (Raids 3, "ToA") is the third raid, built around scalable difficulty through [invocations](Invocations_(Tombs_of_Amascut).md). Gnomonkey covers it from its 2022 release onward: first as a hopeful critic of its rewards, then as a speed-runner and teacher of 300, 400 and 500 raid-level setups, and later as a critic of its depth and loot. His position shifted after the May 2025 [Summer Sweep-up](Update_Summer_Sweep-up.md), which he says made the raid much faster and easier.

[TOC]

## Concept and release

Raids 3 adds a difficulty system of invocations: negative modifiers chosen before the raid that make it harder but more rewarding[^g-y4YBfOfaU-60]. Jagex said the base raid with no invocations would likely be easier than [Chambers of Xeric](Chambers_of_Xeric.md) or [Theatre of Blood](Theatre_of_Blood.md)[^g-y4YBfOfaU-94]. Gnomonkey was happy with about 80% of the rewards but disappointed by a few, mainly the [Heka of Tumeken](Heka_of_Tumeken.md)[^uxwiO2KeF34-0]. In June 2022 he argued that rewards should scale exponentially with each invocation[^g-y4YBfOfaU-680], and feared a reward cap that would make a single set meta optimal[^g-y4YBfOfaU-646].

## Rooms and bosses

**Room order.** In the September 2022 solo 500 he went Akkha, Ba-Ba, Zebak, Kephri, calling Akkha the most RNG and Kephri a free room[^h4hf5hPQHms-0]. In November 2022 he gave the speed-optimal order as Ba-Ba, Kephri, usually Zebak, then Akkha; doing Ba-Ba first lets him pre-pot every useful potion for the [Monkey Room](Monkey_Room.md), so no ranged potion or stamina is needed[^q3D2jeguGz8-133][^q3D2jeguGz8-195]. In May 2024 he explained that the melee rooms (Ba-Ba and Kephri) go first with a pre-pot because salts benefit melee least[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-515]. In June 2025 he says the order should always be Ba-Ba (after the Monkey Room), Kephri, Zebak, Akkha, with basically no exception except at very high raid levels[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-377]. In 2026 the last two rooms are described as flexible depending on room levels, and starting with Zebak is done only around raid level 600 with Pathmaster to avoid a level 4 Zebak; he does not recommend it otherwise[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-264][^N2DCZ1nt4p4-295]. A level 4 Zebak is a one-in-six chance that he calls bad luck rather than a reset, and Akkha can be done first if it occurs[^LxYODChv40A-540][^LxYODChv40A-600].

**[Akkha](Akkha.md).** Butterfly Tech cut his average Akkha time from 3 minutes to 2.5 minutes over 150 solos, mainly by avoiding the Simon Says phase (see [Butterfly Tech](Butterfly_Tech.md))[^pSBxLCy7-44-88]. He notes that Akkha can combo for 270 damage on a mistake[^iG23QZE9pds-1000]. He recommends the invocation that reduces the memory game to one tick between steps, calling it very free[^X-PTHMq_10A-395]. A Tumeken's shadow makes Akkha about ten times easier[^K7VALGO6md8-58].

**[Ba-Ba](Ba-Ba.md).** After a rock-based special, Ba-Ba's next melee attack makes her swap target: to the lowest-HP player in a full team, or between players in a duo[^gO3aMYXs-QA-3363]. Her phase thresholds are 66% and 33% of her HP[^Z5ZONYTlS1w-842].

**[Kephri](Kephri.md).** His 2022 efficiency route chins agile scarabs and skips skulls[^q3D2jeguGz8-1079].

**[Zebak](Zebak.md).** He calls Zebak the hardest room, requiring adrenaline and salt[^h4hf5hPQHms-0]. The thralls drain prayer, so only one should be tanked at a time[^K7VALGO6md8-205].

**Monkey Room.** In 2023 he said the Monkey Room is universally regarded as terrible[^iG23QZE9pds-1590]; he calls it by far the longest puzzle room[^Kzc2e6H8i7o-1068]. [Project Rebalance](Update_Project_Rebalance.md) (April 2024) raised the health of style-based enemies there so they always take max damage from the opposing style[^Kzc2e6H8i7o-1068]; Jagex then clarified that baboon shamans, volatile baboons and cursed baboons would not be changed in that way[^P5yHDSocecY-803]. By June 2024 he says monkeys have 10 health and shamans 16, so with hybrid gear he runs up and one-shots everything[^YizVhRWXnxs-222]. He acknowledges the argument that the room is now zero skill but says Jagex lacked the resources to make it a good room[^YizVhRWXnxs-1375].

**[The Wardens](The_Wardens.md).** The Warden is by far the best point source[^q3D2jeguGz8-0] and the majority of the raid's points, so dying there is the worst death; he and Sardaco settled on about 20% of points lost (he first said about a third)[^X-PTHMq_10A-3228]. If three skulls are not tanked the wrong Warden appears, and for solos he suggests tanking about four[^gO3aMYXs-QA-5342]. Most restores go to the Wardens because of the number of specs[^gO3aMYXs-QA-5831]. In phase 1 he tick-eats the smaller orb because it does more damage[^cmLBaxdoHpQ-2750]. He strongly recommends hiding thralls because they hide ground indicators[^Z5ZONYTlS1w-937].

## Invocations and raid level

His September 2022 tier list ranks invocations by raid level given, usefulness for speed and whether they slow the raid[^a9V3c9mDf9E-1020]. Feeling Special is the best, Insanity second; Medic is the worst, Quiet Prayers second worst and Overly Draining third worst[^a9V3c9mDf9E-2790][^a9V3c9mDf9E-3499].

For a 500 in April 2023 he treats Hardcore, No Help Needed, Walk the Path and Pathfinder as givens, runs Dehydration, and calls Overly Draining trolling[^vQO8khf302s-0][^vQO8khf302s-26][^vQO8khf302s-2672]. He keeps every boss invocation including Ancient Haste[^vQO8khf302s-86], and calls Double Trouble the most valuable room invocation at 20 points[^vQO8khf302s-2738]. His no-Keris 500 (June 2023) has Dehydration off and excludes Pathmaster and Medic[^cmLBaxdoHpQ-30][^cmLBaxdoHpQ-2721]. In May 2024 he likes Pathfinder, Overly Draining off and Overlords as an option, though he personally prefers Overlords off[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-244]; in June 2025 with a Keris he would turn Dehydration on and Overly Draining off[^UhLzyieXrbY-2828]. See [On a Diet / Dehydration / Overly Draining](On_a_Diet_Dehydration_Overly_Draining.md).

Raid level dramatically increases GP/hr[^q3D2jeguGz8-29]. In October 2024 he says raising raid level is always worth it while the raid is still completable, which stops being true above roughly 500[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-2563], and advises adding 25 at a time[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-2563]. Loot does not increase beyond raid level 600[^76_3oQhlW8I-910]. In 2023 he complained that raising raid level only buffs enemies, and that for a while it was not worth going above 150 for loot[^iG23QZE9pds-973]. A bug once allowed raid levels up to 770 ([Bug abuse](Bug_abuse.md))[^76_3oQhlW8I-889].

## Rewards and purple chance

Gnomonkey's stated figures vary by video:

- Leagues, 1000 invocations (2023): 24.5% unique chance[^kfmhAeg0nEM-1294]. May 2024, 500 raid: 14% purple chance[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-1590].
- July 2024: 4.2% purple chance in a 300 expert raid, 0.17% of those a [Tumeken's shadow](Tumeken's_shadow.md), about one shadow per 565 deathless 300 raids[^e0y_auD9JhY-464].
- October 2024: roughly 1 in 20 at 300 and 1 in 9 to 1 in 11 at 400, not to be quoted exactly[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-2619]. October 2025: about 1 in 21 at 300, with [Osmumten's fang](Osmumten's_fang.md) and [Lightbearer](Lightbearer.md) the most common drops[^X-PTHMq_10A-3138].

He says purple values dropped about 90% except the shadow[^e0y_auD9JhY-464], and that the only valuable drop is the shadow[^EVx2KvYGjxM-442]. He argued for a nerf because the fang price (around 9 million) was ridiculously low[^9ugDmmDILoI-816][^9ugDmmDILoI-828][^EVx2KvYGjxM-413] (see [Opinion: Toa loot nerf](Opinion_Toa_loot_nerf.md)). After the Summer Sweep-up, purple chances were nerfed only above raid level 300[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-0].

On GP/hr: in November 2022 an average 510 raid gave just under 16M, at 1.5 raids an hour or about 24M GP/hr[^q3D2jeguGz8-1079]; in July 2024 a 500 makes approximately 20 to 25M GP/hr and ToA is the best solo raid for money[^e0y_auD9JhY-492]. In 2023 he said [Nex](Nex.md) was the best GP/hr with ToA second[^iG23QZE9pds-1050], and in July 2024 that Nex gives better money and a faster return on investment[^LxYODChv40A-462]. He recommends ToA as the next best option after Nex[^e0y_auD9JhY-464].

## Gear and supplies

**Budget 500.** The setup cost 424M in May 2023 (47 minutes, third attempt)[^K7VALGO6md8-0][^K7VALGO6md8-2903][^K7VALGO6md8-3006]. In May 2024, after the Monkey Room update, he called [Mixed hide](Mixed_hide_armour.md) the meta for a 500[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-0]; in June 2024 he said that after running calcs it is not good for a budget 500 unless one has a Twisted bow, since with a dragon crossbow it was about 25% worse than a [Bow of Faerdhinen](Bow_of_Faerdhinen.md)[^YizVhRWXnxs-0]. That budget setup cost 287M[^YizVhRWXnxs-66]. After the Summer Sweep-up his no-Keris 500 setup costs about 280M without the rune pouch[^UhLzyieXrbY-0]. See [Tombs of Amascut gear setup](Tombs_of_Amascut_gear_setup.md).

**Atlatl.** In February 2025 his Gearscape calcs gave [Atlatl](Atlatl.md) DPS of 5.69 against 6.81 for the Bofa (16% less without burn, about 6% with it)[^NxfbgWY3ZQk-521]. He concludes ToA can be done with an Atlatl rather than saving about 250M for a Bofa, with a raid setup around 50M[^NxfbgWY3ZQk-1318].

**Shadow setup.** His shadow rebuild melees Ba-Ba and Kephri, uses the shadow on Zebak, Akkha and the Wardens, and brings a Bofa one-way for Wardens phase 2[^LxYODChv40A-0]; he brought 13 Sanfew serums and used three[^LxYODChv40A-0]. In July 2025 he says a shadow setup needs no flicking[^91vxCCm7Jck-2494]. See [Shadow rebuild setup](Shadow_rebuild_setup.md).

**300 raids.** In October 2024 he completed a 300 in about 40 minutes in about 70M of gear[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-2542]. After the Summer Sweep-up he estimates a clean 300 is 10 to 15 minutes faster than before[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-3507] and says 300s are very easy compared to before[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-3507]. His ironman guide recommends staying at around 300 until an Osmumten's fang drops[^BVZEAFBsj10-2425], and he always picks Power over Chaos at the path screens[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-1466][^N2DCZ1nt4p4-2761].

**Supplies and consumables.** He pre-pots with a Chugging barrel[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-413]. Divine potions keep damage high[^X-PTHMq_10A-544], and salts give unlimited run energy[^X-PTHMq_10A-1748]. Drain defence first with a [Bone dagger](Bone_dagger.md) or [Bandos godsword](Bandos_godsword.md); a warhammer is poor on Baba and Zebak[^NxfbgWY3ZQk-773][^BVZEAFBsj10-2578]. A shadow's accuracy uses magic level, so he drains with a Seercull[^91vxCCm7Jck-2591]. See [Tombs of Amascut supplies](Tombs_of_Amascut_supplies.md).

**Plugins.** The ToA [plugin](RuneLite_plugins.md) solves puzzles before Kephri and shows baboon wave information[^Z5ZONYTlS1w-798][^Z5ZONYTlS1w-820].

## Updates and changes

- April 2024: Project Rebalance changed the Monkey Room and cut Baba's damage through protect from melee to 25% from 33%, which he called basically a non-change at high raid levels[^Kzc2e6H8i7o-1068][^Kzc2e6H8i7o-1110].
- January 2025: the water jugs were fixed so they can be hit with magic[^fLx3y5MTVjw-800].
- May 2025: the Summer Sweep-up removed Ba-Ba's chip damage, reduced Akkha's chip damage and reworked Stay Vigilant, made Kephri's scarabs always move, made Zebak's waves not push but deal 30% more damage, stopped skull bombs landing under the Warden, and shortened the Monkey Room further[^EVx2KvYGjxM-299][^EVx2KvYGjxM-326][^EVx2KvYGjxM-354][^EVx2KvYGjxM-384][^EVx2KvYGjxM-413]. Gnomonkey said it removes only unfair mechanics and one-shots[^EVx2KvYGjxM-1017]. Afterwards he says 500s are much easier[^UhLzyieXrbY-0].
- June 2026: the lifesteal restriction against the Wardens was removed[^6tsf-brc-sE-1146].

## Gnomonkey's opinion

In July 2023 he said he had been incredibly hopeful for ToA, hoping invocations would give veterans a challenge and others an accessible raid, but instead considered it the mid-level raid[^iG23QZE9pds-944]; he had cataloged over 50 bugs and flaws[^iG23QZE9pds-1000] (see [Opinion: Tombs of Amascut lacks depth](Opinion_Tombs_of_Amascut_lacks_depth.md)). In March 2024 he said it is way harder than the [Inferno](Inferno.md), comparable to about 400 invocations[^IR0MIzsbFeM-84]. In May 2025 he called it currently the worst of the three raids, possibly on par with Chambers of Xeric after the update[^EVx2KvYGjxM-1045].
