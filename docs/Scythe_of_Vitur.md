**Scythe of Vitur** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 12 videos between 2020-10-01 and 2026-06-30. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* The scythe is a five-tick weapon, which is why walking Verzik phase 2 with it needs a different cycle than a four-tick whip.[^sCmYo21i2I8-4899]
* Gnomonkey's Grotesque Guardians melee setup is a max melee Scythe of Vitur with Torva armour; he says the gargoyle boss is not weak to crush, despite what you might expect.[^b_kFNIJAcGE-95]
* Gnomonkey melee camped the Colosseum with Scythe and took claws to spec mobs down faster.[^IR0MIzsbFeM-0]
* The Scythe of Vitur rolls three times against larger targets with descending damage hit splats, and benefits from strength bonus more than most melee weapons.[^e0y_auD9JhY-1013]

## Stats and numbers

* In 2020 the scythe of Vitur costs almost 1,300 GP a swing, or about 1.6m GP an hour to use, nearly the cost of dragon darts from a blowpipe.[^7dh6_0u13Nc-389]
* The scythe is over a 25% DPS increase over the Blade of Saeldor in most cases, higher when monsters have reduced defence, for example with Dragon warhammer specs.[^7dh6_0u13Nc-389]
* A scythe is almost always 25% more DPS than a tentacle whip in the Theatre of Blood, enough to speed up completions so much that it makes more money in the long run than a whip even at about 1k per swing; this is why people do scythe rebuilds.[^sCmYo21i2I8-424]
* Against tanky bosses the Scythe accuracy buff can be as much as a 6% DPS increase, which matters at Challenge Mode Chambers where almost everything has high defence, such as Tekton, Vanguards and Olm.[^t5mXf8KJztU-90]
* That, because the Scythe has three hit splats, the minimum hit change gives it roughly 4% increased DPS against 0 defence targets.[^t5mXf8KJztU-121]
* Even using a Salad blade instead of the Scythe is a 30% DPS loss in the Colosseum.[^IR0MIzsbFeM-0]
* A 50 Scythe max is important because dropping to 49 also drops the second max hit of the Scythe from 25 to 24.[^EVOs96kyHSM-56]
* Scythe of Vitur has a 30 crush bonus (between an adamant mace and a rune mace with no defender) versus 125 slash bonus, and with a little Oathplate over 150 slash bonus.[^tDyb-FazeKw-396]

## Tips

* If you are hunting the dust or kits in challenge mode or going after a pet, Gnomonkey says the scythe is probably worth using at a loss to get those items faster.[^7dh6_0u13Nc-389]
* Gnomonkey recommends against using a Blood fury with a scythe because it uses three charges per hit, which is expensive, though it is a great option with a whip while learning.[^sCmYo21i2I8-393]
* With Torva armour the Scythe's max hit is high enough that you no longer lose a max hit by wearing a Blood Fury instead of an amulet of torture at the Grotesque Guardians.[^b_kFNIJAcGE-123]
* Gnomonkey recommends waiting until you have Torva before doing Grotesque Guardians if you seriously want the pet; the Torva defence also helps you survive until food drops start.[^b_kFNIJAcGE-123]
* Scything a mage is slightly better than macing it.[^EVOs96kyHSM-820]
* The Scythe of Vitur is better on lower-defence targets because it rolls accuracy three times, has weird damage breakpoints where certain max hits give bonus hits, and is used less than the other super weapons because its use case is less broad.[^e0y_auD9JhY-1013]
* A scythe is not very good on the tail because crush is not its highest style, even though you would think it would be good with a damage cap.[^e0g1Ff667G0-237]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says you need to make about 6m GP an hour for the scythe to be worth using over the Blade of Saeldor, so it is 100% worth using at the Theatre of Blood and the Nightmare.[^7dh6_0u13Nc-389]
* In the Chambers of Xeric against the Great Olm, Gnomonkey says the scythe is only about 10% more DPS than a lance, so it is not worth using there for profit alone.[^7dh6_0u13Nc-389]
* Gnomonkey says the Scythe has been stomped by other super weapons, the Twisted bow and mainly Tumeken's shadow, for quite a while, and he calced loads of bosses without finding one where the 15 accuracy was the difference maker.[^t5mXf8KJztU-61]
* Gnomonkey says the Scythe's Blood rune cost has not stopped anyone from using it, even ironmen, given how cheap Blood runes are, but the charge cost reduction is welcome.[^t5mXf8KJztU-90]
* Gnomonkey says Scythe of vitur is a must for the Colosseum because the final boss is only weak to slash.[^IR0MIzsbFeM-0]
* Gnomonkey says a Colosseum run with a tent whip instead of the Scythe is about 30% worse and much harder, though it has been done.[^EVOs96kyHSM-1511]
* Gnomonkey says the scythe, used on crush, is best in slot on The Hueycoatl's head, but does not work on the one-by-one targets at the start and is not good on the tail.[^e0g1Ff667G0-27]
* Gnomonkey says Lance is the second best weapon for The Hueycoatl, good for the first section of one-by-one body targets, and Boppers are next best if you have no Lance.[^e0g1Ff667G0-54]
* Gnomonkey says the Scythe of vitur was bad for a long time, but recent content such as the Colosseum and newer bosses gave it more uses, and it is now the most expensive mega rare.[^_XtlpCDD7fI-208]
* Gnomonkey says the Scythe of vitur and Avernic defender are the only Theatre of Blood purples that remain impactful.[^_XtlpCDD7fI-366]
* Gnomonkey says adding melee strength could push the Scythe of vitur past a max-hit breakpoint and make it much stronger.[^_XtlpCDD7fI-1079]
* Gnomonkey says nobody uses Scythe on crush except at Phosani's Nightmare, and that removing its crush option would change nothing except Phosani's Nightmare, so it would not fix crush.[^tDyb-FazeKw-397]
* Gnomonkey says Reddit complaints about Scythe's crush option are parroted by people who do not own a Scythe, and thinks Raids 4 bosses with 250 defence and crush weakness would need an Inquisitor's mace or even a bludgeon rather than a Scythe.[^tDyb-FazeKw-450]
* Gnomonkey says the Scythe will still win on Verzik, but the Breaker may beat it on many other Theatre of Blood bosses with neutral defences.[^mb_FoVFeXgA-1685]
* Gnomonkey says the Breaker might devalue the Scythe somewhat, but that this is unavoidable for a new mega rare in the melee weapon space.[^mb_FoVFeXgA-1747]
* Gnomonkey says the Scythe is best in slot for melee at the Maggot King.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-173]
* Gnomonkey says the Scythe is amazing for melee at the Maggot King, and he rates the Ink mace very, very good and the Soul Reaper axe very good as well.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-548]

## Events

* Jagex is buffing the Scythe by increasing the charges per Blood rune, removing charge use when it hits a triple zero, and adding 15 slash accuracy.[^t5mXf8KJztU-61]
* The blog compares the TzHaar-Ket Breaker to the Scythe of Vitur, saying a crush mega rare was a safe option but one that gains many use cases over time, like the Scythe has over the last couple of years.[^mb_FoVFeXgA-1412]
