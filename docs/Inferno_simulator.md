**Inferno simulator** is an other topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2025-12-07 and 2026-07-13. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Tips

* A fairly accurate Inferno simulator (colosim) lets you set wave 69, configure F keys and gear, and practise any part of Zuk by right-clicking him, such as spawning healers or Jad.[^2xviK0wGI-o-5666]
* The simulator can be set to spawn Jad or healers on demand, which Gnomonkey says is very helpful for practising parts of the fight.[^2xviK0wGI-o-5698]
* The Inferno simulator (inferno.colosim.com) lets you load into wave 69 to practice Zuk, but its default setup lacks treads, the Lightbearer, necklace of rupture and Seeker arrows, so its DPS is much lower than a real run.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-4753]
* The simulator has settings for F keys, UI scale, simulated ping, audio and a metronome, and you can right-click Zuk to toggle parts of the fight such as practicing only Jad or healers.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-4777]
* In the simulator you should press the spawn healer button much later than you would shoot in the real game, since it takes about three ticks for your arrow to land.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-4831]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the simulator is a bit janky and not exactly like the game but close enough to practice Zuk without throwing a run.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-4858]
