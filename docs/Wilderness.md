## Methods

- Odablock's Wilderness plan was to find a player fighting a boss, freeze them, then DD under them with crossbows out and step away on the same tick the boss's attack procs so the boss hits the target. ([2026-01-23 21:37](https://youtu.be/5qsH_-3UNQ8?t=1297))
- To land this, you must catch the freeze immediately on entering the area, so Odablock says to turn on Augury, find the target and freeze right away; make sure skull prevention is off. ([2026-01-23 22:05](https://youtu.be/5qsH_-3UNQ8?t=1325))
- Odablock tells Gnomonkey to bolt the target, DD under, and run at the last tick so the target keeps getting hit by the boss's circles. ([2026-01-23 23:06](https://youtu.be/5qsH_-3UNQ8?t=1386))

## Stats and numbers

- In Leagues 5 the Wilderness Echo boss is King Black Dragon, which drops the Thunder khopesh (a souped-up Voidwaker that calls a double lightning attack on spec and AoE lightning on auto attacks) and the Thousand-dragon ward (an Avernic-like shield with decent defensive stats that protects from all status effects). ([2024-11-25 3:07](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=187))
- The Leagues 5 Wilderness region includes the Chaos Altar for rapid Prayer training, black chinchompas, dark crabs, zombie pirates and buffed Wilderness Agility. ([2024-11-25 3:34](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=214))
- Gnomonkey and Odablock's first kill with this method was worth about 400k, the next was about 697k and the following one about 849k. ([2026-01-23 25:35](https://youtu.be/5qsH_-3UNQ8?t=1535))
- A kill in Callisto's cave was worth at least a million, reported as about 1.2 million. ([2026-01-23 31:33](https://youtu.be/5qsH_-3UNQ8?t=1893))
- Their last Wilderness kill brought the loot to about 687k, putting the total at several million for Gnomonkey's Colosseum run. ([2026-01-23 36:08](https://youtu.be/5qsH_-3UNQ8?t=2168))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey warns that in Leagues 5 many builds can kill you in a single game tick and that the Wilderness will likely be much more active than in the last Leagues. ([2024-11-25 3:34](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=214))
- In the Wilderness boss fights, protect from melee when the boss is near you, and protect from range when it is not. ([2026-01-23 29:37](https://youtu.be/5qsH_-3UNQ8?t=1777))
- After missing about four kills to splashing, Gnomonkey and Odablock invested in better mage bonus gear, with Odablock suggesting Mystic robes, so they could actually cast and land freezes. ([2026-01-23 30:36](https://youtu.be/5qsH_-3UNQ8?t=1836))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey rates Venenatis' pet B tier: it is a big red spider, only 1 in 2,000, one of the easier Wilderness pets since it is at a low Wilderness level and killed faster than other Wilderness bosses, and it is easy to escape PKers but not easy to alt. ([2022-03-12 13:26](https://youtu.be/vwCm9NKJhRE?t=806))
- Gnomonkey says Callisto's pet is annoying to get (you get PKed all the time) but luring on an alt between two worlds is not that slow; it needs a slayer task, is 1 in 2,000, and he rates it B tier, above Venenatis. ([2022-03-12 13:58](https://youtu.be/vwCm9NKJhRE?t=838))
- Gnomonkey says Vet'ion's pet is one of the rarest Wilderness pets that almost nobody has, since killing him above level 30 Wilderness in multi-combat means you cannot teleport and clans can kill you, and he rates it the best Wilderness pet but in B tier like the others. ([2022-03-12 14:44](https://youtu.be/vwCm9NKJhRE?t=884))
- Gnomonkey says Scorpia's pet is the easiest Wilderness boss pet to get after the Chaos Elemental, about 20 hours with a Harmonised staff and an alt, and it can be boosted with around 10 people; he rates it high C tier. ([2022-03-12 15:42](https://youtu.be/vwCm9NKJhRE?t=942))
- Gnomonkey says the Wilderness in 2007 was the RuneScape stomping ground, but is much less populated now due to a changing playerbase, a lack or surplus of updates and focus on the wrong areas; he notes it has been notoriously difficult for the Jagex team to improve. ([2022-07-13 0:00](https://youtu.be/uuJsdK3_KRQ))
- Gnomonkey says with gravestones now in the game the Wilderness is really the only location that still has a risk mechanic, and he thinks the reward should always be gold or saving gold, encouraging risking items for more money. ([2022-07-13 0:59](https://youtu.be/uuJsdK3_KRQ?t=59))
- Gnomonkey says the Wilderness is not his cup of tea but is a great choice for overall points for Dragon cup grinding, and the Thunder khopesh is amazing for Wilderness Slayer tasks. ([2024-11-25 3:07](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=187))

## Trivia

- The Wilderness is the only place in the game where players can attack others and take their items, making it by default the most dangerous area; it gets more dangerous deeper, with a higher level range of players that can attack you and teleports prevented past level 20 Wilderness (some unique teleports work up to level 30). ([2022-07-13 0:31](https://youtu.be/uuJsdK3_KRQ?t=31))
- The Wilderness has multi-combat areas (multiple people can attack a single target) and single-combat areas (one-on-one only), and Old School added content like Wilderness bosses, the Chaos Altar, black chinchompa hunting and revenants. ([2022-07-13 0:59](https://youtu.be/uuJsdK3_KRQ?t=59))
