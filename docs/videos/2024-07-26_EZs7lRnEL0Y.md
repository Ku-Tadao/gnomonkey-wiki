## Summary

Gnomonkey's updated Fortis Colosseum teaching guide, recorded live on stream with a max-budget melee setup. He argues the South-West pillar is far better than the popular North-West/B5 approach because it avoids unsolvable stacks and removes the need for range switches, then walks through a full run wave by wave (invocation choices, manticore flicking, off-ticks, mage block) and the Sol Heredit fight, finishing with a guide to gear downgrades.

- ([22:37](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1357)) Gnomonkey says the Colosseum plugin timer shows when reinforcements spawn, and he says you want that timer on even if you are not speedrunning, since reinforcements spawn at 40.2 seconds every time.

### [Fortis Colosseum pillars](../Fortis_Colosseum_pillars.md)

- ([5:00](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=300)) Gnomonkey says he does the Colosseum differently from every other guide: the common method is starting on the B5 tile and running to the Northwest pillar, but he says to stop using the Northwest pillar and use the Southwest pillar instead.
- ([6:34](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=394)) Gnomonkey explains that reinforcement spawns on the Northwest pillar centre around a tile randomly within a big area, while the Southwest pillar's gate spawn centre is one tile closer to the gate, which he says solves many problems.
- ([7:21](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=441)) Gnomonkey says in the very late waves reinforcements spawn with a Minotaur and a shaman, which is what usually gets people killed, and on the Northwest pillar the shaman can spawn in front of the Minotaur so you cannot put it behind and must off-tick the shaman.
- ([7:52](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=472)) Gnomonkey says on the Northwest pillar there is no solve for some stacks: for example a javelin colossus and a manticore attacking on the same tick means you die unless you get the off-tick at the beginning.
- ([9:24](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=564)) Gnomonkey says on the Southwest pillar the same stack of a manticore and a javelin colossus on the same tick does not kill you: you pray against both and go to the correct side of the pillar.
- ([9:55](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=595)) Gnomonkey says at the Southwest pillar's mage block tile a shaman can see you from any spawn location so it stops moving, while the Minotaur keeps moving to you, meaning you never have to off-tick a shaman from that position.
- ([11:00](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=660)) Gnomonkey says some stacks occur only on the Northwest pillar, such as two mage monsters in front and two ranged in the back, which he says is only solvable by tanking damage, whereas on the Southwest pillar the tile pushes monsters to the side so two things can never be side by side.
- ([12:04](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=724)) Gnomonkey says he tells people posting unsolvable stacks from the Northwest pillar in Coliseum chat to go to the Southwest pillar, and says he even told another streamer that a posted stack was unsolvable.
- ([13:04](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=784)) Gnomonkey says you do not need range switches on the Southwest pillar because you can rotate around the pillar and off-tick enemies so you can melee everything, which gives about five more restores and removes the need for a range potion or a minimum four-way Twisted bow.
- ([14:35](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=875)) Gnomonkey says the Southwest pillar gives you more supplies, fewer bad spawns, and lets you solve everything, though he admits it does not solve every problem, such as a Z-stack.

### [Fortis Colosseum setup](../Fortis_Colosseum_setup.md)

- ([1:21](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=81)) Gnomonkey's max-budget Colosseum setup has no ranged weapons and no range switches except a Venator bow, because skipping them lets him bring more potions and his strategy does not need them.
- ([1:49](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=109)) Gnomonkey brings six brews and divines plus restores in his Colosseum setup, uses the Saradomin godsword for sustain (it can spec the Fremennik ranger and gives health and prayer because it is a guaranteed max hit), and a Scythe as best-in-slot melee weapon for the whole run.
- ([1:49](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=109)) Gnomonkey says for the one-shot of the Fremennik mage he needs a three-way mage setup plus a Saturated heart rather than the four-way mage he used previously, with no Augury needed; he says this is the best possible setup but the heart is expensive.
- ([2:22](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=142)) Gnomonkey says the Venator bow kills the Fremennik mage and will one-shot him if you bounce off the ranged Fremennik after killing the melee one, and Burning claws are very good DPS and let you DPS spec through the waves.
- ([2:49](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=169)) Gnomonkey says being able to DPS spec is nice for the boss and for killing quickly because of the reinforcement mechanic, and that an Osmumten's fang is an optional addition for attacking the shamans with better DPS than a Scythe, though it is not needed.
- ([3:18](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=198)) Gnomonkey walks in with a Dragon warhammer because it is very good on the boss and one-handed, so with a Book of the Dead it saves a slot; he says Elder maul is not a slot save because it is two-handed, and this is the one place in the game he still uses a warhammer.
- ([20:45](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1245)) Gnomonkey says he drops items at the start such as runes (about 19600 soul runes worth) and chaos runes for casting, and starts wave one on a tile similar to B5.
- ([23:57](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1437)) Gnomonkey says that on the later part of the run he found a four-way mage with Augury or a five-way mage without Augury is enough to one-shot the Fremennik berserker, which is less mandatory than he thought, and that you should always hit your heart so you do not fail the one-shot.
- ([55:55](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=3355)) Gnomonkey says you can check any gear question yourself at gearscape.net, which he calls very UI friendly.

- ([57:52](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=3472)) Gnomonkey says you do not need Torva for the Colosseum, with Justiciar and Bandos probably fine, and that you should get the Scythe before Torva because the Scythe matters much more.

### [Colosseum invocations](../Colosseum_invocations.md)

- ([21:28](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1288)) Gnomonkey says to always take Blasphemy first because it is the least bad of the first choices.
- ([21:47](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1307)) Gnomonkey says to always take a new invocation even if it is slightly worse than an upgrade, because new invocations are harder to get than upgrades, and the ones he looks for are Solar Flare and Myopia; he also tries to finish an invocation to take it out of the pool.
- ([21:47](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1307)) Gnomonkey says Myopia reduces your range, which does not matter when you only use a mage weapon and bow, and Solar Flare adds a slow-moving orb that circles the pillar and damages you occasionally and pulls your prayer off.
- ([23:19](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1399)) Gnomonkey says Re-entry is one you never want to take, Doom is one to avoid on a first cape (though Doom 1 is okay toward the end of the run), and Frailty is mid-tier.
- ([27:27](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1647)) Gnomonkey says Solar Flare damage is only active every other tick: it moves two ticks per tile but only hits on one of them.
- ([27:27](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1647)) Gnomonkey says Solar Flare is absolutely better than Frailty, and Flare 3 is the clear choice because it finishes the invocation and opens up new ones.
- ([28:29](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1709)) Gnomonkey says the javelin colossus counts as two hits for Doom stacks even though it hits only once, which has been bugged since release.
- ([33:54](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2034)) Gnomonkey says needing to take Blasphemy 3 on wave 3 is a sign of getting bad seeds on other invocations rather than Blasphemy being bad, and that you can easily complete a run with Blasphemy 3, especially a first cape, though you should maybe leave if your later options are Bees, Totemic and Re-entry.
- ([35:41](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2141)) Gnomonkey says Relentless is amazing on the boss because it does nothing to Sol Heredit, is okay on wave 11 as a last resort, and is terrible early; he also says Quartet is really bad now.
- ([36:43](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2203)) Gnomonkey says for a new Colosseum player, bad invocation choices like these in wave 9 can end the run.
- ([37:55](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2275)) Gnomonkey says Duo is really bad because it creates quad stacks, and Red Flag is one of the worst invocations in the game because it makes the Minotaur run towards you uninterruptible and stacks up enemies.
- ([44:00](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2640)) Gnomonkey says Doom 3 and Totemic are very bad before Sol Heredit, while Volatility is completely fine because Soul only explodes at the end and does very little damage.

### [Fortis Colosseum](../Fortis_Colosseum.md)

- ([24:30](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1470)) Gnomonkey says manticores attack tick after tick with a first, second and third style (mage, range, melee), starting from the tick they see you, and that flicking them is a major skill to learn; you click for the start of the animation.
- ([26:44](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1604)) Gnomonkey says shamans stop moving when they see you (mage block), while Minotaurs keep moving.
- ([28:29](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1709)) Gnomonkey says javelin colossi throw a javelin every five attacks that drops from the air and cannot be blocked with prayer, hitting very hard.
- ([29:29](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1769)) Gnomonkey says shockwave colossi start spawning after wave six and the reinforcements then include a Minotaur, which heals things.
- ([29:59](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1799)) Gnomonkey says the Minotaur attacks with a five-tick melee, and if something in its line of sight is not attacked it will full heal the target if that target has taken damage of over (he thinks) 25% of its health.
- ([29:59](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1799)) Gnomonkey says when you want the Minotaur next to you and not behind, you use his Minotaur drag tile and kill it there, and you can corner trap enemies for free damage.
- ([38:34](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2314)) Gnomonkey says the shaman has a ten-tile range and the shockwave colossus a fifteen-tile range, so against a shockwave that spawns far back you can flick to ranged prayer instead of praying mage and avoid damage.
- ([38:34](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2314)) Gnomonkey says racing the reinforcement clock matters: you must kill things before reinforcements spawn and full-heal them, and with two manticores on you they off-tick so you can always flick them.
- ([40:00](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2400)) Gnomonkey says if a shaman is on the same tick as something else and you are screwed, kill the shaman as fast as possible with specs.

### [Fortis Colosseum pillars](../Fortis_Colosseum_pillars.md)

- ([30:57](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1857)) Gnomonkey says for a normal stack with a ranged thing in the back and a mage thing in front, you click to the corner from the middle of the pillar, which makes the back enemy see you first and the front enemy see you after, lining them up on the same tick.
- ([30:57](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1857)) Gnomonkey says this works because the further-out tile gives line of sight on you before the front enemy is unblocked, and you must start from the middle of the pillar since the start tile also matters.
- ([32:30](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1950)) Gnomonkey says he solved a Z-stack (manticore, mage, javelin) by stepping to a tile so the manticore saw him, then stepping to make the shockwave and then the javelin see him, lining attacks as ranged, mage, melee, mage, ranged with two ticks of downtime to fit off-ticks.
- ([32:30](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1950)) Gnomonkey says a manticore has effectively a five-tick attack cooldown, so there are three ticks of manticore attacks and two ticks for other monsters, and calls the Z-stack the worst, very rare stack.
- ([36:43](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2203)) Gnomonkey says you can tell what a second manticore will attack with before its orbs appear by watching the first manticore's style, which lets you know when something is off-ticked.
- ([37:14](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2234)) Gnomonkey says stepping out from the pillar three tiles makes the front thing see you before the back thing, the opposite of the usual case.
- ([42:55](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2575)) Gnomonkey says when things pull up, if they attack one or two ticks before or after the manticore they are off-ticked, and he warns to be careful what you kill because killing a manticore can ruin your stack.

### [Sol Heredit](../Sol_Heredit.md)

- ([14:49](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=889)) Gnomonkey says Sol Heredit has 800 ranged defence and 800 magic defence, so you need a slash weapon, with the Scythe the best option.
- ([15:06](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=906)) Gnomonkey says the Scythe has better DPS on Sol Heredit than both the Tumeken's shadow and the Twisted bow.
- ([44:00](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2640)) Gnomonkey says four Solar Flare orbs circle in a square on Sol Heredit, pull your prayer off and add Doom stacks, which is very bad if you have Doom.
- ([44:00](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2640)) Gnomonkey says to use the Pi 80 activate/spam-click start for Soul and he says a Soul tile set can be imported with !soultiles from his Twitch chat.
- ([44:40](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2680)) Gnomonkey recommends the Soul Simulator, a 3D rendition of Sol Heredit with adjustable ping, UI size, keybinds and attacks, to practise and calm nerves before the fight.
- ([46:01](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2761)) Gnomonkey's mindset for Sol Heredit's normal attacks is to call out every attack out loud and proactively move to a tile that dodges both of the possible next attacks (for example a spear one or a shield two), rather than reacting.
- ([47:03](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2823)) Gnomonkey says Sol Heredit's first special is the triple parry: you flick melee prayer on three times with roughly 3 ticks, 3 ticks, 3 ticks between, and below half health the last gap is longer (3, 3, 4) so you must turn your prayer off and back on.
- ([48:29](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2909)) Gnomonkey says Sol Heredit's grapple calls out a limb and you can block it by clicking the gear piece, and clicking on the tick before it hits gives a guaranteed max hit that reduces the kill time by about 15 seconds; you click as his hand thrusts at you, and there is a long window.
- ([49:29](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2969)) Gnomonkey says Sol Heredit has five phases, with a phase transition at 10%, 25%, 50%, 75% and when 90% of health is lost, dropping puddles that stack Doom and hit for 20.
- ([49:56](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2996)) Gnomonkey's method is to dodge from a corner for spear one, diagonally for spear two, use the other corner after a phase transition, and then the middle, giving three dodges before he must drag Sol Heredit.
- ([51:11](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=3071)) Gnomonkey says special attacks reset the attack counter, so it is always spear one or shield one after a special, and says to dodge attacks over healing, since spamming brews after a mistake gets you hit again.
- ([55:55](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=3355)) Gnomonkey says to control the boss, never letting it pin you against a wall, either by moving each time a puddle appears or by rotating around it; and to be at 100% special before Soul so the fight is not 30 seconds longer.
