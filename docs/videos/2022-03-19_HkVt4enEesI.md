## Summary

Gnomonkey ranks essentially every OSRS boss and raid in a tier list based purely on how much he enjoys the fight, judged by skill floor and skill cap rather than loot. He puts Inferno at the very top of S tier, rates most simple tank-and-click bosses F, and ends with a full reordering of all tiers.

## Topics covered

### [Opinion: PvM boss tier list](../Opinion_PvM_boss_tier_list.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI)) Gnomonkey's boss tier list ranks bosses purely by how much he enjoys them, based on their skill floor and skill cap; he says it is opinion.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI)) Gnomonkey defines a skill floor as how difficult content is to jump into (a higher floor is worse), and a skill cap as how skilled you can become before the content peaks out (a high cap is good); he says some content has an infinite skill cap.
- ([0:33](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=33)) In the tier list, the greyed-out versions of top bosses are the hard mode variants of Theater of Blood bosses, which he rates separately because they are mechanically different.
- ([1:07](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=67)) Gnomonkey does not rank Challenge Mode variants of Chambers of Xeric bosses separately because they just have higher defence and health and are the same fight.
- ([1:29:05](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=5345)) In the final order, Gnomonkey places the Inferno at the very top of S tier, with Theater of Blood content, Fight Caves, Verzik Vitur, Nylocas, and the Great Olm following in S tier, with Challenge Mode Chambers of Xeric near the bottom of S tier.
- ([1:30:32](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=5432)) Gnomonkey puts The Gauntlet at the top of A tier, with Nex above Phosani's Nightmare and Hallowed Sepulchre right below Nex; Maiden of Sugadinti is the best of the A tier Theater of Blood fights.
- ([1:32:00](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=5520)) In B tier Gnomonkey places Zalcano, Tempoross, Tekton and Xarpus hard mode toward the bottom, with Dagannoth Kings toward the top, Kril Tsutsaroth a step above Vanguards, and Vanguards above Vorkath.
- ([1:32:00](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=5520)) In C tier Gnomonkey puts Kree'arra at the top followed by Giant Mole and Barrows right below it, with Zilyana, Vespula, Cerberus, Alchemical Hydra, Abyssal Sire, Chaos Elemental and Sarachnis below, calling Cerberus mechanically more interesting than most of them.
- ([1:32:00](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=5520)) Gnomonkey says Muttadile is the worst boss in the game, with Wilderness bosses next, then Mimic, Corporeal Beast, Wintertodt, Thermonuclear smoke devil and Kraken, which he says is worse than the smoke devil.
- ([1:37:11](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=5831)) Gnomonkey concludes the list has lots of F-tier bosses but lots of S and A tier content, and calls it a better balance than his pets video.

### King Black Dragon

- ([1:07](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=67)) Gnomonkey says the most interesting thing about King Black Dragon is running to his lair in the Wilderness; the boss itself has no mechanics, just dragonfire, poison and stat drain, and you can kill it with a Twisted bow.
- ([1:30:02](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=5402)) In the final reordering Gnomonkey says any boss with a mechanic must rank above King Black Dragon in the F tier, because KBD is just click the boss.

### [Hallowed Sepulchre](../Hallowed_Sepulchre.md)

- ([1:41](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=101)) Gnomonkey calls Hallowed Sepulchre the agility boss and very good content that pushes movement in the game to its limits.
- ([2:13](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=133)) Gnomonkey says Hallowed Sepulchre floor 5 is great because you can keep learning and getting better, the skill cap is really high, and it has some of the best scaling money in the game; he places it in A tier.
- ([1:30:32](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=5432)) In the final order Gnomonkey places Hallowed Sepulchre just below Nex in A tier and not at the top, noting it is not a boss encounter so the placement is arbitrary.

### [Scorpia](../Scorpia.md)

- ([2:39](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=159)) Scorpia's only mechanic is that below half health she summons two small scorpions that heal her; solo you freeze them and move her away, or with an alt you just DPS through it.
- ([2:39](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=159)) Gnomonkey rates Scorpia F tier because there is no skill involved, and says that since you can no longer X-log at the axe hut, fighting her in level 50 Wilderness is pretty much suicide until you get the pet.

### [Skotizo](../Skotizo.md)

- ([3:07](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=187)) Gnomonkey rates Skotizo easy F tier: he hits hard but if you stay out of his melee range while praying magic he cannot hit you, which Gnomonkey finds boring.
- ([3:07](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=187)) Gnomonkey says the main problem with Skotizo is the crystals that spawn on the arena edge, killed by the melee weapon Arclight in one hit, with no cooldown, so unlucky runs are spent circling the arena hitting crystals.
- ([3:39](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=219)) Gnomonkey says Skotizo would be a lot better if the crystal cooldown were reduced and a cap added on how many spawn at once.

### Zalcano

- ([4:09](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=249)) Gnomonkey calls Zalcano an interesting skilling boss with cool movement options around her portals to avoid damage, and rates it B tier, saying it has more going on than people give it credit for but is not exceptional.
- ([1:32:00](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=5520)) In the final reordering Gnomonkey puts Zalcano toward the bottom of B tier.

### Kraken

- ([4:38](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=278)) Gnomonkey rates Kraken F tier, saying the most skilled thing is throwing a fishing explosive and moving back four tiles before the first cast; he describes it as left-click the boss until it dies, with typeless magic from tentacles.
- ([1:32:00](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=5520)) Gnomonkey says Kraken is worse than Thermonuclear smoke devil in the final F tier ordering.

### Obor

- ([5:11](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=311)) Gnomonkey rates the free-to-play boss Obor F tier: he can stun you sometimes, otherwise he just hits you and you hit him until he dies.
- ([5:41](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=341)) Gnomonkey says it is hard to make a free-to-play boss mechanically interesting with no mechanics to work with.

### Bryophyta

- ([5:11](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=311)) Gnomonkey rates the free-to-play boss Bryophyta F tier, noting her little spawns that you must chop with an axe require no skill or thought.

### [Thermonuclear smoke devil](../Thermonuclear_smoke_devil.md)

- ([5:41](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=341)) Gnomonkey rates the Thermonuclear smoke devil F tier: it is left-click and it dies, with typeless damage like Kraken, though it dies fast.
- ([5:41](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=341)) Gnomonkey notes skips at the smoke devil such as walking under it with a Scythe or harm staff, or freezing it out of range and then going to max range with a sanguinesti staff, but says none requires skill.
- ([6:14](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=374)) Gnomonkey says the most skilled thing at the smoke devil is running an alt from a house and spec transferring over and over, which he dislikes and does not think sets a good precedent.

### [Zulrah](../Zulrah.md)

- ([6:14](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=374)) Gnomonkey says Zulrah has a lot going on, such as knowing how many hits you can fit in before she submerges, saving volatile nightmare staff specs for the melee phase (higher defence), and eight-way switches with lots of running.
- ([6:48](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=408)) Gnomonkey says that during Zulrah's first blue phase you can fit two blowpipe shots then a Twisted bow shot, landing the Twisted bow on the last tick before she submerges.
- ([7:15](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=435)) Gnomonkey places Zulrah in high B tier, saying you cannot put it in A tier because it is not crazy, though there is cool stuff to do.

### Wintertodt

- ([7:42](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=462)) Gnomonkey includes Wintertodt only because the game considers it a boss; he says he does not, describing it as chopping logs and throwing them in a fire, and calls it pretty terrible skilling content that is only better than clicking logs over and over.
- ([1:32:00](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=5520)) Gnomonkey says Wintertodt is almost not a boss and may as well not be one, ranking it very low among the F tier.

### Hespori

- ([8:11](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=491)) Gnomonkey rates Hespori F tier, saying it is only a little interesting in a speedrun, where hitting over a hundred with a chally can skip a phase.
- ([1:37:11](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=5831)) In the final F tier reordering Gnomonkey places Hespori at the top of F because it is at least somewhat interesting in a speedrun.

### [K'ril Tsutsaroth](../K'ril_Tsutsaroth.md)

- ([8:11](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=491)) Gnomonkey says Kril Tsutsaroth has many methods (solo 5-0 with a Twisted bow, DOLO, trolling, resupply alts) and rates him B tier, though even the 5-0 is just clicking the right tiles and then Kril, not exceptional skill.

### [Phosani's Nightmare](../Phosani's_Nightmare.md)

- ([9:14](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=554)) Gnomonkey says he is not a huge fan of the Nightmare and, in the video, assumes he is talking about Phosani's Nightmare because it pretty much replaces the regular Nightmare.
- ([9:41](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=581)) Gnomonkey describes the Nightmare as having a big health pool hit with crush, very high defence, then a totem phase where you eight-way into magic to kill the totems; special attacks include husks, parasites and shadows, and everything kills you in two hits.
- ([10:10](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=610)) Gnomonkey rates Phosani's Nightmare A tier content, saying it takes a lot of skill and practice but is lacking something: the skill is not making a mistake against a high-hitting boss, and he prefers content that pushes you toward better times or more damage.
- ([1:30:02](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=5402)) In the final ordering Gnomonkey places Nex above Phosani's Nightmare in A tier.

### [Grotesque Guardians](../Grotesque_Guardians.md)

- ([10:43](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=643)) Gnomonkey calls Dawn and Dusk (Grotesque Guardians) genuinely the most mechanically interesting Slayer boss, with several phases, lots of gear switching, and last hits with a chally and ballista.
- ([11:13](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=673)) Gnomonkey says Grotesque Guardians has horrible loot, which is why many dislike it, but he is judging only on how interesting the fight is, and places it in high B tier.
- ([11:41](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=701)) Gnomonkey says the Grotesque Guardians fight is mostly hitting the boss and moving away from things on the ground; the heal skip is just shooting a lot and hoping, and the end is switching prayer to whatever the boss last attacked with.
- ([1:32:00](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=5520)) In the final reordering Gnomonkey puts Grotesque Guardians below Zulrah in B tier.

### [Nex](../Nex.md)

- ([12:11](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=731)) Gnomonkey says rating Nex high will be controversial: he calls her high A tier, not S tier, with more going on than people give her credit for.
- ([12:40](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=760)) In his Nex teams Gnomonkey says they use a rapier switch and a four-way to the Twisted bow, and previously a ZCB switch, hitting the boss with the rapier then quickly switching to the Twisted bow on the minions.
- ([13:12](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=792)) Gnomonkey notes Nex's Embrace Darkness attack can kill you if she enters melee mode on someone, hitting 20s repeatedly, so his team positions during Fumus so they can reset her to the middle before Umbra and hit her from a specific area in the shadow phase.
- ([13:12](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=792)) If Nex enters melee mode, Gnomonkey's team goes under her to reset her to the middle.
- ([13:44](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=824)) Gnomonkey says he likes controlling Nex's position and having one person in range of her while the rest stay out of range to avoid damage, and that this is a mechanic people ignore by just ranging the boss with their brain off; he calls it definitely A tier.

### [Cerberus](../Cerberus.md)

- ([14:16](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=856)) Gnomonkey says Cerberus has an algorithm to his auto attacks you could learn to protect against everything, but calls it insanely difficult and not worth the effort; the ghosts only require switching prayer to the ghost colour every two ticks.
- ([14:48](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=888)) Gnomonkey dodges Cerberus's initial lava pool by moving under him with correct timing, which he finds a little interesting.
- ([14:48](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=888)) Gnomonkey rates Cerberus C tier, saying half the fight is pretty much AFK; the most interesting thing is running a resupply alt.

### [Kree'arra](../Kree'arra.md)

- ([15:18](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=918)) Gnomonkey describes Kree'arra (Armadyl) solo as two-ticking the boss and one-ticking the minion with chinchompas to prevent melee hits; DOLO it is almost AFK, praying ranged with Rigor and chinning.
- ([15:49](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=949)) Gnomonkey rates Kree'arra high C tier, saying there is little going on besides juggling food and freezing the boss over the minion by targeting it.

### Chaos Fanatic

- ([16:23](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=983)) Gnomonkey lumps Chaos Fanatic with the archaeologists, saying they are not interesting bosses: they throw out books or green energy that explodes if you stand in it, and otherwise you just attack the boss.

### [Alchemical Hydra](../Alchemical_Hydra.md)

- ([16:55](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1015)) Gnomonkey says the Alchemical Hydra is boring and mindless: if you can count to three attacks you are good at it, and he rates it C tier.
- ([17:24](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1044)) Gnomonkey notes you can lure the Alchemical Hydra onto the vents early for extra DPS, and that the electricity skip and fire skip are simple, with all mechanics learnable in 10 or 20 kills.

### [Kalphite Queen](../Kalphite_Queen.md)

- ([17:50](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1070)) Gnomonkey says the Kalphite Queen's special mechanic is that she hits through armour, which he calls stupid design (negating a mechanic entirely); the eight-way between phases is only about fifteen seconds and requires little thought.
- ([18:20](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1100)) Gnomonkey rates the Kalphite Queen high F tier.

### [Corporeal Beast](../Corporeal_Beast.md)

- ([18:47](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1127)) Gnomonkey calls Corporeal Beast so bad it cannot go anywhere but F: drain all its defence and hit it with a spear, with groups slightly more interesting because you drain it less before fighting.
- ([19:17](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1157)) Gnomonkey says the most interesting Corporeal Beast mechanic is walking back and forth on the dark core, but everyone just freezes it in place; he rates it a higher F tier.

### [Tempoross](../Tempoross.md)

- ([19:17](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1157)) Gnomonkey says Jagex considers Tempoross a boss and that he finds it interesting if you do firefighter, because it plays into Sepulchre-style movement tech by positioning efficiently near many fires.
- ([20:19](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1219)) Gnomonkey says Tempoross is boring for the first half and, done any other way (not solo, or XP solo methods), boring overall; he rates it between C and B and puts it below Zalcano.

### Abyssal Sire

- ([20:19](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1219)) Gnomonkey describes the Abyssal Sire fight: shadow barrage the boss to stun it, kill the respiratory systems with magic, then hit the boss in the second phase; with an alt you land a hammer, then claw, and the last phase is a time attack.
- ([20:52](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1252)) Gnomonkey says the Abyssal Sire's time-attack ending does not play well with RNG because you can flop, and that the only skills are walking the square at the end and moving on when the respiratory XP drop shows; he rates it a higher C tier.

### [General Graardor](../General_Graardor.md)

- ([22:25](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1345)) Gnomonkey calls the solo melee method at General Graardor, with shield flicking and going under the boss to set up minion patterns so you can flick everything, crazy skilled and very cool to see on an Ironman.
- ([22:54](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1374)) Gnomonkey also likes the Bova (9-0 / 7-0) method that involves clicking around the room constantly, and says duo or DOLO is just hitting the boss, which is boring.
- ([23:21](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1401)) Gnomonkey places General Graardor in lower A tier because the shield method makes the boss transcend B tier, saying methods change how interesting a boss is.

### [Dagannoth Kings](../Dagannoth_Kings.md)

- ([23:52](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1432)) Gnomonkey says fighting the Dagannoth Kings in one cave while waiting for respawns is F tier, but going back and forth between two caves with reactive off-ticking of Supreme and Prime is interesting; it is one of the only reactive content besides the Inferno.
- ([24:23](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1463)) Gnomonkey says Dagannoth Kings take some skill to set up off-ticks but are simple once you have a method, and he places them in high B tier, near the top of B.

### [Chaos Elemental](../Chaos_Elemental.md)

- ([24:56](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1496)) Gnomonkey says the Chaos Elemental uses purely random attack styles, protecting from magic prevents the most damage, it teleports you, and its item takeoff attack is the most interesting part because leaving one inventory slot open lets it use a free attack.
- ([25:59](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1559)) Gnomonkey rates the Chaos Elemental low C tier and likes that it is a fast Wilderness boss with a high pet drop rate, saying he hates Wilderness pets in general and wishes more followed that model.

### Sarachnis

- ([26:26](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1586)) Gnomonkey calls Sarachnis a mid-level boss that is not for mid-levels but for high levels wanting elite clues or the pet, which he considers bad design.
- ([26:26](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1586)) Gnomonkey notes that at Sarachnis you pray melee, pray ranged when she webs you (a guaranteed ranged hit), stand in a specific square to be hit only once, and she spawns melee and mage spiders so high mage defence like Karil's is encouraged.
- ([26:56](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1616)) Gnomonkey rates Sarachnis C tier, saying it has the bare minimum of mechanics and thought.

### [Venenatis](../Venenatis.md)

- ([27:23](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1643)) Gnomonkey rates Venenatis easy F tier because you safe spot it, then AFK and hover over your seed pod, and says the only skill is clicking a seed pod when a red dot appears.
- ([27:52](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1672)) Gnomonkey says all Wilderness bosses that can be safe-spotted go in F tier, adding that you should not be able to safe spot them.

### [Giant Mole](../Giant_Mole.md)

- ([27:52](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1672)) Gnomonkey calls Giant Mole the best mid-level boss in the game, with 200 health and a random chance for each hitsplat once below 100 health to make it dig around the room.
- ([28:25](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1705)) Gnomonkey says Giant Mole encourages slow heavy-hitting weapons like a Dharok's set or Twisted bow and discourages claw or dragon dagger specs, which he says no other boss in the game does.
- ([28:54](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1734)) Gnomonkey says fighting Giant Mole taught him corner safe spots for the Inferno, because mole digging works like an Inferno melee.
- ([29:23](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1763)) Gnomonkey rates Giant Mole in highest C tier, saying it has more going on than people give it credit for but is not exceptional.

### Commander Zilyana

- ([29:49](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1789)) Gnomonkey calls Commander Zilyana boring: with a Twisted bow or Armadyl crossbow you run in a big square clicking the boss every five ticks, and the only skill is optionally flicking minions.
- ([30:20](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1820)) Gnomonkey says Zilyana DOLO can be done almost forever with an alt killing the minions and giving run energy, and calls her the only boss that really requires purple sweets besides challenge mode speedruns.
- ([30:48](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1848)) Gnomonkey considers Zilyana genuinely the least interesting God Wars boss and rates her high C tier.

### [The Gauntlet](../The_Gauntlet.md)

- ([31:16](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1876)) Gnomonkey treats the Gauntlet (normal and corrupted) as one piece of content, with the normal Gauntlet an easy mode that teaches corrupted safely.
- ([31:16](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1876)) Gnomonkey says the Gauntlet has the lowest skill floor and the highest skill cap gap in the game: you can start really bad and work up to very good, which he calls one of its best design features.
- ([31:47](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1907)) Gnomonkey dislikes that as you get faster in the Gauntlet you eventually have to chance it, working down the number of fish and dying if you do not hit hard enough, which he says makes the skill cap arbitrarily lower than it needs to be; a perfect 10 fish kill is where he says it is a guaranteed kill.
