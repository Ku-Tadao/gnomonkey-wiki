## Summary

A long July 2020 live walkthrough in which Gnomonkey deletes his RuneLite settings and goes through every vanilla RuneLite plugin alphabetically, showing the settings he uses and why. He calls Inventory Setups (an external plugin hub plugin) one of the best plugins, and GPU his favorite plugin in the game.

## Topics covered

### [RuneLite](../RuneLite.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY)) For the video Gnomonkey completely deleted his settings file out of the RuneLite folder to have a fresh install and then configured each plugin to what he normally uses, recording live with some cuts and voiceover.
- ([0:30](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=30)) Gnomonkey turns on stretched mode and keeps the scaling at 100% because it keeps the UI a bit crisper.
- ([0:56](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=56)) Gnomonkey plays on a 1440p screen and notes settings will be a tiny bit different for the more common 1080p; the RuneLite settings panel is the hub for almost all basic settings.
- ([1:27](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=87)) The resolution setting changes the window to match your game size, so you want to match it to your screen resolution.
- ([1:56](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=116)) Gnomonkey does not keep RuneLite always on top, because a program such as a calculator moved over the window would appear underneath and become inaccessible.
- ([2:23](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=143)) Gnomonkey enables tray notifications and game message notifications (which make messages appear in the bottom area), does not request focus because it is annoying, and uses a custom notification sound from his folder.
- ([2:52](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=172)) Gnomonkey wants notifications always sent, because he sometimes clicks on his screen while still looking at a different monitor.
- ([2:52](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=172)) Gnomonkey keeps a small font for tooltips but a regular interface font; he requires shift for the overlay menu and places tooltips under the cursor.
- ([3:50](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=230)) The overlay color option is really handy if you have macro keys on your mouse, because RuneScape otherwise counts them as clicks; turning it on stops that.
- ([4:19](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=259)) The Plugin Hub holds the external plugins, which are monitored by the RuneLite team and do not break rules but are not good enough to make it into the main build.
- ([4:19](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=259)) Gnomonkey considers none of the Plugin Hub plugins necessary except Inventory Setups, which he thinks is necessary and cannot understand why it does not come with default RuneLite.
- ([4:48](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=288)) In the plugin list, the stars pin plugins to the top; Gnomonkey pins Metronome, which plays a tick sound so you can count ticks in your head.
- ([5:22](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=322)) Gnomonkey recommends logging in to RuneLite with your Google account before any setup, since runelite.net then stores things like loot you have gotten and your Grand Exchange history.
- ([5:56](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=356)) Holding Alt lets you move overlays and pin them into the blue boxes, and you can even move the whole minimap; Gnomonkey says moving the enemy health bars, which he pins at the top, is one of the most useful things.
- ([1:07:13](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=4033)) Gnomonkey ends the video saying these are all of the plugins he has, that he is sure he got some things wrong or players do them differently, but that the setup should mostly be accurate.

### [RuneLite plugins](../RuneLite_plugins.md)

- ([38:53](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2333)) Gnomonkey says Inventory Setups, an external Plugin Hub plugin, is so good and a very underrated plugin because few people know about it.
- ([38:53](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2333)) With Inventory Setups you pull out the items for a setup (for example the Hallowed Sepulchre), press the plus button and name it; with bank filtering, highlighting and only highlighting missing items on, the eyeball shows your setup, including the spellbook needed, and highlights the items in your bank.
- ([38:53](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2333)) Gnomonkey says he would make a setup for every Slayer task, and uses Inventory Setups for raids, Slayer, farm runs and everything.
- ([39:55](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2395)) The update button in Inventory Setups lets you add one more item to a setup and update it, and you can export a setup to the clipboard as text that a friend can paste in to import.

### [RuneLite plugins](../RuneLite_plugins.md)

- ([6:56](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=416)) The Anti-drag plugin used to be called Shift anti-drag and only worked while holding shift; it was changed to work in general as well.
- ([6:56](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=416)) Gnomonkey turns Anti-drag on without shift-only, keeps disable-on-control-press, and sets the drag delay to 10, which he says is a good number (eight to fifteen would be decent).
- ([7:28](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=448)) Anti-drag makes tasks like using a chisel on essence blocks at Blood Runecrafting easy since items do not switch position, and it is really helpful for gear switches at places like raids.
- ([38:23](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2303)) Gnomonkey keeps the Inventory Grid plugin on with a drag delay of 100 ms so he knows when he is dragging, which lines up with the Anti-drag plugin; he enables show grid but not highlighting the background, so a green grid shows where an item will go.

### [RuneLite plugins](../RuneLite_plugins.md)

- ([46:48](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2808)) Gnomonkey calls the Menu Entry Swapper one of the most useful plugins for sure.
- ([47:04](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2824)) Customizable shift click lets you configure what shift-click does, for example making shift-click use instead of drop, or making all your gear shift-click equip.
- ([47:30](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2850)) Gnomonkey sets shift-click in the bank to deposit all and withdraw all, so holding shift deposits everything and releasing it lets you quickly pull items out again.
- ([48:01](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2881)) Gnomonkey says bury swapping with Use is almost overpowered: you can make left click on bones be Use so you can use them on an altar over and over.
- ([48:01](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2881)) Gnomonkey sets the swapper to enter the Corrupted Gauntlet by default because he sees no reason to do the regular Gauntlet.
- ([48:34](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2914)) Shift-clicking NPC Contact repeats the last person you contacted, which Gnomonkey says is good for repairing pouches during ZMI Runecrafting, and teleport spells can be swapped on shift-click, for example to regular Varrock instead of the Grand Exchange if you have the diary.
- ([49:01](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2941)) Gnomonkey sets his shift buy and shift sell amounts to 50, which makes shopping very fast, especially on an Ironman.

### [RuneLite plugins](../RuneLite_plugins.md)

- ([26:49](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1609)) Gnomonkey says the GPU plugin is his favorite plugin in the whole game, because RuneScape normally only uses your processor and the plugin makes your graphics card take some of the power, reducing FPS drops.
- ([27:16](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1636)) Gnomonkey sets the GPU plugin draw distance to 90, which loads the entire chunk at a time so the game no longer slowly loads stuff as you walk.
- ([27:16](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1636)) Gnomonkey turns on anti-aliasing at MSAA x2 as the best balance, noting disabled is best for performance and x16 looks best but is worse for performance.
- ([27:47](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1667)) Gnomonkey sets the GPU UI scaling mode to nearest neighbor because it makes the inventory crystal clear instead of blurry.
- ([28:17](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1697)) Gnomonkey turns on fog in the GPU plugin, which colors the fog based on the area (RuneLite adds colored skyboxes when that plugin is on), and keeps compute shaders on, which lets you extend the draw distance.

### [Grand Exchange](../Grand_Exchange.md)

### [RuneLite plugins](../RuneLite_plugins.md)

- ([29:42](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1782)) Gnomonkey calls Ground Items probably his second favorite plugin and says that not being able to see highlighted drops is the main reason he does not play mobile often.
- ([30:15](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1815)) An asterisk in the Ground Items highlight list makes any words after (or before) it match, so dark totem with an asterisk covers all dark totem pieces, clue with an asterisk covers all clue scrolls, and an asterisk before fossil covers all fossils.
- ([30:42](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1842)) Gnomonkey hides items he does not want to see, such as vile ashes, coins, leaping fish and Suqah teeth.
- ([31:13](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1873)) Gnomonkey turns off do-not-hide-untradables so he gets a message when an untradable item drops, uses menu item quantities, and does not highlight tiles because it makes the game look ugly.
- ([31:13](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1873)) Gnomonkey notifies for highlighted drops and keeps only four items highlighted, relying on value settings to tell him when he gets an expensive drop; he hides any item worth 300 GP or less, kept low so stacks still show.
- ([32:41](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1961)) Collapse ground items is extremely helpful, since a pile of dropped items no longer lists every item in the right-click menu; Gnomonkey says it cleans things up so much.

- ([1:07:46](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=4066)) The XP tracker shows XP gained and XP an hour per skill and can be reset by right-clicking.
- ([1:07:46](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=4066)) The XP updater plugin sends your stats to sites such as CrystalMathLabs, Temple OSRS or Wise Old Man; Gnomonkey usually sends it to Wise Old Man because it is a new open source project he wants to support.

### [RuneLite plugins](../RuneLite_plugins.md)

- ([5:59](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=359)) The Ammo plugin shows the ammo you currently have equipped, which Gnomonkey says is definitely handy.
- ([6:29](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=389)) Animation smoothing is controversial and many people dislike it, but turning it on makes movement look buttery smooth; Gnomonkey says it is really important for the Hallowed Sepulchre specifically and otherwise mostly aesthetic.
- ([8:02](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=482)) The Attack styles plugin lets pures disable attack styles that would gain unwanted XP (for example turning off Defence so the longrange option returns on a crossbow); Gnomonkey is not a pure so he does not use it.
- ([8:28](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=508)) In the Bank plugin Gnomonkey shows Grand Exchange price and seed vault value on hover, and keeps exact bank value off because he finds it annoying.
- ([8:56](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=536)) Gnomonkey turns on the keyboard bank pin, which lets you type your bank pin on the keyboard, saves a ton of time and is also stream safe.
- ([8:56](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=536)) Bank tags let you add tags to items so searching rock shows minerals, or typing ZGS pulls out a tagged Zamorak godsword.
- ([9:56](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=596)) In the Blast Furnace plugin Gnomonkey turns on the top two options showing hitboxes on the conveyor belt and dispenser so you know exactly where to click.
- ([11:54](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=714)) Gnomonkey says for Chambers of Xeric the plugin should have a screenshot saved to clipboard for scouts and show the scout overlay inside every raid, and whitelisted rooms (such as tightrope or crabs) highlight green and blacklisted rooms red.
- ([12:53](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=773)) Gnomonkey turns on double hit spots and the cannonball info box in the Cannon plugin, which shows an overlay with how many cannonballs are in the cannon; it can also warn when the cannon runs out or is low.
- ([18:59](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1139)) The Cooking plugin shows XP an hour plus cooked and burnt counts and burn percentage, which Gnomonkey says is very handy.
- ([19:27](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1167)) Gnomonkey is iffy about the DPS counter, keeping it on his main; it shows damage per second for example while killing Hydra, but he does not look at it much.
- ([20:48](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1248)) Gnomonkey sets Default World to a low-ping world he likes (world 428, a 2k total world) so the client always defaults to it, rather than the last world logged in.
- ([21:56](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1316)) The Discord plugin shows on your Discord profile what you are doing and training, for example doing Hallowed Sepulchre runs and training Agility, so people can see whether you are available.
- ([22:26](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1346)) The Emojis plugin lets you put emojis in chat, which Gnomonkey says can be annoying depending on the person.
- ([23:00](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1380)) The Entity hider can prevent flickering at Ardougne Knights for Thieving, and can hide all players or hide the local player, which Gnomonkey says is nice for action shots in video making.
- ([24:57](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1497)) The Fishing plugin shows catch rate and highlights fishing spots, and Gnomonkey says to keep it on.
- ([24:57](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1497)) The Friend notes plugin lets you add a note to a friend's name that shows on hover, which Gnomonkey says is really handy.
- ([24:57](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1497)) The Fairy rings plugin lets you search for your location in a fairy ring using tags, which Gnomonkey says is super helpful and thought was in the original client.
- ([32:41](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1961)) Ground markers let you shift-click to mark tiles with colors that are retained if the remember-color setting is on, and Gnomonkey keeps drawing tiles on the minimap off because it clutters it.
- ([33:45](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2025)) The High scores plugin lets you look up people on the hiscores via a tab or right-click, and has a bounty hunter option to look up your target's stats.
- ([33:45](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2025)) The Hunter plugin shows color transitions for when traps are about to die and can notify on the maniacal monkey for AFK hunting.
- ([37:43](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2263)) The Interface styles plugin lets you change the UI to look like 2005 or 2006, change health bars to look like RS2 and even change left click, but Gnomonkey keeps it off.
- ([41:41](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2501)) Gnomonkey usually keeps Inventory viewer off, having used it only while streaming so he could hide the panel from his stream and still show his inventory.
- ([41:41](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2501)) The Item charges plugin shows an overlay of the charges on items and warns when a dodgy necklace or binding necklace breaks.
- ([42:20](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2540)) Item identification shows a short (first letter) or medium (full word) label over items, which Gnomonkey uses for herbs, saplings and seeds, since saplings are otherwise hard to tell apart.
- ([44:21](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2661)) The Kourend Library plugin shows where all the books are in the Arceuus Library, and the Kingdom of Miscellania plugin shows the favor in Miscellania; Gnomonkey keeps them on.
- ([44:45](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2685)) The Login screen plugin lets you change the login screen to any of the old ones or a custom image placed in the .runelite folder named login, and can turn off the fire or sync usernames between computers.
- ([46:16](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2776)) The Low detail plugin deletes a bunch of textures to make the game run faster, which Gnomonkey does not need.
- ([49:30](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=2970)) The Mining plugin shows a respawn timer on rocks, and the Motherlode Mine plugin shows information like gems found and amount mined.
- ([50:00](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3000)) The Music plugin can mute certain sounds, which Gnomonkey does not use much since he typically plays without sound, and the Mouse tooltips plugin shows tooltips on hover though Gnomonkey would not show spellbook tooltips.
- ([53:33](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3213)) The Newsfeed plugin shows a tab with RuneLite updates, JMod and Old School RuneScape tweets and game news.
- ([53:33](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3213)) Gnomonkey overrides the NMZ overlay because the normal one is ugly, turns on the power surge notification (usually the only one needed if training XP), an overload notification, and an absorption warning at around 100.
- ([54:03](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3243)) The Notes plugin lets you take notes saved between sessions.
- ([54:14](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3254)) Object markers differ from ground markers because they can mark objects, such as rubble or the entrance to a dungeon, which Gnomonkey did at Fossil Island.
- ([54:34](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3274)) With Opponent information, look up players on interaction shows a player's stats when they attack you, trade you, invite you to a raid or follow you, which Gnomonkey says can be really important information.
- ([55:55](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3355)) Gnomonkey uses Player indicators to highlight friends, friends chat members and Discord party members, and highlights others in red when he goes in the Wilderness so anyone not in his clan is immediately recognizable as a possible threat.
- ([55:59](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3359)) The Player owned house plugin shows icons on the minimap and whether burners are lit.
- ([57:20](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3440)) The Puzzle solver plugin is separate from the clue solver and shows how to solve puzzle boxes; Gnomonkey keeps it on but says players wanting to be purists can turn it off.
- ([57:42](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3462)) The Quest list plugin lets you hide completed quests and search quests in the quest tab.
- ([59:19](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3559)) The Report button plugin changes the time format displayed, for example UTC or Jagex HQ time or the date, and Gnomonkey keeps the login timer on.
- ([59:53](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3593)) Gnomonkey keeps the run energy plugin as text showing exact run energy instead of time left, and the Rune pouch plugin shows an icon of what runes are in the pouch.
- ([1:00:19](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3619)) The Runecrafting plugin highlights altars or the Abyss rift for what you are crafting, shows when a pouch degrades and highlights the dark mage.
- ([1:00:46](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3646)) The Screen markers plugin lets you draw boxes on your screen, handy for things like Construction.
- ([1:01:14](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3674)) The Screenshot plugin saves screenshots in the RuneLite folder for events such as pet drops, valuable drops, untradables or clan kicks.
- ([1:01:45](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3705)) The Skill calculator plugin shows current level and target level and how many actions are needed, for example about 4,000 amethyst to reach the next Mining level.
- ([1:02:30](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3750)) Gnomonkey calls the Skybox plugin one of his favorites and says to definitely turn it on because it looks really nice, with different colors by area, such as teal at the swamp and purple at ZMI.
- ([1:03:11](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3791)) Status bars shows bars with current health and prayer; Gnomonkey does not use them in resizable mode but says in fixed mode they replace the pillars and are really cool.
- ([1:04:33](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3873)) Gnomonkey keeps team capes off, the Tears of Guthix plugin on since the timers are so helpful, and the Tithe Farm plugin on.
- ([1:05:12](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3912)) The Twitch plugin integrates your Twitch chat into the regular chat; the Virtual levels plugin shows levels above 99 but Gnomonkey keeps virtual total level off to see his real total level.
- ([1:05:42](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3942)) The Wiki plugin can change left click to search or lookup, and the World map plugin adds tooltips such as fairy ring codes.
- ([1:06:08](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3968)) World hopper has hotkeys to hop to the previous or next world, and Gnomonkey says showing world ping is really important, with the current ping shown on screen and ping of all worlds on the world switcher, so you hop to low-ping worlds.
- ([1:06:36](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=3996)) The XP drop plugin changes how XP drops look, for example colors for prayers.
