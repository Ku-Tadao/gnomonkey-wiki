## Summary

Gnomonkey does a solo Chambers of Xeric Challenge Mode speedrun attempt on stream and finishes with a new personal best of 27:04, just short of the world record. The commentary is mostly live reaction, with a post-raid room-by-room review.

## Outline

- [0:00](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo) Intro and early commentary, previous attempt result
- [1:38](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo?t=98) Early raid rooms: Ice Demon with dragon warhammer, vanguards, thieving
- [10:51](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo?t=651) Mid-raid progression: pace check at 12:40 down to floor 3
- [20:07](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo?t=1207) Olm: hammer specs, brewing and a streak of zero hits
- [28:11](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo?t=1691) Post-raid review of each room and PB update

## Topics covered

### [Chambers of Xeric Challenge Mode](../Chambers_of_Xeric_Challenge_Mode.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo)) The Chambers of Xeric Challenge Mode solo world record is 26:38, held by rot salad, per the video description.
- ([1:29](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo?t=89)) Gnomonkey kills the Ice Demon in the Chambers of Xeric Challenge Mode speedrun with an Inquisitor's armour set and a dragon warhammer.
- ([12:45](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo?t=765)) Gnomonkey's pace at the time of reaching floor 3 was 12:40, which he considered not great; he says low twelves are nice and sub twelves are very fast.
- ([27:35](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo?t=1655)) Gnomonkey says that without the six zero hits in a row on lances in the middle of Olm and a bit less trolling, the run would likely have been a world record Olm at the very least.
- ([28:08](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo?t=1688)) Gnomonkey reviewed the raid backwards after finishing: Olm, Vasa and Guardians were disgusting (very good), Mystics pretty good, Muttadile kind of bad, Vespula, thieving and Tekton average, and Vanguards and Shamans bad.
- ([29:15](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo?t=1755)) Gnomonkey's 27:04 run was a new personal best in solo Challenge Mode; he reads out the change as 2751 to 2704.2 and says he is still ranked sixth.

### [Great Olm](../Great_Olm.md)

- ([22:22](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo?t=1342)) Gnomonkey kept health high and brewed heavily during Olm, saying not to chance anything while he had loads of brew.
- ([24:01](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo?t=1441)) Gnomonkey hit six zeros in a row on the lance phase of Olm during this run, which he called possibly one in a hundred thousand odds and the cause of losing four hits of time.
- ([25:55](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo?t=1555)) Gnomonkey says he needed to brew every single tick near the end of Olm to survive the final phase.
- ([26:27](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo?t=1587)) Gnomonkey calls the final Olm kill free once the damage landed, the freest it has ever been.

### [Gnomonkey](../Gnomonkey.md)

- ([27:20](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo?t=1640)) Gnomonkey notes his Chambers of Xeric Challenge Mode time at the end of the stream was 27:04.2, and he says it would literally have been sub twenty-seven without the trolling.
- ([29:15](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo?t=1755)) Gnomonkey streams on Twitch full time, per his own comment in the chat during the run.
