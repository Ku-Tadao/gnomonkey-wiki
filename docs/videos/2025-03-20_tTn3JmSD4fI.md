## Summary

Gnomonkey and his wife Hedron duo a Chambers of Xeric raid as Ironmen on the Hardcore Ironman and its backup account, using twin flame staves and a lot of chaotic prep. After a long, death-filled raid they get a Twisted bow drop on the HCIM at 115 kill count, skipping the Bofa grind and not needing Confliction gauntlets.

## Topics covered

### [Twisted bow](../Twisted_bow.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/tTn3JmSD4fI)) In the video description Gnomonkey says he got the Twisted bow on the HCIM and skipped the Bofa grind, with no Confliction gauntlets required.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/tTn3JmSD4fI)) The video description says Hedron was 60 kill count dry at Chambers of Xeric in general and gave Gnomonkey all her RNG for the drop.
- ([44:22](https://youtu.be/tTn3JmSD4fI?t=2662)) Gnomonkey received a Twisted bow from Chambers of Xeric on his Hardcore Ironman (HCIM) in a duo raid, at 115 kill count, which he considers his first Twisted bow.
- ([45:56](https://youtu.be/tTn3JmSD4fI?t=2756)) Gnomonkey said he did not even have a Xeric's talisman to put the Twisted bow on, and viewers reacted in chat that sending a raid with his wife may have been the key.

### [Chambers of Xeric](../Chambers_of_Xeric.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/tTn3JmSD4fI)) Gnomonkey and Hedron do a duo Chambers of Xeric raid as Ironmen, each on a Hardcore Ironman account, to celebrate the Fire cape on the backup HCIM with what Gnomonkey calls the ultimate friendship raid.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/tTn3JmSD4fI)) The video description says Gnomonkey had a few deaths and Hedron about 7 deaths total, with her playing on the backup HCIM.
- ([1:24](https://youtu.be/tTn3JmSD4fI?t=84)) Because they are Ironmen, the duo split the enhance potions: the Tekton and Vespula rooms each give one enhance potion, so they plan who takes each and when the other joins briefly.
- ([40:09](https://youtu.be/tTn3JmSD4fI?t=2409)) Gnomonkey says it was the longest Great Olm fight he had ever done, partly because he had no stamina and few restores left.
- ([44:22](https://youtu.be/tTn3JmSD4fI?t=2662)) The raid ends with a purple in the chest, the Twisted bow, after both players had died repeatedly; Gnomonkey said he died about seven times and had about 1.5k points.
- ([44:22](https://youtu.be/tTn3JmSD4fI?t=2662)) Gnomonkey says the raid was the freest of his life despite the many deaths and a lot of pre-raid dithering.

### Ironman raid supplies

- ([10:34](https://youtu.be/tTn3JmSD4fI?t=634)) Gnomonkey complains that as an Ironman, being unable to share raid potions with his partner is a pain, saying Jagex should just let people have the raid potions.
- ([11:04](https://youtu.be/tTn3JmSD4fI?t=664)) Gnomonkey says you cannot get a rake from the prep room scavengers, so you have to go to the farming room to rake the herb patches, which he finds frustrating.
- ([21:37](https://youtu.be/tTn3JmSD4fI?t=1297)) Gnomonkey says Ironmen can make weaker minus-tier raid potions at 52 Herblore (transcribed as 'Xerx minus') to make their own supplies.
- ([22:09](https://youtu.be/tTn3JmSD4fI?t=1329)) Gnomonkey says the herb patches in the raid give good herb XP, and complains that as an Ironman you cannot take items from your party members or share raid supplies.
- ([40:42](https://youtu.be/tTn3JmSD4fI?t=2442)) During the Olm phase, one of the duo says they lack the Farming level to plant a Buchu and are told to plant anyway, after Gnomonkey says he broke the plant.
