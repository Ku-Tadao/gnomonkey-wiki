Gnomonkey's 17:32 world record Fortis Colosseum speedrun, the record having passed through many hands in the previous week. The transcript is mostly silent gameplay with short reactions, while the description lays out the strategy: Northeast pillar for Venator bow setups on waves 1 to 6, then melee camping with a scythe from the south, using claws for Serpent Mage skips and a Dragon warhammer on Sol. He stresses it is for pure speed with no safety and not what he would do on a first cape.
