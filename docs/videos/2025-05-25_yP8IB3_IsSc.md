## Summary

Gnomonkey, who completed the Radiant Oathplate kit after a 20.5 hour stream, argues the Yama contract system was Jagex's biggest update failure: two of the five kit contracts were impossible at launch, drop rates and contract supply were a mess, and the contracts are gimmicky Tombs of Amascut-style invocations. He walks through each contract, praises base Yama, and criticises Oathplate's drop rate and the secrecy around tradable contracts, while asking people not to harass Jagex moderators.

### [Yama contracts](../Yama_contracts.md)

- ([1:57](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=117)) Before the launch changes, the solo rate was one in 20 for a dossier and one in 33 per contract type, so about 15% of dossiers gave a Radiant Oathplate kit contract, roughly one contract per 125 kills.
- ([2:24](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=144)) Gnomonkey needed about 200 contracts for his kit; at the original rates that would have been about 25,000 Yama kills and roughly 2,100 hours, giving him around 125 Oathplate pieces, 83 Soulflame horns and about 10 pets.
- ([3:27](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=207)) Jagex buffed the dossier rate from one in 20 to one in 17, then after the launch problems to one in 12, a very significant buff.
- ([3:27](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=207)) Jagex adjusted dossier weightings to reduce teleports and increase purifying sigil contracts (the Radiant kit contracts), while acquisition contracts with tradable rewards were adjusted to be obtained at roughly the same rate as before.
- ([3:54](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=234)) Contracts now drop three at a time, and the contract drop rate from Demons in the Chasm is three times more common.
- ([3:54](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=234)) Fulfilling the active sigil's condition makes contracts two times more common, and killing a demon with the special attack glyph (needing a spec, basically a purging staff) makes them five times more common instead of three.
- ([4:27](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=267)) Gnomonkey says the new drop rates are roughly five to six times faster and are what the contracts should have dropped at, and that he had no idea how difficult the contracts would be.
- ([4:55](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=295)) Gnomonkey says he sold half his contracts before launch expecting the content to be finished quickly and the price to drop, and was wrong.
- ([5:29](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=329)) Gnomonkey bought about 50 of each contract before release, paying three to five million each, a little over a billion in total; right after the change some dropped to around 100k to 500k each, and he lost roughly 300 million on bugged contracts.
- ([48:29](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=2909)) Gnomonkey says there is a 500k death fee on top of the contract cost, and that he lost over a billion on contracts in total.

### [Opinion: Yama contract system and tradable contracts](../Opinion_Yama_contract_system_and_tradable_contracts.md)

- ([6:27](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=387)) Gnomonkey says the contract system gated the Yama kit bosses off from normal players, which was what he was concerned about, and many very good players gave up.
- ([7:00](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=420)) Gnomonkey says the Yama contract system is far worse than Awakener's orbs, and he was amazed Jagex claimed it was better than awakened bosses.
- ([7:00](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=420)) Gnomonkey says the best way to get contracts is to kill the trash mobs (Demons in the Chasm) rather than Yama, so even skilled Ironmen would sit at demons for thousands of kills, which he finds backwards.
- ([7:27](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=447)) Gnomonkey says huge bot farms were farming contracts from the demons, wearing granite, a black mask and a Saradomin sword, on every world, which he calls redistribution to bots' pockets.
- ([7:58](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=478)) Mod Goblin replied on Reddit that Jagex heard the feedback loud and clear and did not foresee touching a similar system again unless worked through with players from the ground up, and that work on Yama contract rates was ongoing.
- ([51:58](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=3118)) Gnomonkey says Jagex should stop mystery-style releases: the contracts were not even known to be tradable until Mod Goblin accidentally mentioned it, and he thinks the idea would have been shot down if polled.
- ([52:29](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=3149)) Gnomonkey says it was wrong not to poll tradable contracts, and especially wrong to have guaranteed tradable drops from contracts that may have bugged mechanics.

### [Oathplate armour](../Oathplate_armour.md)

- ([8:56](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=536)) At Yama's release a 30% nerf was applied to the rare drop rates, from roughly one in 400 per piece of Oathplate to one in 600 per piece.
- ([9:56](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=596)) In solo, any piece of Oathplate is about 1 in 200 per kill (three pieces at one in 600 each), and the rates are doubled for duo.
- ([9:56](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=596)) Oathplate shards drop 12 per kill in solo and 6 in duo, and 450 shards let you forge any piece of Oathplate; at one in 17, that is about 620 kills to get another shot at a piece.
- ([10:23](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=623)) Gnomonkey estimates about 12 kills per hour in very good gear, so around 50 hours for full Oathplate, or 60 to 70 hours for a weaker player.
- ([10:23](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=623)) Gnomonkey says 60 to 70 hours is close to how long Bandos armour takes and is too short for Oathplate, saying Oathplate is far too common even after the nerf and it needs another nerf to somewhere over 100 hours.
- ([11:18](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=678)) Gnomonkey says the barrier to entry is low because Emberlight, not Scythe or Shadow, is best in slot, and synapses cost about 70 million, which he calls the lowest cost for a BIS weapon on any boss.
- ([11:45](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=705)) Gnomonkey shows Bandos chestplate falling from 25 million to 20 million and Torva platebody falling from 400 million in September 2024 to 270 million.
- ([12:18](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=738)) Gnomonkey says Oathplate is better than Torva most of the time (about 80%) because melee is mostly slash, leaving Torva mainly for Tombs of Amascut and the Torva helmet for maintaining max hits with the scythe.
- ([13:51](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=831)) Gnomonkey says Oathplate is endgame armour that knocks out the whole progression of Bandos and Torva, which he does not think is healthy and worries could be repeated with other new boss drops.
- ([14:48](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=888)) Gnomonkey says a grind like Oathplate's would make sense for something like Phosani's Nightmare (long eight-minute kills, constant one-shots, harmonised orb at one in 300), but he does not think Yama is the same.
- ([15:20](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=920)) Gnomonkey says the integrity of melee armour is ruined until the next best-in-slot melee armour, which could be three to five years away, and that Bandos and Nex-type armour have effectively been killed.

### [Yama contracts](../Yama_contracts.md)

- ([16:14](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=974)) In the Contract of Forfeit Breath, Yama drains the challenger's run energy passively, one run energy every four game ticks.
- ([16:47](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1007)) In the Contract of Forfeit Breath, Yama also drains stamina on dealing damage, even from glyph touches, and reaching zero run energy kills you; Yama is no longer weak to demonbane.
- ([16:47](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1007)) In Forfeit Breath, Gnomonkey says you cannot kill the void flares with demonbane even though several other contracts that say Yama is not weak to demonbane still allow it on void flares, which the contract does not state.
- ([17:50](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1070)) Gnomonkey calls Forfeit Breath one of the two easiest contracts, though still extremely difficult, taking him about 10 to 15 contracts.
- ([17:50](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1070)) For Forfeit Breath Gnomonkey used a food that heals about 23 health and gives 10 run energy to restore energy.
- ([18:23](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1103)) Gnomonkey says Forfeit Breath is a gimmick (do not let your energy hit zero), too gimmicky for a kit contract and a boring fight.
- ([18:23](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1103)) Gnomonkey says all the contracts give Yama very high health, the lowest being 3,000 and one with 4,000, plus higher defence, stats and damage.

### [Yama contracts](../Yama_contracts.md)

- ([18:57](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1137)) In the Contract of Glyphic Attenuation, glyphs protect you for a much shorter window (roughly four ticks) and are more punishing, and Yama is weak to crush only.
- ([19:23](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1163)) In Glyphic Attenuation, the challenger's prayer bonus inversely affects prayer drain: the higher your prayer bonus, the faster prayer drains but the more damage you deal.
- ([19:23](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1163)) Gnomonkey found Sunfire armour drained prayer every game tick in Glyphic Attenuation, so he swapped to gear with about 13 prayer bonus and did not feel any extra damage.
- ([20:18](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1218)) In Glyphic Attenuation the glyph hit is much weaker if Yama is far away, around 36 compared to a very large hit when Yama is close, so you lure the boss away from the glyph, wait for the special, step on it, then lure him away from the next glyph.
- ([20:45](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1245)) Glyphic Attenuation forces you to melee phase three and prevents the typical Donofly-style methods, which is the real challenge of the contract.
- ([21:13](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1273)) Gnomonkey says Glyphic Attenuation is fun but feels just like a Combat Achievement, as if hard mode Yama with a melee-only CA, and not like fighting an awakened Yama.

### [Yama contracts](../Yama_contracts.md)

- ([21:41](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1301)) In the Contract of Sensory Clouding, Yama's attacks have increased prayer penetration (Gnomonkey saw him hit 40 through melee prayer), Yama pursues forever including in phase three, and void flare explosions are lethal.
- ([22:15](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1335)) In Sensory Clouding the lethal void flares cannot be survived with a hot rock cake stall, Phoenix necklace or Redemption, so you must kill them in time; the challenger also has 50% reduced attack range.
- ([22:48](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1368)) The contract fights take roughly 6 to 15 minutes and Sensory Clouding took Gnomonkey about 12 minutes, one of the longer ones.
- ([22:48](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1368)) Gnomonkey says phases one and two of Sensory Clouding are a boring slog, but phase three, where Yama walks towards you while you run around catching void flares and dodging waves, was a great time.
- ([23:20](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1400)) Gnomonkey calls Sensory Clouding probably the second hardest contract and the one with a mechanic that completely changes how Yama works, saying it was close to a really good boss fight and would not work as a CA.

### [Yama contracts](../Yama_contracts.md)

- ([23:53](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1433)) Gnomonkey calls Divine Severance the worst designed contract; he says it may be the easiest but had a meltdown on stream when he saw the mechanics.
- ([24:25](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1465)) In Divine Severance, prayers are only active for one game tick and then disabled for five ticks, so every prayer must be tick perfect and turned on as you are hit; Yama no longer penetrates prayers.
- ([24:25](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1465)) In Divine Severance Yama spawns a void flare every ten seconds, void flare explosions are lethal and every one must be caught; Gnomonkey says nothing survives them and the boss heals to full if one goes off.
- ([25:20](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1520)) Gnomonkey says Divine Severance is Kephri medic with tick-perfect prayers, if a swarm going in killed you, and that it is easier than Forfeit Breath.
- ([26:50](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1610)) At release Divine Severance was impossible: the void flares had 140 health, the highest hit with purging staff in max mage was about 84, so each needed at least two hits, and three orbs spawned at around 800 boss health and exploded after 20 ticks.
- ([29:06](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1746)) Gnomonkey says Reddit theories such as holy water or Din's bulwark to beat the void flares were wrong; the contract was simply bugged.
- ([29:39](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1779)) Jagex hotfixed Divine Severance at 2 a.m.; the contract was acting as a duo encounter so void flares had twice the intended HP, even though the contract says the challenger must be alone.

### [Yama contracts](../Yama_contracts.md)

- ([32:12](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1932)) Gnomonkey calls Bloody Blows by far the hardest contract, about two times harder than the others including Sensory Clouding, and says it took about 80 of his contracts.
- ([32:43](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1963)) In Bloody Blows, Yama's phase one and two void flare special spawns twice as many orbs, and the flares are more resistant, so you are no longer guaranteed to hit or one-shot them and can splash.
- ([33:11](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=1991)) Bloody Blows gives both Yama and the challenger Wretched strength, the Dharok set effect: the lower your health, the higher your minimum hit, and at one health you are guaranteed your max hit; it does not raise accuracy.
- ([34:10](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=2050)) In Bloody Blows you must be at 15 health or lower in max mage to be guaranteed to one-shot void flares, and the purging staff still works even though the contract says Yama is no longer weak to demonbane.
- ([34:41](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=2081)) In Bloody Blows Yama chips you for a max hit of 12 through prayer in the last phase, so you must camp at 15 health or lower while being perfect, and Gnomonkey got hit for a 70 off-prayer ranged hit.
- ([36:14](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=2174)) In Bloody Blows Gnomonkey used Guthix Rest to raise health and rock cake to lower it, and says he chanced around six times.
- ([36:43](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=2203)) Gnomonkey says Bloody Blows should be hotfixed so you are guaranteed to hit the void flares (not necessarily max) and the chip damage through prayer should be max 10 instead of 12, so a hit does not force an unfixable health state.
- ([38:40](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=2320)) At release Bloody Blows was impossible: the raised minimum hit did not work on the void flares, so you would need to hit max hits twice on each set of flares, six times in a fight.
- ([39:07](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=2347)) Gnomonkey wasted many attempts on Bloody Blows, which was the first contract he tried and the most expensive, before it was found the mechanic was bugged.

### [Yama](../Yama.md)

- ([41:04](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=2464)) Gnomonkey says base Yama is amazing: it has a low skill floor so kills are easy but there is lots of room to improve with mechanics and fly methods people find daily.
- ([41:37](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=2497)) Gnomonkey and Sax found that stepping away from the boss on the second shadow special in phase two does double damage, which helps in tanking tech, and you can vengeance everything in the fight.
- ([48:57](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=2937)) Gnomonkey says the contracts feel like Toa invocations: the same boss fight with one extra gimmick, such as one-shots if you lose focus.
- ([49:27](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=2967)) Gnomonkey would have preferred one really hard contract with many new mechanics, such as Yama chasing you in phase three, over five similar fights with an invocation each.
- ([49:56](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=2996)) Gnomonkey says base Yama has depth like Chambers of Xeric, where getting a completion is easy but mastery takes a long time, and the contracts do not have this depth.

### Opinion: How to make bosses difficult

- ([44:36](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=2676)) Gnomonkey says the contracts are not the tough update the HLC wanted, because they were difficult in a brain-dead way, and compares the lack of a hype around contracts to the excitement of Coliseum attempts.
- ([46:04](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=2764)) Gnomonkey says there are three ways to make content harder: time punishment, execution difficulty and cost, like points of a triangle.
- ([46:04](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=2764)) Gnomonkey gives Inferno as an example of time punishment, where a first cape can take two to three hours and you may need two hours to get back to the hard part.
- ([46:34](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=2794)) Gnomonkey gives the end of Awakened Leviathan and Awakened Vardorvis as examples of execution difficulty, with fights of four to six minutes and the hard part after about three minutes.
- ([47:04](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=2824)) Gnomonkey says the Yama contracts went for long fights of up to 15 minutes, huge health pools and defence, one-shots if you lose focus and high entry cost, not timing-based mechanics.
- ([48:03](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=2883)) Gnomonkey says the contracts increase execution difficulty in the least fun way: not juggling many mechanics but staying focused long enough, which he compares to what people complain about in Tombs of Amascut.
