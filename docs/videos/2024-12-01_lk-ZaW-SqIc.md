## Topics covered

### [Echo Hunllef](../Echo_Hunllef.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/lk-ZaW-SqIc)) Gnomonkey beat Echo Corrupted Gauntlet in Leagues 5 with no inventory, and also did no armor, no food, and no food and no armor runs back to back.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/lk-ZaW-SqIc)) Gnomonkey got the Echo Corrupted Gauntlet orb at around 7 to 8 KC and the Crystal Blessing around 18 Echo kills.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/lk-ZaW-SqIc)) Gnomonkey says he did melee-only attempts with and without armor but they were not fun, though still possible for someone who hates themselves enough.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/lk-ZaW-SqIc)) Gnomonkey considers Echo Corrupted Gauntlet and Echo Sol among the best upgrades to existing content and wishes they could be added as additional levels in the main game.
- ([1:00](https://youtu.be/lk-ZaW-SqIc?t=60)) Gnomonkey says that as soon as the prayer drops in the Echo Corrupted Gauntlet fight, you want to use the wrong weapon to avoid being hit.
- ([1:37](https://youtu.be/lk-ZaW-SqIc?t=97)) Gnomonkey killed Echo Hunllef with no armor in two tries.
- ([5:44](https://youtu.be/lk-ZaW-SqIc?t=344)) Gnomonkey explains that Echo Hunllef hits his normal maximum but as two splats, and that Redemption procs on the first splat even if it would kill you, so with 10 health and two 9s you would live.
- ([7:43](https://youtu.be/lk-ZaW-SqIc?t=463)) Gnomonkey killed Echo Hunllef with no food and no armor, saying he got super lucky and did not have to Redemption proc even once, which he found a little dissatisfying.

### [Leagues V: Raging Echoes](../Leagues_V_Raging_Echoes.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/lk-ZaW-SqIc)) Gnomonkey switched his final Leagues 5 region to Tirannwn (the Elf lands) because he saw how horrible the Echo Cerberus fight was and did not want to be stuck somewhere with a bad boss.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/lk-ZaW-SqIc)) Gnomonkey's next goal after this video was to push for the Last Stand relic and go for a 1000+ damage Dharok bomb hit on Echo Sol.
