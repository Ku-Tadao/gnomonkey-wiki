## Summary

Gnomonkey fights the Whisperer twice in his 'Wiki Guides' series: first with the OSRS Wiki's worst-allowed mage downgrades (Infinity hat, Amulet of power, etc.), then with a budget setup from the Gearscape tool. The worst-gear kill took 448.6 seconds and the budget setup about 3:50, which he found a smaller gain than the stat difference suggested. He also criticises the wiki's downgrades as unrealistic for accounts with Desert Treasure 2 done.

## Topics covered

### [Whisperer](../Whisperer.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8)) The Whisperer requires the grandmaster quest Desert Treasure 2, which Gnomonkey says should make many low-tier gear downgrades irrelevant.
- ([1:23](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=83)) In Gnomonkey's setup the powered staff has nine tile range, the same as a Sanguinesti staff or Toxic trident, so he did not need freezes.
- ([1:23](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=83)) Gnomonkey uses a powered staff on the Whisperer so he can use thralls, and drinks forgotten brews for a 10 magic level boost.
- ([1:23](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=83)) Gnomonkey uses the Rune thrownaxe special attack to kill the Whisperer's Lost Souls.
- ([3:14](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=194)) Gnomonkey says the Whisperer's attack that bounces back only hits two targets rather than all three.
- ([3:24](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=204)) Gnomonkey says awakening sanity loss is more concerning than the Whisperer's damage, and the end of the fight is the only scary part.
- ([6:54](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=414)) In the budget setup Gnomonkey's expected time to kill was 140 seconds, which the calculator says should be about two and a half minutes without downtime.
- ([7:30](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=450)) Gnomonkey says the Whisperer is a boring boss in general and that it feels slow without Shadow, since the boss is balanced around having Shadow.
- ([8:31](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=511)) Gnomonkey ranks the Whisperer roughly third among the Desert Treasure 2 bosses, saying Duke Sucellus is definitely worse.
- ([9:45](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=585)) Gnomonkey finds the Whisperer's reduced damage taken while it does its special attack to be what makes the fight annoying.
- ([10:30](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=630)) Gnomonkey suggests mage gear helps little against the Whisperer because there is so much downtime on the boss, so mage just does not do well here until you have Shadow.

### [Wiki Guides challenge series](../Wiki_Guides_challenge_series.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8)) In this episode Gnomonkey fights the Whisperer using the worst possible gear downgrades listed on the OSRS Wiki's Whisperer page.
- ([0:29](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=29)) The wiki's Whisperer downgrades include Infinity hat and Farseer, a prayer mitre as a downgrade for the Farseer helmet, an Amulet of power instead of a glory, a skill cape instead of a Mage Arena 1 cape, a combat bracelet instead of Barrows gloves, Wizard boots and a Seers ring.
- ([0:55](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=55)) Gnomonkey notes the wiki lists Mage's book as a downgrade to Malediction ward even though Mage's book is more expensive and harder to obtain.
- ([1:49](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=109)) Gnomonkey says the wiki's highest-tier Whisperer gear is fairly accurate (Slayer helm or Ancestral, max mage, Shadow, Eternal boots, Torm, Magus), though Eldritch nightmare staff special over a Cursed sceptre is questionable.
- ([7:34](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=454)) Gnomonkey says the point of the series is not to attack the wiki, and that some people were offended; he says it is a fun series meant to shed light on bad downgrade entries so the wiki can clean them up.
- ([9:38](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=578)) The first Whisperer kill in the wiki's worst downgraded gear took 448.6 seconds (about 7:28), which Gnomonkey calls not that bad all considered.

### [Whisperer](../Whisperer.md)

- ([0:29](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=29)) Gnomonkey's downgraded setup has a max hit of 26 on the Whisperer with an Amulet of power, and the amulet's effect decays instantly.
- ([5:14](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=314)) Gnomonkey used the Gearscape budget tool with a 9 million gp budget; its setup uses void mage and Infinity boots and reaches a max hit of 38 after boosting.
- ([5:29](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=329)) Gnomonkey was surprised the budget tool recommends Infinity boots (about 400k) and a Sears ring, but says accuracy matters so the choice is plausible.
- ([5:38](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=338)) Gnomonkey says Moon (Lunar-themed) gear is turning out more okay than he expected, and that it is very cheap and great for budgets.
- ([7:37](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=457)) Gnomonkey notes the Gearscape budget tool lets you export the generated setup straight to a bank tab.
- ([9:30](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=570)) The wiki's worst downgrade setup had a mage attack bonus of 143 and a 38 max hit; Gnomonkey says the budget setup had about 50% more magic accuracy and 50% higher max hit.
- ([9:46](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=586)) The budget setup killed the Whisperer in about 3 minutes 50 seconds, roughly a minute faster and about 20% faster than the worst-gear kill, whereas Gnomonkey expected about two minutes saved.
- ([9:58](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=598)) Gnomonkey says that despite the budget setup having 13 extra max hits, the improvement was smaller than he expected, and speculates bad gear is just bad at the Whisperer.

### [Opinion: OSRS Wiki gear recommendations](../Opinion_OSRS_Wiki_gear_recommendations.md)

- ([12:30](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=750)) Gnomonkey says the wiki's Desert Treasure 2 boss pages recommend downgrades such as Amulet of power or a God mitre when a Farseer helm is missing, which he calls insane for account progression.
- ([13:00](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=780)) Gnomonkey criticises the wiki for listing Mage's book as a downgrade to Malediction ward and a combat bracelet when Barrows gloves are not owned on an account with Desert Treasure 2 done.
- ([13:27](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=807)) Gnomonkey says some wiki downgrades are reasonable: the boot recommendations (an account might lack them) and the Seers ring make sense.
- ([13:27](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=807)) Gnomonkey says listing a Mage Arena 1 cape as not the minimum is silly because an account with Desert Treasure 2 unlocked would have to be a maniac not to own a regular god cape.
