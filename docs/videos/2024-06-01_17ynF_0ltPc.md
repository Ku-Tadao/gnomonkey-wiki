## Summary

Gnomonkey completes a Tombs of Amascut raid level 500 using the worst possible downgrade gear from the wiki's three recommended setups, which took almost eight hours of attempts over two streams and a 72-minute clear. He then builds a budget setup of roughly equal price around Mixed hide armour and Osmumten's fang and clears the same raid in about 47.5 minutes, concluding Mixed hide is a new meta for first 500s. He says this is likely the last Wiki Guides video.

### [Challenge: Tombs of Amascut 500 in wiki downgrade gear](../Challenge_Tombs_of_Amascut_500_in_wiki_downgrade_gear.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc)) Gnomonkey describes this as by far the hardest Tombs of Amascut challenge he has completed, taking almost 8 hours of total attempts across two streams, with no fang, no Shadow and not even an Occult necklace.
- ([0:27](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=27)) Gnomonkey's downgrade melee setup includes a Nezi helmet, a Glory, an Artie cloak, a Fighter torso and Blessed chaps.
- ([0:27](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=27)) For ranging he wears a SERP helmet, which Gnomonkey says is worse than no helmet at all because of a negative five ranged bonus, even though it means bringing a better melee helmet that is only used for the worse ranged bonus.
- ([0:27](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=27)) The wiki downgrades Ruby bolts to Diamond bolts, so Gnomonkey uses Diamond bolts and an Archer's ring switch for ranged, and Blood bark with a regular Trident for magic.
- ([0:57](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=57)) The setup was so confusing that Gnomonkey used inventory tags: pink items are both melee and mage and blue items are ranged and mage; he says this is the only case where inventory tags make sense, when your gear is not what it appears to be.
- ([1:26](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=86)) The wiki specifies a blowpipe spec as the worst downgrade for the range spec and a dragon dagger as the worst downgrade for the melee spec, so Gnomonkey allows himself to use those.
- ([1:26](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=86)) Gnomonkey also allows himself a Keris even though the wiki does not mention it, because he does not think the 500 is possible without one; he brings one divine melee potion, one divine super combat and one divine ranging, with the blowpipe only for spec.
- ([1:56](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=116)) Gnomonkey had attempted this once on a prior stream and died in the Wardens' P1 due to not having enough restore; he found Divine super combat does not last long enough (20 minutes is not enough) and that leftover Divine ranging is wasted.
- ([2:24](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=144)) His plan is Baba, Kephri, Zebak then Akkha, using both salts on Akkha because he needs the run energy for four-tick butterfly, and using divine ranging to get through Zebak; he says Akkha without butterfly is not possible because it uses too much restore.
- ([2:58](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=178)) He runs Hardcore, Walk the path, Pathfinder and Deadly prayer, typical for a 500 but with no Overly Draining and no Keep Back, because chip damage adds up fast whenever Akkha swaps styles.
- ([4:55](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=295)) Gnomonkey says the footage was recorded several weeks before upload, when the monkey room was still difficult, and that he completed it over two separate streams totalling about eight hours.
- ([5:25](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=325)) Gnomonkey says each room takes an eternity: Baba and Kephri together took about half an hour, drastically draining supplies and requiring over an hour of perfect flicking.
- ([5:25](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=325)) Gnomonkey says the biggest problem was the Wardens' P1, where most deaths happened, because he simply did not have enough DPS, even with a blowpipe, to pass that DPS check.
- ([24:36](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1476)) The downgrade-gear 500 took about 72 to 73 minutes, after six and a half hours of stream attempts, and gave no purple; Gnomonkey says he really expected the Shadow.
- ([25:09](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1509)) Gnomonkey prices the downgrade setup at 39 mil, calling it fairly cheap but terrible, and says you could not choose worse value items if you tried.

### [Mixed hide armour](../Mixed_hide_armour.md)

- ([26:39](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1599)) Gnomonkey describes Mixed hide armour as Hunter armour that costs about 10k and has strength bonus and stab bonus, both of which he says are very good.
- ([26:39](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1599)) Gnomonkey says Mixed hide is best in slot below Masori for a budget setup and better than Karil's in every way, describing it as black dragonhide with strength bonus and stab bonus.
- ([27:39](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1659)) Gnomonkey says Mixed hide loses one max hit behind Bandos, but beats Bandos with a fang against something with very high stab defence.
- ([33:52](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=2032)) Gnomonkey says Mixed hide might genuinely be better than Masori for a first 500 because you get so many restores.
- ([34:26](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=2066)) Gnomonkey says he thinks Mixed hide is the new meta for first 500s, better than Masori, and notes it is the only armour set that matches the Quiver.
- ([35:00](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=2100)) Gnomonkey says Mixed hide has stab, strength and ranged bonus and lets you carry far more Sanfew serums; he says Torva helps face-tank Kephri's hatchlings and zero prayer, but Mixed hide lets him take about six more Sanfew.
- ([36:54](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=2214)) After first thinking the lower defence of Mixed hide made Akkha too dangerous, Gnomonkey concluded the Mixed hide setup made the 500 feel like a 150.
- ([37:54](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=2274)) In the budget run Gnomonkey had seven and a half restores, double what he normally takes.
- ([38:21](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=2301)) Gnomonkey says Mixed hide is the meta for sub-75 tier gear in Tombs of Amascut.
- ([42:53](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=2573)) Gnomonkey finished the Mixed hide budget run with two restores left over.

### [Challenge: Budget Tombs of Amascut 500 with Mixed hide](../Challenge_Budget_Tombs_of_Amascut_500_with_Mixed_hide.md)

- ([25:09](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1509)) Gnomonkey builds his own budget setup of roughly the same value as the 39 mil downgrade setup (he says about 35 mil), which he expects to be about 50% more effective, and gives it a single attempt.
- ([26:10](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1570)) The budget setup uses Blood bark, Book of the Dead as the offhand, a Trident of the Swamp (three max hit higher than the regular Trident), an Occult necklace, a very cheap cape and Mixed hide armour.
- ([26:39](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1599)) The budget setup also uses a Lightbearer, dragon boots, gloves, a Blood fury and a Quiver, which Gnomonkey says are free or cheap.
- ([27:10](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1630)) Gnomonkey uses a Hunter's sunlight crossbow (about 3k, a good MSB with better range) instead of a blowpipe, and says his range switch is a two-way, with 170 ranged accuracy compared with 184 for the Dragon crossbow.
- ([27:39](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1659)) He uses a bone dagger as a very good cheap defence-lowering spec and a DDS for the core, plus Osmumten's fang as his main melee weapon.
- ([28:11](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1691)) Gnomonkey found it is bugged to wear a Quiver in the Tombs of Amascut Tombs setup, which costs him a slot, but he says it does not matter because he has infinite restore.
- ([30:48](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1848)) Gnomonkey says killing things fast is better than blocking damage, because if they die they stop hitting you.
- ([31:50](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1910)) Gnomonkey says he should have brought chinchompas for Kephri's room and regrets it.
- ([32:21](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1941)) Gnomonkey says thralls path towards the southwest tile only, so a thrall he summoned while attacking Kephri failed to find the boss.
- ([43:22](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=2602)) Gnomonkey completed the 500 in the budget setup in 47 minutes 30 seconds, compared with 1 hour 12 minutes in the downgrade setup, and says the budget setup cost about 32 mil.
- ([43:22](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=2602)) The budget run made about 1.1 mil in loot, more than the downgrade run, though Gnomonkey got no purple again.
- ([43:54](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=2634)) Gnomonkey says the budget run avoided the mental tax of flicking an extra half hour with fewer Sanfew and that every room was free, calling a clear under 50 minutes for under 30 mil unbelievable.
