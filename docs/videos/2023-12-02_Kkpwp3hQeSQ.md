### Blob cycle (Chambers of Xeric)

- ([4:42](https://youtu.be/Kkpwp3hQeSQ?t=282)) Standing on the Addy tile lets Gnomonkey automatically two-tick blobs, according to a chat answer.
- ([4:42](https://youtu.be/Kkpwp3hQeSQ?t=282)) There is one spawn where a blob sees you late, but it is two ticks before it sees you, so it is back on a two-tick cycle by default; that is the one behind the north pillar.
- ([5:11](https://youtu.be/Kkpwp3hQeSQ?t=311)) To correct the blob cycle, you check whether they attack: with two ticks on mage and two ticks on range, if you swap from mage to range and a blob attacks you, it is hitting on the second tick of your prayer rather than the first, so it is off-tick and you need to stall a tick.
