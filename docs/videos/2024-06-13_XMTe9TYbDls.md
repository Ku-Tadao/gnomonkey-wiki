## Summary

A long Inferno teaching stream redone after the Project Rebalance changes, aimed at first-cape attempts. Gnomonkey explains the revised gear (Occult nerf, Echo boots, Augury max hit, no autocast delay), the plugins he uses, and then plays through waves explaining blobs, melees, mages, pillars, phantom barrages, corner traps, Jads and Zuk. His main takeaway is that mage accuracy barely matters, max hits and prayer/ranged defence do, and that fighting the wave (not chasing nibblers) keeps you alive.

## Topics covered

### [Inferno](../Inferno.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls)) Gnomonkey redid his Inferno teaching stream after the Project Rebalance changes because he felt Inferno was different enough to warrant it, using a realistic setup that is not absolute max (no Ellie or Eldritch assumed) and no quiver.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls)) Gnomonkey says most people doing a first Inferno cape will not own a Dizana's quiver, and recommends doing Inferno before getting the quiver.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls)) The video description says that after Mage Rebalance you still bring Virtus top, Crystal shield, a 15% damage mage staff and Occult for Inferno, and that a budget version can bring Bow of Faerdhinen instead of Masori/Twisted bow because they are within 15% of each other.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls)) The video description says Gnomonkey does not believe in using Blood or Smoke scepters on first capes, thinking the DPS you lose is worse than the tiny overheal you get, and that killing things faster means less damage taken.
- ([6:13](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=373)) Gnomonkey recommends the Masori mask over tank helmets in Inferno because damage means killing things faster so they hit you less, and says he dislikes the Justiciar faceguard for making life hard for no reason.
- ([7:41](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=461)) Gnomonkey calls bringing Justiciar pieces or a Saradomin godsword to Inferno trolling and a waste of precious inventory slots; Justiciar only helps rotate south and brews also let you rotate south, since while chugging brews you tick-eat every hit.
- ([8:15](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=495)) Gnomonkey brings one divine ranging potion and one regular ranging potion to Inferno: the two can be used during waves 50-60 and then again for Jads and Zuk, the divine keeps range boosted so Jads and Zuk die faster, and the regular one lets you re-pot after brewing.
- ([8:47](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=527)) A typical Inferno inventory in the video is eight brews (very common), a bunch of restores, and one stamina potion for Zuk; he says a regular Bastion is the same as a regular ranging potion once you have brewed.
- ([9:20](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=560)) For Inferno quick prayers, start with Piety-less ranged plus Rigor, then swap to mage after the mage wave starts.
- ([9:53](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=593)) Gnomonkey highly recommends tagging every single NPC in Inferno with NPC Indicators so you know what space they take up for pillar stacks, and using true tile.
- ([25:42](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1542)) Gnomonkey says bats are always the priority when they are hitting you because every hit drains run energy, then melees, then majors; he also says to kill bats from the safe spot.
- ([32:15](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1935)) Gnomonkey says wave 31 is one of the hardest Inferno waves and that anything with a double blob is hard, so players should not be too hard on themselves for dying to it.
- ([33:17](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1997)) Gnomonkey always starts each wave on the same tile at the north pillar because you can always reach nibblers from it and it avoids the majority of spawns up top.
- ([34:15](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2055)) Gnomonkey says time after you have solved a wave does not matter but time before you have solved it matters very much, so if you can kill the mage first while solving a wave you should.
- ([42:04](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2524)) From wave 50 onward a mage and ranger spawn each wave with no attack delay, so the ranger can hit instantly off spawn and ranged defence matters a lot; Gnomonkey puts away his Virtus top and casts in his Masori top for its extra ranged defence.
- ([43:34](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2614)) After wave 50 Gnomonkey gives nibblers one cast and then solves the wave first; he says you should not tank full hits or run out of position anymore and your health is more precious.
- ([55:50](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=3350)) Gnomonkey says the wave before the double ranger wave should be done at 99 health because two rangers could hit about 92 combined right off spawn.
- ([1:04:15](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=3855)) Gnomonkey concludes that Inferno is not that bad and that the big changes are no autocast delay and a lower mage max hit.
- ([1:04:46](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=3886)) Gnomonkey advises people learning Inferno not to be afraid to use brews, saying you are not going to get to Zuk on your first try and spamming brew is good for learning.

### Virtus armour

- ([1:00](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=60)) Gnomonkey says Torm is never worth bringing in Inferno because Virtus legs give the same max hit.
- ([1:00](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=60)) Bringing Virtus legs gives two extra max hits on ice barrage and one extra max hit on blood barrage compared with a mage damage staff and non-Virtus legs.
- ([1:00](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=60)) Gnomonkey does not think Virtus legs are worth bringing to Inferno because they barely change the nibbler one-shot chance, though he says Eldritch users with near-unlimited prayer might consider them.
- ([1:31](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=91)) Against a melee, Gnomonkey's mage accuracy is 66.8% without Virtus legs and 73.5% with them, which he says is about 20 more DPS and only matters for healing quickly.
- ([2:34](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=154)) In Gnomonkey's calculations, mage accuracy against nibblers is 96.39% without Virtus legs and 94.4% in later waves where he is not starting in Virtus.
- ([3:04](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=184)) Gnomonkey's accuracy against a melee is about 58% with a mage top and 41% with the top off, which is why the mage top is brought for healing.
- ([42:04](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2524)) From wave 50 Gnomonkey swaps from his Virtus top to his Masori top for casting because of its higher ranged defence.

- ([4:09](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=249)) Gnomonkey says that if you own Echo boots and it is a first Inferno cape, they are the best boots option with no question.
- ([4:38](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=278)) Gnomonkey ranks Echo boots first for Inferno, Pegasian boots a great next choice, then Eternal boots which give a max hit on your mage with no negatives; he calls devout boots irrelevant now.
- ([4:38](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=278)) Gnomonkey says the most important things to maximise in Inferno are prayer bonus and ranged defence, by far.
- ([5:39](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=339)) Gnomonkey says he hates Echo boots and thinks they are really dumb in general, but that Inferno is the one use case where they are really good.

- ([7:13](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=433)) Gnomonkey says Lightbearer combined with Eldritch lets you spec even more and get more prayer, while the Ring of the Gods is a good choice too; you can get the cape with either but Lightbearer slightly edges it out.

### [Crystal shield](../Crystal_shield.md)

- ([3:04](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=184)) The Crystal shield has -10 mage accuracy bonus, and removing it against a nibbler only raises accuracy from 96.39% to 96.69%, a 0.3% difference that Gnomonkey says is irrelevant.
- ([6:13](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=373)) Gnomonkey says Ellie (Elidinis' ward) is an upgrade to the Crystal shield but the Crystal shield is almost exactly the same, so you do not need to wait for an Ellie.
- ([23:38](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1418)) Gnomonkey says a common mistake is taking the Crystal shield off for 0.3% mage accuracy on early waves and then carrying that habit through every wave, ending up never using it; he says to keep it equipped if you are maging.

### [Jal-Nib](../Jal-Nib.md)

- ([2:03](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=123)) Nibblers have a mage level of 15 and -20 mage defence, so they have less mage defence than a goblin.
- ([2:34](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=154)) Gnomonkey says mage accuracy does nothing for nibblers (96% hit chance, splashing extremely rare) and that what matters is max hit, since the higher your max hit the better the chance of one-shotting all the nibblers.
- ([16:43](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1003)) A six-nibbler stack has 80 health, so it is worth phantom barraging if you are ever low on health going into it.
- ([21:32](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1292)) Gnomonkey uses split XP and says a 30 XP drop tells him that a cast has killed all the nibblers in one hit.
- ([22:03](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1323)) You can phantom barrage the corpses of nibblers, and Gnomonkey phantoms the middle nibbler to check whether a clump of nibblers has died.
- ([44:29](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2669)) Gnomonkey says nibblers are maybe the most deadly NPC in Inferno because chasing them gets people killed, comparing it to chasing a ball into the road in front of a semi-truck.
- ([44:58](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2698)) Gnomonkey says in later waves a nibbler on the west pillar is not a threat and he would be fine losing the west pillar.
- ([46:32](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2792)) In the video one nibbler did about 10 damage to a pillar with over 200 health while Gnomonkey sat there, and he says it would take about eight minutes to destroy it.
- ([49:31](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2971)) Nibblers on your north pillar are a priority because you cannot drop your north pillar, but it is easy to protect since you can hit nibblers from behind the pillar.

### [Jal-Ak](../Jal-Ak.md)

- ([12:20](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=740)) Blobs scan your current protection prayer when you step out from the pillar and launch the opposite style attack three ticks later, on a six-tick attack cycle, reading you every third tick, according to Gnomonkey.
- ([13:23](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=803)) Swapping your protection prayer every game tick (alternating) fully protects you from blobs; Gnomonkey says the timing is to click the next prayer as soon as the current prayer light turns off, keeping both lit.
- ([13:56](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=836)) Gnomonkey calls blobs moving medkits worth 40 health because you can phantom barrage the blob and its bloblets with blood barrage to heal.
- ([14:51](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=891)) Gnomonkey says three bloblet targets can be phantom barraged at once, giving about 40 health per bloblet stack.
- ([25:09](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1509)) Gnomonkey says one-tick alternating prayers is the solve for a blob plus any other monster, always.
- ([26:38](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1598)) Gnomonkey says each bloblet does the same damage as a bat hitting you, so kill them and do not let them sit there hitting you.
- ([27:12](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1632)) Gnomonkey says blobs give the most health of any target (about 40 on a phantom), with rangers about 20.
- ([30:42](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1842)) Blobs can melee you about one time in three if you stand next to them, but a blob melee hit is not a big deal as it maxes the same number.
- ([31:46](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1906)) With two blobs, Gnomonkey says the situation does not change: watch the ranger or mage's animation, start alternating prayers when it begins, and you can flick everything regardless of the number of blobs.
- ([36:11](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2171)) Gnomonkey says you cannot phantom barrage blobs on the south pillar.
- ([38:37](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2317)) On wave 48 (a double blob wave) Gnomonkey says you do not need to look at the blobs, only at the mage: as soon as it flickers, start alternating.
- ([40:58](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2458)) When you miss a blob attack, Gnomonkey says to default to praying mage because the mage attack is about two times more dangerous than the ranged attack, and to always pray mage against bloblets.

### [Jal-Imkot](../Jal-ImKot.md)

- ([16:43](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1003)) Melees only attack with melee damage but after about 50 game ticks they dig to you, then have a brief cooldown before meleeing again; tanking a melee hit resets the dig cooldown.
- ([19:01](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1141)) Melees have about a 25% chance of hitting you with a max hit of 49 because they have a very low attack level, so Gnomonkey says you can tank them in a pinch while killing them.
- ([28:05](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1685)) Gnomonkey says you can reset a melee's dig timer by praying melee and tanking a hit so it does not dig.
- ([29:39](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1779)) Gnomonkey says melees cannot dig while they are attacking you, so off-ticking a melee is a great idea.
- ([38:11](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2291)) Gnomonkey says melees and double blob waves are what kill most people in Inferno because people do not know the corner traps and safe spots, and that once you learn them melees are irrelevant.
- ([40:30](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2430)) When a melee digs, Gnomonkey says to just turn and shoot it with blowpipe specs and it dies, instead of panicking and stopping damage.
- ([50:33](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=3033)) Melees have a big delay before they start hitting after digging, so you can stand in melee range and then step to the corner-trap position.
- ([52:11](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=3131)) When you are under a melee, Gnomonkey says not to click anything and just wait patiently or it will kill you.

### [Corner trapping](../Corner_trapping.md)

- ([11:20](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=680)) Gnomonkey says corner traps work on a line system one tile out from the pillar rather than on exact tiles, so he does not mark them.
- ([17:37](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1057)) Gnomonkey explains that for NPCs without smart pathing the brain is their south-west tile, and OSRS paths east or west before north or south and refuses to path north if the only other option is east or west, so a monster whose south-west tile is blocked cannot reach you.
- ([19:30](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1170)) To corner trap a digging melee you have to be touching its corner, otherwise it can path in.
- ([26:38](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1598)) Gnomonkey says people only corner trap melees but you can corner trap every single mob, including mages and blobs, and that a monster against a pillar cannot line of sight through it.
- ([37:10](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2230)) Gnomonkey says monsters work the same as pillars for corner trapping, so when attacking something you want to stand one tile out from it as if it were a pillar.
- ([50:00](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=3000)) Using a monster as a pillar does not work against melees on the north side of the pillar, but you can still corner trap melees on the pillars themselves.
- ([51:38](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=3098)) Gnomonkey says any monster with a ranged attack or projectile (Jads, rangers, blobs, mages) can melee you on the corners, while melee monsters cannot because they only use melee.

### [Phantom barrage](../Phantom_barrage.md)

- ([10:51](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=651)) Even with the autocast delay removed, Gnomonkey says you still have to manually cast to make a phantom barrage work.
- ([11:20](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=680)) A phantom barrage is manually casting at an NPC after it has died (turned red with the HP tracker); because you cast at the corpse the blood barrage still heals you full health.
- ([11:50](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=710)) Phantoms only work from eight tiles away or further, because the cast travel time has to be long, and you then have to manually cast at the corpse after it dies.
- ([14:51](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=891)) You can phantom barrage three bloblet targets at once, and Gnomonkey says to treat it as the main way you will heal in Inferno.
- ([35:39](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2139)) A phantom barrage does not work if the mage is not pulled in fully against the pillar, so Gnomonkey says to let it pull in first.
- ([52:43](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=3163)) Gnomonkey says you can still phantom barrage on a blob plus ranger stack if the ranger is in the back.

### [Prayer flicking](../Prayer_flicking.md)

- ([23:38](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1418)) Gnomonkey describes lazy flicking for Inferno monsters: click the protection prayer as soon as the monster's animation snaps back to normal, and these attack on a four-tick cycle.
- ([24:09](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1449)) Gnomonkey says in Inferno, as the animation starts you have already been hit, with Jad being the only exception where you are hit once the projectile is on screen.
- ([24:36](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1476)) Gnomonkey says learning lazy flicking timing helps you off-tick monsters and know when damage actually hits you, which matters when you are praying against both a mage and a ranger.
- ([29:06](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1746)) Gnomonkey says you do not need to one-tick flick to get through Inferno; it just speeds things up, and he did not know how to one-tick flick on his own first cape.
- ([33:47](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2027)) A mage's light flickering underneath it shows it has attacked, the same cue as a ranger hunkering down, and mages have a four-tick attack delay so you flick every fourth tick.
- ([46:01](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2761)) Gnomonkey says you cannot one-tick flick a mage if you are going to mess it up, so lazy flick them if you cannot do it consistently.

### [Inferno pillars](../Inferno_pillars.md)
