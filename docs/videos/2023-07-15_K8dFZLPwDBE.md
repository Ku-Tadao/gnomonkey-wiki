## Summary

Gnomonkey teaches the solo Great Olm fight in a regular Chambers of Xeric, covering the 12:0 and 8:1 Tumeken's Shadow running methods and the 4:1 lance (Scythe) method. He explains how to get into cycle, null flame walls, deal with acid and crystals, and what to do when something goes wrong.

## Topics covered

### [Great Olm](../Great_Olm.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE)) In the 12:0 Shadow running method Gnomonkey uses marked tiles numbered by the hit of the cycle: hit from one then run to the side and return, hit from two to be dragged and come back, hit from three, a long range hit from four, and hit from five, then repeat one to five until the hand is dead, usually about two cycles.
- ([0:55](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE?t=55)) In the 8:1 Shadow running method you actually tank a hit: a long-range hit from one, run and come back, drag on the hit, then tank a hit in the middle on two, and get dragged on three, repeating.
- ([0:55](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE?t=55)) Gnomonkey says 8:1 is a lot harder than 12:0 because you actually tank a hit.
- ([1:28](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE?t=88)) In 8:1 you tank the hit in the middle because when the head is on flame phase and does a flame wall, standing on that tile nulls it and he just makes a motion with nothing happening; you can equip ranged armour quickly to block some damage.
- ([1:58](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE?t=118)) Gnomonkey says to start with the Shadow because the melee hand cripples if you attack it with melee before the mage hand is dead; the main idea is to hammer the melee hand, switch to mage and start running.
- ([2:56](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE?t=176)) Gnomonkey says it is really important to use a thrall while learning, because the thrall keeps the hand turning all the way; if you splash or the thrall stops attacking, the hand turns mid-way, which makes it hard to learn.
- ([3:25](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE?t=205)) To get into cycle, wait until Olm is about to turn and click from tile one; Gnomonkey says to hit a tick after he turns.
- ([4:25](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE?t=265)) In the 4:1 lance method you go to the thumb, tank two hits, and then do the same thing every cycle, making sure you are hitting on the right tick.
- ([4:53](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE?t=293)) On the thumb with 4:1 there are three outcomes: Olm uses a special attack (lightning, crystals, teleports) then another attack then nothing, so you wait an attack and run when he does nothing; he does two attacks in a row so you run immediately and do a three-to-one; or he stares at you and does nothing, in which case you are already in cycle.
- ([8:32](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE?t=512)) For the crystal phase, Gnomonkey says it is super easy: do an L walk and just keep moving every tick, tanking a hit, then he does nothing and you are in cycle.
- ([9:35](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE?t=575)) If you attack on the same tick Olm turns (the wrong tick) and have a Scythe, you can swing the Scythe to fix it, and it even gives a bit more DPS for that swing.
- ([10:00](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE?t=600)) Gnomonkey says you can start the third phase with the melee hand because it does not clench, and with a hammer you can hammer, wait for him to turn and be in 4:1 every time; with two hammers it is tick perfect.
- ([10:38](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE?t=638)) For acid, Gnomonkey says to just start attacking and do an L walk: step across, hit, come around, hit, hit, and repeat.
- ([11:26](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE?t=686)) Gnomonkey says if you ever lose a tick you should go back to one and start the cycle again; always fix the cycle.
- ([11:29](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE?t=689)) Gnomonkey says to remove boots at Olm if you can because it is a big difference: minus 12 mage accuracy.
- ([12:30](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE?t=750)) For head running, move every time Olm turns so you line up with the cycle, and you tank a hit every four turns in the middle, which nulls flame walls.
- ([13:32](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE?t=812)) Gnomonkey says if you are close and pulled up at the end it reduces the Twisted bow travel time so it kills him slightly faster, and he has never done a solo Olm without a Twisted bow.
- ([15:07](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE?t=907)) Gnomonkey says you can null flame walls in 4:1 by standing on a specific tile when you tank the attack; on either side you tank the attack and then come in and it also nulls the flame wall.
- ([15:55](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE?t=955)) Gnomonkey says dealing with acid and dealing with flame walls are the two things to know and that Shadow running and 4:1 are not hard, you just have to know what to do and not get pulled out of cycle by weird things.
- ([18:30](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE?t=1110)) Gnomonkey says you should probably do some team runs before solo Chambers of Xeric because solo is pretty hard.
- ([18:57](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE?t=1137)) Gnomonkey says a lot of people are scared of four-to-one and he was too for hundreds of kills because nobody explained it well and there were no good videos.
