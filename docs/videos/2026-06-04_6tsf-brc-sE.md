## Topics covered

### Update: Summer Sweep-Up 2026

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE)) The Summer Sweep-Up 2026 is an Old School RuneScape update blog aimed at improving quality of life, addressing long-standing pain points, and sometimes introducing rebalances of long-standing main game issues.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE)) Gnomonkey says the update has some really good changes, and also some things he wished were included that are not.
- ([0:30](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=30)) The Summer Sweep-Up contains no new content; it cleans up and spruces up existing content, which is why it is called a sweep-up.
- ([47:00](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2820)) Gnomonkey is positive on most of the update, saying it brings a lot of QoL, though a few changes he is unsure about until he sees how they play out in game.

### [Hunter](../Hunter.md)

- ([0:30](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=30)) Gnomonkey says Hunter is a very janky skill to train, especially the early methods, which people try to skip by doing birdhouses, so the Summer Sweep-Up Hunter improvements are very much needed.
- ([0:30](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=30)) In the Summer Sweep-Up, falconry was buffed so the player is no longer held in place while the falcon chases a kebbit, and the player can reposition or walk around while it hunts.
- ([1:03](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=63)) Gnomonkey notes that falcon travel time is based on distance, so being able to move closer to the next kebbit while the falcon chases speeds falconry up.
- ([1:03](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=63)) Players can now ask the falcon to catch a kebbit even when their inventory is full, so they no longer need to drop all the junk drops.
- ([1:03](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=63)) Aerial fishing buff: the area in which quick catches can be performed is increased from a 3x3 area to a 4x4 area, making back-to-back catches every tick more likely.
- ([1:33](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=93)) A toggle was added to Alry the Angler that lets the player's cormorant eat fish in the inventory when there is no appropriate bait, reducing the need to make fish offcuts.
- ([1:33](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=93)) In aerial fishing a larger pool will now spawn that the cormorant can be sent to several times in a row, adding downtime to break up the rapid pace.
- ([2:05](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=125)) The chance of obtaining Mudskipper-style aerial fishing pearls (referred to as 'Mulk pearls' in the transcript) is increased by 50%, and some pearl costs are lowered with prior purchases refunded: the fishing sack is down to 500 pearls and the Angler's outfit down to 50.
- ([2:05](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=125)) Gnomonkey is curious whether aerial fishing is now a decent method for the Angler's outfit compared to Fishing Trawler, saying it may be a similar speed with better XP.
- ([2:38](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=158)) Deadfall trap trap limit is increased from one to two, which Gnomonkey says roughly doubles speed for deadfall hunting like pyrefoxes; this does not include maniacal monkeys.
- ([2:38](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=158)) Deadfall traps now have a chance to preserve their log, and become able to catch eight ticks after being placed instead of 15 ticks.
- ([3:08](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=188)) The wild kebbit catch rate is increased by two and a half times, since wild kebbits were an outlier in catch rate that drastically reduced the utility of deadfall traps at early Hunter levels.
- ([3:08](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=188)) XP from wild, barbed, prickly and sabertooth kebbits and pyrefoxes is lowered by 20%, but the other changes still work out to about 60% more XP per hour from these creatures, to bring them in line with other methods.
- ([3:40](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=220)) Bird snaring now allows two traps to be placed starting at level 1 instead of level 20, which Gnomonkey says doubles XP rates for catching birds early on.
- ([3:40](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=220)) The Ring of Pursuit can reveal full tracks even after one has already been started.
- ([3:40](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=220)) Gnomonkey thinks the deadfall and rumor changes make Hunter rumors much better, especially the very early ones, and that grinding early hunter rumors may become more viable.
- ([4:11](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=251)) Butterfly and moth hunter rumor drop rate is improved from 1 in 75 to 1 in 40, and the pity rate from 150 to 80, because those rumors were notoriously slow and high variance.
- ([4:39](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=279)) Chompy bird hunting now grants some Hunter XP.

### [Birdhouse run](../Birdhouse_run.md)

- ([46:53](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2813)) The Summer Sweep-Up proposes increasing bird nest output from birdhouses by 50% and making it more consistent, while reducing the XP given for checking them, with a big reduction at the low end and a small reduction at the top end.
- ([46:53](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2813)) Gnomonkey plans to make a future video on his thoughts about the birdhouse changes.
- ([47:25](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2845)) Gnomonkey says he has a big hate for birdhouses and thinks they are some of the worst content in the game, and welcomes trying to remove them while buffing low-level Hunter at the same time.
- ([47:52](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2872)) The blog says Jagex is not fully committed to removing the birdhouse XP and will keep them if people are very desperate to keep them.
- ([47:52](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2872)) Gnomonkey considers birdhouses a lame piecemeal piece of content that forces players away from other things they are doing, and dislikes that they are basically the only efficient way to train Hunter at early levels.

### [Hallowed Sepulchre](../Hallowed_Sepulchre.md)

- ([5:05](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=305)) Hallowed Sepulchre floor 4 requirement is lowered to level 77 Agility from 82, and floor 5 to level 87 from 92.
- ([5:35](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=335)) Gnomonkey says each floor higher in the Hallowed Sepulchre is worth about 10k Agility XP an hour more, and that most of the fun and most of the XP is at floor five.
- ([5:35](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=335)) Players can now boost for Hallowed Sepulchre floor entry, so an Agility level 72 player could eat a summer pie at the end of floor three to enter floor four, making the earliest entry to floor five 82 instead of 92.
- ([6:05](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=365)) Floor five's grand hallowed coffin will require either 84 Thieving or 92 Agility to open.
- ([6:05](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=365)) Gnomonkey agrees that levels 72 to 82 Agility on floor three is a slog and gets brain dead after doing it so many times.
- ([6:05](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=365)) Gnomonkey jokes the update is a perfect excuse to burn through a stack of summer pies.

### [Agility](../Agility.md)

- ([4:37](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=277)) Gnomonkey says that as much as it will pain some players, Agility is here to stay, and the Summer Sweep-Up includes Agility changes he had asked for.
- ([7:01](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=421)) The Colossal Wyrm (referred to as the worm course) Agility course in Varlamore is made 25 to 30% longer in duration, with XP and termites increased to match, so it gives the same XP and termites per hour with fewer inputs.
- ([7:01](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=421)) Gnomonkey says the Colossal Worm Agility Course did not seem worth doing in the current game and that the change might make it viable, depending on how AFK it actually is between steps.
- ([7:36](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=456)) A new shortcut was added in Arceuus Library to jump from the top floor down to the bottom floor, which Gnomonkey says could speed up the library Magic or Runecrafting XP method.
- ([7:36](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=456)) New shortcuts were added for clue scroll players: one over to the elite clue spot and one west of Piscatoris, replacing a long run around a mountain.
- ([8:09](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=489)) Several grapple shortcuts become usable without a crossbow or gear if the player is highly overleveled: the River Lum grapple, the Falador wall climb, the Catherby to Taverley rock climb (still 68 Agility), the Catherby water obelisk jump at 72, the Yanille wall climb at 69 (grapple still needed for the diary), and trees south of the Karamja volcano at 78.

### [Guardians of the Rift](../Guardians_of_the_Rift.md)

- ([8:45](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=525)) The Summer Sweep-Up lets players purchase the abyssal needle with pearls (the Goter reward currency), so players who go very dry can buy it.
- ([9:10](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=550)) The Abyssal Lantern is changed to function outside of Guardians of the Rift when burning logs that give an effect beyond the activity; willow, blisterwood, magic and redwood will work, while normal, oak and maple will not.
- ([9:10](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=550)) Under the lantern change, blisterwood increases blood runes crafted by 20%, magic logs increase runes crafted by 10%, and redwood makes essence pouches not degrade and gives 5% more runes.
- ([9:37](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=577)) Gnomonkey says the lantern change is a big deal and good for people manually crafting blood runes with blood essence, for example on an ironman.

### [Tempoross](../Tempoross.md)

- ([9:37](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=577)) The fish barrel and tackle box become purchasable for 6,000 spirit flakes from Tempoross.
- ([9:37](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=577)) Gnomonkey says he always struggles to get the fish barrel on every new hardcore account, so purchasing it may help with dry streaks.

### [Farming](../Farming.md)

- ([10:04](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=604)) Coral (Sailing-era farming coral) changes: all coral now get six harvest lives, up from four, and the chance to obtain coral frags from their associated thieving chest is reduced to 1 in 20 from 1 in 15, with some behind-the-scenes yield stat adjustments.
- ([10:04](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=604)) Gnomonkey admits he has never planted coral on any account, and says coral was giving very good herb XP to ironmen and UIMs at high level, which the change targets.
- ([10:36](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=636)) According to the blog's estimates, the coral changes give about one extra frag at 77 Farming, but about six less at 99 Farming for the first coral, with similar patterns for the other corals: much more at low Farming, less at 99.

### [Fishing](../Fishing.md)

- ([11:10](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=670)) Shark lures are improved so that the shark fishing roll rate goes from every five ticks to every four ticks, effectively a 20% increase in shark catch speed.
- ([11:10](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=670)) Gnomonkey says he wanted to make a rant video about how horrible shark lures were, but Jagex buffed them first; he notes the issue was that doing minnows would get more sharks per hour anyway and he is unsure if lures are now worth using.

### [Corrupted Gauntlet](../Corrupted_Gauntlet.md)

- ([11:51](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=711)) Corrupted Gauntlet changes aimed at reducing prep intensity guarantee a random demiboss spawn in the north, east, south and west rooms from the centre, so four demibosses at fixed locations, though which demiboss appears is still random.
- ([12:14](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=734)) The maximum attack range of Corrupted Gauntlet demibosses is reduced from 16 to 10, so players are less likely to be hit by an off-screen dark beast.
- ([12:41](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=761)) A weapon frame drop is now guaranteed from the first low-tier NPC killed, instead of the third kill.
- ([12:41](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=761)) Friend bark (bark), crystal ore and linum are made stackable, so all resources gathered in Corrupted Gauntlet are now stackable.
- ([13:08](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=788)) Gnomonkey considers stackable resources probably the biggest change of the whole patch, saying it makes tier three much easier and speeds up tier one runs because players can collect more fish on the way.
- ([13:39](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=819)) Gnomonkey is nervous that faster Corrupted Gauntlet completion could increase bot activity, lower the price of Enhanced crystal weapon seeds, and dump resources like dragon arrows and gp into the game.
- ([13:39](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=819)) Gnomonkey is glad the changes focus on convenience and not on drop rates, and says enhanced seed got faster in terms of kills per hour even though some people complained it did not get faster.
- ([14:14](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=854)) Crystal shards are now automatically placed in the inventory when enemies are killed, and the variance in the number of shards dropped by enemies is reduced.
- ([14:14](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=854)) Gnomonkey says shard pickup costs a tick each time, which adds up when making tier three, and that consistent demiboss shard drops (previously from about 20 to 100) will make tier three more consistent.
- ([14:57](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=897)) Regular enemy stats are standardized within tiers so it is no longer better to kill a wolf over a scorpion.
- ([14:58](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=898)) A tier one weapon now costs no crystal shards (down from 20, still needs a weapon frame), and a tier two weapon costs 50 shards, down from 60.
- ([14:58](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=898)) Gnomonkey expects the Corrupted Gauntlet speedrun world record, which he says was stuck at a low four minutes, to become dramatically faster and wonders if the four minute barrier will be broken.
- ([15:46](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=946)) Tier one armor pieces cost 50 shards (up from 40), tier two armor pieces cost 100 (up from 80), and tier three armor pieces cost 100 (up from 80).
- ([16:13](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=973)) Jagex's stated reason for raising armor costs is that they do not want tier three to become a must-have for everyone, and want players to trade comfort for time loss and push down to tier one prep for the fastest clears.
- ([17:11](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1031)) Under the new costs, a full set of tier one armor and two tier three weapons costs 250 shards (down from 280), tier two armor and two tier three weapons costs 450 (down from 460), and tier three armor and two tier three weapons costs 750 (up from 700).
- ([17:11](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1031)) Weapon frames no longer drop if the player already has a total of three weapon frames or crafted weapons.

### [Chambers of Xeric](../Chambers_of_Xeric.md)

- ([20:06](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1206)) The Summer Sweep-Up prevents the Surge Potion's cooldown from being reset on death inside the Chambers of Xeric, because it introduced a feels-bad meta.
- ([20:06](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1206)) Gnomonkey explains the old speedrun meta: players took a surge potion with two doses to the Olm fight, and deliberately died between Olm phases during the mandatory wait while crystals fall, to reset the surge cooldown, sip it, and run back in with some special attack energy to hit Olm immediately.
- ([20:34](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1234)) Gnomonkey thinks the surge reset prevention is a good change, though the Chambers world record will now be very hard to beat.
- ([20:34](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1234)) Progress in the night waves of the Chambers is no longer reset if the player leaves via the door, so it does not have to be done in one run.
- ([21:03](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1263)) According to the blog, Chambers of Xeric unique tables are weighted heavily towards the arcane and dexterous prayer scrolls, so roughly 58% of uniques seen on average will be a prayer scroll, and the same 58% applies to Challenge Mode completions.
- ([21:03](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1263)) Challenge Mode Chambers completion adds the twisted ancestral kit and metamorphic dust to a tertiary loot table but makes no adjustments to the ordinary loot table.
- ([21:29](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1289)) By contrast, Theatre of Blood hard mode adjusts the loot table so that the Avernic defender hilt has its weighting reduced by 7.7%, making other uniques more common.
- ([26:49](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1609)) Gnomonkey says Chambers of Xeric is by far the longest raid to complete in the game, and that it takes a very long time to get a twisted bow.
- ([27:34](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1654)) Gnomonkey feels Theatre of Blood is the perfect raid length for time to complete, Tombs of Amascut is slightly too fast, and Chambers is too long since full ancestral can take longer than getting a twisted bow.
- ([28:03](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1683)) Gnomonkey notes that the dragon sword, dragon thrown axe and dragon harpoon used to be on the Chambers of Xeric drop table and were removed.

### [Opinion: Chambers prayer scroll weighting](../Opinion_Chambers_prayer_scroll_weighting.md)

- ([22:01](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1321)) Gnomonkey says reducing the Chambers prayer scroll weighting, as many players suggest in line with the Theatre of Blood hard mode Avernic change, has some merit but he would not do it.
- ([22:30](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1350)) Gnomonkey argues the Theatre of Blood hard mode Avernic change is really dumb, because the Avernic defender hilt is not a worse drop than the others, and he would rather have an Avernic than Justiciar or even a Sanguinesti staff or Ghrazi rapier these days.
- ([23:04](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1384)) Gnomonkey says the Avernic hilt makes up about half of Theatre of Blood's total profit, so hard mode ToB now being less profitable than normal ToB shows the long-term problem of a short-term loot-weighting change.
- ([23:34](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1414)) Gnomonkey argues that if Jagex later adds a Prayer upgrade (e.g. a stronger Augury or Rigor needing arcane or dexterous scrolls), the scrolls could become expensive and the same problem as Theatre of Blood would appear for CoX.
- ([24:05](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1445)) Gnomonkey notes that even now a dexterous prayer scroll is more valuable than Dinh's bulwark and the twisted buckler, so reducing the dex weighting could increase rates of those cheaper items, and arcane is the cheapest on the table.
- ([24:35](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1475)) Gnomonkey says item values shift over time, citing the Sanguinesti staff getting cheap, and that DHCB could drop in value while dexterous scrolls could rise if many new players chase prayers.
- ([25:08](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1508)) The blog lists three approaches to the scroll problem: reduce scroll weight only, reduce scroll weight and reduce purple chance, or increase purple chance.
- ([25:08](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1508)) Gnomonkey says reducing scroll weight and nothing else is bad because it puts downward pressure on every unique, and items like the twisted buckler, Dinh's bulwark, DHCB and dragon claws cannot absorb that without losing value.
- ([26:36](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1596)) Gnomonkey says the second approach, reducing scroll weight and purple chance, is a very silly change, because purples in Chambers are already too infrequent and no one would like making them rarer.
- ([27:16](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1636)) Gnomonkey prefers the third approach, increasing the purple chance, and thinks it makes the most sense because the raid should be faster than it is.
- ([27:39](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1659)) Gnomonkey describes a fourth approach, moving purples off the CoX table, and agrees CoX has too many purples, but says there is currently no endgame content where the removed drops fit.
- ([28:34](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1714)) Gnomonkey considers Dinh's bulwark and the twisted buckler the best options to move to other bosses, since they still feel raid-worthy.
- ([28:34](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1714)) Gnomonkey suggests dragon claws could go on Vardorvis (slash weak), maybe at 1 in 2,000, but notes there are not really obvious bosses to move items to; Yama, Nex and Nightmare do not make sense for various reasons such as burning claws being better on Yama and drop tables already being stacked.
- ([30:07](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1807)) Gnomonkey thinks Jagex could move items off the drop table later if future endgame content makes the drop sensible, and says a Blooming boss dropping range upgrades would not suit the buckler since it cannot be used with arrows.
- ([31:07](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=1867)) The blog floats a hypothetical where a player who obtains an arcane scroll can withdraw it or re-roll it for a 1 in X chance to roll the unique table again without the arcane scroll, and if the 1 in X chance is missed the arcane scroll is deleted.
