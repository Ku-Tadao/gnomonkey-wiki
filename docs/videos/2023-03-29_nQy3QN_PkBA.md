## Summary

Gnomonkey shows an Inferno speedrun using the Venator bow that began badly but finished sub 44, beating his previous personal best of 46:07. The video is almost entirely live gameplay with sparse commentary; the description explains that the Venator bow replaces an Infernal cape switch and saves a lot of time but is hard to use.

### [Venator bow](../Venator_bow.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/nQy3QN_PkBA)) In his Inferno speedruns the Venator bow takes the place of an Infernal cape switch; Gnomonkey does not use a spec bar so he cannot currently use claws.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/nQy3QN_PkBA)) Gnomonkey calculates the Venator bow maxes 96 with the full 2 ricochets, and still maxes 69 with only 1 ricochet, which is higher than two Toxic blowpipe shots with higher accuracy.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/nQy3QN_PkBA)) Gnomonkey says the Venator bow saves a lot of time in the Inferno but is hard to use, and says it truly saved the day in this run.
- ([11:44](https://youtu.be/nQy3QN_PkBA?t=704)) Gnomonkey notes you kind of just have to guess with the Venator bow, since you cannot tell where the ricochet hit from the XP drop.
