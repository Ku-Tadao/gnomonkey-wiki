## Summary

Gnomonkey gets his Fire cape on the Big Dawgs V1 group hardcore ironman, covering the whole journey: Soul Wars for GP and supplies, Prayer training, Barrows, Monkey Madness I, a 236 kill Temple Spider grind for weapon poison, the Cyclops defender grind, and then a low-level melee Fight Caves. The key trick, suggested by chat, was starting the caves on a high-risk PvP world to use blighted supplies and logging out at wave 62 to kill TzTok-Jad on a normal world.

## Topics covered

### [TzHaar Fight Cave](../TzHaar_Fight_Cave.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U)) Gnomonkey's plan for the day on the group hardcore ironman was to make money from Soul Wars, do Monkey Madness, get a few combat levels, do the defender grind, and then do a melee Fight Caves.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U)) The description lists what Gnomonkey needed before the Fight Caves on a hardcore group ironman: GP and supplies from Soul Wars, a Prayer training method (Varlamore and Barrows brothers), weapon poison (the hard part), a dragon defender, and food.
- ([30:19](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=1819)) Gnomonkey says weapon poison only helps minorly for the defender grind and for the Fight Caves, not massively.
- ([50:13](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=3013)) Gnomonkey's Hitpoints are 64 when starting the Fight Caves, and he notes nothing other than TzTok-Jad can one-shot him.
- ([51:45](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=3105)) Gnomonkey's safety plan in the Fight Caves is that only Jad can one-shot him, so he must not make a prayer mistake there, while everything else he can survive.
- ([52:15](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=3135)) Manta rays heal 22, so Gnomonkey says he needs to be at 42 Hitpoints to get full efficiency out of the food when his max is 64.
- ([53:48](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=3228)) Gnomonkey says the mage monsters in the caves max 52 with magic and 54 if they melee him.
- ([57:18](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=3438)) Gnomonkey says walking monsters out and pulling them to safe spots, such as the northeast corner, lets the ranger and mage get stuck behind other monsters so he can fight them one at a time.
- ([1:02:16](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=3736)) Gnomonkey says the 250 Temple Spiders he had to kill for weapon poison were worth it because the poison also works on Jad and on the caves' monsters.
- ([1:05:50](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=3950)) Gnomonkey says regular Attack potions add about 0.1 DPS against Jad, roughly a 10% increase, but he considers them not worth it.
- ([1:14:22](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=4462)) Gnomonkey estimated about forty minutes to reach Jad, with 21 or 22 mage monsters left to kill at wave 63.
- ([1:22:16](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=4936)) Gnomonkey reads the OSRS Wiki's recommended lowest melee gear for the Fight Caves (including a regular whip, crystal shield, adamant gloves and a ring of suffering) and says his own setup has roughly half the strength bonus.
- ([1:39:56](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=5996)) Gnomonkey says he has never done a low-level Fight Caves before and believes this may be the first no-scythe melee Fight Caves.
- ([1:54:27](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=6867)) Gnomonkey reaches TzTok-Jad about 1 hour 54 minutes into the video, having begun the waves at about 51 minutes.
