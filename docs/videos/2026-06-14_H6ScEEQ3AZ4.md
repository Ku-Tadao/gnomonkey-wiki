## Summary

Gnomonkey completes the Inferno in the Deadman Mode Allstars event with almost no gear (90 Ranged, no prayers, limited bolts and darts, paper armour) while the TzHaar area is multi-combat and enemy teams could kill him outside. He kills TzKal-Zuk with the very last shot, earns a Corrupted weapon and calls it the best moment of his OSRS career.

- [0:00](https://youtu.be/H6ScEEQ3AZ4) Preparing for Zuk: avoiding melee flicking, trapping healers and running through to the boss

## Topics covered

### [TzKal-Zuk](../TzKal-Zuk.md)

- ([2:52](https://youtu.be/H6ScEEQ3AZ4?t=172)) In the Deadman Mode Allstars mission, Gnomonkey fights TzKal-Zuk inside the Inferno with no proper gear: 90 Ranged, no prayers, limited bolts and darts, and paper armour.
- ([3:00](https://youtu.be/H6ScEEQ3AZ4?t=180)) Gnomonkey says the damage from the Jads was bad, but Ruby bolts are very good against TzKal-Zuk.
- ([8:26](https://youtu.be/H6ScEEQ3AZ4?t=506)) Once TzKal-Zuk is below about 340 health, Gnomonkey says he cannot shoot the crossbow anymore, then finds he still can and that it hits about 80.
- ([8:26](https://youtu.be/H6ScEEQ3AZ4?t=506)) Gnomonkey uses diamond bolts for the last stretch of the TzKal-Zuk fight and brews up before continuing.
- ([9:27](https://youtu.be/H6ScEEQ3AZ4?t=567)) During the TzKal-Zuk fight Gnomonkey tells himself to save the shield and to kill Zuk first, worrying about other damage afterwards.
- ([19:35](https://youtu.be/H6ScEEQ3AZ4?t=1175)) Gnomonkey explains that the TzKal-Zuk shield has a long death animation, so while it is dying it still blocks one more shot, which is what happened to his shot at the end.
- ([20:37](https://youtu.be/H6ScEEQ3AZ4?t=1237)) Gnomonkey says he hit about seven zeros in the final DPS race and the very last shot killed TzKal-Zuk, with 10 prayer points, no restore, no range potion, Zuk at 100 health and the next set of monsters spawning.

### [Inferno](../Inferno.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/H6ScEEQ3AZ4)) The Deadman Mode Allstars mission of the day was to defeat TzKal-Zuk inside the Inferno, with a Corrupted weapon of the player's choice as the reward.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/H6ScEEQ3AZ4)) Gnomonkey says the TzHaar area is multi-combat, making it extremely dangerous to even enter the Inferno, and that one mistake could mean being killed outside by a waiting team with no time to retry.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/H6ScEEQ3AZ4)) Gnomonkey says this Inferno run was the best moment of his life of playing OSRS, better than any world first or his sub-40 Inferno.
- ([2:02](https://youtu.be/H6ScEEQ3AZ4?t=122)) While running through the Inferno toward Zuk, Gnomonkey says that if he cannot trap the healers again he has to run through, and asks for a healer trap behind him.
- ([16:01](https://youtu.be/H6ScEEQ3AZ4?t=961)) A teammate calls it the most insane Inferno they have ever witnessed, and Gnomonkey says it was the most insane Inferno he has ever done.
- ([19:01](https://youtu.be/H6ScEEQ3AZ4?t=1141)) Gnomonkey says he played the run flawlessly apart from the Jads taking all of his prayer points.

### [Deadman Mode](../Deadman_Mode.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/H6ScEEQ3AZ4)) The Deadman Mode Allstars team had no gear for the whole week, which Gnomonkey says made the Inferno mission extremely dangerous.
