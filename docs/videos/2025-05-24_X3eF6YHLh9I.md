## Summary

A compilation of Gnomonkey's Yama contract kills from a roughly 20-hour stream in which he completed his Radiant Oathplate kit, which he says was the world's 6th. There is no guide commentary; the video is mostly live reactions to each contract.

## Outline

- [0:00](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I) First contract kill, taking little damage and using Blood Fury healing
- [9:29](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I?t=569) Sigil bottom contract completed in 9:39 with two instant mauls
- [10:27](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I?t=627) Managing stamina and run energy on later contracts
- [20:15](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I?t=1215) Line of sight plugin and full mage contract

- [47:53](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I?t=2873) Final contract completed after twenty and a half hours
- [48:50](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I?t=2930) Trying on the finished Oathplate set

## Topics covered

### [Oathplate armour](../Oathplate_armour.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I)) Gnomonkey completed his Radiant Oathplate kit as the world's 6th player to do so, in May 2025, after a roughly 20 hour stream.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I)) Gnomonkey calls the Radiant Oathplate kit one of the hardest challenges in the game at release.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I)) Per the description, the launch had many issues, including contracts being impossible to complete without hotfixes.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I)) Gnomonkey says 6th place is good enough for him and refers to himself as a D tier PvMer; he says he will not make a guide on these contracts.
- ([47:53](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I?t=2873)) Gnomonkey says completing all the contracts for the kit took twenty and a half hours of stream.
- ([49:21](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I?t=2961)) After completion Gnomonkey tried the new Oathplate on with a Torva helmet and a Zek helmet and thought he was third to finish unless someone else had snuck in.

### [Yama](../Yama.md)

- ([9:29](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I?t=569)) Gnomonkey completed the sigil bottom contract in 9:39 with two instant mauls, which he thought was probably a fairly fast completion; he says the key is keeping run energy high.
- ([9:29](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I?t=569)) Gnomonkey says one contract was one of the easiest of the contracts, and another easier than the previous one.
- ([10:27](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I?t=627)) On the contract kills Gnomonkey used a stamina potion and a food that gives lots of health and 10 run energy when eaten to keep run energy up.
- ([20:15](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I?t=1215)) Gnomonkey used the Line of sight plugin, set to five, for one of the contract kills, which he did in full mage.
- ([29:09](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I?t=1749)) Gnomonkey described one contract as Kephri Medic as a contract, except it kills you if a swarm goes in.
- ([48:41](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I?t=2921)) At completion Gnomonkey got no white Rancour and no pet, only the blue-eyed look he likes, and said a thrall saved his last kill.
