## Summary

Gnomonkey's live-streamed Solo Expert Tombs of Amascut run with no food, finishing in 22:15 overall and 20:22.20 challenge time, which he says retook the world record by 15 seconds about an hour before the update that made Raids 3 far easier. Commentary is mostly live reactions, with a few notes on the boss splits.

## Topics covered

### [Tombs of Amascut](../Tombs_of_Amascut.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/Q9ihU-51774)) Gnomonkey's Solo Expert Tombs of Amascut run with no food whatsoever finished in 22:15 overall and 20:22.20 challenge time, pre-buff, as stated in the description.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/Q9ihU-51774)) Gnomonkey says it took a couple of days to get his world record back, and that he snagged it an hour before the update that made Raids 3 far easier, with the improved Tumeken's shadow.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/Q9ihU-51774)) Gnomonkey says running Solo Expert mode with no food whatsoever is no joke, and expects someone to soon take the record with the improved Tumeken's shadow.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/Q9ihU-51774)) The run was done live on Twitch, and Gnomonkey says he had been streaming non-stop since Raids 3 released.
- ([22:28](https://youtu.be/Q9ihU-51774?t=1348)) Before this run the Solo Expert world record on the leaderboard was 22:30 flat, and Gnomonkey beat it by 15 seconds.

### [Gnomonkey](../Gnomonkey.md)

- ([22:43](https://youtu.be/Q9ihU-51774?t=1363)) After the record run Gnomonkey says his splits for Zebak, Baba, Akkha and Kephri were very fast and close to his best, while the Wardens were a little below average.
- ([22:43](https://youtu.be/Q9ihU-51774?t=1363)) Gnomonkey says he lost about 20 seconds to the crocodile (Zebak) puzzle fight and did not care because he still got the world record.
- ([22:43](https://youtu.be/Q9ihU-51774?t=1363)) Gnomonkey says the world record was back in his hands after seeing it on the leaderboard.

### [Akkha](../Akkha.md)

- ([8:43](https://youtu.be/Q9ihU-51774?t=523)) Gnomonkey says the Akkha fight in this run was the fastest he had ever gone.
- ([8:55](https://youtu.be/Q9ihU-51774?t=535)) In the record run Gnomonkey says he took a lot of damage at Akkha, which was not ideal, and that Akkha stayed on a style he wanted to change back from.
- ([12:00](https://youtu.be/Q9ihU-51774?t=720)) Gnomonkey says a Toxic blowpipe is not that much worse in DPS than Tumeken's shadow at Akkha, so a style that did not return to red was okay.
