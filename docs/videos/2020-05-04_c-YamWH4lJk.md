## Summary

A guide to Gargoyle Slayer tasks in the Slayer Tower, which Gnomonkey describes as an AFK money-maker pulling over 1 million gp an hour along with 30k Slayer XP and 100k melee XP an hour. He covers weapons, the Gargoyle Smasher unlock, gear, inventory, locations and the Marble Gargoyle superior.

## Topics covered

### [Gargoyle](../Gargoyle.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/c-YamWH4lJk)) Gnomonkey says Gargoyle tasks easily pull in over 1 million gp an hour, with a decent 30k Slayer XP an hour and 100k melee XP an hour.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/c-YamWH4lJk)) Gargoyles require 75 Slayer to damage and a rock hammer to finish off.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/c-YamWH4lJk)) Gnomonkey says an inquisitor's mace is the best weapon against Gargoyles because they are weak to crush, but asks why you would fight Gargoyles if you could afford one; a bludgeon is next best and a whip is perfectly acceptable.
- ([0:28](https://youtu.be/c-YamWH4lJk?t=28)) The Abyssal bludgeon gives about 4% more DPS than a whip against Gargoyles.
- ([0:28](https://youtu.be/c-YamWH4lJk?t=28)) Gnomonkey recommends unlocking Gargoyle Smasher for 120 Slayer points from a Slayer Master, as it automatically finishes Gargoyles below 9 HP and makes the task much more AFK.
- ([0:54](https://youtu.be/c-YamWH4lJk?t=54)) Gargoyles drop many alchables, so Gnomonkey says the alchemy runes in a rune pouch are very important; they do not drop herbs so the herb sack can be skipped.
- ([1:25](https://youtu.be/c-YamWH4lJk?t=85)) With worse gear or stats Gnomonkey would do Gargoyles in two trips with divine super combats and fewer prayer potions, as the Slayer Tower is close to a Slayer ring teleport.
- ([1:37](https://youtu.be/c-YamWH4lJk?t=97)) Gargoyles are in the Slayer Tower, reached fastest with a Slayer ring or by running north from fairy ring code CKS; with a task Gnomonkey recommends the basement, which requires a task, and if crowded or without a task the roof can be checked.
- ([1:37](https://youtu.be/c-YamWH4lJk?t=97)) Gnomonkey uses Woox's aggression plugin in RuneLite to make Gargoyles more AFK, since he does not have to worry about them becoming unaggressive.
- ([1:56](https://youtu.be/c-YamWH4lJk?t=116)) Gnomonkey drinks a dose of super combat, turns on piety and fights Gargoyles; with poor defence or worse gear protect from melee may be necessary as they can hit quite hard.
- ([1:56](https://youtu.be/c-YamWH4lJk?t=116)) Gargoyles have a superior, the Marble Gargoyle, which has melee and ranged attacks as well as a special attack; Gnomonkey recommends protect from melee and tanking the ranged attack.
- ([1:56](https://youtu.be/c-YamWH4lJk?t=116)) The Marble Gargoyle's special attack is a slow-moving red and gray orb that can be avoided by walking one tile away; if it hits, the max is 38 and it freezes the player in place.
- ([2:28](https://youtu.be/c-YamWH4lJk?t=148)) The full Gargoyle task took Gnomonkey about 40 minutes and gave 650k gp in loot plus two brimstone keys, because he was on task from Konar.
- ([2:28](https://youtu.be/c-YamWH4lJk?t=148)) A Konar-assigned task can be expected to give one to two brimstone keys per task.
- ([2:28](https://youtu.be/c-YamWH4lJk?t=148)) Gnomonkey got 28k Slayer XP an hour and 100k melee XP an hour on Gargoyles and says to expect a bit more with a bludgeon.

### [Slayer](../Slayer.md)

- ([0:28](https://youtu.be/c-YamWH4lJk?t=28)) For Gargoyles Gnomonkey brings standard melee gear, and says Bandos can be downgraded to fighter torso and obsidian legs, primordial boots to dragon boots, ferocious gloves to Barrows gloves, and torture to fury.
- ([0:54](https://youtu.be/c-YamWH4lJk?t=54)) Gnomonkey brings a gem sack, desert amulet and Karamja gloves for retasking, a Holy wrench for saved prayer doses, and a ring of slaying to get to the tower.
- ([0:54](https://youtu.be/c-YamWH4lJk?t=54)) Gnomonkey likes to finish Gargoyle tasks in a single trip, bringing two divine super combats, ten prayer potions and four sharks.
