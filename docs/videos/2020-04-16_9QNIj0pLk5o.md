## Summary

A short guide to Ankou Slayer tasks from Duradel. Gnomonkey says they give poor loot and XP but are an extremely fast task, so they are worth doing rather than skipping.

## Topics covered

### [Ankou](../Ankou.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o)) A task of 53 Ankou took Gnomonkey roughly seven minutes, making it one of Duradel's shortest tasks.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o)) Gnomonkey says Ankou have horrible drops and terrible XP rates, but like Zygomites and Dark Beasts the task is worth doing just because it is so fast.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o)) Ankou have an average weight of five from Duradel, so they come up occasionally.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o)) Ankou have no requirements to fight and Gnomonkey calls them incredibly easy.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o)) For Ankou Gnomonkey brings standard melee gear, and says the gear does not matter much so pieces can be downgraded.
- ([0:33](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o?t=33)) The Salve amulet works on Ankou but does not stack with the Slayer helmet, and Gnomonkey says the Slayer helmet's bonus is more beneficial.
- ([0:33](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o?t=33)) Ankou do not drop bones, so Gnomonkey says a bone crusher is not needed, and they have no alchable drops so the rune pouch can be skipped.
- ([1:12](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o?t=72)) To reach Ankou, teleport to Xeric's Heart, enter the Catacombs, run south of the altar and climb through a hole to get to them.
- ([1:12](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o?t=72)) With decent stats and armour you can tank multiple Ankou at once without needing food, so Gnomonkey uses auto-retaliate and piety.
- ([1:40](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o?t=100)) Gnomonkey's Ankou task of about seven minutes gave only 30k in loot and 3k Slayer XP.
- ([1:40](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o?t=100)) Ankou drop items worth picking up, such as arrows and runes, but the loot is not great overall.

### [Slayer](../Slayer.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o)) Gnomonkey groups Ankou with Zygomites and Dark Beasts as Slayer tasks worth doing rather than skipping because they are so fast.
- ([0:33](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o?t=33)) For an Ankou task Gnomonkey brings a Xeric's talisman for teleporting to the Catacombs, a Desert amulet and Karamja gloves for restocking and getting a new task, a divine super combat potion, prayer potions to keep piety up, and some sharks.
- ([0:33](https://youtu.be/9QNIj0pLk5o?t=33)) Gnomonkey brings a wrench and Saradomin godsword to save prayer potions on Ankou tasks, and a herb and gem sack to save inventory space.
