## Topics covered

### [Yama](../Yama.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE)) Gnomonkey says the solo Yama guides he made the previous day were complete trolling, because new findings showed maging Yama is terrible.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE)) When you cast a spell at Yama and he stops walking towards you, his magic defence goes up by 90, from negative 30 to 60, according to Gnomonkey.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE)) Gnomonkey says maging is fine for chill kills but dramatically slower, and that melee is better in all phases of Yama.
- ([2:37](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=157)) Yama has three phases and phases every 33.3% of damage dealt.
- ([3:08](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=188)) Yama has three possible attacks like Nightmare: a melee swing, a mage attack shown by little flames on your body, and a ranged attack shown as a shadow around you.
- ([3:08](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=188)) Gnomonkey says to camp protect from melee by default at Yama, because you cannot catch that prayer in time; flick to mage when you see orange flames and to ranged when you see a shadow around yourself.
- ([3:38](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=218)) Every few attacks Yama does a massive special (a giant fireball or shadow projectile) hitting for roughly 40 to 80 damage, which you block by stepping on a glyph.
- ([26:48](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=1608)) Gnomonkey says there is no point at which mage is good at Yama, no matter the setup, that he apologises for bad info in the old guide, and that Emberlight or Arclight is your best option the whole time.
- ([27:27](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=1647)) Per Gnomonkey, meleeing the last phase with Emberlight is roughly 20 to 30% better DPS, and the description says on average meleeing does about 20% more DPS than maging.
- ([28:40](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=1720)) Gnomonkey credits Dono with finding the Yama tech on day one.

### [Dono fly](../Dono_fly.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE)) Per the description, Dono fly can be done in duo by stacking, but there are hiccups such as players not spawning in at the same time.
- ([0:29](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=29)) The butterfly method, called Dono fly, is a Yama tile method found by Dono that lets you melee the entire kill and dodge everything by default, like a Bandos 6-0 style method.
- ([0:59](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=59)) To start the Dono fly method you spawn in, spam-click to the delay tile, then attack Yama; after that each time your true tile reaches the next tile in the cycle (one, two, three, four, 4.1, then start) you attack Yama again.
- ([1:30](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=90)) In the Dono fly cycle you run to a tile and attack from it, which dodges everything including the waves, which go straight through you.
- ([2:06](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=126)) Gnomonkey says the Dono fly cycle breaks down solo eventually, at around 12 of Yama's attacks (Yama attacks every seven ticks), so you mage a little at the end.
- ([9:00](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=540)) The hardest part of Dono fly is reaching Yama in exactly three game ticks after the Judge teleports you back; spam click to the delay tile along true tiles and click Yama on the third tick.
- ([9:26](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=566)) If you reach Yama in two ticks, he punches you for about 50 and spawns an orb, so if you spawn close to the delay tile you must stop on a tile for a tick before attacking.
- ([10:28](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=628)) One tile in the cycle is the only part not simply attack as soon as your true tile reaches it: you click to the Dono tile and wait until you are off attack cooldown, or Yama hits you.
- ([25:03](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=1503)) When dodging the waves with Dono fly, click right before the wave reaches you, basically on top of the tile.
- ([27:01](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=1621)) Gnomonkey says Dono fly lets you brain off and skip everything, making the final phase harder to learn but easier to execute than mage camping.

### [Yama glyphs](../Yama_glyphs.md)

- ([3:38](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=218)) Glyphs around Yama's arena let you protect yourself from the big special; Gnomonkey goes clockwise by default, always starting at one of two starting glyphs.
- ([4:09](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=249)) When Yama shows the big special animation you step on the glyph, which blocks the attack and uses the glyph up, then go back to hitting Yama and work around the room to the next glyph.
- ([5:42](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=342)) Before the fireball special Yama does flames across the sides of the arena; getting hit stuns you in place so you cannot reach the glyph.
- ([6:10](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=370)) Before the shadow special Yama sends waves of energy across the screen that drop your prayer, so you can be comboed; Gnomonkey says this is probably the most dangerous thing in the fight.
- ([6:33](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=393)) In phase two the fire special drops a giant meteor that disables your run energy, so you must be right next to the glyph.
- ([18:23](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=1103)) Sometimes Yama replaces a special with orbs; when the orbs are just above half charged, run onto two glyphs so they pop on the glyphs and do not heal the boss, then skip one glyph in the cycle.
- ([22:26](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=1346)) A special is always preceded by flame snakes; missing the glyph can one-shot you as the hit can exceed 100.

### [Judge of Yama](../Judge_of_Yama.md)

- ([4:38](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=278)) At each phase Yama sends you to a side area where jumps across stones can be done every tick and the Judge of Yama shoots fireballs you must dodge.
- ([4:38](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=278)) The Judge is a guaranteed max hit on the correct style, alternating strictly mage hit then melee hit with no threshold like demonic gorillas.
- ([5:04](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=304)) You can pre-switch and mage the Judge once you reach the stone to hit a bit early; its skulls land on the ground where you stand like Tormented Demons, so step aside.
- ([5:22](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=322)) Stepping underneath the Judge when it shoots a skull makes the skull damage the Judge itself, a minor time save.
- ([23:44](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=1424)) Gnomonkey advises eating when you reach the Judge if low on health, since there is downtime.

### [Yama](../Yama.md)

- ([2:37](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=157)) Anytime Yama melees you in phase three, it spawns an orb you must kill that heals him and hits you hard, around 40.
- ([7:00](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=420)) Gnomonkey says phase three of Yama is the hardest part of the fight and what will kill you.
- ([7:09](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=429)) For a first kill, Gnomonkey says to put on mage gear and not enter melee range in phase three; you can mage just the last phase to get a feel for it.
- ([7:09](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=429)) In phase three Yama throws rows of meteorites in sets of three and you step in the middle perpendicular to each row.
- ([7:35](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=455)) In phase three Yama alternates mage and range attacks, so you alternate prayers, and he also throws huge diamond-shaped waves across the screen.
- ([8:01](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=481)) To dodge the diamond waves, Gnomonkey marks the tile just left of Yama and runs straight through the middle of the diamond.
- ([26:36](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=1596)) In phase three you can throw an Emberlight spec at the orbs; with Mark of Darkness they are guaranteed one-shots, and then you mage the rest.

### [Yama budget setup](../Yama_budget_setup.md)
