## Summary

A narrated Alchemical Hydra kill showing how Gnomonkey lures the boss onto each coloured pool and uses the fire skip method, which he learned from commenters JampS_TV and Jarvis Strawbridge, to avoid the fire phase entirely. It also shows prayer swapping every three attacks and dodging the poison special.

## Topics covered

### [Alchemical Hydra](../Alchemical_Hydra.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/1bBT0uOCQsM)) In the Alchemical Hydra fight Gnomonkey stands on a specific tile after the previous kill so the Hydra respawns lured directly onto the red pool, and starts with protect from magic.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/1bBT0uOCQsM)) After the Alchemical Hydra's first three attacks in a phase it uses its poison special attack, then begins using ranged.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/1bBT0uOCQsM)) Gnomonkey snakes around the Hydra in the first phase to keep doing damage while pulling it a little closer.
- ([0:30](https://youtu.be/1bBT0uOCQsM?t=30)) In the second phase Gnomonkey walks to a particular tile to lure the Hydra onto the green pool, and knows it has been lured correctly when it roars.
- ([0:30](https://youtu.be/1bBT0uOCQsM?t=30)) In the blue phase, after the Hydra's third attack lightning comes out; Gnomonkey waits for the lightning to get a little closer before running to a tile near the bottom, since running too early gets him hit.
- ([0:58](https://youtu.be/1bBT0uOCQsM?t=58)) For the third (red) phase Gnomonkey stands on a specific tile that lures the Hydra right next to the blue pool, calling it pretty much the perfect tile.
- ([1:25](https://youtu.be/1bBT0uOCQsM?t=85)) Fire skip: after three attacks in the red phase the Hydra walks to the middle of the room; as soon as its head moves the player must clip onto a particular tile, which skips the whole fire phase so the player can just stand there.
- ([1:25](https://youtu.be/1bBT0uOCQsM?t=85)) If the player is not on the fire-skip tile when the Hydra's fire attack starts, the player gets bumped, so Gnomonkey says he cut it a little close on the kill shown.
- ([1:25](https://youtu.be/1bBT0uOCQsM?t=85)) In the last phase the first prayer to use is the opposite of the last attack received during the fire phase, then the player alternates prayers.
- ([1:51](https://youtu.be/1bBT0uOCQsM?t=111)) In the final phase the poison comes out after the Hydra's third attack; walk two tiles south to dodge it and then flick alternating prayers.
- ([1:51](https://youtu.be/1bBT0uOCQsM?t=111)) If the final phase goes nine attacks past the first poison shot, the Alchemical Hydra shoots another poison, but this is rare when the player is potted up.
- ([2:21](https://youtu.be/1bBT0uOCQsM?t=141)) After a kill Gnomonkey tidies his inventory, picks up what he wants such as restore potions, decants them, then walks to a tile and waits for the Hydra to respawn so it is perfectly lured for the next fight.
- ([2:21](https://youtu.be/1bBT0uOCQsM?t=141)) This guide originally showcased fire walking, but Gnomonkey reworked and re-uploaded it with the fire skip method after JampS_TV and Jarvis Strawbridge told him about it.
- ([2:21](https://youtu.be/1bBT0uOCQsM?t=141)) Gnomonkey's description says the Alchemical Hydra gives an average of 230k per run.
