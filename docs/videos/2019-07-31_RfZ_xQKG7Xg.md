## Summary

Episode 2 of Gnomonkey's gnome and monkey only Ironman. He struggles through the Tree Gnome Village quest, repeatedly dying while retrieving his items, then trains Ranged with bronze arrows and kills the Khazard Warlord to finish the quest and unlock the gnome amulet and spirit trees.

## Topics covered

### Tree Gnome Village

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/RfZ_xQKG7Xg)) Gnomonkey's goal for the episode is to complete Tree Gnome Village, which gives 11,450 Attack experience and his best-in-slot amulet and also unlocks spirit trees to save time running resources.
- ([0:37](https://youtu.be/RfZ_xQKG7Xg?t=37)) In the quest the general wants six logs for his army and then wants the ballistas fixed; Gnomonkey talks to gnomes to narrow down coordinates and then has to guess where to fire.
- ([1:06](https://youtu.be/RfZ_xQKG7Xg?t=66)) During the part of the quest where the player climbs in to retrieve items, Gnomonkey discovers the player gets stunlocked for five attacks, leaving him on 1 health the first time.
- ([5:02](https://youtu.be/RfZ_xQKG7Xg?t=302)) Gnomonkey finds that grabbing the orb from the chest makes the gnomes no longer stand by him and sets him back, so he just grabs his items and leaves; he returns the king's orb, then has to kill the Khazard Warlord to get the two orbs back.
- ([9:40](https://youtu.be/RfZ_xQKG7Xg?t=580)) Completing the quest brought Gnomonkey right up to level 28 Attack and he receives the gnome amulet.
- ([9:40](https://youtu.be/RfZ_xQKG7Xg?t=580)) The gnome amulet is Gnomonkey's best-in-slot amulet on the account and has decent defence bonuses.

### Khazard Warlord

- ([5:58](https://youtu.be/RfZ_xQKG7Xg?t=358)) Gnomonkey notes the Khazard Warlord is very high-leveled and that normally one would use magic, but he has little access to runes on this account so he uses Ranged with bronze arrows.
- ([7:22](https://youtu.be/RfZ_xQKG7Xg?t=442)) Gnomonkey's first attempt with about level 8 Ranged took about 45 minutes to get the Warlord to half health before he gave up; the Warlord does not despawn so he can keep shooting for a long time.
- ([8:22](https://youtu.be/RfZ_xQKG7Xg?t=502)) After a point the Warlord retreats when attacked, but he retreats to a corner where he is stuck, so Gnomonkey can keep shooting him from range.
- ([8:52](https://youtu.be/RfZ_xQKG7Xg?t=532)) On the final attempt at level 22 Ranged with 467 bronze arrows and flicking Sharp Eye, Gnomonkey killed the Khazard Warlord in about 25 minutes.

### [Gnome and Monkey locked Ironman](../Gnome_and_Monkey_locked_Ironman.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/RfZ_xQKG7Xg)) Episode 2 of the gnome and monkey only Ironman series covers the Tree Gnome Village quest and the Khazard Warlord.
- ([2:05](https://youtu.be/RfZ_xQKG7Xg?t=125)) After dying to the quest part, the minigame teleport to Castle Wars needs 20 minutes to recharge, so Gnomonkey must run back by foot to the Gnome Stronghold for food; he notes he forgot he has to walk past White Wolf Mountain.
- ([4:00](https://youtu.be/RfZ_xQKG7Xg?t=240)) With no food in the bank, Gnomonkey picks up swamp toads for food and has about 43 minutes left on his grave timer when retrying.
- ([5:02](https://youtu.be/RfZ_xQKG7Xg?t=302)) Gnomonkey eats the kebab before the part where he is hit very low and uses Thick Skin, which lets him retrieve his items on the retry.
- ([7:00](https://youtu.be/RfZ_xQKG7Xg?t=420)) About seven hours of footage in the episode were corrupted, so Gnomonkey summarises that he killed hill giants and hobgoblins and obtained an iron kite shield, a steel longsword, an iron full helm and some runes.
- ([10:19](https://youtu.be/RfZ_xQKG7Xg?t=619)) Gnomonkey notes he still needs the Grand Tree quest to use the other spirit trees, but expects it to be a while because the black demon will be hard.

### [Ranged](../Ranged.md)

- ([6:26](https://youtu.be/RfZ_xQKG7Xg?t=386)) Gnomonkey finds a wizard hat from goblins that is a straight upgrade for his head slot.
- ([6:54](https://youtu.be/RfZ_xQKG7Xg?t=414)) Gnomonkey buys a bow from his weapons dealer Gullock and has 140 bronze arrows for Ranged training, finding a safe spot where he can shoot through a small section of gate.
- ([7:55](https://youtu.be/RfZ_xQKG7Xg?t=475)) A merchant in the Tree Gnome Village sells 30 bronze arrows at a time, so Gnomonkey hops worlds and buys all 30 repeatedly, reaching 467 arrows.
- ([8:52](https://youtu.be/RfZ_xQKG7Xg?t=532)) Gnomonkey reaches level 22 Ranged before the final Warlord kill.

### Spirit tree

- ([9:40](https://youtu.be/RfZ_xQKG7Xg?t=580)) Gnomonkey uses the spirit tree for the first time after the quest, and says it saves about a two and a half minute walk each way.
