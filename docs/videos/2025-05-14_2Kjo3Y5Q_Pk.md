## Summary

Day-one duo guide to Yama showing one kill in near-max gear and further kills in a budget setup. Gnomonkey explains the Soulflame horn, glyph handling, the enraged final phase and why a purging staff and Emberlight are the key items.

## Topics covered

### [Yama](../Yama.md)

- ([0:31](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=31)) Gnomonkey considers duo Yama good for kills per hour because the Soulflame horn is very strong and can only be used in a duo.
- ([2:00](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=120)) In this video Gnomonkey notes Yama has 333 crush defence, so an Elder maul does not normally land on him.
- ([2:57](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=177)) In a duo at Yama, Gnomonkey says you can use melee as your main style, whereas in a solo you mage because draining Yama is hard.
- ([2:57](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=177)) Gnomonkey states melee is about 11% better than mage at Yama in this setup.
- ([4:00](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=240)) Yama picks one player as the tank at the start of the fight; the tank can pray melee while in melee range, so the other player does not need to worry about that prayer.
- ([4:30](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=270)) The Judge of Yama is a guaranteed hit and has a shared health pool; both players swap between mage and melee to damage it together before returning to the boss.
- ([5:37](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=337)) Yama's phases occur at 33% and 66% health.
- ([6:40](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=400)) Gnomonkey's first duo kill in near-max gear took about three minutes.
- ([14:44](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=884)) Gnomonkey advises staying north of Yama and never south, towards where the waves start forming, because you do not have time to react to them.
- ([21:12](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1272)) Gnomonkey calls Yama a hard boss with a killer enrage phase, but says it is more doable than expected and genuinely fun.
- ([21:12](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1272)) Gnomonkey says that once you have the glyphs down, Yama rarely hits you except in the last 33% enraged push.
- ([21:20](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1280)) Gnomonkey says that with two synapses (Emberlight and purging staff) you have almost the same DPS as everyone else, since basically all DPS is in those two weapons.

### [Soulflame horn](../Soulflame_horn.md)

- ([1:02](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=62)) As of this May 2025 video, the Soulflame horn costs only around 50 million gp and is a new spec weapon.
- ([1:02](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=62)) The Soulflame horn costs 25% special attack and buffs a teammate; then both players' next melee hits are guaranteed to hit.
- ([1:28](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=88)) Gnomonkey's duo DPS loop with Emberlight is swing, horn, swing, horn, which he calls the best DPS you can do in a spec.
- ([1:28](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=88)) The horn lets an Elder maul land on Yama, and one horn basically puts Yama at the defence cap, so a single Elder maul spec from a teammate is all the defence drain needed.
- ([3:30](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=210)) The players must be near each other for the Soulflame horn to work, so Gnomonkey pre-horns right before starting the fight next to the boss.
- ([6:43](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=403)) The Soulflame horn does not work with any multi-splat hit, such as the scythe, and only works for single-splat hits like the Elder maul or bandos godsword.
- ([7:05](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=425)) Gnomonkey says the Abyssal dagger works with the horn only because it calculates in a weird way.

### [Emberlight](../Emberlight.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk)) Per the video description, Emberlight is always best in slot over the scythe at Yama with Torva helm and Oathplate chest/legs.
- ([4:04](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=244)) Gnomonkey says spamming Emberlight hits with the horn is very strong, and you should get an Emberlight for this boss.
- ([7:44](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=464)) On a budget, Gnomonkey says the goal is to land three Emberlight specs on Yama at the start, then dump burning claw specs.
- ([8:54](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=534)) In a duo budget run, one player acts as backup and only specs Emberlight if the partner's specs miss; you only need three Emberlight specs on the boss.
- ([18:20](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1100)) Emberlight's damage comes almost entirely from your gear's strength bonus: Gnomonkey's melee strength was 19 and rose only to 32 on equipping it, since the weapon itself has 13 strength.
- ([20:16](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1216)) Gnomonkey says each roughly 2 strength bonus adds about 1 max hit for Emberlight.
- ([20:50](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1250)) Gnomonkey advises bringing the highest strength-bonus gear, for example Blood Moon and Blue Moon, to maximise Emberlight's max hit; dropping strength bonus loses many max hits.

### [Purging staff](../Purging_staff.md)

- ([0:31](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=31)) Gnomonkey says the purging staff is effectively equal to the Shadow at Yama, with Shadow slightly better only on the last phase.
- ([2:28](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=148)) Even if you own a Shadow, Gnomonkey still brings the purging staff for one-shotting the orbs.
- ([8:04](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=484)) Gnomonkey says if you only have one synapse, make the purging staff, as it is very hard to replace and Emberlight alone is fairly good.
- ([19:12](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1152)) With the purging staff equipped, Mark of Darkness lasts five minutes instead of one minute, covering basically the whole kill.
- ([19:12](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1152)) Gnomonkey says Staff of the Dead or Sang are not alternatives, because Yama is effectively a super demon and the purging staff gets demonbane multipliers; his purging staff was hitting around 80.
- ([19:42](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1182)) Gnomonkey says it is almost not worth coming to Yama without a purging staff and recommends sending Tormented Demons to get at least one synapse to make one.

### [Mark of Darkness](../Mark_of_Darkness.md)

- ([1:40](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=100)) Gnomonkey brings Ward of Arceus, Thralls, Death Charge and Mark of Darkness, which needs Aether runes; without them you need one extra rune slot.
- ([18:12](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1092)) Mark of Darkness provides a tremendous amount of your DPS when maging Yama, so Gnomonkey says to always cast it when you equip the purging staff and start maging.
- ([19:12](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1152)) With the purging staff equipped, Mark of Darkness lasts five minutes rather than one minute.

### [Ward of Arceuus](../Ward_of_Arceuus.md)

- ([18:12](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1092)) Ward of Arceus reduces damage coming towards you from demons; Gnomonkey believes it takes about 10% off each Yama hit.
- ([18:39](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1119)) Gnomonkey says Ward of Arceus only costs nature runes in addition to what you already bring, so it is worth a rune slot.

### [Yama glyphs](../Yama_glyphs.md)

- ([4:00](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=240)) When Yama begins a special attack and a glyph appears, tapping the glyph blocks the special so it does not hit you.
- ([4:33](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=273)) Gnomonkey says you can null the orbs by touching the glyph as a backup, so you do not have to kill them; the void orb hit while the fire glyph did not because he touched the glyph.
- ([5:03](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=303)) Gnomonkey says the non-tank player should hit the glyphs, so that only the tank gets the full special; in good gear it is always one or two specials.
- ([10:58](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=658)) Gnomonkey says Yama always chooses the same glyph first at the start of the fight, and then specials follow the same order every time (clockwise or counterclockwise).
- ([14:58](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=898)) A huge ring of fire and a giant meteorite are specials you handle by grabbing the glyph; if the glyph-grabber misses the giant meteorite it one-shots the tank.
- ([16:12](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=972)) In a duo both players get the meteorites, but you cannot be hit by the other player's meteorites because it is client side.

### [Yama](../Yama.md)

- ([5:37](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=337)) Gnomonkey says Yama's enraged phase is much harder than the earlier phases and is the hardest part of the whole fight.
- ([6:07](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=367)) In the enraged phase, any time a player takes a melee hit it spawns an orb, so Gnomonkey uses the Shadow or purging staff and avoids tanking melee.
- ([6:07](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=367)) During the enrage meteors, step perpendicular to them; Yama alternates attacks between mage and range, so swap prayer each time.
- ([12:24](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=744)) Gnomonkey says to mage the entire enraged phase; meleeing there spawns many orbs and makes the fight much slower and harder.
- ([12:38](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=758)) In the enraged phase the orbs can be one-shot even without giga max gear, and dropping a spec on them refunds the spec; run early away from the fire attacks.
- ([16:45](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1005)) Gnomonkey says the orbs heal Yama and damage you tremendously if you do not kill them, so someone must deal with them.
- ([17:42](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1062)) Keep your health high during the enrage, because if Yama hits at the same time as a prayer drop it can kill you; Gnomonkey says sitting at about 40 health is not the play.

### [Judge of Yama](../Judge_of_Yama.md)

- ([4:30](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=270)) The Judge of Yama is a guaranteed hit; both players swap between mage and melee and share one health pool.
- ([10:28](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=628)) Gnomonkey recommends being in mage for the Judge because you can hit it on the rocks on the way to it; the Judge phase needs one melee and one mage.
- ([14:42](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=882)) The Judge's own attack can hurt itself: if you step under it while attacking, it hits itself with its skulls for extra damage.

### [Yama budget setup](../Yama_budget_setup.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk)) Per the description, Purging staff is pretty much equal to Shadow in max gear, and Arclight is 5% worse in Bandos than scythe in giga max.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk)) Per the description, use Ultor and surge potions work great at Yama; bow and ranged are useless.
- ([1:40](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=100)) Gnomonkey says two restores are enough for the fight, bringing three to be safe, with lots of food.
- ([2:28](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=148)) Gnomonkey says Oathplate is only about 1.3% better than Torva at Yama, per Hedron, so it is not necessary.
- ([7:44](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=464)) Gnomonkey's mid-level budget setup includes Pharaoh's sceptre-era gear, a fire cape, Bandos, Torms, Blue Moon gear and Emberlight plus a purging staff as the two key weapons.
- ([8:40](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=520)) Gnomonkey recommends the Party plugin so duo partners can see each other's spec landings without needing comms.
- ([13:54](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=834)) Gnomonkey says the scepter is not worth talking about for Yama because you mage so little.
