## Summary

A stream-highlight episode of Gnomonkey's group hardcore ironman run, covering a couple of weeks of Barrows grinding for tank armour ahead of Perilous Moons and the Colosseum. He gets a zombie axe at one kill count, a stretch of Tithe Farm skilling, and an Atlatl plus a Blood moon chestplate at 12 Moons kill count, while the group loses another member to a disconnect.

## Topics covered

### [Group Hardcore Ironman: Big Dawgs V1](../Group_Hardcore_Ironman_Big_Dawgs_V1.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/iUjSbo1TQFQ)) In the video description Gnomonkey says the group spent a couple of weeks grinding Barrows for tank top and bottom to move on to Perilous Moons, where they were spooned an Atlatl and a Blood moon chestplate.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/iUjSbo1TQFQ)) Per the video description, the group lost a life near the end of the week when Gnomonkey's internet disconnected for 14 hours and he had no teleport crystal; he says Scurrius has taken another homie.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/iUjSbo1TQFQ)) Per the description, Gnomonkey says he needs 75 Ranged and some Magic levels so the group can use thralls, and that they would be sending the Colosseum within a week.
- ([3:00](https://youtu.be/iUjSbo1TQFQ?t=180)) Gnomonkey recounts that the group storage was emptied after Sebe received roughly 20 gifted subs to remove everything from it, and that he then put about 20% of it back, so it is almost where it was.
- ([19:31](https://youtu.be/iUjSbo1TQFQ?t=1171)) At the start of the Perilous Moons section of the stream a hardcore death occurs and the group's count is given as three out of five remaining; it is repeated at the end of the stream.
- ([20:30](https://youtu.be/iUjSbo1TQFQ?t=1230)) Gnomonkey says the problem with playing a hardcore account is that when content is boring your brain shuts off, you stop paying attention, and then you die.

### [Barrows](../Barrows.md)

- ([5:53](https://youtu.be/iUjSbo1TQFQ?t=353)) Gnomonkey received a zombie axe, a one in 800 drop, on his first Barrows kill count after the quest.
- ([6:34](https://youtu.be/iUjSbo1TQFQ?t=394)) Gnomonkey says the group is trying to get Barrows tank armour before going to Perilous Moons and that they are basically set apart from the Barrows armour; Barrows gloves would also be nice.
- ([8:38](https://youtu.be/iUjSbo1TQFQ?t=518)) Gnomonkey says he survived a Barrows hit at one health (he calls it his first chance on the account), noting the max hit was 29 and he lasted by one HP.

### [Zombie axe](../Zombie_axe.md)

### Tithe Farm

- ([2:28](https://youtu.be/iUjSbo1TQFQ?t=148)) A chat suggestion that Gnomonkey agrees with: hold off on barrels until 72 Agility and get hard combat achievements for no prayer drain, the herb sack, and humidify to make prayer potions.
- ([14:34](https://youtu.be/iUjSbo1TQFQ?t=874)) Gnomonkey complains that Tithe Farm, the first skilling minigame added, is not instanced and can be interfered with by other players, that you must plant, water and go back repeatedly, and that you get punished if you turn in early.
- ([15:07](https://youtu.be/iUjSbo1TQFQ?t=907)) Gnomonkey says he never has to go back to Tithe Farm again except to get the herb sack, which he expects to need very soon.

### [Perilous Moons](../Perilous_Moons.md)
