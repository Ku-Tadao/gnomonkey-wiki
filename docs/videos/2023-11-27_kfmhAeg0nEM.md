## Summary

Gnomonkey completes a 1000 invocation Tombs of Amascut raid in Leagues in under 25 minutes, using the Berserker and Guardian relics together with a Tumeken's shadow. The run is stream commentary with little narration; the video finishes with a purple (a Tumeken's fang) and discussion of how much faster it could go.

## Topics covered

### [Tombs of Amascut](../Tombs_of_Amascut.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/kfmhAeg0nEM)) The description says Gnomonkey tried earlier to do the sub 25 run with just Mystics, but Baba kept him from completion by hitting over 40 through prayer.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/kfmhAeg0nEM)) To fix this, Gnomonkey farmed Chambers of Xeric and was spooned an Augury and Ancestral chest and legs, then completed the sub 25 raid.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/kfmhAeg0nEM)) The description states Gnomonkey wanted to push the time even lower but lacks time due to Chambers of Xeric QoL and playing around with Inferno Leagues speedruns.
- ([21:02](https://youtu.be/kfmhAeg0nEM?t=1262)) Gnomonkey finishes a 1000 invocation Tombs of Amascut raid on Leagues in sub 25 minutes; at the end he reacts to a sub 24 time.
- ([21:34](https://youtu.be/kfmhAeg0nEM?t=1294)) Gnomonkey notes the unique chance was 24.5% and jokes it rounds up to a 125% chance.
- ([22:00](https://youtu.be/kfmhAeg0nEM?t=1320)) The chest at the end of the sub 25 raid gave a purple, a Tumeken's fang, the third fang.
- ([22:00](https://youtu.be/kfmhAeg0nEM?t=1320)) Gnomonkey says his Akkha was terrible and that the run could easily be faster; he lists 5:55 for Wardens and 5:30 for Akkha, with his best Akkha being around four minutes, and thinks sub 22 is easily possible but sub 20 is pushing it.
- ([22:34](https://youtu.be/kfmhAeg0nEM?t=1354)) Gnomonkey does not know if the run is a record time and assumes Newtype will have a better time than the first one he sent.

### Leagues build for Tombs of Amascut

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/kfmhAeg0nEM)) Gnomonkey's relic setup for this run, per the description, is Production, Fairy, Banker's, Sorcerer's, Bloodthirsty, Farmer's, Berserker and Guardian.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/kfmhAeg0nEM)) The regions used were Khand, Desert and Kourend.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/kfmhAeg0nEM)) Gnomonkey says he has not seen a good argument for the Fire Cell relic and thinks it is not better than Banker's Note; he says everyone he has seen take Fire Cell regrets it, and that Banker's Note is insane early game too.
- ([8:32](https://youtu.be/kfmhAeg0nEM?t=512)) Gnomonkey says Infinity (armor) is pretty garbage and suggests going straight for Virtus.
