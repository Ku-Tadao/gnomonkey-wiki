## Summary

Gnomonkey's master guide to building a main (non-Ironman) OSRS account from scratch toward raiding and endgame PvM. He covers questing and diaries, maxing stats, the money-making methods he recommends (herb runs, Nex, Tombs of Amascut, Coliseum), how damage rolls work, common gear misconceptions, and how to use the Gearscape website for best-setup and DPS calculations. He closes with advice on learning raids by practising and failing.

## Topics covered

### Account building

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY)) Gnomonkey's Ultimate Account Building Guide targets people building a main account from scratch, both newcomers and established players wanting pointers, whether they want to raid or max and AFK at the Grand Exchange in max gear.
- ([0:29](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=29)) Gnomonkey says the guide is mostly for mains because he does not play Ironman in OSRS (he did on RS3), so he does not know most Ironman beginner methods.
- ([0:57](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=57)) Gnomonkey says he cannot tell viewers what will be the most fun, only the fastest way to the endgame, and he leads the guide from the viewpoint of someone who enjoys endgame content and raiding.
- ([4:22](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=262)) Gnomonkey considers grinding for max stats before starting major raid PvM a better path than starting raids at 85+ stats, saying you are shooting yourself in the foot otherwise and making teammates carry you.
- ([8:51](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=531)) Gnomonkey advises grinding the less fun but high-return money-makers first, then being free to do whatever you want in roughly a quarter of the time.
- ([20:12](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1212)) Gnomonkey advises against rebuilds (selling your whole bank to buy one mega rare) in the modern game, because there is too much variety in content with different best-in-slot equipment and you will lose more money than you gain and burn yourself out.
- ([20:12](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1212)) Gnomonkey says rebuilds were something players did when the Twisted bow was the only used mega rare and best in slot everywhere, before Tombs of Amascut was released.

### [Combat damage mechanics](../Combat_damage_mechanics.md)

- ([10:17](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=617)) Gnomonkey explains an OSRS attack first rolls accuracy: a random number based on your current attack level (including potion boosts), prayer and attack style, rolled against the target's defence level multiplied by its defence bonus for that style; if your number is bigger you hit, otherwise you hit zero.
- ([10:49](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=649)) Gnomonkey explains that when an attack lands, the damage is a random number from 1 up to your max hit, so each additional max hit is an extra pip on your die.
- ([11:17](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=677)) Gnomonkey says things that increase max hit are usually more important than accuracy, because a bigger die matters more than a 1% higher chance to roll the die.
- ([11:17](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=677)) Gnomonkey says ranged and magic work the same way, with ranged checking the monster's defence level and ranged defence, and magic checking the monster's magic level and magic defence; he notes weakness changes complicate this slightly.
- ([11:46](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=706)) Gnomonkey says this is why people barrage in melee gear: against a monster with 1 magic level you basically never splash.
- ([11:46](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=706)) Gnomonkey says combat style chooses which accuracy bonus of your gear is rolled, and styles give hidden level boosts: block gives +3 defence and slash on a rune scimitar gives +3 strength.
- ([12:15](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=735)) Gnomonkey says you basically always want your weapon on aggressive for the extra +3 strength levels; magic never gets increased max hit from style, so it matters much less.
- ([12:15](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=735)) Gnomonkey says ranged on rapid is a tick faster for all weapons, which outweighs even potential max hit gains from accurate every single time.
- ([12:15](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=735)) Gnomonkey says special attacks always use their own style regardless of the selected stance: if you put claws on stab, you roll the lower stab bonus against the enemy's slash defence, lowering accuracy against stab-weak enemies.

### [Osmumten's fang](../Osmumten's_fang.md)

- ([13:45](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=825)) Gnomonkey says the Lightbearer is also good for tanky bosses and bosses with downtime, such as Sotetseg, Maze or Duke Sucellus.

### Gear misconceptions

- ([13:45](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=825)) Gnomonkey says Mixed hide armour is ranged/melee hybrid armour with low requirements and cost and stab and strength bonus, roughly equal to Bandos when paired with a fang, slightly worse than black dragonhide with ranged gear, and bad for tanking like at God Wars Dungeon.
- ([13:45](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=825)) Gnomonkey says the Toxic blowpipe is high DPS and fast attacking, very bad on tanky targets over 150 defence, and the best DPS on low-defence targets such as everything drained in Theatre of Blood and Slayer monsters.
- ([14:17](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=857)) Gnomonkey says the only useful offhands are DPS-boosting ones; shields are not useful outside rare tanking situations like the Inferno or rare boss interactions like the Spectral shield at Cerberus.
- ([14:17](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=857)) Gnomonkey says the best-in-slot offhand for melee activity is a defender, for ranged a buckler, and for magic the ward, and that the Book of the Dead is a good inventory-saving offhand because a faster-dying boss hits you less.
- ([14:45](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=885)) Gnomonkey says camping an Elysian spirit shield will often make you take more damage overall than just camping a defender.
- ([15:19](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=919)) Gnomonkey says boots are typically not worth using as switches because they are tiny DPS boosts; melee boots are far better than other styles because of strength bonus.
- ([15:19](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=919)) Gnomonkey says Pegasian boots are almost completely useless everywhere, are not worth purchasing until you have nearly everything else in the game, and never see use in raids.

### [Gearscape](../Gearscape.md)

- ([20:33](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1233)) Gnomonkey recommends gearscape.net, where you plug in all your gear and compare DPS to see the best upgrade for the bosses you fight, and which can also suggest gear by best DPS with a GP limit.
- ([21:01](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1261)) Gnomonkey says he did all of his wiki guide videos using Gearscape, calls it infinitely more useful than any wiki setup, and says the site is constantly being updated.
- ([21:01](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1261)) Gnomonkey says Gearscape's best-setup tool gives an optimal setup for any boss based on your GP, the items you own as an Ironman (which can be imported), or an amount of risk in the Wilderness.
- ([21:34](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1294)) Gnomonkey says your in-game stats can be imported into Gearscape by typing your in-game name using the Wise Old Man plugin in RuneLite, with extra toggles for being on task, in the Wilderness, hard Seers' diary done, or using sunfire runes.
- ([22:00](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1320)) Gnomonkey says Gearscape's boss input includes team size and difficulty modifiers and the boss's stats, weaknesses and tags, such as Kephri being a Tombs of Amascut boss so the shadow and fang are multiplied against her, and you can enter a custom monster with any stats for testing future content.
- ([22:58](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1378)) Gnomonkey says to use the exclude tab in Gearscape for untradeable items or prayers you do not have (such as Infernal cape, quivers, Rigor, Augury) and Zamorakian brews, and the include tab mainly for Ironmen to mark owned items, set to 'mark free' for mains and 'only include' for Irons.
- ([23:28](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1408)) Gnomonkey says an Ironman can import their whole bank into Gearscape using the Bank Memory plugin in RuneLite (copy the saved bank and hit import), or import a bank tag tab for only specific items.
- ([24:04](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1444)) Gnomonkey says Gearscape maximises DPS by default given a GP budget, and for the Wilderness you set the number of expensive items you are willing to risk (typically four) and a value per item; setting it to 11 assumes risk is no worry.
- ([25:04](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1504)) Gnomonkey says Gearscape's output shows DPS, max hit, accuracy, average hit and total cost, plus assumed prayer and potion, and can be saved and imported back into RuneLite; slots can be locked (right-click, lock) when you want a specific item like the Lightbearer or Blood fury.
- ([26:12](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1572)) Gnomonkey says Gearscape's best-setup tool has a limitation: it only gives the best setup at one boss and cannot determine switches across multiple bosses for maximum DPS, so full raid setups still need a calculator and thought.
- ([30:06](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1806)) Gnomonkey says a recently added Gearscape share preset button generates a link that gives someone else the exact same calculator layout.
- ([30:18](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1818)) Gnomonkey says Gearscape's monster DPS output shows how hard monsters hit you in your tank gear, useful at places like Nex or God Wars Dungeon.
- ([30:40](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1840)) Gnomonkey says Gearscape's developers are friendly, fix reported bugs almost immediately via the Discord button on the site, and are working on open-sourcing the tool and integrating more with RuneLite.
