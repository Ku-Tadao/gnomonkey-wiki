## Summary

Gnomonkey completes a solo Nex speedrun in 26:30, which the description calls the fastest time by over 10 minutes. The video is mostly raw gameplay with little spoken explanation; the description credits Saxerpillar's lab work for making solo Nex possible and says the kill took 106 sweets.

## Topics covered

### [Nex](../Nex.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/HeG8Qyfx-gs)) Gnomonkey's world record solo Nex speedrun finished with a final time of 26:30, which the video description says is the fastest time by over 10 minutes.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/HeG8Qyfx-gs)) The video description says solo Nex became possible thanks to over a year of lab work by Saxerpillar.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/HeG8Qyfx-gs)) According to the description, the final hurdles to solo Nex were max distance resets, blood sacrifice and ice prison tick eats, plus simply figuring out to tank through Glacies.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/HeG8Qyfx-gs)) Gnomonkey says solo Nex is absolutely not worth it for GP, although it is still somewhat profitable.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/HeG8Qyfx-gs)) Gnomonkey says solo Nex can be done 100% consistently and plans to speedrun it in the future.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/HeG8Qyfx-gs)) Gnomonkey rates solo Nex as roughly equal in difficulty to a normal solo Theatre of Blood.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/HeG8Qyfx-gs)) The 26:30 solo Nex run took 106 sweets, with many more used on attempts, and dragon arrows were also used up on the failed attempts.
- ([0:54](https://youtu.be/HeG8Qyfx-gs?t=54)) In Gnomonkey's solo Nex run, most of the supplies used over the whole kill go on defeating minions, because he cannot tick-eat while also killing them.
- ([0:54](https://youtu.be/HeG8Qyfx-gs?t=54)) Gnomonkey restores his stats at an altar before fighting Nex in the speedrun.

### [Gnomonkey](../Gnomonkey.md)
