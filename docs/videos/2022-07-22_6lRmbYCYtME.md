## Summary

A recorded live teaching stream in which Gnomonkey completes an Inferno with a Twisted bow while explaining gear, pillar management, monster mechanics, safe spots, corner traps, phantom barrage healing, lazy and one-tick prayer flicking, and the hardest waves, ending with Jad, triple Jad and Zuk. He says it is not a comprehensive guide or every possible solve, but an example of a basic Inferno completion, inspired by Deviate's original Twisted bow teaching stream.

## Topics covered

### [Inferno](../Inferno.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME)) Gnomonkey says the two most important stats for the Inferno are prayer bonus and ranged defense, so the goal with any setup is to maximize those two.
- ([0:28](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=28)) Gnomonkey says Devout boots, Guardian boots and Pegasian boots are all fine for the Inferno, and Armadyl versus crystal armour is also fine, as these are preference.
- ([0:28](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=28)) Gnomonkey says a Crystal shield is totally fine, only barely worse than Ellie's ward (an upgrade), a Kodai wand saves an inventory spot because you do not need to bring waters, and an Occult necklace gives a higher chance of one-shotting nibblers.
- ([0:57](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=57)) Gnomonkey recommends one divine and one regular ranging potion if you have vambraces, because at the max boost the Twisted bow hits up to 75 instead of 73; if you only bring Barrows gloves that matters less and one or two ranging potions is up to you.
- ([0:57](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=57)) Gnomonkey says to wear the max cape if you have Barrows gloves, otherwise wear the Ava's assembler because it is needed to get the max hits.
- ([1:26](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=86)) Gnomonkey finds the Ring of the Gods far better than the Ring of Suffering for the Inferno, since it gives a holy wrench effect (about three doses of restore) and has 8 prayer bonus instead of 4, and says it should be your default.
- ([2:57](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=177)) Gnomonkey says you would normally bring eight brews for the Inferno, but he brings fewer in this stream and uses Rigor on everything.
- ([3:56](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=236)) Gnomonkey says the name of the game in the Inferno is keeping your supplies up, and that pillar health counts as a supply.
- ([9:18](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=558)) Gnomonkey says health is extremely important to get back every wave and you should usually aim for 99 HP before each wave starts, since it gives the highest chance of survival.
- ([14:19](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=859)) Gnomonkey says you can practice two Jads before the Inferno, which is almost exactly the same and gives a feel for it, and that triple Jad is not the hard part of the Inferno at all.
- ([16:19](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=979)) Gnomonkey says the waves are by far the hardest part of the Inferno, harder than Jad or Zuk.
- ([17:16](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1036)) Gnomonkey says wave 63, unless you get lucky, is probably the hardest part of the entire caves, though he notes the healers fight is also very hard.
- ([31:26](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1886)) Gnomonkey says running to South pillar can get you a cape and he won't BM you for it, but you will get through consistently if you learn not to run to South pillar and not to panic.
- ([31:53](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1913)) Gnomonkey says the early waves are free, so you should maintain your resources by keeping pillar health up, not wasting prayer and not wasting anything so the last waves are as easy as they can be.
- ([32:27](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1947)) Gnomonkey says the landmark dangerous waves before mage and ranger are waves 31 and 48, which are ranger-melee double blob and mager-melee double blob respectively.
- ([1:02:56](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=3776)) Gnomonkey says he has already done a pillarless Inferno on stream, found it not very eventful and easy, and might speedrun it when really bored.
- ([1:09:24](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=4164)) Asked for his Inferno PB, Gnomonkey gives it as 49:18 (transcribed '4918') and says he died on a 47:30 pace run.
- ([1:35:49](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=5749)) Gnomonkey completes the Inferno at the end of the stream, in about an hour and a half with almost no flicking, taking only one Jad hit during the run.

### [Inferno pillars](../Inferno_pillars.md)

- ([2:57](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=177)) Gnomonkey says the big early priority is to kill nibblers as fast as possible since nothing can kill you early, so you keep more pillar health.
- ([53:24](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=3204)) Gnomonkey explains his tile markers: yellow tiles are for Zuk and triples, one is the Addy tile not used on a first cape, a green tile lets you hit nibblers on a pillar through it, and another tile lets you cast at nibblers on your pillar; he says the markers can be imported from the pastebin on his Inferno guide.
- ([54:58](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=3298)) Gnomonkey says nibblers after wave 50 get one cast only and you never chase a nibbler after wave 50.
- ([56:25](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=3385)) Gnomonkey says you can instantly turn the corner of a pillar no matter what cycle monsters are in because you always get a tick to switch to the next prayer, and that knowing you can rotate pillars at any moment is very helpful.
- ([57:27](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=3447)) Gnomonkey says West pillar is the least important pillar and he does not risk his health to save it from nibblers.
- ([59:19](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=3559)) Gnomonkey says that for speedruns you want West and South pillars dead, ideally with South pillar going down on wave 50, so monsters come to North pillar instead.
- ([1:07:45](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=4065)) Gnomonkey calls West pillar the decoy pillar and says if you stand a tile off a monster, when a melee digs the other monster can act as a pillar and get stuck on you, which works with mages, rangers and any NPC.
- ([1:10:57](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=4257)) Gnomonkey says North pillar is the one he cares about, because he can cast on things at it from about 50 angles, while things on West and South pillars are hard to get at.
- ([1:12:58](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=4378)) Gnomonkey says he has seen many people die trying to save South and West pillars while leaving things on North pillar, and that North is the one to care about.

- ([13:24](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=804)) Gnomonkey says if a blob reads you on the first tick it attacks three ticks later and then every six ticks, so it goes read, attack, read, attack.
- ([17:46](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1066)) Gnomonkey says to leave blobs for last when in a good phantom position because they are a giant medkit worth about 40 health.
- ([20:47](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1247)) Gnomonkey likes to kill blobs in the position where you cannot phantom them, since it is ideal to take them out of the picture.
- ([35:59](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2159)) Gnomonkey says a blob's ranged hit does about half as much as its mage hit, and is almost always a zero when you are praying mage.

### [Jal-ImKot](../Jal-ImKot.md)

- ([7:34](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=454)) Gnomonkey says the southwest tile of a monster is the brain of the monster, the tile that tries to path straight at you, which is why a melee cannot attack you when it cannot reach you; it can be shown with the NPC Indicators plugin.
- ([30:56](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1856)) Gnomonkey says there is a really long delay on the melee's attack after it digs, which he believes is eight ticks after it comes up before it can attack you.

- ([1:20:32](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=4832)) Gnomonkey says melees almost never hit you because they have really low accuracy, and can hit 49, but that is rare; he says not to tank them on purpose.
- ([1:20:32](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=4832)) Gnomonkey says when a melee is on you it cannot dig.

- ([1:23:28](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=5008)) Gnomonkey says on a first cape with four and a half restores he would not flick two mages and would just kill them, as it is too much risk, though he does it all the time.

### [Corner trapping](../Corner_trapping.md)

- ([7:55](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=475)) Gnomonkey explains that a monster's brain really likes going east or west first towards you, so when a melee is desperately trying to go east through a pillar to hit you, it just sits there; that is a corner trap.
- ([7:55](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=475)) Gnomonkey says corner trap spots exist on several sides of a pillar and should be memorized so they can save you in later waves.
- ([9:50](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=590)) Gnomonkey says if you stand on the bat safe spot when a melee digs, you can corner safe spot, which becomes important later.
- ([13:53](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=833)) Gnomonkey says there is no single corner trap tile because a whole row of tiles can be used, so do not be afraid to use any of them.
- ([22:19](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1339)) Gnomonkey says anytime a melee digs against the side of a pillar you can pull it down to the corner trap by default.
- ([42:21](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2541)) Gnomonkey says you can also pull a corner-trapped melee around the pillar and it will stay stuck, and sometimes you want to rotate to the other side while corner trapping to speed things up.
- ([43:23](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2603)) Gnomonkey says to be proactive with melees by always having a plan for where to stand when it digs, since there is no guarantee you kill something in time.

### [Phantom barrage](../Phantom_barrage.md)

- ([2:24](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=144)) Gnomonkey keeps his autocast on blood barrage because most of the time when barraging for health you are AFKing on it, and he manually casts ice barrage for the most part.

- ([36:58](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2218)) Gnomonkey explains phantom: if you are far enough away, your barrage has an invisible travel time, and while it is in the air you can cast at a corpse and get the full heal off it, so with bloblets you heal off three corpses.

### [Prayer flicking](../Prayer_flicking.md)

- ([15:21](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=921)) Gnomonkey explains lazy flicking: for all attacks in the Inferno the attack is calculated as soon as the animation starts, so you switch the prayer on as soon as the monster hunkers down, and flicking even for two ticks still saves prayer.
- ([15:50](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=950)) Gnomonkey says you have to flick to get through the Inferno and that learning it is really important, with some flicking better than none.
- ([20:47](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1247)) Gnomonkey says to alternate prayers against a ranger and a blob by praying ranged, clicking the ranger, and alternating as soon as you see him hunker down, keeping both prayers lit and clicking the next as soon as one unlights.
- ([21:48](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1308)) Gnomonkey says flinching is a very bad habit and that he never recommends it; he says to learn how to one-tick, which solves just about every wave.
- ([23:25](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1405)) Gnomonkey says he does not recommend two-ticking on a first cape, since people often get their blob off-tick and still take every blob hit, and that one-tick is easier because you just alternate when the prayer turns off.
- ([23:52](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1432)) Gnomonkey says if you need to move or attack you should not be flicking; get into a position where you do not need to move, then start alternating.
- ([25:55](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1555)) Gnomonkey says if you learn to flinch and never learn to one-tick, solves with a ranger and blob that come up a lot are not possible.
- ([28:00](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1680)) Gnomonkey describes one-tick flicking like a Gears of War reload: you click twice per tick, double click repeatedly, and he uses the prayer helper and prayer plugin.
- ([29:31](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1771)) Gnomonkey says to turn your prayers on before double clicking or you are just rapidly turning your prayers off.
- ([35:01](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2101)) Gnomonkey says you can easily get a cape with just lazy flicking and that he did not know how to one-tick flick when he got his first cape; one-tick flicking only speeds things up.
- ([47:39](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2859)) Asked about timing, Gnomonkey says he goes off sound if not looking at the screen and goes off the prayer unlighting if looking at the prayers, and that he does not like relying on sound cues in the game.

### [Off-ticking](../Off-ticking.md)

- ([1:15:27](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=4527)) Gnomonkey explains off-ticking a ranger and mager around a corner: wait for the attack of the thing next to you, then run out to the other monster so it sees you right after, which two-tick off-ticks them by default.
- ([1:19:00](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=4740)) Gnomonkey says a melee is not automatically off-ticked by a mage when it digs; it depends on the mage's attack cycle, and if the mage attacks the same tick the melee digs they are the same tick.
- ([1:19:38](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=4778)) Gnomonkey says when flicking with a melee in the mix he stares at the melee's movement to see when it off-ticks, and says not to be afraid of doing this.
- ([1:22:38](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=4958)) Gnomonkey says he does not care which tick things are off-ticked on, as long as it is off-tick and you can recognize it quickly or force it.
