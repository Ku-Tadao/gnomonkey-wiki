## Summary

A roughly 5 hour 50 minute Kick livestream VOD in which Gnomonkey does Tombs of Amascut with his alt Raddy, a solo Theatre of Blood attempt aiming for sub 55, and duo Nex, while chatting with viewers. It is mostly chat banter and Kick setup talk (founder badges, gifted subs, 7TV), with extended asides on learning Inferno-style content without fear of dying, item lending in OSRS, and PvM technique naming.

### [Tombs of Amascut](../Tombs_of_Amascut.md)

- ([56:03](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=3363)) In duo ToA, Gnomonkey gets Baba onto his main at the very end of the room by tanking a melee, so she swaps to him, then starts the boulder special so her next melee swaps her to his alt Raddy.

- ([1:29:02](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=5342)) In the ToA Wardens phase, Gnomonkey warns that if you do not tank three skulls you get the wrong Warden, and for solos he suggests tanking about four to be safe.

### [Opinion: Dying is part of learning content](../Opinion_Dying_is_part_of_learning_content.md)

- ([47:40](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=2860)) Gnomonkey says hard raids are not content you are expecting to finish every time, and that if you expect to you will not finish any.
- ([48:38](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=2918)) Gnomonkey says every time you enter content you do not know perfectly you are investing in learning it, and that each raid he is still learning something new and refining.
- ([49:07](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=2947)) Gnomonkey criticises players who cash out to lower raid levels because they keep dying at higher ones, saying that is very short-term thinking and that learning the higher level fully would make more money overall.
- ([49:40](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=2980)) Gnomonkey says that when doing something new he expects to die over and over and treats dying as a cost he has already mentally prepared for.
- ([50:09](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=3009)) Gnomonkey says that worrying about the cost of things makes content much harder to play, and he thinks you figure this out once you go for the Inferno.
- ([50:36](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=3036)) Gnomonkey says to commit to losing every supply you walk in with and use everything, without questioning each supply you use, calling it the non-poor-man mindset.
- ([51:39](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=3099)) Gnomonkey says it is impossible to get your Inferno cape if you get triggered instantly, and that this is not only about supply cost but also about not worrying about dying.
- ([53:37](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=3217)) Gnomonkey says that if your mindset shifts a little, everything becomes doable.

### Opinion: Item lending in OSRS

- ([3:56:15](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=14175)) Gnomonkey says to only lend items you are willing to lose, noting everything he has lent is something he is willing to lose.
- ([3:57:15](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=14235)) Gnomonkey says he would only lend a Twisted bow if the borrower had roughly a ten billion coin bank.
- ([3:58:22](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=14302)) Asked whether RuneScape 3's lending system has a place in OSRS, Gnomonkey says he does not see an issue with it, noting RuneScape 3 lending could not be used for degradable items so people only lent party hats.
- ([3:58:22](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=14302)) Gnomonkey says OSRS has many items that would work well with lending, such as the Twisted bow.
- ([4:00:58](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=14458)) Gnomonkey says he once lent a ham joint to a player in the Nightmare who had to step out after his car was hit, and told him to keep the ham joint.

### Opinion: OSRS design inconsistency and engine

- ([4:47:41](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=17261)) Gnomonkey says dropped items despawn in instanced fights like Nex because of lag in instances.
- ([4:49:04](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=17344)) Asked why items stay in other instances like the Chambers of Xeric, Gnomonkey says the one developer working on a piece of content decides without consulting others, which is why everything is different everywhere.
- ([4:49:34](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=17374)) Gnomonkey says prayer flicking as an endgame PvM mechanic exists because some developer coded it that way one day.
- ([4:50:06](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=17406)) Gnomonkey says Jagex would probably like to overhaul the engine, but there are so many snowflake accounts and bugs that are now features.
- ([4:51:55](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=17515)) Gnomonkey says he believes the original developer Mod Ash knew what a game tick was and probably knew prayer flicking was a thing.
