## Summary

A guide to Suqah Slayer tasks on Lunar Isle using a cannon. Gnomonkey says they are terrible for loot but give a clean 60k Slayer XP an hour and are a short task from Duradel.

## Topics covered

### [Suqah](../Suqah.md)

- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40)) Gnomonkey says Suqah give a clean 60k Slayer XP an hour, but their only drops are from the rare drop table.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40)) Suqah tasks from Duradel are incredibly short, each taking about ten minutes, and have a weight of eight, so they come up fairly often.
- ([0:00](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40)) Suqah have no requirements to fight, but to be assigned them the player needs to have progressed Lunar Diplomacy up to getting to the island.
- ([0:27](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40?t=27)) Gnomonkey says Suqah can smack you around quite a bit, so you may need more food with worse defensive gear.
- ([1:08](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40?t=68)) The fastest way to the Suqah is a lunar teleport portal at a player-owned house; Gnomonkey instead uses Fremennik sea boots and takes the boat to Lunar Isle, and a lyre or Relleka house teleport also work.
- ([1:26](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40?t=86)) On arrival Gnomonkey drops his cannon on a specific tile, drinks his divine super combat, and turns on piety and protect from magic.
- ([1:26](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40?t=86)) Suqah randomly use magic attacks that do little damage but freeze the player, which is annoying, so Gnomonkey protects from magic.
- ([1:26](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40?t=86)) Gnomonkey recommends grabbing some Suqah teeth on a first task, as they are used to protect spirit trees from disease.
- ([1:53](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40?t=113)) A task of 67 Suqah took Gnomonkey eight minutes, which he says is definitely one of the shorter tasks in the game.
- ([1:53](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40?t=113)) Gnomonkey got 60k Slayer XP an hour on Suqah, which he calls incredibly good compared with most cannon tasks, but the loot was two rubies, about 6k gp.
- ([1:53](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40?t=113)) Gnomonkey says you do not do Suqah tasks for the loot but the incredible XP easily makes up for it.

### [Slayer](../Slayer.md)

- ([0:27](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40?t=27)) For Suqah Gnomonkey says Bandos can be downgraded to fighter torso and obsidian legs, primordial boots to dragon boots, ferocious gloves to Barrows gloves, and torture to fury.
- ([0:27](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40?t=27)) Gnomonkey brings a gem sack but skips the herb sack on Suqah because they only drop low-value herbs like guam and marrentill.
- ([0:57](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40?t=57)) Gnomonkey brings a Saradomin godsword, bone crusher, wrench and cannon for Suqah, plus a divine super combat and six prayer potions, filling the rest of the inventory with food as they drop no loot.

### [Dwarf multicannon](../Dwarf_multicannon.md)

- ([0:57](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40?t=57)) Gnomonkey brings a cannon for Suqah tasks and says the Slayer XP rate is incredibly good compared with most cannon tasks.
