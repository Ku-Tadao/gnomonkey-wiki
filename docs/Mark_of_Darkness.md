**Mark of Darkness** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2025-05-14 and 2025-05-15. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* Gnomonkey brings Ward of Arceus, Thralls, Death Charge and Mark of Darkness, which needs Aether runes; without them you need one extra rune slot.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-100]

## Stats and numbers

* With the purging staff equipped, Mark of Darkness lasts five minutes rather than one minute.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-1152]

## Tips

* Mark of Darkness provides a tremendous amount of your DPS when maging Yama, so Gnomonkey says to always cast it when you equip the purging staff and start maging.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-1092]
* Gnomonkey casts spells such as thrall, death charge and Ward of Arceus before starting the Yama fight.[^prE1BqM1wBE-643]
* To cast Mark of Darkness at Yama because it is important to your damage; he gained roughly 18 to 20 max hits from it.[^prE1BqM1wBE-781]
* Gnomonkey also casts Death Charge at Yama, noting it works on him.[^prE1BqM1wBE-1310]
