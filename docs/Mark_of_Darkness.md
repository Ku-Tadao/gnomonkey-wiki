# Mark of Darkness

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2025-05-14 to 2025-05-15). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Requirements

- Gnomonkey brings Ward of Arceus, Thralls, Death Charge and Mark of Darkness, which needs Aether runes; without them you need one extra rune slot. ([2025-05-14 1:40](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=100))

## Stats and numbers

- With the purging staff equipped, Mark of Darkness lasts five minutes rather than one minute. ([2025-05-14 19:12](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1152))

## Tips

- Mark of Darkness provides a tremendous amount of your DPS when maging Yama, so Gnomonkey says to always cast it when you equip the purging staff and start maging. ([2025-05-14 18:12](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1092))
- Gnomonkey casts spells such as thrall, death charge and Ward of Arceus before starting the Yama fight. ([2025-05-15 10:43](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=643))
- Gnomonkey says to cast Mark of Darkness at Yama because it is important to your damage; he gained roughly 18 to 20 max hits from it. ([2025-05-15 13:01](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=781))
- Gnomonkey also casts Death Charge at Yama, noting it works on him. ([2025-05-15 21:50](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=1310))
