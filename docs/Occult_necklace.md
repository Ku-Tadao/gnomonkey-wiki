**Occult necklace** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2023-12-22 and 2024-06-13. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Tips

* You could theoretically bring just the Occult and be fine in Inferno but you would be sitting there forever healing, so a mage top is brought to speed up healing.[^XMTe9TYbDls-184]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the Occult necklace providing 10% magic damage is absolutely absurd and always has been.[^t5mXf8KJztU-333]
* Gnomonkey says the nerf will make mid-game magic quite a bit weaker, and spreading the damage through Ancestral robes, which currently cost 330 mil, would lose that damage unless you have absolute max gear.[^t5mXf8KJztU-365]
* Gnomonkey would be okay with moving 5% of the necklace's damage to Augury, since Augury is often unused and much weaker than other best offensive prayers; the downside is that it would be a massive buff to Gauntlet.[^t5mXf8KJztU-365]
* Gnomonkey says the Occult nerf is not worth compensating for by bringing more mage switches in Inferno; you still bring Virtus top, Crystal shield, a 15% damage mage staff and Occult.[^XMTe9TYbDls-0]

## Events

* The Occult necklace is being nerfed: Jagex plans to bring its power down slightly and distribute its magic damage across other parts of magic progression.[^t5mXf8KJztU-333]
* The Occult necklace lost a bunch of damage in Project Rebalance with the damage spread over armours, leaving Gnomonkey's mage setup roughly one max hit lower.[^XMTe9TYbDls-27]
