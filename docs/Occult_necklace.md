# Occult necklace

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2023-12-22 to 2024-06-13). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says you could theoretically bring just the Occult and be fine in Inferno but you would be sitting there forever healing, so a mage top is brought to speed up healing. ([2024-06-13 3:04](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=184))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the Occult necklace providing 10% magic damage is absolutely absurd and always has been. ([2023-12-22 5:33](https://youtu.be/t5mXf8KJztU?t=333))
- Gnomonkey says the nerf will make mid-game magic quite a bit weaker, and spreading the damage through Ancestral robes, which currently cost 330 mil, would lose that damage unless you have absolute max gear. ([2023-12-22 6:05](https://youtu.be/t5mXf8KJztU?t=365))
- Gnomonkey would be okay with moving 5% of the necklace's damage to Augury, since Augury is often unused and much weaker than other best offensive prayers; the downside is that it would be a massive buff to Gauntlet. ([2023-12-22 6:05](https://youtu.be/t5mXf8KJztU?t=365))
- Gnomonkey says the Occult nerf is not worth compensating for by bringing more mage switches in Inferno; you still bring Virtus top, Crystal shield, a 15% damage mage staff and Occult. ([2024-06-13 0:00](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls))

## Events

- The Occult necklace is being nerfed: Jagex plans to bring its power down slightly and distribute its magic damage across other parts of magic progression. ([2023-12-22 5:33](https://youtu.be/t5mXf8KJztU?t=333))
- The Occult necklace lost a bunch of damage in Project Rebalance with the damage spread over armours, leaving Gnomonkey's mage setup roughly one max hit lower. ([2024-06-13 0:27](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=27))
