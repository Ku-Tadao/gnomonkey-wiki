**Royal Titans** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2025-02-26 and 2025-10-15. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Tips

* The Royal Titans' wave attacks, where they slam their spears repeatedly, kill the elementals of the opposite colour.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-771]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* In the video description Gnomonkey says the new Royal Titans prayers mean players can reach the Inferno immediately after Chambers in many cases and grab the Inferno cape before the Chambers grind.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-7]
* Gnomonkey says the Royal Titans 'Elemental Company'-style CA (kill the Royal Titans without attacking any elementals) is the only CA he found across the easy, medium and hard tiers that he considers good.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-708]
* Gnomonkey says the Royal Titans no-elementals CA encourages lower-level players to leave their comfort zone and find creative ways to deal with the elementals, such as dropping walls on them, since it can be a faster way to kill the boss but more dangerous.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-737]
* Gnomonkey says he has very few complaints about Titans, calling them a great low-level boss that teaches a lot of important things and gets players into doing bosses.[^YjYgGFi5878-459]
* Gnomonkey notes that among the 2025 updates he liked, Titans was a low-level boss, showing that his favourites were not the high-level bosses, which had major glaring flaws.[^YjYgGFi5878-582]

## Events

* The Royal Titans update brought upgraded prayers; Gnomonkey says many players, with the 'Bodie guide', are now doing Chambers of Xeric (CG) with the mid-tier prayers.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-0]
