# Nex

Nex is the Zaros general added to the [God Wars Dungeon](God_Wars_Dungeon.md) in January 2022, which Gnomonkey previewed from his RuneScape 2 experience in 2021 and has covered repeatedly since. He calls her the best money-making method in the game, best fought as a duo or small team, and says she is harder and deeper than her reputation suggests, though in 2026 he says he no longer fights her after the 2025 melee changes.

## Access and requirements

- Nex is a raid-style boss: up to 60 players can fight her on a world, and group instances allow any team size as long as one member has the hard combat achievements. ([2022-01-19 0:00](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=0))
- The dungeon requires the miniquest The Frozen Door (Desert Treasure plus a delivered message), which in practice means 70 Hitpoints, Ranged, Strength and Agility. ([2022-01-19 0:00](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=0) [2022-01-19 0:30](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=30))
- Masses need as little as 80 Ranged; small teams want Ranged as high as possible, ideally in the 90s. ([2022-01-19 0:30](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=30))
- She has a killcount like other God Wars bosses (an ancient item avoids the Ancient enemies' aggro), then a lobby with a bank; he recommends spiritual mages, or Blood Reavers without the Spirit level, for killcount. ([2022-01-19 1:03](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=63) [2022-01-19 1:03](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=63) [2022-01-19 1:35](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=95))
- In 2024 he says each trip needs about ten minutes of killcount, wants the old coin skip back, and notes RS2's full ancestral gave instant killcount. ([2024-03-02 5:36:57](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=20217) [2024-03-02 5:37:59](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=20279)) Grandmaster [Combat Achievements](Combat_Achievements.md) give a fake 15 Ancient killcount, and in a duo you always get 15, so none is needed. ([2024-06-23 14:17](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=857))
- Duo Nex needs roughly a 200 million GP starter cache. ([2024-06-15 0:00](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=0)) The 2023 minimum gear was Osmumten's fang, Armadyl crossbow and Karil's, doable on a low bank but very challenging. ([2023-05-28 0:00](https://youtu.be/vFVvnNJnyZQ?t=0)) A budget duo also needs [Thralls](Thralls.md), which he calls most important at Nex because they bypass her high defence, and the Kandarin hard diary for bolt chance. ([2023-07-04 0:00](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=0) [2023-07-04 2:30](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=150))
- World 420 is the free-for-all world for Nex. ([2022-05-11 13:30](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=810))

## The fight

Nex has five phases: Smoke, Shadow, Blood and Ice, then a final Zaros phase. In each of the first four, damaging her to a threshold makes the minion vulnerable and she is invincible until it dies. ([2022-01-19 2:42](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=162) [2022-01-19 3:12](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=192) [2024-06-15 8:56](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=536)) In each phase she starts with a special and then does five regular attacks. ([2022-01-19 3:12](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=192)) She cannot be damaged until a minion's projectile reaches her, so he advises not spam clicking at transitions. ([2022-01-19 5:21](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=321)) Minions have 200 Magic, so the [Twisted bow](Twisted_bow.md) is nearly always best against them. ([2022-01-19 3:42](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=222))

### Smoke and Shadow

- The virus drains prayer and the main combat stat, spreads within one tile and can last the whole kill; face coverings shorten it. ([2022-01-19 5:21](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=321)) In masses players seek a virus tank or a low-population world, and small teams stand in a hexagon around Nex to prevent spread. ([2022-01-19 15:08](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=908) [2022-01-19 19:41](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=1181))
- Shadow projectiles are proximity-based and can hit for zero outside 10 tiles under Protect from Missiles; in 2024 he gives hits of 1 to 3 at distance, so crossbow shots are flinched in. ([2022-01-19 7:18](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=438) [2024-06-15 17:43](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=1063)) Fear the Shadow hits for roughly 50 and Embrace Darkness for 10 to 20 up close; he dislikes its guaranteed death and his team uses techs to avoid it. ([2022-01-19 7:18](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=438) [2022-04-11 5:02](https://youtu.be/Y2KvOrUXUeA?t=302))
- Umbra is the most dangerous minion because the team prays Protect from Missiles for Nex while his damage is magic. ([2022-01-19 7:48](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=468)) Minions are said to max 29 (January 2022) and Umbra 28 (April 2022), then Umbra hits 29s when praying ranged (2023). ([2022-01-19 3:42](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=222) [2022-04-11 7:27](https://youtu.be/Y2KvOrUXUeA?t=447) [2023-07-04 6:38](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=398))
- A pre-assigned Umbra tank (usually the player without a Twisted bow) moves to the north-east corner at 2,200 to 2,300 Nex HP and prays Protect from Magic so Umbra picks them, while the rest stay at the reset wall. ([2022-04-11 7:27](https://youtu.be/Y2KvOrUXUeA?t=447) [2022-04-11 7:54](https://youtu.be/Y2KvOrUXUeA?t=474) [2022-04-11 7:54](https://youtu.be/Y2KvOrUXUeA?t=474))

### Ice

- Her autos drain prayer, and an off-prayer magic hit freezes the target for a long time. ([2022-01-19 9:16](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=556) [2024-06-15 23:31](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=1411))
- Contain This hits for around 50 and toggles prayers off; stepping out of range makes her sit idle, a safe time to brew and crossbow. ([2022-01-19 9:16](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=556) [2023-07-04 10:06](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=606) [2024-06-15 23:01](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=1381))
- Die Now traps a random player and hits for 80 unless another player breaks an icicle with a crush weapon; he calls it her most dangerous special. ([2022-01-19 9:49](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=589) [2022-01-19 10:16](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=616)) In 2024 he says a fang hit on a stalagmite frees everyone. ([2024-06-15 23:31](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=1411))

### Final phase

- Nex heals 500 HP, activates Turmoil and Soul Split and stops using specials while her max hits ramp up. ([2022-01-19 10:49](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=649))
- Prayer cycle: in January 2022, every four attacks, Soul Split then Deflect Melee then none; in 2024, four attacks each of no prayer, Deflect Melee, then Soul Split. ([2022-01-19 11:47](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=707) [2024-06-15 14:39](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=879)) Deflect Melee reflects 50%; Soul Split heals her about 50% of damage dealt; with no prayer her melee leeches stats. ([2024-06-15 14:49](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=889) [2024-06-15 15:25](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=925))
- He swaps to a ranged weapon during Deflect Melee (in 2022 three bow shots) and brews then; the four-hit crossbow section is the hardest part for learners. ([2022-01-19 18:10](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=1090) [2023-07-04 11:05](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=665) [2024-06-15 14:49](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=889) [2024-06-15 15:43](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=943))
- He advises melee range for the team since her magic hits hard through prayer while melee hits only the target. ([2022-01-19 12:18](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=738)) He notes that below 30 health a melee-praying player is safe from melee (max 15), while in 2024 he says never to drop below 15. ([2023-05-28 9:17](https://youtu.be/vFVvnNJnyZQ?t=557) [2024-06-15 34:34](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=2074))

## Targeting and mechanics

- Nex swaps targets every three attacks (2022; 2023 "typically three"), about every two (2023-05, 2024), and about four in the final phase; one 2023 source says she cannot pick the same player twice, another that she can. ([2022-01-19 4:15](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=255) [2023-05-28 2:56](https://youtu.be/vFVvnNJnyZQ?t=176) [2023-07-04 3:29](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=209) [2024-06-15 8:10](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=490))
- Her attack timer is four ticks against a player's five, so stepping under her every fifth tick forces five-tick attacks; he calls five-ticking the biggest duo skill and says it takes 10 to 15 kills to learn. ([2023-05-28 2:23](https://youtu.be/vFVvnNJnyZQ?t=143) [2023-07-04 2:57](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=177) [2023-07-04 12:34](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=754))
- Resets (see [Nex reset](Nex_reset.md)) are the next most important: failing one kills, especially in Shadow. ([2023-05-28 3:23](https://youtu.be/vFVvnNJnyZQ?t=203) [2023-07-04 6:07](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=367) [2024-06-15 20:10](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=1210))
- She has a 10-tile range and cannot target a player outside it. ([2022-04-11 4:30](https://youtu.be/Y2KvOrUXUeA?t=270) [2022-04-11 4:30](https://youtu.be/Y2KvOrUXUeA?t=270))
- When a player is far away she may enter melee mode and run up; if all players are in melee range she melees, with roughly a 20% magic chance (2024). ([2022-01-19 4:15](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=255) [2022-01-19 4:15](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=255) [2024-06-15 7:42](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=462) [2024-06-15 9:47](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=587))
- Under Nex projectiles land instantly and cannot be flicked, and no items can be dropped for others. ([2024-03-02 4:44:16](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=17056) [2024-03-02 4:47:08](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=17228))
- Prayer: melee while she attacks you, magic otherwise; ranged calculates a tick later so melee then ranged can be flicked. ([2023-05-28 4:20](https://youtu.be/vFVvnNJnyZQ?t=260) [2024-06-15 7:42](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=462) [2024-06-23 6:23](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=383))

## Strategy

### Masses

- Masses are worse money but easier to get into; in 2023 mass Nex is "really bad" next to duo Nex. ([2022-01-19 2:42](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=162) [2023-05-28 1:50](https://youtu.be/vFVvnNJnyZQ?t=110))
- A brain-off option is the [Zaryte crossbow](Zaryte_crossbow.md) with a Twisted buckler or Spectral spirit shield (5 to 9% above the Twisted bow); an Armadyl crossbow beats the bow by about 2%. ([2022-01-19 13:20](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=800) [2022-01-19 13:20](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=800))
- Melee-and-bow switching beats it by about 27%, and full [Torva](Torva_armour.md) with a six-way switch by just under 37%, but large masses kill too fast and bolt procs often take MVP. ([2022-01-19 14:13](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=853) [2022-01-19 14:13](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=853))
- Damage on the minions likely counts for MVP. ([2022-01-19 15:08](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=908))

### Small groups and the January 2022 guide

- On crowdsourced defence data, best in slot looked like max melee Torva with the rapier (over 35% above the Zaryte crossbow) with the Dragon warhammer seeming ineffective. ([2022-01-19 2:08](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=128))
- Six players is a sweet spot; he uses a melee plus Twisted bow setup, with [Blood fury](Blood_fury.md) saving brews. ([2022-01-19 18:43](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=1123))
- Small kills take 4 to 6 minutes and give double to quadruple mass loot. ([2022-01-19 25:16](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=1516))
- Phase routines: rapier on Nex, then switch to the Twisted bow for each minion. ([2022-01-19 15:42](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=942) [2022-01-19 16:11](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=971) [2022-01-19 16:41](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=1001) [2022-01-19 17:08](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=1028) [2022-01-19 18:10](https://youtu.be/PRjmKR3P5eg?t=1090))
- In March 2022 his team used a rapier switch with a four-way to the Twisted bow (earlier a ZCB switch) and kept one player in range while the rest stayed out. ([2022-03-19 12:40](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=760) [2022-03-19 13:44](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=824))
- April 2022 stall techs: drag or stand under Nex to stop her attacking; the aim is 40 killcount before banking in a six-man. ([2022-04-11 1:30](https://youtu.be/Y2KvOrUXUeA?t=90) [2022-04-11 8:27](https://youtu.be/Y2KvOrUXUeA?t=507)) He tried a follow stall and found it not worth it. ([2022-04-11 9:52](https://youtu.be/Y2KvOrUXUeA?t=592))

### Duos

- Duo is the most profitable form; he calls it the hardest PvM outside raids and the Inferno. ([2023-05-28 0:00](https://youtu.be/vFVvnNJnyZQ?t=0) [2023-05-28 0:00](https://youtu.be/vFVvnNJnyZQ?t=0))
- Opening: stand opposite each other, dump specs, summon a thrall; a virus may start the fight. ([2024-06-15 16:16](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=976)) Crossbow while drained (rubies ignore stats), then use the blowpipe on the minion. ([2024-06-15 16:54](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=1014))
- Umbra: one player tanks while the other kills him with the blowpipe. ([2024-06-15 19:15](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=1155))
- Brew while crossbowing, since it costs little DPS; brewing while fanging costs a lot. ([2023-07-04 9:37](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=577) [2024-06-15 23:01](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=1381))
- A budget duo needs one player far away resetting with a crossbow while the other pipes. ([2023-07-04 3:56](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=236)) In the distance-based phase he flinches in and out every five ticks with the Armadyl crossbow, and says Torva lets you melee there. ([2023-07-04 5:02](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=302) [2023-07-04 5:36](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=336))
- In the 2024 max budget guide Nex was recently changed to be weaker to crossbows; he melees at the start, then kills Fumus with the Twisted bow, and wants Escape as her last attack before melee Shadow. ([2024-06-23 2:26](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=146) [2024-06-23 4:28](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=268) [2024-06-23 5:55](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=355))
- Kills take about 7:30 in max gear (6:30 best, nine slow); the 2023 budget kill took 9:54 and learners should assume 12 minutes. ([2023-07-04 13:01](https://youtu.be/I1_FHkP4IV4?t=781) [2024-06-23 14:17](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=857))
- Hedron went from a 700 million bank (Karil's, Zaryte crossbow) to full Torva with Twisted bow in about six months. ([2024-07-07 7:13](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=433))
- Trios are similar but easier; start with a trio and move to a duo. ([2024-06-15 35:35](https://youtu.be/rnLS-oZr3wU?t=2135))
