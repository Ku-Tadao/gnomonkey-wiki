**6thBrother death** is an account topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2025-10-29. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey does not like doing the Colosseum on stream because chat backseats badly, telling him which pillar to use.[^0_DI-SbhksU-0]
* Gnomonkey says he still had two brews left and might have survived a two-second crystal at full health, but at 60 hitpoints there was no chance once the prayer swap failed.[^0_DI-SbhksU-186]
* When asked if he finally admits that the North Pillar is better in the Colosseum, Gnomonkey says maybe it is time.[^0_DI-SbhksU-217]
* Gnomonkey says he felt awful and was depressed for a couple of hours after the death, but had settled with it by the time of the video.[^0_DI-SbhksU-243]
* He says he should have been dead long before, because the account had many near-deaths: Blood Moon, Tormented Demons, Bloat (hit for 35), Baba throwing a rock for 92 when he usually entered at 85, around 170 potential damage from four hits at Doom of Mokhaiotl, and the Doom chance being the worst by far.[^0_DI-SbhksU-277]
* Gnomonkey says he does not regret playing aggressively and doing PvM grinds on the Hardcore Ironman, because it made Ironman mode actually enjoyable.[^0_DI-SbhksU-541]

## Events

* Gnomonkey's Hardcore Ironman 6thBrother died in the Fortis Colosseum, off-stream, while he was trying to get dragon arrows for Doom of Mokhaiotl.[^0_DI-SbhksU-0]
* He did not set up a recording for the death run because he always forgets to record, so there is no clip of the death.[^0_DI-SbhksU-33]
* Gnomonkey was flying through the Colosseum, had no thought that he could die, and reached wave 11.[^0_DI-SbhksU-33]
* On wave 11 in the Colosseum, he started on the southwest side and cleared the manticores, a Minotaur and the mage block easily, but finished the wave at around 60 hitpoints with no ranging potion left.[^0_DI-SbhksU-94]
* At around 60 hitpoints with no ranging potion left on wave 11, Gnomonkey decided to kill the remaining stack, planning to brew up for the soul and drink a super combat before killing it.[^0_DI-SbhksU-123]
* His prayers stopped changing during the fight with the Manticore because of an approximately 10-tick lag spike, so all the attacks landed at once and he died; he calls it effectively a disconnect and takes only partial credit.[^0_DI-SbhksU-154]

## Trivia

* Gnomonkey has death screenshots turned off because the folder filled up from dying so many times on his main account; he turned it off about two years before this video, so he showed an artist render of the death instead.[^0_DI-SbhksU-67]
