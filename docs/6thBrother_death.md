## Opinions

- Gnomonkey does not like doing the Colosseum on stream because chat backseats badly, telling him which pillar to use. ([2025-10-29 0:00](https://youtu.be/0_DI-SbhksU))
- Gnomonkey says he still had two brews left and might have survived a two-second crystal at full health, but at 60 hitpoints there was no chance once the prayer swap failed. ([2025-10-29 3:06](https://youtu.be/0_DI-SbhksU?t=186))
- When asked if he finally admits that the North Pillar is better in the Colosseum, Gnomonkey says maybe it is time. ([2025-10-29 3:37](https://youtu.be/0_DI-SbhksU?t=217))
- Gnomonkey says he felt awful and was depressed for a couple of hours after the death, but had settled with it by the time of the video. ([2025-10-29 4:03](https://youtu.be/0_DI-SbhksU?t=243))
- He says he should have been dead long before, because the account had many near-deaths: Blood Moon, Tormented Demons, Bloat (hit for 35), Baba throwing a rock for 92 when he usually entered at 85, around 170 potential damage from four hits at Doom of Mokhaiotl, and the Doom chance being the worst by far. ([2025-10-29 4:37](https://youtu.be/0_DI-SbhksU?t=277))
- Gnomonkey says he does not regret playing aggressively and doing PvM grinds on the Hardcore Ironman, because it made Ironman mode actually enjoyable. ([2025-10-29 9:01](https://youtu.be/0_DI-SbhksU?t=541))

## Events

- Gnomonkey's Hardcore Ironman 6thBrother died in the Fortis Colosseum, off-stream, while he was trying to get dragon arrows for Doom of Mokhaiotl. ([2025-10-29 0:00](https://youtu.be/0_DI-SbhksU))
- He did not set up a recording for the death run because he always forgets to record, so there is no clip of the death. ([2025-10-29 0:33](https://youtu.be/0_DI-SbhksU?t=33))
- Gnomonkey was flying through the Colosseum, had no thought that he could die, and reached wave 11. ([2025-10-29 0:33](https://youtu.be/0_DI-SbhksU?t=33))
- On wave 11 in the Colosseum, he started on the southwest side and cleared the manticores, a Minotaur and the mage block easily, but finished the wave at around 60 hitpoints with no ranging potion left. ([2025-10-29 1:34](https://youtu.be/0_DI-SbhksU?t=94))
- At around 60 hitpoints with no ranging potion left on wave 11, Gnomonkey decided to kill the remaining stack, planning to brew up for the soul and drink a super combat before killing it. ([2025-10-29 2:03](https://youtu.be/0_DI-SbhksU?t=123))
- His prayers stopped changing during the fight with the Manticore because of an approximately 10-tick lag spike, so all the attacks landed at once and he died; he calls it effectively a disconnect and takes only partial credit. ([2025-10-29 2:34](https://youtu.be/0_DI-SbhksU?t=154))
