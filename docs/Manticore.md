**Manticore** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2024-03-25 and 2024-06-15. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* The Manticore's attacks come out the instant it starts its animation, so that is when you switch prayer.[^EVOs96kyHSM-759]
* Colosseum Manticores queue up three orb attacks, and as soon as the projectile starts moving toward you and the animation starts, you flick through Mage, Ranged, Melee protection prayers; the first two orbs can randomly swap but the approach is the same.[^kIfF1pNro2c-390]

## Stats and numbers

* The Manticore's attack speed is 10 ticks, so it starts an attack every 10 ticks.[^EVOs96kyHSM-789]
* Manticores are the tankiest mob in the Colosseum, with more defence than Sol Heredit, so he focuses them and uses a Saradomin godsword spec.[^kIfF1pNro2c-692]

## Tips

* To get ready to pray against the Manticore when you see it pulling up while dealing with other enemies.[^EVOs96kyHSM-758]
* Gnomonkey's solve for one Manticore stack was to come out praying mage and flick the Manticore, camping mage at other times.[^EVOs96kyHSM-1606]
* For the Manticore, Gnomonkey flicks through the three protection prayers as soon as the animation starts moving.[^nEIeyY_m_gw-698]
* When a Fremennik ranger is lined up with the Manticores' start tick, Gnomonkey watches the ranger's animation to start flicking and does not need to look at the Manticores.[^nEIeyY_m_gw-2259]
* If two Manticores are charging up Mage, both will use Mage, so Gnomonkey tanks a ranger and prays against the Manticore because a Manticore is more dangerous than a ranger.[^kIfF1pNro2c-1160]
