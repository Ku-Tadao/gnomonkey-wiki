# Manticore

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2024-03-25 to 2024-06-15). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- The Manticore's attacks come out the instant it starts its animation, so that is when you switch prayer. ([2024-03-25 12:39](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=759))
- Colosseum Manticores queue up three orb attacks, and as soon as the projectile starts moving toward you and the animation starts, you flick through Mage, Ranged, Melee protection prayers; the first two orbs can randomly swap but the approach is the same. ([2024-06-15 6:30](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=390))

## Stats and numbers

- The Manticore's attack speed is 10 ticks, so it starts an attack every 10 ticks. ([2024-03-25 13:09](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=789))
- Gnomonkey says Manticores are the tankiest mob in the Colosseum, with more defence than Sol Heredit, so he focuses them and uses a Saradomin godsword spec. ([2024-06-15 11:32](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=692))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says to get ready to pray against the Manticore when you see it pulling up while dealing with other enemies. ([2024-03-25 12:38](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=758))
- Gnomonkey's solve for one Manticore stack was to come out praying mage and flick the Manticore, camping mage at other times. ([2024-03-25 26:46](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=1606))
- For the Manticore, Gnomonkey flicks through the three protection prayers as soon as the animation starts moving. ([2024-03-26 11:38](https://youtu.be/nEIeyY_m_gw?t=698))
- When a Fremennik ranger is lined up with the Manticores' start tick, Gnomonkey watches the ranger's animation to start flicking and does not need to look at the Manticores. ([2024-03-26 37:39](https://youtu.be/nEIeyY_m_gw?t=2259))
- If two Manticores are charging up Mage, both will use Mage, so Gnomonkey tanks a ranger and prays against the Manticore because a Manticore is more dangerous than a ranger. ([2024-06-15 19:20](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=1160))
