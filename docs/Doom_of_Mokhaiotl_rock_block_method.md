**Doom of Mokhaiotl: rock block method** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2025-07-25 and 2025-08-09. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* In the rock block method you deliberately drop rocks at the start of the fight, since the boulders the boss throws land where you stand; after the shield phase, as soon as the car phase starts, you stand on a specific tile to bait the boss behind you.[^fLFV4RF5XPE-603]
* The first placed rock blocks the first shockwave and protects the second placed rock; you stand in line with the first rock until the next eyeball spawns directly on the second rock, then sidestep so the boss ends on top of the rock.[^fLFV4RF5XPE-636]
* The rock block method uses only four tiles, colour-coded and rotated to each side of the arena; pick the side with the least acid at the start of the cave and use that side's tiles.[^fLFV4RF5XPE-718]
* After the shield phase, go to the colour-coded bait tile and stand there until the eyeball appears behind you, then click out of the way; stand behind the rock that blocks the second rock from being destroyed.[^fLFV4RF5XPE-836]
* After the second rock is hit, the Doom of Mokhaiotl does one more dash; place yourself so it does not destroy any rock, go behind any other rock in the arena and block that last shockwave.[^fLFV4RF5XPE-925]
* On cave 8, time the rock placement to your prayer shutting off: click from rock two to just past rock one as soon as prayer turns off so you path past it in one motion while the second boulder shatters over rock one, then pray against the next attack.[^fLFV4RF5XPE-982]
* Gnomonkey's modified tech adds a second, outside rock to car park via two 'rails' of tiles on the sides: you place one rock on the car park tile and one on a rail tile; the first rock under the boss blocks the first shockwave and the second rock lets you hide for the next.[^draz0HZBq00-32]
* After the shield phase you lure the Doom of Mokhaiotl onto the car park rock, which blocks the first shockwave, stand next to the wall to keep the boss in place and hide behind the second rock, then drag the boss to the centre of the room and hide behind one more rock.[^draz0HZBq00-59]
* All the tiles work as long as you place a second rock for car park, but if you place the rock on a green tile it drags the boss so you end at its corner, out of range, so you can Chally punish; starting a Chally punish from a white tile drags you into melee range of the boss, likely in acid.[^draz0HZBq00-119]
* On cave 8 the Doom of Mokhaiotl throws two rocks that both land under where you are standing; you wait for the first rock to explode and place the rocks where you stand.[^draz0HZBq00-145]
* After the shield phase, drag the Doom of Mokhaiotl against the wall near the rock; you only need to stand fairly close to the rock, not on an exact tile, and the boss will move on top of it.[^draz0HZBq00-359]
* To bait the second dash, stand against the wall one or two tiles from the boss (or directly under it if acid is a problem); the boss slowly turns toward you and stays in place, and the pre-placed rock blocks the second shockwave, though Gnomonkey says there is no clear cue and you learn when the boss reads your position.[^draz0HZBq00-388]
* Rock block is used on Doom of Mokhaiotl delve 6 and up: you place rocks from the boss's attack and stand behind them to avoid the car phase dash shockwaves; Gnomonkey refers viewers to a separate rock block video.[^_q6rmQQkN1I-1548]
* Once two rocks are placed and shield phase ends, you must get within the quadrant before the boss's eyeball appears so the eyeball spawns directly on your rock, then spam specs.[^_q6rmQQkN1I-1740]

## Stats and numbers

* In the car phase the Doom of Mokhaiotl's eyeball spawns exactly four tiles behind your character, and the car comes from the boss's dead centre tile, so you can control where the boss goes as well as where the rocks land.[^fLFV4RF5XPE-603]
* The Doom of Mokhaiotl's shockwave radiates from its dead centre tile, so if the boss ends dead centre on a rock the rock absorbs the whole shockwave: you take no damage and no other rocks in the arena are destroyed.[^fLFV4RF5XPE-664]
* If you are hit by any rubble on the second rock, the rock is placed on a random tile instead of where you want it.[^draz0HZBq00-145]
* Dragging the Doom of Mokhaiotl south or west always destroys the rock, the same way car park only works on the north and east side, and this also applies to the second drag, which is why he had to build a new method.[^draz0HZBq00-241]
* When the Doom of Mokhaiotl is on top of the rock, its shockwave does nothing; Gnomonkey says players are scared to go under the boss but the shockwave does not hit there, so you can reposition after getting out of the way.[^draz0HZBq00-359]

## Tips

* On early caves where the Doom of Mokhaiotl throws only one rock, you may have to wait at about 75% health for a second rock before doing the melee punish; this is the downside on early caves.[^fLFV4RF5XPE-779]
* You do not need to stand exactly on the dodge tile for the rock block method: you only need to be in line with rock one on the axis, so the eyeball is put four tiles behind you onto rock two.[^fLFV4RF5XPE-862]
* On cave 8 the rock block method is better because the Doom of Mokhaiotl shoots two rocks, so you never need to wait for a second rock.[^fLFV4RF5XPE-955]
* The grubs must not be alive while doing rock block; the video description notes that if they die when the boss dashes it will destroy the rock.[^fLFV4RF5XPE-1075]
* If you get overkill on the Doom of Mokhaiotl as it dashes toward a rock, for example by attacking right after sidestepping, it destroys the rock; avoid it by not summoning a thrall (a one-in-four chance of the problem), summoning it only near the very end, and delaying your attack.[^fLFV4RF5XPE-1257]
* To avoid rubble when placing the rock, wait for the rock to explode, step out immediately to a tile with no shadow, then step back onto a tile that had a shadow; Gnomonkey says the boss cannot place rubble on a tile you stepped to.[^draz0HZBq00-176]
* On waves 6 and 7 there are no double rocks, so you must stall: the shield phase starts at 75% health, so if you do too much damage you will not have time to place the rocks.[^draz0HZBq00-241]
* Gnomonkey places one rock on car park and one on a green tile; it can be any of the green tiles, and the green tiles drag you to the corner of the Doom of Mokhaiotl.[^draz0HZBq00-329]
* After the second shockwave, run as fast as you can to the middle of the room because the further you get the boss to dash, the more damage you get to do before the phase ends; then hide behind any rock far out of range, often in a corner, and keep shooting as you run.[^draz0HZBq00-418]
* If you place only one rock (because you pushed the boss into the shield phase too early), the rock block still blocks one shockwave, so you can drag the boss to a corner and hide behind another rock.[^draz0HZBq00-511]
* Even if you place a rock in a bad spot, you can still usually make it by running to hide behind rocks, as long as the first rock is on the right tile.[^draz0HZBq00-597]
* In the video description, Gnomonkey says you can fit 1 Zaryte crossbow shot and 8 Twisted bow shots during the car phase with this tech, and during the first two shockwaves you are in range for a Blowpipe or Dragon knife specs; he reached wave 24 with it.[^draz0HZBq00-1196]
* Imported rock block tiles do not show up until you reach delve 6 because delve 6 and above is different and clears all the acid.[^_q6rmQQkN1I-1566]
* Not to kill the boss too much before placing the second rock, because going below 75% sends it into shield phase and you get no more rocks.[^_q6rmQQkN1I-1592]
* Since holy water does not work on delve 9 or higher, he now drags the boss to a corner or side to maximise dash time instead of pulling it to the middle.[^_q6rmQQkN1I-1740]
* Gnomonkey warns that being dragged 11 tiles out into a shockwave can happen easily, so do not greed shots when far away.[^_q6rmQQkN1I-1895]
* On delve 8 both double-boulder attacks turn off your prayer, so you cannot pre-pray; Gnomonkey recommends learning to look for the shadows on the ground and stepping onto a tile where rubble just landed, since rubble cannot land where a shadow was before.[^_q6rmQQkN1I-1959]
* On delve 8 you can place both rocks with one attack, and it does not matter where the second rock is as long as it is on one of the lines.[^_q6rmQQkN1I-2014]
* To keep the boss in place if your rock is in line with it, since pulling it to a corner would destroy the rock.[^_q6rmQQkN1I-2205]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says that done properly the rock block method means you will never be hit by a shockwave again and that it is guaranteed safe; he says shockwaves with no rocks left had been taking a lot of his health.[^fLFV4RF5XPE-690]
* Gnomonkey considers the new rock block better than car park because the longer the boss is dragged around, the more Twisted bow shots you get; it gives about two extra Twisted bow shots in total, greatly increasing the odds of killing the boss before the orb phase.[^draz0HZBq00-91]
* Gnomonkey says the rock block version in this video works for all waves without stipulations like thralls or zero grubs up, and that jmods confirmed the tech is fine and fair; he says it is very similar to the car park tech that was patched.[^draz0HZBq00-1370]

## Events

* Gnomonkey and Hedron developed the rock block method, which uses preplaced rocks to block the car-phase shockwaves; the rock tile markers were shared on pastebin.[^fLFV4RF5XPE-575]
* After a patch to the Doom of Mokhaiotl, the car park tech still lets you park the boss on a rock, but the shockwave now destroys the rock under the middle of the boss, so you can no longer sit there for the whole car phase.[^draz0HZBq00-0]
