# The Hueycoatl

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2024-09-25 to 2025-05-30). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | boss |
| **Videos** | 3 (first 2024-09-25, latest 2025-05-30) |

## Methods

- The Hueycoatl kill flow: climb the mountain hitting the body segments one by one (Lance works, scythe does not), then run up to the head, scythe it to 50% health, kill the tail out of its cave, then finish the head. ([2024-09-25 0:27](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=27))
- At The Hueycoatl pillars on the side fill a bar when three different protection prayers are active at all times (for example one person melee, two ranged, one mage), which dings and increases damage; Gnomonkey was hitting 93 with claws after it triggered. ([2024-09-25 3:28](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=208))
- Loot at The Hueycoatl works like the Nex loot split: everyone gets the same drop but the more damage you deal the more of the loot you receive; Gnomonkey joined a four-man late and received two rune maces of the 16 dropped. ([2024-09-25 6:10](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=370))
- Gnomonkey describes the fight as AFK hitting the Hueycoatl's body segments while dodging extremely slow lightning, slower than Scurrius' rockfall, with a prayer projectile roughly every 20 seconds, then running up the mountain to the head. ([2024-09-26 0:33](https://youtu.be/fLGVBOVJAqo?t=33))
- The Hueycoatl's pillar mechanic requires three protection prayers of different styles (mage, range and melee) active at all times for a damage buff Gnomonkey believes is 25%, which cannot be done solo or duo. ([2024-09-26 1:01](https://youtu.be/fLGVBOVJAqo?t=61))

## Requirements

- Gnomonkey says you do not want to solo or duo The Hueycoatl; you need a trio or larger because a mechanic reduces your damage in a solo or duo, and you can make your own instance by hitting the flag on the mountain. ([2024-09-25 2:33](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=153))
- Gnomonkey says you can never charge the Hueycoatl pillar bonus in a solo or duo because you cannot have three protection prayers up, which is why those scales are bad. ([2024-09-25 5:29](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=329))

## Stats and numbers

- The Hueycoatl's lightning splotches on the ground hit three ticks after they appear, and its colour-coded prayer projectiles come extremely slowly. ([2024-09-25 3:02](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=182))
- Gnomonkey says the punishment for messing up a mechanic at The Hueycoatl is at worst around a 20 hit and there is really no risk of dying. ([2024-09-25 7:20](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=440))
- Gnomonkey says the Dragon hunter wand sits between a regular trident and the Trident of the swamp on Olm and is not good anywhere. ([2024-09-25 8:20](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=500))
- The Hueycoatl hide creates an improved blessed dragonhide, essentially the same as blessed dragonhide but with two extra prayer bonus; the Tome of Earth only boosts earth spells, useful only on Steel dragons. ([2024-09-25 8:20](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=500))
- The Hueycoatl drops herb seeds for a new herb, Huasca, used to create Prayer enhance potions; these potions were selling for between 30k and 500k on day one. ([2024-09-25 8:51](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=531))
- The Hueycoatl wand's bonus damage is buffed from 20% to 40%, accuracy from 50% to 75%, and it becomes three times more common, dropping around 69 kills rather than 207. ([2025-05-30 9:20](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=560))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says everything at The Hueycoatl is crush: there are no style switches, and Inquisitor's gear is better than Torva here because the boss is weak to crush. ([2024-09-25 0:00](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0))
- Gnomonkey recommends holding the special attack at The Hueycoatl until you hear the ding, because you then do more damage. ([2024-09-25 4:57](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=297))
- The Hueycoatl hits nothing if you stand on a particular tile during the tail out of the cave wave, so Gnomonkey says you can just AFK there. ([2024-09-25 4:57](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=297))
- To dodge a tail wave at The Hueycoatl, step off the tail early and if you see the wave particle in the corner of the tail box, click back on; two green tile markers also let you auto-dodge by waiting a second and then clicking. ([2024-09-25 5:21](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=321))
- Gnomonkey says Max mage with Ward and Magus is only about 1% better in a huge switch and much worse on the tail, so he says not to use magic or ranged on The Hueycoatl. ([2024-09-25 9:40](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=580))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says there is no real reason to kill The Hueycoatl for rewards; the Dragon hunter wand's Dragon Hunter 1 effect is incredibly weak and is basically only for killing Steel dragons early in an ironman. ([2024-09-25 7:52](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=472))
- Gnomonkey says the regular drops from The Hueycoatl are just not very good and that Moons bosses are way better in general. ([2024-09-25 8:51](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=531))
- Gnomonkey says on an ironman he would only use Prayer enhances in extremely difficult challenges such as Colosseum and Inferno where prayer is an issue, because the potions are so hard to get. ([2024-09-25 8:51](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=531))
- Gnomonkey says the herb seeds are fairly rare from the boss and the secondary needed from the herb minigame is fairly hard to get; the other reason to come is the pet, for pet hunters. ([2024-09-25 9:20](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=560))
- Gnomonkey says he is not very impressed with The Hueycoatl, having expected a Moons-tier boss with tutorial-style mechanics and okay regular loot. ([2024-09-25 9:39](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=579))
- Gnomonkey says the new bosses need more gear switches and wonders why Jagex never tries to teach switches. ([2024-09-25 10:00](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=600))
- Gnomonkey says The Hueycoatl's mechanics are below those of Scurrius, whom he used to hate but came to see as great for teaching new players PvM. ([2024-09-26 0:00](https://youtu.be/fLGVBOVJAqo))
- Gnomonkey says in the description that the loot is trash even for mid-level players, Huey offers no real entertainment even to mid-level players, and that unless you are pet hunting he recommends avoiding it and going to Scurrius or Moons instead. ([2024-09-26 0:00](https://youtu.be/fLGVBOVJAqo))
- In the description Gnomonkey says the Dragon hunter wand is not good at Olm, is worse than Trident and cannot run the head with it, and that The Hueycoatl is not weak to magic so the wand is useless there. ([2024-09-26 0:00](https://youtu.be/fLGVBOVJAqo))
- Gnomonkey says the XP from Scurrius lets players skip the early-game grind faster than AFKing crabs or Dew Slayer, which he says is awful for early combat XP. ([2024-09-26 0:33](https://youtu.be/fLGVBOVJAqo?t=33))
- Gnomonkey says The Hueycoatl is designed for a trio or larger with no scaling options below that, and that you can technically solo it with 8-minute kills in full max. ([2024-09-26 1:01](https://youtu.be/fLGVBOVJAqo?t=61))
- Gnomonkey says The Hueycoatl's HP does not scale down for small teams, so you simply do 25% less damage, and he calls forced groups unnecessary at a learner boss like this for a boss with roughly three mechanics where you stand still most of the fight. ([2024-09-26 1:31](https://youtu.be/fLGVBOVJAqo?t=91))
- Gnomonkey says The Hueycoatl's wave mechanic is poorly telegraphed because the waves do not align with where they actually are, is used nowhere else in the game, and should be scrapped. ([2024-09-26 2:34](https://youtu.be/fLGVBOVJAqo?t=154))
- Gnomonkey says the pillar mechanic is a half-hearted attempt to encourage teamwork because everyone just prays something different with no communication, and it hard locks solos and duos. ([2024-09-26 3:01](https://youtu.be/fLGVBOVJAqo?t=181))
- Gnomonkey says The Hueycoatl's click boxes are bad: the body segments are one big box that does not match the visuals and the flopping tail is hard to click, a problem he says is common in Varlamore including the Colosseum. ([2024-09-26 3:32](https://youtu.be/fLGVBOVJAqo?t=212))
- Gnomonkey says the Hueycoatl has a killstreak mechanic whose effect on loot is unknown and says perfect kills seem bugged and fail for no apparent reason. ([2024-09-26 4:05](https://youtu.be/fLGVBOVJAqo?t=245))
- Gnomonkey says Recoil is disabled at The Hueycoatl like at every other new boss and that he does not know why Jagex hates Vengeance so much. ([2024-09-26 4:31](https://youtu.be/fLGVBOVJAqo?t=271))
- Gnomonkey says the Hueycoatl wand becomes the new best-in-slot at rune dragons, since you can stand out of melee range and pray ranged so you only take magic hits; it is also great on adamant dragons, metal dragons, and the Great Olm with its earth weakness, possibly beating Sanguinesti DPS. ([2025-05-30 9:50](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=590))
- Gnomonkey says the wand's range of 10 and thrall dropping mean it is not realistic solo and works best in teams. ([2025-05-30 9:50](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=590))

## Events

- The Hueycoatl's tail HP is reduced to make the fight faster, since Jagex felt the tail was a slog. ([2025-05-30 9:20](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=560))
- The Hueycoatl wand can now auto-cast Ancient spells and has 10% bonus damage; Gnomonkey calls it a straight-up better ancient sceptre and a great mid-game iron option if you lack a nightmare staff or Kodai wand. ([2025-05-30 10:23](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=623))
- The crafting requirement of Hueycoatl hide armour is reduced to below black dragonhide, making it feasible to craft on a lower-level iron. ([2025-05-30 10:23](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=623))
