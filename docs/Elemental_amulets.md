## Stats and numbers

- Once you obtain all four elemental amulets they can be combined into one singular amulet that buffs all elements. ([2026-09-03 0:00](https://youtu.be/PJX1aJr8dQc))
- Gnomonkey says the elemental amulets are much more powerful than the update notes suggested and beat an occult necklace when you are using elemental spells. ([2026-09-03 0:33](https://youtu.be/PJX1aJr8dQc?t=33))
- In Gearscape's calculator against 'Zalra' (a very elementally weak boss with 50% fire weakness, low mage defence and a max hit cap), the harmonised nightmare staff is 0.5% better than the Eye of Ayak even without the amulet, and with the new amulet it is 4.7% better. ([2026-09-03 1:35](https://youtu.be/PJX1aJr8dQc?t=95))
- Gnomonkey says once thrall DPS is included, the amulet setup no longer wins against the Eye of Ayak setup at 'Zalra', because thralls add about 0.625 DPS and work with any weapon, including the ranged half of the kill. ([2026-09-03 2:02](https://youtu.be/PJX1aJr8dQc?t=122))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says many people are blowing the amulets out of proportion by claiming they shift the meta everywhere. ([2026-09-03 0:33](https://youtu.be/PJX1aJr8dQc?t=33))
- Gnomonkey says the only boss he could think of where the new amulet gives a tangible best-in-slot DPS increase is the Maggot King, where it is 2.7% better. ([2026-09-03 2:32](https://youtu.be/PJX1aJr8dQc?t=152))
- Gnomonkey says if you ever kill bosses with elemental spells you want the new amulet, which is a big deal for mid-game and even low-level accounts since it needs very little Magic to obtain, although it doesn't shift best in slot much. ([2026-09-03 4:04](https://youtu.be/PJX1aJr8dQc?t=244))
- Gnomonkey considers the amulets a good gap filler: ironmen casting spells don't have an amulet that substantially affects DPS until the occult necklace, which needs 93 Slayer (as he says). ([2026-09-03 5:04](https://youtu.be/PJX1aJr8dQc?t=304))
- Gnomonkey thinks the amulets pave the way for a future occult upgrade, such as one giving 7% to 8% mage damage instead of 5%. ([2026-09-03 5:31](https://youtu.be/PJX1aJr8dQc?t=331))
- Gnomonkey finds it strange that such strong amulets drop from super low-level mobs and suggests they could have dropped from Slayer monsters in the 50s or 60s, or from low-level bosses like the Titans, to make those worth killing. ([2026-09-03 6:30](https://youtu.be/PJX1aJr8dQc?t=390))

## Events

- The final part of the Summer Sweep-up added elemental magic amulets that buff the specific elemental spell styles; they drop from low-level Slayer mobs, such as the air one from killerwatts and the water one from sea snake hatchlings. ([2026-09-03 0:00](https://youtu.be/PJX1aJr8dQc))
