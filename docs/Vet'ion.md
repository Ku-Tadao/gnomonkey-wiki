# Vet'ion

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2022-03-19 to 2022-07-13). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | boss |
| **Videos** | 2 (first 2022-03-19, latest 2022-07-13) |

## Methods

- At half HP Vet'ion summons two skeletal hellhounds that make him invulnerable until killed, and when his HP is depleted he revives to full HP and turns orange, repeating the fight. ([2022-07-13 3:43](https://youtu.be/uuJsdK3_KRQ?t=223))

## Tips

- All Vet'ion safespots are in multi; the main one is a few steps from level 30 Wilderness, meaning you cannot immediately teleport. ([2022-07-13 4:03](https://youtu.be/uuJsdK3_KRQ?t=243))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey calls Vet'ion the worst Wilderness boss, an easy F tier, because after the X-logging change you are in multi above level 30 Wilderness and are likely to die suiciding for the pet. ([2022-03-19 38:13](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2293))
- Gnomonkey says before the X-logging change you escaped by running to singles and logging out on the red spiders, which was lame itself; now you tank all the way to the ditch or a ladder. ([2022-03-19 38:46](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2326))
- Gnomonkey says the one thing he likes at Vet'ion is dragging the hellhounds back to level 30 Wilderness so you can teleport while killing them. ([2022-03-19 39:39](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2379))
- Gnomonkey says most agree Vet'ion is the most annoying of the three main Wilderness bosses to kill; the lure requires maintaining a three-hit attack cycle with an awkward walk pattern to avoid being hit. ([2022-07-13 3:43](https://youtu.be/uuJsdK3_KRQ?t=223))
