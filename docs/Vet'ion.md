**Vet'ion** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2022-03-19 and 2022-07-13. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* At half HP Vet'ion summons two skeletal hellhounds that make him invulnerable until killed, and when his HP is depleted he revives to full HP and turns orange, repeating the fight.[^uuJsdK3_KRQ-223]

## Tips

* All Vet'ion safespots are in multi; the main one is a few steps from level 30 Wilderness, meaning you cannot immediately teleport.[^uuJsdK3_KRQ-243]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey calls Vet'ion the worst Wilderness boss, an easy F tier, because after the X-logging change you are in multi above level 30 Wilderness and are likely to die suiciding for the pet.[^HkVt4enEesI-2293]
* Gnomonkey says before the X-logging change you escaped by running to singles and logging out on the red spiders, which was lame itself; now you tank all the way to the ditch or a ladder.[^HkVt4enEesI-2326]
* Gnomonkey says the one thing he likes at Vet'ion is dragging the hellhounds back to level 30 Wilderness so you can teleport while killing them.[^HkVt4enEesI-2379]
* Gnomonkey says most agree Vet'ion is the most annoying of the three main Wilderness bosses to kill; the lure requires maintaining a three-hit attack cycle with an awkward walk pattern to avoid being hit.[^uuJsdK3_KRQ-223]
