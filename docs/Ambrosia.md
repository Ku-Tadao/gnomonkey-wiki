# Ambrosia

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2024-10-24 to 2025-06-25). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey describes Ambrosia as a cheat code potion that gives full overhealed health and full prayer, and he has four doses in his bag. ([2024-10-24 31:00](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=1860))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says that if you have four sips of ambrosia for the last Wardens phase you have effectively unlimited health, which is the part that trips people up. ([2024-10-24 39:52](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=2392))
- Gnomonkey says that in a 300 you do not even have to dodge at the end of the Wardens fight as long as you have doses of ambrosia; you can just camp a prayer and hit it. ([2024-10-24 41:41](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=2501))
- Gnomonkey says Ambrosia in the Warden fight supply gives a full overheal, overprayer and full run energy in one sip, with four sips guaranteed, making you basically immortal if used when about to die. ([2025-06-25 40:01](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=2401))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says players stop using ambrosia as they get better at the raid. ([2025-06-25 40:01](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=2401))
