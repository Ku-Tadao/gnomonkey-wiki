**Ambrosia** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2024-10-24 and 2025-06-25. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Gnomonkey describes Ambrosia as a cheat code potion that gives full overhealed health and full prayer, and he has four doses in his bag.[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-1860]

## Tips

* If you have four sips of ambrosia for the last Wardens phase you have effectively unlimited health, which is the part that trips people up.[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-2392]
* In a 300 you do not even have to dodge at the end of the Wardens fight as long as you have doses of ambrosia; you can just camp a prayer and hit it.[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-2501]
* Ambrosia in the Warden fight supply gives a full overheal, overprayer and full run energy in one sip, with four sips guaranteed, making you basically immortal if used when about to die.[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-2401]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says players stop using ambrosia as they get better at the raid.[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-2401]
