# Opinion: Drop-before-completion and endgame moneymakers

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 1 video (2025-10-01). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey argues that dropping rare items early in Doom devalues items like the Tumeken's shadow, citing the shadow's price falling from about 960m to 930m after a week and continuing down after Doom's release. ([2025-10-01 7:29](https://youtu.be/ZB3ldcsIivg?t=449))
- Gnomonkey compares Doom with other content: a scythe cannot drop if you leave Theatre of Blood at Bloat, a shadow needs a Warden kill, and you must clear a Tombs of Amascut room, yet Doom drops uniques before the boss is cleared. ([2025-10-01 10:32](https://youtu.be/ZB3ldcsIivg?t=632))
- Gnomonkey says the problem predates Doom: he points to the Torva platebody price falling around Yama's release on 14 May, and says Torva platebody is much rarer per kill and requires a harder boss than Yama but is objectively worse than Oathplate. ([2025-10-01 11:28](https://youtu.be/ZB3ldcsIivg?t=688))
- Gnomonkey says Oathplate is always better than Torva except with a Osmumten's fang, and even then Oathplate has arguments because it boosts the BGS. ([2025-10-01 11:28](https://youtu.be/ZB3ldcsIivg?t=688))
- Gnomonkey says he cares about endgame moneymakers, that hard bosses should be rewarding, and that there is no reason endgame bosses should make less money than Hydra. ([2025-10-01 11:57](https://youtu.be/ZB3ldcsIivg?t=717))
- Gnomonkey points out that Jagex said it does not want the Colosseum to give super good common loot, so he argues they should stop rewarding people on delve 3 with common loot, saying they are failing their own objective of chasing uniques when uniques drop on delve 2. ([2025-10-01 12:31](https://youtu.be/ZB3ldcsIivg?t=751))
- Gnomonkey says he loves OSRS progression of early, mid and endgame bosses, and fears that making endgame bosses feel nice for mid-level players will leave all mega rares under a bill and everything being as good as Hydra. ([2025-10-01 13:31](https://youtu.be/ZB3ldcsIivg?t=811))
- Gnomonkey wants bosses that are super difficult to be super rewarding, citing a boss like Phosani's Nightmare at about 3m an hour as sad to see, and says hard bosses should typically be better than other options. ([2025-10-01 13:58](https://youtu.be/ZB3ldcsIivg?t=838))
