**Hallowed marks** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2020-06-14. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Gnomonkey's purchase order after the ring: hallowed grapple (grapple coffin challenges have the highest failure rate and waste the most time), then the hallowed hammer (bridge failures cost five rune nails each), then the hallowed focus (reduces enchant jewelry failure chance in portal challenges).[^wVim0ZX-GTc-2462]

## Stats and numbers

* The Dark Graceful recolor costs 300 marks per piece or 1,800 for a full set, and the Dark Acorn Squirrel recolor costs 5,000 marks.[^wVim0ZX-GTc-2494]
* Hallowed crystal shards cost one mark each, and tokens cost ten marks each; popping a token gives an extra minute in the Sepulchre and multiple can be used in a single run.[^wVim0ZX-GTc-2526]
* Any coffin gives 0-6 marks of grace; the fifth floor seems to give a minimum of four marks and a single loot item, and the Grand Coffin gives six to ten marks, two items from its pool and a chance at the Ring of endurance.[^wVim0ZX-GTc-2613]

## Tips

* None of the Sepulchre reward shop items can be used outside the Sepulchre and really only help there; they also work at God Wars Dungeon but are not a great choice there as they have no stats, and Gnomonkey does not think the grapple works for Path to Armadyl.[^wVim0ZX-GTc-2400]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the first thing to buy with hallowed marks is the Hallowed ring (250 marks versus 100 for the other pieces), because it completely prevents trap damage when you fail them and lets you retry sections more quickly.[^wVim0ZX-GTc-2432]
* Gnomonkey bought the hallowed symbol last, since it reduces the vampyre dust needed at braziers from two to one but also reduces the Prayer XP and the brazier cannot be failed anyway.[^wVim0ZX-GTc-2494]
* Gnomonkey thinks tokens may be worth buying to get out of sticky situations, but does not think using them to loot earlier-floor coffins is worth it, though he has not tested this.[^wVim0ZX-GTc-2526]
