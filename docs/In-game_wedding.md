**In-game wedding** is an activity topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2025-03-16. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Events

* Gnomonkey's in-game wedding with Hedron was held at the cosmic entity plane, chosen because it looks pretty and because during the quest a magic essence potion is used there to grow a flower for each person present.[^2S0r-IoEsJ4-0]
* In the video description Gnomonkey thanks the community for showing up and says the in-game wedding let people share the moment who could not attend the real-life wedding.[^2S0r-IoEsJ4-0]
* Gnomonkey noted that the number of blue flowers grew with the number of attendees, and that the flowers stacked.[^2S0r-IoEsJ4-27]
* Attendees brought wedding gifts, including eggs and a 10-pack of Xylas from a guest.[^2S0r-IoEsJ4-130]
* During the ceremony prep a viewer donated $1,000 as a wedding gift.[^2S0r-IoEsJ4-160]
* The ceremony was officiated by a community member who read a humorous service referring to Gnomonkey as an A-tier PvMer and Hedron as his HLC partner, and who praised the couple for years spent doing Nex and building a welcoming community.[^2S0r-IoEsJ4-386]
* Rings were gold rings made with level 5 Crafting using a ring mold and a gold bar at a furnace, exchanged by right-click trading your partner.[^2S0r-IoEsJ4-515]
* After the ceremony an after-party was held at the Blue Moon Inn.[^2S0r-IoEsJ4-574]
