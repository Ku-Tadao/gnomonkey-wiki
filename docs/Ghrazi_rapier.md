## Tips

## Opinions

- Hedron notes people argue that the Theatre of Blood rapier is not used anywhere and has been overshadowed by the Osmumten's fang, and she understands that from a game design view. ([2026-01-02 5:44](https://youtu.be/0sdm6y2SYko?t=344))
- Hedron thinks everything in the game has a dying point and that one day the Twisted bow will no longer be the best mega rare ranged weapon, simply because of how long the game has been alive. ([2026-01-02 6:12](https://youtu.be/0sdm6y2SYko?t=372))
- Hedron thinks affliction mechanics like bleed, burn and poison help Jagex flesh out niches in weapon design without adding power creep. ([2026-01-02 6:12](https://youtu.be/0sdm6y2SYko?t=372))
- Gnomonkey calls the Ghrazi rapier the black sheep of the high-level weapons, very useless compared to the Osmumten's fang. ([2026-05-27 25:59](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=1559))
- Gnomonkey thinks a three-tick rapier would be probably the new best in the Nylocas room of Theatre of Blood, and useful against bosses that can be fully drained of defence like Maiden and Xarpus. ([2026-05-27 26:25](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=1585))
- Gnomonkey thinks a three-tick rapier would compete with the blowpipe against low-defence Slayer monsters and would also raise the cannon's accuracy, since the cannon rolls off the main accuracy style. ([2026-05-27 26:58](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=1618))
- Gnomonkey does not like the Blood rapier idea (adding a blood shard to give passive healing), arguing passive healing is either useless like the Sanguinesti staff or broken. ([2026-05-27 27:32](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=1652))
- Gnomonkey says he would have preferred a more interesting approach, such as a Reddit suggestion to make it three tick with much lower stats, but does not hate this safer change. ([2026-06-04 35:34](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2134))
- Gnomonkey says the Saeldor buff makes it directly better than the Noxious halberd-style Nally (Osmumten's? 'Nally') instead of a sidegrade, and he reads it as Jagex wanting to push people to use Saeldor rather than blowpipes and enhanced seeds, in light of the Corrupted Gauntlet buffs. ([2026-06-04 36:05](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2165))
