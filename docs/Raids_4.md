# Raids 4

## Requirements

- Accessing the Fractured Archive requires completing While Guthix Sleeps, which Gnomonkey thinks is probably the hardest requirement of any raid, harder than Beneath Cursed Sands. ([2026-01-25 16:54](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=1014))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says Gearscape has a Raids 4 reward toggle, and that Raids 4 will add an item that increases the base max hit of all elemental spells by two, which stacks and multiplies with the new elemental amulet. ([2026-09-03 3:32](https://youtu.be/PJX1aJr8dQc?t=212))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey hopes for more creativity at Raids 4 with community feedback before release, and says he has no doubt it will be amazing. ([2025-08-22 24:19](https://youtu.be/Glp4chf4yiA?t=1459))
- Gnomonkey says he believes Old School RuneScape is in its golden age and likes seeing Yama and Doom of Mokhaiotl released close together, hoping for more consistent high-level bosses. ([2025-08-22 24:53](https://youtu.be/Glp4chf4yiA?t=1493))
- Gnomonkey strongly supports the team-raid-with-optional-solo design and says no raid should be easier to solo, and wishes Jagex removed the subject-to-change line; he says team content is needed in the game. ([2026-01-25 15:21](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=921))
- Gnomonkey loves the release wipes because the race to world first is preserved, unlike Chambers of Xeric where nobody knows who got the first completion. ([2026-01-25 16:24](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=984))
- Gnomonkey does not think the Raids 4 mega rare should be a crush weapon because there are not enough crush-weak bosses to support it: Tekton is one boss in Chambers, Ulm has neutral defence, and Tombs of Amascut and Theatre of Blood offer no use. ([2026-03-05 12:05](https://youtu.be/tDyb-FazeKw?t=725))
- Gnomonkey says a crush mega rare could only make sense far down the line, if a Coliseum 2 or similar content had everything weak to crush; otherwise a Scythe would be better 90 percent of the time and the weapon would collect dust like Torva versus Oathplate. ([2026-03-05 13:08](https://youtu.be/tDyb-FazeKw?t=788))
- Gnomonkey says even a non-mega-rare crush drop from Raids 4 would have few uses, as he does not own an Inquisitor's mace and sees no use for a crush weapon only 3 percent better. ([2026-03-05 13:40](https://youtu.be/tDyb-FazeKw?t=820))
- Gnomonkey says adding a mega rare that barely does anything would be lame, and that Jagex might as well not add one. ([2026-03-05 15:29](https://youtu.be/tDyb-FazeKw?t=929))
- Gnomonkey says Raids 4 is something he cares about much more than fixing Sailing combat. ([2026-03-20 11:32](https://youtu.be/fZ5RVn7tNhI?t=692))
- Gnomonkey says Jagex seems to be struggling with Raids 4 reward design, including whether a mega rare is needed, and thinks those should be prioritized over Sailing combat. ([2026-03-20 11:59](https://youtu.be/fZ5RVn7tNhI?t=719))
- Gnomonkey is hyped for the Bork and Archive Guardian animations, but is nervous that Raids 4 might be rushed and not the cleanest raid, since rewards have taken a while to get up. ([2026-05-29 17:02](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=1022))
- Gnomonkey says he is very excited for the Fractured Archive raid to release so he can mess around with all the new reward items. ([2026-07-07 0:00](https://youtu.be/lNaYz-3A15Y))
- Gnomonkey expects that once Raids 4 releases with the amulet, the harmonised nightmare staff will start beating weapons like Shadow and powered staves on things very weak to an element, such as Zulrah and Phosani's Nightmare (whose totems are heavily fire weak, though thralls are very good there). ([2026-09-03 3:35](https://youtu.be/PJX1aJr8dQc?t=215))
- Gnomonkey notes players already voted on the elemental damage reward for Raids 4 without knowing the amulets would be added, and some might not have voted yes had they known. ([2026-09-03 8:31](https://youtu.be/PJX1aJr8dQc?t=511))

## Events

- Raids 4 is called the Fractured Archive, a Guthix-themed raid that is Theatre of Blood-styled with a series of demibosses, no puzzles and a final boss showdown. ([2026-01-25 14:29](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=869))
- Jagex says the Fractured Archive aims, like Theatre of Blood, at simple mechanics leaving plenty of space for optimisation, with difficulty too early to discuss. ([2026-01-25 14:55](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=895))
- Raids 4 is planned, subject to change, to scale between two and eight players, so solo clears are possible but difficult. ([2026-01-25 14:55](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=895))
- A Raids 4 practice mode letting players jump into any raid boss at regular difficulty will launch in the weeks after release, not at launch. ([2026-01-25 15:54](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=954))
- At Raids 4 release, a full team wipe resets the raid, and in the weeks following it moves to a Tombs of Amascut-style wipe system (heard as toe-style) with infinite tries but reduced rewards and dwindling resources. ([2026-01-25 15:54](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=954))
- Gnomonkey's plan for Raids 4's winter release is to team up with Bodhi, build a god team to chase world first for teams, and then try for world first solo. ([2026-01-25 16:54](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=1014))
- Jagex is not ready to talk about Raids 4 rewards yet, and Gnomonkey says he will cover every blog and any item issues, as he did with Moons. ([2026-01-25 17:27](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=1047))
- Raids 4 teased a new boss, Bork, a boss from RS2 now added as a raid boss with little goblin helpers called the smorks, a Bandos-themed encounter with Warhammer 40k vibes. ([2026-05-29 16:04](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=964))
- Jagex also showed the Archive Guardian from the winter summit with new animations, including a hyper-beam-style attack. ([2026-05-29 16:34](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=994))
- Jagex said it will share its first Raids 4 rewards proposal in a blog releasing in mid-June, with concept art, while the numbers and ideas are already together. ([2026-05-29 16:34](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=994))

## Trivia

- Raids 4 concept art looks inspired by the RS3 World Wakes quest, showing God Wars factions fighting, with a skull boss held together by roots and a Saradomin knight boss with a bloodied cape. ([2026-01-25 17:27](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=1047))
- Jagex bills the Fractured Archive (Raids 4) as Old School RuneScape's toughest raid yet. ([2026-07-07 6:22](https://youtu.be/lNaYz-3A15Y?t=382))
