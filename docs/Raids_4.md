**Raids 4**, called the **Fractured Archive**, is a planned fourth raid for Old School RuneScape. Gnomonkey follows it closely: he plans to cover every blog and any item issues, as he did with Moons, and he cares about it much more than he does about fixing Sailing combat[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-1047][^fZ5RVn7tNhI-692]. Jagex bills it as the toughest raid yet[^lNaYz-3A15Y-382]. As of September 2026 he says he is very excited to try the new reward items and has been analysing its rewards through Gearscape[^lNaYz-3A15Y-0][^PJX1aJr8dQc-212].

[TOC]

## Design

The Fractured Archive is a Guthix-themed raid styled after the Theatre of Blood, with a series of demibosses, no puzzles and a final boss showdown[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-869]. Jagex says it aims, like Theatre of Blood, at simple mechanics leaving plenty of space for optimisation, and that difficulty was too early to discuss[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-895]. Its concept art looks inspired by the RS3 World Wakes quest, showing God Wars factions fighting, a skull boss held together by roots and a Saradomin knight boss with a bloodied cape[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-1047].

### Group size

It is planned, subject to change, to scale between two and eight players, so solo clears are possible but difficult[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-895]. Gnomonkey strongly supports a team raid with optional solo, says no raid should be easier to solo, and wishes Jagex would remove the subject-to-change line. He says team content is needed in the game[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-921].

### Wipes and practice mode

At release a full team wipe resets the raid. In the weeks following it moves to a Tombs of Amascut-style wipe system with infinite tries but reduced rewards and dwindling resources[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-954]. A practice mode that lets players jump into any raid boss at regular difficulty will launch in the weeks after release, not at launch[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-954]. Gnomonkey loves the release wipes because they preserve the race to world first, unlike Chambers of Xeric, where nobody knows who got the first completion[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-984].

### Bosses

In May 2026 Jagex teased Bork, an RS2 boss now added as a raid boss with little goblin helpers called the smorks, a Bandos-themed encounter with Warhammer 40k vibes. It also showed the Archive Guardian from the winter summit with new animations, including a hyper-beam-style attack[^O4np4ssz6iw-964][^O4np4ssz6iw-994].

## Requirements

Accessing the Fractured Archive requires completing While Guthix Sleeps, which Gnomonkey thinks is probably the hardest requirement of any raid, harder than Beneath Cursed Sands[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-1014].

## Rewards

Jagex was not ready to talk about rewards in January 2026[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-1047]. Gnomonkey thinks Jagex is struggling with reward design, including whether a mega rare is needed, and thinks that should be prioritised over Sailing combat[^fZ5RVn7tNhI-719]. Jagex said it would share its first rewards proposal in a blog in mid-June 2026, with concept art, with the numbers and ideas already together[^O4np4ssz6iw-994].

### Mega rare

In March 2026 Gnomonkey argued the mega rare should not be a crush weapon. There are not enough crush-weak bosses to support it: Tekton is one boss in Chambers of Xeric, Ulm has neutral defence, and Tombs of Amascut and Theatre of Blood offer no use[^tDyb-FazeKw-725]. A crush mega rare could only make sense far down the line if a Colosseum 2 or similar content had everything weak to crush; otherwise a Scythe would be better 90 percent of the time and the weapon would collect dust like Torva versus Oathplate[^tDyb-FazeKw-788]. Even a non-mega-rare crush drop would have few uses, as he does not own an Inquisitor's mace and sees no use for a crush weapon only 3 percent better[^tDyb-FazeKw-820]. He adds that a mega rare that barely does anything would be lame, and Jagex might as well not add one[^tDyb-FazeKw-929].

### Elemental damage reward

Gearscape has a Raids 4 reward toggle, and the raid will add an item that increases the base max hit of all elemental spells by two, stacking and multiplying with the new elemental amulet[^PJX1aJr8dQc-212]. Gnomonkey expects the harmonised nightmare staff to start beating weapons like the Shadow and powered staves on things very weak to an element, such as Zulrah and Phosani's Nightmare, whose totems are heavily fire weak, though thralls are very good there[^PJX1aJr8dQc-215]. He notes that players already voted on the elemental damage reward without knowing the amulets would be added, and some might not have voted yes had they known[^PJX1aJr8dQc-511].

## Gnomonkey's plans and opinion

His plan for the winter release is to team up with Bodhi, build a god team to chase world first for teams, and then try for world first solo[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-1014]. He hopes for more creativity at Raids 4 with community feedback before release and has no doubt it will be amazing[^Glp4chf4yiA-1459]. He believes the game is in its golden age and likes seeing Yama and Doom of Mokhaiotl released close together, hoping for more consistent high-level bosses[^Glp4chf4yiA-1493]. In May 2026 he was hyped for the Bork and Archive Guardian animations but nervous that the raid might be rushed and not the cleanest, since rewards have taken a while to get up[^O4np4ssz6iw-1022].
