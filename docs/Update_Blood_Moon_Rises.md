# Update: Blood Moon Rises

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 5 videos (2026-01-25 to 2026-06-30). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Requirements

- Blood Moon Rises requires A Night at the Theatre, which Gnomonkey jokes will now make people beg for carries, and 74 Slayer, a high requirement, plus 74 Woodcutting. ([2026-05-29 0:57](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=57))
- The bank chest near Castle Draken must be built using items, and for an Ironman this requires Sailing. ([2026-06-30 0:26](https://youtu.be/0iIPIZTfsBU?t=26))

## Stats and numbers

- The Blisterwood flail upgrade is a four tick weapon instead of five tick, with a 25% damage and accuracy bonus against vampires and no unique special attack, making blood shards much easier to get. ([2026-01-25 4:33](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=273))
- A new stab special attack weapon costs 50% special energy, always deals 70% of its maximum damage on a successful hit, and finishes off the target if its remaining HP is below that value, making it an execute weapon. ([2026-01-25 5:07](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=307))
- A new Woodcutting method gives semi-AFK 50 to 60k XP per hour and produces bloodwood sap, used to make seeker arrows. ([2026-01-25 5:33](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=333))
- The rest of the Blood Moon Rises requirements are manageable and line up with the needs for Song of the Elves on a new Ironman, and Gnomonkey believes the hard one is 74 Slayer. ([2026-05-29 1:31](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=91))
- The Blood Moon Rises rewards include a best-in-slot ranged amulet upgrade (necklace of rupture), a devastating new crush spec weapon (the crimson kiston), and an ammunition upgrade for all arrows (seeker arrows). ([2026-05-29 3:02](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=182))
- Gnomonkey says the Blood Moon Rises rewards will tremendously buff ranged, giving about an 8 percent DPS increase across the board for both the Twisted bow and the Scorching bow. ([2026-05-29 3:02](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=182))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey plans to do Blood Moon Rises on day one on stream, trying for day-one drops. ([2026-05-29 17:37](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=1057))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey thinks the execute spec would be useful for finishing each phase of Fasani and bosses in Tombs of Amascut such as Kephri and Akkha, and calls it a neat gimmick spec that will see use. ([2026-01-25 5:33](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=333))
- Gnomonkey considers the tweaks to the Blood Moon Rises rewards, which curb power creep slightly, generally good changes for the game. ([2026-02-17 0:29](https://youtu.be/kKccfwAJkh8?t=29))
- Gnomonkey says he is really excited for the Blood Moon Rises update, loves killing new bosses on release, and expects the rewards to be very strong. ([2026-02-17 18:37](https://youtu.be/kKccfwAJkh8?t=1117))
- Gnomonkey says the Jagex mods seem really excited about Blood Moon Rises, which makes him extra hyped. ([2026-06-25 1:09:53](https://youtu.be/lJexGv6dxu8?t=4193))
- Gnomonkey says the Maggot King loot needs a bit of tweaking as it is on the low side, but the new drops are incredibly valuable at well over 200m each. ([2026-06-30 0:00](https://youtu.be/0iIPIZTfsBU))
- Gnomonkey says DH bombing looks super fun, and that the Scythe with the harm staff look to be best in slot, with Shadow, ZCB and Twinflame staff in max mage close behind. ([2026-06-30 0:00](https://youtu.be/0iIPIZTfsBU))
