# Gwenith Glide

## Stats and numbers

- Jagex's table says the best Gwenith Glide Marlin XP is equal to before the extractor nerf. ([2025-12-04 7:08](https://youtu.be/OeDcRft1_cU?t=428))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says players have had to relearn the Gwenith Glide crate hitboxes for the fourth update in a row. ([2025-12-03 10:14](https://youtu.be/6BvUKVInPTs?t=614))
- Gnomonkey considers the Marlin lap bonus a fair small buff to the hardest Gwenith Glide Barracuda Trial given the extractor was nerfed. ([2025-12-04 3:59](https://youtu.be/OeDcRft1_cU?t=239))

## Events

- Jagex said they fixed crate hitboxes at Gwenith Glide, but Gnomonkey says some quick flicks that previously grabbed crates no longer work and the hitbox is smaller in places. ([2025-12-03 10:14](https://youtu.be/6BvUKVInPTs?t=614))
- Jagex added a bonus 1,250 XP upon completing a lap of the Marlin Gwenith Glide, intended to keep top-end XP rates roughly in line with before the extractor nerf. ([2025-12-04 3:59](https://youtu.be/OeDcRft1_cU?t=239))
