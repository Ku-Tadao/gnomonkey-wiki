**Gwenith Glide** is an activity topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2025-12-03 and 2025-12-04. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Jagex's table says the best Gwenith Glide Marlin XP is equal to before the extractor nerf.[^OeDcRft1_cU-428]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says players have had to relearn the Gwenith Glide crate hitboxes for the fourth update in a row.[^6BvUKVInPTs-614]
* Gnomonkey considers the Marlin lap bonus a fair small buff to the hardest Gwenith Glide Barracuda Trial given the extractor was nerfed.[^OeDcRft1_cU-239]

## Events

* Jagex said they fixed crate hitboxes at Gwenith Glide, but Gnomonkey says some quick flicks that previously grabbed crates no longer work and the hitbox is smaller in places.[^6BvUKVInPTs-614]
* Jagex added a bonus 1,250 XP upon completing a lap of the Marlin Gwenith Glide, intended to keep top-end XP rates roughly in line with before the extractor nerf.[^OeDcRft1_cU-239]
