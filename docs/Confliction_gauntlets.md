**Confliction gauntlets** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 7 videos between 2025-03-11 and 2026-06-25. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* The Confliction gauntlets are crafted by using Mokha cloth on a tormented bracelet, requiring 83 Crafting and 70 Smithing, and are tradable once crafted.[^-98-rsX1soM-1033]

## Methods

* Gnomonkey's mage switch is a Blood Ancient Sceptre or sceptre, Confliction gauntlets and a crystal shield, freeing up an inventory slot for more brew.[^2xviK0wGI-o-62]

## Stats and numbers

* The Confliction gauntlets are an upgrade to the tormented bracelet with 7% mage damage; after missing a magic attack, your next magic attack of the same type, same weapon or spell against the same enemy rolls accuracy twice.[^-98-rsX1soM-1033]
* In a chart by Gnomonkey's Discord mod sci-fi, the double accuracy roll gives the biggest boost below about 60% base accuracy, a buff of around 5% or more, peaking at about 8% around 34-35% accuracy, and effectively no change above about 80%.[^-98-rsX1soM-1065]
* At around 60% accuracy, such as Akkha with a Sanguinesti staff in non-max mage gear, the gauntlets give about 5% accuracy, taking 60% to roughly 65%.[^-98-rsX1soM-1153]
* Gnomonkey calculates the gauntlets give about a 10% DPS increase against Zebak (about 64% accuracy in max mage with the double-roll), which he says would make the Twisted bow on Zebak dead.[^-98-rsX1soM-1181]
* At Zilyana, with about 70% accuracy, the gauntlets give about a 4% accuracy increase; against Zulrah's blue phase at about 40% accuracy it is around 7%.[^-98-rsX1soM-1215]
* With the new gear, a shadow at Nex beats a Zaryte crossbow camp, and in the Tombs of Amascut Baba shows 51% accuracy and Kephri 54% with the effect.[^-98-rsX1soM-1250]
* With the effect included, Gnomonkey says a shadow beats the Twisted bow on the Nex mages (shadow is 6.5% worse before the bonus), so the Nex meta would be to drop ranged and bring max mage.[^-98-rsX1soM-1300]
* In Gnomonkey's Nex calcs, the gauntlets' double accuracy passive raises shadow accuracy by over 10%, making it about 11% better than the Zaryte crossbow after the update.[^2S0r-IoEsJ4-1229]
* After the change the gauntlets still work with fire surge on a harm staff, but typical staves, ancients and the Tumeken's shadow are no longer affected by the double accuracy roll.[^-E1yidIYQ4Y-390]
* The gauntlets' passive must be against the same target and use the same spell or magic weapon, which Gnomonkey says makes them much less useful when fighting a bunch of monsters.[^-E1yidIYQ4Y-480]
* The Confliction gauntlets enable mage attacks while wearing full plate armour, which is what allows the Inferno mage tank method to work.[^RfISn0t7hWs-26]
* Confliction gauntlets increase magic accuracy by an average of about 25%, so a ranged top switch is no longer needed in the Inferno.[^2xviK0wGI-o-62]
* The Confliction gauntlets passive makes the next cast double roll after a splash, like a Fang, and Gnomonkey says a calc shows accuracy almost identical to bringing a Cultist/Virtus top and mage gear.[^2xviK0wGI-o-2455]
* After testing three to four thousand casts, Gnomonkey's team found Ice Barrage was getting 7% less accuracy than it should with gauntlets, while blood barrage worked normally, so ice spells do not work with the gauntlets even in PvM.[^lJexGv6dxu8-479]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the gauntlets buff the Tumeken's shadow a lot despite Jagex saying it won't benefit much, because many bosses with high defence are mageable, and he does not agree that it avoids buffing shadow.[^-98-rsX1soM-1320]
* Gnomonkey says the gauntlets turn 'shadow everything' into truly everything, with shadow camping Zulrah, the Inferno and Tombs of Amascut becoming viable instead of trolling, and he would not vote yes.[^-98-rsX1soM-1360]
* Gnomonkey says the gauntlets would also hugely buff barrage in the Inferno (50-60% to hit, making freezes land on melee NPCs) and one-way or two-way Sanguinesti staff at Nylocas Vasilias in the Theatre of Blood (about 60% accuracy).[^-98-rsX1soM-1370]
* Gnomonkey says the gauntlets are far more broken than the earlier minimum-hit necklace concern and is concerned they will break the game, calling them utterly broken.[^-98-rsX1soM-1370]
* Gnomonkey says the new Confliction gauntlets from the Mokha update are what make shadow Nex fine to do.[^2S0r-IoEsJ4-0]
* Gnomonkey says it is a shame the gauntlets are band-aided, because the right thing would be to fix the Tumeken's shadow and let the gauntlets work properly on slower and manually cast weapons, but he calls it a good band-aid.[^-E1yidIYQ4Y-390]
* Gnomonkey says the other option was turning the gauntlets into a shield, but Jagex wants to get the reward going, and most people liked the gauntlets and only disliked that they were broken for the shadow.[^-E1yidIYQ4Y-447]
* Gnomonkey says with this change the gauntlets are not broken any more and are a solid item addition.[^-E1yidIYQ4Y-508]
* Gnomonkey says the original Confliction Gauntlets design was kind of dumb and definitely a band-aid fix.[^u-RJBe_5Nmo-0]
* Gnomonkey says he would still prefer the gauntlets only work with Tumeken's shadow and then be removed afterwards, but he finds the one-handed fix fine and notes few two-handed items are affected (such as the Blue moon spear).[^u-RJBe_5Nmo-330]
* Gnomonkey argues the gauntlets would displace other weapons at six locations, including Nex and Tombs of Amascut, two of the best moneymakers, which he says would be broken for weapon prices.[^u-RJBe_5Nmo-629]
* Gnomonkey says it feels bad that the best mage gloves do not get their effect on the best mage weapon and questions whether passives like this are good for the game, since they cause long-term balance issues like with the Osmumten's fang.[^u-RJBe_5Nmo-655]
* Gnomonkey considers it hard for Jagex to patch the Confliction gauntlets passive in Inferno without simply disabling it, which he says would unfairly hit players who do not have a high mage bonus.[^RfISn0t7hWs-113]
* Gnomonkey says gauntlets were not very good for the finale because freezing the opponent adds a lot of damage in PvP, so the team decided Doom was not as valuable and did not need to be rushed.[^lJexGv6dxu8-505]

## Events

* Jagex changed the Confliction gauntlets passive: when you miss a magic attack, your next magic attack within four ticks rolls accuracy twice, so it only works with fast weapons and not manually cast spells such as barrage.[^-E1yidIYQ4Y-359]
* Jagex said that restricting the gauntlets' effect to attacks within the next four ticks was meant to avoid issues with freezes, PvP and blood spell healing, and was not an oversight, but it would now remove the four-tick restriction.[^u-RJBe_5Nmo-298]
* Jagex acknowledged the gauntlets are a band-aid fix that can be pulled off whenever changes to Tumeken's shadow are implemented.[^u-RJBe_5Nmo-298]
* Jagex changed the Confliction Gauntlets' passive effect so that it removes the four-tick restriction and benefits one-handed weapons only.[^u-RJBe_5Nmo-330]
* Mod Ash found in the gauntlets' code that the barrage effect applied only to the target being cast at, and noted that the code was not checking for ice spells.[^lJexGv6dxu8-448]
