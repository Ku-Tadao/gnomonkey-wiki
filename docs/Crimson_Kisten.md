# Crimson Kisten

## Stats and numbers

- In Gnomonkey's calcs, the Crimson Kisten goes from 21 percent worse than Burning claws on phase 2 Verzik to 16 percent worse, so it is still not good in Theatre of Blood. ([2026-02-17 0:57](https://youtu.be/kKccfwAJkh8?t=57))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey expects the Crimson Kisten to be the new best special attack weapon at Phosani's Nightmare, probably at Araxxor, and likely at Sarachnis because she is weak to crush. ([2026-02-17 1:26](https://youtu.be/kKccfwAJkh8?t=86))
- Gnomonkey says the Crimson Kisten has few uses since there are not many crush-weak bosses, nothing in raids is really weak to crush, and for Tekton you want a Warhammer; it is still slightly worse than Void Waker at Kephri. ([2026-02-17 1:26](https://youtu.be/kKccfwAJkh8?t=86))
- Gnomonkey says an Ironman without Burning claws could use the Crimson Kisten in place of them, but not as well; he considers it the least usable of the existing special-attack DPS weapons, a niche rather than bad addition. ([2026-02-17 1:56](https://youtu.be/kKccfwAJkh8?t=116))
- Gnomonkey would not be surprised if the Crimson Kisten is the best special attack at the Maggot King. ([2026-02-17 2:29](https://youtu.be/kKccfwAJkh8?t=149))
- Gnomonkey says the two-handed clarification makes the Crimson Kisten quite a bit worse: it remains the Phosani's Nightmare spec but is less good there, and at places like Theatre of Blood it is now quite a bit worse than Burning claws. ([2026-03-05 1:00](https://youtu.be/tDyb-FazeKw?t=60))
- Gnomonkey says he would have kept the Crimson Kisten one-handed because it was already not that good, but it is already two-handed in the poll. ([2026-03-05 1:27](https://youtu.be/tDyb-FazeKw?t=87))
- Gnomonkey agrees with Jagex that the Crimson Kisten should not be a main hand weapon, since crush weapons are currently not very good and he doubts it would see much use even as a spec. ([2026-03-05 1:27](https://youtu.be/tDyb-FazeKw?t=87))
- Gnomonkey concludes the Crimson Kisten is fine as is, maybe okay as a one-handed weapon, and that it is not a problem to have a lesser-used spec when the boss drops other very strong rewards. ([2026-03-05 11:32](https://youtu.be/tDyb-FazeKw?t=692))
