**Crimson Kisten** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2026-02-17 and 2026-03-05. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* In Gnomonkey's calcs, the Crimson Kisten goes from 21 percent worse than Burning claws on phase 2 Verzik to 16 percent worse, so it is still not good in Theatre of Blood.[^kKccfwAJkh8-57]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey expects the Crimson Kisten to be the new best special attack weapon at Phosani's Nightmare, probably at Araxxor, and likely at Sarachnis because she is weak to crush.[^kKccfwAJkh8-86]
* Gnomonkey says the Crimson Kisten has few uses since there are not many crush-weak bosses, nothing in raids is really weak to crush, and for Tekton you want a Warhammer; it is still slightly worse than Void Waker at Kephri.[^kKccfwAJkh8-86]
* Gnomonkey says an Ironman without Burning claws could use the Crimson Kisten in place of them, but not as well; he considers it the least usable of the existing special-attack DPS weapons, a niche rather than bad addition.[^kKccfwAJkh8-116]
* Gnomonkey would not be surprised if the Crimson Kisten is the best special attack at the Maggot King.[^kKccfwAJkh8-149]
* Gnomonkey says the two-handed clarification makes the Crimson Kisten quite a bit worse: it remains the Phosani's Nightmare spec but is less good there, and at places like Theatre of Blood it is now quite a bit worse than Burning claws.[^tDyb-FazeKw-60]
* Gnomonkey says he would have kept the Crimson Kisten one-handed because it was already not that good, but it is already two-handed in the poll.[^tDyb-FazeKw-87]
* Gnomonkey agrees with Jagex that the Crimson Kisten should not be a main hand weapon, since crush weapons are currently not very good and he doubts it would see much use even as a spec.[^tDyb-FazeKw-87]
* Gnomonkey concludes the Crimson Kisten is fine as is, maybe okay as a one-handed weapon, and that it is not a problem to have a lesser-used spec when the boss drops other very strong rewards.[^tDyb-FazeKw-692]

## Events

* The Crimson Bludgeon (crush claws) is being renamed the Crimson Kisten and buffed with an extra 23 crush accuracy bonus, to plus 80 total crush bonus.[^kKccfwAJkh8-29]

## Trivia

* The Crimson Kisten is the new crush special attack weapon, described as basically crush claws.[^tDyb-FazeKw-31]
