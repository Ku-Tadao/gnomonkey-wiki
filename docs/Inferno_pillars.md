**Inferno pillars** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 10 videos between 2021-05-21 and 2025-12-07. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Methods

* Gnomonkey starts waves west of the pillar then moves to the tile north of the north pillar for more dangerous waves, as it is more likely to protect from the ranger's attacks; going to the northeast corner after casting at nibblers also lets you corner trap dangerous monsters east of the pillar.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1302]
* For a mage and ranger stacked behind the pillar, start in the middle of the pillar and click the monster in the back or step out to the corner; the back monster sees you the first tick you step out and the front monster a tick later, so switch your prayer to the front monster when the back one starts its attack.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1722]
* If a bat is in the front, start one tile away from the pillar in the centre; if a blob is in the front, start two tiles away; blobs elsewhere in the stack are solved the same way by alternating prayer against the back then front monster.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1748]
* To solve a pillar stack, watch for the ranger to start its animation, pray ranged, then alternate prayers; this is why knowing the ranger's exact timing and how to lazy flick it matters.[^h6LxupiygPA-1118]
* On a pillar stack on the southwest side, the thing in the back hits first: you move two tiles out so the ranger reads you then the mage; pray ranged, step out, pray mage, click the mage, and alternate off the range animation.[^h6LxupiygPA-2129]
* On one bad ranger-blob-melee spawn Gnomonkey says to corner trap the mage, rotate the pillar to a certain side, kill the melee as fast as possible while flicking, then pray ranged and alternate with the ranger, which pulls the melee out of the equation.[^h6LxupiygPA-3081]
* Gnomonkey describes Inferno as adding pillar health as an extra resource alongside special attack and health, and his plugin shows current pillar health (244, 240, 255 in the video).[^XMTe9TYbDls-1198]
* A falling pillar hits the south-west tile of monsters, taking half their health away, so mages can be killed by pillar collapse; Gnomonkey says to try to kill both mages at the same time.[^XMTe9TYbDls-3382]
* To solve a stack at the Colosseum pillar, start from the middle of the pillar, go to the stacking tile and pray against the enemy in the back first and then the enemy in the front, which off-ticks them; Gnomonkey says this is the same across the board.[^kIfF1pNro2c-302]
* One stack does not work the normal way: with a Mage in the stack, going the usual direction means the small Mage sees you at the same time, so Gnomonkey instead goes the opposite way, praying against the Mage and running out right after it attacks.[^kIfF1pNro2c-489]
* You can force off-ticks by walking along an axis or running to a corner so an enemy sees you earlier, jiggling positions slightly.[^kIfF1pNro2c-570]
* A Manticore's attack cycle is five ticks of attacking and then five ticks to recharge, ten ticks in total, so its timing matches other mobs.[^kIfF1pNro2c-637]
* With a Manticore in a stack, pray against the thing in the back and then the thing in the front, same as other stacks.[^kIfF1pNro2c-692]
* For a stack with two enemies in front of a Manticore, start two or three tiles out from the pillar and pray against the enemy in front first and then the one in the back, so the order is backwards; the enemy in the back then sees you late.[^kIfF1pNro2c-911]
* Pillar stalling means getting out of Zulrah's line of sight behind a pillar during the green phase, then every six ticks peeking out to cast, then getting back behind.[^bBnPK55ZqUM-87]
* With the Bofa and blowpipe setup, Gnomonkey steps behind the pillar, fits in a blowpipe shot and moves back behind, so Zulrah still does four attacks but you get extra shots.[^bBnPK55ZqUM-1160]
* When pillar stalling with melee, you run up and hit Zulrah on every attack, which increases DPS and reduces damage, and the fourth attack is a Crystal halberd spec.[^ecd_a9E8l5E-336]
* The area between the pillars behind a pillar is usually a safe spot from both sides and is where you will most often handle melees.[^2xviK0wGI-o-891]
* Pillar stack: start in the middle of the pillar, click to the corner, pray against the NPC in the back and begin alternating when its animation starts; a mage respawning something takes eight ticks, two of his attacks, so the cycle does not change.[^2xviK0wGI-o-4394]

## Stats and numbers

* Monsters work off their southwestern tile, the only part that tries to reach or attack you; if a monster's southwest tile can see you but the rest is stuck behind something, it is safe spotted.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1328]
* Any monster can be corner trapped because monsters always try to move west or east before north or south, so pillars keep them stuck; this is why the north pillar is primarily used, as most things spawn south of it.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1328]
* When two monsters are both on a four-tick cycle one tick apart you can alternate, and it just works as math; with two ticks apart you must two-tick instead.[^h6LxupiygPA-2282]
* Every time Zulrah goes underwater you effectively lose about three pieces of food worth of ticks, so keeping her from submerging increases damage dealt.[^bBnPK55ZqUM-1368]
* Gnomonkey estimates pillar stalling makes phases roughly twice as long as without it, because it keeps Zulrah in a phase where she cannot hit you, though you cannot stall during snakeling spawns.[^bBnPK55ZqUM-1395]

## Tips

* Gnomonkey marks the nibbler spawn tile, a tile three tiles behind the pillar where he starts later waves (also the melee corner-trap tile, and a spot to Blood Barrage nibblers on the north pillar), a tile for luring Jad healers at triples, and four tiles for Zuk.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1241]
* To play around the north pillar as much as possible, since it is always possible to solve a wave from there (though it may involve flicking), and learners often panic and run west or south, which is usually a bad instinct.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1281]
* To commit safe-spot knowledge to instinct, as you need to rapidly safe spot melees in the later waves to survive.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1331]
* If your timing is off, immediately get back behind the pillar and drink brew; Gnomonkey points to the We Do Raids Inferno guide by Hayo for almost every pillar stack.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1775]
* The big early priority is to kill nibblers as fast as possible since nothing can kill you early, so you keep more pillar health.[^6lRmbYCYtME-177]
* His tile markers: yellow tiles are for Zuk and triples, one is the Addy tile not used on a first cape, a green tile lets you hit nibblers on a pillar through it, and another tile lets you cast at nibblers on your pillar; he says the markers can be imported from the pastebin on his Inferno guide.[^6lRmbYCYtME-3204]
* Nibblers after wave 50 get one cast only and you never chase a nibbler after wave 50.[^6lRmbYCYtME-3298]
* You can instantly turn the corner of a pillar no matter what cycle monsters are in because you always get a tick to switch to the next prayer, and that knowing you can rotate pillars at any moment is very helpful.[^6lRmbYCYtME-3385]
* Gnomonkey calls West pillar the decoy pillar and says if you stand a tile off a monster, when a melee digs the other monster can act as a pillar and get stuck on you, which works with mages, rangers and any NPC.[^6lRmbYCYtME-4065]
* When a ranger or mage is stacked behind another monster at a pillar, step so the thing in the back sees you first and the front thing sees you a tick later, then pray for the back attacker, click on a target while praying, wait for the flicker and alternate.[^FfNQI63ZKys-3699]
* You cannot click the monster in the front of a pillar stack (you get dragged), but you can click the one in the back, which with Bow of Faerdhinen lets you kill the ranger first if you want.[^FfNQI63ZKys-3757]
* You typically want to kill the mage first, and by default if you are scared you can kill the thing in the back, accepting that it will respawn.[^FfNQI63ZKys-3791]
* While alternating on a pillar stack, a blob in the mix is covered too, because you are effectively flicking all three attackers.[^FfNQI63ZKys-3791]
* On first cape, Gnomonkey says to camp ranged prayer until you kill the bat and then start shooting something, looking for the ranger animation to start alternating.[^h6LxupiygPA-1360]
* One-tick flicking does not always work with two different mobs, though it always works with a blob.[^h6LxupiygPA-2253]
* In a bad spawn Gnomonkey says you can run through a mage, and that you are almost always fine, since the alternative can be taking a lot of damage from everything; he calls this crazy tech no one ever does.[^h6LxupiygPA-2901]
* When the back enemy is too far to see you, Gnomonkey starts a few tiles out from the pillar, prays range as he steps out, then prays mage and runs in, so the mage sees him a tick later.[^EVOs96kyHSM-665]
* Gnomonkey's wave routine is to immediately kill the melee, go behind the pillar and solve the wave while getting ready to kill the mage.[^EVOs96kyHSM-912]
* In the manticore stack, Gnomonkey says the first attack style of the Manticore tells you whether to re-set the stack: if the Manticore is ranged and both saw you on the same tick it is off-ticked, and if it were mage he would go back around and set it up again.[^EVOs96kyHSM-1090]
* The biggest skill in the Colosseum is quickly recognising a problem like an off-ticked stack and quickly doing the pillar stack on the other side, or you will die.[^EVOs96kyHSM-1119]
* In a common pillar stack the enemy in the back sees you first and the enemy in the front sees you a tick later, so you step out praying for the back enemy then the front one.[^EVOs96kyHSM-1327]
* Stepping out from the middle of a pillar means the back enemy sees you before the front, while stepping out from the corner means everything sees you on the same tick.[^EVOs96kyHSM-1842]
* If enemies are off-ticked, you can also immediately SGS spec back, and you should pay attention to when the ranger is attacking you.[^EVOs96kyHSM-1901]
* If pillars have more health you do not need to get to them as quickly; Gnomonkey says a play at low health is not the same as at high health, and at 99 health you can tank something to get nibblers off a pillar.[^XMTe9TYbDls-1231]
* In early waves at 99 health you might as well go grab the nibblers immediately, because pillar health cannot be healed but your own health can.[^XMTe9TYbDls-1260]
* You cannot drop your north pillar, so nibblers on it must be dealt with.[^XMTe9TYbDls-2971]
* Gnomonkey plans to lose his south pillar late and says that once pillars are gone he expects the next thing going to the south pillar to destroy it.[^XMTe9TYbDls-3064]
* You know a mob is off-ticked relative to a Manticore if it attacks during the Manticore's orb attack style, or two ticks before or two ticks after the Manticore attacks.[^kIfF1pNro2c-663]
* When flicking prayers against a Manticore plus another enemy, Gnomonkey stares at whichever target starts its attack first; every two attacks he flicks through his prayers rather than tracking two mobs.[^kIfF1pNro2c-937]
* Off-ticking by saying that right after an enemy attacks you have four or five ticks in which any different attack that lands is off-ticked, so you ask whether something just attacked you.[^kIfF1pNro2c-1310]
* Pillar stalling keeps Zulrah above water longer, lets you do more damage and see fewer phases, making it effectively almost impossible to get a Jad phase with this setup.[^bBnPK55ZqUM-87]
* Gnomonkey recommends the attack timer metronome plugin for pillar stalling because it makes the timing much easier and loses fewer ticks.[^bBnPK55ZqUM-412]
* You can pillar stall Zulrah with melee, though you sometimes need an awkward walk to fit hits in because of the pathing, and you take a little poison damage.[^ecd_a9E8l5E-336]
* Most kills in this setup go through the same pattern with a few pillar stalls, mainly throwing on the Ring of suffering for snakelings.[^ecd_a9E8l5E-645]
* Standing in the middle of the pillar instead of the side lets melees step over and get in, while standing right of the pillar can corner trap melees that come down the side.[^2xviK0wGI-o-2073]
* Spells have a 10 tile range so you can cast at bloblets from the middle of the pillar, and stepping to a corner can drag a spawn closer for a 10-tile weapon.[^2xviK0wGI-o-2333]
* We Do Raids has a solve for basically every pillar stack and a solid Inferno guide from years ago that still holds up.[^2xviK0wGI-o-4459]
* Wave 62 is an extremely hard wave and a pillar stack wave is arguably the hardest wave of Inferno.[^2xviK0wGI-o-4693]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says West pillar is the least important pillar and he does not risk his health to save it from nibblers.[^6lRmbYCYtME-3447]
* Gnomonkey says that for speedruns you want West and South pillars dead, ideally with South pillar going down on wave 50, so monsters come to North pillar instead.[^6lRmbYCYtME-3559]
* Gnomonkey says North pillar is the one he cares about, because he can cast on things at it from about 50 angles, while things on West and South pillars are hard to get at.[^6lRmbYCYtME-4257]
* Gnomonkey says he has seen many people die trying to save South and West pillars while leaving things on North pillar, and that North is the one to care about.[^6lRmbYCYtME-4378]
* Gnomonkey says he does not want things to respawn typically because it causes pillar stacks, and that when a mage respawns things they get stuck in each other and weird things happen.[^FfNQI63ZKys-3850]
* Gnomonkey says to go for off-ticks even if you have to tank a ranged hit to set one up, because it makes life much easier.[^kIfF1pNro2c-1353]
* Gnomonkey argues that pillar stalling is a major reason not to use a Bofa camp setup, saying you cannot meaningfully pillar stall with it.[^bBnPK55ZqUM-1425]
