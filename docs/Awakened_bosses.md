**Awakened bosses** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 4 videos between 2025-05-09 and 2025-05-25. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* An Awakener's Orb is consumed on entering the boss room whether the player dies or kills the boss; it is gone either way.[^moDd-yhWlRs-200]
* Killing all four awakened bosses makes the fourth drop the ancient blood ornament kit, which with a lot of blood runes converts Torva into its Blorva variant.[^moDd-yhWlRs-200]

## Stats and numbers

* Awakener's Orbs are a fairly rare drop from the Desert Treasure 2 bosses, with drop rates varying by boss; taking one to its boss lets the player attempt the awakened version once.[^moDd-yhWlRs-171]
* At the time of the video Awakener's Orbs cost roughly 900k each, varying a lot, whereas on release they cost around 3-4 million.[^moDd-yhWlRs-200]
* According to people Gnomonkey asked in his Discord, getting Blorva usually takes around 100 to 200 orbs, with many players using 200 to 300 and some spending over 100 orbs on Vardorvis or Leviathan, or both.[^moDd-yhWlRs-256]
* At 900k per orb, Gnomonkey calculates 300 orbs is 270 million GP and a lucky 100 orbs is 90 million GP.[^moDd-yhWlRs-316]
* 10 billion GP is enough to buy every equippable item in the game that would be cool to use at a boss, including the harm staff, volatile staff, eldritch staff, scythe, shadow, Tumeken's Shadow items and full Torva.[^moDd-yhWlRs-405]
* Gnomonkey estimates the Awakener's Orb drop rate at roughly an hour to an hour and a half per orb on average, so 100-200 orbs is 100-200+ hours and realistically 500-600 hours for most players, which gates ironmen from the content.[^moDd-yhWlRs-523]
* Gnomonkey calculates Whisperer at about 5.4 million GP per hour after subtracting the Soul Reaper axe, with Awakener's Orbs worth about 540k, roughly 10% of profit.[^Y4S7mK8qoJo-1403][^Y4S7mK8qoJo-1501]

## Tips

* Gnomonkey suggested alternative fix ideas such as paying around 50 Awakener's Orbs once for a splash pass granting unlimited awakened boss access, or paying to enter once with free re-entry after a successful kill.[^H6qB5NmGEFQ-977]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the Yama contract system does share similarities with the Awakener's Orb system but differs in several ways, such as cosmetic contracts being target farmable and likely much cheaper than orbs.[^H6qB5NmGEFQ-641]
* Gnomonkey says the Yama contract system keeps the main issue of Awakener's Orbs: after completing everything you can only continue by buying more contracts, and no free or no-loot access is mentioned.[^H6qB5NmGEFQ-738]
* Gnomonkey says he believes the Yama system is better than Awakener's Orbs, with no question, but still has major flaws.[^H6qB5NmGEFQ-828]
* Gnomonkey says even at a hypothetical 250k per contract, a quarter of an Awakener's Orb, contracts would still not allow grinding, challenges, speedruns or casual fights.[^H6qB5NmGEFQ-828]
* Gnomonkey says aspirational content on a cost-per-attempt basis keeps people from trying new things; with Blorva it is not just mental, because if you do not get it in about 150 attempts you run out of money.[^H6qB5NmGEFQ-890]
* Gnomonkey says cost-per-attempt systems make sense only if the attempt gives great loot or GP, not for a cosmetic, and he believes content should only be gatekept by skill, not GP.[^H6qB5NmGEFQ-946]
* Gnomonkey says gatekeeping makes the cosmetic prestigious in the wrong way, being more about GP than skill, and encourages servicing.[^H6qB5NmGEFQ-1194]
* Gnomonkey says Jagex has taken the terrible Awakener's Orb system and tweaked it to be somewhat better, but he still considers the approach wrong and thinks it actively discourages people from playing.[^H6qB5NmGEFQ-1342]
* Gnomonkey considers Awakener's Orbs one of the biggest mistakes, maybe the biggest, Jagex has made with high-level content.[^moDd-yhWlRs-250]
* Gnomonkey says that once Blorva is unlocked, players can still buy orbs and enter, but no longer have any affordable way to engage with the content, such as speedruns or challenges, unless they are one of a select few very wealthy players.[^moDd-yhWlRs-256]
* Gnomonkey says he has soloed seven shadows yet cannot afford awakened bosses and will never kill them, because only players who sell services or leeches can afford them, not players making GP through methods like PvM.[^moDd-yhWlRs-463]
* Gnomonkey says Awakened Leviathan is in his top five bosses in the game, but he cannot afford to do the challenges or speedruns on it.[^moDd-yhWlRs-488]
* Gnomonkey considers the awakened bosses content that actively dissuades people from engaging with it, which he says is bad design.[^moDd-yhWlRs-523]
* Gnomonkey's suggested fix is making the Sanguine kit work like the Echo Orbs from Leagues, giving permanent unlimited access to awakened bosses once earned.[^moDd-yhWlRs-602]
* Gnomonkey says he and others have decided they cannot afford to keep killing awakened bosses after getting Blorva, so they do not.[^Y4S7mK8qoJo-1098]
* Gnomonkey concludes Awakener's Orbs add only about 5 to 10 percent to profit at awakened bosses, which he considers minor compared to the cost of gatekeeping learners and challenge players.[^Y4S7mK8qoJo-1532]
* Gnomonkey disagrees that Blorva is mostly a flex of GP rather than skill but considers it both a skill and money flex, and believes it would be more impressive and a purer skill flex if players could attempt it earlier with worse gear, such as with a Bow of Faerdhinen.[^Y4S7mK8qoJo-1561]
* Gnomonkey says high-level updates are too rare to dedicate to a one-time cosmetic with no repeatability, and that cheaper contracts also lessen the profit benefit for those who farm them.[^Y4S7mK8qoJo-1681]
* Gnomonkey says wealth siphoning does not require this system and has been done many times before in other ways.[^Y4S7mK8qoJo-1699]
* Gnomonkey says awakened bosses surprised him and were pretty good, with Awakened Leviathan one of the best boss fights he has done and Awakened Vardorvis also one of the best.[^yP8IB3_IsSc-2433]
* Gnomonkey says even the two other awakened bosses he likes less are better than any of the Yama contracts.[^yP8IB3_IsSc-2464]

## Records

* He spent roughly 1.5 billion GP on orbs trying for a no-stun awakened boss attempt and never got it, at a time when orbs cost 3-4 million each; he likens it to buying a Twisted bow.[^moDd-yhWlRs-344]
* Noobtype spent roughly 4 billion GP on the 27-orb Leviathan challenge and around 6 billion going for no stun the first time, a total of about 10 billion.[^moDd-yhWlRs-374]
* Saks spent 3-4 billion GP trying for a world record Vardorvis without achieving it, and spent roughly 7 to 10 billion GP on Whisperer and Duke.[^moDd-yhWlRs-395]
* Porkazar did the 54 Awakened Leviathan challenge and stated in his video that he spent over 3 billion GP on it.[^moDd-yhWlRs-434]

## Events

* Jmods have said they are willing to add a permanent unlock method for the awakened bosses.[^Y4S7mK8qoJo-1561]
