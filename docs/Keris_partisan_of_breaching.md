# Keris partisan of breaching

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2022-05-18 to 2025-06-28). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | item |
| **Videos** | 2 (first 2022-05-18, latest 2025-06-28) |

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey calculates, assuming his maths is right, that the partisan is dramatically better than Scythe on Kalphite Queen: at one hammer it should be 100% accurate on task, making it already better than a two-hammer Scythe with no hammers landed. ([2022-05-18 9:18](https://youtu.be/Kgm0VYX_4no?t=558))
- Gnomonkey says Kalphite Queen would be decimated by Keris partisan of breaching on phase 1 plus Shadow of Tumeken on phase 2, for roughly a 20% total DPS increase with the Raids 3 items. ([2022-05-18 9:44](https://youtu.be/Kgm0VYX_4no?t=584))
- The Keris Partisan has a 15% damage bonus against scarabs and the ability to crit, with a max hit of 150 shown in Gearscape, making it much better on Kephri than on Baba. ([2025-06-28 1:26](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=86))

## Tips

- The black gem does not let you skip the Zamorakian hasta because you still need to get a black gem; Gnomonkey recommends doing your Tormented Demon grind to get a Scobo (as transcribed) and then killing some 6-0 Zammy to get a Zamorakian hasta, which is a very common early drop. ([2025-06-28 0:56](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=56))
- On Kephri, the Keris Partisan is the best DPS weapon against every scarab, and Gnomonkey says you can still throw the Atlatl while running up to a scarab before switching. ([2025-06-28 14:58](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=898))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey expects the accuracy increase to see players bring Torva instead of Inquisitor's because the hammer hit is less impactful: Torva is barely worse at zero hammers and much better at one hammer plus, and it allows claws, boosted by Torva, to be used after one hammer like at Sire. ([2022-05-18 9:44](https://youtu.be/Kgm0VYX_4no?t=584))
- Gnomonkey considers the black Keris Partisan a good option for an Ironman until you have an Osmumten's fang, and the second best option until then. ([2025-06-28 0:29](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=29))
- Gnomonkey says the black Keris Partisan hits very well on Baba and would be very similar in kill time to a Zamorakian hasta. ([2025-06-28 15:28](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=928))
- Gnomonkey says you do not need a yellow-gem (healing) Keris Partisan to do a 300 raid, and recommends not bringing one if you get it early because it wastes your special attack bar; it only becomes useful at very high raid levels. ([2025-06-28 24:51](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=1491))

## Events

- The poll blog adds a Breach of the Scarab upgrade for the Keris partisan, giving it a 33% accuracy multiplier against Kalphite fights. ([2022-05-18 9:18](https://youtu.be/Kgm0VYX_4no?t=558))
- The Tombs of Amascut update added a fourth gem drop, a black gem that can be added to the Keris Partisan for extra stab bonus and stats; Gnomonkey got the black Keris Partisan in his previous raid, and notes in the description that it is typically about 5% better than a Zamorakian hasta but only great until you get an Osmumten's fang, and that you can push up to 400s now. ([2025-06-28 0:00](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10))
