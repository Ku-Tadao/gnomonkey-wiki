# Alt account

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 5 videos (2019-08-27 to 2022-04-28). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- After a Nightmare kill with an alt, you can either leave and have the alt trade your supplies, or stay inside waiting out the timer and have the alt enter and trade you, then have the alt leave. ([2020-10-09 18:23](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=1103))
- If the alt has high magic and Lunars, it can cast energy transfer and heal other to fully heal the main and restore special attack. ([2020-10-09 18:23](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=1103))
- If the alt carries supplies for multiple kills it can cast Dream outside The Nightmare's lair to restore the main to full health between phases. ([2020-10-09 18:52](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=1132))
- The loop with an alt is to kill the Kalphite Queen, house-tab, restore and run back; since she has a long respawn timer, tabbing and getting special attack back gives faster kills than waiting for the respawn. ([2022-02-19 2:30](https://youtu.be/3m9N8ZJ3flQ?t=150))
- Gnomonkey uses an alt (ult) account for Zulrah; it can be a freshly bonded level three, and it is minorly beneficial if it can cast Vengeance on the main before kills. ([2022-04-15 2:26](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=146))
- The alt's main purpose is to pick up the Zulrah loot the main drops to it so the main can keep killing; dropping extra supplies to the main from the alt is theoretically possible but complex, so Gnomonkey prefers banking at Seers' Village when out of supplies. ([2022-04-15 2:56](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=176))
- At the start, run the alt to Narda to stand in front of the altar picking up loot; the alt's altar acts as a full stat restore like a house pool, and the main can overcharge health to 106, take Vengeance from the alt, then teleport to Zulrah with a scroll. ([2022-04-15 4:21](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=261))
- Bosses with unique methods such as Zamorak 50 or Bandos 9-0 when done solo with an alt reduce to clicking the boss on the alt and then on the main, which Gnomonkey finds really relaxing. ([2022-04-28 2:22](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=142))
- Even when the alt cannot attack the boss, such as at Kraken or Cerberus, it can sit nearby casting Heal Other and Energy Transfer repeatedly, take drops when your inventory is full, then run to get more supplies. ([2022-04-28 2:22](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=142))
- After quests and combat training, finish the alt off by AFKing in Nightmare Zone, which can be done while doing anything else on the main; it usually takes a couple of weeks of playtime to get an alt maxed besides melee. ([2022-04-28 3:57](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=237))
- Gnomonkey considers Energy Transfer likely the most important alt spell: it hits your alt for 10 damage, transfers his special attack meter to your main and fully restores run energy; Dream is also useful for increased HP regeneration. ([2022-04-28 4:31](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=271))
- Any boss far from a bank can benefit from an alt running supplies: Nightmare, Cerberus, Abyssal Sire, Dagannoth Kings, God Wars Dungeon bosses, even Kraken and Sarachnis, with the alt also taking loose loot off your hands. ([2022-04-28 7:02](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=422))
- Fresh alts can also hold scouted raids for solo or team Chambers, pick up loot at barrage tasks, King Black Dragon/Kalphite Queen and Zulrah, scout areas in the Wilderness, and touch the boat at Tempoross to start solo games faster. ([2022-04-28 7:32](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=452))
- At Abyssal Sire, skip the house tab each kill and have the alt bring the house to you by using the house pool and running from the fairy ring each kill; at DKs the alt brings a second set of supplies and Dreams there, spec-transferring and healing when needed. ([2022-04-28 7:58](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=478))
- At Cerberus the alt works like at DKs, but can easily run to a bank then teleport to his house in Taverley and run back for more supplies. ([2022-04-28 8:32](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=512))
- At Thermonuclear smoke devil an alt can repeatedly run from house to fairy ring to the cave to provide constant claw specs; at Kraken the alt can Dream behind you, providing constant health and volatile specs and taking unnoted drops like sand/smoke serums. ([2022-04-28 8:32](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=512))
- Alts that can fight the boss alongside the main overlap with higher-level money-making alts (very high requirements) but can dramatically boost kills per hour at many bosses, often raising gp/hr by more than the alt would earn doing Rune dragons, while lowering the boss's intensity. ([2022-04-28 9:01](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=541))
- At God Wars Dungeon bosses such as Zamorak and Bandos, the alt just tanks and the main attacks, which dramatically reduces intensity and increases kills per hour. ([2022-04-28 9:01](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=541))
- Alts also enable solo kills at King Black Dragon, Corporeal Beast, Sarachnis, Scorpia and Callisto, and can be expanded to 'trolo' (trio solo) at many of these bosses or even quadrolo for some. ([2022-04-28 9:34](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=574))

## Requirements

- 83 Construction on an alt is extremely useful for the health pool, fairy ring and teleports to reach bosses quickly; early on a Ring of dueling at Clan Wars can restore but is too slow for many methods. ([2022-04-28 5:04](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=304))
- To upgrade a resupply alt, work towards Lunar Diplomacy and 91 Magic for both Plank/Pot Share and Energy Transfer, or 93 for Vengeance Other. ([2022-04-28 7:32](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=452))

## Stats and numbers

- An alt boosts kills per hour by roughly 10% by extending trip length, about the same as the Elysian shield. ([2022-02-19 3:35](https://youtu.be/3m9N8ZJ3flQ?t=215))
- For many bosses an alt can speed up kills per hour by over 50%, according to Gnomonkey. ([2022-04-28 0:26](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=26))
- At Armadyl solo, an alt speeds up kills per hour from 25 to 30, lets the trip last an entire task, and removes the awkward chin-clicking method of being on Armadyl for two ticks and the minion for one tick each attack. ([2022-04-28 1:53](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=113))
- An alt increases Armadyl to almost 8.5m gp per hour total while making him far easier intensity-wise. ([2022-04-28 2:22](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=142))
- Gnomonkey says every Slayer boss except Gargoyles and Hydra benefits from an alt; for most the alt cannot attack at the same time but helps by running supplies and healing or spec-transferring. ([2022-04-28 7:58](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=478))
- At Saradomin and Armadyl alts can increase trip lengths enormously; Gnomonkey has done an 8-hour solo Armadyl trip, and Slayer tasks of Aviansie are guaranteed done in one trip with an alt even at the max roll of 250. ([2022-04-28 9:34](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=574))

## Tips

- An alt account makes bursting easier: a brand new level 3 account can loot drops and run back and forth to the bank since monsters up to Nechryael are not aggressive. ([2019-08-27 2:24](https://youtu.be/MzYqTycY-uQ?t=144))
- An account with level 55 Magic that can cast high alchemy can stay until the task is finished alching drops. ([2019-08-27 2:24](https://youtu.be/MzYqTycY-uQ?t=144))
- Without an alt account Gnomonkey suggests an Explorer's ring 4, which can cast high and low alchemy 30 times each, allowing only one burst task per day if the player wants to pick up the loot. ([2019-08-27 2:51](https://youtu.be/MzYqTycY-uQ?t=171))
- Gnomonkey says the easiest time to run the alt back to the bank is during the third phase, giving about five minutes to restock the alt and run back. ([2020-10-09 18:52](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=1132))
- Gnomonkey says an alt for the Kalphite Queen is relatively unnecessary but can pick up loot you drop so you can carry more food drops, extending trips; his alt is a fresh account with an earth staff, a rune pouch with astral and death runes and a pile of house tabs. ([2022-02-19 2:30](https://youtu.be/3m9N8ZJ3flQ?t=150))
- If the alt cannot cast Vengeance but has High Level Alchemy, bring that instead so you never need to bank with it. ([2022-02-19 2:30](https://youtu.be/3m9N8ZJ3flQ?t=150))
- An alt without Vengeance can sit inside your house and you drop the Kalphite Queen loot to it after each teleport back; an alt that does cast Vengeance cannot stay in your house because it loses Vengeance when leaving, so sit it at the start of the Kalphite nest, which needs a rope unless it has the diary, or a Hitpoints cape and regeneration bracelet to outheal desert heat. ([2022-02-19 3:01](https://youtu.be/3m9N8ZJ3flQ?t=181))
- Even a low-level alt in the nest is fine because the Kalphite workers are unaggressive; Kalphite Queen heads cannot be dropped to an alt as they are untradeable until banked, so leave them on the ground since they are worth about 1k. ([2022-02-19 3:01](https://youtu.be/3m9N8ZJ3flQ?t=181))
- The only requirement Gnomonkey's gear might lack is a Desert amulet; you can technically use house tabs with an alt chilling in your house to take drops, but that is a lot slower and removes Vengeance when leaving the house. ([2022-04-15 0:32](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=32))
- It is near impossible to venge on the blue phase rotation, so not having Vengeance is not the end of the world. ([2022-04-15 2:26](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=146))
- Gnomonkey recommends the Gear Discord and Petcord, which include many alting methods; Petcord even has a basic Rune Dragon alt guide that doubles as a great PvM alt. ([2022-04-28 0:26](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=26))
- Gnomonkey recommends starting an alt during any long-term AFK grind such as AFK pets or 99s: Redwoods for the Beaver is about a 331-hour grind on average, giving roughly a month of playtime to build a near-maxed alt. ([2022-04-28 3:02](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=182))
- Other AFK pet options are Karambwans with a fish barrel for Heron, Blood runes for Rift Guardian and Amethyst for Rock Golem. ([2022-04-28 3:02](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=182))
- Gnomonkey highly recommends the Quest Helper plugin when making an alt as it trivializes almost every quest; he says Mourning's End Part II took only about 40 minutes on his alt even with agility fails, and One Small Favour about 20 minutes. ([2022-04-28 3:27](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=207))
- Gnomonkey says Magic and Agility are among the most important levels for an alt; Magic unlocks Plank/Pot Share, Energy Transfer and Vengeance Other. ([2022-04-28 4:18](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=258))
- Agility unlocks important shortcuts, helps regenerate run energy for a resupply alt, and unlocks the Graceful outfit, which the alt wears most of the time. ([2022-04-28 4:31](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=271))
- Combat stats are easily trained with barrages, splashing, chinning and Nightmare Zone; max combat is useful because you often dolo bosses with ranged and melee. ([2022-04-28 5:04](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=304))
- Other useful tradable unlocks are a Xeric's talisman and a Chambers tablet for quick banking; leeching raids with the alt gets these easily. ([2022-04-28 5:04](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=304))
- Gnomonkey lists Dragon defender, Avernic defender (if you are rich), Assembler, Barrows gloves (the best tank gloves) and Void as useful alt gear. ([2022-04-28 5:35](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=335))
- Hitpoints cape and Magic cape are very useful on an alt for their perks, and several diaries help: Karamja (Canron/Cerberus path) for bolt proc and Armadyl, Western Provinces for Elite Void, and Wilderness for obelisks. ([2022-04-28 6:07](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=367))
- Many unlocks are not needed on an alt, for example skilling outfits like Prospector or Angler, so you can skip Motherlode Mine. ([2022-04-28 6:07](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=367))
- For most solo-with-alt methods you do not actively play both accounts at once; at God Wars you click the boss on the alt once and then focus on the main. ([2022-04-28 10:03](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=603))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says The Nightmare is incredibly crowded because of its GP rates, so he often hopped through all worlds without finding an open one; alts are so good because they allow you to hold a world indefinitely. ([2020-10-09 5:28](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=328))
- Gnomonkey says alts are incredibly useful: usually people use them to make money, but they can also help tremendously at bosses, raids and some skilling activities; it would be faster to list bosses that do not benefit from alts. ([2022-04-28 0:00](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk))
- Gnomonkey says the sooner you build an alt the sooner it benefits you, so if you are considering one you should just do it; alts require investment for membership, levels and unlocks. ([2022-04-28 0:54](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=54))
- Gnomonkey describes alts as 'effectively summoning from RS2 with extra steps'. ([2022-04-28 1:24](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=84))
- Gnomonkey says money is no object if you enjoy PvM, since raids, Nightmare and Nex bring in lots of gp, and that money-making alts make the game feel more like a chore for him; the real benefit of an alt is dramatically speeding up pet grinds. ([2022-04-28 1:24](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=84))
- Gnomonkey says alts typically reduce the input required at any boss, rather than being 'too sweaty' as some claim, and also boost gp/hr and reduce supply use. ([2022-04-28 1:53](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=113))
- Gnomonkey says these features dramatically increase trip lengths and kills per hour while letting you avoid an expensive Blood fury and Scythe combo. ([2022-04-28 2:54](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=174))
- Gnomonkey says having multiple alts is great, but one solid max alt covers most activities. ([2022-04-28 3:57](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=237))
- Gnomonkey calls the Infernal cape the best offensive and defensive melee cape, with Fire cape fine in the meantime; he recommends getting it on an alt after 99 Ranged and before melee training if you have done Inferno on your main, barraging on defensive for Defence levels. ([2022-04-28 5:35](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=335))
- Gnomonkey says making an alt is basically the same as making a main except you can supply it from the main, like speedrunning the early game; he did not use an alt guide, and focuses on quickly doing optimal quests like Waterfall Quest and training key skills. ([2022-04-28 6:32](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=392))
- Gnomonkey calls one of the most useful and lowest-requirement alts a resupply alt, which for the most part can be a fresh level three account. ([2022-04-28 6:34](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=394))
- A resupply alt saves lots of banking time and can allow using divine potions or more gear switches at bosses where you otherwise would not; Gnomonkey names Dagannoth Kings as a good example. ([2022-04-28 7:02](https://youtu.be/ndCNX2wQNmk?t=422))
