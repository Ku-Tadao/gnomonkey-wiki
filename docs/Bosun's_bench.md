# Bosun's bench

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2026-04-09 to 2026-05-11). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

|---|---|
| **Type** | item |

## Requirements

- The bosun's bench facility requires 63 Sailing and 54 Construction plus a bunch of supplies, and can be built in any facility slot that a ballistic attractor can be built in. ([2026-04-09 6:30](https://youtu.be/8CSztSkDvnM?t=390))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey is concerned that boats are getting too many facilities to fit, especially after the ballistic attractor, and would rather crewmates repair ships automatically based on something like deck handiness. ([2026-04-09 6:50](https://youtu.be/8CSztSkDvnM?t=410))
- Gnomonkey finds multiple ships for facilities clunky and annoying, particularly when the rewards are as rare as dragon nails and dragon metal sheets. ([2026-04-09 7:23](https://youtu.be/8CSztSkDvnM?t=443))
- Gnomonkey is unsure about the repair facility; he says it would be okay as a side thing like the inoculation station or something that installs into the ship's hull and frees up space, but a big clunky table in the middle of the boat would be annoying. ([2026-05-11 2:36](https://youtu.be/7WIZw1stPas?t=156))
- After seeing it can be built in any existing slot that supports it, Gnomonkey says he personally votes no because he does not want even more stations on his boat. ([2026-05-11 9:02](https://youtu.be/7WIZw1stPas?t=542))

## Events

- The new pirates also drop a hammer that lets players build the bosun's bench facility, which assigns crew to repair the boat with repair packs. ([2026-04-09 6:11](https://youtu.be/8CSztSkDvnM?t=371))
- According to Gnomonkey, Jagex said on Reddit that it wants to encourage players to have different boats for different purposes, such as a combat ship and a pirate combat ship. ([2026-04-09 7:23](https://youtu.be/8CSztSkDvnM?t=443))
- Jagex is adding the bosun's bench, a dedicated repair facility, as part of the Red Reef update. ([2026-05-11 2:36](https://youtu.be/7WIZw1stPas?t=156))
