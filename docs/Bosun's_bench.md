**Bosun's bench** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2026-04-09 and 2026-05-11. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* The bosun's bench facility requires 63 Sailing and 54 Construction plus a bunch of supplies, and can be built in any facility slot that a ballistic attractor can be built in.[^8CSztSkDvnM-390]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey is concerned that boats are getting too many facilities to fit, especially after the ballistic attractor, and would rather crewmates repair ships automatically based on something like deck handiness.[^8CSztSkDvnM-410]
* Gnomonkey finds multiple ships for facilities clunky and annoying, particularly when the rewards are as rare as dragon nails and dragon metal sheets.[^8CSztSkDvnM-443]
* Gnomonkey is unsure about the repair facility; he says it would be okay as a side thing like the inoculation station or something that installs into the ship's hull and frees up space, but a big clunky table in the middle of the boat would be annoying.[^7WIZw1stPas-156]
* After seeing it can be built in any existing slot that supports it, Gnomonkey says he personally votes no because he does not want even more stations on his boat.[^7WIZw1stPas-542]

## Events

* The new pirates also drop a hammer that lets players build the bosun's bench facility, which assigns crew to repair the boat with repair packs.[^8CSztSkDvnM-371]
* According to Gnomonkey, Jagex said on Reddit that it wants to encourage players to have different boats for different purposes, such as a combat ship and a pirate combat ship.[^8CSztSkDvnM-443]
* Jagex is adding the bosun's bench, a dedicated repair facility, as part of the Red Reef update.[^7WIZw1stPas-156]
