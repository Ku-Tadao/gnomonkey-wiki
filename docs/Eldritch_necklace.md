**Eldritch necklace** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2025-01-30 and 2025-02-06. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* To equip the proposed necklace, a player must have cleared Delve level 3 and have 80 Magic.[^kkmUE6oQxy4-149]

## Stats and numbers

* The proposed Eldritch necklace is an untradeable upgrade for the Occult necklace that requires level 65 Runecraft and level 65 Crafting to combine.[^kkmUE6oQxy4-96]
* Over the Occult necklace, the proposed Mokha necklace adds 2% magic damage, +1 prayer bonus and +8 magic attack bonus.[^kkmUE6oQxy4-96]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the earlier idea of raising minimum hit to boost weapons that are not the Tumeken's shadow was a really cool idea, and that it was removed from the necklace for no reason because nobody disliked it.[^kkmUE6oQxy4-117]
* Gnomonkey considers the Delve level 3 and 80 Magic equip requirement tacked on and pointless, and asks why it is not the eighth Delve level so it becomes an actual achievement.[^kkmUE6oQxy4-149]
* Gnomonkey says the necklace would be a very small upgrade to Shadow users and would almost never give a max hit on weapons that are not the Shadow, and that Jagex is again scared to make it good because the Shadow exists.[^kkmUE6oQxy4-206]
* Gnomonkey says if the necklace gave 5% magic damage it would be a big-ticket item, but 2% mage damage is minor, comparable to the Zaryte vambraces upgrade from Nex.[^kkmUE6oQxy4-828]
* Gnomonkey suggests making the necklace chargeable so that it goes up to 10% magic damage, with a currency used to charge it.[^kkmUE6oQxy4-1505]
* Gnomonkey says he did not mind the necklace as a reward slot, but it felt disingenuous because Jagex had just nerfed the Occult necklace and was immediately giving that power back.[^XBoGGoJS5k0-127]
* Gnomonkey says he had been thinking of a bracelet slot, such as the Tormented bracelet, as a reward slot instead, though he does not think the necklace was a huge deal.[^XBoGGoJS5k0-127]

## Events

* Jagex said the Eldritch necklace would be dropped as a Delve boss reward and replaced with a completely new reward.[^XBoGGoJS5k0-127]
