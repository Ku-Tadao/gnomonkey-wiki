# Eldritch necklace

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2025-01-30 to 2025-02-06). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

|---|---|
| **Type** | item |

## Requirements

- To equip the proposed necklace, a player must have cleared Delve level 3 and have 80 Magic. ([2025-01-30 2:29](https://youtu.be/kkmUE6oQxy4?t=149))

## Stats and numbers

- The proposed Eldritch necklace is an untradeable upgrade for the Occult necklace that requires level 65 Runecraft and level 65 Crafting to combine. ([2025-01-30 1:36](https://youtu.be/kkmUE6oQxy4?t=96))
- Over the Occult necklace, the proposed Mokha necklace adds 2% magic damage, +1 prayer bonus and +8 magic attack bonus. ([2025-01-30 1:36](https://youtu.be/kkmUE6oQxy4?t=96))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the earlier idea of raising minimum hit to boost weapons that are not the Tumeken's shadow was a really cool idea, and that it was removed from the necklace for no reason because nobody disliked it. ([2025-01-30 1:57](https://youtu.be/kkmUE6oQxy4?t=117))
- Gnomonkey considers the Delve level 3 and 80 Magic equip requirement tacked on and pointless, and asks why it is not the eighth Delve level so it becomes an actual achievement. ([2025-01-30 2:29](https://youtu.be/kkmUE6oQxy4?t=149))
- Gnomonkey says the necklace would be a very small upgrade to Shadow users and would almost never give a max hit on weapons that are not the Shadow, and that Jagex is again scared to make it good because the Shadow exists. ([2025-01-30 3:26](https://youtu.be/kkmUE6oQxy4?t=206))
- Gnomonkey says if the necklace gave 5% magic damage it would be a big-ticket item, but 2% mage damage is minor, comparable to the Zaryte vambraces upgrade from Nex. ([2025-01-30 13:48](https://youtu.be/kkmUE6oQxy4?t=828))
- Gnomonkey suggests making the necklace chargeable so that it goes up to 10% magic damage, with a currency used to charge it. ([2025-01-30 25:05](https://youtu.be/kkmUE6oQxy4?t=1505))
- Gnomonkey says he did not mind the necklace as a reward slot, but it felt disingenuous because Jagex had just nerfed the Occult necklace and was immediately giving that power back. ([2025-02-06 2:07](https://youtu.be/XBoGGoJS5k0?t=127))
- Gnomonkey says he had been thinking of a bracelet slot, such as the Tormented bracelet, as a reward slot instead, though he does not think the necklace was a huge deal. ([2025-02-06 2:07](https://youtu.be/XBoGGoJS5k0?t=127))

## Events

- Jagex said the Eldritch necklace would be dropped as a Delve boss reward and replaced with a completely new reward. ([2025-02-06 2:07](https://youtu.be/XBoGGoJS5k0?t=127))
