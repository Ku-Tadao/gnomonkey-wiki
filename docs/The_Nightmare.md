# The Nightmare

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 7 videos (2020-10-09 to 2026-06-04). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Gnomonkey's Nightmare solo inventory includes a four-way mage switch, occult necklace, god cape, tormented bracelet, and a Sanguinesti staff with an imbued heart. ([2020-10-09 2:59](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=179))
- Gnomonkey also brings dragon claws to the solo because he happens to have them; they are good for killing parasites ASAP but not necessary. ([2020-10-09 3:55](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=235))
- Gnomonkey brings 10 brews, 3 super combats and 5 super restores for a single Nightmare kill; double kill trips will likely require swapping some brews for more super combats and restores. ([2020-10-09 4:27](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=267))
- The Nightmare uses all three attack styles: magic (arms flopping with a red glow, shooting a small red orb), ranged (cracks her bones three times then flings shards), and rarely melee (leans back with hands on the ground and scrapes towards you). ([2020-10-09 6:43](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=403))
- In a group The Nightmare picks its target based on defence bonuses, defence level and the prayer in use; only the tank needs to worry about melee attacks while the rest can stand safely behind her. In a solo you are always the target. ([2020-10-09 8:04](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=484))
- Grasping claws is a special attack that replaces her regular attacks and can be used in any phase: black portals form around the arena and under her, and hands hit for up to 60 if you stand on a portal or walk under her. A portal almost always spawns under you with about three ticks to move, and the attack is only active for one tick. ([2020-10-09 8:35](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=515))
- After her HP is reduced to zero The Nightmare loses her shield and you must swap to magic gear and damage the totems in each quarter. She continues her normal and special attacks during the totem phase, so keep evading. ([2020-10-09 9:06](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=546))
- After all four totems are charged, The Nightmare takes one third of her health in damage, is teleported to the middle, and spawns one sleepwalker per player, so a solo has one sleepwalker. If you fail to attack the sleepwalker before it reaches her she hits you for 74, reduced to five if you kill it first. ([2020-10-09 9:39](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=579))
- The fight repeats through three phases, with the third set of totems charged killing The Nightmare. ([2020-10-09 10:13](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=613))
- In flower phase she spawns four vertical lines of flowers: two lines of rotten flowers with flies and two lines of regular flowers. Regular flowers mark a safe quadrant, and leaving it towards the rotten flowers deals 4 to 8 damage per tick and heals The Nightmare for the damage you take. ([2020-10-09 11:09](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=669))
- Flower phase also spawns two husks with 20 HP each that freeze you in place and attack with magic and ranged: skinny husks shoot blue magic projectiles and fat husks shoot green ranged projectiles. They do 1 to 15 damage each and are weak to melee, but add up quickly alongside Nightmare's attacks. ([2020-10-09 12:08](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=728))
- Parasite phase removes flowers and husks and adds two special attacks. The parasite attack makes her cough up a parasite that infects you; you must cure it with a sand fuse dose or Relicym's balm (clicking the cure health orb drinks a cure automatically), or after three more attacks it bursts from your chest. ([2020-10-09 13:33](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=813))
- If a parasite spawns when it cannot heal anything (totems charged, no totem damage, or the shield just broke) it attacks with ranged and magic instead, which Gnomonkey considers better. Parasites have fairly high magic defence, so swap to melee if they spawn during the totem phase. ([2020-10-09 15:14](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=914))
- Curse is the other parasite-phase special, signalled by a pink glow on screen. For five attacks your protection prayers shift one to the right: mage becomes ranged, ranged becomes melee, melee becomes mage. Count five attacks so you are not caught off prayer; she can also use it during totems. ([2020-10-09 15:47](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=947))
- In the final phase The Nightmare can no longer curse or spawn parasites. Her truck special has her hop into the ground and teleport to a side of the arena, then blast forward and run you over for over 50 if you are in the way. ([2020-10-09 16:25](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=985))
- Mushrooms spawn in a checkerboard pattern; stepping within one tile pops them, preventing you from running for five of her attacks and reducing attack speed by one tick with all weapons, which basically costs one bludgeon hit. You have about two ticks to get away when they spawn. They do zero damage. ([2020-10-09 16:52](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=1012))
- In a group the mushroom effect can be spread by walking next to another player, similar to the Ohm burn attack in the Theatre. ([2020-10-09 17:51](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=1071))
- In a group the player who did the most damage receives big bones, which gives a 5% increased chance to receive a jar, clue scroll or Nightmare pet. ([2020-10-09 17:51](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=1071))

## Stats and numbers

- At the time of the video, the harmonised orb was worth over 1.5 billion gp, the Inquisitor's mace 950 million, and every Nightmare staff drop over 150 million. ([2020-10-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk))
- Getting killed at the end of a solo Nightmare kill costs about 300k in supplies on top of the 60k reclaim fee, plus 20 minutes of time. ([2020-10-09 0:32](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=32))
- It takes about 34 hours of Nightmare killing on average to get a rare drop, which Gnomonkey says costs about 10m in supplies on average, so he recommends having a decent cash stack first. ([2020-10-09 0:32](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=32))
- You have about three ticks to react to The Nightmare's ranged and magic attacks and about two ticks for her faster melee attack. Once a projectile has been thrown it is too late to switch prayer, and correct protection prayer reduces damage by about 75%. ([2020-10-09 7:01](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=421))
- The Nightmare's ranged and magic attacks max over 40, while her melee attack, the most dangerous, maxes over 50, so most melee attacks must be caught with the correct prayer for a kill to be possible. The attacks have obvious sound cues. ([2020-10-09 7:01](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=421))
- The Nightmare has 2000 HP in each phase before her shield breaks. ([2020-10-09 8:35](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=515))
- Each Nightmare phase has two unique special attacks, and she typically uses a phase-specific attack every five regular attacks (including grasping claws), although this is somewhat random. ([2020-10-09 11:09](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=669))
- A burst parasite heals The Nightmare every four ticks for 10 to 80 health. If you forgot to cure the disease, it bursts for about 70 damage and the parasite has 80 health instead of 40. ([2020-10-09 13:43](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=823))
- Each additional player after five adds a 1% roll at a second rare drop; Gnomonkey says more than five players is much more inefficient, so most groups have one to five players. ([2020-10-09 18:23](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=1103))
- A solo Nightmare kill takes about 20 minutes, and about 100 kills (the average for a rare drop) takes about 34 hours if done very fast. ([2020-10-09 18:52](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=1132))
- At the Nightmare, thralls are a huge DPS increase through the entire fight and the mage thrall deals double damage to the pillars. ([2021-07-13 6:57](https://youtu.be/26wPQVuqmNY?t=417))
- A further idea lets the harmonised orb absorb the weighting of the other orbs, making it about 1 in 533 up from 1 in 1,600, and increases the weighting of the nightmare staff from 3 to 4 while raising the chance of hitting the inquisitor/staff loot table by 10%. ([2026-06-04 49:28](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2968))

## Tips

- The Nightmare has extremely high defence against everything except crush, so Gnomonkey's main DPS weapon for the solo is a bludgeon with Bandos and max melee gear. ([2020-10-09 1:07](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=67))
- Gnomonkey says Dragon warhammer is usually not worth bringing in a Nightmare solo, but in a group one person taking a hammer is a very good idea because her defence does not go below 120 and is restored each phase, so only one spec is needed per phase. ([2020-10-09 2:22](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=142))
- Gnomonkey does not bring a teleport to Nightmare because it wastes inventory space; he teleports to Clan Wars after a kill instead. ([2020-10-09 4:27](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=267))
- A free Nightmare world shows The Nightmare slumped on the ground; a world where people have entered but not started shows it floating with a slumped head; an active fight shows it floating with its head to the ceiling. ([2020-10-09 6:34](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=394))
- The symbol under The Nightmare shows the phase being fought: the Oni mask is phase one, the Yin Yang is phase two, and the spiked symbol with three teardrops is the final phase. Gnomonkey looks for the spiked symbol while hopping and right-click inspects The Nightmare to check whether it is a solo, since solo players typically leave after one kill. ([2020-10-09 6:34](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=394))
- It is impossible to enter a Nightmare world once the fight has started, so other players cannot crash your fight. Once you enter, you have 40 seconds before the fight begins, and you can spectate a world to see how close they are to finishing. ([2020-10-09 6:43](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=403))
- In a solo you take what would be the tank role, and Gnomonkey says the easiest approach is to swap back to Protect from Melee after each attack lands, since melee is the hardest attack to catch and the most dangerous. ([2020-10-09 7:33](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=453))
- Gnomonkey recommends immediately sidestepping the grasping claws portals and continuing to attack so as not to waste ticks. ([2020-10-09 8:35](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=515))
- During the totem phase turn boost prayers (including Augury) off completely since they only waste prayer, and try not to heal fully so the Sanguinesti staff can heal you as much as possible. ([2020-10-09 9:06](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=546))
- Gnomonkey specs the sleepwalker with the Saradomin godsword for a massive heal, which is higher with a combat potion, full melee gear and Piety on. Without an SGS he stays in mage gear and attacks the sleepwalker for more time to hit it. ([2020-10-09 9:39](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=579))
- In a group, if Piety gives you enough defence to take the tank role, you can flick it on occasionally to make The Nightmare walk towards you, then turn it off when she gets near to avoid all melee attacks. ([2020-10-09 10:13](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=613))
- A safespot exists below the north pillars: standing just south of the pillar avoids all melee attacks. ([2020-10-09 10:42](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=642))
- Gnomonkey says the easiest way to avoid the flower attack is to stand towards the outside centre of The Nightmare and run to the nearest safe flowers if none spawn under you; you can stand on top of regular flowers. In a group, put the tank on one set of flowers and the rest of the group on the other. ([2020-10-09 11:35](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=695))
- During the totem phase you can hit all four totems from the centre tile, or from a safe quadrant using long-range casting. ([2020-10-09 11:35](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=695))
- Against husks Gnomonkey prays Protect from Magic and immediately attacks them, swapping prayers to match Nightmare's attacks and back; if only fat husks are out he prays ranged. He says it is better to protect against the husks and catch melee than to pray melee and take their attacks at full force. ([2020-10-09 12:08](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=728))
- Do not let husks spawn while you charge the fourth and final totem, because you will be frozen when sleepwalkers spawn; you can still kill them with magic but will miss the Saradomin godsword spec. ([2020-10-09 13:08](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=788))
- The parasite has 40 health and is weak to crush; a claw special almost always one-shots it, otherwise attack it with the bludgeon. Gnomonkey counts three attacks after infection and runs underneath The Nightmare so the parasite spawns under her, where it cannot heal her. ([2020-10-09 14:15](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=855))
- Gnomonkey says he has not found a consistent way to block the parasite, and that grasping claws can hurt you if you stand under her. If the parasite heals her five times it invalidates almost all the damage dealt before it spawned. ([2020-10-09 14:45](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=885))
- The Nightmare almost always uses grasping claws after the five curse attacks, but not always. ([2020-10-09 16:17](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=977))
- While waiting for the truck, Gnomonkey runs to where she will end up so he wastes less time not attacking. ([2020-10-09 16:50](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=1010))
- Watch for grasping claws during mushrooms since she almost always uses a special attack at least once while they are active, and be careful not to touch a mushroom late because being unable to run can coincide with the truck attack. If you cannot avoid them, take the mushrooms and keep attacking. ([2020-10-09 17:18](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=1038))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey considers The Nightmare one of the best bosses in the game for money at the time of the video (2020), saying an alt running supplies plus higher-end gear and maxed combat can make about 12m gp/hr on average. ([2020-10-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk))
- Gnomonkey says The Nightmare has horrible regular drops but incredibly valuable rare drops, comparing fighting it to playing scratch-off lottery tickets. ([2020-10-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk))
- Gnomonkey says The Nightmare is a pretty easy boss in a group, but solo it becomes a roughly 20-minute endurance match. He would not call it hard, but says it is punishing if you mess up. ([2020-10-09 0:32](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=32))
- Gnomonkey names the three phases flower phase (phase one), parasite phase (phase two) and truck phase (phase three), and says phase two is by far the hardest, phase one a close second and phase three by far the easiest. ([2020-10-09 10:49](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=649))
- Gnomonkey says solos are the most profitable way to kill The Nightmare because the rare drop chance is the same from one to five players. ([2020-10-09 17:51](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=1071))
- Gnomonkey says thralls are exceptional at bosses with extremely high defence that cannot be drained, naming the Nightmare and Callisto as great examples. ([2021-07-13 6:26](https://youtu.be/26wPQVuqmNY?t=386))
- Gnomonkey says thralls make the Harmonised nightmare staff not worth using at the regular Nightmare because you have to choose one or the other. ([2021-07-13 6:57](https://youtu.be/26wPQVuqmNY?t=417))
- In Phosani's Nightmare, because the Nightmare has much lower health per phase and more phases with pillars, Gnomonkey says the harmonised staff is just barely better than thralls, with a few seconds faster kills according to the Gear Discord, but thralls are still very useful given the cost barrier. ([2021-07-13 6:57](https://youtu.be/26wPQVuqmNY?t=417))
- Gnomonkey says the Old School team pulled many mechanics for the Great Olm and the Nightmare from Nex's old design. ([2021-11-16 2:08](https://youtu.be/fQySZrGmyOY?t=128))
- Gnomonkey says Nex choosing a tank by range defence for heavy single-target melee hits is very similar to the Nightmare. ([2021-11-16 5:13](https://youtu.be/fQySZrGmyOY?t=313))
- Gnomonkey says many people like the Nightmare pet but he really does not; it takes a lot of hours and is hard to get but is nothing exceptional, so he gives it C tier. ([2022-03-12 1:30](https://youtu.be/vwCm9NKJhRE?t=90))
- Gnomonkey says Phosani's Nightmare has basically replaced the Nightmare as the content people work toward, and calls it a step up from other bosses but still fairly easy. ([2022-05-11 8:02](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=482))
- Gnomonkey rates Nightmare 3/5 for inventory management (tracking specials and supplies and switching to kill parasites, husks and totems), 2/5 for movement (avoiding grasping claws and her melee, and sleepwalkers), and 4/5 for prayer management. ([2022-05-11 8:02](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=482))
- Gnomonkey says Nightmare prayer management requires flicking the appropriate prayer each attack and shuffling prayers with the special attack, and the melee prayer timing can be quite tight, with misses causing extremely high damage. ([2022-05-11 8:34](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=514))
- Gnomonkey is torn on Nightmare ink armor dupe protection: he says ink armor is a good item to trial it on because the pieces are all roughly the same price, but that dupe protection would not work for a drop like Graardor's, where players would leave once the next piece is due to be Bandos boots. ([2024-04-18 8:21](https://youtu.be/P5yHDSocecY?t=501))
- Gnomonkey does not see why the game should be balanced around cloggers and irons, though he likes drops being more common in general and does not think dupe protection would be bad for the game. ([2024-04-18 9:15](https://youtu.be/P5yHDSocecY?t=555))
- Gnomonkey doesn't hate the essence idea but says the essence drops would have to come from endgame content and there is nowhere it fits right now, so it should be done long term as bosses come into the game. ([2026-06-04 48:56](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2936))

## Events

- Jagex proposed dupe protection for the Nightmare's ink armor: after getting one piece such as the legs, the next ink armor drop would be the helmet or chestplate, and after two pieces the next would be the remaining one. ([2024-04-18 8:21](https://youtu.be/P5yHDSocecY?t=501))
- The blog says the Nightmare's loot table feels bloated, with four pieces of Inquisitor, the nightmare staff and three separate orbs, eight unique rewards in total, many of them niche like the orbs. ([2026-06-04 48:24](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2904))
- Ideas floated include moving the volatile and eldritch orbs off the Nightmare table and having the Nightmare drop a generic orb that is charged with a unique essence that drops from other NPCs. ([2026-06-04 48:56](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2936))
- The blog explicitly says the Nightmare changes are intended as long term and would rely on future content, not part of the other changes outlined. ([2026-06-04 49:28](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2968))

## Trivia

- If grasping claws is used while you are frozen by husks she usually does not put a portal under you, though Gnomonkey says it happened anyway twice, and he thinks only the guaranteed portal is skipped. ([2020-10-09 13:08](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=788))
- Gnomonkey says the Nightmare is actually based on 'Nex: Angel of Death', which is basically Nex 2 for RS3. ([2021-11-16 2:41](https://youtu.be/fQySZrGmyOY?t=161))
