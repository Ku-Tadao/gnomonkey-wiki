**Advertising** is an other topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2025-01-16 and 2025-01-17. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey sarcastically describes such ads as, for example, a 30-second ad replacing the Vardorvis capture.[^RkCY5EW5uoc-260]
* Gnomonkey interprets Jagex's wording about ads as meaning ads are coming eventually.[^RkCY5EW5uoc-852]
* Gnomonkey says the survey's mention of ads was one of the things people were upset about and that he does not want ads entering the game.[^cGiq6OwLovw-0]
* Gnomonkey says the only plan of action so far on ads is ads in free-to-play, which he says is not what he would have gone for to rebuild trust.[^cGiq6OwLovw-301]
* Gnomonkey notes in a later segment that Jagex confirmed it will explore ads in free-to-play, and says players answering that as fine because it used to be a browser game does not apply today.[^cGiq6OwLovw-1279]

## Events

* The survey included cheaper membership options with ads included in non-gameplay areas in a non-intrusive manner.[^RkCY5EW5uoc-260]
* In its blog response Jagex said there are no plans to include ads in the base membership or to make ad-supported options a significant part of the game.[^RkCY5EW5uoc-852]
* The CEO's apology said Jagex will not include in-game advertisements in any regular paid membership, but may explore ads in free-to-play in the future, though there are no plans currently.[^cGiq6OwLovw-267]
