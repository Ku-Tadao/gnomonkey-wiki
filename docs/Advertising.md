## Opinions

- Gnomonkey sarcastically describes such ads as, for example, a 30-second ad replacing the Vardorvis capture. ([2025-01-16 4:20](https://youtu.be/RkCY5EW5uoc?t=260))
- Gnomonkey interprets Jagex's wording about ads as meaning ads are coming eventually. ([2025-01-16 14:12](https://youtu.be/RkCY5EW5uoc?t=852))
- Gnomonkey says the survey's mention of ads was one of the things people were upset about and that he does not want ads entering the game. ([2025-01-17 0:00](https://youtu.be/cGiq6OwLovw))
- Gnomonkey says the only plan of action so far on ads is ads in free-to-play, which he says is not what he would have gone for to rebuild trust. ([2025-01-17 5:01](https://youtu.be/cGiq6OwLovw?t=301))
- Gnomonkey notes in a later segment that Jagex confirmed it will explore ads in free-to-play, and says players answering that as fine because it used to be a browser game does not apply today. ([2025-01-17 21:19](https://youtu.be/cGiq6OwLovw?t=1279))

## Events

- The survey included cheaper membership options with ads included in non-gameplay areas in a non-intrusive manner. ([2025-01-16 4:20](https://youtu.be/RkCY5EW5uoc?t=260))
- In its blog response Jagex said there are no plans to include ads in the base membership or to make ad-supported options a significant part of the game. ([2025-01-16 14:12](https://youtu.be/RkCY5EW5uoc?t=852))
- The CEO's apology said Jagex will not include in-game advertisements in any regular paid membership, but may explore ads in free-to-play in the future, though there are no plans currently. ([2025-01-17 4:27](https://youtu.be/cGiq6OwLovw?t=267))
