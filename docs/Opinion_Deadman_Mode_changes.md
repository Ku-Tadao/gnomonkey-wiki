**Opinion: Deadman Mode changes** is an opinion topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2026-06-25. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey wants minimum downtime of eight to ten hours in Deadman Allstars, because six hours of downtime led teams to play the full 18 hours and adrenaline meant it took about two hours to wind down, leaving two to four hours of sleep.[^lJexGv6dxu8-3803]
* Gnomonkey notes other players suggested removing the play cap and downtime entirely so players could go 30+ hour streams early and normal hours later, and says he liked the day off and wants two or three of them.[^lJexGv6dxu8-3831]
* Gnomonkey says every day except the last had two missions and a breach, which was too much because a team wipe costs hours of recovery, and he would have fewer or more spread-out missions.[^lJexGv6dxu8-3863]
* Gnomonkey says players had to stay logged in whenever a breach or mission was happening to avoid losing ground, which made naps very difficult.[^lJexGv6dxu8-3893]
* Gnomonkey would remove the finality sigil next time and prefer normal multi fights, because the sigil guarantees your next special attack lands, making low-accuracy specs like Arcane blades, AGS and javelin spam insanely broken.[^lJexGv6dxu8-3925]
* Gnomonkey liked that most areas were not safe except Varrock, but would have added a small safe zone at the Warriors Guild so tired players could train or chill without being on edge.[^lJexGv6dxu8-3955]
* Gnomonkey suggests rookies or planners such as Foe could be captains instead of the best PvPers, so people with clear insight into the game lead.[^lJexGv6dxu8-3981]
* Gnomonkey says he would do Deadman again, but hopes nobody picks him in the second row again and would be happier in the third or fourth row with lower expectations.[^lJexGv6dxu8-4163]
