# Inferno kill order

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2023-03-30 to 2025-07-13). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

|---|---|
| **Type** | mechanic |

## Stats and numbers

- According to Gnomonkey, melee and ranger NPCs die slowly (the melee is the most dramatic, the ranger slightly less) and the melee bloblet also dies a bit slower, while mages, bats, the other two bloblets and nibblers die almost instantly, so those are good to kill last. ([2025-07-13 8:21](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=501))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says to kill the melee bloblets first in the Inferno because they die a tick slower than the other monsters. ([2023-03-30 4:48](https://youtu.be/1sv6tIkEHjs?t=288))
- Gnomonkey says to avoid killing melees last: melees die very slowly, so leaving a nibbler alive to kill while the melee dies saves time because both die at the same time. ([2023-03-30 5:30](https://youtu.be/1sv6tIkEHjs?t=330))
- Gnomonkey's goal in the Inferno is always to get to the mage first if you can. ([2023-03-30 21:15](https://youtu.be/1sv6tIkEHjs?t=1275))
- Gnomonkey avoids killing blobs before the mage because if the mage respawns a blob, all its bloblets are also respawned, which is a load of health you have to re-deal. ([2023-03-30 22:30](https://youtu.be/1sv6tIkEHjs?t=1350))
- Gnomonkey says killing bats is not a big deal because it does not matter if they respawn, and a melee respawns with only about 40 HP, which is a few pipe shots. ([2023-03-30 22:30](https://youtu.be/1sv6tIkEHjs?t=1350))
- Gnomonkey says avoiding dragging monsters while you kill them is good, because monsters attack slower when moving. ([2023-03-30 26:35](https://youtu.be/1sv6tIkEHjs?t=1595))
- Gnomonkey would rather pick off a melee than a ranger because a ranger respawn is way worse. ([2023-03-30 38:58](https://youtu.be/1sv6tIkEHjs?t=2338))
- A ranger dies slower than a nibbler, and nibblers die really fast, and bats die a little quicker than rangers, so you can kill bats after the ranger to save a little time. ([2023-03-30 39:53](https://youtu.be/1sv6tIkEHjs?t=2393))
- In Inferno speeds all mobs die at different speeds, so kill order matters: melees die super slow, so kill the melee before other things rather than leaving it last. ([2025-07-13 7:41](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=461))
- Gnomonkey says to kill the ranger before the bat when stuck with only those two, because the bat dies faster and starts the next wave sooner. ([2025-07-13 38:10](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=2290))
- Gnomonkey says if the range bloblet is left over and the player is already praying range, leave it and kill the range bloblet after the ranger to save time. ([2025-07-13 39:00](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=2340))
- Gnomonkey says it is always fine to kill bats and melees before the mage, because a melee respawns with only 30 health and can be killed in about two pipe shots, so it is not a big time loss, especially while learning. ([2025-07-13 50:52](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=3052))
- Gnomonkey says never kill a blob before the mage, because the mage respawns the blob at half health with all bloblets at full health, which means an additional 90 damage to deal. ([2025-07-13 50:52](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=3052))
- Gnomonkey says to kill the mage first whenever possible and not to kill a ranger or a blob before the mage, running across the room if needed, because a respawn causes pillar stacks and wastes time. ([2025-07-13 1:08:10](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=4090))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says most people aiming for sub 65 do not manage kill order at all, so tracking it gives a big advantage. ([2025-07-13 39:30](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=2370))
