**Inferno kill order** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2023-03-30 and 2025-07-13. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* According to Gnomonkey, melee and ranger NPCs die slowly (the melee is the most dramatic, the ranger slightly less) and the melee bloblet also dies a bit slower, while mages, bats, the other two bloblets and nibblers die almost instantly, so those are good to kill last.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-501]

## Tips

* To kill the melee bloblets first in the Inferno because they die a tick slower than the other monsters.[^1sv6tIkEHjs-288]
* To avoid killing melees last: melees die very slowly, so leaving a nibbler alive to kill while the melee dies saves time because both die at the same time.[^1sv6tIkEHjs-330]
* Gnomonkey's goal in the Inferno is always to get to the mage first if you can.[^1sv6tIkEHjs-1275]
* Gnomonkey avoids killing blobs before the mage because if the mage respawns a blob, all its bloblets are also respawned, which is a load of health you have to re-deal.[^1sv6tIkEHjs-1350]
* Killing bats is not a big deal because it does not matter if they respawn, and a melee respawns with only about 40 HP, which is a few pipe shots.[^1sv6tIkEHjs-1350]
* Avoiding dragging monsters while you kill them is good, because monsters attack slower when moving.[^1sv6tIkEHjs-1595]
* Gnomonkey would rather pick off a melee than a ranger because a ranger respawn is way worse.[^1sv6tIkEHjs-2338]
* A ranger dies slower than a nibbler, and nibblers die really fast, and bats die a little quicker than rangers, so you can kill bats after the ranger to save a little time.[^1sv6tIkEHjs-2393]
* In Inferno speeds all mobs die at different speeds, so kill order matters: melees die super slow, so kill the melee before other things rather than leaving it last.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-461]
* To kill the ranger before the bat when stuck with only those two, because the bat dies faster and starts the next wave sooner.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-2290]
* If the range bloblet is left over and the player is already praying range, leave it and kill the range bloblet after the ranger to save time.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-2340]
* It is always fine to kill bats and melees before the mage, because a melee respawns with only 30 health and can be killed in about two pipe shots, so it is not a big time loss, especially while learning.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-3052]
* Never kill a blob before the mage, because the mage respawns the blob at half health with all bloblets at full health, which means an additional 90 damage to deal.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-3052]
* To kill the mage first whenever possible and not to kill a ranger or a blob before the mage, running across the room if needed, because a respawn causes pillar stacks and wastes time.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-4090]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says most people aiming for sub 65 do not manage kill order at all, so tracking it gives a big advantage.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-2370]
