**Gridlock Gambit** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2025-06-05. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* The ragging meteors cannot rag the glyphs and instead rag a random tile outside the glyph, so if you can do the entire fight inside a glyph, ragging is not a problem.[^n5pteeFPlqc-110]
* Meteors come in a vertical and horizontal variation rotating clockwise, and there are three wave patterns, one of which forms an axe outside of the glyph; waves can also scuff and hit a tick early at random, giving technically 24 variations of patterns to dodge.[^n5pteeFPlqc-120]
* If you always drop a meteor on the centre tile of the glyph, it is always possible to dodge the meteors within the glyph; the rare exception is a vertical meteor spawn with the giga waves, where you step out of the grid for one tick and come back in, which works because Yama does not drop meteors at that time.[^n5pteeFPlqc-144]
* Ways to die on an Oathplate contract per Gnomonkey: dropping a meteor on a non-centre tile; red X dragging to Yama; tanking a wave (it drops your prayer and you get hit the same tick); yellow-clicking past a void flare; not clicking a void flare; or forgetting which glyph to go to early.[^n5pteeFPlqc-185]

## Stats and numbers

* Tanking an off-prayer hit on an Oathplate contract costs about half your food, according to Gnomonkey.[^n5pteeFPlqc-185]

## Tips

* When Yama drops meteors right after the non-wave void flares, you must dodge in the opposite direction to avoid scuffed waves, making muscle memory harder.[^n5pteeFPlqc-144]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the solves for all variations have different timings that must be memorised and perfected, and calls this some of the hardest and by far the riskiest content he has ever done in the game.[^n5pteeFPlqc-176]

## Trivia

* The Gridlock Gambit was devised by Saxer Pillar and Gnomonkey: Sax found the base dodging pattern and Gnomonkey labbed out most of the cells within the grid.[^n5pteeFPlqc-120]
