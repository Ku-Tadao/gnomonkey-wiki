**Grand Exchange** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2020-07-21 and 2025-05-29. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Items under 50 gp are still not taxed and the Grand Exchange tax cap of 5 million per item remains in place for high-value trades, so it is not 10 million.[^9ugDmmDILoI-731]

## Tips

* Gnomonkey enables actively traded prices from OSBuddy in the RuneLite Grand Exchange plugin, so the Grand Exchange shows the buying price from OSBuddy, and also shows exact total value.[^9neaqoxR-nY-1755]
* A fuzzy fallback search option in the Grand Exchange plugin shows items even if you misspell the name by a character, which Gnomonkey says is super handy.[^9neaqoxR-nY-1755]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says that once Yama contracts are on the Grand Exchange and in players' banks, removing or radically changing the tradable system would require ripping out a huge chunk of content.[^Y4S7mK8qoJo-2263]

## Events

* When asked what to buy or sell for the update, Gnomonkey hinted that a boss some people consider a chicken will be quite important, without elaborating.[^Y4S7mK8qoJo-646]
* As of the late-May 2025 update, the Grand Exchange tax rate was increased from 1% to 2%, doubling it.[^9ugDmmDILoI-731]
* Offers existing at the time of the update stay taxed at 1% or untaxed; only newly created offers after the update use the 2% rate.[^9ugDmmDILoI-731]
* Low-value and early-game utility items such as low-tier food, energy potions, teleport tablets, early-game ammo and mind runes were made fully exempt from Grand Exchange tax.[^9ugDmmDILoI-895]

## Trivia

* Jagex's stated reason for the change is that GP entering the game has steadily increased while money sinks have not kept pace, so the tax increase is meant to curb inflation.[^9ugDmmDILoI-691]
* Jagex explains that most Grand Exchange tax is deleted from the game to remove GP, while a small slice is used by the game to buy certain items from players and permanently delete them (the item sink system).[^9ugDmmDILoI-763]
