# Grand Exchange

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2020-07-21 to 2025-05-29). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | mechanic |
| **Videos** | 3 (first 2020-07-21, latest 2025-05-29) |

## Stats and numbers

- Items under 50 gp are still not taxed and the Grand Exchange tax cap of 5 million per item remains in place for high-value trades, so it is not 10 million. ([2025-05-29 12:11](https://youtu.be/9ugDmmDILoI?t=731))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey enables actively traded prices from OSBuddy in the RuneLite Grand Exchange plugin, so the Grand Exchange shows the buying price from OSBuddy, and also shows exact total value. ([2020-07-21 29:15](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1755))
- A fuzzy fallback search option in the Grand Exchange plugin shows items even if you misspell the name by a character, which Gnomonkey says is super handy. ([2020-07-21 29:15](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=1755))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says that once Yama contracts are on the Grand Exchange and in players' banks, removing or radically changing the tradable system would require ripping out a huge chunk of content. ([2025-05-13 37:43](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=2263))

## Events

- When asked what to buy or sell for the update, Gnomonkey hinted that a boss some people consider a chicken will be quite important, without elaborating. ([2025-05-13 10:46](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=646))
- As of the late-May 2025 update, the Grand Exchange tax rate was increased from 1% to 2%, doubling it. ([2025-05-29 12:11](https://youtu.be/9ugDmmDILoI?t=731))
- Offers existing at the time of the update stay taxed at 1% or untaxed; only newly created offers after the update use the 2% rate. ([2025-05-29 12:11](https://youtu.be/9ugDmmDILoI?t=731))
- Low-value and early-game utility items such as low-tier food, energy potions, teleport tablets, early-game ammo and mind runes were made fully exempt from Grand Exchange tax. ([2025-05-29 14:55](https://youtu.be/9ugDmmDILoI?t=895))

## Trivia

- Jagex's stated reason for the change is that GP entering the game has steadily increased while money sinks have not kept pace, so the tax increase is meant to curb inflation. ([2025-05-29 11:31](https://youtu.be/9ugDmmDILoI?t=691))
- Jagex explains that most Grand Exchange tax is deleted from the game to remove GP, while a small slice is used by the game to buy certain items from players and permanently delete them (the item sink system). ([2025-05-29 12:43](https://youtu.be/9ugDmmDILoI?t=763))
