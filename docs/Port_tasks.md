**Port tasks** is an activity topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2025-12-03 and 2025-12-04. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Gnomonkey recalls port tasks giving around 130k XP per hour.[^6BvUKVInPTs-149]
* Jagex's table lists port tasks at 105% of pre-adjustment XP, but Gnomonkey disagrees, saying once you have the extractor they are about 95% of what they were at higher levels.[^OeDcRft1_cU-394]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says he dislikes port tasks as a training method.[^6BvUKVInPTs-126]
* Gnomonkey says that because the extractor nerf cut around 20k XP per hour from all Sailing methods, the port task buff is a slight net nerf for players who already had the extractor built, though levels 40 up to the extractor level were buffed.[^6BvUKVInPTs-149]
* Gnomonkey thinks Jagex could have buffed port tasks and fleshed them out to be more interesting.[^6BvUKVInPTs-995]
* Gnomonkey thinks port tasks need to be fleshed out rather than just given XP changes, saying there is a lot of untapped potential.[^OeDcRft1_cU-394]
* Gnomonkey says port tasks are very basic (click from point A to point B, maybe trim sails or use charting to unlock rapids) and would feel much better with more things to do for XP without needing higher intensity.[^OeDcRft1_cU-613]
* Gnomonkey says he has given Jagex mods ideas for fleshing out Sailing training methods but does not know if they will be used.[^OeDcRft1_cU-613]

## Events

* The Sailing week 2 update increased completion XP of all courier port tasks from level 40 and above by about 15%, aimed at levels 40-55 and 60-70 and at players in the 90s and beyond 99 working on Rosewood boat upgrades.[^6BvUKVInPTs-126]
