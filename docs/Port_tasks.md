# Port tasks

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2025-12-03 to 2025-12-04). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | activity |
| **Videos** | 2 (first 2025-12-03, latest 2025-12-04) |

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey recalls port tasks giving around 130k XP per hour. ([2025-12-03 2:29](https://youtu.be/6BvUKVInPTs?t=149))
- Jagex's table lists port tasks at 105% of pre-adjustment XP, but Gnomonkey disagrees, saying once you have the extractor they are about 95% of what they were at higher levels. ([2025-12-04 6:34](https://youtu.be/OeDcRft1_cU?t=394))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says he dislikes port tasks as a training method. ([2025-12-03 2:06](https://youtu.be/6BvUKVInPTs?t=126))
- Gnomonkey says that because the extractor nerf cut around 20k XP per hour from all Sailing methods, the port task buff is a slight net nerf for players who already had the extractor built, though levels 40 up to the extractor level were buffed. ([2025-12-03 2:29](https://youtu.be/6BvUKVInPTs?t=149))
- Gnomonkey thinks Jagex could have buffed port tasks and fleshed them out to be more interesting. ([2025-12-03 16:35](https://youtu.be/6BvUKVInPTs?t=995))
- Gnomonkey thinks port tasks need to be fleshed out rather than just given XP changes, saying there is a lot of untapped potential. ([2025-12-04 6:34](https://youtu.be/OeDcRft1_cU?t=394))
- Gnomonkey says port tasks are very basic (click from point A to point B, maybe trim sails or use charting to unlock rapids) and would feel much better with more things to do for XP without needing higher intensity. ([2025-12-04 10:13](https://youtu.be/OeDcRft1_cU?t=613))
- Gnomonkey says he has given Jagex mods ideas for fleshing out Sailing training methods but does not know if they will be used. ([2025-12-04 10:13](https://youtu.be/OeDcRft1_cU?t=613))

## Events

- The Sailing week 2 update increased completion XP of all courier port tasks from level 40 and above by about 15%, aimed at levels 40-55 and 60-70 and at players in the 90s and beyond 99 working on Rosewood boat upgrades. ([2025-12-03 2:06](https://youtu.be/6BvUKVInPTs?t=126))
