# Barrows gloves

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2020-06-27 to 2020-07-11). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | item |
| **Videos** | 2 (first 2020-06-27, latest 2020-07-11) |

## Requirements

- Barrows gloves come from the Recipe for Disaster quest and are not best in slot for magic; the Tormented bracelet is. ([2020-07-11 3:23](https://youtu.be/6QEUIk_5nRU?t=203))

## Stats and numbers

- Barrows gloves are not a huge upgrade over blessed d'hide vambraces for Ranged, offering only +1 ranged attack and some defensive stats. ([2020-06-27 4:12](https://youtu.be/bIShDvVDueM?t=252))
- Barrows gloves give +6 magic attack versus a combat bracelet's +3, so they do not help magic much. ([2020-07-11 3:49](https://youtu.be/6QEUIk_5nRU?t=229))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says Barrows gloves are actually the best in slot for Ranged, and require completing Recipe for Disaster, a quest with pretty high requirements. ([2020-06-27 3:40](https://youtu.be/bIShDvVDueM?t=220))
- Gnomonkey says Barrows gloves are great for all setups and that he would rush them on any account he makes. ([2020-06-27 4:12](https://youtu.be/bIShDvVDueM?t=252))
- Gnomonkey recommends getting Barrows gloves fairly quickly anyway because they are incredibly good for other combat styles and bridge the gap until you can afford the Tormented bracelet. ([2020-07-11 3:49](https://youtu.be/6QEUIk_5nRU?t=229))
