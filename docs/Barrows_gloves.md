**Barrows gloves** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2020-06-27 and 2020-07-11. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* Barrows gloves come from the Recipe for Disaster quest and are not best in slot for magic; the Tormented bracelet is.[^6QEUIk_5nRU-203]

## Stats and numbers

* Barrows gloves are not a huge upgrade over blessed d'hide vambraces for Ranged, offering only +1 ranged attack and some defensive stats.[^bIShDvVDueM-252]
* Barrows gloves give +6 magic attack versus a combat bracelet's +3, so they do not help magic much.[^6QEUIk_5nRU-229]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says Barrows gloves are actually the best in slot for Ranged, and require completing Recipe for Disaster, a quest with pretty high requirements.[^bIShDvVDueM-220]
* Gnomonkey says Barrows gloves are great for all setups and that he would rush them on any account he makes.[^bIShDvVDueM-252]
* Gnomonkey recommends getting Barrows gloves fairly quickly anyway because they are incredibly good for other combat styles and bridge the gap until you can afford the Tormented bracelet.[^6QEUIk_5nRU-229]
