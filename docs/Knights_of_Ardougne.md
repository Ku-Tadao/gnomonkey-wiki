**Knights of Ardougne** is an activity topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2020-06-23. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* One recommended Knights spot is the bank just west of the market; hop into the Thieving Host clan chat to find a world with a 'splasher', who are always active.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-297]
* Gnomonkey prefers the building just northeast of the market, where a specific Knight can be speared into the building so it can only wander in its southern two tiles; pull the knight south of the window right of the door, spear him north, then shut the door behind him.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-327]
* Inventory flicker method: line up your inventory in a specific orientation, rebind the F key for the inventory in RuneLite's Key Remapping plugin (or hold F) so it flickers, leave the top-left inventory slot empty and spam left click, ideally with Mouse Keys numpad 5 or a key rebound to left click.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-385]

## Stats and numbers

* NPCs normally move after standing in one spot for 300 seconds, but this timer does not apply while an NPC is in combat, so you can fix your mouse and camera in a specific orientation and keep thieving without moving.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-297]

## Tips

* Ardougne Knights can be easily trapped and repeatedly clicked on without moving your mouse, and the left-click option can be set to pickpocket by setting the NPC attack options to hidden.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-266]
* The bank spot is two tiles from the bank for withdrawing more food and dodgy necklaces.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-297]
* Gnomonkey usually just hops worlds to find a spot where someone has already lured the Knight into the building; the Knight stays stuck there until killed by someone.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-356]
* With this method your character walks between the tiles as the Knight moves, so the mouse only needs to move for healing and equipping dodgy necklaces.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-385]
* You eventually have to bank occasionally for more necklaces and food, unless above level 95, but the house spot is much more relaxed than the crowded bank spot.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-413]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* The downside of the bank spot is that the right-click option messes up when enough people fail at once, so it becomes annoying when packed, and the inventory flicker method does not work because you cannot line up the inventory with your mouse.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-327]
