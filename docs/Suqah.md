## Requirements

* Suqah have no requirements to fight, but to be assigned them the player needs to have progressed Lunar Diplomacy up to getting to the island.[^SKbk_MQ5L40-0]

## Methods

* The fastest way to the Suqah is a lunar teleport portal at a player-owned house; Gnomonkey instead uses Fremennik sea boots and takes the boat to Lunar Isle, and a lyre or Relleka house teleport also work.[^SKbk_MQ5L40-68]
* On arrival Gnomonkey drops his cannon on a specific tile, drinks his divine super combat, and turns on piety and protect from magic.[^SKbk_MQ5L40-86]

## Stats and numbers

* Suqah give a clean 60k Slayer XP an hour, but their only drops are from the rare drop table.[^SKbk_MQ5L40-0]
* Suqah tasks from Duradel are incredibly short, each taking about ten minutes, and have a weight of eight, so they come up fairly often.[^SKbk_MQ5L40-0]
* Gnomonkey got 60k Slayer XP an hour on Suqah, which he calls incredibly good compared with most cannon tasks, but the loot was two rubies, about 6k gp.[^SKbk_MQ5L40-113]

## Tips

* Suqah can smack you around quite a bit, so you may need more food with worse defensive gear.[^SKbk_MQ5L40-27]
* Suqah randomly use magic attacks that do little damage but freeze the player, which is annoying, so Gnomonkey protects from magic.[^SKbk_MQ5L40-86]
* Gnomonkey recommends grabbing some Suqah teeth on a first task, as they are used to protect spirit trees from disease.[^SKbk_MQ5L40-86]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says you do not do Suqah tasks for the loot but the incredible XP easily makes up for it.[^SKbk_MQ5L40-113]

## Records

* A task of 67 Suqah took Gnomonkey eight minutes, which he says is definitely one of the shorter tasks in the game.[^SKbk_MQ5L40-113]
