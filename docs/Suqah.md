# Suqah

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 1 video (2020-05-01). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- The fastest way to the Suqah is a lunar teleport portal at a player-owned house; Gnomonkey instead uses Fremennik sea boots and takes the boat to Lunar Isle, and a lyre or Relleka house teleport also work. ([2020-05-01 1:08](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40?t=68))
- On arrival Gnomonkey drops his cannon on a specific tile, drinks his divine super combat, and turns on piety and protect from magic. ([2020-05-01 1:26](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40?t=86))

## Requirements

- Suqah have no requirements to fight, but to be assigned them the player needs to have progressed Lunar Diplomacy up to getting to the island. ([2020-05-01 0:00](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says Suqah give a clean 60k Slayer XP an hour, but their only drops are from the rare drop table. ([2020-05-01 0:00](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40))
- Suqah tasks from Duradel are incredibly short, each taking about ten minutes, and have a weight of eight, so they come up fairly often. ([2020-05-01 0:00](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40))
- Gnomonkey got 60k Slayer XP an hour on Suqah, which he calls incredibly good compared with most cannon tasks, but the loot was two rubies, about 6k gp. ([2020-05-01 1:53](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40?t=113))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says Suqah can smack you around quite a bit, so you may need more food with worse defensive gear. ([2020-05-01 0:27](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40?t=27))
- Suqah randomly use magic attacks that do little damage but freeze the player, which is annoying, so Gnomonkey protects from magic. ([2020-05-01 1:26](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40?t=86))
- Gnomonkey recommends grabbing some Suqah teeth on a first task, as they are used to protect spirit trees from disease. ([2020-05-01 1:26](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40?t=86))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says you do not do Suqah tasks for the loot but the incredible XP easily makes up for it. ([2020-05-01 1:53](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40?t=113))

## Records

- A task of 67 Suqah took Gnomonkey eight minutes, which he says is definitely one of the shorter tasks in the game. ([2020-05-01 1:53](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40?t=113))
