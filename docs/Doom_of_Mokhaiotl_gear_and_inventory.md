# Doom of Mokhaiotl: gear and inventory

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2025-07-23 to 2025-07-31). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Gnomonkey's Doom of Mokhaiotl setup is max ranged: Twisted bow with Masori armour and a Fulmasori quiver, which he calls the typical max setup and the best known gear at the time. ([2025-07-23 0:56](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=56))
- Gnomonkey uses dragon arrows (p++) with the Twisted bow at the Doom of Mokhaiotl because the boss can be poisoned and they deal 4 poison damage as free extra DPS; they cost almost 3k each but he considers the premium worth it given the boss's loot. ([2025-07-23 2:27](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=147))
- Gnomonkey brings both a Ralos and a Zaryte crossbow for specs at the Doom of Mokhaiotl; the Zaryte crossbow spec is better on average, but the Ralos lets you fit in more specs. ([2025-07-23 3:30](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=210))
- In later caves Gnomonkey opens with a Ralos spec and uses the Zaryte crossbow later in the phase, because a Zaryte crossbow opener can waste spec energy when the phase takes long; this still lands about 80 to 90 damage. ([2025-07-23 3:30](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=210))
- Gnomonkey's second best Doom of Mokhaiotl setup is a Scorching bow in Void rather than Masori, because the Scorching bow has built-in accuracy and Void gives it four extra max hits; without a Twisted bow you do not need Masori, so this is cheaper and better. ([2025-07-23 6:24](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=384))

## Requirements

- Gnomonkey says double Death Charge is insanely important at the Doom of Mokhaiotl and not worth attempting the content without it; he says there is no better place in the game for double Death Charge, better than Kephri or Tombs of Amascut, and suggests getting it from Yama first. ([2025-07-23 4:32](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=272))
- A Scorching bow is mandatory for Gnomonkey's Doom of Mokhaiotl setup because it is the only bow that is demonbane; you need three types of demonbane, and what matters is having them, not how strong they are. ([2025-07-23 4:58](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=298))
- A Scorching bow is the only truly required item at the Doom of Mokhaiotl according to Gnomonkey, because you need a ranged demonbane weapon; mage demonbane is covered by Dark demonbane cast from any staff (a Skull sceptre or Purging staff works equally) and any melee demonbane weapon such as Arclight, Darklight or even Silverlight can one-shot the grubs. ([2025-07-25 23:03](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=1383))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey first ran Scythe of Vitur with Ink at the Doom of Mokhaiotl because it has better DPS than the Twisted bow, but abandoned it because the boss melees through prayer too hard. ([2025-07-23 1:26](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=86))
- At the Doom of Mokhaiotl the Twisted bow is within about 5% of the DPS of the Scythe with Ink, per Gnomonkey. ([2025-07-23 1:58](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=118))
- In Gearscape (which had added the Doom of Mokhaiotl at the time) the Twisted bow setup is about 11.55 DPS, closer to 12 once free poison damage is included, which Gearscape does not model. ([2025-07-23 5:54](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=354))
- Gnomonkey says a Bow of Faerdhinen setup is about 26% worse than the Twisted bow at the Doom of Mokhaiotl and he would not do the content with it; a Scorching bow setup is about 13% worse than the Twisted bow. ([2025-07-23 6:53](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=413))
- The Ember light spec is a stab spec with only 44% accuracy against the Doom of Mokhaiotl and an average drain of 6 that does not even drain the full amount, so Gnomonkey says it is unusable. ([2025-07-23 35:30](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=2130))
- With max ranged you max 81 with Masori and 86 with Void (five extra max hits) at the Doom of Mokhaiotl, but Void drops about 9% accuracy; Void is better in 100% accurate phases like the car and shield phases. ([2025-07-25 3:33](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=213))
- Gnomonkey says Masori blocks about 10% more mage damage and 17% more ranged damage than Void at the Doom of Mokhaiotl, with little difference for melee, and an off-prayer hit can be up to about 60 damage. ([2025-07-25 4:41](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=281))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey brings a Buckler at the Doom of Mokhaiotl solely to increase the Zaryte crossbow's accuracy; wearing the Book of the Dead instead for an extra inventory slot (such as another brew) is also fine. ([2025-07-23 2:56](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=176))
- At the Doom of Mokhaiotl there are many adds that can be one-shot and constantly give spec back, and Gnomonkey says everything in the fight, including the grubs and the boss's corpse after each cave, gives Death Charge, so he spams the spell constantly. ([2025-07-23 4:32](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=272))
- For the other demonbane sources at the Doom of Mokhaiotl, a purging staff is not required since any Slayer staff casting a demonbane spell is the same, and an Arclight is fine in place of an Ember light, which only does slightly more damage in a couple of parts of the fight. ([2025-07-23 5:27](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=327))
- Gnomonkey says the melee demonbane weapon's job at the Doom of Mokhaiotl is mainly to one-shot the adds (grubs), which an Arclight does just as well as an Ember light. ([2025-07-23 5:27](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=327))
- At the Doom of Mokhaiotl the Elder maul is fairly close to the Ralos because it applies a similar defence drain; the Warhammer is also decent but its accuracy is lower (about 73% against 83% in his check). ([2025-07-23 34:35](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=2075))
- Gnomonkey says that without a Twisted bow you should use a Scorching bow, which is about 14% worse than the Twisted bow but usable for kills. ([2025-07-25 22:30](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=1350))
- Gnomonkey says burning claws are slightly demonbane and also one-shot the grubs. ([2025-07-25 23:29](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=1409))
- For Iron Men without spec weapons, Gnomonkey says you can bring Desert Treasure 2 rings, but pushing past about cave 4 is much harder without a Zaryte crossbow or Dragon knives; at the least bring a Warhammer or Elder maul, which still drain defence with about 80% accuracy in full ranged gear. ([2025-07-25 24:25](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=1465))
- Gnomonkey says Void is extremely good with a Scorching bow at the Doom of Mokhaiotl, giving four extra max hits with no Masori versus Void question since the Scorching bow is fairly accurate. ([2025-07-25 25:47](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=1547))
- If you do not have a Twisted bow, such as using a Scorching bow, the Blowpipe is your best option at the Doom of Mokhaiotl because it is about 5% worse than a Twisted bow, and the rock block tech lets you blowpipe almost the whole time. ([2025-07-31 9:04](https://youtu.be/draz0HZBq00?t=544))
- Without a Zaryte crossbow you can use Blowpipe specs, which are 100% accurate in car phase and give health back; after the second shockwave put your Scorching bow back on and run, and keep piping if a rock is close to the boss. ([2025-07-31 9:31](https://youtu.be/draz0HZBq00?t=571))
- In the video description Gnomonkey gives a spec order of Zaryte crossbow, Chally punish, Blowpipe spec, and for the car phase Zaryte crossbow, Dragon knife, Blowpipe spec; he says a Dragon knife in the car phase surpasses a Zaryte crossbow when the boss is below 350 HP. ([2025-07-31 22:30](https://youtu.be/draz0HZBq00?t=1350))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey does not wear a Venator ring at the Doom of Mokhaiotl because it does not grant a max hit, and considers the Lightbearer far better. ([2025-07-23 1:58](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=118))
- Gnomonkey says there is so much downtime in the Doom of Mokhaiotl fight that a Lightbearer returns spec energy often, so he thinks you very likely want to camp the Lightbearer. ([2025-07-23 2:27](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=147))
- Gnomonkey says clears are possible with a Scorching bow, but good DPS is very important for killing the Doom of Mokhaiotl in time and low damage makes the fight even harder. ([2025-07-23 7:26](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=446))
- Gnomonkey ranks Doom of Mokhaiotl specs when in ranged gear as Zaryte crossbow best, Ralos second, Elder maul third and Warhammer fourth, with everything after that very poor. ([2025-07-23 35:30](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=2130))
- Gnomonkey speculates that if players eventually melee the Doom of Mokhaiotl, Voidwaker may become the best spec, but he has no idea whether melee will be viable. ([2025-07-23 35:30](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=2130))
- Gnomonkey is unsure whether Ward of Arceus works against all Doom of Mokhaiotl attacks or only autos; if it works on everything it would be great and may be worth a slot (with nature runes) over one brew, but he had not tested it. ([2025-07-23 36:03](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=2163))
- Gnomonkey does not bring a surge potion to the Doom of Mokhaiotl because the fight lasts only about 10 minutes through cave 8, giving at most three doses, and a brew is more valuable for the slot; he might change this once the fight is mastered. ([2025-07-23 36:03](https://youtu.be/DUL0hiFwEfk?t=2163))
- Gnomonkey ends up favouring Masori over Void for the Doom of Mokhaiotl because the boss's protection prayers can be partly blocked by armour like at the Leviathan, so off-prayer hits can be reduced; he says Masori is roughly 12% better overall. ([2025-07-25 4:07](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=247))
- Gnomonkey notes Void gives more burst damage in the car phase and is why many players bring it, but it can low-roll and its low accuracy can leave you missing and facing the orb phase; Masori's accuracy is better for Zaryte crossbow and Dragon knife specs outside 100% accurate phases. ([2025-07-25 5:11](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=311))
- Gnomonkey advises against a Serpentine helm at the Doom of Mokhaiotl: it provides poison and venom immunity but has a lot of negative ranged accuracy, so he suggests Sanfew serum or one anti-venom potion instead. ([2025-07-25 24:53](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=1493))
- Gnomonkey says not to use a Bow of Faerdhinen at the Doom of Mokhaiotl and to get a Scorching bow instead; a Bofa is about 15% worse than the Scorching bow, which is about 15% worse than the Twisted bow, so the penalties compound. ([2025-07-25 25:20](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=1520))
- Gnomonkey says Dark light is superior to Ember light when stalling the shield phase because it is so weak (about 8 strength bonus) that your damage is irrelevant and you are just damaging the boss with grubs, and it is cheaper in death costs. ([2025-07-31 22:03](https://youtu.be/draz0HZBq00?t=1323))
- In the video description Gnomonkey says he is back to Void because he needs to get better at zero-damage prayer flicking and out-DPSing the car phase for late delves, though Masori is still great. ([2025-07-31 23:29](https://youtu.be/draz0HZBq00?t=1409))
