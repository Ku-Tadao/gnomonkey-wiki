**Demonic gorilla** is an activity topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2020-09-19 and 2026-06-04. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* With really good stats and gear it is possible to hit 80 demonic gorilla kills an hour, which Gnomonkey says is 4.8m GP an hour with average Zenyte drops.[^4DvqehbIFnQ-430]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey finds demonic gorillas incredibly intensive and annoying, so he would rather not do them even for that kind of money.[^4DvqehbIFnQ-430]

## Events

* A minimum number of player attacks is added before demonic gorillas can change overhead prayers, to prevent constant back-to-back swaps.[^6tsf-brc-sE-1118]
