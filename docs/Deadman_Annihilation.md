**Deadman: Annihilation** is an opinion topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2026-02-04. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* Entering raids in Deadman: Annihilation requires at least 800k risk, but Gnomonkey says players gamed this with inflated GE-value items so it served no purpose.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-688]

## Methods

* The starter sigil (for example melee, magic or ranged) is picked by clicking the skull in the equipment tab, rather than a menu popping up on login like in Leagues; Gnomonkey's chat repeatedly asked how to pick it.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-571]
* The starter crate (tuna, basic starter weapons, and a chronicle to teleport) can be claimed once per day from the Combat Tutor in Lumbridge, or you get it back if you die a PvP death.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-659]
* In Deadman: Annihilation the peek option is disabled on bosses that have one, and you cannot bank items worth over 10k while in a fight, which Gnomonkey learned when overload clicks at a bank did nothing.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-694]

## Stats and numbers

* Gnomonkey observed that Deadman: Annihilation worlds were not as busy as Leagues worlds: a Leagues world would have over a thousand players, while some Deadman worlds were dropping below 200 at almost midnight his time.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-233]

## Tips

* Gnomonkey's Deadman: Annihilation PvP approach was killing skillers doing chinchompas, players thieving wealthy citizens, and players at the Agility Pyramid, targeting people carrying money who would not fight back much.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-175]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says Deadman: Annihilation was casualised by removing things like looting other players' banks, and Jagex marketed it as doable even if you do not do PvP.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-0]
* Gnomonkey did not feel clans were a big problem in multi-combat areas; he was killed only once or twice at Scurrius when a clan swept through, and mostly players in multi areas left each other alone.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-146]
* Gnomonkey says he started to have much more fun in Deadman: Annihilation when he stopped focusing on points and the grind and just killed people for money.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-204]
* Gnomonkey says Deadman: Annihilation streams were not really popping off and the mode did not grab players the way Leagues does.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-233]
* Gnomonkey says Deadman: Annihilation was his first Deadman, and that veterans may find new-player friction less of an issue but it is important if Jagex wants a more casual mode.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-539]
* Gnomonkey says Jagex failed at the easiest step of making Deadman friendly to new players, which is making things obvious, and wants an NPC like the Leagues Sage as a hub for game-mode items such as the bone crossbow and lost gear.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-774]
* Gnomonkey considers Deadman: Annihilation a 'meh' game mode that is not capturing people because Jagex tried to Leagues-ify it.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-1090]
* Gnomonkey is worried that releasing a temporary game mode in the same year as Leagues may leave players burnt out on temporary game modes.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-1330]
* Gnomonkey hopes future Deadman modes drop the jack-of-all-trades approach and focus on PvP, while keeping bank keys removed and making things more obvious.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-1330]

## Events

* Jagex recently released Deadman: Annihilation, a temporary game mode encouraging PvP with a Leagues-like feel.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-0]

## Trivia

* Gnomonkey describes himself as a PvMer and extreme PvP novice who used Deadman: Annihilation to practise for All-Stars, making him the target demographic for the casualised mode.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-31]
* Bone crossbows are sold in the Varrock Archery Shop, which Gnomonkey says new players cannot discover without a wiki.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-602]
* A chest at the Grand Exchange lets players turn in emblems from breaches for points to buy items such as potions, a granite maul handle and a ring pouch.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-630]
