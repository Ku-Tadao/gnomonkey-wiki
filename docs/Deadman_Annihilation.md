## Methods

- The starter sigil (for example melee, magic or ranged) is picked by clicking the skull in the equipment tab, rather than a menu popping up on login like in Leagues; Gnomonkey's chat repeatedly asked how to pick it. ([2026-02-04 9:31](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=571))
- The starter crate (tuna, basic starter weapons, and a chronicle to teleport) can be claimed once per day from the Combat Tutor in Lumbridge, or you get it back if you die a PvP death. ([2026-02-04 10:59](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=659))
- In Deadman: Annihilation the peek option is disabled on bosses that have one, and you cannot bank items worth over 10k while in a fight, which Gnomonkey learned when overload clicks at a bank did nothing. ([2026-02-04 11:34](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=694))

## Requirements

- Entering raids in Deadman: Annihilation requires at least 800k risk, but Gnomonkey says players gamed this with inflated GE-value items so it served no purpose. ([2026-02-04 11:28](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=688))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey observed that Deadman: Annihilation worlds were not as busy as Leagues worlds: a Leagues world would have over a thousand players, while some Deadman worlds were dropping below 200 at almost midnight his time. ([2026-02-04 3:53](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=233))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey's Deadman: Annihilation PvP approach was killing skillers doing chinchompas, players thieving wealthy citizens, and players at the Agility Pyramid, targeting people carrying money who would not fight back much. ([2026-02-04 2:55](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=175))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says Deadman: Annihilation was casualised by removing things like looting other players' banks, and Jagex marketed it as doable even if you do not do PvP. ([2026-02-04 0:00](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI))
- Gnomonkey did not feel clans were a big problem in multi-combat areas; he was killed only once or twice at Scurrius when a clan swept through, and mostly players in multi areas left each other alone. ([2026-02-04 2:26](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=146))
- Gnomonkey says he started to have much more fun in Deadman: Annihilation when he stopped focusing on points and the grind and just killed people for money. ([2026-02-04 3:24](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=204))
- Gnomonkey says Deadman: Annihilation streams were not really popping off and the mode did not grab players the way Leagues does. ([2026-02-04 3:53](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=233))
- Gnomonkey says Deadman: Annihilation was his first Deadman, and that veterans may find new-player friction less of an issue but it is important if Jagex wants a more casual mode. ([2026-02-04 8:59](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=539))
- Gnomonkey says Jagex failed at the easiest step of making Deadman friendly to new players, which is making things obvious, and wants an NPC like the Leagues Sage as a hub for game-mode items such as the bone crossbow and lost gear. ([2026-02-04 12:54](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=774))
- Gnomonkey considers Deadman: Annihilation a 'meh' game mode that is not capturing people because Jagex tried to Leagues-ify it. ([2026-02-04 18:10](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=1090))
- Gnomonkey is worried that releasing a temporary game mode in the same year as Leagues may leave players burnt out on temporary game modes. ([2026-02-04 22:10](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=1330))
- Gnomonkey hopes future Deadman modes drop the jack-of-all-trades approach and focus on PvP, while keeping bank keys removed and making things more obvious. ([2026-02-04 22:10](https://youtu.be/6VJYbPtkvfI?t=1330))

## Events
