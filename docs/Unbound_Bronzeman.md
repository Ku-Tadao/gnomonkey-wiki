**Unbound Bronzeman** is an account topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2026-08-12 and 2026-08-21. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey really dislikes Unbound Bronzeman, saying that previous creator series with the idea always ran into accusations that viewers donated gear, so an official mode would have no stakes and an Unbound Bronzeman is effectively just a main.[^FrZfpY0uoko-218]
* Gnomonkey says Unbound Bronzeman would only have integrity as its own server with separate worlds, but that would invite swapping and probably lack the player base to populate worlds; he doubts it would last.[^FrZfpY0uoko-282]
* Gnomonkey says bundling Unbound Bronzeman, the PvP variant that was the original purpose of the plugin, with regular Bronzeman in one brief paragraph with no explanation of integrity or item drops to other accounts was a mistake.[^8hDdqKnk2cw-152]
* Gnomonkey finds it unfair to the PvP community that Jagex dropped Unbound Bronzeman from the poll but kept regular Bronzeman.[^8hDdqKnk2cw-232]
* In the survey, Gnomonkey would vote for an unranked Unbound Bronzeman with no highscores purely for fun, and says people who argued it doesn't affect them should vote that way.[^8hDdqKnk2cw-1057]

## Events

* Jagex also described Unbound Bronzeman, a variant allowing unlocks from items gained by killing players in PvP, which would have separate highscores and a slightly different icon, like GIM and Unranked GIM.[^FrZfpY0uoko-159]
