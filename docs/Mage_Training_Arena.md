**Mage Training Arena** is an activity topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2025-07-18 and 2026-06-04. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey thinks getting the rune pouch via Mage Training Arena is still slower than Last Man Standing but is a good option because it gives Magic XP, whereas Last Man Standing gives only points.[^0hoSb6coaVk-909]
* Gnomonkey says Mage Training Arena is a very good training method early on an account and a solid option for early irons.[^0hoSb6coaVk-940]
* Gnomonkey says the Master wand buff gives even more value to the Mage Training Arena, especially for ironmen who already rush it, and notes the ancient sceptre buff means you may just want a sceptre instead.[^6tsf-brc-sE-2195]
* Gnomonkey says the Master wand and ancient sceptre buffs make them solid options for the Inferno, since he has said in guides that the Dragon hunter wand at +10% is pretty okay, and now both are equally viable.[^6tsf-brc-sE-2228]

## Events

* The blog adds a rune pouch to the Mage Training Arena, costing roughly 25% less than Bones to Peaches, as an alternate way to get it.[^0hoSb6coaVk-909]
* Master wand gains +10% magic damage bonus, and ancient sceptres have their magic damage bonus increased to +10%.[^6tsf-brc-sE-2195]
