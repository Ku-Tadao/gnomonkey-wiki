**Group Hardcore Ironman** is an account topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2024-09-12 and 2024-09-14. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* In the video description Gnomonkey says the raid was more difficult than he thought with such low stats and 'dogwater' gear.[^HeM0yGenyx8-0]

## Events

* Gnomonkey attempts the Quiver on a hardcore group ironman (HCGIM), where death ends the account, which is why the run is billed as extreme.[^Aysv5rNWgFs-0]
* This video is part of Gnomonkey's group hardcore ironman series; the 300-invocation Tombs of Amascut clear was done with low stats and poor gear.[^HeM0yGenyx8-0]
* Per the description, the group's next goals are for Saebae to get a Scorching bow, to hit K'ril Tsutsaroth for Zamorakian hastae, or for Gnomonkey to get a Fang; Piety and more Blood moon or Ahrim's gear are his next goals.[^HeM0yGenyx8-0]

## Trivia

* At the end of the Colosseum run Gnomonkey estimated his Colosseum loot at roughly 4,500 shards and around 1.5 million in value.[^Aysv5rNWgFs-2385]
