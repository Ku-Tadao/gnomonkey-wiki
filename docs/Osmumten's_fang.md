# Osmumten's fang

## Methods

- Gnomonkey says the Osmumten's fang has a low max hit but rolls accuracy twice, making it extremely accurate. ([2024-07-07 12:44](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=764))
- Gnomonkey says the Osmumten's fang does not benefit as much from strength bonus as most weapons, so it pairs well with the Lightbearer where strength bonus matters less. ([2024-07-07 13:13](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=793))

## Stats and numbers

- According to Gnomonkey the fang is a 1% DPS increase against Nex and nowhere else, so it is not like the rapier as Jagex described but just bad. ([2022-02-26 11:47](https://youtu.be/uxwiO2KeF34?t=707))
- In the naked Challenge Mode Chambers run, Gnomonkey calculated Osmumten's fang as 69.42 percent better than the scythe for Tekton, and 55 percent better than the rapier at another room. ([2023-09-12 1:45:43](https://youtu.be/f1Y2Pd6dQoA?t=6343))
- Gnomonkey says that if you BGS Vorkath to low defence, the blowpipe takes over and is about 10% better than Osmumten's fang, though Fang would also do better with BGS. ([2024-05-18 9:45](https://youtu.be/WvmEY9v6AnI?t=585))
- Gnomonkey says the Osmumten's fang costs 13 million GP at the time of the video and is useful almost everywhere, but he sees many people misuse it. ([2024-07-07 12:44](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=764))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says that when going into Tombs of Amascut naked, Osmumten's fang is probably the best weapon. ([2023-09-12 48:02](https://youtu.be/f1Y2Pd6dQoA?t=2882))
- Gnomonkey says the Fang might be better than the Ghrazi rapier in that naked setup. ([2023-09-12 1:24:10](https://youtu.be/f1Y2Pd6dQoA?t=5050))
- Gnomonkey says the Osmumten's fang is amazing on high-defence bosses weakest to melee, which is most Tombs of Amascut bosses, and bad on anything under 50 defence, which is basically all Slayer monsters. ([2024-07-07 13:13](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=793))
- Gnomonkey notes the Fang kit adds 20 DPS (to slash), but because he was using stab on the Fang he set it to stab and avoided putting the kit on. ([2025-09-06 11:26](https://youtu.be/qCsJAdpSeTU?t=686))

- Gnomonkey says the change makes the Fang worse DPS than Zamorakian spear on a fully drained Corp but far more DPS than before on Nex due to her insane defence; he regards it as a neutral change that solidifies its niche status, though he expects it to be useful inside Raids 3 itself. - Gnomonkey says duo Nex with the Fang is very viable and that you will never use ranged at any scale, calling it much worse and saying it is absurd how fast you can duo with the Fang. ([2022-09-13 32:17](https://youtu.be/h4hf5hPQHms?t=1937))
- Gnomonkey says the Fang has been extremely competitive as a slash weapon against other slash weapons such as Arclight and Saradomin sword, in some cases beating even the Scythe, blurring the line between best stab weapon and best melee weapon. ([2023-12-22 0:00](https://youtu.be/t5mXf8KJztU))

## Events

- The poll blog changed Osmumten's fang, the five-tick hyper-accurate one-handed spear: it now has a 105 stab bonus instead of 175, and melee strength reduced to 103 (from 115), but it re-rolls for accuracy if the first roll fails. ([2022-05-18 7:42](https://youtu.be/Kgm0VYX_4no?t=462))
- Jagex is removing the doubled accuracy roll from the Fang when it is on slash, which Gnomonkey says is exactly what he suggested. ([2023-12-22 0:30](https://youtu.be/t5mXf8KJztU?t=30))
- The Odablock Warriors got four of their five needed fangs before Odablock told Gnomonkey to stop Tombs of Amascut and move to Chambers of Xeric. ([2026-06-25 37:02](https://youtu.be/lJexGv6dxu8?t=2222))
