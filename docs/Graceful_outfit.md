# Graceful outfit

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2020-07-25 to 2026-09-14). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

|---|---|
| **Type** | item |

## Requirements

- The Graceful outfit costs 260 Marks of Grace to buy in full. ([2020-07-25 0:30](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=30))

## Stats and numbers

- The full Graceful set increases run energy restoration by 30%; since a later update, individual pieces also give restoration, and the full set gives an extra 10% bonus. ([2020-07-25 0:30](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=30))
- After buying the Graceful outfit, Marks of Grace can be traded 10 at a time for Amylase packs containing 100 Amylase crystals, used for stamina potions, making each Mark of Grace worth roughly 9.7k GP in 2020. ([2020-07-25 0:30](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=30))
- Under the proposal, a duplicate graceful set costs only GP from Perdu, a total of 430k, and marks of grace spent on existing duplicate pieces will be refunded. ([2026-09-14 6:25](https://youtu.be/3qB8AKXsvWs?t=385))

## Tips

- The most efficient order to buy Graceful pieces for run energy restoration is gloves, hood, boots, cape, top, then legs. ([2020-07-25 0:30](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=30))
- Marks of Grace spawn at 20% of their normal rate if you are 20 levels above a rooftop course's requirement, so Gnomonkey says not to stay long at a single rooftop course. ([2020-07-25 0:30](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=30))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey always tries to rush the Graceful outfit on new accounts because it is extremely useful for most skilling methods and questing. ([2020-07-25 0:30](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=30))
- Gnomonkey strongly approves of the Graceful rework, saying it makes cosmetics less frustrating, lets extra marks go to other rewards, and makes losing graceful in the wilderness easy to recover from. ([2026-09-14 7:30](https://youtu.be/3qB8AKXsvWs?t=450))

## Events

- Jagex proposes making graceful pieces a one-time unlock: after spending the required marks of grace, the piece is permanently unlocked for your account. ([2026-09-14 6:25](https://youtu.be/3qB8AKXsvWs?t=385))
