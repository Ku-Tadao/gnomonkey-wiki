**Off-ticking** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 4 videos between 2022-07-22 and 2025-12-12. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Off-ticking a ranger and mager around a corner: wait for the attack of the thing next to you, then run out to the other monster so it sees you right after, which two-tick off-ticks them by default.[^6lRmbYCYtME-4527]
* In Pillarless Colosseum the two-tile marker is where you stand so that, when reinforcements spawn with a default ranged manticore, the melee NPC (Minotaur or Jaguar) walks up and off-ticks perfectly with the manticore's melee orb.[^E51JMiU2gsk-59]
* The three-tile marker is used when you need to step under a manticore to off-tick it, giving an extra tick while the reinforcement walks up so the melee orb lines up again.[^E51JMiU2gsk-92]
* The grey-looking lines on the side of the arena are drag tiles for far spawns such as javelins and manticores; running to the right of the line with proper timing drags the NPC to off-tick it.[^E51JMiU2gsk-151]
* There are two ways to off-tick in the run: stepping under mages, or dragging NPCs when you cannot step under them such as a distant javelin.[^E51JMiU2gsk-179]
* Gnomonkey defaulted to off-ticking a spawn with a ranged manticore, and if the mage spawn turns out to be a different type you must manually off-tick it and then kill it.[^E51JMiU2gsk-245]

## Tips

* A melee is not automatically off-ticked by a mage when it digs; it depends on the mage's attack cycle, and if the mage attacks the same tick the melee digs they are the same tick.[^6lRmbYCYtME-4740]
* When flicking with a melee in the mix he stares at the melee's movement to see when it off-ticks, and says not to be afraid of doing this.[^6lRmbYCYtME-4778]
* You can easily off-tick monsters by rounding a corner of a pillar: wait for the attack (as you would when alternating), then round the corner to desynchronise the attackers.[^FfNQI63ZKys-5290]
* In a wave with a ranger, a mage and a melee, Gnomonkey stands where the ranger is gapped off the pillar, shoots the mage as the ranger pulls up, then turns the corner as the mage attacks to off-tick both.[^FfNQI63ZKys-5290]
* Speedrunners must be willing to do off ticks, since killing the mage first matters more than staying perfectly safe as in a first cape.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-778]
* Gnomonkey describes an off tick around a round pillar: look for the ranger animation round the corner, pray accordingly, let the mage attack, and push out.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-808]
* If the player messes up a risky off tick they could take 70 to 90 damage, so it is worth waiting a bit when it is super dangerous, but when not dangerous the off tick should be pushed.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-929]
* The corner of the pillar is the position to stand on before rounding it, and that rounding the corner then praying is a bread-and-butter safe trick in speedruns.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-3721]
* Always stand on the corner of pillars before rounding them, because rounding them any other way would get the player hit by everything.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-3721]
* On waves 50 and above players must get comfortable with forced off ticks and it is okay to tank a single melee hit to get them set up, because melees do not hit often.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-3938]
* Gnomonkey demonstrates a splash off tick: splashing on the mage delays its next attack by two ticks, which helps in a messy start-of-wave clump.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-4260]
* The splash off tick does not work if the mage has respawned something, and calls it easy and underused.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-4289]
* The start tile is the best possible because it pushes a bunch of nearby spawns away.[^E51JMiU2gsk-125]
* Walking an NPC does not stop it attacking, so you can walk a serpent shaman back in range without ruining your off-tick.[^E51JMiU2gsk-542]
* Besides off-ticking and flicking prayers, the player must move occasionally to dodge javelin spear tosses, which makes the run more awkward.[^E51JMiU2gsk-843]
* The two and three markers are lines rather than single tiles, so you can stand anywhere on the line to off-tick, and you can move if a javelin toss lands on your tile.[^E51JMiU2gsk-869]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says he does not care which tick things are off-ticked on, as long as it is off-tick and you can recognize it quickly or force it.[^6lRmbYCYtME-4958]
* Gnomonkey says some bad spawns require an off-tick no matter what, and he would not blame you for running to the other side or going into the mage as alternatives.[^FfNQI63ZKys-4794]
