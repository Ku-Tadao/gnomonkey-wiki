# Off-ticking

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 4 videos (2022-07-22 to 2025-12-12). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Gnomonkey explains off-ticking a ranger and mager around a corner: wait for the attack of the thing next to you, then run out to the other monster so it sees you right after, which two-tick off-ticks them by default. ([2022-07-22 1:15:27](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=4527))
- In Pillarless Colosseum the two-tile marker is where you stand so that, when reinforcements spawn with a default ranged manticore, the melee NPC (Minotaur or Jaguar) walks up and off-ticks perfectly with the manticore's melee orb. ([2025-12-12 0:59](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=59))
- The three-tile marker is used when you need to step under a manticore to off-tick it, giving an extra tick while the reinforcement walks up so the melee orb lines up again. ([2025-12-12 1:32](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=92))
- The grey-looking lines on the side of the arena are drag tiles for far spawns such as javelins and manticores; running to the right of the line with proper timing drags the NPC to off-tick it. ([2025-12-12 2:31](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=151))
- There are two ways to off-tick in the run: stepping under mages, or dragging NPCs when you cannot step under them such as a distant javelin. ([2025-12-12 2:59](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=179))
- Gnomonkey defaulted to off-ticking a spawn with a ranged manticore, and if the mage spawn turns out to be a different type you must manually off-tick it and then kill it. ([2025-12-12 4:05](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=245))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says a melee is not automatically off-ticked by a mage when it digs; it depends on the mage's attack cycle, and if the mage attacks the same tick the melee digs they are the same tick. ([2022-07-22 1:19:00](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=4740))
- Gnomonkey says when flicking with a melee in the mix he stares at the melee's movement to see when it off-ticks, and says not to be afraid of doing this. ([2022-07-22 1:19:38](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=4778))
- Gnomonkey says you can easily off-tick monsters by rounding a corner of a pillar: wait for the attack (as you would when alternating), then round the corner to desynchronise the attackers. ([2022-11-04 1:28:10](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=5290))
- In a wave with a ranger, a mage and a melee, Gnomonkey stands where the ranger is gapped off the pillar, shoots the mage as the ranger pulls up, then turns the corner as the mage attacks to off-tick both. ([2022-11-04 1:28:10](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=5290))
- Gnomonkey says speedrunners must be willing to do off ticks, since killing the mage first matters more than staying perfectly safe as in a first cape. ([2025-07-13 12:58](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=778))
- Gnomonkey describes an off tick around a round pillar: look for the ranger animation round the corner, pray accordingly, let the mage attack, and push out. ([2025-07-13 13:28](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=808))
- Gnomonkey says that if the player messes up a risky off tick they could take 70 to 90 damage, so it is worth waiting a bit when it is super dangerous, but when not dangerous the off tick should be pushed. ([2025-07-13 15:29](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=929))
- Gnomonkey says the corner of the pillar is the position to stand on before rounding it, and that rounding the corner then praying is a bread-and-butter safe trick in speedruns. ([2025-07-13 1:02:01](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=3721))
- Gnomonkey says always stand on the corner of pillars before rounding them, because rounding them any other way would get the player hit by everything. ([2025-07-13 1:02:01](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=3721))
- Gnomonkey says on waves 50 and above players must get comfortable with forced off ticks and it is okay to tank a single melee hit to get them set up, because melees do not hit often. ([2025-07-13 1:05:38](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=3938))
- Gnomonkey demonstrates a splash off tick: splashing on the mage delays its next attack by two ticks, which helps in a messy start-of-wave clump. ([2025-07-13 1:11:00](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=4260))
- Gnomonkey says the splash off tick does not work if the mage has respawned something, and calls it easy and underused. ([2025-07-13 1:11:29](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=4289))
- The start tile is the best possible because it pushes a bunch of nearby spawns away. ([2025-12-12 2:05](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=125))
- Walking an NPC does not stop it attacking, so you can walk a serpent shaman back in range without ruining your off-tick. ([2025-12-12 9:02](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=542))
- Besides off-ticking and flicking prayers, the player must move occasionally to dodge javelin spear tosses, which makes the run more awkward. ([2025-12-12 14:03](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=843))
- The two and three markers are lines rather than single tiles, so you can stand anywhere on the line to off-tick, and you can move if a javelin toss lands on your tile. ([2025-12-12 14:29](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=869))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says he does not care which tick things are off-ticked on, as long as it is off-tick and you can recognize it quickly or force it. ([2022-07-22 1:22:38](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=4958))
- Gnomonkey says some bad spawns require an off-tick no matter what, and he would not blame you for running to the other side or going into the mage as alternatives. ([2022-11-04 1:19:54](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=4794))
