**Snape grass** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2025-03-26. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Stats and numbers

* Snape grass gives 82 Farming XP per plant, the highest-XP allotment in the game by a lot.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-54]
* With Magic secateurs, level 99 Farming and Attas planted, Gnomonkey says snape grass averages almost 50 yield per patch (49.5).[^uDhGFEjrpyc-54]
* At 49.5 snape grass per patch times 82 XP, Gnomonkey calculates about 4,063 XP per patch, slightly better than a maple tree.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-86]
* Gnomonkey timed it: manually digging up a snape grass plant takes about 60 seconds, which the Demonic Spade would reduce to eight game ticks.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-86]
* There are 16 allotment patches, which Gnomonkey describes as roughly eight locations with two patches each (except Harmony Island).[^uDhGFEjrpyc-121]
* Per visit to an allotment location with two snape grass patches, Gnomonkey calculates about 8k XP, slightly more than a yew sapling's 7k XP, plus about 100 snape grass.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-142]
* Doing all eight allotment locations with snape grass would give about 65k XP and roughly 800 snape grass (50 per patch across 16 patches), by Gnomonkey's math.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-169]
* Snape grass takes 70 minutes to grow versus 400 minutes for trees, which Gnomonkey says is where his concern comes from.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-169]
* Snape grass seeds cost about 11k each, so about 33k per patch; Gnomonkey says the Amulet of Bounty gives a 25% chance to use only one seed when planting, which offsets seed cost.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-198]
* Snape grass seed price rose from about 8k to almost 11.5k in hype for the spade, and Gnomonkey thinks it was still rising because people expected a revert.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-226]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey argues the spade would have injected mass quantities of snape grass into the Grand Exchange and sent seed prices (tree and herb) plummeting.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-1440]
