**Update: Sailing slayer monsters** is an update topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2025-11-26. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Stats and numbers

* Lava Strikeworms take 50% extra from water spells and are healed by fire spells, which Gnomonkey says is the whole mechanic after Jagex teased that elemental spells would weaken or empower them.[^KRl59CBUR8U-3937]
* Frost Dragons have a 100% fire weakness; with the Dragon Hunter wand, which stacks with the elemental weakness, Gnomonkey says you can get almost 200 kills per hour, but it is still only about a 40k Slayer XP per hour task with a low Slayer weight.[^KRl59CBUR8U-3970]
* Frost Dragons and the other new monsters drop Sailing materials such as dragon metal sheets, dragon nails and dragon cannonballs but nothing useful outside Sailing; Frost Dragon dragon metal sheets are about 1 in 180, Lava Strikeworm sheets about 1 in 500.[^KRl59CBUR8U-3970]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey calls the Sailing Slayer monsters (Aqua Knights, Lava Strikeworms, Frost Dragons) and the Shellbane Gryphon boss a disappointment, with very simple mechanics and nothing that teaches or prepares players for other content.[^KRl59CBUR8U-3844]
* Gnomonkey says the Aqua Knight hopper's double bolt proc is roughly equal to the Stalker offhand in a calculator and nowhere good, so there is not much reason to unlock the new monsters.[^KRl59CBUR8U-4085]
* Gnomonkey predicts most of the new Slayer monsters will be dead content in a month or two once the hype of going for 200 million Sailing XP fades.[^KRl59CBUR8U-4149]
* Gnomonkey would like more Slayer monsters with several mechanics and good XP rates, like Tormented Demons, and is sick of cannoning and bursting every task; he notes RS3 monsters such as Frost Dragons and Lava Strikeworms had mechanics that Jagex did not copy.[^KRl59CBUR8U-4180]
* Gnomonkey criticises that an 87 Sailing requirement monster still gives a worse Slayer XP rate than the average Slayer task, at about 40k an hour with max gear.[^KRl59CBUR8U-4212]
