# Update: Sailing slayer monsters

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 1 video (2025-11-26). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | update |
| **Videos** | 1 (first 2025-11-26, latest 2025-11-26) |

## Stats and numbers

- Lava Strikeworms take 50% extra from water spells and are healed by fire spells, which Gnomonkey says is the whole mechanic after Jagex teased that elemental spells would weaken or empower them. ([2025-11-26 1:05:37](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=3937))
- Frost Dragons have a 100% fire weakness; with the Dragon Hunter wand, which stacks with the elemental weakness, Gnomonkey says you can get almost 200 kills per hour, but it is still only about a 40k Slayer XP per hour task with a low Slayer weight. ([2025-11-26 1:06:10](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=3970))
- Frost Dragons and the other new monsters drop Sailing materials such as dragon metal sheets, dragon nails and dragon cannonballs but nothing useful outside Sailing; Frost Dragon dragon metal sheets are about 1 in 180, Lava Strikeworm sheets about 1 in 500. ([2025-11-26 1:06:10](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=3970))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey calls the Sailing Slayer monsters (Aqua Knights, Lava Strikeworms, Frost Dragons) and the Shellbane Gryphon boss a disappointment, with very simple mechanics and nothing that teaches or prepares players for other content. ([2025-11-26 1:04:04](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=3844))
- Gnomonkey says the Aqua Knight hopper's double bolt proc is roughly equal to the Stalker offhand in a calculator and nowhere good, so there is not much reason to unlock the new monsters. ([2025-11-26 1:08:05](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=4085))
- Gnomonkey predicts most of the new Slayer monsters will be dead content in a month or two once the hype of going for 200 million Sailing XP fades. ([2025-11-26 1:09:09](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=4149))
- Gnomonkey would like more Slayer monsters with several mechanics and good XP rates, like Tormented Demons, and is sick of cannoning and bursting every task; he notes RS3 monsters such as Frost Dragons and Lava Strikeworms had mechanics that Jagex did not copy. ([2025-11-26 1:09:40](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=4180))
- Gnomonkey criticises that an 87 Sailing requirement monster still gives a worse Slayer XP rate than the average Slayer task, at about 40k an hour with max gear. ([2025-11-26 1:10:12](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=4212))
