**Snakeling** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2025-04-24 and 2025-05-07. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Gnomonkey colour-codes snakelings with the radius markers plugin, importing a pastebin string linked in the description.[^bBnPK55ZqUM-327]

## Stats and numbers

* Zulrah's snakelings are randomly 50-50 either a melee snakeling or a mage snakeling; Gnomonkey says you can avoid mage snakeling damage almost entirely, and the melee ones are the ones that hit you.[^bBnPK55ZqUM-327]
* With the Bandos melee defence, melee snakelings sometimes struggle to hit you.[^ecd_a9E8l5E-573]

## Tips

* Snakelings will hit hard if you forget to wear your recoil ring after it breaks.[^bBnPK55ZqUM-210]
* Gnomonkey flicks on the Ring of recoil to kill melee snakelings and otherwise leaves the recoil on unless attacking with the Bellator ring.[^bBnPK55ZqUM-645]
* You should not shield flick unless none of the snakelings are melee ones.[^bBnPK55ZqUM-735]
* To import the snakeling radius markers (pastebin linked in the description), as snakelings are either mage or melee and mage ones are unlikely to hit you.[^ecd_a9E8l5E-787]
