# Snakeling

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2025-04-24 to 2025-05-07). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Gnomonkey colour-codes snakelings with the radius markers plugin, importing a pastebin string linked in the description. ([2025-04-24 5:27](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=327))

## Stats and numbers

- Zulrah's snakelings are randomly 50-50 either a melee snakeling or a mage snakeling; Gnomonkey says you can avoid mage snakeling damage almost entirely, and the melee ones are the ones that hit you. ([2025-04-24 5:27](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=327))
- Gnomonkey says with the Bandos melee defence, melee snakelings sometimes struggle to hit you. ([2025-05-07 9:33](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=573))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey notes that snakelings will hit hard if you forget to wear your recoil ring after it breaks. ([2025-04-24 3:30](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=210))
- Gnomonkey flicks on the Ring of recoil to kill melee snakelings and otherwise leaves the recoil on unless attacking with the Bellator ring. ([2025-04-24 10:45](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=645))
- Gnomonkey says you should not shield flick unless none of the snakelings are melee ones. ([2025-04-24 12:15](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=735))
- Gnomonkey says to import the snakeling radius markers (pastebin linked in the description), as snakelings are either mage or melee and mage ones are unlikely to hit you. ([2025-05-07 13:07](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=787))
