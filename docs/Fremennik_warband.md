# Fremennik warband

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2024-03-25 to 2024-03-26). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Gnomonkey says Fremennik in the Colosseum take a guaranteed max hit if you use the correct combat style against them. ([2024-03-25 2:26](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=146))
- Gnomonkey starts every wave in four-way magic, casts at the melee Fremennik to kill him instantly, goes behind a pillar, puts on the Venator bow and shoots the mage. ([2024-03-25 3:00](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=180))

## Stats and numbers

- The ranger Fremennik only maxes 1, and an SGS spec on him is a guaranteed max hit of about 70 damage that heals about 30 health. ([2024-03-25 17:13](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=1033))
- The mages in the Colosseum are glass cannons that hit very hard, so Gnomonkey says to always start praying mage when a wave has mages. ([2024-03-25 23:56](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=1436))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey brings a four-way Shadow to guarantee a one-shot on the berserker Fremennik, since four switches of magic are enough; with only a Sanguinesti staff, one-way Sang is enough to two-shot it. ([2024-03-25 2:26](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=146))
- The Venator bow is enough to one-shot the mage Fremennik, according to Gnomonkey. ([2024-03-25 3:00](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=180))
- Gnomonkey says the archer (ranger) Fremennik only maxes 1, so it is not a priority. ([2024-03-25 6:42](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=402))
- Gnomonkey says to leave the ranger alive as a med kit that follows you around, SGS specing him whenever you need health; there is no reason to kill him. ([2024-03-25 17:13](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=1033))
- Gnomonkey's custom menu entry swaps attack the Fremennik warband archer, seer and berserker (level 104) and the serpent shaman (level 161), tagged with the Better NPC Highlight plugin. ([2024-03-26 0:00](https://youtu.be/nEIeyY_m_gw))
- In Colosseum Gnomonkey's kill priority is the Fremennik Berserker first, which maxes about 50, then the mage, which maxes about 15. ([2024-03-26 7:57](https://youtu.be/nEIeyY_m_gw?t=477))
- Gnomonkey says to train yourself not to attack the Fremennik archer, as it maxes one damage and attacking it wastes time that can get you killed. ([2024-03-26 7:57](https://youtu.be/nEIeyY_m_gw?t=477))

## Trivia

- The Fremennik warband NPCs in the Colosseum are level 104 for the archer, seer and berserker, and the serpent shaman is level 161. ([2024-03-25 0:04](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=4))
- The Fremennik javelin enemies throw a spear in the air that lands on top of the player every fifth attack, forcing the player to move. ([2024-03-26 5:55](https://youtu.be/nEIeyY_m_gw?t=355))
