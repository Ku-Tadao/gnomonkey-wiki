**Fremennik warband** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2024-03-25 and 2024-03-26. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Fremennik in the Colosseum take a guaranteed max hit if you use the correct combat style against them.[^EVOs96kyHSM-146]
* Gnomonkey starts every wave in four-way magic, casts at the melee Fremennik to kill him instantly, goes behind a pillar, puts on the Venator bow and shoots the mage.[^EVOs96kyHSM-180]

## Stats and numbers

* The ranger Fremennik only maxes 1, and an SGS spec on him is a guaranteed max hit of about 70 damage that heals about 30 health.[^EVOs96kyHSM-1033]
* The mages in the Colosseum are glass cannons that hit very hard, so Gnomonkey says to always start praying mage when a wave has mages.[^EVOs96kyHSM-1436]

## Tips

* Gnomonkey brings a four-way Shadow to guarantee a one-shot on the berserker Fremennik, since four switches of magic are enough; with only a Sanguinesti staff, one-way Sang is enough to two-shot it.[^EVOs96kyHSM-146]
* The Venator bow is enough to one-shot the mage Fremennik, according to Gnomonkey.[^EVOs96kyHSM-180]
* The archer (ranger) Fremennik only maxes 1, so it is not a priority.[^EVOs96kyHSM-402]
* To leave the ranger alive as a med kit that follows you around, SGS specing him whenever you need health; there is no reason to kill him.[^EVOs96kyHSM-1033]
* Gnomonkey's custom menu entry swaps attack the Fremennik warband archer, seer and berserker (level 104) and the serpent shaman (level 161), tagged with the Better NPC Highlight plugin.[^nEIeyY_m_gw-0]
* In Colosseum Gnomonkey's kill priority is the Fremennik Berserker first, which maxes about 50, then the mage, which maxes about 15.[^nEIeyY_m_gw-477]
* To train yourself not to attack the Fremennik archer, as it maxes one damage and attacking it wastes time that can get you killed.[^nEIeyY_m_gw-477]

## Trivia

* The Fremennik warband NPCs in the Colosseum are level 104 for the archer, seer and berserker, and the serpent shaman is level 161.[^EVOs96kyHSM-4]
* The Fremennik javelin enemies throw a spear in the air that lands on top of the player every fifth attack, forcing the player to move.[^nEIeyY_m_gw-355]
