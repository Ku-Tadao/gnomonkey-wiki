## Methods

- Gnomonkey's PvP world plan was to log in to a high-risk PvP world, run the caves skulled with blighted supplies, log out at wave 62, and log back in on a normal world to kill Jad. ([2024-08-08 49:39](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=2979))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says 50 ping is not ideal but acceptable, and that high-risk worlds have very few players so the ping is the only issue. ([2024-08-08 54:50](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=3290))

## Tips

- A chat member suggested doing the Fight Caves on a PvP world so he could use blighted food, logging in at wave one and logging out at wave 62. ([2024-08-08 48:13](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=2893))
- Gnomonkey custom-swapped a ring of dueling so he can teleport to Ferox Enclave in one click, which is a safe zone on a PvP world, as an escape method. ([2024-08-08 52:15](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=3135))
- Gnomonkey says the same technique could be used for the Colosseum, since its bank is a safe zone, and for the Inferno to save prayer potions. ([2024-08-08 1:26:21](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=5181))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey stresses that this is not a safe death: dying on the PvP world would cost him a life, even though normally he would not lose one on a hardcore. ([2024-08-08 54:22](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=3262))
- Gnomonkey hopes Jagex does not patch out the PvP world trick, noting the Sepulchre bug was left for a week while this would be patched instantly. ([2024-08-08 1:25:48](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=5148))

## Events

- Gnomonkey starts the Fight Caves on PvP world 579, a high-risk world, and gets skulled. ([2024-08-08 50:41](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=3041))
- Gnomonkey says his teammates stood guard outside protecting him on the PvP world. ([2024-08-08 58:44](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=3524))
