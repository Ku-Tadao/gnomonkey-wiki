**PvP World** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2024-08-08. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Gnomonkey's PvP world plan was to log in to a high-risk PvP world, run the caves skulled with blighted supplies, log out at wave 62, and log back in on a normal world to kill Jad.[^_cIv_pRhm9U-2979]

## Stats and numbers

* 50 ping is not ideal but acceptable, and that high-risk worlds have very few players so the ping is the only issue.[^_cIv_pRhm9U-3290]

## Tips

* A chat member suggested doing the Fight Caves on a PvP world so he could use blighted food, logging in at wave one and logging out at wave 62.[^_cIv_pRhm9U-2893]
* Gnomonkey custom-swapped a ring of dueling so he can teleport to Ferox Enclave in one click, which is a safe zone on a PvP world, as an escape method.[^_cIv_pRhm9U-3135]
* The same technique could be used for the Colosseum, since its bank is a safe zone, and for the Inferno to save prayer potions.[^_cIv_pRhm9U-5181]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey stresses that this is not a safe death: dying on the PvP world would cost him a life, even though normally he would not lose one on a hardcore.[^_cIv_pRhm9U-3262]
* Gnomonkey hopes Jagex does not patch out the PvP world trick, noting the Sepulchre bug was left for a week while this would be patched instantly.[^_cIv_pRhm9U-5148]

## Events

* Gnomonkey starts the Fight Caves on PvP world 579, a high-risk world, and gets skulled.[^_cIv_pRhm9U-3041]
* His teammates stood guard outside protecting him on the PvP world.[^_cIv_pRhm9U-3524]
