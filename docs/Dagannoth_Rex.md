# Dagannoth Rex

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2022-04-19 to 2026-06-10). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- If Rex aggros onto you while dealing with Supreme, run to the back corner and safespot him while praying ranged, and kill Rex first; Gnomonkey wears Armadyl to protect more from the Spinolyps in that case. ([2022-04-19 7:57](https://youtu.be/QNv-UATHGPs?t=477))
- After collecting everything, bring Rex to a corner to safespot him, praying magic to protect from the Spinolyps; after killing all three, grab the loot, go up and jump into the other cave. ([2022-04-19 8:28](https://youtu.be/QNv-UATHGPs?t=508))
- Gnomonkey loaded the Dagannoth Rex into the team's position and told everyone to mage the Rex. ([2026-06-10 14:28](https://youtu.be/qyG_lTC4pDU?t=868))

## Tips

- If you need health, have the alt spec-transfer you, and if you run out of prayer potions, trade more supplies over from the alt. ([2022-04-19 8:28](https://youtu.be/QNv-UATHGPs?t=508))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey likes killing Rex last because it lets him pray melee and run around collecting drops from the other kings while attacking Rex, which saves a lot of time; in magic gear with the Tome he can also alch the drops while running. ([2022-04-19 7:57](https://youtu.be/QNv-UATHGPs?t=477))

## Events

- While the Rex was being killed, rival teams were fighting each other, which Gnomonkey described as really good for his team. ([2026-06-10 14:58](https://youtu.be/qyG_lTC4pDU?t=898))
