**Dagannoth Rex** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2022-04-19 and 2026-06-10. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* If Rex aggros onto you while dealing with Supreme, run to the back corner and safespot him while praying ranged, and kill Rex first; Gnomonkey wears Armadyl to protect more from the Spinolyps in that case.[^QNv-UATHGPs-477]
* After collecting everything, bring Rex to a corner to safespot him, praying magic to protect from the Spinolyps; after killing all three, grab the loot, go up and jump into the other cave.[^QNv-UATHGPs-508]
* Gnomonkey loaded the Dagannoth Rex into the team's position and told everyone to mage the Rex.[^qyG_lTC4pDU-868]

## Tips

* If you need health, have the alt spec-transfer you, and if you run out of prayer potions, trade more supplies over from the alt.[^QNv-UATHGPs-508]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey likes killing Rex last because it lets him pray melee and run around collecting drops from the other kings while attacking Rex, which saves a lot of time; in magic gear with the Tome he can also alch the drops while running.[^QNv-UATHGPs-477]

## Events

* While the Rex was being killed, rival teams were fighting each other, which Gnomonkey described as really good for his team.[^qyG_lTC4pDU-898]
