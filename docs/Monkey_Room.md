# Monkey Room

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 7 videos (2023-04-25 to 2025-07-06). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

|---|---|

## Methods

- In the ToA monkey room Gnomonkey drops three potions, prays melee, grabs a hammer and grabs a potion before starting. ([2024-10-24 7:08](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=428))
- In the monkey room, colour-coded baboons take a guaranteed max hit if you use the correct style: melee the green ones, ranged the blue ones and mage the red ones, so one-way switches can kill them all. ([2024-10-24 7:08](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=428))
- In the Monkey room Gnomonkey enters wearing ranged gear without the Bow of faerdhinen equipped, drops a few potions on the ground, then equips the bow to start tagging monkeys. ([2025-06-25 7:36](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=456))
- There are red, green and blue monkeys that follow the combat triangle, and using the correct style on them is a guaranteed max hit, so Gnomonkey only uses one-way weapon swaps. ([2025-06-25 8:26](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=506))
- After the update shamans take guaranteed max hits from ranged, so Gnomonkey kills them with two blowpipe shots; curse baboons are also guaranteed max hits with ranged and he kills them with the Bow of faerdhinen. ([2025-06-25 8:26](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=506))
- In the Monkey room Gnomonkey swaps to fang with Piety for the green monkeys and to the trident for the red monkeys, switching between Piety and Rigor and never needing Augury. ([2025-06-25 9:00](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=540))
- In the Monkey room, Gnomonkey pours the stinky potion into a vent when a skull is over it, and drops three brews on the entry tile before the room starts. ([2025-06-25 9:30](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=570))
- In the Monkey room Gnomonkey runs up, drops three potions, equips a range weapon and uses a one-way trident to kill the adds, praying ranged when ranged baboons spawn and magic when mages spawn. ([2025-06-27 4:21](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=261))
- With the Atlatl, the shamans in the monkey room are a two-shot and are now guaranteed to be ranged; with range prayer, even Eagle Eye, the Atlatl one-shots the cursed baboons if you do not have Rigor. ([2025-06-28 7:09](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=429))
- In the first wave of the monkey room there are melee monkeys that you tag with a trident; you do not need to pray against shamans, and you need Piety to one-shot the big monkeys. ([2025-06-28 7:38](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=458))
- In the monkey room, pray against the primary thing hitting you and change prayers dynamically as the next set spawns, praying melee as the default because the thralls hit you. ([2025-07-06 10:37](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=637))

## Requirements

- The only thing in the Atlatl monkey room that needs Rigor (or any range prayer) is the cursed baboons. ([2025-06-28 7:38](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=458))

## Stats and numbers

- Each Monkey Room shaman summons four thralls that are assigned to it before it starts maging you, so chinning the pile of thralls stops the shaman maging. ([2023-04-25 4:59](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=299))
- Gnomonkey says the Monkey room shamans have 50 health and the Bow of faerdhinen hits about 48, so it just barely does not one-shot them, which is why he uses two blowpipe shots. ([2025-06-25 9:00](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=540))

## Tips

- In the Tombs of Amascut Monkey Room, Gnomonkey says to prioritise the shamans when they are the ones draining prayer. ([2023-04-25 3:17](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=197))
- Gnomonkey says the thralls are assigned only to their shaman, so it can be hard to tell, and there can be a ton of thralls in the room while shamans are still summoning. ([2023-04-25 5:26](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=326))
- Gnomonkey says avoiding the throttles (spinning attackers) in the Monkey Room matters a lot because you waste a lot of prayer there. ([2023-04-25 44:04](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=2644))
- In the monkey room, Gnomonkey says not to tank thralls because they drain prayer, and not to tank a clump of them at once. ([2023-06-30 2:22](https://youtu.be/cmLBaxdoHpQ?t=142))
- If you get through the monkey room with any prayer remaining, Gnomonkey says you can do Baba with zero prayer point use, meaning zero supplies in that room, which is the goal. ([2023-06-30 2:22](https://youtu.be/cmLBaxdoHpQ?t=142))
- In the monkey room Gnomonkey uses chinchompas to clear out thralls and flicks lots of prayer; you can play offensive prayer in the room. ([2023-06-30 2:22](https://youtu.be/cmLBaxdoHpQ?t=142))
- When tanking a bunch of thralls you should step to the side; Gnomonkey says not to tank them. ([2023-06-30 4:38](https://youtu.be/cmLBaxdoHpQ?t=278))
- Gnomonkey says not to turn on Smite in the monkey room. ([2023-06-30 5:34](https://youtu.be/cmLBaxdoHpQ?t=334))
- Gnomonkey says the only baboons that do not get one-shot in the monkey room are the shamans, which summon little thralls that hit you. ([2024-10-24 7:56](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=476))
- Gnomonkey says you do need Piety and that prayer one-way is enough to kill everything in the monkey room. ([2024-10-24 8:28](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=508))
- Cursed baboons leave a trail of venom that still does damage even with a Serpentine helm, so Gnomonkey says not to run through it. ([2024-10-24 8:28](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=508))
- Volatile baboons (the red ones, killed with mage) walk up and explode, and can blow up other monkeys; Gnomonkey says to avoid them while learning and later use their volleys to your advantage. ([2024-10-24 8:28](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=508))
- The exploding volatile baboons deal a lot of damage and are probably the main way people die in the Monkey room; Gnomonkey says to avoid standing next to them when they blow up, and they also blow up other monkeys, which he uses to clear them. ([2025-06-25 9:30](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=570))
- In the 500 Monkey room Gnomonkey says you need to get through without using any brew, by praying against the primary baboon of each wave, focusing the monsters that are hitting you, then putting melee prayer back on for the thralls. ([2025-06-27 4:01](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=241))
- In the Monkey room's melee wave Gnomonkey says to kill the shamans first, and the wave after is the one wave where you have to tank, so go straight for the ranged baboon. ([2025-06-27 5:21](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=321))
- In the monkey room always kill the shamans first, then the ranged baboon, and leave the melee baboon; use the volleys to clean up the last wave, which is helpful when you do not have chinchompas. ([2025-06-28 8:38](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=518))
- Gnomonkey brews up before going into Baba after the monkey room, over-brewing, then repots stats. ([2025-06-28 9:41](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=581))
- In the monkey room Gnomonkey always camps his ranged gear and swaps to other styles, no matter what setup he runs. ([2025-07-06 10:11](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=611))
- Blowpipe specs are guaranteed hits so you can heal back health lost in the monkey room; Gnomonkey says you do not want to use the Keris there because it wastes a lot of prayer. ([2025-07-06 11:04](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=664))
- If you are at about 50 health at the end of the monkey room you can poke a couple of monkeys with the Keris to get health back; over 80 health it does not matter, especially with Blood Fury. ([2025-07-06 11:58](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=718))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says that with the changes Jagex made, the monkey room is a lot easier than it used to be. ([2024-10-24 8:58](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=538))
- Gnomonkey says the Monkey room is a puzzle room and is probably the hardest room of the raid, harder than the bosses, even though two waves were removed. ([2025-06-25 7:53](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=473))
- Gnomonkey says the Tombs of Amascut monkey room is now a joke, even though Reddit says it is impossible with this setup, and thanks Jagex for making it easier. ([2025-06-28 4:56](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=296))

## Records

- Gnomonkey took only about 10 damage in the Monkey room and healed it back with Blood fury; he says you will probably end the room with 30 to 50 health and should heal slowly with Blood fury at Baba instead of using brews. ([2025-06-27 6:21](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=381))
- Gnomonkey finished the monkey room with 90 health, which he considers ideal. ([2025-07-06 11:31](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=691))

## Events

- After the last update the shamans and cursed baboons in the monkey room have less health, so a blowpipe can now one-shot the cursed baboons. ([2025-07-06 10:11](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=611))

## Trivia

- Gnomonkey says a little known fact is that the small blue baboon thralls summoned by shamans also damage your prayer, so you want to avoid tanking many of them. ([2024-10-24 8:58](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=538))
- In the monkey room, the small blue monkey thralls that melee you drain a little prayer if they hit, so avoid tanking a whole pile of them. ([2025-06-28 9:08](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=548))
