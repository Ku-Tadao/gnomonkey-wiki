**Fortis Colosseum pillars** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 6 videos between 2024-03-26 and 2025-03-21. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* For a common pillar stack where one enemy attacks differently at the back than the front, Gnomonkey stands two tiles out from the pillar and steps to the corner tile, praying against the thing in the back first, then the thing in the front, which sees you a tick later.[^nEIeyY_m_gw-868]
* When something is hanging over the edge of the pillar, Gnomonkey goes three tiles back for the thing in front; the thing in front sees you before the thing in the back, so the order is reversed.[^nEIeyY_m_gw-1545]
* Gnomonkey starts speedrun waves at the Northeast pillar, by far the most dangerous starting position, because it does not trap any melee enemies and gets the most Venator bow setups for maximum DPS.[^K-FUaBwu0DE-58]
* The Minotaur drag tile pulls a Minotaur from behind things down to you, and the Minotaur heal block is behind the pillar; if you reach the heal block tile before the Minotaur gets there, it never gets line of sight and does not heal its target.[^IfJVUHF6fyc-111]
* Reinforcement spawns on the Northwest pillar centre around a tile randomly within a big area, while the Southwest pillar's gate spawn centre is one tile closer to the gate, which he says solves many problems.[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-394]
* In the very late waves reinforcements spawn with a Minotaur and a shaman, which is what usually gets people killed, and on the Northwest pillar the shaman can spawn in front of the Minotaur so you cannot put it behind and must off-tick the shaman.[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-441]
* For a normal stack with a ranged thing in the back and a mage thing in front, you click to the corner from the middle of the pillar, which makes the back enemy see you first and the front enemy see you after, lining them up on the same tick.[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-1857]
* This works because the further-out tile gives line of sight on you before the front enemy is unblocked, and you must start from the middle of the pillar since the start tile also matters.[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-1857]
* He solved a Z-stack (manticore, mage, javelin) by stepping to a tile so the manticore saw him, then stepping to make the shockwave and then the javelin see him, lining attacks as ranged, mage, melee, mage, ranged with two ticks of downtime to fit off-ticks.[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-1950]

## Stats and numbers

* Gnomonkey's tiles are three below the pillar, one out from the pillar, four down, five down from the pillar for the heal block, and the Roy tile is also five down from the pillar on the other side.[^IfJVUHF6fyc-140]
* A manticore has effectively a five-tick attack cooldown, so there are three ticks of manticore attacks and two ticks for other monsters, and calls the Z-stack the worst, very rare stack.[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-1950]

## Tips

* The pillar stack solve only works if you step to the correct corner of the pillar, and recommends practising the first stack repeatedly because you must execute stacks quickly or you will die.[^nEIeyY_m_gw-868]
* Gnomonkey died on a stack with a mage in the front because a mage in the front is too small and changes the line of sight, so he had to approach from the other side of the pillar.[^nEIeyY_m_gw-1029]
* The three-tiles-back tile works regardless of where you start, that it is due to the size of the NPCs, and that the southwest pillar is the absolute best position.[^nEIeyY_m_gw-1900]
* The Minotaur only heals if there is line of sight from its centre tile to the other NPC's centre tile, so if the other mob is two tiles behind the pillar it will not heal, but if both are two tiles out it will.[^nEIeyY_m_gw-2231]
* The video's description says the squares in the middle of the arena (Sol tiles) are available via the !soltiles chat command.[^IfJVUHF6fyc-20]
* Gnomonkey's mage block tile is three tiles down from the pillar, letting the mage see you so you can block it and put it behind the Minotaur.[^IfJVUHF6fyc-111]
* Only five Colosseum tiles matter, and really only two, plus the Roy tiles; the tiles are line markers, not tile markers, so a line marker plugin is needed.[^IfJVUHF6fyc-1423]
* Gnomonkey recommends starting at South Pillar, either on the B5 tile or on his own speed tile, for learners.[^kIfF1pNro2c-0]
* Gnomonkey's personal start tile is not the typical B5 tile; he starts a couple of tiles out so something can spawn right next to him, which lets him pray against it behind the pillar and often avoid damage, and he thinks it is better than B5 even for learners.[^kIfF1pNro2c-840]
* On the Southwest pillar the same stack of a manticore and a javelin colossus on the same tick does not kill you: you pray against both and go to the correct side of the pillar.[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-564]
* At the Southwest pillar's mage block tile a shaman can see you from any spawn location so it stops moving, while the Minotaur keeps moving to you, meaning you never have to off-tick a shaman from that position.[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-595]
* You do not need range switches on the Southwest pillar because you can rotate around the pillar and off-tick enemies so you can melee everything, which gives about five more restores and removes the need for a range potion or a minimum four-way Twisted bow.[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-784]
* You can tell what a second manticore will attack with before its orbs appear by watching the first manticore's style, which lets you know when something is off-ticked.[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-2203]
* Stepping out from the pillar three tiles makes the front thing see you before the back thing, the opposite of the usual case.[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-2234]
* When things pull up, if they attack one or two ticks before or after the manticore they are off-ticked, and he warns to be careful what you kill because killing a manticore can ruin your stack.[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-2575]
* The pillar he uses in the Colosseum gives better stacks and lets you rotate both ways around it, so every stack is guaranteed solvable.[^c6oOmOGl5Lg-1653]
* He says that at this pillar you control the wave instead of it controlling what you do, because pillar stacks can be solved in either direction, whereas the North pillar only allows one direction.[^c6oOmOGl5Lg-1686]
* Mage block only works consistently on this side, with the Minotaur and Shaman, but works only about 50% of the time on the North pillar.[^c6oOmOGl5Lg-1686]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says North Pillar has unsolvable double stacks and you cannot 100% mage block the Minotaur waves there, and that suggesting North for learners is a noob trap because you lose safety and pillar rotation options and coin flip whether the Shaman stacks behind Minotaurs.[^kIfF1pNro2c-0]
* Gnomonkey hates North Pillar because it prevents rotating the pillar and doing stacks, whereas on South Pillar stacks can be solved on both sides and you can rotate the pillar both ways.[^kIfF1pNro2c-328]
* Gnomonkey also says North Pillar is worse for reinforcements, which is why he always uses South.[^kIfF1pNro2c-356]
* Gnomonkey says South Pillar always gives a way to solve a bad wave if you are quick (including mage blocking), whereas on North Pillar a spawn like a bad wave 10 just kills you.[^kIfF1pNro2c-1190]
* Gnomonkey says he does the Colosseum differently from every other guide: the common method is starting on the B5 tile and running to the Northwest pillar, but he says to stop using the Northwest pillar and use the Southwest pillar instead.[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-300]
* Gnomonkey says on the Northwest pillar there is no solve for some stacks: for example a javelin colossus and a manticore attacking on the same tick means you die unless you get the off-tick at the beginning.[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-472]
* Gnomonkey says some stacks occur only on the Northwest pillar, such as two mage monsters in front and two ranged in the back, which he says is only solvable by tanking damage, whereas on the Southwest pillar the tile pushes monsters to the side so two things can never be side by side.[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-660]
* Gnomonkey says he tells people posting unsolvable stacks from the Northwest pillar in Coliseum chat to go to the Southwest pillar, and says he even told another streamer that a posted stack was unsolvable.[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-724]
* Gnomonkey says the Southwest pillar gives you more supplies, fewer bad spawns, and lets you solve everything, though he admits it does not solve every problem, such as a Z-stack.[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-875]

## Events

* He has multiple teaching streams covering this pillar.[^c6oOmOGl5Lg-1749]

## Trivia

* A 1x1 Serpent Mage spawns in waves 1 through 6 in Colosseum, which lets Gnomonkey create specific setups so the Venator bow triple ricochets.[^K-FUaBwu0DE-58]
* Mage block does not work every time on the North pillar because enemies spawn further out from the door than at the other pillar.[^c6oOmOGl5Lg-1717]
