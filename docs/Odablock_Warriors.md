## Tips

* The Odablock Warriors tell all remaining team members to meet at Falador after the chase, with Gnomonkey waiting on his skull before joining.[^FwjOiTIPQXE-147]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* The Odablock Warriors assume the Dino Nuggets are stalling like the Westham Weasels and Rhinos, so they discuss how to play the stand-off.[^FwjOiTIPQXE-94]
* Gnomonkey tells the Odablock Warriors that worst comes to worst they should rush the Dino Nuggets team, because five men were clumped together.[^qyG_lTC4pDU-0]
* Gnomonkey says the Odablock Warriors were in better gear than a rival team, but the rival team had better prayers, so the Odablock Warriors decided not to risk a fight because they were not there to fight.[^qyG_lTC4pDU-1193]
* After escaping, the Odablock Warriors told each other this was their best breach and not to take it as a loss.[^qyG_lTC4pDU-1618]
* Gnomonkey says Evscape's plan on day one may have been scuffed, but Evscape redeemed himself and that was the team's breach.[^qyG_lTC4pDU-2118]
* Gnomonkey says the Odablock Warriors were underdogs and had probably the most deaths in Deadman history, which he attributes to a skill-set mismatch in the draft.[^lJexGv6dxu8-254]
* Gnomonkey says captain Odablock is insanely good at PvP and fairly good at PvM but is not super good at delegating roles.[^lJexGv6dxu8-254]
* Gnomonkey says Raikesy is incredibly good at PvP but struggled with early Ironman routing and quick early-game gearing.[^lJexGv6dxu8-283]
* Gnomonkey says Evscape did a very good job grinding Doom without drops, doing the grinds nobody else wanted, and was the backbone of the team.[^lJexGv6dxu8-283]
* Gnomonkey says Dubie Dobies is an insanely good NH player and was the team's first pick, but is less suited to Ironman-style gearing and planning, and never had gear to shine until the finale.[^lJexGv6dxu8-316]
* Gnomonkey says he was picked second row by Odablock only because Odablock thought he would not be available later, with very little PvP and no Deadman experience beyond about two days of seasonal Deadman.[^lJexGv6dxu8-350]
* Gnomonkey says Odablock should have picked Mika (strong planning and shot-calling in multi) and Mutz (more Deadman and PvP experience, and capable of all of Gnomonkey's PvM) instead of him and Dubie Dobies.[^lJexGv6dxu8-383]
* Gnomonkey says the Odablock Warriors were used to playing ahead, with winner mentalities, so they struggled to pivot after being killed early; the team included two-time winner Rexy and Evscape and Dubies from Bodhi's team.[^lJexGv6dxu8-568]
* Gnomonkey says the team played for content and fun, chasing every breach and mission, which made the slow, boring rebuilding strategy unattractive.[^lJexGv6dxu8-657]
* Gnomonkey says he does not hate or blame any teammate; Raikesy did not know how to make a pie, Evscape had terrible gear and bad Doom luck, Dubies could not do other PvM tasks, and Odablock had to PK to get the team blood money.[^lJexGv6dxu8-2472]

## Events

* The Odablock Warriors, Gnomonkey's Deadman Mode Season 3 team, clear out the Dino Nuggets during the first breach and are then chased into a retreat.[^FwjOiTIPQXE-0]
* The Odablock Warriors see one of the Rhinos die and conclude the Rhinos were probably pushed by the Dino Nuggets, so they plan to sneak in again.[^FwjOiTIPQXE-265]
* At Falador the Odablock Warriors share a spade, a lit candle and a tinderbox so every teammate can re-enter, and say they are doing well for the cards they have been dealt.[^FwjOiTIPQXE-293]
* The Odablock Warriors decided to kill the last three monsters and then escape, because they expected the rival teams to come back in full force; they said they already had the tier fives they wanted.[^qyG_lTC4pDU-1399]
* Early on the Odablock Warriors put all their resources into Odablock, with temporary grinds, instead of grinding skills and quests, which put them further behind.[^lJexGv6dxu8-601]
* Gnomonkey gave a speech comparing the team to a big oak tree with dead roots that any wind knocks over, arguing they needed to fortify, skip breaches and rebuild, but the team struggled to do it.[^lJexGv6dxu8-628]
* After the plan changed, the Odablock Warriors stopped feeding Odablock every resource, raised stats, did quests and built a foundation, and Gnomonkey says it started working and they might have won if they had done it earlier.[^lJexGv6dxu8-1140]
* Odablock got all five Zenyte shards quickly on his own, Raikesy got toxic staffs and a blowpipe from Zulrah and several Serp helms, Dubie Dobies kept the team stocked with Barrows and Moons gear and got the team's one Void Waker after a long grind, and Gnomonkey farmed Tombs of Amascut for fangs.[^lJexGv6dxu8-1168]
