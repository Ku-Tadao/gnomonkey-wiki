## Stats and numbers

- Eclipse armour is the other drop from Eclipse Moon; the helmet and chest have three strength bonus each and the legs one, and it has decent ranged accuracy slightly below Karil's according to Gnomonkey. ([2025-02-22 0:56](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=56))
- The full Eclipse set effect gives a 20% chance when you hit something to proc one burn damage, which Gnomonkey says means one in five hits adds an extra 10 damage. ([2025-02-22 2:23](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=143))
- Gnomonkey says the Eclipse burn proc adds up to roughly 1.1 DPS assuming 100% accuracy, since it does not work on a miss, which is slightly under two thralls' worth of DPS. ([2025-02-22 2:54](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=174))
- Gnomonkey says each piece of the Eclipse set costs under 1.3 million gp, with the helmet under a million. ([2025-02-22 6:04](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=364))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey notes the Eclipse set effect is not included in Gearscape or, as far as he knows, the OSRS Wiki DPS calculator, so those under-report the Atlatl's DPS. ([2025-02-22 3:19](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=199))
- Gnomonkey says in raids the Atlatl's strength-based melee gear means the Eclipse set can double as melee gear, reducing switches: his melee switch in Chambers of Xeric is two items (maybe three with a defender). ([2025-02-22 6:40](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=400))
- To add the burn damage to a calculator result, Gnomonkey multiplies 10/9 (1.1 repeating) by the Atlatl's accuracy against the target and adds that to the displayed DPS. ([2025-02-22 8:38](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=518))
- Gnomonkey says the Eclipse burn effect does not apply to everything: targets such as Vasa (once it reaches the crystal) and Zulrah (whenever she goes underwater) lose it. ([2025-02-22 16:52](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=1012))
