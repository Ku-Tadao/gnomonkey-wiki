**Eclipse armour** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2025-02-22. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Stats and numbers

* Eclipse armour is the other drop from Eclipse Moon; the helmet and chest have three strength bonus each and the legs one, and it has decent ranged accuracy slightly below Karil's according to Gnomonkey.[^NxfbgWY3ZQk-56]
* The full Eclipse set effect gives a 20% chance when you hit something to proc one burn damage, which Gnomonkey says means one in five hits adds an extra 10 damage.[^NxfbgWY3ZQk-143]
* The Eclipse burn proc adds up to roughly 1.1 DPS assuming 100% accuracy, since it does not work on a miss, which is slightly under two thralls' worth of DPS.[^NxfbgWY3ZQk-174]
* Each piece of the Eclipse set costs under 1.3 million gp, with the helmet under a million.[^NxfbgWY3ZQk-364]

## Tips

* The Eclipse set effect is not included in Gearscape or, as far as he knows, the OSRS Wiki DPS calculator, so those under-report the Atlatl's DPS.[^NxfbgWY3ZQk-199]
* In raids the Atlatl's strength-based melee gear means the Eclipse set can double as melee gear, reducing switches: his melee switch in Chambers of Xeric is two items (maybe three with a defender).[^NxfbgWY3ZQk-400]
* To add the burn damage to a calculator result, Gnomonkey multiplies 10/9 (1.1 repeating) by the Atlatl's accuracy against the target and adds that to the displayed DPS.[^NxfbgWY3ZQk-518]
* The Eclipse burn effect does not apply to everything: targets such as Vasa (once it reaches the crystal) and Zulrah (whenever she goes underwater) lose it.[^NxfbgWY3ZQk-1012]
