**Woox walking** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2019-04-08 and 2026-07-22. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* The 5 step: click a tile 5 tiles away (counting the tile stood on), click Vorkath once the tile indicator disappears, then click 5 tiles back to the start and click Vorkath again, repeating.[^f_QYQ9TMF40-674]
* The 4 step is like the 5 step, but after each attack the player walks back one tile and left or right four tiles, moving in a horizontal line so they can walk underneath acid when there is not enough room for a 5 step.[^f_QYQ9TMF40-733]
* The 3 step is the most click-intensive and hardest method and needs only 6 tiles of space: attack Vorkath, click 3 tiles away on the back wall, click behind where the player attacked from, and click Vorkath again.[^f_QYQ9TMF40-733]
* Unkinking: if there is room for a 5 step but the character is oriented so it is not possible, click four tiles away, then when the indicator disappears click a tile left and back right (or vice versa); this wastes one tick but is worth it to Woox walk more easily.[^f_QYQ9TMF40-766]

## Tips

* The dragon hunter crossbow can fire every five ticks on rapid, so the player must walk at least four tiles before firing at Vorkath again during the acid phase.[^f_QYQ9TMF40-640]
* The back wall is exactly one tile away from the range at which the player can fire at Vorkath, so clicking Vorkath makes the player step forward; as soon as Vorkath throws his head back the player should walk to the back wall and begin Woox walking.[^f_QYQ9TMF40-640]
* RuneLite will not generate a tile indicator unless the tile itself is clicked directly, for example clicking the wall behind the tile gives no indicator.[^f_QYQ9TMF40-674]
* Gnomonkey likes the halberd in the Corrupted Gauntlet because you can Woox walk the tornadoes with it, a more advanced strategy that makes the fight easier once learned.[^CA6-2CqM_NQ-1491]
* Woox walking the Corrupted Gauntlet tornadoes is not foolproof, since he took a tornado hit while setting it up, but you can move back and forth to avoid most tornadoes.[^CA6-2CqM_NQ-1584]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey calls the 5 step the least click-intensive and simplest Woox walking method for Vorkath and recommends it for players who have never Woox walked, though it needs a lot of space.[^f_QYQ9TMF40-704]
* Gnomonkey says the 3 step is very rarely needed and recommends it only to players very comfortable with Vorkath.[^f_QYQ9TMF40-766]
