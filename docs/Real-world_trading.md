**Real-world trading** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2025-09-16. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Tips

* He has heard, as hearsay and not as something he knows, that buyers used to get a one to two week ban and have the cash stack removed only if it was left in the bank, while gold spent on items like a Zaryte crossbow or put into the Blast Furnace coffer was not taken away.[^K4IobWysTEs-445]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says Jagex has an incentive to be lenient with gold buyers because buyers are paying members who keep playing, while a banned bot just comes back and pays for membership again.[^K4IobWysTEs-385]
* Gnomonkey says if buyers could keep items bought with the gold, the only consequence would be a temporary ban, so he doubts whether Jagex will follow through on item confiscation and says the only way to find out is hearing from people who get banned.[^K4IobWysTEs-500]
* Gnomonkey asks whether Jagex will track muling, such as buyers trading the gold to alt accounts to avoid confiscation or bans.[^K4IobWysTEs-530]
* Gnomonkey says Jagex needs to lean towards permanent bans and take a scorched-earth approach to gold buyers, because they are the root of the market and bots will never stop coming back while gold buyers exist.[^K4IobWysTEs-558]
* Gnomonkey says he hopes Jagex really swings the hammer, believes they want to, and notes they have been hiring many people in anti-cheat.[^K4IobWysTEs-973]

## Events

* In September 2025 Jagex published a new blog post about real-world trading, announcing it would take a harsher line against players caught buying gold from illicit sellers.[^K4IobWysTEs-0]
* Jagex's blog defines real-world trading as buying or selling in the real world for real money in exchange for anything of value relating to Old School accounts, including items, names and services.[^K4IobWysTEs-211]
* Jagex's blog says real-world trading creates a financial incentive for people who do not care about the game to flood it with bots and throwaway accounts to gather gold to sell.[^K4IobWysTEs-211]
* Historically Jagex treated gold buyers more leniently than sellers when issuing penalties for real-world trading.[^K4IobWysTEs-359]
* According to the blog, Jagex will issue bans, temporary or permanent depending on the situation, even for first offences, and will confiscate items from offenders who buy gold.[^K4IobWysTEs-415]
