## Tips

- Gnomonkey says he has heard, as hearsay and not as something he knows, that buyers used to get a one to two week ban and have the cash stack removed only if it was left in the bank, while gold spent on items like a Zaryte crossbow or put into the Blast Furnace coffer was not taken away. ([2025-09-16 7:25](https://youtu.be/K4IobWysTEs?t=445))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says Jagex has an incentive to be lenient with gold buyers because buyers are paying members who keep playing, while a banned bot just comes back and pays for membership again. ([2025-09-16 6:25](https://youtu.be/K4IobWysTEs?t=385))
- Gnomonkey says if buyers could keep items bought with the gold, the only consequence would be a temporary ban, so he doubts whether Jagex will follow through on item confiscation and says the only way to find out is hearing from people who get banned. ([2025-09-16 8:20](https://youtu.be/K4IobWysTEs?t=500))
- Gnomonkey asks whether Jagex will track muling, such as buyers trading the gold to alt accounts to avoid confiscation or bans. ([2025-09-16 8:50](https://youtu.be/K4IobWysTEs?t=530))
- Gnomonkey says Jagex needs to lean towards permanent bans and take a scorched-earth approach to gold buyers, because they are the root of the market and bots will never stop coming back while gold buyers exist. ([2025-09-16 9:18](https://youtu.be/K4IobWysTEs?t=558))
- Gnomonkey says he hopes Jagex really swings the hammer, believes they want to, and notes they have been hiring many people in anti-cheat. ([2025-09-16 16:13](https://youtu.be/K4IobWysTEs?t=973))

## Events

- In September 2025 Jagex published a new blog post about real-world trading, announcing it would take a harsher line against players caught buying gold from illicit sellers. ([2025-09-16 0:00](https://youtu.be/K4IobWysTEs))
- Jagex's blog defines real-world trading as buying or selling in the real world for real money in exchange for anything of value relating to Old School accounts, including items, names and services. ([2025-09-16 3:31](https://youtu.be/K4IobWysTEs?t=211))
- Jagex's blog says real-world trading creates a financial incentive for people who do not care about the game to flood it with bots and throwaway accounts to gather gold to sell. ([2025-09-16 3:31](https://youtu.be/K4IobWysTEs?t=211))
- Gnomonkey says historically Jagex treated gold buyers more leniently than sellers when issuing penalties for real-world trading. ([2025-09-16 5:59](https://youtu.be/K4IobWysTEs?t=359))
- According to the blog, Jagex will issue bans, temporary or permanent depending on the situation, even for first offences, and will confiscate items from offenders who buy gold. ([2025-09-16 6:55](https://youtu.be/K4IobWysTEs?t=415))
