**Theatre of Blood: Hard Mode** is a raid topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2022-03-19 and 2023-10-29. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* In hard mode ToB, each room is harder, fewer supplies are given, and more supplies are needed to complete a run than in regular ToB.[^43IYFOMfalw-0]

## Tips

* The description credits SamSquanches, Chriskies and Flash, described as solo HMT masters, for guidance.[^43IYFOMfalw-0]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says hard mode Maiden makes blood spawns invincible so you want to freeze them, and each crab that reaches Maiden makes her attack a tick faster; he thinks it is about on par with regular Maiden, just more punishing, with less fun skill tech like MG or stacking.[^HkVt4enEesI-4600]
* Gnomonkey says hard mode Bloat turns more often and more randomly, drops meat even when he is down, and has more health, making Bloat less interesting because stomp tick tricks are now random and he can turn right into you, though it is still A tier.[^HkVt4enEesI-4693]
* Gnomonkey says hard mode Nylocas adds mini-bosses and reflects damage when you hit clumps with the wrong style, making it less skilled because chinning and barraging clumps is lost, and says hard mode Nylocas Vasilias punishes you for teammates' positioning (magic hits only the targeted player if clumped, ranged hits all) and is a step down from regular, still S tier.[^HkVt4enEesI-4752]
* Gnomonkey says hard mode Sotetseg shoots two death orbs to be split across teammates and has per-person maze chunks, which adds little, placing it about equal to regular Sotetseg.[^HkVt4enEesI-4925]
* Gnomonkey calls hard mode Xarpus a joke and easier than regular Xarpus because of lower defence and a final phase where he turns toward the last person who hit him, making it an AFK fight; he says no hard mode boss should be easier and puts it in high B tier.[^HkVt4enEesI-4987]
* Gnomonkey says hard mode Verzik is a big step up: rocks in phase 1, poison on the ground in phase 2, three yellow tiles per person in phase 3, a green ball you cannot tank so you must bounce it, and a heal at sub-5% health; he calls it one of the best-changed fights.[^HkVt4enEesI-5109]
* Gnomonkey says Theater of Blood hard mode overall is a step down from the regular raid since it takes longer and forces safer play, and he expected more mechanical changes; he ranks it right below regular Theater of Blood, though hard mode Verzik he places above regular Verzik.[^HkVt4enEesI-5291]
* Gnomonkey considers a solo Theatre of Blood hard mode run the hardest thing in Old School RuneScape outside of speedrunning: take solo ToB and multiply its difficulty several times over.[^43IYFOMfalw-0]
* Gnomonkey says the Nylocus Vasilias room is easily the hardest boss in the entire game, even harder than Awakened Leviathan.[^43IYFOMfalw-0]
* Gnomonkey summarises the solo hard mode ToB experience as one that sucks: Maiden is an RNG-dependent room with roughly a two in three chance of getting through, Bloat is six minutes of nothing, Nylocus is interesting but probably the hardest thing in the game, Sotetseg is normal Sotetseg with two balls, Xarpus is hard with limited tiles, and Verzik is winnable.[^43IYFOMfalw-2484]

## Records

* Gnomonkey completed his first solo hard mode ToB with zero deaths and an overall time stated as 11148 and a tick, after days of trial and error and roughly 200-300 attempts.[^43IYFOMfalw-2424]

## Events

* The successful solo HMT run was the very last run of a Halloween Cersei Lannister cosplay stream, which was a subscriber incentive for reaching 1000 subs in September.[^43IYFOMfalw-2424]

## Trivia

* Only a handful of people had completed a solo hard mode ToB; the description puts it at approximately 20-30 people, and Gnomonkey did not think he would ever attempt it.[^43IYFOMfalw-0]
* The completion chest gave no purple (no unique drop); the loot was around 400k.[^43IYFOMfalw-2451]
