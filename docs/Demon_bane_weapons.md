**Demon bane weapons** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2024-05-12 and 2024-07-15. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Gnomonkey reads that Demon Bane spells have an accuracy bonus of 20% with the Mark of Darkness spell active, and the accuracy bonus is increased to 40% with the player additionally gaining a 25% damage bonus.[^_nMz6X90hb0-29]

## Tips

* A Skull sceptre can autocast Demon Bane spells, which is why the wiki recommends it for K'ril Tsutsaroth.[^_nMz6X90hb0-25]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says Demon Bane is so bad that it seems like it was designed never to be used, speculating that Jagex did not want to power creep Shadow at K'ril; he says manually casting Demon Bane with Shadow would be overpowered.[^_nMz6X90hb0-270]
* Gnomonkey says Gearscape will not choose Demon Bane for K'ril even when he tries to force it, which he takes as proof of how bad the spell is.[^_nMz6X90hb0-375]
* Gnomonkey says you would have to be stupid to use Demon Bane at K'ril right now and that the only way to make the tool use it is to exclude about 80% of the mage weapons.[^_nMz6X90hb0-405]
* In the video description Gnomonkey says he hopes to see Jagex pull more ideas from RS3 and refine them to better suit OSRS.[^eIAkPiQivJk-0]
* Gnomonkey says Emberlight is an extremely good demon bane melee weapon, nearly as good as the Scythe on Cerberus and the new best-in-slot at Sire.[^eIAkPiQivJk-590]
* Gnomonkey says the Scorching bow is a big mention for those who want to essentially AFK Zamorak (K'ril Tsutsaroth) because it snares him and does a lot of damage, but it is only really good there, where it is absolutely disgusting.[^eIAkPiQivJk-590]
* Gnomonkey says the Purging staff seems by far the weakest of the three, and he would like Jagex to have it give all non-demon bane spells at least a small damage buff so it could be used to barrage other demons.[^eIAkPiQivJk-590]
* Gnomonkey says the demon bane weapons are not a bridge to anything in normal content but are best in slot for demons.[^eIAkPiQivJk-621]

## Events

* There are three new demon bane weapons, a bow, a staff and a sword, made by getting a synapse drop from Tormented Demons and infusing it into your weapon.[^eIAkPiQivJk-563]
* The Purging staff and Scorching bow are bugged at the time of the video, having a 20% lower max hit than they should against Tormented Demons.[^eIAkPiQivJk-621]
