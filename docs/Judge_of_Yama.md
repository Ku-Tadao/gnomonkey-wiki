## Methods

- Gnomonkey recommends being in mage for the Judge because you can hit it on the rocks on the way to it; the Judge phase needs one melee and one mage. ([2025-05-14 10:28](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=628))
- During the Judge phase, he swaps styles like a Tormented Demon, so the player must alternate melee and mage each attack. ([2025-05-14 5:02](https://youtu.be/5_IkechUxrw?t=302))
- The Judge fires skulls at the player's current location, and standing under him makes the skulls hit him for damage, like Araxxor step-unders, so stepping under and switching gives bonus damage. ([2025-05-14 5:32](https://youtu.be/5_IkechUxrw?t=332))
- After the Judge phase the specials are boosted into huge attacks; they are blocked the same way by stepping on the glyph tile but are more punishing if mistimed. ([2025-05-14 6:06](https://youtu.be/5_IkechUxrw?t=366))
- Waves in the fight take off the player's prayer, and the meteor turns off run so the player must be close to the glyph before it starts and cannot control-click like at Tormented Demons. ([2025-05-14 6:43](https://youtu.be/5_IkechUxrw?t=403))

## Tips

- The Judge's own attack can hurt itself: if you step under it while attacking, it hits itself with its skulls for extra damage. ([2025-05-14 14:42](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=882))
- You can pre-switch and mage the Judge once you reach the stone to hit a bit early; its skulls land on the ground where you stand like Tormented Demons, so step aside. ([2025-05-15 5:04](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=304))
- Stepping underneath the Judge when it shoots a skull makes the skull damage the Judge itself, a minor time save. ([2025-05-15 5:22](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=322))
- Gnomonkey advises eating when you reach the Judge if low on health, since there is downtime. ([2025-05-15 23:44](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=1424))

## Trivia

- The Judge of Yama is a guaranteed hit; both players swap between mage and melee and share one health pool. ([2025-05-14 4:30](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=270))
- At each phase Yama sends you to a side area where jumps across stones can be done every tick and the Judge of Yama shoots fireballs you must dodge. ([2025-05-15 4:38](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=278))
- The Judge is a guaranteed max hit on the correct style, alternating strictly mage hit then melee hit with no threshold like demonic gorillas. ([2025-05-15 4:38](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=278))
