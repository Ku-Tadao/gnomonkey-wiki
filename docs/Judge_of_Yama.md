**Judge of Yama** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2025-05-14 and 2025-05-15. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Gnomonkey recommends being in mage for the Judge because you can hit it on the rocks on the way to it; the Judge phase needs one melee and one mage.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-628]
* During the Judge phase, he swaps styles like a Tormented Demon, so the player must alternate melee and mage each attack.[^5_IkechUxrw-302]
* The Judge fires skulls at the player's current location, and standing under him makes the skulls hit him for damage, like Araxxor step-unders, so stepping under and switching gives bonus damage.[^5_IkechUxrw-332]
* After the Judge phase the specials are boosted into huge attacks; they are blocked the same way by stepping on the glyph tile but are more punishing if mistimed.[^5_IkechUxrw-366]
* Waves in the fight take off the player's prayer, and the meteor turns off run so the player must be close to the glyph before it starts and cannot control-click like at Tormented Demons.[^5_IkechUxrw-403]

## Tips

* The Judge's own attack can hurt itself: if you step under it while attacking, it hits itself with its skulls for extra damage.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-882]
* You can pre-switch and mage the Judge once you reach the stone to hit a bit early; its skulls land on the ground where you stand like Tormented Demons, so step aside.[^prE1BqM1wBE-304]
* Stepping underneath the Judge when it shoots a skull makes the skull damage the Judge itself, a minor time save.[^prE1BqM1wBE-322]
* Gnomonkey advises eating when you reach the Judge if low on health, since there is downtime.[^prE1BqM1wBE-1424]

## Trivia

* The Judge of Yama is a guaranteed hit; both players swap between mage and melee and share one health pool.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-270]
* At each phase Yama sends you to a side area where jumps across stones can be done every tick and the Judge of Yama shoots fireballs you must dodge.[^prE1BqM1wBE-278]
* The Judge is a guaranteed max hit on the correct style, alternating strictly mage hit then melee hit with no threshold like demonic gorillas.[^prE1BqM1wBE-278]
