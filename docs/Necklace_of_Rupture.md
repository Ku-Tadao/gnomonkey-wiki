# Necklace of Rupture

## Stats and numbers

- Before the changes, the Necklace of Rupture with Seeker arrows was roughly an 8 percent DPS increase with the Twisted bow, but it did not buff other ranged weapons like the Toxic blowpipe or Bow of Faerdhinen much. ([2026-02-17 4:29](https://youtu.be/kKccfwAJkh8?t=269))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey prefers the name Rupture over Paroxysm. ([2026-02-17 3:32](https://youtu.be/kKccfwAJkh8?t=212))
- Gnomonkey prefers a Reddit mock-up design in the Maggot King colour theme over Jagex's design, which he says looks like a kitted Berserker necklace. ([2026-02-17 4:01](https://youtu.be/kKccfwAJkh8?t=241))
- Jagex states it does not see balance as static and aims for no single combat style to be the obvious choice everywhere; Gnomonkey says the change is just enough that the arrows do not overtake items where they should not. ([2026-02-17 5:01](https://youtu.be/kKccfwAJkh8?t=301))
- Gnomonkey says the Seeker arrows are ridiculous, giga broken, and that Zuk is a different boss fight these days. ([2026-07-13 24:54](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=1494))

## Events

- The Necklace of Pursuit has its accuracy lowered to plus 20 and is being renamed either the Necklace of Rupture or the Necklace of Paroxysm. ([2026-02-17 3:02](https://youtu.be/kKccfwAJkh8?t=182))
- The necklace of rupture and Seeker arrows are new gear from the Maggot King content that Gnomonkey says make a first Inferno cape easier, with a massive DPS increase. ([2026-07-13 0:00](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8))
