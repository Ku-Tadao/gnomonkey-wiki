**Necklace of Rupture** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2026-02-17 and 2026-07-13. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Before the changes, the Necklace of Rupture with Seeker arrows was roughly an 8 percent DPS increase with the Twisted bow, but it did not buff other ranged weapons like the Toxic blowpipe or Bow of Faerdhinen much.[^kKccfwAJkh8-269]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey prefers the name Rupture over Paroxysm.[^kKccfwAJkh8-212]
* Gnomonkey prefers a Reddit mock-up design in the Maggot King colour theme over Jagex's design, which he says looks like a kitted Berserker necklace.[^kKccfwAJkh8-241]
* Jagex states it does not see balance as static and aims for no single combat style to be the obvious choice everywhere; Gnomonkey says the change is just enough that the arrows do not overtake items where they should not.[^kKccfwAJkh8-301]
* Gnomonkey says the Seeker arrows are ridiculous, giga broken, and that Zuk is a different boss fight these days.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1494]

## Events

* The Necklace of Pursuit has its accuracy lowered to plus 20 and is being renamed either the Necklace of Rupture or the Necklace of Paroxysm.[^kKccfwAJkh8-182]
* The necklace of rupture and Seeker arrows are new gear from the Maggot King content that Gnomonkey says make a first Inferno cape easier, with a massive DPS increase.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-0]
