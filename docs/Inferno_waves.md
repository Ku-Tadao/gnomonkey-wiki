## Methods

- On wave 63, Gnomonkey prays Augury to take less damage, uses a corner trap on the melee and kills the melee, calling this maneuver his default solve for the wave. ([2022-07-22 1:16:57](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=4617))
- Gnomonkey drops a few brews at the start of the Inferno and kills the first nibblers, which run to the nearest pillar and attack it. ([2026-07-13 8:30](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=510))
- On a wave with a melee, mager and bat, Gnomonkey shoots the mager from a tile where he can insta corner trap the melee when it digs, and prays mage, clicks the mager, specs, and resumes alternating. ([2026-07-13 40:12](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=2412))

## Tips

- Until wave 48 the goal is to maintain prayer and pillar health; Gnomonkey says you realistically shouldn't have used any brew until at least wave 48, but this is only a soft goal while learning. ([2021-05-21 23:08](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1388))
- On first attempts it is a good idea to use up your brews and restores to confirm you get later into the waves to learn them. ([2021-05-21 23:08](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1388))
- For waves with mages you can start waves without your crystal shield on to increase your chance of landing barrages. ([2021-05-21 24:12](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1452))
- From wave 49 (which introduces a ranger) do not put the ancestral top on at the start of waves; stay in Armadyl with an occult, Kodai and your shield, and start waves praying Augury to cut splash chance and damage from the ranger and blobs. ([2021-05-21 27:44](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1664))
- On waves with a mage and ranger, you must rapidly isolate them; if both are on you the same tick you will probably die, so get a pillar between you and one of them or off-tick and flick them if confident. ([2021-05-21 28:12](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1692))
- From wave 57 each wave has a mage, ranger and melee, all of which must be isolated under the melee's dig timer; priorities are melee first, then mage, then ranger, and the west pillar is irrelevant by then. ([2021-05-21 30:29](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1829))
- As soon as the last mage of wave 66 dies all pillars instantly go down, so do not stand next to one, and pause this wave as Jad is next. ([2021-05-21 32:30](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1950))
- On a bad wave 62 spawn with a ranger, mager and bats, Gnomonkey puts on Rigor, does an off-tick on ranger and mage, and does not kill the ranger to avoid a pillar stack. ([2022-07-22 1:14:31](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=4471))
- Gnomonkey says wave 65 is a good wave to be using Armadyl armour versus crystal as it is complete preference, with Armadyl having less prayer bonus and ranged defense but more DPS which is good for Zuk and triples. ([2022-07-22 1:19:00](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=4740))
- Gnomonkey says wave 65 is double ranger and it is really important to pre-Augury on it because you can get wombo'd, making it the most important wave to have 99 health, potentially taking about 95 damage at the start. ([2022-07-22 1:21:06](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=4866))
- On wave 66 Gnomonkey says the main idea is to kill the mages right next to each other so they do not respawn each other, and pull them together so you can use the blowpipe. ([2022-07-22 1:22:58](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=4978))
- From around wave 50 onward Gnomonkey wears his crystal body and prays Augury at wave start to prepare for a possible range hit. ([2022-11-04 1:10:40](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=4240))
- Gnomonkey says wave 64 is much easier and the pillar position is the easiest solve: anytime you see a mage there you should always go to the pillar. ([2022-11-04 1:34:00](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=5640))
- Gnomonkey says the top Inferno priority is not dying; if you can safely kill nibblers do it first in a wave, but after wave 50 you cannot safely chase nibblers, so never chase them. ([2026-07-13 8:30](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=510))
- Gnomonkey says it is okay for pillars to take some damage on dangerous waves like wave 31 rather than chasing nibblers. ([2026-07-13 9:01](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=541))
- Gnomonkey says to start every wave on the same tile because it has the most line of sight on nibblers and the least line of sight on mobs, minimising off-spawn hits. ([2026-07-13 9:28](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=568))
- Gnomonkey advises not to unequip your Crystal shield between waves: its negative mage bonus only changes accuracy from 96.35% to 96.69%, and people end up forgetting to put it on at wave 50. ([2026-07-13 10:01](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=601))
- Gnomonkey says to start waves in the 50s and 60s at full health, as health gives you options, and that you do not chase nibblers at that point, with nibblers 99% of the time getting just one cast. ([2026-07-13 46:30](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=2790))
- Gnomonkey says you can start wave 53 with Rigor or Augury and he prefers Rigor so you are set up to shoot something immediately. ([2026-07-13 46:30](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=2790))
- Gnomonkey says you rarely have to off-tick waves unless you get a very bad spawn, and a one-tick alternate that tanks a blob punch is inefficient but fine; he says many people discount an area because a monster is there when that area is still usable. ([2026-07-13 50:06](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=3006))
- On wave 63, Gnomonkey says to stay behind the pillar because running out would be deadly, corner trap the melee, heal off the ranger, then one-tick alternate the mager and a blob. ([2026-07-13 59:00](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=3540))
- Gnomonkey says the wave after (double mager) cannot be lost and not to flick it, and that no one has ever lost it. ([2026-07-13 1:02:19](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=3739))
