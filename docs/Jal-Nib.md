# Jal-Nib

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 8 videos (2021-05-21 to 2025-12-07). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Every time two of the same mob type are killed in a wave, a nibbler group (six nibblers) spawns in the middle of the arena. ([2025-02-26 10:26](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=626))
- From wave 50 onward, Gnomonkey says nibblers get one cast each and then you solve the wave, except nibblers on the north pillar, which you must get rid of. ([2025-12-07 1:02:38](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=3758))

## Stats and numbers

- Nibblers spawn every wave in groups of three, except the in-between waves where there are six and no other monsters, and they stop spawning on the final three waves. ([2021-05-21 11:11](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=671))
- Each wave three nibblers head to a random pillar (all nibblers go to the same pillar); each maxes a 4 and rapidly damages pillars when they arrive. ([2021-05-21 11:11](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=671))
- Nibblers have negative magic defence and extremely low stats (15 defence), making them the best target for Saradomin godsword specs and a great target for Blood Barrage. ([2021-05-21 11:51](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=711))
- Gnomonkey says nibblers have 15 magic level and negative 20 magic defence, so you can cast freeze spells on them no matter what even without much magic bonus. ([2023-07-12 0:58](https://youtu.be/I1NgO3f61yE?t=58))
- Nibblers have a mage level of 15 and -20 mage defence, so they have less mage defence than a goblin. ([2024-06-13 2:03](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=123))
- A six-nibbler stack has 80 health, so it is worth phantom barraging if you are ever low on health going into it. ([2024-06-13 16:43](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1003))
- In the video one nibbler did about 10 damage to a pillar with over 200 health while Gnomonkey sat there, and he says it would take about eight minutes to destroy it. ([2024-06-13 46:32](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2792))
- A single nibbler left on a pillar for a whole wave only did about 60 damage to the pillar, which Gnomonkey says shows chasing nibblers in late waves is not worth the risk. ([2025-12-07 1:11:48](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=4308))

## Tips

- Pillar priority is north first, south second and west lowest; the more aggressive you are with nibblers early, the more you can ignore them later. ([2021-05-21 17:15](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1035))
- At the start of a wave send an Ice or Blood Barrage at the nibblers depending on your health; if you cannot kill them safely, send one cast and get behind the pillar until the monsters fall into place. ([2021-05-21 17:15](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1035))
- On wave 66, nibblers can kill whichever pillar they go to; do not stand next to a pillar when it goes down or you take massive damage. ([2021-05-21 32:30](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1950))
- Gnomonkey says to kill Jal-Nib nibblers early as fast as possible so they do not eat pillar health, and avoid letting them munch on your pillar. ([2022-07-22 2:57](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=177))
- Gnomonkey says if only one nibbler is left, do not waste time barraging it over and over; just use the Toxic blowpipe, which is much faster and touches your pillar less. ([2022-07-22 14:53](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=893))
- Gnomonkey says a 40 XP drop on a barrage tells him the nibblers are gone. ([2022-07-22 30:29](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1829))
- Gnomonkey says a 30 XP drop tells him the nibblers are not all dead and he needs another cast. ([2022-07-22 30:29](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1829))
- Gnomonkey says when nibblers are on South pillar there is a specific tile that lets you shoot them from there, which he calls a godsend. ([2022-07-22 35:01](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2101))
- Gnomonkey says to just tank one blob hit when going to get nibblers rather than two-ticking and losing ticks and risking a mage hit, which equals about ten blob hits, because blobs' ranged hits almost never hit through high range defense. ([2022-07-22 35:59](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2159))
- Gnomonkey notes you can cast spells in a melee-only Inferno and that the magic bonus is only needed to catch the nibblers. ([2023-07-12 0:58](https://youtu.be/I1NgO3f61yE?t=58))
- Typically nibblers get one cast and then you solve the wave, going back to the nibblers once it is solved. ([2024-01-17 2:50](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=170))
- Gnomonkey says nibblers can be hit from a certain safe spot, and that magic is ideal because it is more likely to kill them, though Bow of Faerdhinen also works. ([2024-01-17 18:10](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=1090))
- Gnomonkey says you can also blood barrage nibblers to heal if you start a wave with lower health. ([2024-01-17 35:33](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=2133))
- Gnomonkey uses split XP and says a 30 XP drop tells him that a cast has killed all the nibblers in one hit. ([2024-06-13 21:32](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1292))
- You can phantom barrage the corpses of nibblers, and Gnomonkey phantoms the middle nibbler to check whether a clump of nibblers has died. ([2024-06-13 22:03](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1323))
- Gnomonkey says in later waves a nibbler on the west pillar is not a threat and he would be fine losing the west pillar. ([2024-06-13 44:58](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2698))
- Nibblers on your north pillar are a priority because you cannot drop your north pillar, but it is easy to protect since you can hit nibblers from behind the pillar. ([2024-06-13 49:31](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2971))
- If you are low on health at the start of a wave, you can heal off nibblers, since they have no mage defence. ([2025-02-26 18:31](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=1111))
- Gnomonkey says if it is six nibblers and one is not frozen, kill that one first, because you want the pillars safe and not taking unnecessary damage. ([2025-02-26 20:10](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=1210))
- Gnomonkey advises chasing nibblers as much as possible in waves 1-49 even if it costs health, because in the late waves there will be too many enemies to go get them, so you are preparing for the post-50 waves. ([2025-02-26 21:00](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=1260))
- In the 50s and later, Gnomonkey says to stop chasing every nibbler: only if one nibbler is on a pillar it does little damage over time, but two or three become a problem, and with high pillar health he starts letting nibblers go. ([2025-02-26 55:00](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=3300))
- Gnomonkey says not to chase nibblers in Inferno speeds: the two non-north pillars do nothing and can be dropped, and running to save them loses time and causes damage that must be healed. ([2025-07-13 11:01](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=661))
- Gnomonkey says to kill nibblers on the north pillar as fast as possible, but nibblers on the west or south pillars should only be dealt with when convenient, since with no pillar the nibblers come to you. ([2025-07-13 12:20](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=740))
- Gnomonkey says to only grab a nibbler if it is free to do so; if reaching it means tanking a melee and blob hit, give it one cast, go to the other side of the pillar and get it later. ([2025-07-13 17:00](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=1020))
- Gnomonkey does not freeze nibblers and uses blood barrage on them, because ice barrage splits the group so the other two keep going to the pillar, while blood barrage keeps them clumped to be killed together. ([2025-07-13 36:10](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=2170))
- Gnomonkey says six nibbler waves are guaranteed free health: a player low on health with a six nibbler wave coming can blood barrage them (phantom) for about a full heal and avoid brewing. ([2025-07-13 36:50](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=2210))
- Gnomonkey says that when both side pillars are gone the player should be aggressive with nibblers and deal with them whenever possible, killing any group of more than one immediately. ([2025-07-13 54:54](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=3294))
- Gnomonkey says you can phantom nibblers: a manual cast at a nibbler's corpse still hits the others, giving free health. ([2025-12-07 25:23](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=1523))
- Nibblers on your pillar are slightly too close (seven tiles away) to phantom, because a phantom needs at least eight tiles. ([2025-12-07 58:55](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=3535))
- In late waves you can pray a defensive prayer such as Piety because you almost always hit nibblers anyway, since they have negative mage defence and you have about 95% accuracy. ([2025-12-07 1:03:12](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=3792))

## Opinions

- On a first cape, Gnomonkey says nibblers are the absolute top priority to kill, since losing the north or south pillar can end a run. ([2021-05-21 11:51](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=711))
- Gnomonkey says mage accuracy does nothing for nibblers (96% hit chance, splashing extremely rare) and that what matters is max hit, since the higher your max hit the better the chance of one-shotting all the nibblers. ([2024-06-13 2:34](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=154))
- Gnomonkey says nibblers are maybe the most deadly NPC in Inferno because chasing them gets people killed, comparing it to chasing a ball into the road in front of a semi-truck. ([2024-06-13 44:29](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2669))
- Gnomonkey says not to chase nibblers to your south or west pillar in late waves because it is not worth dying for, comparing it to chasing a beach ball into traffic. ([2025-12-07 1:02:38](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=3758))

## Events

- In the full run Gnomonkey barely touched the nibblers on bad waves and the pillars still lasted, though he says he should have been more attentive to nibblers on north. ([2025-07-13 1:14:34](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=4474))
