**Tightrope (Chambers of Xeric)** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2024-11-08 and 2025-05-01. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* To have more than 22 health, that is at least 23 hitpoints, before the tightrope room, because the max hit of those enemies is 22.[^jdvQVwg8niM-1469]

## Methods

* For the two-Phoenix-necklace rope skip, equip crystal and a Lance defender, cross with ranged prayer up (Gnomonkey uses Augury for defence), and equip a Phoenix necklace so it procs and nulls the incoming damage, then grab the crystal, put on another Phoenix necklace and cross back.[^jdvQVwg8niM-1469]

## Stats and numbers

* The Phoenix necklace nulls all incoming damage after the first hit splat, so you take only a single hit splat each way.[^jdvQVwg8niM-1500]

## Tips

* There is a way to skip the Tightrope room but it is difficult and switch-heavy, so while learning he recommends just killing them.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2232]
* Against the Deathly mage at Tightrope, flinch in because it attacks every four ticks while the bow is five ticks, making it attack slower.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2247]
* To try to brew every fifteen seconds on your overload timing so brewing costs no stat drain, and to swap into melee armour at Tightrope to take less damage.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2247]
* In a no-prep run killing the rope wastes a lot of brew, so skipping rope in a no-prep CM makes it a lot safer.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-4511]
* You can also kill the rope, which is solid for points; bring brews for it and take more from the chest.[^jdvQVwg8niM-1564]
* The double Phoenix necklace skip will not work in a full Challenge Mode because the max hits are too high, which is why people use the Kirby skip there.[^jdvQVwg8niM-1597]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the two-Phoenix-necklace rope skip is much easier than the Kirby skip because the timing is more lax.[^jdvQVwg8niM-1532]
