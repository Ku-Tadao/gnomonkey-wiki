# Tightrope (Chambers of Xeric)

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2024-11-08 to 2025-05-01). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- For the two-Phoenix-necklace rope skip, equip crystal and a Lance defender, cross with ranged prayer up (Gnomonkey uses Augury for defence), and equip a Phoenix necklace so it procs and nulls the incoming damage, then grab the crystal, put on another Phoenix necklace and cross back. ([2025-05-01 24:29](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1469))

## Requirements

- Gnomonkey says to have more than 22 health, that is at least 23 hitpoints, before the tightrope room, because the max hit of those enemies is 22. ([2025-05-01 24:29](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1469))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says the Phoenix necklace nulls all incoming damage after the first hit splat, so you take only a single hit splat each way. ([2025-05-01 25:00](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1500))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says there is a way to skip the Tightrope room but it is difficult and switch-heavy, so while learning he recommends just killing them. ([2024-11-08 37:12](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=2232))
- Against the Deathly mage at Tightrope, flinch in because it attacks every four ticks while the bow is five ticks, making it attack slower. ([2024-11-08 37:27](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=2247))
- Gnomonkey says to try to brew every fifteen seconds on your overload timing so brewing costs no stat drain, and to swap into melee armour at Tightrope to take less damage. ([2024-11-08 37:27](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=2247))
- Gnomonkey says in a no-prep run killing the rope wastes a lot of brew, so skipping rope in a no-prep CM makes it a lot safer. ([2024-11-08 1:15:11](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=4511))
- Gnomonkey says you can also kill the rope, which is solid for points; bring brews for it and take more from the chest. ([2025-05-01 26:04](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1564))
- Gnomonkey says the double Phoenix necklace skip will not work in a full Challenge Mode because the max hits are too high, which is why people use the Kirby skip there. ([2025-05-01 26:37](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1597))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the two-Phoenix-necklace rope skip is much easier than the Kirby skip because the timing is more lax. ([2025-05-01 25:32](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1532))
