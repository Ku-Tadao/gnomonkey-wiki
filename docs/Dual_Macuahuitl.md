# Dual Macuahuitl

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2024-09-25 to 2024-09-28). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says the Bopper maces are about 20% worse than a scythe on The Hueycoatl, which he calls not that much worse. ([2024-09-25 1:25](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=85))
- Gnomonkey says the Bopper set is only good with the full Blood moon set because the set effect makes it a three-tick attack, boosting damage a lot. ([2024-09-25 6:35](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=395))
- The full Blood moon set gives a 33% chance to attack one tick earlier on a successful hit, allowing another attack after three ticks instead of four; both hits count independently, giving a five in nine chance, for roughly a 3.5 tick attack speed. ([2024-09-28 4:30](https://youtu.be/-h7yElF9Z0c?t=270))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says if you do not have Bopper maces but have glacial hammers, they are very similar and a solid alternative; Boppers alone can be used for the whole kill. ([2024-09-25 0:36](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=36))
- Gnomonkey says on an ironman he would first kill sulphur nagas without a double-hit weapon until getting Sulphur blades, possibly take them to the frost nagas, then use glacial hammers, and eventually go to the Boppers by doing Moons. ([2024-09-28 5:06](https://youtu.be/-h7yElF9Z0c?t=306))
- Gnomonkey says Blood moon with the Bopper maces is best in slot for sulphur nagas even in max gear. ([2024-09-28 10:19](https://youtu.be/-h7yElF9Z0c?t=619))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey calls the Dual macuahuitl (Bopper) maces best in slot on The Hueycoatl's tail, saying they are not optional for the body parts. ([2024-09-25 0:54](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=54))
- Gnomonkey says the new frost nagas' glacial hammers are a lot better on the tail than a Lance, that he bought a pair on day one for 1 million gp, and that they should be cheap in general. ([2024-09-25 6:54](https://youtu.be/e0g1Ff667G0?t=414))
- Gnomonkey says you want a double-hit weapon on sulphur nagas, to get the extra eight from flat armour instead of the extra four. ([2024-09-28 2:34](https://youtu.be/-h7yElF9Z0c?t=154))
- Gnomonkey says the Bopper maces (Dual macuahuitl) are the best weapon for sulphur nagas because they are crush, have a higher crush bonus and strength, and are not that rare from the boss, about one in a hundred as he says. ([2024-09-28 4:30](https://youtu.be/-h7yElF9Z0c?t=270))
