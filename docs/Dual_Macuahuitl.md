**Dual Macuahuitl** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2024-09-25 and 2024-09-28. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* The Bopper maces are about 20% worse than a scythe on The Hueycoatl, which he calls not that much worse.[^e0g1Ff667G0-85]
* The Bopper set is only good with the full Blood moon set because the set effect makes it a three-tick attack, boosting damage a lot.[^e0g1Ff667G0-395]
* The full Blood moon set gives a 33% chance to attack one tick earlier on a successful hit, allowing another attack after three ticks instead of four; both hits count independently, giving a five in nine chance, for roughly a 3.5 tick attack speed.[^-h7yElF9Z0c-270]

## Tips

* If you do not have Bopper maces but have glacial hammers, they are very similar and a solid alternative; Boppers alone can be used for the whole kill.[^e0g1Ff667G0-36]
* On an ironman he would first kill sulphur nagas without a double-hit weapon until getting Sulphur blades, possibly take them to the frost nagas, then use glacial hammers, and eventually go to the Boppers by doing Moons.[^-h7yElF9Z0c-306]
* Blood moon with the Bopper maces is best in slot for sulphur nagas even in max gear.[^-h7yElF9Z0c-619]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey calls the Dual macuahuitl (Bopper) maces best in slot on The Hueycoatl's tail, saying they are not optional for the body parts.[^e0g1Ff667G0-54]
* Gnomonkey says the new frost nagas' glacial hammers are a lot better on the tail than a Lance, that he bought a pair on day one for 1 million gp, and that they should be cheap in general.[^e0g1Ff667G0-414]
* Gnomonkey says you want a double-hit weapon on sulphur nagas, to get the extra eight from flat armour instead of the extra four.[^-h7yElF9Z0c-154]
* Gnomonkey says the Bopper maces (Dual macuahuitl) are the best weapon for sulphur nagas because they are crush, have a higher crush bonus and strength, and are not that rare from the boss, about one in a hundred as he says.[^-h7yElF9Z0c-270]
