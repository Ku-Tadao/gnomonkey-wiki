**Dagannoth Kings** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2022-03-12 and 2022-04-19. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* Dagannoth Kings has about the highest gear requirement of almost any boss because you are tribrid, especially with the alt account.[^QNv-UATHGPs-33]
* Much of the gear can be downgraded, but a Twisted bow is very important for being able to hop between caves; a Bow of Faerdhinen would technically work but is 30% worse DPS than Twisted bow here.[^QNv-UATHGPs-62]

## Methods

* His efficient method jumps between the DK caves, which dramatically speeds the kills up and turns it into a 'mini Inferno solve'; he calls it great practice for Inferno.[^QNv-UATHGPs-0]
* With an alt carrying supplies, Gnomonkey is max ranged initially with Primordial boots and Berserker ring, and uses a six-way melee switch (downgrading Torva to Bandos is not a big deal).[^QNv-UATHGPs-90]
* He brings Harmonised nightmare staff with only the major damage-boosting magic items; Ancestral would be a DPS boost but trip lengths are limited by prayer potions so he does not bring it.[^QNv-UATHGPs-116]
* Main account supplies are two antidotes and two each of divine super combat and divine ranging, two rune throwing axes to get into the caves, eight prayer potions, Saradomin godsword for sustain, and a Max cape; replace the cape with a construction cape or Relleka teleport if you do not have one.[^QNv-UATHGPs-116]
* He uses the stamina boost on the Max cape to reach DKs (otherwise a stamina or Explorer's ring), and a rune pouch with Wrath, Nature and Air runes to alch drops with the Tome of fire/water equipped.[^QNv-UATHGPs-149]
* The alt wears Graceful with some tank gear (Hitpoints cape, regen bracelet, Bryophyta's staff) and packs three sets of divines, two extra antidotes, a stamina potion, an emergency brew and fourteen extra prayer potions (one should be a restore if bringing the brew).[^QNv-UATHGPs-175]
* The alt also brings a Relleka teleport and cosmic, astral, blood, body and law runes to cast Heal Other, Energy Transfer and Dream to regenerate HP faster; the highest requirement is 91 Magic for Energy Transfer, and an alt without the Magic level can still carry supplies.[^QNv-UATHGPs-175]
* Without an alt, downgrade the Harmonised staff for something else and bring a special weapon if you have it since Spinolyps can drain your prayer; bring about three regular ranging and super combat potions (no spare HP for divines), plus Sanfew serums for prayer and antidote effect.[^QNv-UATHGPs-205]
* Without an alt you need a pet rock to get there, two rune throwing axes, an emergency brew, Saradomin godsword, Imbued heart, and a pouch with Blood, Death and Soul runes on Ancients for Blood Barrage healing on Rex, plus an Explorer's ring for the run through the lair and alching.[^QNv-UATHGPs-235]
* Prep a stamina dose on the main, have both accounts teleport to Relleka with the alt following the main, head to Waterbirth Island and into the cave, and camp prayer on both accounts.[^QNv-UATHGPs-267]
* In the first cave, stand on both pressure plates to open the door, run past the crabs and use a rune throwing axe special attack on the barricade; the axes can be dropped afterwards.[^QNv-UATHGPs-267]
* Prayer along the run: ladder down praying magic, next ladder ranged, past the crabs praying melee, then ranged past the Dagannoths, magic against the wall of Sulki (Slashers/Spinolyps area), then melee past the crabs, with Gnomonkey spec-transferring from his alt for the final burst of run energy.[^QNv-UATHGPs-300]
* At the end of the run, pray melee past the lobsters, pass the DK trapdoors and stand in the safe corner on both accounts; the alt rests there to recover HP and, once he loses aggro after about ten minutes, sits in front of the route to Heal Other and spec-transfer the main when he comes up from the king's lair.[^QNv-UATHGPs-328]
* To recover: grab a pet rock, throwing axes, prayer potions and (more than he does) food from the bank, run the same route, go down praying ranged, and wait until Prime is not at the grave by going up and down; right-click the grave and loot, going up and down to equip gear and drop items for the rest.[^QNv-UATHGPs-536]

## Stats and numbers

* At an average of about 27k gp per king, 120 king kills per hour comes to about 3.2m gp per hour before supply costs, which Gnomonkey says is a lot higher than he expected.[^QNv-UATHGPs-33]
* Zaryte vambraces are absolutely massive here, raising max hit from 83 to 86 with the max ranged boost; the boost is smaller if you cannot use divines because you have no alt.[^QNv-UATHGPs-90]
* The kings' main drops are the rings; the only valuable ones are Berserker ring (currently 2.2m) and Archers ring (about 4m); Gnomonkey says he does not know why the Archers ring has value because it is nearly useless.[^QNv-UATHGPs-565]
* The regular drops, mainly bones, add up; Harmonised nightmare staff helps reduce supply cost, but it is still not cheap, though 3.2m gp per hour before supplies is not terrible.[^QNv-UATHGPs-595]
* There is a pet for each king, each 1/5,000; on paper that is about 125 hours for all three at 120 kills per hour, but in practice longer because of the run and because efficiency drops drastically once you get one pet.[^QNv-UATHGPs-595]

## Tips

* He highly recommends completing Fremennik Elites (the Fremennik elite diary) before coming, as it notes all the bone drops, which are a significant chunk of the profit.[^QNv-UATHGPs-62]
* Gnomonkey found the alt alone was not enough sustain, hence the Saradomin godsword.[^QNv-UATHGPs-116]
* Equip the Tome first in the switch or it will remove your autocast, and make sure you are on the standard spellbook.[^QNv-UATHGPs-149]
* Without an alt, Gnomonkey says it will take some flicking to get through a task.[^QNv-UATHGPs-205]
* Before going to the DK lair, turn the NPC Aggression Timer plugin on for the alt; it must be done before arriving or it will not work.[^QNv-UATHGPs-235]
* Hop worlds at the crack until you find a world with no adventurers in either cave.[^QNv-UATHGPs-359]
* At DKs the grave stays in the lair and you have fifteen minutes to get back, which Gnomonkey says is more than enough time, despite horror stories of people sending their grave to Death's coffer and paying over 30m to retrieve items.[^QNv-UATHGPs-536]
* If you die a second time while grabbing items they will likely go to the coffer before you can get back, so be careful and do not panic.[^QNv-UATHGPs-565]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the Dagannoth Rex pet is super common (everyone grinds Rex as a noob), a bland little grey dinosaur; he thinks Prime is the best of the Dagannoth Kings' pets and Prime is better than Supreme, but none are great.[^vwCm9NKJhRE-349]
* Gnomonkey places the Dagannoth Kings' pets in D tier initially and later E tier, saying he does not like them because they are bland and not super rare.[^vwCm9NKJhRE-349]
* Gnomonkey says fighting the Dagannoth Kings in one cave while waiting for respawns is F tier, but going back and forth between two caves with reactive off-ticking of Supreme and Prime is interesting; it is one of the only reactive content besides the Inferno.[^HkVt4enEesI-1432]
* Gnomonkey says Dagannoth Kings take some skill to set up off-ticks but are simple once you have a method, and he places them in high B tier, near the top of B.[^HkVt4enEesI-1463]
* Gnomonkey describes Dagannoth Kings as the old classic staple of Old School RuneScape, known as a fairly easy and boring Slayer boss.[^QNv-UATHGPs-0]
* Gnomonkey says the main reason anyone does Dagannoth Kings is the pets, and recommends doing regular Dagannoths for Slayer until you have strong gear to come back to the kings.[^QNv-UATHGPs-33]
* Gnomonkey says Blood fury could be used for no max hit loss with Torva but is expensive and kind of unnecessary, and may allow claws instead of SGS though he has not tested it.[^QNv-UATHGPs-90]
* Gnomonkey thinks Dream and Heal Other are plenty of HP for the alt, though you could theoretically use Guthan's to heal.[^QNv-UATHGPs-204]
* Gnomonkey calls this one of the longest and hardest runs for any boss in the game.[^QNv-UATHGPs-235]

## Records

* Doing all three kings with his method, Gnomonkey says you can reach about 120 king kills per hour.[^QNv-UATHGPs-0]

## Trivia

* Gnomonkey died during the trip in a 'very dumb way' and used it to show how to recover items, since DKs is one of the most dangerous places to die in the game.[^QNv-UATHGPs-508]
