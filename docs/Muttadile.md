# Muttadile

## Methods

- The strategy for the Muttadile room is running up to the meat tree and chopping it down while casting at the baby Muttadile until it is under 50% HP. ([2023-02-02 11:27](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=687))
- After chopping the tree he equips full mage gear and summons a thrall; the walk under method with offensive prayer flicks reduces damage a little and mostly increases survival odds. ([2023-02-02 12:29](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=749))
- Gnomonkey brews up until running low on brews, then starts sweet tick eating every attack; when one of them falls he grabs brew and revite and moves on. ([2023-02-02 12:58](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=778))
- Gnomonkey kills Muttadile first, takes the overload it drops and sips it immediately for the rest of the raid, so he only needs one dose of a pre-raid potion rather than multi-dose. ([2024-10-27 6:03](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=363))
- In Muttadile's room, Gnomonkey runs straight to the tree, drops an item, and chops; between each XP drop he throws a hammer-style attack at Muttadile (throwing at the baby does not interrupt chopping), and chopping damage depends on his Woodcutting level. ([2024-10-27 6:34](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=394))
- Pray melee when near Muttadile and range when far; every XP drop he casts at it, since chopping and attacking share the same five-tick cycle, and he tries to get it near 50% health before chopping the tree down, as below 50% it eats the tree. ([2024-11-08 46:04](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=2764))
- Gnomonkey camps Augury for defence, flinches under the Muttadile and keeps health high, one-way maging, then throws on the Zamorak godsword and specs just before it drops below 50% health to freeze it. ([2025-05-01 31:45](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1905))

## Stats and numbers

- The combined max hit of the baby's off-prayer ranged attack and Mama Muttadile's magic attack means you can potentially take 89 damage in a single game tick. ([2023-02-02 12:00](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=720))
- Mama Muttadile maxes 38 with any given attack, so even constantly brewing she can kill you if she maxes each attack. ([2023-02-02 12:29](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=749))
- Gnomonkey says Muttadiles max hit is 38. ([2023-06-14 21:08](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=1268))

## Tips

- There is a Tentacle skip setup involving waiting a tick before the room to run past the Tentacles a bit faster. ([2023-02-02 11:27](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=687))
- Burning through brews in the Muttadile room can mean the difference between having two brews or six brews for Olm, and it is completely random. ([2023-02-02 12:00](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=720))
- Gnomonkey uses a tendril skip at Muttadiles by clicking to a spot so the tendril spawns behind him and he runs straight past it. ([2023-06-14 19:40](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=1180))
- The Muttadiles trees work the same as Ice Demon trees: every five ticks, as long as you are chopping when it gets red. ([2023-06-14 19:40](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=1180))
- Gnomonkey says Muttadiles is four-tick, so you five-tick step under it exactly like Nex. ([2023-06-14 20:39](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=1239))
- Gnomonkey says if Muttadile drops below 50% health he heals off the tree, so you want to avoid that, and says to pray melee when he gets close and expect to take a lot of damage from random ranged attacks. ([2024-10-27 7:02](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=422))
- Gnomonkey says with Muttadile you can do two throws then step under until you are off cooldown and only take one hit to two of yours. ([2024-10-27 7:34](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=454))
- Gnomonkey says not to walk into melee range of the mother Muttadile because she can one-shot you, and that she does at least three mage attacks before switching to ranged, so you can pray mage for three attacks then swap back. ([2024-10-27 8:05](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=485))
- Before Muttadile Gnomonkey equips tank (melee) armour and just the Sanguinesti staff, and brews a lot, since Muttadile can hit for 70 damage at any moment. ([2024-11-08 45:23](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=2723))
- Gnomonkey mentions Tendril skip as an option but does not demonstrate it in this run. ([2024-11-08 45:50](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=2750))
- When re-overloading in the Muttadile room, spam brew. ([2024-11-08 46:04](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=2764))
- Gnomonkey says the Zamorak godsword saves brews in no-prep because you freeze both Muttadiles before they reach the tree and kill them, rather than tanking the whole time. ([2025-05-01 32:15](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1935))
- For the mother Muttadile, get to the corner quickly, and Gnomonkey flicks Bandos to make himself tankier, which is optional. ([2025-05-01 32:45](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1965))
- If your Zamorak godsword spec misses, stand on a particular tile because the mother Muttadile gets stuck there when going back to eat the tree and cannot hit you. ([2025-05-01 33:15](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1995))
- Switch to protect from magic when the mother Muttadile starts maging, because she guarantees at least three mage hits in a row. ([2025-05-01 33:15](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1995))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey calls Muttadile the worst boss in the game and says one of the main reasons he made the video was to rant about it. ([2022-03-19 44:48](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2688))
- Gnomonkey says Big Muttadile has an instant-kill melee attack and that you can be comboed for a 60 or more in one hit; he says Jagex reduced combo potential by raising the minimum and lowering the maximum hit, but he would be happy to see the boss removed. ([2022-03-19 46:23](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2783))
- Gnomonkey says Muttadiles are still the worst boss in the game, as he has said in the past. ([2023-02-02 11:27](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=687))
- Gnomonkey says Muttadile is a very high damage room where you should be ready to spam brew. ([2024-10-27 8:38](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=518))
- Gnomonkey calls Muttadile the worst boss in the entire game. ([2024-11-08 45:05](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=2705))

## Trivia

- Gnomonkey describes the small Muttadile as sporadic, sometimes backing off and sometimes approaching, switching between ranged and melee even while biting, and sometimes not allowing safe spotting around the tree. ([2022-03-19 44:48](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2688))
- Gnomonkey says freezing the small Muttadile needs someone beyond Ancient magic, big Muttadile cannot really be frozen because of its very high magic level (needing a ZGS), and in CMs at most scales you just chop the tree while being hit. ([2022-03-19 45:51](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2751))
