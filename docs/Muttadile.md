**Muttadile** is the [Chambers of Xeric](Chambers_of_Xeric.md) meat tree room, and Gnomonkey calls it the worst boss in the game. He first states this in March 2022 in a video made partly to rant about it[^HkVt4enEesI-2688], repeats it in 2023[^F0qIr7azCwg-687] and again in 2024[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2705], and says he would be happy to see it removed[^HkVt4enEesI-2783]. He calls it a very high damage room where the player should be ready to spam brew[^wxGBi89vE_s-518].

## Mechanics

* The small Muttadile is sporadic: sometimes backing off, sometimes approaching, switching between ranged and melee even while biting, and sometimes not allowing safe spotting around the tree[^HkVt4enEesI-2688]. Freezing it needs someone beyond Ancient magic, and the big Muttadile cannot really be frozen because of its very high magic level (needing a ZGS). In Challenge Mode at most scales the player just chops the tree while being hit[^HkVt4enEesI-2751].
* Big Muttadile has an instant-kill melee attack and can combo for 60 or more in one hit. Jagex reduced combo potential by raising the minimum and lowering the maximum hit[^HkVt4enEesI-2783]. The combined max hit of the baby's off-prayer ranged attack and Mama Muttadile's magic attack means the player can take 89 in a single game tick[^F0qIr7azCwg-720]. Mama maxes 38 with any given attack, so even constantly brewing she can kill the player if she maxes each attack[^F0qIr7azCwg-749][^8JxarzSiiME-1268]. In 2024 he warns she can hit for 70 at any moment[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2723].
* Mama does at least three mage attacks in a row before switching to ranged[^wxGBi89vE_s-485][^jdvQVwg8niM-1995].
* Muttadiles are four-tick, so the player five-tick steps under it exactly like Nex[^8JxarzSiiME-1239]. The trees work the same as the Ice Demon trees: every five ticks, as long as the player is chopping when it gets red[^8JxarzSiiME-1180].
* If the Muttadile drops below 50% health it heals off the tree, which the player wants to avoid[^wxGBi89vE_s-422][^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2764].

## Strategy

### Basic approach

Run up to the meat tree and chop it down while casting at the baby Muttadile until it is under 50% HP[^F0qIr7azCwg-687]. In 2024 he runs straight to the tree, drops an item and chops; between each XP drop he throws a hammer-style attack at it (throwing at the baby does not interrupt chopping), and chopping damage depends on Woodcutting level[^wxGBi89vE_s-394]. Chopping and attacking share the same five-tick cycle, so he casts on every XP drop, trying to get the Muttadile near 50% before the tree falls[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2764]. Do not walk into melee range of the mother, who can one-shot[^wxGBi89vE_s-485].

Prayer: pray melee when near Muttadile and range when far[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2764], and pray melee when she gets close, expecting a lot of damage from random ranged attacks[^wxGBi89vE_s-422]. Switch to Protect from Magic when the mother begins maging, since she guarantees at least three mage hits in a row[^wxGBi89vE_s-485][^jdvQVwg8niM-1995].

After chopping the tree, he equips full mage gear and summons a thrall. The walk-under method with offensive prayer flicks reduces damage a little and mostly increases survival odds[^F0qIr7azCwg-749]. With two throws then stepping under until off cooldown, the player takes one hit to two of his own[^wxGBi89vE_s-454].

### Supplies

He brews until running low, then sweet tick eats every attack; when one of them falls he grabs brew and revite and moves on[^F0qIr7azCwg-778]. Burning brews in the room can mean the difference between two and six brews for Olm, and it is completely random[^F0qIr7azCwg-720]. He equips tank (melee) armour and just the Sanguinesti staff and brews a lot[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2723]. When re-overloading, spam brew[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2764]. He kills Muttadile first and sips the overload it drops immediately for the rest of the raid, so only one dose of a pre-raid potion is needed rather than a multi-dose[^wxGBi89vE_s-363].

### Skips and tricks

* Tentacle skip: wait a tick before the room to run past the tentacles a bit faster[^F0qIr7azCwg-687].
* Tendril skip: click to a spot so the tendril spawns behind the player, then run straight past it[^8JxarzSiiME-1180]. In November 2024 he mentions it as an option but does not demonstrate it[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2750].

### No-prep approach (2025)

In May 2025 he camps Augury for defence, flinches under the Muttadile and keeps health high, one-way mages, and throws on the Zamorak godsword to spec just before it falls below 50% health, freezing it[^jdvQVwg8niM-1905]. This saves brews in no-prep because both Muttadiles are frozen before they reach the tree and killed instead of being tanked throughout[^jdvQVwg8niM-1935]. For the mother, get to the corner quickly; he flicks Bandos to be tankier, which is optional[^jdvQVwg8niM-1965]. If the Zamorak godsword spec misses, stand on a particular tile because the mother gets stuck there when going back to eat the tree and cannot hit the player[^jdvQVwg8niM-1995].
