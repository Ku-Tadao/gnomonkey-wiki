**Muttadile** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 6 videos between 2022-03-19 and 2025-05-01. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* The strategy for the Muttadile room is running up to the meat tree and chopping it down while casting at the baby Muttadile until it is under 50% HP.[^F0qIr7azCwg-687]
* After chopping the tree he equips full mage gear and summons a thrall; the walk under method with offensive prayer flicks reduces damage a little and mostly increases survival odds.[^F0qIr7azCwg-749]
* Gnomonkey brews up until running low on brews, then starts sweet tick eating every attack; when one of them falls he grabs brew and revite and moves on.[^F0qIr7azCwg-778]
* Gnomonkey kills Muttadile first, takes the overload it drops and sips it immediately for the rest of the raid, so he only needs one dose of a pre-raid potion rather than multi-dose.[^wxGBi89vE_s-363]
* In Muttadile's room, Gnomonkey runs straight to the tree, drops an item, and chops; between each XP drop he throws a hammer-style attack at Muttadile (throwing at the baby does not interrupt chopping), and chopping damage depends on his Woodcutting level.[^wxGBi89vE_s-394]
* Pray melee when near Muttadile and range when far; every XP drop he casts at it, since chopping and attacking share the same five-tick cycle, and he tries to get it near 50% health before chopping the tree down, as below 50% it eats the tree.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2764]
* Gnomonkey camps Augury for defence, flinches under the Muttadile and keeps health high, one-way maging, then throws on the Zamorak godsword and specs just before it drops below 50% health to freeze it.[^jdvQVwg8niM-1905]

## Stats and numbers

* The combined max hit of the baby's off-prayer ranged attack and Mama Muttadile's magic attack means you can potentially take 89 damage in a single game tick.[^F0qIr7azCwg-720]
* Mama Muttadile maxes 38 with any given attack, so even constantly brewing she can kill you if she maxes each attack.[^F0qIr7azCwg-749]
* Muttadiles max hit is 38.[^8JxarzSiiME-1268]

## Tips

* There is a Tentacle skip setup involving waiting a tick before the room to run past the Tentacles a bit faster.[^F0qIr7azCwg-687]
* Burning through brews in the Muttadile room can mean the difference between having two brews or six brews for Olm, and it is completely random.[^F0qIr7azCwg-720]
* Gnomonkey uses a tendril skip at Muttadiles by clicking to a spot so the tendril spawns behind him and he runs straight past it.[^8JxarzSiiME-1180]
* The Muttadiles trees work the same as Ice Demon trees: every five ticks, as long as you are chopping when it gets red.[^8JxarzSiiME-1180]
* Muttadiles is four-tick, so you five-tick step under it exactly like Nex.[^8JxarzSiiME-1239]
* If Muttadile drops below 50% health he heals off the tree, so you want to avoid that, and says to pray melee when he gets close and expect to take a lot of damage from random ranged attacks.[^wxGBi89vE_s-422]
* With Muttadile you can do two throws then step under until you are off cooldown and only take one hit to two of yours.[^wxGBi89vE_s-454]
* Not to walk into melee range of the mother Muttadile because she can one-shot you, and that she does at least three mage attacks before switching to ranged, so you can pray mage for three attacks then swap back.[^wxGBi89vE_s-485]
* Before Muttadile Gnomonkey equips tank (melee) armour and just the Sanguinesti staff, and brews a lot, since Muttadile can hit for 70 damage at any moment.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2723]
* Tendril skip as an option but does not demonstrate it in this run.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2750]
* When re-overloading in the Muttadile room, spam brew.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2764]
* The Zamorak godsword saves brews in no-prep because you freeze both Muttadiles before they reach the tree and kill them, rather than tanking the whole time.[^jdvQVwg8niM-1935]
* For the mother Muttadile, get to the corner quickly, and Gnomonkey flicks Bandos to make himself tankier, which is optional.[^jdvQVwg8niM-1965]
* If your Zamorak godsword spec misses, stand on a particular tile because the mother Muttadile gets stuck there when going back to eat the tree and cannot hit you.[^jdvQVwg8niM-1995]
* Switch to protect from magic when the mother Muttadile starts maging, because she guarantees at least three mage hits in a row.[^jdvQVwg8niM-1995]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey calls Muttadile the worst boss in the game and says one of the main reasons he made the video was to rant about it.[^HkVt4enEesI-2688]
* Gnomonkey says Big Muttadile has an instant-kill melee attack and that you can be comboed for a 60 or more in one hit; he says Jagex reduced combo potential by raising the minimum and lowering the maximum hit, but he would be happy to see the boss removed.[^HkVt4enEesI-2783]
* Gnomonkey says Muttadiles are still the worst boss in the game, as he has said in the past.[^F0qIr7azCwg-687]
* Gnomonkey says Muttadile is a very high damage room where you should be ready to spam brew.[^wxGBi89vE_s-518]
* Gnomonkey calls Muttadile the worst boss in the entire game.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2705]

## Trivia

* Gnomonkey describes the small Muttadile as sporadic, sometimes backing off and sometimes approaching, switching between ranged and melee even while biting, and sometimes not allowing safe spotting around the tree.[^HkVt4enEesI-2688]
* Freezing the small Muttadile needs someone beyond Ancient magic, big Muttadile cannot really be frozen because of its very high magic level (needing a ZGS), and in CMs at most scales you just chop the tree while being hit.[^HkVt4enEesI-2751]
