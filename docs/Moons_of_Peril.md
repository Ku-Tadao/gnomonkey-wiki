**Moons of Peril** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2024-09-28 and 2025-05-30. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* To train on sulphur nagas you need to have completed the Moons of Peril quest, killing all the moons.[^-h7yElF9Z0c-150]
* The hard requirements are the Moons of Peril quest and about 48 Slayer, and a soft requirement of 55 Herblore.[^-h7yElF9Z0c-150]

## Methods

* In Moons of Peril there is a circle icon you follow around the moons and stay inside; outside the circle you take constant damage that ramps up to 16s, which will kill you.[^8-oPKk38mOY-501]
* Every moon attack swings three times (three hit splats) and checks accuracy for each; if any splat misses, the later splats are zero, and the third splat maxes twice as high (20 instead of 10) and carries the ramping curse.[^8-oPKk38mOY-522]
* Step-unders: you cannot be attacked by the moon if you step under it, so by clicking under the moon when its animation starts the third hit splat can never hit you.[^8-oPKk38mOY-646]

## Tips

* Because the sulphur nagas are in the Moons area you have unlimited supplies, since you make your own potions there.[^-h7yElF9Z0c-92]
* Shield flicking helps a lot at Moons for roughly 20% less damage taken, and a default RuneLite plugin tracks your curse value.[^8-oPKk38mOY-646]
* To eat after every rotation of the moons, and to check whether you have enough food and potions for the next three moons when you reach Blue Moon's campsite.[^8-oPKk38mOY-1067]

## Events

* The Moons of Peril ranged boss adds a new tank ring so players going for a rune crossbow can get it without needing to visit Dagannoth Rex first.[^EVx2KvYGjxM-656]
* The number of tornadoes is reduced for Blue Moon.[^EVx2KvYGjxM-683]
* The Granite ring gets dramatically better stats; Gnomonkey notes someone told him the listed stats were a typo of the current stats.[^EVx2KvYGjxM-683]
