# Fortis Colosseum setup

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2024-03-25 to 2024-07-26). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says the mace is the exact same as the rapier and Saildoor (as transcribed) alternatives, with the only difference being two extra prayer bonus; it can be swapped for the other two weapons without a big deal. ([2024-03-25 0:27](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=27))
- Gnomonkey's Colosseum setup has 385 range defence, making rotating pillar stacks not a big deal. ([2024-03-25 1:22](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=82))
- Gnomonkey says his cape gets him to 50 max anyway and lets him start waves in about 300 range defence versus about 170 for an ink top (as transcribed). ([2024-03-25 3:17](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=197))
- Gnomonkey wears a crystal shield as his tank shield for rotating pillars and starts waves with it on, and says the setup has 367 ranged defence, which he says is about as tanky as Torva casting with the shadow. ([2024-03-26 0:00](https://youtu.be/nEIeyY_m_gw))
- Gnomonkey says the max hit in his budget setup is about 51, that the Avernic defender is not actually a max hit upgrade, and that Saradomin sword is slightly better than a whip. ([2024-03-26 0:00](https://youtu.be/nEIeyY_m_gw))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey brings Ellie (the Elidinis' ward) to tank rotating pillar stacks that go badly, since you probably have to swap pillars at least once; a Falador shield also gives some defence and a lot of prayer, and he says you could instead wear your thrall book and bring an extra brew or restore. ([2024-03-25 0:27](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=27))
- At wave start Gnomonkey wears Torva to begin waves with its high defence, replacing some inventory slots with brews. ([2024-03-25 3:33](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=213))
- Without Ultor, Gnomonkey says a B-ring is better than the alternatives but you are at a much larger disadvantage not having Ultor; everything in his layout is required to reach a 50 max. ([2024-03-25 5:36](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=336))
- Gnomonkey uses the Charge calculator plugin and sets 20 as the amount he refills his Shadow with, giving 40 soul runes, which is enough for the entirety of the Colosseum; he drops two brews and uncharges his Shadow at the start. ([2024-03-25 6:11](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=371))
- Gnomonkey says to wear the B ring rather than the Lightbearer for the melee budget setup because it is better. ([2024-03-26 3:53](https://youtu.be/nEIeyY_m_gw?t=233))
- Barrows gloves would not be a worthwhile swap for mage or range here, since the Atlatl and Trident hits are guaranteed max regardless of accuracy. ([2024-03-26 4:21](https://youtu.be/nEIeyY_m_gw?t=261))
- Gnomonkey's max-budget Colosseum setup has no ranged weapons and no range switches except a Venator bow, because skipping them lets him bring more potions and his strategy does not need them. ([2024-07-26 1:21](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=81))
- Gnomonkey brings six brews and divines plus restores in his Colosseum setup, uses the Saradomin godsword for sustain (it can spec the Fremennik ranger and gives health and prayer because it is a guaranteed max hit), and a Scythe as best-in-slot melee weapon for the whole run. ([2024-07-26 1:49](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=109))
- Gnomonkey says for the one-shot of the Fremennik mage he needs a three-way mage setup plus a Saturated heart rather than the four-way mage he used previously, with no Augury needed; he says this is the best possible setup but the heart is expensive. ([2024-07-26 1:49](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=109))
- Gnomonkey says the Venator bow kills the Fremennik mage and will one-shot him if you bounce off the ranged Fremennik after killing the melee one, and Burning claws are very good DPS and let you DPS spec through the waves. ([2024-07-26 2:22](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=142))
- Gnomonkey says being able to DPS spec is nice for the boss and for killing quickly because of the reinforcement mechanic, and that an Osmumten's fang is an optional addition for attacking the shamans with better DPS than a Scythe, though it is not needed. ([2024-07-26 2:49](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=169))
- Gnomonkey walks in with a Dragon warhammer because it is very good on the boss and one-handed, so with a Book of the Dead it saves a slot; he says Elder maul is not a slot save because it is two-handed, and this is the one place in the game he still uses a warhammer. ([2024-07-26 3:18](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=198))
- Gnomonkey says he drops items at the start such as runes (about 19600 soul runes worth) and chaos runes for casting, and starts wave one on a tile similar to B5. ([2024-07-26 20:45](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1245))
- Gnomonkey says that on the later part of the run he found a four-way mage with Augury or a five-way mage without Augury is enough to one-shot the Fremennik berserker, which is less mandatory than he thought, and that you should always hit your heart so you do not fail the one-shot. ([2024-07-26 23:57](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1437))
- Gnomonkey says without a Tumeken's shadow, the Sanguinesti staff two-shots the Fremennik berserker rather than one-shotting it (roughly 8 ticks versus the shadow's 5), heals you, and can pull blood runes; the Trident also two-shots and pulls runes but does not heal. ([2024-07-26 54:28](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=3268))
- Gnomonkey says you can check any gear question yourself at gearscape.net, which he calls very UI friendly. ([2024-07-26 55:55](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=3355))
- Gnomonkey says a Soulreaper axe is a fine downgrade from the Scythe, about 10% worse, with recent changes making stacks refund slower and refund health; a tentacle whip or a mace with ink is nearly as good. ([2024-07-26 55:55](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=3355))
- Gnomonkey says in full melee gear with Piety you can kill the Fremennik mage in two shots with a Venator bow equivalent, and says a Venator bow is an equal side-grade, with a blowpipe taking three shots, so you do not need to worry about buying a Venator bow. ([2024-07-26 56:26](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=3386))
- Gnomonkey says the Bellator ring is very close to the Ultor ring and much cheaper, so acceptable, with the Berserker ring also fine; he says do not bring a Lightbearer, since you melee the whole time and need a melee ring. ([2024-07-26 57:52](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=3472))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says you want max max melee, rather than Echo boots or camping Ankle eggs, because it gets you to a 50 Scythe max, and dropping to 49 also lowers the second Scythe hit from 25 to 24. ([2024-03-25 0:56](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=56))
- Gnomonkey says the longer it takes to kill the boss the harder it is, and he thinks the Colosseum is one of the few places where Torva makes a massive difference because of its defence. ([2024-03-25 1:22](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=82))
- Gnomonkey begs players, including Ironmen, to get a Saradomin godsword, calling it 40 free health for 50% spec and one of the most important items in the setup, and says getting through the Colosseum without a Blood fury or a Saradomin godsword is trolling. ([2024-07-26 54:55](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=3295))
- Gnomonkey says a Dragon warhammer is better than the Elder maul in the Colosseum because it does not waste a slot. ([2024-07-26 57:25](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=3445))
- Gnomonkey says nobody should bring a range switch such as Crystal and Bofa to a first Colosseum, comparing it to bringing Justin (a crutch) to a first Inferno, and says to embrace pillar stacks and use the slots for potions. ([2024-07-26 57:25](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=3445))
- Gnomonkey says you do not need Torva for the Colosseum, with Justiciar and Bandos probably fine, and that you should get the Scythe before Torva because the Scythe matters much more. ([2024-07-26 57:52](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=3472))
