**Perilous Moons** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2024-08-17 and 2025-02-22. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* To fight the Perilous Moons you need the Moons of Peril quest (around 50 Slayer), and Gnomonkey wants 55 Herblore before coming, since you make your own supplies there and 55 Herblore gives the super strength effect.[^8-oPKk38mOY-27]
* 70 Herblore gives the extreme defence effect from the Moons potions, which is really helpful there.[^8-oPKk38mOY-55]

## Methods

* To make potions at Moons you grind up herbs with a pestle and mortar and decant; Gnomonkey grabs eight and decants them, which covers quite a few kills, and the doses you get are random, possibly based on Herblore level.[^8-oPKk38mOY-468]

## Stats and numbers

* At Perilous Moons the food heals you for half your Hunter level or a third of your Cooking level, whichever is lower, so with both in the 70s Gnomonkey says the food heals about 25.[^8-oPKk38mOY-85]

## Tips

* The plan for the rest of that stream is going to 75 Ranged to reach the Colosseum.[^iUjSbo1TQFQ-1287]
* Bone shards are always the best Moons drop, and that 300 bone shards from a combat achievement is really good XP.[^iUjSbo1TQFQ-1320]
* Hunter and Cooking also help at Moons: Hunter is easy with Hunter contracts and Cooking is trained with Trouble Brewing, which was recently buffed to over 200k Cooking XP an hour.[^8-oPKk38mOY-55]
* Moons is the one place in the game where you need tank armour, because each moon's negative effect when you get hit matters; all of the moons attack with slash so slash defence is key.[^8-oPKk38mOY-90]
* To get the Sulphur blades and Glacial hammers from the sulphur nagas and frost nagas before going to Moons; Sulphur blades are slash and best until a whip on Blood Moon, Glacial hammers are best until Boppers, and he considers Boppers best until scythe.[^8-oPKk38mOY-230]
* He wears an Amulet of glory for defence even though strength is a bit better for DPS, and Mixed hide boots are tankier than climbing boots.[^8-oPKk38mOY-270]
* Gnomonkey wears the Blood moon helmet at Moons because it has higher slash defence than a Neitiznot faceguard, which is plenty good if you do not have one; he also recommends getting a tank top and bottom from Barrows or at least Dragon platelegs.[^8-oPKk38mOY-305]
* The kill order is Eclipse, then Blue Moon, then Blood Moon, which speeds up looting the chest and getting back in.[^8-oPKk38mOY-306]
* Thralls are really good at Moons and uses the Ghostly grasp spell to keep the thrall attacking; if you have no thralls bring no spells.[^8-oPKk38mOY-346]
* At the campsite you can right click to make cuppa, which gives full run energy each time, so you do not need stamina potions at Moons.[^8-oPKk38mOY-366]
* Gnomonkey catches lizards rather than fish at Moons because lizards cook into multiple food, up to three depending on your Cooking level, whereas fish heal only 33% of your fishing or cooking level, whichever is lower, and take longer.[^8-oPKk38mOY-382]
* Gnomonkey collects six lizards, enough for about two rounds of kill count, and cooks more of them after fights; he says you only need enough food for the current encounter.[^8-oPKk38mOY-441]
* Five food should be good for Eclipse.[^8-oPKk38mOY-468]
* The Moons chest drops include super compost (easy to make into ultra compost), Irit and Harralander herbs for Herblore training, and bones including Wyrmling bones, Bone shards and Sunkist bones for passive Prayer training of about 10k Prayer XP per hour, per Gnomonkey.[^8-oPKk38mOY-1076]
* Moons chest drops water orbs, which are really good Crafting training through battlestaves and also give profit.[^8-oPKk38mOY-1111]
* Moons has dupe protection, so a Blood moon roll after getting the armour will be a missing piece; Gnomonkey says that once you have a full set from one moon, you can stop killing that moon and kill the other two, which does reduce your common loot.[^8-oPKk38mOY-1289]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the Perilous Moons are coded, and that one of the Moons drops he got is now his best helmet for Tombs of Amascut for now.[^iUjSbo1TQFQ-1496]
* Gnomonkey says Moons has completely changed the ironman meta: you do Moons instead of getting Bandos, and all of the drops are super good.[^8-oPKk38mOY-0]
* In the description Gnomonkey says the Perilous Moons update replaced the tedious early/mid level grind, so ironmen no longer farm Barrows or Bandos, and with Moons dupe protection you get stronger armour in less time.[^8-oPKk38mOY-0]
* Gnomonkey says Barrows is not dead content but is becoming dangerously close if Jagex keeps releasing extremely overpowered early/mid-game content like this.[^8-oPKk38mOY-0]
* Gnomonkey says the Rune kite shield is a surprisingly good option at Moons with over 50 slash defence, with a Crystal shield about six higher.[^8-oPKk38mOY-119]
* Gnomonkey says the Atlatl is amazing because it is effectively a mini blowpipe and is your ranged option until you have a blowpipe; the Eclipse set gives a burn chance on Atlatl hits for an extra 10 damage and he has used it at Tombs of Amascut and Chambers of Xeric.[^8-oPKk38mOY-1141]
* Gnomonkey says Blue moon armour is basically Ahrim's with a strength bonus and not as heavy, super good at Tombs of Amascut, and the Blue moon spear gives 5% magic damage early.[^8-oPKk38mOY-1168]
* Gnomonkey says Blood moon is Bandos armour and the helmet gives one more strength than a Neitiznot faceguard, and that Dual macuahuitl (Boppers) are an amazing main hand weapon, best until very late, with the full set giving roughly 3.5 tick attack speed instead of four.[^8-oPKk38mOY-1199]
* Gnomonkey says everything from Moons is useful, so it is really good to rush Moons on an ironman because it gives you everything you need.[^8-oPKk38mOY-1229]
* Gnomonkey says Moons has shifted the meta: you do not have to go to Bandos early or do Barrows to full collection log, it supercharges the mid-game, with no supply use and incredibly good drops that train your skills, so you should come as early as possible.[^8-oPKk38mOY-1367]
* Gnomonkey says Moons is extremely easy to kill compared to the Corrupted Gauntlet and far less KC is needed to get the Eclipse set than a Bofa.[^NxfbgWY3ZQk-460]

## Events

* On his first Perilous Moons stream, at 12 kill count, Gnomonkey received an Atlatl and a Blood moon chestplate, which a viewer calls essentially a Bandos chestplate.[^iUjSbo1TQFQ-1287]
* After starting his hardcore ironman he grinded Moons and did calculations, concluding the Atlatl is Old School's most underrated mid-game weapon and he will pass on the Bofa grind.[^NxfbgWY3ZQk-0]
