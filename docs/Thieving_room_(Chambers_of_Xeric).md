**Thieving room (Chambers of Xeric)** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2023-02-02 and 2025-05-01. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* In the Challenge Mode Thieving room Gnomonkey dumps after collecting about 15 grubs and keeps the creature fed so it cannot regenerate health; after 30 total grubs dumped the room is done.[^F0qIr7azCwg-391]
* Gnomonkey deposits around 15 grubs in the chests at a time if you have lower Thieving, and the grub counter shows the total deposited; the room is done at 30 total.[^wxGBi89vE_s-918]
* In the Thieving room you give grubs to the scavenger; he eats 30 worms before he gets out of your way, and if he stops eating he starts healing, so keep feeding him.[^jdvQVwg8niM-1081]

## Stats and numbers

* Grubs now stack in the Chambers thieving room, and the number of grubs you get depends on your Thieving level, so higher Thieving gives more grubs per steal.[^wxGBi89vE_s-892]
* With his Thieving he gets two grubs every time and sometimes three, whereas lower Thieving gets one and sometimes two, and he thinks 95 is the breakpoint.[^wxGBi89vE_s-918]

## Tips

* The only real skill in the Thieving room is banking properly afterward, banking his harmstaff, tome, hammer, scythe, Max cape and stamina and pulling out axe, pickaxe, mace, brews and a Kodai potion.[^F0qIr7azCwg-391]
* A lockpick is really good for the Thieving room if you do not have a high Thieving level, and with his high Thieving he is guaranteed at least two grubs each time.[^jdvQVwg8niM-1130]
