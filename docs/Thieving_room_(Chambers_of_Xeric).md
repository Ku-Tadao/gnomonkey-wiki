# Thieving room (Chambers of Xeric)

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2023-02-02 to 2025-05-01). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- In the Challenge Mode Thieving room Gnomonkey dumps after collecting about 15 grubs and keeps the creature fed so it cannot regenerate health; after 30 total grubs dumped the room is done. ([2023-02-02 6:31](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=391))
- Gnomonkey deposits around 15 grubs in the chests at a time if you have lower Thieving, and the grub counter shows the total deposited; the room is done at 30 total. ([2024-10-27 15:18](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=918))
- In the Thieving room you give grubs to the scavenger; he eats 30 worms before he gets out of your way, and if he stops eating he starts healing, so keep feeding him. ([2025-05-01 18:01](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1081))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says grubs now stack in the Chambers thieving room, and the number of grubs you get depends on your Thieving level, so higher Thieving gives more grubs per steal. ([2024-10-27 14:52](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=892))
- Gnomonkey says with his Thieving he gets two grubs every time and sometimes three, whereas lower Thieving gets one and sometimes two, and he thinks 95 is the breakpoint. ([2024-10-27 15:18](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=918))

## Tips

- The only real skill in the Thieving room is banking properly afterward, banking his harmstaff, tome, hammer, scythe, Max cape and stamina and pulling out axe, pickaxe, mace, brews and a Kodai potion. ([2023-02-02 6:31](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=391))
- Gnomonkey says a lockpick is really good for the Thieving room if you do not have a high Thieving level, and with his high Thieving he is guaranteed at least two grubs each time. ([2025-05-01 18:50](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1130))
