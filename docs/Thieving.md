**Thieving** is a skill topic that Gnomonkey covers in 8 videos between 2020-05-21 and 2025-11-04. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* Thieving, Woodcutting and Mining levels close to 99 help in CM.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-379]

## Methods

* Doing the quest shown on screen in the video gives 22,500 Thieving XP and skips straight to level 37.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-144]
* From levels 1 to 5, Gnomonkey's best option is pickpocketing men or women, found in a bunch in Lumbridge; if you are not a hardcore Ironman he would just pickpocket them until you die.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-144]
* After level 5, go to Ardougne for the baker's stall, the fastest method up to level 25: stand underneath the baker where guards can never catch you and spam click in place for a semi-AFK method.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-177]
* At level 25 you can steal from the fruit stalls in Great Kourend's Piscarilius house, which is efficient until level 45 (or, to skip blackjacking, up to level 55 before moving to Ardougne Knights).[^k0NPoqgsiQY-207]
* For Thieving, Gnomonkey would do a mix of Ardougne knights and stealing artefacts, with the majority likely being Ardougne knights.[^6OkBpQdcXRw-349]
* In the Thieving room the optimised route is opening three or four chests in a set order then dumping the grubs; you need 30 grubs to finish the room, and you should keep the creature fed because it heals slowly if it stops eating.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2089]

## Stats and numbers

* Elf thieving gave Gnomonkey about 150k Thieving XP an hour.[^Ydau_VX-3kw-533]
* The Rogues' Den maze requires 50 Thieving and 50 Agility.[^-6Jg4JTUA1w-31]
* The Sorceress's Garden summer garden gives up to 140k Thieving XP an hour, versus about 80k an hour from Ardougne Knights at level 65.[^nl7tQ37Uwng-0]
* Thieving equipment only affects pickpocketing success rates, not alternate training such as Pyramid Plunder or stalls.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-0]
* Level 5 Thieving takes only 49 successful pickpockets, and hardcore Ironmen need to bring food to avoid dying; dodgy necklaces also help here.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-177]
* You need to steal from the Ardougne baker's stall 466 times to get from level 5 to 25.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-207]
* Running between the fruit stalls is about 20% faster than clicking in place, and you can eat the strange fruits you receive to restore run energy; Gnomonkey would probably just click in place.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-236]
* You need to steal 5,672 fruit to get from level 25 to 55 at the fruit stalls; coming from the quest shortcut at level 37, level 37 to 55 takes 4,971 successful thieves.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-266]
* At level 95 with the Ardougne cape you stop failing Knight pickpockets, so Knights require no resources beyond that point and can be done all the way to 99.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-413]
* Sorcerer's Garden gives 140k Thieving XP an hour, significantly better than Knights of Ardougne at level 65, which give around 80k XP an hour.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-440]
* The Thieving breakpoint is 95, giving a chance for three grubs per chest and saving roughly 30 seconds in the Thieving room.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-435]
* The blog adds Thieving pickpocketing and chest opening that drops cannonball fragments and sources of coral, plus a new way to get clue scrolls: medium clues and hard clues from a level 76 chest.[^qiA-iaA19-M-1138]
* New thieving stalls have guards on rotation around the market; when you see them leave you can pickpocket repeatedly until they return, which Gnomonkey compares to wealthy citizens.[^qiA-iaA19-M-1199]
* Jagex has said Thieving will be a decent source of things like cannonballs and certain upgrade parts.[^pwZKK8dpq8o-445]

## Tips

* As an Ironman it may be smart to bank the cakes from the baker's stall for early game food; otherwise Gnomonkey would drop everything for faster XP.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-177]
* The best place to steal from the Piscarilius fruit stalls is a building where you cannot get caught; you can click in place for lower intensity, or run back and forth between them to save one tick on each respawn.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-236]
* The Kalphite Queen head pet is so easily earned by using his ultimate strength on Fremennik stalls that he would not worry about pet chance for the grind to 99 Thieving.[^6OkBpQdcXRw-349]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says elf thieving is the best money-making skilling method in the game, with the highest requirements of any skilling method except maybe wrath runes.[^Ydau_VX-3kw-0]
* Gnomonkey recommends getting the Rogue's outfit immediately at 50 Thieving, as it doubles pickpocket loot.[^-6Jg4JTUA1w-0]
* Gnomonkey recommends the garden from 65 to 75 or 80 Thieving, since Ardougne Knights overtake its XP an hour at about level 80.[^nl7tQ37Uwng-29]
* Gnomonkey considers Thieving a great early 99 to get, giving a sizable amount of cash, and a pretty relaxing, low-intensity skill if you take the semi-AFK route.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-0]
* Gnomonkey says the early Thieving levels up to level 55 are quite intensive, the opposite of relaxing, and recommends doing quests to skip as much of the grind as possible.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-144]
* Gnomonkey calls Knights of Ardougne one of the lowest-intensity methods in the game, available from level 55.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-266]
* Gnomonkey says it is a good idea to do Sorcerer's Garden at least from level 65 to 75, or even to 80 if you prefer the garden to Knights.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-440]
* Gnomonkey would probably leave the Sorcerer's Garden at 75 if you get no money from it, since Knights become comparable then and completely overtake it for both XP and money at level 80.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-440]
* Gnomonkey lists men/women, baker's stall, fruit stalls, Knights and Sorcerer's Garden as the best low-intensity options to 99, with Elves and Vampyres as options to boost gp an hour.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-472]
* Gnomonkey says the new stalls could be a really solid semi-AFK thieving option, which thieving does not have, especially if you steal automatically while the guards are gone.[^qiA-iaA19-M-1199]
* Gnomonkey says he likes that the thieving additions give other ways to get new items such as cannonballs and fish and could change how you train Thieving, rather than adding the same old stuff.[^qiA-iaA19-M-1232]
* Gnomonkey does not think the Sailing Thieving requirement gives access to any important single resource, so you probably do not need it.[^pwZKK8dpq8o-445]
