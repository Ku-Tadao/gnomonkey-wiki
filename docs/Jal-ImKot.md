## Methods

* The southwest tile of a monster is the brain of the monster, the tile that tries to path straight at you, which is why a melee cannot attack you when it cannot reach you; it can be shown with the NPC Indicators plugin.[^6lRmbYCYtME-454]
* A melee cannot dig while you are shooting a mage off-ticked, so there is nothing to worry about.[^6lRmbYCYtME-2667]
* When a melee is on you it cannot dig.[^6lRmbYCYtME-4832]
* Melee monsters dig to you after 50 game ticks, and after digging they take eight ticks to start attacking you, so make sure your prayer is up.[^h6LxupiygPA-529]
* If a melee monster hits you, tanking it resets its dig timer, so as long as it keeps hitting you it will not dig.[^h6LxupiygPA-558]
* Melees only attack with melee damage but after about 50 game ticks they dig to you, then have a brief cooldown before meleeing again; tanking a melee hit resets the dig cooldown.[^XMTe9TYbDls-1003]
* The Inferno melee monster digs to your location roughly every 50 game ticks if it cannot attack you, which stops safe spotting from working.[^6trKOSUr4EM-756]
* Safe spotting a melee: the southwest tile of a monster is its brain tile and the only tile it considers when pathing to you, so tiles on the other side of a pillar are safe even a few tiles out.[^6trKOSUr4EM-833]
* A corner trap works because monsters never path north before going east; east and west are prioritised before north and south, so a monster that cannot go east or west towards you shuts down.[^6trKOSUr4EM-899]
* A melee mob digs to your location every 50 game ticks if it has not attacked you, and the timer resets from the start each time it attacks.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-895]
* A melee's south-west tile is its 'brain': it only tries to reach you with that tile in a straight line, so you can safe spot up to three tiles out on both sides of a pillar by checking whether the south-west tile can get in line.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-903]
* In a corner trap, a monster refuses to go north or south before going east or west, so it keeps trying to go east into an obstruction and never reaches you; corner traps work on both sides of a pillar, and you can corner trap anything in the game, not just melees.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-970]
* A monster's movement is controlled by its southwest true tile (its brain), which you can mark with NPC indicators; knowing this tells you when you can safe spot, for example when the brain stays in line with you and the monster is too big to go forward.[^2xviK0wGI-o-834]
* Corner trap: because monsters always move east or west to reach you before moving north or south, a melee whose corner touches you at a pillar edge gets stuck and cannot attack, working from any edge of a pillar.[^2xviK0wGI-o-918]
* After 50 game ticks the melee digs to your current position and then takes a delay of about five seconds before it can hit you again.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1030]
* Safe spotting a melee works because the monster only uses its southwest tile for pathing, as if it were one tile, so if the straight line to you is blocked it gets stuck even at a distance from the pillar.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1087]

## Stats and numbers

* Melee monsters spawn after wave 9 (flipped from the Fight Caves, where rangers come third), max a 49, are extremely accurate and cannot realistically be tanked.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-870]
* If a melee has not attacked you for 20 seconds it digs to your current location with a short window before it can attack; the timer resets if you pray melee and let it hit you once, or take an off-prayer hit.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-900]
* Jal-ImKot melee monsters dig roughly every 50 ticks, and tanking a melee hit stops it from digging for the next 15 ticks (he says 'I think'), so it waits until the next dig cycle.[^6lRmbYCYtME-448]
* There is a really long delay on the melee's attack after it digs, which he believes is eight ticks after it comes up before it can attack you.[^6lRmbYCYtME-1856]
* Melees almost never hit you because they have really low accuracy, and can hit 49, but that is rare; he says not to tank them on purpose.[^6lRmbYCYtME-4832]
* Inferno melees dig to you roughly every 50 ticks, but hitting you resets that timer so it will not dig, and you can manually reset it if you need it not to dig.[^FfNQI63ZKys-537]
* Melees have about a 25% chance of hitting you with a max hit of 49 because they have a very low attack level, so Gnomonkey says you can tank them in a pinch while killing them.[^XMTe9TYbDls-1141]
* Tanking a melee with a blob on you has never hit lower than 46 in his experience and the melee is about 30% accurate.[^6trKOSUr4EM-2115]
* Inferno melee mobs (Jal-ImKot) max hit 49, are about 25% accurate and usually hit zero, and Gnomonkey says they kill the most people in combination with blobs because people panic and tank hits.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-873]
* Melee NPCs (Jal-ImKot) max 49 and hit very hard, and after 50 ticks of spawning they dig to your current position, creating time pressure.[^2xviK0wGI-o-805]
* Melee NPCs have about 30% accuracy against you so you can often tank a couple of hits.[^2xviK0wGI-o-1041]
* Inferno melee monsters can hit up to 49 but have very low accuracy, about 25% when you wear a shield in this setup, so they often hit zero.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-994]

## Tips

* Melees can be safe spotted, corner trapped or frozen, or you can pray melee.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-870]
* You cannot realistically flick a melee and a blob; instead safe spot the melee away from the blobs, with corner traps possible on any corner north or south of the north pillar.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1485]
* The first time you reach a melee in the waves, Gnomonkey suggests keeping it alive and experimenting to see how it can be safe spotted, since it matters a lot later.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1514]
* A corner trap: melee movement priority is always east or west before north or south, so a melee trying to go east into a pillar will not go north or south, trapping it.[^FfNQI63ZKys-448]
* A monster's pathing brain is its southwest tile, so as long as the southwest tile sees you and is trying to go in a straight line toward you around a pillar, it will not reach you.[^FfNQI63ZKys-478]
* You can corner trap against any side of the pillar, even from the bat safe spot, and that corner trapping after a dig is something you use all the time.[^FfNQI63ZKys-1558]
* In some pillar positions you can corner trap the melee and kill the mage first, and that it also helps to rotate around the pillar so melee cannot get past.[^FfNQI63ZKys-2907]
* To prioritise killing the mage first and the melee second, since the melee is the time constraint.[^FfNQI63ZKys-5265]
* Gnomonkey recommends using blowpipe specs on melee monsters in a bad spot, since blowpipe specs not only give health back but are more accurate.[^h6LxupiygPA-623]
* Gnomonkey warns that something being dead does not mean it cannot get an extra attack off, so don't assume you are safe just because they are disappearing.[^h6LxupiygPA-830]
* Not to freeze melee monsters, because they have a fairly high magic level and so a high chance to splash; it is almost always better to spam blowpipe specs and kill them.[^h6LxupiygPA-889]
* Gnomonkey does not like healing off melee monsters because the chance of splashing on them is really high.[^h6LxupiygPA-1451]
* You can reset a melee's dig timer by praying melee and tanking a hit so it does not dig.[^XMTe9TYbDls-1685]
* Melees cannot dig while they are attacking you, so off-ticking a melee is a great idea.[^XMTe9TYbDls-1779]
* When a melee digs, Gnomonkey says to just turn and shoot it with blowpipe specs and it dies, instead of panicking and stopping damage.[^XMTe9TYbDls-2430]
* Melees have a big delay before they start hitting after digging, so you can stand in melee range and then step to the corner-trap position.[^XMTe9TYbDls-3033]
* When you are under a melee, Gnomonkey says not to click anything and just wait patiently or it will kill you.[^XMTe9TYbDls-3131]
* Corner traps work on melees and also on ranged targets, blobs, mages and rangers, which cannot hit you while trapped; you can corner trap on both sides of the pillar.[^6trKOSUr4EM-928]
* Tanking a melee resets its 50 tick dig timer, and a splash on it delays its attack two ticks.[^6trKOSUr4EM-1098]
* You can just kill melees with the melee killer ring and blowpipe specs, as melees have pretty low health.[^6trKOSUr4EM-1218]
* Melees have a long cooldown after they dig.[^6trKOSUr4EM-1743]
* Gnomonkey's rule when a melee is on you is to just kill it: if you kill the melees you will be better than 80% of players, because many people sit and full-tank them.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-1088]
* You can tank a mage hit rather than all the hits of a melee but if you can reposition to safety do so, and that running around praying randomly will get you killed.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-1318]
* A typical solve Gnomonkey gives for a melee plus other spawns is to kill the melee first because it digs, noting it is also harder to heal off because it has a high mage level.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-1793]
* To always have a plan for when a melee digs, which is usually to move to a corner-trap tile; if one digs while you are on the bat safe spot or further up, any of those tiles can corner trap it.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-1874]
* A melee that is dragged to a corner can still be reached and hit with the Bofa if you drag it first, because it cannot see you from the corner.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-1907]
* Double melee waves are easy: both dig at the same time, so you can corner trap both.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-2926]
* Gnomonkey describes a rare solve for a melee on wave 64 where you go behind the digging melee, and notes this came up twice in the run.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-4400]
* To kill the melee first when possible because a wave is not solved while a melee can still dig to you.[^2xviK0wGI-o-805]
* If you tank a melee hit its dig timer resets to another full 50 ticks, giving you more time when you are worried about time pressure.[^2xviK0wGI-o-979]
* Gnomonkey recommends learning corner traps and safe spots early on early waves so you do not have to learn them under time pressure later.[^2xviK0wGI-o-1074]
* Things only hit you once you fully round the corner, so you can safely rotate round the pillar and pray as you do so, and melees can dig while frozen.[^2xviK0wGI-o-3669]
* You should not freeze melee NPCs because freezing takes about three casts on average, in which time you could have killed it with pipe specs.[^2xviK0wGI-o-4149]
* Gnomonkey recommends corner trapping as a default move at the start of a wave: cast, go to the pillar corner and immediately corner trap whatever is there, whether it is a blob, bat, mage or ranger.[^2xviK0wGI-o-4340]
* Letting the melee hit you resets its dig timer to 50 ticks, which Gnomonkey notes as a pinch option.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1139]
* Just shooting a melee works a surprising amount of the time because of its very low accuracy, and he wears the Lightbearer so more specs help kill it before it kills you.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1212]
* The melee is often the first target in a wave because it provides time pressure by digging; removing the melee puzzle piece simplifies the wave.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1718]
* You can safe spot melees on the side of the pillar many people never use, and that sometimes you need to go there.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-2243]
* When a melee digs and you stand on the dig tile, click around the pillar first and then down to the corner trap, since the melee can wiggle free if you run straight through it.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-2330]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says melees alone are not very threatening but combined with blobs or other dangerous monsters they can easily get you killed.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-900]
* Gnomonkey says he never likes freezing melees or standing under them because it is risky and inconsistent.[^FfNQI63ZKys-6250]
* Gnomonkey calls Jal-ImKot (melee monsters) probably the second bane of the first cape, saying blob in combination with melee kills people the most.[^h6LxupiygPA-529]
* Gnomonkey says melees and double blob waves are what kill most people in Inferno because people do not know the corner traps and safe spots, and that once you learn them melees are irrelevant.[^XMTe9TYbDls-2291]
* Gnomonkey says Inferno melees kill players more than anything because players panic when the melee digs to them.[^6trKOSUr4EM-784]
* Gnomonkey says not to ice barrage melees, as you have very low mage accuracy against them and it is not consistent.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1242]
