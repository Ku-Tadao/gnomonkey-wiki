# Antler guard

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2025-01-09 to 2025-01-14). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Stats and numbers

- The Antler guard is a tribrid shield dropped in the new Final Dawn slayer dungeon, with five melee strength, two ranged strength and massive negative defence. ([2025-01-09 20:23](https://youtu.be/4YGVut4hMtA?t=1223))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the Antler guard is not best in slot anywhere but could be useful on ironmen, who find ranged strength offhands hard to get, for example with a Warhammer or a ring crossbow at Zebak where defence does not matter. ([2025-01-09 20:54](https://youtu.be/4YGVut4hMtA?t=1254))
- Gnomonkey thought the Antler guard was fine as it was and expected to use it as a ranged off-hand for bosses like Zebak and while progressing on his hardcore account. ([2025-01-14 11:40](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=700))

## Events

- Jagex said it deliberately designed the Antler guard to sit below the Dragon defender, to keep players going until they get the Dragon defender. ([2025-01-14 11:10](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=670))
- Jagex said the Antler guard is intended to be particularly useful for players using the RCB (rune crossbow) or the Hunter crossbow, or those using a proselyte set on a Slayer task. ([2025-01-14 11:10](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=670))
- Jagex is reducing the Antler guard's negative defence values. ([2025-01-14 11:40](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=700))
