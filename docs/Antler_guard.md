**Antler guard** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2025-01-09 and 2025-01-14. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* The Antler guard is a tribrid shield dropped in the new Final Dawn slayer dungeon, with five melee strength, two ranged strength and massive negative defence.[^4YGVut4hMtA-1223]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the Antler guard is not best in slot anywhere but could be useful on ironmen, who find ranged strength offhands hard to get, for example with a Warhammer or a ring crossbow at Zebak where defence does not matter.[^4YGVut4hMtA-1254]
* Gnomonkey thought the Antler guard was fine as it was and expected to use it as a ranged off-hand for bosses like Zebak and while progressing on his hardcore account.[^kd6oHdt4BB0-700]

## Events

* Jagex said it deliberately designed the Antler guard to sit below the Dragon defender, to keep players going until they get the Dragon defender.[^kd6oHdt4BB0-670]
* Jagex said the Antler guard is intended to be particularly useful for players using the RCB (rune crossbow) or the Hunter crossbow, or those using a proselyte set on a Slayer task.[^kd6oHdt4BB0-670]
* Jagex is reducing the Antler guard's negative defence values.[^kd6oHdt4BB0-700]
