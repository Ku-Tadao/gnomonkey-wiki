**Triple Jad** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 4 videos between 2022-07-22 and 2024-10-03. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Gnomonkey stands at a specific triple Jad pillar tile and has already potted divine; he says you can overbrew to 115 if it is your first time at triples, since he believes they max 113 and so can't kill you.[^h6LxupiygPA-3351]

## Stats and numbers

* Triple Jads have three ticks per attack, which is a ton of time, so you do one action per attack: one click to attack and one click to pray against the next attack.[^6lRmbYCYtME-5192]
* The max hit from a Jad is 113, so you cannot be killed when brewed up to 115 hitpoints, though he is not fully sure.[^FfNQI63ZKys-6199]
* Jad attacks every three ticks in the triple fight, so you have loads of time and only need one action per attack.[^FfNQI63ZKys-6233]
* In triple Jads you have 1.8 seconds per prayer, which Gnomonkey says is the exact same as regular Jad, only faster: do one action per prayer catch and one tag per prayer on healers.[^6trKOSUr4EM-2438]

## Tips

* To start triples at a specific tile, always start triples there, and to prepot a brew and restore and click a divine before logging out so they respawn when you log back in.[^6lRmbYCYtME-5192]
* You can camp Rigor for triples if you have over four restores.[^6lRmbYCYtME-5221]
* As soon as a projectile is on screen, switch to the next prayer.[^6lRmbYCYtME-5250]
* You want 99 health before triple Jad and that the order for triples is to kill the Jad next to you first, after which the order does not matter.[^FfNQI63ZKys-6162]
* Gnomonkey recommends pre-brewing before triples on your first attempt, which takes you up to 115 health, because they cannot kill you when overbrewed.[^FfNQI63ZKys-6162]
* You can tag healers with Bow of Faerdhinen, which is a little safer, and that for the last Jad he likes to stand a bit further up, hoping for pre-lured healers.[^FfNQI63ZKys-6261]
* Zebak at level three enrage (level two or higher) has the same three-tick timing as triple Jad, so you can practise on him by running in with Pathfinder, getting him to 25% and stopping.[^FfNQI63ZKys-6384]
* If you miss a prayer swap on a Jad hit you can tick-eat (a brew) to survive, and that triples are actually much easier than waves 62 or 63.[^h6LxupiygPA-3378]
* Gnomonkey prefers standing on the side of Jad that makes the healer tag a lot easier, and that it is ideal because more often than not it gives the highest chance of the healers being blocked.[^h6LxupiygPA-3473]
* He wants to save two Divine bastion doses before triple Jads: one for triples and one for Zuk.[^6trKOSUr4EM-2233]
* The pillar that goes down at the end of a wave halves your health if you are standing next to it.[^6trKOSUr4EM-2476]
* To stand on a tile where healers cannot reach melee range of Jad, because then they will not heal him and you do not need to tag them, and the tile also corner traps a Jad so you take no damage.[^6trKOSUr4EM-2531]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the only way you die to triples is bad nerves or trying to explain something to a bunch of people, and says talking out loud helps some people.[^6lRmbYCYtME-5250]
* Gnomonkey says healers is easy and you can kill the Jads in any order, which is all preference.[^6lRmbYCYtME-5348]
* Gnomonkey says triple Jads is incredibly easy and one of the easiest parts of the Inferno, much easier than the waves before it.[^6trKOSUr4EM-2438]
