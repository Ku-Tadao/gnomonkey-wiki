# Triple Jad

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 4 videos (2022-07-22 to 2024-10-03). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

|---|---|
| **Type** | mechanic |
| **Videos** | 4 (first 2022-07-22, latest 2024-10-03) |

## Methods

- Gnomonkey stands at a specific triple Jad pillar tile and has already potted divine; he says you can overbrew to 115 if it is your first time at triples, since he believes they max 113 and so can't kill you. ([2024-01-17 55:51](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=3351))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says triple Jads have three ticks per attack, which is a ton of time, so you do one action per attack: one click to attack and one click to pray against the next attack. ([2022-07-22 1:26:32](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=5192))
- Gnomonkey says the max hit from a Jad is 113, so you cannot be killed when brewed up to 115 hitpoints, though he is not fully sure. ([2022-11-04 1:43:19](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=6199))
- Jad attacks every three ticks in the triple fight, so you have loads of time and only need one action per attack. ([2022-11-04 1:43:53](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=6233))
- In triple Jads you have 1.8 seconds per prayer, which Gnomonkey says is the exact same as regular Jad, only faster: do one action per prayer catch and one tag per prayer on healers. ([2024-10-03 40:38](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=2438))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says to start triples at a specific tile, always start triples there, and to prepot a brew and restore and click a divine before logging out so they respawn when you log back in. ([2022-07-22 1:26:32](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=5192))
- Gnomonkey says you can camp Rigor for triples if you have over four restores. ([2022-07-22 1:27:01](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=5221))
- Gnomonkey says as soon as a projectile is on screen, switch to the next prayer. ([2022-07-22 1:27:30](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=5250))
- Gnomonkey says you want 99 health before triple Jad and that the order for triples is to kill the Jad next to you first, after which the order does not matter. ([2022-11-04 1:42:42](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=6162))
- Gnomonkey recommends pre-brewing before triples on your first attempt, which takes you up to 115 health, because they cannot kill you when overbrewed. ([2022-11-04 1:42:42](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=6162))
- Gnomonkey says you can tag healers with Bow of Faerdhinen, which is a little safer, and that for the last Jad he likes to stand a bit further up, hoping for pre-lured healers. ([2022-11-04 1:44:21](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=6261))
- Gnomonkey says Zebak at level three enrage (level two or higher) has the same three-tick timing as triple Jad, so you can practise on him by running in with Pathfinder, getting him to 25% and stopping. ([2022-11-04 1:46:24](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=6384))
- Gnomonkey says if you miss a prayer swap on a Jad hit you can tick-eat (a brew) to survive, and that triples are actually much easier than waves 62 or 63. ([2024-01-17 56:18](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=3378))
- Gnomonkey prefers standing on the side of Jad that makes the healer tag a lot easier, and that it is ideal because more often than not it gives the highest chance of the healers being blocked. ([2024-01-17 57:53](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=3473))
- Gnomonkey says he wants to save two Divine bastion doses before triple Jads: one for triples and one for Zuk. ([2024-10-03 37:13](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=2233))
- Gnomonkey says the pillar that goes down at the end of a wave halves your health if you are standing next to it. ([2024-10-03 41:16](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=2476))
- Gnomonkey says to stand on a tile where healers cannot reach melee range of Jad, because then they will not heal him and you do not need to tag them, and the tile also corner traps a Jad so you take no damage. ([2024-10-03 42:11](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=2531))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the only way you die to triples is bad nerves or trying to explain something to a bunch of people, and says talking out loud helps some people. ([2022-07-22 1:27:30](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=5250))
- Gnomonkey says healers is easy and you can kill the Jads in any order, which is all preference. ([2022-07-22 1:29:08](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=5348))
- Gnomonkey says triple Jads is incredibly easy and one of the easiest parts of the Inferno, much easier than the waves before it. ([2024-10-03 40:38](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=2438))
