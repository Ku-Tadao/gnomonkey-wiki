## Methods

- Gnomonkey includes understanding character pathing, avoiding tile hazards and managing monster movement in the movement pillar. ([2022-05-11 1:54](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=114))
- Our character moves by teleporting two tiles per tick and can move in a square of directions each tick; Gnomonkey says the easiest place to learn exactly how this works is Hallowed Sepulchre. ([2022-05-11 4:00](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=240))

## Tips

- Mastering Hallowed Sepulchre floor five gives a deep understanding of all forms of L-running and diagonal movement, while the other floors teach it more simply and progressively. ([2022-05-11 4:19](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=259))
- Learning to Woox walk Vorkath is a surprisingly great foundation for many later encounters. ([2022-05-11 4:19](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=259))
- Dodging floor attacks in Theatre of Blood and Chambers, Olm cycles and Sotetseg maze all require very solid movement understanding. ([2022-05-11 5:21](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=321))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says monster movement (corner traps, southwest tile shenanigans, safespots) is even more complicated and there are few places to learn it; he highly recommends Giant Mole and Dagannoth Kings. ([2022-05-11 4:30](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=270))
- Gnomonkey calls Giant Mole 'basically an Inferno melee, he even digs', and says messing with safespots around her lair gives a solid foundation for Inferno melee monsters; Dagannoth Rex safespots help too. ([2022-05-11 4:30](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=270))
- Movement is important in every high-level PvM encounter (Gauntlet, Chambers, Theatre of Blood and especially Inferno); Gnomonkey says Inferno is the only one needing a solid understanding of both your own movement and monster movement. ([2022-05-11 4:50](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=290))
