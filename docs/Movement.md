**Movement** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2022-05-11. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Gnomonkey includes understanding character pathing, avoiding tile hazards and managing monster movement in the movement pillar.[^IuHh4gfs39o-114]
* Our character moves by teleporting two tiles per tick and can move in a square of directions each tick; Gnomonkey says the easiest place to learn exactly how this works is Hallowed Sepulchre.[^IuHh4gfs39o-240]

## Tips

* Mastering Hallowed Sepulchre floor five gives a deep understanding of all forms of L-running and diagonal movement, while the other floors teach it more simply and progressively.[^IuHh4gfs39o-259]
* Learning to Woox walk Vorkath is a surprisingly great foundation for many later encounters.[^IuHh4gfs39o-259]
* Dodging floor attacks in Theatre of Blood and Chambers, Olm cycles and Sotetseg maze all require very solid movement understanding.[^IuHh4gfs39o-321]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says monster movement (corner traps, southwest tile shenanigans, safespots) is even more complicated and there are few places to learn it; he highly recommends Giant Mole and Dagannoth Kings.[^IuHh4gfs39o-270]
* Gnomonkey calls Giant Mole 'basically an Inferno melee, he even digs', and says messing with safespots around her lair gives a solid foundation for Inferno melee monsters; Dagannoth Rex safespots help too.[^IuHh4gfs39o-270]
* Movement is important in every high-level PvM encounter (Gauntlet, Chambers, Theatre of Blood and especially Inferno); Gnomonkey says Inferno is the only one needing a solid understanding of both your own movement and monster movement.[^IuHh4gfs39o-290]
