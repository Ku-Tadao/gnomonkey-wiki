**Sunfire runes** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2023-11-06 and 2026-06-30. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Sunfire runes provide a 10% minimum hit to fire spells.[^88kOTb9qUKc-147]
* Jagex said burning pages can be upgraded to searing pages using Sunfire runes so they can be placed inside a tome of fire.[^88kOTb9qUKc-179]

## Tips

* It is extra important to add searing pages to your tome or use sunfire runes at the Maggot King, because sunfire runes increase your minimum hit a lot.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-202]
* Getting sunfire runes will be kind of annoying for an Ironman but is pretty important to do before fighting the Maggot King.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-202]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says this is a big buff to Harmonised nightmare staff but it still does not overtake Shadow anywhere; it would be much more powerful at Ice Demon and a decent gap filler up to Shadow.[^88kOTb9qUKc-179]
* Gnomonkey could see the Harmonised nightmare staff being used in Tombs of Amascut by experienced players who do not have a Tumeken's shadow yet.[^88kOTb9qUKc-179]
