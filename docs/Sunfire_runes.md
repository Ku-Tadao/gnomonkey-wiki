# Sunfire runes

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2023-11-06 to 2026-06-30). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | item |

## Stats and numbers

- Sunfire runes provide a 10% minimum hit to fire spells. ([2023-11-06 2:27](https://youtu.be/88kOTb9qUKc?t=147))
- Jagex said burning pages can be upgraded to searing pages using Sunfire runes so they can be placed inside a tome of fire. ([2023-11-06 2:59](https://youtu.be/88kOTb9qUKc?t=179))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says it is extra important to add searing pages to your tome or use sunfire runes at the Maggot King, because sunfire runes increase your minimum hit a lot. ([2026-06-30 3:22](https://youtu.be/0iIPIZTfsBU?t=202))
- Gnomonkey says getting sunfire runes will be kind of annoying for an Ironman but is pretty important to do before fighting the Maggot King. ([2026-06-30 3:22](https://youtu.be/0iIPIZTfsBU?t=202))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says this is a big buff to Harmonised nightmare staff but it still does not overtake Shadow anywhere; it would be much more powerful at Ice Demon and a decent gap filler up to Shadow. ([2023-11-06 2:59](https://youtu.be/88kOTb9qUKc?t=179))
- Gnomonkey could see the Harmonised nightmare staff being used in Tombs of Amascut by experienced players who do not have a Tumeken's shadow yet. ([2023-11-06 2:59](https://youtu.be/88kOTb9qUKc?t=179))
