## Stats and numbers

- Per the blog, the new Yama slash armour set (soul armour) is a side grade to Torva: as a full set it gives +38 slash accuracy over Torva at the cost of seven melee strength, with no prayer bonus. ([2025-03-01 7:31](https://youtu.be/GcMlWY-2-v4?t=451))
- Gnomonkey says the soul armour's defence is weaker than Torva's except for slash defence, which is much higher; he says the only boss where this matters is Vardorvis, who slashes you and is very tanky against slash. ([2025-03-01 7:58](https://youtu.be/GcMlWY-2-v4?t=478))
- Compared to Torva, soul armour is a bit weaker to stab, crush and ranged, slightly better against magic and significantly tougher against slash. ([2025-03-01 8:30](https://youtu.be/GcMlWY-2-v4?t=510))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says soul armour will be incredibly strong in Challenge Mode Chambers of Xeric, where a scythe is used almost constantly, and is a solid alternative to Torva for ironmen who do not want to grind Nex. ([2025-03-01 9:04](https://youtu.be/GcMlWY-2-v4?t=544))
- Gnomonkey thinks players in the Theatre of Blood will likely wear only partial pieces of soul armour, since draining enemies to zero defence makes accuracy less valuable. ([2025-03-01 9:04](https://youtu.be/GcMlWY-2-v4?t=544))
- Gnomonkey likes the armour shard and ore system as luck mitigation, preferring it to Perilous Moons (where you receive pieces in a set order) and Desert Treasure 2, since it is an addition to drops that lets players fill gaps at the end of the grind. ([2025-03-01 16:00](https://youtu.be/GcMlWY-2-v4?t=960))

## Events

- Jagex said the soul armour will have its own cosmetic obtained through a Yama contract, and Gnomonkey says he wants to go for a world first on stream. ([2025-03-01 8:30](https://youtu.be/GcMlWY-2-v4?t=510))
- Jagex said a new mining method in the Chasm of Fire gives a new ore that can be combined with armour shards dropped by Yama to forge a soul armour piece, alongside the normal drops for each piece. ([2025-03-01 15:34](https://youtu.be/GcMlWY-2-v4?t=934))
- Jagex said the shard and ore system should self-regulate: if many people mine, ore goes down in price and Yama's GP/hr goes up, and if few mine, shards lose value and mining's GP/hr goes up. ([2025-03-01 16:10](https://youtu.be/GcMlWY-2-v4?t=970))
