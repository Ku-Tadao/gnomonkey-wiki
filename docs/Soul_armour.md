**Soul armour** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2025-03-01. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Per the blog, the new Yama slash armour set (soul armour) is a side grade to Torva: as a full set it gives +38 slash accuracy over Torva at the cost of seven melee strength, with no prayer bonus.[^GcMlWY-2-v4-451]
* The soul armour's defence is weaker than Torva's except for slash defence, which is much higher; he says the only boss where this matters is Vardorvis, who slashes you and is very tanky against slash.[^GcMlWY-2-v4-478]
* Compared to Torva, soul armour is a bit weaker to stab, crush and ranged, slightly better against magic and significantly tougher against slash.[^GcMlWY-2-v4-510]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says soul armour will be incredibly strong in Challenge Mode Chambers of Xeric, where a scythe is used almost constantly, and is a solid alternative to Torva for ironmen who do not want to grind Nex.[^GcMlWY-2-v4-544]
* Gnomonkey thinks players in the Theatre of Blood will likely wear only partial pieces of soul armour, since draining enemies to zero defence makes accuracy less valuable.[^GcMlWY-2-v4-544]
* Gnomonkey likes the armour shard and ore system as luck mitigation, preferring it to Perilous Moons (where you receive pieces in a set order) and Desert Treasure 2, since it is an addition to drops that lets players fill gaps at the end of the grind.[^GcMlWY-2-v4-960]

## Events

* Jagex said the soul armour will have its own cosmetic obtained through a Yama contract, and Gnomonkey says he wants to go for a world first on stream.[^GcMlWY-2-v4-510]
* Jagex said a new mining method in the Chasm of Fire gives a new ore that can be combined with armour shards dropped by Yama to forge a soul armour piece, alongside the normal drops for each piece.[^GcMlWY-2-v4-934]
* Jagex said the shard and ore system should self-regulate: if many people mine, ore goes down in price and Yama's GP/hr goes up, and if few mine, shards lose value and mining's GP/hr goes up.[^GcMlWY-2-v4-970]
