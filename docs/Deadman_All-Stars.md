**Deadman All-Stars** is an other topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2026-01-25 and 2026-06-25. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Mika wore a Zuriel's staff and Malediction ward and spammed only mage with blood barrage, since Zuriel's increases blood barrage efficiency by 50% and healing does not change even if the barrage is prayed against.[^lJexGv6dxu8-2650]
* Against Skilly specs, Gnomonkey planned to venge, full tank the opening VLS special and then hit an AGS special to KO him, but he had used both venge trinkets on Coxie and could not execute it.[^lJexGv6dxu8-3542]

## Stats and numbers

* Gnomonkey died to a 27 barrage after running out of food while getting Mika almost completely out of food; the stats showed Mika dealt 666 damage to Gnomonkey's 229, with 103 attacks versus 44.[^lJexGv6dxu8-2925]
* Mika healed 154 total health with blood barrage, which Gnomonkey says is probably the most healing in any finale fight, equal to two and a half extra brews, versus 15 healed by Gnomonkey.[^lJexGv6dxu8-2983]
* The opposing team landed many freezes with Purging staff, which has about 35 magic accuracy, and Mystic boots, giving them slightly higher magic accuracy than the Odablock Warriors in Blue Moon and dragon boots.[^lJexGv6dxu8-3166]
* In the Coxie fight Gnomonkey had 46 total attacks, 28 off prayer, about 15 off prayer and on robes, and only 171 damage despite many fang specs, with a freeze success of 3 out of 15.[^lJexGv6dxu8-3510]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says his gear had dragon boots and a crystal shield, both with negative magic accuracy, so he splashed a lot when forced to mage, and that sitting in the dead centre of the arena was exactly where Mika wanted him.[^lJexGv6dxu8-2716]
* Gnomonkey says he went for javelins like an idiot while Mika outranged them, when crossbow might have reached and done a lot of damage.[^lJexGv6dxu8-2743]
* Gnomonkey says his biggest mistake, which the refs would not let him change, was not having a Karil's skirt, which would have given much more magic defence and might have let him win the fight.[^lJexGv6dxu8-2828]
* Gnomonkey says he made mistakes in the Mika fight, such as not dumping his spec early with a javelin spec, dying with 75% spec left, and forgetting a final venge, but played okay considering the gear difference.[^lJexGv6dxu8-3037]
* Gnomonkey says the Odablock Warriors lost partly because Odablock faced Skilly with half his food and then had to kill Perp as well, with a gear gap, and says every team played to win and played very well.[^lJexGv6dxu8-3743]
* Gnomonkey says he is happy with his finale because he was not KO'd with food, which was his only condition for coming home happy.[^lJexGv6dxu8-3776]

## Events

* Deadman All-Stars takes place on June 20 in person in Chicago with 3,000 seats, so it will be played on LAN rather than risking DDoS.[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-0]
* Gnomonkey fought Mika from the Westham Weasels in the finale for his row; Mika is arguably the best PvPer in that row, and there was a giant gear difference.[^lJexGv6dxu8-2558]
* Mika used both Void Wakers to finish Raikesy, who was on 65 hitpoints with a fang spec, before fighting Gnomonkey; Gnomonkey calls this a smart play.[^lJexGv6dxu8-2617]
* In the second finale fight Gnomonkey fought Coxie, who had extremely lucky RNG; Gnomonkey says he played great, but lost, and Coxie apologised afterwards for how good his RNG was.[^lJexGv6dxu8-3068]

## Trivia

* Gnomonkey is one of the people involved with Deadman All-Stars and says he will attend; he has never done Deadman before and has been dipping into PvP.[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-30]
