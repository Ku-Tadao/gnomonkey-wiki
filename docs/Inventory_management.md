## Methods

- Switching is quickly changing gear, typically during weapon cooldown; an eight-way switch swaps the weapon and every other gear slot. ([2022-05-11 1:23](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=83))
- Most inventory management is used in raids (Chambers and Theatre of Blood); other places to learn switching are safe and easy bosses like Kalphite Queen, Dagannoth Kings and Barrows to an extent, which have huge downtime for switching. ([2022-05-11 2:24](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=144))
- Bosses requiring quick switches are Zulrah, Grotesque Guardians, Nightmare and melee Nex; Gnomonkey says Zulrah is a safer introduction to quick switching, while Nightmare and Nex are harder but will not kill you for slow switches. ([2022-05-11 2:55](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=175))
- The Gauntlet teaches other aspects of inventory management: it needs no real switches but requires careful tracking of food and prayer and where they are in the inventory. ([2022-05-11 3:21](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=201))

## Stats and numbers

- The fastest an eight-way switch can typically be done is three ticks for the entire switch, sometimes two ticks; Gnomonkey has seen a one-tick eight-way but says very few people can do it. ([2022-05-11 1:23](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=83))

## Tips

- Inventory management also includes keeping track of food, prayer and special attacks, both the meter and the items. ([2022-05-11 1:23](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=83))
- Gnomonkey highly recommends laying out switches in a horizontal or vertical 2x2 pattern and zigzagging the mouse to equip them, which makes switches faster and more accurate. ([2022-05-11 2:24](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=144))
- In Chambers, Muttadile, Vanguards, Vasa and Olm all need decent switches, with the 4-0 Olm method arguably the most demanding switches in the game; in Theatre of Blood switching is reactionary and depends on role, such as mages at Maiden and the melee role hybriding to magic at Nylocas. ([2022-05-11 3:21](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=201))
- Raids also require careful supply management, even dropping potions in good locations so you can equip or unequip items; Gnomonkey calls Inferno the definition of supply management, though gear switches are not used there. ([2022-05-11 3:50](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=230))
