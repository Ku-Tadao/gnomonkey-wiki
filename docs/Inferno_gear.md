This page collects Gnomonkey's gear, inventory and supply recommendations for completing the [Inferno](Inferno.md) for a first cape, as they changed between 2021 and 2026. Across the period he moves from a prayer-bonus-first, Kodai-and-Ring-of-the-Gods setup to a damage-first setup with a crystal shield, a Lightbearer, a Blood scepter and prayer regeneration potions instead of stamina potions and heavy tanking gear.

[TOC]

## Priorities

- In 2021 his priorities were first prayer bonus (restores must last through [Zuk](TzKal-Zuk.md), aiming for at least four restores left on reaching Zuk), then ranged and melee defence, then ranged accuracy and damage bonus; mage defence does not help as the player prays mage against mages[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-196].
- By 2025 he says to maximise ranged defence with DPS first and then ranged defence[^H-Iup_IFUVc-120], and in 2025-12 that damage matters more than tanking, so healing damage away is more valuable than avoiding it[^2xviK0wGI-o-218]. In 2026 he says 200 ranged defence is totally fine and the player already has plenty[^7F5_YBzxtE8-436].
- Common mistake (2021): worrying about magic accuracy to kill nibblers faster; they have zero defence, the freeze almost always lands and accuracy only affects whether the hit lands, not damage[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-211]. In 2024 he gives nibblers as 15 magic and negative 20 magic defence, about 96% accuracy regardless, so damage bonus and the chance to one-shot matter[^h6LxupiygPA-1211][^H-Iup_IFUVc-234].

## Weapons

### Ranged

- 2021: dragon arrows with the [Twisted bow](Twisted_bow.md); a blowpipe with rune darts until Zuk and then dragon darts, nothing below rune darts[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-440].
- 2022: a [Toxic blowpipe](Toxic_blowpipe.md) is necessary because it is useful on many monsters and mandatory for healers; he uses it on everything except melees, mages, Jads and Zuk, where he uses the [Bow of Faerdhinen](Bow_of_Faerdhinen.md)[^FfNQI63ZKys-4358].
- 2025-12: bring the best darts possible, with dragon darts amazing and amethyst darts runnable, while worse hurts against melee NPCs[^2xviK0wGI-o-153]. He says never to bring a Scorching bow and that the Bofa is the bare minimum for a first cape[^2xviK0wGI-o-311].
- 2026-07: the best weapon is the Twisted bow and the Bofa is second best; do not bring a Scorching bow, crossbow, Zaryte crossbow or Armadyl crossbow, and without a Bofa or Twisted bow the player is not ready[^7F5_YBzxtE8-154]. About 200 to 300 darts are used per Inferno and dying wastes more supplies[^7F5_YBzxtE8-247].

## Armour and accessories

### Body, legs, helmet and cape

- **First cape setup (2021):** Justiciar faceguard for 67 ranged defence and melee defences in the 60s with only -2 ranged accuracy and +2 prayer[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-297]. Armadyl has slightly better ranged and melee defence than god d'hide and slightly better ranged accuracy; blessed d'hide is acceptable[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-354]. In 2025-12 he reverses this: do not bring [Justiciar armour](Justiciar_armour.md) and do not wear a Justiciar helm over a Masori or crystal helmet, because damage matters for triples and Zuk and Masori's bonus is bigger[^2xviK0wGI-o-218][^2xviK0wGI-o-250].
- **Capes:** the ranged cape is better on task for +4 prayer and defence, a [Max cape](Max_cape.md) is better still (same stats as the ranged cape, collects ammunition, counts as a holy wrench, negative weight and doubled health regeneration)[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-354][^qnw3kGlpiuQ-385].
- **Crystal armour (2025-02):** each piece gives two prayer bonus and good ranged defence but poor melee defence; the setup weighs about 27 kilos, which is why stamina is not needed[^H-Iup_IFUVc-475].
- **Mage top:** in 2021 the ancestral top is optional and used for waves 1 to 47[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-467]. In 2022 he removes it for the crystal body from around wave 50 because of a potential range hit at any wave start, and considers the twisted cult ornament ancestral set necessary for his setup (an Ahrim's top would replace it)[^FfNQI63ZKys-4260][^FfNQI63ZKys-2559]. In 2024-10 he added mage legs to his mage top for a two max hit total and a higher chance of one-shotting nibblers[^6trKOSUr4EM-178]. In 2025-02 he brings Ahrim's robes only for accuracy when healing; Blue Moon armour is arguably better than Ahrim's, Virtus is best-in-slot and Ancestral is better[^H-Iup_IFUVc-261][^H-Iup_IFUVc-289].
- **2026-07 mage gear:** he takes only a Nightmare staff and gauntlets rather than a mage top, because the gauntlet setup has one higher max hit and slightly better accuracy than vambraces with a Virtus top, with defence almost the same as Zaryte vambraces (range defence three lower)[^7F5_YBzxtE8-310]. A mage top after wave 50 gives monsters a 9% higher chance of hitting off spawn (41.5% shown)[^7F5_YBzxtE8-342]; without gauntlets a mage top is needed because accuracy is down about 15% and healing off monsters requires it[^7F5_YBzxtE8-369].
- **Virtus (2024):** best in slot for the Inferno, and best for casting ancients[^h6LxupiygPA-0][^6trKOSUr4EM-91].

### Gloves and quiver

Zaryte vambraces are better than typical gloves, and Barrows gloves are fine; an assembler is fine though a quiver would be better[^h6LxupiygPA-0][^H-Iup_IFUVc-120]. His 2026 max setup also uses the quiver, [Confliction gauntlets](Confliction_gauntlets.md) and vambraces[^7F5_YBzxtE8-121].

| 2023-03 | Pegasian boots or holy sandals either work, the pace is not that tight[^1sv6tIkEHjs-1266] |

| 2025-12 | Best are Pegasian, devout, echo or best of all [Treads](Avernic_treads.md), but boots matter little; he wears a Ranger boots variant; Rada's Blessing 2 for prayer bonus is a big deal[^2xviK0wGI-o-184] |

### Shield

- The [Crystal shield](Crystal_shield.md) is his go-to for its insane ranged defence in 2021; an Elysian spirit shield is the obvious choice if owned, and an arcane spirit shield is decent with 52 ranged defence and +3 prayer[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-413].
- In 2022 he said a crystal shield or ward would be ideal, with the ward about 150 million; his mage attack bonus was 34 with the shield versus 44 without, but nibblers have negative 10 mage defence, so keep it on always, taking it off only when blood barraging for health[^FfNQI63ZKys-55][^FfNQI63ZKys-88][^FfNQI63ZKys-987].
- 2024-10: Crystal shield over the Elidinis' ward for the extra range defence (80), since the ward's one max hit does not matter[^6trKOSUr4EM-272]. 2025-12: the ward has about 28 less ranged defence than the crystal shield, so he always runs the crystal shield, and it should be worn when mage-ing and at the start of each wave[^2xviK0wGI-o-281][^2xviK0wGI-o-2010]. 2026-07: it is the best shield bar the Elidinis' ward and better than a ward or spectral or arcane spirit shield[^7F5_YBzxtE8-121].
- He says to start waves with the shield on and leave it on, since it costs less than 0.5% mage accuracy against nibblers, and a habit of not wearing it carries into the 50s waves where defence matters[^H-Iup_IFUVc-1666][^2xviK0wGI-o-2010].

### Rings

| Date | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| 2021-05 | Ring of the Gods (+8 prayer and the holy wrench effect), or Ring of suffering with a max cape; not granite or archer ring[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-297] |
| 2022-11 | Suffering fine; Ring of the Gods the default for prayer bonus and wrench effect[^FfNQI63ZKys-2604] |
| 2024-01 | Prefers [Lightbearer](Lightbearer.md) for extra Toxic blowpipe specs; with an Eldritch nightmare staff it gives more prayer than Ring of the gods, otherwise about equal. He used Ring of the gods for his first cape only because Lightbearer did not exist then; the spec damage also helps on Zuk, beating even the Venator ring, though Lightbearer is overrated everywhere in the game except the Inferno[^h6LxupiygPA-57][^h6LxupiygPA-84] |
| 2024-10 | Recommends Lightbearer for more frequent blowpipe specs, the melee kill ring rather than a healing ring; Suffering not worth it; Ring of the Gods not bad but not needed with the new potion[^6trKOSUr4EM-298][^6trKOSUr4EM-322] |
| 2025-02 | Devout Lightbearer user because double blowpipe specs kill melee mobs; no Suffering or Gods[^H-Iup_IFUVc-179][^H-Iup_IFUVc-205] |
| 2025-12 | Lightbearer is the only ring to run[^2xviK0wGI-o-121] |
| 2026-07 | Venator ring gives two max hits because the player is off task, so Lightbearer is no longer the automatic choice; he still thinks Lightbearer is much better for getting through the waves and a Venator ring may be worth it once comfortable and after dying to Zuk a few times. Do not bring Ring of suffering[^7F5_YBzxtE8-29][^7F5_YBzxtE8-59] |

Lightbearer lets the player spec the blowpipe more, with specs healing and giving double accuracy and damage, so a melee can be speced before it kills the player[^7F5_YBzxtE8-59]. He noted in 2024 that the Lightbearer sells for about one million at the Tombs of Amascut and is undervalued[^6trKOSUr4EM-1816].

## Other items and warnings

- **Saradomin godsword:** 2021: can replace a third restore as melee gear to restore prayer and drive-by nibblers, but never to cheese specs, and only once Zuk is reached; 2022: never bring it, since it is misused on first capes while Eldritch nightmare staff is worth a ton of prayer; 2025-12: do not bring an SGS, as it wastes more prayer than it saves, and Bandos chestplate is unnecessary because more brew is better than tanking gear[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-559][^FfNQI63ZKys-2644][^2xviK0wGI-o-218]. See [Saradomin godsword](Saradomin_godsword.md).
- **Falador shield 4:** restores prayer twice a day, best used early in attempts[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-528]. In the 2021 setup, pre-entry: drink a divine ranging/Bastion, heal to full, eat an anglerfish, drink a stamina and use an [Imbued heart](Imbued_heart.md)[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-591].
- **Vengeance:** be pre-venged before waves (2023)[^1sv6tIkEHjs-59]. See [Vengeance](Vengeance.md).
- **Blood Barrage:** when barraging for HP there is no time limit, so negative magic bonus pieces can be removed[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-211].
- **Mage tank (2026):** he is not a fan: it makes the waves easy but gives a much longer and harder Zuk, makes healers slow and dangerous and leaves little defence outside the full tank setup; he recommends a normal cape[^7F5_YBzxtE8-270]. See [Pillarless mage tank Inferno](Pillarless_mage_tank_Inferno.md).
- **Chat reminders:** have a chat member remind about equipping wrong items, such as Anguish[^1sv6tIkEHjs-2812].

## Inventory and supplies

| Date | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| 2021-05 | Mage switch, blowpipe, about eight brews to start (at least four on reaching Zuk), restores for the rest, a rune pouch with maximum soul, death and blood runes, a stamina and a Bastion potion for Zuk; with Justiciar, swap two restores for pieces[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-495][^qnw3kGlpiuQ-559] |
| 2023-03 | Flicking is not the focus; bring enough restore not to flick much; do not panic about prayer (he finished with over two spare); the pillar does exactly half of the HP of the monster remaining, so drag monsters into it[^1sv6tIkEHjs-523][^1sv6tIkEHjs-2812][^1sv6tIkEHjs-2909] |
| 2024-01 | Two restores on Zuk is infinite prayer, so Rigour and a protection prayer can be camped; a divine ranging potion for Zuk since it drains about four levels[^h6LxupiygPA-2488][^h6LxupiygPA-3534] |
| 2025-02 | Three [Prayer regeneration potions](Prayer_regeneration_potion.md) for a Bofa run (arguably four if over two hours), each effectively two restores and not scaling with Prayer level; stamina only as a backup, otherwise an extra restore; a divine ranging potion mostly restores stats drained by brews; about two restores and two brews for Zuk; be fully potted for Zuk[^H-Iup_IFUVc-409][^H-Iup_IFUVc-442][^H-Iup_IFUVc-475][^H-Iup_IFUVc-2034][^H-Iup_IFUVc-3987] |
| 2025-12 | Pre-pot with Anglerfish, divine ranging and a prayer regeneration; no stamina potion since the run energy changes[^2xviK0wGI-o-345] |
| 2026-07 | Seven regular brews plus three armor brews (about ten), ten restores plus three prayer regeneration potions (16 restores in total), one Divine bastion potion, which he calls overkill; no stamina potion since the run energy rework[^7F5_YBzxtE8-369][^7F5_YBzxtE8-427] |

In 2021 he also noted that overcharging prayer points in the player's house with max melee gear is possible before entering[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-591].
