The **Jal-Xil**, the ranger of the [Inferno](Inferno.md), first spawns on wave 18, maxes a 46 and has no unique mechanics, attacking with ranged or melee every four ticks[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-900][^2xviK0wGI-o-1464][^7F5_YBzxtE8-1454]. Gnomonkey describes it as incredibly basic, very accurate and inaccurate melee, and treats it above all as a source of healing and as the first enemy a player primarily flicks against[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-932][^6trKOSUr4EM-1098][^2xviK0wGI-o-1464]. A ranger has about double the accuracy of a melee monster, so it is a bigger threat even though it maxes lower, and the player should not tank ranged hits[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1511].

## Stats

| Stat | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Max hit (ranged) | 46 |[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-900][^7F5_YBzxtE8-1454] |
| Melee punch max | 19, one higher than a bloblet's |[^h6LxupiygPA-1269][^6trKOSUr4EM-1098][^2xviK0wGI-o-2897] |
| Defence | 60 (bats 55, nibblers 15) |[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-960] |
| Accuracy | Very accurate at range; ranger hits around 40% on the double ranger plus mage wave |[^H-Iup_IFUVc-4500][^2xviK0wGI-o-1464] |

Rangers have the lowest defence of any Inferno monster besides bats and nibblers, and a big pile of health[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-960]. From wave 50, rangers can hit instantly off spawn with no delay; at the start of a wave a ranger can instantly hit for as much as a 46, so health should be at 99 when starting waves that contain rangers[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1672][^XMTe9TYbDls-2524]. Because the melee punch maxes only 19, being forced to be close to a ranger is not a big deal and punches are not to be feared[^h6LxupiygPA-1269][^2xviK0wGI-o-2897][^7F5_YBzxtE8-1894].

## Prayer flicking

The player starts waves praying ranged until mages spawn. Rangers are the first enemy primarily flicked, since their attack is calculated when the crouch animation starts[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-932]. To flick, turn the ranged prayer on as soon as it does its animation and comes to a full standing position; knowing the exact tick it is calculated is essential for pillar stacks later[^h6LxupiygPA-889]. His "lazy flick" teaching changed over time: in October 2024 he said to lazy flick any attacker as the animation starts, as prayer goes up the tick the animation starts, then back down; in February 2025 he said to click the prayer on just before the ranger is about to hunker down, which saves prayer and teaches attack timing[^6trKOSUr4EM-1129][^H-Iup_IFUVc-1342]. In July 2026 he recommended learning to one-tick flick rangers by turning the prayer on the instant the animation starts, mainly to know exactly when it attacks so the player can alternate with a blob[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1481]. Two-tick flicking is very good for speedruns and mobility but much harder than it looks, and first-cape players should not bother[^h6LxupiygPA-1360].

To solve a ranger and blob together, wait until the ranger has attacked, then start one-tick alternating; every fourth tick the ranger attack is covered because it attacks on an even cycle. He alternates by watching the prayer lights: keep both lit and click the next prayer as soon as the previous one turns off[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1572][^7F5_YBzxtE8-1630].

## Healing and targets

Rangers have a very low mage level, so the player rarely splashes and can heal off them like a medkit; they are great targets for [Blood Barrage](Blood_Barrage.md) and give about 20 health on a phantom, the next best healing target after blobs[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-960][^6lRmbYCYtME-624][^XMTe9TYbDls-1632][^2xviK0wGI-o-1464]. With 108 health from the Blood scepter, the player can simply go for the nibblers while casting at rangers[^6trKOSUr4EM-1365]. The [Toxic blowpipe](Toxic_blowpipe.md) is better than a magic weapon against rangers since they have less defence than melee monsters, and rangers are a squishy target for [Saradomin godsword](Saradomin_godsword.md) specs[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-960][^h6LxupiygPA-889]. Gnomonkey says to turn Rigor on before a blowpipe special for health because it gives a lot more healing on average[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1630]. Rangers can be mageed pretty easily, making them a decent healing target[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1454].

## Positioning and pillars

Never kill rangers first: killing them is wasting time in mage plus ranger waves, and if a mage respawns it places the ranger behind the mage, creating a bad pillar stack. Avoid killing a ranger unless necessary[^6lRmbYCYtME-4471][^XMTe9TYbDls-2614][^H-Iup_IFUVc-3300][^2xviK0wGI-o-4635]. A ranger can serve as a pillar the same way as a mage, since any monster works as a pillar[^XMTe9TYbDls-3000]. It can see the player from the corner of a pillar as it walks up, and still sees the player for a moment after stepping out of line, so this matters when going for nibblers; casting from behind a pillar at nibblers works even if they are directly on the pillar[^6lRmbYCYtME-979][^H-Iup_IFUVc-1342][^H-Iup_IFUVc-3993]. On the double ranger plus mage wave, go to the side without the mage, since the mage hit is almost guaranteed while ranger hits have around 40% accuracy[^H-Iup_IFUVc-4500].
