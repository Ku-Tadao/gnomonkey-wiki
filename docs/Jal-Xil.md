## Methods

- To solve a ranger and blob together, wait until the ranger has attacked, then start one-tick alternating; every fourth tick the ranger attack is covered because he attacks on an even cycle. ([2026-07-13 26:12](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=1572))

## Stats and numbers

- Rangers start spawning on wave 18, max a 46 and have no unique mechanics, attacking with ranged or melee every four ticks. ([2021-05-21 15:00](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=900))
- Rangers have 60 defence, bats 55 and nibblers 15, so rangers have the lowest defence of any Inferno monster besides bats and nibblers; they also have a big pile of health, making them free medkits for Blood Barrage and a squishy target for Saradomin godsword specs. ([2021-05-21 16:00](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=960))
- Gnomonkey says in the Inferno melee monsters max 19 when a ranger punches you, so being forced to be close to a ranger is not a big deal. ([2024-01-17 21:09](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=1269))
- From wave 50, rangers can hit you instantly off spawn with no delay. ([2024-06-13 42:04](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2524))
- Inferno rangers are described as incredibly basic and punch for very little; their melee punch max is 19, one higher than a bloblet, and they are incredibly inaccurate. ([2024-10-03 18:18](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=1098))
- The ranger (Jal-Xil) has no special mechanic, maxes 46 and is very accurate. ([2025-12-07 24:24](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=1464))
- A ranger's melee punch only maxes 19, the same as tanking a bloblet, so Gnomonkey says not to fear ranger punches. ([2025-12-07 48:17](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=2897))
- The Inferno ranger has a max hit of 46 on a four-tick attack speed and nothing special; Gnomonkey says you can pretty easily mage him, so rangers are a decent target to heal from. ([2026-07-13 24:14](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=1454))
- In the demo Gnomonkey notes a ranger punch maxing 19 in a particular pinch, saying ranger hits are not dangerous at that point. ([2026-07-13 31:34](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=1894))

## Tips

- You start waves praying ranged until mages spawn; rangers are the first enemy you primarily flick, as their attack is calculated when the crouch animation starts. ([2021-05-21 15:32](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=932))
- A ranger can instantly hit you for as much as a 46 at the start of a wave, so keep health at 99 when starting the waves with rangers. ([2021-05-21 27:52](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1672))
- Gnomonkey says rangers are great targets for phantom healing because they have a really low mage level, so you will not splash on them often. ([2022-07-22 10:24](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=624))
- Gnomonkey says the ranger can see you from a corner of the pillar as it walks up, so do not assume monsters cannot see you just because you are in a certain spot. ([2022-07-22 16:19](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=979))
- Gnomonkey says not to kill rangers when you do not have to, because if one respawns you get a pillar stack. ([2022-07-22 1:14:31](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=4471))
- To flick a Jal-Xil (ranger), flick your ranged prayer on as soon as it does its animation and comes to a full standing position; Gnomonkey calls knowing the exact tick it is calculated essential for pillar stacks later. ([2024-01-17 14:49](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=889))
- Rangers give about 20 health on a phantom and are the next best target for healing after blobs, as they have the next lowest mage level and a lot of health. ([2024-06-13 27:12](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1632))
- A ranger can be used as a pillar the same way as a mage, as the video says any monster works as a pillar. ([2024-06-13 50:00](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=3000))
- Gnomonkey says to lazy flick any attacker you pray against as the attack animation starts: prayer goes up the tick the animation starts, then back down. ([2024-10-03 18:49](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=1129))
- Gnomonkey says rangers heal you like medkits when you cast at them, and with 108 health from the Blood scepter you can just go get the nibblers. ([2024-10-03 22:45](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=1365))
- Gnomonkey teaches 'lazy flicking' against rangers: click the prayer on just before they are about to hunker down, which saves prayer and teaches you their attack timing. ([2025-02-26 22:22](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=1342))
- Gnomonkey says you can cast from behind a pillar at nibblers even if they are directly on the pillar. ([2025-02-26 22:22](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=1342))
- Gnomonkey says a ranger still sees you for a moment even when you step out of line, so remember that when going to get nibblers. ([2025-02-26 1:06:33](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=3993))
- On the double ranger plus mage wave, Gnomonkey says to go to the side without the mage, because the mage hit is almost guaranteed while ranger hits have around 40% accuracy. ([2025-02-26 1:15:00](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=4500))
- Rangers have very low mage defence so you can heal off them like a med kit. ([2025-12-07 24:24](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=1464))
- Gnomonkey says avoid killing rangers before mages if possible because a mage respawning the ranger leaves you a bad pillar stack. ([2025-12-07 1:17:15](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=4635))
- Gnomonkey recommends learning to one-tick prayer flick rangers by turning the prayer on the instant his animation starts, mainly to know exactly when he attacks so you can alternate with a blob. ([2026-07-13 24:41](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=1481))
- Gnomonkey alternates by watching the prayer lights: keep both lit and click the next prayer as soon as the previous one turns off. ([2026-07-13 27:10](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=1630))
- Gnomonkey says to turn on Rigor before a blowpipe special attack for health because it gives a lot more healing on average. ([2026-07-13 27:10](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=1630))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the Toxic blowpipe is better than a magic weapon against rangers since they have less defence than the melee monsters. ([2024-01-17 14:49](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=889))
- Gnomonkey calls two-tick flicking really good for speedruns and mobility but a lot harder than it looks to pull off, and says first-cape players should not bother. ([2024-01-17 22:40](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=1360))
- Gnomonkey says killing the ranger first is wasting time for no reason in mage plus ranger waves. ([2024-06-13 43:34](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2614))
- Gnomonkey says never to kill rangers first and to avoid killing a ranger if you do not have to, because when a mage respawns it puts the ranger behind the mage and creates bad stacks; also you can heal off a ranger. ([2025-02-26 55:00](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=3300))
- Gnomonkey says rangers have about double the accuracy of a melee and are a bigger threat even though they max lower, so do not tank ranged hits. ([2026-07-13 25:11](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=1511))
