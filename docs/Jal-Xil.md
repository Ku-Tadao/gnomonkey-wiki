## Methods

* To solve a ranger and blob together, wait until the ranger has attacked, then start one-tick alternating; every fourth tick the ranger attack is covered because he attacks on an even cycle.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1572]

## Stats and numbers

* Rangers start spawning on wave 18, max a 46 and have no unique mechanics, attacking with ranged or melee every four ticks.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-900]
* Rangers have 60 defence, bats 55 and nibblers 15, so rangers have the lowest defence of any Inferno monster besides bats and nibblers; they also have a big pile of health, making them free medkits for Blood Barrage and a squishy target for Saradomin godsword specs.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-960]
* In the Inferno melee monsters max 19 when a ranger punches you, so being forced to be close to a ranger is not a big deal.[^h6LxupiygPA-1269]
* From wave 50, rangers can hit you instantly off spawn with no delay.[^XMTe9TYbDls-2524]
* Inferno rangers are described as incredibly basic and punch for very little; their melee punch max is 19, one higher than a bloblet, and they are incredibly inaccurate.[^6trKOSUr4EM-1098]
* The ranger (Jal-Xil) has no special mechanic, maxes 46 and is very accurate.[^2xviK0wGI-o-1464]
* A ranger's melee punch only maxes 19, the same as tanking a bloblet, so Gnomonkey says not to fear ranger punches.[^2xviK0wGI-o-2897]
* The Inferno ranger has a max hit of 46 on a four-tick attack speed and nothing special; Gnomonkey says you can pretty easily mage him, so rangers are a decent target to heal from.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1454]
* In the demo Gnomonkey notes a ranger punch maxing 19 in a particular pinch, saying ranger hits are not dangerous at that point.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1894]

## Tips

* You start waves praying ranged until mages spawn; rangers are the first enemy you primarily flick, as their attack is calculated when the crouch animation starts.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-932]
* A ranger can instantly hit you for as much as a 46 at the start of a wave, so keep health at 99 when starting the waves with rangers.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1672]
* Rangers are great targets for phantom healing because they have a really low mage level, so you will not splash on them often.[^6lRmbYCYtME-624]
* The ranger can see you from a corner of the pillar as it walks up, so do not assume monsters cannot see you just because you are in a certain spot.[^6lRmbYCYtME-979]
* Not to kill rangers when you do not have to, because if one respawns you get a pillar stack.[^6lRmbYCYtME-4471]
* To flick a Jal-Xil (ranger), flick your ranged prayer on as soon as it does its animation and comes to a full standing position; Gnomonkey calls knowing the exact tick it is calculated essential for pillar stacks later.[^h6LxupiygPA-889]
* Rangers give about 20 health on a phantom and are the next best target for healing after blobs, as they have the next lowest mage level and a lot of health.[^XMTe9TYbDls-1632]
* A ranger can be used as a pillar the same way as a mage, as the video says any monster works as a pillar.[^XMTe9TYbDls-3000]
* To lazy flick any attacker you pray against as the attack animation starts: prayer goes up the tick the animation starts, then back down.[^6trKOSUr4EM-1129]
* Rangers heal you like medkits when you cast at them, and with 108 health from the Blood scepter you can just go get the nibblers.[^6trKOSUr4EM-1365]
* Gnomonkey teaches 'lazy flicking' against rangers: click the prayer on just before they are about to hunker down, which saves prayer and teaches you their attack timing.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-1342]
* You can cast from behind a pillar at nibblers even if they are directly on the pillar.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-1342]
* A ranger still sees you for a moment even when you step out of line, so remember that when going to get nibblers.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-3993]
* On the double ranger plus mage wave, Gnomonkey says to go to the side without the mage, because the mage hit is almost guaranteed while ranger hits have around 40% accuracy.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-4500]
* Rangers have very low mage defence so you can heal off them like a med kit.[^2xviK0wGI-o-1464]
* Avoid killing rangers before mages if possible because a mage respawning the ranger leaves you a bad pillar stack.[^2xviK0wGI-o-4635]
* Gnomonkey recommends learning to one-tick prayer flick rangers by turning the prayer on the instant his animation starts, mainly to know exactly when he attacks so you can alternate with a blob.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1481]
* Gnomonkey alternates by watching the prayer lights: keep both lit and click the next prayer as soon as the previous one turns off.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1630]
* To turn on Rigor before a blowpipe special attack for health because it gives a lot more healing on average.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1630]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the Toxic blowpipe is better than a magic weapon against rangers since they have less defence than the melee monsters.[^h6LxupiygPA-889]
* Gnomonkey calls two-tick flicking really good for speedruns and mobility but a lot harder than it looks to pull off, and says first-cape players should not bother.[^h6LxupiygPA-1360]
* Gnomonkey says killing the ranger first is wasting time for no reason in mage plus ranger waves.[^XMTe9TYbDls-2614]
* Gnomonkey says never to kill rangers first and to avoid killing a ranger if you do not have to, because when a mage respawns it puts the ranger behind the mage and creates bad stacks; also you can heal off a ranger.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-3300]
* Gnomonkey says rangers have about double the accuracy of a melee and are a bigger threat even though they max lower, so do not tank ranged hits.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1511]
