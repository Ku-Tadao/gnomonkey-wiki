**Bronzeman Mode** is an account topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2026-08-12 and 2026-08-21. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Gnomonkey lists items whose grinds Bronzeman would skip: one armor seed lets you buy all six, one enhanced seed lets you buy a Blade of Saeldor, one tormented synapse unlocks all Tormented Demon weapons, one burning claw lets you buy the other, one Venator shard lets you buy the other four, one Oathplate shard lets you buy a full Oathplate, and one Zenyte lets you buy all four Zenytes.[^8hDdqKnk2cw-441]

## Tips

* Gnomonkey suggests Jagex could have added a trade blacklist for items such as armor seeds, enhanced weapon seeds and synapses, polled as an option.[^8hDdqKnk2cw-456]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey thinks the Bronzeman blog needs far more detail, such as what counts as unlocking an item, and asks whether killing a Mad Angel for a super combat would permanently bypass the Herblore requirement, or a dragon dart from a dragon impling would bypass Fletching.[^FrZfpY0uoko-60]
* Gnomonkey says if Bronzeman unlocks completely bypass skilling requirements, that would change people's opinion of the mode, since many players care about meeting skilling requirements as well as PvM drops.[^FrZfpY0uoko-90]
* Gnomonkey says Bronzeman solves supply upkeep, such as unlocking toxic blowpipe scales from Zulrah with a single kill, but does not solve the real problem that ironmen get stuck on stupidly long grinds, which is why people quit.[^FrZfpY0uoko-570]
* Gnomonkey says a Bronzeman would still have to sit through the Corrupted Gauntlet grind for the Bow of Faerdhinen, possibly only buying the Blade of Saeldor or leftover armor seeds, and an endgame Bronzeman is basically a main.[^FrZfpY0uoko-617]
* Gnomonkey says Bronzeman feels gimmicky as a slightly different Ironman, that a hundred Ironman variants should not exist and each would bloat the highscores, and that Bronzeman-like restrictions can be done on a main with a plugin.[^FrZfpY0uoko-1353]
* Gnomonkey says he personally would not play Bronzeman and, after weighing it, would vote no on both regular and Unbound Bronzeman, although a better fleshed-out version might win him over.[^FrZfpY0uoko-1531]
* Gnomonkey says 'why not, it has no dev cost' is not a good reason to add it, comparing it to RuneScape 3 adding unused content such as minigames outside Falador that later had to be cleaned up.[^FrZfpY0uoko-1531]
* Gnomonkey notes the blog never defined what an item unlock entails, and says details such as getting a potion drop from a clue scroll counting as an unlock were only discussed by a Jagex developer (J Mod) on a podcast and in Reddit replies.[^8hDdqKnk2cw-266]
* Gnomonkey says Bronzeman unlocks skip entire skilling grinds, for example one super combat drop would let you buy super combats without Herblore.[^8hDdqKnk2cw-383]
* Gnomonkey says Bronzeman would also skip PvM grinds, which he considers a bigger problem than skipping skilling, because PvM grinds are why people play ironman at all.[^8hDdqKnk2cw-414]
* Gnomonkey says Jagex calling it 'Iron Man Light' or 'Diet Iron' left no clear identity (Ironman without upkeep versus a main with few restrictions), making it easy for people to find something they disliked.[^8hDdqKnk2cw-512]
* Gnomonkey raises an integrity concern: a Bronzeman could sell a low-volume item on the Grand Exchange and a main could buy it for 100 million, effectively giving the Bronzeman unlimited GP.[^8hDdqKnk2cw-579]
* Gnomonkey says there was no mention of how bonds would work, and that restricting Bronzeman to buying only (no selling) would have solved both the low-volume item and bond problems.[^8hDdqKnk2cw-642]
* Gnomonkey argues Bronzeman is not zero dev cost, because every new boss drop, skilling update or dry-protection system (like Oathplate shards) must be considered for Bronzeman, comparing it to Ultimate Ironman needing consideration for updates like Sailing cargo.[^8hDdqKnk2cw-753]
* Gnomonkey says one option would be no highscores for Bronzeman so it is a purely for-fun mode that gets no future consideration.[^8hDdqKnk2cw-804]
* Gnomonkey says he would have voted yes if Bronzeman were clearly Ironman without supply upkeep: no selling on the Grand Exchange, blacklisted shard and synapse items, and unlocks only once you have the level to make the item, so you never worry about Zulrah scales, blowpipe darts or dragon arrows.[^8hDdqKnk2cw-1281]
* Gnomonkey says less clearly defined in-between modes (play your own way with some restrictions) work better as a plugin, since an official mode becomes hard to balance.[^8hDdqKnk2cw-1380]

## Events

* Jagex polled Bronzeman Mode for Old School RuneScape, a community-designed game mode described as Iron Man Light where, once you have unlocked an item legitimately, you can buy and sell that item exclusively through the Grand Exchange.[^FrZfpY0uoko-0]
* Jagex said Bronzeman was the most popular option in its recent quarterly survey, that the mods worked on an official implementation during Game Jam 8, and that it is essentially ready to go; it would show unlocks in an interface and possibly have collection-log-like milestone ranks.[^FrZfpY0uoko-0]
