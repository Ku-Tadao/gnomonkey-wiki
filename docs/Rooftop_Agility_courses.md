**Rooftop Agility courses** is an activity topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2020-07-25. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* From level 10 to 20 use the Draynor Village rooftop course, the first course that can give Marks of Grace; it gives about 9k XP an hour and takes 28 laps to reach level 20.[^pEzKRL_6dSQ-168]
* The Al Kharid rooftop course from 20 to 30 is marginally faster than Draynor, needs stamina potions, and would take an additional 50 laps from level 20 to 30.[^pEzKRL_6dSQ-196]
* From level 30 to 40 use the Varrock rooftop course, which gives about 13k XP an hour, drains a small amount of run energy each lap so needs stamina potions, and takes 101 laps to level 40.[^pEzKRL_6dSQ-234]
* From level 40 to 60 Gnomonkey typically uses the Canifis rooftop course, which is very good for Marks of Grace and gives up to 20k XP an hour without failing; 40 to 60 there takes 986 laps.[^pEzKRL_6dSQ-248]
* The Wilderness Agility Course from level 52 to 60 gives upwards of 35k XP an hour but gives no Marks of Grace; Gnomonkey recommends it if you do not mind getting the Graceful outfit later.[^pEzKRL_6dSQ-248]
* From level 60 to 72 or 75 use the Seers' Village rooftop course.[^pEzKRL_6dSQ-284]
* At level 90 the Ardougne rooftop course unlocks; it is very low intensity and, with the Elite Ardougne Diary giving 25% more Marks of Grace, is the best course for Marks of Grace in the game.[^pEzKRL_6dSQ-485]

## Stats and numbers

* The Ardougne rooftop course gives a maximum of about 62k XP an hour and about 220k GP an hour, slightly less XP than the Prifddinas course but more GP.[^pEzKRL_6dSQ-485]

## Tips

* Gnomonkey recommends staying at Draynor until level 30 (102 laps) because the Al Kharid course is only about 400 XP an hour faster and needs stamina potions.[^pEzKRL_6dSQ-168]
* Gnomonkey left Marks of Grace on the ground until they hit a stack of ten and then picked them up, because a disconnect instantly despawns all marks on the ground.[^pEzKRL_6dSQ-485]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey tells players to skip Werewolf Skullball, joking that you will regret it.[^pEzKRL_6dSQ-196]

## Trivia

* On the Ardougne rooftop course Marks of Grace only spawn on a single tile, and they do not disappear for ten minutes, with the timer resetting each time a mark drops.[^pEzKRL_6dSQ-485]
