## Methods

- From level 10 to 20 use the Draynor Village rooftop course, the first course that can give Marks of Grace; it gives about 9k XP an hour and takes 28 laps to reach level 20. ([2020-07-25 2:48](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=168))
- The Al Kharid rooftop course from 20 to 30 is marginally faster than Draynor, needs stamina potions, and would take an additional 50 laps from level 20 to 30. ([2020-07-25 3:16](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=196))
- From level 30 to 40 use the Varrock rooftop course, which gives about 13k XP an hour, drains a small amount of run energy each lap so needs stamina potions, and takes 101 laps to level 40. ([2020-07-25 3:54](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=234))
- From level 40 to 60 Gnomonkey typically uses the Canifis rooftop course, which is very good for Marks of Grace and gives up to 20k XP an hour without failing; 40 to 60 there takes 986 laps. ([2020-07-25 4:08](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=248))
- The Wilderness Agility Course from level 52 to 60 gives upwards of 35k XP an hour but gives no Marks of Grace; Gnomonkey recommends it if you do not mind getting the Graceful outfit later. ([2020-07-25 4:08](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=248))
- From level 60 to 72 or 75 use the Seers' Village rooftop course. ([2020-07-25 4:44](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=284))
- At level 90 the Ardougne rooftop course unlocks; it is very low intensity and, with the Elite Ardougne Diary giving 25% more Marks of Grace, is the best course for Marks of Grace in the game. ([2020-07-25 8:05](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=485))

## Stats and numbers

- The Ardougne rooftop course gives a maximum of about 62k XP an hour and about 220k GP an hour, slightly less XP than the Prifddinas course but more GP. ([2020-07-25 8:05](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=485))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey recommends staying at Draynor until level 30 (102 laps) because the Al Kharid course is only about 400 XP an hour faster and needs stamina potions. ([2020-07-25 2:48](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=168))
- Gnomonkey left Marks of Grace on the ground until they hit a stack of ten and then picked them up, because a disconnect instantly despawns all marks on the ground. ([2020-07-25 8:05](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=485))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey tells players to skip Werewolf Skullball, joking that you will regret it. ([2020-07-25 3:16](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=196))

## Trivia

- On the Ardougne rooftop course Marks of Grace only spawn on a single tile, and they do not disappear for ten minutes, with the timer resetting each time a mark drops. ([2020-07-25 8:05](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=485))
