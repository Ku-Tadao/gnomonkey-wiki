## Methods

- The Rogues' Den is located under the bar in Burthorpe, and can be accessed quickly with a games necklace. ([2020-05-23 0:31](https://youtu.be/-6Jg4JTUA1w?t=31))
- Gnomonkey enters the maze by talking to Brian O'Richard outside the gate, since agility is drained throughout the maze. ([2020-05-23 0:59](https://youtu.be/-6Jg4JTUA1w?t=59))
- The maze has a long route and a short route; the short route takes a shortcut requiring 80 Thieving that skips half the maze. ([2020-05-23 2:34](https://youtu.be/-6Jg4JTUA1w?t=154))
- Near the end of the long route the player must pick up the flash powder on the right, use it on the guard, and run past while he is stunned, then pass two pendulums to the wall safes. ([2020-05-23 7:31](https://youtu.be/-6Jg4JTUA1w?t=451))
- The short route begins the same as the long route, then diverges at a door which is lockpicked, and ends with the flash powder, guard, two pendulums and the safes. ([2020-05-23 9:00](https://youtu.be/-6Jg4JTUA1w?t=540))

## Stats and numbers

- Disarming a thieving trap in the maze reduces your Thieving level and passing an agility obstacle reduces Agility; if either hits zero you are booted out, and triggering a single trap also boots you to the start. ([2020-05-23 1:29](https://youtu.be/-6Jg4JTUA1w?t=89))

## Tips

- You cannot bring anything into the Rogues' Den maze, but a stamina potion effect carries over, so Gnomonkey recommends drinking a one-dose potion before entering. ([2020-05-23 0:31](https://youtu.be/-6Jg4JTUA1w?t=31))
- Gnomonkey recommends enabling the Rogues' Den, Ground Markers and Object Markers RuneLite plugins; the Rogues' Den plugin highlights all traps and the markers let you shift-click tiles or objects you need to stand on or move to. ([2020-05-23 0:59](https://youtu.be/-6Jg4JTUA1w?t=59))
- In the Rogues' Den maze you can run over a maximum of three traps if you start from the tile before them, which Gnomonkey says to use liberally. ([2020-05-23 1:29](https://youtu.be/-6Jg4JTUA1w?t=89))
- In the maze pendulums are no threat if you walk in front of them, ledges cannot be failed, and saw blades must be clicked on top of or the character paths around them and gets hit. ([2020-05-23 3:06](https://youtu.be/-6Jg4JTUA1w?t=186))
- All wall safes in the maze are the same, so Gnomonkey cracks whichever is closest. ([2020-05-23 7:31](https://youtu.be/-6Jg4JTUA1w?t=451))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey does not recommend waiting until 80 Thieving to use the shortcut because you would have wasted a lot of gp reaching that level, but says to use it if you already have 80. ([2020-05-23 2:34](https://youtu.be/-6Jg4JTUA1w?t=154))
