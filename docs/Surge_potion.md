# Surge potion

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 4 videos (2023-08-18 to 2025-07-06). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Gnomonkey says in RS3 you unlock the Surge ability (a dash) by completing the circus agility course, and that the XP from it is insane. ([2023-08-18 3:31](https://youtu.be/KIticRFI1Rc?t=211))

## Requirements

- Making a Surge potion needs level 81 Herblore, demonic tallow (a new drop from the new boss), a Torstol, and a drop of your own blood, which makes it an untradeable potion. ([2025-03-29 2:03](https://youtu.be/W1OX5PVaPxs?t=123))

## Stats and numbers

- A Surge potion lets you sip once every five minutes to restore 25% of your special attack energy. ([2025-03-29 1:27](https://youtu.be/W1OX5PVaPxs?t=87))
- Jagex's blog suggests Surge potions would help during rage phases, clutch moments on low hit points, speedrunning, or to start every kill with a defence-reducing special attack. ([2025-03-29 1:27](https://youtu.be/W1OX5PVaPxs?t=87))
- In the surge special Nightmare jumps into the ground and appears on an edge of the arena, then after a few ticks instantly jumps to the other side; standing in the area she runs through deals around 85 damage. ([2025-07-04 9:11](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=551))
- A surge potion gives 25% special attack energy and you can only drink a dose every five minutes; in a 500 you get to use the whole potion, which Gnomonkey says is rare. ([2025-07-06 4:05](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=245))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says early in the RS3 account he unlocked teleports to Catherby, Taverley and Burthorpe via lodestones. ([2023-08-18 5:31](https://youtu.be/KIticRFI1Rc?t=331))
- During surge you can still hit Nightmare from her sides, or quickly get a hit in and step out of the way. ([2025-07-04 9:39](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=579))
- Gnomonkey brings surge potions for extra Void Waker DPS, noting they are cheap to make and Yama drops a lot of them for Ironmen. ([2025-07-06 4:05](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=245))
- Gnomonkey says to use the whole surge potion, anywhere you would Void Waker, but save a dose for Warden P4, the most important time to have spec energy. ([2025-07-06 38:07](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=2287))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey calls getting Surge a high-priority early mobility unlock and assigns it its own hotkey. ([2023-08-18 5:48](https://youtu.be/KIticRFI1Rc?t=348))
- Gnomonkey says Surge potions are fairly weak, not broken, and that people are scared of them because potions that give damage carry the stigma of overloads and RS3 adrenaline potions. ([2025-03-29 0:28](https://youtu.be/W1OX5PVaPxs?t=28))
- Gnomonkey disagrees with a claim that another player could make Surge potions for you in bulk, noting the recipe requires your own blood. ([2025-03-29 2:03](https://youtu.be/W1OX5PVaPxs?t=123))
- Gnomonkey says the 'rule that every future boss and combat achievement will be balanced around Surge potions' worry is a combat-achievement design problem, not a reason the potion should not exist. ([2025-03-29 2:28](https://youtu.be/W1OX5PVaPxs?t=148))
- Gnomonkey guesses Surge potions would be expensive, at least a few thousand gp and possibly over 10k per dose, because the tallow should not drop in massive quantities, though he stresses he is only guessing. ([2025-03-29 14:48](https://youtu.be/W1OX5PVaPxs?t=888))
- Gnomonkey says he finds the fearmongering around Surge potions weird, that in many cases it will not be worth the slot over other items, and that people should check the numbers. ([2025-03-29 15:45](https://youtu.be/W1OX5PVaPxs?t=945))

## Events

- Surge potions passed their poll with only about 73% of the vote, whereas most updates get around 85 to 95%. ([2025-03-29 0:00](https://youtu.be/W1OX5PVaPxs))
