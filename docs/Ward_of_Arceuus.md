# Ward of Arceuus

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2024-05-12 to 2025-05-15). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|

## Stats and numbers

- Ward of Arceuus makes demons hit you 10% less, reducing the damage by 10% rounded down, which Gnomonkey says makes it not great. ([2024-05-12 1:31](https://youtu.be/_nMz6X90hb0?t=91))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says he does not need death runes for the Ward of Arceuus and Mark of Darkness casts he plans against K'ril. ([2024-05-12 1:31](https://youtu.be/_nMz6X90hb0?t=91))
- Gnomonkey says Ward of Arceus only costs nature runes in addition to what you already bring, so it is worth a rune slot. ([2025-05-14 18:39](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1119))

## Trivia

- Ward of Arceus reduces damage coming towards you from demons; Gnomonkey believes it takes about 10% off each Yama hit. ([2025-05-14 18:12](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1092))
- Gnomonkey brings nature runes for Ward of Arceus at Yama because it reduces his damage by 10%. ([2025-05-15 10:43](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=643))
