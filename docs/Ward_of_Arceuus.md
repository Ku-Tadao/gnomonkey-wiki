**Ward of Arceuus** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2024-05-12 and 2025-05-15. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Ward of Arceuus makes demons hit you 10% less, reducing the damage by 10% rounded down, which Gnomonkey says makes it not great.[^_nMz6X90hb0-91]

## Tips

* He does not need death runes for the Ward of Arceuus and Mark of Darkness casts he plans against K'ril.[^_nMz6X90hb0-91]
* Ward of Arceus only costs nature runes in addition to what you already bring, so it is worth a rune slot.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-1119]

## Trivia

* Ward of Arceus reduces damage coming towards you from demons; Gnomonkey believes it takes about 10% off each Yama hit.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-1092]
* Gnomonkey brings nature runes for Ward of Arceus at Yama because it reduces his damage by 10%.[^prE1BqM1wBE-643]
