**Scorpia** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2022-03-19 and 2022-07-13. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Scorpia is in a cave just north of the Chaos Elemental, is very weak to magic and only attacks with melee, so players freeze her in place to avoid being attacked; at half HP she summons two healing scorpions which can be killed, frozen and lured away, or she can be burst down by multiple people.[^uuJsdK3_KRQ-276]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey rates Scorpia F tier because there is no skill involved, and says that since you can no longer X-log at the axe hut, fighting her in level 50 Wilderness is pretty much suicide until you get the pet.[^HkVt4enEesI-159]
* Gnomonkey says Scorpia is the only Wilderness boss that overlaps with PvP weaponry (Harmonised nightmare staff if doloing or duoing), which is also great for killing PKers; he wants more bosses like her that incentivise bringing anti-PK weapons.[^uuJsdK3_KRQ-334]

## Trivia

* Scorpia's only mechanic is that below half health she summons two small scorpions that heal her; solo you freeze them and move her away, or with an alt you just DPS through it.[^HkVt4enEesI-159]
