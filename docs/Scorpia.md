# Scorpia

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2022-03-19 to 2022-07-13). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Scorpia is in a cave just north of the Chaos Elemental, is very weak to magic and only attacks with melee, so players freeze her in place to avoid being attacked; at half HP she summons two healing scorpions which can be killed, frozen and lured away, or she can be burst down by multiple people. ([2022-07-13 4:36](https://youtu.be/uuJsdK3_KRQ?t=276))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey rates Scorpia F tier because there is no skill involved, and says that since you can no longer X-log at the axe hut, fighting her in level 50 Wilderness is pretty much suicide until you get the pet. ([2022-03-19 2:39](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=159))
- Gnomonkey says Scorpia is the only Wilderness boss that overlaps with PvP weaponry (Harmonised nightmare staff if doloing or duoing), which is also great for killing PKers; he wants more bosses like her that incentivise bringing anti-PK weapons. ([2022-07-13 5:34](https://youtu.be/uuJsdK3_KRQ?t=334))

## Trivia

- Scorpia's only mechanic is that below half health she summons two small scorpions that heal her; solo you freeze them and move her away, or with an alt you just DPS through it. ([2022-03-19 2:39](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=159))
