**On a Diet / Dehydration / Overly Draining** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2022-09-25. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* On a Diet is worth 15 raid level and Gnomonkey says it hampers you very little, making it usually free raid level; he ranks it A tier.[^a9V3c9mDf9E-476]
* Dehydration is worth 30 raid level and prevents you from drinking any healing potion, including ambrosia and brews.[^a9V3c9mDf9E-540]
* Overly Draining is worth 15 raid level; with 75% spec energy and 50 prayer points a Kiri gives 118 health, which Overly Draining prevents.[^a9V3c9mDf9E-606]

## Tips

* He uses Overly Draining for 540-level raids because it is what gets him up to 540 raid level, but avoids it at all costs otherwise.[^a9V3c9mDf9E-666]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the real problem with Dehydration is losing ambrosia, not brews, since with yellow Kiris you do not need brews and if you are good at Wardens P4 you do not need ambrosia.[^a9V3c9mDf9E-540]
* Gnomonkey ranks Dehydration B tier: usable and worth a lot but not always a take, only used at very high raid levels and never at expert level.[^a9V3c9mDf9E-573]
* Gnomonkey considers Overly Draining the third worst invocation in the game because it stops you from healing as much and blocks special attacks such as claws, Bandos godsword, bone dagger and hammer.[^a9V3c9mDf9E-606]
* Gnomonkey says bandages are really underrated and make On a Diet almost free, though not as free as people claim.[^a9V3c9mDf9E-2891]
