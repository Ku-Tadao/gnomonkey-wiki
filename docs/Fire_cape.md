**Fire cape** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2020-04-27 and 2024-08-08. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Gnomonkey describes the fire cape as the best in slot melee cape until the infernal cape.[^oG65hOavtJg-0]
* A Fire cape's strength bonus matters less than stab accuracy from an Ardougne cloak 4 and Fighter hat against Rune Dragons off task.[^Ji2xv1IbHTs-56]

## Tips

* A Fire cape cannot be worn in Soul Wars.[^_cIv_pRhm9U-0]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey would obtain the Fire cape first among the untradeable unlocks since it only requires a decent Ranged level.[^Ji2xv1IbHTs-139]
* Gnomonkey says getting a Fire cape before any death is better than a rival group's progress, noting they died to Monkey Madness, so they are better than the farmers.[^_cIv_pRhm9U-7267]

## Records

* Gnomonkey gets his Fire cape on the Big Dawgs V1 group hardcore ironman account, using a low-level melee setup.[^_cIv_pRhm9U-7179]
