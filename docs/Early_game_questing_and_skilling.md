**Early game questing and skilling** is an activity topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2023-08-18 and 2024-07-30. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Gnomonkey follows a guide for RS3 ironman progression, which tells him which XP lamps to spend (for example on Mining and Farming) and which items to buy in advance.[^KIticRFI1Rc-1054]

## Tips

* Getting 25 quest points unlocks a free 250k GP, after which he plans to start Crossing/Croesus-style GP to keep progression going; one quest requires buying 352 limestone bricks but the shop only sells 250 per day, so he must resume it on the second day.[^KIticRFI1Rc-1420]
* One guide step needs training Runecrafting to level 50 and buying 1,000 each of air, water, earth and fire runes for a quest.[^KIticRFI1Rc-1491]
* Hazeel Cult was good for gold as well as quest progress.[^ETOLP88HtYU-1065]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the RS3 questing and Christmas-themed quest chain he runs into is not to his taste, and that spam clicking works in one Desert Treasure-style quest.[^KIticRFI1Rc-1055]
* Gnomonkey says his main early game concerns are questing and getting combat levels up for Slayer and moons, and that all progress is important so there is no crystal clear focus yet.[^ETOLP88HtYU-167]
* Gnomonkey says many quests are Monty Python inspired and one barrel-related quest is probably the most bizarre quest in the game, with several random ways to fail.[^ETOLP88HtYU-1004]
* Gnomonkey says Ardougne Easy diary is super good but pointless until you get fairy rings, and he thinks rushing it immediately is pointless.[^ETOLP88HtYU-1119]
