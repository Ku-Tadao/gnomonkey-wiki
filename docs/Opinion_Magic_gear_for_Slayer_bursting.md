# Opinion: Magic gear for Slayer bursting

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 1 video (2020-07-11). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | opinion |
| **Videos** | 1 (first 2020-07-11, latest 2020-07-11) |

## Methods

- Gnomonkey's basic bursting setup: Slayer helmet (a black mask will not work for dust devil tasks, so unlock the helmet if considering bursting), best blessing, best Mage Arena cape, an Occult necklace, an Ancient staff, Proselyte armour, Barrows gloves, Mystic boots, and no ring or shield. ([2020-07-11 5:41](https://youtu.be/6QEUIk_5nRU?t=341))

## Stats and numbers

- The Occult necklace costs about 450k and provides a massive 10% magic damage bonus; Gnomonkey says to hold off on bursting entirely if you cannot afford it. ([2020-07-11 6:13](https://youtu.be/6QEUIk_5nRU?t=373))

## Tips

- An unholy book is also a great bursting choice if you already own one, with the same prayer bonus and only two less magic attack. ([2020-07-11 6:46](https://youtu.be/6QEUIk_5nRU?t=406))
- Gnomonkey notes you can buy the Master wand and sell it back later to afford the Tormented bracelet, and that the later upgrades are very expensive, so wait until you have a decently large bank value. ([2020-07-11 7:48](https://youtu.be/6QEUIk_5nRU?t=468))
- The Ring of the Gods is only very good for bursting; Gnomonkey might skip it if close to 99 Magic and Slayer, but it saves money, and you can uncharge it for 80% of its NMZ points back and resell it after 99 Slayer. ([2020-07-11 9:46](https://youtu.be/6QEUIk_5nRU?t=586))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says for burst tasks most people wear Proselyte armour for its prayer bonus, and some argue for vestment robes from clue scrolls, which give about 1% more DPS than Proselyte but 3 less prayer bonus; Ahrim's is only about 1.5% better DPS than Proselyte. ([2020-07-11 4:20](https://youtu.be/6QEUIk_5nRU?t=260))
- Gnomonkey recommends typically using Proselyte for bursting, reasoning that if you do not own Ancestral robes you probably do not have money to waste on prayer potions. ([2020-07-11 4:47](https://youtu.be/6QEUIk_5nRU?t=287))
- Gnomonkey's upgrade priority for burst tasks is damage bonuses first, prayer bonuses second and magic accuracy last. ([2020-07-11 5:15](https://youtu.be/6QEUIk_5nRU?t=315))
- For bursting off task, Gnomonkey would buy a God mitre over a Farseer helm, since the mitre has +4 magic accuracy versus the Farseer helm's +6 and the best-in-slot prayer bonus of +5. ([2020-07-11 5:41](https://youtu.be/6QEUIk_5nRU?t=341))
- Gnomonkey's first bursting purchase is a Book of Darkness, which for about 500k gives +10 magic attack and +5 prayer, making it best in slot for burst tasks since even an Arcane spirit shield has two less prayer bonus. ([2020-07-11 6:13](https://youtu.be/6QEUIk_5nRU?t=373))
- Gnomonkey chose an imbued Seers ring before Devout boots; it costs only about 300k and gives 12 magic accuracy. ([2020-07-11 6:46](https://youtu.be/6QEUIk_5nRU?t=406))
- Next are Devout boots, best in slot boots for prayer bonus at +5 for about 1.1M, which save a lot of money in the long run if bought early. ([2020-07-11 7:19](https://youtu.be/6QEUIk_5nRU?t=439))
- Next Gnomonkey saves up for a Tormented bracelet, a big price jump at 17M, which gives a 5% magic damage bonus and +2 prayer; he calls it a gigantic upgrade and skipped the Master wand to save for it. ([2020-07-11 7:19](https://youtu.be/6QEUIk_5nRU?t=439))
- Next he buys a Master wand for about 3.5M, +5 magic attack over the Ancient staff (a total of +20), a small upgrade bought mainly to avoid the Ancient staff's -1 prayer bonus. ([2020-07-11 7:48](https://youtu.be/6QEUIk_5nRU?t=468))
- If you plan a lot of bursting, Gnomonkey would grab a Ring of the Gods (imbued) next, which costs 20M, gives +8 prayer bonus and works as a holy wrench to save an inventory space; he says +8 prayer matters more than the Seers ring's +8 magic attack. ([2020-07-11 9:46](https://youtu.be/6QEUIk_5nRU?t=586))
- Next Gnomonkey would buy Ancestral robes, 162M for top and legs; if bursting off task he would buy the 13M hat first, as each piece gives a 2% magic damage bonus and significant magic attack, though you lose Proselyte's prayer bonus. ([2020-07-11 10:15](https://youtu.be/6QEUIk_5nRU?t=615))
- The final bursting upgrade is the Kodai wand at 83.5M, a very small DPS upgrade whose rune saving helps in the long run; Gnomonkey calls this setup the absolute max gear he would buy for bursting with infinite money. ([2020-07-11 10:44](https://youtu.be/6QEUIk_5nRU?t=644))
