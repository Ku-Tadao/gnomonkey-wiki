# One-tick flicking

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2023-03-07 to 2025-02-26). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | mechanic |
| **Videos** | 2 (first 2023-03-07, latest 2025-02-26) |

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says you do not need one-tick flicking for the Inferno at all; it only speeds things up a bit and lazy flicking will get you all the way. ([2025-02-26 23:23](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=1403))
- Gnomonkey says learning lazy flicking is useful even if you can one-tick, because it locks in the attack timing of mobs so you can flick them better. ([2025-02-26 23:49](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=1429))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey sees no reason to prevent one-tick flicking with the listed prayers, calling it arbitrary like the lack of a spec orb in the Wilderness, and says it lowers the skill cap and makes flicking more annoying. ([2023-03-07 13:00](https://youtu.be/trol59VccdE?t=780))
- Gnomonkey says lazy flicking is not easier than one-tick flicking and usually takes more effort, and that one-tick flicking is balanced by being unable to do anything else while doing it. ([2023-03-07 13:30](https://youtu.be/trol59VccdE?t=810))
- Gnomonkey calls Reddit's claim that one-tick flicking is simply infinite prayer points ridiculously ignorant, and says preventing it just makes Inferno and high level Tombs of Amascut more annoying while not affecting other content. ([2023-03-07 14:00](https://youtu.be/trol59VccdE?t=840))

## Events

- Jagex said in the prayer blog before this one that they would make one-tick flicking impossible as a trade-off with the new prayers; Gnomonkey assumes they are sticking to it as the new blog did not mention it. ([2023-03-07 13:00](https://youtu.be/trol59VccdE?t=780))
