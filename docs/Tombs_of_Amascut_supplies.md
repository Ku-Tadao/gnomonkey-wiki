# Tombs of Amascut supplies

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2025-06-25 to 2025-07-06). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Stats and numbers

- Prayer regeneration is like two restores in one slot because it slowly gives you prayer over time; Gnomonkey says he used nothing and had four restores left after a test run. ([2025-07-06 5:03](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=303))

## Tips

- At the choice after Kephri Gnomonkey always picks Power (Salts), saying you need enough salt to carry you through the next rooms, which are an overload-like stat boost, and he picks Life second. ([2025-06-25 24:32](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=1472))
- At the Warden supply choice Gnomonkey picks Life with no thought, and says that as you improve you will take double Power, and never needs to look at Chaos. ([2025-06-25 39:02](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=2342))
- Before the Warden Gnomonkey pulls out one of every supply, maybe two brews and a restore, and says one of the supplies is a prayer item that restores prayer rapidly when thrown on the ground. ([2025-06-25 39:50](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=2390))
- Gnomonkey pre-pots prayer regeneration, a divine super combat and a divine ranging potion before entering. ([2025-07-06 9:40](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=580))
- Gnomonkey used one sand fuse on the monkey room and Baba combined, and says not to be worried about using sand fuses liberally. ([2025-07-06 15:22](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=922))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says BGS is a bit better than a bone dagger for respeccing, and you can also whack the Warden core with it; if you are poor he says to sell it for a bone dagger, but BGS is only about 19 mil. ([2025-07-06 6:40](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=400))
