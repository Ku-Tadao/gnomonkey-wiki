**Tombs of Amascut supplies** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2025-06-25 and 2025-07-06. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Prayer regeneration is like two restores in one slot because it slowly gives you prayer over time; Gnomonkey says he used nothing and had four restores left after a test run.[^91vxCCm7Jck-303]

## Tips

* At the choice after Kephri Gnomonkey always picks Power (Salts), saying you need enough salt to carry you through the next rooms, which are an overload-like stat boost, and he picks Life second.[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-1472]
* At the Warden supply choice Gnomonkey picks Life with no thought, and says that as you improve you will take double Power, and never needs to look at Chaos.[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-2342]
* Before the Warden Gnomonkey pulls out one of every supply, maybe two brews and a restore, and says one of the supplies is a prayer item that restores prayer rapidly when thrown on the ground.[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-2390]
* Gnomonkey pre-pots prayer regeneration, a divine super combat and a divine ranging potion before entering.[^91vxCCm7Jck-580]
* Gnomonkey used one sand fuse on the monkey room and Baba combined, and says not to be worried about using sand fuses liberally.[^91vxCCm7Jck-922]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says BGS is a bit better than a bone dagger for respeccing, and you can also whack the Warden core with it; if you are poor he says to sell it for a bone dagger, but BGS is only about 19 mil.[^91vxCCm7Jck-400]
