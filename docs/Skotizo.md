**Skotizo** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2022-03-12 and 2022-03-19. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says everyone has the Skotos pet, a cool-looking purple demon with a big orb on its back, and puts it at the top of E tier for design.[^vwCm9NKJhRE-1479]
* Gnomonkey rates Skotizo easy F tier: he hits hard but if you stay out of his melee range while praying magic he cannot hit you, which Gnomonkey finds boring.[^HkVt4enEesI-187]
* Gnomonkey says the main problem with Skotizo is the crystals that spawn on the arena edge, killed by the melee weapon Arclight in one hit, with no cooldown, so unlucky runs are spent circling the arena hitting crystals.[^HkVt4enEesI-187]
* Gnomonkey says Skotizo would be a lot better if the crystal cooldown were reduced and a cap added on how many spawn at once.[^HkVt4enEesI-219]
