# Soulreaper axe

## Stats and numbers

- In Project Rebalance the Soul Reaper axe stacks are no longer lost instantly upon switching weapons, and stacks start decrementing after 20 ticks (12 seconds) without attacking, up from 10 ticks (6 seconds). ([2024-04-17 1:32](https://youtu.be/Kzc2e6H8i7o?t=92))
- When Soul Reaper axe stacks naturally degrade, they will heal the player for the damage taken while building the stacks. ([2024-04-17 1:59](https://youtu.be/Kzc2e6H8i7o?t=119))
- After its recent change the Soulreaper axe is described by Gnomonkey as sitting between the Tent Whip (Abyssal tentacle) and the Scythe of Vitur: it is 11% better than the Tent Whip but costs about 400 mil, making it usable in Colosseum. ([2024-06-15 0:00](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c))
- Following the change, Soulreaper axe stacks decay every 20 ticks instead of the previous (as he recalls) 10 ticks, and the axe refunds 8 health each time it loses a stack, so the wielder never loses health from decaying stacks. ([2024-06-15 0:27](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=27))
- Asked in chat about running no Blood Fury, Gnomonkey said the Soulreaper axe's great advantage is that it does not drain (the run's resources) as fast as the Scythe, and even drains less than the Tent Whip, which is a four-tick weapon. ([2024-06-15 1:56](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=116))
- The Soul Reaper axe takes 8 hitpoints per swing for up to five swings, costing up to 40 health before it does its full damage. ([2026-05-27 41:03](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=2463))
- The Soul Reaper axe takes about 3,000 Desert Treasure 2 boss kills on average to obtain, almost as long as a scythe, and is worse than the Noxious halberd in many situations. ([2026-05-27 41:34](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=2494))
- The Summer Sweep-up gave the Soulreaper axe +4 strength bonus; its stack limit stays at five with 6% damage bonus per stack. ([2026-07-22 3:27](https://youtu.be/EPlArwCehYY?t=207))
- Soulreaper axe stacks now take 50 ticks to start decrementing, up from 20 ticks, which Gnomonkey describes as slightly longer than a Bloat down. ([2026-07-22 3:32](https://youtu.be/EPlArwCehYY?t=212))
- Soulreaper axe stacks no longer drain HP after the Summer Sweep-up; as a trade-off the special attack does not give health back. ([2026-07-22 4:01](https://youtu.be/EPlArwCehYY?t=241))
- The Soulreaper axe special attack now gains 12% accuracy per stack (up from 6%), reaching 60% accuracy at full stacks instead of 30%, and gains a 6% minimum hit per stack. ([2026-07-22 4:29](https://youtu.be/EPlArwCehYY?t=269))

## Tips

- With the previous update the Soul Reaper axe no longer triggers Blasphemy or Doom in Colosseum, so Gnomonkey says it becomes completely usable there as a second fist. ([2024-04-17 2:30](https://youtu.be/Kzc2e6H8i7o?t=150))
- Soulreaper axe stacks keep decaying between Colosseum waves, which heals the player back, so Gnomonkey says Sanguinesti staff's start-of-wave healing is rarely needed with the axe. ([2024-06-15 0:27](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=27))
- Frailty is normally an acceptable invocation, but Gnomonkey avoids it with the Soulreaper axe for as long as possible because it prevents banking health from stacks. ([2024-06-15 3:56](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=236))
- The Spec Overlay plugin shows special attack energy while the Soulreaper axe is equipped; a viewer pointed it out and Gnomonkey said he would keep it on. ([2024-06-15 3:56](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=236))
- Gnomonkey suggests the Soul Reaper axe reach full power at two charges instead of five while still banking up to five, so bosses with downtime like Bloat or Sotetseg's maze do not wipe its stacks. ([2026-05-27 41:34](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=2494))
- Gnomonkey says the Soulreaper axe's stacks will probably still be lost between phases on Ulm's melee hand, but quick-switching to melee between mage hits on the next mage hand can maintain them. ([2026-07-22 4:01](https://youtu.be/EPlArwCehYY?t=241))
- With five stacks the Soulreaper axe special guarantees a hit of 30% of your max hit, which Gnomonkey says makes it a very good finisher on Vardorvis; if the spec fails to kill, the next hit builds a new stack that can be used immediately for a guaranteed 6% minimum hit. ([2026-07-22 4:29](https://youtu.be/EPlArwCehYY?t=269))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the Soul Reaper axe takes roughly the same time to acquire as a Scythe and is useful at only a single location, Vardorvis, the boss that drops a piece of it. ([2023-12-22 5:02](https://youtu.be/t5mXf8KJztU?t=302))
- Gnomonkey personally thinks the Soul Reaper axe never should have been added, since there are already loads of slash weapons and it does not fit the game easily. ([2023-12-22 5:02](https://youtu.be/t5mXf8KJztU?t=302))
- Gnomonkey says the axe should not be buffed to stomp on Scythe's toes, yet arguably should be given how rare it is; at the least he wants stacks not lost when swapping weapons and the weapon not to hide current special attack energy. ([2023-12-22 5:33](https://youtu.be/t5mXf8KJztU?t=333))
- Gnomonkey thinks the Soul Reaper axe changes are massive, make it usable in almost every location, and open interesting tick-eating setups such as one axe swing on the P1 obelisk to tick eat the orbs. ([2024-04-17 1:59](https://youtu.be/Kzc2e6H8i7o?t=119))
- Gnomonkey considers the Soulreaper axe near best in slot for Vardorvis and notes that Torva is basically the only other upgrade. ([2024-05-13 8:35](https://youtu.be/hI6cALUKOzQ?t=515))
- Gnomonkey finds he gets a bit less use out of the Saradomin godsword spec with the Soulreaper axe because he is trying to keep the axe's stacks up, but still considers the spec very useful. ([2024-06-15 1:25](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=85))
- Gnomonkey finds that the Frailty invocation is noticeably worse than usual with the Soulreaper axe, so he tries to avoid taking it. ([2024-06-15 2:27](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=147))
- After using it in the run, Gnomonkey's verdict on the Soulreaper axe is that it seems quite good because the stacks decay really slowly. ([2024-06-15 17:30](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=1050))
- Gnomonkey says the Soulreaper axe is very close to the Scythe on Sol Heredit and is not bad at all there. ([2024-06-15 34:10](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=2050))
- Gnomonkey considers the Soulreaper axe a good weapon, and says that on Phantom Muspah the wiki-gear setup using it was not as bad as expected. ([2025-09-03 2:33](https://youtu.be/vuti6ln_q10?t=153))
- Gnomonkey notes the Soulreaper axe is extremely expensive, so he does not know who would use this setup, but he is impressed it works better than expected. ([2025-09-03 11:26](https://youtu.be/vuti6ln_q10?t=686))
- Gnomonkey says the change would make using the Soul Reaper axe's special attack for quick HP less punishing, since only two charges are needed afterward, as at Vardorvis where players spec at 50 health and then cannot hit. ([2026-05-27 42:01](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=2521))
- Gnomonkey says the change gives the same damage increase with a faster ramp time and less HP loss, and wonders whether the axe will beat the scythe in places like the Duke or Vardorvis, noting the axe has higher accuracy than the scythe. ([2026-06-04 35:06](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2106))
- Gnomonkey says the slower stack decay means Soulreaper axe stacks are not lost in Theatre of Blood Sote maze phases, Bloat downs or between Colosseum waves. ([2026-07-22 3:32](https://youtu.be/EPlArwCehYY?t=212))
- Gnomonkey considers the reworked Soulreaper axe far better and mainly much more usable; he says the best places to use it are the Maggot King and now also Theatre of Blood and the Colosseum, where it was questionable before. ([2026-07-22 5:03](https://youtu.be/EPlArwCehYY?t=303))
