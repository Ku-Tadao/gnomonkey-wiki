**Black chinchompa** is an activity topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2021-04-02. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Black chinchompas can be hunted from level 73 Hunter and are found just south of the canoe, just above level 30 Wilderness.[^dzkCFEvMgt8-242]
* Bring a quick ranged weapon to tag chinchompas that run off, such as a magic shortbow or hunter's crossbow; Gnomonkey found darts really good because they are so fast you can use them between resetting traps and waste no ticks.[^dzkCFEvMgt8-305]
* Gnomonkey's black chinchompa gear: a Mage Arena 1 cape, Verac's helm, black d'hide, Barrows gloves and mystic boots, with an ancient staff switch and Dinh's bulwark in the inventory.[^dzkCFEvMgt8-338]
* Gnomonkey's black chinchompa inventory includes six box traps, a few anglerfish, a pile of brews, around four restores, flowers for the hobgoblin x-log, stamina potions and anti-venom.[^dzkCFEvMgt8-338]
* Gnomonkey builds six traps in one formation and tags one chinchompa with RuneLite so he knows where it respawns, which keeps him busy without tick manipulation; if it runs away he throws a dart at it as he resets the trap.[^dzkCFEvMgt8-421]

## Stats and numbers

* Black chinchompas have the exact same catch rate as red chinchompas and give around 1 million GP an hour without tick manipulation.[^dzkCFEvMgt8-242]
* Without tick manipulation or alting, Gnomonkey got roughly 120k XP an hour from black chinchompas in the nineties, similar to herbivore; it is slower until you can use a sixth box trap at level 80 Hunter.[^dzkCFEvMgt8-242]
* The Chinchompa pet drops at 1 in 83,000 per black chinchompa caught, versus 1 in 100,000 for red chinchompas, so black chins are the best option for the pet.[^dzkCFEvMgt8-274]

## Tips

* Black chinchompas are not in multi-combat and are only a few steps from level 30 Wilderness where you can teleport away; if you get tele-blocked you can run west to the hobgoblins and x-log.[^dzkCFEvMgt8-274]
* People often use a toxic staff of the dead to venom you at black chinchompas; Gnomonkey brings a trash axe for the canoe, a gnome seed pod to teleport at level 30 Wilderness (or a glory), and house tabs to resupply.[^dzkCFEvMgt8-364]
* Gnomonkey typically banks after collecting about 500 chinchompas, though he says you could likely catch thousands safely.[^dzkCFEvMgt8-508]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says black chinchompas are the best money a hunter can make and also the fastest XP if you tick manipulate all the way to 99.[^dzkCFEvMgt8-242]
* Gnomonkey says he does not claim the best method for chinchompas and you can get better rates if you are sweaty about it; he found tick manipulation not really worth it here without an ultimate ironman to shoot chinchompas for you.[^dzkCFEvMgt8-391]

## Trivia

* On 2200 total worlds, Gnomonkey only encountered PKers every couple of hours while hunting black chinchompas; on other worlds PKers are more common but typically wear salad robes or mystic and cannot really kill you.[^dzkCFEvMgt8-274]
