# Herblore

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 5 videos (2024-07-30 to 2025-11-04). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Gnomonkey's potion routine for sulphur naga training is to grab a full inventory of grubs, grind them up, drop four or six on the ground, and make sets of potions. ([2024-09-28 2:26](https://youtu.be/-h7yElF9Z0c?t=146))

## Requirements

- The stats of the potions you make in the Moons area depend on your Herblore level, and you need 55 Herblore for the full super strength effect. ([2024-09-28 2:26](https://youtu.be/-h7yElF9Z0c?t=146))
- The Herblore level listed in the Sailing prep is the level required for the new Armadillo brew; Gnomonkey says it needs 77 Hunter for the crab used to make it. ([2025-11-04 6:52](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=412))

## Stats and numbers

- A teammate says the current quest plan does not get the group to level 4 Herblore, only three and three quarters. ([2024-07-30 13:21](https://youtu.be/ETOLP88HtYU?t=801))
- Gnomonkey calculates the highest-level herb Torstol gives about 9.42 yield at maximum yield times 224.5 harvest XP, an extra roughly 2,000 XP per patch next to each allotment. ([2025-03-26 27:43](https://youtu.be/uDhGFEjrpyc?t=1663))
- Across eight allotment runs, the herb patches next to them would give an extra 16k to 17k Farming XP by Gnomonkey's math. ([2025-03-26 28:10](https://youtu.be/uDhGFEjrpyc?t=1690))
- Anti-bleed tonic, made with corals and paste from fishing, cures bleed and heals 5 hitpoints but does not prevent bleed from being reapplied; Gnomonkey notes the only thing that applies bleed is Vardorvis's axe and he expects it to be good there. ([2025-07-31 13:24](https://youtu.be/qiA-iaA19-M?t=804))
- Super fishing potions boost Fishing by six, which Gnomonkey thinks will help with diaries and fishing sturgeon at Barbarian Fishing earlier, similar to summer pies for the Rooftop course. ([2025-07-31 14:26](https://youtu.be/qiA-iaA19-M?t=866))
- Extreme energy potions restore 40% run energy and Gnomonkey says they would be really good at Blast Furnace; a super hunter potion boosts Hunter by six. ([2025-07-31 14:59](https://youtu.be/qiA-iaA19-M?t=899))
- Extended stamina potions give a 70% reduction in run energy depletion for four minutes (up from two minutes for a regular stamina potion), and each dose adds two charges to the ring of endurance and consumes two charges from the ring. ([2025-07-31 15:29](https://youtu.be/qiA-iaA19-M?t=929))
- Armadyl brew (Armadil brew in the transcript) needs about 89 Herblore and Umbral coral and crab paste; it heals 10% plus 2 hitpoints and raises Ranged by 10% plus 4, but lowers Attack, Strength, Magic and Defence by 10% plus 2; Gnomonkey estimates 11 healed per dose, 44 per potion. ([2025-07-31 16:27](https://youtu.be/qiA-iaA19-M?t=987))
- Gnomonkey says Armadillo brew gives a little less health than a normal brew but drains every combat stat except Ranged, and boosts Ranged by the same amount as a normal ranging potion. ([2025-11-04 6:52](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=412))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says he wants Herblore for herb tar immediately (insta), though it takes a while. ([2024-07-30 13:21](https://youtu.be/ETOLP88HtYU?t=801))
- Gnomonkey says the Jungle Potion quest might get the group to the Herblore needed for Herb tar, calling Herb tar massive. ([2024-07-30 21:09](https://youtu.be/ETOLP88HtYU?t=1269))
- Gnomonkey says making potions in the Moons area gives effectively unlimited super combat and Prayer potions at minor input. ([2024-09-28 1:32](https://youtu.be/-h7yElF9Z0c?t=92))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey notes that doing the Colosseum skips the Vorkath grind and the Herblore level needed for super antifires. ([2024-07-30 2:47](https://youtu.be/ETOLP88HtYU?t=167))
- Gnomonkey says the spade would devalue Torstol seeds and Torstols, which are already low in value and dropped by many bosses. ([2025-03-26 28:10](https://youtu.be/uDhGFEjrpyc?t=1690))
- Gnomonkey says protecting herb seed and herb value is important; much of the profit at Tombs of Amascut comes from ranarr and snapdragon seeds, and much of the value from Chambers of Xeric comes from grimy herbs. ([2025-03-26 28:38](https://youtu.be/uDhGFEjrpyc?t=1718))
- Gnomonkey says herb runs are consistently the best early-game moneymaker and he tells people to do herb runs until they reach mid-level bosses, so devaluing them would ruin early moneymaking. ([2025-03-26 29:06](https://youtu.be/uDhGFEjrpyc?t=1746))
- Gnomonkey notes nobody was discussing Torstols, which would be harvested at the same time as snape grass or watermelons, causing a cascading problem for the economy. ([2025-03-26 29:32](https://youtu.be/uDhGFEjrpyc?t=1772))
- Gnomonkey thinks extended stamina would give about eight minutes of stamina with the ring of endurance, which he calls wild and says will greatly cut down stamina potion use for Iron Men. ([2025-07-31 15:56](https://youtu.be/qiA-iaA19-M?t=956))
- Gnomonkey thinks Armadyl brew could be the strongest offshoot brew in the game for pure ranging, such as Zulrah, Chinning, God Wars with a Twisted bow or Scorching bow, and the Doom of Mokhaiotl, where the melee punish bonus damage is unaffected by level. ([2025-07-31 16:59](https://youtu.be/qiA-iaA19-M?t=1019))
- Gnomonkey would like an upgraded Zamorak brew that raises all combat stats, to be a super-combat style brew near level 99. ([2025-07-31 17:56](https://youtu.be/qiA-iaA19-M?t=1076))
- Gnomonkey expects Armadillo brews to be very strong in the Inferno, against Zulrah, and at Doom of Mokhaiotl (which he calls Mocha), among other places. ([2025-11-04 6:52](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=412))

## Events

- An extended stamina potion is also coming with Sailing, and Gnomonkey says getting Herblore up will be very helpful. ([2025-11-04 6:52](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=412))
