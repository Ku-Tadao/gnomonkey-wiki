## Methods

- Gnomonkey's 5 Jad approach is to only click healers or use supplies when not being attacked by Jads with tight timing, and to kill one Jad from each tight-timing set before moving on to the others. ([2021-03-08 5:18](https://youtu.be/Nu5mkhqaWlI?t=318))
- Gnomonkey starts the five Jad challenge with the northern Jad, standing on a tile that gives the highest chance of healers getting stuck and corner-traps most of the healers, typically letting only one healer hit him. ([2021-03-08 5:18](https://youtu.be/Nu5mkhqaWlI?t=318))
- After the first Jad, Gnomonkey moves on to the southeastern Jad, which he calls the last Jad with tight timing, then the southwestern Jad with the tightest timing remaining; after that the kill order does not matter and the run is basically over if you make no major mistake. ([2021-03-08 5:49](https://youtu.be/Nu5mkhqaWlI?t=349))
- Stand west of where Jad spawns since healers never spawn east of Jad, letting you tag them easily; five healers spawn when Jad reaches half HP. ([2021-05-21 33:31](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=2011))
- Gnomonkey tags healers by manually Blood Barraging clumps of them with the bow on, or by standing close to Jad and piping all five, or with the twisted bow if uncomfortable. ([2021-05-21 33:31](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=2011))
- After tagging all five, enter melee range of Jad praying melee, run up and down in front of him so the healers stack, wait for his next attack to protect, then run through Jad so the healers get stuck behind him, then Blood Barrage them to get to 99 HP before triples. ([2021-05-21 33:57](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=2037))
- Start triples by praying Rigor and attacking the southern Jad, doing one action per protected attack, exactly as with a single Jad. ([2021-05-21 34:52](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=2092))
- Start triples one tile away from Jad in line with the southern Jad's southwest tile to get healers stuck on each other; a healer behind you does not need tagging, and walking backwards two tiles can make healers stick on each other. ([2021-05-21 35:20](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=2120))
- After the first Jad move to the next, standing southwest of the northwestern Jad for the same healer trap; Gnomonkey once tagged all three healers with one barrage when they surrounded Jad's southwest tile. ([2021-05-21 35:53](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=2153))
- Gnomonkey says Jad's projectile attack is calculated as soon as it is on the screen. ([2022-07-22 1:24:28](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=5068))
- To handle Jad healers, Gnomonkey clumps them by running back and forth with melee prayer on and then clicks Jad; stacking and running through should be west to east or east to west, not north to south, because of pathing. ([2022-07-22 1:25:00](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=5100))
- Gnomonkey says his thralls out-damage him on Jad, which is why he brought thralls instead of relying on Barrage spells. ([2024-05-09 33:38](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=2018))
- On the Jad fight Gnomonkey kills the healers first, and needs to be damaged by the healers because at full health he could die to Jad; he steps out with ranged prayer then waits for a mage hit before tick eating. ([2024-08-08 1:58:09](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=7089))
- To pull Jad correctly, Gnomonkey told a teammate to come out a little further when grabbing it, to a specific tile, and tag it again to drag it down. ([2026-06-10 1:06](https://youtu.be/qyG_lTC4pDU?t=66))
- For the single Jad, Gnomonkey stands one tile away from Jad on the tile right of his spawn, because healers spawn at half of Jad's health and can spawn behind you, where you do not have to deal with them. ([2026-07-13 1:04:17](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=3857))
- If healers spawn both behind you and in front of you, tag the one healing Jad with your staff, tag the one in front, and step backwards so they get trapped on each other and cannot hit you; healers do a surprising amount of damage. ([2026-07-13 1:04:44](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=3884))
- On triple Jads each Jad attacks every three game ticks; when the projectile appears on screen you swap to the next Jad's prayer, and the direction the order goes is random. ([2026-07-13 1:06:13](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=3973))
- On triple Jads there are only three healers instead of five; put on your staff, click Jad to tag the healers next to a southwest tile, and back up a couple tiles if multiple healers hit you, as they get stuck on each other and you only tank one. ([2026-07-13 1:07:07](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=4027))

## Stats and numbers

- TzTok-Jad has 250 health and two attack styles, ranged and magic, which it switches between randomly with a max hit of 97, and it can melee a player who walks within melee distance. ([2020-04-27 11:36](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=696))
- Jad's attacks have a delay of 4.8 seconds between them, so Gnomonkey advises keeping to one action per attack, such as flicking the next attack, drinking a prayer dose, then returning to protecting. ([2020-04-27 12:43](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=763))
- Below half health (125 HP) Jad spawns four healers that run to Jad and heal him; they must be tagged and lured more than four tiles away to stop, and if killed before Jad is healed to full they will not respawn. ([2020-04-27 12:43](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=763))
- Inferno Jads are tougher than regular Jad: max hit 113 versus 97 for normal Jad, the same defence level of 480, nastier combat stats, and 350 HP versus 250 for regular Jad. ([2021-03-08 2:28](https://youtu.be/Nu5mkhqaWlI?t=148))
- In the five Jad challenge the east and southeast Jads have no delay between their attacks (one tick per attack). ([2021-03-08 4:30](https://youtu.be/Nu5mkhqaWlI?t=270))
- In the five Jad challenge there is a one-tick delay between the southeast Jad's attack and the southwest Jad's attack, and another one-tick delay between the southwest Jad and the west Jad. ([2021-03-08 4:50](https://youtu.be/Nu5mkhqaWlI?t=290))
- In the five Jad challenge the west and northwest Jads have no delay between attacks, so they attack each tick, followed by another one-tick delay before the east Jad and the cycle repeats. ([2021-03-08 4:50](https://youtu.be/Nu5mkhqaWlI?t=290))
- In the Inferno, Jad spawns in the northwest corner, can max a 113, has more health than Fight Caves Jad, and its healers are more dangerous with a max of 18. ([2021-05-21 33:02](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1982))
- The triple Jads are nine-tick attackers instead of the usual eight-tick, giving a clean 3 ticks (nearly 2 full seconds) between each attack, and each has only three healers. ([2021-05-21 34:52](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=2092))
- Gnomonkey says the average Jad hit is high 40s, about 49, so you can almost always tank one hit. ([2022-07-22 1:29:40](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=5380))
- Gnomonkey states Ket-Zek have 240 magic level and TzTok-Jad has 480 magic level, making a mage-only Jad fight very difficult. ([2024-05-09 1:55](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=115))
- Jad's healers will heal Jad if within four tiles of him, stop healing if interrupted, and re-aggro Jad if dragged within five tiles of Jad's southwest tile. ([2024-05-09 2:22](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=142))
- Gnomonkey estimates about 27% accuracy against Jad's 480 magic level with his mage-only setup. ([2024-05-09 32:05](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=1925))
- Gnomonkey says he only has one DPS against Jad and that this will be a slow and nervous fight. ([2024-08-08 57:50](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=3470))
- Gnomonkey calculates his DPS against Jad as 0.9 without weapon poison, and thinks closer to 1 with poison, which he says will take a small eternity. ([2024-08-08 1:33:50](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=5630))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey reads Jad's animations rather than sound cues: if he rears up lifting a leg he fires a magic attack, and if he slams both feet down a rock falls dealing ranged damage. ([2020-04-27 12:10](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=730))
- If Jad's health is restored to full, the healers respawn at half health. Many players bring a crossbow switch to long-range Jad, but Gnomonkey does not, since the blowpipe's range of five lets players stack the healers behind or next to them. ([2020-04-27 13:17](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=797))
- Healers' semi-inaccurate melee has a max of 14, so with sub-60 Defence Gnomonkey recommends luring and killing them immediately, and otherwise luring them behind you and killing them if they cause trouble. ([2020-04-27 13:47](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=827))
- In the five Jad challenge the two Jads on the west and northwest have tight timing and the two Jads on the east and southeast have tight timing, which determines the kill order and when you can click a healer or use a supply. ([2021-03-08 5:18](https://youtu.be/Nu5mkhqaWlI?t=318))
- It would technically be better to run all the way to the southwest tile of a Jad to get more healers stuck, but Gnomonkey avoids it when it makes Jad's attacks harder to see. ([2021-03-08 6:43](https://youtu.be/Nu5mkhqaWlI?t=403))
- Gnomonkey would not pray Rigor during the single Jad wave unless you have around 6 restores, and with fewer than 4 restores you should flick Jad as much as possible; damage is calculated when the projectile is on screen so you can safely turn the prayer off. ([2021-05-21 33:02](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1982))
- Before the final Jad pause the wave, probably turn Rigor off unless you have a ton of resources, stand west of the Jad and tag healers like the single Jad, then do not run around afterwards as run energy does not recover once a wave pauses and you need it for Zuk. ([2021-05-21 36:22](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=2182))
- Gnomonkey says to stand on a specific tile for the Inferno Jad, which gives the highest chance of healers ending up behind you. ([2022-07-22 1:24:28](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=5068))
- Gnomonkey says clicking log out pauses the wave so nothing spawns, which he uses before starting triples. ([2022-07-22 1:25:59](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=5159))
- Gnomonkey says his biggest tip is not to explain to people how to do Jads while doing Jads, because it causes you to get hit. ([2022-07-22 1:30:13](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=5413))
- Gnomonkey's plan for Jad healers is to let them heal, tag them, and lead them far away rather than kill them, as they have 120 magic level and 100 mage defence. ([2024-05-09 2:22](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=142))
- Gnomonkey positions himself northeast of Jad to avoid Jad's southwest tile so the healers can be tagged without re-aggroing Jad. ([2024-05-09 31:08](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=1868))
- Gnomonkey uses tick eating with strength brews against Jad and keeps strength potions for Jad specifically, wanting one or two doses. ([2024-08-08 1:58:09](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=7089))
- In breach 5 Jad had a huge range, so Gnomonkey told teammates to stand behind a rock while dragging it to the team's position. ([2026-06-10 0:32](https://youtu.be/qyG_lTC4pDU?t=32))
- Gnomonkey tells the team to focus on Jad and to hit Jad from their position, and later to switch between range and mage prayer when Jad attacks. ([2026-06-10 9:00](https://youtu.be/qyG_lTC4pDU?t=540))
- Gnomonkey recommends queueing a log out before the last Jad so you can log out before Zuk, and does one prayer per Jad attack. ([2026-07-13 1:07:07](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=4027))
- Gnomonkey drinks the Divine bastion before triples for extra damage and to have extra doses to work with. ([2026-07-13 1:07:07](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=4027))
- Gnomonkey stands on a particular side of Jad in the triples because you are more likely to trap a healer there, and it lets you barrage Jad directly to hit every healer around him. ([2026-07-13 1:08:44](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=4124))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says Jad is only considered a tough boss because of how long it takes to get to him and his potential to one-shot you, and that he is considerably easier than most bosses in the game. ([2020-04-27 11:36](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=696))
- Gnomonkey says triple Jads are a lot easier than people make them out to be, just really intimidating; before starting you can overbrew, restore stats and optionally drink a Bastion. ([2021-05-21 34:40](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=2080))
- Gnomonkey complains that Jad was looking at one teammate but hitting him, which he calls cross-eyed. ([2026-06-10 9:00](https://youtu.be/qyG_lTC4pDU?t=540))
