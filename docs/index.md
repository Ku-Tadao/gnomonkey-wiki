# GnomonkeyRS Wiki

Welcome to the **GnomonkeyRS Wiki**, an unofficial, Old-School-RuneScape-Wiki-style encyclopedia built from the YouTube channel [GnomonkeyRS](https://www.youtube.com/@GnomonkeyRS). It has **561** articles written from **324** videos (2018-11-22 to 2026-09-14).

## About this wiki

Every article records what Gnomonkey said in his videos (auto-transcribed, then edited into articles). Statements are cited with footnotes that link to the video at the right moment; it is **not** independently verified game fact. When he repeats himself the article merges it, and when a later video updates or contradicts an earlier one the article gives the current position and the **Revision history** at the bottom lists what changed in which video.
