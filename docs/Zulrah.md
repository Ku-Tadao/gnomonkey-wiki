Zulrah is a mid-level boss that Gnomonkey has covered with guides for max-efficiency mage/ranged switching (2022), budget Atlatl setups (2025) and, after Jagex made her meleeable, a halberd setup (May 2025). In 2022 he called her no longer the money boss she once was; in 2025 he describes her as easy and free to kill with several cheap setups.

## Mechanics

- Zulrah has four rotations: a mage phase, a green phase and two red phases; every kill starts with a green phase and then differs[^bBnPK55ZqUM-140]. Gnomonkey recommends memorising rotations to pre-switch gear, using the Zulrah helper plugin (see [RuneLite plugins](RuneLite_plugins.md)) while learning and dropping it around 50 KC[^bBnPK55ZqUM-382]. The RuneLite side panel shows where she will be[^ecd_a9E8l5E-449].
- Max hit against her is capped at 50, and any hit above 50 is re-rolled into a 45 to 50 hit[^YR0HB76BSPU-188]. Her blue-phase magic attack maxes a 41[^YR0HB76BSPU-235]; he notes her maximum hit is 41 and says to always hover food on her[^bBnPK55ZqUM-530].
- The blue-phase attack can technically be tick-eaten, but the previous projectile has not landed before the next is fired, so the second hit reads health from before the first (at 45 HP taking a 25, the second can still hit a 41). He advises choosing intelligently between tick eating and combo eating[^YR0HB76BSPU-235].
- Snakelings are magic or melee; when Zulrah is not attacking and snakelings are out, ideally pray Protect from Magic[^YR0HB76BSPU-207]. In the green phase that spawns snakelings, pray mage rather than range[^bBnPK55ZqUM-884]. If both snakelings are mage-type, the mage prayer can stay up without switching[^ecd_a9E8l5E-692].
- In the mage phase he stands in the middle of the island so some mage snakelings get stuck[^bBnPK55ZqUM-502]. The orange (tanky) phase has the lowest damage output but mage damage against it is much better, hence a mage bonus and boots switch[^bBnPK55ZqUM-521].
- Killing Zulrah in the short green phase before she dives gets the Grandmaster Combat Achievement speed kill[^YR0HB76BSPU-420].

## Max-efficiency mage and ranged method (2022)

- Gear: max mage gear with a [Harmonised nightmare staff](Harmonised_nightmare_staff.md) or Volatile nightmare staff; without a Harmonised staff, thralls and a [Sanguinesti staff](Sanguinesti_staff.md) give similar kills per hour, with an Arcane spirit shield offhand[^YR0HB76BSPU-32]. Ring of suffering is camped; Archers and Seers ring switches are possible but harder and mainly for PvP[^YR0HB76BSPU-61]. Without a Volatile staff, blowpipe specs give similar DPS[^YR0HB76BSPU-61]. The inventory has an 8-way range switch; [Barrows gloves](Barrows_gloves.md) instead of Zaryte vambraces is a very small loss[^YR0HB76BSPU-61].
- The blowpipe used to beat the [Twisted bow](Twisted_bow.md) on blue-face Zulrah but now the bow wins; the pipe is still used for pillar stalling and extra hits[^YR0HB76BSPU-61]. Dragon arrows are preferred as they raise the chance of landing 45 to 50[^YR0HB76BSPU-207]. In his setup the Harmonised staff maxes a 49 on a four-tick attack, far better for accuracy and speed than the bow[^YR0HB76BSPU-188]. The Volatile staff special uses 55% energy, is very accurate and maxes an 80; he uses it on red phase[^YR0HB76BSPU-207].
- Supplies: two Divine ranging potions and two Anti-venom+, each potted only when useful; no prayer potions[^YR0HB76BSPU-87]. Also Gothics rest items for tick eating, carbon bonds for combo eating, and a [Max cape](Max_cape.md) (replace with food otherwise)[^YR0HB76BSPU-87]. Desert amulet for restores, Zulrah's teleport scrolls, and an [Imbued heart](Imbued_heart.md), a minor boost on red Zulrah[^YR0HB76BSPU-118]. Law, Fire, Air and Wrath runes; Air runes stay out of the pouch because the Harmonised staff burns through them (16,000 can run out in a session), and Law runes teleport to the Seers' Village bank[^YR0HB76BSPU-118]. A fire rune on hand stops autocast being removed from the Harmonised staff[^YR0HB76BSPU-146]. Scales can be carried to stack them, and two slots left spare for loot[^YR0HB76BSPU-146].
- Opening: Preserve on during the run to the boat, Imbued heart, special primed; Augury after the boat, right-click Zulrah when the cutscene ends and use the Volatile spec[^YR0HB76BSPU-261]. If the spec is forgotten it can be used in the first phase, which gives four Harmonised casts and one spec[^YR0HB76BSPU-292]. The first phase transition is where ticks are most often lost: Rigor on with range switch hovered for blue, Augury for red (50% of the time), and for green he tanks the first hit to proc [Vengeance](Vengeance.md)[^YR0HB76BSPU-292].
- Green rotation: take the first venge, attack, pray Protect from Magic on snakelings; the Volatile spec barely recovers as she goes under, so it is primed and used on red[^YR0HB76BSPU-323]. She typically dies on red or earlier on green; blue is the hardest phase for lost ticks and needs Rigor primed and a fast switch to ranged[^YR0HB76BSPU-356]. With clean timing two blowpipe hits fit in blue; otherwise one pipe hit then back to the bow[^YR0HB76BSPU-389]. Blue example: six attacks before she dives[^YR0HB76BSPU-389]. In the first blue phase, two blowpipe shots then a Twisted bow shot fits, the bow landing on the last tick before submerging[^HkVt4enEesI-408].
- Red Zulrah has two diverging paths; he covers south green rather than west first, and red pokes twice before diving[^YR0HB76BSPU-420]. After the final cast he runs through the fog to the pillar corner switching to ranged, taking a little poison damage to fit four Twisted bow shots by pillar stalling (bow, behind the pillar, pipe shot, bow, repeat for four attacks)[^YR0HB76BSPU-447]. If she has not damaged him by her third attack, he drops Protect from Magic and tanks the last hit on purpose, because venge will not proc if she appears west[^YR0HB76BSPU-447]. After the fourth shot she comes up south: run across switching to magic, use the Volatile spec, she comes up red, pokes twice, then blue (Twisted bow)[^YR0HB76BSPU-480]. On the west, keep praying mage while she only spawns snakelings[^YR0HB76BSPU-480].
- Between kills: teleport to Narda on an alt account, drop loot, use the altar, have the alt cast Vengeance, and teleport back with a scroll[^YR0HB76BSPU-419].

## Budget Atlatl setup (2025)

- A mid-game guide for Ironmen starting Zulrah that also works on mains[^bBnPK55ZqUM-0]. He uses magic ([Twinflame staff](Twinflame_staff.md)) on green or red phases and the [Eclipse atlatl](Eclipse_atlatl.md) otherwise[^bBnPK55ZqUM-382]. Kills take roughly 120 to 140 ticks, usually two kills per trip (sometimes one or three), enough to maintain scrolls; with a Bofa, two to three kill trips give effectively unlimited scrolls[^bBnPK55ZqUM-798][^bBnPK55ZqUM-825].
- Without teleport scrolls, bank with a dual ring and use fairy ring BJS; health can be restored at the fairy ring pool[^bBnPK55ZqUM-259]. The stepping stone on the route requires 76 Agility, and a summer pie pre-pot can allow the jump[^bBnPK55ZqUM-289].
- He says the Twinflame staff makes Zulrah much easier, a totally different boss, especially for mid-game Irons[^bBnPK55ZqUM-1470]. The [Gearscape](Gearscape.md) calculator can show all phases via its groups tab[^bBnPK55ZqUM-1578].
- In February 2025 he said the [Atlatl](Atlatl.md) is about 14% worse than the Bofa at Zulrah; the burn effect is lost when she dives, making it perhaps 20% effective and about 12% worse overall[^NxfbgWY3ZQk-970]. Since a player uses mage about half the time, the Atlatl suffices without a roughly 12 million Bofa[^NxfbgWY3ZQk-970][^NxfbgWY3ZQk-1000].

## Melee with halberds (May 2025)

- Jagex changed Zulrah so she can be meleed with halberds; she has zero melee defence[^ecd_a9E8l5E-0]. Gnomonkey says a [Noxious halberd](Noxious_halberd.md) melee approach has basically replaced camping a single style such as Bofa, Twisted bow or Shadow[^ecd_a9E8l5E-0]. The halberd does the same damage on every phase since it ignores her defences, so long red phases are no problem[^ecd_a9E8l5E-449][^ecd_a9E8l5E-542].
- Mid-level setup: Serpentine helm, Bandos, Barrows gloves, Dragon boots, Ring of suffering; upgrades or downgrades remain good[^ecd_a9E8l5E-26]. Pharaoh's gloves are amazing on a main, a [rancour](Amulet_of_rancour.md) is much better on an Iron, and [Torva](Torva_armour.md) is insane[^ecd_a9E8l5E-59]. Amulet of blood fury is great and extends trips, which are limited mostly by restores; rancour and torture are also good[^ecd_a9E8l5E-845]. Inventory: divine super combats and a few restores, no anti-venom with the Serpentine helm[^ecd_a9E8l5E-93].
- Rings: a Bellator ring until snakelings spawn, then the Ring of suffering, worth two max hits; switch back when they are gone, and put it on whenever snakelings hit[^ecd_a9E8l5E-121][^ecd_a9E8l5E-246][^ecd_a9E8l5E-756]. Bellator over Ultor since max hit matters less with the hit cap[^ecd_a9E8l5E-873]. Echo boots recoil in a 3x3 and kill snakelings, so the suffering ring is not needed with them[^ecd_a9E8l5E-873]. He is unsure about the [Lightbearer](Lightbearer.md) but thinks it probably fine since the player is over the hit cap[^ecd_a9E8l5E-845].
- Kills typically take 110 to 140 ticks, occasionally 145 to 150, with 86 and 101 seen; he nearly got a Grandmaster time with the cheap setup if the Crystal halberd spec hits three 100s[^ecd_a9E8l5E-513]. Four, almost five kills with seven food left; a six or seven kill trip is probable[^ecd_a9E8l5E-756]. He estimates about six fewer kills per hour than a maxed Twisted bow plus Harmonised staff switch setup[^ecd_a9E8l5E-606].

## Profit, drops and opinion

- April 2022 figures: about 35 kills an hour in a good hour versus about 25 with Twisted bow camping; a kill worth 122k gives 4.3m an hour, or about 3.5m GP/hr after 35 scrolls (about 800k)[^YR0HB76BSPU-539]. He says he does Zulrah for the mutagens and pet, not profit[^YR0HB76BSPU-539].
- He says she is no longer the "Money Snake": since the blowpipe nerf profit dropped and [Vorkath](Vorkath.md) has effectively taken her place; she is still a common Twisted bow rebuild boss and has the untradeable Magma and Tanzanite mutagens[^YR0HB76BSPU-0]. Mutagens are 1/3,277 for the table or 1/6,553 per colour; with Torva he calls her kind of dead content and cares less about mutagens, though still without one[^YR0HB76BSPU-566].
- The pet is 1/4,000, the only pet at that rarity he knows of, about a 115 hour grind at 35 kills an hour[^vwCm9NKJhRE-719][^YR0HB76BSPU-566]. He rated it low B tier (many gold farmers have it, now a grind)[^vwCm9NKJhRE-719].
- In the March 2022 PvM boss tier list he placed Zulrah in high B tier, with plenty going on (hit counts before she dives, volatile staff specs for the melee phase, eight-way switches) but not A tier[^HkVt4enEesI-374][^HkVt4enEesI-435].
- In May 2025 he called kills simple and told viewers to go kill Zulrah because it is free and easy[^ecd_a9E8l5E-929].
