**Echo weapons** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2026-04-23. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey considers Echo weapons in Leagues 6 generally much weaker and less central than in Raging Echoes.[^8Teya9Oz78Y-718]
* Gnomonkey says Echo Thermonuclear smoke devil's item effectively enables the elemental builds for mage, is almost required and heavily buffs damage, and is core to that build.[^8Teya9Oz78Y-718]
* Echo Hunllef's blessing still works for mage this time, making it solid for all builds, and many players run the crystal armour.[^8Teya9Oz78Y-751]
* Echo Dagannoth Kings' helmet is fairly weak but gives a chance to null damage, which is okay for the thorns build, and most players did not pick Fremennik jewellery.[^8Teya9Oz78Y-751]
* Echo Kalphite Queen still drops the Drygore blowpipe, but throne weapons are not as good as crossbows with the new builds.[^8Teya9Oz78Y-777]
* Echo Sarachnis drops a fire dagger, which is a light weapon that procs fire damage like a melee-fire hybrid, but Gnomonkey has not seen anyone use it.[^8Teya9Oz78Y-777]
* The Grotesque Guardians drop the Lithic sceptre, a trident that charges up an AoE like the Soul Spear last time, but Gnomonkey calls it a weak little cousin of the Sunlight spear.[^8Teya9Oz78Y-804]
* Gnomonkey says the Echo King Black Dragon crossbow is also very strong and enables the crossbow build.[^8Teya9Oz78Y-864]
