## Opinions

- Gnomonkey considers Echo weapons in Leagues 6 generally much weaker and less central than in Raging Echoes. ([2026-04-23 11:58](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=718))
- Gnomonkey says Echo Thermonuclear smoke devil's item effectively enables the elemental builds for mage, is almost required and heavily buffs damage, and is core to that build. ([2026-04-23 11:58](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=718))
- Echo Hunllef's blessing still works for mage this time, making it solid for all builds, and many players run the crystal armour. ([2026-04-23 12:31](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=751))
- Echo Dagannoth Kings' helmet is fairly weak but gives a chance to null damage, which is okay for the thorns build, and most players did not pick Fremennik jewellery. ([2026-04-23 12:31](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=751))
- Echo Kalphite Queen still drops the Drygore blowpipe, but throne weapons are not as good as crossbows with the new builds. ([2026-04-23 12:57](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=777))
- Echo Sarachnis drops a fire dagger, which is a light weapon that procs fire damage like a melee-fire hybrid, but Gnomonkey has not seen anyone use it. ([2026-04-23 12:57](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=777))
- The Grotesque Guardians drop the Lithic sceptre, a trident that charges up an AoE like the Soul Spear last time, but Gnomonkey calls it a weak little cousin of the Sunlight spear. ([2026-04-23 13:24](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=804))
- Gnomonkey says the Echo King Black Dragon crossbow is also very strong and enables the crossbow build. ([2026-04-23 14:24](https://youtu.be/8Teya9Oz78Y?t=864))
