# Challenge: Karamja banana picking (20 minutes)

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 1 video (2023-10-21). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Gnomonkey picked 27 bananas per inventory and ran to Port Khazard to deposit, which he found was the fastest route tick-wise to a bank, even faster than deposit boxes. ([2023-10-21 0:00](https://youtu.be/7N_K5rlrdBA))
- Gnomonkey hopped worlds when the close banana trees were gone, noting it took eleven ticks to the bank. ([2023-10-21 1:02](https://youtu.be/7N_K5rlrdBA?t=62))

## Requirements

- The rules: no teleports, no baskets, no satchels, only inventory space, and no alts to help or hold the bananas, and no stamina potion sharing; he was allowed to wear things like Graceful, Explorer's ring 4 and Max cape boosts. ([2023-10-21 0:00](https://youtu.be/7N_K5rlrdBA))

## Stats and numbers

- Each banana trip, picking an inventory and banking, took about 1 minute 20 seconds. ([2023-10-21 4:29](https://youtu.be/7N_K5rlrdBA?t=269))
- Banana trees on Karamja have roughly a four minute respawn, which Gnomonkey counted during the run. ([2023-10-21 4:29](https://youtu.be/7N_K5rlrdBA?t=269))

## Records

- Gnomonkey picked 378 bananas, worth $378, in the 20 minutes; Maverick thought he would only get around 250. ([2023-10-21 7:54](https://youtu.be/7N_K5rlrdBA?t=474))

## Events

- Gnomonkey, as the Banana Man, was challenged by a viewer called Maverick to pick as many Karamja bananas as possible in 20 minutes, with every banana worth $1. ([2023-10-21 0:00](https://youtu.be/7N_K5rlrdBA))
- Early in the run, the close Karamja bananas were already gone because other players or chatters had picked them, which Gnomonkey called being griefed; he held the Charter ship option as far away as possible and had 65 bananas in the bank. ([2023-10-21 0:22](https://youtu.be/7N_K5rlrdBA?t=22))
- On his world, a random ironman player was also picking bananas on Karamja, the only other person all day to pick a single banana there, and Gnomonkey says he lost about 20 bucks to that player and another. ([2023-10-21 3:08](https://youtu.be/7N_K5rlrdBA?t=188))
- Per the description, the interference came from the challenger Maverick himself, to save his own wallet, and Twitch chatters Hoshi and Condor7000. ([2023-10-21 3:08](https://youtu.be/7N_K5rlrdBA?t=188))
- Near the end Gnomonkey says they had exhausted Karamja of its bananas on his world, and he hoped to pick up one more inventory before time ran out. ([2023-10-21 5:32](https://youtu.be/7N_K5rlrdBA?t=332))
