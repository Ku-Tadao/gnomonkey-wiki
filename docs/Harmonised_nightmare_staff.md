**Harmonised nightmare staff** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2020-07-11 and 2026-09-03. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Stats and numbers

* The Harmonised nightmare staff costs about 1.6 billion and allows casting regular spells a tick faster.[^6QEUIk_5nRU-937]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the Harmonised nightmare staff is technically best in slot for magic in most places but loses the venom or healing of the Trident of the swamp or Sanguinesti staff, and says it is the absolute best option in solo raids after you have practised and no longer need Sanguinesti healing.[^6QEUIk_5nRU-937]
* Gnomonkey does not think the Harmonised nightmare staff is worth buying, believing you would make more money investing the 1.6 billion elsewhere than from its tiny DPS boost, and notes it cannot be used for bursting, which can be annoying.[^6QEUIk_5nRU-969]
* Gnomonkey says that right now the harmonised nightmare staff is slightly better than the Eye of Ayak on paper at Phosani's Nightmare but not worth using, and that might change after Raids 4.[^PJX1aJr8dQc-244]
* Gnomonkey says the harmonised nightmare staff is currently not useful and only has a use at the Maggot King, so a couple more uses make sense to him.[^PJX1aJr8dQc-544]
