## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says the Herblore minigame rewards were so poorly scaled that the first pre-pot device was not yet obtained after an 8.5 hour stream that began an hour after the update, with an estimated 10 to 15 hours at max efficiency. ([2024-09-26 6:32](https://youtu.be/fLGVBOVJAqo?t=392))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey suggests each piece of the Mixology outfit automate one of the three machines' bonus actions, so wearing the outfit gives faster AFK Mixology, which he thinks would not be broken because Mixology is slow. ([2026-05-27 10:03](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=603))

## Opinions

- In the description Gnomonkey calls the herb minigame a joke even for ironmen, with terrible XP rates and rewards that take over 15 hours to get. ([2024-09-26 0:00](https://youtu.be/fLGVBOVJAqo))
- Gnomonkey says the new Herblore minigame is very complicated, with many potion types and stations, and he decided to wait for a plugin before engaging further; he cannot imagine doing it as a mobile player. ([2024-09-26 5:39](https://youtu.be/fLGVBOVJAqo?t=339))
- Gnomonkey says the Herblore minigame should have default highlights and arrows for which potion to make, which station to go to and the timing click speed-up bonus, like a Giants' Foundry plugin provides, and says relying on RuneLite plugins for indicators is becoming less acceptable to him. ([2024-09-26 6:00](https://youtu.be/fLGVBOVJAqo?t=360))
- Gnomonkey says the minigame helps ironmen who do not want to get secondaries get XP, but that XP is not amazing and the rewards take an eternity to grind. ([2024-09-26 6:05](https://youtu.be/fLGVBOVJAqo?t=365))
- Gnomonkey says the outfit is an even bigger grind per piece, so by the time you get it you will not be levelling Herblore any more. ([2024-09-26 6:32](https://youtu.be/fLGVBOVJAqo?t=392))
- Gnomonkey says the minigame locks the secondary components of the Prayer enhance potion, the pre-pot device and potion storage behind countless hours of grinding, and that an ironman is better off getting secondaries and getting 50% more XP per hour. ([2024-09-26 7:00](https://youtu.be/fLGVBOVJAqo?t=420))
- Gnomonkey says only the goggles of the Mixology outfit do anything at the minigame right now. ([2026-05-27 10:03](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=603))
- Gnomonkey likes the Mixology outfit buff because it only works in the minigame, so the outfit is not necessary everywhere like the graceful outfit. ([2026-05-27 10:35](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=635))
