# Oathplate armour

## Stats and numbers

- In May 2025 a piece of Oathplate was worth around 90 million gp according to Gnomonkey. ([2025-05-18 3:26](https://youtu.be/AyPfaq5Kvnk?t=206))
- At Yama's release a 30% nerf was applied to the rare drop rates, from roughly one in 400 per piece of Oathplate to one in 600 per piece. ([2025-05-25 8:56](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=536))
- In solo, any piece of Oathplate is about 1 in 200 per kill (three pieces at one in 600 each), and the rates are doubled for duo. ([2025-05-25 9:56](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=596))
- Oathplate shards drop 12 per kill in solo and 6 in duo, and 450 shards let you forge any piece of Oathplate; at one in 17, that is about 620 kills to get another shot at a piece. ([2025-05-25 9:56](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=596))
- Gnomonkey estimates about 12 kills per hour in very good gear, so around 50 hours for full Oathplate, or 60 to 70 hours for a weaker player. ([2025-05-25 10:23](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=623))
- Gnomonkey shows Bandos chestplate falling from 25 million to 20 million and Torva platebody falling from 400 million in September 2024 to 270 million. ([2025-05-25 11:45](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=705))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey calls the Radiant Oathplate kit one of the hardest challenges in the game at release. ([2025-05-24 0:00](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I))
- Gnomonkey says 6th place is good enough for him and refers to himself as a D tier PvMer; he says he will not make a guide on these contracts. ([2025-05-24 0:00](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I))
- Gnomonkey says 60 to 70 hours is close to how long Bandos armour takes and is too short for Oathplate, saying Oathplate is far too common even after the nerf and it needs another nerf to somewhere over 100 hours. ([2025-05-25 10:23](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=623))
- Gnomonkey says the barrier to entry is low because Emberlight, not Scythe or Shadow, is best in slot, and synapses cost about 70 million, which he calls the lowest cost for a BIS weapon on any boss. ([2025-05-25 11:18](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=678))
- Gnomonkey says Oathplate is better than Torva most of the time (about 80%) because melee is mostly slash, leaving Torva mainly for Tombs of Amascut and the Torva helmet for maintaining max hits with the scythe. ([2025-05-25 12:18](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=738))
- Gnomonkey says Oathplate is endgame armour that knocks out the whole progression of Bandos and Torva, which he does not think is healthy and worries could be repeated with other new boss drops. ([2025-05-25 13:51](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=831))
- Gnomonkey says a grind like Oathplate's would make sense for something like Phosani's Nightmare (long eight-minute kills, constant one-shots, harmonised orb at one in 300), but he does not think Yama is the same. ([2025-05-25 14:48](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=888))
- Gnomonkey says the integrity of melee armour is ruined until the next best-in-slot melee armour, which could be three to five years away, and that Bandos and Nex-type armour have effectively been killed. ([2025-05-25 15:20](https://youtu.be/yP8IB3_IsSc?t=920))
- Gnomonkey says Oathplate price was crashing because people were panic selling, but when he did the content for about 10 hours the price went steadily up while contracts dropped in price; he thinks the contract values are based on speculation. ([2025-06-05 0:26](https://youtu.be/n5pteeFPlqc?t=26))
- Gnomonkey says contracts affect Oathplate value by at most 5-10% even if the whole community becomes perfect, but he would still like to see Oathplate nerfed because it was already common. ([2025-06-05 0:57](https://youtu.be/n5pteeFPlqc?t=57))
- Gnomonkey says Oathplate is fairly easy to obtain in the grand scheme and completely devalues Torva, which has dropped a ton in value and is outclassed by Oathplate almost everywhere. ([2025-10-15 9:11](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=551))
- Gnomonkey says there is really no reason to get Torva anymore and he would rather Oathplate not be a direct upgrade. ([2025-10-15 9:11](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=551))

## Records

- Gnomonkey completed his Radiant Oathplate kit as the world's 6th player to do so, in May 2025, after a roughly 20 hour stream. ([2025-05-24 0:00](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I))
- Gnomonkey says completing all the contracts for the kit took twenty and a half hours of stream. ([2025-05-24 47:53](https://youtu.be/X3eF6YHLh9I?t=2873))
- As of the video Gnomonkey had 47 pieces of Oathplate in his collection log, his success rate on Oathplate contracts was over 90%, and his best win streak was around 20 in a row. ([2025-06-05 3:35](https://youtu.be/n5pteeFPlqc?t=215))
- The video description states Gnomonkey's best Oathplate contract kill time was 5:50 and worst 9:10, with about 500m gp/hr profit, and that it took him about three days and 50-100 different contracts to learn. ([2025-06-05 3:35](https://youtu.be/n5pteeFPlqc?t=215))
