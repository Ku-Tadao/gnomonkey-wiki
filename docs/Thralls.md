# Thralls

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 12 videos (2021-07-13 to 2025-10-04). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Gnomonkey used thralls in the Colosseum and pulled soul runes from his Shadow for Death Charge to keep SGS specs going. ([2024-03-22 0:29](https://youtu.be/IR0MIzsbFeM?t=29))
- Gnomonkey points out much content forces a specific thrall type: a mage thrall on Vasa Nistirio at Chambers of Xeric because the crystals are immune to ranged damage, a specific thrall type for each hand of Ulm and a ranged thrall for its head, and a melee thrall at Theatre of Blood P1 or damage is reduced. ([2025-01-12 7:27](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=447))

## Requirements

- The Book of the Dead is needed to summon thralls, which Gnomonkey says are really important for Olm. ([2025-05-01 2:01](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=121))
- The group debated whether thralls and the Book of the Dead were allowed; Showbeck objected, but the other players voted to allow thralls and the Book of the Dead. ([2025-09-23 7:03](https://youtu.be/QkJq2KHiKW8?t=423))

## Stats and numbers

- The skeleton and ghost thralls are effectively the same, both with a range of six tiles. ([2021-07-13 3:06](https://youtu.be/26wPQVuqmNY?t=186))
- At Callisto the mage thrall does no damage because he is completely invulnerable to magic. ([2021-07-13 3:34](https://youtu.be/26wPQVuqmNY?t=214))
- Many Chambers bosses have flat damage reduction to a style: the Great Olm's hands and head each have a 50% damage reduction against the wrong style, which must be kept in mind when choosing a thrall. ([2021-07-13 3:34](https://youtu.be/26wPQVuqmNY?t=214))
- Gnomonkey says he does somehow more DPS to Jad than a thrall, and that a thrall costs 10 fire runes while Undead Grasp costs 12 per cast. ([2024-05-09 3:42](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=222))
- Gnomonkey says a thrall on the Ice Demon hits a one about one in four hits, and when it would hit a three it hits a one, otherwise a zero, so it is optional but he does not knock people for using it. ([2024-10-27 49:00](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=2940))
- Thralls hit 0 to 3 (average 1.5) every 4 ticks, adding about 0.625 DPS according to Gnomonkey. ([2025-03-29 6:36](https://youtu.be/W1OX5PVaPxs?t=396))
- Gnomonkey says thralls add 0.625 DPS on every boss, around two thirds DPS, which speeds up the raid by several minutes especially with weaker gear. ([2025-06-25 3:01](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=181))
- Gnomonkey says thralls are always 0.6 DPS and give roughly a 15% DPS increase for Sardaco's gear, and the lower your gear is, the bigger a chunk of your DPS they are. ([2025-10-04 2:34](https://youtu.be/X-PTHMq_10A?t=154))
- Gnomonkey says thralls added about 2k extra damage in the raid because everything in Tombs of Amascut is very tanky; with 4 DPS from a player, 0.6 of that DPS is the thrall, which is bigger than a weapon upgrade. ([2025-10-04 46:27](https://youtu.be/X-PTHMq_10A?t=2787))
- Gnomonkey says thralls never miss: they roll zero to three damage every hit, ignore defence and just punch through. ([2025-10-04 50:49](https://youtu.be/X-PTHMq_10A?t=3049))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says the zombie (melee) thrall often wastes ticks walking around, so you typically want to avoid summoning that variant. ([2021-07-13 3:06](https://youtu.be/26wPQVuqmNY?t=186))
- Where a monster is not completely invulnerable to a combat style, such as Dagannoth Kings or the Kalphite Queen, which have high defence rather than invulnerability, the thrall style does not matter and full damage goes through in all phases. ([2021-07-13 3:06](https://youtu.be/26wPQVuqmNY?t=186))
- Thrall style matters a lot at the Great Olm as the wrong-style thrall's max hit is reduced to one: use mage on the mage hand, zombie (melee) on the melee hand and skeleton on the head phase. ([2021-07-13 5:02](https://youtu.be/26wPQVuqmNY?t=302))
- Gnomonkey says the key to any challenge run is using thralls always, because they deal a lot of damage while his own damage is low. ([2024-05-09 2:51](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=171))
- Gnomonkey suggests that a minimal thrall upgrade would be to make thralls hit one to three, or to give thralls different effects and make them typeless so they work. ([2025-01-14 12:07](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=727))
- At the Ice Demon a thrall maxes one and rarely hits, so Gnomonkey does not bother summoning one there. ([2025-05-01 9:52](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=592))
- Gnomonkey says thralls are super important for learning Olm because without one, a zero hit makes Olm turn midway and hit you. ([2025-05-01 52:46](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=3166))
- Gnomonkey uses a Book of the Dead as his offhand switch to summon thralls in ToA. ([2025-06-25 3:01](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=181))
- Gnomonkey says thralls speed up a Tombs of Amascut raid by one to two minutes, and he uses death charge because many things give it: monkeys at Baba, Kephri's swarms and adds, Akkha's shadows and the core on Warden P2. ([2025-06-28 4:56](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=296))
- Gnomonkey summons a zombie thrall for the last phase of Akkha. ([2025-06-28 21:58](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=1318))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says that after he switched to thralls in the Colosseum, the waves became easy, and that thralls provide so much DPS that Ancients is not the play. ([2024-03-22 0:00](https://youtu.be/IR0MIzsbFeM))
- Gnomonkey says everything in the Colosseum has high magic defence, and SGS plus Bloodfury gives him the same sustain as camping Death Charge. ([2024-03-22 0:00](https://youtu.be/IR0MIzsbFeM))
- Gnomonkey chose thralls over Barrage in the mage-only Fight Caves because thralls out-damaged his own casts on the high-mage-defence monsters. ([2024-05-09 32:30](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=1950))
- Gnomonkey says Chambers is one of the few raids where you do not use a thrall spellbook by default and there are many reasons to use different spellbooks, but thralls are really good for learning solo Olm. ([2024-10-27 6:03](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=363))
- Gnomonkey objects to the thrall boomerang because many bosses are in walled areas (Ulm, Warden P3, Duke Sucellus) where a boomerang cannot pass, and bosses like Vasa Nistirio wander around. ([2025-01-12 0:29](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=29))
- Gnomonkey says any thrall upgrade must always benefit thralls so there is a reason to use the currency; otherwise players keep spamming the consumable at bosses where the mechanic does not work and get no benefit. ([2025-01-12 1:00](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=60))
- Gnomonkey says the simplest community suggestion, increasing thrall damage from 0-3 to 1-3 with no thrall book, would always buff thralls and work as a baseline, but he wants something better than baseline. ([2025-01-12 1:26](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=86))
- Gnomonkey says the 'no thrall book' part of the damage-increase suggestion would be a huge buff. ([2025-01-12 1:59](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=119))
- Gnomonkey dislikes the idea of an AoE around the thrall's location because players have no control over where a thrall spawns; for example a thrall summoned one tile off Kephri would miss, forcing constant re-summoning and more currency use. ([2025-01-12 2:29](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=149))
- Gnomonkey says a buff for standing with your thrall (such as a minimum hit of four) would push players to use thralls at bosses like Vespula or Zuk where thralls cannot reach, which he does not want; the buff should apply where thralls are already used. ([2025-01-12 3:26](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=206))
- Gnomonkey likes the idea of user-controlled thrall placement (click the thrall, get a targeting reticle, click the ground to place it), and says the boomerang idea would work much better if players could control where the thrall is summoned. ([2025-01-12 3:57](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=237))
- Gnomonkey says thralls are currently set-it-and-forget-it, which makes them poor to build a play-around bonus on, and thinks they may need a rework. ([2025-01-12 4:24](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=264))
- Gnomonkey thinks having a thrall upgrade charge the book of the dead is less clunky than carrying tokens in the ammo slot, which he finds really frustrating, and says it should charge something even if the book is later removed by a quest. ([2025-01-12 6:55](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=415))
- Gnomonkey finds the idea of typeless thralls with different effects appealing, saying it would turn the choice into decision-making rather than APM. ([2025-01-12 7:58](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=478))
- Gnomonkey says making all thralls apply damage over time adds little because thralls already deal small damage over time, and notes burn is buggy and appears to count as ranged damage so it hits zeros on ranged-immune targets. ([2025-01-12 8:26](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=506))
- Gnomonkey likes that there are three thrall types but dislikes that all thralls feel the same, and says a typeless omni-thrall concept would reduce complexity. ([2025-01-12 8:57](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=537))
- Gnomonkey thinks a thrall debuff idea that builds a damage-over-time debuff on the target is too complicated, and says changes should be simple: a thrall is summoned and does damage; Avenge is cast and reflects damage. ([2025-01-12 9:24](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=564))
- Gnomonkey is leaning toward three differentiated thrall types where the mage, ranged and melee thralls each do their own simple thing, so players decide which suits each situation. ([2025-01-12 9:54](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=594))
- Gnomonkey considers the idea of the thrall exploding at the player's location, or a thrall bomb with huge AoE, too crazy an effect for something that should be simple and small. ([2025-01-12 10:27](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=627))
- Gnomonkey says many people complain thralls are far less interesting than Vengeance, and he agrees: thralls are just summon, deal damage and lose a little prayer, whereas Vengeance involves deciding when to dump a lot of health on a target. ([2025-01-12 11:25](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=685))
- Gnomonkey suggested on stream that doing something right with your thrall could grant a mini Vengeance or recoil proc, making the decision of when to spend a Vengeance more interesting, though it would require reworking thralls. ([2025-01-12 11:52](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=712))
- Gnomonkey says players almost never take Vengeance anywhere unless spellbook swapping in Theatre of Blood, and mostly just camp thralls everywhere. ([2025-01-12 12:22](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=742))
- Gnomonkey says he asked players for ideas for the thrall upgrade in a previous video, received hundreds of comments, and was shocked that Jagex chose to drop the idea instead of using one of them. ([2025-01-14 6:25](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=385))
- Gnomonkey calls dropping the thrall currency and upgrade one of the worst changes he has heard of pre-update, saying he did not see a single comment asking for the currency to be removed. ([2025-01-14 7:16](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=436))
- A Reddit post Gnomonkey reads says Thralls are in a state where you lose DPS if you do not bring them, and compares Surge potions to them; Gnomonkey disagrees and says Surge potions are not the same. ([2025-03-29 2:28](https://youtu.be/W1OX5PVaPxs?t=148))
- Gnomonkey explains he does not run Blood Barrage in the Atlatl setup because thralls and death charge give more specs and DPS and he considers Blood Barrage unnecessary. ([2025-06-28 4:56](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=296))

## Events

- The Doom of Mokhaiotl blog proposed a consumable stored in the ammo slot that augments thralls with an additional effect: when thralls despawn they boomerang back to the player and deal damage if they hit the target. ([2025-01-12 0:29](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=29))
- Jagex removed the planned thrall upgrade from the Doom of Mokhaiotl rewards. ([2025-01-14 6:25](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=385))
- Players noted during Kephri that the only damage getting done by the group was through thralls, and the group repeatedly told everyone to re-thrall during the raid. ([2025-09-23 13:00](https://youtu.be/QkJq2KHiKW8?t=780))
- Gnomonkey said his thralls probably did about half of his damage in the raid, which affected how the top score was viewed. ([2025-09-23 1:21:15](https://youtu.be/QkJq2KHiKW8?t=4875))
