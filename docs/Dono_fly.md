**Dono fly** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2025-05-15. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* To start the Dono fly method you spawn in, spam-click to the delay tile, then attack Yama; after that each time your true tile reaches the next tile in the cycle (one, two, three, four, 4.1, then start) you attack Yama again.[^prE1BqM1wBE-59]
* The hardest part of Dono fly is reaching Yama in exactly three game ticks after the Judge teleports you back; spam click to the delay tile along true tiles and click Yama on the third tick.[^prE1BqM1wBE-540]
* One tile in the cycle is the only part not simply attack as soon as your true tile reaches it: you click to the Dono tile and wait until you are off attack cooldown, or Yama hits you.[^prE1BqM1wBE-628]

## Stats and numbers

* The Dono fly cycle breaks down solo eventually, at around 12 of Yama's attacks (Yama attacks every seven ticks), so you mage a little at the end.[^prE1BqM1wBE-126]

## Tips

* When dodging the waves with Dono fly, click right before the wave reaches you, basically on top of the tile.[^prE1BqM1wBE-1503]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says Dono fly lets you brain off and skip everything, making the final phase harder to learn but easier to execute than mage camping.[^prE1BqM1wBE-1621]

## Events

* The butterfly method, called Dono fly, is a Yama tile method found by Dono that lets you melee the entire kill and dodge everything by default, like a Bandos 6-0 style method.[^prE1BqM1wBE-29]

## Trivia

* Per the description, Dono fly can be done in duo by stacking, but there are hiccups such as players not spawning in at the same time.[^prE1BqM1wBE-0]
* In the Dono fly cycle you run to a tile and attack from it, which dodges everything including the waves, which go straight through you.[^prE1BqM1wBE-90]
* If you reach Yama in two ticks, he punches you for about 50 and spawns an orb, so if you spawn close to the delay tile you must stop on a tile for a tick before attacking.[^prE1BqM1wBE-566]
