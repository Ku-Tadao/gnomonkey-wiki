## Methods

- To start the Dono fly method you spawn in, spam-click to the delay tile, then attack Yama; after that each time your true tile reaches the next tile in the cycle (one, two, three, four, 4.1, then start) you attack Yama again. ([2025-05-15 0:59](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=59))
- The hardest part of Dono fly is reaching Yama in exactly three game ticks after the Judge teleports you back; spam click to the delay tile along true tiles and click Yama on the third tick. ([2025-05-15 9:00](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=540))
- One tile in the cycle is the only part not simply attack as soon as your true tile reaches it: you click to the Dono tile and wait until you are off attack cooldown, or Yama hits you. ([2025-05-15 10:28](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=628))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says the Dono fly cycle breaks down solo eventually, at around 12 of Yama's attacks (Yama attacks every seven ticks), so you mage a little at the end. ([2025-05-15 2:06](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=126))

## Tips

- When dodging the waves with Dono fly, click right before the wave reaches you, basically on top of the tile. ([2025-05-15 25:03](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=1503))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says Dono fly lets you brain off and skip everything, making the final phase harder to learn but easier to execute than mage camping. ([2025-05-15 27:01](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=1621))

## Events

- The butterfly method, called Dono fly, is a Yama tile method found by Dono that lets you melee the entire kill and dodge everything by default, like a Bandos 6-0 style method. ([2025-05-15 0:29](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=29))

## Trivia

- Per the description, Dono fly can be done in duo by stacking, but there are hiccups such as players not spawning in at the same time. ([2025-05-15 0:00](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE))
- In the Dono fly cycle you run to a tile and attack from it, which dodges everything including the waves, which go straight through you. ([2025-05-15 1:30](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=90))
- If you reach Yama in two ticks, he punches you for about 50 and spawns an orb, so if you spawn close to the delay tile you must stop on a tile for a tick before attacking. ([2025-05-15 9:26](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=566))
