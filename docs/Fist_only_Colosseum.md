**Fist only Colosseum** is a challenge topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2024-03-28. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* The monsters have low melee defence, and that an ink-type crush bonus is good for crush accuracy, which is how his fists hit as hard as they do.[^IfJVUHF6fyc-1346]
* Punching is a four-tick action.[^IfJVUHF6fyc-1920]

## Tips

* He is not afraid to use brews during the waves in the fist-only run because he is confident he can do the boss with no brews and thinks brews do not do much for him at the boss.[^IfJVUHF6fyc-1008]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the fist-only Colosseum run is almost as difficult as speedrunning Colosseum, because there is no out-DPSing anything before the Minotaur and reinforcements arrive.[^IfJVUHF6fyc-0]

## Records

* Gnomonkey finished the fist-only Colosseum after about 52 minutes, with 14 perfect parries on the boss, a seven-minute boss kill, and received a large stack of sunfire splinters.[^IfJVUHF6fyc-2329]

## Events

* Gnomonkey maxed out as much crush bonus as he could to kill his way through Fortis Colosseum using only fists, after a chatter recommended killing the boss like a Fremennik with some punching and kicking.[^IfJVUHF6fyc-0]
* On the previous fist-only attempt Gnomonkey got Sol Heredit down to 250 health with a kick-only setup but died.[^IfJVUHF6fyc-828]
* Gnomonkey took Relentless 3 on the final waves, calling it probably the first and last time he would ever pick it, and fought the boss with a torture amulet and a kick/punch setup.[^IfJVUHF6fyc-1864]

## Trivia

* Despite killing the Colosseum boss with his fists, Gnomonkey did not get the Sol Heredit pet drop in this run.[^IfJVUHF6fyc-2385]
