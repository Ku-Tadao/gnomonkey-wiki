# Fist only Colosseum

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 1 video (2024-03-28). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says the monsters have low melee defence, and that an ink-type crush bonus is good for crush accuracy, which is how his fists hit as hard as they do. ([2024-03-28 22:26](https://youtu.be/IfJVUHF6fyc?t=1346))
- Gnomonkey notes that punching is a four-tick action. ([2024-03-28 32:00](https://youtu.be/IfJVUHF6fyc?t=1920))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says he is not afraid to use brews during the waves in the fist-only run because he is confident he can do the boss with no brews and thinks brews do not do much for him at the boss. ([2024-03-28 16:48](https://youtu.be/IfJVUHF6fyc?t=1008))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the fist-only Colosseum run is almost as difficult as speedrunning Colosseum, because there is no out-DPSing anything before the Minotaur and reinforcements arrive. ([2024-03-28 0:00](https://youtu.be/IfJVUHF6fyc))

## Records

- Gnomonkey finished the fist-only Colosseum after about 52 minutes, with 14 perfect parries on the boss, a seven-minute boss kill, and received a large stack of sunfire splinters. ([2024-03-28 38:49](https://youtu.be/IfJVUHF6fyc?t=2329))

## Events

- Gnomonkey maxed out as much crush bonus as he could to kill his way through Fortis Colosseum using only fists, after a chatter recommended killing the boss like a Fremennik with some punching and kicking. ([2024-03-28 0:00](https://youtu.be/IfJVUHF6fyc))
- On the previous fist-only attempt Gnomonkey got Sol Heredit down to 250 health with a kick-only setup but died. ([2024-03-28 13:48](https://youtu.be/IfJVUHF6fyc?t=828))
- Gnomonkey took Relentless 3 on the final waves, calling it probably the first and last time he would ever pick it, and fought the boss with a torture amulet and a kick/punch setup. ([2024-03-28 31:04](https://youtu.be/IfJVUHF6fyc?t=1864))

## Trivia

- Despite killing the Colosseum boss with his fists, Gnomonkey did not get the Sol Heredit pet drop in this run. ([2024-03-28 39:45](https://youtu.be/IfJVUHF6fyc?t=2385))
