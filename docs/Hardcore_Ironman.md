**Hardcore Ironman** is an account topic that Gnomonkey covers in 7 videos between 2025-01-19 and 2026-05-27. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Dying in the Inferno is a safe death on a regular hardcore ironman and does not lose hardcore status, whereas on a group hardcore ironman dying there loses status.[^gH0WVB_jMjQ-735]
* Group hardcore is locked to prestige, and a solo group hardcore would lock him out of raids like the Theatre of Blood or Nex, so he can only get items by soloing.[^gH0WVB_jMjQ-1610]
* If your boat dies you wash up at the port on 1 HP, and since you can also be poisoned at sea, you could die the instant you return to shore.[^pwZKK8dpq8o-740]
* Escape crystals detect if a player disconnects and teleport them the next time they take damage, with a configurable timer, and cost 75k because they are the same as the crystals for Theatre of Blood.[^NzW7JFgc8qU-722]

## Tips

* To get Grandmaster combat achievements time for Nightmare he would need roughly full Ink potentially plus the scythe, which would also have meant doing the Phosani's Nightmare grind that he hates, and a lot of combat achievements from God Wars and Desert Treasure 2 bosses.[^0_DI-SbhksU-339]
* Gnomonkey warns hardcore ironman players that large areas of the sea are multi-combat, so your boat can be attacked by many sources.[^pwZKK8dpq8o-740]
* Tier 3 prep protects hardcores from disconnecting during tornadoes in the Corrupted Gauntlet.[^R6F2mWXa_fM-33]
* Gnomonkey suggests the wizard who sells escape crystals hands a new Hardcore Ironman a free bundle of three the first time, and that the mysterious stranger outside Theatre of Blood gives another three as a bonus for reaching it.[^NzW7JFgc8qU-781]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says activities like the Nightmare Zone and Pest Control are dangerous on hardcore, and that group hardcore is more dangerous despite having more lives.[^gH0WVB_jMjQ-795]
* Gnomonkey says he would prefer Inferno to be a dangerous death on hardcore because it would be more fun, but says hardcore was made with these rules originally and should stay that way, which is why group hardcore was made different.[^gH0WVB_jMjQ-1427]
* Gnomonkey says Chambers of Xeric is a really good group raid as an Ironman, and that it could not be any better if Jagex tried.[^tTn3JmSD4fI-1444]
* Gnomonkey says the Colosseum is very dangerous for a regular hardcore account.[^c6oOmOGl5Lg-412]
* Gnomonkey says he was close to getting Grandmaster combat achievements on the account, so he would never kill an account on purpose for content.[^0_DI-SbhksU-298]
* Gnomonkey was nervous about being dry at Doom of Mokhaiotl, seeing people post 2000 deep delve collection logs without boots, and says he would not have made it to 1000 Doom kills.[^0_DI-SbhksU-308]
* Gnomonkey says he was somewhat relieved to be free of the upcoming dangerous combat achievement grinds, and says he does not particularly like combat achievements.[^0_DI-SbhksU-339]
* Gnomonkey says he never cared about the prestige of doing everything himself and came to play Hardcore just to try the game mode; getting Grandmaster was never the goal.[^0_DI-SbhksU-521]
* Gnomonkey says the Hardcore account was living on borrowed time with dangerous grinds coming up, and calls it a pretty good run for what is technically his third Hardcore Ironman, given the other two did not get as far.[^0_DI-SbhksU-521]
* Gnomonkey says getting spooned everything made him skip big chunks of the game, so he expects a more natural Hardcore progression on the backup account to be nicer and different.[^0_DI-SbhksU-721]
* Gnomonkey says getting Grandmaster on some account would be great, but the plan has always just been to play a Hardcore Ironman.[^0_DI-SbhksU-755]
* Gnomonkey thinks it is silly to touch Sailing on a hardcore ironman on day one because of the dangers and the bugs he expects, and does not think Jagex will return hardcore status on anything.[^pwZKK8dpq8o-767]
* Gnomonkey says Corrupted Gauntlet is incredibly dangerous for Hardcore Ironmen but a necessary grind for the Bow of Faerdhinen, and tier 3 armor with a fast escape crystal makes it basically safe.[^R6F2mWXa_fM-0]
* Gnomonkey says early on a Hardcore Ironman escape crystals are unaffordable, and a player probably only has the money around a total level of about 400.[^NzW7JFgc8qU-752]
* Gnomonkey says he lets his wife do boring grinds off-stream on the Hardcore accounts he plays because he does not like Ironman and finds getting things himself a waste of time with content he does not enjoy.[^NzW7JFgc8qU-3639]
* Gnomonkey says players who constantly make Hardcore after Hardcore and push dangerous content, such as Hardcore Grandmasters, would be the audience for a Hardcore Main mode without Ironman grinds.[^NzW7JFgc8qU-3671]

## Records

* The HCIM reaches rank 19 for Colosseum glory on the hardcore high scores.[^c6oOmOGl5Lg-2076]
* 6thBrother had a six-hour session at Doom of Mokhaiotl without success, and Gnomonkey never got to Theatre of Blood on the account.[^0_DI-SbhksU-308]
* Gnomonkey has levelled three Hardcore Ironmen and had never died on one before 6thBrother; Hedron has never died on one.[^0_DI-SbhksU-521]

## Events

* Gnomonkey is doing the stream on a hardcore ironman account, with a low level range and a level 92 prayer.[^gH0WVB_jMjQ-2]
* The Twisted bow was received on the HCIM unlocked account at 115 kill count, and that they were heading to the Colosseum the next day with gold body paint.[^tTn3JmSD4fI-0]
* The raid was run on Hedron's backup HCIM to celebrate it earning its Fire cape.[^tTn3JmSD4fI-0]
* The run is on Gnomonkey's HCIM account, which now has the Twisted bow and the golden quiver.[^c6oOmOGl5Lg-0]
* Gnomonkey plans to attempt Sailing on his hardcore ironman only after a week or two, once things are ironed out.[^pwZKK8dpq8o-767]
