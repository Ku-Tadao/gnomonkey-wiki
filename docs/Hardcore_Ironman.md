A **Hardcore Ironman** (HCIM) is an Ironman account that loses its hardcore status on a dangerous death. Gnomonkey played the mode mainly to try the game mode and never cared about the prestige of doing everything himself[^0_DI-SbhksU-521]. He has levelled three Hardcore Ironmen and had never died on one before 6thBrother; Hedron has never died on one[^0_DI-SbhksU-521]. He does not particularly like Ironman, finds getting things himself a waste of time with content he does not enjoy, and has his wife do boring grinds off-stream on Hardcore accounts[^NzW7JFgc8qU-3639].

## Rules and death

* Dying in the [Inferno](Inferno.md) is a safe death on a regular hardcore ironman and does not lose hardcore status, whereas on a group hardcore ironman it does[^gH0WVB_jMjQ-735]. Gnomonkey would prefer Inferno to be a dangerous death because it would be more fun, but says hardcore was made with these rules and should stay that way, which is why group hardcore was made different[^gH0WVB_jMjQ-1427].
* Activities like the Nightmare Zone and Pest Control are dangerous on hardcore, and group hardcore is more dangerous despite having more lives[^gH0WVB_jMjQ-795].
* Group hardcore is locked to prestige. A solo group hardcore would lock him out of raids like the Theatre of Blood or Nex, so he can only get items by soloing[^gH0WVB_jMjQ-1610].
* The Colosseum is very dangerous for a regular hardcore account[^c6oOmOGl5Lg-412]. The Corrupted Gauntlet is incredibly dangerous but a necessary grind for the Bow of Faerdhinen; tier 3 armour with a fast escape crystal makes it basically safe[^R6F2mWXa_fM-0], and tier 3 prep protects against disconnecting during tornadoes[^R6F2mWXa_fM-33].
* He warns that large areas of the sea in [Sailing](Sailing.md) are multi-combat, so a boat can be attacked by many sources. If the boat dies the player washes up at the port on 1 HP, and since poison is possible at sea, they could die the instant they return to shore[^pwZKK8dpq8o-740][^pwZKK8dpq8o-740]. He thinks it silly to touch Sailing on day one because of the dangers and expected bugs, and does not think Jagex will return hardcore status on anything. He plans to attempt it only after a week or two[^pwZKK8dpq8o-767][^pwZKK8dpq8o-767].

## Escape crystals

Escape crystals detect if a player disconnects and teleport them the next time they take damage, with a configurable timer. They cost 75k because they are the same as the crystals for the Theatre of Blood[^NzW7JFgc8qU-722]. Early on they are unaffordable, and a player probably only has the money around a total level of about 400[^NzW7JFgc8qU-752]. The wizard who sells them gives a new Hardcore Ironman a free bundle of three the first time, and the mysterious stranger outside the Theatre of Blood gives another three as a bonus for reaching it[^NzW7JFgc8qU-781].

## Gnomonkey's accounts

* **January 2025:** he streams on a hardcore ironman account with a low level range and a level 92 prayer[^gH0WVB_jMjQ-2].
* **March 2025:** the Twisted bow arrives on the HCIM at 115 kill count, with the Colosseum planned next with gold body paint[^tTn3JmSD4fI-0]. A raid is run on Hedron's backup HCIM to celebrate its Fire cape[^tTn3JmSD4fI-0]. He calls Chambers of Xeric a really good group raid as an Ironman, which could not be better if Jagex tried[^tTn3JmSD4fI-1444]. The account now has the Twisted bow and the golden quiver[^c6oOmOGl5Lg-0], and reaches rank 19 for Colosseum glory on the hardcore high scores[^c6oOmOGl5Lg-2076].
* **October 2025:** the account dies. He was close to Grandmaster combat achievements and says he would never kill an account on purpose for content[^0_DI-SbhksU-298]. Remaining grinds were dangerous: Nightmare Grandmaster needs roughly full Ink potentially plus the scythe, meaning the Phosani's Nightmare grind he hates, and many God Wars and Desert Treasure 2 combat achievements[^0_DI-SbhksU-339]. He was nervous about being dry at Doom of Mokhaiotl, seeing people post 2000 deep delve collection logs without boots, and would not have made it to 1000 Doom kills[^0_DI-SbhksU-308]. 6thBrother had a six-hour session at Doom without success, and Gnomonkey never got to Theatre of Blood on the account[^0_DI-SbhksU-308]. He was somewhat relieved to be free of the dangerous grinds, and does not like combat achievements[^0_DI-SbhksU-339]. He calls it a pretty good run for what is technically his third Hardcore Ironman, since the other two did not get as far[^0_DI-SbhksU-521]. Getting Grandmaster on some account would be great, but the plan was always to play a Hardcore Ironman[^0_DI-SbhksU-755]. Getting everything spooned made him skip big chunks of the game, so he expects a more natural progression on the backup account to be nicer and different[^0_DI-SbhksU-721].

## Hardcore Main

Gnomonkey says players who constantly make Hardcore after Hardcore and push dangerous content, such as Hardcore Grandmasters, would be the audience for a Hardcore Main mode without Ironman grinds[^NzW7JFgc8qU-3671].
