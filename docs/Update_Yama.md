# Update: Yama

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2025-05-09 to 2025-05-13). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | update |
| **Videos** | 3 (first 2025-05-09, latest 2025-05-13) |

## Stats and numbers

- The AMA stated the armor from Yama requires 78 Mining and 83 Smithing to make. ([2025-05-13 6:06](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=366))
- Gnomonkey says the gravestone fee is 500k for a maxed player, much costlier than deaths at Fasani, about 50k, or Nex, about 100k. ([2025-05-13 6:58](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=418))
- Arcane said solo kills during testing took about eight minutes depending on setups and effort, which Gnomonkey says is close to a typical Fasani kill. ([2025-05-13 12:42](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=762))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey encourages viewers to try Yama on release day, since it seems doable even without an Inferno cape or a duo partner, and to keep giving feedback after release since Jmods said they are willing to rework things. ([2025-05-13 39:43](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=2383))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says he is happy Jagex shared an early blog on Yama contracts but is not excited or at ease with most of it. ([2025-05-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/H6qB5NmGEFQ))
- Gnomonkey says waiting a week after release for contracts to be turned on kills a lot of the hype, since he wanted to rush the hard variant on day one and try for world first. ([2025-05-09 4:19](https://youtu.be/H6qB5NmGEFQ?t=259))
- Gnomonkey says contracts being off for the first week mostly affects streamers, and he would prefer the hard variant be available instantly so players can rush to learn the bosses. ([2025-05-09 5:19](https://youtu.be/H6qB5NmGEFQ?t=319))
- Gnomonkey says a week before launch, a lot of the community's complaints about the Yama contract system cannot be fixed, particularly because the contracts are tied to the market. ([2025-05-09 18:54](https://youtu.be/H6qB5NmGEFQ?t=1134))
- Gnomonkey says he is still very excited for the Yama boss and will do it on release despite his concerns about its drop system. ([2025-05-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/moDd-yhWlRs))
- Gnomonkey compares the Yama contract to the Awakener's Orb system, where unlocking Blorva requires doing the four awakened bosses. ([2025-05-09 2:22](https://youtu.be/moDd-yhWlRs?t=142))
- Gnomonkey says that by release there will be no way to remove contracts from people's banks, so he wants Jagex to explain exactly how they work before release while there is still time to fix the system. ([2025-05-09 21:56](https://youtu.be/moDd-yhWlRs?t=1316))

## Events

- In Gnomonkey's Twitch chat, Mod Goblin answered a question about Yama contracts by saying they are tradable and used on entry, so players should hope they can finish the fight or lose the GP they spent on the contract. ([2025-05-09 0:53](https://youtu.be/moDd-yhWlRs?t=53))
- Per Gnomonkey, it is confirmed that Yama contracts are a drop and are tradable, but full details of how the system works have not been released. ([2025-05-09 1:24](https://youtu.be/moDd-yhWlRs?t=84))
- Mod Goblin said he feels the contract system is a lot better than the Awakener's Orb system, which Gnomonkey says could be true but cannot be judged without full details. ([2025-05-09 1:24](https://youtu.be/moDd-yhWlRs?t=84))
- Mod Goblin confirmed in the Reddit thread that Yama contracts are not a pay-GP-for-better-drop-rates system, i.e. people are not meant to use them to get better loot, the same stated goal as Awakener's Orbs. ([2025-05-09 1:51](https://youtu.be/moDd-yhWlRs?t=111))
- Mod Goblin said in the thread that Yama contracts will mechanically change the boss, and they are used to unlock the white variant of Oathplate, an ornament kit for the new armor. ([2025-05-09 2:22](https://youtu.be/moDd-yhWlRs?t=142))
- Gnomonkey says he will stream Yama as soon as it is released. ([2025-05-09 23:16](https://youtu.be/moDd-yhWlRs?t=1396))
- Gnomonkey notes that Yama is one of two high-level updates this year, alongside Mocha, and that Coliseum, the previous one, was roughly a year earlier. ([2025-05-09 23:16](https://youtu.be/moDd-yhWlRs?t=1396))
- Jagex Jmods held a Reddit AMA about Yama after community confusion over contracts, and Gnomonkey summarizes the confirmed info and the developers' thoughts in this video. ([2025-05-13 0:00](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo))
- Arcane said a player with high 90s stats and Blood Moon gear can definitely get Yama kills once they learn the mechanics, with slower kills, more damage and more prayer lost; he said he planned to attempt it on his 1600 total ironman that weekend. ([2025-05-13 0:57](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=57))
- Jmods described Yama as quite punishing with a lot of movement plus some prayer and gear switching, with few confusing mechanics and one mechanic that kills the player outright if they fail it entirely. ([2025-05-13 1:57](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=117))
- If one player dies in a Yama duo, they go straight to Lumbridge and the remaining player can stay and try to close out the fight, though the dead player does not get a role in the loot, similar to Duo Nex. ([2025-05-13 2:30](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=150))
- Per the AMA, the Combat Achievement tier for Yama is likely elite as the lowest tier, with no medium combat achievements expected. ([2025-05-13 3:34](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=214))
- Yama will have no demon bane damage reduction, and Monster Examine will be enabled on release so players can check his stats. ([2025-05-13 3:34](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=214))
- Jmods said Yama can be entirely soloed, with some mechanics less forgiving and tight; it encourages duo play more like Royal Titans than Theatre of Blood, and the boss does not scale in stats or mechanics. ([2025-05-13 4:04](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=244))
- Dying in Yama's domain leaves a gravestone at the bottom of the Chasm of Fire, with gravestone fees reduced by 75% until the player has at least 10 Yama kill count. ([2025-05-13 6:22](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=382))
- Jmods said Yama's gameplay loop might be more like Vorkath or Grotesque Guardians than Royal Titans, so there is a short downtime before the fight starts and a joining player is not locked out after a few seconds. ([2025-05-13 7:20](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=440))
- Yama drops uniques outright with loot to the floor and also drops Oathplate shards as a form of soft drop protection that doubles as GP-per-hour and creates demand for Infernal shale. ([2025-05-13 7:59](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=479))
- Jmods said there is room for players to bring unusual tech to Yama, but did not say whether players will opt for it. ([2025-05-13 8:38](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=518))
- Jmods said the fight has some high-intensity phases and downtime like P3 Verzik, and that tribrid is better left to raids since switching three gear sets is exhausting and takes inventory space. ([2025-05-13 9:20](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=560))
- Drop rates for Yama will be shared fairly quickly after launch, within a week or two; Jmods looked at Nex and Bandos as comparative grinds and said Oathplate time-to-completion sits somewhere in the middle. ([2025-05-13 11:17](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=677))
- Jmods said base Yama is a notch beyond regular Vardorvis in difficulty, and soloing the fight length is closer to Fasani's Nightmare but less intensely punishing. ([2025-05-13 12:08](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=728))
- There is no bank near Yama, but there is a Fairy Ring DJR nearby and new agility shortcuts within the Chasm; the fastest way there is the Fairy Ring. ([2025-05-13 13:13](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=793))
- Jmods said Yama is absolutely doable on a one-defence pure and has been in testing. ([2025-05-13 13:41](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=821))
- Gnomonkey plans to stream Yama on release, duo with Saks, attempt a world first solo, then try to put out a guide quickly and do challenges such as a lower-budget solo. ([2025-05-13 39:43](https://youtu.be/Y4S7mK8qoJo?t=2383))
