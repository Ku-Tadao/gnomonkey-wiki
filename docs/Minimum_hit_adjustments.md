# Minimum hit adjustments

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2023-12-22 to 2024-04-17). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | mechanic |
| **Videos** | 2 (first 2023-12-22, latest 2024-04-17) |

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey explains that the game rolls accuracy, then rolls damage from zero to max hit; the change as originally expected would roll from one to max hit, roughly a 5 percent DPS buff on zero-defence targets. ([2024-04-17 11:57](https://youtu.be/Kzc2e6H8i7o?t=717))
- The actual Project Rebalance proposal made damage roll between 1 and the current max hit minus one, so with a max hit of 58 you roll 1 to 57. ([2024-04-17 12:36](https://youtu.be/Kzc2e6H8i7o?t=756))
- Gnomonkey found that the scythe works properly with the change since it uses the true max hit to make its splats, so it only drops 1 max hit but is not hurt as he feared. ([2024-04-17 13:38](https://youtu.be/Kzc2e6H8i7o?t=818))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says with the minimum hit change as proposed you will not one-shot the Fremennik ranger with a scythe in Colosseum. ([2024-04-17 20:00](https://youtu.be/Kzc2e6H8i7o?t=1200))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey notes that after the change the Dragon warhammer will never hit a zero after succeeding on accuracy, and that weapons with fast attack speeds or multiple hit splats benefit a lot. ([2023-12-22 8:05](https://youtu.be/t5mXf8KJztU?t=485))
- Gnomonkey does not like this change as it complicates every weapon for no reason, giving the example that Fang, which rolls accuracy twice, would get a minimum hit of 10 instead of 8, and the Venator bow would lose two max hits on ricochets. ([2024-04-17 13:07](https://youtu.be/Kzc2e6H8i7o?t=787))
- Gnomonkey says Jagex should either make zeros not exist while keeping the normal max hit, or not do the change at all, calling it complicated for no benefit. ([2024-04-17 15:00](https://youtu.be/Kzc2e6H8i7o?t=900))

## Events

- Jagex announced that the minimum hit splat will no longer be a zero but a one on a successful accuracy roll. ([2023-12-22 2:01](https://youtu.be/t5mXf8KJztU?t=121))
