**Minimum hit adjustments** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2023-12-22 and 2024-04-17. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* The game rolls accuracy, then rolls damage from zero to max hit; the change as originally expected would roll from one to max hit, roughly a 5 percent DPS buff on zero-defence targets.[^Kzc2e6H8i7o-717]
* The actual Project Rebalance proposal made damage roll between 1 and the current max hit minus one, so with a max hit of 58 you roll 1 to 57.[^Kzc2e6H8i7o-756]
* Gnomonkey found that the scythe works properly with the change since it uses the true max hit to make its splats, so it only drops 1 max hit but is not hurt as he feared.[^Kzc2e6H8i7o-818]

## Tips

* With the minimum hit change as proposed you will not one-shot the Fremennik ranger with a scythe in Colosseum.[^Kzc2e6H8i7o-1200]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey notes that after the change the Dragon warhammer will never hit a zero after succeeding on accuracy, and that weapons with fast attack speeds or multiple hit splats benefit a lot.[^t5mXf8KJztU-485]
* Gnomonkey does not like this change as it complicates every weapon for no reason, giving the example that Fang, which rolls accuracy twice, would get a minimum hit of 10 instead of 8, and the Venator bow would lose two max hits on ricochets.[^Kzc2e6H8i7o-787]
* Gnomonkey says Jagex should either make zeros not exist while keeping the normal max hit, or not do the change at all, calling it complicated for no benefit.[^Kzc2e6H8i7o-900]

## Events

* Jagex announced that the minimum hit splat will no longer be a zero but a one on a successful accuracy roll.[^t5mXf8KJztU-121]
