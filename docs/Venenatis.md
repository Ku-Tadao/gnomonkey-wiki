**Venenatis** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2022-03-19 and 2022-07-13. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* The main Venenatis safespot has you drag her one tile into multi, then move east a few tiles so you stand in singles.[^uuJsdK3_KRQ-180]

## Stats and numbers

* Venenatis and Callisto have the highest defence levels of any boss in the game at 490 and 440 respectively; Inferno Jad at 480 defence is the only boss that comes close.[^uuJsdK3_KRQ-210]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey rates Venenatis easy F tier because you safe spot it, then AFK and hover over your seed pod, and says the only skill is clicking a seed pod when a red dot appears.[^HkVt4enEesI-1643]
* Gnomonkey says all Wilderness bosses that can be safe-spotted go in F tier, adding that you should not be able to safe spot them.[^HkVt4enEesI-1672]
* Gnomonkey says most people like Venenatis best as it is the lowest Wilderness level of any Wilderness boss in singles, so it is incredibly easy to escape PKers.[^uuJsdK3_KRQ-180]
