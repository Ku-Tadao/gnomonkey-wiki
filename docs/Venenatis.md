# Venenatis

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2022-03-19 to 2022-07-13). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- The main Venenatis safespot has you drag her one tile into multi, then move east a few tiles so you stand in singles. ([2022-07-13 3:00](https://youtu.be/uuJsdK3_KRQ?t=180))

## Stats and numbers

- Venenatis and Callisto have the highest defence levels of any boss in the game at 490 and 440 respectively; Inferno Jad at 480 defence is the only boss that comes close. ([2022-07-13 3:30](https://youtu.be/uuJsdK3_KRQ?t=210))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey rates Venenatis easy F tier because you safe spot it, then AFK and hover over your seed pod, and says the only skill is clicking a seed pod when a red dot appears. ([2022-03-19 27:23](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1643))
- Gnomonkey says all Wilderness bosses that can be safe-spotted go in F tier, adding that you should not be able to safe spot them. ([2022-03-19 27:52](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1672))
- Gnomonkey says most people like Venenatis best as it is the lowest Wilderness level of any Wilderness boss in singles, so it is incredibly easy to escape PKers. ([2022-07-13 3:00](https://youtu.be/uuJsdK3_KRQ?t=180))
