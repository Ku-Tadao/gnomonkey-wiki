**Spawn manipulation** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2024-03-22 and 2024-03-25. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* In the Colosseum, if the player is too close to a spawn point, mobs will not spawn there; a red radius shows the area around the character where spawns are blocked, and grey tiles are spawn points.[^IR0MIzsbFeM-63]
* Colosseum spawns will not spawn next to the player, in a radius around the character model, so standing in the B5 tile area pushes spawns away and guarantees they spawn in a certain area.[^EVOs96kyHSM-271]

## Tips

* Gnomonkey used B5's spawn tile in the Colosseum to manipulate monster spawns into being easier to deal with.[^IR0MIzsbFeM-29]
* B5's tile blocks the majority of spawns and allows rotation to the closed pillar, giving much easier spawns to deal with.[^IR0MIzsbFeM-63]
* The B5 tiles allow you to stack things much better, so starting waves on them is best.[^EVOs96kyHSM-271]
* Gnomonkey defaults to the south pillar tile because a second spawn comes out of the gate and a melee coming out of that gate walks and gets stuck behind the pillar, which does not happen on the north pillar.[^EVOs96kyHSM-303]
* Gnomonkey keeps the other tile only for when permanent Reentry has damaged the south pillar tile very badly, in which case you can move to the other pillar; he says you want to hang out on the south pillar whenever possible.[^EVOs96kyHSM-337]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the south-west pillar is the best because it blocks the most spawns.[^EVOs96kyHSM-820]

## Events

* Gnomonkey credits charts made by his mods Chilled and Hedron and a screenshot by B5 showing wave placements for helping him get the Colosseum kill.[^IR0MIzsbFeM-0]
