# Spawn manipulation

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2024-03-22 to 2024-03-25). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- In the Colosseum, if the player is too close to a spawn point, mobs will not spawn there; a red radius shows the area around the character where spawns are blocked, and grey tiles are spawn points. ([2024-03-22 1:03](https://youtu.be/IR0MIzsbFeM?t=63))
- Colosseum spawns will not spawn next to the player, in a radius around the character model, so standing in the B5 tile area pushes spawns away and guarantees they spawn in a certain area. ([2024-03-25 4:31](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=271))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey used B5's spawn tile in the Colosseum to manipulate monster spawns into being easier to deal with. ([2024-03-22 0:29](https://youtu.be/IR0MIzsbFeM?t=29))
- Gnomonkey says B5's tile blocks the majority of spawns and allows rotation to the closed pillar, giving much easier spawns to deal with. ([2024-03-22 1:03](https://youtu.be/IR0MIzsbFeM?t=63))
- Gnomonkey says the B5 tiles allow you to stack things much better, so starting waves on them is best. ([2024-03-25 4:31](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=271))
- Gnomonkey defaults to the south pillar tile because a second spawn comes out of the gate and a melee coming out of that gate walks and gets stuck behind the pillar, which does not happen on the north pillar. ([2024-03-25 5:03](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=303))
- Gnomonkey keeps the other tile only for when permanent Reentry has damaged the south pillar tile very badly, in which case you can move to the other pillar; he says you want to hang out on the south pillar whenever possible. ([2024-03-25 5:37](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=337))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the south-west pillar is the best because it blocks the most spawns. ([2024-03-25 13:40](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=820))
