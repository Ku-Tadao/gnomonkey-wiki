**Great Olm 3-0 method** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2024-10-27 and 2025-05-01. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* To start the 3-0 mage hand method, Gnomonkey goes to the middle of the room, drops a brew, summons a mage thrall and hammers, forcing Olm to turn his head to the side.[^wxGBi89vE_s-1573]
* Gnomonkey opens each Olm phase by hammering the melee hand, swapping to mage, running to the mage hand and casting, then running along to a tile out of line of sight as Olm turns, which avoids all damage.[^jdvQVwg8niM-2248]
* 3-0 maging is called that because you get three hits in per cycle while taking zero of Olm's attacks.[^jdvQVwg8niM-2280]

## Stats and numbers

* There is a one in four chance that his thrall never hits a number and he splashes, in which case Olm may turn mid-cycle and you tank one hit.[^jdvQVwg8niM-2309]

## Tips

* Olm has a four-tick attack cycle, and the 3-0 cycle is tick-perfect: Olm can never hit you because you can move and attack on the same tick when you get dragged, which is why the weapon must be on accurate.[^wxGBi89vE_s-1600]
* To get into the 3-0 cycle you go to a particular tile and click the instant Olm turns his head, pre-empting it, and that this is probably the hardest thing to learn at the start.[^wxGBi89vE_s-1629]
* With a thrall every thrall hit is good enough to turn Olm's head, so even if you splash Olm does not turn middle and hit you, and the same applies to the melee hand.[^wxGBi89vE_s-1662]
* 3-0 is very simple and always the same, with no cycles to worry about because you are not taking any hits.[^wxGBi89vE_s-1964]
* If you tank a hit during 3-0, Gnomonkey says to throw up a protection prayer and keep going.[^jdvQVwg8niM-2336]
