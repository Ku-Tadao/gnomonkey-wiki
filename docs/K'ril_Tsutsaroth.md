**K'ril Tsutsaroth** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2022-03-19 and 2024-05-12. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Gnomonkey completed a K'ril kill on his first try using the 5-0 method with the wiki's downgraded mage gear, estimating from Ward of Arceuus that the kill took about two and a half minutes.[^_nMz6X90hb0-288]

## Stats and numbers

* Gnomonkey's downgraded K'ril setup has 20 melee defence, 17 range defence and 74 mage defence, which he describes as essentially no defence.[^_nMz6X90hb0-123]
* K'ril Tsutsaroth has 200 mage level and 130 mage defence, so magic accuracy matters a lot against him.[^_nMz6X90hb0-623]

## Tips

* The imps in the K'ril Tsutsaroth room are demons, so Demon Bane can be used on them.[^_nMz6X90hb0-123]
* On the 5-0 method Gnomonkey notes you need full run energy, and a stamina potion helps because the bridge drains some run energy.[^_nMz6X90hb0-270]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says Kril Tsutsaroth has many methods (solo 5-0 with a Twisted bow, DOLO, trolling, resupply alts) and rates him B tier, though even the 5-0 is just clicking the right tiles and then Kril, not exceptional skill.[^HkVt4enEesI-491]
* Gnomonkey says that normally with Shadow you would kill K'ril in about three hits, and that the wiki's downgraded setup is what you would use only if going for a combat achievement.[^_nMz6X90hb0-278]
* Gnomonkey says that against K'ril, where he has 200 mage and 130 mage defence, accuracy starts mattering a lot, so about 20% more accuracy gives a big improvement.[^_nMz6X90hb0-623]

## Events

* Gnomonkey attempts K'ril Tsutsaroth using Demon Bane spells with the OSRS Wiki's worst recommended mage downgrades, choosing it because the wiki suggests a Skull sceptre.[^_nMz6X90hb0-0]
* Gnomonkey's attempt to face-tank K'ril in the wiki's worst mage gear failed after he got K'ril to about half health, so he switched to the 5-0 method.[^_nMz6X90hb0-206]
