## Methods

- Banshees: kill them on the east side of the ground floor of the Slayer Tower, teleport there with a slayer ring and drink a stamina dose at the start as you run around a lot. ([2021-04-27 3:57](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=237))
- Bats: go west of Paterdomus near the Rag and Bone Man, use a digsite teleport, set up the cannon and run east to kill the bats the cannon cannot see. ([2021-04-27 4:10](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=250))
- Bears: south of the Legends' Guild, reached fastest with the quest cape teleport, fairy ring BLR, or a house teleport; the task also counts for Callisto if you prefer to do it there. ([2021-04-27 4:22](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=262))
- Birds: penguins at fairy ring AJS are the most useful task for a bulwark; wear full melee with piety and drop all of them with bulwark specs, hopping worlds if one spec kills them all, and teleporting home to restore special attack. ([2021-04-27 4:41](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=281))
- Cave bugs: go to the dungeon reached via fairy ring AJQ (transcribed as Dorikshin Dungeon) with a light source or you will die, run northwest of the fairy ring for the most spawns and kill only the small ones when possible, as large cave bugs have over 100 HP. ([2021-04-27 5:04](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=304))
- Cave crawlers: in the Fremennik Slayer Dungeon, reached with the slayer ring or fairy ring AJR; their poison does a negligible amount of damage. ([2021-04-27 5:21](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=321))
- Cave slime: in the same dungeon via fairy ring AJQ, just north of the ring, with a light source needed. ([2021-04-27 5:30](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=330))
- Cows: in the eastern Lumbridge cow pen, reached with the nexus teleport to Lumbridge and a run; a cannon placed in the middle of the pen ends the task within 60 seconds. ([2021-04-27 5:40](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=340))
- Crawling hands: only found in the Slayer Tower; Gnomonkey uses a slayer ring to get there and runs around piping them with chinchompas as he goes. ([2021-04-27 5:51](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=351))
- Desert lizards: one of the more annoying tasks, done at fairy ring DLQ with a stack of ice coolers; Gnomonkey places a cannon and throws ice coolers at the lizards it tags, and says waterskins are not needed because the task ends before desert heat matters. ([2021-04-27 5:59](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=359))
- Dogs: kill jackals just south of the lizards at fairy ring DLQ, placing the cannon roughly in one spot and piping any jackals that come near. ([2021-04-27 6:23](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=383))
- Dwarves: kill them in the Taverley Dungeon, teleport with a construction cape to the Taverley house portal or a redirected house tab, and drop the cannon in the centre of the tables. ([2021-04-27 6:34](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=394))
- Goblins: teleport to Lumbridge with the house nexus and run east; the cannon position does not matter and the task ends extremely quickly. ([2021-04-27 6:45](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=405))
- Ghosts: kill them in the Catacombs of Kourend, teleporting with the Xeric's talisman or the Great Kourend teleport; the best spot is northeast of the entrance, and you can dump bulwark specs before piping them all. ([2021-04-27 6:54](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=414))
- Ice fiends: teleport with Lassar teleport tabs or to the Monastery and run north, then put the cannon on one tile. ([2021-04-27 7:05](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=425))
- Kalphites: go to the Kalphite Slayer cave, reached with Desert amulet 4 directly, or a glory to Al Kharid and a short run east after the Shanty Pass; place the cannon in the centre of the small Kalphites. ([2021-04-27 7:13](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=433))
- Minotaurs: in the Stronghold of Security, teleported to with the skull sceptre; go one south through the gates, then east a little, and a cannon in the centre of the room finishes the task in seconds. ([2021-04-27 7:48](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=468))
- Monkeys: take a gnome seed pod to a glider, glide to Ape Atoll, go through the trapdoor and enter the first hole with a light source, stack monkeys against a wall and throw chinchompas on a medium fuse; without Monkey Madness II you can cannon at fairy ring CKR. ([2021-04-27 7:59](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=479))
- Rats: in the Varrock sewers, teleport with the nexus and run northeast to the manhole, drop the cannon and bulwark specs work there; Gnomonkey calls it the fastest task Turael assigns by far. ([2021-04-27 8:23](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=503))
- Scorpions: in the Al Kharid mine, teleport to the Duel Arena and run north, cannon on one spot; you can optionally kill Scorpia for the task. ([2021-04-27 8:37](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=517))
- Skeletons: bring a rope, teleport to the digsite and use the rope on the hole to go down, then cannon; you can optionally kill Vet'ion for this task. ([2021-04-27 8:47](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=527))
- Spiders: near the HAM hideout, teleport to Lumbridge with the nexus and run west with a cannon; a very fast task, and you can optionally kill Sarachnis or Venenatis for it. ([2021-04-27 9:15](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=555))
- Wolves: use a glider (via seed pod or ring of dueling to Duel Arena) to White Wolf Mountain and place the cannon where it hits all the wolves. ([2021-04-27 9:27](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=567))
- Zombies: use an ectophial and run west to the farm, cannon and kill everything; you can optionally kill Vorkath for this task. ([2021-04-27 9:36](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=576))
- Turael point boosting means doing nine tasks with Turael, the easiest Slayer master, and then every tenth task with your best possible master to boost points. ([2025-08-28 2:22](https://youtu.be/BWnqrytMj8I?t=142))
- Turael cancelling means asking Turael for a task you do not like so he resets it with one of his own, which resets your streak. ([2025-08-28 2:22](https://youtu.be/BWnqrytMj8I?t=142))
- Gnomonkey's Turael block list is lizards, dogs and skeletons plus (if you have the slots) bats, dwarves and wolves, and then Kalphite if you plan to cancel tasks. ([2025-08-28 2:53](https://youtu.be/BWnqrytMj8I?t=173))

## Stats and numbers

- Turael can cancel a Slayer task for no Slayer point cost and give you one of his own. ([2021-04-27 0:00](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8))
- Cancelling a task with Turael resets your task streak and requires you to complete four tasks before you can start gaining points again. ([2021-04-27 0:26](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=26))
- Turael assigns 25 different tasks. ([2021-04-27 3:24](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=204))
- Kalphite is the lowest-weight Turael task and a fast, multi task, so Gnomonkey would otherwise leave it unblocked, but blocking lets you cancel a huge Kalphite task from another master, such as 180 Kalphite. ([2025-08-28 5:17](https://youtu.be/BWnqrytMj8I?t=317))

## Tips

- Many of Turael's tasks count for bosses, such as spiders for Sarachnis or Venenatis, or zombies for Vorkath, and the small amount he assigns can make him a fun Slayer boss master for breaking up the grind. ([2021-04-27 0:55](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=55))
- With a Slayer cape, contact Turael before leaving your completed task, as there is a chance of getting a back-to-back task while boosting. ([2021-04-27 3:24](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=204))
- Gnomonkey says that if you do not have an ornate pool and fairy ring in your house, it is probably better to just go to Lumbridge chickens for the Birds task. ([2021-04-27 4:54](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=294))
- Optionally you can cannon sulphur lizards in Konar's cave for Desert lizards, which is slower but much more AFK and less annoying. ([2021-04-27 5:59](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=359))
- Turael cannot cancel Kalphite tasks he assigns himself, so if a better Slayer master gives them while you are cancelling you have to do the task; Gnomonkey says if cancelling for a long time it is probably best to block Kalphites. ([2021-04-27 7:27](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=447))
- For Kalphites you could also kill the Kalphite Queen for the task if you prefer. ([2021-04-27 7:27](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=447))
- You can also kill tortured gorillas for the Monkeys task if you prefer. ([2021-04-27 8:20](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=500))
- Gnomonkey says to block Kalphite on Turael if you cancel tasks: Turael refuses to cancel a task he can give you, but if you block it he forgets it and will skip it, so you need not block Kalphite on other masters. ([2025-08-28 5:17](https://youtu.be/BWnqrytMj8I?t=317))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says Turael cancelling is a great option after 99 Slayer when looking for specific tasks such as Slayer bosses, shamans (so you can use a slayer helmet in the Chambers of Xeric) or Jad/Inferno tasks. ([2021-04-27 0:26](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=26))
- Gnomonkey does not recommend cancelling with Turael before 99 Slayer, saying it is much more efficient to skip tasks with points than to regear to do Turael tasks until you get good ones. ([2021-04-27 0:26](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=26))
- Gnomonkey calls Sourhogs one of the worst tasks, as they are single combat with a decent health pool and so fairly slow; he gets there with a chronicle and the rock shortcut and drops a cannon. ([2021-04-27 8:58](https://youtu.be/FvML4QHXBh8?t=538))
- Gnomonkey says to block lizards on Turael because they have 50-something health, require ice coolers or the reptile freezer, and are slow; the alternative is cannoning sulfur lizards in Mount Karuulm. ([2025-08-28 2:53](https://youtu.be/BWnqrytMj8I?t=173))
- Gnomonkey says bats are the Turael block he would leave unblocked if short on slots, since they are spread out near the Dig Site and need long range, but are only barely cannonable and slow. ([2025-08-28 3:14](https://youtu.be/BWnqrytMj8I?t=194))
- Gnomonkey says dogs on Turael are very slow because few spawns exist (best near the Agility Pyramid), they have a lot of health and waste time attacking you, so blocking is great. ([2025-08-28 3:53](https://youtu.be/BWnqrytMj8I?t=233))
- Gnomonkey says dwarves (killed in White Wolf Tunnel with about 30 health) and wolves are the least impactful Turael blocks and can be left unblocked if short on slots; wolves are multi so a Venator bow kills them fairly quickly. ([2025-08-28 4:20](https://youtu.be/BWnqrytMj8I?t=260))
- Gnomonkey says skeletons on Turael are extremely slow even with a method of Padawa-teleporting to Edgeville Dungeon, cannoning and running through, so they are a great block. ([2025-08-28 4:49](https://youtu.be/BWnqrytMj8I?t=289))
