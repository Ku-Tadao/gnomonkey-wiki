# Corner trapping

## Methods

- Gnomonkey explains that a monster's brain really likes going east or west first towards you, so when a melee is desperately trying to go east through a pillar to hit you, it just sits there; that is a corner trap. ([2022-07-22 7:55](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=475))
- Gnomonkey explains that a monster's southwest tile is its 'brain' (this can be shown with NPC indicators): whenever the southwest tile can see you but the rest of the monster is stuck, it is safe-spotted. ([2024-01-17 1:52](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=112))
- A corner trap in the Inferno is when a monster is right up on the corner of a safe spot and has to move east or west towards you before it can move north or south; if it is trying to go east into a pillar it is stuck. ([2024-01-17 2:24](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=144))
- Gnomonkey says corner traps work on a line system one tile out from the pillar rather than on exact tiles, so he does not mark them. ([2024-06-13 11:20](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=680))
- Gnomonkey explains that for NPCs without smart pathing the brain is their south-west tile, and OSRS paths east or west before north or south and refuses to path north if the only other option is east or west, so a monster whose south-west tile is blocked cannot reach you. ([2024-06-13 17:37](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1057))
- A corner trap is an engine quirk: an NPC always moves west or east before moving south or north, so if you stand against a pillar corner the monster keeps trying to go west or east into the pillar and cannot reach you. ([2026-07-13 18:29](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=1109))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says any monster with a ranged attack or projectile (Jads, rangers, blobs, mages) can melee you on the corners, while melee monsters cannot because they only use melee. ([2024-06-13 51:38](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=3098))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says corner trap spots exist on several sides of a pillar and should be memorized so they can save you in later waves. ([2022-07-22 7:55](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=475))
- Gnomonkey says if you stand on the bat safe spot when a melee digs, you can corner safe spot, which becomes important later. ([2022-07-22 9:50](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=590))
- Gnomonkey says there is no single corner trap tile because a whole row of tiles can be used, so do not be afraid to use any of them. ([2022-07-22 13:53](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=833))
- Gnomonkey says anytime a melee digs against the side of a pillar you can pull it down to the corner trap by default. ([2022-07-22 22:19](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1339))
- Gnomonkey says you can also pull a corner-trapped melee around the pillar and it will stay stuck, and sometimes you want to rotate to the other side while corner trapping to speed things up. ([2022-07-22 42:21](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2541))
- Gnomonkey says to be proactive with melees by always having a plan for where to stand when it digs, since there is no guarantee you kill something in time. ([2022-07-22 43:23](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2603))
- Bats have a short range, so you can safe spot them from one tile behind the pillar. ([2024-01-17 2:50](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=170))
- Against a melee, the lines on the sides of the pillar work as corner trap tiles, three tiles on each side, and you can attack the melee freely from them. ([2024-01-17 10:23](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=623))
- Gnomonkey says the motion of casting at nibblers from one tile then moving to a certain tile corner traps any mob in a certain position, and is almost always the correct plan; any time he sees something there, he clicks to that tile without thinking. ([2024-01-17 13:16](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=796))
- To corner trap a digging melee you have to be touching its corner, otherwise it can path in. ([2024-06-13 19:30](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1170))
- Gnomonkey says people only corner trap melees but you can corner trap every single mob, including mages and blobs, and that a monster against a pillar cannot line of sight through it. ([2024-06-13 26:38](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1598))
- Gnomonkey says monsters work the same as pillars for corner trapping, so when attacking something you want to stand one tile out from it as if it were a pillar. ([2024-06-13 37:10](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2230))
- Using a monster as a pillar does not work against melees on the north side of the pillar, but you can still corner trap melees on the pillars themselves. ([2024-06-13 50:00](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=3000))
- Corner trapping works on all Inferno mobs, so melees can come out a few tiles and you can still attack; Gnomonkey says it solves the majority of waves. ([2026-07-13 19:11](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=1151))
- Gnomonkey says to stand on the lines along the pillar in the right orientation to corner trap, and that monsters can also be used as pillars: stand on the bat tile of a mager to corner trap a melee. ([2026-07-13 19:44](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=1184))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says you can corner trap anything and many learners let a corner trap slip away and then tank a melee and mage at once. ([2026-07-13 54:37](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=3277))
