**Corner trapping** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 4 videos between 2022-07-22 and 2026-07-13. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* A monster's brain really likes going east or west first towards you, so when a melee is desperately trying to go east through a pillar to hit you, it just sits there; that is a corner trap.[^6lRmbYCYtME-475]
* A monster's southwest tile is its 'brain' (this can be shown with NPC indicators): whenever the southwest tile can see you but the rest of the monster is stuck, it is safe-spotted.[^h6LxupiygPA-112]
* A corner trap in the Inferno is when a monster is right up on the corner of a safe spot and has to move east or west towards you before it can move north or south; if it is trying to go east into a pillar it is stuck.[^h6LxupiygPA-144]
* Corner traps work on a line system one tile out from the pillar rather than on exact tiles, so he does not mark them.[^XMTe9TYbDls-680]
* For NPCs without smart pathing the brain is their south-west tile, and OSRS paths east or west before north or south and refuses to path north if the only other option is east or west, so a monster whose south-west tile is blocked cannot reach you.[^XMTe9TYbDls-1057]
* A corner trap is an engine quirk: an NPC always moves west or east before moving south or north, so if you stand against a pillar corner the monster keeps trying to go west or east into the pillar and cannot reach you.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1109]

## Stats and numbers

* Any monster with a ranged attack or projectile (Jads, rangers, blobs, mages) can melee you on the corners, while melee monsters cannot because they only use melee.[^XMTe9TYbDls-3098]

## Tips

* Corner trap spots exist on several sides of a pillar and should be memorized so they can save you in later waves.[^6lRmbYCYtME-475]
* If you stand on the bat safe spot when a melee digs, you can corner safe spot, which becomes important later.[^6lRmbYCYtME-590]
* There is no single corner trap tile because a whole row of tiles can be used, so do not be afraid to use any of them.[^6lRmbYCYtME-833]
* Anytime a melee digs against the side of a pillar you can pull it down to the corner trap by default.[^6lRmbYCYtME-1339]
* You can also pull a corner-trapped melee around the pillar and it will stay stuck, and sometimes you want to rotate to the other side while corner trapping to speed things up.[^6lRmbYCYtME-2541]
* To be proactive with melees by always having a plan for where to stand when it digs, since there is no guarantee you kill something in time.[^6lRmbYCYtME-2603]
* Bats have a short range, so you can safe spot them from one tile behind the pillar.[^h6LxupiygPA-170]
* Against a melee, the lines on the sides of the pillar work as corner trap tiles, three tiles on each side, and you can attack the melee freely from them.[^h6LxupiygPA-623]
* The motion of casting at nibblers from one tile then moving to a certain tile corner traps any mob in a certain position, and is almost always the correct plan; any time he sees something there, he clicks to that tile without thinking.[^h6LxupiygPA-796]
* To corner trap a digging melee you have to be touching its corner, otherwise it can path in.[^XMTe9TYbDls-1170]
* People only corner trap melees but you can corner trap every single mob, including mages and blobs, and that a monster against a pillar cannot line of sight through it.[^XMTe9TYbDls-1598]
* Monsters work the same as pillars for corner trapping, so when attacking something you want to stand one tile out from it as if it were a pillar.[^XMTe9TYbDls-2230]
* Using a monster as a pillar does not work against melees on the north side of the pillar, but you can still corner trap melees on the pillars themselves.[^XMTe9TYbDls-3000]
* Corner trapping works on all Inferno mobs, so melees can come out a few tiles and you can still attack; Gnomonkey says it solves the majority of waves.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1151]
* To stand on the lines along the pillar in the right orientation to corner trap, and that monsters can also be used as pillars: stand on the bat tile of a mager to corner trap a melee.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-1184]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says you can corner trap anything and many learners let a corner trap slip away and then tank a melee and mage at once.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-3277]
