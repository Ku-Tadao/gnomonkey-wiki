# Sunfire splinter

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2025-01-30 to 2025-10-01). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | item |
| **Videos** | 2 (first 2025-01-30, latest 2025-10-01) |

## Stats and numbers

- At about three Colosseum kills an hour, Gnomonkey calculates roughly 11 million of profit, with half of it coming from splinters (quivers converted to 7.6 million worth of splinters plus about 6,000 splinters worth 3.7 million). ([2025-01-30 11:59](https://youtu.be/kkmUE6oQxy4?t=719))
- Colosseum wave 1 drops a guaranteed 80 Sunfire splinters and takes about 15 seconds to clear, for example by going in with a bulwark, killing the wave, ticking the splinters and leaving. ([2025-10-01 1:25](https://youtu.be/ZB3ldcsIivg?t=85))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the Sunfire splinter currency is really the only thing that has kept the Colosseum profitable. ([2025-01-30 11:59](https://youtu.be/kkmUE6oQxy4?t=719))
- Gnomonkey asks Jagex to bring back a currency like the one that worked great for the Colosseum so that Mokha stays profitable even when drops are poor, saying it could be a venge upgrade, prayer upgrade or something that charges an item. ([2025-01-30 25:45](https://youtu.be/kkmUE6oQxy4?t=1545))
- Gnomonkey says wave 1 Colosseum farming has been a good money-making method for a long time with basically no requirements, and bots love it; he says bots are still doing it even with splinter prices very low. ([2025-10-01 1:25](https://youtu.be/ZB3ldcsIivg?t=85))
- Gnomonkey concludes the main reason Sunfire splinters dropped in value is wave 1 Colosseum farm bots, and suggests Jagex could just patch that out. ([2025-10-01 1:58](https://youtu.be/ZB3ldcsIivg?t=118))
