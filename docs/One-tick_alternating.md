# One-tick alternating

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2024-10-03 to 2025-12-07). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- One-tick alternating means swapping prayers every game tick so you take no damage from a blob, and you can do it even when the blob and a ranger are far away; it works always. ([2025-12-07 8:37](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=517))
- Wait for the ranger's attack animation to start, then swap prayers every game tick, which dodges both the ranger and the blob. ([2025-12-07 27:22](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=1642))
- Gnomonkey's tip for keeping a one-tick alternate is to keep both prayers lit at the same time and click as soon as the last prayer unlights so you never lose timing. ([2025-12-07 37:29](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=2249))
- For a pillar stack, start in the middle of the pillar, click to the corner, watch the animation of the NPC in the back and start alternating. ([2025-12-07 1:13:14](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=4394))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says one-tick alternating prayers is a key skill to learn because it solves many waves and is how you flick a blob into any other mob besides a melee. ([2024-10-03 8:51](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=531))
- To keep your alternating cycle, Gnomonkey says to look at the two prayer lights; as soon as the light turns off on the other prayer, click the next one. ([2024-10-03 31:53](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=1913))
- Gnomonkey says you can alternate against a ranger or mage in the Inferno and that you will also do this a lot in the Colosseum. ([2024-10-03 31:53](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=1913))
- Gnomonkey says to start one-tick alternating on the first tick of the animation of the attacker that is hitting you. ([2024-10-03 34:00](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=2040))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says to learn alternating early rather than avoid it because by wave 58 you may be forced to do it. ([2025-12-07 28:19](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=1699))
- Gnomonkey says two-tick alternating is extremely useful when speedrunning but adds confusion for normal runs because the blob must be on the correct tick; one-tick alternating works always, so learn that for a first cape. ([2025-12-07 53:24](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=3204))
- Gnomonkey says he alternated three waves in a row by wave 56, which is why you need to learn it. ([2025-12-07 1:06:17](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=3977))
