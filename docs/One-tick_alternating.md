**One-tick alternating** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2024-10-03 and 2025-12-07. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* One-tick alternating means swapping prayers every game tick so you take no damage from a blob, and you can do it even when the blob and a ranger are far away; it works always.[^2xviK0wGI-o-517]
* Wait for the ranger's attack animation to start, then swap prayers every game tick, which dodges both the ranger and the blob.[^2xviK0wGI-o-1642]
* Gnomonkey's tip for keeping a one-tick alternate is to keep both prayers lit at the same time and click as soon as the last prayer unlights so you never lose timing.[^2xviK0wGI-o-2249]
* For a pillar stack, start in the middle of the pillar, click to the corner, watch the animation of the NPC in the back and start alternating.[^2xviK0wGI-o-4394]

## Tips

* One-tick alternating prayers is a key skill to learn because it solves many waves and is how you flick a blob into any other mob besides a melee.[^6trKOSUr4EM-531]
* To keep your alternating cycle, Gnomonkey says to look at the two prayer lights; as soon as the light turns off on the other prayer, click the next one.[^6trKOSUr4EM-1913]
* You can alternate against a ranger or mage in the Inferno and that you will also do this a lot in the Colosseum.[^6trKOSUr4EM-1913]
* To start one-tick alternating on the first tick of the animation of the attacker that is hitting you.[^6trKOSUr4EM-2040]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says to learn alternating early rather than avoid it because by wave 58 you may be forced to do it.[^2xviK0wGI-o-1699]
* Gnomonkey says two-tick alternating is extremely useful when speedrunning but adds confusion for normal runs because the blob must be on the correct tick; one-tick alternating works always, so learn that for a first cape.[^2xviK0wGI-o-3204]
* Gnomonkey says he alternated three waves in a row by wave 56, which is why you need to learn it.[^2xviK0wGI-o-3977]
