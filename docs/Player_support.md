**Player support** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2025-01-17 and 2026-03-10. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Tips

* Gnomonkey wants status updates about server problems and updates posted in one obvious place on every platform (in game, Twitter, Reddit), instead of only a single line from a Jagex moderator in Discord, which also enables photoshopped fake mod quotes.[^1cRMBXNb4rc-1197]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey wants concrete details on player support improvements, such as hiring more people or reducing response time to a day or two, since support has been one of the worst aspects of Old School for years.[^cGiq6OwLovw-394]
* Gnomonkey says Old School's support system is effectively through Twitter and Reddit, and that you may need over 2,000 upvotes on Reddit to be heard.[^cGiq6OwLovw-724]
* Gnomonkey says Jagex player support has been replaced with an AI that responds, which is arguably better than no response, but support does not feel any better and lost hardcore accounts or items from bugs and disconnects are generally not recovered.[^1cRMBXNb4rc-268]
* Gnomonkey says other games like WoW will return your items if you are hacked, whereas Jagex will not, and even simple requests like transferring membership to another account can take a couple of weeks.[^1cRMBXNb4rc-297]
* Gnomonkey says even content creators do not get a hardcore back after a server disconnect, and he has seen more posts about false bans, though more get appealed.[^1cRMBXNb4rc-1154]
* Gnomonkey says player support was replaced with an AI chatbot while PvM update quality remained as buggy as ever, so the previous roadmap does not justify the increase.[^1cRMBXNb4rc-1285]

## Events

* The apology said improved player support should not require additional charges, that changes to baseline support are already in progress, and that Jagex hopes to talk more about it soon.[^cGiq6OwLovw-365]
* The large streamer Odablock was griefed on stream by someone abusing bugs to kill him, messaged moderators for days, and no action was taken.[^cGiq6OwLovw-724]
* Gnomonkey recounts that in RuneScape 3 he needed his membership transferred and it took two weeks to get a response, with a passive-aggressive reply.[^cGiq6OwLovw-758]
