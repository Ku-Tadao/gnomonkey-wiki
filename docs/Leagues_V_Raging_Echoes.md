# Leagues V: Raging Echoes

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 6 videos (2024-11-25 to 2024-12-20). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Gnomonkey says that with the Leagues 5 tier six ranged relic and a ranged weapon equipped, all spells are 100% accurate, including Ice Barrage. ([2024-12-20 4:53](https://youtu.be/m8m-6TBJqhQ?t=293))

## Stats and numbers

- Leagues 5 gives massively buffed XP rates, relics that give large buffs to skills, utility and combat, and a unique Echo boss fight in each region with very powerful item drops. ([2024-11-25 0:00](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY))
- In Leagues 5 every player starts with Misthalin and Karamja unlocked, neither of which has an Echo boss; completing tasks then lets you choose a first, second and third region, and the order matters because a first region without early-game boosts can make the start much harder. ([2024-11-25 0:29](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=29))
- In Leagues 5 each raid can drop a mega rare note instead of the usual Scythe, Twisted bow or Shadow, so every raid has access to every mega rare item and you are not locked to one raid for a build. ([2024-11-25 1:04](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=64))
- In Leagues 5 raid purples drop for every person in the team if anyone rolls one, so group raids will farm purples extremely quickly. ([2024-11-25 1:04](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=64))
- In Leagues 5 partial drops that normally need combining, such as the Ultor ring, Rancor and Avernic components, drop as the completed item, so for example choosing Morytania means you do not need Asgarnia to finish an Avernic defender. ([2024-11-25 1:04](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=64))
- Leagues 5 melee mastery tier 1 gives a 25% chance to roll damage twice and take the higher roll, which Gnomonkey says is a quite big average DPS increase. ([2024-11-25 9:29](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=569))
- Leagues 5 melee mastery tier 2 gives a 10% chance to generate an echo hit dealing 50% of your initial hit; Gnomonkey says this is less DPS than tier 1 but can generate massive hits. ([2024-11-25 9:29](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=569))
- Leagues 5 melee mastery tier 3 gives 80% attack speed rounded down, strengthening early four-tick weapons such as the Rune scimitar. ([2024-11-25 10:04](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=604))
- Leagues 5 melee mastery tier 4 gives a 5% chance to heal 40% of damage dealt, which Gnomonkey equates to about one-third of a permanently attached Blood fury but with larger, rarer heals. ([2024-11-25 10:04](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=604))
- Leagues 5 melee mastery tier 5 gives 50% attack speed rounded down, producing a two-tick Scythe and a three-tick Dharok's set. ([2024-11-25 10:28](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=628))
- Leagues 5 melee mastery tier 6 raises echo hits to a 20% chance and lets them chain off each other up to eight times, a 1 in 80,000 chance of a full chain; Gnomonkey says it is a small DPS increase but allows crazy RNG boss kill times. ([2024-11-25 10:31](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=631))
- Leagues 5 ranged mastery tier 1 sets a minimum damage roll of 30% of your max hit, which Gnomonkey says is incredibly good with fast weapons. ([2024-11-25 10:58](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=658))
- Leagues 5 ranged mastery tier 2 increases the max hit of each subsequent ranged hit by 5%, stacking up to 20% and then resetting. ([2024-11-25 10:58](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=658))
- Leagues 5 ranged mastery tier 3 gives faster attack speed, which reduces two-tick ranged weapons to one tick, so you can have a one-tick Toxic blowpipe within seconds of starting the game. ([2024-11-25 10:58](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=658))
- Leagues 5 ranged mastery tier 4 heals 5 hit points on every fifth ranged hit, which Gnomonkey notes is reliable HP usable for tick eating if you hit. ([2024-11-25 11:27](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=687))
- Leagues 5 ranged mastery tier 5 gives the same attack speed boost as melee, producing a two-tick Twisted bow and two-tick Zaryte crossbow. ([2024-11-25 11:27](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=687))
- Leagues 5 ranged mastery tier 6 means ranged attacks no longer miss, which Gnomonkey says ignores defence such as Bloat's 800 ranged defence and Sol Heredit's 825. ([2024-11-25 11:27](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=687))
- Leagues 5 magic mastery tier 1 makes hits that roll above 90% of max hit hit 50% harder, which Gnomonkey says rarely procs; tier 2 increases magic max hit by 5% per tick between attacks, which he calls super bad. ([2024-11-25 12:01](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=721))
- Leagues 5 magic mastery tier 3 gives faster attack speed; tier 4 heals 10% of damage dealt when rolling over 90% of max hit, which Gnomonkey says heals far more than other styles and ideally stacks with Blood barrage; tier 5 is a two-tick Shadow and Barrage. ([2024-11-25 12:30](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=750))
- Leagues 5 magic mastery tier 6 increases max hit by 1% per 100 hit points remaining on the target, up to 10%, and you max if the target is below your weapon's max hit. ([2024-11-25 12:30](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=750))
- Gnomonkey notes that onyx bolt (e) procs in Leagues 5 boost damage and heal, which is better than the pure healing he first assumed. ([2024-12-09 5:30](https://youtu.be/7VKipTJkybM?t=330))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey notes that with the Devil's element you can cast a 100 max hit Fire Surge at an Ice demon in Leagues 5. ([2024-11-25 12:57](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=777))
- Gnomonkey says all the Echo items and relics are in the Gearscape DPS calculator, which can also generate setups using only items in your bank or only items available in your region. ([2024-11-25 18:55](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=1135))
- In Leagues 5 Gnomonkey says he does the Fight Caves for another combat mastery as an alternative to repeatedly attempting the Colosseum. ([2024-11-28 2:30](https://youtu.be/M0Y3Hj3YIGw?t=150))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says this guide is his perspective on having fun rather than a min-maxing spreadsheet, and that choosing a non-meta pick to have more fun elsewhere is never a bad idea. ([2024-11-25 0:00](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY))
- Gnomonkey considers melee a very solid Leagues 5 choice but ranged far stronger. ([2024-11-25 10:31](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=631))
- Gnomonkey considers ranged stupidly busted this Leagues because of the combat masteries. ([2024-11-25 11:27](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=687))
- Gnomonkey considers magic the weakest build in Leagues 5, saying it is so weak compared to the magic of last Leagues that he suspects Jagex saw its high pick rate and wanted to squash it. ([2024-11-25 12:01](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=721))
- Gnomonkey considers the harpoon relic (tier 1, buffing Fishing and Hunter) absurdly better than the mining or woodcutting options, with Hunter perks of faster chinchompas and traps that never fail, especially good with the Hunter Guild in Varlamore. ([2024-11-25 13:15](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=795))
- Gnomonkey says the Leagues 5 woodcutting relic's extra Firemaking effect of never failing to light a log is bafflingly bad, while AFK fishing from the fishing relic will be a great food source. ([2024-11-25 13:15](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=795))
- Gnomonkey picks the Forager relic at tier 2 because buffing Herblore is always solid for ironman modes, saving secondaries 90% of the time and making four-dose potions so he never needs a herb run during the league. ([2024-11-25 13:54](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=834))
- Gnomonkey considers the tier 2 Agility relic nice for passive training but not needed since he already has Sepulchre, and calls Dodgy Deals for thieving GP and blood shards a clear second choice, with Tirannwn-style AFK thieving of Tizar-type guards possible if you have ice gloves. ([2024-11-25 14:29](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=869))
- Gnomonkey says the Clue Compass is the clear best tier 3 relic because its clue-location teleports cover the bank and fairy ring options of the other relics, for example teleporting to a clue stash to bank. ([2024-11-25 14:53](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=893))
- Gnomonkey picks Reloaded at tier 4 to take Dodgy Deals, with thieving options in his region of Vyres for blood shards, Wealthy citizens for GP, and Tizar with ice gloves from Asgarnia for AFK training. ([2024-11-25 15:30](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=930))
- Gnomonkey says the tier 4 Golden God relic for Prayer training only seems exceptional with a house altar or the Wilderness, and calls Equilibrium, buffed to 20% when the trained skill is your lowest, weak and boring. ([2024-11-25 15:30](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=930))
- Gnomonkey picks the Slayer Master relic at tier 5 because being on task all the time, including boss tasks and all of God Wars Dungeon, is a clear choice for PvM; he says Production Master is insanely strong for money from gem stalls and Treasure Arbiter is popular for clues. ([2024-11-25 15:59](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=959))
- Gnomonkey says tier 6 Recall remembers your stats before locking in, useful for abusing specs on bosses and recalling for free spec energy or teleporting to Barrows, while Banker's Note suits skillers; he took the note last Leagues and prefers something different this time. ([2024-11-25 16:36](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=996))
- Gnomonkey picks Grimoire at tier 7 (a new tier this Leagues) because using all offensive prayers and swapping spellbooks gives huge DPS and utility; he says Pocket Kingdom suits bulk resource collection and Overgrown suits players with very limited time. ([2024-11-25 16:58](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=1018))
- Gnomonkey considers the tier 8 relics disappointing: Last Stand's ridiculous 3 minute cooldown makes it a non-pick, and the Guardian Horn is just a super thrall for a minor DPS gain, worse than last Leagues since its two-tick resummon tech was patched out. ([2024-11-25 17:42](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=1062))
- Gnomonkey considers the special attack relic by far the best tier 8 choice, and says it changed his region pick from the Elf lands to Asgarnia since his only spec options would otherwise be Burning claws and Bone dagger. ([2024-11-25 18:24](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=1104))
- Gnomonkey considers Leagues 5 one of the most well-rounded Leagues to date, with something for skillers chasing the Dragon cup, players wanting a challenge, and those wanting to try late-game content. ([2024-11-25 18:39](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=1119))
- Gnomonkey considers it strange that magic was shafted while ranged dominates the Leagues 5 combat masteries, and says more thought on the combat relics would have helped; he also notes the Echo bosses were downplayed on stream. ([2024-11-25 21:00](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY?t=1260))
- Gnomonkey says he had been having a blast so far in Leagues 5 and had only just started. ([2024-11-28 30:30](https://youtu.be/M0Y3Hj3YIGw?t=1830))
- Gnomonkey describes the 1 HP Dharok bomb build as 'a disgusting build' and says he could not beat Dharok in the tunnels so he joined him. ([2024-11-30 0:04](https://youtu.be/yJCJ-7T_oxQ?t=4))
- Gnomonkey says that if you are scared of trying Inferno even in Leagues 5 you should just do it, because it is easy and there is no downside to trying. ([2024-12-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/7VKipTJkybM))
- He says ranged in Leagues 5 heals about five times more than melee does when shooting, and speculates the designers knew Blood fury existed and decided ranged did not need more. ([2024-12-09 3:57](https://youtu.be/7VKipTJkybM?t=237))
- Gnomonkey thinks the Leagues 5 ranged build is enjoyable precisely up until tier six, and expects it to be stupid at tier six. ([2024-12-09 5:58](https://youtu.be/7VKipTJkybM?t=358))
- Gnomonkey says he thinks Leagues 5 was the best Leagues yet and that Jagex should continue to refine and do more PvM-related Leagues events. ([2024-12-20 0:00](https://youtu.be/m8m-6TBJqhQ))
- Gnomonkey says he had not unlocked his final combat mastery, which he thinks hurt his 1000 invocation attempt. ([2024-12-20 0:00](https://youtu.be/m8m-6TBJqhQ))
- Chat suggests making an account built around total 1000 invocation raids with no combat mastery; Gnomonkey says he would be bored doing it and that it would mostly be banker's note plus redemption-style tricks. ([2024-12-20 7:17](https://youtu.be/m8m-6TBJqhQ?t=437))
- Gnomonkey says the melee relic would be strong on the Warden's core but not on phase 2. ([2024-12-20 15:34](https://youtu.be/m8m-6TBJqhQ?t=934))

## Events

- Leagues 5 (Raging Echoes) was about to launch a few days after this video (the release date linked in the description is November 27, 2024); Leagues is a temporary ironman-locked game mode where players are restricted to certain areas and unlock more by completing tasks. ([2024-11-25 0:00](https://youtu.be/ZVceO0RWJuY))
- This video covers a roughly 16-hour stream at the start of Leagues 5 where Gnomonkey was just beginning his league, and he planned to stream it for a while and make a second ranged account. ([2024-11-28 0:00](https://youtu.be/M0Y3Hj3YIGw))
- Gnomonkey says he was having a blast in Leagues 5 doing funny melee builds, and planned to do a ranged build after the weekend, with progress on his stream. ([2024-11-30 0:00](https://youtu.be/yJCJ-7T_oxQ))
- Gnomonkey switched his final Leagues 5 region to Tirannwn (the Elf lands) because he saw how horrible the Echo Cerberus fight was and did not want to be stuck somewhere with a bad boss. ([2024-12-01 0:00](https://youtu.be/lk-ZaW-SqIc))
- Gnomonkey's next goal after this video was to push for the Last Stand relic and go for a 1000+ damage Dharok bomb hit on Echo Sol. ([2024-12-01 0:00](https://youtu.be/lk-ZaW-SqIc))
- Gnomonkey did the Leagues 5 Inferno on the range machine build right before tier 6 (T6) Mastery, because he thinks the build gets stale once you can one-shot everything. ([2024-12-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/7VKipTJkybM))
- After finishing the range build and getting a 1000+ hit with Dragon hunter lance, Gnomonkey says he will return to the Hardcore Ironman he has been AFKing on the side. ([2024-12-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/7VKipTJkybM))
