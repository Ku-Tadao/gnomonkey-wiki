**Blood Barrage** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 5 videos between 2021-05-21 and 2026-06-25. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Phantom heal: if you are at least 8 tiles away when you cast Blood Barrage (range 10, same as a twisted bow) and the cast will kill the target, you can send another barrage right before it dies and get the healing for both casts.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1361]
* Blood Barrage heals for damage you would have dealt even if the monster has low HP, so even 15 HP blobs can give massive healing.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1452]
* A blob in the phantom position is worth about 40 health, a bat about 15 (20 to 25 with phantom), and a ranger about 30.[^FfNQI63ZKys-635]
* Ice barrage is more accurate and has one higher max hit, but that blood barrage is faster overall because it keeps nibblers clumped together.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-2170]
* Zuriel's staff increases blood barrage efficiency by 50% and that blood barrage heals the same amount even if it is prayed against.[^lJexGv6dxu8-2650]

## Tips

* You can always Blood Barrage your health back, so tanking some damage to kill nibblers is fine; the final wave priority is to reach 99 health before the next wave using Blood Barrage or special attacks.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1035]
* You can get knocked out extremely quickly in the Inferno, so having maximum health on tough waves is a must.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1142]
* Phantom healing only works with manual casting; autocast never grants a phantom heal.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1361]
* The Phantom technique: if you kill something from a certain range or further with a cast, you can cast again at its corpse for extra healing, so you effectively get two Blood Barrages on it instead of one.[^FfNQI63ZKys-290]
* With a Phantom Blood Barrage on bloblets, all three bloblets are barraged a second time, giving a lot of extra health.[^FfNQI63ZKys-290]
* To drag a blob against the pillar to phantom it, since it needs to be in the correct position, and that you can phantom anything in that position, not only blobs.[^FfNQI63ZKys-748]
* Blood Barraging a melee is slow because the melee has a high magic level, and that it is the worst thing to barrage if you can avoid it.[^FfNQI63ZKys-2100]
* Healing off rangers with Blood Barrage is really good because they have very low magic level, and he likes to be above 70 health to be safe.[^FfNQI63ZKys-2290]
* He originally used thralls for the Quiver run, but with low Prayer and Magic levels he found blood magic better because it saved just enough food to get through the boss.[^Aysv5rNWgFs-1630]
* On the final run Gnomonkey healed using Blood Barrage on mobs (his nickname for one of them was 'Big Beefy') while heading to Sol Heredit.[^Aysv5rNWgFs-1630]
* Gnomonkey barely touches ice barrage in the Inferno run and blood barrages almost entirely, using ice barrage only for a slight damage boost at full health in very rare situations.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-197]
* Gnomonkey freezes with ice barrage only when targets are already clumped, for the slightly higher kill chance and to stop one reaching the pillar.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-2290]
* Not to use Augury at the beginning of waves, since it adds only one max hit to barrage and wastes prayer; he starts waves with Augury from wave 50 onward for the defence.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-2405]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says Ice Barrage versus Blood Barrage DPS on nibblers is marginal because you almost always hit; Ice Barrage keeps them in place.[^FfNQI63ZKys-1138]
* Gnomonkey says to always know how you will get back to full health before the next wave, and never waste brews because of miscalculation after a wave is already solved.[^FfNQI63ZKys-3910]
* Gnomonkey says Mika's strategy of pure blood barrage mage with Zuriel's staff was the right play against a team with only javelins and no ACBs, and he cannot blame him for it.[^lJexGv6dxu8-3010]
