# Blood Barrage

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 5 videos (2021-05-21 to 2026-06-25). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Stats and numbers

- Phantom heal: if you are at least 8 tiles away when you cast Blood Barrage (range 10, same as a twisted bow) and the cast will kill the target, you can send another barrage right before it dies and get the healing for both casts. ([2021-05-21 22:41](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1361))
- Blood Barrage heals for damage you would have dealt even if the monster has low HP, so even 15 HP blobs can give massive healing. ([2021-05-21 24:12](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1452))
- Gnomonkey says a blob in the phantom position is worth about 40 health, a bat about 15 (20 to 25 with phantom), and a ranger about 30. ([2022-11-04 10:35](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=635))
- Gnomonkey notes ice barrage is more accurate and has one higher max hit, but that blood barrage is faster overall because it keeps nibblers clumped together. ([2025-07-13 36:10](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=2170))
- Gnomonkey says Zuriel's staff increases blood barrage efficiency by 50% and that blood barrage heals the same amount even if it is prayed against. ([2026-06-25 44:10](https://youtu.be/lJexGv6dxu8?t=2650))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says you can always Blood Barrage your health back, so tanking some damage to kill nibblers is fine; the final wave priority is to reach 99 health before the next wave using Blood Barrage or special attacks. ([2021-05-21 17:15](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1035))
- You can get knocked out extremely quickly in the Inferno, so having maximum health on tough waves is a must. ([2021-05-21 19:02](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1142))
- Phantom healing only works with manual casting; autocast never grants a phantom heal. ([2021-05-21 22:41](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1361))
- Gnomonkey explains the Phantom technique: if you kill something from a certain range or further with a cast, you can cast again at its corpse for extra healing, so you effectively get two Blood Barrages on it instead of one. ([2022-11-04 4:50](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=290))
- With a Phantom Blood Barrage on bloblets, all three bloblets are barraged a second time, giving a lot of extra health. ([2022-11-04 4:50](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=290))
- Gnomonkey says to drag a blob against the pillar to phantom it, since it needs to be in the correct position, and that you can phantom anything in that position, not only blobs. ([2022-11-04 12:28](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=748))
- Gnomonkey says Blood Barraging a melee is slow because the melee has a high magic level, and that it is the worst thing to barrage if you can avoid it. ([2022-11-04 35:00](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=2100))
- Gnomonkey says healing off rangers with Blood Barrage is really good because they have very low magic level, and he likes to be above 70 health to be safe. ([2022-11-04 38:10](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=2290))
- Gnomonkey says he originally used thralls for the Quiver run, but with low Prayer and Magic levels he found blood magic better because it saved just enough food to get through the boss. ([2024-09-12 27:10](https://youtu.be/Aysv5rNWgFs?t=1630))
- On the final run Gnomonkey healed using Blood Barrage on mobs (his nickname for one of them was 'Big Beefy') while heading to Sol Heredit. ([2024-09-12 27:10](https://youtu.be/Aysv5rNWgFs?t=1630))
- Gnomonkey barely touches ice barrage in the Inferno run and blood barrages almost entirely, using ice barrage only for a slight damage boost at full health in very rare situations. ([2025-07-13 3:17](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=197))
- Gnomonkey freezes with ice barrage only when targets are already clumped, for the slightly higher kill chance and to stop one reaching the pillar. ([2025-07-13 38:10](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=2290))
- Gnomonkey says not to use Augury at the beginning of waves, since it adds only one max hit to barrage and wastes prayer; he starts waves with Augury from wave 50 onward for the defence. ([2025-07-13 40:05](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=2405))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says Ice Barrage versus Blood Barrage DPS on nibblers is marginal because you almost always hit; Ice Barrage keeps them in place. ([2022-11-04 18:58](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=1138))
- Gnomonkey says to always know how you will get back to full health before the next wave, and never waste brews because of miscalculation after a wave is already solved. ([2022-11-04 1:05:10](https://youtu.be/FfNQI63ZKys?t=3910))
- Gnomonkey says Mika's strategy of pure blood barrage mage with Zuriel's staff was the right play against a team with only javelins and no ACBs, and he cannot blame him for it. ([2026-06-25 50:10](https://youtu.be/lJexGv6dxu8?t=3010))
