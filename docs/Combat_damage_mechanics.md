## Methods

* An OSRS attack first rolls accuracy: a random number based on your current attack level (including potion boosts), prayer and attack style, rolled against the target's defence level multiplied by its defence bonus for that style; if your number is bigger you hit, otherwise you hit zero.[^e0y_auD9JhY-617]
* When an attack lands, the damage is a random number from 1 up to your max hit, so each additional max hit is an extra pip on your die.[^e0y_auD9JhY-649]
* Ranged and magic work the same way, with ranged checking the monster's defence level and ranged defence, and magic checking the monster's magic level and magic defence; he notes weakness changes complicate this slightly.[^e0y_auD9JhY-677]
* Combat style chooses which accuracy bonus of your gear is rolled, and styles give hidden level boosts: block gives +3 defence and slash on a rune scimitar gives +3 strength.[^e0y_auD9JhY-706]
* Ranged on rapid is a tick faster for all weapons, which outweighs even potential max hit gains from accurate every single time.[^e0y_auD9JhY-735]
* Special attacks always use their own style regardless of the selected stance: if you put claws on stab, you roll the lower stab bonus against the enemy's slash defence, lowering accuracy against stab-weak enemies.[^e0y_auD9JhY-735]

## Tips

* This is why people barrage in melee gear: against a monster with 1 magic level you basically never splash.[^e0y_auD9JhY-706]
* You basically always want your weapon on aggressive for the extra +3 strength levels; magic never gets increased max hit from style, so it matters much less.[^e0y_auD9JhY-735]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says things that increase max hit are usually more important than accuracy, because a bigger die matters more than a 1% higher chance to roll the die.[^e0y_auD9JhY-677]
