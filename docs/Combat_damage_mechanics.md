## Methods

- Gnomonkey explains an OSRS attack first rolls accuracy: a random number based on your current attack level (including potion boosts), prayer and attack style, rolled against the target's defence level multiplied by its defence bonus for that style; if your number is bigger you hit, otherwise you hit zero. ([2024-07-07 10:17](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=617))
- Gnomonkey explains that when an attack lands, the damage is a random number from 1 up to your max hit, so each additional max hit is an extra pip on your die. ([2024-07-07 10:49](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=649))
- Gnomonkey says ranged and magic work the same way, with ranged checking the monster's defence level and ranged defence, and magic checking the monster's magic level and magic defence; he notes weakness changes complicate this slightly. ([2024-07-07 11:17](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=677))
- Gnomonkey says combat style chooses which accuracy bonus of your gear is rolled, and styles give hidden level boosts: block gives +3 defence and slash on a rune scimitar gives +3 strength. ([2024-07-07 11:46](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=706))
- Gnomonkey says ranged on rapid is a tick faster for all weapons, which outweighs even potential max hit gains from accurate every single time. ([2024-07-07 12:15](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=735))
- Gnomonkey says special attacks always use their own style regardless of the selected stance: if you put claws on stab, you roll the lower stab bonus against the enemy's slash defence, lowering accuracy against stab-weak enemies. ([2024-07-07 12:15](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=735))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says this is why people barrage in melee gear: against a monster with 1 magic level you basically never splash. ([2024-07-07 11:46](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=706))
- Gnomonkey says you basically always want your weapon on aggressive for the extra +3 strength levels; magic never gets increased max hit from style, so it matters much less. ([2024-07-07 12:15](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=735))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says things that increase max hit are usually more important than accuracy, because a bigger die matters more than a 1% higher chance to roll the die. ([2024-07-07 11:17](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=677))
