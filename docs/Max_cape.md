**Max cape** is a skill topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2022-08-03 and 2025-09-18. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* To finish the max cape Gnomonkey would buy the Bible 99s: Construction, Herblore, Crafting, Fletching, Smithing, Farming and Prayer, using the most efficient opportunity-cost methods such as mahogany tables.[^6OkBpQdcXRw-238]
* For Slayer, Gnomonkey would point boost to unlock Slayer rings, a perfect block list, the Slayer helmet and extensions, then do a mix of efficient XP tasks and any Slayer boss he is assigned.[^6OkBpQdcXRw-390]
* Firemaking can be done at any point for a little cash through Wintertodt, and if he got spooked the Phoenix he would switch to redwood log Firemaking at level 90 for significantly faster XP.[^6OkBpQdcXRw-410]

## Tips

* If his alt were also purged, Gnomonkey would work on it during very AFK grinds such as Caranbwan fishing or redwoods.[^6OkBpQdcXRw-238]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey calls Firemaking by far the most impressive skill in the game to level.[^6OkBpQdcXRw-410]

## Events

* When Sailing launches, players will keep their max cape but cannot equip it until they have re-maxed; if worn at next login it is unequipped automatically, like the quest cape when requirements are no longer met.[^sK0iG_wg3QA-997]
* Cosmetic variants of the max cape (Gnomonkey mentions Inferno cape buyers) can be dismantled, returning both the base max cape and the item it was combined with.[^sK0iG_wg3QA-997]
