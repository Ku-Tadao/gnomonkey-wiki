# Max cape

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2022-08-03 to 2025-09-18). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- To finish the max cape Gnomonkey would buy the Bible 99s: Construction, Herblore, Crafting, Fletching, Smithing, Farming and Prayer, using the most efficient opportunity-cost methods such as mahogany tables. ([2022-08-03 3:58](https://youtu.be/6OkBpQdcXRw?t=238))
- For Slayer, Gnomonkey would point boost to unlock Slayer rings, a perfect block list, the Slayer helmet and extensions, then do a mix of efficient XP tasks and any Slayer boss he is assigned. ([2022-08-03 6:30](https://youtu.be/6OkBpQdcXRw?t=390))
- Gnomonkey says Firemaking can be done at any point for a little cash through Wintertodt, and if he got spooked the Phoenix he would switch to redwood log Firemaking at level 90 for significantly faster XP. ([2022-08-03 6:50](https://youtu.be/6OkBpQdcXRw?t=410))

## Tips

- If his alt were also purged, Gnomonkey would work on it during very AFK grinds such as Caranbwan fishing or redwoods. ([2022-08-03 3:58](https://youtu.be/6OkBpQdcXRw?t=238))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey calls Firemaking by far the most impressive skill in the game to level. ([2022-08-03 6:50](https://youtu.be/6OkBpQdcXRw?t=410))

## Events

- When Sailing launches, players will keep their max cape but cannot equip it until they have re-maxed; if worn at next login it is unequipped automatically, like the quest cape when requirements are no longer met. ([2025-09-18 16:37](https://youtu.be/sK0iG_wg3QA?t=997))
- Cosmetic variants of the max cape (Gnomonkey mentions Inferno cape buyers) can be dismantled, returning both the base max cape and the item it was combined with. ([2025-09-18 16:37](https://youtu.be/sK0iG_wg3QA?t=997))
