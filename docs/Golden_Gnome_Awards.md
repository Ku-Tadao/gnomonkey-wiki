**Golden Gnome Awards** is an update topic that Gnomonkey covers in 4 videos between 2025-03-21 and 2026-08-12. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Events

* The video description thanks viewers for the Golden Gnome votes and says the HCIM quiver is completed as promised as the fully painted Golden Gnome, with glitter everywhere.[^c6oOmOGl5Lg-0]
* Gnomonkey shows the banger photos of the golden body paint during the run, saying the pictures turned out better than he imagined.[^c6oOmOGl5Lg-1529]
* After the clear he takes the quiver to Ava and notes it needs to be used on Ava herself rather than the accumulator.[^c6oOmOGl5Lg-2133]
* Gnomonkey asks viewers to nominate him for the Golden Gnomes in categories including best Old School RuneScape video creator, best new guide of the year (such as his Inferno or Tombs of Amascut guides or the new Gauntlet guide), best streamer, and gamer of the year for the Inferno and Deadman.[^nLY-srkZ_N8-760]
* Voting for the Golden Gnomes runs through Monday and asks for votes for best streamer, best guide (the Inferno Golden Trio video) and gamer of the year for the Deadman Inferno clutch.[^U6bZhE88Tp0-560]
* Gnomonkey is a finalist in four Golden Gnome categories: best streamer, best new video guide (with both the Corrupted Gauntlet guide and the Inferno Golden Trio guide nominated), gamer of the year, and best Old School RuneScape video creator; voting ended August 14th.[^FrZfpY0uoko-1685]
* Gnomonkey's gamer-of-the-year case lists the Deadman Mode Inferno clutch, a five million GP budget Theatre of Blood with no untradables, first to complete Delve level 8, second in Gielinor Games, his own Pillarless Colosseum, and devising Oathplate contract methods with Saxer Pillar.[^FrZfpY0uoko-1749]

## Trivia

* He won Best Guide Creator in the previous Golden Gnomes and painted himself gold in celebration.[^nLY-srkZ_N8-813]
* He will wear a golden suit at RuneFest, and that his channel recently received the Purple Heart Twitch Award for five million hours of streams watched.[^FrZfpY0uoko-1813]
