## Tips

* The blog offers players the option of being NDA'd and giving feedback to Jagex, which Gnomonkey highly recommends doing.[^8CSztSkDvnM-1281]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey thinks having two win conditions (sink the hull with cannons or kill the crew) gives Jagex an easy way to balance cannons against other weapon styles, which Sailing combat has struggled with.[^8CSztSkDvnM-117]

## Events

* Jagex's Sailing blog proposed pirate ships at various locations across the sea that players fight for loot, with the pirates dropping mid-level crossbow armour that boosts heavy weapons including the ballista, crossbows and ship cannons.[^8CSztSkDvnM-0]
* In the pirate encounters, players attack the enemy boat with the ship's cannons and fight the NPCs on the enemy ship with their own magic and ranged attacks; traditional combat cannot attack boats and cannons cannot attack pirate NPCs.[^8CSztSkDvnM-30]
* When an enemy pirate ship reaches a certain health threshold, pirates may jump on the player's boat, where the crew panics and pauses their actions until the player has dealt with the threat.[^8CSztSkDvnM-62]
* Enemy pirates are defeated either by killing the whole pirate crew, which sinks the boat, or by destroying the hull of the ship, causing it to sink.[^8CSztSkDvnM-88]
* Jagex said the encounters are comparable in complexity to Tormented Demons: a more involved fight, but not quite a boss.[^8CSztSkDvnM-146]
* Jagex said the content is aimed at mid-level and higher-level players, with some encounters in the 50 to 70 Sailing and combat range and others in the 75 plus range with upscaled stats.[^8CSztSkDvnM-176]
* Jagex said pirate encounters will be added to bounty tasks and the activity is focused on rewards, not XP, so they give only some XP and are not meant to compete with other Sailing training methods.[^8CSztSkDvnM-176]
* Jagex said it is not currently planning a major expansion to Sailing combat beyond the pirate content, but that NPC ship combat needs significant underlying infrastructure that could support future Sailing combat, and anything of larger scale (beyond the player-designed island, Wormscraig) would not be seen that year.[^8CSztSkDvnM-1281]
