**Jad Challenge** is a challenge topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2021-03-08. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* Players with a fire cape can access the first two Jad Challenges (1 Jad and 2 Jads at once); the next four challenges, from 3 to 6 Jads, can only be accessed by players who own an infernal cape.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-0]

## Methods

* Gnomonkey's recommended gear for the Jad Challenge is max ranged: Armadyl, Pegasian boots, Anguish, Barrows gloves and dragon arrows; he says you can downgrade to god d'hide and still easily get the completion.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-54]
* At 99 Prayer, the inventory for 5 Jad and under needs around three prayer potions plus a Divine ranging potion, with the rest being your best food and optionally some chinchompas.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-54]

## Stats and numbers

* Elite void ranged is significantly worse DPS than Armadyl against Jad because of Jad's high defence, around 4% worse, and also gives worse defence, so Gnomonkey does not recommend void.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-54]
* The Jads in the challenge have spaced-out attack cycles that are relatively normal (for the lower challenges).[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-114]

## Tips

* Supplies are not really the worry in the Jad Challenge, so you want the best ranged DPS gear you can get.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-54]
* For 6 Jad, Gnomonkey adds another prayer potion to be safe, and says that one extra anglerfish can be the difference maker.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-84]
* Chinchompas are great for tagging healers but very difficult to use before you have killed one or two of the Jads in harder modes; Gnomonkey tags healers with his twisted bow personally.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-84]
* Chinchompas speed things up significantly and are basically necessary if you use a blowpipe; the type of chinchompa does not matter, and in March 2021 grey and red chinchompas had similar prices so he says red chins were probably best.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-84]
* 4 Jads and under should be fairly simple to do with some attempts, so his video focuses on 5 and 6 Jads as by far the hardest challenges.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-114]
* For 5 and 6 Jad, Gnomonkey highly recommends re-entering without pre-potting for a while to practise the prayer timings, which helps a lot.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-114]
* You can pre-pot a Divine ranging potion, or Bastion potion, and anglerfish up to 121 HP before entering the Jad Challenge.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-114]
* You can optionally pre-pot an imbued heart as it gets reset every attempt, but Gnomonkey says it makes a very small difference for tanking Jad's magic hits.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-114]
* Anything that reduces the number of clicks you have to do helps dramatically with the Jad Challenge.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-233]
* If you are overhealed it is impossible for Jad to one-shot you; Gnomonkey says it is absolutely possible to take Jad hits (he took multiple in his successful run) and what matters is how you deal with them.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-233]
* After taking a Jad hit, do not panic: eat an anglerfish in between the Jad cycles, repeat until back to stable health, then click back on Jad and refocus on prayer switching.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-233]
* If you get an unlucky spawn where several healers attack you, you may need to heal while killing the starting Jads.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-233]
* Six Jad is much simpler than five Jad in layout but much harder anyway, since from the north Jad all the way around to the northwest Jad there is no delay between attacks.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-463]
* In 6 Jad you must switch prayer each tick an attack comes out; Gnomonkey waits for a Jad's projectile to be on screen before switching, and if the Jads are alternating you can just click the next prayer as soon as your prayer activates.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-463]
* In 6 Jad the only time you can really do other actions is after the northwest Jad's attack at the end of a full cycle, when there are two ticks before the next Jad's attack is fired.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-493]
* Five Jads is barely any easier than six, since you still have tick-perfect timing for five attacks and just get one extra tick in between cycles.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-523]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey highly recommends a twisted bow for 6 Jad; he says it is definitely possible with just a toxic blowpipe but much harder.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-27]
* Gnomonkey says strategy only helps so much and what matters in the Jad Challenge is throwing attempt after attempt until the prayer timings are down.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-263]

## Records

* In Gnomonkey's 6 Jad run he tanked several Jad hits, including one for 98 while healers were also attacking him, healed up carefully when possible and still completed the challenge.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-578]

## Events

* The Jad Challenge is brand new PvM content, released in March 2021, for players who have completed the Inferno.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-0]

## Trivia

* The 1 and 2 Jad challenges are good for people who want to get more comfortable with the Inferno Jads.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-0]
* Completing the 6 Jad challenge unlocks a transmog that turns Jad pets into the Inferno variant, and the challenges also reward some tokkul for challenges completed.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-0]
* The Jad Challenge is located just south of the Mor Ul Rek bank chest.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-27]
