**Dragon hunter crossbow** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2019-04-08 and 2020-06-27. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* Gnomonkey recommends completing the Candar (Western Provinces) hard diary because it increases the bolts' special effect proc chance by 10%.[^f_QYQ9TMF40-122]

## Stats and numbers

* The Dragon hunter crossbow is significantly better against any dragon-based enemy.[^bIShDvVDueM-710]
* The Dragon hunter crossbow has 95 ranged attack bonus versus the Armadyl crossbow's 100, losing the special attack and prayer bonus, but gets a 30% ranged attack and strength bonus against dragons.[^bIShDvVDueM-742]

## Tips

* In the guide's gear setup the dragon hunter crossbow is used with enchanted ruby dragon bolts, with diamond dragon bolts in the inventory for a switch.[^f_QYQ9TMF40-132]
* Some players swap ruby bolts for diamond bolts at 35% Vorkath health, but he swaps at 40% because diamond bolts' proc chance is 5% higher than ruby bolts' and ruby bolts do the same damage regardless of Vorkath's health.[^f_QYQ9TMF40-1007]
* The Dragon hunter crossbow is useful at Vorkath, the Chambers of Xeric and any chromatic dragon, including brutal black dragons.[^bIShDvVDueM-771]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey states in 2019 that the dragon hunter crossbow is the most effective weapon to kill Vorkath with, and the guide is specifically built around it.[^f_QYQ9TMF40-0]
* Gnomonkey says the Dragon hunter crossbow is worth buying on any account eventually, and before Armadyl armour if you want to farm Vorkath for money.[^bIShDvVDueM-771]
