# Dragon hunter crossbow

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2019-04-08 to 2020-06-27). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | item |
| **Videos** | 2 (first 2019-04-08, latest 2020-06-27) |

## Requirements

- Gnomonkey recommends completing the Candar (Western Provinces) hard diary because it increases the bolts' special effect proc chance by 10%. ([2019-04-08 2:02](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=122))

## Stats and numbers

- The Dragon hunter crossbow is significantly better against any dragon-based enemy. ([2020-06-27 11:50](https://youtu.be/bIShDvVDueM?t=710))
- The Dragon hunter crossbow has 95 ranged attack bonus versus the Armadyl crossbow's 100, losing the special attack and prayer bonus, but gets a 30% ranged attack and strength bonus against dragons. ([2020-06-27 12:22](https://youtu.be/bIShDvVDueM?t=742))

## Tips

- In the guide's gear setup the dragon hunter crossbow is used with enchanted ruby dragon bolts, with diamond dragon bolts in the inventory for a switch. ([2019-04-08 2:12](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=132))
- Gnomonkey says some players swap ruby bolts for diamond bolts at 35% Vorkath health, but he swaps at 40% because diamond bolts' proc chance is 5% higher than ruby bolts' and ruby bolts do the same damage regardless of Vorkath's health. ([2019-04-08 16:47](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40?t=1007))
- The Dragon hunter crossbow is useful at Vorkath, the Chambers of Xeric and any chromatic dragon, including brutal black dragons. ([2020-06-27 12:51](https://youtu.be/bIShDvVDueM?t=771))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey states in 2019 that the dragon hunter crossbow is the most effective weapon to kill Vorkath with, and the guide is specifically built around it. ([2019-04-08 0:00](https://youtu.be/f_QYQ9TMF40))
- Gnomonkey says the Dragon hunter crossbow is worth buying on any account eventually, and before Armadyl armour if you want to farm Vorkath for money. ([2020-06-27 12:51](https://youtu.be/bIShDvVDueM?t=771))
