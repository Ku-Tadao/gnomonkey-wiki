# Mimic

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2022-03-19 to 2025-04-08). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says the mimic is a drop of around one in five masters when five master clues are completed; the transcript phrasing is unclear. ([2025-04-08 16:51](https://youtu.be/WQ9Zg4fQRJo?t=1011))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey rates the Mimic F tier but says it is just a loot roll on a clue scroll, so he does not mind that it is not exceptional. ([2022-03-19 42:45](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2565))
- Gnomonkey notes that the mimic is an RNG drop from clues, so it is unlikely to be a quick unlock; he says getting a mimic takes hours and hours on average and could take forever if unlucky. ([2025-04-08 4:00](https://youtu.be/WQ9Zg4fQRJo?t=240))

## Events

- Under the Poll 84 proposal, defeating the mimic would increase the clue scroll stack capacity across all tiers by one. ([2025-04-08 3:32](https://youtu.be/WQ9Zg4fQRJo?t=212))

## Trivia

- Gnomonkey says you kill the Mimic by going max melee with claws and speccing twice, then switching to max mage, killing it in about five seconds. ([2022-03-19 42:45](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2565))
