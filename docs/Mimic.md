**Mimic** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2022-03-19 and 2025-04-08. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* The mimic is a drop of around one in five masters when five master clues are completed; the transcript phrasing is unclear.[^WQ9Zg4fQRJo-1011]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey rates the Mimic F tier but says it is just a loot roll on a clue scroll, so he does not mind that it is not exceptional.[^HkVt4enEesI-2565]
* Gnomonkey notes that the mimic is an RNG drop from clues, so it is unlikely to be a quick unlock; he says getting a mimic takes hours and hours on average and could take forever if unlucky.[^WQ9Zg4fQRJo-240]

## Events

* Under the Poll 84 proposal, defeating the mimic would increase the clue scroll stack capacity across all tiers by one.[^WQ9Zg4fQRJo-212]

## Trivia

* You kill the Mimic by going max melee with claws and speccing twice, then switching to max mage, killing it in about five seconds.[^HkVt4enEesI-2565]
