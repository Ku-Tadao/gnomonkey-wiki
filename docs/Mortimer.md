# Mortimer

## Requirements

- Mortimer requires 70 Slayer and 100 combat, or 99 Slayer; the only existing Slayer master with a Slayer requirement is Duradel at 50 Slayer. ([2026-05-29 9:46](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=586))

## Stats and numbers

- Mortimer presents two assignments to choose from, and after completing enough tasks for him a third choice is unlocked. ([2026-07-24 1:06](https://youtu.be/nLY-srkZ_N8?t=66))
- Skipping a Mortimer assignment costs 100 Slayer points, Turael cannot reset Mortimer assignments so Turael skipping is not possible, and while using Mortimer you only have two block slots, each costing 120 Slayer points. ([2026-07-24 1:32](https://youtu.be/nLY-srkZ_N8?t=92))
- If you get a Mortimer task you do not want, you can skip it, store it (task stores are shared with the normal Slayer system and use one of your store slots), or finish it before taking another assignment. ([2026-07-24 1:59](https://youtu.be/nLY-srkZ_N8?t=119))
- Mortimer exclusively offers tasks with superior monsters, which gives more agency over superior unique grinds such as the imbued heart, but means he cannot assign tasks that have no superiors. ([2026-07-24 1:59](https://youtu.be/nLY-srkZ_N8?t=119))
- Because Mortimer only assigns superior tasks, demons such as tormented demons and demonic gorillas, and bosses such as Cerberus, are not assigned by him, so a different Slayer master is needed for those grinds. ([2026-07-24 2:29](https://youtu.be/nLY-srkZ_N8?t=149))
- Mortimer gives zero Slayer points per task; the only way to get Slayer points from him is to take the points modifier, which ranges from 5 to 40 points depending on the task. ([2026-07-24 2:29](https://youtu.be/nLY-srkZ_N8?t=149))
- Every Mortimer task has a modifier that can add Slayer points, increase or decrease task quantity, boost Slayer XP, improve clue scroll drop rates, or boost the chance to hit the superior creature's unique table. ([2026-07-24 2:29](https://youtu.be/nLY-srkZ_N8?t=149))
- Mortimer modifier unlocks by number of tasks completed: at first only Slayer points and task quantity modifiers, clue scroll bonuses after 25 tasks, Slayer XP modifiers at 50, superior drop rate modifiers at 75, and a third task choice at 100. ([2026-07-24 2:55](https://youtu.be/nLY-srkZ_N8?t=175))
- Mortimer's modifier strength varies by task: superior unique modifiers give a small increase on tasks like Araxytes and a massive increase on slower ones like Dark beasts, where the drop rate increase goes from 200% to 300%. ([2026-07-24 3:31](https://youtu.be/nLY-srkZ_N8?t=211))
- Mortimer tasks include very low-level ones like crawling hands and cave crawlers with low quantities and decreased-quantity modifiers, which help you reach the 100-task unlock faster. ([2026-07-24 4:09](https://youtu.be/nLY-srkZ_N8?t=249))
- Mortimer's clue scroll modifier ranges from about 35% up to 150% increase, and Gnomonkey notes most seem to be hard clues, which he says are not the clues people usually go for. ([2026-07-24 5:36](https://youtu.be/nLY-srkZ_N8?t=336))
- Mortimer's Slayer XP modifier is balanced per creature: tasks that are already good such as smoke devils get only 5% to 15%, while worse tasks like Dark beasts get 35% to 75%. ([2026-07-24 6:24](https://youtu.be/nLY-srkZ_N8?t=384))
- The unique modifier on Mortimer tasks gives only small increases on tasks already good for the heart such as smoke devils and Araxytes, but up to a 300% increase on tasks with a bad heart chance. ([2026-07-24 6:57](https://youtu.be/nLY-srkZ_N8?t=417))
- Task extensions work with Mortimer, but task unlocks that specify certain masters do not, so Mortimer can assign Griffins, Aquanites, Basilisks, warped terror birds and more from the start without unlocking them. ([2026-07-24 7:29](https://youtu.be/nLY-srkZ_N8?t=449))
- In the Mortimer FAQ, all five modifier types have an equal chance of appearing, multiple task choices can have the same modifier, and Mortimer never offers the same creature twice within one set of choices. ([2026-07-24 8:00](https://youtu.be/nLY-srkZ_N8?t=480))
- Jagex says Slayer XP bonuses on Mortimer tasks vary because tasks already vary significantly in efficiency, so XP modifiers are balanced individually per creature. ([2026-07-24 8:28](https://youtu.be/nLY-srkZ_N8?t=508))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey expects Mortimer to become the meta Slayer master because of the choice of multiple tasks, with everyone moving over unless he has many bad tasks. ([2026-05-29 9:46](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=586))
- Gnomonkey finds the Slayer task modifier bonuses like better clue scroll rates feel a little private server and unnecessary, since choosing multiple tasks is already plenty of incentive. ([2026-05-29 10:40](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=640))
- Gnomonkey asks why Mortimer also needs to be best for superior spawns for imbued heart grinds and for clue scroll rates, so that everybody is forced to use him. ([2026-05-29 11:08](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=668))
- Gnomonkey says he is unsure whether Mortimer's points modifier is multiplied by task streak and wants clarification, and thinks it will not be a good way to get points. ([2026-07-24 5:19](https://youtu.be/nLY-srkZ_N8?t=319))
- Gnomonkey says he does not see much benefit in the clue scroll modifier at a glance and sees the XP modifier as odd, because he believes Duradel with a solid block list and many burst tasks will still be best for XP. ([2026-07-24 5:36](https://youtu.be/nLY-srkZ_N8?t=336))
- Gnomonkey thinks Mortimer's two blocks would probably go on warped creatures and drakes, but admits he is unsure because almost everything has the same weight. ([2026-07-24 8:31](https://youtu.be/nLY-srkZ_N8?t=511))

## Events

- Mortimer is an undead skeleton Slayer master from Wormscraig who offers multiple task choices instead of assigning a single task, so players can choose between tasks instead of regularly skipping. ([2026-05-29 9:18](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=558))
- After completing a certain number of tasks with Mortimer, players see task modifiers, powerful bonuses that might include enhanced Slayer XP, Slayer points, clue scroll rates, superior spawns and more. ([2026-05-29 9:18](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=558))
- Jagex said Duradel would still be better if you are not picky about tasks and that Konar would still be the best for Slayer points. ([2026-05-29 10:12](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=612))
- Mortimer is the new Slayer master coming with Wyrmscraig; he has a Sailing level requirement to reach his island and is the highest level Slayer master in the game, requiring 70 Slayer and 100 combat. ([2026-07-24 0:00](https://youtu.be/nLY-srkZ_N8))
- Jagex says Mortimer presents multiple assignments each with its own modifier rather than letting players choose a task outright, offering an alternative style of play and not replacing existing masters. ([2026-07-24 0:33](https://youtu.be/nLY-srkZ_N8?t=33))
