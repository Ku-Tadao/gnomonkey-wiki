**Kick** is an other topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2024-03-02. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Tips

* The 7TV emote extension was buggy on Kick, with bugs that had cropped up the day before and that 7TV said they were working on; he got it working through the 7TV command.[^gO3aMYXs-QA-115]
* 7TV on Kick is set up via the extension's onboarding page, where you choose which platforms to connect, select Kick, and go through the whole flow; he says it is in beta and buggy.[^gO3aMYXs-QA-4978]

## Events

* At the start of this Kick stream, Gnomonkey says the first 25 subs get founder badges and that they are running out very fast.[^gO3aMYXs-QA-26]
* The one thing Kick has differently from Twitch is that it has no channel points, so he planned to let subs request things like wearing a hat in game.[^gO3aMYXs-QA-588]
* Gnomonkey agreed on sub-based redeems for his Kick chat, with three subs for hats (as transcribed), and noted the prices of those redeems had gone up quite a bit.[^gO3aMYXs-QA-618]
* Hoshi gifted 50 subscriptions to Gnomonkey's community on Kick, which broke the activity feed, and a viewer named ABZ later gifted 100 subs.[^gO3aMYXs-QA-1120]
* Gnomonkey said that viewers who missed out on founder badges would be given OG badges instead so they would get something.[^gO3aMYXs-QA-1408]
* The 7TV emote list button is missing because of a bug reported the day before; typing a colon followed by the emote name still works.[^gO3aMYXs-QA-5493]
* Twelve hour streams are not planned quite yet, but his Kick streams are going to be longer in general.[^gO3aMYXs-QA-11487]
* His Kick streaming schedule is 5 p.m. to about 1 a.m. PST, and that he is only taking Mondays off.[^gO3aMYXs-QA-20975]
