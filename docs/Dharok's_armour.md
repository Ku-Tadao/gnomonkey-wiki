**Dharok's armour** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2020-03-08 and 2024-11-30. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* On entering, Gnomonkey sips overload (hits for 50 HP), drinks absorption to 200 and no higher, reduces health to 1, turns on Piety, auto-retaliate and the interval timer, and sits on the sides to reduce being hit by multiple enemies.[^zpBhPn5MJNA-299]

## Stats and numbers

* Most equipment sets' DPS scale only with Strength and Attack, but Dharok's also scales heavily with Hitpoints level, each level being about a 1% DPS increase.[^zpBhPn5MJNA-24]
* Having maxed combat with 70 Hitpoints versus maxed combat with 99 Hitpoints is a 35% DPS difference with Dharok's.[^zpBhPn5MJNA-24]
* With high melee stats and Hitpoints or maxed combat, a whip setup gives about 20% less DPS than Dharok's, and a Ghrazi rapier with best-in-slot melee gear is still about 5% worse than Dharok's.[^zpBhPn5MJNA-61]
* The only setup that beats Dharok's DPS-wise is a charged scythe at about 10% more DPS; an uncharged scythe is still worse.[^zpBhPn5MJNA-61]
* In Leagues 5, the Gloves of the Damned double the effect of Dharok's set, which Gnomonkey used for the 1 HP Dharok bomb on Echo Sol.[^yJCJ-7T_oxQ-0]
* The maximum Dharok's hit without echoes is 147, and he hit a 183 at one point which he joked is the worst because he can do too much damage and kill things early.[^yJCJ-7T_oxQ-548]

## Tips

* Gnomonkey assumes basic gear: Dharok's, a blessing, an imbued Berserker ring and a fire cape; an amulet of torture gives 2% more DPS than a fury, Ferocious gloves 2% more than Barrows gloves, and Primordial boots a fraction of a percent (Dragon boots are acceptable).[^zpBhPn5MJNA-120]
* Gnomonkey's spec weapon is dragon claws, which he bought with 50 mil he had around and says are just slightly better than a granite maul with a handle; a granite maul is totally fine.[^zpBhPn5MJNA-147]
* With Dragon Slayer II completed, a locator orb is the most efficient way to damage yourself outside the Wilderness, hitting for 10 each click, unlike the dwarven rock cake whose damage scales down as health lowers; neither can kill you.[^zpBhPn5MJNA-147]
* Inventory is 12 doses of overload, 24 doses of absorption, a holy wrench and the rest prayer potions; Gnomonkey says this is the perfect ratio to last one hour.[^zpBhPn5MJNA-179]
* Gnomonkey brings extra absorptions in case the overload runs out and he gets hit hard, and has run out of absorptions before prayer potions; more locator orbs can be obtained from Ava at Draynor Manor.[^zpBhPn5MJNA-179]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Because of how slowly the axe swings, with base 70 combat stats a Proselyte-and-whip setup gives better DPS than Dharok's; Dharok's begins to beat a whip setup at about 85 base combat stats and Hitpoints.[^zpBhPn5MJNA-24]
* Gnomonkey says that for the 99.99% of players who cannot spend 1.4 million an hour on a scythe at NMZ, Dharok's is probably going to stay the best method.[^zpBhPn5MJNA-93]
* The only downside of Dharok's is that it is less AFK than a Proselyte whip setup, but it gives significantly better XP an hour and costs much less over time because the player does not pray Protect from Melee.[^zpBhPn5MJNA-93]

## Events

* After this Echo Sol kill Gnomonkey says he plans to try Dharok bombing all of Theatre of Blood.[^yJCJ-7T_oxQ-0]
