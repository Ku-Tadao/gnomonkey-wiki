Zebak is the crocodile boss room of [Tombs of Amascut](Tombs_of_Amascut.md). In 2022 Gnomonkey called it the hardest room of the raid[^h4hf5hPQHms-0]; by 2024 to 2025 he treated it as a room that uses few supplies and little or no prayer, whose main danger is a level 4 Zebak making the later [Wardens](The_Wardens.md) fight harder[^vQO8khf302s-2616][^cmLBaxdoHpQ-1448][^Mg9hcqcFC1U-791][^UhLzyieXrbY-1053].

## Path level

- **Odds of level 4.** In April 2023 he said there is a one in three chance of a level four Zebak[^vQO8khf302s-706], and in May 2023 that he got one on all three of his attempts, a one in three chance, calling it bad but doable[^K7VALGO6md8-1159]. In May 2024 he said there was at that time a one in two chance of level four, and overall one in six with Pathfinder[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-515]. In July 2025 he gave a level four Zebak as a 1 in 6 chance[^91vxCCm7Jck-1221].
- **Why level 4 matters.** He says a level four Zebak makes the room harder and speeds up the Wardens' attacks, making their phase 4 a lot harder[^vQO8khf302s-835][^cmLBaxdoHpQ-1168]. At level 4 his enrage takes two ticks instead of three, but the faster Warden attacks are the bigger issue[^vQO8khf302s-1226]. In May 2024 he described the Wardens' projectiles as a tick faster[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-1415]. The attack also drains prayer at level 4, so he warns not to get smited[^K7VALGO6md8-1491].
- **Preferences.** In April 2023 he said a lower-level Zebak and higher-level Akkha is wanted[^vQO8khf302s-773]. He recommends checking Zebak's level early: at level three or lower, do Zebak first; at level four, [Akkha](Akkha.md) may come earlier[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-732]. In July 2025 he said not to reset on a level four, and that this is why he does Zebak before Akkha[^91vxCCm7Jck-1221]. In June 2023 he said a level four Zebak could theoretically justify doing Akkha first, but the player is kind of screwed regardless, and that he would not reset or do weird tricks over it[^cmLBaxdoHpQ-1168][^cmLBaxdoHpQ-1412]. He also said a level four Zebak sucks really badly[^cmLBaxdoHpQ-660].
- **Contrary view (June 2024).** In his wiki-guides finale run he said a level four Zebak is actually good because Akkha becomes much easier and Zebak wastes more supplies, saying he would take a level four Akkha and level two Zebak or the reverse[^17ynF_0ltPc-636]. He later noted that run also had a level four Zebak when reaching the Wardens, and said it was going to suck[^17ynF_0ltPc-1241].
- **Rare rolls.** At 1000 invocations he got a level three Zebak and called it very rare, and a Perfect Zebak kill[^m8m-6TBJqhQ-323][^m8m-6TBJqhQ-409].

## Blood barrage and blood spawns

- **Blood barrage and catching it.** In 2022 he said the barrage can be fully caught at level 2 or 3 by watching the indicator below Zebak and praying mage, even after the enrage; at other levels it lines up with the ranged attack and cannot be avoided[^q3D2jeguGz8-572][^q3D2jeguGz8-572]. In April and May 2023 he said a level 4 barrage cannot be caught, so it just hits[^vQO8khf302s-1366][^K7VALGO6md8-1462]. In May 2024 he said at level four it is a 50-50 coin toss with chip damage, and at level three all barrages can be caught[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-819][^Mg9hcqcFC1U-848]. In June 2025 he said magic prayer can be flicked to block it so it neither heals the boss nor damages the player, but at level one when enraged it cannot be blocked, only at level two or three[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-1678]. Also in 2025 he said at level 3 all barrages in the last phase can be flicked with no damage, while at level 4 they cannot be blocked and chip damage is guaranteed[^UhLzyieXrbY-1291]. In April 2026 he said at level 0 to 1 the blood animation attack cannot be flicked, but at level 2 or 3 the player can flick to mage right before it lands[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-1913]. Mage prayer with Rigour catches both Zebak's Blubberage and his mage attack, making it the most likely prayer[^YizVhRWXnxs-722]. In October 2024 he said the player can flick prayer to catch it or tank it for about a 9 hit[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-1747].
- **Level 4 chip damage.** In July 2024 he gave level 4 Zebak's chip damage as 15, and 16 at level 5 with Blood Rage, so the player should never go below 16 health without healing[^LxYODChv40A-1050]. At level 4 he also said no damage should be taken until the final rage if all barrages are caught[^LxYODChv40A-959].
- **Blood spawns.** Blood clouds spawn every three barrages, though they can randomly spawn at the beginning, between one and three barrages in[^LxYODChv40A-938]. The spawns follow the player with a ticking health bar and die eventually; standing on the opposite side of the room from where they spawned means they die as they reach the player, otherwise they heal the boss[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-1589][^UhLzyieXrbY-1351]. Marked red tiles on the left and right of the room are safe spots from spawns on the opposite side and from acid[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-819][^LxYODChv40A-1020][^N2DCZ1nt4p4-1704]. Standing on the opposite red side tiles means the spawns reach the player and die, guaranteed safe[^BVZEAFBsj10-1129]. The orbs follow where the player was on a delay, so they can be trailed around the room[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-1764]. In a 2025 raid he noted that at the lowest raid level blood splats do not off-tick, which a teammate called terrible game design[^QkJq2KHiKW8-1369].
- He says to avoid Zebak's shadow orbs in a 500 because they hit for 25[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-878].

## Puzzle room

- The puzzle is to water the tree quickly without making a mistake[^q3D2jeguGz8-515]. In the water jug video, using the jug on the waterfall and the full jug on the tree each save a tick, and he recommends swapping the left-click of the jug to use[^q3D2jeguGz8-515]. In 2024 he used this to beat the first lizard spawn[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-1514]. If the tree is watered quickly, lizards give 25 health of leeway and can mostly be ignored; if the tree is unwatered they bite hard[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-1617].
- **Spikes.** Run through them with a running start from the correct tile, avoiding their line of sight[^q3D2jeguGz8-515]. Spikes cannot hit a moving player, so never stand still before running through; only gas must be dodged manually[^BVZEAFBsj10-1017][^N2DCZ1nt4p4-1583][^X-PTHMq_10A-1271]. Gnomonkey shared his tile markers in 2025: copy them, import via the world map, and as long as the player's shoot tile touches the marked tile the player can run through without looking[^X-PTHMq_10A-1271][^X-PTHMq_10A-1271]. In 2026 he describes bringing back two full jugs, and being hit on the way back loses half the water[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-1511].

## Mechanics of the fight

- **Default attack.** A rock (ranged) or jug (mage) shot as a red energy ball; having the right protection prayer up when it touches the player avoids the damage completely[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-1640].
- **Melee.** Flinch by running two tiles away; walking back still gets the player hit for over a hundred[^q3D2jeguGz8-540]. The bite can kill in one hit, but is delayed, and dodging two tiles back avoids it[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-1622][^N2DCZ1nt4p4-1976]. Zebak's melee will probably kill a player who stays in front after a spec[^91vxCCm7Jck-1396].
- **Special attacks.** Zebak alternates between two specials roughly every 15 to 18 percent of damage: acid with stone barricades the player hides behind from his repeated roars, and a wave attack with a small gap[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-1737]. Below roughly a quarter health he enrages and attacks very quickly[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-1913].
- **Jug solve.** Jugs can be pushed or pulled, including diagonally, with right-click pull[^q3D2jeguGz8-540][^BVZEAFBsj10-1159][^N2DCZ1nt4p4-1761].
  - 2022: be on the left side when barricades are thrown; this typically gives an easy first solve[^q3D2jeguGz8-540].
  - April 2023: stay on the left for the first jugs and in the middle for the next[^vQO8khf302s-1278]; May 2023: stand in the centre for a solve that always works[^K7VALGO6md8-1371].
  - 2024 to 2025: the top left tile always has a solve for the first set and the middle for the second[^LxYODChv40A-959][^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-1734]; in June 2024 the Upset Stomach solve was the left side for the first barricade and the middle for the second[^YizVhRWXnxs-740]; in October 2024 and June 2025 he stayed on the left because the solution is always there[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-1589][^UhLzyieXrbY-1139][^BVZEAFBsj10-1073][^91vxCCm7Jck-1341]; in June 2025 he said the barricade solve is in the middle except about 5% of the time when the bottom left jug is needed[^UhLzyieXrbY-1182].
  - June 2025 (post-update): he noted the update seems to have changed Zebak's jug patterns; there used to always be a solve in the middle, but he has seen examples where there is not[^BVZEAFBsj10-1103].
  - April 2026: the first barricade solve is always in the left start area and the second set always in the middle of the room[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-1761]; the first puzzle is always on him and the wave puzzle is always left first then right, with slightly smaller gaps at higher raid level[^X-PTHMq_10A-1331].
  - The shadow has a slow travel time, so jugs should be hit early with it[^91vxCCm7Jck-1308]. Jugs can also be kept to throw into the crocodile's mouth to feed him during the rage phase, with attacks three ticks apart[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-1715].
- **Waves.** True tile marks the waves, which are normal NPCs called wave[^vQO8khf302s-1305][^K7VALGO6md8-1371][^cmLBaxdoHpQ-1358]. The green line is the max range to hit him from with the T-Bow line[^cmLBaxdoHpQ-1288]; attacking from outside the green lane pulls the player into the wave, which can kill[^LxYODChv40A-1050]. In 2025 the green tiles show the 10 tile range, the farthest the [Bow of Faerdhinen](Bow_of_Faerdhinen.md) can reach[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-1558]. He turns the camera to look down the room[^UhLzyieXrbY-1231] and does not run straight behind waves because they drag and deal damage, even though the change made this a bit less bad[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-1707]. When swarms spawn during waves the player must dodge them in a zigzag[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-1764].
  - **Wave skip.** In 2022 and 2023 he called wave skipping really easy: walk away one tile, then run through with a running start[^q3D2jeguGz8-601][^vQO8khf302s-1305][^cmLBaxdoHpQ-1358]. By July 2024 he marked red tiles so wave skips are not needed, saying they needlessly endanger the run[^LxYODChv40A-1020]. In July 2025 he said wave skip is very rarely worth doing, mostly when one wave is in front of Zebak and another behind it, and comes up more at level four[^91vxCCm7Jck-1458][^91vxCCm7Jck-1458][^91vxCCm7Jck-1489].
  - In June 2025 Jagex did not go ahead with making waves non-pushing but dealing 30% more damage; in the follow-up blog they said they would investigate smarter pushing with pathfinding, and if that dead-ends, waves would deal reduced damage on subsequent hits. Gnomonkey said either option makes Zebak more consistent[^JjIAGBBDmL8-554][^JjIAGBBDmL8-569].

## Gear, specs and supplies

- **Specs.** In 2022 he used the [Bandos godsword](Bandos_godsword.md) until Zebak was drained 20 defence levels[^q3D2jeguGz8-515], and recommended an adrenaline dose at the start because [Zaryte crossbow](Zaryte_crossbow.md) specs are incredibly powerful there[^q3D2jeguGz8-540]. In July 2024 (shadow build) he said to shadow Zebak and not spec, because the godsword lowers defence level but magic rolls against magic level[^LxYODChv40A-908]. In October 2024 he ran in, used the Bone dagger special for a full 20 drain and ran away, noting that getting bitten kills[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-1559]. In June 2025 he ran in and used the godsword for 20[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-1622]; in the 300 ironman guide he hit salt, poked Zebak and ran away, and noted burning claws are not great and that he does not spec in his mouth range[^BVZEAFBsj10-1047][^BVZEAFBsj10-1189]. In October 2025 he said a bone dagger hit of 20 is the max defence drain and he hit over 20 every time[^X-PTHMq_10A-1422]. In April 2026 he hits salt and opens with the godsword until 20 damage; Death Charge does not matter here[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-1976].
- **Weapon choice.** In May 2024 he said it is better to range Zebak even in [Mixed hide](Mixed_hide_armour.md), based on his calc[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-768]. In October 2024 the [Osmumten's fang](Osmumten's_fang.md) looked like better DPS but, by his check, was close and surprisingly not better than the crossbow[^vEs4Qvs_Rag-1559]. Boots have negative one range, so taking them off gives a bit of ranged accuracy[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-1764].
- **Prayer.** Zero-prayer room because it is killed so fast (2023)[^vQO8khf302s-2616]; no doses of anything, even at level four, camping prayer with 64 prayer points (June 2023)[^cmLBaxdoHpQ-1448]. At level four he used at least two doses (May 2023)[^K7VALGO6md8-1371]. In 2024 he said it can be a free prayer room with quick prayers set to Rigor and mage prayer and one-tick flicking most of the time[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-791][^YizVhRWXnxs-722]. He recommends not turning the overhead off against Zebak and just flipping Rigour[^K7VALGO6md8-1462]. A Falador shield is worth one and a half restores worth of prayer[^K7VALGO6md8-1460]. In July 2025 he used no prayer flicking and a single dose in the room, largely thanks to prayer regeneration[^91vxCCm7Jck-1521].
- **Other.** He needs two salts and was at level four invocation for the room in 2022, and plays a song at the start[^h4hf5hPQHms-1241]. He always takes power at the choice before the Wardens because he needs two salts later[^BVZEAFBsj10-987]. He says specing Zebak safely means stepping at least two tiles away immediately or speccing from the sides[^91vxCCm7Jck-1396]. Zebak uses very few supplies[^S4Z7Mjjdt4I-1764].

## Invocations

| Invocation | Gnomonkey's description |
|---|---|
| Not Just a Head | 10 raid level; Zebak fires a blood-barrage style attack that heals him and must be on for Arterial Spray and Blood Thinners. It is shown by an icon beneath Zebak and can be caught with Protect from Magic unless Zebak is level four, in which case it can land on the same tick as range projectiles. Ranked high A and always on (2022)[^a9V3c9mDf9E-1058][^a9V3c9mDf9E-1058][^a9V3c9mDf9E-1121]. In 2025: summons a meatball that chases the player and heals the boss on touch[^DaUViQQ8TL8-537]. |
| Arterial Spray | 10 raid level; increases range and healing of Zebak's blood magic; does nothing in solo (2022)[^a9V3c9mDf9E-1153]. In 2025: makes his blood magic hit harder[^DaUViQQ8TL8-537]. |
| Blood Thinners | 5 raid level; splits the large 3x3 blood spawn into three small ones. First called S tier, then high A after hearing of a change to how the spawns die (2022)[^a9V3c9mDf9E-1214]. In 2025: splits the meatball into three that all chase the player[^DaUViQQ8TL8-537]. |
| Upset Stomach | Now 15 raid level (was 5); shrinks the jug cleanse area from 5x5 to 3x3 (2022)[^a9V3c9mDf9E-1260]. Ranked high B, obnoxious rather than hard[^a9V3c9mDf9E-1275]. In 2025: the only difficult Zebak invocation, doable by standing in the right part of the room[^DaUViQQ8TL8-537][^DaUViQQ8TL8-558]; 2026: many struggle with it[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-1779]. |

In June 2025 he said the other three Zebak invocations are basically free and should always be on; the only issue is when three meatballs spawn when a jug must be exploded, and the player can [Woox walk](Woox_walking.md) or [corner-trap](Corner_trapping.md) the blood spawns[^DaUViQQ8TL8-589]. See [Invocations (Tombs of Amascut)](Invocations_(Tombs_of_Amascut).md).

## Records and notable runs

- 2022 solo 500: Zebak completed in under five minutes, which he said he had never seen before and could not do again; he described the sub-five Zebak as absurd, and chat recalled an eight-minute Zebak[^h4hf5hPQHms-1660][^h4hf5hPQHms-1693].
- [Combat Achievements](Combat_Achievements.md) note: Zebak zero prayer at level four is not close to the hardest Combat Achievement, with about five harder; it needs 85 mining to hit on the first try or a spec boost up to 85 from 82 or above[^YizVhRWXnxs-788]. He also said four-tick butterfly is not worth doing if it can be avoided because it is slow and shadow exclusive[^YizVhRWXnxs-800].
- September 2025 WoW vs OSRS raid: the group reached Zebak after Kephri, a teammate failed to wait a tick when using the water container, and players flicked range prayer for waves and tried to two-tile the wall mechanic[^QkJq2KHiKW8-948][^QkJq2KHiKW8-976][^QkJq2KHiKW8-1221].
