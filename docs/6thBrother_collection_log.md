**6thBrother collection log** is an account topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2025-10-29. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says it was fun being so spooned and doing crazy content with all those items, though it made the death more painful given he had a shadow, a Twisted bow and near-max gear.[^0_DI-SbhksU-930]

## Records

* 6thBrother was a very spooned account overall, with very little dryness; Araxxor and Rex were a little dry, and basically everything else was handed to him.[^0_DI-SbhksU-755]
* 6thBrother had the Alchemical Hydra basically completed at 701 kill count, and Araxxor done, though it went a little dry at around 1400 kill count.[^0_DI-SbhksU-785]
* Cerberus on 6thBrother was basically done except pegasian crystals at 374 kill count; Dagannoth Kings was slow and took forever to get the Berserker ring.[^0_DI-SbhksU-785]
* Gnomonkey went giga dry at Doom of Mokhaiotl on 6thBrother but still held rank one on the Doom of Mokhaiotl leaderboard, which he expects to hold until Wide's Hardcore reaches it.[^0_DI-SbhksU-785]
* 6thBrother never completed Song of the Elves, never got gauntlets, and only had a crystal seed because he bought it from Last Man Standing.[^0_DI-SbhksU-816]
* 6thBrother got 23 fire capes because he was doing the Inferno for Slayer, and had a good Inferno kill count, with the Inferno pet Jal-Nib-Rek.[^0_DI-SbhksU-816]
* 6thBrother got a dragon pickaxe from the Kalphite Queen, had a very dry K'ril Tsutsaroth log (Gnomonkey mentions 15 for the spear), and was two out of three on the Vardorvis ring but never got it.[^0_DI-SbhksU-842]
* The raids loot on 6thBrother was two Twisted bows; he had six purples in 34 kill count in Tombs of Amascut with the Tumeken's shadow, and got the Theatre of Blood pet without much else.[^0_DI-SbhksU-876]
* 6thBrother got an imbued heart on the fifth superior slayer creature, and a dragon warhammer at 600 kill count from the Lizardmen shamans.[^0_DI-SbhksU-876]
