# Grotesque Guardians

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2022-03-16 to 2022-03-19). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- To reach the Grotesque Guardians, teleport to the Slayer Tower with a Slayer ring, climb the chain, go up the stairs, then climb the fence stairs; ring the bell across the room to start the fight. ([2022-03-16 4:01](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=241))

## Requirements

- To fight the Grotesque Guardians you need 75 Slayer and a gargoyle Slayer task; Gnomonkey recommends only fighting them at max melee and ranged levels, especially ranged. ([2022-03-16 0:31](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=31))
- The Grotesque Guardians roof is unlocked by a brittle key dropped by gargoyles at 1/150; once the roof is unlocked the boss is permanently accessible. ([2022-03-16 1:02](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=62))

## Stats and numbers

- Grotesque Guardians drop two rolls per kill, with lots of ores, bars, alchables, coins, and combat potions, prayer potions or mushroom potatoes. ([2022-03-16 10:14](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=614))
- A standard Grotesque Guardians trip at max efficiency lasts around 30 minutes. ([2022-03-16 10:48](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=648))
- At max efficiency Gnomonkey gets 36 Grotesque Guardians kills per hour, which he says is an 83 hour grind on average for the pet. ([2022-03-16 10:48](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=648))
- The Grotesque Guardians granite maul, granite ring, granite gloves and granite hammer drops are all under 150k and are unexceptional rare drops, per Gnomonkey. ([2022-03-16 10:48](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=648))
- The only decent Grotesque Guardians drop is the black tourmaline core (1/500), which creates guardian boots. ([2022-03-16 10:48](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=648))
- The Jar of Stone from the Grotesque Guardians is worth about 1.2 million gp and drops at 1/5,000. ([2022-03-16 10:48](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=648))
- The Grotesque Guardians pet drops at 1/3,000; it is a flying purple gargoyle in its noon form or a huge grounded glowing orange gargoyle in its midnight form, which Gnomonkey calls one of the coolest pets in the game. ([2022-03-16 10:48](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=648))

## Tips

- The first five Grotesque Guardians kills have a short cutscene before the fight; after five kills the bell gets a quick start option that skips it. ([2022-03-16 4:01](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=241))
- Gnomonkey says to start the Grotesque Guardians fight in ranged equipment. ([2022-03-16 4:01](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=241))
- Dusk has a trample attack when fought on his own, so at no point should you stand under him in those phases; while fighting Dawn you can stand under Dusk safely. ([2022-03-16 4:34](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=274))
- Because the Grotesque Guardians fight is an instance, the loot stays on the ground practically forever, so Gnomonkey picks up only the main items he can carry during the trip and loots everything once out of supplies. ([2022-03-16 10:14](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=614))
- After the fight, Gnomonkey teleports to his house to resupply before the next Grotesque Guardians kill. ([2022-03-16 10:41](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=641))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey calls Dawn and Dusk (the Grotesque Guardians, also called grotesques, guardians or GGs) easily the hardest main Slayer boss in the game; they require a gargoyle Slayer task. ([2022-03-16 0:00](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE))
- Gnomonkey says the Grotesque Guardians' loot is not exceptional because the main rewards are tank gear (granite ring, granite gloves, guardian boots), and that in max efficiency gear you lose a little money here. ([2022-03-16 0:00](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE))
- Gnomonkey says most people kill the Grotesque Guardians for the pet and the passive granite dust, and that the guide focuses on max efficiency because they are not worth doing for loot. ([2022-03-16 0:31](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=31))
- Gnomonkey considers the Grotesque Guardians pet (a large glowing orange or flying purple gargoyle) one of the best pets in the game. ([2022-03-16 0:31](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=31))
- Gnomonkey calls Dawn and Dusk (Grotesque Guardians) genuinely the most mechanically interesting Slayer boss, with several phases, lots of gear switching, and last hits with a chally and ballista. ([2022-03-19 10:43](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=643))
- Gnomonkey says Grotesque Guardians has horrible loot, which is why many dislike it, but he is judging only on how interesting the fight is, and places it in high B tier. ([2022-03-19 11:13](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=673))
- Gnomonkey says the Grotesque Guardians fight is mostly hitting the boss and moving away from things on the ground; the heal skip is just shooting a lot and hoping, and the end is switching prayer to whatever the boss last attacked with. ([2022-03-19 11:41](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=701))
- In the final reordering Gnomonkey puts Grotesque Guardians below Zulrah in B tier. ([2022-03-19 1:32:00](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=5520))

## Trivia

- The Grotesque Guardians are fought on the roof of the Slayer Tower and require both ranged and melee to kill. ([2022-03-16 0:00](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE))
- In the Grotesque Guardians fight Dawn is the female purple flying ranged gargoyle and Dusk is the big orange melee gargoyle; the fight swaps between them over several phases, ending with Dawn killed and Dusk, who has absorbed Dawn's essence, finished off. ([2022-03-16 4:34](https://youtu.be/b_kFNIJAcGE?t=274))
