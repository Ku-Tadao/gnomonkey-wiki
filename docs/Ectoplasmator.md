**Ectoplasmator** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2022-04-05 and 2024-09-28. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Stats and numbers

* The ectoplasmator costs 250 zeal and, when killing ghosts, provides prayer XP equal to 20% of the ghost's hit points.[^2tfcS0k0OF4-267]
* The Ectoplasmator is bought from Soul Wars for 250 Zeal, or just under 300 if you do the solo method, and takes about an hour to grind, per Gnomonkey.[^-h7yElF9Z0c-418]
* With an Ectoplasmator each sulphur naga kill gives 20 Prayer XP, per Gnomonkey, for about 5k Prayer XP an hour.[^-h7yElF9Z0c-418]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey calls the ectoplasmator effect incredibly minor, useful for some minor benefit in Nightmare Zone (many monsters are ghosts) and Ankou Slayer tasks; whether it is worth a little over half an hour of zeal is up to you.[^2tfcS0k0OF4-297]
