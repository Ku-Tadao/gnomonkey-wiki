# Ectoplasmator

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2022-04-05 to 2024-09-28). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Stats and numbers

- The ectoplasmator costs 250 zeal and, when killing ghosts, provides prayer XP equal to 20% of the ghost's hit points. ([2022-04-05 4:27](https://youtu.be/2tfcS0k0OF4?t=267))
- The Ectoplasmator is bought from Soul Wars for 250 Zeal, or just under 300 if you do the solo method, and takes about an hour to grind, per Gnomonkey. ([2024-09-28 6:58](https://youtu.be/-h7yElF9Z0c?t=418))
- With an Ectoplasmator each sulphur naga kill gives 20 Prayer XP, per Gnomonkey, for about 5k Prayer XP an hour. ([2024-09-28 6:58](https://youtu.be/-h7yElF9Z0c?t=418))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey calls the ectoplasmator effect incredibly minor, useful for some minor benefit in Nightmare Zone (many monsters are ghosts) and Ankou Slayer tasks; whether it is worth a little over half an hour of zeal is up to you. ([2022-04-05 4:57](https://youtu.be/2tfcS0k0OF4?t=297))
