# Vanguards

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 5 videos (2022-03-19 to 2025-05-01). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Gnomonkey opens Vanguards by casting at the melee Vanguard, then bowing the mage if it hits big, then switching the bow to accurate for the ranger; they typically all go down right after. ([2023-02-02 5:30](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=330))
- After that he always follows the ranged Vanguard, scythes it below 70 HP while swapping to Masori for defence, steps under it after switching to range the mage Vanguard, and then attacks the melee Vanguard on a big shot, flicking both Vanguards' prayers. ([2023-02-02 5:30](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=330))
- The numbers above the Vanguards are the health they reset at: if you hit past that number it resets, and once both are at reset the numbers disappear and you just kill them. ([2023-06-14 8:45](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=525))
- There are three Vanguards (ranged, mage, melee) spawning at fixed spots in random order; each is weak to the combat-triangle style: melee the ranged one, range the mage one, and mage the melee one. ([2024-11-08 27:48](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=1668))
- If you do too much damage to a single Vanguard, all of them heal to full and the room resets. ([2024-11-08 27:48](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=1668))
- Gnomonkey fangs the ranged Vanguard, Twisted bows the mage Vanguard and Sanguinesti-staffs the melee Vanguard. ([2024-11-08 28:17](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=1697))
- Gnomonkey's Vanguard plan: use the ranged Vanguard as an anchor (it cannot move and stops attacking you if you stand under it), get it below the threshold with the fang (his max about 49), bow the mage Vanguard below 90, then mage the melee Vanguard. ([2024-11-08 29:43](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=1783))
- Gnomonkey's default plan: follow the ranged Vanguard (he is rooted and cannot hurt you if you stand underneath him), start with the ranged one, then bow to the mage Vanguard, and when they dig, go to the melee one, alternating each dig. ([2025-05-01 14:17](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=857))
- The mage Vanguard can be safe spotted by trapping it on a corner of a crystal; the melee Vanguard can be corner-trapped at other corners, but whether it aggros depends on how far it is from its spawn. ([2025-05-01 15:50](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=950))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says anything below about 110 on Vanguards is really fast and his fastest is about 50 seconds. ([2023-06-14 7:14](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=434))
- Gnomonkey's maximum Twisted bow hit on Vanguards is 83. ([2023-06-14 8:11](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=491))
- Gnomonkey notes his Vanguards time here was 230, calling it the most average Vanguards of his life. ([2023-06-14 9:36](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=576))
- Gnomonkey says the mage Vanguard is the one you reset on, because the Twisted bow can hit around 90 and the number only updates after the projectile lands, and his reset threshold in that run was 110. ([2024-11-08 28:49](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=1729))
- The melee Vanguard hits up to 16 through prayer three times per attack (potentially 48 damage) on a four-tick attack speed, so it is the last priority. ([2024-11-08 30:15](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=1815))
- There are three Vanguards, each a different colour: a melee, a ranged and a magic Vanguard, and the number on each is the damage threshold you can deal before they reset. ([2025-05-01 13:21](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=801))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey tries to have at least 80 HP by Vanguards, and with his setup he does not get a brew for them, so eating sweets while running is extremely important. ([2023-02-02 4:58](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=298))
- You always get three attacks at the start of Vanguards. ([2023-02-02 4:58](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=298))
- A plugin asterisk indicates when a Vanguard can be killed once the others are low enough; the supplies from Vanguards are completely random, the worst being three brews and no revite. ([2023-02-02 6:04](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=364))
- Gnomonkey says to flick Rigour a little at Vanguards because you only need a little prayer; it is not an exact science. ([2023-06-14 6:10](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=370))
- Gnomonkey spams sweets on the way to Vanguards because you need all the health you can get. ([2023-06-14 7:41](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=461))
- For Vanguards, Gnomonkey sets his staff on long range, bow on accurate and scythe on aggressive; with Blood Fury and Brimstone ring instead of Berserker ring (torture) you want it on accurate. ([2023-06-14 7:41](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=461))
- Gnomonkey says to take your boots off for the first hits at Vanguards, and normally to cast at the melee Vanguard. ([2023-06-14 7:42](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=462))
- Gnomonkey says you typically follow the ranger through the whole room and like swinging at a Vanguard when it is below 50. ([2023-06-14 8:11](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=491))
- At the end of Vanguards, once they are at reset, Gnomonkey follows the melee Vanguard because he can safespot it and be completely safe. ([2023-06-14 8:45](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=525))
- Gnomonkey flicks Masori in Vanguards because Ink (Vanguard) has no defence out of the armour. ([2023-06-14 9:19](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=559))
- Gnomonkey recommends the Light bearer for an extra claw spec on Vanguards, on Muttadiles and a bunch of hammers at Olm. ([2023-06-14 32:05](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=1925))
- Gnomonkey uses a Cox Editions plugin that shows a number over each Vanguard indicating how much damage you can do before it resets, and you need to know the max hit of each weapon. ([2024-11-08 28:17](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=1697))
- There are safe spots against the melee Vanguard: get it stuck on a lip, in a cave slot, or corner-trap it against a crystal and step down. ([2024-11-08 30:47](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=1847))
- The mage Vanguard does one guaranteed projectile plus two splats that are not guaranteed to hit; Gnomonkey says to keep your health high in this room. ([2024-11-08 31:47](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=1907))
- After Vanguards, pick up as much brew and revitalisation as you can, sip and drop the prayer enhance (it lasts through Vespula), and bank what you do not need. ([2024-11-08 34:17](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=2057))
- The Vanguards must be kept at roughly the same health; hitting one above its threshold makes all of them fully heal and restarts the room. ([2025-05-01 13:51](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=831))
- Gnomonkey says to stand still while hitting the ranged and mage Vanguards, as you only get hit by their ricochet rocks if you move. ([2025-05-01 15:17](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=917))
- When the numbers disappear the Vanguards are at threshold and can be killed. ([2025-05-01 16:49](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1009))
- Gnomonkey says the Vanguards are the main source of supplies for no-prep raids and give a lot of potions; he suggests summoning a thrall, picking up all the brews and refreshing overload and enhance. ([2025-05-01 17:22](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=1042))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey dislikes the Vanguards reset mechanic and would prefer it to punish you with something like meteors, saying that if it did he would put Vanguards in high A tier. ([2022-03-19 48:54](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2934))
- Gnomonkey rates Vanguards high B tier, calling it one of the better Chambers of Xeric fights with a lot of thought in keeping health similar and timing claw specs, but annoying when a hard Twisted bow shot forces a restart. ([2022-03-19 49:22](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2962))
- Gnomonkey considers Vanguards probably the hardest room of Challenge Mode besides Olm, and says you often need to risk a reset or death for speed; resetting their health or dying there is typically a reset. ([2023-02-02 4:58](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=298))
- Gnomonkey says Vanguards is the hardest room in a Chambers of Xeric raid other than Olm; they hit very hard and are very tanky. ([2024-11-08 27:03](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=1623))
- Gnomonkey considers the Vanguards the hardest room of the raid. ([2025-05-01 13:21](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=801))
- Gnomonkey says the melee Vanguard is by far the most dangerous, and recommends safe spotting it or hitting it last in the cycle. ([2025-05-01 14:17](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=857))

## Trivia

- Gnomonkey says there are three Vanguards (mage, melee, ranged) that you hit with the combat triangle and must be killed at the same time; if you do too much damage to one they reset and heal to full. ([2022-03-19 48:21](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2901))
