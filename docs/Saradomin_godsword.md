**Saradomin godsword** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2020-10-09 and 2024-07-26. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Any melee attack on the Fremennik archer is a guaranteed max hit, so a Saradomin godsword special on it heals 30 health, or 50 if Blood Fury procs.[^nEIeyY_m_gw-531]

## Tips

* At the start of a Nightmare kill you have full special attack, and it is fully restored by the totem phase.[^gDZKQ3FTuFk-123]
* The Saradomin godsword is incredibly good at The Nightmare because after the totems you can spec the sleepwalker with it; the hit always counts as max hit, giving a massive heal and prayer restore.[^gDZKQ3FTuFk-235]
* The Saradomin godsword heal on the Nightmare sleepwalker is higher if you are combat potted, wearing full melee gear and using Piety.[^gDZKQ3FTuFk-579]
* Gnomonkey leaves the Fremennik archer alive because it does only 1 damage max and acts as a walking medkit next to you that is always available for a special attack.[^nEIeyY_m_gw-531]
* A Blood Fury proc combined with a Saradomin godsword special on the archer heals about 30 HP to full in one special, according to Gnomonkey.[^nEIeyY_m_gw-1118]
* Gnomonkey uses his godsword special partway through the boss fight; he says Fang is still worse than spec off the rip followed by Death Charge.[^nEIeyY_m_gw-2415]
* On the Fremennik ranger a Saradomin godsword spec is a guaranteed max hit, healing about 40 health and restoring a lot of prayer, and if it procs a Blood fury the heal is about 60, instantly putting you at full health.[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-999]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says that without a Saradomin godsword the late waves of Colosseum become dramatically harder, and that its healing is the reason it is so good, for example going from about 60 to 90 health.[^nEIeyY_m_gw-985]
* Gnomonkey says being able to spec the Saradomin godsword more often is infinitely more valuable than Blood barrage for health in the Colosseum, unlike the Inferno.[^EZs7lRnEL0Y-999]
