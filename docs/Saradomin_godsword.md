# Saradomin godsword

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2020-10-09 to 2024-07-26). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | item |
| **Videos** | 3 (first 2020-10-09, latest 2024-07-26) |

## Stats and numbers

- Any melee attack on the Fremennik archer is a guaranteed max hit, so a Saradomin godsword special on it heals 30 health, or 50 if Blood Fury procs. ([2024-03-26 8:51](https://youtu.be/nEIeyY_m_gw?t=531))

## Tips

- At the start of a Nightmare kill you have full special attack, and it is fully restored by the totem phase. ([2020-10-09 2:03](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=123))
- Gnomonkey says the Saradomin godsword is incredibly good at The Nightmare because after the totems you can spec the sleepwalker with it; the hit always counts as max hit, giving a massive heal and prayer restore. ([2020-10-09 3:55](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=235))
- The Saradomin godsword heal on the Nightmare sleepwalker is higher if you are combat potted, wearing full melee gear and using Piety. ([2020-10-09 9:39](https://youtu.be/gDZKQ3FTuFk?t=579))
- Gnomonkey leaves the Fremennik archer alive because it does only 1 damage max and acts as a walking medkit next to you that is always available for a special attack. ([2024-03-26 8:51](https://youtu.be/nEIeyY_m_gw?t=531))
- A Blood Fury proc combined with a Saradomin godsword special on the archer heals about 30 HP to full in one special, according to Gnomonkey. ([2024-03-26 18:38](https://youtu.be/nEIeyY_m_gw?t=1118))
- Gnomonkey uses his godsword special partway through the boss fight; he says Fang is still worse than spec off the rip followed by Death Charge. ([2024-03-26 40:15](https://youtu.be/nEIeyY_m_gw?t=2415))
- Gnomonkey says on the Fremennik ranger a Saradomin godsword spec is a guaranteed max hit, healing about 40 health and restoring a lot of prayer, and if it procs a Blood fury the heal is about 60, instantly putting you at full health. ([2024-07-26 16:39](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=999))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says that without a Saradomin godsword the late waves of Colosseum become dramatically harder, and that its healing is the reason it is so good, for example going from about 60 to 90 health. ([2024-03-26 16:25](https://youtu.be/nEIeyY_m_gw?t=985))
- Gnomonkey says being able to spec the Saradomin godsword more often is infinitely more valuable than Blood barrage for health in the Colosseum, unlike the Inferno. ([2024-07-26 16:39](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=999))
