# Chambers of Xeric: Challenge Mode

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2023-02-02 to 2024-11-08). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

|---|---|
| **Type** | raid |

## Methods

- Gnomonkey runs the Surge Thrall setup: the standard spellbook up through the Ice Demon, then Max cape or Magic cape to swap to the Arceuus spellbook for thralls, which he calls by far the fastest setup. ([2023-02-02 1:57](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=117))
- Gnomonkey kills the Ice Demon in the Chambers of Xeric Challenge Mode speedrun with an Inquisitor's armour set and a dragon warhammer. ([2023-05-02 1:29](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo?t=89))

## Requirements

- Challenge Mode requires valuable items used almost nowhere else, such as Inquisitor's armour and mace and a harmonised nightmare staff for the Ice Demon. ([2023-02-02 1:02](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=62))
- 95 Thieving is needed to receive three grubs from the chest in Challenge Mode, and some setups use a Thieving cape to boost to 100 for a chance at four grubs. ([2023-02-02 1:02](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=62))
- 96 Woodcutting is needed for a chance at 8 kindling from the Ice Demon, and 99 is preferred to speed up the Muttadile tree. ([2023-02-02 1:02](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=62))
- In Gnomonkey's setup you need 99 Mining or you lose a max hit, and you need an absurd amount of purple sweets to be competitive. ([2023-02-02 1:30](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=90))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says you eat more purple sweets in a solo Challenge Mode than in a solo Theatre of Blood, but you do pay for them with the drops received with some profit on the side. ([2023-02-02 1:30](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=90))
- Gnomonkey says he can run at most five Challenge Mode raids a day, typically doing them for a few hours on stream, and jokes that Grandmasters of Combat Achievements should get a bonus to cape swaps. ([2023-02-02 1:57](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=117))
- Gnomonkey is looking for sub 13 minutes at the hole on the last floor, with his best ever being just barely sub 12 minutes. ([2023-02-02 9:08](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=548))
- Gnomonkey's pace at the time of reaching floor 3 was 12:40, which he considered not great; he says low twelves are nice and sub twelves are very fast. ([2023-05-02 12:45](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo?t=765))
- In Challenge Mode the Twisted bow's damage cap is even higher than in regular Chambers because enemy stats are higher; Gnomonkey says it maxes around 96 on quite a few bosses. ([2024-11-08 1:00](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=60))

## Tips

- Challenge Mode always has the exact same room layout and always starts with Tekton. ([2023-02-02 2:30](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=150))
- Gnomonkey describes Chambers of Xeric Challenge Mode (CM) as a regular Chambers of Xeric with every room's stats cranked: everything hits harder, is tankier and has more health. ([2024-11-08 0:00](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0))
- In CM the boss order and layout are always identical, and each room (starting with Tekton) drops supplies such as overload, prayer enhance and restore, so you can bring few potions and farm more as you go. ([2024-11-08 2:27](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=147))
- Gnomonkey says that with the Twisted bow loadout from his first run he made time (the solo CM time) even while AFK-ing about 20-30 minutes explaining rooms, and that the Grandmaster time of 38:30 is feasible with this setup. ([2024-11-08 1:09:20](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=4160))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey considers Challenge Mode Chambers of Xeric genuinely one of the toughest pieces of content in the game, and had not learned it until recently, when he began speedrunning it on Twitch. ([2023-02-02 0:00](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg))
- Gnomonkey says Challenge Mode raids are not the best for money, and that most of the skill is preparing for the following room and keeping inventory and banking under control, a skill not used much in other raids. ([2023-02-02 0:00](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg))
- Gnomonkey says Challenge Mode has by far the highest gear and stat requirements of any raid. ([2023-02-02 0:32](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=32))
- Gnomonkey says anyone running Challenge Mode is definitely not doing it for the GP. ([2023-02-02 1:57](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=117))
- After a raid Gnomonkey tries to remember that purples do not exist: his goal is a good time rather than paying back the hundreds of sweets used. ([2023-02-02 14:59](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=899))
- Gnomonkey reviewed the raid backwards after finishing: Olm, Vasa and Guardians were disgusting (very good), Mystics pretty good, Muttadile kind of bad, Vespula, thieving and Tekton average, and Vanguards and Shamans bad. ([2023-05-02 28:08](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo?t=1688))
- Gnomonkey notes the benefit of CM over regular Chambers is that there is no scouting and you always do the same raid, but that it is harder. ([2024-11-08 0:31](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=31))
- Gnomonkey says you should not enter Challenge Mode without a Twisted bow; with only a Bow of Faerdhinen he says you will have a bad time and should do regular Chambers instead, which gives more loot per hour. ([2024-11-08 1:00](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=60))
- Gnomonkey says you do not need a Scythe of Vitur for CM: because everything is so tanky, the Abyssal tentacle-style comparison is that a Osmumten's fang is effectively the same as a scythe, and a Lance is basically the same as a scythe at Olm. ([2024-11-08 1:45](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=105))
- Gnomonkey says CM is similar to regular Chambers, and the difference is whether you can quickly fix problems when Olm throws a lot of garbage at you; in CM you get hit much harder when things go wrong. ([2024-11-08 1:08:13](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=4093))
- Gnomonkey says without at least a Twisted bow, Sanguinesti staff and a decent mage setup you should just farm regular Chambers, because the only reason to do CM is the kit chance and normal raids give better purples even factoring in scouting time. ([2024-11-08 1:09:03](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=4143))

## Records

- Gnomonkey says if Olm is pacing sub 28:30 he reaches Olm in under 20 minutes on the clock, and he was still three seconds off a sub 28:30 time when this video was made. ([2023-02-02 13:29](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=809))
- The Chambers of Xeric Challenge Mode solo world record is 26:38, held by rot salad, per the video description. ([2023-05-02 0:00](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo))
- Gnomonkey's 27:04 run was a new personal best in solo Challenge Mode; he reads out the change as 2751 to 2704.2 and says he is still ranked sixth. ([2023-05-02 29:15](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo?t=1755))

## Events

- Gnomonkey says that without the six zero hits in a row on lances in the middle of Olm and a bit less trolling, the run would likely have been a world record Olm at the very least. ([2023-05-02 27:35](https://youtu.be/syNH7SASbTo?t=1655))

## Trivia

- Challenge Mode gives extra kits at the end of the raid and bonus points if you make the time; in solo, Gnomonkey says the time to beat is about 1 hour 15 minutes. ([2024-11-08 0:00](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0))
