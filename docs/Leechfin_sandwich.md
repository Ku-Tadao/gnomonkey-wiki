## Stats and numbers

- Eating the leech fin sandwich heals 16, increases your next attack delay by two ticks, and sets your next eat delay to nine ticks, so you can eat only one in the time you could eat three fish. ([2026-01-25 9:32](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=572))
- The leech fin sandwich's next successful melee hit heals an additional 16 HP, but the secondary heal must be triggered within 12 seconds or it is lost, for 32 health if you melee. ([2026-01-25 9:59](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=599))
- The Leechfin sandwich is a two-tick food that heals 16 HP and heals an additional 16 HP when you melee, but you must wait a full nine ticks before eating another. ([2026-02-17 9:43](https://youtu.be/kKccfwAJkh8?t=583))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey finds the leech fin sandwich gimmicky and confusing, thinks it would be super good food at Theatre of Blood and Vardorvis, and wishes it was tuned into a simpler delayed heal. ([2026-01-25 10:32](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=632))
- Gnomonkey does not think the Leechfin sandwich adds anything to the game, says it just rewards melee-specific bosses such as Vardorvis, and thinks combat-style-specific foods would confuse new players, though it is not the end of the world; he would not vote yes. ([2026-02-17 10:36](https://youtu.be/kKccfwAJkh8?t=636))

## Events

- Jagex said it is keeping the Leechfin sandwich's quirky design because it gives flexibility to differentiate food effects by combat style in future. ([2026-02-17 10:24](https://youtu.be/kKccfwAJkh8?t=624))
