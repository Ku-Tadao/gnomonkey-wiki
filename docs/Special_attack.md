## Tips

- Gnomonkey says that a character doing Corp with 30 million gp gear probably lacks the elite Desert diary and a house pool, so they cannot restore special attack. ([2024-05-16 16:08](https://youtu.be/BShbhmfpnZE?t=968))
- Gnomonkey notes an Ironman can use Rimmington house teleports, but he does not think Ironmen can use another player's house, and there is no way to get spec back without a pool. ([2024-05-16 16:08](https://youtu.be/BShbhmfpnZE?t=968))
- Gnomonkey says the Dragon dagger special is an extremely good spec on Tormented demons, because the guaranteed hit makes this inaccurate hard-hitting spec effective, with Burning claws best if you have them and the Abyssal dagger slightly better than the Dragon dagger. ([2025-03-04 3:26](https://youtu.be/TgWNIkkUpdg?t=206))
- Gnomonkey says Burning claws, dropped by Tormented demons, are amazing early and a great best spec weapon for a long time on an iron, especially with bad gear. ([2025-03-04 15:20](https://youtu.be/TgWNIkkUpdg?t=920))
- Gnomonkey says sipping from a spec pool removes the Surge potion cooldown immediately, so at any boss you spec-tab you can use a dose every kill (for example Vorkath, Zulrah with scrolls or Sire). ([2025-03-29 10:10](https://youtu.be/W1OX5PVaPxs?t=610))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey notes there are not many spec-tab bosses now because recent design places bosses far from teleports, so the potion gives bonus value only in a limited number of cases. ([2025-03-29 10:38](https://youtu.be/W1OX5PVaPxs?t=638))
- Gnomonkey says he does not think Surge potions are worth an inventory slot at the Colosseum, where inventories are crammed, as it is only an extra half a claw. ([2025-03-29 10:38](https://youtu.be/W1OX5PVaPxs?t=638))
- Gnomonkey says Chambers of Xeric has so much downtime that Surge potions would only matter for hammers at Ice demon-style spec rooms like Ulm-adjacent rooms or extreme speed runs, and he does not see them being brought for normal Chambers, calling them utter min-maxing. ([2025-03-29 11:09](https://youtu.be/W1OX5PVaPxs?t=669))
- Gnomonkey says special attacks are some of the best forms of skill expression because they often require bringing multiple items, and that if Jagex adds power creep, spec weapons are a good way to do it. ([2025-03-29 13:33](https://youtu.be/W1OX5PVaPxs?t=813))
- Gnomonkey says he would rather have interesting spec weapons than weapons that simply hit hard like Tumeken's shadow or the Twisted bow. ([2025-03-29 14:04](https://youtu.be/W1OX5PVaPxs?t=844))
- Gnomonkey says a wide variety of spec weapons makes it easier to design interesting encounters and he wants Jagex to keep adding unique spec weapons and ways to restore or boost spec energy. ([2025-03-29 16:14](https://youtu.be/W1OX5PVaPxs?t=974))
