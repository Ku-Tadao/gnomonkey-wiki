**Special attack** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2024-05-16 and 2025-03-29. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Tips

* A character doing Corp with 30 million gp gear probably lacks the elite Desert diary and a house pool, so they cannot restore special attack.[^BShbhmfpnZE-968]
* An Ironman can use Rimmington house teleports, but he does not think Ironmen can use another player's house, and there is no way to get spec back without a pool.[^BShbhmfpnZE-968]
* The Dragon dagger special is an extremely good spec on Tormented demons, because the guaranteed hit makes this inaccurate hard-hitting spec effective, with Burning claws best if you have them and the Abyssal dagger slightly better than the Dragon dagger.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-206]
* Burning claws, dropped by Tormented demons, are amazing early and a great best spec weapon for a long time on an iron, especially with bad gear.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-920]
* Sipping from a spec pool removes the Surge potion cooldown immediately, so at any boss you spec-tab you can use a dose every kill (for example Vorkath, Zulrah with scrolls or Sire).[^W1OX5PVaPxs-610]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey notes there are not many spec-tab bosses now because recent design places bosses far from teleports, so the potion gives bonus value only in a limited number of cases.[^W1OX5PVaPxs-638]
* Gnomonkey says he does not think Surge potions are worth an inventory slot at the Colosseum, where inventories are crammed, as it is only an extra half a claw.[^W1OX5PVaPxs-638]
* Gnomonkey says Chambers of Xeric has so much downtime that Surge potions would only matter for hammers at Ice demon-style spec rooms like Ulm-adjacent rooms or extreme speed runs, and he does not see them being brought for normal Chambers, calling them utter min-maxing.[^W1OX5PVaPxs-669]
* Gnomonkey says special attacks are some of the best forms of skill expression because they often require bringing multiple items, and that if Jagex adds power creep, spec weapons are a good way to do it.[^W1OX5PVaPxs-813]
* Gnomonkey says he would rather have interesting spec weapons than weapons that simply hit hard like Tumeken's shadow or the Twisted bow.[^W1OX5PVaPxs-844]
* Gnomonkey says a wide variety of spec weapons makes it easier to design interesting encounters and he wants Jagex to keep adding unique spec weapons and ways to restore or boost spec energy.[^W1OX5PVaPxs-974]
