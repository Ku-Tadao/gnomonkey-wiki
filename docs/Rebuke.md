# Rebuke

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2023-03-07 to 2023-04-19). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

|---|---|
| **Type** | other |

## Stats and numbers

- Rebuke is an overhead that reflects 15% of damage taken but increases damage taken by 25%. ([2023-03-07 5:50](https://youtu.be/trol59VccdE?t=350))
- At Kalphite Queen, Gnomonkey says Rebuke would raise her max hit from 31 to 38, allowing a maximum recoil of 34 instead of 23 with Vengeance and the prayer combined. ([2023-03-07 6:22](https://youtu.be/trol59VccdE?t=382))
- Rebuke raised Gnomonkey's max hit to 100 when paired with Vengeance on Tekton, which he calls very substantial added damage. ([2023-04-19 4:33](https://youtu.be/8VyPkG4sEwQ?t=273))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey calls Rebuke a great idea and the Vengeance buff he wanted in his own prayer suggestions: paired with Vengeance it raises damage taken and so Vengeance's damage, and adds recoil on top, which would make Vengeance meta over thralls in quite a few places. ([2023-03-07 5:50](https://youtu.be/trol59VccdE?t=350))
- Gnomonkey says the new offensive prayers all feel in a good place damage-wise and should remain as is. ([2023-04-19 4:02](https://youtu.be/8VyPkG4sEwQ?t=242))
- Gnomonkey really likes Rebuke: its usage is rare but it can do cool things, and he loved Venge bombing Tekton with it. ([2023-04-19 4:02](https://youtu.be/8VyPkG4sEwQ?t=242))
