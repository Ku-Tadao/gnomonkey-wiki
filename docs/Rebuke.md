**Rebuke** is an other topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2023-03-07 and 2023-04-19. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Stats and numbers

* Rebuke is an overhead that reflects 15% of damage taken but increases damage taken by 25%.[^trol59VccdE-350]
* At Kalphite Queen, Gnomonkey says Rebuke would raise her max hit from 31 to 38, allowing a maximum recoil of 34 instead of 23 with Vengeance and the prayer combined.[^trol59VccdE-382]
* Rebuke raised Gnomonkey's max hit to 100 when paired with Vengeance on Tekton, which he calls very substantial added damage.[^8VyPkG4sEwQ-273]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey calls Rebuke a great idea and the Vengeance buff he wanted in his own prayer suggestions: paired with Vengeance it raises damage taken and so Vengeance's damage, and adds recoil on top, which would make Vengeance meta over thralls in quite a few places.[^trol59VccdE-350]
* Gnomonkey says the new offensive prayers all feel in a good place damage-wise and should remain as is.[^8VyPkG4sEwQ-242]
* Gnomonkey really likes Rebuke: its usage is rare but it can do cool things, and he loved Venge bombing Tekton with it.[^8VyPkG4sEwQ-242]
