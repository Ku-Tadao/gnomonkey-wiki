**Ice Demon** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 5 videos between 2023-02-02 and 2025-05-01. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* For the Ice Demon, Gnomonkey drops two potions, equips his axe, drops his tinderbox, deposits everything and gets chopping.[^F0qIr7azCwg-225]
* If the Ice Demon dies before the five minute mark, Gnomonkey swaps spellbooks and moves on to Shamans.[^F0qIr7azCwg-271]
* Gnomonkey fills three braziers for Ice Demon: the first with as much as you can, at least 24 kindling (28 is the most you can hold), the second the same way, and the third with about ten.[^wxGBi89vE_s-2914]
* For Ice Demon, Gnomonkey drops a few potions on the ground and starts chopping immediately, aiming for 24-28 kindling before depositing, with at least 10 kindling needed.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-1240]
* Gnomonkey uses an Elder maul on Ice Demon and lures it by standing two tiles from the exit and flinching in and out, which is the same cycle as Vespula and uses almost no prayer because the 3x3 mage AoE misses.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-1301]
* The Ice Demon room is a puzzle where you fill braziers with wood until he wakes up; the trees have a five-tick chop cycle, so as long as you are on a tree on the fifth tick you get wood and can move between trees.[^jdvQVwg8niM-500]
* Gnomonkey puts 24 to 28 kindling into each of the first two braziers (the most you can hold is 28), then about 10 into a third.[^jdvQVwg8niM-559]

## Stats and numbers

* Dragon warhammer on the Ice Demon works differently and actually increases your magic accuracy, so missing the hammer can often be the difference between continuing and resetting.[^F0qIr7azCwg-271]
* Ice Demon is on a three-tick attack cycle, so you move every time he does the animation, though sometimes it does not show, and the AoE attack needs you to move two tiles.[^wxGBi89vE_s-2940]
* Against Ice Demon all damage is one-third effective, and fire spells deal 1.5 times normal damage, so Gnomonkey says using Surge saves about a minute.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-1357]
* The Ice Demon is on a three-tick cycle, so you need to move every three ticks, and he will mage you if you are not praying range.[^jdvQVwg8niM-592]

## Tips

* Chopping is a five tick action, so you can move between chop ticks to spread out chops and reduce the chance of chopping the tree closest to the bank.[^F0qIr7azCwg-225]
* Once he has dumped for the third time he drops the tinderbox and removes the axe, placing it back where it was in his inventory so his inventory stays exactly as it was.[^F0qIr7azCwg-241]
* In this room he banks as many items as he can for Vanguards; the room is more about banking skill and inventory management than anything else.[^F0qIr7azCwg-241]
* At Ice Demon, Gnomonkey drops his tinderbox and equips an axe in a particular inventory corner, which keeps the inventory in place for banking afterwards.[^8JxarzSiiME-210]
* Chopping the Ice Demon roots/trees happens every five ticks; you can move between trees as long as you are at the tree when you chop, which gives a lower chance of chopping the sapling you want to keep up.[^8JxarzSiiME-210]
* You need all but three kindling before you go, so it is roughly two and a half braziers' worth.[^8JxarzSiiME-242]
* Gnomonkey wants the Ice Demon to spawn at around low four minutes, and he uses the light bearer to spec twice or until he hits.[^8JxarzSiiME-308]
* Gnomonkey's cutoff out of Ice Demon is five minutes when going for a record; if it is slower he would normally reset.[^8JxarzSiiME-339]
* An axe chop cycle is five ticks and you can move three tiles per chop if you move as soon as the XP drop happens, so you can chop on the way to Ice Demon.[^wxGBi89vE_s-2886]
* To pray ranged against Ice Demon because if you do not, it will sometimes mage you.[^wxGBi89vE_s-2968]
* Surge saves about a minute at Ice Demon but means using daily cape swaps (to switch to thralls after Ice Demon); Gnomonkey says you can use up the capes and still do this setup.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-1217]
* You receive kindling every five ticks while chopping, even if you move between trees, so Gnomonkey moves between trees to avoid chopping down the one that would block the exit.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-1240]
* Ice Demon drops enough supplies for prepping, so you do not need to farm the crabs room for supplies.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-1397]
* The Ice Demon's magic defence is affected by his normal defence, so Gnomonkey hammers him to make himself hit more often.[^jdvQVwg8niM-559]
* Gnomonkey banks the first chest items, such as the peanuts, after the Ice Demon to make space.[^jdvQVwg8niM-559]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says in a speedrun it is better to have the south pillar gone and optimal to have both pillars gone.[^8JxarzSiiME-274]
* Gnomonkey runs Lightbearer partly because it gives an extra Ice Demon spec, and many of his runs end because he does not hit a hammer on Ice Demon.[^8JxarzSiiME-2064]
