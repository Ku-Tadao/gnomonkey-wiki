**PvM skill pillars** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2022-05-11. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Gnomonkey calls inventory management, movement and prayer management the three main pillars of PvM skill; they are not all-encompassing but cover the majority of skills you can acquire.[^IuHh4gfs39o-64]
* Gnomonkey adds a secret fourth pillar called 'rhythm': an understanding of attack cycles and the tick system in general.[^IuHh4gfs39o-418]

## Stats and numbers

* As an extreme example, Gnomonkey says full Armadyl is typically only a 0.5% DPS increase over Blessed dragonhide at 80m for the set.[^IuHh4gfs39o-55]
* Old School RuneScape is a rhythm game at 100 beats per minute (0.6 seconds per beat), which matters at every encounter, scaling or bossing.[^IuHh4gfs39o-418]

## Tips

* Rhythm cannot be learned at one specific place; it is gradual, and even tick-manipulation skilling methods can carry over to PvM. Examples where people say you 'just need to get a rhythm' include Verzik tanking, Xarpus scythe walking, Olm cycles and 3-tick 4-granite.[^IuHh4gfs39o-418]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says higher-level PvM is often touted as very difficult to get into, but with the right attitude, gear and resources learning PvM is usually just a matter of jumping in.[^IuHh4gfs39o-0]
* Gnomonkey says stats are far greater for DPS than gear 99% of the time: each Strength level is roughly a 1-2% DPS increase depending on whether it gives a max hit, and the same applies to Ranged and Magic.[^IuHh4gfs39o-33]
* Gnomonkey says not training stats in Nightmare Zone to decent levels before PvM is 'griefing' yourself and your teammates because they are such simple stats to train.[^IuHh4gfs39o-33]
