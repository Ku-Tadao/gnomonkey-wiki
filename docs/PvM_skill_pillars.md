# PvM skill pillars

## Methods

- Gnomonkey calls inventory management, movement and prayer management the three main pillars of PvM skill; they are not all-encompassing but cover the majority of skills you can acquire. ([2022-05-11 1:04](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=64))
- Gnomonkey adds a secret fourth pillar called 'rhythm': an understanding of attack cycles and the tick system in general. ([2022-05-11 6:58](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=418))

## Stats and numbers

- As an extreme example, Gnomonkey says full Armadyl is typically only a 0.5% DPS increase over Blessed dragonhide at 80m for the set. ([2022-05-11 0:55](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=55))
- Gnomonkey says Old School RuneScape is a rhythm game at 100 beats per minute (0.6 seconds per beat), which matters at every encounter, scaling or bossing. ([2022-05-11 6:58](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=418))

## Tips

- Rhythm cannot be learned at one specific place; it is gradual, and even tick-manipulation skilling methods can carry over to PvM. Examples where people say you 'just need to get a rhythm' include Verzik tanking, Xarpus scythe walking, Olm cycles and 3-tick 4-granite. ([2022-05-11 6:58](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=418))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says higher-level PvM is often touted as very difficult to get into, but with the right attitude, gear and resources learning PvM is usually just a matter of jumping in. ([2022-05-11 0:00](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o))
- Gnomonkey says stats are far greater for DPS than gear 99% of the time: each Strength level is roughly a 1-2% DPS increase depending on whether it gives a max hit, and the same applies to Ranged and Magic. ([2022-05-11 0:33](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=33))
- Gnomonkey says not training stats in Nightmare Zone to decent levels before PvM is 'griefing' yourself and your teammates because they are such simple stats to train. ([2022-05-11 0:33](https://youtu.be/IuHh4gfs39o?t=33))
