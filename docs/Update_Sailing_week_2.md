# Update: Sailing week 2

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 1 video (2025-12-03). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey opens the video sarcastically repeating complaints that people who whined about Sailing XP rates ruined the skill, noting the real public opinion is the opposite: players felt Sailing was being nerfed. ([2025-12-03 0:29](https://youtu.be/6BvUKVInPTs?t=29))
- Gnomonkey says the update as a whole is 'a big stinker' and that Sailing is worse this week than it was the week before, with players calling for a revert. ([2025-12-03 13:43](https://youtu.be/6BvUKVInPTs?t=823))
- Gnomonkey thinks Jagex rushed Sailing out for November right before the holiday break so Jagex staff cannot make further changes until around January, and suspects greed. ([2025-12-03 18:03](https://youtu.be/6BvUKVInPTs?t=1083))
- Gnomonkey says Jagex should explain their thinking before updates land so the community can give feedback in advance, rather than nerfs arriving 'like a left hook out of a dark alleyway'. ([2025-12-03 19:08](https://youtu.be/6BvUKVInPTs?t=1148))
- Gnomonkey says Jagex tends to nerf strong methods instantly while leaving weak or over-nerfed ones unchanged for weeks, which makes players feel they should abuse things early before they get nerfed. ([2025-12-03 19:39](https://youtu.be/6BvUKVInPTs?t=1179))

## Events

- The Sailing week 2 update (around 3 December 2025) nerfed essentially every Sailing training method, which Gnomonkey summarises as 'the answer is everything'. ([2025-12-03 0:00](https://youtu.be/6BvUKVInPTs))
- Jagex posted a follow-up update the same day in response to negative feedback, saying they planned further tweaks and would publish a more detailed post the next day on where rates are expected to sit. ([2025-12-03 0:59](https://youtu.be/6BvUKVInPTs?t=59))
- Jagex's response did not revert the changes; they said they would look at improving self-sort XP and give the Gwenith Glide trials some extra XP back, with no specific plans for port task weeks. ([2025-12-03 12:14](https://youtu.be/6BvUKVInPTs?t=734))
