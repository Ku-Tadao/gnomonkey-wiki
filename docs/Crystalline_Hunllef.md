**Crystalline Hunllef** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2019-07-26. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Stats and numbers

* After the 10 minutes expire the player is teleported directly to the Hunllef's room and forced to fight; there is no way to exit once inside, and the player may also enter manually before the timer expires.[^guGTfLIzYBc-214]
* Hunllef can hit up to 35 with his basic attacks, and praying either magic or ranged negates half of the incoming damage.[^guGTfLIzYBc-245]
* Floor tiles turn blue and then orange; blue tiles only indicate where orange will appear, and orange tiles damage the player each tick for around 10.[^guGTfLIzYBc-245]

## Tips

* The boss switches to a full protection prayer after being attacked seven times by a different weapon than it is protecting against, forcing the player to bring two different weapons.[^guGTfLIzYBc-245]
* The Hunllef can use every combat style but only melees if approached, which is why Gnomonkey finds the halberd underwhelming.[^guGTfLIzYBc-245]
* The Hunllef throws tornadoes that slowly approach the player and do the same damage as orange tiles; later in the fight he throws two and then three at once, which can stack and do insane damage.[^guGTfLIzYBc-278]
* The Hunllef can randomly disable the player's prayers, requiring them to be turned back on, and all of his attacks can be used at the same time.[^guGTfLIzYBc-278]
* Gnomonkey keeps Protect from Magic on at all times, noting the boss seems to use magic more often, and swaps between Rigor and Augury (or Eagle Eye and Mystic Eye) to greatly increase DPS.[^guGTfLIzYBc-393]
* Gnomonkey recommends using F keys to swap between inventory and prayers for this fight, and says RuneLite has a plugin to rebind F keys to the top row of number keys.[^guGTfLIzYBc-393]
