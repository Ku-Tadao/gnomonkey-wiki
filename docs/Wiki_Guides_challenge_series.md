**Wiki Guides challenge series** is a challenge topic that Gnomonkey covers in 9 videos between 2024-05-10 and 2025-09-06. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* The Wiki Guides challenge is to kill a boss using the worst gear that the OSRS Wiki's setup template suggests, deliberately in bad faith, then redo it with a better setup that uses the same amount of GP to show where the wiki is misleading.[^KIII4sXUDTk-29]
* To choose the improved setup, Gnomonkey takes the total budget of the wiki's bad setup (which he says is usually fairly high because of the poor suggestions) and spends it on a much easier setup.[^KIII4sXUDTk-1000]
* On old wiki setups slots are often missing; Gnomonkey's rule for this episode was that any empty slot stays empty, so he wore nothing in his glove, boot and helmet slots.[^qCsJAdpSeTU-26]

## Stats and numbers

* The wiki's Whisperer downgrades include Infinity hat and Farseer, a prayer mitre as a downgrade for the Farseer helmet, an Amulet of power instead of a glory, a skill cape instead of a Mage Arena 1 cape, a combat bracelet instead of Barrows gloves, Wizard boots and a Seers ring.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-29]
* The wiki's worst Vardorvis downgrades include Amulet of strength or Amulet of power, a regen bracelet instead of Barrows gloves, rune boots, a Lightbearer and a Chali (as transcribed) special attack weapon.[^hI6cALUKOzQ-0]

## Tips

* Gnomonkey brings thralls as he always does in this series.[^hI6cALUKOzQ-237]
* The hairy cyclopes in the Warriors Guild are a higher level, so do not kill those.[^WvmEY9v6AnI-56]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey notes the wiki lists Mage's book as a downgrade to Malediction ward even though Mage's book is more expensive and harder to obtain.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-55]
* Gnomonkey says the wiki's highest-tier Whisperer gear is fairly accurate (Slayer helm or Ancestral, max mage, Shadow, Eternal boots, Torm, Magus), though Eldritch nightmare staff special over a Cursed sceptre is questionable.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-109]
* Gnomonkey says the point of the series is not to attack the wiki, and that some people were offended; he says it is a fun series meant to shed light on bad downgrade entries so the wiki can clean them up.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-454]
* Gnomonkey says the wiki's K'ril page downgrades straight to a skill cape, whereas other magic setups often downgrade imbued cape, a 'majorina' cape (as transcribed), fire cape or Inferno cape in that order before a skill cape.[^_nMz6X90hb0-585]
* Gnomonkey questions why the wiki setup includes things that can autocast when users would just face-tank K'ril and need to cast manually if they run under him and eat.[^_nMz6X90hb0-600]
* Gnomonkey says he found the challenge more fun than expected and the upgrade quite a bit better than he thought.[^_nMz6X90hb0-621]
* Gnomonkey says the Zombie axe is the first time he has ever used one, and notes the wiki lists an Elysian spirit shield as an option for accounts without a rune defender or Dragonfire shield even though it should not be on the list.[^hI6cALUKOzQ-27]
* Gnomonkey says the wiki gives slash entries in its gear table which he treats as 'use the worst one'.[^hI6cALUKOzQ-149]
* Gnomonkey says he asks why the wiki suggests a leaf-bladed spear for Corp and that nobody knows.[^BShbhmfpnZE-342]
* Gnomonkey says the series is not meant to attack the OSRS Wiki, which he considers the best video game wiki he knows of; his criticism is only aimed at the gearing template.[^KIII4sXUDTk-876]
* Gnomonkey says he does not hate the wiki and only thinks it could be better in the setup/gear section; he gets frequent comments claiming he does.[^KIII4sXUDTk-1963]
* Gnomonkey says digging through revision history for the worst page variant would make any wiki page look bad, so this is just a fun concept for an easy-to-find boss challenge and not a criticism of the wiki.[^qCsJAdpSeTU-0]
* Gnomonkey says he will not revise the wiki himself because it takes a lot of time and people would revert anything he adds out of spite, and he finds the table format unhelpful outside of Max, Mid, Min, Iron and Pure tiers.[^qCsJAdpSeTU-0]

## Records

* The first Whisperer kill in the wiki's worst downgraded gear took 448.6 seconds (about 7:28), which Gnomonkey calls not that bad all considered.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-578]

## Events

* In this episode Gnomonkey fights the Whisperer using the worst possible gear downgrades listed on the OSRS Wiki's Whisperer page.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-0]
* This episode of Gnomonkey's Wiki Guides series uses the wiki's worst allowed K'ril Tsutsaroth gear: Skull sceptre, glory, skill cape and Wizard boots.[^_nMz6X90hb0-0]
* For Vardorvis, Gnomonkey follows the wiki's worst possible downgrades, including a skill cape, tank body and legs, a Zombie axe and an Elysian spirit shield.[^hI6cALUKOzQ-0]
* Gnomonkey tests Amulet of strength versus Amulet of power on a dummy and finds Amulet of power better even with the max hit loss, so he runs the amulet of power.[^hI6cALUKOzQ-31]
* Gnomonkey brings both amulets for the joke because the wiki's wording does not rule it out; he says he will use the Amulet of strength for the final hits at the special attack.[^hI6cALUKOzQ-185]
* In the Corporeal Beast episode Gnomonkey follows the wiki's low-price-range setup, which he describes as horrendous and not worth it even for an Ironman.[^BShbhmfpnZE-0]
* He has mostly ignored the wiki's text descriptions earlier in the series but follows the Corp one because it matters.[^BShbhmfpnZE-27]
* The most anticipated wiki setup left is the Tombs of Amascut one, which he already did on stream but is saving for later, plus maybe a Challenge Mode Chambers of Xeric one.[^BShbhmfpnZE-60]
* He failed to get a kill in the original low-drain setup and so stamps it as technically possible but stupid before trying a real setup.[^BShbhmfpnZE-475]
* The wiki's worst downgrade for Vorkath is a Rune defender, which he did not own, so he first grinds one at the Warriors Guild.[^WvmEY9v6AnI-0]
* Gnomonkey plans Vardorvis-style follow-ups including Muspah next, but says he will avoid Wilderness bosses on stream.[^WvmEY9v6AnI-0]
* Gnomonkey grinds the Rune defender at the Warriors Guild cyclopes using the Vorkath downgrade gear with an attack cape, finding granite gloves very bad.[^WvmEY9v6AnI-27]
* He picked the melee Justiciar setup over the blowpipe or crossbow options partly because the wiki lists a Armadyl godsword as its worst spec, which raised the funniness value.[^WvmEY9v6AnI-150]
* The Rune defender drop that Gnomonkey expected to take a long time took only about 10 seconds.[^WvmEY9v6AnI-678]
* He did not learn about Mixed hide until he did the Tombs of Amascut wiki guide, which he planned to put up relatively soon after this stream.[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-1565]
* The wiki-gear Tombs of Amascut run he was preparing as a wiki guide was super difficult because he had to use really terrible gear.[^Mg9hcqcFC1U-1612]
* This video is the finale of Gnomonkey's Wiki Guides series, where he completes content using the OSRS Wiki's recommended gear; his previous video attempted a Phosani's Nightmare kill using the worst possible downgrades.[^17ynF_0ltPc-0]
* The wiki's Tombs of Amascut guide lists three setups (melee, ranged and magic) that include items you realistically should never bring anywhere, such as a DFS and a Ring of Suffering, and he attempts a 500 with the worst possible choice for each.[^17ynF_0ltPc-0]
* Per the description, Gnomonkey may later run a Challenge Mode raid in wiki gear for fun but cannot currently because the mage thrall is still broken and will not turn the Olm head properly.[^17ynF_0ltPc-0]
* This is likely his last wiki guide because they are starting to get a bit stale, and calls this the most interesting run he did and the finale of the series.[^17ynF_0ltPc-357]
* Gnomonkey brought back his Wiki Guides series in September 2025 because his Twitch September goal for Wiki Guides was reached; Yama was the first boss covered.[^KIII4sXUDTk-0]
* The community Wiki Guides goal for September was met, and the stream is working towards a community day, then longer-term goals of a No Pillar Coliseum run and a possible PvP collab with Oda.[^KIII4sXUDTk-1975]
* A viewer called Toma showed Gnomonkey that the OSRS Wiki revision history contains very old, terrible Vorkath setups, which led to this second Wiki Guides Vorkath episode using an even worse variant.[^qCsJAdpSeTU-0]
