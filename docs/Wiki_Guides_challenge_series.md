The **Wiki Guides** series is a challenge in which Gnomonkey completes boss content using the worst gear that the OSRS Wiki's gear setup template suggests, deliberately in bad faith. He then redoes the boss with a better setup that spends the same amount of gp, to show where the wiki is misleading.[^KIII4sXUDTk-29] To pick the improved setup, he takes the total budget of the bad setup, which is usually fairly high because of the poor suggestions, and spends it on a much easier setup.[^KIII4sXUDTk-1000]

He repeatedly stresses that the series is not an attack on the wiki: he considers it the best video game wiki he knows, and his criticism is aimed only at the gearing template and the table format, which he finds unhelpful outside the Max, Mid, Min, Iron and Pure tiers.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-454][^KIII4sXUDTk-876][^KIII4sXUDTk-1963][^qCsJAdpSeTU-0] Some people were offended and he gets frequent comments claiming he hates the wiki.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-454][^KIII4sXUDTk-1963] He says he will not revise the wiki himself, because it takes a lot of time and people would revert anything he adds out of spite.[^qCsJAdpSeTU-0]

## 2024 run

The first run took place in May 2024 and ended on 1 June. By the end he called the Whisperer run the most interesting he did, and in the finale said it was likely his last wiki guide because they were becoming stale.[^17ynF_0ltPc-0][^17ynF_0ltPc-357]

|---|---|---|
| The Whisperer | 2024-05-10 | Worst downgrades: Infinity hat and Farseer, a prayer mitre instead of the Farseer helmet, Amulet of power, skill cape, combat bracelet, Wizard boots and a Seers ring.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-0][^9cYL7QTJ5G8-29] First kill took 448.6 seconds (about 7:28), "not that bad".[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-578] He notes the wiki lists Mage's book as a downgrade to Malediction ward although it is more expensive and harder to get, and calls the highest-tier gear fairly accurate except the Eldritch nightmare staff special over a Cursed sceptre.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-55][^9cYL7QTJ5G8-109] |

| [Corporeal Beast](Corporeal_Beast.md) | 2024-05-16 | The low-price-range setup is "horrendous and not worth it even for an Ironman"; he asks why a leaf-bladed spear is suggested and says nobody knows.[^BShbhmfpnZE-0][^BShbhmfpnZE-342] He failed to get a kill in the original low-drain setup and stamped it as technically possible but stupid before using a real setup.[^BShbhmfpnZE-475] He had mostly ignored the wiki's text descriptions earlier but followed this one.[^BShbhmfpnZE-27] |
| [Vorkath](Vorkath.md) | 2024-05-18 | The worst downgrade is a Rune defender, which he did not own, so he first got one from the Warriors Guild cyclopes (not the higher-level hairy cyclopes) with Vorkath downgrade gear and an attack cape, finding granite gloves very bad; the drop took about 10 seconds.[^WvmEY9v6AnI-0][^WvmEY9v6AnI-27][^WvmEY9v6AnI-56][^WvmEY9v6AnI-678] He chose melee Justiciar partly because the wiki lists an Armadyl godsword as its worst spec.[^WvmEY9v6AnI-150] |

Other planned episodes: a Challenge Mode [Chambers of Xeric](Chambers_of_Xeric.md) run in wiki gear, blocked while the mage thrall was broken and would not turn the Olm head, and Muspah as a Vardorvis-style follow-up; he said he would avoid Wilderness bosses on stream.[^BShbhmfpnZE-60][^WvmEY9v6AnI-0][^17ynF_0ltPc-0] He brings thralls in all episodes as he always does.[^hI6cALUKOzQ-237]

## 2025 revival

In September 2025 the series returned because the Twitch September goal for Wiki Guides was reached; [Yama](Yama.md) was the first boss.[^KIII4sXUDTk-0][^KIII4sXUDTk-1975] The next goals were a community day, a No Pillar Coliseum run and a possible PvP collaboration with Oda.[^KIII4sXUDTk-1975]

A viewer, Toma, pointed out that the wiki's revision history contains very old, terrible Vorkath setups, which led to a second Vorkath episode on 6 September using an even worse variant.[^qCsJAdpSeTU-0] Gnomonkey says digging through revision history for the worst variant would make any page look bad, so this is a fun concept rather than a criticism.[^qCsJAdpSeTU-0] On old setups, slots are often missing; his rule was that any empty slot stays empty, so he wore nothing in his glove, boot and helmet slots.[^qCsJAdpSeTU-26]
