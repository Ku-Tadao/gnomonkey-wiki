## Opinions

- Gnomonkey's pet tier list is unscripted, unlike most of his videos, and is based on several factors with pet design and rarity most important, plus size and idle animation such as flying. ([2022-03-12 0:00](https://youtu.be/vwCm9NKJhRE))
- Gnomonkey stresses the list is purely his opinion and that anyone else making a tier list would make something different; he expects it to change in a few months. ([2022-03-12 0:29](https://youtu.be/vwCm9NKJhRE?t=29))
- Final S tier according to the video's placements: the Zamorak pet (K'ril Tsutsaroth), the dusted Lil' Zik from Theatre of Blood, the Chambers of Xeric dusted pets (Olmlet), the Inferno pet Jal-nib-rek, and the Nexling. ([2022-03-12 2:44](https://youtu.be/vwCm9NKJhRE?t=164))
- Gnomonkey says opinions on this list change with perspective and that lower-level accounts would probably completely change it. ([2022-03-12 29:59](https://youtu.be/vwCm9NKJhRE?t=1799))
- Final A tier (as built during the video) includes Youngllef, Kalphite Queen, Kree'arra, Zilyana, Baby mole, Baby chinchompa, Bloodhound, Corporeal Beast's Dark core, Penance Queen, TzRek-Jad and the Dawn/Dusk pets at the bottom of A. ([2022-03-12 31:02](https://youtu.be/vwCm9NKJhRE?t=1862))
- Final B tier includes Tangleroot, Smolcano, Ikkle Hydra, Bandos' Lil' Bandos, Zulrah's snakeling (low B), the three Wilderness pets (Venenatis, Callisto, Vet'ion), Rift guardian, Giant squirrel and Rock golem (low B). ([2022-03-12 31:02](https://youtu.be/vwCm9NKJhRE?t=1862))
- Final C tier includes Tempoross' Tiny tempor at the top, Little nightmare, Scorpia's offspring, Abyssal orphan, Sraracha, Prince Black Dragon, the Thermonuclear smoke devil pet and the Beaver at the bottom. ([2022-03-12 31:02](https://youtu.be/vwCm9NKJhRE?t=1862))
- Final D tier includes Phoenix at the top, Lil' Creator, Hellpuppy, Vorkath's Vorki, Heron, Rocky and Herbi; E tier has the Dagannoth Kings' pets, Chompy chick and Skotos; F tier has Chaos Elemental's pet and Kraken at the very bottom. ([2022-03-12 31:02](https://youtu.be/vwCm9NKJhRE?t=1862))
