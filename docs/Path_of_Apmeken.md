**Path of Apmeken** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2026-04-01. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Enter the monkey room wearing Ava's assembler and melee gear to be extra tanky; skulls appear over pillars or vents at times and you must grab a hammer and potions from the crates and solve the puzzle within about 10 seconds or take a lot of damage.[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-357]
* A one-way blowpipe one-shots small mage monkeys while Rigour lets it one-shot big mages; small melee monkeys die to a basic Eye cast, big ones need a three-way, and the Osmumten's fang one-shots ranged monkeys without prayer.[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-418]
* Cursed baboons leave a venom trail and have about 20 health; any ranged hit one-shots them, and pre-potting anti-venom before this room means you do not need to bring it in.[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-479]
* Baboon shamans are the highest priority each wave; they have more health so need two basic blowpipe shots (or one Bow of Faerdhinen in a big crystal switch) and summon small thrall monkeys that run up and punch.[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-509]

## Stats and numbers

* Main monkeys come in three types: red melee, green ranged and blue mage; each is one-shot with the style it is weak to, and higher-level versions have 30 health instead of 25.[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-391]
* Volatile baboons walk toward you and explode for 50 damage if you are next to them, but moving one tile away avoids the hit, and the blast also kills nearby monkeys.[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-465]

## Tips

* A Voidwaker, with a four-tick instead of five-tick attack, also one-shots everything and is better in the monkey room.[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-452]
* The thrall monkeys drain prayer points even if you pray against them, which Gnomonkey says can waste about two extra doses every wave if ten hit you at once.[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-528]
* Gnomonkey recommends the Tombs of Amascut RuneLite plugin's Path of Apmeken wave helper to see what will spawn, so you can swap your protection prayer to match the most attackers, and its radius markers around monkeys and volatile baboons.[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-537]
* In his demonstration Gnomonkey dropped three potions on one tile, equipped Eye of Ayak first, and the last wave with many shamans and volatile baboons took two minutes, roughly one prayer dose and about 20 health.[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-598]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the Path of Apmeken monkey room is the hardest room of the raid because it drains the most supplies and is easy to mess up.[^N2DCZ1nt4p4-350]
