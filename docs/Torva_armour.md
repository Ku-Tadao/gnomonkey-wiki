## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says Nex rolls her punch against 335 crush defence in Torva, so there is a good chance you take a zero, and every zero taken saves brew and brewing time. ([2024-06-23 1:27](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=87))
- In Gnomonkey's calcs, Zeal does not change armor choices: at Maiden fully drained, Oathplate with a Torva helmet is 1% worse than full Torva, but two Oathplate pieces plus a Torva helmet still reach the 52 max hit needed. ([2026-07-07 18:00](https://youtu.be/lNaYz-3A15Y?t=1080))
- In Gnomonkey's calcs, at Verzik phase 2 the Oathplate-with-Torva-helmet setup is 4% worse than full Torva, so the Theatre of Blood armor meta stays the same with Zeal. ([2026-07-07 18:32](https://youtu.be/lNaYz-3A15Y?t=1112))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says do not do P2 melee without Torva, and do not do it in Bandos because it is not worth it. ([2024-06-23 0:54](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=54))
- Gnomonkey says he starts in maximum tank gear with maximum potions for a first cape and only brings range switches on money runs to speed things up. ([2024-07-26 16:01](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=961))
- Gnomonkey says you still run full Oathplate in Chambers of Xeric challenge mode for the higher overload strength boost, while in teams you can run full Torva where you can drain defense to zero. ([2026-07-07 18:32](https://youtu.be/lNaYz-3A15Y?t=1112))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey predicts Torva armour (helmet, chestplate and legs) will be Nex's main big-ticket drop, each piece giving a dramatic strength bonus increase over Bandos, for a total of +8 strength, along with higher defence stats. ([2021-11-16 11:18](https://youtu.be/fQySZrGmyOY?t=678))
- Gnomonkey predicts each Torva piece requires shredded Bandos armour: the body needs three components, legs two and helmet one, with a Bandos chestplate giving three components and tassets two; he expects the components and the repaired Torva to be tradable. ([2021-11-16 11:50](https://youtu.be/fQySZrGmyOY?t=710))
- Gnomonkey doubts this will dramatically increase the price of Bandos but expects it to help keep it at its current price. ([2021-11-16 11:50](https://youtu.be/fQySZrGmyOY?t=710))
- Gnomonkey expects Torva to be best in slot anywhere Bandos was used and to replace Inquisitor's armour in many places, such as challenge mode raids, the Kalphite Queen and Cerberus, and to be her most valuable drop. ([2021-11-16 12:20](https://youtu.be/fQySZrGmyOY?t=740))
- Gnomonkey says the Bandos duo is a great early money maker especially with the rise in price of Bandos items due to the Nex hype, and expects Bandos pieces might rise further in the coming days. ([2021-11-25 0:58](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=58))
- Gnomonkey says he does not expect the Bandos kill value to stay this high, but with the current hype around Nex, now is the time to be killing Bandos. ([2021-11-25 8:43](https://youtu.be/2rTHCO7fK8A?t=523))
- Gnomonkey says full Torva enables P2 melee at Nex, which is amazing for damage: fanging during that phase is much better than using the Zaryte crossbow, and you can only do it with Torva because its defence is double that of the alternatives. ([2024-06-23 0:26](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=26))
- Gnomonkey says Torva is worth the two switch slots even though the DPS increase is small, because the majority of Nex's attacks on you are crush and the crush defence is really important, more so than mage defence since most of her attacks are melee and her mage attacks in Blood phase always hit. ([2024-06-23 0:54](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=54))
- Gnomonkey says two brews are saved just by wearing Torva, because you take less damage and kill the boss faster, so you use the same supplies overall. ([2024-06-23 1:54](https://youtu.be/AzCKJvLDRxo?t=114))
- Gnomonkey says wearing Torva with 300 ranged defence lets you tank the world in the Colosseum, and that starting waves in Ancestral gets you killed, since you want ranged defence as in the Inferno. ([2024-07-26 15:06](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=906))
