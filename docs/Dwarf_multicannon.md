**Dwarf multicannon** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2020-04-17 and 2020-05-01. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* On his 175-Dagannoth task the cannon used almost a thousand cannonballs costing about 170k.[^mgiqzT3U6WE-152]
* Gnomonkey's 176-troll task used 550 cannonballs costing about 90k.[^cXlY9a7Ne04-123]

## Tips

* Gnomonkey considers Dagannoth one of the most useful tasks to bring a cannon to.[^mgiqzT3U6WE-29]
* Gnomonkey brings a cannon for Suqah tasks and says the Slayer XP rate is incredibly good compared with most cannon tasks.[^SKbk_MQ5L40-57]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says troll tasks are a great cannonable task.[^cXlY9a7Ne04-0]
