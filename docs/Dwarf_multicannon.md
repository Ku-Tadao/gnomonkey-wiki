# Dwarf multicannon

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2020-04-17 to 2020-05-01). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Stats and numbers

- On his 175-Dagannoth task the cannon used almost a thousand cannonballs costing about 170k. ([2020-04-17 2:32](https://youtu.be/mgiqzT3U6WE?t=152))
- Gnomonkey's 176-troll task used 550 cannonballs costing about 90k. ([2020-04-23 2:03](https://youtu.be/cXlY9a7Ne04?t=123))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey considers Dagannoth one of the most useful tasks to bring a cannon to. ([2020-04-17 0:29](https://youtu.be/mgiqzT3U6WE?t=29))
- Gnomonkey brings a cannon for Suqah tasks and says the Slayer XP rate is incredibly good compared with most cannon tasks. ([2020-05-01 0:57](https://youtu.be/SKbk_MQ5L40?t=57))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says troll tasks are a great cannonable task. ([2020-04-23 0:00](https://youtu.be/cXlY9a7Ne04))
