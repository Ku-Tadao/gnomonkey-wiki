# Jad

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2023-03-30 to 2023-07-12). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

|---|---|

## Methods

- For triple Jad in melee, Gnomonkey does two Scythe hits into one Scythe hit; you must attack the same tick that Jad attacks, though he thinks it is about a two-tick window. ([2023-07-12 1:01:19](https://youtu.be/I1NgO3f61yE?t=3679))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey uses prayer on Jad triples without bothering to flick, although he normally flicks the hell out of triples. ([2023-03-30 51:35](https://youtu.be/1sv6tIkEHjs?t=3095))
- Gnomonkey says without chinchompas he plays the final waves chill and waits for the shield to start moving, and kills all the healers. ([2023-03-30 54:40](https://youtu.be/1sv6tIkEHjs?t=3280))
- For a single Jad, Gnomonkey sits in melee range and, with healers, stays in a corner to corner-trap the healers and keep them off you. ([2023-07-12 1:00:49](https://youtu.be/I1NgO3f61yE?t=3649))
- At the Jads, Gnomonkey tries tanking the healers instead of luring them, and says to be safe above all else and not get meleed. ([2023-07-12 1:01:50](https://youtu.be/I1NgO3f61yE?t=3710))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says he will do a Jad skip because it is required for the diary anyway, and thinks anyone going for sub 65 will also be able to Jad skip. ([2023-03-30 51:01](https://youtu.be/1sv6tIkEHjs?t=3061))
