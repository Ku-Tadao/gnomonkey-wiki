**Jad** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2023-03-30 and 2023-07-12. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* For triple Jad in melee, Gnomonkey does two Scythe hits into one Scythe hit; you must attack the same tick that Jad attacks, though he thinks it is about a two-tick window.[^I1NgO3f61yE-3679]

## Tips

* Gnomonkey uses prayer on Jad triples without bothering to flick, although he normally flicks the hell out of triples.[^1sv6tIkEHjs-3095]
* Without chinchompas he plays the final waves chill and waits for the shield to start moving, and kills all the healers.[^1sv6tIkEHjs-3280]
* For a single Jad, Gnomonkey sits in melee range and, with healers, stays in a corner to corner-trap the healers and keep them off you.[^I1NgO3f61yE-3649]
* At the Jads, Gnomonkey tries tanking the healers instead of luring them, and says to be safe above all else and not get meleed.[^I1NgO3f61yE-3710]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says he will do a Jad skip because it is required for the diary anyway, and thinks anyone going for sub 65 will also be able to Jad skip.[^1sv6tIkEHjs-3061]
