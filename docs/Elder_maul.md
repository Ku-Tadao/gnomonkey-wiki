**Elder maul** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2023-12-22 and 2024-04-18. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Project Rebalance gives the Elder maul a special attack costing 50 percent spec energy that reduces the target's defence by 35 percent, compared to 30 percent for the Dragon warhammer.[^Kzc2e6H8i7o-150]

## Tips

* Elder maul is really strong at Tekton so Ink mace would probably not be brought there.[^Kzc2e6H8i7o-4291]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey finds the Elder maul buff very questionable, as there are already tons of crush weapons and he does not see it fitting the game if it is just a good crush weapon.[^t5mXf8KJztU-140]
* Gnomonkey says the Scythe and the Inquisitor's mace need love, noting the mace's only use, at Tekton, has fallen to the wayside with the Chambers QoL; devaluing them further seems like a bad idea, and the Elder maul would likely only make other items useless unless it does something unique like the Soul Reaper axe.[^t5mXf8KJztU-154]
* Gnomonkey says buffing things simply because they are rare is typically not a good idea; he points to how rare corpse shields are, saying that if they blocked so much damage they were useful they would trivialise content, especially the Inferno, and the Elder maul might be the same.[^t5mXf8KJztU-154]
* Gnomonkey says this makes the Elder maul a direct upgrade to the Dragon warhammer: more accurate, drains more defence and does not need an off-hand like an Avernic defender.[^Kzc2e6H8i7o-179]
* Gnomonkey says that since Colosseum's rarest drop, the Glaive, also drains defence but is garbage, the Elder maul as the new BIS warhammer is nonsensical; he thinks the Glaive should drain 20 percent per splat instead of 10 percent.[^Kzc2e6H8i7o-212]
* Gnomonkey says the Elder maul is already a far more accurate special than the Dragon warhammer and does not need an Avernic, and that he never saw anyone call it inaccurate, so the change seems weird.[^P5yHDSocecY-766]
* Gnomonkey says the Glaive is still ignored and left useless next to the Elder maul, and that the Glaive still needs a buff.[^P5yHDSocecY-788]

## Events

* The Elder maul is planned to be buffed, though the blog did not say exactly how.[^t5mXf8KJztU-140]
* In the beta the Elder maul can smash crabs and build the chest but the Tekton first special was not a guaranteed hit, which Gnomonkey assumes is a bug.[^Kzc2e6H8i7o-179]
* Jagex proposed giving the Elder maul special attack an extra 25% accuracy, citing feedback that its accuracy was still low and not much better than the Dragon warhammer.[^P5yHDSocecY-746]
