**Poll: Stackable clues and skip tokens** is an opinion topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2025-04-08. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says his solution is a passive buff to clues: make all clues remember their step progress even when dropped, with no skip tokens, reroll tokens or bonus currencies to hold on to.[^WQ9Zg4fQRJo-1074]
* Gnomonkey says polls repeatedly offer only yes or no, with no 'yes if changed' option, so players who want stackable clues feel pressured to vote yes on a bad implementation; he suggests a feedback box.[^WQ9Zg4fQRJo-1074]
* Gnomonkey calls this a terrible implementation that would ruin clue scrolls for mains, because all of the value would come from the tickets.[^WQ9Zg4fQRJo-1118]
* Gnomonkey urges viewers not to vote yes just because they want scroll boxes, and to vote yes only once the system is changed, since he expects Jagex to deliver stackable clues eventually anyway.[^WQ9Zg4fQRJo-1344]
* Gnomonkey tells viewers not to fear losing stackable clues by voting no, because players have asked for them for a long time and Jagex is seeing the feedback.[^WQ9Zg4fQRJo-1370]

## Events

* Poll 84 proposed stackable clue scrolls, delivered as scroll boxes, together with tradable skip tokens; Gnomonkey covers only the clue scroll parts and not the other changes in the blog, such as the Sire changes.[^WQ9Zg4fQRJo-0]
* Under the proposal, players who have completed the quest X Marks the Spot would receive scroll boxes instead of regular clue scrolls, and could hold a maximum of five scroll boxes, or four plus one open clue scroll.[^WQ9Zg4fQRJo-201]
* Under the poll, geodes, bottles and birds' nests would give scroll boxes instead of loose clue scrolls.[^WQ9Zg4fQRJo-201]
* The proposed reduction of the clue scroll despawn timer back to three minutes would happen whether or not the stackable clues poll passes.[^WQ9Zg4fQRJo-420]
* Jagex stated the stack limit would firmly stay at five and that there were no plans to expand it.[^WQ9Zg4fQRJo-508]
