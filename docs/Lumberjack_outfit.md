**Lumberjack outfit** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2018-12-03 and 2020-09-02. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* The outfit is obtained by going through Temple Trekking solely to find undead lumberjacks, which drop the outfit pieces.[^I-8v5RpD8KY-33]
* With Morytania Legs 3, the whole Lumberjack outfit takes less than thirty minutes to obtain by killing undead lumberjacks in Temple Trekking.[^_eDJaUupzBc-214]

## Stats and numbers

* Done correctly, obtaining the Lumberjack outfit takes 30 to 45 minutes and saves over 300,000 XP on the way to 99 Woodcutting.[^I-8v5RpD8KY-0]
* The Lumberjack outfit gives a 2.5% increase in Woodcutting XP and is, according to Gnomonkey, objectively the fastest skilling outfit you can obtain in the game.[^_eDJaUupzBc-214]

## Tips

* If a piece of the outfit drops, the player should teleport out and start again, because a maximum of one piece can be obtained per event.[^I-8v5RpD8KY-97]
* To definitely get the Lumberjack outfit if you have at least Morytania Legs 3, as it saves quite a bit of time overall, and refers to an older video on it that he says is still accurate.[^_eDJaUupzBc-214]
* Before heading to the Blisterwood tree Gnomonkey recommends getting the Lumberjack outfit from Temple Trekking, which he says takes about half an hour with the Morytania Hard diary done.[^UCnmQbwhjBs-61]
* The Lumberjack outfit is worth wearing at the Blisterwood tree as part of his setup, alongside a Drakan's medallion instead of a Woodcutting cape or bird nest items.[^UCnmQbwhjBs-94]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey considers the Lumberjack outfit one of the easiest and fastest outfits to obtain, unlike the farmer's and angler's outfits, which he says take a long time and do not save enough XP to be worth it.[^I-8v5RpD8KY-0]
* Gnomonkey was upset that he waited until 80 Woodcutting to obtain the outfit and hopes the video helps others avoid that.[^I-8v5RpD8KY-130]
