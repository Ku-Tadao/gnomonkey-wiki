# Lumberjack outfit

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2018-12-03 to 2020-09-02). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | item |
| **Videos** | 3 (first 2018-12-03, latest 2020-09-02) |

## Methods

- The outfit is obtained by going through Temple Trekking solely to find undead lumberjacks, which drop the outfit pieces. ([2018-12-03 0:33](https://youtu.be/I-8v5RpD8KY?t=33))
- With Morytania Legs 3, the whole Lumberjack outfit takes less than thirty minutes to obtain by killing undead lumberjacks in Temple Trekking. ([2020-07-31 3:34](https://youtu.be/_eDJaUupzBc?t=214))

## Stats and numbers

- Done correctly, obtaining the Lumberjack outfit takes 30 to 45 minutes and saves over 300,000 XP on the way to 99 Woodcutting. ([2018-12-03 0:00](https://youtu.be/I-8v5RpD8KY))
- The Lumberjack outfit gives a 2.5% increase in Woodcutting XP and is, according to Gnomonkey, objectively the fastest skilling outfit you can obtain in the game. ([2020-07-31 3:34](https://youtu.be/_eDJaUupzBc?t=214))

## Tips

- If a piece of the outfit drops, the player should teleport out and start again, because a maximum of one piece can be obtained per event. ([2018-12-03 1:37](https://youtu.be/I-8v5RpD8KY?t=97))
- Gnomonkey says to definitely get the Lumberjack outfit if you have at least Morytania Legs 3, as it saves quite a bit of time overall, and refers to an older video on it that he says is still accurate. ([2020-07-31 3:34](https://youtu.be/_eDJaUupzBc?t=214))
- Before heading to the Blisterwood tree Gnomonkey recommends getting the Lumberjack outfit from Temple Trekking, which he says takes about half an hour with the Morytania Hard diary done. ([2020-09-02 1:01](https://youtu.be/UCnmQbwhjBs?t=61))
- Gnomonkey notes the Lumberjack outfit is worth wearing at the Blisterwood tree as part of his setup, alongside a Drakan's medallion instead of a Woodcutting cape or bird nest items. ([2020-09-02 1:34](https://youtu.be/UCnmQbwhjBs?t=94))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey considers the Lumberjack outfit one of the easiest and fastest outfits to obtain, unlike the farmer's and angler's outfits, which he says take a long time and do not save enough XP to be worth it. ([2018-12-03 0:00](https://youtu.be/I-8v5RpD8KY))
- Gnomonkey was upset that he waited until 80 Woodcutting to obtain the outfit and hopes the video helps others avoid that. ([2018-12-03 2:10](https://youtu.be/I-8v5RpD8KY?t=130))
