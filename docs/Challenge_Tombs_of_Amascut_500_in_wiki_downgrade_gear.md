# Challenge: Tombs of Amascut 500 in wiki downgrade gear

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 1 video (2024-06-01). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

|---|---|
| **Type** | challenge |

## Methods

- Gnomonkey's downgrade melee setup includes a Nezi helmet, a Glory, an Artie cloak, a Fighter torso and Blessed chaps. ([2024-06-01 0:27](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=27))
- The wiki downgrades Ruby bolts to Diamond bolts, so Gnomonkey uses Diamond bolts and an Archer's ring switch for ranged, and Blood bark with a regular Trident for magic. ([2024-06-01 0:27](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=27))
- The setup was so confusing that Gnomonkey used inventory tags: pink items are both melee and mage and blue items are ranged and mage; he says this is the only case where inventory tags make sense, when your gear is not what it appears to be. ([2024-06-01 0:57](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=57))
- Gnomonkey also allows himself a Keris even though the wiki does not mention it, because he does not think the 500 is possible without one; he brings one divine melee potion, one divine super combat and one divine ranging, with the blowpipe only for spec. ([2024-06-01 1:26](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=86))
- His plan is Baba, Kephri, Zebak then Akkha, using both salts on Akkha because he needs the run energy for four-tick butterfly, and using divine ranging to get through Zebak; he says Akkha without butterfly is not possible because it uses too much restore. ([2024-06-01 2:24](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=144))
- He runs Hardcore, Walk the path, Pathfinder and Deadly prayer, typical for a 500 but with no Overly Draining and no Keep Back, because chip damage adds up fast whenever Akkha swaps styles. ([2024-06-01 2:58](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=178))

## Stats and numbers

- For ranging he wears a SERP helmet, which Gnomonkey says is worse than no helmet at all because of a negative five ranged bonus, even though it means bringing a better melee helmet that is only used for the worse ranged bonus. ([2024-06-01 0:27](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=27))
- Gnomonkey says each room takes an eternity: Baba and Kephri together took about half an hour, drastically draining supplies and requiring over an hour of perfect flicking. ([2024-06-01 5:25](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=325))
- Gnomonkey prices the downgrade setup at 39 mil, calling it fairly cheap but terrible, and says you could not choose worse value items if you tried. ([2024-06-01 25:09](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1509))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the biggest problem was the Wardens' P1, where most deaths happened, because he simply did not have enough DPS, even with a blowpipe, to pass that DPS check. ([2024-06-01 5:25](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=325))

## Records

- Gnomonkey says the footage was recorded several weeks before upload, when the monkey room was still difficult, and that he completed it over two separate streams totalling about eight hours. ([2024-06-01 4:55](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=295))
- The downgrade-gear 500 took about 72 to 73 minutes, after six and a half hours of stream attempts, and gave no purple; Gnomonkey says he really expected the Shadow. ([2024-06-01 24:36](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=1476))

## Events

- Gnomonkey had attempted this once on a prior stream and died in the Wardens' P1 due to not having enough restore; he found Divine super combat does not last long enough (20 minutes is not enough) and that leftover Divine ranging is wasted. ([2024-06-01 1:56](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=116))

## Trivia

- Gnomonkey describes this as by far the hardest Tombs of Amascut challenge he has completed, taking almost 8 hours of total attempts across two streams, with no fang, no Shadow and not even an Occult necklace. ([2024-06-01 0:00](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc))
- The wiki specifies a blowpipe spec as the worst downgrade for the range spec and a dragon dagger as the worst downgrade for the melee spec, so Gnomonkey allows himself to use those. ([2024-06-01 1:26](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=86))
