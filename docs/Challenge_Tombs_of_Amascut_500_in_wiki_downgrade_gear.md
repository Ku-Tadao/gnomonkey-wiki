**Challenge: Tombs of Amascut 500 in wiki downgrade gear** is a challenge topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2024-06-01. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Gnomonkey's downgrade melee setup includes a Nezi helmet, a Glory, an Artie cloak, a Fighter torso and Blessed chaps.[^17ynF_0ltPc-27]
* The wiki downgrades Ruby bolts to Diamond bolts, so Gnomonkey uses Diamond bolts and an Archer's ring switch for ranged, and Blood bark with a regular Trident for magic.[^17ynF_0ltPc-27]
* The setup was so confusing that Gnomonkey used inventory tags: pink items are both melee and mage and blue items are ranged and mage; he says this is the only case where inventory tags make sense, when your gear is not what it appears to be.[^17ynF_0ltPc-57]
* Gnomonkey also allows himself a Keris even though the wiki does not mention it, because he does not think the 500 is possible without one; he brings one divine melee potion, one divine super combat and one divine ranging, with the blowpipe only for spec.[^17ynF_0ltPc-86]
* His plan is Baba, Kephri, Zebak then Akkha, using both salts on Akkha because he needs the run energy for four-tick butterfly, and using divine ranging to get through Zebak; he says Akkha without butterfly is not possible because it uses too much restore.[^17ynF_0ltPc-144]
* He runs Hardcore, Walk the path, Pathfinder and Deadly prayer, typical for a 500 but with no Overly Draining and no Keep Back, because chip damage adds up fast whenever Akkha swaps styles.[^17ynF_0ltPc-178]

## Stats and numbers

* For ranging he wears a SERP helmet, which Gnomonkey says is worse than no helmet at all because of a negative five ranged bonus, even though it means bringing a better melee helmet that is only used for the worse ranged bonus.[^17ynF_0ltPc-27]
* Each room takes an eternity: Baba and Kephri together took about half an hour, drastically draining supplies and requiring over an hour of perfect flicking.[^17ynF_0ltPc-325]
* Gnomonkey prices the downgrade setup at 39 mil, calling it fairly cheap but terrible, and says you could not choose worse value items if you tried.[^17ynF_0ltPc-1509]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the biggest problem was the Wardens' P1, where most deaths happened, because he simply did not have enough DPS, even with a blowpipe, to pass that DPS check.[^17ynF_0ltPc-325]

## Records

* The footage was recorded several weeks before upload, when the monkey room was still difficult, and that he completed it over two separate streams totalling about eight hours.[^17ynF_0ltPc-295]
* The downgrade-gear 500 took about 72 to 73 minutes, after six and a half hours of stream attempts, and gave no purple; Gnomonkey says he really expected the Shadow.[^17ynF_0ltPc-1476]

## Events

* Gnomonkey had attempted this once on a prior stream and died in the Wardens' P1 due to not having enough restore; he found Divine super combat does not last long enough (20 minutes is not enough) and that leftover Divine ranging is wasted.[^17ynF_0ltPc-116]

## Trivia

* Gnomonkey describes this as by far the hardest Tombs of Amascut challenge he has completed, taking almost 8 hours of total attempts across two streams, with no fang, no Shadow and not even an Occult necklace.[^17ynF_0ltPc-0]
* The wiki specifies a blowpipe spec as the worst downgrade for the range spec and a dragon dagger as the worst downgrade for the melee spec, so Gnomonkey allows himself to use those.[^17ynF_0ltPc-86]
