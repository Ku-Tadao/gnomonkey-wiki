## Methods

- Gnomonkey's ZMI gear was full Graceful, an amulet of glory (a fury is better if you have one), a dust battlestaff, a Dragonfire shield (not necessary), and he says to bring the best defensive shield you have. ([2020-06-09 3:28](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=208))
- The inventory needs a rune pouch with cosmic, astral and mine runes: cosmic and astral for NPC Contact and the Ourania Teleport, and mine runes for opening the bank because they are the cheapest rune in the game. Law runes can be used instead of cosmics, pulling out a cosmic when you need to repair pouches. ([2020-06-09 4:30](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=270))
- Set the spellbook to Lunar and use the Ourania Teleport, then go down the ladder to the north; Gnomonkey recommends marking the ladder so it is easier to click. ([2020-06-09 5:34](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=334))
- The banker near the ladder offers quick payment deals; selecting mine runes means a left click on the banker takes a payment of twenty runes and opens the bank. Any type of rune can pay, and mine runes are cheapest at 3 gp each at the time. ([2020-06-09 5:34](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=334))
- At the bank, pull out essence and fill your pouches. A stamina potion lasts about two runs, so pull it out the second time you take essence if you have a giant pouch, or the first time if you do not. ([2020-06-09 6:00](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=360))
- Once all pouch essence is used, teleport back to Ourania and start another run. ([2020-06-09 6:58](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=418))
- To repair degraded pouches, use NPC Contact and talk to the Dark Mage and complete his dialogue; afterward the spell's right-click menu shows the last person contacted, so you can right-click and choose Dark Mage to save time. ([2020-06-09 7:25](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=445))

## Requirements

- To do ZMI effectively you need Lunar Diplomacy completed for the Lunar spellbook, as you need it for the Ourania Teleport and for NPC Contact to repair your pouches. ([2020-06-09 3:01](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=181))

## Stats and numbers

- ZMI has no runecrafting level requirement, but it scales with your runecrafting level, so a higher level gives more XP and better runes. ([2020-06-09 3:01](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=181))
- The chance of better runes at ZMI only increases every 10 levels, such as 1 to 9 and 9 to 19, all the way up to 99. ([2020-06-09 3:28](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=208))
- The Ring of endurance lets you use two doses of stamina and reduces run energy drain rate by 15%, which stacks with Graceful's 30% run recharge bonus. ([2020-06-09 4:00](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=240))
- You use the ZMI bank three times a run with a giant pouch, or two times without. ([2020-06-09 6:00](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=360))
- Each ZMI run gives about 25 seconds of AFK time, at which point you craft all your essence. ([2020-06-09 6:30](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=390))
- A pouch degrades after about ten trips when using the giant pouch, or after around thirty-one trips without it. ([2020-06-09 6:58](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=418))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey recommends not starting ZMI until you have a high Agility level and the Graceful outfit, as the method involves a lot of running. ([2020-06-09 3:01](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=181))
- For ZMI gear, Gnomonkey prioritises run energy bonuses and then defensive bonuses, because there are so many players at ZMI that you very rarely get attacked by the enemies in the dungeon. ([2020-06-09 3:28](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=208))
- Gnomonkey also brings an Explorer's ring for run energy restoration and a blessing in case he needs prayer. ([2020-06-09 4:00](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=240))
- Gnomonkey skips law runes because you almost always craft at least one law rune at the ZMI altar. ([2020-06-09 5:01](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=301))
- Bring all the runecrafting pouches you can to ZMI, as without all of them it becomes more likely you will not get a rune; they can be obtained in the Abyss, reached with fairy ring code ALR. ([2020-06-09 5:01](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=301))
- Talking to the Dark Mage in the Abyss to receive the Abyssal book skips a dialogue option with him when using NPC Contact. ([2020-06-09 5:01](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=301))
- Gnomonkey prefers one-dose stamina potions because with vial smashing unlocked at the Barbarian Outpost you do not have to bank the remaining potion. ([2020-06-09 5:01](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=301))
- Remove placeholders from your pouches and rune pouch and fill your bank with bank fillers, so you can quickly bank the runes you craft. ([2020-06-09 6:00](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=360))
- Many players follow other players to AFK the run to the ZMI altar; Gnomonkey recommends using something such as a piece of essence on a player, which improves pathing and prevents getting stuck on geometry. Huge trains of people can be found on world 327. ([2020-06-09 6:30](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=390))
- Drop the cheap runes between crafts to improve XP rates, since otherwise you have to pull essence out of your pouches and craft extra times. ([2020-06-09 6:30](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=390))
- If you are hit too hard to keep your HP up at ZMI, Gnomonkey recommends Rapid Heal and Protect from Ranged and praying at the altar between runs; he barely got hit because of the huge number of players there. ([2020-06-09 6:58](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=418))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey describes ZMI as a more complicated runecrafting method than others but much less intensive. ([2020-06-09 3:01](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=181))
- Gnomonkey says the best in slot ring for ZMI is the Ring of endurance, which came out with the Sins of the Father quest and is obtainable from the Hallowed Sepulchre; at the time it cost around 40 million. ([2020-06-09 4:00](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=240))
- Gnomonkey says reducing the energy drain rate is a unique effect of the Ring of endurance and is incredibly useful at ZMI, so it is the priority if you can afford it; an imbued ring of suffering is also a good choice for its extra defensive bonuses. ([2020-06-09 4:30](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=270))
