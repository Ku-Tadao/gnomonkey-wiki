# Runecrafting

## Methods

- Gnomonkey would train Runecrafting through either mud runes or blood runes after Guardians of the Rift, both of which supply lots of GP and have a high pet chance. ([2022-08-03 4:25](https://youtu.be/6OkBpQdcXRw?t=265))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says the colossal pouch makes the Rift Guardian pet grind from other Runecrafting much faster. ([2022-03-30 19:52](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=1192))

## Tips

- For Guardians of the Rift Gnomonkey brings all his Runecrafting pouches (optionally banking the small pouch to click less), a chisel, and a rune pouch with NPC Contact runes (astral, air, cosmic). ([2022-03-30 2:26](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=146))
- Gnomonkey brings his best pickaxe and any Varrock armour piece, and Prospector gear helps a little; with a crystal pickaxe he wears a crystal signet ring to reduce charges, noting it uses around 50 charges per game. ([2022-03-30 2:57](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=177))
- A mining cape is minorly beneficial for its chance of extra essence (Gnomonkey is not certain it works there); a Runecrafting cape helps prevent pouch degradation until you get the lantern; a max cape is the best choice; Graceful is not needed. ([2022-03-30 2:57](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=177))
- Feeding the main guardian stones gives run energy. ([2022-03-30 3:28](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=208))
- Once you have two or three outfit pieces and/or the lantern, Gnomonkey starts bringing a binding necklace, wearing a tome of fire, and carrying earth and water runes loose to make combination runes, which greatly speeds up games with some multipliers. ([2022-03-30 3:28](https://youtu.be/y7Vb_k3Er2I?t=208))
- Gnomonkey says mud runes are incredibly fast XP and GP with the Ring of the Elements, so he would do a mix of that with AFKing blood runes. ([2022-08-03 4:25](https://youtu.be/6OkBpQdcXRw?t=265))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says Daeyalt ZMI gives slightly worse runecrafting XP an hour than regular ZMI but is much more AFK. ([2020-06-09 0:31](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=31))
- Gnomonkey does not think Daeyalt ZMI beats Zeah runecrafting at a higher runecrafting level; he says Blood runecrafting provides a lot of gp an hour and is only a bit worse than ZMI even at his level. ([2020-06-09 8:51](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=531))
- Gnomonkey would bet Blood runecrafting beats ZMI for XP an hour from level 77 to 79. ([2020-06-09 8:51](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=531))
- Gnomonkey says Soul runecrafting should still always give more profit and more XP an hour than Daeyalt ZMI while being of similar intensity. ([2020-06-09 8:51](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=531))
- Gnomonkey says a good reason to use pure essence at ZMI is lacking the quest requirement for the Daeyalt mine, but even with it, pure essence ZMI is a bit faster in XP, much more gp an hour and has double the pet chance. ([2020-06-09 9:21](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=561))
- Gnomonkey personally probably will not use pure essence at ZMI on any account he makes, because it is much less AFK for only a bit more XP in runes. ([2020-06-09 9:21](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=561))
- Gnomonkey says lava runecrafting is still the way to go for faster XP, and that Daeyalt ZMI now seems the best bet for low-intensity runecrafting. ([2020-06-09 9:21](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=561))

## Records

- Gnomonkey got about 63k runecrafting XP an hour at level 86 using Daeyalt ZMI without being fast at banking or crafting, and says it could easily be higher; worse pouches or a lower runecrafting level would give a bit less. ([2020-06-09 8:22](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=502))
- Gnomonkey made about 300k gp profit an hour at Daeyalt ZMI, noting this is not per hour of mining since time is spent mining the essence. ([2020-06-09 8:22](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=502))
