# Opinion: OSRS Wiki gear recommendations

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2024-05-10 to 2024-05-13). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | opinion |
| **Videos** | 2 (first 2024-05-10, latest 2024-05-13) |

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the wiki's Desert Treasure 2 boss pages recommend downgrades such as Amulet of power or a God mitre when a Farseer helm is missing, which he calls insane for account progression. ([2024-05-10 12:30](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=750))
- Gnomonkey criticises the wiki for listing Mage's book as a downgrade to Malediction ward and a combat bracelet when Barrows gloves are not owned on an account with Desert Treasure 2 done. ([2024-05-10 13:00](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=780))
- Gnomonkey says some wiki downgrades are reasonable: the boot recommendations (an account might lack them) and the Seers ring make sense. ([2024-05-10 13:27](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=807))
- Gnomonkey says listing a Mage Arena 1 cape as not the minimum is silly because an account with Desert Treasure 2 unlocked would have to be a maniac not to own a regular god cape. ([2024-05-10 13:27](https://youtu.be/9cYL7QTJ5G8?t=807))
- Gnomonkey says the wiki's Vardorvis page lists no mention of Void even though Void is quite good there. ([2024-05-13 10:33](https://youtu.be/hI6cALUKOzQ?t=633))
- Gnomonkey considers the wiki clearly brain dead on setups in the downgrade tables, but says notes calculated by someone, such as the Inquisitor's mace one, are likely accurate. ([2024-05-13 10:33](https://youtu.be/hI6cALUKOzQ?t=633))
