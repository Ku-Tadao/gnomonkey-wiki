**Opinion: OSRS Wiki gear recommendations** is an opinion topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2024-05-10 and 2024-05-13. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the wiki's Desert Treasure 2 boss pages recommend downgrades such as Amulet of power or a God mitre when a Farseer helm is missing, which he calls insane for account progression.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-750]
* Gnomonkey criticises the wiki for listing Mage's book as a downgrade to Malediction ward and a combat bracelet when Barrows gloves are not owned on an account with Desert Treasure 2 done.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-780]
* Gnomonkey says some wiki downgrades are reasonable: the boot recommendations (an account might lack them) and the Seers ring make sense.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-807]
* Gnomonkey says listing a Mage Arena 1 cape as not the minimum is silly because an account with Desert Treasure 2 unlocked would have to be a maniac not to own a regular god cape.[^9cYL7QTJ5G8-807]
* Gnomonkey says the wiki's Vardorvis page lists no mention of Void even though Void is quite good there.[^hI6cALUKOzQ-633]
* Gnomonkey considers the wiki clearly brain dead on setups in the downgrade tables, but says notes calculated by someone, such as the Inquisitor's mace one, are likely accurate.[^hI6cALUKOzQ-633]
