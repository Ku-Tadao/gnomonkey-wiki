**Doom of Mokhaiotl: holy water** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2025-07-25 and 2025-08-22. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* If you kill the Doom of Mokhaiotl while doing the melee punish, it shoots holy water onto random tiles in all directions: holy water that lands in acid clears a 3x3 area and holy water that lands on you heals you.[^fLFV4RF5XPE-156]

## Stats and numbers

* The holy water mechanic cannot be used after about cave 5 because the Doom of Mokhaiotl never does the melee punish (smite) phase later, so you never finish the boss during it.[^fLFV4RF5XPE-156]
* The holy water mechanic was bugged so that if the first hitsplat of your attack did not kill the boss (Scythe swing, Chally spec, or a godsword with an echo hit finishing) no holy water would proc.[^Glp4chf4yiA-957]

## Tips

* The spot where the rock block drags the Doom of Mokhaiotl to the middle of the room is a perfect location to clear acid with the holy water punish, and the tech should also work with it once patched.[^draz0HZBq00-1163]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey calls holy water baffling, a mechanic Jagex added and then nerfed when people used it, which he says felt like a last-minute untested addition.[^Glp4chf4yiA-957]

## Events

* The holy water punish was broken at the time and would probably return in the next patch, allowing you to punish the boss and clear acid on later waves (8+).[^draz0HZBq00-1163]
* Holy water, which drops when a melee punish finishes the Doom of Mokhaiotl and heals you, restores prayer and clears acid, was found to be bugged and not proc on delve 9 plus, and Jagex kept that because it would make later delves too easy; Gnomonkey agrees, noting people reach almost delve 200 without it.[^Glp4chf4yiA-903]
* After players found that not attacking the boss after the orb phase avoided shield phase and allowed holy water on delves 6 to 8, Jagex made the boss start shield phase after about three attacks regardless, so holy water only works if a melee punish comes within them.[^Glp4chf4yiA-931]
