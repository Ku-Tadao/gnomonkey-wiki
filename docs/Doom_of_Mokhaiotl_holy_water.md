# Doom of Mokhaiotl: holy water

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2025-07-25 to 2025-08-22). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | mechanic |
| **Videos** | 3 (first 2025-07-25, latest 2025-08-22) |

## Methods

- If you kill the Doom of Mokhaiotl while doing the melee punish, it shoots holy water onto random tiles in all directions: holy water that lands in acid clears a 3x3 area and holy water that lands on you heals you. ([2025-07-25 2:36](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=156))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says the holy water mechanic cannot be used after about cave 5 because the Doom of Mokhaiotl never does the melee punish (smite) phase later, so you never finish the boss during it. ([2025-07-25 2:36](https://youtu.be/fLFV4RF5XPE?t=156))
- The holy water mechanic was bugged so that if the first hitsplat of your attack did not kill the boss (Scythe swing, Chally spec, or a godsword with an echo hit finishing) no holy water would proc. ([2025-08-22 15:57](https://youtu.be/Glp4chf4yiA?t=957))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says the spot where the rock block drags the Doom of Mokhaiotl to the middle of the room is a perfect location to clear acid with the holy water punish, and the tech should also work with it once patched. ([2025-07-31 19:23](https://youtu.be/draz0HZBq00?t=1163))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey calls holy water baffling, a mechanic Jagex added and then nerfed when people used it, which he says felt like a last-minute untested addition. ([2025-08-22 15:57](https://youtu.be/Glp4chf4yiA?t=957))

## Events

- Gnomonkey says the holy water punish was broken at the time and would probably return in the next patch, allowing you to punish the boss and clear acid on later waves (8+). ([2025-07-31 19:23](https://youtu.be/draz0HZBq00?t=1163))
- Holy water, which drops when a melee punish finishes the Doom of Mokhaiotl and heals you, restores prayer and clears acid, was found to be bugged and not proc on delve 9 plus, and Jagex kept that because it would make later delves too easy; Gnomonkey agrees, noting people reach almost delve 200 without it. ([2025-08-22 15:03](https://youtu.be/Glp4chf4yiA?t=903))
- After players found that not attacking the boss after the orb phase avoided shield phase and allowed holy water on delves 6 to 8, Jagex made the boss start shield phase after about three attacks regardless, so holy water only works if a melee punish comes within them. ([2025-08-22 15:31](https://youtu.be/Glp4chf4yiA?t=931))
