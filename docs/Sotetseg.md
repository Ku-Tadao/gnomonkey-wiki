**Sotetseg** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2021-01-29 and 2023-10-29. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Sotetseg is a stationary boss that uses melee attacks (protect from melee only halves the damage; Bandos is good here for melee defense) and magic orbs that split to two teammates as either red magic or grey ranged orbs that must be protected against; a single mistake can get you killed or wipe the team.[^sCmYo21i2I8-3004]
* After Sotetseg has fired ten orbs (melee attacks do not count) he fires a large red death ball at a random player; tanking it alone instantly kills you regardless of health, while stacking with teammates spreads the damage evenly.[^sCmYo21i2I8-3062]
* At 66% and 33% health Sotetseg chooses a random player, teleporting the team back to the gate; the chosen player runs a maze in another dimension following a red path while taking small damage, and their tiles appear red to the others, who must follow.[^sCmYo21i2I8-3062]
* In the Sotetseg maze a red tornado spawns behind the followers after the third row and slowly follows; if you are too slow it hits someone and damages the whole team for roughly 50. A player stepping on a grey tile damages the whole team 10 to 20 per tick, and if the chosen clicks across a grey tile every follower walks across it, which can immediately KO the team.[^sCmYo21i2I8-3091]
* On a first Sotetseg visit, mark the five tiles showing where each player stands (the middle tile is where you stack for death balls); stand equal distances from Sotetseg on the four corner tiles so you can see orbs.[^sCmYo21i2I8-3119]
* Sotetseg regains his defense completely after each maze, has 200 total defense and his defense is capped at 100 minimum, so BGS is a poor spec weapon here. In quads one player hammers in phases 1 and 2, one in phases 1 and 3, one in phases 2 and 3, and a fourth fills if someone misses; in trios the fill is dropped.[^sCmYo21i2I8-3151]
* First-time Sotetseg strategy: camp magic prayer and switch to ranged prayer for incoming orbs without using protect from melee, since melee can be outhealed unlike orbs; once comfortable, start praying protect from melee between orbs. When a death ball comes, stack on the middle tile and pray mage.[^sCmYo21i2I8-3209]
* When someone is chosen for the maze, turn off prayers, equip the hammer and activate spec if specing next phase, and eat leftover Nylocas sharks if drained; the chosen steps to the third row and waits for the team before running.[^sCmYo21i2I8-3238]
* Sotetseg improvements: open with an Ice Barrage or Twisted bow shot and switch into melee gear while running up, and keep the team venged; with a scythe, Gnomonkey activates Redemption instead of protection prayers when venged to tank melee hits, since Redemption is not disabled by orbs, and tanks a single orb for extra venge damage.[^sCmYo21i2I8-3361]
* To tick eat a Sotetseg death ball, step one tile back from him and wait, then eat when the ball completely envelops your character (or when he starts his next attack animation); you cannot tick eat with something that does not overheal if at max HP, so use an anglerfish, brew or Guthix rest.[^sCmYo21i2I8-3391]
* Hard mode Sotetseg is basically the same as regular ToB, except the player has to tick two balls.[^43IYFOMfalw-632]

## Stats and numbers

* Sotetseg's melee attack has a max hit of 45 and his orbs have a max hit of 50; the orbs are far more accurate than his melee, and an orb hitting you off prayer disables your prayers for a short time, which is devastating if chain-gunned.[^sCmYo21i2I8-3033]

## Tips

* Players signal their hammer phase by typing the phase numbers in chat and type 1 for a hit or 0 for a miss if not using voice; if most hammers landed and the fill has specs left in the last phase, the fill can dump claw specs since lowering defense further is worthless.[^sCmYo21i2I8-3179]
* Gnomonkey recommends running straight lines early on and mentions his movement guide and an online Sotetseg maze trainer (linked in the description); he says not to over-train past the point of being tick perfect through mazes when raiding with newer players, because they cannot keep up and you can wipe the team by being too good.[^sCmYo21i2I8-3269]
* If following in the maze, click the next red tile as soon as your true tile updates, never stop on a grey tile, and if the chosen mistakenly crosses grey tiles keep going; stopping on a grenade tile guarantees killing your whole team, and messing up a single grey tile will likely kill at least one person.[^sCmYo21i2I8-3302]
* Use a Crystal halberd spec on Sotetseg if you have more than 30% spec near the end of the fight.[^sCmYo21i2I8-3330]
* Below 45 health, where Sotetseg's melee can kill, Gnomonkey flinches Sotetseg's attacks; with a whip you lose ticks flinching, so he does not recommend tanking Vengeance without a scythe.[^sCmYo21i2I8-3391]
* You can skip death balls by baiting one out when you are one or two hits from the next maze, then hitting Sotetseg down to start the maze as soon as it is fired; Gnomonkey says you can get through the whole fight without taking a death ball this way.[^sCmYo21i2I8-3451]
* If your whole team dies and you start a maze, Sotetseg chooses you and you must run the maze with no indicators; take a screenshot of the maze and mark the tiles with RuneLite after exiting the portal.[^sCmYo21i2I8-3451]
* After Sotetseg is the final resupply chest before Verzik: drop sub-four-dose potions or bruise, leftover sharks, any Book of Darkness, soul runes, chins and Guthix rest; drop or drink super combat doses beyond eight; each player buys one stamina potion, three to four super restores and fills the rest of the inventory with brews, buying these last.[^sCmYo21i2I8-3485]
* You can group up in Sotetseg's main fight to spread the orb damage, or tick eat it, and that you can pop Vengeance the whole fight because there is no reason to keep health high, flinching with the Scythe.[^HkVt4enEesI-4137]
* Gnomonkey treats hard mode Sotetseg as a zero-supply room by using a prayer flicking pattern to reduce prayer usage.[^43IYFOMfalw-632]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the fight part of Sotetseg is not that good: it has three phases with high defence that resets each phase, so teammates plan spec order to drain defence, and he dislikes defence-draining mechanics and missed hammers.[^HkVt4enEesI-3956]
* Gnomonkey loves the Sotetseg maze: one person goes to the underworld and runs a tile path that the team must follow in the overworld, with about 20s damage per tick if they step wrong and a tornado if too slow.[^HkVt4enEesI-4044]
* Gnomonkey says he kills learners in the Sotetseg maze by running as fast as he can, which makes people go fast, and rates Sotetseg A tier.[^HkVt4enEesI-4109]
* Gnomonkey describes hard mode Sotetseg as just normal Sotetseg, but with two balls.[^43IYFOMfalw-2484]

## Trivia

* Sotetseg fires a magic or ranged attack at someone that splits into two orbs toward teammates, with skill in timing your melee prayer between orbs, and that the Bandos or Torva defence encourages leaving Void.[^HkVt4enEesI-4012]
