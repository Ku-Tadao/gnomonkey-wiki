# Aquanite hopper

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2025-09-18 to 2026-05-11). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Stats and numbers

- An aquanite tendon, which Aquanites can drop, can be combined with a mithril bar to create the Aquanite hopper, a ranged off-hand. ([2025-09-18 7:07](https://youtu.be/sK0iG_wg3QA?t=427))
- The Aquanite hopper gives crossbows a 10% chance to fire twice, with the second bolt having reduced damage, accuracy and enchanted bolt chance (blog says 67% reduced). ([2025-09-18 7:36](https://youtu.be/sK0iG_wg3QA?t=456))
- The Aquanite hopper gives +12 ranged attack and +1 ranged strength, against the Twisted buckler's +18 and +10 as Gnomonkey remembers them. ([2025-09-18 9:05](https://youtu.be/sK0iG_wg3QA?t=545))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey assumes the second bolt works at 33% damage and accuracy, given the blog says it won't replace the Twisted buckler as best in slot, and notes it would add only about a 2% extra chance at a ruby proc. ([2025-09-18 8:05](https://youtu.be/sK0iG_wg3QA?t=485))
- Gnomonkey thinks the Aquanite hopper could be a gap filler for ironmen before the Twisted buckler, because ranged off-hands are hard to obtain, and calls it either pretty good or pretty garbage depending on implementation. ([2025-09-18 8:35](https://youtu.be/sK0iG_wg3QA?t=515))
- Gnomonkey calls the Aqua Knight Hopper complete trash that no one uses for any reason, and says it definitely needs help. ([2026-05-11 4:01](https://youtu.be/7WIZw1stPas?t=241))
- Gnomonkey says he would not bring the Aqua Knight Hopper anywhere even if he had it, and that the Antler guard would be a faster way of getting effectively the same DPS. ([2026-05-11 7:02](https://youtu.be/7WIZw1stPas?t=422))

## Events

- Potential buffs for the Aqua Knight Hopper and Bell's Folly are being considered as part of the next summer sweep-up, though nothing is confirmed. ([2026-05-11 4:01](https://youtu.be/7WIZw1stPas?t=241))
