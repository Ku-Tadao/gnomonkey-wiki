**Aquanite hopper** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2025-09-18 and 2026-05-11. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* An aquanite tendon, which Aquanites can drop, can be combined with a mithril bar to create the Aquanite hopper, a ranged off-hand.[^sK0iG_wg3QA-427]
* The Aquanite hopper gives crossbows a 10% chance to fire twice, with the second bolt having reduced damage, accuracy and enchanted bolt chance (blog says 67% reduced).[^sK0iG_wg3QA-456]
* The Aquanite hopper gives +12 ranged attack and +1 ranged strength, against the Twisted buckler's +18 and +10 as Gnomonkey remembers them.[^sK0iG_wg3QA-545]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey assumes the second bolt works at 33% damage and accuracy, given the blog says it won't replace the Twisted buckler as best in slot, and notes it would add only about a 2% extra chance at a ruby proc.[^sK0iG_wg3QA-485]
* Gnomonkey thinks the Aquanite hopper could be a gap filler for ironmen before the Twisted buckler, because ranged off-hands are hard to obtain, and calls it either pretty good or pretty garbage depending on implementation.[^sK0iG_wg3QA-515]
* Gnomonkey calls the Aqua Knight Hopper complete trash that no one uses for any reason, and says it definitely needs help.[^7WIZw1stPas-241]
* Gnomonkey says he would not bring the Aqua Knight Hopper anywhere even if he had it, and that the Antler guard would be a faster way of getting effectively the same DPS.[^7WIZw1stPas-422]

## Events

* Potential buffs for the Aqua Knight Hopper and Bell's Folly are being considered as part of the next summer sweep-up, though nothing is confirmed.[^7WIZw1stPas-241]
