# Purging staff

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2025-03-04 to 2025-05-15). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Stats and numbers

- With the purging staff equipped, Mark of Darkness lasts five minutes instead of one minute, covering basically the whole kill. ([2025-05-14 19:12](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1152))
- Per the description, Shadow is best in slot on the Yama boss by 3%, because Yama's defence when attacked by the tanking player changes to his enrage stats. ([2025-05-15 0:00](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says that when a Tormented demon's shield is down you can cast a spell, even in melee gear, since you cannot miss, which changes the demon's prayer away from melee so you can keep meleeing. ([2025-03-04 11:20](https://youtu.be/TgWNIkkUpdg?t=680))
- Gnomonkey says he uses a Purging staff with Mark of Darkness and that any staff works to cast once, but it is especially important if you do not have a Scorching bow yet. ([2025-03-04 11:20](https://youtu.be/TgWNIkkUpdg?t=680))
- Even if you own a Shadow, Gnomonkey still brings the purging staff for one-shotting the orbs. ([2025-05-14 2:28](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=148))
- Per the description, you still need the purging staff to nuke the orbs, so you need a three-way swap with mage and Mark of Darkness. ([2025-05-15 0:00](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says mage is not worth it against Tormented demons because there is no seven-tick mage weapon (besides Twinflame) and the runes cost a lot, including Mark of Darkness upkeep, even though mage is technically about 5% more DPS than the Scorching bow. ([2025-03-04 18:10](https://youtu.be/TgWNIkkUpdg?t=1090))
- Gnomonkey says the purging staff is effectively equal to the Shadow at Yama, with Shadow slightly better only on the last phase. ([2025-05-14 0:31](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=31))
- Gnomonkey says if you only have one synapse, make the purging staff, as it is very hard to replace and Emberlight alone is fairly good. ([2025-05-14 8:04](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=484))
- Gnomonkey says it is almost not worth coming to Yama without a purging staff and recommends sending Tormented Demons to get at least one synapse to make one. ([2025-05-14 19:42](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1182))
- Gnomonkey says synapses are almost the same DPS as a Shadow at Yama and that you should get them, though you can get kills without them. ([2025-05-15 18:35](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=1115))
- Gnomonkey says if you only have one synapse you actually want a purging staff, which he calls crazy because it has always been the most worthless one but is the important one at Yama. ([2025-05-15 18:59](https://youtu.be/prE1BqM1wBE?t=1139))

## Trivia

- Gnomonkey says Staff of the Dead or Sang are not alternatives, because Yama is effectively a super demon and the purging staff gets demonbane multipliers; his purging staff was hitting around 80. ([2025-05-14 19:12](https://youtu.be/2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk?t=1152))
