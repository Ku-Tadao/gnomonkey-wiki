**Purging staff** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2025-03-04 and 2025-05-15. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* With the purging staff equipped, Mark of Darkness lasts five minutes instead of one minute, covering basically the whole kill.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-1152]
* Per the description, Shadow is best in slot on the Yama boss by 3%, because Yama's defence when attacked by the tanking player changes to his enrage stats.[^prE1BqM1wBE-0]

## Tips

* When a Tormented demon's shield is down you can cast a spell, even in melee gear, since you cannot miss, which changes the demon's prayer away from melee so you can keep meleeing.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-680]
* He uses a Purging staff with Mark of Darkness and that any staff works to cast once, but it is especially important if you do not have a Scorching bow yet.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-680]
* Even if you own a Shadow, Gnomonkey still brings the purging staff for one-shotting the orbs.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-148]
* Per the description, you still need the purging staff to nuke the orbs, so you need a three-way swap with mage and Mark of Darkness.[^prE1BqM1wBE-0]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says mage is not worth it against Tormented demons because there is no seven-tick mage weapon (besides Twinflame) and the runes cost a lot, including Mark of Darkness upkeep, even though mage is technically about 5% more DPS than the Scorching bow.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-1090]
* Gnomonkey says the purging staff is effectively equal to the Shadow at Yama, with Shadow slightly better only on the last phase.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-31]
* Gnomonkey says if you only have one synapse, make the purging staff, as it is very hard to replace and Emberlight alone is fairly good.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-484]
* Gnomonkey says it is almost not worth coming to Yama without a purging staff and recommends sending Tormented Demons to get at least one synapse to make one.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-1182]
* Gnomonkey says synapses are almost the same DPS as a Shadow at Yama and that you should get them, though you can get kills without them.[^prE1BqM1wBE-1115]
* Gnomonkey says if you only have one synapse you actually want a purging staff, which he calls crazy because it has always been the most worthless one but is the important one at Yama.[^prE1BqM1wBE-1139]

## Trivia

* Staff of the Dead or Sang are not alternatives, because Yama is effectively a super demon and the purging staff gets demonbane multipliers; his purging staff was hitting around 80.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-1152]
