## Methods

- To lazy flick a mage, Gnomonkey says to watch for the mage flickering under it, the same visual cue as the ranger hunkering down, and turn the prayer on just before it flickers. ([2022-07-22 33:56](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2036))
- To off tick, wait for an attack from the monster closest to you, step up on that tick, and repeat; Gnomonkey says it is not that hard. ([2023-03-30 37:42](https://youtu.be/1sv6tIkEHjs?t=2262))
- A mage takes eight ticks to respawn something, perfectly lining up with its four tick cycle, and respawning does not change its cycle. ([2024-01-17 26:35](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=1595))
- Mages can respawn monsters you have killed at half health, with roughly a 20% chance per attack, from a pool of the monsters you have killed, and each can only do so once. ([2024-06-13 33:47](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2027))
- Inferno mages attack at the same speed as rangers and are flicked the same way; their special mechanic is that they can respawn dead monsters from their pool a single time, with respawn target at half health. ([2024-10-03 23:42](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=1422))
- A mage can respawn any mob that has been killed once, at half health, but cannot respawn nibblers; the respawn takes exactly two of its attacks and Gnomonkey says there is roughly a 10% chance each attack. ([2025-02-26 36:48](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=2208))
- Gnomonkey says a splash on the mage is treated by the game as a melee, so casting and splashing flinches the mage for two ticks, an emergency off-tick; it fails if the mage respawns something after you killed the ranger. ([2025-12-07 1:18:41](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=4721))
- The Inferno mager can respawn an enemy that has been killed once per wave with half health (not nibblers), including bats, blobs, rangers and melees. ([2026-07-13 33:12](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=1992))
- Kill the mager first when there is no melee providing time pressure, because killing the other monster first lets the mager respawn it. ([2026-07-13 36:09](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=2169))
- If a ranger is killed before the mager and gets respawned, Gnomonkey says to solve it by starting from the middle of the pillar, praying the thing in the back, stepping out when you see the attack animation, and alternating so the attacks land one tick apart like a blob. ([2026-07-13 47:44](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=2864))
- Stepping out the wrong side of the pillar lets two monsters see you immediately and hit on the same tick, but stepping out the right side works because the mager must first get around the pillar. ([2026-07-13 47:44](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=2864))

## Stats and numbers

- Mages are one of the most dangerous standard Inferno monsters, max a 70 with magic and appear from wave 35 on; you start waves praying mage and their attack is calculated when the light under them flickers. ([2021-05-21 16:30](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=990))
- Instead of a regular attack, a mage can respawn any monster you have killed (except nibblers) at half health in the centre of the room; this takes eight ticks instead of the standard four and a monster can only be respawned once. ([2021-05-21 16:30](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=990))
- Gnomonkey says the mage is by far the most accurate and can put you to minus 20 health before the next wave starts, so always pray mage after pulling out and do not tank him. ([2022-07-22 38:50](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2330))
- When the Inferno mage is at 34 HP or lower Gnomonkey says piping it is better than using the Twisted bow. ([2023-03-30 32:06](https://youtu.be/1sv6tIkEHjs?t=1926))
- Splashing a mage on purpose stalls it by two ticks, allowing you to off tick; this only works if it has not respawned anything. ([2023-03-30 43:43](https://youtu.be/1sv6tIkEHjs?t=2623))
- In the Inferno a mage's max hit is 70, compared to 46 for a ranger and 49 for a melee, and the mage's hit is roughly two times more accurate than the ranger's hit, so it is more dangerous. ([2024-01-17 25:08](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=1508))
- Inferno mages can hit up to 70 and Gnomonkey calls them the first big scary thing. ([2024-06-13 33:17](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1997))
- Gnomonkey says Inferno mages can hit up to seventy, and not to take the hit. ([2024-10-03 23:43](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=1423))
- Inferno mages max hit 70 and almost always hit you, so Gnomonkey says you never want to tank one. ([2025-02-26 36:18](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=2178))
- A mage's attack takes exactly eight ticks, which matters for flicking later. ([2025-02-26 37:51](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=2271))
- The mage (Jal-Zek) maxes 70 and does not miss, roughly double the average damage of a ranged hit. ([2025-12-07 43:23](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=2603))
- A mage has roughly a 10% chance to respawn anything else you have killed in the wave, and it respawns it once with half its original health (for example a bat with 13 health instead of 25). ([2025-12-07 43:53](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=2633))
- Most Inferno monsters can melee you; a mage's melee hit maxes around 54 and is less accurate than its mage hit, and a blob's melee is scary, but gnomonkey says a ranger punch is harmless. ([2025-12-07 47:44](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=2864))
- The mager can hit a 70 and is extremely accurate, roughly double the danger of a ranger and double his average damage. ([2026-07-13 33:28](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=2008))
- A mager meleeing you maxes around 50 and hits very hard because of its accuracy, so do not get meleed by one. ([2026-07-13 37:30](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=2250))

## Tips

- If a mage respawns a blob, the blob has half HP but the blobs it splits into have full health; Gnomonkey notes mage respawns can be used as a tool to regain health with Blood Barrage. ([2021-05-21 17:03](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1023))
- At full health it is usually best to kill the mage first to prevent respawns, but if you need health it can be good to kill the blob, Blood Barrage it, and let the mage respawn it for more HP. ([2021-05-21 26:14](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1574))
- On wave 66 (double mages) just pray mage; mages can respawn each other so you may need to kill four, and a mage whose southwest tile is next to a falling pillar takes massive damage, over a hundred in Gnomonkey's experience. ([2021-05-21 32:30](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1950))
- Gnomonkey says a 40 XP drop after barraging a mage and melee usually means he killed the ranger and mage has the highest mage defense so it is probably the one left, so he switches to mage. ([2022-07-22 30:29](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1829))
- Gnomonkey says to always kill the mage first if you can, because the mage can respawn things, and calls it the queen of the chessboard and the most dangerous piece. ([2022-07-22 37:55](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2275))
- Gnomonkey says on a first cape with four and a half restores he would not flick two mages and would just kill them, as it is too much risk, though he does it all the time. ([2022-07-22 1:23:28](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=5008))
- Gnomonkey warns the mage can see you from certain tiles, so be careful when pulling nibblers. ([2023-03-30 32:06](https://youtu.be/1sv6tIkEHjs?t=1926))
- Gnomonkey says the big thing with sub 65 is not being afraid to off tick stuff: just send it, and forcing yourself to do it teaches you quickly. ([2023-03-30 33:32](https://youtu.be/1sv6tIkEHjs?t=2012))
- On a bad spawn where you do not know the splash off tick, Gnomonkey says you can simply wait for the melee to dig. ([2023-03-30 44:14](https://youtu.be/1sv6tIkEHjs?t=2654))
- Gnomonkey says to almost always kill the mage first because when a mage respawns a mob it goes right behind the mage, which creates a pillar stack for yourself if it is a blob or ranger. ([2024-01-17 26:01](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=1561))
- Gnomonkey says that if you appear to be splashing on nibblers you are probably not splashing, you just roll a zero after passing accuracy, and it is rare to splash on a nibbler. ([2024-01-17 26:35](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=1595))
- Gnomonkey prefers killing a melee over a blob while the mage is still up, because it respawning a melee is less annoying than it respawning a blob; a mage can only respawn one enemy a single time. ([2024-01-17 31:13](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=1873))
- With two mages that can respawn each other, Gnomonkey says to get both low and finish them together, and not to stand next to the pillar when killing the last mage as it hurts the player for half their current HP when the pillar collapses. ([2024-01-17 54:55](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=3295))
- Gnomonkey says mages are typically the priority to kill first, though melees can be an exception if they are going to cause you trouble. ([2024-06-13 33:47](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2027))
- When a mage is on the upper spawn next to the pillar you can reach it with a Twisted bow. ([2024-06-13 39:36](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2376))
- Gnomonkey says mage waves are completely free so he does not even cast at the nibblers, using the pillar collapse to do damage. ([2024-06-13 53:17](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=3197))
- Gnomonkey says to try to kill mages before other things since they can respawn things, and respawning blobs is bad because the bloblets are still full health. ([2024-10-03 24:19](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=1459))
- Gnomonkey says you should not kill a blob before the mage, and the only things to kill before a mage are bats and sometimes melees. ([2024-10-03 25:31](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=1531))
- Gnomonkey says to stand on a tile that lets you corner trap the melee when it digs while you attack the mage. ([2024-10-03 26:52](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=1612))
- Gnomonkey says to never kill a ranger before a mage, except in rare waves where doing so is fine. ([2024-10-03 32:02](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=1922))
- To flick a mage, watch for the flicker underneath it, which is the sign that it is attacking; put your prayer on right before it starts the flicker. ([2025-02-26 36:48](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=2208))
- Gnomonkey says you typically kill the mage before anything else because it can respawn mobs, though he sometimes kills a melee first. ([2025-02-26 36:48](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=2208))
- A mage standing on one particular tile cannot be reached from behind a pillar; Gnomonkey notes that is the only spot you cannot reach. ([2025-02-26 39:14](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=2354))
- Gnomonkey shows that the space past a mage can be used as a safe area, because the melee cannot come through when the mage is too far down. ([2025-02-26 40:07](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=2407))
- Gnomonkey says to step one tile out because the mage acts as a pillar for corner trapping, which gives really good insurance that few players use. ([2025-02-26 42:18](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=2538))
- Gnomonkey says priorities are melees first because of time pressure and then the mage because it respawns things, and if there is no melee to kill, kill the mage first. ([2025-12-07 44:23](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=2663))
- Gnomonkey says to learn to lazy flick the mage immediately, because it saves prayer and builds the foundation for praying other things while the mage attacks. ([2025-12-07 44:50](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=2690))
- The best place for a mage to spawn is where you can reach and kill it quickly, which Gnomonkey calls a chef's kiss spawn. ([2025-12-07 47:10](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=2830))
- Gnomonkey says off-ticking a mage is only rarely needed to get a cape, and sometimes you kill the ranger first instead. ([2025-12-07 1:08:41](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=4121))
- On the double mage wave, camp mage prayer, get one mage as low as possible then blowpipe both back to back so one cannot respawn the other. ([2025-12-07 1:23:22](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=5002))
- Gnomonkey says the flicker of the mager is the key to knowing when it has attacked, and it lines up with the audio cue, and you want your prayer on as it flickers. ([2026-07-13 34:40](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=2080))
- Gnomonkey says not to pray ranged at the start of waves from wave 40 onward and to start every wave with Protect from Magic ideally in tank gear. ([2026-07-13 36:00](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=2160))
- Gnomonkey says to stand in the middle of the pillar when monsters drag up so they get stuck behind it, otherwise the mager comes down the side and removes your options. ([2026-07-13 38:00](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=2280))
- If you are ever unsure whether a mager can see you, pray mage, since it is better to tank something else than a mage hit. ([2026-07-13 47:00](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=2820))
- Gnomonkey says killing the ranger first is only fine if the mager is at the side of the pillar and it avoids having to off-tick something. ([2026-07-13 53:00](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=3180))
- On the final double mager wave, Gnomonkey says to kill both at the same time because they can respawn each other, and not to stand next to the pillar that falls at the end of the wave, since it takes half your health. ([2026-07-13 1:00:37](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=3637))
