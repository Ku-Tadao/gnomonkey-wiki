**Opinion: Leagues ideas** is an opinion topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2025-10-15. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the only sensible starting zone for a region-locked league is Varlamore because it has a lot of good options early, and having it unlocked by default would be nice since choosing Varlamore previously meant losing other options.[^YjYgGFi5878-1510]
* Gnomonkey rejects granular chunk-based region locking, with small map squares unlocking adjacent to existing ones, as boring, and prefers Leagues with huge area unlocks that let you do many bosses.[^YjYgGFi5878-1574]
* Gnomonkey likes the idea of additional content being added to a league during the league, citing Echo bosses.[^YjYgGFi5878-1574]
* Gnomonkey says drop protection (the longer without a rare, the likelier it drops) should never be added to the main game but sounds amazing in Leagues, where going dry is frustrating.[^YjYgGFi5878-1604]
* Gnomonkey thinks combo bosses that combine appearance, mechanics and loot of bosses from different regions sound cool, if they actually mix two bosses together like a chimera rather than just putting two in one arena.[^YjYgGFi5878-1604]
* Gnomonkey does not care for cooperative league challenges where all players must collectively unlock things, saying RS3 has done similar and nobody cares because players play Leagues for themselves.[^YjYgGFi5878-1631]
* Gnomonkey likes the idea of drop protection that removes a rare drop from the table once received until you get the other rares, saying that trying for a full Dharok set in Echo Leagues was really annoying.[^YjYgGFi5878-1658]
* Gnomonkey finds a region-locking system where a single square grows as you complete league tasks super unappealing.[^YjYgGFi5878-1658]
* Gnomonkey dislikes the idea of a league clone of your character that you control the gear and fight style of, saying that if it were passive like a thrall, with a chestplate, legs and weapon, he would find it somewhat appealing, like hiring mercenaries in Diablo 2.[^YjYgGFi5878-1689]

## Events

* He hopes the upcoming bingo league is cool and says he will definitely play it from day one and stream it.[^YjYgGFi5878-1720]
