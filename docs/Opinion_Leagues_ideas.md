## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the only sensible starting zone for a region-locked league is Varlamore because it has a lot of good options early, and having it unlocked by default would be nice since choosing Varlamore previously meant losing other options. ([2025-10-15 25:10](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=1510))
- Gnomonkey rejects granular chunk-based region locking, with small map squares unlocking adjacent to existing ones, as boring, and prefers Leagues with huge area unlocks that let you do many bosses. ([2025-10-15 26:14](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=1574))
- Gnomonkey likes the idea of additional content being added to a league during the league, citing Echo bosses. ([2025-10-15 26:14](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=1574))
- Gnomonkey says drop protection (the longer without a rare, the likelier it drops) should never be added to the main game but sounds amazing in Leagues, where going dry is frustrating. ([2025-10-15 26:44](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=1604))
- Gnomonkey thinks combo bosses that combine appearance, mechanics and loot of bosses from different regions sound cool, if they actually mix two bosses together like a chimera rather than just putting two in one arena. ([2025-10-15 26:44](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=1604))
- Gnomonkey does not care for cooperative league challenges where all players must collectively unlock things, saying RS3 has done similar and nobody cares because players play Leagues for themselves. ([2025-10-15 27:11](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=1631))
- Gnomonkey likes the idea of drop protection that removes a rare drop from the table once received until you get the other rares, saying that trying for a full Dharok set in Echo Leagues was really annoying. ([2025-10-15 27:38](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=1658))
- Gnomonkey finds a region-locking system where a single square grows as you complete league tasks super unappealing. ([2025-10-15 27:38](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=1658))
- Gnomonkey dislikes the idea of a league clone of your character that you control the gear and fight style of, saying that if it were passive like a thrall, with a chestplate, legs and weapon, he would find it somewhat appealing, like hiring mercenaries in Diablo 2. ([2025-10-15 28:09](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=1689))
