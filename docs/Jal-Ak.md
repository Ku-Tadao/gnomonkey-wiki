**Jal-Ak** (the blob) is an [Inferno](Inferno.md) monster that attacks with magic or ranged from a very long distance. Gnomonkey calls it the bane of every first-cape player's existence and the hardest thing a player deals with, but easy once the player is good at the Inferno[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-774][^h6LxupiygPA-235]. It is the mob people struggle with most because of its prayer cycle, and it is what ends up getting newer players killed, yet it is not the most dangerous mob in the Inferno and is very easy to handle once understood[^6trKOSUr4EM-1596][^H-Iup_IFUVc-713].

The blob is also valued as a "medkit". Killing it and its bloblets with a phantom [Blood Barrage](Blood_Barrage.md) heals about 40 health, which is more than any other target[^h6LxupiygPA-324][^XMTe9TYbDls-836][^XMTe9TYbDls-1632][^7F5_YBzxtE8-756]. Gnomonkey's standard approach is to learn one-tick alternating prayers to avoid all of its damage, and to leave it for last in a wave so that its health is available if a flick elsewhere goes wrong[^h6LxupiygPA-1031][^H-Iup_IFUVc-685][^7F5_YBzxtE8-2243].

## Attack cycle

A blob has a six-tick attack cycle. It reads the player's active protection prayer on the third tick and, three ticks later, attacks with the opposite style (mage if the player is praying ranged, ranged if praying mage)[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-774][^6lRmbYCYtME-236][^h6LxupiygPA-264][^XMTe9TYbDls-740][^6trKOSUr4EM-502][^H-Iup_IFUVc-647][^2xviK0wGI-o-459][^7F5_YBzxtE8-631]. A blob that sees the player step out from behind the pillar reads the prayer immediately, on the first tick it sees them, and attacks three ticks later, then continues every six ticks, so it goes read, attack, read, attack[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-774][^6lRmbYCYtME-804][^2xviK0wGI-o-459]. If the player is praying nothing or Protect from Melee when it reads, it fires a random attack[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-774].

Its mage hit is about twice as dangerous as its ranged hit. The ranged hit does about half the damage of the mage hit and is almost always a zero against a player praying mage[^6lRmbYCYtME-2159][^XMTe9TYbDls-2458]. If a player stands next to a blob, it can also melee about one time in three; a blob melee hit maxes the same number and is not a big deal[^XMTe9TYbDls-1842].

## Handling the prayer cycle

### One-tick alternating

Swapping protection prayers every game tick means the blob never lands a hit, wherever it is in the arena and not only behind a pillar[^h6LxupiygPA-293][^h6LxupiygPA-324][^6trKOSUr4EM-521][^H-Iup_IFUVc-685][^2xviK0wGI-o-517][^7F5_YBzxtE8-688]. The setup can allow one hit through (a 50-50 if unlucky) while the blob corrects, and after that it is fully consistent[^h6LxupiygPA-293][^7F5_YBzxtE8-688]. The timing is to click the next prayer as soon as the current prayer light turns off, keeping both lit[^XMTe9TYbDls-803]. While alternating, the blob stops being a problem for the player[^6trKOSUr4EM-2024].

Because a blob always ends its sixth tick on the opposite prayer, alternating lets the player flick other mobs at the same time, and it solves many later waves in which a blob is combined with another monster[^XMTe9TYbDls-1509][^H-Iup_IFUVc-685][^2xviK0wGI-o-517][^7F5_YBzxtE8-3353]. To alternate while another attacker threatens, the player clicks that attacker (for example a ranger), watches its animation and starts alternating when the animation starts[^6trKOSUr4EM-1213]. On a double-blob wave such as wave 48, the blobs do not need to be watched, only the mage or ranger: as soon as it flickers, alternating starts[^XMTe9TYbDls-1906][^XMTe9TYbDls-2317][^7F5_YBzxtE8-3353].

### Two-tick alternating and a missed read

In 2021 Gnomonkey recommended alternating either every tick or every two ticks to negate blobs entirely, and suggested entering the Inferno with just a [blowpipe](Toxic_blowpipe.md) to practise on the first blobs[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-803]. By 2024 he had changed his position on two-tick alternating: it is a 50-50 whether the player is on a bad tick, so he does not recommend it for learners or first capes[^6trKOSUr4EM-560][^7F5_YBzxtE8-717]. It needs the player to know which tick of the blob cycle they are on, and its only benefit is time for switches between prayers that a first cape will not make[^7F5_YBzxtE8-717]. The player knows they are on the right two-tick cycle when the attack starts on the second tick of the prayer[^6trKOSUr4EM-594].

After a missed blob attack, he advises defaulting to Protect from Magic, because the mage attack is about twice as dangerous as the ranged attack[^XMTe9TYbDls-2458].
### Learning it

He suggests practising the default timing on the first blob wave for about 10 minutes until the player stops taking damage, and experimenting with the first blobs of a first Inferno[^6trKOSUr4EM-1596][^H-Iup_IFUVc-685][^7F5_YBzxtE8-662].

## Bloblets

When a blob dies it takes four ticks to spill its bloblets, time that can be used to kill other mobs[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-1914]. It splits into three smaller bloblets: red melee, green ranged and blue mage[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-836][^2xviK0wGI-o-548]. Each has 15 HP and maxes an 18[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-836]. They cannot realistically be tanked and should be killed or safe-spotted straight away, ideally by switching to mage gear and sending an Ice Barrage or Blood Barrage[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-836]. A bloblet does the same damage as a bat, so they should not be left to sit and hit the player[^XMTe9TYbDls-1598].

The mage bloblet hits by far the hardest, about double the others, and is surprisingly accurate, up to 18; Gnomonkey says to pray mage whenever tanking a set of bloblets and, if nothing else is in play, against bloblets in general[^h6LxupiygPA-357][^2xviK0wGI-o-746][^7F5_YBzxtE8-3457]. After killing the ranger bloblet the player should swap to mage, since the mage bloblet can chunk them down to 70 health before a hard wave like 63[^2xviK0wGI-o-1404].

## Healing from blobs

Blobs and their bloblets are one of the best targets for regaining health with Blood Barrage because phantom heals work on all three bloblets[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-836][^XMTe9TYbDls-836][^H-Iup_IFUVc-715]. Sending a phantom Blood Barrage after killing all three bloblets can heal as much as 40 health. Gnomonkey did not know this on his first cape and now uses it all the time[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1452]. Three bloblet targets can be hit at once, for about 40 health per bloblet stack[^XMTe9TYbDls-891][^H-Iup_IFUVc-715]. Standing on the corner of a blob lines the bloblets up in a triangle so one cast hits all three, and the [Twisted bow](Twisted_bow.md) usually one-shots a blob[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-1914]. The bloblets and the Mother Blob have 95 defence, so they are a poor target for [Saradomin godsword](Saradomin_godsword.md) specs[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-836].

Positioning matters for the heal:

* A blob left behind the pillar cannot be phantomed, so it should be dragged up to the pillar area first, so that bloblets spawn in a better position to heal off. Gnomonkey says never to kill a blob away from a pillar[^H-Iup_IFUVc-3300][^2xviK0wGI-o-689][^7F5_YBzxtE8-2243]. The blobs also cannot be phantom barraged on the south pillar[^XMTe9TYbDls-2171].
* In 2022 he said he liked to kill blobs in the position where they could not be phantomed, to take them out of the picture[^6lRmbYCYtME-1247]. Later claims prefer the pillar approach and describe killing a blob early as mostly useless[^2xviK0wGI-o-3853][^7F5_YBzxtE8-2243].
* Barraging the southwest tile of a melee hits the bloblets and heals while killing things faster[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-2130].

## Kill order

He leaves blobs for last when in a good phantom position, since they are a giant medkit and a missed flick elsewhere may cost a 40[^6lRmbYCYtME-1066][^h6LxupiygPA-1031][^7F5_YBzxtE8-2243]. He rarely kills the blob first, and calls the one spawn with both blobs beside the mage a very rare solve[^h6LxupiygPA-3081]. Killing a blob early usually does nothing useful: it respawns, and the bloblets only generate damage, so sometimes it is better to do nothing than to shoot the blob[^2xviK0wGI-o-3853].
### Double blobs

In 2021 Gnomonkey called any wave with double blobs dangerous, since two sets of missed flicks risk a knockout[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1530]. Later he states that two blobs attack on the same game tick as one, so one-tick alternating handles both and both can be phantomed[^XMTe9TYbDls-1906][^7F5_YBzxtE8-1678]. With two blobs, he kills the one further back first, gives its bloblets a single cast, then kills the next blob and lets the leftover melee bloblet stack with the next set[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-1027].

## Other tips

* For a melee corner trap, click around the corner first and then down, rather than straight onto the corner trap tile, because pathing through the melee can give it about a 50% chance of leaving the trap[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-1082].
* Keep a special attack from ending a wave, because pipe specs land a tick slower than a regular shot[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-2690].
* A respawned blob has an eight-tick respawn that aligns with its attack cycle[^6trKOSUr4EM-2024].
