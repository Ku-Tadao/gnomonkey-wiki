## Methods

- Blobs read your active prayer and then use the opposite style against you, so you need to get them to read you on one prayer and then swap to the other. ([2024-01-17 3:55](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=235))
- One-tick alternating prayers against a blob (alternating every tick after it reads you) means it never hits you; it can hit you once when you set it up, a 50-50 if unlucky, and after that it is 100% consistent. ([2024-01-17 4:53](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=293))
- When a blob is killed it releases bloblets; Gnomonkey says to pray mage against bloblets if nothing else is in play, because those hit you the hardest. ([2024-01-17 5:57](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=357))
- Blobs scan your current protection prayer when you step out from the pillar and launch the opposite style attack three ticks later, on a six-tick attack cycle, reading you every third tick, according to Gnomonkey. ([2024-06-13 12:20](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=740))
- Blobs read your prayer every three ticks and three ticks later use the opposite attack of what they read, giving a weird prayer cycle. ([2024-10-03 8:22](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=502))
- One-tick alternating means swapping your prayers every game tick, which avoids 100% of a blob's damage no matter how far away the blob is. ([2024-10-03 8:41](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=521))
- To one-tick alternate when a blob and another attacker both threaten you, click the other attacker (for example a ranger), watch its animation and start alternating when the animation starts. ([2024-10-03 20:13](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=1213))
- While you one-tick alternate a blob stops existing, and a respawned blob has an eight-tick respawn that aligns with its attack cycle. ([2024-10-03 33:44](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=2024))
- Blobs scan which prayer you are using and three ticks later attack with the opposite style, on a six-tick cycle; you can swap to the other prayer every three ticks. ([2025-02-26 10:47](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=647))
- When a blob dies it spawns three small bloblets (melee, ranged and mage), which Gnomonkey says are perfect for healing off, and which can all be hit at once with a barrage. ([2025-02-26 11:55](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=715))
- Gnomonkey says swapping prayers every game tick (one-tick alternating) means you never get hit by a blob and solves many later waves where a blob is combined with another monster. ([2025-12-07 8:37](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=517))
- Blobs in the Inferno read your prayer, and three ticks later they attack with the opposite of what you were praying, with another three ticks before the attack lands. ([2026-07-13 10:31](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=631))
- One-tick alternate: swapping your protection prayers every game tick means a blob never hits you regardless of where you are in its cycle, at worst you tank one blob hit before it corrects. ([2026-07-13 11:28](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=688))

## Stats and numbers

- Blobs appear after wave 4, attack with mage or ranged from a very long distance, max a 29 and are on a six-tick attack cycle. ([2021-05-21 12:54](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=774))
- A blob reads your current prayer (mage or ranged) on the third tick of its attack and fires the opposite on the sixth tick; if you are praying nothing or protect from melee it fires a random attack, and if you step out of a safe spot it reads your prayer the first tick it sees you and fires three ticks later. ([2021-05-21 12:54](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=774))
- When killed, a blob splits into three smaller blobs (red melee, green ranged, blue mage) that max an 18 and have 15 HP; they cannot realistically be tanked and must be killed or safe spotted immediately, ideally by switching to mage gear and sending an Ice or Blood Barrage. ([2021-05-21 13:56](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=836))
- Gnomonkey says Jal-Ak blobs have a six-tick cycle: they read your prayer on the third tick, and three ticks later attack with the opposite style. ([2022-07-22 3:56](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=236))
- Gnomonkey says if a blob reads you on the first tick it attacks three ticks later and then every six ticks, so it goes read, attack, read, attack. ([2022-07-22 13:24](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=804))
- Gnomonkey says a blob's ranged hit does about half as much as its mage hit, and is almost always a zero when you are praying mage. ([2022-07-22 35:59](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2159))
- Blobs have a six tick attack speed: every three ticks they read you and the next three ticks they attack. ([2024-01-17 4:24](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=264))
- Gnomonkey says each blob is worth about 40 HP, so you can get a ton of health back off them. ([2024-01-17 5:24](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=324))
- Blobs can melee you about one time in three if you stand next to them, but a blob melee hit is not a big deal as it maxes the same number. ([2024-06-13 30:42](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1842))
- Gnomonkey says a blob takes four ticks to die and spill its bloblets, and the time can be used to kill other mobs meanwhile. ([2025-07-13 31:54](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=1914))
- Jal-Ak (blob) attacks you, waits three ticks to read your current prayer, and three ticks later launches the attack style opposite to the prayer it read; if you step out from the pillar it reads you instantly. ([2025-12-07 7:39](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=459))
- When killed, a blob splits into three bloblets. ([2025-12-07 9:08](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=548))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey recommends entering the Inferno with just a blowpipe, killing waves up to the first blob and practising flicking them; alternating mage and ranged prayer every tick, or every two ticks, completely negates them. ([2021-05-21 13:23](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=803))
- Mini blobs are one of the best targets for regaining health with Blood Barrage because phantom heals work on all three, but they and the Mother Blob have 95 defence so they are a poor target for Saradomin godsword specs. ([2021-05-21 13:56](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=836))
- Sending a phantom Blood Barrage after killing all three mini blobs can heal as much as 40 health; Gnomonkey did not know this on his first cape and now uses it all the time. ([2021-05-21 24:12](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1452))
- Gnomonkey says to leave blobs for last when in a good phantom position because they are a giant medkit worth about 40 health. ([2022-07-22 17:46](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1066))
- Gnomonkey likes to kill blobs in the position where you cannot phantom them, since it is ideal to take them out of the picture. ([2022-07-22 20:47](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=1247))
- One-tick alternating against blobs works from anywhere in the entire arena, not just behind a pillar. ([2024-01-17 5:24](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=324))
- Gnomonkey says to almost always leave the blob for last, because it gives so much health and you never know if you'll mess up a flick and take a 40. ([2024-01-17 17:11](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=1031))
- Swapping your protection prayer every game tick (alternating) fully protects you from blobs; Gnomonkey says the timing is to click the next prayer as soon as the current prayer light turns off, keeping both lit. ([2024-06-13 13:23](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=803))
- Gnomonkey calls blobs moving medkits worth 40 health because you can phantom barrage the blob and its bloblets with blood barrage to heal. ([2024-06-13 13:56](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=836))
- Gnomonkey says three bloblet targets can be phantom barraged at once, giving about 40 health per bloblet stack. ([2024-06-13 14:51](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=891))
- Gnomonkey says one-tick alternating prayers is the solve for a blob plus any other monster, always. ([2024-06-13 25:09](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1509))
- Gnomonkey says each bloblet does the same damage as a bat hitting you, so kill them and do not let them sit there hitting you. ([2024-06-13 26:38](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1598))
- With two blobs, Gnomonkey says the situation does not change: watch the ranger or mage's animation, start alternating prayers when it begins, and you can flick everything regardless of the number of blobs. ([2024-06-13 31:46](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1906))
- Gnomonkey says you cannot phantom barrage blobs on the south pillar. ([2024-06-13 36:11](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2171))
- On wave 48 (a double blob wave) Gnomonkey says you do not need to look at the blobs, only at the mage: as soon as it flickers, start alternating. ([2024-06-13 38:37](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2317))
- When you miss a blob attack, Gnomonkey says to default to praying mage because the mage attack is about two times more dangerous than the ranged attack, and to always pray mage against bloblets. ([2024-06-13 40:58](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2458))
- Gnomonkey says two-tick alternating can be bad against blobs because it is 50-50 that you are on a bad tick and take damage, and he does not recommend it for learners or first capes. ([2024-10-03 9:20](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=560))
- Gnomonkey says you know you are on the right two-tick cycle when the attack starts on the second tick of the prayer. ([2024-10-03 9:54](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=594))
- Gnomonkey says blobs are the mob people struggle with the most because of the prayer cycle, and suggests messing with the first blobs of your first Inferno to get a feel for it. ([2024-10-03 26:36](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=1596))
- If you swap your prayer every single game tick, a blob always ends on the sixth tick on the opposite prayer so it never hits you, which lets you flick other mobs at the same time; Gnomonkey says to practise this the first time you reach a blob. ([2025-02-26 11:25](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=685))
- Gnomonkey says to always pull a blob up to the pillar area before killing it, since a blob that is left behind the pillar can no longer be phantomed. ([2025-02-26 55:00](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=3300))
- With two blobs, Gnomonkey says to kill the one further back first, give its bloblets a single cast, then kill the next blob and let the leftover melee bloblet stack up with the next set. ([2025-07-13 17:07](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=1027))
- For a melee corner trap, Gnomonkey says to click around the corner first and then down rather than clicking straight to the corner trap tile, because pathing through the melee can give it about a 50% chance of leaving the corner trap. ([2025-07-13 18:02](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=1082))
- Gnomonkey recommends standing on the corner of a blob to line the bloblets up in a triangle so one cast hits all three, and says the Twisted bow usually one-shots a blob. ([2025-07-13 31:54](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=1914))
- Gnomonkey says you can barrage the southwest tile of a melee to hit the bloblets and get health back, and that this kills things faster. ([2025-07-13 35:30](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=2130))
- Gnomonkey says to keep a spec from ending a wave, because pipe specs land a tick slower than a regular shot. ([2025-07-13 44:50](https://youtu.be/UmAjGV8bBAQ?t=2690))
- When a blob is away from the pillar you should drag it up first so the bloblets spawn in a better position for you to heal off. ([2025-12-07 11:29](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=689))
- Gnomonkey says to pray mage against bloblets because the mage bloblet hits by far the hardest, about double the other bloblets. ([2025-12-07 12:26](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=746))
- After killing the ranger bloblet, swap to praying mage, because the mage bloblet hits very accurately for up to 18 and can chunk you down to 70 health before a hard wave like 63. ([2025-12-07 23:24](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=1404))
- Gnomonkey says killing a blob early usually does nothing useful because it respawns and the bloblets just generate damage for you, so sometimes it is better to do nothing than shoot the blob. ([2025-12-07 1:04:13](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=3853))
- Gnomonkey suggests practicing the default blob prayer timing on the first blob wave for about 10 minutes until you stop taking damage. ([2026-07-13 11:02](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=662))
- Gnomonkey says blobs are really good as med kits, since killing them gives you health back via Phantom barrage. ([2026-07-13 12:36](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=756))
- When there are two blobs they attack on the same game tick as one blob, so one-tick alternating handles both and you can phantom both. ([2026-07-13 27:58](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=1678))
- Gnomonkey always tries to kill the blob last in a wave because it gives health back if you mess up a flick elsewhere. ([2026-07-13 37:23](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=2243))
- Gnomonkey says never kill a blob away from a pillar, as you cannot then do the Phantom barrage; always kill it against a pillar after letting it drag in. ([2026-07-13 37:23](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=2243))
- Gnomonkey says one-tick alternating solves a wave by default as long as you flick on the right tick, so you do not need to watch the other blob. ([2026-07-13 55:53](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=3353))
- Gnomonkey says bloblets are surprisingly accurate, especially the mage bloblet, so pray mage whenever you tank a set of bloblets. ([2026-07-13 57:37](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=3457))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says blobs will be the bane of your existence on a first cape. ([2021-05-21 12:54](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=774))
- Gnomonkey says any wave with double blobs is dangerous, since missing two sets of flicks on two blobs risks a knockout. ([2021-05-21 25:30](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1530))
- Gnomonkey calls Jal-Ak (blobs) the bane of every first-cape player's existence, the hardest thing you will deal with, though easy once you are good at the Inferno. ([2024-01-17 3:55](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=235))
- Gnomonkey says he does not like killing blobs first and rarely does so, calling one spawn where both blobs are beside the mage a very rare solve. ([2024-01-17 51:21](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=3081))
- Gnomonkey says blobs give the most health of any target (about 40 on a phantom), with rangers about 20. ([2024-06-13 27:12](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1632))
- Gnomonkey says blobs are not the most dangerous mob in the Inferno and are very easy to deal with once understood, but are awkward to learn and are what end up getting newer players killed. ([2025-02-26 11:53](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=713))
- Gnomonkey does not recommend a two-tick alternate for a first cape because it needs you to know which tick of the blob cycle you are on, and its only benefit is time for switches between prayers that a first cape will not do. ([2026-07-13 11:57](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=717))
