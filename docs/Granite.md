## Opinions

- Gnomonkey disagrees with Reddit comments that granite mining is ruining the game. ([2026-05-22 0:31](https://youtu.be/hDfm2Yq1zQ0?t=31))
- Gnomonkey says Rubium was not intended to be a granite 2.0, since it was meant to be an activity for a valuable resource rather than a method where you drop everything, but as released it was granite but better in every way: easier, better money and the same speed. ([2026-05-22 4:09](https://youtu.be/hDfm2Yq1zQ0?t=249))
- Gnomonkey says some Granite sweats may want its integrity maintained, but he does not care about Granite being devalued by a better method, and does not think Jagex cares either. ([2026-05-26 2:34](https://youtu.be/Fo-0k8WROo4?t=154))
- Gnomonkey says the integrity of Mining in general should be maintained, and that a method that makes Granite pointless at the same requirements would not be healthy. ([2026-05-26 3:04](https://youtu.be/Fo-0k8WROo4?t=184))
