**Granite** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2026-05-22 and 2026-05-26. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Stats and numbers

* Granite mining at maximum efficiency (three-tick) gives about 134k Mining XP per hour.[^hDfm2Yq1zQ0-124]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey disagrees with Reddit comments that granite mining is ruining the game.[^hDfm2Yq1zQ0-31]
* Gnomonkey says Rubium was not intended to be a granite 2.0, since it was meant to be an activity for a valuable resource rather than a method where you drop everything, but as released it was granite but better in every way: easier, better money and the same speed.[^hDfm2Yq1zQ0-249]
* Gnomonkey says some Granite sweats may want its integrity maintained, but he does not care about Granite being devalued by a better method, and does not think Jagex cares either.[^Fo-0k8WROo4-154]
* Gnomonkey says the integrity of Mining in general should be maintained, and that a method that makes Granite pointless at the same requirements would not be healthy.[^Fo-0k8WROo4-184]
