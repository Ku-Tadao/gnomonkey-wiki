# Callisto

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2021-07-13 to 2022-07-13). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | boss |
| **Videos** | 3 (first 2021-07-13, latest 2022-07-13) |

## Methods

- Callisto has a more complicated lure where you pull him south into singles and attack him with a chainmace, completely AFK. ([2022-07-13 3:18](https://youtu.be/uuJsdK3_KRQ?t=198))

## Stats and numbers

- The mage thrall does no damage at Callisto because he is completely invulnerable to magic. ([2021-07-13 3:34](https://youtu.be/26wPQVuqmNY?t=214))
- Gnomonkey says Callisto gets the highest DPS increase from thralls of any boss, roughly 20%, because of his insane defence. ([2021-07-13 6:26](https://youtu.be/26wPQVuqmNY?t=386))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey considers Callisto the second-worst Wilderness boss, unaffected by the X-logging change, with one interesting trick: luring it on a different world and killing it on your main while hopping. ([2022-03-19 41:10](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2470))
- Gnomonkey says Callisto may have the highest defence of any boss, enough that thralls provide something like 20% of your DPS, and that high-defence bosses are not fun. ([2022-03-19 41:42](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2502))

## Trivia

- Gnomonkey jokes that his bone dagger tech puts Callisto in S tier: when your alt uses vulnerability, you can use a bone dagger as the first hit on the main. ([2022-03-19 42:12](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2532))
