**Callisto** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2021-07-13 and 2022-07-13. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Callisto has a more complicated lure where you pull him south into singles and attack him with a chainmace, completely AFK.[^uuJsdK3_KRQ-198]

## Stats and numbers

* The mage thrall does no damage at Callisto because he is completely invulnerable to magic.[^26wPQVuqmNY-214]
* Callisto gets the highest DPS increase from thralls of any boss, roughly 20%, because of his insane defence.[^26wPQVuqmNY-386]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey considers Callisto the second-worst Wilderness boss, unaffected by the X-logging change, with one interesting trick: luring it on a different world and killing it on your main while hopping.[^HkVt4enEesI-2470]
* Gnomonkey says Callisto may have the highest defence of any boss, enough that thralls provide something like 20% of your DPS, and that high-defence bosses are not fun.[^HkVt4enEesI-2502]

## Trivia

* Gnomonkey jokes that his bone dagger tech puts Callisto in S tier: when your alt uses vulnerability, you can use a bone dagger as the first hit on the main.[^HkVt4enEesI-2532]
