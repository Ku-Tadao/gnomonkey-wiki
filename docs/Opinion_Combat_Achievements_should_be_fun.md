**Opinion: Combat Achievements should be fun** is an opinion topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2025-09-11. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Tips

* Gnomonkey suggests a CA for getting the maximum number of hits on Bloat in Theatre of Blood (he thinks six with a scythe), reaching him on the first possible tick and then getting out of the way to tank the stomp.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-2117]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says he does not care whether Combat Achievements are hard or easy; what he cares about is whether they are fun, engaging and interesting, and he says there are easy CAs that are good and hard CAs that are good.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-174]
* Gnomonkey says a bad CA is one that is meaningless (could fit as a regular achievement diary task), depends heavily on RNG, or depends on other players' skill rather than your own.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-230]
* Gnomonkey says there are two types of Combat Achievement players: the hyper-elite sweaty player (the 0.13% Grandmasters) and the casual player who picks up CAs as they go for points and rewards, and Jagex cannot decide which it prefers.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-1223]
* Gnomonkey says if Jagex designs CAs for the hyper-elite, players who cannot get them should accept the tasks are not made for them, but that Jagex cannot include tasks where doing it right fails instantly.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-1285]
* Gnomonkey says if CAs are meant to be a pat on the back for little dev time, they should not include hyper-challenging tasks; if they are meant to be skill-based, Jagex should put in the time to make them engaging.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-1346]
* Gnomonkey considers himself the skill-based, 'sweaty' type of player and says he would like Jagex to take a stance that CAs are skill-based, fair, fun and engaging.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-1374]
* Gnomonkey says he guesses Jagex has very limited development time for CAs, so instead of adding about fifteen CAs per release including many pat-on-the-back tasks, he would like fewer CAs with more time devoted to each.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-1493]
* Gnomonkey says he likes it when skill lets you get around a gear requirement, and calls a CA that gets you to 'take the training wheels off' the peak of what Combat Achievements can be.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-1614]
* Gnomonkey says tasks like entering Bloat from the wrong side, killing a fire giant or killing a Slayer monster are participation awards that belong in regular achievement diaries, and says they should be removed from Combat Achievements.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-1793]
* Gnomonkey says restriction CAs should not be so restrictive that they become unfun; he says a melee-only CA for Doom of Mokhaiotl sounds fun but a one-handed melee-only requirement is too claustrophobic.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-1853]
* Gnomonkey says CAs should not add to the grind of getting drops, whether through RNG, kill counts or very long tasks; he says PvM itself does not feel like a grind and CAs are not collection logs or achievement diaries.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-1885]
* Gnomonkey says Combat Achievements should reward stepping outside your comfort zone and approaching a boss in a completely different, open-ended way that leaves you a better player.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-1915]
* Gnomonkey says no one can make 625 CAs interesting and engaging, so the bad ones have to be cut down, which he compares to the Slayer and summer sweep-up style QoL clean-up.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-1949]
* Gnomonkey says all kill-count CAs should have gone when Jagex reduced their requirements; he is fine with a single kill count at a boss, such as a first raid completion, but not with tasks like 50 or 150 raid completions, such as the one in Tombs of Amascut.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-2036]
* Gnomonkey says he wants Jagex to pick a lane, whether CAs are a bonus for doing bosses (clogger style) or a push for skill, and says he would be a little sad if they went the clogger route but they would at least be consistent.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-2090]
* Gnomonkey says perfection CAs do not work well because what Jagex considers perfect differs from what players do, as players sometimes tank mechanics for vengeance or extra damage; he prefers single achievements for doing something very well.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-2143]
* Gnomonkey says he wants Jagex to clean up Combat Achievements like a summer sweep-up (adding some meat to the CAs, removing bad ones) and wants the discussion to move on from difficulty to how to make CAs more fun.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-3382]
* Gnomonkey says he does not think his opinion is the be-all and end-all and that being a Grandmaster does not mean he gets to decide how all achievements should be made.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-3446]
