**Opinion: Deadman should push dangerous PvP** is an opinion topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2026-02-04. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says awarding points for nearly any activity (PvM, skilling, diaries, combat achievements, clues) lets players earn points without PvP, which goes against pushing them to dangerous areas.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-925]
* Gnomonkey says Jagex should make freezes, myth seeds, escaping and teleporting accessible to new players; he only learned about myth seeds in the last hours of a roughly 25-hour playthrough.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-978]
* Gnomonkey would give access to Desert Treasure from the start of Deadman instead of locking it behind points, since freezes are mandatory for escaping and otherwise you hope you have entangle sacks.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-1040]
* Gnomonkey would make Barrows give the most points of any boss, more than raids, because going to Barrows is more dangerous than doing Tombs of Amascut and Barrows gear is more useful for PvP than raid gear.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-1056]
* Gnomonkey suggests opening Deadman chests, going to dangerous areas, or getting one kill in a breach should give big chunks of points, for example 500 points for a kill, to encourage fights even if players use alts.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-1056]
* Gnomonkey says players hiding in raids and AFKing skills for points are not interacting with anything that makes the game mode special.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-1124]
* Gnomonkey likes the lower risk and the absence of bank keys in Deadman: Annihilation, but wants more incentive for dangerous activity and less for hiding in raids.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-1124]

## Trivia

* Myth seeds come from Legends' Quest, about eight quests deep with no unlock for it in Deadman, so the only way to get seeds is killing zombie pirates.[^6VJYbPtkvfI-978]
