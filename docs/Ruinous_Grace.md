**Ruinous Grace** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2023-03-07 and 2023-04-19. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Stats and numbers

* Ruinous Grace drains prayer points instead of run energy while active, with the rate modified by prayer bonus and not accounting for effects like stamina.[^trol59VccdE-579]
* Ruinous Grace in the beta trains about five prayer points per run energy at zero prayer bonus, and prayer bonus is the only thing that affects it.[^8VyPkG4sEwQ-122]
* At maximum prayer bonus, Ruinous Grace restores between 1 and 2 prayer points per run energy.[^8VyPkG4sEwQ-122]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey thinks Ruinous Grace would be rarely used like Vile Vigour but could see use in places like Challenge Mode Chambers of Xeric or skilling spots like ZMI if it scales heavily with prayer bonus.[^trol59VccdE-579]
* Gnomonkey says Ruinous Grace is worse in every way than Vile Vigour even at max prayer bonus, and that Vile Vigour itself is useless outside of Challenge Mode and maybe some rare cases, so Ruinous Grace can just be scrapped.[^8VyPkG4sEwQ-122]
