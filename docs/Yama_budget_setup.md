**Yama: budget setup** is an other topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2025-05-14 and 2025-05-15. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* Gnomonkey's mid-level budget setup includes Pharaoh's sceptre-era gear, a fire cape, Bandos, Torms, Blue Moon gear and Emberlight plus a purging staff as the two key weapons.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-464]
* Gnomonkey's budget Yama setup uses a cheap max cape, Bandos face guard, Torture instead of Rancour, Berserker ring instead of Ultor, and Arma-style starter gear.[^prE1BqM1wBE-1177]

## Methods

* For budget solo Yama, Gnomonkey brings Blue Moon armour, which has a strength bonus and is best if owned (another armour, transcribed as 'Aaron's', works fine), plus okay mage gear and the Purging staff, which he says is fairly close to Shadow for casting at the boss.[^ux6YsZIXGUk-0]
* Gnomonkey uses a minor melee switch with Burning claws and Light bearer to get more Burning claw specs off.[^ux6YsZIXGUk-28]
* Gnomonkey brings lots of food, using Anglerfish and Moonlight antelope; the antelope heals slightly more but slowly over time, and he calls this the highest-healing food available.[^ux6YsZIXGUk-28]
* Sand fuse cures the poison in the fight, though Restores and Antidote could also be used.[^ux6YsZIXGUk-28]
* Gnomonkey uses Forgotten brew over the Imbued heart because the heart only boosts accuracy, not max hit of demon bane, but Forgotten brew drains melee, which is not ideal.[^ux6YsZIXGUk-61]
* Gnomonkey brings runes for Death Charge, Thralls and Mark of Darkness; Mark of Darkness is the important one because it lets the player deal damage, while the others reduce damage and add DPS, and Death Charge works on almost everything in the fight.[^ux6YsZIXGUk-61]

## Stats and numbers

* Per the description, Purging staff is pretty much equal to Shadow in max gear, and Arclight is 5% worse in Bandos than scythe in giga max.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-0]
* Oathplate is only about 1.3% better than Torva at Yama, per Hedron, so it is not necessary.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-148]
* Gnomonkey's previous budget kill took about eight and a half minutes, showing accuracy matters a lot in the budget setup.[^ux6YsZIXGUk-222]
* Gnomonkey estimates the budget gear setup costs roughly 200 million GP, two synapses and mid-level gear.[^ux6YsZIXGUk-677]
* Arclight is usable and better than a scythe at Yama because demonbane weapons are multiplied by 1.2 there and the scythe is very inaccurate.[^prE1BqM1wBE-1139]
* Gnomonkey's max gear kill time was 5:46 with missed specs, and his budget kill was 6:30 with landed specs, which he calls a roughly one-minute difference showing how much the synapses carry.[^prE1BqM1wBE-1591]

## Tips

* Per the description, use Ultor and surge potions work great at Yama; bow and ranged are useless.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-0]
* Two restores are enough for the fight, bringing three to be safe, with lots of food.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-100]
* Gnomonkey recommends the Party plugin so duo partners can see each other's spec landings without needing comms.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-520]
* Forgetting Mark of Darkness drops DPS by roughly 50 percent, which Gnomonkey says was a huge mistake in his kill.[^ux6YsZIXGUk-617]
* Better mage accuracy from Virtus or Ancestral gear makes a huge difference to budget Yama kills.[^ux6YsZIXGUk-677]
* Gnomonkey leaves an inventory spot open at Yama to equip claws and avoids food juggling.[^prE1BqM1wBE-1198]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the scepter is not worth talking about for Yama because you mage so little.[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-834]
* Gnomonkey says to try to get both synapses if possible even though they are expensive, including for Iron Men.[^prE1BqM1wBE-1139]

## Records

* He has seen sub-5 minute Yama kills with the Dono fly method if you land your specs.[^prE1BqM1wBE-1050]

## Trivia

* Yama poisons, so Gnomonkey uses Sanfew serums; pre-potting extended antivenom also works and lasts long enough.[^prE1BqM1wBE-1198]
