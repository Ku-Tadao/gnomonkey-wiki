**Update: Wyrmscraig** is an update topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2026-05-29 and 2026-07-29. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Requirements

* Wormscraig is accessible at 62 Sailing.[^O4np4ssz6iw-493]

## Tips

* Ways to reach the Mad Angel: a necklace of passage, a Slayer ring and then going through Wyrmscraig Cavern past the 'Skollem' (as auto-transcribed) up the stairs (the second closest), or the tradable scroll dropped by the boss herself, which teleports you right to her and is the fastest.[^dlM9cpU1-Tc-93]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey notes Jagex said in the video that the Mad Angel is aimed as a mid-level boss, which fits the low requirements of 62 Sailing and a basic quest.[^O4np4ssz6iw-558]
* Gnomonkey says the Hallowfell sword is not very useful and kind of weak for melee training, though the regular Mad Angel drops are quite good.[^dlM9cpU1-Tc-32]

## Events

* Wormscraig is the winner of the Player Island Competition and offers an introductory quest, a repeatable boss encounter, a new high-level Slayer master and two new skilling activities.[^O4np4ssz6iw-493]
* The Wormscraig quest introduces a new boss, the Mad Angel, whose arena has a throne with handcuffs chaining the boss down in an old decaying chapel.[^O4np4ssz6iw-526]
* Completing the Wormscraig quest unlocks the same boss as a repeatable encounter, with rewards still being finalised but expected to include a new weapon, a teleport unlock and a pet.[^O4np4ssz6iw-526]
* Wyrmscraig is the player-designed island competition winner, newly added to the game, and comes with the quest Fallen from Grace that unlocks the Mad Angel boss.[^dlM9cpU1-Tc-0]
