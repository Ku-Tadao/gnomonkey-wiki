## Requirements

- Wormscraig is accessible at 62 Sailing. ([2026-05-29 8:13](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=493))

## Tips

- Ways to reach the Mad Angel: a necklace of passage, a Slayer ring and then going through Wyrmscraig Cavern past the 'Skollem' (as auto-transcribed) up the stairs (the second closest), or the tradable scroll dropped by the boss herself, which teleports you right to her and is the fastest. ([2026-07-29 1:33](https://youtu.be/dlM9cpU1-Tc?t=93))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey notes Jagex said in the video that the Mad Angel is aimed as a mid-level boss, which fits the low requirements of 62 Sailing and a basic quest. ([2026-05-29 9:18](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=558))
- Gnomonkey says the Hallowfell sword is not very useful and kind of weak for melee training, though the regular Mad Angel drops are quite good. ([2026-07-29 0:32](https://youtu.be/dlM9cpU1-Tc?t=32))

## Events

- Wormscraig is the winner of the Player Island Competition and offers an introductory quest, a repeatable boss encounter, a new high-level Slayer master and two new skilling activities. ([2026-05-29 8:13](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=493))
- The Wormscraig quest introduces a new boss, the Mad Angel, whose arena has a throne with handcuffs chaining the boss down in an old decaying chapel. ([2026-05-29 8:46](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=526))
- Completing the Wormscraig quest unlocks the same boss as a repeatable encounter, with rewards still being finalised but expected to include a new weapon, a teleport unlock and a pet. ([2026-05-29 8:46](https://youtu.be/O4np4ssz6iw?t=526))
- Wyrmscraig is the player-designed island competition winner, newly added to the game, and comes with the quest Fallen from Grace that unlocks the Mad Angel boss. ([2026-07-29 0:00](https://youtu.be/dlM9cpU1-Tc))
