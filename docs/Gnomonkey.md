# Gnomonkey

Gnomonkey is the creator of the GnomonkeyRS YouTube channel, which publishes Old School RuneScape PvM guides, teaching streams, speedruns and opinion videos, with live streams on Twitch. This page collects what he says in his videos about himself, his accounts, his channel and his personal bests.

## Background

- Gnomonkey says he started playing RuneScape in 2009 ([2024-03-02 4:06:11](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=14771)). He played RuneScape 3 for a long time and moved to Old School RuneScape in 2017 ([2025-04-02 8:59](https://youtu.be/hfrGzE8rGj0?t=539)); he also says he has played Old School since the Fossil Island update, which he thinks was 2017 ([2024-03-02 4:06:40](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=14800)), and that plugins were one of the driving factors in leaving RuneScape 3 ([2025-10-15 14:39](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=879)).
- He says he was a solo player until he made his YouTube channel ([2021-05-21 38:16](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=2296)).
- He reports over 10,000 hours on his main account in August 2022 ([2022-08-03 7:53](https://youtu.be/6OkBpQdcXRw?t=473)), and in July 2023 over 13,000 hours in Old School RuneScape and a similar amount in RS3 ([2023-07-30 24:25](https://youtu.be/iG23QZE9pds?t=1465)).
- He says he is 6'2" ([2023-07-15 17:42](https://youtu.be/K8dFZLPwDBE?t=1062)) ([2024-03-02 1:06:54](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=4014)).
- He describes himself as a PvMer who cares about bosses, depth and complexity, clear communication and quality of life ([2025-10-15 1:23](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=83)) ([2025-10-15 39:40](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=2380)), as not a PKer though he takes part in some Wilderness content ([2023-03-07 0:29](https://youtu.be/trol59VccdE?t=29)), and as someone who does not PvP at all ([2026-01-23 0:00](https://youtu.be/5qsH_-3UNQ8?t=0)). He says he dislikes tournaments, comparing speedrunning against other people to poker with luck involved ([2023-08-18 13:48](https://youtu.be/KIticRFI1Rc?t=828)) ([2024-03-02 43:05](https://youtu.be/gO3aMYXs-QA?t=2585)).
- He refers to himself as a speedrunner ([2024-06-13 29:06](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1746)) and as the Banana Man ([2023-10-21 0:00](https://youtu.be/7N_K5rlrdBA?t=0)), and says he would never take a sponsor or advert that forced an opinion untrue to what he thinks, calling himself a free thinker ([2025-11-26 4:28](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=268)).
- He lists past work as a ditch digger in Arizona heat, a plumber, in retail, at McDonald's and Target, and as an air traffic control trainee, and says he failed air traffic control training ([2026-06-25 18:03](https://youtu.be/lJexGv6dxu8?t=1083)) ([2026-06-25 1:06](https://youtu.be/lJexGv6dxu8?t=66)). He calls Deadman the hardest thing he has ever done ([2026-06-25 18:03](https://youtu.be/lJexGv6dxu8?t=1083)).
- In a 2022 stream chat reply he joked that he has been a chess player for 40 years ([2022-07-22 38:26](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2306)).
- The character Gnomonkey is the subject of the [Gnome and Monkey locked Ironman](Gnome_and_Monkey_locked_Ironman.md) series; episode 1 opens with a narrated intro about a hybrid born of neither gnome nor monkey ([2019-07-24 0:00](https://youtu.be/O6INgH6Adw0?t=0)) ([2019-07-24 1:21](https://youtu.be/O6INgH6Adw0?t=81)).
