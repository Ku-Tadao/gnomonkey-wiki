# Challenge Mode setups

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2023-06-14 to 2024-11-08). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Gnomonkey's beginner-to-intermediate speed setup uses Brimstone ring and Blood Fury, which helps survive Vanguards and Olm, plus a stamina potion. ([2023-06-14 32:25](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=1945))
- An advanced setup skips the stamina: torture plus Brimstone (with Berserker ring) gets to 60 max hit on guardians, and Gnomonkey says you want the accuracy from torture and the max hits from Berserker ring. ([2023-06-14 33:22](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=2002))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says to use one stamina dose at Vespula and keep the three other doses for Olm. ([2023-06-14 32:52](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=1972))
- Gnomonkey says Brimstone plus Blood Fury is a max hit threshold setup, and with it you put the scythe on accurate instead of aggressive; he says Brimstone is a little better than Berserker ring with Blood Fury. ([2023-06-14 32:52](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=1972))
- Gnomonkey says Dragon pegs (Pegasian boots) are quite good in CM because they add three to four percent DPS with the Twisted bow. ([2024-11-08 2:57](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=177))
- Gnomonkey says Void waker is a really good all-round spec weapon in CM because it goes through defence, and it doubles as the stab weapon for Vasa's crystals. ([2024-11-08 3:58](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=238))
- Gnomonkey says you can get away with two stamina potions in solo CM. ([2024-11-08 58:20](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=3500))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says he used to run a Thieving cape but now runs the Lightbearer because it gets an extra Ice Demon spec, an extra claw at Vanguards and Muttadiles and a bunch of hammers at Olm. ([2023-06-14 33:54](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=2034))
- Gnomonkey does not like camping the Light bearer with Blood Fury, saying it costs a lot of max hit on the scythe for little gain; bringing it as a switch makes sense. ([2023-06-14 34:51](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=2091))
- Gnomonkey says Eternal boots are only worth bringing if you are using a shadow. ([2024-11-08 3:58](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=238))
- Gnomonkey does not use a Lightbearer in CM because you recharge spec running between rooms and cannot spec Vespula, Tightrope or Guardians, so it just sits in your inventory. ([2024-11-08 4:25](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=265))
- Gnomonkey recommends a melee strength ring such as Ultor ring; without it he suggests Berserker ring or Brimstone ring (for its accuracy for maging). ([2024-11-08 5:22](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=322))
- Gnomonkey recommends using Amulet of blood fury at first rather than Amulet of torture or Necklace of anguish-style Rancor options, because the healing lets you brew less; torture or Rancor become good once you can time brewing perfectly. ([2024-11-08 5:51](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=351))
- Gnomonkey would not bring a Bandos godsword in CM or even regular Chambers, saying a Dragon warhammer is really important. ([2024-11-08 1:10:07](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=4207))
