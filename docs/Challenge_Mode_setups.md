**Challenge Mode setups** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2023-06-14 and 2024-11-08. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Gnomonkey's beginner-to-intermediate speed setup uses Brimstone ring and Blood Fury, which helps survive Vanguards and Olm, plus a stamina potion.[^8JxarzSiiME-1945]
* An advanced setup skips the stamina: torture plus Brimstone (with Berserker ring) gets to 60 max hit on guardians, and Gnomonkey says you want the accuracy from torture and the max hits from Berserker ring.[^8JxarzSiiME-2002]

## Tips

* To use one stamina dose at Vespula and keep the three other doses for Olm.[^8JxarzSiiME-1972]
* Brimstone plus Blood Fury is a max hit threshold setup, and with it you put the scythe on accurate instead of aggressive; he says Brimstone is a little better than Berserker ring with Blood Fury.[^8JxarzSiiME-1972]
* Dragon pegs (Pegasian boots) are quite good in CM because they add three to four percent DPS with the Twisted bow.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-177]
* Void waker is a really good all-round spec weapon in CM because it goes through defence, and it doubles as the stab weapon for Vasa's crystals.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-238]
* You can get away with two stamina potions in solo CM.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-3500]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says he used to run a Thieving cape but now runs the Lightbearer because it gets an extra Ice Demon spec, an extra claw at Vanguards and Muttadiles and a bunch of hammers at Olm.[^8JxarzSiiME-2034]
* Gnomonkey does not like camping the Light bearer with Blood Fury, saying it costs a lot of max hit on the scythe for little gain; bringing it as a switch makes sense.[^8JxarzSiiME-2091]
* Gnomonkey says Eternal boots are only worth bringing if you are using a shadow.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-238]
* Gnomonkey does not use a Lightbearer in CM because you recharge spec running between rooms and cannot spec Vespula, Tightrope or Guardians, so it just sits in your inventory.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-265]
* Gnomonkey recommends a melee strength ring such as Ultor ring; without it he suggests Berserker ring or Brimstone ring (for its accuracy for maging).[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-322]
* Gnomonkey recommends using Amulet of blood fury at first rather than Amulet of torture or Necklace of anguish-style Rancor options, because the healing lets you brew less; torture or Rancor become good once you can time brewing perfectly.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-351]
* Gnomonkey would not bring a Bandos godsword in CM or even regular Chambers, saying a Dragon warhammer is really important.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-4207]
