# Echo boots

## Stats and numbers

- Echo boots have 4 prayer bonus and 24 ranged defence, with only -3 mage and -1 ranged accuracy; Gnomonkey thinks of them as an upgraded devout boots. ([2024-06-13 4:09](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=249))
- In the 1,000 Jad run Gnomonkey used 1,369 Echo boot charges, and notes the boots only use one charge every four ticks. ([2026-05-05 6:57](https://youtu.be/AuUbRTt0YnI?t=417))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says that if you own Echo boots and it is a first Inferno cape, they are the best boots option with no question. ([2024-06-13 4:09](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=249))
- Gnomonkey ranks Echo boots first for Inferno, Pegasian boots a great next choice, then Eternal boots which give a max hit on your mage with no negatives; he calls devout boots irrelevant now. ([2024-06-13 4:38](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=278))
- Gnomonkey says the most important things to maximise in Inferno are prayer bonus and ranged defence, by far. ([2024-06-13 4:38](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=278))
- Gnomonkey says he hates Echo boots and thinks they are really dumb in general, but that Inferno is the one use case where they are really good. ([2024-06-13 5:39](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=339))
