**Echo boots** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2024-06-13 and 2026-05-05. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Stats and numbers

* Echo boots have 4 prayer bonus and 24 ranged defence, with only -3 mage and -1 ranged accuracy; Gnomonkey thinks of them as an upgraded devout boots.[^XMTe9TYbDls-249]
* In the 1,000 Jad run Gnomonkey used 1,369 Echo boot charges, and notes the boots only use one charge every four ticks.[^AuUbRTt0YnI-417]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says that if you own Echo boots and it is a first Inferno cape, they are the best boots option with no question.[^XMTe9TYbDls-249]
* Gnomonkey ranks Echo boots first for Inferno, Pegasian boots a great next choice, then Eternal boots which give a max hit on your mage with no negatives; he calls devout boots irrelevant now.[^XMTe9TYbDls-278]
* Gnomonkey says the most important things to maximise in Inferno are prayer bonus and ranged defence, by far.[^XMTe9TYbDls-278]
* Gnomonkey says he hates Echo boots and thinks they are really dumb in general, but that Inferno is the one use case where they are really good.[^XMTe9TYbDls-339]
