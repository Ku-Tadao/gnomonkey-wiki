# Phosani's Nightmare

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 5 videos (2022-03-19 to 2025-07-04). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | boss |
| **Videos** | 5 (first 2022-03-19, latest 2025-07-04) |

## Methods

- The wiki's Phosani's Nightmare downgrade setup used a Dwarven helmet for crush bonus, an amulet of glory, an obsidian cape, Verac's armour, an obsidian shield, a combat bracelet, guardian boots, a ring of suffering, a dragon mace as the special weapon and a Trident of the Swamp. ([2024-05-06 0:26](https://youtu.be/zHxGjKaeTNU?t=26))
- In Phosani's Nightmare you damage her shield to zero, then kill all four totems in the corners with magic; the totems then hit Phosani's Nightmare for chunks of her health bar and a set of sleepwalkers spawns. ([2025-07-04 4:03](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=243))
- After the final set of four sleepwalkers, Phosani's Nightmare spawns in the middle of the room, heals 150 health and constantly spams shadow claws while sleepwalkers walk toward her, each chipping you 15 health when it reaches her; depleting her remaining 150 health finishes the kill. ([2025-07-04 4:30](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=270))
- Her ranged attack is a repeated back snap before hurling a spike, her magic attack is red particles with arms swung in a circle, and her melee attack, the most dangerous and fastest, has her place her arms behind her and swing toward you. ([2025-07-04 4:30](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=270))

## Requirements

- Phosani's Nightmare needs pretty high stats, at least 90s, and the Attack level matters much more than usual because of her very high defence. ([2025-07-04 0:26](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=26))
- For mage gear in Phosani's Nightmare, only your max hit matters because you are guaranteed to hit with magic. ([2025-07-04 2:33](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=153))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey notes the Osmumten's fang does not benefit from the Dwarven helmet's crush bonus, so the helmet gives no strength bonus with a fang. ([2024-05-06 0:55](https://youtu.be/zHxGjKaeTNU?t=55))
- Phosani's Nightmare has a heavy weakness to crush, so you basically need to use a crush weapon, and you use both melee and magic in the fight. ([2025-07-04 0:26](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=26))
- Almost any mistake in Phosani's Nightmare is punished by 60 to 80 damage, so any two mistakes will kill you almost instantly. ([2025-07-04 3:33](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=213))
- Phosani's Nightmare has three attack styles that must be prayed after the animation, and all of them max over 70 damage; game sounds give clear cues for each. ([2025-07-04 4:30](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=270))
- Phosani's Nightmare can only use two special attacks per phase and then uses two new specials next phase, cycling through them; they are amped-up versions of regular Nightmare specials and can one-shot you if you blink more than once in a row. ([2025-07-04 5:24](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=324))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says the fang worked better on Phosani's Nightmare before its first nerf, and that the fang special does not bind her. ([2024-05-06 6:17](https://youtu.be/zHxGjKaeTNU?t=377))
- In the video Gnomonkey finds the wiki-recommended dragon mace special one-shots a parasite, though he later notes parasites may have around 55 HP and that it does not always do so. ([2024-05-06 6:17](https://youtu.be/zHxGjKaeTNU?t=377))
- The Soul Reaper axe needs constant recharging after maging, but there are a couple of transition cutscenes where you can begin charging it early by hitting Phosani's Nightmare for zeros. ([2025-07-04 0:54](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=54))
- Gnomonkey says Blood Fury can be nice while learning, but the accuracy and max hit boost of the Ring of Rancor is incredibly important here. ([2025-07-04 1:28](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=88))
- Gnomonkey says it is incredibly important to get a Void Waker before Phosani's Nightmare if possible, because it is over two times better than other spec options. ([2025-07-04 1:28](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=88))
- If you do not have a Void Waker and use a worse spec weapon such as claws, or your melee ring, Gnomonkey says Dinh's (bulwark spec) is also a good option for specing husks when she spawns them, as it instantly kills them. ([2025-07-04 2:33](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=153))
- Gnomonkey highly recommends getting Mystic Vigor from the Royal Titans before Phosani's Nightmare, as it is the same as Augury in almost every case and maxes the same at half the prayer drain while maging. ([2025-07-04 3:05](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=185))
- Sleepwalkers in Phosani's Nightmare must be killed before they reach her or you take massive or even lethal damage; the cycle is repeated three times in total. ([2025-07-04 4:03](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=243))
- Phosani's Nightmare melee attack can also be avoided by simply stepping away, causing it to miss. ([2025-07-04 4:56](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=296))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says he is not a huge fan of the Nightmare and, in the video, assumes he is talking about Phosani's Nightmare because it pretty much replaces the regular Nightmare. ([2022-03-19 9:14](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=554))
- Gnomonkey rates Phosani's Nightmare A tier content, saying it takes a lot of skill and practice but is lacking something: the skill is not making a mistake against a high-hitting boss, and he prefers content that pushes you toward better times or more damage. ([2022-03-19 10:10](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=610))
- In the final ordering Gnomonkey places Nex above Phosani's Nightmare in A tier. ([2022-03-19 1:30:02](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=5402))
- Gnomonkey says the regular Nightmare of Ashihama requires catching prayers before they land and some very basic movement, and that its only real difficulty is that it is a very long fight. ([2023-07-30 6:03](https://youtu.be/iG23QZE9pds?t=363))
- Gnomonkey says Phosani's Nightmare, added in 2021, removed the one aspect the Nightmare had going for it, the ability to do it with friends, by making it solo only. ([2023-07-30 7:33](https://youtu.be/iG23QZE9pds?t=453))
- Gnomonkey says Phosani's mechanics are not harder than the regular Nightmare except that she punishes mistakes with very high damage, and argues that solo she is easier than the regular Nightmare because the fight is much shorter and there is no chip damage through prayer. ([2023-07-30 7:33](https://youtu.be/iG23QZE9pds?t=453))
- Gnomonkey says Phosani's Nightmare has a really good hitbox, one of the best in the game, with clickboxes on its runes and a full box around it, making it hard to misclick. ([2024-05-06 0:26](https://youtu.be/zHxGjKaeTNU?t=26))
- Gnomonkey mocks the wiki setup's use of Verac's at Phosani's Nightmare, saying the hits are guaranteed and cannot be blocked so you might as well wear nothing, and that a ring of suffering does nothing there. ([2024-05-06 0:55](https://youtu.be/zHxGjKaeTNU?t=55))
- Gnomonkey says the Osmumten's fang is not good on Phosani's Nightmare, even if every hit maxes it would not get a grandmaster time, and that a cudgel is still better. ([2024-05-06 5:22](https://youtu.be/zHxGjKaeTNU?t=322))
- Gnomonkey says the fight shows why bots can kill Phosani's Nightmare in terrible gear. ([2024-05-06 10:25](https://youtu.be/zHxGjKaeTNU?t=625))
- Gnomonkey says this is effectively a kills-per-hour buff and loot buff, bringing the long fight closer in line with Yama kill times and making drops easier to get. ([2025-05-30 7:51](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=471))
- Gnomonkey says he is not a Phosani's Nightmare expert and honestly hates the boss, but with the new summer update she is much more tolerable, and viewers voted for this to be the next guide. ([2025-07-04 0:00](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI))
- Gnomonkey says the three weapons at the top for Phosani's Nightmare are the Scythe of Vitur (slightly ahead), the Inquisitor's mace (transcribed as Inkmace) and the Soul Reaper axe. ([2025-07-04 0:26](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=26))
- Gnomonkey says the next best weapons are close between the Osmumten's fang, the Bludgeon and the Blood Moon set; he prefers the fang even though the bludgeon beats it by about 2% DPS, because the fang lets you five-tick the shadow phases. ([2025-07-04 0:54](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=54))
- Gnomonkey says Inquisitor's armour is the best-in-slot melee armour for Phosani's Nightmare, then Torva, and then Oathplate, Bandos and Blood Moon are acceptable downgrades with identical strength bonus. ([2025-07-04 1:28](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=88))
- Gnomonkey says Lightbearer is a much better option than people give it credit for: by his maths camping it while maging saves over double the time of a Magus ring, and camping it with a Scythe only loses around 3 seconds over the whole kill despite the 6 max hit loss. ([2025-07-04 1:59](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=119))
- Gnomonkey says Harmonised nightmare staff is technically best in slot with Sunfire runes, but losing double death charge and thralls makes it slightly worse than the Tumeken's shadow; a four-weight Trident of the Swamp is plenty good at about 20% worse than the shadow setup. ([2025-07-04 2:33](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=153))
- Gnomonkey contrasts the regular Nightmare, a slow group boss that does not punish mistakes hard but has constant chip damage, with Phosani's Nightmare, which is the opposite. ([2025-07-04 3:33](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=213))
- Gnomonkey says the Phosani's Nightmare fight feels a lot better, more approachable and less of a slog after the sleepy tablet and phase removal, and recommends trying it even if the rewards have kept you away. ([2025-07-04 10:11](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=611))

## Records

- Gnomonkey completed a Phosani's Nightmare kill in the wiki's recommended downgrade gear in about 13 minutes, using four pieces of food, three restores and a dose, and called it surprisingly not that bad. ([2024-05-06 8:42](https://youtu.be/zHxGjKaeTNU?t=522))
- With the Gearscape-suggested Maracas setup, Gnomonkey killed Phosani's Nightmare in about eleven minutes, roughly two and a half minutes faster than with the wiki setup despite making mistakes and taking a triple parasite heal. ([2024-05-06 17:04](https://youtu.be/zHxGjKaeTNU?t=1024))
- Gnomonkey found about 5.5 kills per hour in max gear fairly consistent after the summer update, which is just under GM time and means upwards of 8 million gp/hr with good gear, versus about 3 million gp/hr before. ([2025-07-04 0:00](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI))
- With the lowest recommended budget setup, Gnomonkey's Phosani's Nightmare kills took around six and a half to seven minutes, while his max setup averaged around five and a half minutes. ([2025-07-04 10:11](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=611))

## Events

- The update removes a phase from Phosani's Nightmare so it has three phases instead of four, without nerfing the loot. ([2025-05-30 7:51](https://youtu.be/EVx2KvYGjxM?t=471))
- The summer update removed an entire phase of Phosani's Nightmare while leaving the loot the exact same. ([2025-07-04 0:00](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI))
- After the summer update, Phosani's Nightmare kill times are similar to a solo Yama; kills are quick, deadly and completely solo. ([2025-07-04 3:33](https://youtu.be/BBd0ViAfZRI?t=213))

## Trivia

- Gnomonkey describes the Nightmare as having a big health pool hit with crush, very high defence, then a totem phase where you eight-way into magic to kill the totems; special attacks include husks, parasites and shadows, and everything kills you in two hits. ([2022-03-19 9:41](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=581))
