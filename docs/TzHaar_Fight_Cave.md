The TzHaar Fight Cave is a 63-wave activity ending with [TzTok-Jad](TzTok-Jad.md), completed to obtain the [fire cape](Fire_cape.md). Gnomonkey describes it as far less complicated than its reputation for players with decent stats and a [toxic blowpipe](Toxic_blowpipe.md), and has covered a standard blowpipe guide, Slayer-task and pet value, speedruns, a mage-only challenge and a low-level melee run.

[TOC]

## Requirements and preparation

- There are no requirements, but Gnomonkey recommends completing Animal Magnetism (for the ava's accumulator) and Rum Deal (for the Holy wrench)[^oG65hOavtJg-0]. The Holy wrench gives 1 to 2 extra prayer points per prayer dose while in the inventory, which typically saves more than a full prayer potion[^oG65hOavtJg-0].
- The caves are doable with only 43 Prayer, though higher Prayer eases supplies; he recommends 60+ Defence, and says Rigor greatly speeds up the caves[^oG65hOavtJg-31].
- He recommends getting a Slayer task before the first run (a viewer suggestion he repeats)[^puC-_fc7y0o-745]. An imbued black mask or Slayer helmet greatly raises DPS on a Fight Caves task, but failing or leaving early cancels the task[^oG65hOavtJg-58].
- Route: fairy ring code BLP and run north, or the Karamja teleport on an amulet of glory then west to the volcano; a bank is next to the cave[^oG65hOavtJg-275].

## Gear and inventory (2020 blowpipe guide)

- His footage used max gear and Void. Downgrades he lists: boots to Blessed dragonhide or snakeskin, necklace of anguish to fury or glory, assembler to accumulator[^oG65hOavtJg-116]. He calls the Armadyl armour deal very expensive for a small DPS boost, and suggests black dragonhide vambraces or a regen bracelet without [Barrows gloves](Barrows_gloves.md)[^oG65hOavtJg-150].
- On a new account with no Void and no task he would bring a fury, Blessed dragonhide boots (or glory and snakeskin boots) and black dragonhide vambraces[^oG65hOavtJg-184].
- Inventory: no Saradomin brews, since they stop use of the three divine ranging potions; a wrench, 15 prayer potions and eight sharks[^oG65hOavtJg-184]. With budget gear he suggests manta rays or dark crabs, and with Defence below 60 maybe 10 manta rays and two fewer prayer potions[^oG65hOavtJg-218].
- Each divine ranging dose damages the player by 10 but keeps the boost for five minutes; the blowpipe special can restore the health[^oG65hOavtJg-246]. He stays potted nearly the whole caves and saves at least one dose for Jad[^oG65hOavtJg-398]. He flicks Eagle Eye or Rigor to raise DPS and save prayer[^oG65hOavtJg-246].
- He uses blowpipe specials when health is below what they can restore (50% of the max hit, 28 at 75 Ranged with Eagle Eye and a boost potion), preferably on Rangers[^oG65hOavtJg-398][^oG65hOavtJg-424].

## Enemies and waves

Each wave adds another of the weakest enemy, and two of the same enemy combine into the next tier in the following wave[^oG65hOavtJg-279].

| Enemy | Gnomonkey's notes |
|---|---|
| 22 (bats) | Harmless but sap a prayer point per attack; fairly high priority[^oG65hOavtJg-310] |
| 45 (Keks) | Lowest priority; only hurt at low defence[^oG65hOavtJg-310] |
| 90 (Rangers) | Max hit 13, fairly accurate, main source of damage; high priority[^oG65hOavtJg-339] |
| 180 (melee) | Max hit 25, can heal others, can be safe-spotted; low priority[^oG65hOavtJg-339] |
| 360 (magers) | Max hit 54 magic or melee; protect from magic when around; medium to high priority[^oG65hOavtJg-365] |

- He stays in the south-western part under the peninsula, a safe spot, for about 99% of the caves[^oG65hOavtJg-365].
- Waves 1-6 matter little; the first Ranger spawns at wave 7, after which he uses protect from missiles when safe until wave 15[^oG65hOavtJg-446]. The first 180 appears at wave 15; he stands below the rock so it cannot attack, and 180s use fewer supplies than Rangers[^oG65hOavtJg-455]. A 180 spawning adjacent is lured by tanking one hit with protect from melee and running back through[^oG65hOavtJg-482].
- From wave 22 a Ranger and a 180 chase together; the Ranger takes priority if reachable first[^oG65hOavtJg-482]. Wave 29 (two Rangers and a 180) is tough and wave 30 has two melee enemies, one usually lured[^oG65hOavtJg-548].
- Wave 31 is the first 360; he keeps protect from magic on for the rest and does not flick magic, since one misflick can devastate supplies[^oG65hOavtJg-548]. Protect from magic comes off only if a 360 is trapped in the north; from wave 38 each wave has a 360 and a Ranger, Ranger first[^oG65hOavtJg-580].
- Wave 45 has two Rangers and a 360; 180s return from wave 46, which he says makes later waves easier[^oG65hOavtJg-612]. A 360 spawning adjacent can be attacked after one step toward it, gaining a tile of range while still trapping the 180[^oG65hOavtJg-639].
- From wave 53 to 61 Rangers, 180s and three 360s are on the board[^oG65hOavtJg-639]. Wave 60 (two Rangers, a 180, a 360) is the most dangerous besides Jad; wave 61 has two 180s and a 360[^oG65hOavtJg-665]. Wave 62 has two 360s, one bright orange marking Jad's spawn; boosts, health and prayer should be restored first[^oG65hOavtJg-696].

## Rewards and Slayer task

- Completion gives a fire cape and 8,032 tokkul, about 85k GP if saved for onyx[^oG65hOavtJg-844]. He recommends destroying the first duplicate cape at TzHaar to access the [Inferno](Inferno.md), then sacrificing extra capes for pet rolls[^oG65hOavtJg-844].
- The pet is 1 in 100 per task base, plus 1 in 200 from sacrificing a cape, about 1 in 67 per task[^oG65hOavtJg-861][^vwCm9NKJhRE-1021]. The 2020 guide gives 1 in 100 on task versus 1 in 200 off task[^oG65hOavtJg-86].
- The task gives 50k Slayer XP an hour[^oG65hOavtJg-86]. In a later video, finishing on task gives an extra 25k XP plus kill XP (37k total), and a 40-minute blowpipe run gives about 60k Slayer XP an hour[^4DvqehbIFnQ-188][^4DvqehbIFnQ-217]. With a [Twisted bow](Twisted_bow.md) he considers it worth doing even with the pet, with times near 30 minutes[^4DvqehbIFnQ-248].
- He got the Jad pet on his 30th Fight Caves and relocked the task[^4DvqehbIFnQ-217]; in 2020 he had already disabled the task for the same reason[^oG65hOavtJg-86]. Fight Caves tasks are great for a Jad pet[^4DvqehbIFnQ-188]. If starting a new account he would do Slayer after getting the fire cape[^oG65hOavtJg-58].

## Speedruns and opinions

- In March 2022 he placed a standard run in B tier and Fight Caves speedrunning in S tier, calling speedruns skill-intensive: third age bow, long-range fast hits, a website of run routes, not dragging enemies, venging when healthy, and Guthix rest to avoid wasted ticks; Jad is double-venged to end on one health[^HkVt4enEesI-1971][^HkVt4enEesI-2027]. He dislikes that speedruns require grinding Slayer tasks but is glad of an off-task category[^HkVt4enEesI-1998].
- He put the TzRek-Jad pet in low A tier, less cool than the Inferno pet[^vwCm9NKJhRE-1052].
- He likes the "Jad, no time for a drink" [Combat Achievement](Combat_Achievements.md) (no prayer points lost): a bat touching the player fails it, and he calls it the hardest prayer task in the game, building [prayer flicking](Prayer_flicking.md) and spawn handling like the Inferno[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-865][^ZjG3P7PTeIs-898][^ZjG3P7PTeIs-926].
- He says the caves came out in October 2005 and were beaten on day one[^puC-_fc7y0o-905]. Spawns are much faster than the Inferno's[^puC-_fc7y0o-850], and NPC click boxes are better than Inferno hitboxes[^puC-_fc7y0o-569].

## Challenge runs

**Mage only (2024).** For the Wiki Guides challenge series he did a mage-only run with the OSRS Wiki setup and the worst listed downgrades[^puC-_fc7y0o-0]. Infinity robes were worn throughout, fire runes were carried loose (262,000 at the start)[^puC-_fc7y0o-4]. He considers the wiki setup poor, since it relies on Infinity-tier gear and Barrage spells he cannot afford runes for[^puC-_fc7y0o-4], and prefers thralls over Ice or Blood Barrage here[^puC-_fc7y0o-57]. His gear had melee strength 6 and prayer 15[^puC-_fc7y0o-87]. Rangers have magic level 60, and he says each tier roughly doubles the previous magic level; the melee monster has about 80 health and magic 120[^puC-_fc7y0o-315][^puC-_fc7y0o-437]. The melee monster drains prayer and heals only while meleeing[^puC-_fc7y0o-468][^puC-_fc7y0o-495]. Base staves cannot autocast Flames of Zamorak (max hit 20, no god cape needed)[^puC-_fc7y0o-1670][^puC-_fc7y0o-1688]. Finished in 121:09, using about 17,000 fire runes[^puC-_fc7y0o-1925][^puC-_fc7y0o-2048]. He says the wiki's better setup (Shadow and full Ancestral, Sang, Ward, Eternal boots, Magus ring) would be much better[^puC-_fc7y0o-970], and no boots beats the wiki's boots downgrade[^puC-_fc7y0o-257].

**Low-level melee on group hardcore ironman (2024).** On a [Group Hardcore Ironman](Group_Hardcore_Ironman.md) he planned Soul Wars money, Monkey Madness, combat levels, the defender grind, then a melee run[^_cIv_pRhm9U-0]. Needs: supplies, Prayer training, weapon poison (the hard part), a dragon defender and food[^_cIv_pRhm9U-0]. He started with 64 Hitpoints, noting only Jad can one-shot him, so he must not misplay prayer there[^_cIv_pRhm9U-3013][^_cIv_pRhm9U-3105]. Manta rays heal 22, needing 42 Hitpoints for full efficiency at max 64[^_cIv_pRhm9U-3135]. Mage monsters max 52 with magic and 54 in melee[^_cIv_pRhm9U-3228]. Pulling monsters to safe spots such as the northeast corner lets him fight them one at a time[^_cIv_pRhm9U-3438]. The 250 Temple Spiders killed for poison were worth it[^_cIv_pRhm9U-3736], though poison helps only minorly for the defender grind and caves[^_cIv_pRhm9U-1819]; Attack potions add about 0.1 DPS on Jad, not worth it[^_cIv_pRhm9U-3950]. His setup has roughly half the strength bonus of the wiki's lowest melee gear[^_cIv_pRhm9U-4936]. He estimated forty minutes to Jad with 21 or 22 mage monsters left at wave 63, and reached Jad about 1:54 into the video, waves starting at about 51 minutes[^_cIv_pRhm9U-4462][^_cIv_pRhm9U-6867]. He believes it may be the first no-scythe melee Fight Caves[^_cIv_pRhm9U-5996].
