# TzHaar Fight Cave

The TzHaar Fight Cave is a 63-wave activity ending with [TzTok-Jad](TzTok-Jad.md), completed to obtain the [fire cape](Fire_cape.md). Gnomonkey describes it as far less complicated than its reputation for players with decent stats and a [toxic blowpipe](Toxic_blowpipe.md), and has covered a standard blowpipe guide, Slayer-task and pet value, speedruns, a mage-only challenge and a low-level melee run.

## Requirements and preparation

- There are no requirements, but Gnomonkey recommends completing Animal Magnetism (for the ava's accumulator) and Rum Deal (for the Holy wrench) ([2020-04-27 0:00](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=0)). The Holy wrench gives 1 to 2 extra prayer points per prayer dose while in the inventory, which typically saves more than a full prayer potion ([2020-04-27 0:00](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=0)).
- The caves are doable with only 43 Prayer, though higher Prayer eases supplies; he recommends 60+ Defence, and says Rigor greatly speeds up the caves ([2020-04-27 0:31](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=31)).
- He recommends getting a Slayer task before the first run (a viewer suggestion he repeats) ([2024-05-09 12:25](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=745)). An imbued black mask or Slayer helmet greatly raises DPS on a Fight Caves task, but failing or leaving early cancels the task ([2020-04-27 0:58](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=58)).
- Route: fairy ring code BLP and run north, or the Karamja teleport on an amulet of glory then west to the volcano; a bank is next to the cave ([2020-04-27 4:35](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=275)).

## Gear and inventory (2020 blowpipe guide)

- His footage used max gear and Void. Downgrades he lists: boots to Blessed dragonhide or snakeskin, necklace of anguish to fury or glory, assembler to accumulator ([2020-04-27 1:56](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=116)). He calls the Armadyl armour deal very expensive for a small DPS boost, and suggests black dragonhide vambraces or a regen bracelet without [Barrows gloves](Barrows_gloves.md) ([2020-04-27 2:30](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=150)).
- On a new account with no Void and no task he would bring a fury, Blessed dragonhide boots (or glory and snakeskin boots) and black dragonhide vambraces ([2020-04-27 3:04](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=184)).
- Inventory: no Saradomin brews, since they stop use of the three divine ranging potions; a wrench, 15 prayer potions and eight sharks ([2020-04-27 3:04](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=184)). With budget gear he suggests manta rays or dark crabs, and with Defence below 60 maybe 10 manta rays and two fewer prayer potions ([2020-04-27 3:38](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=218)).
- Each divine ranging dose damages the player by 10 but keeps the boost for five minutes; the blowpipe special can restore the health ([2020-04-27 4:06](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=246)). He stays potted nearly the whole caves and saves at least one dose for Jad ([2020-04-27 6:38](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=398)). He flicks Eagle Eye or Rigor to raise DPS and save prayer ([2020-04-27 4:06](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=246)).
- He uses blowpipe specials when health is below what they can restore (50% of the max hit, 28 at 75 Ranged with Eagle Eye and a boost potion), preferably on Rangers ([2020-04-27 6:38](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=398)) ([2020-04-27 7:04](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=424)).

## Enemies and waves

Each wave adds another of the weakest enemy, and two of the same enemy combine into the next tier in the following wave ([2020-04-27 4:39](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=279)).

| Enemy | Gnomonkey's notes |
|---|---|
| 22 (bats) | Harmless but sap a prayer point per attack; fairly high priority ([2020-04-27 5:10](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=310)) |
| 45 (Keks) | Lowest priority; only hurt at low defence ([2020-04-27 5:10](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=310)) |
| 90 (Rangers) | Max hit 13, fairly accurate, main source of damage; high priority ([2020-04-27 5:39](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=339)) |
| 180 (melee) | Max hit 25, can heal others, can be safe-spotted; low priority ([2020-04-27 5:39](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=339)) |
| 360 (magers) | Max hit 54 magic or melee; protect from magic when around; medium to high priority ([2020-04-27 6:05](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=365)) |

- He stays in the south-western part under the peninsula, a safe spot, for about 99% of the caves ([2020-04-27 6:05](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=365)).
- Waves 1-6 matter little; the first Ranger spawns at wave 7, after which he uses protect from missiles when safe until wave 15 ([2020-04-27 7:26](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=446)). The first 180 appears at wave 15; he stands below the rock so it cannot attack, and 180s use fewer supplies than Rangers ([2020-04-27 7:35](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=455)). A 180 spawning adjacent is lured by tanking one hit with protect from melee and running back through ([2020-04-27 8:02](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=482)).
- From wave 22 a Ranger and a 180 chase together; the Ranger takes priority if reachable first ([2020-04-27 8:02](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=482)). Wave 29 (two Rangers and a 180) is tough and wave 30 has two melee enemies, one usually lured ([2020-04-27 9:08](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=548)).
- Wave 31 is the first 360; he keeps protect from magic on for the rest and does not flick magic, since one misflick can devastate supplies ([2020-04-27 9:08](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=548)). Protect from magic comes off only if a 360 is trapped in the north; from wave 38 each wave has a 360 and a Ranger, Ranger first ([2020-04-27 9:40](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=580)).
- Wave 45 has two Rangers and a 360; 180s return from wave 46, which he says makes later waves easier ([2020-04-27 10:12](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=612)). A 360 spawning adjacent can be attacked after one step toward it, gaining a tile of range while still trapping the 180 ([2020-04-27 10:39](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=639)).
- From wave 53 to 61 Rangers, 180s and three 360s are on the board ([2020-04-27 10:39](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=639)). Wave 60 (two Rangers, a 180, a 360) is the most dangerous besides Jad; wave 61 has two 180s and a 360 ([2020-04-27 11:05](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=665)). Wave 62 has two 360s, one bright orange marking Jad's spawn; boosts, health and prayer should be restored first ([2020-04-27 11:36](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=696)).

## Rewards and Slayer task

- Completion gives a fire cape and 8,032 tokkul, about 85k GP if saved for onyx ([2020-04-27 14:04](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=844)). He recommends destroying the first duplicate cape at TzHaar to access the [Inferno](Inferno.md), then sacrificing extra capes for pet rolls ([2020-04-27 14:04](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=844)).
- The pet is 1 in 100 per task base, plus 1 in 200 from sacrificing a cape, about 1 in 67 per task ([2020-04-27 14:21](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=861)) ([2022-03-12 17:01](https://youtu.be/vwCm9NKJhRE?t=1021)). The 2020 guide gives 1 in 100 on task versus 1 in 200 off task ([2020-04-27 1:26](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=86)).
- The task gives 50k Slayer XP an hour ([2020-04-27 1:26](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=86)). In a later video, finishing on task gives an extra 25k XP plus kill XP (37k total), and a 40-minute blowpipe run gives about 60k Slayer XP an hour ([2020-09-19 3:08](https://youtu.be/4DvqehbIFnQ?t=188)) ([2020-09-19 3:37](https://youtu.be/4DvqehbIFnQ?t=217)). With a [Twisted bow](Twisted_bow.md) he considers it worth doing even with the pet, with times near 30 minutes ([2020-09-19 4:08](https://youtu.be/4DvqehbIFnQ?t=248)).
- He got the Jad pet on his 30th Fight Caves and relocked the task ([2020-09-19 3:37](https://youtu.be/4DvqehbIFnQ?t=217)); in 2020 he had already disabled the task for the same reason ([2020-04-27 1:26](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=86)). Fight Caves tasks are great for a Jad pet ([2020-09-19 3:08](https://youtu.be/4DvqehbIFnQ?t=188)). If starting a new account he would do Slayer after getting the fire cape ([2020-04-27 0:58](https://youtu.be/oG65hOavtJg?t=58)).

## Speedruns and opinions

- In March 2022 he placed a standard run in B tier and Fight Caves speedrunning in S tier, calling speedruns skill-intensive: third age bow, long-range fast hits, a website of run routes, not dragging enemies, venging when healthy, and Guthix rest to avoid wasted ticks; Jad is double-venged to end on one health ([2022-03-19 32:51](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1971)) ([2022-03-19 33:47](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2027)). He dislikes that speedruns require grinding Slayer tasks but is glad of an off-task category ([2022-03-19 33:18](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=1998)).
- He put the TzRek-Jad pet in low A tier, less cool than the Inferno pet ([2022-03-12 17:32](https://youtu.be/vwCm9NKJhRE?t=1052)).
- He likes the "Jad, no time for a drink" [Combat Achievement](Combat_Achievements.md) (no prayer points lost): a bat touching the player fails it, and he calls it the hardest prayer task in the game, building [prayer flicking](Prayer_flicking.md) and spawn handling like the Inferno ([2025-09-11 14:25](https://youtu.be/ZjG3P7PTeIs?t=865)) ([2025-09-11 14:58](https://youtu.be/ZjG3P7PTeIs?t=898)) ([2025-09-11 15:26](https://youtu.be/ZjG3P7PTeIs?t=926)).
- He says the caves came out in October 2005 and were beaten on day one ([2024-05-09 15:05](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=905)). Spawns are much faster than the Inferno's ([2024-05-09 14:10](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=850)), and NPC click boxes are better than Inferno hitboxes ([2024-05-09 9:29](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=569)).

## Challenge runs

**Mage only (2024).** For the Wiki Guides challenge series he did a mage-only run with the OSRS Wiki setup and the worst listed downgrades ([2024-05-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=0)). Infinity robes were worn throughout, fire runes were carried loose (262,000 at the start) ([2024-05-09 0:04](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=4)). He considers the wiki setup poor, since it relies on Infinity-tier gear and Barrage spells he cannot afford runes for ([2024-05-09 0:04](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=4)), and prefers thralls over Ice or Blood Barrage here ([2024-05-09 0:57](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=57)). His gear had melee strength 6 and prayer 15 ([2024-05-09 1:27](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=87)). Rangers have magic level 60, and he says each tier roughly doubles the previous magic level; the melee monster has about 80 health and magic 120 ([2024-05-09 5:15](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=315)) ([2024-05-09 7:17](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=437)). The melee monster drains prayer and heals only while meleeing ([2024-05-09 7:48](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=468)) ([2024-05-09 8:15](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=495)). Base staves cannot autocast Flames of Zamorak (max hit 20, no god cape needed) ([2024-05-09 27:50](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=1670)) ([2024-05-09 28:08](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=1688)). Finished in 121:09, using about 17,000 fire runes ([2024-05-09 32:05](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=1925)) ([2024-05-09 34:08](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=2048)). He says the wiki's better setup (Shadow and full Ancestral, Sang, Ward, Eternal boots, Magus ring) would be much better ([2024-05-09 16:10](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=970)), and no boots beats the wiki's boots downgrade ([2024-05-09 4:17](https://youtu.be/puC-_fc7y0o?t=257)).

**Low-level melee on group hardcore ironman (2024).** On a [Group Hardcore Ironman](Group_Hardcore_Ironman.md) he planned Soul Wars money, Monkey Madness, combat levels, the defender grind, then a melee run ([2024-08-08 0:00](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=0)). Needs: supplies, Prayer training, weapon poison (the hard part), a dragon defender and food ([2024-08-08 0:00](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=0)). He started with 64 Hitpoints, noting only Jad can one-shot him, so he must not misplay prayer there ([2024-08-08 50:13](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=3013)) ([2024-08-08 51:45](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=3105)). Manta rays heal 22, needing 42 Hitpoints for full efficiency at max 64 ([2024-08-08 52:15](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=3135)). Mage monsters max 52 with magic and 54 in melee ([2024-08-08 53:48](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=3228)). Pulling monsters to safe spots such as the northeast corner lets him fight them one at a time ([2024-08-08 57:18](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=3438)). The 250 Temple Spiders killed for poison were worth it ([2024-08-08 1:02:16](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=3736)), though poison helps only minorly for the defender grind and caves ([2024-08-08 30:19](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=1819)); Attack potions add about 0.1 DPS on Jad, not worth it ([2024-08-08 1:05:50](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=3950)). His setup has roughly half the strength bonus of the wiki's lowest melee gear ([2024-08-08 1:22:16](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=4936)). He estimated forty minutes to Jad with 21 or 22 mage monsters left at wave 63, and reached Jad about 1:54 into the video, waves starting at about 51 minutes ([2024-08-08 1:14:22](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=4462)) ([2024-08-08 1:54:27](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=6867)). He believes it may be the first no-scythe melee Fight Caves ([2024-08-08 1:39:56](https://youtu.be/_cIv_pRhm9U?t=5996)).
