The Corrupted Gauntlet is a timed prep-then-fight activity in which the player gathers resources and crafts gear in a dungeon before fighting the [Corrupted Hunllef](Corrupted_Hunllef.md). Gnomonkey has covered it since 2020 as a no-gear-cost money method and the source of the [Bow of Faerdhinen](Bow_of_Faerdhinen.md); his advice changed substantially after the 2026 Summer Sweep-up, which made prep much faster.

## Requirements

- The Gauntlet requires the Song of the Elves quest, which needs many quests and level 70 in most stats ([^wD8VnN6vTrk-125]). In 2024 he adds that decent stats and the three prayers unlocked are needed ([^6twm5s6DZHg-29]).
- 2020: at least 85 in all combat stats, a very high Prayer level and Rigor unlocked, since one struggles significantly without Rigor ([^wD8VnN6vTrk-125]). Augury is not needed with his method but may help in an unlucky run ([^wD8VnN6vTrk-156]).
- 2022: 7.5 completions an hour would not be possible until Magic and Ranged were maxed and Augury and Rigor unlocked ([^6OkBpQdcXRw-120]).
- 2026: base 80 combat stats is enough for consistent kills and for starting tier one armour; AFKing combat stats first makes it easier ([^CA6-2CqM_NQ-57][^CA6-2CqM_NQ-1857]).
- Ironmen should try to get Rigor and Augury beforehand, since they are locked behind [Chambers of Xeric](Chambers_of_Xeric.md); Piety with the halberd is great and a Bow of Faerdhinen is not needed that early ([^6twm5s6DZHg-1550]).
- It has no gear requirement and no death cost ([^6twm5s6DZHg-0]). Any armour or inventory is allowed on entry, but pets cannot be brought in ([^wD8VnN6vTrk-183]).
- 2020 recommendation: hardcore ironmen should stay away because death is not safe ([^wD8VnN6vTrk-92]); see [Tier 3 prep for hardcore ironmen](#tier-3-prep-for-hardcore-ironmen) for his 2026 position.

## Overview of a run

- The player has seven and a half minutes (the maximum) to gather the items needed and then fight the Corrupted Hunllef in the middle of the dungeon ([^6twm5s6DZHg-56]). If prep time runs out the player is teleported in, or can walk in ([^CA6-2CqM_NQ-782]).
- In 2020 he described the Corrupted Hunllef as one of the toughest bosses in RuneScape, with 1000 health, a max hit of 58 and the ability to protect against the player's attacks ([^nOgO8mPz0SI-0]); he entered with tier three halberd and bow and a full set of tier one armour ([^nOgO8mPz0SI-0]).
- Gnomonkey compares it to RS2 Dungeoneering ([^wD8VnN6vTrk-217]). The player starts with a scepter that opens rooms, carried to the end (about a mithril mace in bonuses), and a hatchet, pickaxe, harpoon, pestle and mortar and one teleport seed ([^wD8VnN6vTrk-217][^wD8VnN6vTrk-244]). If the scepter is dropped, rooms cannot be opened ([^CA6-2CqM_NQ-298]). The centre has a singing bowl for crafting, a water pump for vials and a range for fish ([^6twm5s6DZHg-86][^CA6-2CqM_NQ-298]).
- 2020 goal: tier three bow and halberd, three potions and 25 paddlefish ([^wD8VnN6vTrk-244]). 2024 prep: two tier three weapons, two potions (two vials, 20 shards, two herbs), tier one armour and a full inventory of fish ([^6twm5s6DZHg-150]). 2026: three prayer potions (two at very high Prayer), a set of armour, two tier three weapons and as much fish as can be held while learning ([^CA6-2CqM_NQ-298]).
- Paddlefish heal 20; a Gauntlet potion restores a flat 30 prayer plus 40 run energy ([^wD8VnN6vTrk-270]). Tier three weapons have far higher DPS than main-game weapons since boosts cannot be used ([^wD8VnN6vTrk-270]).
- Two tier three weapons are needed because the Hunllef's protection prayer switches to the style being used after six hits ([^6twm5s6DZHg-212]). A full set of tier one armour lowers his max hit to 13; a partial set does not ([^6twm5s6DZHg-182]).
- Total shard needs (2024): 320 shards, or 340 if the staff upgrade is not obtained ([^6twm5s6DZHg-240]).

## Prep guidance

**Two-run method (2020).** Gnomonkey used two passes through the dungeon. Run one: a C-shape around the start for three ores, wood and wool (tier one armour), paddlefish, a weapon frame, spiders, bats and rats, avoiding high-level wolves, unicorns and scorpions, which always drop a frame ([^wD8VnN6vTrk-301][^wD8VnN6vTrk-327]). Craft a tier two bow, then the armour with body first, legs and helmet after ([^wD8VnN6vTrk-386]). Run two: demi-bosses (dark beast, bear, dragon) for the spike and bowstring, then the tier three weapons ([^wD8VnN6vTrk-415][^wD8VnN6vTrk-444]). He did not want the dragon's orb staff because Augury does not boost damage like other prayers ([^wD8VnN6vTrk-475]). Holding an orb when killing a second bear or dark beast guarantees the wanted part; without one it is 50/50 for a piece not already held ([^wD8VnN6vTrk-502]). He spam-clicked the oven to cook in one tick ([^wD8VnN6vTrk-386]).

**Tier one prep (2024).** As soon as a weapon frame is held, make a tier one staff and two vials (40 shards), since early staff tiers are the best ([^6twm5s6DZHg-354]). Tier one armour needs three each of wool, ore and bark ([^6twm5s6DZHg-478]). Tier three weapons need demi-boss materials from the three outer edge rooms ([^6twm5s6DZHg-396]). Harvest and attack at once, and make a teleport crystal at the bowl before a second trip ([^6twm5s6DZHg-1215]). Vials can be filled from fishing pots; he advises not opening side rooms from the edge ([^6twm5s6DZHg-543]). Combo food is worse than regular fish, except for hardcore ironmen ([^6twm5s6DZHg-2030]). Examples left three minutes, and about five minutes ([^6twm5s6DZHg-633][^6twm5s6DZHg-1262]). He never used tier two armour ([^6twm5s6DZHg-1073]).

**Weapons.** The halberd has the highest max hit and DPS by a little but is harder because of its one-time range and cannot be spammed while running ([^6twm5s6DZHg-1242]); all three tier three weapons are almost equal ([^6twm5s6DZHg-1245]). In 2026 he considers bow and staff the easiest, the halberd awkward for beginners ([^CA6-2CqM_NQ-506]), and always makes a tier two staff early ([^CA6-2CqM_NQ-450]). A second dark beast drops the orb rather than a second bow upgrade ([^CA6-2CqM_NQ-506]).

**Tools and plugins.** RuneLite: XP globes off, UI cleaned up, health bars removed, F keys rebound to number keys ([^wD8VnN6vTrk-183]). In 2026: the Gauntlet plugin (resource tracking, tornado true tile), Tile Indicators, CG Recolor (not with HD), and an attack timer metronome ([^R6F2mWXa_fM-33][^R6F2mWXa_fM-64]); resource counts grey out once satisfied ([^CA6-2CqM_NQ-472]).

## Tier 3 prep for hardcore ironmen

In February 2026 Gnomonkey described tier 3 prep as a harder prep for an easier, safer fight, mainly for Hardcore Ironmen and for players chasing the combat achievement ([^R6F2mWXa_fM-0][^R6F2mWXa_fM-33]). Armour makes the boss a standard fight ([^R6F2mWXa_fM-655]). He advised 65 to 70 HP or more to survive one tick of all four tornadoes (which can max 60) ([^R6F2mWXa_fM-659]), and called the halberd an unnecessary risk for hardcores ([^R6F2mWXa_fM-181]).

| Requirement | Figure |
|---|---|
| Minimum | 13 of each resource, two vials with two herbs, bowstring, orb, 740 crystal shards, eight fish ([^R6F2mWXa_fM-126]) |
| Phase one | C pattern, 220 shards for tier 2 staff, tier 1 armour and two vials ([^R6F2mWXa_fM-154][^R6F2mWXa_fM-181]) |
| Phase two | Demi hunting; last 10 of every resource, second weapon frame, 520 more shards ([^R6F2mWXa_fM-181]) |
| Phase three | Flexible clean-up; extra teleport crystal costs 40 shards ([^R6F2mWXa_fM-208][^R6F2mWXa_fM-239]) |

Tips: harvest with a kick (four-tick) rather than the scepter ([^R6F2mWXa_fM-272]); there are 12 rooms and always six demi-bosses on the three edge sides ([^R6F2mWXa_fM-703]); a triple fish spawn is always next to the boss ([^R6F2mWXa_fM-758]); breaking a demi drop gives 80 shards ([^R6F2mWXa_fM-718]); time is the biggest problem ([^R6F2mWXa_fM-748]). In July 2026 he said tier three armour is only about two minutes faster to prep, recommending tier two for first kills and tier three only for hardcore accounts ([^CA6-2CqM_NQ-1749]).

## Drops and profit

| Date | Figure |
|---|---|
| 2020-04-14 | Three loot rolls (regular Gauntlet two); Blade of Saeldor 1/400, pet 1/800 (regular 1/2000); crystal seed 1 in 50 (regular 1 in 120) ([^wD8VnN6vTrk-125]). About 5M gp/hr at 6.5 runs/hr, minimum about 1M without blade or seed ([^wD8VnN6vTrk-27][^wD8VnN6vTrk-789]). |
| 2022-08-03 | 7.5 completions/hr, about 6.5M gp/hr; best early moneymaker ([^6OkBpQdcXRw-89]). |
| 2024-11-02 | 4 to 8M gp/hr ([^6twm5s6DZHg-0]); average kill about 315k, enhanced weapon seed 1 in 400 at about 170M, almost 7M gp/hr at eight-minute kills ([^6twm5s6DZHg-1822]); 12-minute kills about 4.5M ([^6twm5s6DZHg-1936]). |
| 2026-01-23 | A run of about 180k is above the average of about 100k ([^5qsH_-3UNQ8-1056]). |

In 2020 Gnomonkey said it should be a better money method than [Vorkath](Vorkath.md) because it has no supply cost, but most profit comes from the blades ([^wD8VnN6vTrk-60]). By 2025 he said the Colosseum gives far more GP, and irons need not rush it ([^NxfbgWY3ZQk-1344]). Armour seeds are worth about ten million each ([^6twm5s6DZHg-1881]).

## Speed and combat achievement

The speedrunner combat achievement required under 6:30 ([^ZjG3P7PTeIs-802]); Gnomonkey considered it nearly consistent for top speedrunners ([^ZjG3P7PTeIs-831]). His fastest kill in 2020 was about 3.5 minutes and slowest near 6 ([^wD8VnN6vTrk-732]). After the Summer Sweep-up the time became 6:05 ([^JmglE_R2gGs-0]), though he thinks it is easier because prep is so much faster ([^JmglE_R2gGs-633]). Route: pre-venge, a weapon frame and 70 shards in the first room, tier two staff and two vials, collect three of each resource, kill two demi-bosses (220 shards total), then craft two tier three weapons and tier one armour with four to eight fish ([^JmglE_R2gGs-633][^JmglE_R2gGs-698][^JmglE_R2gGs-725][^JmglE_R2gGs-751]). Target prep 2:30 or faster; his example was 2:03 prep, 3:33 boss, about 5:36 ([^JmglE_R2gGs-781]).

## Bow of Faerdhinen grind

About 400 kills are needed on average for the Bofa ([^NxfbgWY3ZQk-460]), and ironmen are only done once they have all the armour seeds (around 300 kill count) ([^NzW7JFgc8qU-2554][^NzW7JFgc8qU-2616]). In May 2026 he opposed making it quick, favouring more avenues around it ([^NzW7JFgc8qU-2773][^NzW7JFgc8qU-2805]). After the update, he recommends it for an early iron and calls it effectively free money on a main ([^CA6-2CqM_NQ-1857]).

## Summer Sweep-up changes (2026)

The [Summer Sweep-up](Update_Summer_Sweep-up.md) update changed prep as follows.

| Aspect | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Demi-bosses | Three outer rooms | Guaranteed in the rooms opposite the boss and any outer edge room ([^CA6-2CqM_NQ-476][^6tsf-brc-sE-711]) |
| Demi attack range | 16 | 10 ([^6tsf-brc-sE-734]) |
| Weapon frame | Third kill | First low-tier kill ([^6tsf-brc-sE-761]) |
| Resources | Dropped | Stackable, shards magnetised ([^6tsf-brc-sE-761][^CA6-2CqM_NQ-0]) |
| Tier 1 weapon | 20 shards | 0 ([^6tsf-brc-sE-898]) |
| Tier 2 weapon | 60 | 50 ([^6tsf-brc-sE-898]) |
| Armour (T1/T2/T3) | 40/80/80 | 50/100/100 ([^6tsf-brc-sE-946]) |
| Full sets | 280/460/700 | 250/450/750 ([^6tsf-brc-sE-1031]) |

Gnomonkey considers stackable resources probably the biggest change ([^6tsf-brc-sE-788]) and tier two now only about a 30 second loss, where before he said never make it at a minute and a half ([^CA6-2CqM_NQ-1281]). He tested a run 30 seconds to a minute faster, and several minutes faster for tier two or three ([^EPlArwCehYY-61]), and worries about bots and seed price ([^6tsf-brc-sE-819]).
