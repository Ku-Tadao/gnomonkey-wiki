## Tips

- Gnomonkey says Ruby bolt (e) procs carried this crossbow Inferno, making TzKal-Zuk nearly as fast as his average Twisted bow runs. ([2023-09-17 0:00](https://youtu.be/PaJs_xLnNwc))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says that in this run he is pretty sure the bolt procs at Zuk were diamond-level or better, and notes a missed damage chance where a ruby proc would have been big. ([2023-09-17 40:40](https://youtu.be/PaJs_xLnNwc?t=2440))
- Gnomonkey says the lack of Ruby bolts was the main thing that made the downgrade run harder, since the wiki states Diamond bolts are a downgrade of Ruby bolts. ([2024-06-01 0:00](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc))
- Gnomonkey says Ruby bolts are not good at all in general, but after shooting Diamond bolts they feel like a real gun compared with a BB gun. ([2024-06-01 40:25](https://youtu.be/17ynF_0ltPc?t=2425))

## Events
