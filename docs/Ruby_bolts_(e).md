**Ruby bolts (e)** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2023-09-17 and 2024-06-01. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Tips

* Ruby bolt (e) procs carried this crossbow Inferno, making TzKal-Zuk nearly as fast as his average Twisted bow runs.[^PaJs_xLnNwc-0]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says that in this run he is pretty sure the bolt procs at Zuk were diamond-level or better, and notes a missed damage chance where a ruby proc would have been big.[^PaJs_xLnNwc-2440]
* Gnomonkey says the lack of Ruby bolts was the main thing that made the downgrade run harder, since the wiki states Diamond bolts are a downgrade of Ruby bolts.[^17ynF_0ltPc-0]
* Gnomonkey says Ruby bolts are not good at all in general, but after shooting Diamond bolts they feel like a real gun compared with a BB gun.[^17ynF_0ltPc-2425]

## Events

* In the budget run Gnomonkey got a Ruby bolt proc on the first shot at the Wardens' P4 and called it a sign of power.[^17ynF_0ltPc-2392]
