Yama is an endgame duo boss in Old School RuneScape, fought in the Chasm of Fire. Gnomonkey covers it from its RuneFest reveal through its May 2025 release and later updates, and considers base Yama a solid, enjoyable boss while strongly criticising its contract system.

[TOC]

## Announcement and release

At RuneFest Yama was revealed as an endgame duo boss due in spring 2025, before the delve boss[^GcMlWY-2-v4-0]. Jagex described it as a demon who has taken control from other demons, with disciples and political influence, fought in the Chasm of Fire with room for one friend[^GcMlWY-2-v4-32]. Jagex said the baseline would be a step beyond the [Desert Treasure II](Desert_Treasure_II.md) bosses, using blood magic, bloodthirsty minions, cultists and fireballs[^GcMlWY-2-v4-59]. A contract mechanic lets players trade a harder fight for different rewards, which Jagex expected to be as tough as the Awakened bosses[^GcMlWY-2-v4-90].

Gnomonkey compared the announced fight to duo [Nex](Nex.md) or Fasani ([Phosani's Nightmare](Phosani's_Nightmare.md)) and a step above some of the hardest single bosses[^GcMlWY-2-v4-59]. He liked the reward design, calling the gear cool and powerful without being broken, and wanted more group content after the [Royal Titans](Royal_Titans.md)[^GcMlWY-2-v4-1050]. He contrasted it favourably with the [Doom of Mokhaiotl](Doom_of_Mokhaiotl.md) rewards[^GcMlWY-2-v4-1050]. Gnomonkey dates the release to 14 May 2025[^ZB3ldcsIivg-688].

## Fight mechanics

**Phases.** On release he gave the phase thresholds as 33% and 66% health[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-337], and described Yama as phasing every 33.3% of damage dealt[^prE1BqM1wBE-157]. At the first two thresholds the fight moves to a side fight against the [Judge of Yama](Judge_of_Yama.md)[^5_IkechUxrw-302][^5_IkechUxrw-512]. In the Shard Acquisition contract the transitions are at 75% and 50% instead[^iYGGzzPschI-179].

**Attacks.** Yama has a melee swing, a magic attack shown by orange flames on the player, and a ranged attack shown as a shadow around the player, similar to the Nightmare[^prE1BqM1wBE-188]. In the solo guide he described the black smoke and shadow attacks as ranged with the other as magic, requiring prayer flicking[^5_IkechUxrw-214]. Gnomonkey recommends camping Protect from Melee by default, flicking to mage on orange flames and to ranged on a shadow[^prE1BqM1wBE-188]. Yama poisons the player, so he uses Sand fuse[^5_IkechUxrw-214]. In a duo Yama picks one player as the tank at the start; that player can pray melee in melee range, so the other does not need that prayer[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-240].

**Specials and glyphs.** Every few attacks Yama uses a massive special, described as hitting around 80 in the 14 May videos[^5_IkechUxrw-273][^ux6YsZIXGUk-185] and as roughly 40 to 80 in the 15 May video[^prE1BqM1wBE-218]. It is blocked (0 damage) by stepping on the glyph matching the attack colour, alternating roughly every three attacks[^5_IkechUxrw-273][^ux6YsZIXGUk-154]. Two of the same glyph can occur in a row[^ux6YsZIXGUk-273]. See also [Yama glyphs](Yama_glyphs.md). The further Yama is from a glyph, the less damage the glyph does to the player; recoil can be up to 12 if he is next to it, so he advises keeping Yama away from glyphs, which he says is especially useful for contracts[^KIII4sXUDTk-1064][^KIII4sXUDTk-1095]. If Yama is blocked by glyphs too long, glyphs can run out because they respawn slowly, so one glyph-blocked orb should be killed rather than blocking both[^KIII4sXUDTk-1095].

**Judge phase.** The Judge's hits are guaranteed and the health pool is shared; both players alternate mage and melee on every attack, so accuracy and armour do not matter and boosting strength raises the max hit[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-270][^5_IkechUxrw-85]. Waiting under the Judge makes his skull attack hit himself, like an [Araxxor](Araxxor.md) step-under[^ux6YsZIXGUk-303]. [Death Charge](Death_Charge.md) works in this phase and is strong[^5_IkechUxrw-456]. He says what is brought for Judge DPS barely matters, costing only a few seconds[^KIII4sXUDTk-1708].

**Orbs.** Orbs heal Yama and damage the player (up to 80 if ignored) unless killed[^5_IkechUxrw-242]; in the 15 May video he put the hit at around 40[^prE1BqM1wBE-157]. The [Purging staff](Purging_staff.md) two-shots them in budget gear, and finishing them with a special refunds the special cost and speeds the next attack[^ux6YsZIXGUk-242]. Gnomonkey warns against greeding when orbs are up[^ux6YsZIXGUk-427][^5_IkechUxrw-482]. If Yama does not spawn void flares in the first phase, he will not in the following phase either[^KIII4sXUDTk-1159]. In the last phase the flares can be two-shot, and the one the player has fewer glyphs for should be killed first[^KIII4sXUDTk-149][^KIII4sXUDTk-240].

**Final phase and enrage.** Gnomonkey consistently calls the last phase the hardest part of the fight[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-337][^ux6YsZIXGUk-458][^prE1BqM1wBE-420]. Yama's defence rises sharply and orbs appear almost constantly[^5_IkechUxrw-512]; the enrage is where the fight becomes an actual challenge because damage becomes very poor[^KIII4sXUDTk-424]. Attacks alternate between mage and range every time, so prayer is swapped each attack[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-367][^prE1BqM1wBE-455]. Meteors come in rows of three and are dodged by stepping perpendicular to them, in the middle[^prE1BqM1wBE-429][^ux6YsZIXGUk-489]. Yama also sends huge diamond-shaped waves; Gnomonkey marks the tile left of Yama and runs straight through the middle of the diamond[^prE1BqM1wBE-481]. Waves hit for about 20 and drop prayer, making it easy to be comboed[^ux6YsZIXGUk-398], and a player can be comboed for around 50[^5_IkechUxrw-665]. Health should be kept high, since a hit coinciding with a prayer drop can kill; sitting at about 40 is not advised[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-1062]. He advises staying north of Yama[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-884]. In the enraged phase any melee hit taken spawns an orb[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-367][^prE1BqM1wBE-157].

## Combat style: mage versus melee

Gnomonkey's advice changed within days of release.

- **14 May 2025:** in a duo, melee is the main style, about 11% better than mage[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-177]. For solo he chose mage as the primary style, because draining Yama is hard and mage was his best DPS without heavy training; he expected [Scythe](Scythe_of_Vitur.md) or [Emberlight](Emberlight.md) with Bandos godsword draining to be better in a duo[^5_IkechUxrw-0]. He advised maging the whole enraged phase because meleeing spawns many orbs[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-744].
- **15 May 2025:** he said the previous day's solo guides were "complete trolling", because casting a spell on Yama (when he stops walking towards the player) raises his magic defence by 90, from negative 30 to 60[^prE1BqM1wBE-0]. Maging is fine for chill kills but dramatically slower, and melee is better in all phases[^prE1BqM1wBE-0]. He apologised for the old guide's bad information and said Emberlight or Arclight is the best option throughout[^prE1BqM1wBE-1608]. Meleeing the last phase with Emberlight is roughly 20 to 30% better DPS, and about 20% more on average[^prE1BqM1wBE-1647]. Gnomonkey credited Dono with finding the tech on day one[^prE1BqM1wBE-1720] ([Dono fly](Dono_fly.md)).
- **18 May 2025:** he restated that maging has much worse DPS than melee[^AyPfaq5Kvnk-173], and gave a wave-dodging method for maging: stand one tile west of the arena centre and click on the wave to run through both[^AyPfaq5Kvnk-146]. The Scythe is about 10% worse than Emberlight and worse than Arclight, used only for an extra hit[^AyPfaq5Kvnk-222].
- **1 September 2025:** he called melee the hardest way to kill Yama[^KIII4sXUDTk-29] and ranked the [Bandos godsword](Bandos_godsword.md) special as the second-best spec weapon, right below [Elder maul](Elder_maul.md) with the [Soulflame horn](Soulflame_horn.md), and better than Emberlight[^KIII4sXUDTk-89].

## Gear and inventory

- Duo Yama is good for kills per hour because the Soulflame horn is very strong and can only be used in a duo[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-31]. Yama has 333 crush defence, so an Elder maul does not normally land[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-120]. With two synapses (Emberlight and Purging staff) DPS is almost the same as everyone else's, since nearly all DPS is in those two weapons[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-1280].
- The original solo mage setup: max mage gear with the Purging staff and Shadow ([Tumeken's shadow](Tumeken's_shadow.md)), a small melee switch for the Judge, and [Burning claws](Burning_claws.md) as the spec[^5_IkechUxrw-30][^5_IkechUxrw-58]. He suggested few melee switches while learning[^5_IkechUxrw-85], avoided brews because re-potting mage is hard[^5_IkechUxrw-85], and brought runes for Death Charge, [Thralls](Thralls.md), Ward of Arceus and [Mark of Darkness](Mark_of_Darkness.md)[^5_IkechUxrw-117]. A viewer pointed out that Pharaoh's sceptre is better than the cape, which he agreed with[^5_IkechUxrw-117]. See also [Yama: budget setup](Yama_budget_setup.md).
- Specing the Burning claws in the last phase summons jellyfish, so it may not be worth it[^ux6YsZIXGUk-649]. An Emberlight spec at the orbs with Mark of Darkness is a guaranteed one-shot[^prE1BqM1wBE-1596].
- Mark of Darkness lasts about 15 minutes in his estimation, so it needs no reapplying in a normal kill[^KIII4sXUDTk-1625].
- With under about 100 health left, Yama can be full tanked and killed before the orbs pop, compared to a P2 skip in [Verzik](Verzik_Vitur.md)[^AyPfaq5Kvnk-173].
- Yama's moves can be vengeanced almost entirely, including the planet attack[^yP8IB3_IsSc-2497][^ZjG3P7PTeIs-982]. Stepping away from the boss on the second shadow special in phase two does double damage, helping tanking tech[^yP8IB3_IsSc-2497].

## Getting there and starting the fight

Yama is reached with Fairy Ring DJR, ideally via a house ring, then down into the Chasm of Fire[^5_IkechUxrw-150]. The chain shortcut needs 83 Agility; a slower cliff jump and cage route needs about 73[^5_IkechUxrw-150][^ux6YsZIXGUk-94]. The fight starts with right-click Challenge, as with Verzik P1[^5_IkechUxrw-208]. The 29 May update changed the Chasm of Fire teleport scroll to land in front of the Voice of Yama, skipping the agility shortcut[^9ugDmmDILoI-130].

## Duo versus solo

Gnomonkey compared solo difficulty to Fasani: not easy, but doable for decent players[^5_IkechUxrw-0]. His first near-max duo kill took about three minutes[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-400]; his example solo kill took seven minutes[^5_IkechUxrw-665]. In October 2025 he said Yama is much easier in duos and much harder solo, but solo gives double loot, so remains worth doing[^YjYgGFi5878-520]. In March 2026 he said many players were disappointed it is not a true duo boss: it is more a solo boss done together, with no teamwork beyond one player tanking[^1cRMBXNb4rc-118].

## Contracts

Contracts are covered on [Yama contracts](Yama_contracts.md). Summary of what Gnomonkey said:

- On 25 May 2025 he said the contracts felt like [Tombs of Amascut](Tombs_of_Amascut.md) invocations: the same fight with one extra gimmick. He would have preferred one very hard contract with many new mechanics, such as Yama chasing the player in phase three, over five similar fights, and said contracts lack the depth of base Yama[^yP8IB3_IsSc-2937][^yP8IB3_IsSc-2967][^yP8IB3_IsSc-2996].
- The 29 May update raised the contract limit from five to fifty, and allowed 100 noted pineapple pizzas to be brought to practise the empowered form of the hardest contracts (Worm, Catalyst and Shard Acquisition) while still receiving loot[^9ugDmmDILoI-185][^9ugDmmDILoI-518]. He estimated the pizzas at about 275k per attempt and called the requirement the one really stupid thing in the update, suggesting about 10 pizzas and a permanent option; Jagex stated on Reddit the cost is not intended to be per attempt[^9ugDmmDILoI-551][^9ugDmmDILoI-581].
- Shard Acquisition: a mage-camp fight at giga max mage with a scepter and SGS for restoration[^iYGGzzPschI-62]. Prayer restoration and healing are halved[^iYGGzzPschI-121]. Off-prayer hits reach 70 and waves 50[^iYGGzzPschI-154], and a good kill is sub-3 minutes[^iYGGzzPschI-212]. His phase-three routine includes entering with 109 overheal and never sitting below 99 HP[^iYGGzzPschI-431]. In a duo only about four sets of void flares occur, roughly two and a half times shorter than his solo footage[^iYGGzzPschI-755]; the run pattern is credited to Kirby[^iYGGzzPschI-755].
- After release, two of the five contracts were impossible, so the world-first contract race was effectively cancelled[^1cRMBXNb4rc-90]. In October 2025 he called the contracts one of the worst updates in the game's history and said they were clearly untested[^YjYgGFi5878-520][^YjYgGFi5878-1299], and in 2026 that Yama and Doom came out in an unacceptable state[^1cRMBXNb4rc-1005]. See [Opinion: Yama contract system and tradable contracts](Opinion_Yama_contract_system_and_tradable_contracts.md).
- Oathplate kit contracts: he completed the sigil bottom contract in 9:39 with two instant mauls, keeping run energy high with a stamina potion and a food giving run energy[^X3eF6YHLh9I-569][^X3eF6YHLh9I-627]. He compared one contract to Kephri Medic that kills if a swarm goes in[^X3eF6YHLh9I-1749]. See [Oathplate armour](Oathplate_armour.md).

## Combat achievements

Per the 29 May 2025 video, the achievements require consistent performance rather than RNG[^9ugDmmDILoI-0].

- Perfectionist-style: no wrong-style hit on the Judge and no more than one instance of damage taken, with 50 kills required; he would rather Jagex stop requiring kill counts[^9ugDmmDILoI-33].
- Speed-master-style: no healing except plain pizza and no potions[^9ugDmmDILoI-33].
- A no-shadow-hit and no-hit-beyond-one-tile-of-crash achievement, and a no-fire-hit achievement with void flares only killed by specials[^9ugDmmDILoI-67].
- Speed: average time under 2:34 over the last four kills; he would have made it 2:30[^9ugDmmDILoI-67]. In September he quoted it as "under 234" and said it is good because of the tech available, such as vengeing everything and healing with butterflies in duo[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-952][^ZjG3P7PTeIs-1015].
- He called them reasonable and fair, with only minor issues[^9ugDmmDILoI-38][^9ugDmmDILoI-97]. See [Combat Achievements](Combat_Achievements.md).

## Patch notes (29 May 2025)

Gnomonkey covered the update and hotfixes: the Yama colouring fix; a message naming the eligible style when no contract is received; a fix so dragon claws and Burning claws guarantee their second splat from the Soulflame horn; the [Surge potion](Surge_potion.md) six tick delay in PvP areas; crushing infernal shale guaranteeing at least as many crushed shale as crushed; the Pernix staff spec counting for the Contract of Glyphic Attenuation; an empty option on the barrel; fixes to the screen fade, Oathplate icon and collection log shards; and a hotfix making teleport scrolls rarer but dropped in higher quantities (18 per dossier)[^9ugDmmDILoI-130][^9ugDmmDILoI-157][^9ugDmmDILoI-185][^9ugDmmDILoI-214]. A further hotfix increased radiant contract drop rates and reduced the horn and Oathplate rates[^9ugDmmDILoI-244]. See [Update: Yama contracts](Update_Yama_contracts.md).

## Economy and balance

In August 2025 he said Yama made [Demon bane](Demon_bane_weapons.md) too strong: Emberlight and the Purging staff rival or outcompete their mega-rare counterparts, bots mage camp Yama with an 8m gp staff of the dead, and he spotted bots with over 100 million magic XP from Yama alone, calling the 120% Demon bane strength progression-breaking[^Glp4chf4yiA-269][^Glp4chf4yiA-285]. He rated Yama the easiest endgame content, a step above [Vardorvis](Vardorvis.md)[^Glp4chf4yiA-180]. In October he said Yama's release devalued the [Torva](Torva_armour.md) platebody and the Basilisk jaw, that Yama is 1.2 times weak to Demon bane, and that Emberlight being complete best-in-slot is part of the problem[^ZB3ldcsIivg-688][^ZB3ldcsIivg-717][^ZB3ldcsIivg-904][^ZB3ldcsIivg-991]. He criticised the pet being 1 in 100 per contract completion, returned each kill[^Glp4chf4yiA-721].

## Gnomonkey's opinion

He called Yama hard with a killer enrage but more doable than expected and genuinely fun[^2Kjo3Y5Q_Pk-1272], praised its low skill floor with high ceiling and daily-discovered tech[^yP8IB3_IsSc-2464], compared its depth to [Chambers of Xeric](Chambers_of_Xeric.md)[^yP8IB3_IsSc-2996], and called base Yama good and solid[^YjYgGFi5878-487]. He rated the Yama update neutral overall[^YjYgGFi5878-551].
