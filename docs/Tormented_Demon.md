**Tormented Demon** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 5 videos between 2024-07-15 and 2026-04-09. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Methods

* Tormented Demons have a shield giving a permanent 20% damage reduction against all non-abyssal and non-demon bane weapons, so only things like the new demon weapons, Arclight, Dragon... bludgeon and the abyssal dagger do their full max hit.[^eIAkPiQivJk-85]
* Tormented Demons have two mechanics that make specific items good: a punish mechanic for super slow weapons, and guaranteed accuracy rolls.[^eIAkPiQivJk-114]
* At the start of almost every kill the Tormented Demon does a roar, which means your next attack, whatever it is, bypasses their damage reduction; a crush weapon or a ballista or crossbow on slash/accurate gets a huge bonus damage with its attack speed slowed, and the slower the weapon the more damage.[^eIAkPiQivJk-114]
* After the punish a timer runs, and after 25 game ticks, roughly 4 Tormented Demon attacks, all of your attacks become unable to miss, continuing until the demon has done 10 total attacks.[^eIAkPiQivJk-147]
* At the end of that cycle the Tormented Demon snares you in place and drops fireballs, which you can dodge by control-clicking away or walking in a specific pattern, and it then does another roar allowing another punish and repeating the cycle.[^eIAkPiQivJk-147]
* Tormented Demons change combat style like demonic gorillas, every 150 damage dealt, swapping to the last style used against them.[^eIAkPiQivJk-176]

## Stats and numbers

* To calculate a weapon's true DPS against Tormented Demons you take 13 of its hits against the demon's normal defence and 17 hits against zero defence and average those 30 hits, which is roughly how many you get per roar.[^eIAkPiQivJk-206]
* The abyssal dagger special is a 4-tick, 25% spec weapon that maxes 98 in max melee gear with Blood fury, and its Blood fury procs should outweigh any sustain the Saradomin godsword provides.[^eIAkPiQivJk-327]
* Against Tormented demons, Gnomonkey says the Atlatl just beats a blowpipe with adamant darts on paper even before burn damage, and with the burn added the Atlatl is about 18% better.[^NxfbgWY3ZQk-1062]
* According to Gnomonkey, the Atlatl has about 41% accuracy versus 35% for the blowpipe against Tormented demons, because it punches through defence better.[^NxfbgWY3ZQk-1087]
* The blowpipe needs dragon darts to just barely beat the Atlatl on paper at Tormented demons (about 1.8% better), and that rune darts would be about 7% worse than the Atlatl.[^NxfbgWY3ZQk-1087]
* A Tormented demon uses the same attack style ten times in a row, then does a fireball special attack that turns your run off, which you can avoid being hit by while holding the control key to click out; it then swaps to another random style.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-405]
* They give a huge XP multiplier, with about 1k Slayer XP per kill drop.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-520]
* The shield drops roughly after four attacks, then you cannot miss, including mage hits in non-mage gear, until you punish again and the fires come back.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-600]
* Tormented demons change their protection prayer every 25% of damage you deal.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-682]
* According to Gnomonkey, kill rates are at most about 80 kills per hour for a fully maxed setup, 50 to 60 for less experienced players with worse gear, and he gets about 60 per hour on his account, giving around 60k Slayer XP per hour.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-788]
* Tormented demons give around 10 million gp per hour at 80 kills per hour (about 7.5 million at 60 kills per hour) as profit after supplies, noting that the on-screen estimate used an outdated synapse price (synapses about 56 million and rising).[^TgWNIkkUpdg-822]
* An extended Slayer task of Tormented demons gives 200 to 250 greater demons and takes about a minute each, making roughly a four-hour task.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-1003]
* Tormented Demons give upwards of 60k Slayer XP an hour, lots of combat XP and incredibly good GP, with demon bane weapons as very useful rare drops.[^8CSztSkDvnM-833]

## Tips

* Tormented Demons have a huge aggro radius, so people on other sides of the room or in tunnels can aggro your demons, making them de-aggro completely or mess up their prayer and special rotations.[^eIAkPiQivJk-28]
* Tormented Demons sometimes bug out and stop completely so you cannot attack them, and they can aggro people on entirely different planes; because worlds are packed you can get extra aggro or lose aggro when others are around.[^eIAkPiQivJk-58]
* A 100% accurate Toxic blowpipe is some of the best DPS in the game but is bad when it actually rolls against the Tormented Demon's large defence, which is why it feels strangely good during the guaranteed-accuracy window.[^eIAkPiQivJk-176]
* Arclight is a great budget option that costs nothing and is basically the second-best weapon for Tormented Demons, as long as it is on stab, with a bludgeon about 5% worse if you do not want to use Arclight's charges.[^eIAkPiQivJk-206]
* For the punish you absolutely need a ballista or a crossbow on accurate, because a slower weapon deals more damage; he is not sure whether the Zaryte crossbow or heavy ballista is better but calls both similar, with the ballista a great cheap option.[^eIAkPiQivJk-233]
* You also want a melee punish option for when the demons pray ranged, and that Dharok's greataxe on crush is the clear winner, with Dragon 2H sword or Abyssal maul a close second.[^eIAkPiQivJk-233]
* With all the cash you need the best options are the Twisted bow and Emberlight, and that when not 100% accurate the Twisted bow is directly better than the blowpipe, and even at 100% accuracy it is the same DPS.[^eIAkPiQivJk-263]
* He brings a Toxic blowpipe as a switch to inflict venom when the demon cannot be missed, but calls that pretty marginal.[^eIAkPiQivJk-294]
* The best spec option against Tormented Demons is, against all odds, the abyssal dagger: it bypasses their shield so it maxes higher than a Dragon dagger special, and costs only 25% spec.[^eIAkPiQivJk-294]
* Tormented demons require two combat styles, similar to demonic gorillas but, in his words, not boring.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-0]
* Eclipse armour doubles as both ranged and melee armour for Tormented demons, letting you bring fewer switches.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-0]
* Tormented demons attack with a random style (mage, range or melee), and if they walk towards you they are meleeing; if you safe spot a meleeing one they switch to other styles.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-353]
* By properly judging the style you will usually take about one hit per demon kill.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-434]
* To wait to use your special attack until the fires go out, as it lets a Dragon dagger special, for example, hit repeatedly for around fifty.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-640]
* He trained most of his combat stats by Turael-cancelling to get Tormented demon tasks.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-1003]
* When three Tormented Demons are tagged together, one fires skulls while the other two off-tick with alternating attacks and still do their triple punish attack, which Gnomonkey says makes it its own mini boss fight.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-771]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey enjoys the Tormented Demons and says they offer a lot more depth than their RS3 counterpart, and that he loves Old School taking something from RS3 and making it enjoyable.[^eIAkPiQivJk-0]
* Gnomonkey says the Toxic blowpipe is awful before it ramps up but then deletes Tormented Demons, and is directly better than the Bow of Faerdhinen or any crossbow as the main weapon.[^eIAkPiQivJk-233]
* Gnomonkey says Tormented Demons' base loot table is non-existent, a common community complaint, and he would like Jagex to keep very bad drops with sparse good drops to prevent inventory clogging, with a buff to the occasional good drops being enough.[^eIAkPiQivJk-563]
* Gnomonkey says a loot table like the Desert Treasure 2 bosses would work really well for Tormented Demons.[^eIAkPiQivJk-563]
* Gnomonkey considers Tormented demons the best Slayer monsters in the game for GP right now, and recommends any new account to get them as soon as possible.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-0]
* Gnomonkey says that for mains with under a 200 million bank it is really hard to beat Tormented demons for GP per hour, and early grinding them on a main is great.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-825]
* Gnomonkey says killing multiple Tormented demons at once is technically faster because the punish is slightly faster, but in practice it barely raises kills per hour, so it is not worth it.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-1043]
* Gnomonkey says you should always do Tormented demons on task rather than off task, because you get the Slayer helm bonus and the 1k Slayer XP drop per kill, and you can Turael-cancel to get a better task.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-1050]
* Gnomonkey says the Tormented Demon CAs are quite good, including the 'Three times the thrashing' CA of killing three Tormented Demons within three seconds.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-771]
* Gnomonkey says the three-Tormented-Demons CA is difficult with a lot of APM, but encourages swapping prayers and movement and makes you a better player.[^ZjG3P7PTeIs-802]
* Gnomonkey says Tormented Demons are his favourite Slayer monster and more complicated than any Slayer boss, so he does not believe the pirates will be as interesting to fight.[^8CSztSkDvnM-149]
* Gnomonkey does not buy Jagex's comparison of the pirates to Tormented Demons, since the pirates give worse XP and the current Sailing systems cannot support mechanics as engaging.[^8CSztSkDvnM-833]
* Gnomonkey says Sailing combat is not engaging and has little depth, so there is not much Jagex can do beyond trading blows with the pirate ship and then hitting the boarding pirates with weapons.[^8CSztSkDvnM-860]

## Events

* The Scorching bow would be the superior option but is currently bugged with a reduced max hit, and if it was patched the next Wednesday it would be better than the Twisted bow.[^eIAkPiQivJk-263]
* The Purging staff is also bugged and should be 5% better DPS than the Scorching bow, but since it cannot punish with a weapon like other styles he thinks it is probably worse than the Scorching bow.[^eIAkPiQivJk-263]
* Since his earlier guide, Jagex added an animation so that the flames on a Tormented demon's back go out when its shield drops, telling you that you cannot miss.[^TgWNIkkUpdg-593]
* According to Gnomonkey, when Jagex collected Sailing feedback, the main point players gave was that the combat is weak and needs to be changed.[^8CSztSkDvnM-890]

## Trivia

* The new Tormented Demons are Slayer monsters unlocked by completing the new quest, and are the exact mobs Jagex took the idea for demonic gorillas from, so they were completely changed mechanically from their RS3 counterpart.[^eIAkPiQivJk-0]
