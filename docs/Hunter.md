## Methods

- For Hunter, Gnomonkey would do a mix of bird houses and drift net fishing up to level 80. ([2022-08-03 5:20](https://youtu.be/6OkBpQdcXRw?t=320))
- At Hunter level 80 Gnomonkey would immediately move to herbiboar, which would generate maximum profit since he would have bought Herblore anyway; if he got spooked herbiboar he would switch to black chinchompas to try to get that pet before 99. ([2022-08-03 5:20](https://youtu.be/6OkBpQdcXRw?t=320))
- At one point Gnomonkey pivoted to Hunter in Deadman to get moths because the team was out of prayer, then mixed hide for ranged gear, sunlight antelope crossbows (sunlight crossbows) and chinchompas for ranged levels. ([2026-06-25 14:25](https://youtu.be/lJexGv6dxu8?t=865))

## Requirements

- Many important high-level quests require Hunter, namely Monkey Madness II at 60 Hunter and Song of the Elves at 70 Hunter. ([2021-04-02 0:00](https://youtu.be/dzkCFEvMgt8))
- Armadillo brew requires 77 Hunter to catch the crab needed for it. ([2025-11-04 7:25](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=445))

## Stats and numbers

- Hunter is trained by trapping creatures, and the number of traps you can lay increases every twenty levels, up to five traps at level 80 Hunter. ([2021-04-02 0:00](https://youtu.be/dzkCFEvMgt8))
- Gnomonkey gets 120k Hunter XP per hour from Drift net fishing, which he says competes fairly well with dedicated intensive Hunter methods on its own. ([2022-03-23 0:00](https://youtu.be/Hs2ur8jhnto))
- The new crab hunting method sets a trap and after an exact amount of time the crab enters the trap, rather than a random chance; higher-level crabs seem slower, and the crab meat is used for potions. ([2025-07-31 7:54](https://youtu.be/qiA-iaA19-M?t=474))
- Crab traps are faster if you set them manually than automatically, and Gnomonkey thinks the main idea would be the slow but consistent automatic option. ([2025-07-31 8:53](https://youtu.be/qiA-iaA19-M?t=533))
- Anti-odour salt is a stackable salt that is automatically consumed to smoke a Hunter trap, increasing catch rates for things like chinchompas; Gnomonkey says smoking traps was an old, buggy system nobody used. ([2025-07-31 13:56](https://youtu.be/qiA-iaA19-M?t=836))
- The Hunter level in the Sailing prep is used to catch crabs, which are ground into crab meat used in potions. ([2025-11-04 7:25](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=445))
- A new Hunter activity needs 76 Hunter to collect bait and 82 Hunter to lure and catch the creatures (heard as stim pikes); it gives pure XP and no rewards. ([2026-01-25 8:03](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=483))

## Tips

- Hunter has little in the way of equipment upgrades other than the traps you buy; Gnomonkey recommends buying a decent pile of ropes, small nets and box traps. ([2021-04-02 0:27](https://youtu.be/dzkCFEvMgt8?t=27))
- The most useful outfit for Hunter is the Graceful outfit because you do a lot of running around. ([2021-04-02 0:27](https://youtu.be/dzkCFEvMgt8?t=27))
- A handful of quests give Hunter XP but, unfortunately, all of them require Hunter levels. ([2021-04-02 0:56](https://youtu.be/dzkCFEvMgt8?t=56))
- If you want more Hunter XP alongside Drift net fishing, buy supplies for birdhouses so you can do a birdhouse run while nearby when they are ready. ([2022-03-23 0:57](https://youtu.be/Hs2ur8jhnto?t=57))
- Gnomonkey wears a digsite pendant when doing birdhouse runs on Fossil Island alongside Drift net fishing. ([2022-03-23 1:30](https://youtu.be/Hs2ur8jhnto?t=90))
- Gnomonkey notes that falcon travel time is based on distance, so being able to move closer to the next kebbit while the falcon chases speeds falconry up. ([2026-06-04 1:03](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=63))
- Gnomonkey says the 99 Hunter cape teleport to the Hunter Guild was a very safe bank teleport since he never saw anyone there, and the daily teleports to black chinchompas were good for reaching revenants. ([2026-06-25 14:57](https://youtu.be/lJexGv6dxu8?t=897))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey describes Hunter as a very profitable skill that is typically relatively intensive through most methods. ([2021-04-02 0:00](https://youtu.be/dzkCFEvMgt8))
- Gnomonkey says Hunter starts with slow, terrible methods but speeds up dramatically once you can lay five traps. ([2021-04-02 0:00](https://youtu.be/dzkCFEvMgt8))
- Gnomonkey says there are many alternatives to black chinchompas, which are definitely intensive considering their location, and that his preferred method to 99 Hunter is herbivore, unlocked at level 80. ([2021-04-02 8:30](https://youtu.be/dzkCFEvMgt8?t=510))
- Because monkey trapping has no pet drop, Gnomonkey typically prefers herbivore to it, as herbivore gives decent money and better XP at relatively low intensity. ([2021-04-02 11:50](https://youtu.be/dzkCFEvMgt8?t=710))
- Gnomonkey says Hunter lacks a relaxed training option, because it is usually trained actively or with birdhouse runs, so he is okay with the AFK enhanced traps being added. ([2025-01-27 4:13](https://youtu.be/3kmWbjwmZqI?t=253))
- Gnomonkey says box traps for chinchompas could be crazy with the self-resetting enhanced traps because chinchompas are already really good Hunter XP. ([2025-01-27 4:13](https://youtu.be/3kmWbjwmZqI?t=253))
- Gnomonkey says the crab hunting method is a set cycle (for example, every 25 ticks you reset the trap, then look away for 10 to 15 seconds) and is closest to herbiboar, a method he really likes; he hopes it does not have terrible XP. ([2025-07-31 8:25](https://youtu.be/qiA-iaA19-M?t=505))
- Gnomonkey thinks the super hunter potion (boosting Hunter by six) could allow boosting to high-level hunter contracts such as herbiboar or black chinchompas early, as there is currently no good consistent high-level boost. ([2025-07-31 14:59](https://youtu.be/qiA-iaA19-M?t=899))
- Gnomonkey thinks the new Hunter method will be more relaxed than tick-manipulated chinchompas, with active bait collecting and chill catching, and calls it not a bad idea. ([2026-01-25 8:34](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=514))
- Gnomonkey says Hunter is a very janky skill to train, especially the early methods, which people try to skip by doing birdhouses, so the Summer Sweep-Up Hunter improvements are very much needed. ([2026-06-04 0:30](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=30))
- Gnomonkey is curious whether aerial fishing is now a decent method for the Angler's outfit compared to Fishing Trawler, saying it may be a similar speed with better XP. ([2026-06-04 2:05](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=125))
- Gnomonkey thinks the deadfall and rumor changes make Hunter rumors much better, especially the very early ones, and that grinding early hunter rumors may become more viable. ([2026-06-04 3:40](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=220))

## Events

- Ent branches from the new Fletching activity can make enhanced bird snares, net trap kits and box traps that have a 50% chance to reset themselves if they fail, which Gnomonkey calls a huge buff to Hunter. ([2025-01-27 3:43](https://youtu.be/3kmWbjwmZqI?t=223))
- In the Summer Sweep-Up, falconry was buffed so the player is no longer held in place while the falcon chases a kebbit, and the player can reposition or walk around while it hunts. ([2026-06-04 0:30](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=30))
- Players can now ask the falcon to catch a kebbit even when their inventory is full, so they no longer need to drop all the junk drops. ([2026-06-04 1:03](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=63))
- Aerial fishing buff: the area in which quick catches can be performed is increased from a 3x3 area to a 4x4 area, making back-to-back catches every tick more likely. ([2026-06-04 1:03](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=63))
- A toggle was added to Alry the Angler that lets the player's cormorant eat fish in the inventory when there is no appropriate bait, reducing the need to make fish offcuts. ([2026-06-04 1:33](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=93))
- In aerial fishing a larger pool will now spawn that the cormorant can be sent to several times in a row, adding downtime to break up the rapid pace. ([2026-06-04 1:33](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=93))
- The chance of obtaining Mudskipper-style aerial fishing pearls (referred to as 'Mulk pearls' in the transcript) is increased by 50%, and some pearl costs are lowered with prior purchases refunded: the fishing sack is down to 500 pearls and the Angler's outfit down to 50. ([2026-06-04 2:05](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=125))
- Deadfall trap trap limit is increased from one to two, which Gnomonkey says roughly doubles speed for deadfall hunting like pyrefoxes; this does not include maniacal monkeys. ([2026-06-04 2:38](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=158))
- Deadfall traps now have a chance to preserve their log, and become able to catch eight ticks after being placed instead of 15 ticks. ([2026-06-04 2:38](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=158))
- The wild kebbit catch rate is increased by two and a half times, since wild kebbits were an outlier in catch rate that drastically reduced the utility of deadfall traps at early Hunter levels. ([2026-06-04 3:08](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=188))
- XP from wild, barbed, prickly and sabertooth kebbits and pyrefoxes is lowered by 20%, but the other changes still work out to about 60% more XP per hour from these creatures, to bring them in line with other methods. ([2026-06-04 3:08](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=188))
- Bird snaring now allows two traps to be placed starting at level 1 instead of level 20, which Gnomonkey says doubles XP rates for catching birds early on. ([2026-06-04 3:40](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=220))
- The Ring of Pursuit can reveal full tracks even after one has already been started. ([2026-06-04 3:40](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=220))
- Butterfly and moth hunter rumor drop rate is improved from 1 in 75 to 1 in 40, and the pity rate from 150 to 80, because those rumors were notoriously slow and high variance. ([2026-06-04 4:11](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=251))
- Chompy bird hunting now grants some Hunter XP. ([2026-06-04 4:39](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=279))
