# Minotaur

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2024-03-25 to 2025-12-12). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

|---|---|
| **Type** | mechanic |

## Methods

- Gnomonkey says Minotaurs seem to fully heal other mobs when their centre tile sees the mob's centre tile, so you need to deal with them. ([2024-03-25 21:33](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=1293))
- Colosseum Minotaurs only melee, so you can pray melee against them, but as a replacement for their attack they fully heal anything in range. ([2024-06-15 13:06](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=786))
- A Minotaur only heals enemies that are damaged, as Gnomonkey believes below 20% or more of their max health, so you have to hit them hard for the heal to matter. ([2024-06-15 13:36](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=816))
- A Minotaur heal block (mage block) is done by letting a Shaman see you from a spot that puts the Minotaur in front of the mage every time; Gnomonkey watches his timer, hits once, stands there praying mage, then moves back out of the way. ([2024-06-15 18:10](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=1090))

## Stats and numbers

- If the Minotaur heals, it goes on a 10-tick delay from the instant it heals, like an 8-tick mage respawn delay in the Inferno, making it incredibly hard to off-tick. ([2025-12-12 11:33](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=693))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says with Minotaurs you can come down one tile and completely avoid the Solar Flare orb. ([2024-03-25 21:33](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=1293))
- Gnomonkey drags enemies into the pillar side the Minotaur cannot see, so he can kill them without the Minotaur healing them. ([2024-03-25 24:30](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=1470))
- A Minotaur coming right over can be safe spotted behind a pillar, according to Gnomonkey. ([2024-03-25 28:15](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=1695))
- To avoid a Minotaur's heal, stay within its melee range because it will then always attack you instead of healing; also if you have the Minotaur corner-trapped on your diagonal, it will not heal. ([2024-06-15 13:36](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=816))
- Whenever a Minotaur is present, Gnomonkey kills a low HP target and then stops hitting things so the Minotaur does not heal. ([2025-12-12 11:33](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=693))
- On wave 9 Gnomonkey swaps to mage and puts the Minotaur behind the javelin so he does not have to deal with it, using all the tools in his tool belt. ([2025-12-12 15:27](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=927))
- Wave 10 is the first wave with a Minotaur plus a serpent shaman, so both must be off-ticked while making sure the Minotaur does not heal. ([2025-12-12 18:38](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=1118))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey calls the Minotaur a big problem for the Pillarless Colosseum run. ([2025-12-12 11:04](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=664))
