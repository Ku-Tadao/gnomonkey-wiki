**Minotaur** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2024-03-25 and 2025-12-12. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Minotaurs seem to fully heal other mobs when their centre tile sees the mob's centre tile, so you need to deal with them.[^EVOs96kyHSM-1293]
* Colosseum Minotaurs only melee, so you can pray melee against them, but as a replacement for their attack they fully heal anything in range.[^kIfF1pNro2c-786]
* A Minotaur only heals enemies that are damaged, as Gnomonkey believes below 20% or more of their max health, so you have to hit them hard for the heal to matter.[^kIfF1pNro2c-816]
* A Minotaur heal block (mage block) is done by letting a Shaman see you from a spot that puts the Minotaur in front of the mage every time; Gnomonkey watches his timer, hits once, stands there praying mage, then moves back out of the way.[^kIfF1pNro2c-1090]

## Stats and numbers

* If the Minotaur heals, it goes on a 10-tick delay from the instant it heals, like an 8-tick mage respawn delay in the Inferno, making it incredibly hard to off-tick.[^E51JMiU2gsk-693]

## Tips

* With Minotaurs you can come down one tile and completely avoid the Solar Flare orb.[^EVOs96kyHSM-1293]
* Gnomonkey drags enemies into the pillar side the Minotaur cannot see, so he can kill them without the Minotaur healing them.[^EVOs96kyHSM-1470]
* A Minotaur coming right over can be safe spotted behind a pillar, according to Gnomonkey.[^EVOs96kyHSM-1695]
* To avoid a Minotaur's heal, stay within its melee range because it will then always attack you instead of healing; also if you have the Minotaur corner-trapped on your diagonal, it will not heal.[^kIfF1pNro2c-816]
* Whenever a Minotaur is present, Gnomonkey kills a low HP target and then stops hitting things so the Minotaur does not heal.[^E51JMiU2gsk-693]
* On wave 9 Gnomonkey swaps to mage and puts the Minotaur behind the javelin so he does not have to deal with it, using all the tools in his tool belt.[^E51JMiU2gsk-927]
* Wave 10 is the first wave with a Minotaur plus a serpent shaman, so both must be off-ticked while making sure the Minotaur does not heal.[^E51JMiU2gsk-1118]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey calls the Minotaur a big problem for the Pillarless Colosseum run.[^E51JMiU2gsk-664]
