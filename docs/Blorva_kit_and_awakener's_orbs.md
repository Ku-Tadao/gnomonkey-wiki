## Stats and numbers

- The ancient blood ornament kit drops on the fourth unique awakened boss killed, and is used to turn Torva into Blorva. ([2026-05-27 16:31](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=991))
- Awakener's orbs cost 450k each at the time of the video, ranging between roughly 200k and 1 million, and each attempt at an awakened boss uses one. ([2026-05-27 17:34](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=1054))
- At 39 kills per hour of Vardorvis, Gnomonkey says orbs add only about 200k GP per hour of profit, a little less than the diamond, which is not a major drop. ([2026-05-27 18:06](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=1086))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey suggests the ancient blood ornament kit act as a reusable awakener's orb, similar to the Echo orb from Leagues, because the only reason to fight awakened bosses now is to do it once for Blorva. ([2026-05-27 17:02](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=1022))
- Gnomonkey suggests the reusable orb idea could apply to Radiant bosses too, though Radiant already drops the contract if completed, so it matters less there. ([2026-05-27 19:03](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=1143))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey argues that awakener's orb costs are not a big sink post-Blorva because few players bother doing the bosses again. ([2026-05-27 17:34](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=1054))
- Gnomonkey says awakened bosses have three times higher drop rates but often take more than three times as long to kill, with the Awakened Whisperer taking about four times as long, so they are already balanced by kill time. ([2026-05-27 18:32](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=1112))
- Gnomonkey says Awakened Leviathan and Awakened Vardorvis are insanely good bosses in his top five in the game, but they are effectively blocked unless a player is filthy rich. ([2026-05-27 19:03](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=1143))
