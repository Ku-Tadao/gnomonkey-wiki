**Jal-MejRah** (the **bats**) are monsters of the [Inferno](Inferno.md) that spawn from the very first wave[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-711]. Gnomonkey treats them as the highest kill priority whenever they are on the player: they hit hard, drain stats and run energy, and he has seen people die to a couple of bats[^h6LxupiygPA-1561][^2xviK0wGI-o-2777]. His advice has been the same since 2021: do not tank bats, kill them first[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1418][^XMTe9TYbDls-1542][^H-Iup_IFUVc-594].

## Combat stats

* Max hit 19 with ranged attacks, which can be devastating if two are tanked at once[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-711][^H-Iup_IFUVc-594][^2xviK0wGI-o-574]. Two bats hurt a lot and push things around and stack things up. He took about forty damage from two bats while praying Rigor[^6lRmbYCYtME-4558].
* Bats attack every three game ticks, the fastest attacking mob in the Inferno[^2xviK0wGI-o-574].
* Bats have a short range of four tiles, whereas the blowpipe has a maximum range of five[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-742][^H-Iup_IFUVc-594][^7F5_YBzxtE8-541]. They and the nibblers are the only Inferno monsters with short range[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-742].
* Bats cannot melee, while blobs can[^XMTe9TYbDls-1811][^6trKOSUr4EM-1098].
* Bats have negative 20 mage defence[^XMTe9TYbDls-2761].

## Drains

Every hit drains run energy by three, including zero hits, and it happens even while praying[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-742][^H-Iup_IFUVc-594]. A hit off prayer also has a chance to drain a combat stat by one, which the bat adds to its own stat[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-742][^2xviK0wGI-o-574]. Being drained below 94 Magic removes the player's autocast[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-742][^h6LxupiygPA-652].

As of a Jagex patch (noted in 2021), bats can no longer drain stats if the player prays ranged correctly, but they still drain run energy[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-774]. Gnomonkey therefore prays ranged at the start of waves where bats spawn[^7F5_YBzxtE8-541]. Bats also heal off the player and boost their own stats when they hit, so a shot that would kill them can let them heal and survive[^h6LxupiygPA-2546].

## Safe spot and tactics

* **Safe spot.** A tile where the bats cannot reach the player but a blowpipe can, since bats have four-tile range and the blowpipe five[^XMTe9TYbDls-621][^6trKOSUr4EM-414]. One such tile is one tile away from the corner of the pillar, which lets the player shoot bats even on rapid without being attacked[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1418]. The player can avoid an attack by stepping one corner out from the pillar to just outside range[^2xviK0wGI-o-689]. The same tile doubles as a melee corner trap tile when a melee is about to dig[^2xviK0wGI-o-1103]. Killing bats from the safe spot stops them draining run[^XMTe9TYbDls-1714].
* **Priority.** When several monsters are on the player, always kill the bats first[^XMTe9TYbDls-1542][^H-Iup_IFUVc-594][^2xviK0wGI-o-574].
* **Special attacks.** A great time to use special attacks is on bats[^6lRmbYCYtME-2359]. He double pipe-specs them to kill them instantly, saying the blowpipe spec is the "kill button" rather than the "heal button"[^H-Iup_IFUVc-3300].
* **Rigor.** Anytime two bats must be killed, he usually puts on Rigor, since it gives defence[^6lRmbYCYtME-2989].
* **Healing.** The best targets for phantom heals are bats, blobs, bloblets and later rangers[^6lRmbYCYtME-624]. Bats are decent monsters to heal off, and good health can be gained from them with blood barrage[^XMTe9TYbDls-2761][^7F5_YBzxtE8-2158].
