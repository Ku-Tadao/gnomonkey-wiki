**Jal-MejRah** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 8 videos between 2021-05-21 and 2026-07-13. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Bats can drain your run energy and drain your stats when they hit you, which can be a problem if it drains your magic level.[^h6LxupiygPA-652]
* Bats can heal off you and boost their stats when they hit you, so there are times a shot that would kill them lets them heal and survive.[^h6LxupiygPA-2546]
* Inferno bats have a very short ranged attack and drain your run energy when they hit you, and drain your stats on a hit; Gnomonkey says to kill them and not let them stay on you.[^6trKOSUr4EM-414]
* If a bat hits you off prayer it drains your run energy and your stats, so Gnomonkey prays ranged at the start of waves where bats spawn.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-541]

## Stats and numbers

* Bats spawn from the very first wave and max a 19 with ranged attacks, which can be devastating if you tank two at once.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-711]
* Bats have a short range of four tiles whereas the blowpipe has a max range of five, so you can pipe them safely from max range even on rapid; they and nibblers are the only Inferno monsters with short range.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-742]
* Bats drain run energy by three on every hit, including zeros, and have a chance to drain a combat stat by one that they add to their own; being drained below 94 Magic removes your autocast.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-742]
* Bats cannot melee you, while blobs can.[^XMTe9TYbDls-1811]
* Bats have negative 20 mage defence, and Gnomonkey says you can get good health off bats with blood barrage.[^XMTe9TYbDls-2761]
* Inferno bats max hit 19, drain your stats if they hit you off prayer and drain your run energy with every hit even if you are praying.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-594]
* Inferno bats have a range of four tiles, so a blowpipe just barely clears them.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-594]
* Bats (Jal-MejRah) have a four tile range, so a blowpipe is just barely further, and attack every three game ticks, the fastest attacking mob in the Inferno.[^2xviK0wGI-o-574]
* If a bat hits you off prayer it drains your run energy and your combat stats, and bats max 19.[^2xviK0wGI-o-574]
* Bats in the Inferno have exactly four tile range, so a blowpipe at exactly five tiles can shoot one without it hitting you.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-541]

## Tips

* A bat safe spot one tile away from the corner of the pillar lets you shoot bats even on rapid with a blowpipe without them attacking you, and Gnomonkey says to avoid tanking bats where possible.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-1418]
* The best targets for phantom heals are bats, blobs, bloblets and later rangers.[^6lRmbYCYtME-624]
* A great time to use special attacks is on bats, and that bats hit you really hard so they should be taken out of the equation.[^6lRmbYCYtME-2359]
* Anytime you have to kill two bats you usually put on Rigor, since it gives defense.[^6lRmbYCYtME-2989]
* To always kill a bat that is on you since they hit really hard, drain your stats and run energy, and he has seen people die to a couple of bats on them.[^h6LxupiygPA-1561]
* The bat safe spot is a tile where bats cannot reach you but a blowpipe can, since bats have a four tile range and blowpipe has five.[^XMTe9TYbDls-621]
* Bats hurt your run energy every time they hit you, so always kill bats first when multiple things are on you.[^XMTe9TYbDls-1542]
* To always try to kill bats from the safe spot so they do not drain your run.[^XMTe9TYbDls-1714]
* Blowpipe range reaches a bat from a tile where the bat cannot reach you.[^6trKOSUr4EM-414]
* A bat cannot punch you; it only has ranged attacks.[^6trKOSUr4EM-1098]
* Not to tank bats but always kill bats first when they are on you with other monsters.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-594]
* Gnomonkey double pipe-specs bats to kill them instantly, saying bats are always the first thing you kill and that the blowpipe spec is the 'kill button' rather than the 'heal button'.[^H-Iup_IFUVc-3300]
* Bats should always be the priority to kill if you are tanking them, and that blowpipe specs are great against bats.[^2xviK0wGI-o-574]
* You can avoid being attacked by a bat by stepping one corner out from the pillar so you are just barely outside its range.[^2xviK0wGI-o-689]
* A bat can be safe spotted at the pillar, and that same bat safe spot tile doubles as a melee corner trap tile when a melee is about to dig.[^2xviK0wGI-o-1103]
* Bats attacking you are an extremely high priority to kill.[^2xviK0wGI-o-2777]
* To always kill a bat when it is hitting you, and that bats are also decent monsters to heal off.[^7F5_YBzxtE8-2158]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says two bats are no good, that they hurt a lot, they push things around and stack things up, and he took about forty damage from two bats while praying Rigor.[^6lRmbYCYtME-4558]

## Events

* As of a Jagex patch, bats can no longer drain your stats if you pray ranged correctly against them, but they still drain run energy each attack.[^qnw3kGlpiuQ-774]
