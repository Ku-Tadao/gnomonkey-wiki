# Jal-MejRah

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 8 videos (2021-05-21 to 2026-07-13). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Bats can drain your run energy and drain your stats when they hit you, which can be a problem if it drains your magic level. ([2024-01-17 10:52](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=652))
- Bats can heal off you and boost their stats when they hit you, so there are times a shot that would kill them lets them heal and survive. ([2024-01-17 42:26](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=2546))
- Inferno bats have a very short ranged attack and drain your run energy when they hit you, and drain your stats on a hit; Gnomonkey says to kill them and not let them stay on you. ([2024-10-03 6:54](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=414))
- If a bat hits you off prayer it drains your run energy and your stats, so Gnomonkey prays ranged at the start of waves where bats spawn. ([2026-07-13 9:01](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=541))

## Stats and numbers

- Bats spawn from the very first wave and max a 19 with ranged attacks, which can be devastating if you tank two at once. ([2021-05-21 11:51](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=711))
- Bats have a short range of four tiles whereas the blowpipe has a max range of five, so you can pipe them safely from max range even on rapid; they and nibblers are the only Inferno monsters with short range. ([2021-05-21 12:22](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=742))
- Bats drain run energy by three on every hit, including zeros, and have a chance to drain a combat stat by one that they add to their own; being drained below 94 Magic removes your autocast. ([2021-05-21 12:22](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=742))
- Bats cannot melee you, while blobs can. ([2024-06-13 30:11](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1811))
- Bats have negative 20 mage defence, and Gnomonkey says you can get good health off bats with blood barrage. ([2024-06-13 46:01](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=2761))
- Inferno bats max hit 19, drain your stats if they hit you off prayer and drain your run energy with every hit even if you are praying. ([2025-02-26 9:54](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=594))
- Inferno bats have a range of four tiles, so a blowpipe just barely clears them. ([2025-02-26 9:54](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=594))
- Bats (Jal-MejRah) have a four tile range, so a blowpipe is just barely further, and attack every three game ticks, the fastest attacking mob in the Inferno. ([2025-12-07 9:34](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=574))
- If a bat hits you off prayer it drains your run energy and your combat stats, and bats max 19. ([2025-12-07 9:34](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=574))
- Bats in the Inferno have exactly four tile range, so a blowpipe at exactly five tiles can shoot one without it hitting you. ([2026-07-13 9:01](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=541))

## Tips

- A bat safe spot one tile away from the corner of the pillar lets you shoot bats even on rapid with a blowpipe without them attacking you, and Gnomonkey says to avoid tanking bats where possible. ([2021-05-21 23:38](https://youtu.be/qnw3kGlpiuQ?t=1418))
- Gnomonkey says the best targets for phantom heals are bats, blobs, bloblets and later rangers. ([2022-07-22 10:24](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=624))
- Gnomonkey says a great time to use special attacks is on bats, and that bats hit you really hard so they should be taken out of the equation. ([2022-07-22 39:19](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2359))
- Gnomonkey says anytime you have to kill two bats you usually put on Rigor, since it gives defense. ([2022-07-22 49:49](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=2989))
- Gnomonkey says to always kill a bat that is on you since they hit really hard, drain your stats and run energy, and he has seen people die to a couple of bats on them. ([2024-01-17 26:01](https://youtu.be/h6LxupiygPA?t=1561))
- The bat safe spot is a tile where bats cannot reach you but a blowpipe can, since bats have a four tile range and blowpipe has five. ([2024-06-13 10:21](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=621))
- Gnomonkey says bats hurt your run energy every time they hit you, so always kill bats first when multiple things are on you. ([2024-06-13 25:42](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1542))
- Gnomonkey says to always try to kill bats from the safe spot so they do not drain your run. ([2024-06-13 28:34](https://youtu.be/XMTe9TYbDls?t=1714))
- Gnomonkey says Blowpipe range reaches a bat from a tile where the bat cannot reach you. ([2024-10-03 6:54](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=414))
- Gnomonkey says a bat cannot punch you; it only has ranged attacks. ([2024-10-03 18:18](https://youtu.be/6trKOSUr4EM?t=1098))
- Gnomonkey says not to tank bats but always kill bats first when they are on you with other monsters. ([2025-02-26 9:54](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=594))
- Gnomonkey double pipe-specs bats to kill them instantly, saying bats are always the first thing you kill and that the blowpipe spec is the 'kill button' rather than the 'heal button'. ([2025-02-26 55:00](https://youtu.be/H-Iup_IFUVc?t=3300))
- Gnomonkey says bats should always be the priority to kill if you are tanking them, and that blowpipe specs are great against bats. ([2025-12-07 9:34](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=574))
- You can avoid being attacked by a bat by stepping one corner out from the pillar so you are just barely outside its range. ([2025-12-07 11:29](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=689))
- A bat can be safe spotted at the pillar, and that same bat safe spot tile doubles as a melee corner trap tile when a melee is about to dig. ([2025-12-07 18:23](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=1103))
- Gnomonkey says bats attacking you are an extremely high priority to kill. ([2025-12-07 46:17](https://youtu.be/2xviK0wGI-o?t=2777))
- Gnomonkey says to always kill a bat when it is hitting you, and that bats are also decent monsters to heal off. ([2026-07-13 35:58](https://youtu.be/7F5_YBzxtE8?t=2158))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says two bats are no good, that they hurt a lot, they push things around and stack things up, and he took about forty damage from two bats while praying Rigor. ([2022-07-22 1:15:58](https://youtu.be/6lRmbYCYtME?t=4558))
