# Ghommal's lucky penny

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2025-09-11 to 2026-06-04). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | item |

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says the Lucky penny has only a 5% chance of saving charges, and works on items such as the Ash sanctifier, Bone crusher, Celestial ring, crystal tools and the Games necklace, but not on the Blood fury. ([2025-09-11 45:40](https://youtu.be/ZjG3P7PTeIs?t=2740))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says the Lucky penny, the Master-tier Combat Achievement reward, is one of the five dumbest items Jagex has added, because it must be worn in the ammo slot, which conflicts with using ammo at bosses. ([2025-09-11 45:12](https://youtu.be/ZjG3P7PTeIs?t=2712))
- Gnomonkey says the Lucky penny should only apply to bossing items such as the Scythe of Vitur, Crystal armour, Toxic blowpipe and Blood fury, and its chance should be raised to 10%. ([2025-09-11 47:11](https://youtu.be/ZjG3P7PTeIs?t=2831))
- Gnomonkey says the Lucky penny should instead be a Master-tier reward given by an NPC that grants a 10% charge-saving chance rather than needing the ammo slot. ([2025-09-11 47:43](https://youtu.be/ZjG3P7PTeIs?t=2863))
- Gnomonkey, sarcastically thanking Jagex, calls the old lucky penny a horrible garbage item and says the change makes it a really good unlock for Master combat achievements, though he would still love it to work on the Blood Fury. ([2026-06-04 45:46](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2746))

## Events

- The lucky penny's charge saving effect becomes an account unlock for completing the master tier combat achievements instead of requiring the item to be worn. ([2026-06-04 45:46](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2746))
