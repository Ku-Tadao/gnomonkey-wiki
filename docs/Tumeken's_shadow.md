# Tumeken's shadow

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 11 videos (2022-05-18 to 2026-03-05). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- The Shadow has a unique passive like the Twisted bow: it triples the magic accuracy and magic strength bonuses of your gear, including from Slayer helmet or Salve amulet, so packing many magic switches raises the staff's effectiveness dramatically. ([2022-05-18 0:59](https://youtu.be/Kgm0VYX_4no?t=59))
- Gnomonkey says Tumeken's shadow triples accuracy and damage bonuses from magic gear, and quadruples them within Tombs of Amascut. ([2024-07-07 16:25](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=985))
- Gnomonkey says the Tumeken's shadow's base max hit increases with magic level boosts such as overload and salts, and it punches through magic defence. ([2024-07-07 16:25](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=985))

## Requirements

- The 5-tick cast speed of the Tumeken's shadow is why Butterfly Tech works best with it. ([2022-08-30 0:28](https://youtu.be/pSBxLCy7-44?t=28))

## Stats and numbers

- The proposed Shadow of Tumeken is a two-handed powered staff with a flat five-tick attack speed, 35 magic attack and no inherent magic strength; at 99 Magic its base max hit is 34 compared with Sanguinesti staff's base max of 32. ([2022-05-18 0:32](https://youtu.be/Kgm0VYX_4no?t=32))
- Gnomonkey says the Tumeken's shadow multiplies max hits tremendously, so you need all the magic damage bonus you can get. ([2024-07-01 35:20](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=2120))
- Gnomonkey states that without Ancestral, using Virtus loses five max hits across the roughly 80% of the raid spent mageing; swapping in only Ancestral pieces gets back two max hits for one. ([2024-07-01 35:20](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=2120))
- Gnomonkey says that in Tombs of Amascut, dropping from Ancestral to Virtus armour while using Tumeken's shadow loses about five max hits of damage. ([2025-01-12 13:57](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=837))
- Gnomonkey says Tumeken's shadow has a 100% cap from gear, so gear cannot provide more than a 100% damage bonus. ([2025-03-28 11:57](https://youtu.be/u-RJBe_5Nmo?t=717))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says he used Tumeken's shadow against several Chambers bosses in the naked run, such as Mystics, because of the accuracy and damage without any gear. ([2023-09-12 1:24:10](https://youtu.be/f1Y2Pd6dQoA?t=5050))
- Gnomonkey says the shadow travels very slowly, so you need to click early when casting at the jug. ([2024-07-01 8:22](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=502))
- Gnomonkey says that dumping your bank for a Tumeken's shadow would mean being locked at Tombs of Amascut 300s for around six months to get your gear back. ([2024-07-07 20:12](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1212))
- Shadow is useful on very tanky bosses such as Abyssal portal and Mystics in CM. ([2024-11-08 1:12:10](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=4330))
- Gnomonkey says you can bring Sanguinesti staff in addition to the shadow to make Olm easier if you have not learned Shadow running, but the shadow is much faster on Olm. ([2024-11-08 1:12:40](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=4360))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey's favourite thing about the Shadow is that it does not take 30 minutes to read about and understand what it does, unlike the Heka. ([2022-05-18 1:27](https://youtu.be/Kgm0VYX_4no?t=87))
- Gnomonkey loves that the Shadow encourages max-mage switches (usually where players skimp), gives major benefits to items like Ancestral hat, Seers ring and Brimstone ring that are typically worthless, and encourages less tanky gear, making it interesting at places like God Wars Dungeon: take more damage but deal much more. ([2022-05-18 1:27](https://youtu.be/Kgm0VYX_4no?t=87))
- Gnomonkey expects the Shadow to be best in slot at a multitude of bosses, and where the Harmonised nightmare staff was best in slot the Shadow would be best by roughly four percent; his calculations assume best gear for each style (Bis range, Torva melee, and Ward with Sanguinesti). ([2022-05-18 2:01](https://youtu.be/Kgm0VYX_4no?t=121))
- Gnomonkey says the Shadow's design is much simpler than the Heka but deceptively complex, and there will be times you will not want to use it even when it is best in slot because of worse defence and the many switches required. ([2022-05-18 6:06](https://youtu.be/Kgm0VYX_4no?t=366))
- Gnomonkey loves the idea of a glass-cannon meta for magic to rise, says Heal Other will become much more important for solo bossers like him, and likes that the weapon mostly avoids regular defence, which is why it is good at undrainable bosses like Armadyl. ([2022-05-18 6:06](https://youtu.be/Kgm0VYX_4no?t=366))
- Gnomonkey says players worrying that the Shadow of Tumeken will devalue Twisted bow and Scythe are wrong; the Shadow is far less useful than either, being better than Twisted bow in about four locations, better than Scythe in about two, and equal in a few. ([2022-05-25 9:57](https://youtu.be/SO4k9z1A8-0?t=597))
- Gnomonkey calls the Shadow a tactical choice that keeps everything relevant: it is amazing where bosses do not have a high magic level, but requires considering which switches to bring and whether defence matters, as Ancestral is paper thin. ([2022-05-25 10:16](https://youtu.be/SO4k9z1A8-0?t=616))
- Gnomonkey says the Shadow devalues Twisted bow and Scythe in such a small and calculated way that it is balanced: Scythe still shreds large, melee-weak bosses and Twisted bow still annihilates anything with a high magic level and lower defence, while the Shadow shreds bosses with lower magic level where you can bring the gear. ([2022-05-25 10:46](https://youtu.be/SO4k9z1A8-0?t=646))
- Gnomonkey says Tumeken's shadow seems to be the best possible DPS on Akkha himself, while being a bit worse than the Toxic blowpipe on Akkha's shadows. ([2022-08-30 1:28](https://youtu.be/pSBxLCy7-44?t=88))
- Gnomonkey says no one in chat could calculate Tumeken's shadow versus Twisted bow properly during the naked run, mentioning the shadow being 20-55 percent better depending on the calculator. ([2023-09-12 1:14:00](https://youtu.be/f1Y2Pd6dQoA?t=4440))
- In the video description Gnomonkey says he does not recommend using the Tumeken's shadow without Ancestral, and that a Twisted bow is still best-in-slot at Zebak, with the shadow only beating it at Zebak if you have salts and max magic. ([2024-07-01 0:20](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=20))
- Gnomonkey says he would never recommend a Shadow rebuild: a Twisted bow rebuild is better than a Shadow rebuild, and the setup is aimed at people who were spooned a shadow, which he says is common on ironman accounts. ([2024-07-01 7:42](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=462))
- Gnomonkey says the Tumeken's shadow is disgusting for Wardens phase 1 and that it is a huge boost on the final row. ([2024-07-01 36:40](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=2200))
- Gnomonkey says the Tumeken's shadow is extremely weak without powerful magic gear equipped, though it is decent in some unconventional places like Zebak. ([2024-07-07 16:25](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=985))
- Gnomonkey says Ancestral robes are extremely overinflated in value due to their benefit with the shadow, and not worth the money without one: one max hit over Virtus for the whole set and two over Ahrim's for the whole set, so buying them before a shadow is simply trolling. ([2024-07-07 18:25](https://youtu.be/e0y_auD9JhY?t=1105))
- Gnomonkey says Tumeken's shadow is roughly a five-minute time save in CM and is insanely strong in addition to the Twisted bow, but is not a replacement for it. ([2024-11-08 1:11:50](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=4310))
- Gnomonkey says to use Sang at Crabs rather than shadow, since the shadow is really slow there. ([2024-11-08 1:13:20](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=4400))
- Gnomonkey argues the way Tumeken's shadow scales with magic damage bonus is too powerful, which is why tiny 1% magic damage buffs like the new Mage cape feel pitiful for anything but the shadow. ([2025-01-12 13:28](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=808))
- Gnomonkey suggests knocking the shadow's multiplier in Tombs of Amascut down to about 2x or maybe 3x, one multiplier lower, and making its base damage solid, which would make players less reliant on magic gear. ([2025-01-12 13:57](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=837))
- Gnomonkey says the shadow was scaling too hard from the get-go, and that this is becoming obvious as tiny magic damage increases need trade-offs to balance; he says it should keep its power but use a different scaling system. ([2025-01-12 14:24](https://youtu.be/rUFbUjrcJZA?t=864))
- Gnomonkey says the Tumeken's shadow is a hard-to-use weapon with a learning curve at Ulm, like Butterfly Akkha, which makes sense for an extremely expensive item, in contrast to the forgiving Sanguinesti staff. ([2025-03-11 13:30](https://youtu.be/-98-rsX1soM?t=810))
- Gnomonkey calls the Tumeken's shadow the most used mega rare in the game, though he says the scythe is the most useful and expensive. ([2025-03-11 25:18](https://youtu.be/-98-rsX1soM?t=1518))
- Gnomonkey says Shadow definitely needs help and that it holds magic in a vice grip, and he is glad Jagex says so. ([2025-03-28 3:03](https://youtu.be/u-RJBe_5Nmo?t=183))
- Gnomonkey says it is lame that Tumeken's shadow has a damage cap, because mage upgrades stop helping once it hits the cap, and he would prefer it scaled properly. ([2025-03-28 10:55](https://youtu.be/u-RJBe_5Nmo?t=655))
- Gnomonkey says people bring up the Shadow cap as if it solves everything, but once new mage upgrades do nothing for Shadow it will feel bad. ([2025-03-28 11:29](https://youtu.be/u-RJBe_5Nmo?t=689))
- The Jagex blog describes the Tumeken's shadow as an outlier because it is good everywhere, which has come with no shortage of problems. ([2026-03-05 3:01](https://youtu.be/_XtlpCDD7fI?t=181))
- Gnomonkey says the Shadow now needs rebalancing because it is impossible to add new gear items with it. ([2026-03-05 17:59](https://youtu.be/_XtlpCDD7fI?t=1079))
- Gnomonkey says the Shadow is so strong that a 3 percent mage damage bonus cannot be added to an item at Mocha without the Shadow making it too powerful. ([2026-03-05 22:37](https://youtu.be/_XtlpCDD7fI?t=1357))

## Events

- Jagex says the Tumeken's shadow is good across the entire game but its slow attack speed means it is not always the right tool, which the Eye of Ayak is meant to complement. ([2025-03-11 3:53](https://youtu.be/-98-rsX1soM?t=233))
- Jagex said there are fundamental issues with Tumeken's shadow that dictate what it can do around magic rewards, but it could not adjust the weapon alongside its other Varlamore work. ([2025-03-28 3:59](https://youtu.be/u-RJBe_5Nmo?t=239))
- Jagex committed to making adjustments to Tumeken's shadow before discussing any future best-in-slot magic reward space, without a firm timeline. ([2025-03-28 4:30](https://youtu.be/u-RJBe_5Nmo?t=270))
- Jagex said it may make changes to the Shadow if it explores the magic reward space; Gnomonkey says a potential Shadow rework could come fairly soon and would get its own video. ([2026-03-05 34:52](https://youtu.be/_XtlpCDD7fI?t=2092))
