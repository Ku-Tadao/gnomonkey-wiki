The **Twisted bow** is a [Chambers of Xeric](Chambers_of_Xeric.md) ranged weapon with a relatively low 70 ranged attack bonus, +4 prayer bonus and +20 ranged strength. It cost about 1.3 billion gp in 2020[^bIShDvVDueM-771], and Gnomonkey notes that people liquidate their whole bank to buy one and rebuild afterwards[^bIShDvVDueM-832]. Its value comes from its scaling: damage and accuracy rise with the target's magic level[^bIShDvVDueM-799][^e0y_auD9JhY-1013]. Gnomonkey considers it best in slot for a long list of bosses[^bIShDvVDueM-832] and the most important item for Challenge Mode Chambers[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-60]. He also says that levelling stats to max gets a player a free Twisted bow's worth of damage[^e0y_auD9JhY-292].

He got his own Twisted bow from the Chambers of Xeric on his hardcore ironman in a duo raid at 115 kill count, which he considers his first Twisted bow[^tTn3JmSD4fI-2662]. It let him skip the Bofa grind, with no Confliction gauntlets required[^tTn3JmSD4fI-0]. Hedron was 60 kill count dry at the raid and gave him all her RNG for the drop[^tTn3JmSD4fI-0]. He did not even have a Xeric's talisman to put it on, and viewers joked that sending a raid with his wife may have been the key[^tTn3JmSD4fI-2756].

## Mechanics
The bow's damage scales with the enemy's magic level, capped at level 250 in the main game or 350 inside the Chambers of Xeric. This gives up to a 140% accuracy boost and 215% damage boost outside raids, or up to 248% damage inside[^bIShDvVDueM-799]. The max hit and accuracy therefore rise with the opponent's magic level, with a higher cap in Chambers[^e0y_auD9JhY-1013].

Consequences he lists:
* It is useless on things with low magic level[^e0y_auD9JhY-1013].
* It has weird damage breakpoints where certain max hits give bonus hits[^e0y_auD9JhY-1013].
* It is better against low-defence targets but can punch through a decent amount of defence[^e0y_auD9JhY-1013].
* In Challenge Mode, monsters have higher stats, so the bow hits harder: it maxes about 96 on many bosses[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-60].
* With vambraces and a max-boost divine ranging potion it hits up to 75 instead of 73 in the Inferno[^6lRmbYCYtME-57].

## Best in slot uses
At release it was best in slot for Giant Mole, Zilyana, Kree'arra, Zamorak, King Black Dragon, the Dagannoth Kings, Chambers of Xeric, Zulrah, Abyssal Sire, Cerberus, Alchemical Hydra and brutal black dragons. Bandos' magic level had to be reduced because the bow was too overpowered there[^bIShDvVDueM-832].

### Ammunition
Gnomonkey says users should basically always use dragon arrows over amethyst arrows[^7dh6_0u13Nc-321]. Dragon arrows are about a 4% DPS upgrade and cost about 140k gp an hour versus about 40k gp an hour for amethyst, so about 2m gp an hour is technically needed for them to be worth it[^7dh6_0u13Nc-321]. The one place he uses amethyst arrows is Zulrah, since damage is capped at 50 there and better arrows barely increase DPS[^7dh6_0u13Nc-321].

### Where not to use it
* Akkha: he says never to butterfly Akkha with a twisted bow because Akkha's magic level is very low, calling it terrible and worse than a rune crossbow[^6Qmuhnpa0gc-1049]. A player who owns only a rune crossbow and a twisted bow should bring the crossbow swap[^6Qmuhnpa0gc-1079].
* Inferno: a Twisted bow is not needed for a sub 65 Inferno. With a Bofa a player would wear a Slayer helmet and add about five minutes (his sub 53 would be about 58)[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-5224]. Mage gear can all be downgraded, Masori is not required, and Crystal gear is the next best after Masori, even with a Twisted bow, because of its strong range defence[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-5250].

## Inferno
In a 2022 stream Gnomonkey did a basic Inferno completion with the Twisted bow, saying he got so much use out of Deviate's original teaching stream that he made his own[^6lRmbYCYtME-0]. He says the Bow of Faerdhinen is almost the same as the Twisted bow on everything except Zuk, where it is 15% worse, and about 8% worse (not exact) on mages, so the Bofa is totally viable for the Inferno[^6lRmbYCYtME-2842]. Asked whether Ahrim's/Master wand and Bofa is good enough, he says that is plenty as long as the Bofa is owned; it will be harder than with the Twisted bow but definitely doable[^6lRmbYCYtME-523].

## Challenge Mode Chambers
Gnomonkey calls the Twisted bow the ground floor for getting into Challenge Mode and the most important item for the raid[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-60], and says that without one a player effectively cannot do Challenge Mode sensibly, since the gear requirement is high[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-4143]. At Vasa he picks up Twisted items dropped in the room, and the bow makes the fight extremely fast. He calls his minute-long kill basically a world-record Vasa[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2607]. Shots on the Mage Vanguard can do around 80-90, so the Vanguard's reset threshold must be watched to avoid over-damaging it[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-3490].

## Other uses
* Jad Challenge (6 Jad): he highly recommends the bow, though it can be done with just a blowpipe[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-27]. He uses it rather than chinchompas to tag healers[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-84]. Because each challenge Jad has 5 healers, it is basically impossible to out-DPS them with the bow, unlike regular Jad[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-148].
* Nex: the bow is way, way better than the Toxic blowpipe against her minions because they have a lot of defence[^AzCKJvLDRxo-209]. Its long range lets Gnomonkey and Hedron do step unders at Kruar and keep Nex stalled[^AzCKJvLDRxo-436].
