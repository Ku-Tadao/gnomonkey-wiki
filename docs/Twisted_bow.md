**Twisted bow** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 10 videos between 2020-06-27 and 2025-07-13. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Gnomonkey does the stream as an example of a basic Inferno completion using a Twisted bow, saying he got so much use out of Deviate's original Twisted bow teaching stream that he made his own.[^6lRmbYCYtME-0]
* The Twisted bow's max hit and accuracy increase with the opponent's magic level, with a higher cap in Chambers of Xeric.[^e0y_auD9JhY-1013]

## Stats and numbers

* The Twisted bow cost about 1.3 billion at the time and has a relatively low 70 ranged attack bonus, +4 prayer bonus and +20 ranged strength.[^bIShDvVDueM-771]
* The Twisted bow's damage scales with the enemy's magic level, capped at level 250 in the main game or 350 inside the Chambers of Xeric, giving up to a 140% accuracy boost and 215% damage boost outside raids or up to 248% inside.[^bIShDvVDueM-799]
* Dragon arrows are about a 4% DPS upgrade and cost about 140k GP an hour versus about 40k GP an hour for amethyst arrows, so you technically need about 2m GP an hour for dragon arrows to be worth it.[^7dh6_0u13Nc-321]
* Because each challenge Jad has 5 healers, Gnomonkey says it is basically impossible to out-DPS the healers with a twisted bow in the Jad Challenge, unlike regular Jad.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-148]
* With vambraces and a divine ranging potion at the max boost, Gnomonkey says the Twisted bow hits up to 75 instead of 73 in the Inferno.[^6lRmbYCYtME-57]
* Bow of Faerdhinen is almost the same as the Twisted bow on everything except Zuk, where it is 15% worse, and about 8% worse (not exact) on mages, so he calls Bofa totally viable for the Inferno.[^6lRmbYCYtME-2842]
* Because Challenge Mode monsters have higher stats, the Twisted bow hits harder there; Gnomonkey says it maxes about 96 on many bosses.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-60]

## Tips

* The only place Gnomonkey would use amethyst arrows with a twisted bow is Zulrah, since damage is capped at 50 there and better arrows barely increase DPS.[^7dh6_0u13Nc-321]
* Gnomonkey uses his twisted bow, rather than chinchompas, to tag healers during 6 Jad.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-84]
* The Twisted bow's long range lets him and Hedron do step unders at Kruar and keep Nex stalled.[^AzCKJvLDRxo-436]
* The Twisted bow is useless on things with low magic level, has weird damage breakpoints where certain max hits give bonus hits, is better against low-defence targets, but can punch through a decent amount of defence.[^e0y_auD9JhY-1013]
* At Vasa, Gnomonkey picks up Twisted items dropped in the room and says having the Twisted bow makes the Vasa fight extremely fast; he calls his minute-long kill basically a world-record Vasa.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-2607]
* The Twisted bow shots on the Mage Vanguard can do around 80-90, so you must watch the Vanguard's reset threshold to avoid over-damaging it.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-3490]
* To never butterfly Akkha with a twisted bow because Akkha's magic level is very low, calling it terrible and worse than a rune crossbow.[^6Qmuhnpa0gc-1049]
* If a player only owns a rune crossbow and a twisted bow, Gnomonkey says to bring the crossbow swap, as it is better than a twisted bow at Akkha; the description says explicitly do not use a tbow.[^6Qmuhnpa0gc-1079]
* A Twisted bow is not needed for a sub 65 Inferno; with a Bofa a player would wear a Slayer helmet and add about five minutes (his sub 53 would be about 58).[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-5224]
* Mage gear can all be downgraded, Masori is not required, and Crystal gear is the next best after Masori, even if you own a Twisted bow, because of its strong range defence.[^UmAjGV8bBAQ-5250]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the Twisted bow is best in slot for so many bosses, including Giant Mole, Zilyana, Kree'arra, Zamorak, King Black Dragon, the Dagannoth Kings, Chambers of Xeric, Zulrah, Abyssal Sire, Cerberus, Alchemical Hydra and brutal black dragons, and that Bandos' magic level had to be reduced because it was too overpowered there when the bow came out.[^bIShDvVDueM-832]
* Gnomonkey says twisted bow users should basically always use dragon arrows over amethyst arrows.[^7dh6_0u13Nc-321]
* Gnomonkey highly recommends a twisted bow if attempting the 6 Jad challenge, though it can be done with just a blowpipe.[^Nu5mkhqaWlI-27]
* Asked whether Ahrim's/Master wand and Bofa is good enough gear for the Inferno, Gnomonkey says that is plenty as long as you have Bofa; it will be harder than with the Twisted bow but definitely doable.[^6lRmbYCYtME-523]
* Gnomonkey says the Twisted bow is way, way better than the Toxic blowpipe against Nex's minions because they have a lot of defence.[^AzCKJvLDRxo-209]
* Gnomonkey says leveling your stats to max gets you a free Twisted bow's worth of damage.[^e0y_auD9JhY-292]
* Gnomonkey says the Twisted bow is the ground floor for getting into Challenge Mode Chambers of Xeric and is the most important item for the raid.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-60]
* Gnomonkey says that without a Twisted bow you effectively cannot do CM sensibly, since the gear requirement is high.[^2r5Wlx2BiI0-4143]

## Records

* In the video description Gnomonkey says he got the Twisted bow on the HCIM and skipped the Bofa grind, with no Confliction gauntlets required.[^tTn3JmSD4fI-0]
* Gnomonkey received a Twisted bow from Chambers of Xeric on his Hardcore Ironman (HCIM) in a duo raid, at 115 kill count, which he considers his first Twisted bow.[^tTn3JmSD4fI-2662]

## Events

* The video description says Hedron was 60 kill count dry at Chambers of Xeric in general and gave Gnomonkey all her RNG for the drop.[^tTn3JmSD4fI-0]
* Gnomonkey said he did not even have a Xeric's talisman to put the Twisted bow on, and viewers reacted in chat that sending a raid with his wife may have been the key.[^tTn3JmSD4fI-2756]

## Trivia

* People liquidate their whole bank to buy a Twisted bow and then rebuild their bank afterwards.[^bIShDvVDueM-832]
