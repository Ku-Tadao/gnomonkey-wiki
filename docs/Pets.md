## Tips

- Gnomonkey suggests pets should stay next to the player at all times, since they often disappear after entering an instance and the player may not notice for half an hour or forget they had it out and lose it. ([2026-05-27 10:35](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=635))
- Gnomonkey suggests allowing pets in more places, such as underwater seaweed runs, perhaps with a fishbowl, and at Nex where the pet can be there when you get it but cannot be taken in again. ([2026-05-27 11:35](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=695))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says pets feel so awkward and annoying to use, with having to put them in the bank and take them out, that he often does not bother using a pet even with a cool rare drop. ([2026-05-27 12:02](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=722))
- Gnomonkey loves the pet change as it means much less messing around with the pet interface, though it is far from the full list of areas where a pet must be put away. ([2026-06-04 46:20](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2780))

## Events

- Pets are now allowed to follow the player into content like the Gauntlet, the Fossil Island underwater area and various minigames, and respawn with the player on death. ([2026-06-04 46:20](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=2780))
