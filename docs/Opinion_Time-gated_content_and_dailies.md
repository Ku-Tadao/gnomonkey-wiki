## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says that if he had his way there would be no time-gated content in OSRS: no daily herb boxes, no daily battlestaves, no daily charges on the Explorer's ring or Phosani's / Phalador shield, no daily spellbook swaps; instead items would be recharged or bought. ([2025-03-26 12:24](https://youtu.be/uDhGFEjrpyc?t=744))
- Gnomonkey says he comes from RS3 and has seen what dailies do to a game. ([2025-03-26 12:24](https://youtu.be/uDhGFEjrpyc?t=744))
- Gnomonkey says he will vote no to anything time-gated on hourly, daily, weekly or monthly timers, regardless of whether it is good, bad or dead content. ([2025-03-26 20:19](https://youtu.be/uDhGFEjrpyc?t=1219))
- Gnomonkey says time gating is what keeps him from playing WoW or any other MMO, which time-gate raids and add dailies to make you log in. ([2025-03-26 20:50](https://youtu.be/uDhGFEjrpyc?t=1250))
- Gnomonkey notes even FPS games such as Apex Legends, Overwatch and Escape from Tarkov use daily challenges and bonuses to inflate player numbers. ([2025-03-26 21:22](https://youtu.be/uDhGFEjrpyc?t=1282))
- Gnomonkey says OSRS avoids these pitfalls because it has no microtransactions and only very minor dailies, and nothing in OSRS makes him feel guilty for not logging in. ([2025-03-26 21:54](https://youtu.be/uDhGFEjrpyc?t=1314))
- Gnomonkey says he has over 30,000 hours in OSRS, more than 10 times his time in any other game, yet it respects his time more than any other game. ([2025-03-26 21:54](https://youtu.be/uDhGFEjrpyc?t=1314))
- Gnomonkey says OSRS lets you grind a boss as long as you like (for example 100,000 kill count) with no daily kill cap, and lets you switch to any skill when you are not enjoying something. ([2025-03-26 22:22](https://youtu.be/uDhGFEjrpyc?t=1342))
- Gnomonkey says the OSRS grind is impossibly long but fair because players have many options and nothing forces them to three-tick fish or 1.5-tick woodcut; they can AFK redwoods, anglerfish or Karambwans. ([2025-03-26 23:18](https://youtu.be/uDhGFEjrpyc?t=1398))
- Gnomonkey says he does not want any more hourly content added to the game. ([2025-03-26 24:00](https://youtu.be/uDhGFEjrpyc?t=1440))
