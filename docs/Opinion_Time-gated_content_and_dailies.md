**Opinion: Time-gated content and dailies** is an opinion topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2025-03-26. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says that if he had his way there would be no time-gated content in OSRS: no daily herb boxes, no daily battlestaves, no daily charges on the Explorer's ring or Phosani's / Phalador shield, no daily spellbook swaps; instead items would be recharged or bought.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-744]
* Gnomonkey says he comes from RS3 and has seen what dailies do to a game.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-744]
* Gnomonkey says he will vote no to anything time-gated on hourly, daily, weekly or monthly timers, regardless of whether it is good, bad or dead content.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-1219]
* Gnomonkey says time gating is what keeps him from playing WoW or any other MMO, which time-gate raids and add dailies to make you log in.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-1250]
* Gnomonkey notes even FPS games such as Apex Legends, Overwatch and Escape from Tarkov use daily challenges and bonuses to inflate player numbers.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-1282]
* Gnomonkey says OSRS avoids these pitfalls because it has no microtransactions and only very minor dailies, and nothing in OSRS makes him feel guilty for not logging in.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-1314]
* Gnomonkey says he has over 30,000 hours in OSRS, more than 10 times his time in any other game, yet it respects his time more than any other game.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-1314]
* Gnomonkey says OSRS lets you grind a boss as long as you like (for example 100,000 kill count) with no daily kill cap, and lets you switch to any skill when you are not enjoying something.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-1342]
* Gnomonkey says the OSRS grind is impossibly long but fair because players have many options and nothing forces them to three-tick fish or 1.5-tick woodcut; they can AFK redwoods, anglerfish or Karambwans.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-1398]
* Gnomonkey says he does not want any more hourly content added to the game.[^uDhGFEjrpyc-1440]
