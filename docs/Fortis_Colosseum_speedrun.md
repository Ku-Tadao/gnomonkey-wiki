# Fortis Colosseum speedrun

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2024-03-30 to 2024-04-01). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Gnomonkey uses claws to nuke the Serpent Mage for skips, and saves the claw special when he may need it to skip something. ([2024-03-30 0:00](https://youtu.be/4pRA7czMLlA))
- Gnomonkey's pre-pot includes a heart, and he sips a Menaphite remedy to break the divine effect so the heart ticks down; as long as he has over a plus-three boost, his three-way mage one-shots the Berserkers. ([2024-04-01 0:00](https://youtu.be/O_eC5OV3w1o))
- Gnomonkey starts some waves in melee armour, casts at the Berserker, then flicks on the Bulwark while praying mage, making him very tanky for the one off-prayer hit he takes, and on one wave takes no damage. ([2024-04-01 0:33](https://youtu.be/O_eC5OV3w1o?t=33))

## Stats and numbers

- Dragon warhammer saves 7 seconds each time on the boss and Gnomonkey says it is better than double clawing the boss. ([2024-03-30 0:00](https://youtu.be/4pRA7czMLlA))
- The Bulwark gives Gnomonkey a lot of survivability in the early waves and in a couple of cases is better DPS than a claw spec. ([2024-04-01 0:33](https://youtu.be/O_eC5OV3w1o?t=33))
- The 16:10 run had about 5:15 to wave 7 (Gnomonkey says 5:16 was the fastest by about 20 seconds), a 110-second wave 5, a wave 11 under two minutes (1:58, where it is usually around two and a half minutes), and a 14:12 entry to the boss. ([2024-04-01 20:27](https://youtu.be/O_eC5OV3w1o?t=1227))
- Gnomonkey lost roughly 10 seconds on the boss from missing a Dragon warhammer special, and says the boss, the only slow wave, could have been about 10 seconds faster with both hammers landing. ([2024-04-01 20:49](https://youtu.be/O_eC5OV3w1o?t=1249))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey notes that rich PvMers were also abusing the wave one to two cash-out tech at this time. ([2024-03-30 0:00](https://youtu.be/4pRA7czMLlA))
- Gnomonkey uses the Moonlight meat to help him on wave 6 early. ([2024-03-30 0:00](https://youtu.be/4pRA7czMLlA))
- The plugins Gnomonkey uses are Fortis Colosseum by LemonDuck, Better NPC Highlight with orb ID 12826, and a Line Marker plugin for his Sol tiles. ([2024-03-30 0:00](https://youtu.be/4pRA7czMLlA))
- The only change from his previous record setup is using a Bulwark instead of the Moonlight meat. ([2024-04-01 0:00](https://youtu.be/O_eC5OV3w1o))
- Gnomonkey uses the Blowpipe only for low HP targets to kill them quickly and save ticks. ([2024-04-01 4:37](https://youtu.be/O_eC5OV3w1o?t=277))
- You can regenerate run energy between waves; Gnomonkey took a pee break to get run energy back and called it tech. ([2024-04-01 12:28](https://youtu.be/O_eC5OV3w1o?t=748))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey thinks a sub-16 Colosseum run is possible, but it would have to include all the reinforcement skips as well as extremely good RNG with invocations and spawns. ([2024-03-30 0:00](https://youtu.be/4pRA7czMLlA))
- Gnomonkey stresses the run is not what he would do on a first cape and nothing in the video reflects that; it is for pure speed and no safety. ([2024-03-30 0:00](https://youtu.be/4pRA7czMLlA))
- Gnomonkey tried the Bulwark but says its line of sight issues need to be flushed out a lot more before it beats just clawing targets. ([2024-03-30 0:00](https://youtu.be/4pRA7czMLlA))
- Gnomonkey says the Bulwark was slightly better than a claw on one wave because it kills the archer but he took damage for it, and on another says it is barely better than a claw. ([2024-04-01 11:58](https://youtu.be/O_eC5OV3w1o?t=718))
- Gnomonkey says he does not think sub-15 is feasible for Colosseum without new knowledge or changes, and that around 15:30 is roughly the limit for how fast Colosseum can be done at this point. ([2024-04-01 20:20](https://youtu.be/O_eC5OV3w1o?t=1220))
- Gnomonkey says the run had incredible RNG for waves and damage and would be very difficult to beat; he thinks a sub-16 would require absurd luck, and that with a good boss and better play on one wave it could have been 15:50. ([2024-04-01 20:49](https://youtu.be/O_eC5OV3w1o?t=1249))
