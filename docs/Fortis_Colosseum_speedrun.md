**Fortis Colosseum speedrun** is an activity topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2024-03-30 and 2024-04-01. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Gnomonkey uses claws to nuke the Serpent Mage for skips, and saves the claw special when he may need it to skip something.[^4pRA7czMLlA-0]
* Gnomonkey's pre-pot includes a heart, and he sips a Menaphite remedy to break the divine effect so the heart ticks down; as long as he has over a plus-three boost, his three-way mage one-shots the Berserkers.[^O_eC5OV3w1o-0]
* Gnomonkey starts some waves in melee armour, casts at the Berserker, then flicks on the Bulwark while praying mage, making him very tanky for the one off-prayer hit he takes, and on one wave takes no damage.[^O_eC5OV3w1o-33]

## Stats and numbers

* Dragon warhammer saves 7 seconds each time on the boss and Gnomonkey says it is better than double clawing the boss.[^4pRA7czMLlA-0]
* The Bulwark gives Gnomonkey a lot of survivability in the early waves and in a couple of cases is better DPS than a claw spec.[^O_eC5OV3w1o-33]
* The 16:10 run had about 5:15 to wave 7 (Gnomonkey says 5:16 was the fastest by about 20 seconds), a 110-second wave 5, a wave 11 under two minutes (1:58, where it is usually around two and a half minutes), and a 14:12 entry to the boss.[^O_eC5OV3w1o-1227]
* Gnomonkey lost roughly 10 seconds on the boss from missing a Dragon warhammer special, and says the boss, the only slow wave, could have been about 10 seconds faster with both hammers landing.[^O_eC5OV3w1o-1249]

## Tips

* Rich PvMers were also abusing the wave one to two cash-out tech at this time.[^4pRA7czMLlA-0]
* Gnomonkey uses the Moonlight meat to help him on wave 6 early.[^4pRA7czMLlA-0]
* The plugins Gnomonkey uses are Fortis Colosseum by LemonDuck, Better NPC Highlight with orb ID 12826, and a Line Marker plugin for his Sol tiles.[^4pRA7czMLlA-0]
* The only change from his previous record setup is using a Bulwark instead of the Moonlight meat.[^O_eC5OV3w1o-0]
* Gnomonkey uses the Blowpipe only for low HP targets to kill them quickly and save ticks.[^O_eC5OV3w1o-277]
* You can regenerate run energy between waves; Gnomonkey took a pee break to get run energy back and called it tech.[^O_eC5OV3w1o-748]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey thinks a sub-16 Colosseum run is possible, but it would have to include all the reinforcement skips as well as extremely good RNG with invocations and spawns.[^4pRA7czMLlA-0]
* Gnomonkey stresses the run is not what he would do on a first cape and nothing in the video reflects that; it is for pure speed and no safety.[^4pRA7czMLlA-0]
* Gnomonkey tried the Bulwark but says its line of sight issues need to be flushed out a lot more before it beats just clawing targets.[^4pRA7czMLlA-0]
* Gnomonkey says the Bulwark was slightly better than a claw on one wave because it kills the archer but he took damage for it, and on another says it is barely better than a claw.[^O_eC5OV3w1o-718]
* Gnomonkey says he does not think sub-15 is feasible for Colosseum without new knowledge or changes, and that around 15:30 is roughly the limit for how fast Colosseum can be done at this point.[^O_eC5OV3w1o-1220]
* Gnomonkey says the run had incredible RNG for waves and damage and would be very difficult to beat; he thinks a sub-16 would require absurd luck, and that with a good boss and better play on one wave it could have been 15:50.[^O_eC5OV3w1o-1249]

## Records

* Gnomonkey obtained the Fortis Colosseum world record again with a time of 17:32, after the record had been passing through many hands over the previous week, and hopes to hold it for a while.[^4pRA7czMLlA-0]
* His 5:37 time at the end of wave six is record pace to wave seven.[^4pRA7czMLlA-397]
* Gnomonkey estimates the run will reach the boss at roughly 15:30, which he calls absolute record pace, and later the pace shows 15:12 at the boss.[^4pRA7czMLlA-968]
* Gnomonkey got a 16:10 Fortis Colosseum world record, over a minute faster than his own previous record, which had been beaten by Ausah, and it happened in only the first few hours of the stream.[^O_eC5OV3w1o-0]

## Events

* He took a deal with Kick and streams for a couple of hours on Twitch and YouTube before moving to Kick, which is why he has been streaming a lot of hours.[^O_eC5OV3w1o-1280]
