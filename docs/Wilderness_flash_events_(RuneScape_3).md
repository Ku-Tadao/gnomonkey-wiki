**Wilderness flash events (RuneScape 3)** is a mechanic topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2023-10-26 and 2025-09-19. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Stats and numbers

* Wilderness flash events in RS3 occur every hour, such as a shooting star, an evil tree or a herd of spiders, and take about five minutes for a sack reward.[^wRHsOi8plqM-212]
* Most wilderness flash events give one normal wilderness sack, containing useful items like prayer ashes, bloodweed seeds, energies and alkabulls, and Gnomonkey says you can pull around 250k coins out of them.[^wRHsOi8plqM-212]
* Only three flash events can give a very wild sack and two of them require combat, so Evil Tree is the only way Gnomonkey can get one at his level.[^wRHsOi8plqM-245]
* A very wild sack can contain tier 80 plus weapons, which Gnomonkey says are roughly equivalent to a twisted bow, upgrades to skilling items and rings, plus more alkabulls, ashes and seeds.[^wRHsOi8plqM-245]
* RS3 Wilderness flash events happen every hour on the hour, and Gnomonkey says they are incredibly rewarding.[^bHKYk5VY_oI-656]
* The Unnatural outcrop event near the Mage of Zamorak asks players to mine 25 ores with level 1 Mining and hand them in, after which they receive sacks of wild rewards after five minutes.[^bHKYk5VY_oI-682]
* Wild sack rewards include 10 to 20 black salamanders, 40 to 60 red chinchompas, 1 to 2 bloodweed seeds, 20 to 40 infernal ashes, and cash amounts of 20-40k, 48-100k and 73k.[^bHKYk5VY_oI-714]
* At 70 Mining the Unnatural outcrop event gives about 22k Mining XP for five minutes of work, plus around 100k cash and maybe 30k prayer XP.[^bHKYk5VY_oI-746]
* The evil tree event gives three lots of XP, three baby sacks and one very special wild sack with even more loot; Gnomonkey says a chat viewer set a midnight alarm so as not to miss it.[^bHKYk5VY_oI-810]
* XP for flash events was scaled for Leagues, so missing 20k Mining was like missing 200k.[^bHKYk5VY_oI-958]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the Evil Tree is the longest and best flash event, giving three normal sacks and then a very wild sack at the end.[^wRHsOi8plqM-212]
* Gnomonkey says flash events are super important especially for an early ironman, since they also give woodcutting, farming and firemaking XP.[^wRHsOi8plqM-277]
