# Tekton

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 7 videos (2022-03-19 to 2025-05-01). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Methods

- Gnomonkey runs into the raid on a specific tick to get Tekton off the anvil as fast as possible. ([2023-02-02 2:30](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=150))
- He casts Vulnerability at Tekton as he moves toward him, draining his defence by 10%, then hammers twice and uses a specific attack pattern to get the maximum hits before he returns to the anvil, repeating when he leaves the anvil. ([2023-02-02 2:58](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=178))
- Gnomonkey runs up next to Tekton to get him off his anvil, runs to the back of the room, hammers twice, then crushes him with his hasta while running around him. ([2024-10-27 8:38](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=518))
- Gnomonkey kills CM Tekton with the Osmumten's fang, saying it is almost the same as a Scythe there because Tekton has a ridiculous amount of health and defence; he does two hammers into a fang hit for a better cycle. ([2024-11-08 8:35](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=515))
- To avoid Tekton, get him to swing at you and, right before he swings again, swap to the other side of him. ([2025-05-01 4:55](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=295))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey figures around 10 to 20 minutes of Tekton resets before you actually get a run going. ([2023-02-02 2:30](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=150))
- Gnomonkey notes hits of 46 for 3 hits and 46 for 2 hits on Tekton as a good result. ([2023-06-14 1:57](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=117))
- Gnomonkey says Tekton's max hit in CM is 87, so a Vengeance cast on you from an alt can give a lot of value. ([2024-11-08 8:15](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=495))
- Gnomonkey says Tekton has 246 defence and that a Dragon warhammer special drains one third of current defence, so hitting a hammer is very important for Tekton. ([2024-11-08 1:10:16](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=4216))
- Gnomonkey says Tekton swings every three ticks and his animation is not accurate, so he is always one cycle behind where he looks. ([2025-05-01 4:29](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=269))

## Tips

- In a Challenge Mode speedrun you go for a one anvil Tekton, which Gnomonkey has heard is a 1 in 4 chance on two landed hammers but requires a lot of resetting. ([2023-02-02 2:30](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=150))
- In the crab room after Tekton, waiting a tick before the loading zone at the crystal is huge for making the crabs behave the same each time; after that it is following an exact set of attacks. ([2023-02-02 3:30](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=210))
- Gnomonkey tanks Avenge on Tekton, so he spams as many sweets as possible in the crab room to recover HP. ([2023-02-02 3:30](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=210))
- Gnomonkey says he stays on Tekton for anything under 250 and that doing the hits in an exact order lets everything fit perfectly. ([2023-06-14 1:28](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=88))
- At Tekton, Gnomonkey says you make him swing twice on each side. ([2023-06-14 1:28](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=88))
- Gnomonkey says if the crystal despawns late, you can wait a tick outside the crystal to get the right crab spawn every single time. ([2023-06-14 2:27](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=147))
- Gnomonkey eats a sweet to delay his Twisted bow shot so it attacks at the right time, otherwise it can unlight the crab, then does hammer, cast, hammer, cast. ([2023-06-14 2:27](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=147))
- Gnomonkey says the south pillar falling on wave 50 plus is not ideal but not a big deal; the only pillar you care about is the north one. ([2023-06-14 2:30](https://youtu.be/8JxarzSiiME?t=150))
- Gnomonkey says he wanted to mace spec Tekton, and was unsure whether he could kill Tekton with this gear at all. ([2023-10-09 4:30](https://youtu.be/N7UM0bqdcU4?t=270))
- Gnomonkey explains Tekton's attacks are not where they look like they are, since he is always one cycle behind; he baits out a second attack on the side that hits him and then runs around Tekton, and says to pre-melee because he hits hard. ([2024-10-27 9:09](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=549))
- Gnomonkey says Tekton's room shortcut can be a rock, a tree or a boulder you push; clearing it gives a few points and is a bit faster. ([2024-10-27 10:09](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=609))
- Gnomonkey enters Tekton with the Cox Editions timer over his head so Tekton immediately leaves the anvil and comes to him, and notes the first hammer hit is guaranteed after a recent update but the second is not. ([2024-11-08 8:15](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=495))
- If you miss the second hammer on Tekton, Gnomonkey says to just leave the raid, since you can get back in fairly quickly. ([2024-11-08 8:15](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=495))
- When Tekton is orange (enraged phase) he is much tankier and harder to hit, which is why the fang's ability to cut through defence is so good. ([2024-11-08 9:45](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=585))
- Gnomonkey usually resets until he gets two hammer hits, but says it is unnecessary at this scale, and the first hammer is guaranteed to hit. ([2025-05-01 3:56](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=236))
- Gnomonkey says to summon a melee thrall, the only thrall that works against Tekton. ([2025-05-01 4:29](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=269))
- If you leave Tekton's range when he launches an attack, he walks back early, so always stay in range and do not stand on his corners, which counts the same as not being next to him. ([2025-05-01 4:55](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=295))
- Gnomonkey says to pray melee when learning Tekton. ([2025-05-01 5:29](https://youtu.be/jdvQVwg8niM?t=329))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says Tekton is a high-defence boss that is fun if you hit hammers and miserable if you do not, with a challenge mode tech involving landing vulnerability and luring him over, and dislikes that he regenerates defence on the way to the anvil. ([2022-03-19 43:49](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2629))
- Gnomonkey rates Tekton lower B tier and notes a last hit with a harder-hitting weapon like a Scythe instead of a mace as he walks to the anvil. ([2022-03-19 44:16](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2656))
- Gnomonkey calls Tekton effectively the lobby before the raid, where all the Tekton resetting happens. ([2023-02-02 2:58](https://youtu.be/F0qIr7azCwg?t=178))
- Gnomonkey says that without a hammer Tekton could take upwards of six anvils, and that this is the biggest time loss in the raid, which is why he says to grind the hammer first. ([2024-10-27 10:09](https://youtu.be/wxGBi89vE_s?t=609))

## Records

- A viewer said they killed Tekton in seven to eight minutes with a rune scimitar in a hundred-K gear challenge, and Gnomonkey says his dragon mace kill was faster, noting a Dragon Defender and other boosting jewellery help. ([2023-10-09 8:22](https://youtu.be/N7UM0bqdcU4?t=502))
- Gnomonkey killed Tekton with a dragon mace in 8 minutes 21 seconds during the clue scroll run. ([2023-10-09 14:29](https://youtu.be/N7UM0bqdcU4?t=869))

## Events

- Tekton is the first boss in Challenge Mode and always comes first; he drops overload, prayer enhance and restore. ([2024-11-08 2:29](https://youtu.be/2r5Wlx2BiI0?t=149))

## Trivia

- Gnomonkey says at Tekton you can either cycle his hits every third tick or run around him in a square, which breaks his animation so he looks like he is hitting you but is not. ([2022-03-19 43:18](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=2598))
