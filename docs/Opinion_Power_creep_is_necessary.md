## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says, as a former RuneScape 3 player who has seen extreme power creep, that power creep is less of a problem than the Old School community makes it out to be. ([2022-05-25 0:00](https://youtu.be/SO4k9z1A8-0))
- Gnomonkey says any update that adds something devalues something else: the blowpipe devalued crossbows, Twisted bow further devalued the blowpipe, and even Guardians of the Rift removing the delay after crafting essence or shift-click dropping counts. ([2022-05-25 0:26](https://youtu.be/SO4k9z1A8-0?t=26))
- Gnomonkey says power creep is a problem because it devalues content: old bosses become easier and items become irrelevant to grind for economically. ([2022-05-25 0:55](https://youtu.be/SO4k9z1A8-0?t=55))
- Gnomonkey argues that every update ever added was power creep in some way, so there is no solution but to either stop adding updates and let everything decay, or accept a small amount of power creep while curbing it in various ways as other MMOs do. ([2022-05-25 1:53](https://youtu.be/SO4k9z1A8-0?t=113))
- Gnomonkey says a small amount of power creep is not only inevitable but necessary to keep a game fun and interesting, as long as it is maintained with checks and balances; without a Twisted bow in Chambers or a Scythe in Theatre of Blood those raids would be much less fun. ([2022-05-25 7:10](https://youtu.be/SO4k9z1A8-0?t=430))
- Gnomonkey says the OSRS team recently decided it is OK to start adding actual best-in-slot items like Torva and Masori armour; he says when the team struggles to find enticing rewards comparable to existing ones, that weapon or armour tier is fully fleshed out and it is time to move on. ([2022-05-25 7:42](https://youtu.be/SO4k9z1A8-0?t=462))
- Gnomonkey says raids need solid rewards to be interesting and engaging, and that gear choices (how many switches you can afford, and the impact of each) keep raids engaging; e.g. one challenge mode player might bring Torva for BGS specs while others use Inquisitor's to land hammer specs. ([2022-05-25 7:42](https://youtu.be/SO4k9z1A8-0?t=462))

## Trivia

- Gnomonkey defines power creep (reading from Google) as the gradual unbalancing of a game due to successive releases of new content leaving older content underpowered; the community commonly calls it devaluing. ([2022-05-25 0:26](https://youtu.be/SO4k9z1A8-0?t=26))
