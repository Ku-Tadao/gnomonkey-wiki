# Varlamore: The Final Dawn

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2025-01-09 to 2025-01-14). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Stats and numbers

- The new weapon from the quest is a one-handed melee weapon similar to the Dragon scimitar with decent slash and stab, slightly worse strength, one Prayer bonus and a four-tick attack speed. ([2025-01-09 0:27](https://youtu.be/4YGVut4hMtA?t=27))
- The new weapon's special attack is a 50% spec that performs a single attack with 50% increased accuracy and 50% increased damage and guarantees one stack of burn on a successful hit. ([2025-01-09 0:27](https://youtu.be/4YGVut4hMtA?t=27))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey considers the new burn weapon a decent mid-level spec option until Burning claws, and thinks the Dragon dagger-style weapon might still beat it depending on the target's defence. ([2025-01-09 0:57](https://youtu.be/4YGVut4hMtA?t=57))
- Gnomonkey sees the new melee weapon as a competitor to a de-skim-type weapon (as he describes it) and a decent mid-game or earlier special attack weapon that will be solid until you get something like Burning claws, comparing it to how the Dragon dagger is good in some places. ([2025-01-14 10:14](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=614))

## Events

- The Varlamore: Final Dawn update includes a new quest (like Tombs of Amascut has) that unlocks a new weapon and the Doom of Mokhaiotl encounter. ([2025-01-09 0:27](https://youtu.be/4YGVut4hMtA?t=27))
- Jagex said the new melee weapon from the Final Dawn quest will only work as a slash weapon, so a requested stab weapon is something it is open to working on in the future. ([2025-01-14 9:45](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=585))
- Jagex said that as the Varlamore arc closes it plans to bring more burn effects to the game, and it is removing the area-of-effect portion of the new weapon's special attack. ([2025-01-14 10:14](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=614))
- Jagex reduced the special attack cost of the new Final Dawn melee weapon. ([2025-01-14 10:14](https://youtu.be/kd6oHdt4BB0?t=614))
