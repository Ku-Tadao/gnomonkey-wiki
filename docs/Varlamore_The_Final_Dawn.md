**Varlamore: The Final Dawn** is a quest topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2025-01-09 and 2025-01-14. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* The new weapon from the quest is a one-handed melee weapon similar to the Dragon scimitar with decent slash and stab, slightly worse strength, one Prayer bonus and a four-tick attack speed.[^4YGVut4hMtA-27]
* The new weapon's special attack is a 50% spec that performs a single attack with 50% increased accuracy and 50% increased damage and guarantees one stack of burn on a successful hit.[^4YGVut4hMtA-27]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey considers the new burn weapon a decent mid-level spec option until Burning claws, and thinks the Dragon dagger-style weapon might still beat it depending on the target's defence.[^4YGVut4hMtA-57]
* Gnomonkey sees the new melee weapon as a competitor to a de-skim-type weapon (as he describes it) and a decent mid-game or earlier special attack weapon that will be solid until you get something like Burning claws, comparing it to how the Dragon dagger is good in some places.[^kd6oHdt4BB0-614]

## Events

* The Varlamore: Final Dawn update includes a new quest (like Tombs of Amascut has) that unlocks a new weapon and the Doom of Mokhaiotl encounter.[^4YGVut4hMtA-27]
* Jagex said the new melee weapon from the Final Dawn quest will only work as a slash weapon, so a requested stab weapon is something it is open to working on in the future.[^kd6oHdt4BB0-585]
* Jagex said that as the Varlamore arc closes it plans to bring more burn effects to the game, and it is removing the area-of-effect portion of the new weapon's special attack.[^kd6oHdt4BB0-614]
* Jagex reduced the special attack cost of the new Final Dawn melee weapon.[^kd6oHdt4BB0-614]
