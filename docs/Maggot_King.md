**Maggot King** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 4 videos between 2026-01-25 and 2026-07-22. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* To reach the Maggot King, teleport with a Draken's medallion to outside Castle Draken, run east and cross an agility shortcut, and the boss is nearby.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-26]
* A faster route to the Maggot King: go past the boss to find a man being hit by mosquitoes, save him by killing the mosquitoes and talk to him; he reappears elsewhere and takes you via a transport to the shortcut leading into the boss.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-56]
* On entering the Maggot King arena, pray Protect from Missiles and Augury, then run to the left side of the arena (the right side also works) to put all the boss's rag poison in one corner.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-231]
* The Maggot King's ranged attack is poison that hits for 8, so sip an antidote; when it screeches you turn your protection prayer off, and after each screech you swap between Protect from Missiles and Protect from Magic.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-259]
* Each Maggot King screech launches carrion on the ground, which a grub eats while moving back and forth, healing the boss one per bite; roughly one maggot is shot out per screech.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-319]
* At 66% health the Maggot King begins a melee phase where it comes down off the ramp so you can melee it, and from then on it comes down to melee after each screech.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-349]
* In the Maggot King melee phase it uses a thrust melee attack that can be dodged by stepping out of the way, and it shoots out small bits of acid; you can punish the attack by clicking right as it pops out.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-376]
* Below 20% health the Maggot King is permanently in melee and never stops meleeing, so you just spam your attacks to finish the kill.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-502]

## Stats and numbers

* Maggot King rewards include the Necklace of Pursuit, an upgrade to the Necklace of anguish, and the Crimson bludgeon.[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-725]
* Gnomonkey's best Maggot King kill time with his budget setup is 2:30 (he says '230'), and he says an average of about three minutes is achievable.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-88]
* Gnomonkey reports two and a half to three minute Maggot King kills with his budget setup despite not-great gear.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-173]
* The Maggot King's screech drops rocks that do minor damage, and the acid it spits is mage-based, so Gnomonkey turns on Protect from Magic after the screech.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-259]
* Acid on the floor at the Maggot King hurts a little and drains melee stats, while poison on the floor only hits a little and does nothing more if you are immune to poison, so Gnomonkey says nothing at the boss is super punishing.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-292]
* The only thing that hits hard at the Maggot King is its melee attacks, which hit about 50; Gnomonkey says those are what will kill you.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-442]
* With a Zombie axe, a five-tick weapon, Gnomonkey's Maggot King kills run about two and a half to three minutes once the fight is understood.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-534]

## Tips

* Gnomonkey found it better to leave the Maggot King's maggots alone rather than kill them, though he says there is a trick to one-shot them that he does not cover in this video.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-319]
* In the Maggot King melee phase the maggot attack ignores your attack cooldown, so you can step aside, attack, and immediately swing again for a double hit.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-409]
* Gnomonkey puts all the acid on one side of the room, then when the Maggot King comes down to melee he slowly walks it to the other side of the room.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-442]
* You can hit the Maggot King's maggot neck from just about anywhere in the room as long as you are next to it, which gives many options for dodging acid.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-473]
* You can make the Maggot King attack faster, so if you are using a faster weapon such as an Ink mace or a bludgeon you would want to do that.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-502]
* The Maggot King five kills in nine minutes combat achievement, a little under two minutes per kill, is definitely doable and could probably be done with the Soulreaper axe.[^EPlArwCehYY-880]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Jagex says the Maggot King suits players eyeing the Inferno or in the middle of their Fasani grind, and Gnomonkey reads this as slightly harder than Yama, which he is glad about.[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-725]
* Gnomonkey loves how disgusting the Maggot King looks, thinks the animations and art design are great, and says it reminds him of a Bloodborne boss.[^O4np4ssz6iw-215]
* In the five seconds of Maggot King footage, Gnomonkey notices two types of acid on the ground, a green poison and a yellow acid, and speculates one may venom the player and the other act like the Griffin-style shield acid.[^O4np4ssz6iw-247]
* Gnomonkey thinks the Maggot King, which stands raised on a cliff while the player shoots it at distance among acid and attacks little maggot minions, gives him Doom of Mokhaiotl vibes.[^O4np4ssz6iw-278]
* Gnomonkey notes the Maggot King has a thrust attack that hits an area, like Sol Heredit, and wonders about diagonal variations.[^O4np4ssz6iw-278]
* Gnomonkey calls his Maggot King guide a day-one guide for the new boss from Blood Moon Rises: nothing is known for certain, things are hard to calculate, and everything he says could be wrong or change.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-0]
* Gnomonkey admits this guide is sloppy with no numbers, a 'mouthfeel' guide, because DPS calcs cannot currently handle the boss, and he promises a more fleshed-out guide in a few days.[^0iIPIZTfsBU-619]
* Gnomonkey strongly appreciates the decision not to add a Grandmaster combat achievement when no good idea exists, saying he has long asked Jagex to cut down on the bulk of combat achievements; the only idea he had was a very fast speed time, which would just require killing without dying.[^EPlArwCehYY-757]
* Gnomonkey considers the Maggot King's nine combat achievements reasonable and far from the worst batch he has seen, noting there did not need to be 20 to 30 of them.[^EPlArwCehYY-880]

## Records

* The Maggot King's combat achievements: hard is simply Kill the Maggot King; elites are kill 25 times, kill using only weapons found in Vampyrium, kill without moving more than 40 tiles, and kill without moving beyond the back row of tiles; further tasks listed include kill five times in nine minutes, kill 12 times without leaving the arena, kill without missing a single melee punish, and a perfect kill (no avoidable damage and never healed), plus a perfect kill 12 times in a row without leaving.[^EPlArwCehYY-828]

## Events

* The Maggot King is the new boss from Blood Moon Rises, where survival relies on quick repositioning, reacting to environmental threats and adapting as the arena shifts and hazards spread.[^O4np4ssz6iw-215]
* Jagex said the Maggot King is planned to be similar in difficulty to Phosani's Nightmare, so it will not be easy.[^O4np4ssz6iw-305]
* Jagex chose not to add any Grandmaster combat achievements to the Maggot King, saying it could not find a task that added challenge without feeling contrived.[^EPlArwCehYY-757]

## Trivia

* The Maggot King is the new solo repeatable boss: a parasite on the corpse of a venator, related to Zarpus' race, with a huge maggot sticking up.[^h-RfJ1kx2pU-691]
