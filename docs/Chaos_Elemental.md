**Chaos Elemental** is a boss topic that Gnomonkey covers in 3 videos between 2022-03-12 and 2022-07-13. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* The Chaos Elemental is an actual (very chaotic) fight using random attacks in random styles, can teleport you and can remove your items; it is located just above level 50 Wilderness in multi.[^uuJsdK3_KRQ-263]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the Chaos Elemental pet is literally the most commonly obtained pet in the game and nearly the fastest grind at about five hours, and puts it in F tier because it looks like a fart cloud with tentacles.[^vwCm9NKJhRE-305]
* Gnomonkey rates the Chaos Elemental low C tier and likes that it is a fast Wilderness boss with a high pet drop rate, saying he hates Wilderness pets in general and wishes more followed that model.[^HkVt4enEesI-1559]
* Gnomonkey says for Scorpia and Chaos Elemental the pet drops are the only incentive, which he truly despises.[^uuJsdK3_KRQ-454]

## Trivia

* The Chaos Elemental uses purely random attack styles, protecting from magic prevents the most damage, it teleports you, and its item takeoff attack is the most interesting part because leaving one inventory slot open lets it use a free attack.[^HkVt4enEesI-1496]
