**Trinitas** is an other topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2023-03-07 and 2023-04-19. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Stats and numbers

* Trinitas gives a 15% accuracy and strength boost to all combat styles, and accuracy only for magic.[^trol59VccdE-216]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey considers Trinitas the only poorly designed prayer on the whole prayer book: it is meant to ease people into PvM by removing the need to switch offensive prayers, which goes against the book's theme.[^trol59VccdE-216]
* Gnomonkey says Trinitas is a crutch encouraging players not to learn a basic skill important in all PvM encounters, and he would vote no on it, though few people would use it.[^trol59VccdE-247]
* Gnomonkey voted no to Trinitas when it was proposed, says it is still as dumb as it was then, and wants it scrapped as a low-skill prayer on the high-skill prayer book.[^8VyPkG4sEwQ-214]
