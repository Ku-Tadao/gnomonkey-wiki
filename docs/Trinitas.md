# Trinitas

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2023-03-07 to 2023-04-19). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

## Stats and numbers

- Trinitas gives a 15% accuracy and strength boost to all combat styles, and accuracy only for magic. ([2023-03-07 3:36](https://youtu.be/trol59VccdE?t=216))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey considers Trinitas the only poorly designed prayer on the whole prayer book: it is meant to ease people into PvM by removing the need to switch offensive prayers, which goes against the book's theme. ([2023-03-07 3:36](https://youtu.be/trol59VccdE?t=216))
- Gnomonkey says Trinitas is a crutch encouraging players not to learn a basic skill important in all PvM encounters, and he would vote no on it, though few people would use it. ([2023-03-07 4:07](https://youtu.be/trol59VccdE?t=247))
- Gnomonkey voted no to Trinitas when it was proposed, says it is still as dumb as it was then, and wants it scrapped as a low-skill prayer on the high-skill prayer book. ([2023-04-19 3:34](https://youtu.be/8VyPkG4sEwQ?t=214))
