# Butterfly Tech

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 3 videos (2022-08-30 to 2025-06-25). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

|---|---|
| **Type** | mechanic |
| **Videos** | 3 (first 2022-08-30, latest 2025-06-25) |

## Methods

- Gnomonkey's preparation for the method is to creep out of the stamina boost, activate Augury, take salts, summon a maze troll if available, and start the ring; starting from the north or south quadrant makes no difference. ([2022-08-30 2:53](https://youtu.be/pSBxLCy7-44?t=173))
- Stand still attacking Akkha until he crosses the attack tile, then run to the furthest quadrant's attack tile; starting the cycle correctly is the hardest part and takes practice. ([2022-08-30 3:20](https://youtu.be/pSBxLCy7-44?t=200))
- To start the cycle, look at which side of the quadrant Akkha is on and start on the opposite side, attack Akkha until his tile is one tile away, then click to start the cycle and follow the red tiles around Akkha, attacking him from each attack tile. ([2022-08-30 3:53](https://youtu.be/pSBxLCy7-44?t=233))
- At 80% HP when the shadows spawn, attack Akkha again but hold shift when clicking the run tiles so you can walk through the shadow, attack the shadow from the attack tiles, and once the cast killed it click Akkha again from the attack tiles. ([2022-08-30 4:21](https://youtu.be/pSBxLCy7-44?t=261))
- Each time Akkha is nearly down another 20%, finish the phase with a cast and move to the next quadrant, and Gnomonkey says it is very important to run vertically to the next quadrant or Akkha will not drag properly, since going horizontally causes many problems. ([2022-08-30 4:50](https://youtu.be/pSBxLCy7-44?t=290))
- In Gnomonkey's sequence he runs vertically to the next quadrant first, then diagonally to the other quadrant, then vertically again for the final 20% of Akkha's HP. ([2022-08-30 4:50](https://youtu.be/pSBxLCy7-44?t=290))
- Gnomonkey marks tiles with the external plugin Tile Packs: search 'butterfly' and use the four-tick tiles, not the normal butterfly tiles. ([2025-06-25 2:04](https://youtu.be/6Qmuhnpa0gc?t=124))

## Requirements

- Butterfly Tech is a 5-tick attack cycle, so it is only really good with the new Tumeken's shadow, although it can be done with a Sanguinesti staff or Trident while losing a tick each attack. ([2022-08-30 0:28](https://youtu.be/pSBxLCy7-44?t=28))
- Butterfly Tech requires at least one dose of stamina potion, which must be brought into the raid. ([2022-08-30 0:28](https://youtu.be/pSBxLCy7-44?t=28))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says at most raid levels you will kill Akkha before your run energy runs out if you drank one stamina dose. ([2022-08-30 6:24](https://youtu.be/pSBxLCy7-44?t=384))

## Tips

- Gnomonkey says you could even manually cast Ice Barrage on each attack, but it would be very hard to click Barrage and then Akkha after pathing to the attack tile on a single game tick. ([2022-08-30 0:28](https://youtu.be/pSBxLCy7-44?t=28))
- Gnomonkey provides tile markers via a pastebin link in the description; copy them to the clipboard and right-click the world map and choose import to load them all into RuneLite. ([2022-08-30 1:56](https://youtu.be/pSBxLCy7-44?t=116))
- Gnomonkey says to enable Shift Click Walk Here in the Menu Entry Swapper plugin. ([2022-08-30 2:23](https://youtu.be/pSBxLCy7-44?t=143))
- To show Akkha's true tile, use NPC Indicators or copy Gnomonkey's Akkha radius markers; they also show which style Akkha is attacking with. ([2022-08-30 2:23](https://youtu.be/pSBxLCy7-44?t=143))
- Gnomonkey adds the line 'attack Akkha's Shadow *' in the Custom Swapper plugin so that Attack is prioritized on the shadows and not Akkha. ([2022-08-30 2:23](https://youtu.be/pSBxLCy7-44?t=143))
- Gnomonkey adds Akkha to the monster HP percentage plugin, which shows a green percent over Akkha so he knows when Akkha will phase each 20% HP at any raid level. ([2022-08-30 2:53](https://youtu.be/pSBxLCy7-44?t=173))
- If Akkha paths to you directly from his center tile, the method is impossible until he hits you, so Gnomonkey says to tank the hit and stand still for a couple of ticks before starting the cycle. ([2022-08-30 5:56](https://youtu.be/pSBxLCy7-44?t=356))
- Gnomonkey says it is extremely hard to take other actions such as drinking potions during the cycle, so use the time transitioning between quadrants to drink stamina doses or heal. ([2022-08-30 5:56](https://youtu.be/pSBxLCy7-44?t=356))
- In teams, if Akkha passes over a teammate he will melee them while still going towards the tank, so Gnomonkey says to communicate and stand in one of the quadrant corners out of Akkha's way. ([2022-08-30 6:24](https://youtu.be/pSBxLCy7-44?t=384))
- Gnomonkey sets up colour-coded radius markers for Akkha's three attack styles (type !akkha color in his Twitch chat to see his), which doubles as a true tile indicator; otherwise add Akkha in NPC indicators and highlight true tile and the southwest true tile. ([2025-06-25 2:28](https://youtu.be/6Qmuhnpa0gc?t=148))
- Add a custom menu swap that prioritises Akkha's shadow for left-click attack, since Akkha walks over the shadow and you do not want to click the invincible Akkha; the custom menu swap text is 'attack,akkha's shadow*' as in the description. ([2025-06-25 4:55](https://youtu.be/6Qmuhnpa0gc?t=295))
- Enable shift-click walk under in menu entry swapper to walk under the shadow without clicking it, and add the Monster HP percentage plugin with Akkha's name to see his health over his body to know when to switch to the shadow. ([2025-06-25 5:20](https://youtu.be/6Qmuhnpa0gc?t=320))
- Gnomonkey brings a ranging potion and stamina in his demonstration; with salts you get unlimited run energy so stamina is unnecessary. ([2025-06-25 6:48](https://youtu.be/6Qmuhnpa0gc?t=408))
- In the video's demonstration the Tombs of Amascut plugin also shows the shadows' HP so you can see whether you will beat the DPS check. ([2025-06-25 20:15](https://youtu.be/6Qmuhnpa0gc?t=1215))
- Gnomonkey provides the butterfly tiles through a Pastebin import (right-click the world map, import), plus colour-coded radius markers via !akkha color in his Twitch chat; the radius markers double as the true tile indicator. ([2025-06-25 3:55](https://youtu.be/7ZyZC0OcvsU?t=235))
- Without the colour markers, add Akkha in NPC indicators with True Tile and Southwest True Tile highlights. ([2025-06-25 4:27](https://youtu.be/7ZyZC0OcvsU?t=267))
- The Tombs of Amascut plugin has a toggle to show the shadows' health, telling you whether you need a DPS skip, and the Monster HP percentage plugin set to Akkha shows his health over his chest; every 20% health he loses spawns shadows. ([2025-06-25 5:01](https://youtu.be/7ZyZC0OcvsU?t=301))
- Because the Atlatl has six tile range you can keep hitting Akkha while Akkha does Simon Says (five rounds at his raid level 2-3), and Gnomonkey notes you do not always need to be on a specific tile. ([2025-06-25 6:12](https://youtu.be/7ZyZC0OcvsU?t=372))
