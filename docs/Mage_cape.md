**Mage cape** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2025-01-12. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Stats and numbers

* The new Mage cape, an upgrade of the Mage Arena 2 cape, has 1% extra magic damage, +5 magic accuracy, lower defence bonuses than the Mage Arena 2 cape (he cites about 15 magic defence) and a -2 prayer bonus.[^rUFbUjrcJZA-774]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says the new Mage cape feels lame and weak because only Tumeken's shadow benefits much from its 1% magic damage, and even there his calcs show at best about a 5% DPS increase.[^rUFbUjrcJZA-774]
* Gnomonkey compares the new Mage cape to the Venator ring: it rarely changes a max hit and mostly just sits in your inventory, especially if you do not own a Tumeken's shadow.[^rUFbUjrcJZA-808]
* Gnomonkey does not think the new Mage cape should require the Mage Arena 2 cape, comparing the quiver (obtainable without Desert Treasure 2 or Dragon Slayer 2) and the Infernal cape, which you simply receive as a full cape; he would be fine with a harder or more expensive alternative route and thinks it should just drop as a full cape.[^rUFbUjrcJZA-894]
* A Reddit post with about 2,000 upvotes criticised the new Mage cape's -2 prayer bonus and its tradability, noting the quiver and Infernal cape are two BIS capes from effortful content; Gnomonkey agrees the cape feels weak and like a slap in the face.[^rUFbUjrcJZA-950]
* Gnomonkey thinks the new Mage cape should have no prayer penalty (+0 prayer) since 1% magic damage and +5 accuracy are enough to go for the cape, and would argue the bonus should be higher, such as 2% or 3%.[^rUFbUjrcJZA-981]
* Gnomonkey does not think there is prestige in the Mage Arena 2 cape, arguing it only requires killing three fairly basic Wilderness bosses once each, unlike the quiver or the Inferno cape.[^rUFbUjrcJZA-1162]
* Gnomonkey would be okay with having to clear cave eight of the Doom of Mokhaiotl once to equip the new Mage cape as a compromise, but thinks players should still be allowed to buy it and that it should be a rare drop worth grinding endless for.[^rUFbUjrcJZA-1309]
* Gnomonkey rejects the community request to give the new Mage cape a book of the dead slot, arguing that thralls are not tied to magic since you can use them with any combat style.[^rUFbUjrcJZA-1576]
* Gnomonkey's closing wish is that Jagex makes the new Mage cape a little stronger while keeping it tradable.[^rUFbUjrcJZA-2041]
