## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey made roughly 550 air orbs an hour in the video; at 550 orbs an hour the method earns 330,000 gp an hour. ([2018-11-22 1:34](https://youtu.be/cBBPBxq6KxI?t=94))
- As of 2018, Gnomonkey says the next best money-making Crafting method is gold bracelets at 130,000 gp an hour. ([2018-11-22 1:34](https://youtu.be/cBBPBxq6KxI?t=94))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey presents blowing and charging air orbs as the new most profitable way to train Crafting. ([2018-11-22 0:00](https://youtu.be/cBBPBxq6KxI))
- Gnomonkey notes gold bracelets also give less Crafting XP than the air orb method, which additionally grants bonus Magic XP. ([2018-11-22 2:07](https://youtu.be/cBBPBxq6KxI?t=127))

## Events

- Gnomonkey finds he cannot string bowstrings on an account this low in level and fears there may be no way to train Crafting on the account. ([2019-07-24 4:52](https://youtu.be/O6INgH6Adw0?t=292))
- While theorycrafting Crafting methods Gnomonkey finds he cannot get wool, cook pottery or blow glass, and has no way to get molten glass; he hopes Gnome Restaurant's opal drop is the key. ([2019-07-24 8:43](https://youtu.be/O6INgH6Adw0?t=523))
- The wiki claims gnomes drop items, which Gnomonkey considers completely wrong, hoping it is right at least half the time. ([2019-07-24 8:43](https://youtu.be/O6INgH6Adw0?t=523))
