**Crafting** is a skill topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2018-11-22 and 2019-07-24. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Gnomonkey made roughly 550 air orbs an hour in the video; at 550 orbs an hour the method earns 330,000 gp an hour.[^cBBPBxq6KxI-94]
* As of 2018, Gnomonkey says the next best money-making Crafting method is gold bracelets at 130,000 gp an hour.[^cBBPBxq6KxI-94]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey presents blowing and charging air orbs as the new most profitable way to train Crafting.[^cBBPBxq6KxI-0]
* Gnomonkey notes gold bracelets also give less Crafting XP than the air orb method, which additionally grants bonus Magic XP.[^cBBPBxq6KxI-127]

## Events

* Gnomonkey finds he cannot string bowstrings on an account this low in level and fears there may be no way to train Crafting on the account.[^O6INgH6Adw0-292]
* While theorycrafting Crafting methods Gnomonkey finds he cannot get wool, cook pottery or blow glass, and has no way to get molten glass; he hopes Gnome Restaurant's opal drop is the key.[^O6INgH6Adw0-523]
* The wiki claims gnomes drop items, which Gnomonkey considers completely wrong, hoping it is right at least half the time.[^O6INgH6Adw0-523]
