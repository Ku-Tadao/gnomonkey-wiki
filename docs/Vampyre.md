**Vampyre** is an activity topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2020-06-23 and 2020-09-19. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Stats and numbers

* Vampyre thieving gives rare blood shards used to make the Blood fury; because the Blood fury was pretty bad at the time, shards sat at only about 6.5M and are 5 times rarer than crystal seeds from elves.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-534]

## Tips

* If you are still doing gargoyle tasks you should definitely unlock vampyres and do them too, and maybe even extend.[^4DvqehbIFnQ-545]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says that if Jagex buffs the Blood fury (which was being discussed) and shards hit around 12M, vampyre thieving could equal or beat elves, and advises checking prices of both before choosing.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-560]
* Gnomonkey unlocked vampyres recently: an extremely AFK task for melee combat XP of upwards of 80k an hour, with solid GP of about 1.5m an hour, no prayer potion cost, but only about 20k Slayer XP an hour.[^4DvqehbIFnQ-545]

## Events

* Vampyre thieving was added with the Sins of the Father quest, is very similar to Elves, and requires 82 Thieving versus 85 for elves.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-534]
* Gnomonkey took down an earlier video on vampyre thieving after finding the shards were 1 in 5k rather than the 1 in 1k he thought.[^k0NPoqgsiQY-534]
