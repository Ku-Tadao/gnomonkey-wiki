**Ascension crossbow** is an item topic that Gnomonkey covers in 2 videos between 2026-06-16 and 2026-07-07. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

[TOC]

## Methods

* Ascension bolts will be readily obtainable by chiselling down ascension shards from the Fractured Archive, because Jagex concluded the crossbows could not be balanced around existing bolts and their effects.[^mb_FoVFeXgA-1100]

## Stats and numbers

* The Ascension crossbows can only fire ascension (fractured) bolts, which can be tipped with diamond or onyx; they cannot shoot ruby bolts.[^mb_FoVFeXgA-1035]
* The Ascension crossbows attack every two ticks, which Gnomonkey describes as like a heavy blowpipe, and enchanted bolt effects have a 50% reduced chance to trigger.[^mb_FoVFeXgA-1068]
* Diamond ascension bolts are a guaranteed hit when they proc and give a little damage bonus, while onyx ascension bolts heal the player.[^mb_FoVFeXgA-1068]
* A 50% reduced proc chance on a two-tick crossbow is still a higher proc chance than a normal five-tick crossbow, equivalent to a two-and-a-half-tick crossbow.[^mb_FoVFeXgA-1100]
* The Ascension crossbows have 34 range, 3 magic attack, some negative melee bonuses and 10 magic defence, have no special attack, and can only use ascension bolts.[^mb_FoVFeXgA-1100]
* The ascension bolts have 53 ranged strength, 58 with a gem; Gnomonkey says that after accounting for their two-tick speed they are a bit stronger than dragon bolts.[^mb_FoVFeXgA-1191]

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says onyx ascension bolts will be insanely expensive (an onyx bolt tip is 8k) at two-tick speed, comparing it to dragon darts costing 8k each, but thinks it is fine for the best items to be expensive ammo sinks.[^mb_FoVFeXgA-1133]
* Gnomonkey says the passive onyx healing could be very good for content like the Inferno, and the blog expects the crossbows to be a raid-tier replacement for the Toxic blowpipe.[^mb_FoVFeXgA-1224]
* Gnomonkey says he thinks the Ascension crossbows are the best thing in the blog: a blowpipe upgrade is the perfect raid reward, they look awesome, and he is glad they cannot use rubies because that would be problematic.[^mb_FoVFeXgA-1257]
* Gnomonkey says he expects most players to use diamond bolts, thinks the crossbows are good anywhere a blowpipe is good, and calls them a super blowpipe that is much more accurate.[^mb_FoVFeXgA-1286]
* Gnomonkey would not hate if sapphire bolts were added as another Ascension bolt type, so the crossbow could be used at Muspah.[^lNaYz-3A15Y-172]
* Gnomonkey says plentiful bolts would be good, as it is very annoying for Iron Men when a certain ammo type takes forever to obtain.[^lNaYz-3A15Y-211]

## Events

* The Raids 4 blog proposes tradable dual crossbows copied from RuneScape 3's Ascension crossbows.[^mb_FoVFeXgA-1035]
* Jagex said it will stick with limiting Ascension crossbow bolt effects to regular diamond and onyx bolts, leaving ruby bolts off the table because rebalancing them is a big task.[^lNaYz-3A15Y-152]
* Jagex wants players to have enough Ascension shards to make bolts and charge the Breaker, so there is never a massive grind for an hour of crossbow usage, and would like an AFK way to obtain them similar to ancient essence or demon tiers.[^lNaYz-3A15Y-172]
