# Ascension crossbow

## Methods

- Ascension bolts will be readily obtainable by chiselling down ascension shards from the Fractured Archive, because Jagex concluded the crossbows could not be balanced around existing bolts and their effects. ([2026-06-16 18:20](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1100))

## Stats and numbers

- The Ascension crossbows can only fire ascension (fractured) bolts, which can be tipped with diamond or onyx; they cannot shoot ruby bolts. ([2026-06-16 17:15](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1035))
- The Ascension crossbows attack every two ticks, which Gnomonkey describes as like a heavy blowpipe, and enchanted bolt effects have a 50% reduced chance to trigger. ([2026-06-16 17:48](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1068))
- Diamond ascension bolts are a guaranteed hit when they proc and give a little damage bonus, while onyx ascension bolts heal the player. ([2026-06-16 17:48](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1068))
- Gnomonkey says a 50% reduced proc chance on a two-tick crossbow is still a higher proc chance than a normal five-tick crossbow, equivalent to a two-and-a-half-tick crossbow. ([2026-06-16 18:20](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1100))
- The Ascension crossbows have 34 range, 3 magic attack, some negative melee bonuses and 10 magic defence, have no special attack, and can only use ascension bolts. ([2026-06-16 18:20](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1100))
- The ascension bolts have 53 ranged strength, 58 with a gem; Gnomonkey says that after accounting for their two-tick speed they are a bit stronger than dragon bolts. ([2026-06-16 19:51](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1191))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says onyx ascension bolts will be insanely expensive (an onyx bolt tip is 8k) at two-tick speed, comparing it to dragon darts costing 8k each, but thinks it is fine for the best items to be expensive ammo sinks. ([2026-06-16 18:53](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1133))
- Gnomonkey says the passive onyx healing could be very good for content like the Inferno, and the blog expects the crossbows to be a raid-tier replacement for the Toxic blowpipe. ([2026-06-16 20:24](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1224))
- Gnomonkey says he thinks the Ascension crossbows are the best thing in the blog: a blowpipe upgrade is the perfect raid reward, they look awesome, and he is glad they cannot use rubies because that would be problematic. ([2026-06-16 20:57](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1257))
- Gnomonkey says he expects most players to use diamond bolts, thinks the crossbows are good anywhere a blowpipe is good, and calls them a super blowpipe that is much more accurate. ([2026-06-16 21:26](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1286))
- Gnomonkey would not hate if sapphire bolts were added as another Ascension bolt type, so the crossbow could be used at Muspah. ([2026-07-07 2:52](https://youtu.be/lNaYz-3A15Y?t=172))
- Gnomonkey says plentiful bolts would be good, as it is very annoying for Iron Men when a certain ammo type takes forever to obtain. ([2026-07-07 3:31](https://youtu.be/lNaYz-3A15Y?t=211))

## Events

- The Raids 4 blog proposes tradable dual crossbows copied from RuneScape 3's Ascension crossbows. ([2026-06-16 17:15](https://youtu.be/mb_FoVFeXgA?t=1035))
- Jagex said it will stick with limiting Ascension crossbow bolt effects to regular diamond and onyx bolts, leaving ruby bolts off the table because rebalancing them is a big task. ([2026-07-07 2:32](https://youtu.be/lNaYz-3A15Y?t=152))
- Jagex wants players to have enough Ascension shards to make bolts and charge the Breaker, so there is never a massive grind for an hour of crossbow usage, and would like an AFK way to obtain them similar to ancient essence or demon tiers. ([2026-07-07 2:52](https://youtu.be/lNaYz-3A15Y?t=172))
