**Opinion: Sailing as released** is an opinion topic that Gnomonkey covers in 1 video on 2025-11-26. This article is a stub: his statements are grouped by type, repeated ones merged.

## Gnomonkey's opinion

* Gnomonkey says Sailing had been in development for three years, so he argues it is fair to judge it right after release, which is when feedback matters most.[^KRl59CBUR8U-757]
* Gnomonkey says that with three years of dev time it is frustrating that Sailing is not more fleshed out; he says it does not have something for everyone the way fully fleshed-out skills have a method for every playstyle.[^KRl59CBUR8U-788]
* Gnomonkey says he generally dislikes Sailing and thinks it is not impossible for it to become good, but right now considers Sailing very weak.[^KRl59CBUR8U-942]
* Gnomonkey says some of his Sailing complaints are subjective, but there are many objectively bad things such as balance problems, many bugs, and friction in training the skill.[^KRl59CBUR8U-975]
* Gnomonkey thinks Sailing needed a whole other year to be in an acceptable state for Old School, whether as a part two or a further delay, despite taking about three years to develop.[^KRl59CBUR8U-4392]
* Gnomonkey argues the three years of Sailing development could have been spent fleshing out weak skills such as Firemaking, early Hunter and Agility, which people compare Sailing favourably against.[^KRl59CBUR8U-4451]
* Gnomonkey argues Sailing came at a cost to other updates because it had basically the entire dev team working on it to some extent, so it is not fair to say it cost nothing.[^KRl59CBUR8U-4541]
* Gnomonkey says he is not sure he fully trusts Jagex to fix Sailing with a part two; he expects clickboxes and bugs to be ironed out but doubts major issues like the combat will be fixed.[^KRl59CBUR8U-4883]
* Gnomonkey thinks the big thing Jagex needs to focus on is making Sailing training more interesting and varied, especially with more Barracuda Trials.[^KRl59CBUR8U-4883]
