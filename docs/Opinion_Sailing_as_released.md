## Opinions

- Gnomonkey says Sailing had been in development for three years, so he argues it is fair to judge it right after release, which is when feedback matters most. ([2025-11-26 12:37](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=757))
- Gnomonkey says that with three years of dev time it is frustrating that Sailing is not more fleshed out; he says it does not have something for everyone the way fully fleshed-out skills have a method for every playstyle. ([2025-11-26 13:08](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=788))
- Gnomonkey says he generally dislikes Sailing and thinks it is not impossible for it to become good, but right now considers Sailing very weak. ([2025-11-26 15:42](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=942))
- Gnomonkey says some of his Sailing complaints are subjective, but there are many objectively bad things such as balance problems, many bugs, and friction in training the skill. ([2025-11-26 16:15](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=975))
- Gnomonkey thinks Sailing needed a whole other year to be in an acceptable state for Old School, whether as a part two or a further delay, despite taking about three years to develop. ([2025-11-26 1:13:12](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=4392))
- Gnomonkey argues the three years of Sailing development could have been spent fleshing out weak skills such as Firemaking, early Hunter and Agility, which people compare Sailing favourably against. ([2025-11-26 1:14:11](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=4451))
- Gnomonkey argues Sailing came at a cost to other updates because it had basically the entire dev team working on it to some extent, so it is not fair to say it cost nothing. ([2025-11-26 1:15:41](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=4541))
- Gnomonkey says he is not sure he fully trusts Jagex to fix Sailing with a part two; he expects clickboxes and bugs to be ironed out but doubts major issues like the combat will be fixed. ([2025-11-26 1:21:23](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=4883))
- Gnomonkey thinks the big thing Jagex needs to focus on is making Sailing training more interesting and varied, especially with more Barracuda Trials. ([2025-11-26 1:21:23](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=4883))
