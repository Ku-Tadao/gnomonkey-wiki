# Crystal halberd

Gnomonkey covers this topic in 2 videos (2021-01-29 to 2025-05-07). Everything below is what he said, cited to the video.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | item |

## Methods

- Gnomonkey tries to end each phase with a Crystal halberd spec, then puts on the Ring of suffering and goes back to work. ([2025-05-07 3:38](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=218))

## Stats and numbers

- Gnomonkey says the Crystal halberd special attack is the highest possible damage of any single spec but can be inaccurate compared to Dragon claws, and is very slow at seven ticks. ([2021-01-29 14:13](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=853))
- Gnomonkey says you cannot main-hand the Crystal halberd because it has a seven-tick attack speed, and anything other than the Noxious halberd is not good for melee here. ([2025-05-07 9:02](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=542))

## Tips

- Halberd specs are typically used right before a boss transitions to an invulnerability phase or when it would outright kill the boss, so that the seven-tick cooldown is avoided completely. ([2021-01-29 14:13](https://youtu.be/sCmYo21i2I8?t=853))
- Gnomonkey says if you do not have a Crystal halberd, use a Dragon halberd, which Zulrah drops, but he recommends buying a Crystal halberd before doing Zulrah if possible. ([2025-05-07 13:36](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=816))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey calls the Crystal halberd the new best spec weapon at Zulrah, with potential to hit up to 100 total across its two hits (50/50) and no tick loss if used right as she goes underwater. ([2025-05-07 2:01](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=121))
