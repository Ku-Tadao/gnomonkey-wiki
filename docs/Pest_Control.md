## Methods

- The goal is to move from portal to portal killing each as quickly as possible, killing monsters in the downtime; RuneLite shows which portal's shield will drop next so the player can run to it beforehand. ([2019-07-16 0:58](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI?t=58))

## Stats and numbers

- The veteran boat gives 5 points a game and requires 100 combat; the intermediate boat gives 4 points and requires 70 combat; the novice boat gives 3 points and requires 40 combat. ([2019-07-16 4:33](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI?t=273))
- On the veteran boat a regular void ranged set costs 850 points and takes about six and a half hours; a full elite void ranged set costs 1250 points and takes 9.5 hours on average; a complete set with all helmets costs 1650 points and takes almost 12.5 hours. ([2019-07-16 5:01](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI?t=301))
- The novice boat is 40% slower than the veteran boat for points; the regular void ranged set would take about 10 hours using just the novice boat. ([2019-07-16 5:53](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI?t=353))

## Tips

- A Pest Control game has four shielded portals (Western, Eastern, Southeastern and Southwestern) that spill monsters trying to break through the gate and kill the player and the Void Knight. ([2019-07-16 0:00](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI))
- If the Void Knight is killed the game is lost immediately; killing all four portals wins the game immediately and returns players to the Pest Control dock. The Void Knight takes the portal shields down one by one over time. ([2019-07-16 0:00](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI))
- Pest Control is a safe minigame: dying sends the player back to the boat and they can run back in. If a player dies too many times or does not do enough damage they receive no points when the game ends. ([2019-07-16 0:30](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI?t=30))
- Gnomonkey says to bring best non-degradable armor and weapons, a spec weapon, and optionally stat-boosting combat potions for more XP an hour; food and prayer potions are not needed. ([2019-07-16 0:30](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI?t=30))
- Splatters have low HP and explode upon death dealing damage; Gnomonkey says to ignore them. ([2019-07-16 0:58](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI?t=58))
- Brawlers take up four tiles and cannot be walked through or shot past using ranged or magic. ([2019-07-16 0:58](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI?t=58))
- Defilers fire ranged attacks and can shoot through the wooden walls. ([2019-07-16 0:58](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI?t=58))
- Ravagers destroy barricades and doors, shifters use melee and can teleport like abyssal demons, and torchers use magic and can cast through the wooden walls. ([2019-07-16 1:28](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI?t=88))
- Spinners do not attack players but heal the portals, and Gnomonkey calls them the most important enemy to be conscious of. ([2019-07-16 1:28](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI?t=88))
- With fewer than four players attacking a portal it is best to kill spinners whenever possible; with a larger crowd players can out-DPS the spinners and kill the portal, which Gnomonkey calls the fastest and best option. ([2019-07-16 2:02](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI?t=122))
- If a spinner is alive when its portal is killed it explodes and poisons surrounding players, but the poison damage can be ignored given how short the games are. ([2019-07-16 2:02](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI?t=122))
- Rushing the portals with teammates and killing monsters between shields almost always defeats the portals before the knight dies and makes games faster and more consistent. ([2019-07-16 2:29](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI?t=149))
- Players can repair barricades and doors to gain points without combat, which Gnomonkey says is really only useful for non-combat accounts and barely helps the team. ([2019-07-16 2:29](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI?t=149))
- Games do not take longer on a harder boat, so Gnomonkey says to take the best boat available for faster points. ([2019-07-16 4:33](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI?t=273))
- At level 40 combat, while training melee anyway, Gnomonkey says the novice boat is a fairly efficient place to train and obtain void at the same time, though XP an hour is much worse than Sand Crabs because Pest Control monsters have defence. ([2019-07-16 5:27](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI?t=327))

## Opinions

- Gnomonkey does not recommend AFKing with auto-retaliate in front of the Void Knight, since many AFK players dramatically slow games and cause losses, and in his testing AFKing sometimes gets the player detected as a leech and awarded no points. ([2019-07-16 2:29](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI?t=149))
- Gnomonkey says void's power increases with Ranged level; if training early Ranged with Slayer, the Black Mask is more useful, but for cannoning or chinning Void can raise XP an hour by quite a bit. ([2019-07-16 5:27](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI?t=327))
- Gnomonkey thinks getting void on the novice boat while training melee, before having access to Nightmare Zone, is most efficient. ([2019-07-16 5:53](https://youtu.be/tMJcFMKEaOI?t=353))
