Sailing is Old School RuneScape's first new skill, released on 19 November 2025 ([2025-10-24 0:00](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=0)). Gnomonkey covered it from the April 2025 alpha through the May 2026 blog changes: he praises its boat systems, quests and art, but repeatedly criticises its training methods, combat and lack of endgame reasons to train it, and as of March 2026 calls it "pretty good" as a skill with bad, tacked-on combat ([2026-03-20 6:35](https://youtu.be/fZ5RVn7tNhI?t=395)).

## Alpha (April 2025)

- The alpha showed rough training methods but was missing combat and several methods ([2025-04-02 0:28](https://youtu.be/hfrGzE8rGj0?t=28)). Gnomonkey summarises the alpha methods as cargo missions with Slayer-like tasks, "Woodcutting 2" (salvaging) and a janky Sepulchre ([2025-04-02 3:27](https://youtu.be/hfrGzE8rGj0?t=207)).
- Jagex said a couple more methods would come later in a "Forestry part two" way, including a Slayer and Hunter combination of hunting leviathans and a mini raid; both were confirmed not to be in the release ([2025-04-02 3:27](https://youtu.be/hfrGzE8rGj0?t=207)).
- Gnomonkey was very impressed with the alpha, found the core boat mechanics okay and said the skill had a long way to go ([2025-04-02 3:57](https://youtu.be/hfrGzE8rGj0?t=237)). He liked ship turning, the sail speed boost and the whirlpool speed boost, but said the way the skill is trained "is just not it" ([2025-04-02 22:27](https://youtu.be/hfrGzE8rGj0?t=1347)). He had no issue with any single method, or with slow AFK methods existing, but did not want them to be the only or primary way to train ([2025-04-02 21:21](https://youtu.be/hfrGzE8rGj0?t=1281)).
- He said Sailing would arrive whether players wanted it or not because it passed the poll, so the best approach was to look for ways to improve it ([2025-04-02 22:58](https://youtu.be/hfrGzE8rGj0?t=1378)). He argued it must launch fleshed out rather than being fixed 6 to 12 months later, as with [Forestry](Forestry.md) ([2025-04-02 23:58](https://youtu.be/hfrGzE8rGj0?t=1438)), and warned that uninteresting training methods after day-one hype would lose a large share of supporters ([2025-04-02 24:24](https://youtu.be/hfrGzE8rGj0?t=1464)).
- He said Sailing would not feel like Old School RuneScape and that this is acceptable ([2025-04-02 26:44](https://youtu.be/hfrGzE8rGj0?t=1604)), did not want it to repeat the mistake of many [RuneScape 3](RuneScape_3.md) skills of simply not being good ([2025-04-02 26:45](https://youtu.be/hfrGzE8rGj0?t=1605)), and wanted further alphas including one showing combat ([2025-04-02 26:46](https://youtu.be/hfrGzE8rGj0?t=1606)).

## Launch contents

Per the October 2025 blog as relayed by Gnomonkey, Sailing launches with:

- Shipbuilding and ship upgrades, like player-owned-house upgrades for the ship, using different materials and requiring Construction and Sailing ([2025-10-24 0:33](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=33)).
- Crew and multiplayer: up to 10 NPCs can be asked to join the crew and friends can sail on the boat, with the blog saying up to five crewmates ([2025-10-24 0:33](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=33)).
- Ship combat, which had a beta Gnomonkey tried hands-on ([2025-10-24 0:33](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=33)).
- Sailing XP earned by successfully interacting with boat facilities, the simplest being trimming the sails for a speed boost ([2025-10-24 1:06](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=66)).
- New quests, areas and resources, including new mining rocks and thieving, and new training methods for [Woodcutting](Woodcutting.md), [Hunter](Hunter.md) and [Fishing](Fishing.md) ([2025-10-24 3:04](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=184)).
- Unique items: a teleport to boat spell, the gleaming rapier (like the zombie axe for stab) and the Horn of Plenty (boosts Hunter), both from the new Griffin boss, and the Aqua Knight Hopper, which double bolt shots ([2025-10-24 3:37](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=217)). Gnomonkey says the new weapons and armour are nothing super high level and mostly flesh out the mid-game ([2025-10-24 3:37](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=217)).

The September 2025 blog covered new Slayer creatures and unique drops, many of them returning from RuneScape 3 ([2025-09-18 0:00](https://youtu.be/sK0iG_wg3QA?t=0) [2025-09-18 0:28](https://youtu.be/sK0iG_wg3QA?t=28)). It also announced stress testing before launch and a feedback survey ([2025-09-18 15:15](https://youtu.be/sK0iG_wg3QA?t=915)).

## Training methods

See also [Port tasks](Port_tasks.md), [Salvaging](Salvaging.md), [Barracuda Trials](Barracuda_Trials.md), [Deep sea trawling](Deep_sea_trawling.md) and [Charting](Charting.md).

- **Port tasks**: cargo or delivery missions to NPCs across the sea, or combat missions such as killing a number of monsters ([2025-04-02 0:28](https://youtu.be/hfrGzE8rGj0?t=28) [2025-10-24 1:34](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=94)). Up to five port tasks can be stored at once, each requiring a Sailing level ([2025-10-24 1:34](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=94)). Gnomonkey compares them to Motherlode Mine: not super high intensity but not AFK ([2025-04-02 1:00](https://youtu.be/hfrGzE8rGj0?t=60)), and likes that five can be held at once, unlike one-at-a-time [Slayer](Slayer.md) ([2025-10-24 1:34](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=94)).
- **Salvaging**: pulling up salvage from shipwrecks for loot, "just like mining or woodcutting" ([2025-04-02 1:00](https://youtu.be/hfrGzE8rGj0?t=60)). The alpha had two-tick salvaging, found by a player called HeBox, using a three-tick action with two rolls ([2025-04-02 1:29](https://youtu.be/hfrGzE8rGj0?t=89)). At launch Gnomonkey describes it as two rolls every four ticks, because the player does a three-tick action but must step away and back, wasting a tick ([2025-10-24 2:01](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=121)). He thinks it may be the XP meta but is not sure ([2025-10-24 2:01](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=121)). In November 2025 he calls salvaging his favourite thing about Sailing and maybe the most AFK activity in the game ([2025-11-26 16:45](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=1005)).
- **Barracuda Trials**: boat races collecting cargo for a bonus; Gnomonkey calls them a Sailing version of the [Hallowed Sepulchre](Hallowed_Sepulchre.md) ([2025-10-24 2:30](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=150)). In the alpha he said they were theoretically the best XP method but looked janky, and that they were the thing he was most excited for ([2025-04-02 2:16](https://youtu.be/hfrGzE8rGj0?t=136) [2025-04-02 2:28](https://youtu.be/hfrGzE8rGj0?t=148)). In the October 2025 beta he again found them janky but not terrible, expecting refinement ([2025-10-24 2:30](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=150)). In April 2026 he calls them fast but repetitive ([2026-04-09 3:56](https://youtu.be/8CSztSkDvnM?t=236)).
- **Deep sea trawling**: a Fishing Trawler-like activity catching large groups of fish, including a new best healing fish and one needed for anti-bleed wraps ([2025-10-24 3:04](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=184)). He says the fishing shoals give about 5k Sailing XP an hour and 20k Fishing XP an hour, which he calls weak and unbalanced ([2025-11-26 1:18:32](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=4712)).
- **Charting**: one-time XP for reaching a new area, aimed at completionists and cloggers rather than a real training method ([2025-04-02 1:59](https://youtu.be/hfrGzE8rGj0?t=119)); compared to lamps and not a long-term method ([2025-10-24 2:30](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=150)).
- **Cannonball thieving**: the new stall needs 87 [Thieving](Thieving.md) and gives about 230k XP an hour, but he says spam-clicking [Knights of Ardougne](Knights_of_Ardougne.md) gives the same XP and more money ([2025-11-26 1:18:32](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=4712)).

Overall he rates the launch offering as not enough for training but acceptable for a launch, comparing the whole offering to the amount of [Mining](Mining.md) content ([2025-10-24 4:05](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=245) [2025-10-24 4:34](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=274)). He notes a good spread of intensity (click-intensive racing, AFK or tick-manipulated trawling, cargo missions in between) while finding the methods not very engaging ([2025-10-24 4:34](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=274)). In April 2026 he says Sailing needs newer ways to train, finding the bounty task system annoying and salvaging very boring and AFK, though most people choose it ([2026-04-09 3:56](https://youtu.be/8CSztSkDvnM?t=236)).

## Preparation and economy

- Jagex's preparation list covered items (logs, planks, swamp tar, flax, cannonballs, rope, swamp paste, coins, nails), quests and skills ([2025-11-04 1:58](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=118)). Many items are used to upgrade the ship, and flax is used for sails ([2025-11-04 1:58](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=118)). Gnomonkey assumes the listed quests unlock docks at different ports, such as a dock off Prifddinas and a Lunar Isle dock ([2025-11-04 3:24](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=204)). [Crafting](Crafting.md) 73 is needed for upgrades such as fishing nets and sails ([2025-11-04 8:56](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=536)).
- Advice he attributes to Modelena, whom he describes as the lead of the Sailing project: mains must decide whether to make or spend money at release, since rushing 99 will be costly until prices settle ([2025-11-04 2:26](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=146)). Mains who want to make money should get Sailing high enough to farm the resources rushers will need ([2025-11-04 2:55](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=175)). Ironmen should expect to do much more than Sailing, because upgrading the boat is key ([2025-11-04 2:55](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=175)); Gnomonkey infers this means hundreds, maybe thousands, of planks and other resources ([2025-11-04 2:55](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=175)).
- Gnomonkey says casual players do not need 99 Sailing: the 70s or 80s unlock almost all content, and rushing to 99 is expensive and gains little ([2025-11-04 9:23](https://youtu.be/pwZKK8dpq8o?t=563)).

## Boats and combat

- He considers the boat movement not as tight as normal game movement but solid, refined from the beta, and never sails into land ([2025-11-26 19:19](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=1159)). A direct diagonal is slightly slower, and well-timed speed boosts, which can be stored, give snappy movement ([2025-11-26 19:52](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=1192)).
- He praises the art and animations ([2025-11-26 20:22](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=1222)), the quests (including Current Affairs, each under half an hour) ([2025-11-26 21:22](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=1282)), the facilities and upgrades that give a sense of progression like building a player-owned house ([2025-11-26 21:51](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=1311)), and the truly unlimited ale keg ([2025-11-26 22:21](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=1341)).
- In the October 2025 beta, ship combat felt janky and strange, though runnable for release ([2025-10-24 1:06](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=66)). In December 2025 he calls overhauling combat, which Jagex admitted does not feel good, an extremely high priority ([2025-12-04 11:18](https://youtu.be/OeDcRft1_cU?t=678)) and says [Jagex](Jagex.md) cannot add interesting PvM content on boats until combat is fixed ([2025-12-04 13:18](https://youtu.be/OeDcRft1_cU?t=798)).
- January 2026 roadmap: cannons are affected by ranged gear, players get double cannonballs per bar, and regular weapons deal full normal damage ([2026-01-25 2:00](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=120)). Gnomonkey thinks this is probably the best direction available, working fine though not amazing ([2026-01-25 2:27](https://youtu.be/h-RfJ1kx2pU?t=147)).
- March 2026 update: an attractor facility saves roughly 60 percent of cannonballs; cannons fire slower (seven-tick attack speed) with higher max hits; veiled Krakens add a source of the dragon cannon barrel at roughly 1 in 1,000 per the wiki; off-boat attacks such as a Tumeken's shadow now deal full damage to sea monsters without a cap ([2026-03-20 0:00](https://youtu.be/fZ5RVn7tNhI?t=0) [2026-03-20 0:26](https://youtu.be/fZ5RVn7tNhI?t=26)). - Monster data he lists from the wiki: Albatross feathers for the Gale catcher drop at 1 in 29 with 179 health (five needed); Manta rays drop Ray barbs at 1 in 16 with 225 health (about eight needed for the best trawling net) ([2026-03-20 3:26](https://youtu.be/fZ5RVn7tNhI?t=206)); Narwhal horn drops at 1 in 100 from 220-health monsters; Great white sharks, which drop for the best salvaging hook, have 243 hit points ([2026-03-20 3:26](https://youtu.be/fZ5RVn7tNhI?t=206)); Armored Krakens drop the bottled storm item at 1 in 1,000, and the new dragon cannon barrel source has 225 health at roughly 1 in 1,000 ([2026-03-20 3:53](https://youtu.be/fZ5RVn7tNhI?t=233)).
- He says combat feels tacked on and should have been a "part two" rather than launching with the skill ([2026-03-20 6:35](https://youtu.be/fZ5RVn7tNhI?t=395) [2026-03-20 6:39](https://youtu.be/fZ5RVn7tNhI?t=399)), but that no combat items are mandatory and there are many ways to train outside bounty tasks ([2026-03-20 8:26](https://youtu.be/fZ5RVn7tNhI?t=506)). He sees a possible future in Leviathan hunting, pirate-ship jumping combat or mini raids, requiring a careful rework ([2026-03-20 9:35](https://youtu.be/fZ5RVn7tNhI?t=575)).
- April 2026 pirate update: Jagex said it balances content on XP per hour, GP per hour and unique drops ([2026-04-09 4:25](https://youtu.be/8CSztSkDvnM?t=265)), and said on Reddit that combat is intentionally not a viable way to train Sailing, so it is balanced around rewards ([2026-04-09 17:13](https://youtu.be/8CSztSkDvnM?t=1033)). The update adds a sail cosmetic system and a pirate flag drop ([2026-04-09 6:11](https://youtu.be/8CSztSkDvnM?t=371) [2026-04-09 6:17](https://youtu.be/8CSztSkDvnM?t=377)). Gnomonkey argued Jagex should hit the brakes on combat and fix existing problems ([2026-04-09 0:27](https://youtu.be/8CSztSkDvnM?t=27)), that the pirates risk becoming a legacy fight ([2026-04-09 16:53](https://youtu.be/8CSztSkDvnM?t=1013)), and that more charting and methods beyond Barracuda Trials are a better use of effort ([2026-04-09 17:24](https://youtu.be/8CSztSkDvnM?t=1044)).

## Gnomonkey's opinion

- **Reason to train**: the biggest argument against Sailing is the lack of reason to train it, since it unlocks nothing endgame beyond new potions and foods ([2025-11-26 1:17:04](https://youtu.be/KRl59CBUR8U?t=4624)). He wants an endgame method, better Slayer tasks and good methods for other skills ([2025-12-04 11:51](https://youtu.be/OeDcRft1_cU?t=711)), and says players most want a reason to train beyond the 99 cape ([2026-04-09 19:18](https://youtu.be/8CSztSkDvnM?t=1158)).

- **High-level players**: the launch is mostly low to mid-level content, a big reason for the hate camp ([2025-10-24 17:32](https://youtu.be/oKtiMu3iFGo?t=1052) [2025-09-18 19:42](https://youtu.be/sK0iG_wg3QA?t=1182)).
