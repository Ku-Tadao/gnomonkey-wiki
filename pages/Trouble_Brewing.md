**Trouble Brewing** is a two-player minigame that gave a flat, free source of Cooking, Fletching and Hunter XP. Gnomonkey considers it relevant even for mains because it has no input cost, and especially good for early ironmen [c17]. It is the subject of two videos: a guide in November 2024 after Jagex heavily buffed it, and a January 2025 video after the XP was nerfed again, which he says made the method completely dead [c1 c22].

## Requirements
* One other person is needed: a friend or an alt, and both sides can play at the same time without competing, each skilling on their own side [c5].
* Decent quest requirements, including many stats in the 40s and 50s and Prayer, which must be raised first [c25].
* No items can be brought in the inventory and no helmet can be worn, but other equipment such as Graceful can be [c7].
* Higher Agility (about 60 rather than 52) and Graceful help a lot because the player runs around constantly [c8]. A better axe (a Dragon axe is ideal) and higher Woodcutting speed up the loop [c9].

## XP rates
After the buff, a game gave about 200k Cooking XP per hour plus roughly 100k per hour split between Fletching and Hunter, around 50k each, adjustable depending on the levels needed [c2]. A full game in the video took about 20 minutes and gave the expected 200k Cooking and about 100k split between Hunter and Fletching [c19]. Skipping Cooking XP and fletching only with tick manipulation should give around 170k Fletching XP per hour [c18]. At one point his XP counter showed about 460k per hour, which he says is not an accurate sustained rate [c16].

The XP does not scale with level, so it is best for skipping very low early levels and less good after roughly the 70s in these stats [c3]. On a fresh hardcore ironman he arrived with 9 Hunter and reached 28 Hunter in a single game [c4]. The method gives a flat 200k Cooking and 100k Fletching with no input cost, which suits an account that rushes there early [c26].

## Method
**Lobby:** grab a knife, a tinderbox for passive Firemaking (about 20k Firemaking XP per hour at most) and about 10 meat, using custom swaps to make the left click Take-5 [c10]. There is a three-minute timer between games. With an alt that hops worlds, the timer can be waited out by entering the lobby early and then starting instantly by joining [c6].

**Before the game:** mark the grub mound and the scrapie tree, plus a second set on the other end of the island that he does not typically use [c11].

**Loop:**
1. Put rat meat into the grub mound (one-tick spam) until a swarm spawns or the mound depletes [c12].
2. Run to the tree's blue tile (the swarm's aggro radius) so the swarm stops attacking, chop the tree, then return and repeat [c12]. He tick-manipulates the tree, turning the usually four-tick chop into three ticks, roughly 25 percent faster [c13].
3. While waiting for the mound or tree to respawn, throw a regular log in the boiler for passive Firemaking. Before the update he said it gave 320 and was a decent way to get early Firemaking levels [c14].
4. With a full inventory, deposit logs in the fletching hopper (one-ticked), then the grubs and bark into the sweet grub and bark hoppers for Cooking. Repeat until the needed levels are done [c15].

## Buff and nerf
The 2024 buff came about two months before the November video [c1], though the January 2025 video places it roughly six months before, when he made a guide [c21]. Before the nerf, Trouble Brewing gave a little over 100k Fletching XP per hour along with about 200k Cooking XP per hour [c20]. Jagex then nerfed the XP rates to a little under one-third of what they were. The same update patched the exploit that gave Firemaking XP in the one boiler, which no longer gives XP [c22 c23].

## Gnomonkey's opinion
In January 2025 he says Trouble Brewing was never meta and should not have been nerfed at all. No Ironman Discord communities were reporting records broken with it [c31]. It was an early hybrid method, not as good as the best methods for either Fletching or Cooking even with combined XP [c28]. Cooking karambwans and Fletching broad arrows with zero-time Fletching were so strong that some HCIM players laughed at him for using it [c24]. He thinks it better to unlock broad arrows and buy cooked karambwans with gp from other methods, leaving Trouble Brewing as an option for players who do not want to put in input [c30].

Its downsides are the big quest requirements and the need for a second player, though the latter encouraged communication and group play [c27]. Its only rewards are cosmetics, a teleport back and a small amount of brewing stuff that nobody uses [c29].
