Ba-Ba (also written Baba) is the baboon boss room of [Tombs of Amascut](Tombs_of_Amascut.md). Gnomonkey has attacked the fight with the "red X" stall method since 2022, and describes it after the June 2025 changes as a zero-damage, zero-brew room that needs no special tech ([2022-09-13 8:53](https://youtu.be/h4hf5hPQHms?t=533)) ([2022-11-18 4:36](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=276)) ([2025-06-22 10:30](https://youtu.be/JjIAGBBDmL8?t=630)).

## Overview

- Gnomonkey does Ba-Ba first in the raid so his pre-pot helps in the monkey room, which wants ranging and strength boosts ([2024-10-24 7:08](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=428)) ([2025-06-28 20:20](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=1220)). In the 2022 solo 500 he instead did Ba-Ba second because salt is not needed for it ([2022-09-13 8:53](https://youtu.be/h4hf5hPQHms?t=533)).
- Early opinion (2022): he hoped Jagex would fix a bug or issue with Ba-Ba and stop her hitting so hard through prayer ([2022-09-13 17:29](https://youtu.be/h4hf5hPQHms?t=1049)).
- Current position (June 2025 onward): Ba-Ba no longer hits through prayer, so praying melee means no damage from her ([2025-06-22 0:30](https://youtu.be/JjIAGBBDmL8?t=30)) ([2025-06-27 6:50](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=410)) ([2026-04-01 11:42](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=702)). He calls it a zero-brew room with no Red X and no five-to-one, saying even in a 500 it is totally free and just takes a while, especially when the [Bandos godsword](Bandos_godsword.md) misses ([2025-06-22 10:30](https://youtu.be/JjIAGBBDmL8?t=630)) ([2025-06-27 10:45](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=645)).
- In a duo, he called the monkey room the biggest supply drain of the whole raid, more than any boss, and said Ba-Ba has a lot of drain too (2025) ([2025-10-04 11:37](https://youtu.be/X-PTHMq_10A?t=697)) ([2025-10-04 15:11](https://youtu.be/X-PTHMq_10A?t=911)). He recommends targeting the main monkey to reduce drain ([2025-10-04 11:37](https://youtu.be/X-PTHMq_10A?t=697)).

## Mechanics

- **Shockwave / slam**: Ba-Ba slams the back of the room every 33 percent of her health, killing the player instantly in front of the pit; Gnomonkey marks safe tiles and uses a Monster HP Percentage plugin ([2026-04-01 13:09](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=789)). Boulder phases occur at 66% and 33% ([2025-06-25 12:28](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=748)). Chip damage: she drops one rock on the head and two elsewhere for about 50 damage each; a thrown big rock is avoided by standing next to one of the rocks or a sarcophagus, though a sarcophagus hit twice opens and releases balls of death ([2026-04-01 12:07](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=727)). Not standing next to a rock when the thrown rock lands does ten times more damage; standing next to a sarcophagus halves damage ([2024-10-24 9:57](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=597)) ([2024-10-24 10:29](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=629)).
- **Area attack**: if the cycle is messed up in the red X setup, the area attack hits for about 30 ([2023-04-25 8:23](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=503)). The gap does not kill from the top row ([2023-06-30 9:31](https://youtu.be/cmLBaxdoHpQ?t=571)) ([2024-06-27 5:42](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=342)).
- **Monkeys**: the spawned monkeys are one-tick, ignore attack delay, and are good for gaining double [Death Charge](Death_Charge.md) spec (2026) ([2025-06-28 10:13](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=613)) ([2026-04-01 12:35](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=755)). After the June 2025 update any damage guarantees a one-shot, and they can be skipped at low health because they despawn ([2025-06-25 11:55](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=715)) ([2025-06-28 12:16](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=736)); with Circle swap or a blowpipe they are one-ticked ([2025-07-06 12:24](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=744)) ([2025-06-27 7:23](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=443)). Earlier (2024) he killed the small baboons with a crossbow because they do a lot of damage ([2024-10-24 10:29](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=629)).
- **Coffins**: still usable to block one hit each without breaking, and one can stand a tile off one and take no damage (2025) ([2025-07-06 13:24](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=804)).
- **Boulders**: one of five boulders is cracked and must be one-shot with the [Bow of Faerdhinen](Bow_of_Faerdhinen.md); he waits until the next boulder touches his character on a marked tile row, then runs up and hits Ba-Ba with the fang ([2026-04-01 13:40](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=820)) ([2025-07-06 14:22](https://youtu.be/91vxCCm7Jck?t=862)). Standing on the corner of the boulders gives room to run to the other corner when she knocks back or places the shadow AoE ([2025-06-27 8:43](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=523)) ([2025-06-28 10:43](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=643)). In 2025 he said not to range her while boulders roll, since melee DPS is much better ([2025-06-28 12:16](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=736)). The Voidwaker did not one-shot the cracked boulder after the May 2024 update without a potion, so he suggested an accurate blowpipe with a full switch and a pre-potted ranging potion ([2024-05-30 7:42](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=462)); the Bow of Faerdhinen avoids boulder issues ([2024-07-01 4:57](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=297)).
- **Shadow step marker**: run across her to the other side; running under her and clicking melee drags the player into the boulder ([2026-04-01 15:15](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=915)).

## Red X method (2022-2024)

Before the June 2025 update Gnomonkey used the red X method to negate all of her damage: a red click keeps Ba-Ba in place and monsters cannot path under the player, forcing her to proc Shadow Stomp repeatedly and not attack ([2022-09-13 17:47](https://youtu.be/h4hf5hPQHms?t=1067)) ([2022-11-18 4:36](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=276)) ([2024-06-27 7:30](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=450)).

- Ba-Ba attacks at six-tick speed, so red X slows melee to that speed ([2022-11-18 5:09](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=309)). Setup (2022): wait right of the gap, hit once, walk right one tile, right click the obelisk, walk three under, click Ba-Ba to walk three back, then cycle two under and two back ([2022-11-18 5:09](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=309)). Restart after boulders: drop a potion left of Ba-Ba, run to the top right, hit once, step back one tile, step three under, attack, then cycle ([2022-11-18 5:37](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=337)). Boulders are avoided by stepping diagonally ([2022-11-18 5:09](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=309)).
- Entry shortcuts: a four-tick hit (Keris) then any hit and two under ([2024-06-27 5:42](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=342)); Voidwaker on stab then four-tick hit into a two-step under ([2024-05-30 7:22](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=442)); drop a Sanfew serum, poke with the [Keris](Keris_partisan_of_breaching.md), swap to the [Osmumten's fang](Osmumten's_fang.md) and go two under two back on the swing ([2024-07-01 5:38](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=338)). Without Keris: a four-tick hit into a Fang hit into two steps under ([2023-06-30 8:04](https://youtu.be/cmLBaxdoHpQ?t=484)).
- Cue to continue the cycle is her paw going up, then the obelisk click; the window is two ticks ([2023-04-25 7:51](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=471)). Switches between attacks are possible, such as a Zaryte crossbow spec and back to melee ([2022-11-18 6:04](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=364)). He said the red X is the easy way: 500s are possible without it but harder than needed ([2024-05-30 7:22](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=442)), and fighting conventionally is always fastest but she becomes much less consistent to face tank with Dehydration ([2022-11-18 4:36](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=276)). See [Tombs of Amascut gear setup](Tombs_of_Amascut_gear_setup.md).

After the update, the red X is no longer required: Jagex's wording that Red X "still has its place" was confusing to Gnomonkey; he reported it was confirmed on Reddit that the Ba-Ba changes in the initial blog were kept ([2025-06-22 1:52](https://youtu.be/JjIAGBBDmL8?t=112)).

## Specs, gear and supplies

- Bandos godsword: drain at least 20 defence levels (the 20 damage cap) before melee, usable while red Xing since it is six-tick ([2022-11-18 6:04](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=364)) ([2025-06-25 11:29](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=689)) ([2026-04-01 14:39](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=879)). A 2024 note puts a threshold at about 1000 damage ([2024-06-27 7:30](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=450)). He then uses [Burning claws](Burning_claws.md) on stab (bosses have around 500 slash defence), or Voidwaker ([2026-04-01 14:39](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=879)) ([2024-07-01 6:32](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=392)). Voidwaker spec again at the 90% boulder costs fifty spec (2023) ([2023-06-30 6:32](https://youtu.be/cmLBaxdoHpQ?t=392)). Double Death Charge can recover 100% spec around 50% if the godsword missed ([2025-06-27 7:49](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=469)). Bone dagger special drains her on entry (2024 300 raid) ([2024-10-24 9:31](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=571)) ([2024-09-14 4:44](https://youtu.be/HeM0yGenyx8?t=284)). See [Bone dagger](Bone_dagger.md).
- Nothing in the room is weak to crush, so no Dragon warhammer or Arclight ([2023-06-30 7:01](https://youtu.be/cmLBaxdoHpQ?t=421)). He considers bringing Barrage trolling because thralls are too valuable ([Thralls](Thralls.md)); he does not thrall constantly in this room to avoid a dose ([2023-05-09 4:53](https://youtu.be/K7VALGO6md8?t=293)) ([2023-06-30 7:31](https://youtu.be/cmLBaxdoHpQ?t=451)).
- Prayer and supplies: Ba-Ba needs no prayer and no resources if done properly, aiming for zero doses (2023) ([2023-04-25 6:26](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=386)) ([2023-04-25 43:36](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=2616)). He did the room on two prayer points and walked out with the same ([2023-05-09 4:24](https://youtu.be/K7VALGO6md8?t=264)), used one restore dose in May 2024 saying he could have used zero ([2024-05-30 8:35](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=515)), and one brew in October 2024, expecting one to two on average ([2024-10-24 12:02](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=722)). Under the June 2025 changes: no brews needed, use them freely after chip damage (practice used two doses) ([2025-06-25 11:01](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=661)) ([2025-06-25 14:00](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=840)); April 2026: no brews, one restore dose with some Piety flicking, two or three without flicking ([2026-04-01 17:11](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=1031)). With a Blue moon setup no tanky armour is needed and he one-tick flicks Piety with protect from melee ([2025-06-27 6:50](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=410)).
- Freezing Ba-Ba (and Kephri) keeps them still ([2023-05-09 9:52](https://youtu.be/K7VALGO6md8?t=592)). Re-pot a super combat after each full brew ([2024-10-24 10:29](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=629)).

## Monkey room

- All monkeys except thralls (brawler, thrower, mage, shaman, volatile) count toward a cap preventing the next wave. First two waves need zero alive, the second to last has a cap of two, and others one. Kill low-HP monkeys and blow up volatiles quickly; chinchompas roll off defence so can kill throwers and brawlers from thralls; summoned thralls do not attack cursed baboons ([2022-11-18 4:03](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=243)) ([2022-11-18 4:36](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=276)). See [Monkey Room](Monkey_Room.md).
- Supply drain is highest here in duo runs ([2025-10-04 11:37](https://youtu.be/X-PTHMq_10A?t=697)); a "use" tech on the green vial shift-click cures a teammate instantly ([2025-10-04 12:11](https://youtu.be/X-PTHMq_10A?t=731)).
