Colosseum invocations are the random modifiers offered between waves of the [Fortis Colosseum](Fortis_Colosseum.md). Gnomonkey describes the system as heavily RNG-driven, with many invocations that end runs, and since March 2024 has argued that it needs an overhaul; his picks centre on locking in Solar Flare and Myopia ([2024-03-22 0:00](https://youtu.be/IR0MIzsbFeM?t=0)) ([2024-03-24 0:00](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=0)) ([2024-03-25 7:14](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=434)).

## Overview

- In the description of his world record video, Gnomonkey states the invocation system has to be overhauled or Colosseum will die out, and that over 80% of invocations lose a run automatically or cause reset spam as it stands ([2024-03-22 0:00](https://youtu.be/IR0MIzsbFeM?t=0)). He describes a lot of RNG, no guarantee of good runs, and likely resets ([2024-03-22 0:29](https://youtu.be/IR0MIzsbFeM?t=29)); a good chunk of invocations are instant run-enders, so getting the right set likely means resetting a lot ([2024-03-24 0:00](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=0)).
- On the stream he estimated he resets about one in three runs on wave two and perhaps another one in three before wave four, guessing about 33 of 100 runs reach the end with perfect play (an estimate he was unsure about) ([2024-03-22 14:42](https://youtu.be/IR0MIzsbFeM?t=882)). Some invocations make the boss nearly impossible, which is a reason runs get reset ([2024-03-22 16:42](https://youtu.be/IR0MIzsbFeM?t=1002)).
- What he dislikes about the Colosseum is the random invocations, but otherwise he finds it pretty good ([2024-03-28 28:08](https://youtu.be/IfJVUHF6fyc?t=1688)). See also [Opinion: Colosseum invocation overhaul](Opinion_Colosseum_invocation_overhaul.md).
- His Discord has an in-depth invocation tier list and custom menu swaps for the Fremmys under Colosseum Resources ([2024-06-15 0:00](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=0)).

## Choosing invocations

- **New versus upgrade**: Gnomonkey always prioritises taking a new invocation over upgrading an existing one, because the game throws tier 2 or 3 upgrades at the player more often than new non-terrible invocations ([2024-03-22 0:29](https://youtu.be/IR0MIzsbFeM?t=29)) ([2024-03-26 10:23](https://youtu.be/nEIeyY_m_gw?t=623)) ([2024-07-26 21:47](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1307)). His second priority, after a new upgradable invocation, is a new one-off invocation ([2024-03-24 0:29](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=29)). The game often offers an upgrade plus two other choices, and he picks a good upgradable choice when available ([2024-03-24 0:00](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=0)); taking Solar Flare or Myopia at the start means he is not forced into a bad choice, and he locks in upgradable invocations whenever possible ([2024-03-25 7:14](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=434)) ([2024-03-25 9:38](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=578)). In July 2024 he added that he tries to finish an invocation to take it out of the pool, and that Solar Flare 3 is the clear choice because it finishes the invocation and opens new ones ([2024-07-26 21:47](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1307)) ([2024-07-26 27:27](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1647)).
- **Best start**: the best possible invocations at the start are Solar Flare or Myopia, with Quartet also good but not upgradable (March 2024); his own choice is Solar Flare, and it is worth restarting until one of them appears, which he says takes about four runs ([2024-03-25 7:14](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=434)). If Myopia 1 is already held and Myopia 2 or Solar Flare 1 is offered, he takes Solar Flare 1 ([2024-03-24 0:00](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=0)).
- **Wave matters**: which wave the run is on changes what is a good or bad choice ([2024-03-22 0:29](https://youtu.be/IR0MIzsbFeM?t=29)). Avoid ending up with all three slots filled by Doom, Doom Scorpion, Totemic or Relentless, which is an instant run-ender ([2024-03-24 0:29](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=29)).
- **Early wave farming**: for farming higher waves rather than reaching the boss, Myopia can be a bad choice and replacing it with Ender Bees is probably the play ([2024-03-24 2:45](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=165)).

## Individual invocations

**Blasphemy.** Drains prayer by 20% of all damage received, including things like divine potions; upgrades bump this to 40% and 60% ([2024-03-24 1:24](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=84)). Gnomonkey calls it one of the worst invocations, but forced at the start because the other options are worse ([2024-03-22 16:42](https://youtu.be/IR0MIzsbFeM?t=1002)) ([2024-03-24 1:24](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=84)); it chunks through prayer doses and can smite the player quickly ([2024-03-24 1:24](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=84)). Avoid upgrades: tier 2 forced on wave 2 or 3 is an instant restart, and tier 3 on wave 6 or earlier is likely restart-worthy ([2024-03-24 1:24](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=84)). He waits on it as long as possible (seeing it only at wave 10 was ideal), and Blasphemy 2 is very bad because being smited is the end ([2024-03-25 9:05](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=545)) ([2024-03-25 30:42](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=1842)) ([2024-03-25 32:48](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=1968)) ([2024-03-26 12:25](https://youtu.be/nEIeyY_m_gw?t=745)). In July 2024 he said always take Blasphemy first because it is the least bad of the first choices, and that needing Blasphemy 3 on wave 3 signals bad seeds on other invocations rather than Blasphemy being bad; a run can still be completed with it, especially a first cape ([2024-07-26 21:28](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1288)) ([2024-07-26 33:54](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2034)).

**Doom.** Before the April 2024 update, monster hits while off prayer applied doom and 20 stacks killed instantly; in practice any damage applied it, so Gnomonkey said Doom could never be taken ([2024-03-24 1:54](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=114)). After the update (see below) he considers Doom level 1 free for almost any player and takes tier 3 in speedruns. Later he said Doom is fairly good but he would only take Doom 1 on a first Quiver, and never Doom 2 unless forced, since it makes wave 11 hell ([2024-06-15 12:00](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=720)) ([2024-06-15 16:04](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=964)). In July 2024: avoid on a first cape, though Doom 1 is okay late; Doom 3 is very bad before [Sol Heredit](Sol_Heredit.md) ([2024-07-26 23:19](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1399)) ([2024-07-26 44:00](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2640)). The javelin colossus counts as two hits for Doom stacks though it hits once, bugged since release ([2024-07-26 28:29](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1709)). In December 2025 he called Doom 1 okay and Doom 2 a little scary when setting things up ([2025-12-12 6:05](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=365)). He locked Doom early in a 2025 run against Sol ([2025-03-21 1:26](https://youtu.be/c6oOmOGl5Lg?t=86)).

**Doom Scorpion.** Spawns a slow-moving scorpion applying two stacks of doom on hit; upgrades make it faster and apply four and eight stacks. Run-ending if forced ([2024-03-24 1:54](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=114)) ([2024-03-25 12:06](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=726)).

**Dynamic Duo.** Spawns an additional shockwave colossus beside the existing one; typically does not change solves and is worth taking nearly every time in March 2024 ([2024-03-24 2:25](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=145)) ([2024-03-25 20:35](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=1235)). In July 2024 he said Duo is really bad because it creates quad stacks ([2024-07-26 37:55](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2275)).

**Frailty.** Reduces max HP by 10% and prevents overhealing; upgrades bump this to 20% and 40% ([2024-03-24 2:25](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=145)). March 2024: tier 1 is the only manageable tier, realistically only after wave 10, to be avoided at all costs; only worth taking very late ([2024-03-24 2:25](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=145)) ([2024-03-25 13:40](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=820)). Changes: in April 2024 he started locking Frailty first for speedruns, capping health at 60 ([2024-04-01 0:33](https://youtu.be/O_eC5OV3w1o?t=33)) ([2024-04-01 10:26](https://youtu.be/O_eC5OV3w1o?t=626)); in July 2024 he called it mid-tier, with Solar Flare absolutely better ([2024-07-26 23:19](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1399)) ([2024-07-26 27:27](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1647)); in the Pillarless Colosseum he finds it very bad but more runnable than Relentless ([2025-12-12 5:33](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=333)).

**Myopia.** Reduces range by 2 tiles; upgrades 4 and 6 ([2024-03-24 2:45](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=165)). Gnomonkey calls it the best invocation for completion, since the boss is killed with a melee setup and Myopia does not affect melee ([2024-03-24 2:45](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=165)) ([2024-03-25 16:47](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=1007)) ([2024-03-26 9:56](https://youtu.be/nEIeyY_m_gw?t=596)). It matters only for the Berserker at the start ([2024-03-25 16:47](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=1007)). In July 2024 he noted it does not matter when only using a mage weapon and bow ([2024-07-26 21:47](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1307)). With Myopia he sets up line of sight manually ([2025-03-21 2:58](https://youtu.be/c6oOmOGl5Lg?t=178)) ([2025-03-21 19:29](https://youtu.be/c6oOmOGl5Lg?t=1169)). In the Pillarless Colosseum it is fine because shadow spells reach everything outside the boundary ([2025-12-12 5:02](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=302)).

**Quartet.** Adds an extra random Fremennik each wave; March 2024 he said it is easy with his strategy and should be taken, but the extra Fremennik also spawns on the boss and must be killed at the start ([2024-03-24 3:57](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=237)). Later opinions are negative: a big time loss for speedruns, really bad because it forces dealing with something at the start of a wave and spawns an extra enemy on Sol, and "really bad now" in July 2024 ([2024-04-01 1:38](https://youtu.be/O_eC5OV3w1o?t=98)) ([2024-06-15 9:30](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=570)) ([2024-07-26 35:41](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2141)). Its extra spawn at Sol can be used to get a free [Death Charge](Death_Charge.md) ([2024-06-15 24:00](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=1440)).

**Red Flag.** Minotaurs lose safe spotting; they gain smart pathing and walk through enemies. Avoid; wave 11 only if no other option ([2024-03-24 3:22](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=202)) ([2024-03-25 13:40](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=820)). One of the worst in the game ([2024-07-26 37:55](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2275)). In the Pillarless Colosseum it is only a bit worse than normal, though scary on wave 10 because the Minotaur cannot be hidden behind another NPC ([2025-12-12 6:38](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=398)) ([2025-12-12 17:38](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=1058)).

**Reentry / Re-entry.** Javelins fall toward the player, leaving permanent damage and creep cleaned after the wave; upgrades increase area and make it permanent for the whole Colosseum. Tier 1 on wave 10 or later, avoided if possible; tier 2 can be taken on the boss ([2024-03-24 3:06](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=186)). He called it one of the worst invocations ever, though Reentry 2 is okay at wave 11 ([2024-03-25 35:24](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=2124)); never take it per July 2024 ([2024-07-26 23:19](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1399)). In the speedrun he took Re-entry 2 on wave 11 over Quartet ([2024-04-01 10:26](https://youtu.be/O_eC5OV3w1o?t=626)) ([2024-04-01 15:27](https://youtu.be/O_eC5OV3w1o?t=927)).

**Relentless.** Before the update, enemies do not miss and max hits increase; it turns off the Torva armour's effect, so it is a non-choice until the boss ([2024-03-24 3:22](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=202)) ([2024-03-25 14:13](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=853)). Reset if forced earlier ([2024-03-24 3:22](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=202)). Later: very late it is okay, it does nothing on Sol, amazing on the boss, okay on wave 11 as a last resort, terrible early ([2024-06-15 9:30](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=570)) ([2024-06-15 24:00](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=1440)) ([2024-07-26 35:41](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2141)). He took Relentless 2 on wave 8 and 3 before Sol in 2025 ([2025-03-21 12:30](https://youtu.be/c6oOmOGl5Lg?t=750)). In the Pillarless Colosseum it is among the really bad ones ([2025-12-12 5:33](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=333)) ([2025-12-12 7:32](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=452)).

**Solar Flare.** Adds a slow-moving orbiting damage orb around the pillars; upgrades add damage and the final upgrade turns off overhead prayer for one tick ([2024-03-24 3:43](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=223)). It is as good as Myopia and the orb is easy once tagged with the ID shown on screen (12826 with Better NPC Highlight) ([2024-03-24 3:43](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=223)) ([2024-03-25 0:00](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=0)). Orb movement: two ticks per tile and five ticks on pillar corners; the final upgrade sits on corners for a single tick, i.e. two ticks on corners then one-tick speed ([2024-03-25 8:12](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=492)) ([2024-03-25 15:43](https://youtu.be/EVOs96kyHSM?t=943)) ([2024-03-26 11:24](https://youtu.be/nEIeyY_m_gw?t=684)). Damage is active every other tick ([2024-07-26 27:27](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=1647)). About 20 damage before the third tier, which disables prayer for one tick ([2024-03-26 9:56](https://youtu.be/nEIeyY_m_gw?t=596)). Standing away from the pillar avoids it ([2024-03-26 11:53](https://youtu.be/nEIeyY_m_gw?t=713)). In the Pillarless Colosseum it is especially good since it only matters against Sol ([2025-12-12 5:02](https://youtu.be/E51JMiU2gsk?t=302)).

**Totemic.** Spawns a healing totem once enemies are below 50% HP; totems have 1 HP and any weapon kills them ([2024-03-24 4:09](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=249)). Forced Totemic is an instant reset and affects the boss, but only one totem spawns for the boss ([2024-03-24 4:09](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=249)) ([2024-03-22 29:26](https://youtu.be/IR0MIzsbFeM?t=1766)). Per the April 2024 update, respawn timer 1 to 2 minutes and healing reduced from 40 to 30 percent; he says fine for the boss, a no-go for waves ([2024-04-12 5:06](https://youtu.be/dkYhPWxsOGk?t=306)) ([2024-04-12 5:35](https://youtu.be/dkYhPWxsOGk?t=335)). Bad before Sol in July 2024 ([2024-07-26 44:00](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2640)).

**Volatility.** Enemies explode on death; upgrades increase range and leave permanent creep. Usually an instant restart if forced, though tier one is theoretically manageable after wave 10; great on the boss wave ([2024-03-24 4:31](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=271)). Completely fine before Sol (July 2024) ([2024-07-26 44:00](https://youtu.be/EZs7lRnEL0Y?t=2640)).

**Others.** Mayhem is avoided because a misclick doubles the Manticore damage ([2024-06-15 6:42](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=402)). Bees is called a run killer and the worst invocation ([2024-06-15 9:30](https://youtu.be/kIfF1pNro2c?t=570)).

## April 2024 update

In the video on the new invocations (2024-04-12) Gnomonkey described these changes ([2024-04-12 1:25](https://youtu.be/dkYhPWxsOGk?t=85)) ([2024-04-12 4:06](https://youtu.be/dkYhPWxsOGk?t=246)) ([2024-04-12 4:40](https://youtu.be/dkYhPWxsOGk?t=280)) ([2024-04-12 5:06](https://youtu.be/dkYhPWxsOGk?t=306)):

- Doom stacks reset to zero at the end of each wave, death at 15, 10 or 5 stacks depending on tier, and only Colosseum sources apply stacks, so self-dealt damage such as the [Soulreaper axe](Soulreaper_axe.md) or divine potions no longer adds Doom. He calls tier 1 a completely free invocation for almost any player, though he counted 17 hits in his first completion; he takes tier 3 for speedruns, and wishes some stacks carried over ([2024-04-12 1:31](https://youtu.be/dkYhPWxsOGk?t=91)) ([2024-04-12 2:03](https://youtu.be/dkYhPWxsOGk?t=123)).
- Myopia now affects autocast spells while manual casts are unaffected; he calls this beyond dumb ([2024-04-12 4:06](https://youtu.be/dkYhPWxsOGk?t=246)).
- Relentless tiers 1 and 2 now ignore 33 and 66 percent of defence (tier 3 ignores all), with bonus minimum hits 1, 3 and 6. He now thinks tier 1 is very viable in later waves, avoiding tiers 2 and 3 ([2024-04-12 4:40](https://youtu.be/dkYhPWxsOGk?t=280)).
- Totemic: respawn 1 to 2 minutes and healing 40 to 30 percent; he wants zero-tick, guaranteed-hit totems like [Zebak](Zebak.md) jugs ([2024-04-12 5:06](https://youtu.be/dkYhPWxsOGk?t=306)).

## Suggested changes

Gnomonkey suggested Jagex add two reroll tokens, completely rebalance the invocations, and potentially remove boring ones, since many are too punishing to take. He found it frustrating that Jagex said it was only contemplating changing Doom, calling Doom absurdly bad ([2024-03-24 4:57](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=297)) ([2024-03-24 5:26](https://youtu.be/wiJAXY2rvSo?t=326)).
