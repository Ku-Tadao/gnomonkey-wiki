The **Jal-Xil**, the ranger of the [[Inferno]], first spawns on wave 18, maxes a 46 and has no unique mechanics, attacking with ranged or melee every four ticks [c1 c24 c28]. Gnomonkey describes it as incredibly basic, very accurate and inaccurate melee, and treats it above all as a source of healing and as the first enemy a player primarily flicks against [c2 c16 c24]. A ranger has about double the accuracy of a melee monster, so it is a bigger threat even though it maxes lower, and the player should not tank ranged hits [c30].

Rangers have the lowest defence of any Inferno monster besides bats and nibblers, and a big pile of health [c3]. From wave 50, rangers can hit instantly off spawn with no delay; at the start of a wave a ranger can instantly hit for as much as a 46, so health should be at 99 when starting waves that contain rangers [c4 c13]. Because the melee punch maxes only 19, being forced to be close to a ranger is not a big deal and punches are not to be feared [c10 c26 c34].

## Prayer flicking

The player starts waves praying ranged until mages spawn. Rangers are the first enemy primarily flicked, since their attack is calculated when the crouch animation starts [c2]. To flick, turn the ranged prayer on as soon as it does its animation and comes to a full standing position; knowing the exact tick it is calculated is essential for pillar stacks later [c8]. His "lazy flick" teaching changed over time: in October 2024 he said to lazy flick any attacker as the animation starts, as prayer goes up the tick the animation starts, then back down; in February 2025 he said to click the prayer on just before the ranger is about to hunker down, which saves prayer and teaches attack timing [c17 c19]. In July 2026 he recommended learning to one-tick flick rangers by turning the prayer on the instant the animation starts, mainly to know exactly when it attacks so the player can alternate with a blob [c29]. Two-tick flicking is very good for speedruns and mobility but much harder than it looks, and first-cape players should not bother [c11].

To solve a ranger and blob together, wait until the ranger has attacked, then start one-tick alternating; every fourth tick the ranger attack is covered because it attacks on an even cycle. He alternates by watching the prayer lights: keep both lit and click the next prayer as soon as the previous one turns off [c31 c32].

## Healing and targets

Rangers have a very low mage level, so the player rarely splashes and can heal off them like a medkit; they are great targets for [[Blood Barrage]] and give about 20 health on a phantom, the next best healing target after blobs [c3 c5 c12 c25]. With 108 health from the [[Blood scepter]], the player can simply go for the nibblers while casting at rangers [c18]. The [[Toxic blowpipe]] is better than a magic weapon against rangers since they have less defence than melee monsters, and rangers are a squishy target for [[Saradomin godsword]] specs [c3 c9]. Gnomonkey says to turn Rigor on before a blowpipe special for health because it gives a lot more healing on average [c33]. Rangers can be mageed pretty easily, making them a decent healing target [c28].

## Positioning and pillars

Never kill rangers first: killing them is wasting time in mage plus ranger waves, and if a mage respawns it places the ranger behind the mage, creating a bad pillar stack. Avoid killing a ranger unless necessary [c7 c14 c21 c27]. A ranger can serve as a pillar the same way as a mage, since any monster works as a pillar [c15]. It can see the player from the corner of a pillar as it walks up, and still sees the player for a moment after stepping out of line, so this matters when going for nibblers; casting from behind a pillar at nibblers works even if they are directly on the pillar [c6 c20 c22]. On the double ranger plus mage wave, go to the side without the mage, since the mage hit is almost guaranteed while ranger hits have around 40% accuracy [c23].
