**Jal-MejRah** (the **bats**) are monsters of the [[Inferno]] that spawn from the very first wave [c1]. Gnomonkey treats them as the highest kill priority whenever they are on the player: they hit hard, drain stats and run energy, and he has seen people die to a couple of bats [c11 c30]. His advice has been the same since 2021: do not tank bats, kill them first [c5 c14 c22].

## Combat stats

* Max hit 19 with ranged attacks, which can be devastating if two are tanked at once [c1 c21 c26]. Two bats hurt a lot and push things around and stack things up. He took about forty damage from two bats while praying Rigor [c9].
* Bats attack every three game ticks, the fastest attacking mob in the Inferno [c25].
* Bats have a short range of four tiles, whereas the blowpipe has a maximum range of five [c2 c23 c31]. They and the nibblers are the only Inferno monsters with short range [c2].
* Bats cannot melee, while blobs can [c16 c20].
* Bats have negative 20 mage defence [c17].

## Drains

Every hit drains run energy by three, including zero hits, and it happens even while praying [c3 c21]. A hit off prayer also has a chance to drain a combat stat by one, which the bat adds to its own stat [c3 c26]. Being drained below 94 Magic removes the player's autocast [c3 c10].

As of a Jagex patch (noted in 2021), bats can no longer drain stats if the player prays ranged correctly, but they still drain run energy [c4]. Gnomonkey therefore prays ranged at the start of waves where bats spawn [c32]. Bats also heal off the player and boost their own stats when they hit, so a shot that would kill them can let them heal and survive [c12].

## Safe spot and tactics

* **Safe spot.** A tile where the bats cannot reach the player but a blowpipe can, since bats have four-tile range and the blowpipe five [c13 c19]. One such tile is one tile away from the corner of the pillar, which lets the player shoot bats even on rapid without being attacked [c5]. The player can avoid an attack by stepping one corner out from the pillar to just outside range [c28]. The same tile doubles as a melee corner trap tile when a melee is about to dig [c29]. Killing bats from the safe spot stops them draining run [c15].
* **Priority.** When several monsters are on the player, always kill the bats first [c14 c22 c27].
* **Special attacks.** A great time to use special attacks is on bats [c7]. He double pipe-specs them to kill them instantly, saying the blowpipe spec is the "kill button" rather than the "heal button" [c24].
* **Rigor.** Anytime two bats must be killed, he usually puts on Rigor, since it gives defence [c8].
* **Healing.** The best targets for phantom heals are bats, blobs, bloblets and later rangers [c6]. Bats are decent monsters to heal off, and good health can be gained from them with blood barrage [c17 c33].
