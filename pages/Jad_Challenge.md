The **Jad Challenge** is PvM content released in March 2021 for players who have completed the [[Inferno]] [c1]. It is a series of six challenges that put the player against 1 to 6 Jads at once. Gnomonkey considers 4 Jads and under fairly simple with some attempts, so his guide focuses on 5 and 6 Jads as by far the hardest challenges [c14]. He says strategy only helps so much, and that what matters is throwing attempt after attempt until the prayer timings are down [c23].

## Requirements and rewards

A fire cape unlocks the first two challenges (1 Jad and 2 Jads); the next four, from 3 to 6 Jads, require an infernal cape [c2]. The 1 and 2 Jad challenges are good for players who want to get more comfortable with the Inferno Jads [c3]. Completing the 6 Jad challenge unlocks a transmog that turns Jad pets into the Inferno variant, and the challenges also give some tokkul for challenges completed [c4].

## Gear and supplies

Supplies are not really the worry, so the aim is the best ranged DPS gear available [c7]. Gnomonkey's recommended gear is max ranged: Armadyl, Pegasian boots, Anguish, Barrows gloves and dragon arrows. God d'hide can be used as a downgrade and the completion is still easily possible [c9]. He highly recommends a twisted bow for 6 Jad, and says it is definitely possible with just a toxic blowpipe but much harder [c6]. Elite void ranged is around 4% worse DPS than Armadyl against Jad because of Jad's high defence, and gives worse defence, so he does not recommend void [c8].

At 99 Prayer, the inventory for 5 Jad and under needs around three prayer potions plus a Divine ranging potion, with the rest being the best food and optionally some chinchompas [c10]. For 6 Jad he adds another prayer potion to be safe, and says one extra anglerfish can be the difference maker [c11].

A Divine ranging or Bastion potion and anglerfish up to 121 HP can be pre-potted before entering [c17]. An imbued heart can also be pre-potted since it resets every attempt, but he says it makes a very small difference for tanking Jad's magic hits [c18].

### Chinchompas

Chinchompas are great for tagging healers but very difficult to use before one or two of the Jads have been killed in harder modes; Gnomonkey tags healers with his twisted bow personally [c12]. They speed things up significantly and are basically necessary with a blowpipe. The type does not matter, and since grey and red chinchompas had similar prices in March 2021, he says red chins were probably best [c13].

## Strategy

Anything that reduces the number of clicks helps dramatically [c19]. For 5 and 6 Jad, he highly recommends re-entering without pre-potting for a while to practise the prayer timings [c15]. The Jads in the lower challenges have spaced-out, relatively normal attack cycles [c16].

### Taking hits

If the player is overhealed, Jad cannot one-shot them. Gnomonkey says it is absolutely possible to take Jad hits, as he took multiple in his successful run, and what matters is how they are handled [c20]. After a hit, the player should not panic: eat an anglerfish in between Jad cycles, repeat until back to stable health, then click back on Jad and refocus on prayer switching [c21]. On an unlucky spawn where several healers attack the player, healing may be needed while killing the starting Jads [c22].

### 5 and 6 Jad

Six Jad is much simpler than five in layout but much harder anyway, because from the north Jad all the way around to the northwest Jad there is no delay between attacks [c24]. Prayer must be switched on each tick an attack comes out. Gnomonkey waits for a Jad's projectile to be on screen before switching, and if the Jads are alternating the next prayer can be clicked as soon as the prayer activates [c25]. The only time other actions are really possible is after the northwest Jad's attack at the end of a full cycle, when there are two ticks before the next Jad's attack is fired [c26].

Five Jads is barely any easier than six, since there is still tick-perfect timing for five attacks with just one extra tick between cycles [c27].

## Records

In Gnomonkey's 6 Jad run he tanked several Jad hits, including one for 98 while healers were also attacking him, healed up carefully when possible and still completed the challenge [c28].
