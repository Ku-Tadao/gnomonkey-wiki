The **Toxic blowpipe** is a cheap [[Ranged]] weapon that Gnomonkey regards as the clearest example of power creep in the game. Since 2020 he has called it the best main weapon for the [[Fight Caves]] and, other than the [[Twisted bow]] (or the [[Dragon hunter crossbow]] in specific situations), the highest-DPS ranged weapon. [c1 c4 c8] In 2022 he called it the worst power creep in OSRS history by far, and in 2025 he still uses it as a heal and a damage special across the Inferno, Tombs of Amascut and Doom of Mokhaiotl. [c22 c27 c34 c43]

## Why it is strong

It is one of the few weapons giving both ranged strength and accuracy, and darts do not slow it: it still attacks every two ticks on rapid. [c6] Gnomonkey says it was intended to be three ticks, but a Jmod forgot that rapid reduces attack speed by a tick. [c23] It was added with [[Zulrah]] near the start of OSRS, when developers were less experienced and had worse tools. [c22 c24] Even after the Twisted bow arrived, it challenged that weapon in many locations, with a mid-level boss dropping arguably the best ranged weapon in the game. [c24] The developers later nerfed it so that weapons like the [[Bow of Faerdhinen]] could shine in their tier, but he still thinks it is fairly overpowered and says it has shaped the direction of the game. [c25]

All [[Fight Caves]] enemies can be envenomed except Jad, who can only be poisoned, and the DPS plus venom make it the clear choice; on its own it annihilates every enemy in the cave. [c2 c4] A Twisted bow is better against Jad and the 360s if the player can afford one. [c3]

## Cost and darts

In 2020 the blowpipe cost about 400k gp/hr in scale cost alone, before darts, and about 450k gp/hr with adamant darts (more if the darts are left on the ground). [c5 c9 c10] Because it fires every two ticks, darts are lost faster than on other ranged weapons. [c12]

| Dart | Ranged strength | Added cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adamant | +10 | About 45k gp/hr | Gnomonkey's baseline; use almost always [c10 c11 c16] |
| Rune | +14 | Just under 600k gp/hr | [c10 c11] |
| Dragon | +20 | Just under 1.5m gp/hr, about 1m gp/hr over rune | [c10 c11 c17] |

Each dart tier has a small accuracy bonus and typically gives one extra max hit, roughly 4% DPS. [c11] That upgrade is worth using only when making over 10m gp/hr, and it breaks even on paper at just over 12m gp/hr. [c13] In 2020 he knew of only the [[Theatre of Blood]] (up to 11m gp/hr with perfect trios) and the [[Nightmare]], where the blowpipe is not used, earning that much. [c14] Skeletal Mystics and the [[Chambers of Xeric]] are among the few high-defence targets where the pipe is used, and there the upgrade is about 5% DPS. [c15] He says dragon darts, over 20m gp/hr to pay off, are only for pet hunters or boss kill count high scores. [c17]

By 2025 he treats dragon darts as the standard in demanding content: a dragon-dart pipe is basically always better than a Bofa except on triple Jads and Zuk, so it can be main-handed. Amethyst darts cost only one max hit on normal attacks and two on specs. [c37 c40 c42]

## Special attack

The special does double damage and double accuracy, so it is good for killing and not just healing. [c29 c39] It heals the user, which makes it a staple at [[Zulrah]] even when the pipe is not the main weapon. [c27] He recommends using it even at full health just to start the spec energy recharging. [c28 c31]

* Best used on the tankiest enemy, the melee, whenever the player has spec and full health; a good time to spec is while tanking damage. [c30 c44]
* Spec bats rather than tanking them, since bats drain stats and run energy, slow the player and hit hard. [c32]
* A mage under about 30 HP can be finished with the blowpipe, saving about three ticks over a Twisted bow shot that would deal only about five. [c33]
* If all else fails against a melee, use [[Deadeye]] or Rigour with specs, which heal with the [[Light bearer]]. [c39]

## Usage notes

| Place | Advice |
|---|---|
| Zulrah | Overtakes the [[Atlatl]] only with at least rune darts, since the atlatl is a lot more accurate. [c26] |
| Grotesque Guardians | Fill with dragon darts, or the heal skip is rarely achieved; Barrows gloves are fine since Zaryte vambraces are a very small upgrade. [c18 c19] During Dawn, move to a set tile between shots while praying ranged and Rigour, which two-tick offs both bosses; in the heal-orb phase stand next to Dawn on melee prayer and Rigour and DPS her instead of chasing orbs. [c20 c21] |
| Doom of Mokhaiotl | Without a ZCB the budget specs are the blowpipe and [[Dragon knife]]s; the pipe heals, the knives cost half the spec energy and hit slightly higher but do not heal, so he brings both. [c34 c35] A dragon-dart pipe is about 5% worse than a Twisted bow and about 11% better than the Scorching bow on the car phase, which he calls one of the most ignored important items. [c36 c37] Spamming knife specs, or anything with double hitsplats, spawns a lot of acid on delve 3 and above. [c38] |
| Inferno | The Inferno cannot be learned without a pipe, since it is vital for Zuk's healers; tagging them all with Bofa would add about 15 shots. [c43] A Bofa is better against tanky targets if only amethyst darts are available, but a viewer noted the pipe is better whenever range potted, which is most of the time on waves that matter. [c41 c42 c45] |
