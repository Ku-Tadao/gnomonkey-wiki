The **Nex gear setup** is Gnomonkey's evolving advice on what to bring to [[Nex]], from the 2022 Zaryte crossbow setup to the budget duo setups of 2023 and 2024. Core themes: a crossbow for the boss, a Twisted bow or pipe for the minions, an ancient item for the altar, and thralls and Death Charge for DPS. [c36 c37 c39]

## Weapons

His April 2022 setup added a ZCB (Zaryte crossbow) switch and a shield switch, which also requires bolts. [c1] The ZCB is around 15% better DPS on Nex than the Twisted bow without counting its special attack, but the Twisted bow is still incredibly good against the minions. [c2] In May 2023 he is torn between a six-way (BGS/Fang) with a ZCB and a Twisted bow with Pegasian boots and Assembler, noting the T-bow is only 2% worse than the ZCB when just autoing at full ranged potion. [c8] The T-bow is really good for minions and Reavers; the ZCB is really good for potting down; he sees the choice as very close. [c9] In July 2023 an eight-way Twisted bow setup with Assembler is within 2% of a ZCB, so a ZCB need not be bought. [c23]

By June 2024 he states a Twisted bow plus Zaryte crossbow is the meta, with the Twisted bow replacing the blowpipe. [c35] Because Nex is weak to crossbows, an adamant crossbow does more than a Twisted bow on Nex, so a crossbow is always brought and the Twisted bow never camped; the Twisted bow is for minions and the crossbow for the boss. [c36 c37] An adamant crossbow beats a rune crossbow since its higher range lets players flinch out of Nex's close-range damage, though both fire rubies. [c41] The toxic blowpipe is second best for minions but poor against high defence. [c38] He tells people never to bring the Bow of Faerdhinen: it is worse than an adamant crossbow on Nex and worse than a blowpipe on minions. [c40]

### Special attacks and melee

* Voidwaker is a better special than the Claws/ZCB option [c10], roughly 15% more efficient per spec than the ZCB (July 2023). [c22] By June 2024 he lists Voidwaker or ZCB as better than Fang, with the ZCB best in slot for spec. [c27] He typically uses Voidwaker on phase 2: one spec used to leave 100% energy and sit on it. [c14]
* No good special weapon exists before ZCB or Voidwaker, so use Fang specs until then. [c21] The [[Osmumten's fang]] is about 35% better than a crossbow at Nex and also the fallback spec weapon. [c27] The Lightbearer is better with a good spec weapon, since Fang max hits matter little when it is so accurate. [c25]
* Upgrade path (July 2023): Masori top and bottom first, then Voidwaker, then sell it for a ZCB, then sell the ZCB for a Twisted bow. Do not buy Dragon claws (about 90m) because claws are bad. [c20] In June 2024: Voidwaker (about 80m then) is the next major purchase and way better than Fang specs; sell it if a ZCB later becomes affordable, though it is a solid backup. [c32]
* Pharaoh's gloves give a huge strength bonus, so a bracers switch is best. [c37] Budget melee uses Ferocious gloves, Primordial boots and a regular dragon defender; the Avernic defender (about 50m) is the next upgrade. [c31] Do not bring a rune defender: the 5 stab attack bonus lost matters more than 1 strength. [c45]

## Armour

| Setup | Notes |
|---|---|
| Karil's (budget duo, 2023) | Good mage defence and no negative melee stats, with the best melee gear otherwise, a Fang, a four-way range switch, an ACB and a pipe for minions and Reavers. [c15] Cannot work at the high end; without Masori or better you get shredded. [c26] |
| Masori | First upgrade, the most important: better mage defence, a good ranged bonus and no negative melee, way better than Karil's. [c17] |
| Mixed hide (budget, 2024) | Lower max hit than Bandos but with a stab bonus, so better DPS at Nex; doubles as ranged armour and is extremely cheap. [c28] Without Masori, run Mixed hide; with it, camping Masori is slightly faster, and Mixed hide with a Masori switch is faster still as you improve. [c29] |
| Torva | Full Torva for duo Nex reduces a lot of damage; about the same supplies (two brews dropped), allowing melee on phase 2 and speeding it up dramatically. [c11 c24] The Torva helmet alone is a solid upgrade for defence and max hit. [c35] Extra potions are fine for learning rather than skipping full Torva. [c47] |

The cape swap and Masori helmet switch are not max hits, so a struggling setup can drop the switch, bring an extra potion and dump the bolts. [c30] Without a Quiver (no ammo slot), ditch the pegs (about 1% better than none, a fairly big DPS increase) [c46] and swap the boots; a boot switch is not necessary but very nice. [c49] In the budget setup he skips the Light bearer because only Fang specs are available, and skips Death Charge as the specs are bad anyway. [c16]

Always wear the Lightbearer at Nex: it is better DPS than any other ring, including an Ultor or Venator ring, even with Fang specs, and he would not recommend Nex until you own one. [c42] The Buckler gives about 5% DPS when ranging and is extremely good [c50], though in 2022 he saw it as not worth the inventory slot over a brew. [c3]

## Shield

In 2022 he ranks Elysian over Spectral; with neither, run no shield. [c3] The Elysian saves roughly two brew doses per kill; the Spectral saves a bit less but also prayer. [c4] Whenever Nex targets him he camps the shield to prevent the damage, then returns to a Vernic; he flicks it whenever possible to lengthen trips, and supplies are shared by who was targeted most. [c5] He hits Nex a tick early at the start so shield flicking is easy. [c6] With a Spectral you can take the virus and camp it, reducing the prayer drain. [c7]

## Altar and supplies

The altar gives full health, prayer, run energy and spec restore, but only with an ancient item equipped. [c34] Bring some Zaros item (such as ancient bracers) to use the Ultar altar: without one it restores only prayer. A ZCB counts. [c18] Zaryte vambraces (about 100m, previously about 180m) are recommended for this. [c33] If you leave without an ancient item equipped, wear the Zaryte item or you will be hit and killed. [c43]

* Drink Menaphite remedy first: it restores stats every 15 seconds like an overload when brewed down, and cancels divine potions if drunk after them. [c19] A Super Combat minify potion is a nice pre-pot since it restores stats after brewing and on virus. [c54]
* Peak 2023 baseline: four Prayer restores (his setup saves about a dose; five or six when learning) [c12], and seven brews (about eight when learning, dropping the Buckler). [c13] In 2024 he brings five resource and eight brews, and says four resource potions are plenty for a normal kill. [c52] The second range potion is optional; ditch it for an extra brew or restore if struggling. [c51]
* Bring runes for Death Charge and thralls; thralls are a huge part of DPS because they hit through Nex's very high defence ([[Thralls]]). [c39] Fire runes go in the pouch; Death Charge works on minions and Reavers and carries into the next kill. [c53]
* It is hard for a learner in low gear to find a Nex duo, since poor gear and poor Nex skill go together, though kills in bad gear are absolutely doable. [c44]
* He ranks the ZCB with a nine-way (fast switching only mattering for Blood Sacrifice) and the Twisted bow with an eight-way for quick minion switches. [c48]
