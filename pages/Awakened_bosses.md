**Awakened bosses** are the harder versions of the [[Desert Treasure II]] bosses, entered by spending an [[Awakener's orb]]. Gnomonkey considers the fights excellent but the orb system one of the biggest mistakes, possibly the biggest, Jagex has made with high-level content. [c14 c36] His core objection is that the system gates content by GP rather than skill, which he says actively dissuades people from engaging with it. [c6 c26]

## Orb system

Awakener's orbs are a fairly rare drop from the Desert Treasure 2 bosses, with the rate varying by boss. Taking one to its boss allows a single attempt at the awakened version. [c10] The orb is consumed on entering the boss room, whether the player dies or kills the boss. [c11] Killing all four awakened bosses makes the fourth drop the ancient blood ornament kit, which with a lot of blood runes converts Torva into its Blorva variant. [c13]

### Cost

At the time of the May 2025 video orbs cost roughly 900k each, varying a lot, compared with around 3-4 million on release. [c12] Players Gnomonkey asked in his Discord report that Blorva usually takes around 100 to 200 orbs, many using 200 to 300 and some spending over 100 on Vardorvis or Leviathan, or both. [c15] At 900k, 300 orbs is 270 million GP and a lucky 100 is 90 million. [c17] He estimates an orb drops about every hour to hour and a half, so 100-200 orbs is 100-200+ hours and realistically 500-600 hours for most players, which gates ironmen from the content. [c25]

| Player | Spend |
|---|---|
| Gnomonkey | about 1.5 billion GP on orbs chasing a no-stun attempt, at 3-4m each, without success [c18] |
| Noobtype | about 4 billion on the 27-orb Leviathan challenge and about 6 billion for no stun, about 10 billion total [c19] |
| Saks | 3-4 billion on a world record Vardorvis attempt, not achieved; about 7 to 10 billion on Whisperer and Duke [c20] |
| Porkazar | over 3 billion on the 54 Awakened Leviathan challenge [c22] |

For scale, 10 billion GP would buy every equippable item that would be cool to use at a boss, including the harm, volatile and eldritch staves, the scythe, Tumeken's shadow and full Torva. [c21]

## Gnomonkey's criticism

* Once Blorva is unlocked, players can still buy orbs but have no affordable way to engage with the content, such as speedruns or challenges, unless very wealthy. [c16] He and others decided after getting Blorva that they cannot afford to keep killing awakened bosses, so they do not. [c28]
* Cost-per-attempt aspirational content keeps people from trying new things; with Blorva a player who does not get it in about 150 attempts runs out of money. Such systems only make sense if an attempt gives great loot or GP, not a cosmetic, and he believes content should be gated only by skill. [c5 c6]
* Gatekeeping makes the cosmetic prestigious in the wrong way, about GP rather than skill, and encourages servicing. [c8] He has soloed seven shadows yet cannot afford awakened bosses, because only players selling services or leeches can, not those earning GP through PvM. [c23]
* Awakened Leviathan is in his top five bosses, but he cannot afford its challenges or speedruns. [c24]
* He disagrees that Blorva is mostly a GP flex rather than skill but calls it both, and thinks it would be a purer skill flex if players could attempt it earlier in worse gear such as a Bow of Faerdhinen. [c33]
* High-level updates are too rare to spend on a one-time cosmetic without repeatability. Wealth siphoning does not need this system and has been done many times before. [c34 c35]

### Profit analysis

Using Temple OSRS EHB of 21 kills per hour for Whisperer and 39 for Vardorvis, and subtracting the Soul Reaper axe, he calculates about 10 million GP per hour at Vardorvis, with orbs worth about 430k (roughly 4.3% of profit), and about 5.4 million GP per hour at Whisperer, with orbs worth about 540k (roughly 10%). [c29 c30] He concludes orbs add only 5 to 10 percent of profit, minor next to the cost of gatekeeping learners and challenge players. [c31]

## Proposed fixes

* Make the Sanguine kit work like the Echo Orbs from Leagues, with permanent unlimited access once earned. [c27]
* Pay around 50 orbs once for a splash pass granting unlimited access, or pay to enter once with free re-entry after a successful kill. [c7]
* Gnomonkey said in May 2025 that Jmods had said they are willing to add a permanent unlock method. [c32]

## Comparison with the Yama contract system

In May 2025 he said the [[Yama]] contract system shares similarities with orbs but differs in several ways, for instance cosmetic contracts being target farmable and likely much cheaper. [c1] He believes it is better than orbs, with no question, but it keeps the main issue: after completing everything, one can only continue by buying more contracts, with no free or no-loot access mentioned. Jagex took the terrible orb system and tweaked it, but he considers the approach wrong. [c2 c3 c9] Even at a hypothetical 250k per contract, a quarter of an orb, contracts would still not allow grinding, challenges, speedruns or casual fights. [c4] Cheaper contracts would also lessen the profit benefit for those who farm them. [c34]

Later that month he said the awakened bosses surprised him and were pretty good, with Awakened Leviathan and Awakened Vardorvis among the best fights he has done, and even the two he likes less beat any of the Yama contracts. [c36 c37]
