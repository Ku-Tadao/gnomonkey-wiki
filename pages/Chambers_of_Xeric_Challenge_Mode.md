**Chambers of Xeric: Challenge Mode** (CM) is a regular [[Chambers of Xeric]] with every room's stats cranked: everything hits harder, is tankier and has more health [c22]. Gnomonkey considers it genuinely one of the toughest pieces of content in the game. He had not learned it until early 2023, when he began speedrunning it on Twitch [c1]. It has by far the highest gear and stat requirements of any raid [c3]. It is not for money: anyone running it is definitely not doing it for the gp [c2 c9].

The benefit over regular Chambers is that there is no scouting and the same raid is done every time, but it is harder [c24]. CM gives extra kits at the end and bonus points if the time is made [c23]. In late 2024 he advises doing regular Chambers instead unless a player has at least a Twisted bow, Sanguinesti staff and a decent mage setup, because the only reason to do CM is the kit chance and normal raids give better purples even counting scouting time [c30].

## Requirements
* **Twisted bow:** the player should not enter without one. With only a Bow of Faerdhinen he says the run will go badly and regular Chambers, which gives more loot per hour, should be done instead [c25]. See [[Twisted bow]], whose damage cap is higher still in CM, maxing around 96 on quite a few bosses [c26].
* **Not needed:** a Scythe of Vitur. Everything is so tanky that an Osmumten's fang is effectively the same as a scythe, and a Lance is basically the same as a scythe at Olm [c27].
* **Valuable items used almost nowhere else**, such as Inquisitor's armour and mace and a harmonised nightmare staff for the Ice Demon [c4].
* **Skills:**
  * 95 Thieving to receive three grubs from the chest. Some setups use a Thieving cape to boost to 100 for a chance at four grubs [c5].
  * 96 Woodcutting for a chance at 8 kindling from the Ice Demon, with 99 preferred to speed up the Muttadile tree [c6].
  * In his setup, 99 Mining, or a max hit is lost [c7].
* **Purple sweets:** an absurd number are needed to be competitive [c7]. A solo CM eats more than a solo Theatre of Blood, but they are paid for by the drops received, with some profit on the side [c8].

## Mechanics and layout
The room layout and boss order are always the same, starting with Tekton [c12 c28]. Each room drops supplies such as overload, prayer enhance and restore, so few potions need to be brought and more can be farmed as the run goes [c28]. Gnomonkey describes the raid as similar to regular Chambers, the difference being whether problems can be fixed quickly when Olm throws a lot of garbage at the player, since hits are much harder when things go wrong [c29]. Much of the skill is preparing for the following room and keeping inventory and banking under control, a skill not used much in other raids [c2].

## Setup
Gnomonkey runs the Surge Thrall setup: the standard spellbook through the Ice Demon, then a Max cape or Magic cape swap to the Arceuus spellbook for thralls. He calls it by far the fastest setup [c10]. In his 2023 speedrun he kills the Ice Demon with an Inquisitor's armour set and a dragon warhammer [c17]. In 2024 he says that with the Twisted bow loadout from his first run he made the solo time even while AFK-ing about 20 to 30 minutes explaining rooms, and that the Grandmaster time of 38:30 is feasible with this setup [c31].

He can do at most five CM raids a day, typically for a few hours on stream, and jokes that Grandmasters of Combat Achievements should get a bonus to cape swaps [c11].

## Speedrunning
| Time | Detail |
|---|---|
| Solo world record | 26:38, held by rot salad (video description, 2023) [c16] |
| Gnomonkey, 2023 PB | 27:04.2, replacing 27:51, still ranked sixth [c21] |
| Floor 3 pace | 12:40 in his May 2023 run, which he considered not great. Low twelves are nice and sub twelves very fast [c18] |
| Hole on the last floor | Targets sub 13 minutes, with his best barely sub 12 [c13] |
| Olm | If pacing sub 28:30 he reaches Olm in under 20 minutes on the clock [c14] |

In February 2023 he was still three seconds off a sub 28:30 time [c14]. In the May 2023 run, six zero hits in a row on lances in the middle of Olm and a bit less trolling cost what would likely have been a world record Olm at the very least [c19].

After a raid he tries to remember that purples do not exist: the goal is a good time, not paying back the hundreds of sweets used [c15].

### Room ratings (May 2023)
Reviewing the raid backwards, he rated Olm, Vasa and Guardians as disgusting (very good), Mystics pretty good, Muttadile kind of bad, Vespula, thieving and Tekton average, and Vanguards and Shamans bad [c20].
