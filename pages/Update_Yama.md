The **Yama update** covers the run-up to the release of the boss [[Yama]], which Gnomonkey treated across a blog reaction on 9 May 2025 and a summary of a Jmod Reddit AMA on 13 May 2025 [c1 c15]. Yama is one of two high-level updates in the year, alongside Mocha, and the previous one, the Coliseum, came roughly a year earlier [c14]. Gnomonkey is very excited for the boss and planned to do it on release despite concerns about its drop system [c5].

## Contracts

Yama contracts are a drop and are tradable, but full details of the system had not been released [c7]. A moderator in Gnomonkey's chat said they are used on entry, so players should hope they can finish the fight or lose the GP spent on the contract [c6]. In the Reddit thread, Mod Goblin confirmed they are not a pay-GP-for-better-drop-rates system, the same stated goal as [[Awakener's Orb]]s, that they mechanically change the boss, and that they are used to unlock the white variant of Oathplate, an ornament kit for the new armour [c9 c10]. Gnomonkey compares the system to the Awakener's Orb setup, where unlocking Blorva requires the four awakened bosses [c11]. Mod Goblin felt the contract system is a lot better than the Awakener's Orb one, which Gnomonkey said could be true but cannot be judged without full details [c8].

Gnomonkey's concerns, from the 9 May video:

* He is happy that Jagex shared an early blog but is not excited or at ease with most of it [c1].
* Contracts being off for the first week after release kills much of the hype, since he wanted to rush the hard variant on day one for a world first. He considers this mostly a streamer issue and would prefer the hard variant be available instantly [c2 c3].
* A week before launch, many community complaints cannot be fixed, particularly because contracts are tied to the market. By release there will be no way to remove contracts from banks, so he wants Jagex to explain exactly how they work while there is still time [c4 c12].

## Plans

Gnomonkey plans to stream Yama as soon as it is released, duo with Saks, attempt a world-first solo, then publish a guide quickly and try challenges such as a lower-budget solo [c13 c35]. He encourages viewers to try it on release day, as it seems doable without an Inferno cape or a duo partner, and to keep giving feedback since Jmods said they are willing to rework things [c36].
