The Corrupted Gauntlet is a timed prep-then-fight activity in which the player gathers resources and crafts gear in a dungeon before fighting the [Corrupted Hunllef](Corrupted_Hunllef.md). Gnomonkey has covered it since 2020 as a no-gear-cost money method and the source of the [Bow of Faerdhinen](Bow_of_Faerdhinen.md); his advice changed substantially after the 2026 Summer Sweep-up, which made prep much faster.

## Requirements

- The Gauntlet requires the Song of the Elves quest, which needs many quests and level 70 in most stats (([2020-04-14 2:05](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=125))). In 2024 he adds that decent stats and the three prayers unlocked are needed (([2024-11-02 0:29](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=29))).
- 2020: at least 85 in all combat stats, a very high Prayer level and Rigor unlocked, since one struggles significantly without Rigor (([2020-04-14 2:05](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=125))). Augury is not needed with his method but may help in an unlucky run (([2020-04-14 2:36](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=156))).
- 2022: 7.5 completions an hour would not be possible until Magic and Ranged were maxed and Augury and Rigor unlocked (([2022-08-03 2:00](https://youtu.be/6OkBpQdcXRw?t=120))).
- 2026: base 80 combat stats is enough for consistent kills and for starting tier one armour; AFKing combat stats first makes it easier (([2026-07-22 0:57](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=57)) ([2026-07-22 30:57](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=1857))).
- Ironmen should try to get Rigor and Augury beforehand, since they are locked behind [Chambers of Xeric](Chambers_of_Xeric.md); Piety with the halberd is great and a Bow of Faerdhinen is not needed that early (([2024-11-02 25:50](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=1550))).
- It has no gear requirement and no death cost (([2024-11-02 0:00](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=0))). Any armour or inventory is allowed on entry, but pets cannot be brought in (([2020-04-14 3:03](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=183))).
- 2020 recommendation: hardcore ironmen should stay away because death is not safe (([2020-04-14 1:32](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=92))); see [Tier 3 prep for hardcore ironmen](#tier-3-prep-for-hardcore-ironmen) for his 2026 position.

## Overview of a run

- The player has seven and a half minutes (the maximum) to gather the items needed and then fight the Corrupted Hunllef in the middle of the dungeon (([2024-11-02 0:56](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=56))). If prep time runs out the player is teleported in, or can walk in (([2026-07-22 13:02](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=782))).
- In 2020 he described the Corrupted Hunllef as one of the toughest bosses in RuneScape, with 1000 health, a max hit of 58 and the ability to protect against the player's attacks (([2020-04-14 0:00](https://youtu.be/nOgO8mPz0SI?t=0))); he entered with tier three halberd and bow and a full set of tier one armour (([2020-04-14 0:00](https://youtu.be/nOgO8mPz0SI?t=0))).
- Gnomonkey compares it to RS2 Dungeoneering (([2020-04-14 3:37](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=217))). The player starts with a scepter that opens rooms, carried to the end (about a mithril mace in bonuses), and a hatchet, pickaxe, harpoon, pestle and mortar and one teleport seed (([2020-04-14 3:37](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=217)) ([2020-04-14 4:04](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=244))). If the scepter is dropped, rooms cannot be opened (([2026-07-22 4:58](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=298))). The centre has a singing bowl for crafting, a water pump for vials and a range for fish (([2024-11-02 1:26](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=86)) ([2026-07-22 4:58](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=298))).
- 2020 goal: tier three bow and halberd, three potions and 25 paddlefish (([2020-04-14 4:04](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=244))). 2024 prep: two tier three weapons, two potions (two vials, 20 shards, two herbs), tier one armour and a full inventory of fish (([2024-11-02 2:30](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=150))). 2026: three prayer potions (two at very high Prayer), a set of armour, two tier three weapons and as much fish as can be held while learning (([2026-07-22 4:58](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=298))).
- Paddlefish heal 20; a Gauntlet potion restores a flat 30 prayer plus 40 run energy (([2020-04-14 4:30](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=270))). Tier three weapons have far higher DPS than main-game weapons since boosts cannot be used (([2020-04-14 4:30](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=270))).
- Two tier three weapons are needed because the Hunllef's protection prayer switches to the style being used after six hits (([2024-11-02 3:32](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=212))). A full set of tier one armour lowers his max hit to 13; a partial set does not (([2024-11-02 3:02](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=182))).
- Total shard needs (2024): 320 shards, or 340 if the staff upgrade is not obtained (([2024-11-02 4:00](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=240))).

## Prep guidance

**Two-run method (2020).** Gnomonkey used two passes through the dungeon. Run one: a C-shape around the start for three ores, wood and wool (tier one armour), paddlefish, a weapon frame, spiders, bats and rats, avoiding high-level wolves, unicorns and scorpions, which always drop a frame (([2020-04-14 5:01](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=301)) ([2020-04-14 5:27](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=327))). Craft a tier two bow, then the armour with body first, legs and helmet after (([2020-04-14 6:26](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=386))). Run two: demi-bosses (dark beast, bear, dragon) for the spike and bowstring, then the tier three weapons (([2020-04-14 6:55](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=415)) ([2020-04-14 7:24](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=444))). He did not want the dragon's orb staff because Augury does not boost damage like other prayers (([2020-04-14 7:55](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=475))). Holding an orb when killing a second bear or dark beast guarantees the wanted part; without one it is 50/50 for a piece not already held (([2020-04-14 8:22](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=502))). He spam-clicked the oven to cook in one tick (([2020-04-14 6:26](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=386))).

**Tier one prep (2024).** As soon as a weapon frame is held, make a tier one staff and two vials (40 shards), since early staff tiers are the best (([2024-11-02 5:54](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=354))). Tier one armour needs three each of wool, ore and bark (([2024-11-02 7:58](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=478))). Tier three weapons need demi-boss materials from the three outer edge rooms (([2024-11-02 6:36](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=396))). Harvest and attack at once, and make a teleport crystal at the bowl before a second trip (([2024-11-02 20:15](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=1215))). Vials can be filled from fishing pots; he advises not opening side rooms from the edge (([2024-11-02 9:03](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=543))). Combo food is worse than regular fish, except for hardcore ironmen (([2024-11-02 33:50](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=2030))). Examples left three minutes, and about five minutes (([2024-11-02 10:33](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=633)) ([2024-11-02 21:02](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=1262))). He never used tier two armour (([2024-11-02 17:53](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=1073))).

**Weapons.** The halberd has the highest max hit and DPS by a little but is harder because of its one-time range and cannot be spammed while running (([2024-11-02 20:42](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=1242))); all three tier three weapons are almost equal (([2024-11-02 20:45](https://youtu.be/6twm5s6DZHg?t=1245))). In 2026 he considers bow and staff the easiest, the halberd awkward for beginners (([2026-07-22 8:26](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=506))), and always makes a tier two staff early (([2026-07-22 7:30](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=450))). A second dark beast drops the orb rather than a second bow upgrade (([2026-07-22 8:26](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=506))).

**Tools and plugins.** RuneLite: XP globes off, UI cleaned up, health bars removed, F keys rebound to number keys (([2020-04-14 3:03](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=183))). In 2026: the Gauntlet plugin (resource tracking, tornado true tile), Tile Indicators, CG Recolor (not with HD), and an attack timer metronome (([2026-02-07 0:33](https://youtu.be/R6F2mWXa_fM?t=33)) ([2026-02-07 1:04](https://youtu.be/R6F2mWXa_fM?t=64))); resource counts grey out once satisfied (([2026-07-22 7:52](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=472))).

## Tier 3 prep for hardcore ironmen

In February 2026 Gnomonkey described tier 3 prep as a harder prep for an easier, safer fight, mainly for Hardcore Ironmen and for players chasing the combat achievement (([2026-02-07 0:00](https://youtu.be/R6F2mWXa_fM?t=0)) ([2026-02-07 0:33](https://youtu.be/R6F2mWXa_fM?t=33))). Armour makes the boss a standard fight (([2026-02-07 10:55](https://youtu.be/R6F2mWXa_fM?t=655))). He advised 65 to 70 HP or more to survive one tick of all four tornadoes (which can max 60) (([2026-02-07 10:59](https://youtu.be/R6F2mWXa_fM?t=659))), and called the halberd an unnecessary risk for hardcores (([2026-02-07 3:01](https://youtu.be/R6F2mWXa_fM?t=181))).

| Requirement | Figure |
|---|---|
| Minimum | 13 of each resource, two vials with two herbs, bowstring, orb, 740 crystal shards, eight fish (([2026-02-07 2:06](https://youtu.be/R6F2mWXa_fM?t=126))) |
| Phase one | C pattern, 220 shards for tier 2 staff, tier 1 armour and two vials (([2026-02-07 2:34](https://youtu.be/R6F2mWXa_fM?t=154)) ([2026-02-07 3:01](https://youtu.be/R6F2mWXa_fM?t=181))) |
| Phase two | Demi hunting; last 10 of every resource, second weapon frame, 520 more shards (([2026-02-07 3:01](https://youtu.be/R6F2mWXa_fM?t=181))) |
| Phase three | Flexible clean-up; extra teleport crystal costs 40 shards (([2026-02-07 3:28](https://youtu.be/R6F2mWXa_fM?t=208)) ([2026-02-07 3:59](https://youtu.be/R6F2mWXa_fM?t=239))) |

Tips: harvest with a kick (four-tick) rather than the scepter (([2026-02-07 4:32](https://youtu.be/R6F2mWXa_fM?t=272))); there are 12 rooms and always six demi-bosses on the three edge sides (([2026-02-07 11:43](https://youtu.be/R6F2mWXa_fM?t=703))); a triple fish spawn is always next to the boss (([2026-02-07 12:38](https://youtu.be/R6F2mWXa_fM?t=758))); breaking a demi drop gives 80 shards (([2026-02-07 11:58](https://youtu.be/R6F2mWXa_fM?t=718))); time is the biggest problem (([2026-02-07 12:28](https://youtu.be/R6F2mWXa_fM?t=748))). In July 2026 he said tier three armour is only about two minutes faster to prep, recommending tier two for first kills and tier three only for hardcore accounts (([2026-07-22 29:09](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=1749))).

## Speed and combat achievement

The speedrunner combat achievement required under 6:30 (([2025-09-11 13:22](https://youtu.be/ZjG3P7PTeIs?t=802))); Gnomonkey considered it nearly consistent for top speedrunners (([2025-09-11 13:51](https://youtu.be/ZjG3P7PTeIs?t=831))). His fastest kill in 2020 was about 3.5 minutes and slowest near 6 (([2020-04-14 12:12](https://youtu.be/wD8VnN6vTrk?t=732))). After the Summer Sweep-up the time became 6:05 (([2026-07-23 0:00](https://youtu.be/JmglE_R2gGs?t=0))), though he thinks it is easier because prep is so much faster (([2026-07-23 10:33](https://youtu.be/JmglE_R2gGs?t=633))). Route: pre-venge, a weapon frame and 70 shards in the first room, tier two staff and two vials, collect three of each resource, kill two demi-bosses (220 shards total), then craft two tier three weapons and tier one armour with four to eight fish (([2026-07-23 10:33](https://youtu.be/JmglE_R2gGs?t=633)) ([2026-07-23 11:38](https://youtu.be/JmglE_R2gGs?t=698)) ([2026-07-23 12:05](https://youtu.be/JmglE_R2gGs?t=725)) ([2026-07-23 12:31](https://youtu.be/JmglE_R2gGs?t=751))). Target prep 2:30 or faster; his example was 2:03 prep, 3:33 boss, about 5:36 (([2026-07-23 13:01](https://youtu.be/JmglE_R2gGs?t=781))).

## Bow of Faerdhinen grind

About 400 kills are needed on average for the Bofa (([2025-02-22 7:40](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=460))), and ironmen are only done once they have all the armour seeds (around 300 kill count) (([2026-05-27 42:34](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=2554)) ([2026-05-27 43:36](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=2616))). In May 2026 he opposed making it quick, favouring more avenues around it (([2026-05-27 46:13](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=2773)) ([2026-05-27 46:45](https://youtu.be/NzW7JFgc8qU?t=2805))). After the update, he recommends it for an early iron and calls it effectively free money on a main (([2026-07-22 30:57](https://youtu.be/CA6-2CqM_NQ?t=1857))).
