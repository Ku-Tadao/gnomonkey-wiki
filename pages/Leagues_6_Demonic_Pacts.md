**Leagues 6: Demonic Pacts** is a Leagues season built around a huge pact skill tree. Gnomonkey considers the tree one of its best features, a very cool system for combat, and recommends the season to people on the fence [c4]. He believes it was worth playing and not a waste of time, because the tree gives Jagex many avenues to test items and ideas that provide value to the main game, even though some are bummed about two seasonal modes in one year (Deadman and Leagues) [c42]. It is not his favourite Leagues: he prefers Raging Echoes for its endgame potential with Echo Sol and more impactful Echo weapons [c40].

## The pact tree

The tree is much deeper than past Leagues, where the approach was to shove points into one style, and he says it feels like playing old Diablo [c10]. There are about 10 different minor builds, including a light weapon spec melee build and a thorns melee build that makes enemies damage themselves [c6]. Builds should all be viable for most end-game content, with some tuned to specific content, such as a clip of a thorns build AFKing the six Jad challenge [c9]. Building teammates into different builds for different roles is a cool part of raiding [c12].

| Branch | Effect |
|---|---|
| Earth magic | Drains defence and adds damage based on the defence drained [c7] |
| Fire magic | Ricochets to other enemies [c7]. The ricochet damage is typeless, and one fire mage can clear the entire Nylocas room in Theatre of Blood [c11] |
| Water magic | Heals a lot, up to about 180 total hitpoints [c7] |
| Air magic | Full maxes every hit with enough prayer bonus [c7] |
| Ranged | Crossbows are guaranteed to max hit but much slower; double chin builds throw faster [c8] |

His own build uses a seven-tile halberd, the same range as a ring crossbow, which gives a damage and accuracy bonus at full max range [c2], and a blind bag, which gives a chance to pull a heavy weapon from the inventory and swing it with one-tick attack speed on every attack [c3]. Melee attacks have a chance to trigger ranged damage echo splats; because he specced into the ranged tree these cascade into more ranged splats, producing procs on procs [c5]. He planned a stream to kill Zuk through his healers with this melee build and halberd [c43].

### Earning points

Points are unlocked by doing specific tasks in each region, ranging from easy ones, like killing a troll in Asgarnia, to extremely hard ones [c13]. Some final-tier tasks were extremely long, such as full Oathplate in Kourend, or ranger boots in Asgarnia (about 200 medium clues), while the Kandarin final task was easier [c14]. Some tier tasks need 25 Chambers of Xeric and 25 Theatre of Blood completions for a single point [c15].

## Update one week after launch

Jagex released changes one week after launch [c1]:

* Four bonus points were added, so 44 points are obtainable for a cap of 40, letting players skip some tasks [c16].
* Easier point tasks were added, such as equipping a piece of Moons armour or an obby cape [c17].
* Many neutral nodes were buffed with more accuracy and prayer penetration, and percent damage nodes now also give accuracy [c18].
* The Flask relic, which was bugged against bosses in walls such as Ulm and Verzik, was fixed so it works on everything [c27].
* The quiver extra slot, which would not regenerate ammo and broke the ranged tree and crystal blessing crossbow builds, was fixed on the day of the video [c35].
* A Morytania task requiring the ectophial was auto-finished [c30].

## Relics

He thinks the relics were solid compared to the last Leagues, with no obviously wrong choice like Fairy's flight in Raging Echoes [c23]. Butler's Bell versus Transmute was a big discussion point because both were very good, and the clue relic remains very good for getting points [c24]. His only regret was his tier eight pick of the Minion relic, which picks up loot, has damage boosted by giving it Zamorak items and can do AoE [c25]; he took it because the Flask relic was bugged [c27]. The Flask relic has tech where stalling with NPC Contact extends the invincibility frames, allowing maze skips and repeated hits on Bloat while invincible [c26].

## Bugs

He says Leagues 6 was probably the buggiest Leagues, with really annoying bugs [c28], though he credits Jagex for fixing basically all of the ones he listed, even if it sucks to take a week [c37]. Problems included:

* Total level achievements not unlocking for several days, apparently because Sailing was stuck at level one [c33].
* The Transmute relic, meant to auto-transform resources every five ticks, stopping whenever the player did anything else such as chopping, fishing or mining [c34].
* Many regions not unlocking the Slayer tasks they should. He skipped around 2,000 points of tasks trying to get Hydras, which appeared only after telling the Slayer master he would do hard tasks, at combat level 121 [c36].
* Many tasks bugged, such as chopping about a thousand redwood logs for the 'chop 250 redwood logs into shafts' task, a free 200 point task, which never completed [c32].
* Players reported very low drop rates at deep delves of the Doom of Mokhaiotl; he could not confirm this and only did delves one through eight [c29].
* Perdu is not in every region, so without him there was no way to get the ectophial in Morytania. He says Perdu should always be in a Leagues starting area, and that getting the Ivandis flail was much harder without him [c30 c31].

## Opinion

* He thinks Jagex should always stick with a skill-tree system for future Leagues, since it adds complexity, strategy, engagement and player choice [c19].
* He has minor balance nitpicks, finding some trees weaker than others, and says Jagex clearly had an idea how players would spec [c20].
* He sees Leagues as a free opportunity to experiment with systems such as a thorns piece of equipment or ricocheting magic, which could become future raid rewards [c21].
* Leagues 6 is much slower than previous Leagues: it took him three days to finish his halberd and begin the melee build. The slower pace grows on him because decisions matter more and feel more rewarding [c22], and it helped him benefit from the bug fixes unlike in the last Leagues [c37].
* He finds the questing very tedious and hopes the next Leagues lets players bypass quests, like the buyable quest unlocks in Shattered Relics. He was annoyed that the quest for Echo Moxie, the first Echo boss, was required, and that Sins of the Father was required for several pact points [c38 c39].
* He predicts the next Leagues will be the best ever, because Jagex spent so much effort on the pact tree that it dropped other focuses like Echo bosses and an uber endgame boss like Yama [c41].

## Infinite Jad challenge

Players can set self-imposed challenges, and Gnomonkey chose killing 1,000 Jads in a row [c44]. The Leagues Discord has a prize for the most Jads killed, but he is disqualified because he joined after Leagues had started [c45]. He had originally planned a thorns build after finishing with the blind bag [c46]. He used no mage because he lacks the regions that would suit the challenge; ideally a water mage tank build would kill Jads faster [c47]. The tier 8 Minion relic is the only relic he considers relevant, as the minion can automatically tag most of the healers [c48]. Completing the run rewarded 39,470 total Leagues points (toggle reward) [c49], and at the rate of the run he would need only 26 hours of Jads to get his onyx [c50].
