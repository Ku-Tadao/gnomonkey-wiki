RuneLite is the third-party Old School RuneScape client that Gnomonkey uses for all play. Across 2018 to 2026 he promotes its plugins (GPU, ground items, inventory setups, tile indicators and boss-specific helpers), shows how he configures it, and states that he will never play the official client over it.

For a plugin-by-plugin list see [RuneLite plugins](RuneLite_plugins.md).

## Gnomonkey's opinion
- 2024: RuneLite is more widely used than the vanilla client, runs better and dramatically improves the experience; anyone doing PvM without it is "shooting themselves in the foot" ([2024-02-25 0:00](https://youtu.be/Z5ZONYTlS1w?t=0)). Plugins bridge a quality-of-life gap Jagex cannot currently provide, and being able to use RuneLite was one of the main reasons he came over from [RuneScape 3](RuneScape_3.md) ([2024-02-25 0:00](https://youtu.be/Z5ZONYTlS1w?t=0)). He would not want to play without Menu Entry Swapper and Custom Menu Swaps ([2024-02-25 1:28](https://youtu.be/Z5ZONYTlS1w?t=88)).
- 2025: he says plugin support is extremely important to him and was one of the driving factors in leaving RuneScape 3 in 2017; his top plugin category is visual helpers, since Jagex is bad at visual cues, audio cues and clickboxes ([2025-10-15 14:10](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=850) [2025-10-15 14:39](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=879)). He prefers third-party plugins only, is unhappy with the state of the official client, and says he will never play the official client over RuneLite, calling it ten times worse than RuneLite even compared with RS3's client, lacking quest helpers, minigame helpers and tile indicators ([2025-10-15 16:10](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=970)). If Jagex removed RuneLite, as he says they have tried before, he does not know whether he would still play ([2025-10-15 16:42](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=1002)).
- He calls RuneLite key to the game's success and community-made, with built-in custom modes such as Prop Hunt and [Bronzeman Mode](Bronzeman_Mode.md); he wants Jagex to stay out of it, noting every minigame gets a plugin the day it releases and plugins fix things developers break, such as the Rune Pouch update ([2025-10-15 17:12](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=1032) [2025-10-15 17:41](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=1061)).
- For official plugins he ranks menu entry swapper and quest helper most important, quest helper first even though it helps once per account ([2025-10-15 15:11](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=911)). The official mobile menu entry swapper cannot swap some bank entries and rune pouches are similarly annoying; minigame plugins matter, but he never wants to play minigames like Giants' Foundry, Mixology or the Fletching activity on mobile ([2025-10-15 15:41](https://youtu.be/YjYgGFi5878?t=941)).

## Safety and the Plugin Hub
- He warns that scams and phishing through the wrong RuneLite client are common; the real one is at RuneLite.net or linked from the official OSRS page, and the only third-party clients allowed by Jagex are RuneLite and HDOS ([2024-02-25 0:30](https://youtu.be/Z5ZONYTlS1w?t=30) [2024-02-25 1:02](https://youtu.be/Z5ZONYTlS1w?t=62)).
- The wrench button has three sections: default plugin settings, profiles (saving whole-client configurations) and the plugin hub. Plugins should only be taken from the hub inside RuneLite ([2024-02-25 1:02](https://youtu.be/Z5ZONYTlS1w?t=62) [2024-02-25 1:28](https://youtu.be/Z5ZONYTlS1w?t=88)).
- In May 2020 he explains that external plugins are not in vanilla RuneLite, are confirmed safe by the RuneLite developers, and carry a use-at-your-own-risk warning because they may be unmaintained; all those in the video had worked well for over a year ([2020-05-14 0:00](https://youtu.be/NjF_kFyA02E?t=0)). In July 2020 he considers none necessary except Inventory Setups, which he cannot understand missing from default RuneLite ([2020-07-21 4:19](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=259)).
- He cannot export his RuneLite settings; some of his tile markers are on a pastebin and others, such as radius markers, must be found via commands in his Twitch chat ([2024-02-25 0:00](https://youtu.be/Z5ZONYTlS1w?t=0)).
- He recommends logging in with a Google account before setup so runelite.net stores loot and Grand Exchange history ([2020-07-21 5:22](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=322)).

## Setup (2020)
In July 2020 he deleted his settings file to demonstrate configuring RuneLite from scratch ([2020-07-21 0:00](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=0)). Settings he describes:
- Stretched mode on, scaling at 100% for a crisper UI; resolution matched to the screen (he plays on 1440p) ([2020-07-21 0:30](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=30) [2020-07-21 0:56](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=56) [2020-07-21 1:27](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=87)).
- Not always on top, tray and game message notifications on, notifications always sent and focus requests off, with a custom notification sound ([2020-07-21 1:56](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=116) [2020-07-21 2:23](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=143) [2020-07-21 2:52](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=172)).
- Small tooltip font, regular interface font, shift required for the overlay menu, tooltips under the cursor ([2020-07-21 2:52](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=172)). Overlay color on stops mouse macro keys counting as clicks ([2020-07-21 3:50](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=230)).
- Metronome pinned to the top for counting ticks ([2020-07-21 4:48](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=288)). Holding Alt moves overlays; moving the enemy health bars to the top is one of the most useful changes ([2020-07-21 5:56](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=356)).
- He closes the video saying these are all his plugins and the setup should mostly be accurate ([2020-07-21 1:07:13](https://youtu.be/9neaqoxR-nY?t=4033)).

## Official plugins
- **GPU:** in 2018 he calls it an absolute godsend even with bugs, since the graphics card drives the game; its extended click radius allows clicks up to 45 tiles away versus about 20, and he told viewers to abuse it while they could because he expected Jagex to have it removed ([2018-11-22 0:00](https://youtu.be/cBBPBxq6KxI?t=0) [2018-11-22 2:07](https://youtu.be/cBBPBxq6KxI?t=127)). In 2019 he adds that it gives better render distance, anti-aliasing and a skybox, and with animation smoothing makes the game look closer to RuneScape 2; Zoom Unlimiter plus GPU render distance brings great utility ([2019-04-14 0:31](https://youtu.be/w7POGb4jwco?t=31) [2019-04-14 2:03](https://youtu.be/w7POGb4jwco?t=123)).
- **Ground Items:** his favourite plugin, nearly necessary, showing drop names and values with customizable colours; he wanted it on mobile and the desktop client ([2019-04-14 0:00](https://youtu.be/w7POGb4jwco?t=0) [2019-04-14 0:31](https://youtu.be/w7POGb4jwco?t=31)).
- **Clue Scroll Solver:** controversial because it solves steps when opened, which he says is not much different from googling; it handles every clue type ([2019-04-14 2:26](https://youtu.be/w7POGb4jwco?t=146)).
- **Tile Indicators:** highlight current true tile shows the server tile, and hovered tile shows where a click will go ([2020-06-14 6:33](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=393)).
- **Timers, Nightmare Zone and notifications:** he enables game notifications for events such as a power surge or low prayer ([2020-03-08 4:00](https://youtu.be/zpBhPn5MJNA?t=240)); in the NMZ plugin only power surge power-ups are checked, with notifications for overload ending and absorption below 50 ([2020-03-08 4:00](https://youtu.be/zpBhPn5MJNA?t=240)). A 50-second interval timer lets the player flick rapid heal to keep health at 1 ([2020-03-08 4:31](https://youtu.be/zpBhPn5MJNA?t=271)). A boost timer for stamina potions is useful in the Timers plugin ([2020-06-09 8:22](https://youtu.be/5sBQOXz4VBE?t=502)).
- **Agility:** the Sepulchre trap hitbox highlighting was moved into it and is on by default ([2020-06-14 6:03](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=363)).

## Jagex membership survey (January 2025)
- The survey's cheaper-membership limitations included not being able to use plugins or third-party clients, which would mean paying for the premium tier to use RuneLite; he says the plugins Jagex would restrict are basically required to play ([2025-01-16 3:21](https://youtu.be/RkCY5EW5uoc?t=201) [2025-01-16 3:53](https://youtu.be/RkCY5EW5uoc?t=233)). Gating an API behind payment would block plugins pulling highscore data unless players pay ([2025-01-16 4:48](https://youtu.be/RkCY5EW5uoc?t=288)).
- The CEO apology said Jagex will not make plugins or features on the official client paid options, nor gate features including for community clients such as RuneLite and HDOS ([2025-01-17 5:31](https://youtu.be/cGiq6OwLovw?t=331)).
