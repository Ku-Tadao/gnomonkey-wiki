**The Gauntlet** is a 2019 boss activity in which the player enters with nothing, gathers resources for about 10 minutes, then uses the scraps to try to kill a boss [c1 c37]. Gnomonkey likens it to Dungeoneering as it was in RuneScape just before the Evolution of Combat, but considers the boss much harder than any Dungeoneering boss, requiring careful preparation [c1 c2]. He treats the normal and corrupted Gauntlet as one piece of content, with the normal version an easy mode that teaches the corrupted one safely [c29].

He rates it A tier, great content but not S, and places it at the top of A tier in his final order [c32]. It has a lot of mechanical depth and was the hardest content of 2019; completions are not very difficult, but fast completions and speedruns require a lot of game knowledge [c37]. It is not quite raid difficulty for the most part, but a good step up from other boss encounters [c33].

## Requirements

For consistent completion he recommends 85+ Magic, Ranged and Defence, plus the highest possible Mining, Fishing, Farming and Woodcutting to collect resources faster [c3].

## Preparation

The player starts with no armor and a crystal scepter equipped; the scepter opens new rooms, so dropping it means the player cannot advance [c4]. Also given are a crystal hatchet, pickaxe and harpoon for the three main resources, a pestle and mortar to grind crystal shards for potions, and a teleport seed that returns the player to the starting room [c5]. Items left on the ground do not despawn, unlike in Dungeoneering [c15].

### Resources

Crystal shards from foraging and killing monsters let the player forge weapons, armor, teleport seeds and potions at the singing bowl in the first room [c6]. Trees, ore veins and spools throughout the dungeon each give three resources before depleting permanently, and gathering can also yield shards [c14]. Paddlefish can be fished from pools, four fish per pool before it depletes; they heal exactly as much as sharks and are the only healing method [c16].

Regular monsters are fairly standard beasts such as wolves, spiders and scorpions; higher combat monsters give more shards and are more likely to drop resources [c17].

### Crafting

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Vial | 10 shards; fill at the pump, add a grimy leaf and 10 ground shards to make a potion [c7] |
| Potion effect | Same prayer points as a dose of prayer potion plus a stamina potion's effects, restoring 40 run energy [c8] |
| Weapon (tier 1) | 20 shards for a halberd, bow or staff, after getting a weapon mold from a monster [c9] |
| Weapon tier 2 | 60 additional shards [c10] |
| Weapon tier 3 | Upgrade piece from a demiboss [c10] |
| Armor tier 1 | One each of bark, linen and ore plus 40 shards per piece [c11] |
| Armor tier 2 | 2 of each resource and 60 shards [c11] |
| Armor tier 3 | 3 of each resource and 80 shards [c11] |

The bow and staff need no arrows or runes, so he finds them more useful than the halberd [c9]. Armor has equal defences against all attack styles and no negative attack bonuses [c12]. He compares the weapon upgrades to going from a rune scimitar to a dragon scimitar and then a Ghrazi rapier, so they are very important [c13].

### Demibosses

Three demibosses can spawn on the dungeon's outskirts. The bear drops an upgrade component for the halberd, the dragon for the staff and the dark beast for the bow. The bear uses melee, the dragon magic and the dark beast ranged, and they hit fairly hard, so the player should protect against the matching style [c18 c20]. He hunts a dragon or dark beast as soon as possible because they drastically increase damage output [c19].

## Standard run (2019)

He collects nine of each resource for a full tier 2 armor set, thinking tier 3 armor costs too much time and helps little over tier 2 [c21]. He tries to get at least his bow or staff to tier 3, which raises DPS an insane amount, needs only two potions for the whole dungeon, and enters the boss with 25 food, a tier 2 and tier 3 weapon and full tier 2 armor [c22].

1. Find basic enemies fast for two weapon molds, collect resources as encountered, make a bow as soon as possible and upgrade it to tier 2 if there are enough shards [c23].
2. Once one weapon is tier 2, run to the outskirts to hunt a dragon or dark beast for the tier 3 upgrade, using a teleport seed since the outskirts are far from the start [c24].
3. Make the two potions when low on energy and start building armor; kill enemies with the tier 3 weapon if shards run low, and have 25 fish before the timer runs out [c25].
4. Before the boss, drop all tools, shards, leftover teleport seeds and the scepter, and decant the potions to about four doses. Spare time can go into tier 3 armor or starting the fight early [c26].

## Fight and skill

He rates the Gauntlet 2/5 for inventory management (no switches, but tracking items during prep and supply usage), 5/5 for movement and 3/5 for prayer management [c34]. Prep movement between skilling nodes and pathing matters, and movement ramps up in the Hunllef fight, where the player avoids ground tiles, tornadoes and walking under Hunllef [c35]. Prayer management is mostly counting Hunllef's attacks, switching prayer and turning prayers back on when he disables them, though it can be hard to keep track [c36].

He says it has the lowest skill floor and the highest skill cap gap in the game, so players can start really bad and work up to very good, which he calls one of its best design features [c30]. He dislikes that as players get faster they eventually have to chance it, working down the number of fish and dying if they do not hit hard enough, which makes the skill cap arbitrarily lower than necessary; a perfect 10 fish kill is where he says a kill is guaranteed [c31]. For the same reason it is not his favourite content [c38].

## Rewards

In 2019 he said rewards were unknown for lack of data: after six attempts he received two elite clue scrolls, and a new sword drop may come only from hard mode [c27].

## Speed run (2026)

After the Summer Sweep-up the regular Gauntlet speed time combat achievement is 3:45 instead of 4:00 [c39]. He thinks the new time is much harder than before and rough, even though the Corrupted Gauntlet time is easier [c40]. His example run finished in 3:26 [c49].

* **Armor.** None is made, since Hunllef's max hit without armor is only 12 [c41].
* **Weapons.** One tier three weapon plus one tier two weapon, using the five to one method [c41]. A halberd and an extra weapon frame are needed for it, so if the edge demi-boss is a bear and he has no extra frame he usually resets [c44].
* **Prep goal.** About four to eight fish, one potion, no armor, one tier three weapon and an extra weapon; the player resets constantly, for example if the first room has no monsters [c42]. One herb and one vial suffice (two herbs only for two potions), and a second vial is a bonus for extra Redemptions [c48].
* **First room.** He prefers the closest first room. He kills one mob for a weapon frame and at least 50 shards, makes a tier two staff, and with 70 shards also makes two vials; killing all three mobs is fine but he leaves once he has 70+ shards [c43 c47].
* **Route.** Kill the demi-boss opposite the boss, pick up its item, teleport back, make the tier three weapon and extra weapon if needed, cook fish, make the potion and go in [c45].
* **Vengeance.** He pre-venges before entering both speed runs and uses the vengeance on a demi-boss, since the venge damage carries into the Gauntlet for some bonus damage [c46].
* **Variance.** Boss kills vary from about 1:30 to 2:30. With only tier one armor he is so inaccurate that it may take several tries, and he does not expect the speed time on a first attempt [c50].
