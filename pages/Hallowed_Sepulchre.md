The Hallowed Sepulchre is a five-floor agility minigame in Darkmire that was added with the Sins of the Father quest. Gnomonkey calls it objectively the fastest agility XP in the game and the best agility training method for gp, and regards it as one of his favorite skilling updates ([2020-06-14 0:00](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=0)) ([2025-03-26 11:29](https://youtu.be/uDhGFEjrpyc?t=689)).

## Overview

- The Sepulchre is similar to Pyramid Plunder in that a timer locks the player out of looting coffins if too slow, but it is mechanically challenging on top of that ([2020-06-14 0:00](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=0)).
- Gnomonkey equates its difficulty to a decently difficult boss such as [Vorkath](Vorkath.md) or [Zulrah](Zulrah.md), requiring a lot of effort and practice ([2020-06-14 2:00](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=120)). In 2020 he said it practically turns [Agility](Agility.md) into a boss and is great practice for movement useful in places like raids and the [Inferno](Inferno.md) ([2020-07-25 5:23](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=323)).
- He found it fun enough to play 12 hours a day without getting bored, despite typically only doing AFK methods ([2020-06-14 2:00](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=120)), and did 92 to 99 Agility there ([2020-07-25 5:23](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=323)).
- In 2025 he stated that Agility is his favorite skill besides combat only because of the Sepulchre, which he calls interactive and engaging. He considers Agility just rooftops until level 52 and then the Sepulchre all the way to 99, and would like mini Sepulchre-style activities earlier and more methods at higher levels ([2025-03-26 10:17](https://youtu.be/uDhGFEjrpyc?t=617)) ([2025-03-26 26:14](https://youtu.be/uDhGFEjrpyc?t=1574)). See also [Rooftop Agility courses](Rooftop_Agility_courses.md).
- Mod Husky, lead developer on the Sepulchre, commented that he would like to see more agility XP for completing mini challenges but felt it was in a fairly good place; Gnomonkey said he would not oppose a buff ([2020-06-14 46:05](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=2765)).

## Requirements

- The Sepulchre is in Darkmire and requires Sins of the Father to be completed ([2020-06-14 2:27](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=147)).
- Floor requirements (2020): five floors, from level 52 Agility for floor one to level 92 for floor five ([2020-06-14 2:27](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=147)); in 2020 he gave floor 3 as the entry point from level 72, floor 4 at 82 and floor 5 at 92 ([2020-07-25 5:23](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=323)) ([2020-07-25 7:18](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=438)) ([2020-07-25 9:22](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=562)).
- Looting any coffin needs at least 66 Thieving; certain coffins also need 54 Prayer, 56 Construction and a high Magic level, with the highest enchant jewelry spell being best (93 Magic for the top one) ([2020-06-14 2:58](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=178)). The prayer coffins are not on floor five, so Prayer could possibly be skipped, but he likes to loot floor four as well ([2020-06-14 3:28](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=208)).
- Update (June 2026, [Summer Sweep-up](Update_Summer_Sweep-up.md)): floor 4 now needs 77 Agility (was 82) and floor 5 needs 87 (was 92). Players can now boost for floor entry, so a level 72 player could eat a summer pie at the end of floor three to enter floor four, making the earliest entry to floor five 82 instead of 92. Floor five's grand hallowed coffin will require either 84 Thieving or 92 Agility to open ([2026-06-04 5:05](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=305)) ([2026-06-04 5:35](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=335)) ([2026-06-04 6:05](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=365)).

## Gear and inventory

Described in 2020 ([2020-06-14 3:28](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=208)) ([2020-06-14 3:57](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=237)) ([2020-06-14 4:31](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=271)):

- Equipment: full [Graceful](Graceful_outfit.md), a Mithril grapple, a Dorgeshuun crossbow (the lightest crossbow, relevant because several heavy rune platebody drops can be received) and a Saradomin stole. Eventually a hallowed ring, focus, grapple and symbol help with looting coffins.
- Inventory: a saw and hammer (hallowed hammer once bought), a decent stack of rune nails, runes for the highest enchant jewelry spell, eight mahogany planks (two per bridge), a strange old lockpick, and about four vampyre dust with a hallowed symbol (more without it; each shrine takes two dust, or one with the symbol), plus a slot for hallowed marks. He does not use a rune pouch because coffins give blood, soul and cosmic runes he does not want to place in it; with a lower Magic level he would bring the pouch.
- A Saradomin item (stole, Book of Law, Holy Symbol) or any hallowed item restores [run energy](Run_energy.md) between floors ([2020-06-14 3:28](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=208)), so stamina potions are not needed; an Explorer's ring might help if the recharge is forgotten on floor five ([2020-06-14 5:00](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=300)).
- Strange old lockpicks cost about 175k when recorded ([2020-06-14 4:31](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=271)).
- After entering, the mysterious stranger gives five hallowed crystal shards that teleport back to the Sepulchre; more cost one mark each, and he recommends keeping some in the bank ([2020-06-14 5:33](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=333)). Wearing Vyre noble robes while running through Darkmire avoids attacks from the Vyrewatch ([2020-06-14 5:33](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=333)).

## Mechanics

Getting damaged by a trap sends the player back to a checkpoint unless stated otherwise; each trap type has three variants that become more common deeper in ([2020-06-14 7:36](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=456)).

- **Sword traps**: Saradomin sword traps are thrown by a giant statue and cover nine tiles. The regular variant (floors 1-2) moves at two tiles per tick, the blessed variant (floor 3) at three, and the giant blue variant (floors 4-5) at four ([2020-06-14 8:03](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=483)). They are passed by running through in a free moment, using blue teleporters, or running around using tile markers ([2020-06-14 8:32](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=512)).
- **Teleporter tiles**: appear on every floor in clumps of nine, active four ticks at a time. Yellow tiles teleport backwards and blue tiles forward. Both give roughly two ticks of invulnerability to traps (his estimate). Running over a teleporter from the tile before it avoids it entirely, and he lays out tile placements to hit more blue than yellow ([2020-06-14 8:59](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=539)) ([2020-06-14 9:29](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=569)).
- **Lightning**: floors 4 and 5 only; stuns for about two ticks instead of sending the player to a checkpoint. It can be avoided by standing on the tile before the first spot and running through (checkerboard pattern). Walking diagonally through is safe, but in the last section he runs straight through every time ([2020-06-14 9:29](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=569)) ([2020-06-14 10:01](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=601)).
- **Crossbow statues**: come in groups of three, fire at three tiles per tick. The plain variant on floors 1-2 fires from one statue at a time; gold-trim variants on floors 3-5 fire two bolts at once randomly and cannot be run through even with perfect timing. The statues' animation shows the next pattern. To avoid a pattern, click two tiles ahead to keep running speed ([2020-06-14 10:01](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=601)) ([2020-06-14 10:33](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=633)).
- **Flame wizard statues**: cannot be run through; scorch marks show where flames hit. All have a four-tick delay between attacks. Stone statues (floors 1-2) fire a three-tick flame in the same direction each time; golden-trim statues (floors 3-4) last three ticks but run cycles; blue-and-gold statues (floor 5) last two ticks with long, complicated cycles ([2020-06-14 11:05](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=665)). Counting rule: stone, wait three ticks then run; golden, count the flames three times then run; golden-blue, count two times then run ([2020-06-14 12:08](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=728)). A bug making flames start a tick late was fixed by Jagex, so fire can be counted by tick rate ([2020-06-14 12:08](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=728)).
- **Timer and layout**: the timer pauses on taking the stepping stone to the next floor or crossing to the Grand Hallowed Coffin ([2020-06-14 16:29](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=989)). Running out of time only prevents looting; agility XP and pet chance are still earned, so he never resets by logging out ([2020-06-14 15:59](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=959)). Floors reset on entry unless another player is present, since the Sepulchre is not instanced; other players can occasionally help but mostly waste time, so a low-population world with low ping is advised ([2020-06-14 16:29](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=989)) ([2020-06-14 17:01](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=1021)).
- **Floor variants**: floors 1 and 2 have four variants each (north, east, south, west), floors 3 and 4 have two that converge, and floor 5 has one ([2020-06-14 15:31](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=931)).

## Coffins and challenges

- Coffin mini challenges can be failed but never send the player back ([2020-06-14 12:08](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=728)). Loot scales with depth; earlier coffins have a similar table with more junk and fewer hallowed marks (one per coffin on floor one), while later coffins give rune items, large piles of blood and soul runes, ranarr weed and seeds and four to six hallowed marks ([2020-06-14 1:02](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=62)) ([2020-06-14 12:39](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=759)). In 2020 he typically only did coffin challenges on floors four and five ([2020-06-14 12:39](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=759)).
- Lockpicking has a small chance to fail and poison for two damage; a strange old lockpick reduces the chance further and the Hallowed ring outheals the poison ([2020-06-14 13:11](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=791)).
- Grapple: failure is common and wastes about five ticks, as it requires grappling across and back; the hallowed grapple raises success ([2020-06-14 13:38](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=818)).
- Portal frame: cast an enchant jewelry spell, float across, lockpick and float back; a second failure cracks the portal. With level 7 enchant he never cracked a portal and failed once in about fifty runs with the Hallowed focus; with Onyx Enchant he failed once in ten with the focus ([2020-06-14 14:06](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=846)). He recommends a lava staff and skipping Zenyte Enchant once the focus is owned ([2020-06-14 14:37](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=877)).
- Bridge: two planks and five nails; each failure wastes about one tick and bends five nails. He uses runite nails, and mahogany planks for extra Construction XP ([2020-06-14 14:37](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=877)) ([2020-06-14 15:04](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=904)).
- Brazier: cannot fail; two vampyre dust (one with the symbol) gives 60 [Prayer](Prayer.md) XP per dust ([2020-06-14 15:04](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=904)).
- Floor five's portal challenge is almost always taken, as its coffins are very rewarding for money and marks ([2020-06-14 34:57](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=2097)). With over a minute left at the end of floor five he takes the bridge coffin before the final stepping stones and portal ([2020-06-14 39:27](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=2367)). The timer can read zero seconds and still allow the Grand Coffin ([2020-06-14 39:27](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=2367)). Afterward, quick exit via the obelisk restarts at the lobby ([2020-06-14 40:00](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=2400)).

## Training advice

- Early on, skip coffins to have time to finish, then add coffins starting from the end of the run backwards ([2020-06-14 17:30](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=1050)). He recommends slow runs copying his tile markers, laying out every marker on floor five ([2020-06-14 15:59](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=959)) ([2020-06-14 33:57](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=2037)).
- Floor 5 is the hardest and longest floor with three total levels, and its last section is the hardest single section; it took many run-throughs to do consistently ([2020-06-14 33:29](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=2009)) ([2020-06-14 37:26](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=2246)). Its fire statues stay active only two ticks, so counting is twice, not three times ([2020-06-14 33:57](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=2037)). Floor 5 is often warped by other players because it is the only variant ([2020-06-14 33:57](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=2037)).
- In 2020 he listed route notes for floors 1 to 5: floor one north and south ([2020-06-14 18:02](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=1082)) ([2020-06-14 19:02](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=1142)), floor two north and west ([2020-06-14 20:27](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=1227)) ([2020-06-14 23:30](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=1410)), floor three ([2020-06-14 24:29](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=1469)) ([2020-06-14 26:30](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=1590)) ([2020-06-14 27:30](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=1650)), floor four ([2020-06-14 28:32](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=1712)) ([2020-06-14 29:03](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=1743)) ([2020-06-14 30:34](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=1834)) ([2020-06-14 33:29](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=2009)) and floor five ([2020-06-14 34:57](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=2097)) ([2020-06-14 38:00](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=2280)) ([2020-06-14 38:56](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=2336)).
- Gnomonkey says only the very last part of floor 5 needs intense focus ([2020-07-25 10:49](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=649)). In 2026 he said each floor higher is worth about 10k Agility XP an hour more, with most of the fun and XP at floor five; the 72 to 82 stretch on floor three is a slog ([2026-06-04 5:35](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=335)) ([2026-06-04 6:05](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=365)).
- Fletching: bolt tipping suited the Sepulchre better than rapid fletching, since time spent waiting for traps can be AFK tipping. He could tip about 1,000 bolts per five-floor run, adding 50k GP profit and 7k Fletching XP per run, about 50k XP an hour and 350k extra GP an hour. It needs a large cash stack and time for [Grand Exchange](Grand_Exchange.md) offers (buy limits typically 11,000) ([2020-08-11 5:22](https://youtu.be/_dz_wbfSEmc?t=322)) ([2020-08-11 6:48](https://youtu.be/_dz_wbfSEmc?t=408)). See [Fletching](Fletching.md).

## Records and milestones

- Dark Graceful recolor: just over a hundred Grand Coffin loots, with about twelve fails ([2020-06-14 42:33](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=2553)).
- Grand Coffin looted 485 times by level 99, with about 20 failed runs while learning ([2020-07-25 9:22](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=562)). He planned to go for the Agility pet there after 99 as the best pet chance before the penguin course ([2020-07-25 10:49](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=649)).
- A bug let a player lowered to one HP and wearing the hallowed ring run through traps without being hit or teleported back. Records set this way still stand on the board, which Gnomonkey says makes them impossible to beat legitimately; the bug also affected max XP rates. He maintains the Sepulchre is the best XP in the game ([2026-05-07 15:38](https://youtu.be/76_3oQhlW8I?t=938)) ([2026-05-07 16:11](https://youtu.be/76_3oQhlW8I?t=971)). See [Bug abuse](Bug_abuse.md).

## Gnomonkey's opinion

- XP and gp rates are tremendously better than any other agility method, but not without practice ([2020-06-14 1:29](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=89)). Lower floors have lower rates; in 2020 he thought it only worth doing at 92 Agility, though lower-level rates were untested ([2020-06-14 2:58](https://youtu.be/wVim0ZX-GTc?t=178)), and later said level 72 is where it becomes efficient, starting at floor 3, looting only floor 3 at first ([2020-07-25 5:23](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=323)).
- He does not consider it overpowered and asked Jagex not to nerf it, as it is one of the most difficult skilling methods and most value is ring drops ([2020-07-25 10:49](https://youtu.be/pEzKRL_6dSQ?t=649)).
- In the 2022 [PvM boss tier list](Opinion_PvM_boss_tier_list.md) he called it the agility boss and very good content that pushes movement to its limits, with floor 5 great for its high skill cap and some of the best scaling money in the game. He placed it in A tier, just below [Nex](Nex.md), noting that the placement is arbitrary as it is not a boss ([2022-03-19 1:41](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=101)) ([2022-03-19 2:13](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=133)) ([2022-03-19 90:32](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=5432)).
- In 2026 he noted that the update lets players skip the floor 3 slog and joked it is an excuse to burn through summer pies ([2026-06-04 6:05](https://youtu.be/6tsf-brc-sE?t=365)).
