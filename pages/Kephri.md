Kephri is the scarab boss room of [Tombs of Amascut](Tombs_of_Amascut.md). Gnomonkey treats it as one of the easier rooms, a free room in a solo 500 raid ([2022-09-13 28:08](https://youtu.be/h4hf5hPQHms?t=1688)) ([2025-06-25 15:56](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=956)), with the arcane scarab as the one add that can kill the player ([2024-05-30 10:49](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=649)) ([2024-06-27 9:30](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=570)). His handling of the room changed after the June 2025 Tombs of Amascut update, which lowered the scarabs' defence.

## Room layout and puzzle

- The puzzle room is solved by tagging four tiles on both sides, and the puzzle solver plugin shows exactly what to do ([2025-06-28 12:48](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=768)). In 2022 he also recommended the Tombs of Amascut plugin to speed it up ([2022-11-18 6:19](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=379)). See also [RuneLite plugins](RuneLite_plugins.md).
- For the 300 budget guide he does one puzzle, climbs through the hole and does the next, avoiding the obelisk puzzle as slow, and prays ranged through the puzzle room because small scarabs spawn and damage the player ([2024-10-24 12:32](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=752)).
- Order: in the 2022 solo 500 he did Kephri last because it is a free room ([2022-09-13 28:08](https://youtu.be/h4hf5hPQHms?t=1688)); in the 300 budget guide (2024) he always does Kephri second ([2024-10-24 12:32](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=752)). In April 2023 he said the room level does not matter, only Zebak's and Akkha's do ([2023-04-25 12:53](https://youtu.be/vQO8khf302s?t=773)).

## Mechanics

- **Bombs and fireballs.** Kephri launches fireballs that land where a shadow appears under the player's feet; the player moves or prays magic in a pinch ([2025-06-25 15:56](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=956)). Her bombs do mage damage, around 70 in the centre with Aerial Assault ([2026-04-01 17:20](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=1040)). If she charges the red swarms underneath her she slams for around 60, survivable with mage prayer ([2024-10-24 15:39](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=939)).
- **Eggs.** Smaller eggs explode in a small area for a little damage; larger eggs spawn an enemy and do not explode, with a three by three area around them dealing damage ([2025-06-25 19:24](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=1164)) ([2024-10-24 13:37](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=817)). Dark eggs spawn agile scarabs that hit with ranged damage, so the player mostly camps protect from range ([2026-04-01 17:44](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=1064)). Agile scarabs often stack in corners; a stack of three or more is worth chinning for points because of their very low defence ([2022-11-18 6:59](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=419)).
- **Dung line.** When swarm flies appear and a buzzing sound plays, Kephri shoots a line of dung that blocks movement. Gnomonkey places it to block the scarabs' path and steps aside ([2025-06-25 16:23](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=983)) ([2025-06-28 13:19](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=799)). Standing two tiles away when she does the swarm special forms a clean line ([2026-04-01 21:16](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=1276)); he cautions not to stand where the dung can trap the player after a phase change ([2026-04-01 23:47](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=1427)). He steps away from her when she surrounds the player with bugs to create a line, since the lines cannot be walked through ([2024-10-24 13:04](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=784)). Using the swarm lines to trap one of the two summoned bugs leaves only the Magic scarab to deal with ([2024-10-24 14:39](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=879)). In 2025 he notes a second dung line is needed, and with a Keris crit the dung line must go first or the melee scarab will kill the player ([2025-06-28 14:25](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=865)); with 500 raid level there is plenty of time to drop both ([2025-06-27 11:16](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=676)).
- **Swarms.** Swarms spawn and run into Kephri clockwise to heal her. Shooting them with the blowpipe heals her less (about 100 each); her body is five tiles long and the blowpipe range is five ([2026-04-01 19:48](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=1188)). With level 2 or higher the swarms come in two batches of 3 each time she goes down ([2022-11-18 6:59](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=419)).
- **Scarabs after each down.** With Overlords, after Kephri hits zero she summons two and then three scarabs: a ranged scarab, a melee scarab and, in phase two, an arcane scarab that charges a ring and fires a massive mage hit unless attacked enough times to make it move ([2026-04-01 18:18](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=1098)). The Overlords invocation adds a melee scarab first and a spinning scarab second ([2025-06-26 7:35](https://youtu.be/DaUViQQ8TL8?t=455)).
- **Defence reset.** In 2022 he said Kephri can be drained 20 defence levels and resets her defence only once the shield is fully removed in the final phase ([2022-11-18 6:30](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=390)). In 2024 he said she regains full defence on her last health bar once the Bone dagger drain wears off and it is not worth daggering again ([2024-10-24 16:36](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=996)). In 2025 he said after the third shield she goes to green health, which resets defence, but she dies very quickly ([2025-06-25 22:22](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=1342)). In 2026 he said a 20-damage drain lasts until the end of the fight ([2026-04-01 20:15](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=1215)).

## The arcane scarab

The arcane scarab is the priority: it is the one thing in the room that can kill the player, and should not be trusted not to ([2024-05-30 10:49](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=649)) ([2024-06-27 9:30](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=570)). In 2022 he used the [Twisted bow](Twisted_bow.md) against it as best DPS with a ranged potion, otherwise [Osmumten's fang](Osmumten's_fang.md) ([2022-11-18 8:01](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=481)); in 2024 he used the fang most of the time and the Shadow if he cannot reach it ([2024-05-30 10:49](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=649)). At full HP on the second down he considered praying protect from magic and ignoring it, since it typically only needs tanking once and hits between 30 and 60 through magic ([2022-11-18 7:29](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=449)). If the player stands to the right of it, the ranged scarab does not hit them ([2023-05-09 15:42](https://youtu.be/K7VALGO6md8?t=942)).

How it is made to jump has changed over time:

| Date | Rule as stated |

| 2023-06-30 | A hit of 39 or higher teleports it instantly, or three hit slots of 4 to 38; hits of 1 to 3 do not count, so thralls do not count. Each extra person raises the required max hit and number of hits by one ([2023-06-30 17:05](https://youtu.be/cmLBaxdoHpQ?t=1025)) ([2023-06-30 17:05](https://youtu.be/cmLBaxdoHpQ?t=1025)) ([2023-06-30 17:36](https://youtu.be/cmLBaxdoHpQ?t=1056)) |
| 2024-05-30 | A hit of 39 or higher, or three hit splats higher than three ([2024-05-30 10:49](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=649)) |
| 2024-06-27 | Any hitsplat over 40, or any three hitsplats above three; he hard focuses it and spams specs ([2024-06-27 9:30](https://youtu.be/YizVhRWXnxs?t=570)) |
| 2024-07-01 | Every three hits, or over 40 for an instant jump ([2024-07-01 8:49](https://youtu.be/LxYODChv40A?t=529)) |

| 2025-06-27 | Jumps off thrall hitsplats and any zeros; three splats and it jumps, so it is no longer a worry ([2025-06-27 13:45](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=825)) |

He says everyone who knows Tombs of Amascut hates the Magic scarab and says to kill it as fast as possible with the fang or mage, since it is weak to mage ([2024-10-24 15:06](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=906)). After the June 2025 update he says bullying the arcane scarab might be his favourite thing about the update ([2025-06-27 14:40](https://youtu.be/UhLzyieXrbY?t=880)).

## Swarm catching

In 2022 he said perfectly catching Kephri's swarms cuts her HP by roughly 70%, so it is worth it if safe ([2022-11-18 7:29](https://youtu.be/q3D2jeguGz8?t=449)). In 2023 he recommended freezing Kephri so she stays still ([2023-05-09 9:50](https://youtu.be/K7VALGO6md8?t=590)), and waiting for the second swarm and dung when landing a big Bandos godsword hit ([2023-06-30 12:30](https://youtu.be/cmLBaxdoHpQ?t=750)). In May 2024 he said not to shoot swarms when the arcane scarab exists in a 500 unless speedrunning ([2024-05-30 11:18](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=678)), though with Pathfinder he gets triple swarms and chinchompas might be worth it ([2024-05-30 8:35](https://youtu.be/Mg9hcqcFC1U?t=515)). In 2024-10 he said blowpipe swarm-shooting does not matter while learning ([2024-10-24 14:10](https://youtu.be/vEs4Qvs_Rag?t=850)). After the 2025 update he tags as many swarms as possible with the blowpipe in the first phase ([2025-06-25 17:28](https://youtu.be/S4Z7Mjjdt4I?t=1048)), but in April 2026 he says not to tag swarms in phase two because it is risky and to kill the arcane scarab as fast as possible instead ([2026-04-01 21:16](https://youtu.be/N2DCZ1nt4p4?t=1276)). In the 300 ironman/budget guide he said that with a blowpipe it is worth tagging swarms, otherwise letting her heal gives more points though a slower raid ([2025-06-28 15:59](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=959)). He called the [Atlatl](Atlatl.md) too slow to be worth using on the swarms ([2025-06-28 14:25](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=865)).

## Changes in the June 2025 update

Gnomonkey describes the [Tombs of Amascut changes](Update_Tombs_of_Amascut_changes.md) as lowering the soldier scarab's mage defence and ending the scaling of scarab defence with raid level, so spitting scarabs and others die far faster to the fang ([2025-06-22 6:43](https://youtu.be/JjIAGBBDmL8?t=403)). He says overlords now have dramatically reduced defence at all raid levels ([2025-06-28 14:25](https://youtu.be/BVZEAFBsj10?t=865)), and that the arcane scarab now moves after a set number of attacks regardless of hit or miss ([2025-06-22 7:21](https://youtu.be/JjIAGBBDmL8?t=441)). He summarised the through line as a faster, more consistent raid with less brew and supply use ([2025-06-22 8:40](https://youtu.be/JjIAGBBDmL8?t=520)).
