The **Vanguards** are a [[Chambers of Xeric]] room with three monsters, each a different colour: a melee, a ranged and a magic Vanguard. They spawn at fixed spots in random order and must be killed at the same time. [c1 c23 c34] Gnomonkey considers the room the hardest of the raid apart from [[Great Olm|Olm]], and probably the hardest room of Challenge Mode besides Olm; the Vanguards hit very hard and are very tanky. [c22 c33 c4]

His opinion of the fight has been constant in outline. In 2022 he rated it high B tier, one of the better Chambers fights with a lot of thought in keeping health similar and timing claw specs, but annoying when a hard [[Twisted bow]] shot forces a restart. He dislikes the reset mechanic and would put the room in high A tier if it punished the player with something like meteors instead. [c2 c3]

## Mechanics

Each Vanguard is weak to the combat triangle style: melee the ranged one, range the mage one, and mage the melee one. [c23 c1] The room resets if too much damage is done to a single Vanguard: all of them heal to full. [c1 c24] The numbers over the Vanguards are the health at which each resets, so hitting past that number causes a reset; the Cox Editions plugin shows this, and the player needs to know the max hit of each weapon. [c17 c25] A plugin asterisk indicates when a Vanguard can be killed once the others are low enough. [c9] Once both remaining Vanguards are at reset the numbers disappear and they can simply be killed. [c17 c40] The player always gets three attacks at the start. [c6]

* The ranged Vanguard is rooted: it cannot move and stops attacking if the player stands under it. [c28 c37]
* The melee Vanguard hits up to 16 through prayer three times per attack (potentially 48 damage) on a four-tick attack speed, which makes it the last priority and by far the most dangerous. [c29 c36]
* The mage Vanguard throws one guaranteed projectile plus two splats that are not guaranteed to hit, so health should be kept high. [c31]
* The player only takes ricochet rocks from the ranged and mage Vanguards when moving, so should stand still while hitting them. [c38]
* Safe spots: the melee Vanguard can be stuck on a lip, in a cave slot, or corner-trapped against a crystal; the mage Vanguard can be trapped on a crystal corner; whether the melee Vanguard aggros at other corners depends on how far it is from its spawn. [c30 c39] At the end of the room, with all at reset, he follows the melee Vanguard because he can safespot it and be completely safe. [c18]

## Strategy

His plan treats the ranged Vanguard as an anchor: follow it throughout, get it under its threshold with the weapon of choice, bow the mage Vanguard, and mage the melee Vanguard. [c28 c37 c16] When they dig, he goes to the melee Vanguard, alternating each dig. [c37]

|---|---|---|
| 2023 (Challenge Mode) | Cast at the melee Vanguard, bow the mage if it hits big, switch the bow to accurate for the ranger, then follow the ranger, scythe it below 70 HP while swapping to Masori for defence, step under it, range the mage, then hit the melee Vanguard on a big shot, flicking both Vanguards' prayers | [c7 c8] |
| 2024 | Fang the ranged Vanguard (max about 49), Twisted bow the mage Vanguard below 90, Sanguinesti staff the melee Vanguard | [c26 c28] |
| 2025 | Start with the ranged Vanguard, bow the mage, then the melee Vanguard on each dig | [c37] |

The mage Vanguard is the one he resets on, because the Twisted bow can hit around 90 and the number only updates after the projectile lands; his reset threshold in that run was 110. [c27] His maximum Twisted bow hit on Vanguards is 83. [c15] He likes swinging at a Vanguard below 50 and follows the ranger through the room. [c16]

### Setup and supplies
* Weapon settings: staff on long range, bow on accurate and scythe on aggressive; with Blood Fury and Brimstone ring instead of Berserker ring (torture) he wants it on accurate. [c13]
* He takes his boots off for the first hits and normally casts at the melee Vanguard. [c14]
* He flicks Masori because Ink has no defence out of the armour, and flicks Rigour only a little, since a little prayer is enough. [c19 c10]
* He aims for at least 80 HP by Vanguards and, with his setup, has no brew for them, so he spams sweets on the way: all available health is needed. [c5 c12]
* He recommends the Light bearer for an extra claw spec on Vanguards, on Muttadiles and for a bunch of hammers at Olm. [c21]
* Supplies from Vanguards are completely random, the worst being three brews and no revitalisation. [c9] After the room he picks up as much brew and revitalisation as possible, sips and drops the prayer enhance (it lasts through Vespula) and banks the rest. [c32] They are the main supply source for no-prep raids, so he suggests summoning a thrall, picking up all the brews and refreshing overload and enhance. [c41]

Anything below about 110 is a really fast Vanguards time and his fastest is about 50 seconds; one run in 2023 was 230, which he called the most average Vanguards of his life. [c11 c20] Doing too much to one Vanguard, or dying there, is typically a reset in Challenge Mode, and he often risks a reset or death for speed. [c4]
