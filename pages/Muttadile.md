**Muttadile** is the [[Chambers of Xeric]] meat tree room, and Gnomonkey calls it the worst boss in the game. He first states this in March 2022 in a video made partly to rant about it [c1], repeats it in 2023 [c6] and again in 2024 [c23], and says he would be happy to see it removed [c4]. He calls it a very high damage room where the player should be ready to spam brew [c22].

## Mechanics

* The small Muttadile is sporadic: sometimes backing off, sometimes approaching, switching between ranged and melee even while biting, and sometimes not allowing safe spotting around the tree [c2]. Freezing it needs someone beyond Ancient magic, and the big Muttadile cannot really be frozen because of its very high magic level (needing a ZGS). In Challenge Mode at most scales the player just chops the tree while being hit [c3].
* Big Muttadile has an instant-kill melee attack and can combo for 60 or more in one hit. Jagex reduced combo potential by raising the minimum and lowering the maximum hit [c4]. The combined max hit of the baby's off-prayer ranged attack and Mama Muttadile's magic attack means the player can take 89 in a single game tick [c8]. Mama maxes 38 with any given attack, so even constantly brewing she can kill the player if she maxes each attack [c11 c16]. In 2024 he warns she can hit for 70 at any moment [c24].
* Mama does at least three mage attacks in a row before switching to ranged [c21 c32].
* Muttadiles are four-tick, so the player five-tick steps under it exactly like Nex [c15]. The trees work the same as the Ice Demon trees: every five ticks, as long as the player is chopping when it gets red [c14].
* If the Muttadile drops below 50% health it heals off the tree, which the player wants to avoid [c19 c26].

## Strategy

### Basic approach

Run up to the meat tree and chop it down while casting at the baby Muttadile until it is under 50% HP [c7]. In 2024 he runs straight to the tree, drops an item and chops; between each XP drop he throws a hammer-style attack at it (throwing at the baby does not interrupt chopping), and chopping damage depends on Woodcutting level [c18]. Chopping and attacking share the same five-tick cycle, so he casts on every XP drop, trying to get the Muttadile near 50% before the tree falls [c26]. Do not walk into melee range of the mother, who can one-shot [c21].

Prayer: pray melee when near Muttadile and range when far [c26], and pray melee when she gets close, expecting a lot of damage from random ranged attacks [c19]. Switch to Protect from Magic when the mother begins maging, since she guarantees at least three mage hits in a row [c21 c32].

After chopping the tree, he equips full mage gear and summons a thrall. The walk-under method with offensive prayer flicks reduces damage a little and mostly increases survival odds [c10]. With two throws then stepping under until off cooldown, the player takes one hit to two of his own [c20].

### Supplies

He brews until running low, then sweet tick eats every attack; when one of them falls he grabs brew and revite and moves on [c12]. Burning brews in the room can mean the difference between two and six brews for Olm, and it is completely random [c9]. He equips tank (melee) armour and just the Sanguinesti staff and brews a lot [c24]. When re-overloading, spam brew [c27]. He kills Muttadile first and sips the overload it drops immediately for the rest of the raid, so only one dose of a pre-raid potion is needed rather than a multi-dose [c17].

### Skips and tricks

* Tentacle skip: wait a tick before the room to run past the tentacles a bit faster [c5].
* Tendril skip: click to a spot so the tendril spawns behind the player, then run straight past it [c13]. In November 2024 he mentions it as an option but does not demonstrate it [c25].

### No-prep approach (2025)

In May 2025 he camps Augury for defence, flinches under the Muttadile and keeps health high, one-way mages, and throws on the Zamorak godsword to spec just before it falls below 50% health, freezing it [c28]. This saves brews in no-prep because both Muttadiles are frozen before they reach the tree and killed instead of being tanked throughout [c29]. For the mother, get to the corner quickly; he flicks Bandos to be tankier, which is optional [c30]. If the Zamorak godsword spec misses, stand on a particular tile because the mother gets stuck there when going back to eat the tree and cannot hit the player [c31].
