The **Wiki Guides** series is a challenge in which Gnomonkey completes boss content using the worst gear that the [[OSRS Wiki]]'s gear setup template suggests, deliberately in bad faith. He then redoes the boss with a better setup that spends the same amount of gp, to show where the wiki is misleading. [c36] To pick the improved setup, he takes the total budget of the bad setup, which is usually fairly high because of the poor suggestions, and spends it on a much easier setup. [c38]

He repeatedly stresses that the series is not an attack on the wiki: he considers it the best video game wiki he knows, and his criticism is aimed only at the gearing template and the table format, which he finds unhelpful outside the Max, Mid, Min, Iron and Pure tiers. [c5 c37 c39 c43] Some people were offended and he gets frequent comments claiming he hates the wiki. [c5 c39] He says he will not revise the wiki himself, because it takes a lot of time and people would revert anything he adds out of spite. [c43]

## 2024 run

The first run took place in May 2024 and ended on 1 June. By the end he called the Whisperer run the most interesting he did, and in the finale said it was likely his last wiki guide because they were becoming stale. [c31 c34]

## 2025 revival

In September 2025 the series returned because the Twitch September goal for Wiki Guides was reached; [[Yama]] was the first boss. [c35 c40] The next goals were a community day, a No Pillar Coliseum run and a possible PvP collaboration with Oda. [c40]

A viewer, Toma, pointed out that the wiki's revision history contains very old, terrible Vorkath setups, which led to a second Vorkath episode on 6 September using an even worse variant. [c41] Gnomonkey says digging through revision history for the worst variant would make any page look bad, so this is a fun concept rather than a criticism. [c42] On old setups, slots are often missing; his rule was that any empty slot stays empty, so he wore nothing in his glove, boot and helmet slots. [c44]
