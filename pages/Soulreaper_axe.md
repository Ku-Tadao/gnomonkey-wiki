The **Soulreaper axe** (also written Soul Reaper axe) is a stack-building slash weapon dropped in pieces by Vardorvis. Gnomonkey originally judged it a niche item: it takes roughly as long to acquire as a [[Scythe of Vitur]] yet was useful at only one location, Vardorvis, and he personally thought it should never have been added because the game already has many slash weapons and the axe does not fit in easily [c1 c2].

A string of changes has moved his verdict from sceptical to positive. Project Rebalance in April 2024 made the stacks far more forgiving, and he called the changes massive and said they made the axe usable in almost every location [c4 c6]. By June 2024 he rated it quite good because the stacks decay so slowly [c19], and after the July 2026 Summer Sweep-up rework he considers it far better and mainly much more usable [c36].

## Mechanics

The axe builds stacks as the wielder attacks, and each stack adds to damage. Stack handling has been changed several times:

| Date | Stack decay | Health effect | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dec 2023 | Stacks lost instantly when switching weapons; start decrementing after 10 ticks (6 seconds) | Taking stacks costs 8 hitpoints per swing for up to five swings (up to 40 health) | [c4 c23] |
| Apr 2024 (Project Rebalance) | No longer lost on switching; decrement after 20 ticks (12 seconds) without attacking | Natural decay heals the player for the damage taken while building the stacks; 8 health refunded per stack lost | [c4 c5 c12] |
| Jul 2026 (Summer Sweep-up) | Stacks start decrementing after 50 ticks, slightly longer than a [[Bloat]] down | Stacks no longer drain HP | [c30 c33] |

In the 2024 version the refund meant the wielder never lost health from decaying stacks, and the stacks keep decaying between [[Colosseum]] waves, which heals the player back [c12 c13]. After the 2026 update the special attack does not give health back, as a trade-off for the stacks no longer draining HP [c33].

The Summer Sweep-up gave the axe +4 strength bonus; the stack limit stays at five with 6% damage per stack [c29]. A 4 June 2026 announcement had listed a strength bonus rise to +125 from +121, +10% damage per stack (up from 6%) and a stack cap of 3 (down from 5). Gnomonkey read that as the same damage increase with a faster ramp time and less HP loss, and noted the axe has higher accuracy than the scythe [c27 c28]. The July 2026 claims, which keep five stacks at 6%, supersede the stack figures from that earlier announcement [c29].

### Special attack

The special attack now gains 12% accuracy per stack (up from 6%), reaching 60% accuracy at full stacks instead of 30%, and gains a 6% minimum hit per stack [c34]. With five stacks it guarantees a hit of 30% of the wielder's max hit, which Gnomonkey says makes it a very good finisher on Vardorvis. If the special fails to kill, the next hit builds a new stack that can be used immediately for a guaranteed 6% minimum hit [c35].

Earlier, he found he got somewhat less use out of the [[Saradomin godsword]] special while keeping the axe's stacks up, though he still considered the spec very useful [c14]. A [[Spec Overlay]] plugin shows special attack energy while the axe is equipped, since the weapon otherwise hides it; he kept the plugin on [c18]. Before the rework he had asked that the axe not hide current special attack energy [c3].

## Uses

* **Vardorvis.** Near best in slot, with [[Torva armour]] basically the only other upgrade [c9]. When Vardorvis first came out some people used the [[Inquisitor's mace]] because nobody had the axe yet and best in slot was not known [c8]. A kill with the budget setup took 125 seconds, around average for the axe [c10]. Players who spec at 50 health could previously not hit afterwards [c26].
* **Colosseum.** After the update the axe no longer triggers Blasphemy or Doom, so it becomes completely usable as a second fist [c7]. In June 2024 Gnomonkey placed it between the [[Abyssal tentacle]] (tent whip) and the Scythe: 11% better than the tent whip but costing about 400 mil, making it usable there [c11]. It drains run resources more slowly than the Scythe, and even less than the four-tick tent whip [c15]. Because stacks decay and heal between waves, the [[Sanguinesti staff]]'s start-of-wave healing is rarely needed [c13]. It is very close to the Scythe on [[Sol Heredit]] [c20]. After the 2026 update, stacks are not lost between Colosseum waves [c31].
* **Theatre of Blood.** The slower decay means stacks are not lost in Sotetseg maze phases or Bloat downs [c31].
* **Ulm.** Stacks will probably still be lost between phases on Ulm's melee hand, but quick-switching to melee between mage hits on the next mage hand can maintain them [c32].
* **Tick-eating.** The 2024 changes opened setups such as one axe swing on the P1 obelisk to tick eat the orbs [c6].
* **Phantom Muspah.** In September 2025 he said the wiki-gear setup using the axe was not as bad as expected and that he was impressed it works better than expected, though he noted the axe is extremely expensive and did not know who would use the setup [c21 c22].

The [[Frailty]] invocation is noticeably worse than usual with the axe, because it prevents banking health from stacks, so he avoided taking it as long as possible [c16 c17].

## Gnomonkey's opinion

At release he said the axe should not be buffed to stomp on the Scythe's toes, yet arguably should be given how rare it is; at the least he wanted stacks not lost on weapon swaps and the weapon not to hide special energy [c3]. Both requests were addressed by Project Rebalance and later changes [c4]. In May 2026 he noted that the axe takes about 3,000 Desert Treasure II boss kills on average to obtain, almost as long as a scythe, and that it is worse than the [[Noxious halberd]] in many situations [c24]. He suggested the axe reach full power at two charges instead of five while still banking up to five, so bosses with downtime such as Bloat or Sotetseg's maze do not wipe its stacks, and so using the special for quick HP is less punishing [c25 c26].

After the July 2026 rework he names the best places to use it as the Maggot King and now also Theatre of Blood and the Colosseum, where it was questionable before [c36]. He wondered whether it would beat the scythe at places like the Duke or Vardorvis [c28].
