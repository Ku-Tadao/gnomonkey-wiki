Zulrah is a mid-level boss that Gnomonkey has covered with guides for max-efficiency mage/ranged switching (2022), budget Atlatl setups (2025) and, after Jagex made her meleeable, a halberd setup (May 2025). In 2022 he called her no longer the money boss she once was; in 2025 he describes her as easy and free to kill with several cheap setups.

## Mechanics

- Zulrah has four rotations: a mage phase, a green phase and two red phases; every kill starts with a green phase and then differs ([2025-04-24 2:20](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=140)). Gnomonkey recommends memorising rotations to pre-switch gear, using the Zulrah helper plugin (see [RuneLite plugins](RuneLite_plugins.md)) while learning and dropping it around 50 KC ([2025-04-24 6:22](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=382)). The RuneLite side panel shows where she will be ([2025-05-07 7:29](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=449)).
- Max hit against her is capped at 50, and any hit above 50 is re-rolled into a 45 to 50 hit ([2022-04-15 3:08](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=188)). Her blue-phase magic attack maxes a 41 ([2022-04-15 3:55](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=235)); he notes her maximum hit is 41 and says to always hover food on her ([2025-04-24 8:50](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=530)).
- The blue-phase attack can technically be tick-eaten, but the previous projectile has not landed before the next is fired, so the second hit reads health from before the first (at 45 HP taking a 25, the second can still hit a 41). He advises choosing intelligently between tick eating and combo eating ([2022-04-15 3:55](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=235)).
- Snakelings are magic or melee; when Zulrah is not attacking and snakelings are out, ideally pray Protect from Magic ([2022-04-15 3:27](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=207)). In the green phase that spawns snakelings, pray mage rather than range ([2025-04-24 14:44](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=884)). If both snakelings are mage-type, the mage prayer can stay up without switching ([2025-05-07 11:32](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=692)).
- In the mage phase he stands in the middle of the island so some mage snakelings get stuck ([2025-04-24 8:22](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=502)). The orange (tanky) phase has the lowest damage output but mage damage against it is much better, hence a mage bonus and boots switch ([2025-04-24 8:41](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=521)).
- Killing Zulrah in the short green phase before she dives gets the Grandmaster Combat Achievement speed kill ([2022-04-15 7:00](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=420)).

## Max-efficiency mage and ranged method (2022)

- Gear: max mage gear with a [Harmonised nightmare staff](Harmonised_nightmare_staff.md) or Volatile nightmare staff; without a Harmonised staff, thralls and a [Sanguinesti staff](Sanguinesti_staff.md) give similar kills per hour, with an Arcane spirit shield offhand ([2022-04-15 0:32](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=32)). Ring of suffering is camped; Archers and Seers ring switches are possible but harder and mainly for PvP ([2022-04-15 1:01](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=61)). Without a Volatile staff, blowpipe specs give similar DPS ([2022-04-15 1:01](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=61)). The inventory has an 8-way range switch; [Barrows gloves](Barrows_gloves.md) instead of Zaryte vambraces is a very small loss ([2022-04-15 1:01](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=61)).
- The blowpipe used to beat the [Twisted bow](Twisted_bow.md) on blue-face Zulrah but now the bow wins; the pipe is still used for pillar stalling and extra hits ([2022-04-15 1:01](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=61)). Dragon arrows are preferred as they raise the chance of landing 45 to 50 ([2022-04-15 3:27](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=207)). In his setup the Harmonised staff maxes a 49 on a four-tick attack, far better for accuracy and speed than the bow ([2022-04-15 3:08](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=188)). The Volatile staff special uses 55% energy, is very accurate and maxes an 80; he uses it on red phase ([2022-04-15 3:27](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=207)).
- Supplies: two Divine ranging potions and two Anti-venom+, each potted only when useful; no prayer potions ([2022-04-15 1:27](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=87)). Also Gothics rest items for tick eating, carbon bonds for combo eating, and a [Max cape](Max_cape.md) (replace with food otherwise) ([2022-04-15 1:27](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=87)). Desert amulet for restores, Zulrah's teleport scrolls, and an [Imbued heart](Imbued_heart.md), a minor boost on red Zulrah ([2022-04-15 1:58](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=118)). Law, Fire, Air and Wrath runes; Air runes stay out of the pouch because the Harmonised staff burns through them (16,000 can run out in a session), and Law runes teleport to the Seers' Village bank ([2022-04-15 1:58](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=118)). A fire rune on hand stops autocast being removed from the Harmonised staff ([2022-04-15 2:26](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=146)). Scales can be carried to stack them, and two slots left spare for loot ([2022-04-15 2:26](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=146)).
- Opening: Preserve on during the run to the boat, Imbued heart, special primed; Augury after the boat, right-click Zulrah when the cutscene ends and use the Volatile spec ([2022-04-15 4:21](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=261)). If the spec is forgotten it can be used in the first phase, which gives four Harmonised casts and one spec ([2022-04-15 4:52](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=292)). The first phase transition is where ticks are most often lost: Rigor on with range switch hovered for blue, Augury for red (50% of the time), and for green he tanks the first hit to proc [Vengeance](Vengeance.md) ([2022-04-15 4:52](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=292)).
- Green rotation: take the first venge, attack, pray Protect from Magic on snakelings; the Volatile spec barely recovers as she goes under, so it is primed and used on red ([2022-04-15 5:23](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=323)). She typically dies on red or earlier on green; blue is the hardest phase for lost ticks and needs Rigor primed and a fast switch to ranged ([2022-04-15 5:56](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=356)). With clean timing two blowpipe hits fit in blue; otherwise one pipe hit then back to the bow ([2022-04-15 6:29](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=389)). Blue example: six attacks before she dives ([2022-04-15 6:29](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=389)). In the first blue phase, two blowpipe shots then a Twisted bow shot fits, the bow landing on the last tick before submerging ([2022-03-19 6:48](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=408)).
- Red Zulrah has two diverging paths; he covers south green rather than west first, and red pokes twice before diving ([2022-04-15 7:00](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=420)). After the final cast he runs through the fog to the pillar corner switching to ranged, taking a little poison damage to fit four Twisted bow shots by pillar stalling (bow, behind the pillar, pipe shot, bow, repeat for four attacks) ([2022-04-15 7:27](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=447)). If she has not damaged him by her third attack, he drops Protect from Magic and tanks the last hit on purpose, because venge will not proc if she appears west ([2022-04-15 7:27](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=447)). After the fourth shot she comes up south: run across switching to magic, use the Volatile spec, she comes up red, pokes twice, then blue (Twisted bow) ([2022-04-15 8:00](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=480)). On the west, keep praying mage while she only spawns snakelings ([2022-04-15 8:00](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=480)).
- Between kills: teleport to Narda on an alt account, drop loot, use the altar, have the alt cast Vengeance, and teleport back with a scroll ([2022-04-15 6:59](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=419)).

## Budget Atlatl setup (2025)

- A mid-game guide for Ironmen starting Zulrah that also works on mains ([2025-04-24 0:00](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=0)). He uses magic ([Twinflame staff](Twinflame_staff.md)) on green or red phases and the [Eclipse atlatl](Eclipse_atlatl.md) otherwise ([2025-04-24 6:22](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=382)). Kills take roughly 120 to 140 ticks, usually two kills per trip (sometimes one or three), enough to maintain scrolls; with a Bofa, two to three kill trips give effectively unlimited scrolls ([2025-04-24 13:18](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=798)) ([2025-04-24 13:45](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=825)).
- Without teleport scrolls, bank with a dual ring and use fairy ring BJS; health can be restored at the fairy ring pool ([2025-04-24 4:19](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=259)). The stepping stone on the route requires 76 Agility, and a summer pie pre-pot can allow the jump ([2025-04-24 4:49](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=289)).
- He says the Twinflame staff makes Zulrah much easier, a totally different boss, especially for mid-game Irons ([2025-04-24 24:30](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=1470)). The [Gearscape](Gearscape.md) calculator can show all phases via its groups tab ([2025-04-24 26:18](https://youtu.be/bBnPK55ZqUM?t=1578)).
- In February 2025 he said the [Atlatl](Atlatl.md) is about 14% worse than the Bofa at Zulrah; the burn effect is lost when she dives, making it perhaps 20% effective and about 12% worse overall ([2025-02-22 16:10](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=970)). Since a player uses mage about half the time, the Atlatl suffices without a roughly 12 million Bofa ([2025-02-22 16:10](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=970)) ([2025-02-22 16:40](https://youtu.be/NxfbgWY3ZQk?t=1000)).

## Melee with halberds (May 2025)

- Jagex changed Zulrah so she can be meleed with halberds; she has zero melee defence ([2025-05-07 0:00](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=0)). Gnomonkey says a [Noxious halberd](Noxious_halberd.md) melee approach has basically replaced camping a single style such as Bofa, Twisted bow or Shadow ([2025-05-07 0:00](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=0)). The halberd does the same damage on every phase since it ignores her defences, so long red phases are no problem ([2025-05-07 7:29](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=449)) ([2025-05-07 9:02](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=542)).
- Mid-level setup: Serpentine helm, Bandos, Barrows gloves, Dragon boots, Ring of suffering; upgrades or downgrades remain good ([2025-05-07 0:26](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=26)). Pharaoh's gloves are amazing on a main, a [rancour](Amulet_of_rancour.md) is much better on an Iron, and [Torva](Torva_armour.md) is insane ([2025-05-07 0:59](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=59)). Amulet of blood fury is great and extends trips, which are limited mostly by restores; rancour and torture are also good ([2025-05-07 14:05](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=845)). Inventory: divine super combats and a few restores, no anti-venom with the Serpentine helm ([2025-05-07 1:33](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=93)).
- Rings: a Bellator ring until snakelings spawn, then the Ring of suffering, worth two max hits; switch back when they are gone, and put it on whenever snakelings hit ([2025-05-07 2:01](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=121)) ([2025-05-07 4:06](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=246)) ([2025-05-07 12:36](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=756)). Bellator over Ultor since max hit matters less with the hit cap ([2025-05-07 14:33](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=873)). Echo boots recoil in a 3x3 and kill snakelings, so the suffering ring is not needed with them ([2025-05-07 14:33](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=873)). He is unsure about the [Lightbearer](Lightbearer.md) but thinks it probably fine since the player is over the hit cap ([2025-05-07 14:05](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=845)).
- Kills typically take 110 to 140 ticks, occasionally 145 to 150, with 86 and 101 seen; he nearly got a Grandmaster time with the cheap setup if the Crystal halberd spec hits three 100s ([2025-05-07 8:33](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=513)). Four, almost five kills with seven food left; a six or seven kill trip is probable ([2025-05-07 12:36](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=756)). He estimates about six fewer kills per hour than a maxed Twisted bow plus Harmonised staff switch setup ([2025-05-07 10:06](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=606)).

## Profit, drops and opinion

- April 2022 figures: about 35 kills an hour in a good hour versus about 25 with Twisted bow camping; a kill worth 122k gives 4.3m an hour, or about 3.5m GP/hr after 35 scrolls (about 800k) ([2022-04-15 8:59](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=539)). He says he does Zulrah for the mutagens and pet, not profit ([2022-04-15 8:59](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=539)).
- He says she is no longer the "Money Snake": since the blowpipe nerf profit dropped and [Vorkath](Vorkath.md) has effectively taken her place; she is still a common Twisted bow rebuild boss and has the untradeable Magma and Tanzanite mutagens ([2022-04-15 0:00](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=0)). Mutagens are 1/3,277 for the table or 1/6,553 per colour; with Torva he calls her kind of dead content and cares less about mutagens, though still without one ([2022-04-15 9:26](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=566)).
- The pet is 1/4,000, the only pet at that rarity he knows of, about a 115 hour grind at 35 kills an hour ([2022-03-12 11:59](https://youtu.be/vwCm9NKJhRE?t=719)) ([2022-04-15 9:26](https://youtu.be/YR0HB76BSPU?t=566)). He rated it low B tier (many gold farmers have it, now a grind) ([2022-03-12 11:59](https://youtu.be/vwCm9NKJhRE?t=719)).
- In the March 2022 PvM boss tier list he placed Zulrah in high B tier, with plenty going on (hit counts before she dives, volatile staff specs for the melee phase, eight-way switches) but not A tier ([2022-03-19 6:14](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=374)) ([2022-03-19 7:15](https://youtu.be/HkVt4enEesI?t=435)).
- In May 2025 he called kills simple and told viewers to go kill Zulrah because it is free and easy ([2025-05-07 15:29](https://youtu.be/ecd_a9E8l5E?t=929)).
