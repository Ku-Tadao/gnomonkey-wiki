**Burning claws** are a special attack weapon similar to Dragon claws, but they apply a burn and had a lower special attack cost of 30% until 2026 [c2 c26 c43]. Ironmen need two Burning claws to combine them into the full set [c1]. They are unique among spec weapons in that they can spec on either stab or slash, which makes them good in unusual situations [c3]. The Tormented Demon grind is how they are obtained [c35].

Gnomonkey's verdict changed over time. In 2024 and 2025, after extensive testing, he calls them excellent and often better than Dragon claws; in mid-2026 he agrees they were overtuned, accepts the nerf and says they are now an early spec weapon outclassed by others [c25 c44 c48 c49].

## Mechanics

They apply a burn (45% chance to proc on spec) with a 30% spec cost at the time of the 2024 videos [c26]. Against demon-type monsters they get a slight 5% damage buff, usually about two max hits [c4]. They take longer to spec with than normal claws because of the low spec cost, so the main-hand weapon is swung a bit less; Dragon claws equal out when the main hand is around 14 DPS [c22]. Overall they are 2-6% better than the places Dragon claws would be used, unless the target has low HP or extremely low defence [c5]. He guesses they max 42 against the Scythe's 50 but are close, without having calced it [c28].

Special attack style is changeable: they can go on stab or slash, and because most of Tombs of Amascut is weak to stab, stab makes a super accurate special on every boss there [c33]. A remark in June 2025 puts the quirk differently, saying the special itself uses only one style [c35].

## Where they are used

### Tombs of Amascut

In 2024 he calls them the best spec weapon in Tombs of Amascut until raid level 375, extremely high value at around 20 to 23 mil [c29]. They must be on stab, as most things are weak to stab over slash, or they will be very bad [c30]. He prefers them to Dragon claws because Dragon claws do not spec using slash defence while Burning claws can [c31]. By 2025 he calls them amazing against Baba, Kephri and Akkha, with best possible spec DPS until above raid level 375, where Akkha is avoided [c36]. At a 300 raid level they cost about 10 million and beat a Voidwaker, and he recommends bringing them [c34].

| Boss | Notes |
|---|---|
| Kephri | Specs hit a consistent 40; the red hitsplats are burn damage dealing an additional 10 over time [c32]. He drops a brew, instantly specs, then summons a thrall; the claws hit well even on her fairly high defence [c37] |
| Akkha | Amazing, but Akkha jumps as soon as they are used, so he recommends two Keris pokes first, which makes him jump around less [c38]. Good on his shadows but not the final phase, because the burn makes him jump [c7] |
| Warden | He swaps to slash since Warden has no weakness and the claws have more slash bonus (95) than stab (83) [c39]. With adrenaline potions they allow many specs, which he calls incredibly strong [c40]. In 2024 he said they were not great here because of extremely high defence breakpoints [c8] |
| Ba-Ba, Zebak | Good on Ba-Ba; on Zebak only if a Zaryte crossbow is not available, and not great [c6] |
| Core | Significantly worse than a Dragon dagger special: lower max hit and the burn does not apply [c8] |

In the 2024 videos he rated them good in ToA for anything under a 400 invocation with defence drain [c6].

### Theatre of Blood

Technically better at Theatre of Blood but bugged: the burn is halved and rounded down to zero when Bloat stands up and runs around, and the Nylocas Vasilias is completely immune [c10]. They are very good on Sotetseg at all drains and beat Dragon claws, since leftover spec is usually available and Chally is poor at finishing Sotetseg [c11]. On Xarpus the burn triggers his turn and he kills you after Screech, so they should not be used unless the team fully drains him, and then they only match Dragon claws' DPS [c12]. On Verzik they should not be used in P1 even though the burn hits her; in P2 she heals the burn damage with red crabs, so timing matters; in P3 they are really good, especially post-tornadoes [c13]. Overall they are worth bringing in addition to Dragon claws but not replacing them, and absolutely do not replace the Chally for its last-hit value [c14].

### Other content

* **Colosseum:** best in slot by about 5% over Dragon claws, especially on Soul where there is leftover spec, and all mobs have gigantic health pools so the burn ticks [c16]. On Sol Heredit they give a bonus spec at the end of the kill, and in 2024 he considered them directly better than Dragon claws while expecting a nerf [c27].
* **Vardorvis:** best in slot, with the spec best used around the beginning and middle of the kill [c19].
* **Vorkath:** a simple melee camp walk gave his first-attempt Grandmaster time, a 53.4 second personal best against a typical 60 [c20]. Triple speccing stacks the burn, though it is halved during the acid phase and nullified during the freeze, and they are still the best option [c21]. In September 2025 he calls them an amazing spec on the 50M budget setup, basically a max claw spec [c41 c42].
* **Chambers of Xeric:** the Zaryte crossbow and Voidwaker remain king because of high defences and nothing being weak to slash or stab; they were awful on Vasa Cristallus [c9].
* **Inferno:** not as good, since nothing lives long enough for the burn, and claws exist to finish targets quickly [c15].
* **Nex:** defence too high and too much healing; ranked below the Voidwaker and far below the Zaryte crossbow, and testing there dragged on too long [c17].
* **Corporeal Beast and Sarachnis:** use a Voidwaker spec at Corp, the only thing not reduced by his mechanics. Sarachnis is completely immune to burn despite being a demon, which he calls an oversight [c18].
* **Alternatives:** a good spec if the Voidwaker is not yet owned or Wilderness content is being avoided on an Ironman, but wherever a Zaryte crossbow is used they will not beat it [c23].

## Release and nerf

After release there was controversy, with some calling them bad and some good, and he notes an untested item at the start of an update is volatile [c24]. He tested them eight-plus hours a day in every location, outside of calcs that were not yet updated, lacked proper formulas or were bugged, and with Mo Goat Kirby concluded they are good [c2 c25].

In the Summer Sweep-up the special attack cost rose to 35% from 30% [c43 c47]. He agrees they were overtuned: very good in Tombs of Amascut and the Colosseum and a sidegrade to Dragon claws when they were supposed to be a worse stepping stone, and they were better than the Voidwaker up to invocation level 375, which felt strange [c44 c45]. He thinks the nerf is justified but sad for people who liked using them everywhere, especially early on an ironman [c46]. Regular claws, and probably the Voidwaker, are now better nearly all the time [c48]. He enjoyed using them everywhere, for example super good in early Tombs of Amascut, and they are now an early spec weapon that gets outclassed by others [c49].
